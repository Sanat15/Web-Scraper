The new co-author, Timothy Trull, a specialist in psychopathology, clinical assessment, and personality disorders from the University of Missouri-Columbia, has enhanced the book's readability, modernized its content, condensed and brightened its presentation, and added significant new material. In addition to thoroughly updating the discussion of clinical psychology, this new edition sharpens the focus on empirically validated assessments and interventions. This edition presents the wide diversity that exists within the clinical psychology field regarding theoretical perspectives and assessment-research approaches.
