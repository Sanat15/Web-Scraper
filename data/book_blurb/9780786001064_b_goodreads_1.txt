By day, he is a fashionable Boston gentleman. Come night, he is Midnight Rider -- a masked avenger on a mission for justice and liberty. Until one woman forces him to choose between freedom and love...

She is Laura Chandler, the daughter of powerful Tory sympathizers. For her, he will risk death at the hands of his treacherous British enemies. For him, she will brave scandal and ruin to play a role in a desperate charade. Now, as revolution simmers across the land, they will bare their most intimate secrets... as a forbidden passion ignites in wild, unquenchable love...
