PRIDE AND PREJUDICE

With his old-world values and passionate nature, Dominic Lorenzo had built a fortune by keeping three things constant: work, family and pride. He had thrown over convention only once, in the untraditional way he'd obtained custody of his young son. But now he was up against Fiona Evans. The beautiful woman was challenging him and stirring his soul. Would she also discover his deepest secret?

Dominic's fiercely attractive appearance and constant attention threatened to lure Mona into ignoring the awful truth--that Dominic had kidnapped his little boy away from the child's heartbroken mother. Yes, Dominic's hard, masterful embrace could make Fiona forget anything... but would she ever be able to forgive?
