In the Black Mountains there’s nothing but snow. But it’s there that the magical blue lily grows, which can help Lily Quench stop the evil Black Count from invading her homeland. With her friend Queen Dragon, Lily embarks on a perilous mission to bring the blue lily back to Ashby. Captured and imprisoned, then befriended by the count’s son, they flee to the eerie heights of Dragon’s Downfall... the place where Lily must confront her greatest fear.
