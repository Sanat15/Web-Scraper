In this magical book, you'll find lots of ideas for making fairies and other fairyland creatures. To transform yourself into a dazzling fairy, just follow the simple steps to make sparkling fairy wings, a silver tiara and a glittering wand. You'll also find out how to make fairy puppets, fairy cards, a fairy door sign and lots more. When you've finished, you can decorate what you've made with lots of shiny silver stickers from the middle pages of this book. 32 pages.
