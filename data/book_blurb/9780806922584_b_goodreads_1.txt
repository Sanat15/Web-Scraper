Enjoy your own personal decorative painting workshop with the world-recognized master of the form. Through her media appearances and seminars, Priscilla Hauser has become the craft's ambassador and a much sought-after teacher. If you don't have five days to attend her class, do the book version of her "quick-fix" program at your convenience. Try Hauser's unique approach to brush strokes and proper preparation of surfaces, and learn her distinctive pen-and-ink and fabric painting techniques. Then you're ready for 14 charming projects that take just a few hours to complete, including Fish on a Tin Tub, Sunflowers Coat Rack, Blueberries Ensemble, Garden Window, Ladybug Garden Set, Seashells Headboard, Leaf Bordered Picture Frame, and Woodland Ferns Table. A Main Selection of the Crafter's Choice Book Club.
