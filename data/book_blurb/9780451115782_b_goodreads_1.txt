On those days when you'd just like to see a happy face, let the world-famous cartoon character Ziggy remind you that pets are friends you like who like you right back.

Whether you're a cat lover, dog lover, bird, turtle, or pet-rock fancier, Ziggy and his little pals will keep you entertained with their wacky adventures, as they cope with everything from visits to the vet to vacations at the beach.

Tom Wilson, writer, illustrator, painter, and creator of Ziggy, is also the vice-president of the Creative Division of American Greetings Corporation.
