Author:  Stefan Zweig and Ernst Wiechert translated by Cornelia Fuykschot  The Eyes of My Brother, Forever and The White Buffalo - These two novellas demonstrate in their comparison to today's society that civilization will never learn all the answers; that justice and injustice are separated by a line so fine that those who dispense it must themselves be experienced in life on both sides of the punishment system in order to understand the gravity of a sentence; and that true power is not owned by the powerful but by those whose actions are deemed powerful by others.
