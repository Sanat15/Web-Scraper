Jill and Tom Douglas were the happiest couple in the world. They inherited a great big mansion and were expecting their first baby. 

Then one day Jill started seeing things-like a young girl who should have been dead. And hearing things-like a baby crying in the empy nursery. 

Now Jill is very scared. No one believes her. But Jill knows there is something in the house. Something dark. Something evil. 

And it wants her unborn child.
