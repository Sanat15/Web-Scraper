ZEKE -- An Arrogant Outdoorsman Whose Mouth Made Her Want to Melt — He frowned as if the smoky Saratoga Springs nightclub was the last place on earth he wanted to be, but Zeke North was imagining how it would feel to make love to a woman whose hands created such sensual pleasure! Chelsea Connors was a black-haired siren -- in a wicked white dress, a seductive angel whose playing could drive a man mad with yearning, but he didn't want her involved in his brother's trouble.

 He Played Her as She Played the Piano, All Passion and Fire

 Zeke made her breath catch, her body tremble, and her spirit long for the music they'd made together, but Chelsea ached to share his fight, to soothe old sorrows. He'd never taken anything from anyone before, never confessed what he needed for fear of losing his soul. She'd touched him where he kept his fears, but could she finally hold him by giving a love so deep, he'd have no choice but to take it?
