Includes thorough information for gardeners of all skill levels on propagating and cultivating popular varieties of geraniums. The book also shows you how to use scented geraniums to flavor baked goods and make lovely topiaries, dried arrangements, and potpourri. With scents of rose, lemon, ginger, and chocolate mint, the pelargoniums are easy to grow and will enrich your life with fragrance. Scented geraniums recall the Victorian era of gracious living. Lining a walkway, they enchant passers by with their heady aroma. Inside, growing on a windowsill, they infuse a home with their scents.
