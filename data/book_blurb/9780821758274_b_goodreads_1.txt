BOUND BY DUTY
With her parents long-dead, Lady Charlotte had devoted herself to raising her only sibling Sydney, the Duke of Audley. But when the callow youth developed a tendre for the most unsuitable young woman, Charlotte turned to Sir Roland March for help. Surely the handsome, worldly-wise duke must have navigated the sometimes treacherous waters of love, and would guide her inexperienced brother. And yet, Charlotte had given no thought to her own heart...and her unexpected, ardent response to Sir Roland. 

FREED BY LOVE  
Sir Roland had watched as his lovely neighbor managed burdens that would overset many a lady of more mature years. To look after Sydney, Charlotte had denied herself much--a London season and her chance to shine as the belle of her year. But as the duke had also noticed, Charlotte was still young, still beautiful, and most of all, desirable. Now, the time had come to convince Charlotte that her duty to her brother was done, to think of her own happiness...and the Man who yearned to make her his beloved.
