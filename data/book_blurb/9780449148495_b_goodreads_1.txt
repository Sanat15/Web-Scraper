Psychiatrist/author Harry Kline's latest book touches off a masacre by a deranged Vietnam veteran. And the book soars to the top of the bestseller list. Then a chilling fan letter from a serial killer invites Harry to join a dance of death--by chronicling the killer's carnage in a new book. Harry plunges into a desperate manhunt, racing to decipher taunting clues--as new victims are sadistically butchered. And the promised finale to the spree will thrust Harry into a deadly confrontation with the face of violent madness. It will be a session from hell he'll remember for as long--or as briefly--as he lives...
