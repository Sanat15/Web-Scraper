Award-winning creator Keith Moseley pulls out all the stops in this inventive, educational, and just plain fun 3-D book. Featuring popular and classic models--including Harleys, Nortons, Hondas, Indians, Triumphs, and more--this multi-dimensional book includes fascinating text, detailed illustrations, striking photos, and five pop-up spreads.

Stand-ups depict classic motorcycles, including the 1967 Norton Commando, 1995 Daytona Super III, Ducati 916 SP, 1995 Kawasaki 16v ZXR750, and 1948 Harley-Davidson 45.
