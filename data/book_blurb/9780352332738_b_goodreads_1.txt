A casual meeting draws Pascal into one of his long - held fantasies. He surrenders his mundane life to follow the mysterious and alluring Casper to Tarne, an isolated community where slavery thrives. At first he is everything a slave should be, and willing so - until he begins to sense a sinister aspect to Casper's magnetism. Tarne is everything he has dreamed  a place where his true nature, his need to be debased and owned, can finally be satisfied. But is his safety - and the safety of those he has grown to love - really under threat? And what indignities will he suffer before he's ready to give up the pleasures of submission?
