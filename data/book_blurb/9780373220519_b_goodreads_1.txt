WHO IS THAT SEXY GUY?

Name: Matthew Raleton - He never met a dollar--or a woman--he couldn't make.

Experience: English lord and international investor--dripping in money...and sex		appeal.

Objective: Prove his stepbrother's death was no accident. When he rescues beautiful American Diana MacKenzie, Matthew finds his first suspect--one who claims she's his stepbrother's wife. But Matthew can spot a fake at fifty paces. Too bad the passion she stirs in him is the real thing--the forever kind that threatens to make a marrying man out of this wealthy bachelor....
