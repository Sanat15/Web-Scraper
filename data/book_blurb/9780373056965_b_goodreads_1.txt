Name: Prospero "Roe" Hunter
Retreat: The seclusion and solitude of the sleepy Mediterranean seaside.
Uninvited Guest: Virginia Potter a.k.a. Gingie. The American beauty with a voice that could stir the soul of even the most unwilling man.

After juggling the crises of his extended family, Roe hunter was looking forward to the peace and tranquility of the isolated island of Sontara. But then Gingie arrived ....

The sexy songstress with a reputation for creating chaos was a reminder of the world Roe was desperate to forget. But she was an enigma he couldn't ignore. Her mix of childlike innocence and womanly wisdom threatened to turn the quiet town topsy-turvy...and wreak havoc on Roe's heart!
