NO MAN AND WOMAN HAD EVER HATED SO FIERCELY ... AND LOVED SO RECKLESSLY ...

AILIS ... As wild and beautiful as her fiery Scottish heritage, she faced the torment of marriage to a man she despised. Then, without warning, she was kidnapped by her worst enemy ...

ALEXANDER ... As strong and brutally handsome as he was arrogant, he plotted against her beloved family. Now Ailis was his to use for revenge ... to ravish with his eyes ... to inflame with his touch.

Their hatred was passionate. Their desire was dangerous. Their surrender to a forbidden love was ... Reckless.
