A DREAM SO VIVID...
The young Egyptian queen first appeared to Catherine in a dream . . . an exotic beauty clad in flowing robes and sandalwood beads, watching over the waters of the Nile for her returning lover.

The dream gave way to reality when Catherine was chosen to join David Pallister's archaeological expedition. A noted archaeologist, Catherine was thrilled by the chance to uncover the tomb of the mystical Queen Alifa. But the closer they came to the discovery, the more David saw beneath Catherine's cool veneer, and he was determined to unleash the warm, passionate woman within. So began the search for remnants of past treasure, answers to ancient puzzles and a love that would endure beyond forever.
