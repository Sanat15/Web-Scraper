The last thing Kat Thistlewait needed was another scandal..so marry she must!

Though no stranger to scandal, Kat vowed to be the pinnacle of propriety this season to ensure that her brother and sister made suitable matches. She herself was content to wait for true love.
Fate intervened, however, when Kat rushed to save her mischievous twin from jeopardy - only to land herself in a decidedly compromising position with a French nobleman . . .
