Who Will Teach Me? has been compared to a religious version of Dr. Spock. The approach used in this book is psychologically oriented, but presented in a way that any parent can understand. The author believes strongly that Jesus came not to impose a law but to teach us how to enjoy the freedom of God's children and in the process become beautiful people. Jesus' religion was people oriented and that was the secret of his great popularity. This book has captured the real spirit of Jesus, and if people follow the guidelines set forth in this book, they will go far in molding their children into really beautiful people fashioned after the pattern of Christ.
