Rosalyn Delaney's husband vanished six years ago. Following a private detective's lead, she arrives at Rose House, an old mansion reputed to be haunted, only to discover her missing husband has been killed, and his business partner is wanted for his murder. When Rosalyn decides to stay to uncover the secrets surrounding the murder, a charming "ghost" attempts to scare her away. But she doesn't rattle easily, and the man behind the hauntings may just steal her heart.
