This book shows how numerology explains and brings harmony to human relationships for young and old, helps in solving personal problems, and promotes right action for all endeavors. Everyone has a Personal Year Number which exerts a continuing influence through the lifetime. The author delineates general trends for each year of the nine personal years, and within each year discusses the guidelines for every month. The author Juno Jordan is internationally recognized as one of the outstanding numerologists of the present time. For over fifty years she had developed her time entirely to the science of numbers in the areas of research and counseling.
