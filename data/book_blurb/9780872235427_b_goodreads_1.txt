A NOVEL OF MURDEROUS OBSESSION.

Norman Weedon was not your usual antique dealer. He was big and handsome, with eyes so blue that women found him irresistible.
THE IDEA came to him after his first wife's automobile accident netted him $50,000. This went a long way toward soothing his grief.
Norman found this a terrific inspiration. In fact, he was soon putting together another "accident" for another unwary wife.
Then a lovely young thing at the IRS-Samantha caught on to what he was doing.
And the hunter became the hunted....
