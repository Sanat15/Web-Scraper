Nicole Stewart's refined life had not prepared her for the likes of brawny Aaron Wilde, the uncivilized he-man sailing her to the Bahamas. Nor did it prepare her for shipwreck--on an uncharted isle--in the Bermuda Triangle! With her clothes and composure ravaged beyond repair, her sole source of comfort was this bare-chested Wilde man!

Aaron had long ago left high society behind, and he was none too pleased to be stranded with this prim prima donna. Then again, a slightly bedraggled Nicole was kind of adorable ....

Primitive passion took the twosome by storm, scattering pride and prejudice to the winds. Their island idyll couldn't last, but would Nicole breeze back to her sheltered existence once she'd sampled sweet freedom in Aaron's arms?
