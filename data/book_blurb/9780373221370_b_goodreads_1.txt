Little Jenny was missing

And Leigh Chandler, a kindergarten teacher, felt the dreadful echoes from her recent past return. Were the same forces from that previous experience at work? One moment Jenny Buchanon was skiing with classmates at the lavish Winterglow Resort in Colorado. The next, she'd vanished.

Leigh feared the worst. But this time, she had Rick Morgan for solace, for support. Rick, a doctor attending a medical convention at the resort, was also the missing girl's uncle, and together they set about the task of locating the five-year-old, even as bitter cold seeped in from the north. In this game, they learned, only two hearts could defy the odds; only two hearts could battle the evil that lurked in the icy fairy-tale world of Winterglow....
