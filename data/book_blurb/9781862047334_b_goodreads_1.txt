Get set for the future with this complete and practical guide to Planetary Cycles and their significance in your personal life and to everything around you. With absolutely no calculations to perform, this book offers you insight, support and awareness with regard to the critical stages of your life  Your emotional life-The Cycle of the Moon, revealing the ebb and flow, the meaning and direction of your emotional life. It also helps you to recast your past and come to terms with it. Your growth and development-The Cycle of Jupiter, informing you of your "Go-Zones" or when and where your opportunities for advancement and understanding occur. Your status and responsibilities-The Cycle of Saturn, setting out the nature of the School of Life and how to successfully follow your course through it. Your pattern of change and chance-Your Uranus Cycle, showing you how the coincidences and unexpected events in your life are clues to your talents and the ways to a more stimulating life. Addictions and inspirations-Your Neptune Cycle, helping to dispel the confusions that haunt you and lead you towards a more enlightened sense of self and the meaning of life. Endings and beginnings-Your Pluto Cycle, making known those important times when one door closes and another one opens-letting go of the past and embracing the future.
