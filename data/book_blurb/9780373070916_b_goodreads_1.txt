A SEASON FOR SURPRISES .. .Mistaken impressions were only the first barrier to love for Season Ashford and Gard Kincannon. Season's ex-husband thought he had hired Gard to keep an eye on Season, but he couldn't have been more wrong. Gard's only reason for watching Season was a very personal one.Unfortunately, Gard was a cop and involved in some pretty risky business, and his relationship with Season put not only both their hearts in jeopardy but her life as well!
