Sara would have traded her fortune for some privacy...

 Sara Chance was a jet-setter. As the wealthy widow of the beloved Maxwell Chance, she led a glamorous, opulent life. Yet Sara was miserable. All the money in the world couldn't make up for her not being able to appear in public unrecognized. But then Sam Berenger came into her life.

 He seemed to be the perfect man, but then strange things began to happen. Pages of Max's personal journal were missing. His coveted Grammy award was gone. And then Sara's diamond engagement ring disappeared. As the danger mounted, Sara turned to Sam, only to find out how badly she'd been deceived!
