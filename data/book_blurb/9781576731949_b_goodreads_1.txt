Toddlers and preschoolers will love this new book concept -- part book, part toy. With movable arms and legs securely attached to chubby die-cut board books, these book-ish characters almost spring to life! Each charming board book features a fruit of the spirit; and the story inside shows how each character faces challenges that teach them to practice their special traits. Children will come to relate to, and love, their new friends: Strawberry Sam, Peachy Pete, Blackberry Bart, Anna Apple, and more! Nine books total!
