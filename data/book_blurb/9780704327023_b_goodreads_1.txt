Tiffauges, a black and white cat, is the narrator of this highly original autobiography. Abel, his owner, is a writer struggling to produce the definitive novel. Together, they divide their time between a flat in Paris and a house in the South of France. Tiffauges' perceptive observations of the everyday life of his companion, not to mention his own close involvement with the biped, result in this unusual commentary on the world. Tiffauges acquires two wives, both brought home by Abel: Tiffany, a rebel, and Tityre, a beautiful vamp. His union with these ladies can have no issue. Tityre, however, does produce kittens…by another.
