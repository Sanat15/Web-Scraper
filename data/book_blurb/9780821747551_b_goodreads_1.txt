Notorious rake and adventurer Burton Horne's plans for revenge against the Duke of Bayswater and his family go awry when he falls head over heels for his quarry's lovely daughter. Original.

THE CHASE IS ON FOR A LADY'S HAND ...

For Elaine Bayworth, the lovely young Countess of Wicksteed, her first London season promised to be an unrivaled success. Yet with suitors gathered round and one intriguing Frenchman having caught her eye, it was the very handsome, very outré adventurer Burton Horne who captured her attention. Before the first dance was done, she was tingling all over; before the evening was over, she was in his arms. Little did she know that he had something even more scandalous planned. Burton had come to the ball with vengeance and ruin in mind. 

...AND A ROGUE'S HEART

Spying his beautiful quarry at the center of a gaggle of simpering young men, he decided that desperate times called for desperate measures. But when Elaine became engaged to a foppish foreigner, forcing Burton to abduct her--right at the altar--the hunter suddenly became the hunted. For Cupid had taken aim ... and the arrow was flying straight for Burton's heart!
