A brief encounter...and a lasting love

Lady Juliana Westlake lived a staid and secluded life...or so it seemed. For the green-eyed beauty had a secret life as a spy - and it was while she was disguised as a French barmaid that she carried out the daring rescue of Nicholas Framingham. All the handsome earl remembered was a brief glimpse of her alluring eyes and auburn hair...yet he vowed to find her again, for she haunted his memory.

Then came an unexpected complication: an arranged marriage to the drab Julianna Westlake! Little did Nicholas realize that his mystery woman was nearer than he imagined.
