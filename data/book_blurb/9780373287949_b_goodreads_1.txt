HARLEQUIN HISTORICAL #194

The gossips of Paris branded Celeste Valvert a femme fatale. That was no surprise to Edmond, Comte de Beaufort. He knew an adventuress when he saw one. A woman with lips so ripe and eyes so willing could only have scandal racing through her veins. And passion enough to slake any man's desire. — A misguided vow had brought Celeste Valvert to Paris. Guilt had kept her there. Now before her stood Edmond de Beaufort -- dark, brooding, mesmerizing -- guarding his family honor like an avenging angel. And daring her to submit to the ardor that raged between them.
