The Technique of Spiritual Warfare Paul E. Billheimer, author of the bestseller Destined for the Throne Do you know how to exercise spiritual authority so you can overcome instead of buckling under the pressures of sin and satan? This is about overcoming! PART 1: The Technique of Spiritual Warfare helps the believer to recognize his "weapons" and use them effectively. PART Developing the Personal prayer Life shows the believer that prayer is training for future rulership. PART Turning on the Switch presents some important truths concerning divine healing. PART Spiritual Warfare in Our Own Lives is a personal glimpse into the life and ministry of the Billheimers, showing how these principles of spiritual warfare have been developed.
