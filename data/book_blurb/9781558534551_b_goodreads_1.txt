When you want to rejuvenate and renew the romance in your relationship, a bed and breakfast or small country inn can be an excellent hideaway. The romantic feeling of such a getaway can be created at home with tempting recipes that "put love on a plate." Here are recipes such as ones that encourage two people to linger over coffee and liqueur. Gail Greco has chosen the most romantic recipes from some of the most charming inns to help you enjoy life and all its endless possibilities for romance. "Anyone can find romance," she says, by appealing "to the five senses and using them imaginatively."
