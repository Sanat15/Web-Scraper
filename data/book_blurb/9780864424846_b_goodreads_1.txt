'Lighthearted and informative. Amusing ... lively ... and eminently accessible. A fascinating store of information.' — JDA Widdowson, Director, The National Centre for English Cultural Tradition, The University of Sheffield

This book helps you: 
- Understand slang and colloquial usage
- Avoid embarrassing British - US differences
- Learn about culture, festivals, and food and shopping
- Fit in at a football match or follow the cricket
- Order a pimple and blotch (scotch) at the rub-a-dub-dub (pub) and have a rabbit and pork (talk) with the locals

This phrasebook includes a tour of the dialects of Britain, plus sections on Scottish Gaelic and Welsh.
