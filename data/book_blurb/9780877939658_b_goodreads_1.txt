Are your mornings hectic? Is it hard to find time to pray in your day? Well here's a way to enjoy a quick conversation with Jesus and get your day started on just the right foot. A Cup of Grace . . . to Go is intended for people "on the go." Offering honest, down-to-earth, personal reflections based on a day's scripture passage, it also includes brief responses from Jesus-things he might say to you today. It's a great jump-start into deeper prayer and relationship with God. Like a morning cup of coffee-quick, refreshing, and eye-opening-A Cup of Grace . . . to Go might be just what you've been looking for.
