Everyone loves to be near the water. Whether it's for a meal overlooking a picturesque harbor, a stroll on the beach, or a room in a country inn with a view of a sparkling lake, we are drawn to the water's edge. For all water lovers, then, here is the complete guidebook. Waterside Escapes takes you from Virginia's Eastern Shore to the Maritime Provinces of Canada. Along the way you'll visit lakes and rivers, islands and beaches, coves and inlets, bays and harbors. There are restaurants by waterfalls and inns alongside a canal. The fun of windsurfing and canoeing, the pleasures of dining on the waterfront, the intrigue of lobstermen hauling in their bounty, the magic of an island vacation are here for you to discover. This book guides you to a Victorian seaside village in New Jersey and to the islands of Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket. The mighty Hudson, the tranquil Delaware and the picturesque Connecticut rivers are explored, as are New York's Finger Lakes and Thousand Island, Maine's rockbound coast and Atlantic Canada's scenic shoreline.
