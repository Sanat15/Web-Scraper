Rumpole is on the case again with several interesting cases of murder and suspicious doings passing through chambers.

Rumpole and the Bright Seraphim.
Rumpole and the Judge's Elbow.
Rumpole and the Official Secret.
Rumpole and the Old, Old Story.
Rumpole's Last Case.
