Life is sweet again for Oz Blackstone, private investigator turned money magnet. Successful television performer, lottery winner, on the edge of movie stardom, and reunited with his partner Primavera Phillips, things couldn’t be better—until a visit from his police pal Mike Dylan resurrects his career as an investigator, on the trail of a poison pen. Oz thinks it will be nothing much, until the threats start coming and his nearest and dearest become targets themselves.
