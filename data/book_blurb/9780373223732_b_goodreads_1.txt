Welcome to The Other Side of ParadiseWhen Emily planned on hyping her new B&B by re-publicizing a fifteen-year-old unsolved murder, she never dreamed the townspeople would react so bitterly. Or that attempts on her life would be part of the deal...Her life in Paradise quickly became a living hell.But then Matthew Steele blew into town...and everything changed. As Emily sized up the mysterious stranger astride his Harley, she found herself lost in his arresting gray eyes. Was it more coincidence that he resembled the alleged killer from all those years ago, or could he possibly be her knight in shining black leather?
