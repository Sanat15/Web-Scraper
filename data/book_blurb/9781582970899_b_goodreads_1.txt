Many people today suppress their creative urges longing for expression, allowing fear to keep their creative voices and talents silent. This book is dedicated to helping readers free themselves and let their creative voice sing. Everything they need to be creative is already inside them; the trick is to find a way to let it out. In each chapter of this magnificent book, the authors provide inspirational quotes and stories along with "things to do" exercises to develop skills to: * Improve sensory awareness * Meet their muse and stimulate creativity through silence and meditation * Increase intuition, brainstorming and lateral thinking * Honor their creative selves by supercharging their creativity and silencing the negative voices within
