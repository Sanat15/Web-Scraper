In this sensitive book, Barbie addresses the issue of physical disability. She shows Stacey and her friends that there's no reason to be uncomfortable around a person with a disability, and that we can all share the same activities. Ages 2-5.

In this upbeat story about summer campers, Barbie is the well- loved camp counselor. When she announces to the girls that she will be away for a few days, the campers wonder if the substitute counselor, Becky, can handle the job. Becky uses a wheelchair, but the friendly, athletic young woman quickly wins the girls over, Becky can play volleyball and tell ghost stories just as well as Barbie. Colorful photographs make Barbie's camping world real and appealing.
