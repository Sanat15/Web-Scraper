ONLY BY YOUR TOUCH

Sworn to avenge his brother's death, Edward deWorthe, the sixth Viscount Hawkesly, is determined to woo, wed and ruin the beautiful stepsister of the man responsible. But Lindsay Granville will not be easily won --- for she possesses a strong independent spirit ... and a dark secret that compels her to resist the virile, seductive stranger. Struggling to keep his hatred alive, Edward finds his resolve weakened by the sensuous innocence of the headstrong heiress who enchants and defies him, while Lindsay fears she must ultimately surrender to the man whose touch ignites in her a reckless desire and whom her heart insists she love, passionately, deeply ... and forever.
