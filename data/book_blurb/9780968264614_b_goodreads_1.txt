This story, set in Jamaica, is about a young woman whose mother was mysteriously, but brutally killed for unknown reasons.  Although her father had explained the circumstances surrounding this twenty-seven year old occurance, she was never satisfied or trusted his words.

Christina grew up hating black men, especially Jamaicans, for depriving her of a mother's love.  After graduating from the RCMP college, Christina embarked on a trip to that country to reopen and investigate the age-old murder.  She was fortunate to meet Dready on the flight to Montego Bay, who agreed to assist with the endeavours.
