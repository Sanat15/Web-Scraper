Eppie Award Finalist - Best Young Adult Novel When her father uproots the family to move halfway across the country to the middle of nowhere, bookwormish city girl, Alyx Mossman, prays the miserable school year will pass quickly. She wants to be left alone to come to terms with her best friend's death, and to read her beloved romance novels where knights in shining armor always save the day. Much to her surprise, she discovers that knights can dwell in the most unexpected of places. And sometimes their armor is nothing more than a stubborn streak that hides a tortured soul.
