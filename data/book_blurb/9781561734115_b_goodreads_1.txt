Do your children ask you questions about the world around them? Are they fascinated by animals and moving objects, interested in their own bodies and nature, and captivated by what they eat and where everyday things come from?

If you feel  that you want to encourage their curiosity, but are running short on answers, The Big Book of Questions & Answers may be your solution. It is written for children ages 3 and up, answering the questions they ask in a way that they will understand. Beautifully illustrated, it will also teach your children in a way that will be fun for the whole family.

The Big Book of Questions & Answers has hundreds of questions on:
* Animals
* Nature
* Food
* Things That Go
* The Human Body
* Everyday People & Things
If you want to answer your children's questions and encourage their curiosity, read to them from The Big Book of Questions & Answers.
