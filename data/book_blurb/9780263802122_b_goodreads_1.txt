‘I wouldn’t like you to go into a state of shock.’

Having hired Colin Forbes as locum for the base, Jack Gregory was stunned when Kelly Jackson announced that she was Colin Forbes! Colin was newly her brother-in-law, and on his honeymoon, so Kelly was his replacement. Worse, as Colin Forbes, she was supposed to be sharing Jack’s flat on the fifteenth floor. What she failed to tell Jack was that she was frightened of heights, and scared spitless of flying!

Jack in turn found Kelly infuriating and intriguing, and was amazed to be so attracted to her. But as they both carried baggage from the past, would they ever be able to get past their difficulties...
