The pampered daughter of a successful hotelier, Marnie Montgomery had everything she ever wanted- except independence. Now that would change. No man would ever again tell her what to do-certainly not infuriating, domineering, undeniably attractive Adam Drake.Adam was determined to clear his name of the false charges that had ruined his career at Montgomery Inns. If that meant deceiving the Montgomery daughter, and even stowing away on her boat, so be it. If she had secrets, he would discover every single one.…
