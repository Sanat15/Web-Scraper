AFTER DARK...

After Sebastian Dark there had been no other love for Everly Viverette. She'd established herself as a top-selling mystery writer, but her heart had remained untouched.

Now Sebastian was back, regretting the way he'd once sent Everly away. The time he had spent wrongfully held in a Soviet prison had left him too empty inside to love, but his heart was ready for commitment. He didn't know what his chances were of winning Everly back; he only knew he had to try - and that he wouldn't settle for anything less than success.
