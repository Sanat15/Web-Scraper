All that glitters...

The Price of Privilege

In a matter of days Melissa Danfort had been uprooted from her life of rustic simplicity and installed at the center of London's highest society. Yet as childhood fancies became reality, the brilliance of Melissa's new life was worthless without the love of Brandon de Forrest--the man who despised her very name.

Merchant trader Brandon de Forrest had sold his soul to the wily Duke of Danfort. Escorting the old man's "long-lost" granddaughter to India would settle the debt and make him a free man. But how could Brandon then walk away from the one temptation he'd sworn to resist--Melissa's love?
