We all know the benefit that times of meditation can bring to our busy lives. In this inspirational book Sister Wendy draws on her years of silent contemplation to share her unique wisdom and personal insights into the blessing and inner calm that can be Silence.  Inspired by the profound spiritual power of great art, Sister Wendy uses artwork and the theme of Silence to show how to achieve a positive state of calm contemplation. Inspirational and informative, this is the perfect companion on the journey of self-discovery.
