When the soothsayer warned Leilani Howe that a tall blond haole would soon invade her Hawaiian paradise — and her heart — bringing passion yet leaving behind pain, Lei planned to stand her ground and solidly defy fate. But the moment her company's temporary troubleshooter stepped onto the island, the very earth seemed to tremble beneath her feet, and her heart quaked in recognition of her nemesis. Instantly she knew she would have to draw deeply on her inner strength, rally her staunchest defenses, to deny the precarious destiny that beckoned so seductively. For Paul Lindstrom's sensual heat rivaled the tropical sun, and she didn't need a prophecy to tell her that he planned to make her his...
