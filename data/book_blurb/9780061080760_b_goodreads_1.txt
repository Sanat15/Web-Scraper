Prescott Trefarrow discovers a very different and challenging new world when he is plucked from his beloved Texas ranch to become the new Earl of St. Keverne in Cornwall, until he meets the woman of his dreams. Original.

AN UNLIKELY NOBLEMAN
It happened so fast it made his head spin. One day Prescott was a rough-riding cattle rancher in TX, & the next he was reigning Earl of St. Keverne in Cornwall. He felt as out of place in his new post as a lone mustang in a stable full of throughbreds--until he met the raven-haired beauty of his dreams.

AN OUTCAST AMOUNT HER PEOPLE
Her smoky gray eyes & warm lilting laugh would have turned heads anywhere else, but to proper English society, Lucinda was the illegitimate child of a fallen woman. The circumstances of her birth had deterred lesser men, but nothing could stop this big-hearted Texan. Prescott wanted her, needed her, & would move heaven & earth to have her.
