AN UNEXPECTED TWIST
Revenge. It was the only thing on Joanna Beckwirth's mind on her wedding day. She had been swept off her feet by the handsome Stephen Drake only to be left standing alone at the altar. Never mind the memory of his passion-filled kiss; she would make herself forget the pleasure his caresses could give. So what if just thinking about Stephen was enough to make her skin quiver and her heart race--she would just have tout the burning thoughts of him out of her mind. If it was the last thing she did, Joanna would find a way to pay back Stephen's cruelty...

AN UNWILLING TURN
Revenge. It was the only thing on Stephen Drake's mind the night his best friend Max was killed. He would avenge Max's death by stalking the killer--but Stephen was totally unprepared for the revelation that his intended victim was a woman! So he decided to drug her with his passion, to burn her with the heat of his kiss. He knew that once she tasted ecstasy's sweetness, she would be forever addicted to his touch. The only thing wrong with his plan was that he, too, fell under Joanna's spell, only to be driven wild by DESTINY'S DESIRE.
