Could he be trusted? 

Jamie Maxwell's life was turned upside down when her best friend drowned in the azure waters of Bermuda. And when she demanded answers about the accident, Bermuda's friendly police gave her the cold shoulder. 

Was it foul play in paradise? The only insider willing to help her find out was Turner Garrett. But he had one major flaw. 

Like Bermuda itself, Turner was romantic, alluring...and dangerous. Could she risk everything on a man who was a criminal, yet whose touch made her hotter than the blazing sun?
