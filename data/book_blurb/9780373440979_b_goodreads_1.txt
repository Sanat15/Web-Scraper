Three Babies and a Bargain
Good things come in threes?
To Professor Jillian Marshall, organization was the key to happiness. So looking after her two-year-old triplet nephews for a while would be a piece of cake ...until she discovered why they call them the terrible twos! Luckily, hunky Nick Callahan, her sister 's handyman, was there to help her out. She definitely had her hands full with the three little guys. Her only problem now was keeping her hands off the gorgeous big one!
Baby Bonus?
A bouncing dividend?
Armed bank robbers were easy for security expert Mitch Flaherty. So were temperamental clients and beautiful women. What baffled him were five pregnant tellers and their lovely manager who refused to do things his way—the right way! Jessica Kendall smiled at everyone, instead of just Mitch. She looked demure, but he 'd tasted her sizzle. She fought him at every turn, while he only wanted her to surrender to him. Until that night in the vault...
