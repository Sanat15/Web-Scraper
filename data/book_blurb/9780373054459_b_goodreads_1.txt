After years of struggle, Amy had at last built a decent life for herself and her family. Yet, now that world was being threatened by Nicholas Browning-her husband and the one man with the power to destroy everything she'd fought so desperately to attain...
Once, long ago, Amy had loved Nick. But he'd betrayed that love, and a cruel twist of circumstances had forced her to take part in a terrible deception. Now Nick was back to claim what was rightfully his. And Amy was faced with her greatest trail yet: to keep her husband from discovering the truth about "Triple"--the child she loved as her very own...
