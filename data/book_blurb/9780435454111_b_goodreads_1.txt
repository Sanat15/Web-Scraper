The accompanying CD-ROM forms an integral part of the learning process. It contains photos and clipart together with examples of presentations to demonstrate good and bad practices. Screen shots feature throughout the text to enable students to become familiar with the new features more easily. Information boxes offer simple design and layout hints so students can easily improve the professionalism of their visuals. Features of the text  planning and creating a new presentation, using templates, adding graphics, presenting charts, adding sound and video, rehearsing and delivering the presentation.
