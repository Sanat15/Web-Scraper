Jenni believed their meeting was meant to be.

When she met Clay Cunningham's startling blue eyes across the crowded marketplace, it just seemed right. The obvious chemistry between them and their mutual success in the antique trade were instant bonds.

Yet it wasn't easy for Jenni to cope with her feelings once Clay made it clear he wasn't prepared to compromise as far as relationships were concerned. If she wanted him, she had to accept him on his terms.

Just as fate had drawn Jenni and Clay together, it seemed that love was tearing them apart.
