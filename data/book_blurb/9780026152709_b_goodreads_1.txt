When you think of valuable gifts, you probably think of a new car, a leather coat, or maybe even a trip to an exotic locale.  But would you believe it if someone told you that the most valuable gifts your can give don't cost a penny and will last an entire lifetime?Dr. Daniel Sugarman's Priceless Gifts reveals how the best gifts are the gifts of yourself.  First published in 1978, but more relevant today than ever before, his book is and enlightening antidote for the materialism of our "me first" society.  Learn how these nontangible gifts can make an important difference in the lives of those you love.Presented with case histories drawn from Dr. Sugarman's own practice and his extensive psychological research, this heart-warming guide offers a simple, more giving approach to life.By heeding the wisdom in Priceless Gifts, you will discover more happiness and fulfillment that you thought possible.
