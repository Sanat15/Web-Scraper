Lines reveals parallels between his early life and that of his native Australia during the 20th century. He describes how the natural environment has been destroyed over vast tracts of the continent in the pursuit of opulence. Naming names, he accuses politicians, scientists, and economists of turning the country's attention from fostering community and nurturing indigenous life to an obsession with economics.
