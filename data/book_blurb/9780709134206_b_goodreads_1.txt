Here are exciting landscapes where Arab palaces sparkle in the fresh air, where alleys resound with spontaneous, vivid Moorish life, where harems abound with concubines, where surreptitious slavery still exists, where camel caravans laden with elephants tusks and gold arrive from the Sahara. From Seville the author takes us to the incomparable Alhambra. After visiting Cordoba we travel to the Moorish heartland, Morocco.
