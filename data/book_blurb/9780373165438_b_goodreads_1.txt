MORE THAN HER BODY WAS AT RISK

A bodyguard's job was to protect people. And Lucas Jordan had mastered it ...until Shelley Kingston became his client. Then he knew he'd want to do more to her body than just guard it ....

A single mom and a public defender, Shelley had never been defeated by fear. But with her life and her child's life in danger, she had no choice but to put herself in Lucas's hands... no choice but to seek haven in his arms.
