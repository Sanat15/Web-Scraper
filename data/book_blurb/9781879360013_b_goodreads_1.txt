Tired of traveling to the same old places and visiting the same old tourist traps? Mary Davis takes you off the beaten path to places that will surprise and entertain you with their originality. But GOING OFF THE BEATEN PATH is more than just a guide to unique places--it also introduces you to people and organizations that are environmentally aware, and shows you how to reconnect with the Earth in responsible ways.

Among the sites listed are:
*The largest North American urban colony of Mexican Free-tail bats
*The birthplaces of George Washington Carver and Rachel Carson
*The best of our National Forests
*A bike trail stretching from Oregon to Virginia
*An elementary school built underground
*The first National Wildlife Refuge
*A hiking trail tracing the Lewis and Clark Expedition
*White water rafting and kayaking trips

GOING OFF THE BEATEN PATH also shows you where to find organic farms, vegetarian restaurants, and environmental artworks--among the many untraditional places that can be found for the traveler who goes off the beaten path.
