The astonishing festival clothing of the Miao tribes of Guizhong province in southwest China is the subject of this beautifully illustrated volume featuring 20 pieces from the textile collection of the British Museum in London. Corrigan, an independent specialist in Miao textiles, has written a detailed essay on the people, their traditions, and the techniques used for this major textile art. A glossary, bibliography and index are included. Annotation c. Book News, Inc., Portland, OR (booknews.com)
