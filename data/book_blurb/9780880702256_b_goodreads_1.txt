This Living, Loving, Leading book by David and Karen Mains offers a tangible, seeable model for encouraging your family's spiritual development. Join David and Karen as they suggest some helpful, biblical ideas for raising the spiritual temperature of your home. Walk with them as they describe their own struggles and triumphs, and as they search for workable solutions to the crucial question, How can we become the spiritual leaders of our family that God wants us to be? Can you afford to wait any longer? Part of our problem, says David and Karen Mains, is that we're not sure what spiritual leadership looks like. We have problems fleshing it out because we can't see it.
