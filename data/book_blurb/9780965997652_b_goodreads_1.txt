Aimed at people who are living unhappy, unfulfilled lives, this groundbreaking book affords them the opportunity to realize their full potential and start living up to it immediately. The first step to loving oneself is identifying one’s life message and magnificence and getting beyond the “form” of one’s life—job title, age, sex, or wealth. Step two is cherishing one’s magnificence by giving oneself solitude, not comparing oneself to others, and surrounding oneself with people who provide honor and empowerment. Step three is the defeating of any challenges to magnificence by living from the soul, dropping guilt, and living without the constraints of physical age. Step four involves learning to live in the heart of one's magnificence by being thankful for the enormous good in their lives, loving others generously, and expressing joy.
