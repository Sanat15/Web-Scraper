STATIC ELECTRICITY

Garrett Williams was a devastating combination of sexy good looks and literary talent. But while his dark, muscled build attracted Denelle Thompson, his infuriating view on women nearly drove her crazy. She'd show him she wasn't like the helpless stereotypes he portrayed in that lousy bestselling book of his!

Lovely Denelle presented quite a challenge to Garrett, and he had always found a good challenge . . . stimulating. He thought he could wear down her let's-stick-to-business attitude, but she wasn't exactly falling at his feet. Had he seen a more than purely professional interest in those smoky green eyes of hers? Perhaps . . . and where there was smoke, there was usually fire.
