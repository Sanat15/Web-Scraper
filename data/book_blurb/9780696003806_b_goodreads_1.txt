"Stitchery and Crafts," a Better Homes and Gardens publication, is a complete guide to the most rewarding stitchery and craft projects for the whole family. Topics include weaving, appliqueing, knitting, paper designs, mosaics, bazaar ideas, quilting, origami, crewel, embroidery, collages, stenciling, silk screening, and much more. Patterns and complete detailed directions appear at the back. Colorfully illustrated with photographs of finished projects.
