One reckless teenage night, April Hollis tempted a boy from the wrong side of town, and it changed her life forever. Jesse Cawthorne was poor, but his kisses were rich with desire.. . April yielded her body, her soul--and bore a child Jesse never knew of. For his stubborn pride drove him away, leaving her heart in tatters.

Leather-jacketed factory worker Jesse was contemptuous of "Princess" April--until the friction between them sparked flames that melted even his icy pride. But, certain a pauper would never fit into her wealthy world, he fled.

Now, ten years later, Jesse was back, and he and April were forced to confront the same impossible passion that had once made them yield ... and damn the consequences.
