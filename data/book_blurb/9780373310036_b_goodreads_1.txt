The best-laid plans of men ... and women
Frustrated and helpless, Elizabeth Norland vowed revenge on the rakishly handsome Lord Darrow. He was the one she blamed for the death of her beloved brother. — When fate threw Elizabeth and Lord Darrow together some years later, she knew just what she had to do. But what she didn't know was that fate had a few plans of its own....

One was to open Elizabeth's eyes to the truth, another to show her that revenge and romance make strange bedfellows indeed!
