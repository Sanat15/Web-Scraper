This revised new edition of Teacher’s Internet Companion builds on the pedagogically sound principles for classroom Internet use that teachers value so highly in the award-winning earlier editions. Whether you are searching for tips on how to get connected, reasons why you should even consider it, or great sites because you’re "already there," Heide and Stilborne have condense years of experience and volumes of information into one valuable teaching tools. The book is rich with the useful information that educators require to incorporate the Internet successfully, not only into their own lives, but into their classrooms. Major features 
*How to use Internet technologies like steaming audio, off-line browsers, browser plug-ins as well as older resources like FTP and telnet
*Important new educational sites plus updates of site addressed from previous editions
*Challenging classroom activities for all levels
*New ways to make web searching and web development easy.
