"I'm not one-hundred-percent gentleman.... "Alexandra wasn't sure if Jay's warning was a threat--or a promise! Still, sharing a handsome stranger's bed certainly wasn't how Alex had envisioned the start of her month's stay in Conway County, Kentucky. Not that anything about this bittersweet visit could be considered normal, of course.From the moment Alex learned that Ty Conway had left her his property, her life had been turned upside down. Ty was her father! Her birth father! And Alex hadn't known he existed.She'd been lied to--by everyone who'd mattered to her. And as much as she was attracted to Jay, she wasn't about to trust him, despite his friendship with her father. And she was right not to. After all, he'd sworn he wasn't one-hundred-percent gentleman. But he was--darn it!
