A BATTLE OF WILLS ... AND A WILLFUL BRIDE-TO-BE

An arranged marriage was not what Hillary Astaire had hoped for, but she was honor bound to keep her promise to her late father. Until she learned that her betrothed, the viscount of Wolverton, known as Wolf to his friends, considered her to be merely an awkward necessity. Hillary vowed to break off her engagement, but not before fate brought the couple together in Italy, where another amorous war was being waged between the prince regent and his wife, Princess Caroline.

Lord Wolverton soon discovered that Hillary Astaire was a most bewitching minx, with powerful royal allies. If he wanted to wed her, he would have to win the most difficult duel of all.
