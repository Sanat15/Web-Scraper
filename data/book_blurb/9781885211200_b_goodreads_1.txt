In this pocket tome, Marybeth Bond, award-winning editor of A Woman's World and author of the bestselling Gutsy Women, dispenses invaluable advice on how to stay healthy on the road, traveling abroad and close to home, traveling to adopt abroad, keeping children of all ages entertained and adults energized. It's chock full of "Voices of Experience" - anecdotes from traveling mothers, from the funny to the poignant, from France to Fiji and India to Italy. An enlightening guide for mothers, daughters, mothers-to-be, grandmothers - and fathers.
