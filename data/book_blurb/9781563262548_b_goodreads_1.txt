High in the palace is a tower room where no one ever goes. In that room there is evil at work. Accidentally, Aladdin interferes with a curse cast there by Jafar - before Jafar was cast out of the palace for good. Now Jasmine is in great danger, and Aladdin is up against the dark magic of a power-mad sorceress! Can he untangle the mess he's made and rescue Jasmine - without the Genie's help?
