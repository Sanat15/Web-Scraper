Throughout its pages, the Bible claims to be the very Word of God. And the Bible contains many passages that describe God - as the infinite, all-powerful creator and keeper of the universe, but also as the generous and loving Father of mankind. The Everyday Guide To God is a carefully compiled selection of Bible verses that address the many stunning characteristics of God. Arranged in alphabetical order - from Absolute to Zealous - God is described in all of His power, love, and glory. You'll be amazed as you meet the One who already knows you completely. Want to know God better? Read The Everyday Guide To God.
