Zref thought it was impossible for an Interface like himself to have human emotions—until he defied the Guild and began to remember his own forgotten past.

But was Zref really the legendary Mazemaster, who once hid the Maze Object in the lost City so its magic could never again destroy the galaxy?

Zref had to find out. Already half the galaxy was after him, including deadly space pirates, Wild Interfaces, and the treacherous Thsee Rith—all in a desperate race to the ruins of an evil empire where Zref was programmed to carry out one final order before he could become fully human:

SELF DESTRUCTION!
