Organization 2000 presents the next - and most important - step for companies to become successful in the new economy. It provides sound advice for anyone wondering why their current reorganization is proving less than successful, and is an invaluable tool for organizations contemplating or in the midst of major change. Organization 2000 offers vital messages for everyone in the organization, whether CEO or front-line worker. With the aid of case studies from Canadian and U.S. companies, questionnaires and tips, it provides practical advice on how  * create energy
* find balance between the old values and the new
* develop organizational wisdom
* create powerful partnerships
* develop virtual and traditional teams that excel
* create a value-based organization, and
* quit struggling and create substantive change with greater ease
