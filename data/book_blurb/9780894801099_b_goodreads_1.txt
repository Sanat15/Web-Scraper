Owls are special, not only to poets, lovers, insomniacs, dramatist and night watchers but to owl people everywhere.  Mysterious, crepescular creatures heard, but not often seen, it is their lonely cries in the night which makes us feel safe and warm in bed.

 From the vast literature of owls, Lynn Hughes selects some of his favorites by Gilbert White, Henry Vaughan, Ted Hughes, Edward Lear, Pliny, Dylan Thomas and Edward Thomas and matched them with some of the world's finest owl illustrations. 

 A haunting collection...
