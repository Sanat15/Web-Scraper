She turned his consequence into his conscience. Gabriella Gordon knew that she would have to agree to a London Season sooner or later or her Society sister would plague her to death. So when the celebrated Duke of Ravenham came to call, supposedly owing to the outcome of a wager, the duke was obliged to lend Gabriella his escort and consequence. Gabriella's sister was in raptures, but Gabriella most definitely was not. She was determined to be as difficult as possible but found that was easier said than done...
