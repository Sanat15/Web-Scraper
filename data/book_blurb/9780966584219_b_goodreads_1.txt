This book is not meant to discourage anyone from buying an RV and enjoying this beautiful United States with a "home on wheels." There are many states, national parks, lakes, cities, and historical sites to visit. Sometimes it is fun driving around and making new friends at the various places of interest. The author is not evaluating one RV manufacturer over another; the information is about the author's experiences only. A retired couple can really enjoy owning an RV to visit friends and family. You will not be imposing on their lifestyle or home because you have your own "home on wheels" to stay in. Normally you can park in front of their home for a few days while visiting. If you are towing a car you can stay in an RV park and drive to your destination. If the family is gone during the day you can visit the surrounding area
