From the earliest recorded writings to the present day, nothing has fascinated, amused, and enraged as many people as love in all its facets. This collection of observations, witticisms, and paeans to gay male love brings together the thoughts of some of the great literary and historical figures of the ages. From first love and longing, love at first sight and jealousy, to true love and the wondrous excellence of couplehood, In Your Eyes reveals the full spectrum of gay male love in all its many colorful variations.
