The Gospel According to Jesus Christ (O Evangelho segundo Jesus Cristo) is a fictional re-telling of Jesus Christ's life, depicting him as a flawed, humanised character with passions and doubts. The novel proved controversial, especially to representatives of the Roman Catholic Church, with the Vatican newspaper L'Osservatore Romano accusing Saramago of having a 'substantially anti-religious vision'. It was praised by other critics as a 'deeply philosophical, provocative and compelling work'.
