For her thirty-fifth birthday, divorced Alice Meyers received news of her upcoming grandmotherhood, a pink slip, a thousand seed pearls to sew on her sister's wedding veil and a pistol pointed at her by a gorgeous, gun-wielding stranger who insisted on being her houseguest...

Federal Agent Gabriel Book had to figure out who'd set him up, and where better to hide than in plain sight - at the home of an everyday woman planning a huge family wedding. But who'd have guessed that Alice's relatives would be far more complicated than mere traitors; or that masquerading as Alice's new boyfriend would provide such passionate undercover work? Or that accompanying Alice through her whole zany life would start to look so very appealing...?
