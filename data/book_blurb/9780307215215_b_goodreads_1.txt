“The rudder's broken. It's lucky we didn't tear a big hole in the bottom of the boat!”
Trixie sensed a note of anxiety in Peter's voice.

“Someone will be coming along soon who will give us a tow, although there aren't man boats out this early in the season.”

The minutes ticked away without any sign of help showing up. The disabled sailboat was being carried down the bay, and, as they all realized, out to sea…
