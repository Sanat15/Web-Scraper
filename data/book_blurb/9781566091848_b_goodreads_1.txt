This text details ways for the reader to find out what online discussion and information groups exist on the Internet, and explains how to connect with them. The book features mini-reviews of 2300 newsgroups, Frequently Asked Question (FAQ) files, a 5500-word, alphabetically-organized subject index, and a list of 8000 newsgroups. The new edition has been updated with 700 new newsgroups added, updated reviews and new images. Other features include coverage of the World Wide Web and Web browser, Mosaic.
