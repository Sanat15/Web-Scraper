A collection of white-hot fiction from one of contemporary gay erotica's hottest talents. Forbidden Love contains a baker's dozen of this writer's best, most passion-packed tales. A soul-searching young lawyer "find himself", courtesy of three randy studs; a horny cop becomes far too involved with a mysterious hunk; a struggling artist immerses himself in the all-male culture of pro-wrestling; and many other delicious scenarios make this one of Adam's wildest volumes.
