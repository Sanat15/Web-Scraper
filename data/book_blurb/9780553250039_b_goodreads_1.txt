"Give up, sweetheart. Nobody wants to listen to a bunch of girls."

When Scott Bailey, the cutest boy in school, sneers those cutting words at rock group leader Tammy, does Tammy pack it all in? Not a chance. Exercising her Leadership Potential, she comes up with The Idea: turn her all-girl Teenettes into the all-boy Tommy and the Tigers.

The disguises work perfectly, and now they're the hottest band in town. But success brings problems. Like what bathroom to use. Or how to deal with screaming girl groupies. Or what to do when local hoods crash the Halloween Hop, threatening to rip the shirts off their backs.

Then Scott finds out the truth about the Tigers. And the rest is rock 'n' roll history.
