Ken Owen, president of Racers for Christ, draws on his love of fast-paced action and years of ministering to professional drivers and their crews to create daily devotions that reveal God's wisdom, grace, and provision through-- Joy of car ownership-- Excitement of traveling at high speeds-- The thrill of victory-- Exhilaration of the open road-- Deep satisfaction of relationshipsFrom making pit stops for maintenance and spiritual renewal to roaring under the checkered flag for the win and an opportunity to glorify God, readers will experience the excitement of the racing world and discover the unparalleled freedom found in Christ.
