MR. JANUARY

Name: Stefan Szyszko
Allergic To: Horses...and marriage-minded women.
His Ex-Girlfriend: Carrie Pierce. A long-legged filly just lookin' for love.

For Stefan, footloose and fancy-free was the only way to live. Even Carrie, his frisky ex, couldn't make him change his mind. Besides, he'd already dated-and discarded-her...

Then he began to notice that there wasn't a man in Texas who didn't have an eye for Carrie. And when one of them tried to rope her in, Stefan realized it was time to get Carrie into his corral-for good!
