WORLDS APART

Alex Moreau commanded respect both in the wilds of Montana and in Washington's political arenas. But when Mary Clancy rolled into town ready for a fight, Alex's ordered life was turned upside down. For here was a woman whose passion stirred his blood and whose heart and soul echoed his own desire.

Mary Clancy was determined to save her fledgling town, even if it meant running a railroad through Alex's beloved Crow territory. But the Crow leader radiated more charm than Satan himself. And when he pulled her into his tempting arms, Mary was afraid that the battle was already lost.
