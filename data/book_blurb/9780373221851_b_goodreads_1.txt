She was running for her life. He was trying to forget the past. 

Dana Carmody and Gil Webb met on a rain drenched night, high on a cliff overlooking San Francisco Bay, two strangers who embraced in a kiss—that saved both their lives. It was the beginning of an alluring and dangerous relationship. Dana was FBI, and Gil her only hope. In a case with no leads, there were only deadly consequences. As the pressure mounted, Gil had to find the answers fast—or else Dana was a dead woman.
