To Dana Landry, Will Baker looked as wild as a Texas tornado. Despite her cautious attitude toward men, Dana was mesmerized by Will. This cowboy's combination of dimples and sun-streaked golden hair lent him a boyish charm, but his eyes held an allure that was one hundred percent grown-up male...

...and zero percent husband. A rodeo cowboy running from relationships, Will couldn't offer the fairy-tale marriage and family Dana longed for. And with endearing Dana stripping away the barriers that protected his heart, Will wanted to run again. But this time it wouldn't be so easy.
