LeBeaux Plantation... — Bordered by sweetly scented hyacinths, gently blooming magnolia trees and lush, green fields of cotton, the magnificent Natchez plantation house sat proudly along the moonswept banks of the vast Mississippi River. And for Aimee LeBeaux, her late husband's recently restored ancestral home was also a place of welcome refuge....  more » But the lovely widow's peaceful world was shattered the fateful night darkly handsome Dominic Valcour arrived at LeBeaux. As her husband's business partner, the rugged, half-breed adventurer held legal claim to half her home and he seemed determined to prove his mastery over her as well. Yet try as she might to resist, Aimee was soon trembling beneath the heat of his searing gaze. And when he swept her into his powerful arms-- his fiery kisses branding her his forever--she knew that surrendering to her deepest desires could cost her her heritage and her heart!
