Attraction is the story of a love affair. It begins where so many of our first loves do, in an unexpected encounter at university. Prentis Stone could not believe his luck as he stood naked in the bedroom of the girl he had admired from afar. From this moment, his and Anne-Marie's lives would become irretrievably entangled. And they would marry. But whatever Prentis had imagined of their future together, it was certainly not this. Now he sits in an anonymous police interview room, being asked 'a simple enough question. Do you love your wife?' Attraction is a novel of passion, of fate and of the unpredictability that lies at the heart of all our lives. Written with remarkable insight and assurance, it is as compelling as it is moving. It marks the debut of a stunning new literary voice.
