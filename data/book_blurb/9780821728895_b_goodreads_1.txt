With fury raging in his heart, Texas Ranger Adam Prescott rode into Mexico, determined to capture the pillaging general who called himself the Chaparral Fox. 

Anita's blood boiled as the arrogant American undressed her with his eyes. He was her sworn foe - a vile scoundrel as wild as any outlaw. But as the treacherously handsome gringo embraced her, she became a slave to her own wanton desires. Though she knew he'd never really love her, and would soon leave her, she also knew she couldn't resist the passion he offered.
