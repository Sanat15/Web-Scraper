"New York is so expensive!"
Not true - at least, not if you know where to look. Mr. Cheap has found hundreds of places where you can save big money on shopping, restaurants, lodging, and entertainment.

C" knows it all - as well as the best ways to enjoy theater, concerts, museums, and children's activities while hardly spending a dime.
And if you're under the mistaken impression you can't afford to eat out in the Big Apple, you're in for a pleasant surprise. Mr. Cheap tells you the best places to stuff yourself on honest-to-goodness sit-down restaurant cooking for under $10.00 -or where you can find a complete gourmet French meal for just fifteen bucks.
