"In these reminiscences dealing with the decades which saw the transition from open range to limited ranching, the late Harold J. Cook continues the story begun by his father, the famous frontiersman Captain James H. Cook, in Fifty Years on the Old Frontier (1923). At the center of the tales is the 04 Ranch (renamed and now known as Agate Springs Ranch) on the upper Niobrara [River in northwest Nebraska]. . .the author has recreated a unique period in Western history and peopled it with a cast of characters that ranges from boys in the bunkhouse to Indian chiefs to world-renowned paleontologists."
