Lady Araminta Beresford was quite capable of taking care of herself. After roaming eastern Europe and Syria-often unchaperoned!-she had little patience, upon returning to London, with the conservative dictates of the ton, and even less tolerance for the overbearing orders from a devilishly handsome family friend. Ironically, the Duke of Harwood was the very Jason Harwood who, not knowing her identity, had tried to get her foxed in Dover! Still, while her encounters with him yielded friction, she could not deny the romantic sparks that flew when their eyes and then lips met...

As the Duke of Harwood, Jason knew all too well the wrath of London society toward scandalous behavior-for which the feisty Lady Araminta seemed to have a penchant. Therefore, he remained determined to protect the lady from enduring what would surely be a painful experience-even if she spat at him like a riled kitten for his concerned tyranny. But his motives weren't entirely selfless, for ever since he'd laid eyes on the red-gold tresses and jade green eyes of this spirited minx, he'd had the oddest desire to tame her...just enough to claim her as his own!
