A KID FROM TEXAS IN A LAND OF TROUBLE

Ben Wilkerson is young, but he's in enough hot water in Texas to go on the run south of the border. But refuge is not what he finds.

Ben runs into the Mexican Revolution, a whirlwind of strife raging over a land of mountain and desert where life is as cheap as a bullet and death can come just as fast. The famed General Pancho Villa and his army are on the loose in a wild ride after power and glory, and Ben is dragged along into a disorganized war. In short order he becomes a man and a seasoned soldier, falling in love with a brave, beautiful and vulnerable girl he valiantly tries to shield from the horrors surrounding them.

Out of the action and excitement of the past and the timeless passions of the human heart come a rousing adventure...and the story of a young man growing to manhood as a nation reshapes its destiny.
