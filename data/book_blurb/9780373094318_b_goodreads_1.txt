Andrea Darnell didn't want to fall for Joe Underwood, the handsome newcomer to Neptune, Nebraska. For one thing - her ex-husband hadn't left her with a very good impression of men. For another, Joe had lost his own children, and she was afraid her main attraction for him was her ready-made family. She was worrying for nothing. Much as Joe loved Andrea's children, it was Andrea herself who really filled his heart. Somehow he had to convince her of that - but it would be the toughest summer job of his life!
