Ever the spitfire, never the wife. Just as well, too, for Miss Julia Weston , in all her twenty-four years, had never found a man she wanted to spend ten minutes with, let alone a lifetime. — So what in the name of heaven was she doing at Grayhall, Lord Brandon's country estate, with a gaggle of marriage-minded debutantes vying for the attentions of the arrogant, insufferable Lord Brandon himself? Here indeed was a man she couldn't tolerate for two seconds! Why did he disturb her so? Did her anger conceal a passion for this man she couldn't abide?
