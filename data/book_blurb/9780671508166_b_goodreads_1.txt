Exploring the world of words is an adventure no one should miss. With The Simon & Schuster Young Readers' Thesaurus, a great resource of one of the world's richest languages is opened up to you. Now you can expand your vocabulary, learn to express yourself more clearly and creatively, and have loads of fun doing it! With its easy-to-read type and colorful illustrations, this volume provides you with a comprehensive, organized way to find synonyms for over 6,000 words. With each list of synonyms, there's a corresponding antonym designated. Homonyms (words that sound the same but have different meanings) are printed in capital letters, and all of this is done in a pocket-size, easy to carry format. Perfect for students and word lovers of all ages!!
