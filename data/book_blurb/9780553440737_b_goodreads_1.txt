Mike Harris would be the perfect shipboard romance, Caitlin Grant decided when she bumped into the gorgeous stranger in the Armani suit! He was just right for walking on moonlit decks and dancing cheek to cheek, but she'd always dated rebels, not successful doctors who couldn't accept her wanderer's spirit and pleasure in taking life as it came. Mike succumbed instantly to the beguiling nymph whose endless legs and sea-green eyes kept him up nights, but he wondered if her restless heart had simply never found a home. Caitlin taught him the sizzling fun of escaping from expectations, and made Mike long to lose control, but was surrendering her freedom the price of joining her life to his? She'd always chosen adventure over a schedule, but seduction took time, and true tenderness meant a promise to stay He never took to the road, while her bags were always packed, but could Mike and Caitlin find a lovers' path to journey together?
