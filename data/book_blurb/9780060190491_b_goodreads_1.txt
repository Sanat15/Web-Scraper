Brownmiller, feminist author and a network television newswriter during the Vietnam era, recounts her journeys in Vietnam in the grand tradition of Evelyn Waugh and Graham Greene, combining reportage, memoir, and adventure in a portrait of a people and a country we have fought but never known. Her descriptions of her encounters with Buddhist monks, Hanoi nightlife, and Saigon markets are interspersed with political insights on the country's history and current situation.
