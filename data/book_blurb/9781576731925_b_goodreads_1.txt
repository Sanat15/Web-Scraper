Meet Penny Pear.  She is shy and quiet.  Her only friends are the flowers in her garden.  But soon she will discover the secret to making real friends.  Follow this sweet story and see how Penny learns that, like flowers, friends need to be handled gently.    Plus, Penny Pear is also a toy!  The board book is shaped like a pear and features bendable arms and legs.  She will even stand on her own.  With Penny Pear, kids get double the fun while learning the importance of lending a gentle hand and making friends.
