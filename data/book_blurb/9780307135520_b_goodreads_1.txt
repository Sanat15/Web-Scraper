The search for fast pilots leads Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi into the Mos Eisley cantina, the roughest, toughest alien hangout in the galaxy. Follow Luke as he follows his nose through the smelly cantina toward the greatest adventure of his life.  When you scratch the stickers and sniff the far-out scents, you'll wish you, too, were in a galaxy far, far away.
