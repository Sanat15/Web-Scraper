Barbie is one of the top girls' properties - the world's #1 fashion doll. Now Scholastic is publishing a tie-in with "Barbie as Rapunzel," Mattel's second direct-to-video Barbie movie.

It's an all-new take on the classic fairy tale of Rapunzel. Rapunzel (Barbie) is a painter living alone in a tall tower. Her only friend is Penelope, a flying dragon. But when Rapunzel gets a magic paintbrush, she finds herself able to enter her paintings -- and reach the real world. There she meets handsome Prince Stefan, who falls in love with her. But her cruel aunt is determined to keep them apart. Now it's up to Rapunzel to find a way to be with her prince. In the process, she just might change the lives of everyone around her.
