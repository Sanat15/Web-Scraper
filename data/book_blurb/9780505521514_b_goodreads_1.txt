ONCE A UPON A TIME ... THEY LIVED HAPPILY EVER AFTER ...

THE LOVE OF A HEART PURE WILL QUELL THE LONELY FATE OF THE SNOW QUEEN'S SPELL ...

When Boston-bred Hetty Malone arrived at the Colorado Springs train station, she was full of hope that she would soon marry her childhood sweetheart and live happily ever after. Yet life amid the ice-capped Rockies had changed Michael Ryan. No longer the hot-blooded suitor Hetty remembered, the young doctor had grown as cold and distant as the snowy mountain peaks. Determined to revive Michael's passionate longing, Hetty quickly realized that no modern medicine could cure what ailed him. But in the enchanted splendor of her new home, she dared to administer the only remedy that might melt his frozen heart: a dose of good old-fashioned loving.
