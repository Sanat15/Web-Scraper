SHE WAS AN UNMATCHED MATCHMAKER
To save herself from a marriage without love, Lady Psyche Veringbam bad long ago decided to pose herself as "Lady Bluestocking," a sharp-tongued chit with no interest in wedded bliss. A resigned spinster at three and twenty, Psyche was now turning her keen mind to the task of finding a suitor for her young charge, Amanda. The catch of the Season was undoubtedly the darkly handsome Earl of Southdon, and he would be the perfect match for Amanda. Unfortunately, the earl was most uncooperative; in fact, he seemed deliberately set on misunderstanding that Psyche wished to win his heart for her charge and not herself!

HE WAS THE PERFECT MATCH
A long-time bachelor, Justin St. James, Earl of Southdon, bad seen a thousand frothy debutantes. Not one of those empty-headed confections bad ever given him pause, including the newest debutante Amanda. But her sponsor, Lady Psyche, was another matter entirely. Now here was a woman who could pique a man's interest. Whether entertained by her witty banter or infuriated by her schemes of leg-shackling him to her pretty, young protege, Justin found this lovely bluestocking a most diverting challenge; and it would be his pleasure to change her mind about men...
