WHEN WORLDS COLLIDE

As soon as he saw beautiful reserved Emily Hartman, charismatic Jeremy Jones knew they were from two different worlds. His was a fast-paced, glamorous rat race; hers was the stable, comfortable existence he'd been longing to give his little daughter. If only he could find a way to join their disparate lives... 

Emily was smitten by Jeremy's masculine charm -- and enchanted by his little girl. But despite Jeremy's obvious appreciation for her new diamond-studded image, Emily feared he'd see she was just an ordinary girl next door. Dare she hope that she was exactly what Jeremy wanted? With all her heart!
