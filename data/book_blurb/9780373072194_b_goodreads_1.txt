SEARCHING...
C.J. Perkins had to find her brother, but the British authorities in Hong Kong were being no help at all. Darius Kane, a big, blond adventurer who had no love for the British, knew what was going on, but he wouldn't talk to her, either.

C.J. simply wouldn't take no for an answer. Darius was her only hope, and she had all but shanghaied him into helping her. The only problem was that the closer they got to finding her brother, the more she was threatened herself. In a very short time, it wasn't just her life that was in danger, it was her heart. And the only man who could protect her -- if he loved her enough -- was Darius!
