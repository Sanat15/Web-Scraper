The Unequal Contest

The superbly handsome Earl of Knowlton was a rake without equal. No woman, whether wanton or well-born, could resist him, just as none could satisfy him for long. But Knowlton had grown bared with easy conquests. He sought a sterner test of his powers of seduction to stir his jaded sense of sport. 

 Then he met Katherine Mayfield This widowed beauty with her air of unassailable virtue and flaming red hair provided the spark to set Knowlton's passion ablaze. Thus the struggle was joined between this licentious lord and a perfect lady who had to keep the infamous earl at bay and her oWn heart in check. ...
