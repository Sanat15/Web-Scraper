Second Grade is on the horizon and young learners will have fun preparing for it with Daily Summer Activities, Moving from First to Second Grade. The many exercises it contains, while brief, add just the right amount of complexity to keep children challenged. Daily Summer Activities provides ten weeks of appropriate, teacher-developed, full-color, practice activities in major subject areas to help your child make a successful transition from one grade to the next. This title is comprised of ten weekly sections that provide skill practice in several subject areas. The activities are full-color, fun, and engaging. There are two pages of short, focused practice for each day of the week. Each weekly section begins with a record form where the child tracks work completed, logs the number of minutes of recreational reading, and records interesting events of the week.
