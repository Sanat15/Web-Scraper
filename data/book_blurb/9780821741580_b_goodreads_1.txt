At 50, eastern schoolteacher Gail Bricker hasn't found the love of her life, but she still has hopes, dreams and a sense of humor.  She spends her summer vacation on Montana's Northern Cheyenne Reservation, and suddenly, Gail is at the beginning of the biggest adventure of her life, discovering it's never too late to fall in love for the first time, in Love at Last.Author Biography: Author of nine historical and contemporary romance novels, Garda Parker is a long-time university managerial staff member, feature writer for regional newspapers, and teacher of teleclasses in writing.  Romantic Times magazine says she is "a rare combination of good storyteller and accomplished writer who delivers a captivating read."
