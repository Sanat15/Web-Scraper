RAPTURE REMEMBERED
Clara Ehler nearly fainted when Trace Garrett stepped onto the porch of the Santa Clara ranch. He was just as handsome as she'd remembered, with his dark, wavy hair tumbling across his forehead and his midnight-black eyes claiming her very soul. She thought she'd left him and her dreams of love behind forever, yet here he was, making her head spin with his teasing smile. She didn't know what magic had brought him to Texas, but the spell he wove around her heart made her yearn to recapture all her lost fantasies of romance in his strong arms.

ECSTASY AWAITED
Looking into Clara's wide blue eyes, Trace realized she felt the same shock of recognition and desire he did. But she'd be less than happy about his arrival once she learned just who held the deed to her ranch. He'd heard she was planning to sell the place and head back East, but Trace knew she belonged on the wild Texas frontier. To him, she was all that was beautiful, untamed, and free he wanted to lose himself in her sweet, satiny skin, drink in the perfume of her strawberry-blonde curls, and share with her the glory and splendor of the passion-filled TEXAS TWILIGHT.
