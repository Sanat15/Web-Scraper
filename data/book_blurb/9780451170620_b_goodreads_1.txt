The Heartless Heartbreaker

Lady Drewe Carlisle's beauty was as legendary as her conduct was scandalous. She was famous for making the most highborn lords her humble suitors, and infamous for dashing their hopes.

It seemed a simple mater for the dazzling Lady Drewe to win a wager by making a London newcomer like Lord GlenRoss her latest victim. Too late she learned that this strong-minded stranger from Scotland was no toy to be played with but a force to be reckoned with...as the one man who made it infuriatingly clear that he did not want her hand took possession of her heart....
