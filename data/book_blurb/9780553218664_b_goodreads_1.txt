Jeannie Kilmartin was dangerous -- and the most desirable woman Elliot Escudier had ever met! When the impetuous travel writer invaded his idyllic Louisiana marshes in search of a story, he knew she spelled trouble... and he was just the man to give it to her. Jeannie thrilled to the deep, caressing drawl of the powerful landowner as he tried to send her away... but when he challenged her to stay and learn the mysteries of the fog-shrouded island, she succumbed to the sultry promise in the scented air, and the cool fire in Elliot's roguish eyes. Charmed by age-old Cajun traditions, Jeannie reveled in the local stories and people, but once Elliot claimed her with his mouth and hands, she was consumed by a steamy passion that nothing but their bodies could quench. Could a woman who'd roamed the world be happy living and loving one man and one place?
