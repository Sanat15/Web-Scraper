According to legend, a faerie must perform a loving deed to earn her magic wand. So, too, must the leprechaun accomplish a special task before receiving his pot of gold. But the most mystical, magical challenge of all is... helping two mortals fall in love. 

In 1899, the citizens of Woodbury, Pennsylvania, were quite superstitious. But not Patrick O'Toole. A practical Irish banker, he knew that wishing for true love by tossing a coin into a well was a waste of good money. If only he could convince the young Annie Brennan of that. The fanciful lass had inherited a small fortune, but her head was filled with dreams of art and passion. Patrick was utterly baffled by the girl... 

Enter Rosleen and Eamon -- a faerie and a leprechaun disguised in human form. Their task is simple: Bring two mortal souls together. But their subjects were impossible! Patrick O'Toole and Annie Brennan go together like oil and water -- even when they're crowned King and Queen of the May. With a pair like these two, the faerie and the leprechaun have their work cut out for them. Ah, what fools these mortals be!
