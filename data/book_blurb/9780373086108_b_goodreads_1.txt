It was time for Annie Milano to get on with her life. She'd been a widow for two years, and her late husband's friend and partner, Sal Giordiano, had assigned himself the job of her protector. Though he was tailor-made for the task, Annie was beginning to wonder just who he was protecting her from.Sal might have started out as a good Samaritan watching over his best friend's widow, but lately he'd been feeling more than sympathy for Annie. Why hadn't he ever noticed howbreathtakingly beautiful she was? And this good Samaritan vowed that Annie Milano wouldn't remain a widow for long--or his name wasn't Smooth, Suave Sal!
