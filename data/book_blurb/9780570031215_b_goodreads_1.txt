These 49 devotionals and brief prayers based on Bible texts help the Christian practice his greatest privilege - to courageously plead his needs before the great God Himself. In these first-person prayer meditations, Leslie Brandt puts into words what many feel but rarely find words to express. The Stream-of-consciousness style allows the asker to stand before the Giver and pour out his own discouragement and confusion, doubt and faith, joy and faith in Christ.
