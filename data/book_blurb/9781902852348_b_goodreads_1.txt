The eagerly awaited third novel from the publicity conscious gay porn star and poet, Aiden Shaw. A new novel guaranteed to attract attention. Primarily located in the hedonistic London clubs so vividly characterised in Shaw's Brutal and reintroducing David, Joe, Ryan and the disturbed Flora, from Boundaries, this is a far more rounded and intense piece of work. Here he has created a vast panoramic view of a world of which some may disapprove, but by which most are fascinated.
