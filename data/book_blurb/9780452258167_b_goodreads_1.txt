This wryly elegant comedy of manners is a journey into quintessential Pym country. Written during the novelist's years at Oxford, Crampton Hodnet is a previously unpublished, wickedly funny farce starring her famous clergymen, excellent women, dotty dons, ingenuous students, assorted academic hangers-on — and a hilariously romantic plot revolving around a pair of eminently unsuitable attachments that fondly shows human nature at its height of foolishness.
