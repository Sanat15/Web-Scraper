Kids want to know all about how the world works, what makes things tick, and how living things breath and move. In this new book, The Best of You Can with Beakman and Jax, Jok Church puts together an amazing array of scientific explorations. In it, kids can learn how mirrored sunglasses work and why water expands when it freezes. Experiments using things easily found at home help them put it all together, and increase their knowledge by testing their own results.You Can With Beakman and Jax has been the basis for the critically acclaimed television series, Beakman's World, which airs weekly on the CBS television network.
