The babies are excited. Dil has just been chosen to be on the label of Rocket Pickle jars! It looks like the youngest Pickles is headed for fame and fortune -- or is he? Angelica can't stand it that Dil is getting all the attention, and she'll do anything to get herself into the picture. Will her plan work, or will the new model for Rocket's dill pickles be Dil Pickles?
