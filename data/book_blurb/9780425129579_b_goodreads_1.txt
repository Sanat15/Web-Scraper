A MAN AND A WOMAN ON A WILD COLLISION COURSE WITH LOVE
 She hungered for high-flying adventure. He fought for power and wealth.
 Their dreams were worlds apart-but desire bound their hearts to a whirlwind destiny Beautiful and spirited, Rory Kavanaugh was determined to save her father's hot-air balloon company from financial ruin, and her only hope was Zeke Morrison. He was a darkly handsome millionaire with fierce ambitions of his own, caring only for money, power, and the glitter of Manhattan society. Until he met Rory. Swept away by reckless passion-and their own secret needs-they soared to new heights of breathless rapture where love was the wildest adventure of all. ESCAPADE
