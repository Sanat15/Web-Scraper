* winner 1989 Vicky Metcalf Short Story Award
* winner 1991 Boston Globe Horn Book Honor Choice Award
* nominated for the Governor General's Award

The teenagers in these stories are struggling to learn the rituals and meaning of love in their lives and those of their parents. 

Ardis is humiliated when her love for the handsomest boy in school is ridiculed. Deirdre tries to encourage her dad to date. Naomi is hostile to the man her grandmother "lives in sin" with, while his wife is in a nursing home. Denis remembers his uncle's dead girlfriend even if his uncle doesn't. Elizabeth is uncomfortable with her father's fifth girlfriend Valdeen.

Martha Brooks captures the mystery and uncertainty of adolescent romance - drives and movies and rollerskating and soft drinks at the town cafe. These stories are compelling and entertaining for young readers and for adults who remember their own coming of age.
