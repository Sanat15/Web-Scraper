Ali Van Horne
A pyschic. After "watching" the brutal slaying of her husband, a police detective, she just couldn't take it anymore. So she picked up the pieces of her life and fled to a small town in upstate New York.

Sam Tremaine
A cop. He had no use for psychics and wasn't impressed when his chief ordered him to enlist the aid of Alessandra Van Home to solve the Munro case. But when he met Ali, he was totally confused. How could he be so attracted to a woman he didn't trust?

Ali and Sam form a reluctant partnership - each afraid to believe in the other, each afraid to risk falling in love again. But in the background lurks a killer. A killer who wants Ali off the case...
