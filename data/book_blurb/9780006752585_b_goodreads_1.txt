'Dear Little Snuffler,' said Mrs Gussie. 'I'm sure he didn't mean to be naughty.'

Snuffler is the youngest of the family and the only boy. His sisters think he is horrible, greedy and spoilt. They need to teach him a lesson! Giving him candy floss and reading him bedtime stories are all part of their plan, but unfortunately for the girls. Snuffler is also very clever!

Two stories about Mrs Gussie Guinea Pig and her five children - Minnie, Winnie, Tillie, Millie and Snuffler.
