Microsoft NetMeeting conferencing software lets you meet and work face to face with colleagues and friends, no matter how far apart you are, via the internet or your corporate intranet. And this guide - the first of its kind - helps you understand and learn how to use this remarkable software. Here you'll find clear, detailed descriptions of video conferencing, application sharing, and every other component of NetMeeting - which makes this a great book for corporate network managers as well.
