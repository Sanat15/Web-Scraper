ONE MAN AND A BABY

Hunter Phillips never thought he'd fall in love, but when he saw her honey blond hair, sparkling blue eyes and adoring smile, his heart melted. Only problem was, she was eight months old.

The niece he'd inherited certainly changed his life. These days the hip bachelor was strictly a one-woman man. Until he met Jeannie Ross.  Once the sexy baby wrangler cleaned farina off his shirt and formula from his pants, Hunter knew that becoming an instant father didn't mean he was no longer a single man ...
