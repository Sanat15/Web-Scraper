On the terrifying day of her father's death, Camille Braxton Beaufort went searching the gambling halls and bordellos of the muddy Mississippi for the one man her father promised she could trust, and the man she would manly--Charles Longmont.

But on an ill-fated night, while her husband cavorts in those same gambling halls and bordellos, Camille follows a mysterious gentleman bandit into the moonlight. As the mist rises from the mighty river, Camille is kidnapped and thrown in a riverboat bound for new Orleans where she is sold asp love slave.

Only one man can help her--the man who has stolen her heart.
