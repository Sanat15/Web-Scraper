THE BEGINNING OF THE END . . .

In 1972, when Veronica Pace was nine years old, she watched a masked intruder brutally murder her mother. Now a seasoned FBI agent, Veronica is determined to hunt down her mother's killer, still at large after twenty years.

The old police records classify the crime as a burglary gone wrong. But Veronica and her lover, psychiatrist Harry Kline, uncover disturbing traces of a more subtle and intimate violence--a trail of terrifying threats, deep-seated hatred, and ruthless betrayals.

Meanwhile, a murderer is once again on the prowl. . . .
