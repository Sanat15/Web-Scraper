SLOW TO TRUST...So what if school librarian Alicia Donnelly was great with kids? Ever since her single parent ex-fiance had let it slip that maternalism was her main--and only--attraction, Alicia had sworn off struggling fathers looking for love. Fortunately, Mitch Johnson, the new handyman at her family's lodge, seemed to appreciate her beauty and her brains. Suddenly, reliable Alicia began to feel a little reckless, and another summer in the Canadian Rockies seemed anything but routine.But when Mitch belatedly announced the imminent arrival of his young daughter, Alicia couldn't, help feeling betrayed. Was he just another man in search of a live-in baby-sitter? Would this Mr. Fix-it mend or break her heart?
