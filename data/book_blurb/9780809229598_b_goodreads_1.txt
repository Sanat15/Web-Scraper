Parent Soup -- the premiere online gathering place on the Web and America Online for thousands of parents -- has collected the best advice found on the Internet for parents of toddlers and compiled it in a comprehensive, easy-to-use format. With advice from experienced parents to proven experts, this guide helps moms and dads cope with their toddler's needs and behavior, with topics such as biting, anger, attention span, and discipline.Parents will be reassured that they are not alone in their confusion and frustration in raising their toddlers -- in fact, they have access to a whole online community that has been there, done that and is willing to share its experiences. This handy volume will be helpful for problem solving and easing parental anxiety.
