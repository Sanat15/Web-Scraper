"Another contemporary tale sure to please her fans." BOOKLIST Out of the blue, divorcee Kathy Marshall receives a telephone call to say that her younger daughter Marcie has been charged with the murder of her husband. Kathy is stunned - the allegations are crazy... Lawyer Lee Ramsey is determined to gain a conviction in her first real case - the murder trial of Marcie Loeb... As the case progresses, these two women will turn out to have one vital thing in common - the events of one spring night thirty-one years ago. Can the two of them forget the past and form an alliance to prove Marcie's innocence?
