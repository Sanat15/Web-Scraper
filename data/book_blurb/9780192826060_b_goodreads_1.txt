Cousin Bette (1846), long considered Balzac's last great novel, is a key work in his Comedie humaine . Grounded in a meticulous documentation of contemporary France, this tale is set in the prosperous Paris of Louis-Phillipe and details a jealous woman's campaign of persecution against her own
family. This new translation has an introduction by David Bellos which sets this work in its social, historical, and literary context.
