When her flamboyant aunt whisked her to romantic Rome and practically threw her at dashing American playboy Brandon Powers, sensible Misty Carlysle was baffled, bedazzled . . . and reluctantly charmed. Whispering smooth-as-silk lines, Brandon spun her into a breathless social whirl and soon bound her heart to his.

But when a series of jewel thefts rocked the jet set, with Brandon always too close to the crimes, Misty''s suspicions mounted. Worse, Brandon seemed eager to implicate her aunt--maybe even Misty herself!--in the burglaries. Had she naively handed her precious love to a sophisticated thief?
