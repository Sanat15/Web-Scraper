The Marine Biology Coloring Book , through a unique application of the coloring process, vividly brings to life a wide diversity of marine forms and structures at the same time clarifying their relationship to one another and to their watery environment. The coloring plates, each accompanied by a clear, explanatory text, illustrate the major marine environments, introduce the organisms that inhabit them and explain their life cycle and interactions. Both the serious student of Marine Biology and the weekend beachcomber, in quest of a more thorough understanding of the amazing creatures that inhabit the sea will find the Marine Biology Coloring Book to be an invaluable source of that knowledge.
