Every parent can take advantage of everyday opportunities to help a child grow and thrive. Filled with thoughtful advice and easy-to-use ideas, Everyday Opportunities for Extraordinary Parenting gives parents practical tools and creative tips for guiding kids through a happy, healthy childhood.
Nationally recognized parenting expert Bobbi Conner helps parents understand the emotional, intellectual, physical and social context of their children's actions, then offers priceless, everyday opportunities for developing the positive skills every parent wants their children to have.
From encouraging curiosity and creativity to helping kids discover courage, this book covers many of parents' key concerns, including:
--Acquiring problem-solving and decision-making skills --Finding the spirit of hope and optimism --Learning communication skills --Experiencing a sense of responsibility --Appreciating and expressing humor --Learning respect for others --Identifying a unique family identity
