Anouk Turabi, an Egyptian woman pilot receives an urgent plea to rescue a teenage German girl from a forced marriage in Yemen. Preparing for this dangerous task Anouk is distracted by a beautiful American photographer, Karen Jensen. When they can no longer deny their passion, Karen insists on going with her. They sneak into Yemen disguised as man and wife, but the rigid Islamic laws test them to the limits of their love and power in this death-defying thriller.
