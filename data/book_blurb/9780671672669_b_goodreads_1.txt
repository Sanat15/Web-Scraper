They were casualties of the cold war - flying into the maelstrom. 

1958. The United States and China have clashed head-on over two obscure, barren islands in the Straits of Taiwan - Quemoy and Matsu. Suddenly the cold war turns hot as the aircraft carrier-based "Black Knights" Navy squadron is placed on full alert. Lieutenant Charly Rohr and his buddies in the elite squadron are on call for World War III, patrolling the Red Chinese coast in their F-8 Crusader fighter-bombers. They take off and land on storm-tossed carriers at sea, pushed to the breaking point by a hard-nosed commander who is honing them for all-out battle. Outfitted with a payload of nuclear arms, the Black Knights are ready to deliver the first strike to the Chinese mainland, as they live - and die on ...THE BRINK.
