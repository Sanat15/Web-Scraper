This book is meant to be an idea book for kids and adults to use together. It's a reminder of all the wonderfull things Oahu has to offer. From creating art at the Contemporary Art Museum's Art Spree, to observing fish deep underwater in a submarine, to exploring native Hawaiian plants on hikes, to taking singing, dancing, or acting lessons - Tons of Things to Do for Hawaii's Kids offers so many ideas summer won't be long enough to do them all.
