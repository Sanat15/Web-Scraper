Heather and Scott had a plan ...

Heather Langley couldn't believe that Scott McPherson was her new boss. The last time she'd seen him he'd been flat out on his back in hospital-and all because of her! And now because they so obviously knew each other everyone at the hospital believed they were a hot item! They needed to stop the gossip-fast. So Heather and Scott concocted a plan. They would pretend to date and then end the relationship. Easy simple or was it?
