AGAINST THE McCORD WAYS, SHE HAD NO CHANCE

Kate Bannister was going home to Florida, on her first project as a graduate architect. She hadn't counted on wealthy builder Chance McCord blowing into her life with as much fury as the summer storm that forced her off the road.

Three days of togetherness in an isolated cabin had almost persuaded Kate to throw caution to the winds and trust him. But sanity returned and she fled.

That didn't stop Chance. Despite the dangers in his life, the one certainty was Kate. That's how it was with McCord men: they knew, and they committed. Convincing Kate was another matter....
