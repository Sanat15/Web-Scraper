As twins, athletic, popular Stacy and shy, violin whiz Alix couldn't be more different - or closer, even though they were separated when their parens divorced and moved two thousand miles apart. So on a joint vacation to their grandmother's farm, their joint idea seems only natural. They decide to switch places for the summer and try out each other's lives. Alix soon discovers that she's a good flirt and jock. as well as musician. And Stacy, intent on fixing up Alix's social life. falls in love with the boy she's picked for her sister! Before the weird and wonderful summer is over. Alix and Stacy will have learned a lot - not the least of which is you can foll some of the people some of the time but you can never fool your twin!
