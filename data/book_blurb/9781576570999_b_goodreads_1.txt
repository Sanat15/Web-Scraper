Never make a Mayan mad at you!
Unfortunately for Harry, that's just what he does. It seems he and his archaeologist parents are deep in the jungle of Guatemala exploring ancient Mayan ruins. There, Harry finds a far different world than the one he knows back home. The jungle is a world of intense heat, kid-eating snakes, and bugs the size of trucks. A kid could get killed here! But when Harry unearths the skull of a Mayan ruler, fear completely takes over. That's when a century-old curse is released. A curse that threatens danger, doom, and destruction to Harry and his parents. 
JUNGLE FEVER OF FEAR....
