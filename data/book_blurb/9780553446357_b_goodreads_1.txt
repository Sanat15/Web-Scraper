"Laugh like that again, and I'll have to kiss you. . . ."He thought he might be dreaming when he spotted the woman on the balcony, but Josh Kita was intrigued to discover that his fantasy Juliet was enchantingly real! River guide Amy Larkspur wore her bridesmaid dress as if it were a costume, but kissing the handsome widower made her feel like a woman instead of a tomboy. Could a man who yearned for a constant love cherish a dreamer with hopes as big as the sky?Crafting a novel as delightfully unpredictable as its spirited heroine, Caragh O'Brien presents a fresh take on the eternal puzzle of attraction--and the delectable pleasures solving it can bring! She was just the kind of trouble he vowed he wasn't looking for, but could this captivating gypsy beguile him into taking just one more chance on love?
