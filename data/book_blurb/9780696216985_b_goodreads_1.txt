Home decorators find creative freedom when given the opportunity to mix and match fabrics, and incorporate interesting trims and embellishments in their projects. Decorating Projects You Can Make presents more than 40 projects to sew including pillows, window treatments, slipcovers, and bedding from Waverly, a premier home furnishings brand. Each project is achievable because of detailed illustrations, easy-to-read instructions, materials and tools lists, skill level rating, and estimated time requirements. Colorful photography shows how to incorporate finished products in room settings, from living rooms, dining rooms, and kitchens to bedrooms and sunrooms. Dozens of tips and practical techniques appear throughout. Decorating Projects You Can Make is designed to appeal to all decorating tastes, skill levels, and budgets.
