The action of the novel "Master Georgie" takes place in England in the middle of the last century and ends on the fields of the Crimean War of 1853-1856, where the course of events brings four Englishmen. The war, depicted in all its inhumanity, serves as a background for the main thing: in her famous whimsical and verified style, Bainbridge tells about the strangeness of love, the cruelty of fate, loyalty and nobility.
