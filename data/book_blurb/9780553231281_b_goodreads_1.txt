The most important book on modern physics ever written for the general reader, The Cosmic Code goes on a fantastic journey into the Microcosmos. 

Without complicated mathematics, eminent physicist Heinz Pagels gives us a richer understanding of the recent scientific discoveries that have extended human consciousness to the far reaches of space and time.

Wonderful anecdotes from the personal documents of Einstein, Oppenheimer, Niels Bohr, and Max Planck provide an intimate glimpse into brilliant persons who have shaped civilization and changed our world. 

Exciting, elegantly written, The Cosmic Code is an opportunity to celebrate the physicist's vision of reality…and how physics is moving toward an understanding of the cosmos.
