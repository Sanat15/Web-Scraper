The moment Susan Devane met Galen Sedgewick aboard their London-bound ship, she knew he was a terrible -- and terribly attractive -- flirt whose teasing remarks brought a blush to her cheeks. But when her coquettish sister Patricia joined in the fun, Susan's eyes turned even greener than usual -- with envy. 

Her fellow traveler had indeed captured Susan's heart but when her aunt decreed that the girls must marry English lords -- or forfeit their inheritance -- the waters of love grew even more treacherous...
