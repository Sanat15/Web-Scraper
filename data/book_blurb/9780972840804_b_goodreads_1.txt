Award-winning writers Nina Salley Hepburn and Beverly Williams have collaborated on this collection of twenty stories with a Southern accent. From the front porches of small-town Tennessee to the lazy Florida beaches to Hotlanta. From Sunset Carson to an Ole Miss party girl to the ultra-trashy Vine family. From a philandering clergyman to a war-time daddy bent on sharing. A funeral crasher and a wronged sheriff. And of course, Elvis. The characters that populate these delightful stories spring to life, told with fond humor, a sharp eye and a twist of irony.
