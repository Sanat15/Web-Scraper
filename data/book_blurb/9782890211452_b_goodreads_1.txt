About the characters in the Marcus series:  Marcus is a rowdy child who gets poor grades in school. One day, his best friend Jenny finds out that Marcus's father is an alcoholic. This series treats a serious theme with great sensitivity.    About the Premier Roman series:  The Premier Roman series was especially designed for children learning to read. These novels feature universal themes, endearing characters and plenty of humor, presented in short chapters and simple sentences with an airy layout and many illustrations.
