Two books in one, 'Knights in Shining Armor Sometimes Play Guitars' tells of an author's struggles with fame once her book is published. Kate Margaux, a business manager trapped in a stale relationship, authors a best seller about a professional woman and a famous rock singer and finds herself living out her own version of the dream. Follow Johanna and Ian as they fall in love in Kate's novel, then watch Kate when the dashing Rock Star of her fantasies reads Kate's book and seeks her out... 

Published as an e-book under the title Never In My Life

"Romance for today's thinking woman."- Octavia Randolph, author of Ceridwen of Kilton
