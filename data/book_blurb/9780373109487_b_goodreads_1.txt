Right man, wrong time!

As a busy model, Neille Trenchard was furious when her overprotective boyfriend, Lewis, hired a bodyguard to protect her in Paris. Try as she might to dodge the man--and the attraction sparking between them--Jack Rea stuck like glue!

In no time they were in love. Yet Neille put off telling Lewis. She knew the delay supported her ''good-time girl'' label from the gossip columns. But she wasn't dangling two men on the line, fishing for the best offer.

In a week her motives would be crystal clear. But could such a fragile new love survive the wait?
