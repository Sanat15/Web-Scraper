Love story
PAST... When a tough fourteen-year-old rescued a lost little girl, friendship was inevitable. Wealthy Whitney Grand didn't care that he was "that boy from trash town." Dean Russell was her hero, and no one could ever break them up! 
PRESENT... The impossible had happened. Something was breaking them up, but even Whitney couldn't have predicted...hormones! What else could make her fantasize about Dean's kisses? Certainly not the L word! 
FUTURE... Dean knew there was no escape. But Whitney seemed bent on showing him that he was the only man for her. And he was beginning to believe her...
