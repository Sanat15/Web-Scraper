Widowed and wary April DuBois thought she had everything she needed in Rusty her six-year-old son, and her chic Tulsa boutique. But from the moment Vic Leyland invaded her life, she was threatened by passions--and fears she'd never known before. Vic pursued her with single-minded intensity determined to discover the secrets she desperately tried to hide, determined to smash all the barriers to love. Their dangerous liaison ignited long-banked fires that threatened to take an even higher custody of Rusty the only male with an unquestioned claim on April's heart. She had only one last desperate to publicly proclaim her love for Vic, to marry him and let him join her in the fight to keep the child she adored.
