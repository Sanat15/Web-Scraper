A Wild Stampede Of Untamed Desire Thundered Across The Lawless Land...

Burning Love - For beautiful raven-haired Isabella, that love was Cordova, her family's vast estate, a sun-scorched land that made her hot Spanish blood race with pride.

Chilling Hate - For Jason Rawlings, the suntanned, blue-eyed stranger, it was a palpable force that had driven him across two continents and brought him to Texas.

Caught in the deadly crossfire, trapped between violence and desire, Jason would stand beside Isabella, defend Cordova against men whose hunger for land and power knew no limit. He was sworn to her struggle...fated to avenge the past...destined to unleash the fiery passions of A Woman of Texas
