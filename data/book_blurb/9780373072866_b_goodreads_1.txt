HIDDEN LONGINGS — Alexa Cord had the most spectacular view on the Greek island of Kavos - but the house she'd inherited needed major renovations to become the most romantic hotel in the Aegean. Her silent prayers for a miracle worker were answered by Mark Everett, a blond, blue-eyed Adonis who quoted poetry while he fixed her sink. Could she risk loving this man of mystery?

In the beginning, it had been just another mission. He had no reason to tell Alexa who he was or what he wanted. But Mark hadn't counted on the passion that consumed them both. When Alexa learned the truth, could she ever forgive his deception?
