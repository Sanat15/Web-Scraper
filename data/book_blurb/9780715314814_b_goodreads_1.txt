Learn to make a variety of teddy bears the easy way for oneself, friends or family! The teddy bear is a favorite toy and makes a perfect gift for every occasion. Renowned teddy bear makers Brian and Donna Gibbs have created a fabulous collection of bears designed for crafters of all skill levels. Step-by-step and illustrated instructions in each chapter help readers learn how easy teddy bear-making can be. With a dozen adorable designs to choose from, including plush, bean bag and shaggy bears, among others, this book has something for everyone wanting to make their first bear or make one to share with someone else. Also included is advice and guidance on the tools and materials crafters will need for their bears.
