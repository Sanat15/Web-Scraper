NEW ORLEANS, 1803
Her name is Janine le Fevre, a French aristocrat by birth -- witty, charming, magnificently sensual. What is really astonishing, however, is her ability to ride, fence, and shoot like a man.

Fleeing from the turbulence and dangers of a France in upheaval, she arrives in New Orleans alone and friendless -- and blunders into a trap where she is kidnapped and forced into a bordello.

But Janine is not without resources. Soon, she tells herself, soon I will be free.

What happens when she gains her freedom is a tale of shocking discovery and wild sexual adventure....
