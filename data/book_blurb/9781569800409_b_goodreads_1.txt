Romance is never simple but it becomes more involved when the age difference between the man and woman is fifteen to twenty or more years. Beliza Ann Furman offers practical and sound advice as she frankly discusses prenuptial agreements, how to get along with stepchildren, financial matters a younger wife should know about, dealing with power and control issues, and being a widow. The author has been married for twenty seven years to a man fifteen years her senior. She offers practical advice as founder of the international support group Wives of Older Men (W.O.O.M.).
