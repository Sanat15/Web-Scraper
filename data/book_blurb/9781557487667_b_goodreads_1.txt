When Dr. Emily Richards returns to her hometown to set up her medical practice, she is burdened with guilt. The town has pooled its resources to set up an impressive clinic for her, but Emily has plans to leave after a two-year commitment to join her future husband, Ryan, a missionary doctor in Africa.

While Ryan's letters telling of life and death situations make her doubt her work in this rural community, Emily also struggles with unresolved emotions about her father's death in a logging accident and her mother's new romantic relationship. And then there's handsome Matthew Barnes, the new owner of Morgan Lumber.

Matthew can't be the right man for her, Emily reasons, but is there a perfect time or place for love?
