It's the night before Christmas, and Winnie the Pooh has the strangest feeling that something is missing. It's not his Christmas tree--that's festively decorated with candles tucked in empty  honeypots. It's not snow--that's falling softly over the Hundred-Acre Wood. Then he realizes what's missing--he's forgotten to get gifts for all his friends! What is a forgetful bear to do?
