"Let Us Worship" shares vital principles about worship and how to lead others into worship. Judson Cornwall loved to worship God, and his love for worship shows on every page of this spirit-filled classic. He wrote, "Worship is best learned by worshiping. We learn by associating with the object of our worship, for He is an excellent teacher." In this book Dr. Cornwall shows the reader that worship is" - The beginning of revival. -An interpersonal relationship between man and God. -a present-moment activity. -Love responding to love.- An expression of faith. - The total release of our spirit to God's Spirit. And so much more!
