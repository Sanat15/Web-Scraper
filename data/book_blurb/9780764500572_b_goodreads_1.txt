Just when you thought that you had no choice but to learn HTML -- or if you've had to contend with all those cryptic HTML tags in order to be able to lay out an attractive Web page -- along comes Microsoft FrontPage. And now, to help you get the most out of this leading WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get) Web-authoring tool, we offer FrontPage Web Publishing & Design For Dummies . In this book, author Asha Dornfest offers all the practical, how-to information you need in order to use FrontPage effectively. At the same time, Dornfest shares her own secrets for creating impressive, successful Web sites. Plus, the book's accompanying bonus CD-ROM contains everything from ready-to-use software and demos to templates you can easily adapt for your own use.
