Break the chain...and he'd break your neck

 Vicky Green had been leading a pleasant but quiet life, running her gift store in Madison, Wisconsin, and nursing her drooping plants. If she hadn't received the chain letter, she never would have met Justin Coe.

 Daring and dashing, with movie-star good looks and a murky past, Justin didn't resemble any college professor Vicky had ever had. And he wasn't the sort of man she usually found herself falling for, either!

 In fact, all Vicky and Justin had in common was that a psychotic killer had promised to kill them both.
