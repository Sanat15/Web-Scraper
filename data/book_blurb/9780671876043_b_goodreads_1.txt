This collection brings together short stories from Joe Haldeman, Connie Willis, Lois McMaster Bujold, Suzy McKee Charnas and other winners of the Hugo Awards, one of the top prizes for science fiction. 

Contents:  	

Introduction / Connie Willis
Kirinyaga / Mike Resnick
Schrödinger's Kitten / George Alec Effinger
The Last of the Winnebagos / Connie Willis
Boobs / Suzy McKee Charnas
Enter a Soldier. Later: Enter Another / Robert Silverberg
The Mountains of Mourning / Lois McMaster Bujold
Bears Discover Fire / Terry Bisson
The Manamouki / Mike Resnick
The Hemingway Hoax / Joe Haldeman
