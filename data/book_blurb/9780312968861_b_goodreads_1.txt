Four Texas authors render tales of the long, tall Texan male and the women who lasso their hearts... 

Journey to Texas, where the passion is hotter than a branding iron and the men are as rugged as the land they work. Join four beautiful and courageous women, as each set out on a perilous adventure of the heart, risking all they have for the love of a cowboy.

Evelyn Crowe gets a city girl into the saddle to convince the man she loves she never betrayed him - even if it means following him across his ranch all miserable day long.

Lorraine Heath heats things up when a hired gunslinger falls in love with the woman he's been contracted to kill.

Vivian Vaughn gives the daughter of a ranch-hand the courage to love the ranchowner's son and the grit to keep him from riding the most dangerous, outlaw horse in the land.

Eileen Wilks turns a tomboy into a woman and ignites the heart of her childhood friend who always thought of her as 'one of the guys.'

Best Laid Plans by Evelyn Crowe
Long Stretch of Lonesome by Lorraine Heath
Sweetheart of the Rodeo by Vivian Vaughn
A Tempting Offer by Eileen Wilks
