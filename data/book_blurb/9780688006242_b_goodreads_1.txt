This book is really funny. This book is about three Catholic fathers who served Irish immigrants. One is grouchy, Father Duddleswell; one is young, Father Neil who keeps the senior father out of trouble; and Father Abe, an old drinking, smoking, and gambling father getting up in age. They sell Holy Water for fertility, keep undertakers from fighting, and put up with the pushy bishop. They also help to let old Father Abe do services. Funniest book I've ever read in some time.(Amazon customer)
