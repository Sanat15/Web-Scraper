***COURTESY OF TWOBEARS BOOKS*** Eastman Kodak Company makes a variety of fine black-and-white photographic papers for use in making photographic prints. The purpose of this publication is to provide information about the characteristics of these papers and instructions on their use. This Data Book is the combination of two previous KODAK  No. G-1, "Kodak Black-and-White Photographic Papers" and No. G-5, "Professional Printing in Black-and-White." The information in these two publications has been expanded and updated for this edition. Nearly all the illustrations are new.
