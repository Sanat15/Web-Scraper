"If you force me to marry him, I will never forgive you!"

Passionate in her support of the American patriots, beautiful Gillian Winthrop was horrified to learn that her grandmother meant her to wed a traitor to the cause. Her body might yearn for Philip's masterful touch, but she was determined not to give her hand—or any other part of herself—to the handsome Tory.

"I intend to make you mine! Don't make the mistake of trying to fight me!"

Equally determined in his desire to possess Gillian, Philip Meredith was a man with a mission of his own. But his false identity as a Tory sympathizer might cost him his love, unless he could convince Gillian that he, too, was a passionate rebel.
