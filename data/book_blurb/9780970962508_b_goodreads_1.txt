This unparalleled reference is the first guide ever to libertarian-themed films.  It contains over 250 reviews of films touching upon subjects such as free speech, the draft, drug laws, taxation, regulation, sexual liberty, immigration, and many more.  These insightful film selections, accumulated through exhaustive research, are:  *A handy source of recommended outreach material for libertarian activists *A fun way for newcomers to libertarianism to become familiar with freedom-oriented ideas *A source of inspiration and pleasure for anyone interested in liberty.  This book is a must-read and an important reference for all liberty-loving people.
