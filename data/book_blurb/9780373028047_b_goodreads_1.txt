I'll do the work, he'll get the creditKit firmly believed that's what would happen when she had to team up with the great Adam Rothbury in making a TV documentary.Adam was brilliant, attractive, sarcastic - and Kit had never liked him. Adam acknowledged Kit's competence, but believed she owed her position to being the TV company owner's niece.In tracing the life of a deceased Polish wartime flyer and poet, Adam and Kit discovered each other - the true selves behind their working facades. And they discovered love!
