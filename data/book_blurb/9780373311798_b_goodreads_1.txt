Since Miss Alys Champion had not succeeded these past eight years in bringing her suitor up to scratch, she was not averse to accepting assistance from a fellow houseguest. The Honourable Duncan Todd was legion for his hunting skills, and maintained there was little difference between snagging a hare or a husband.  Under Duncan's tutelage, Alys became quite successful in the art of the hunt, never suspecting his trap had been set for her!
