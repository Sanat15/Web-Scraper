TREASURE HUNTMarina Cafferty was determined to salvage her grandfather's ship. Giddeon Duval's Espiritu Estelle had carried enough gold to pay the taxes on the family's Texas ranch for years to come, and it wasn't doing anyone any good at the bottom of the Gulf of Mexico. Nothing was going to stand in her way -- certainly not some arrogant archaeologist obsessed with claiming the cargo for Mexico! Then she looked into Rafe Santana's merciless dark eyes and knew she'd met a man whose will was as strong as her own. Even worse than that was the shocking urge she felt to put everything -- including herself! -- in his masterful hands.PLEASURE QUESTRafe Santana was not about to let national treasures fall into the grasp of a scheming American, even one as beautiful as the emerald-eyed Marina. The stubborn Texas vixen was the only one who knew the ship's location, though, so she'd need careful handling. He'd let her lead the way, then he'd take over ... in more ways than one. For he'd had enough of her defiant glances and taunting words. It was time he sampled her treacherous lips, explored her creamy flesh, and unearthed a passion more priceless than TEXAS GOLD.
