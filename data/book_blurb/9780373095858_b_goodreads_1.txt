Rugged Steven Merrick deemed society darling JoAnna Stowe a frivolous, if desirable, bit of feminine fluff. Grudgingly, he knew he should rescind his public challenge to be partners on a grueling ten day motorcycle race. Then again, the thought of beautiful JoAnna, completely out of her element, utterly dependent on him, was strangely appealing... Feisty JoAnna vowed to see arrogant, virile Steven Merrick on his knees, eating crow for his judgmental ways. Somehow, she'd survive the race's rigors, providing she ignored the shocking intimacy of sharing his seat by day, his tent by night. Then again, the impossibly close quarters seemed to strain Steven's manly stamina as well... Win, lose or draw, this public gamble was fast becoming a very personal wager!
