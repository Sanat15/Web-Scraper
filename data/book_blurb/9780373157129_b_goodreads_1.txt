She had the wedding dress all she needed was the man!

Rose Franklin is fed up with kissing frogs - she wants to marry Prince Charming.... And then she finds Duncan Burke's personal organizer and realizes that she's met the perfect man - on paper, at least. The reality doesn't disappoint. Only, where Duncan is tall, dark and handsome, Rose is average, mousy - and even her best friend tells her that she lacks oomph! He's out of her league, unless...

A new wardrobe, an overenthusiastic hairdresser and a little help from her friends transform Rose into Cinderella. Now all she has to do is convince Prince Charming that she's the perfect woman to fit into his life, and his heart!
