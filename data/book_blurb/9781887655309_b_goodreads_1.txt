The sport of angling has many lessons to teach. Whether we vivist the neighborhood pond, the bubbling brook, or the open seas, the message of the waters is the same: Be prepared, be patient, and enjoy the moment. This book examines nine timeless principles based on the art of angling. Utilizing the words of renowned fishermen, writers and philosophers, each principle is examined in light of its application to fishing and, more importanatly, its application to life.
