THE RANCHER AND THE RUNAWAY

Fine silks, beautiful jewels ... once upon a time, Amber Johnson reveled in the kind of pampered existence she'd always coveted. Then reality stepped in, and she began searching for something more: self-reliance and independence.

Fleeing to the untamed landscape of Colorado, Amber sought a new life uncomplicated by men or money ... until Jake Kassidy appeared. The most eligible bachelor in the county made no secret of his desire for Amber. From their first tempestuous meeting, there was no denying the electricity that crackled between them. It was seductive --- and became more and more dangerous with each passing moment ...
