As a deputy marshal in lawless Western Arkansas, Harry Cole was well qualified for the job. He took a passionate pride in the knowledge that he was better at it than almost anybody else.Cole let nothing interfere with his not friendship or personal loyalties—nor even the love of his beautiful wife, Cordelia.As a grand climax to his bloody career as lawman, he took on the infamous Sutter gang. Along the way he left a trail of dead men and broken lives. Finally, having lost everything but his badge, he had to make the most bitter choice of his life...
