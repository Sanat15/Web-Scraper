Con destino a la comunicación ( Paul Chandler, Rafael Gómez, Constance Kihyet, Michael Sharron) is an innovative intermediate level (2nd or 3rd year) text and accompanying workbook. Throughout its 15 chapters, students work on the development of their conversation and composition skills in Spanish while exploring interesting themes and intriguing questions. An authentic reading in each chapter also helps students to develop reading skills. An audio component is integrated into both the core textbook and the workbook. This text is a collaboration with McGraw-Hill Higher Education, WGBH, and the Annenberg/CPB Project.
