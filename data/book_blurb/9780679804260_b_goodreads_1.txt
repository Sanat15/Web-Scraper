They're the three youngest Little Leaguers to make the Angel Park Dodgers, and they've got talent to spare. Kenny is the all around star--his father was in the majors and Kenny hopes to follow in his footsteps. Jacob is a master of trivia who's constantly giving funny radio announcer broadcasts of the games--but he's no slouch when it's his turn at bat. And then there's Harlan, big boned, brimming with raw energy, and living proof that hard work and determination really can pay off. 

They're all anxious to prove themselves to the older guys, but they haven't counted on the antagonism of team slugger (and bully) Rodney Bunson, who'll do anything to stay on top. Can the rookies survive Rodney's version of hardball? Can the Dodgers?
