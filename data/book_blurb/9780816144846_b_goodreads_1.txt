Publishers WeeklyThe compelling novel about midlife crisis and middle-class life in a society where old-fashioned values are hard to find and hard to hold onto.Divorced, coping with joint custody, trying to stay relevant professionally and socially, Doug Gardner, a sports columnist, is a man whose life is falling apart, and to top it all off, he is facing 50.As Stefan Kanfer wrote in Time magazine, "Avery Corman has a literary gift for dialogue and predicament. 50 could tell future generations more about contemporary middle-aged mores than a library of sociological theses."
