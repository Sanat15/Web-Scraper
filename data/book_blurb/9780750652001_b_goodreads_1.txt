The Best Labour Possible? is intended to help women assess their individual needs in order to make informed choices on the care they receive. It explores care of the mother and baby in antenatal preparation through to labour and the birth itself, offering reassuring, sensible advice.

A useful resource for midwives to help women make the right choices.

* offers advice to women on how to take a full participating role in their own care and in the birth of their babies
* addresses women at an individual level, explaining options available and decisions they can make
* useful appendices include information on the patient's charter and the midwives information and resource service (MIDIRS)
