Rugged Texas Ranger Texas Ranger Wade Jordan knew the Trans-Pecos territory was no place for an alluring tenderfoot like Caroline Leigh-Hampton. But with her slender, sensual body and brazen green eyes, Caroline was impossible to ignore, and soon Wade heard himself volunteering to escort the impetuous beauty to her brother's ranch. After all, the Rancho del Norte was smack in the middle of a range war, and protecting a lady from danger was the honorable thing to do. But it wasn't honor that made him ache to savor the sweetness of her lips and sample the seductive delights of her silken flesh. It was desire, plain and simple -- a desire he meant to satisfy.
