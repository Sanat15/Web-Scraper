A FAMILY SET AGAINST ITSELFThe wealth, power and daring of the Carlisles sweeps them from London to Boston to the China Sea. It catapults them into the political turmoil of Washington and 19th century organized crime.Ellen, heir to her father's unbridled ambition, bears her lover's child in her husband's home...Quentin, a brooding and sensitive soul, weds a black woman and becomes an outcast...Jonathon, the sailor, lives torn between his New England wife and children and a beautiful Chinese woman from the mystic city of Macao.
