She was only sixteen...a young woman dressed in boy's clothing, at home in a man's world. As captain of the Highland Swift, she blazed a trail of adventure from the exotic Hawaiian isles to the rugged Canadian coast...from the staid shores of England to the lawless Texas flatlands.

Catherine Swift -- the blood of a proud Scotch seaman and a fiery South Seas beauty mingled in her veins. She was bold and free, yet destiny bound her to Ty Gallant. A rogue and adventurer, only he could match her wild spirit, still the storms of her turbulent heart, and bring her home at last-a woman-in the tender passions of a silver sundown.
