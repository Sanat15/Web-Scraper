Parker was a man dogged by a secret out of his past, left to protect the vast range of a dead cattle baron - almost singlehandedly...

Goodpasture, hard-edged and handsome - already ruler of the biggest cattle empire in Wyoming, he hungered for the land - and person - of

Leslie Kendrake, high-born English beauty, whose innocence hid a blazing spirit that would ignite passions as large as the land.

For this volume, "Lee Davis Willoughby" is actually author Robert Vaughan.
