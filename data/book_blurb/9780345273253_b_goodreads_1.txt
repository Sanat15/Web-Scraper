She was born to be immortalized by the artist who would take all London by storm with his brilliant talent and her breathtaking beauty. But a world of dark experience lay between wily Meg Watkins, a London slum wench, and her dazzling destiny.Dick Fox had found her, loved her and left her. She would search all Paris to find him; risk her love to keep him; gratify the perverse whims of the Earl of Eggleston and submit to a sinister marriage to help him. She would cross the ocean to primitive America. She would move heaven and earth to leave Rogue Fox's obstinate genius to its just reward, to share with him the treasure of their unabated desire, the realization of a golden dream.
