Stephanie Burnham was about to marry, about to savor her first taste of happily ever after. Then tragedy struck, leaving her fiance critically injured. With her life in shambles, Stephanie's only solace was a man in white-powerful, compassionate Dr. Peter Canfield. 

Peter was no miracle worker--his personal life was proof of that. But he valued human hope over medical science. Ultimately that hope would be Stephanie's salvation. 

As they fought for a life, they fought their attraction because deep down they knew they'd be loving on borrowed time ...
