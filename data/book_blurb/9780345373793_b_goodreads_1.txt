Heidi Waldrop spent most of her adult life caught in the grips of a compulsive relationship with a deceptively innocuous substance-food. Over­weight from the time she was twelve, she weighed 330 pounds by the time she was twenty-nine. In SHOWING UP FOR LIFE, she shares her story - ­her struggle to stop depending on food for secu­rity and comfort and to begin making peace with her body. After years of wishing she could buy clothes in normal sizes, play tennis, and build intimate relationships, Heidi finally discovered a program without miracle diets - a program based on hon­esty, acceptance, and nurturing the good within. A testament to the healing power of recovery, this book describes how a Twelve Step program offers support for eating disorders, helping members enjoy life unencumbered by past anger and future fears. She vividly describes the gradual emotional and spiritual trans­formation that made it possible for her to give up her addiction and face life squarely without a numbing substance.
