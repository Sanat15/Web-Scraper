While the Ellison girls were out paying calls and drinking tea like proper Victorian ladies, a maid in their own household was strangled to death.  Quiet, young Inspector Pitt found no one above suspicion--and his investigation at the staid Ellison home caused many a composed façade to crumble into far-from-elegant panic.

But it was not panic beating in the heart of pretty Charlotte Ellison.  And something more than brutal murder was on Inspector Pitt's mind.  Yet, such a romance between a society girl and so unsuitable a suitor was impossible in the midst of a murder...
