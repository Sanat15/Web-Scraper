Danny Cox is a master of motivation. He has received the National Speakers Association's Council of Peers Award for Excellence, a distinction that places him in the company of such giants as Norman Vincent Peale ane Zig Ziglar. He's brought innovative and imaginative leadership concepts to hundreds of companies around the world-and this book brilliantly distills his unique, inspirational wisdom. Cox writes for achievement-oriented managers faced with the responsibilities of leadership in the shake-out driven world of the nineties. He shows how to survive and thrive by cultivating seven simple management skills, based on abilities that readers will find to be reassuringly familiar.
