Julie was just like her father--a rebel.

Julie had no idea of the disruptive effect she'd have on the Renards--relatives she hadn't known until her grandfather's invitation to come to Australia.

Now, she sensed the conflicts amongst them. Only when she fell in love with Paul Durand, head of the Renard-Durand family wine industry, did she learn that her father had once loved Paul's mother.

Would she have to leave the valley as her father had? Or would their love overcome past bitterness?
