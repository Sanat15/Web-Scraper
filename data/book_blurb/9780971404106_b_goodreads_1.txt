When a diplomat is killed outside the Diogenes Club, Mycroft summons his brother, Sherlock Holmes, to discover a possible connection between the victim and a stolen aeronautic trophy.  The investigation leads to a rumored weapon of unthinkable power, and to a university where someone's been digging through Professor Moriarty's posthumous papers.  Amidst this backdrop are press reports of a strange, but elusive rodent rummaging the sewers of London.
