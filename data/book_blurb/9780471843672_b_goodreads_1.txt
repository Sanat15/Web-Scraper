Explains the fundamentals of options contracts and strategies for buying and selling calls and puts, spreads, straddles and combinations, synthetic positions, and arbitrage. Reviews all types of options, including stock index options, along with the traditional stock options and new commodities options. Presents decision structures for each option so readers understand their choices in various market environments. Traces the investment from the initiation of the option strategy through changing conditions in the market to the closing of the total strategy.
