SURVIVAL OF THE FITTEST

Dependable businesswoman Cassandra Phillips couldn't believe she was out in the middle of nowhere slinking around an honest-to-goodness log cabin in search of her reckless runaway sister. And unfortunately she had to ask sexy mountain man Slade Mason for help. He was everything Cassie scorned--brazen and brash, untamed and unpredictable. He was also totally wrong for her, wasn't he?

Slade wanted nothing to do with a city woman--especially a rich, interfering one like Cassie! But she seemed determined to stick to him like glue until he led her to her wayward sibling. He figured she'd never survive a long trek through the wilderness, but then she surprised him -- in more ways than one ....
