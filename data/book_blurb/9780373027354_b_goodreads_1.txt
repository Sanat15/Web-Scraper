Could she ever hope to fit into his world?
Bright, attractive and successful, Lorna had left a glamorous career as a fashion photographer in Toronto to work with an archaeological team exploring ancient ruins in Crete.

It was an entirely new experience, and so was falling in love with the fiery Jason Peritakis.

But could she ever bridge the gap between the old world and the new, especially when Jason's prejudice against North American women was so obvious?
