He is known as the Painter of Light. His stunning paintings have transported us to quaint country cottages . . ocean-side lighthouses . . magnificent mountainsides . . and so many other destinations. Off the Beaten Path features Kinkade's masterful depictions of pastoral landscapes -- scenes that emphasize the importance of taking time to "smell the roses." For anyone seeking to escape the frantic pace of life, the encouraging devotional passages found in Off the Beaten Path provide the consummate retreat. It is an ideal supplement for daily devotional time.
