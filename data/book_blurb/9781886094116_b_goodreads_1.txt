At a finance convention in Boston, someone just made a killing - the kind that leaves behind a corpse. The victim's talents for acquiring money, lovers, and enemies cast a shadow of suspicion that could blot out Beacon Hill. Overwhelmed by a bull market of suspects and motives, the police are desperate for some inside information. Will the help of Lisa King, financial instructor and amateur detective, put the police on the right track in time? Or will a white-collar killer return to work Monday morning after getting away with murder?
