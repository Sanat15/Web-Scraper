1984, W. H. Smith / Gallery Books, oversize hardcover reprint edition. 65 pages. Photo collection with extended captions. A lot of these types of books are so-so. This particular title is a handsome book, indeed, with many rare breeds caught on camera. Color here is superb. There is also an extended "In the Wild" section, highlighting such beasts  ocelots, lynx, margays, forest wild cat, rare golden cat of Africa, and more. Fascinating stuff and extremely well done. Anyone who likes pussycats will appreciate this book.
