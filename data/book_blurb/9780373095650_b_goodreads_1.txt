Bliss Goodwin had survived a shipwreck and the perils of a desert island, but in Hollywood she was utterly at sea. Then handsome, commanding Hunter Lord rescued her from the hungry media and became her one safe haven in a frightening new world. As her "man Friday" he was gentle, attentive; downright maddeningly provocative! She owed him everything . . . and offered it gladly. Why was he refusing her love?

World-weary Hunter nearly went overboard for Bliss's chaste innocence. But he vowed to protect her from tinseltown sharks-including himself. He'd seen how fast misty-eyed tenderness vaporized in the hot lights of Hollywood. Brave though she was, could this fragile, beautiful Miss Robinson Crusoe possibly take the heat?
