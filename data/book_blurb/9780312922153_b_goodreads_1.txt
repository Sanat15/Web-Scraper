Kick, punch and leap your way to videogame victory — then show your friends how it's done! No one knows video games like Jeff Rovin. And in this all-new companion to his two previous bestsellers, he takes some of the mystery — but none of the challenge — out of many of today's most formidable video games. (In addition, Tengen's Super Sprint game — not authorized by Nintendo — is also covered.) Train for triumph with his vital tips, including:

* Passwords and secret codes
* How to regain powers, weapons and lives
* Important updates on all your favorite games
* And much more!

Perfect the power-punch atop the Big Rig in Bad Dudes. Make every flying drop-kick count in Ninja Gaiden. Learn more fascinating tips about Ninja Turtles. Using this special guide, make your reflexes lightning-fast, your eyes eagle-sharp — and your scores totally unbeatable!
