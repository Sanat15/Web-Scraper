Whether you’ve recently moved to a new home or would just like to give a fresh look to your décor, deciding on a style and choosing a color scheme can be a daunting task. In Complete Home Makeover, the Changing Rooms team offers a whole range of tips and techniques to get you started, guiding you step-by-step in three easy-to-follow sections—Choose and Change Color, Manage That Makeover, and Try This at Home. Complete Home Makeover also includes inspiring case studies showing you how the professionals put their ideas into practice.
