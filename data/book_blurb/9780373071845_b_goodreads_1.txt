DESERT INTRIGUEDamon Noble was handsome, charming, exciting-and the wrong man to fall in love with. After meeting Damon at a posh Washington, D.C., party, Jessica Stanton realized that she couldn't resist him. Still, she was wary of loving a man with such an enigmatic past, and returning to her diplomatic work in Afghanistan could give her time to think.After Damon followed Jessica to the Khyber Pass, she could no longer deny the electricity that flowed between them. But still, he knew too much about the work she did-and about the activities of the mountain rebels. Could Jessica trust her love to this mysterious-and dangerous man?
