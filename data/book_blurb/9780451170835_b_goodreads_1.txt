The Iskra Incident is a good, old-fashioned techno-thriller with America against the Soviet Union late in the Cold War.

When a plane carrying top Soviet officials crashes en route to an epoch-making summit in San Francisco, and a US Air Force jet missile is blamed for the crash, it suddenly looks like WW III is about to begin. USAF Colonel Jack Phillips must get past the Russians and the US military to find answers that no one seems to want him to find.  Dutton.
