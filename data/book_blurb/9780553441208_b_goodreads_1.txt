Her Enemy... or Her Love?

Clay Parrish was a troublemaker who'd come to the pretty town of Silver Rapids to bring change-or at least Jennifer Allan suspected that the charismatic city planner was prepared to turn her world upside down! She'd plotted a battle royal-but she hadn't expected a sweet-talking guy with an irrepressible grin to breach her defenses with a single, sizzling kiss! Jennifer made Clay feel like a moonstruck teenager-tantalized, aroused, and frustrated by the tension that crackled between them-but how could he get her to trust him when she'd already made up her mind? Jennifer told Clay he was just too handsome, the kind of man she'd always warned her overly romantic mother about, but once he understood her fears she had nowhere left to run. Clay made her long to be cherished, but could she learn to stop hedging her bets when it came to love?
