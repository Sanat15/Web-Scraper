Can you imagine what it would be like to be swept off your feet by a royal prince, to live a charmed life in the marble palaces of an oil-rich nation - and then to watch your fairy-tale romance turn into a nightmare of Islamic superstition, isolation, betrayal, and abuse? What would you do if you managed to escape your life of torment - and then your children were kidnapped by their own father? In Once I Was a Princess, Jacqueline recounts her part in this controversial, headline-grabbing, international drama with heart-rending honesty.
