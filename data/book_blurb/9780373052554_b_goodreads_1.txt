IN THE BOOK OF LOVE. 
Samantha Monroe planned to spend the summer swept away in a world of fantasy created by her favorite romance author. 

Then one day a real live fantasy man appeared before her on the beach. Jason Armstrong seemed to have stepped from the pages of Love's Sweet Bondage. When he revealed himself as her beloved romance writer "Cathryn James," Samantha knew she was in trouble. 

Jason's cavalier attitude toward love was nothing like what she expected from a writer of romances. Samantha believed in love. Was it possible that even though Jason could capture a woman's most intimate fantasies on paper, he himself had no heart to lose?.

*** later republished as Samantha James under the title The Unsung Hero
