Out of a full and joyous heart, Marjorie Holmes recalls the magical moments of her small-town childhood in Iowa-the simple pleasures that gave life special zest and favor.

Spring, with homemade kites soaring and dipping in the sky...Golden days in grandpa's garden before fruits and vegetables grew on supermarket shelves...The comfort and companionship of suppertime with the whole family gathered together...The luscious aroma of bread baking in the old-fashioned coal stove...Santa Claus riding into town on a bobsied...The thrill of getting a secondhand Tin Lizzie.

"Things didn't happen very often," says Marjorie Holmes, "but when they did they happened grand."
