Discover the birds of North America’s western region in this compact, beautifully designed, and easy-to-use pocket guide, which includes 80 stunning full-color photos, illustrated accounts and appendices.

Whether your home is in a remote countryside or in a populous city, you will derive hours of pleasure observing and learning about birds, and the National Audubon Society Pocket Guide to Familiar Birds of North  West is an excellent and convenient reference guide to accompany any bird-lover.
