Imaginary friend... or deadly foe?

Her daughter's imaginary playmate was scaring single mother Jillian Stewart. The young girl spoke with "him" as if he were human and said his name was Something Beautiful.

And then there was the matter of Jillian's newly hired handyman. Steven Sayers seemed harmless. Yet she couldn't shake the feeling that the sexy stranger was hiding something... dangerous.

Jillian knew Steven was a man she shouldn't trust, though every instinct told her to take a chance-and believe in what he claimed to be. Still, she wondered if their attraction might very well become something... deadly.
