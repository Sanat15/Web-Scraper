Author Jeffrey Steffes provides valuable information for Chevelle owners and those contemplating the restoration or purchase of one. After a brief overview of each year of the Chevelle from 1964-1972, Steffes delves into documenting a Chevelle. Using photocopies of important documentation, Steffes describes what each piece is and what it can - and perhaps more importantly - what it cannot tell you about a particular car. Of special interest are photos of casting numbers and their locations so you have an easier time finding them. There is also a section on decoding VINs and body tags. It's 112 pages of information, color photos, and illustrations to help educate and entertain you on America's favorite muscle car.
