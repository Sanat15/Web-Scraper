Patients need attention, doctors need assistance, the front desk needs records filled out-when you're one of the 2.6 million nurses in America today, everyone needs something! So where's the encouragement, care, and medicine for the soul that a busy nurse needs in order to be an agent of hope and healing? On Call delivers a vital  366 devotions that help busy nurses go direct to the source of all hope and healing. The daily meditations are brief, uplifting, and supplemented by Scripture. In one year, readings go through the New Testament and psalms to help nurses gain important insight into their own spiritual health as well as learn how to better help others. Written by a nurse with more than forty years experience, this unique devotional speaks directly to the essential needs anyone in the demanding health care profession.
