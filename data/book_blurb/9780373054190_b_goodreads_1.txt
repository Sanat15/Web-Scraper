ALL IN THE FAMILY

When is attention from a gorgeous man annoying? Kelly Sullivan could answer that question in one word: ex-brother-in-law. She was finally starting to enjoy her hard-won independence, and the last thing she needed was another Sullivan man to romance her. But there was a question Kelly couldn't answer. Of all the men in the world, why was Beau the one she couldn't resist?

Beau, the family enigma, was notorious for his footloose life-style. His private detective work took him around the world, but after one look into Kelly's midnight-blue eyes, his feet mysteriously lost their itch. It was time for some detecting of the most private sort.
