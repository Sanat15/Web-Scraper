In a departure from the standard, comprehensive travel guide format, Frommer's China makes it easy to plan a trip without plowing through an overwhelming 1,000 pages of data.We've chosen the very top travel experiences -- and after an evocative description of each one, we tell readers how to have the same experiences themselves. Here is the very best of China: the Great Wall; fabled cities, both ancient and modern; sea and river cruises; the Silk Road; and much, much more. Each chapter describes the experience and site, with fascinating cultural information, and includes travel directions, tour information, and where to stay and dine.
