Their marriage was eternal magic, a fiery fusion of body and soul. But in one blind moment of betrayal, attorney Fergus Lachlan forfeited Sharon's precious love and lost his paradise on earth.

Five hellish years elapsed. Then a bolt came from the blue. Sharon was accused of murder! Could her former husband defend her life and justify his endless love? Their fierce, unstoppable passion flared once more. But was this just a breathless, desperate reprieve? Or truly paradise regained?
