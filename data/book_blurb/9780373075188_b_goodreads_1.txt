GHOST OF A CHANCE

He'd seen her from afar. Beautiful, beguiling and wearing old-fashioned clothes. But when handsome Daniel Brant finally met Elly Raftree up close, he realized the woman he'd first met wasn't really her at all! Could it have been the ghost of her ancestor, Eleanora? Hardheaded Daniel didn't want to believe it, but there was no other explanation.

Elly had never met anyone who affected her quite the way Daniel did. But the pretty professor was beset by memories of betrayal, and she knew a man as magnetic as Daniel was bound to break her heart.

But if the spirit of true love -- and Eleanora -- to do with it, Elly and Daniel were about to have their chance at everlasting happiness....
