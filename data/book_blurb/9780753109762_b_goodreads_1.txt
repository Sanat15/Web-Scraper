Patricia Highsmith's debut novel made her famous. Two men, a tennis star and a psychopath, meet by chance on a train and "swap" murders. "Some people are better off dead -- like your wife and my father, for instance" as Bruno, the rich psychopath puts it, before proceeding to carry out his half of the bargain. A chance meeting and a rash conversation can lead to all sorts of trouble..... This cunningly plotted melodrama, which inspired Alfred Hitchcock's film of the same name, is a classic suspense, to be reread with enjoyment time after time.
