She went ape over himAnthropologist Erica Swanson had great respect for the intelligence of chimps. So she wasn't at all surprised to find runaway Sheena had ended up in the arms of Nick Barron. Even Erica herself couldn't have chosen a more desirable haven.Not that she could imagine getting serious about the man. She and Nick had nothing in common...except a monumental physical attraction. And that was fine because Erica didn't want anything to interfere with the adventures she had planned for her future. After all, she was as footloose and fancy-free as any chimp...wasn't she?
