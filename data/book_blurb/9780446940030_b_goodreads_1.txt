They played the conventional games of love -- but where hearts or empires at stake?

Was Alicia the innocent she seemed, delighting in her first London Season? How he hoped she was! Was Rob the dashing young blade he appeared to be as he escorted her to routs and ridottos? How she hoped he was! It would be so easy to fall in love while they waltzed -- if only she were freed from her dreams and her were freed from his duty.
