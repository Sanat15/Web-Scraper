For private investigator Mick Dante, casual surveillance of ethereal, Emily Thorne, the beautiful redhead who lived in the next building, was definitely his pleasure--and none of his business. Until the day she innocently walked into his office to hire him....

Mick steeled himself against her sea-green eyes, her gentle voice, her sweet intuitions, as together they tracked her missing brother. But there was no fighting it now that Emily was at close range, he was falling--hard. But she deserved a nice life with a nice guy ... not him. Passion with Emily would surely be heaven, but ending it--as he must--would surely be hell.
