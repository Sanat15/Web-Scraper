HE WAS A HALF-BREED GUNSLINGER WITH A PRICE ON HIS HEAD…SHE WAS HIS DEFIANT CAPTIVE…TWO HEARTS FATED TO CLASH AND AWAKEN TO LOVE ON THE LAWLESS NEW MEXICO PLAINS! 

Few dared to tangle with rugged Zack Banning, the son of a Comanche chief, and one of the most dangerous gunfighters in the Texas territory. But Deborah Hamilton was not most people. The headstrong Natchez beauty had journeyed west to wed a wealthy Spanish nobleman, but her wedding night ended in tragedy when an avenging band of Comanche raiders killed her bridegroom and spirited her across the New Mexico border. Now the prisoner of this fierce, blue-eyed warrior—a man she knew only as Hawk—Deborah was stunned at the fiery passion he awoke with his first searing caress. Sworn to deny his desire and one day flee his savage world, she found she could not escape his dangerous embrace... or the rapturous love he awoke in her captive heart!
