Married to a stranger?

Emma Wadsworth couldn't remember the car wreck that put her in the hospital - or the past five years of her life. But there was no denying her instant attraction to the sexy stranger she saw when she opened her eyes. Grant Wadsworth - her husband...?

With every look, every touch, Grant showed her how much he wanted her. And seeing Grant with their baby daughter stirred emotions that seemed like memories. But whoever had caused Emma's wreck wasn't finished with her yet. As a killer closed in, Emma sought shelter in the one place she felt safe - the arms of the stranger she married...
