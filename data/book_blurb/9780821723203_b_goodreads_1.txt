GLITTERING PRIZE
Catherine Diamond had never seen such a beautiful horse anywhere before—and she was determined to have it for herself. But when she challenged the horse's owner in a card game, she didn't expect to gamble with her heart. If truth be told, Rory O'Shay was the most magnificent man Catherine had ever met, and she couldn't deny the mysterious feelings he stirred within her. His blue eyes mirrored her own desire, and when their lips touched in a fiery kiss, Catherine felt the sparks of passion down to her very toes...

SHIMMERING JEWEL
Rory O'Shay was a renegade, plain and simple. He was running from the law when he met Catherine Diamond, but one look at the beautiful young woman made him stop dead in his tracks. With one touch of her smooth skin, Rory had no past, only a future—and only enough time to take Catherine in his arms for a night of passion, a night to remember, a night to quench FORTUNE'S DESIRE.
