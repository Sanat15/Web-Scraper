SINCLAIR'S SLEEPING BEAUTY

Janice Jennings refused to be any man's "trophy" ever again.  So she hid behind drab duds and thick glasses.  And the camouflage worked.  Not one man looked beneath the surface to discover the woman inside.  Until Mr. Sinclair came along...

Taylor Sinclair was sophisticated, sexy, irresisiible.  Janice was sure he'd had his share of beauties, and had broken a number of hearts.  But something about Sinclair just didn't jibe.  He didn't mind her plain appearance; he wanted to kiss her, hold her, drab duds and all!  Once, she would have believed Taylor was too good to be true.  Dare she trust her heart now?
