As a young man growing up in an Italian family in New York, Johnny experienced a life that was both eccentric and filled with love. Both of his parents were in the music industry with big bands and doing well. Although unaware at the time, the mafia pervaded the industry and his life. Later, he found that there was more of a connection than he first realized. This anthology of related stories is fictitious and, in a humorous, unusual way, depicts the life of a young Italian man who grows up questioning his participation in war, love and family. Dah-Link captures the eloquence, character development and vernacular of an Italian family in much the same way that Alice McDermott's "Charming Billy" and Frank McCourt's "Angela's Ashes" capture the insights of Irish-Americans.
