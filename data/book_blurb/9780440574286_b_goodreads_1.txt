This extraordinary work is one of the most devastating novels to have appeared in modern American literature, a memorable portrayal of the loss of youth, ideals, and soul. Set in Connecticut, with its hero and heroine at the crossroads of their individual and married lives, Revolutionary Road interweaves the themes of meaningless work, empty adultery, futile rebellion, and increasing self-deception to paint an unforgettable portrait of the gradual disintegration of two potentially rich lives. It stands as the definitive critique of the 1950s in America, a novel of cutting clarity, rich compassion, and prophetic insight.
