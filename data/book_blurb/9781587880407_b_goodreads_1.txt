Weaver, whose "Unzipped" column was Salon magazine's most popular feature, lends an indulgent ear to her friends as they wrestle with the lure of having sex with exes, predict bedroom prowess with kisses, and search for the most tactful way to reclaim favorite pieces of clothing left behind after messy breakups. Harriet is convinced that a concrete set of 1950's-era rules is the only way to catch a mate, while Jemma turns to her own shocking set of sexual rules to fulfill her desires. Meanwhile, Weaver's single-mom hairstylist Marie, lends a sympathetic ear and acerbic tongue when she, too, uncovers a sexual scandal in her own backyard.
