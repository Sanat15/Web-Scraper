The Pocket Reference Guide Cats is an ideal compact and complete identification guide to all the officially recognised breeds of the western world and to many others as yet unrecognised. An easy-to-follow system of colour-coded bands and symbols leads rapidly and easily to the correct section of the book. A full-colour illustration and concise, informative text give details of each cat's physical appearance, from the shape of body and head to type of coat and colour of eyes, while the origins, temperament and suitability as a house pet are also covered.
