Her heart belonged to no man... her passions were ruled by one...

Regina...as irrepressible as Moll Flanders, as bawdy as Tom Jones, as determined as Scarlett O'Hara -- a woman you'll never forget.

Regina was born into the dregs of English society - her father was hanged as a thief; her mother was a strumpet who cast her, a child, out into the streets. There she became a beggar and a thief, and was transported to the Colonies and sold as a servant.

Determined to become a lady, she sets out on a new life in America where she finds many loves and adventures.
