Irish Comfort

The opportunity to restore an Irish castle was a dream come true for medieval historian Carra O'Neill. But she didn't intend to be charmed by Glencorrah's chief architect, Michael Fitzgerald. Carra was immune to the romance of Ireland—and to big bold men with emerald eyes and cheeky grins.

Mike had made a career out of building walls, but he'd never seen defenses like Carra's. They were as impenetrable as those around the castle's keep. But with help from the moonlight and some Irish magic, Mike was determined to show this lady in waiting that love demanded trust...and to make the fortress around her heart crumble.
