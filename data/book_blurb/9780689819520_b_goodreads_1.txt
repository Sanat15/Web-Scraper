Sophy has fallen in love with Carlos—the best ballroom dancer at Dance Tech—and with ballroom dancing. But practicing with Carlos is cutting into her time at the barre. And Sophy can’t help feeling that with every tango step she takes, she’s dancing even further away from her lifelong goal of becoming a prima ballerina.
With the big ballroom competition coming up, Carlos starts putting on the pressure: is Sophy going to be his partner or not? For Sophy, the answer isn’t easy. In fact, it’s probably the hardest decision she’s ever had to make. Will she choose Carlos, or living out her dream?
