He created a tempest of dangerous emotions...

If all was fair in love and war, Dr. Ryan Jericho declared the battle lines drawn! Summer Keeton's golden beauty was true temptation, but the software she'd designed would cost him a halfway house for the kids he counseled--unless he distracted her long enough to break the deal. Scorched by a gaze that lit afire of longing, could Summer survive his seduction strategy without losing her heart? 

Could a computer whiz discover her heart's hidden desire in the arms of a rebel with a cause?
