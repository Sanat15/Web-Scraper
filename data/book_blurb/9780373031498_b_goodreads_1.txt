Sarah wasn't pleased to see Radolf Nauta again

It was his fault she'd lost her job at the hospital and had been forced to look elsewhere for work. She hadn't expected to meet him in this new environment. 

Radolf was just the sort of man Sarah disliked most - arrogant, opinionated and entirely too sure of himself. 

And yet, each time she saw him, she found herself wondering what he really thought of her.
