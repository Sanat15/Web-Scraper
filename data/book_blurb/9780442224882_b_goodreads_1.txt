Speed Sewing will build self-confidence in anyone willing to give home sewing a second chance. If your machine is lying dormant because you have never been sure how to master it - or if you are actively sewing but want to try new techniques to capture the full potential of you machine - Jan Saunders will show you the most effective ways to put it to good, creative use.

Here is the first book to show exactly what happens when the needle and fabric meet under the presser foot of your sewing machines. This all-inclusive approach organized according to the stitching techniques for the three types of sewing machines - straight stitch, zigzag, and reverse-action - can help cut your sewing time up to 30 percent, save money, and help your your homemade projects into handmade originals.

Saunders' easy-to-understand instructions cover everything from making sweaters and machine-embroidering garments to recycling old clothes and maintaining your machine. Beginning and experienced seamstresses alike will find all the information needed to sew with more speed, efficiency, and expertise. The step-by-step instructions are accompanied by 150 helpful photographs and line drawings.
