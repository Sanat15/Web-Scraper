Brynn Sinclair couldn't stand being lied to, and there was something about Laker Garrison that shouted honesty and trust.  That was the only explanation she could think of for the impulsive way she'd hired the sexy carpenter to work on her building restoration project.  But that didn't mean she was going to fall for a man like him - here one day, gone the next.

Laker had a job to do.  He was supposed to keep his eyes on Brynn Sinclair - and his hands off!  He had his beautiful boss convinced that he was a drifter, when he wanted nothing more than to stay.  But he knew if he told her the real reason he was here, he'd risk losing her forever...
