The hormonal changes women face during menopause have been researched and commonly discussed, but male menopause remains shrouded in both medical and social mystery. This controversial guide explores how aging affects a man's life and how hormones play a pivotal role in mental fitness, stress, male problems, midlife crises, virility, and proper preventative measures. Everything that is important to men-and the women who share their lives-is included, plus new facts about how men are controlled by their hormones at work and during sex.
