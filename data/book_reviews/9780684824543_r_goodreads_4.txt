Rating: 5/5

Happy Birthday to the legend himself, James Stewart, and to honor the occasion I finished this book! I really enjoyed learning about his life, various acting roles (theatre, movies and television!) and his overall impact on so many lives, he’s impacted his family, friends and GENERATIONS of people! He is truly one of a kind and I don’t believe anyone else could replace him! I love how humble and patriotic he is, he received so many awards, yet would take time to have a one on one conversation with his fan. You don’t see that often anymore but for Jimmy it’s an everyday thing because he has such quality standards he lives by. Some quotes that describe Jimmy perfectly include;
-“He was also a loving father, a regular churchgoer, and a gentleman, qualities that made him something of an anomaly in Hollywood”
-“It’s A Wonderful Life became Stewart’s favorite film (and Capra’s as well). Part of the reason that it was so special to Stewart was its place in his own life story; it was his first film after the war, the picture that showed him that he could still have a career in Hollywood.-It bears out my feeling of the picture business, that it’s not a production line business-but magic.”
-“The great thing about the movies ... is you're giving people little ... tiny pieces of time ... that they never forget.”
-“I’d like to be remembered, as somebody who worked hard for what happened, and who had certain values that he believed in. Love of family, love of community, love of country, love of God.” 
Jimmy Stewart is THE portrayal of what Americans need to be, the kind, humble everyday men that make the most impact on people’s lives because of their genuine sincerity ❤️
