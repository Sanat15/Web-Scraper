Rating: 5/5

I found this book in a old used book store among a bunch of pulp books. Randomly the title caught my eye, and the cover (different from the one above, featured a young woman on the ground in front of him) sold me to buy it. I was pleased to know it centered on a reality television show, one of the first, and the background going ons of the showbiz industry, but then focusing on our main character, the last general practitioner, and him coming to terms that his line of work is being overshadowed by specialist doctors, and a fateful night where a battered woman is left at his door to die, his moral compass with race, life, change, himself and happiness makes a very well done story, please give it a try
