Rating: 1/5

Bodice ripper of the worst sort.
18-year-old's widower dad wants her to marry ASAP, and is picking out grooms. She's having none of it and runs to secret glade in trashy clothes, where for first time she skinny dips, then falls asleep.
32-year-old returns to England, where granddad wants him to marry, produce heirs. He's having none of it and runs to secret glade, when he finds naked beauty asleep and starts 'making love' to her. When she wakes up, he doesn't stop — its too later — and she then starts to enjoy it. 
Then there's a lot of recriminations, they part, and there's a lot of overblown dialogue, and florid writing. And the rest (I'm one of those people who MUST finish a book no matter how bad -- and this was bad) is no better.
There is a plot with privateers, secret messages, and spies during the War of 1812, but historical fact is a little superfluous.
Basically she acts like an 18-year-old, and he isn't much better.
