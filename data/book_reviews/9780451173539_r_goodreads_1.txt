Rating: 3/5

Another well done, intelligent Regency romance from Charlotte Dolan with a little murder mystery thrown in. Misses a 4 star for some minor reasons.

The heroine, a sweet little Regency miss, has been given the nickname The Black Widow as she has had three or four fiancees but has never made it to the altar as they all die. It's sport for the idiot Regency young men to see who will be brave enough to dance with her.

The H's Manipulative Mother demands he come to London to rescue her baby boy. VERY reluctantly the H goes to London. He knows his mother. There he sees the situation and is disgusted with his brother and the other ninnies in how they treat the heroine. No insta-love as the hero has a practical idea of his ideal women which makes no sense here and detracts from 4 stars. He wants to help the heroine overcome her ridiculous reputation but faces opposition from his mother, the heroine's spiteful sister and their guardian.

Gentle romance and the mystery progress and it becomes very obvious who the evil-doer is. There is an OW in the shape of the H's fiancee who jilted him and tries to cause some trouble, but is dropped all too quickly.

The romance concludes as does the mystery and all in all well done but lacking a little excitement and pizzaz. A refreshing change from ripped bodice and quivering loins.
