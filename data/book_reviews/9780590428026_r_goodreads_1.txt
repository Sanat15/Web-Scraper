Rating: 4/5

Val is a young girl who is loving the season of winter and is excited for Christmas. Val and her friends love to hang out after school. They walk to her fathers office at the vet. They overherd one of the women in there talking about killing the deer species. Val was furious because she loves animals. Her and her friends take a stand to try and stop citizens from hunting deer. Will her friends keep their promise and help Val? Read the book to find out!!!
