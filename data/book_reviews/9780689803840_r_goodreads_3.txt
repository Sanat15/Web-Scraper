Rating: 1/5

Oh dear. For a book that's only 150 pages long, it goes on for an awfully long time! The plot doesn't move along all that well, and the characters are all completely flat. The writing is definitely not the best and there are long passages where nothing happens but every single action someone takes is described in explicit, boring detail! Could have really benefited from a glossary at the end as well, because there's plenty of terms that I don't think your average young reader would be all that familiar with and it doesn't engage the reader enough to seek out any more information on the topic! The plot is awfully thin, and seems to be much more of an excuse to talk about why it was so awful to be a girl in the Puritan period rather than actually teach. Readers could choose far better books on the Puritan experience!
