1. Metaphysical reasoning provides conditions for philosophical reflection (e.g., reflecting on the complexity of the human condition that, coupled with the rationality of philosophy, assumes a dynamic dimension, allowing the reader intellectual and ethical enrichment). 
2. Romance/essay—The writer states, "A writer presents himself." This work is a novel "whose balance is a reflection," and that aims to address the problem. " The questions raised relate to the meaning of life and death (with the death of God and the consequent anguish and loneliness of Man, as limits of the human condition recognized, will bring the reconquest of harmony of Man with life.) It is necessary to know what one is and desires and conquer Truth. 
3. The romance of character—Since the protagonist occupies the center of the work, he feels he has to fight to overcome constraints and limitations until he discovers himself and others. This narrator is not a pure individuality insofar as it represents the model of the human being: "The self I deal with concerns all men." 
4. The romance of Space - Considering the importance of the physical space (Évora-Alentejo) and, above all, the common area. (Agrarian bourgeoisie, latifundia, traditional).
