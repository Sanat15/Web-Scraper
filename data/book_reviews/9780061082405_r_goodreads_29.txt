Rating: 1/5

I DNF'd this one. I just couldn't get into it. Granted, I am more of a contemporary/paranormal/dark romance reader. Sometimes, you are just craving those old-school historical romances, and when I picked up this book, I was in that headspace. But when you try several times and the book still can't hold your interest...its time to move on. I grabbed another of my many SW historicals (courtesy of my MIL &amp; her mad rummage sale skills), so here's hoping I do better on the next.
