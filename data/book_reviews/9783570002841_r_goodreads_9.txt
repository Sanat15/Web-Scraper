Rating: 2/5

It is not that this book is bad, it is just limited, although it may not have been written as a tie-in for a tv show it might as well have been. I don't want to say that the only thing worth reading about the Nazi's and their world, or rather the world they created, are books by proper historians, but there are so many good books, very readable books, by authors, to name but one, like Michael Burleigh, who strip bare the reality of the Nazis so brilliantly, so devastatingly, so shockingly and so well written, that it is a shame to waste time on this sort of book. But if you aren't going to try something, maybe a tiny bit challenging, or if this is your introduction to the horrors of that period then go ahead. It is neither completely bad nor is it misleading - but you could get so much more from so many other books.
