Rating: 5/5

The Voyage of The Arctic Tern is just one of those books you don't forget about. I can honestly say I've never read anything like it. The story in itself is one that pulls you in: Fishermen, vikings, pirates, and a curse? I'm in.

But what really cinches this book is its format. This book is a modern classic, an epic poem written completely in verse. 

For a lover of poetry, this book is a must-read, if purely for the experience. For those who dislike poetry, I would still recommend trying it (just not buying it). To me, this book is a classic -- one I could read again and again.
