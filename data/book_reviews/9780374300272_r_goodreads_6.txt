"The door had been opened, and the winds of time were blowing against it, keeping it from closing..."
Today's a perfect sunny morning - I'm glad I'm finishing this series in such a sunshiny atmosphere like this - reading this story feels like walking in a leafy forest dappled in the sun. So glad I kept this final title in store! (didn't plan to, though. It just seemed like some other books were always preventing me from picking the final installment up)
Such a wonderful feeling to return to a Wrinkle in Time world...It looks like its world became a special one for me from the first pages. 
A Swiftly Tilting Planet was like a portal into another worlds, the thread leading safely into another space and time. My favourite from the series.
