Rating: 3/5

Too busy widowed father hires niece of woman who has cared for the family for years. 
While she is having replacement surgery housekeeper asks her teacher niece to fill in for her since summer is upon them. She neglects to tell him her niece is coming. Did she forget or is she playing cupid?
How will the father receive her? How will the quiet, untrusting young son receive her? 
Nice story.
