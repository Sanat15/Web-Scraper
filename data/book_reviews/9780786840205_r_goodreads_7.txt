Rating: 4/5

This book was great. It's set up like a chapter book but is short and easy to read and understand so young children will love it. The pictures were great and the book told the whole story of Snow White. It was almost exactly like the movie. The only thing that could have made it better was if it had added some of the lyrics from the movie where they would have come in. One problem I did have with it was that some of the wording was different than the movie which most people probably wouldn't have noticed but serious fans of Snow White who have seen the movie and listened to the music over and over may have some problems with that. I would definitely recommend this book to anyone who loves Snow White.
