Rating: 1/5

Well, I always feel guilty doing a poor review but wow, this was pretty cheesy. If a squeaky clean “proper” romance book was a 5 and a bodice-ripper was a 1 This would probably rank as a 3. Lots of descriptive kissing while clothed and getting all hot and bothered but nothing further. But nothing felt believable.

Also, this is NOT the cover of the version I read. Had it been so I probably never would have picked it up. 😆
