Rating: 4/5

An interesting and funny read with a bit more blood and less coziness this time. 

The A-team, Superintendent Richard Jury of Scotland Yard and the title-less lord Merlose Plant have to deal with what seems to be a serial killer. A cultured one, who leaves behind snippets of Elizabethan poetry. I enjoyed this volume more than the previous one and I am looking forward to reading the 5th in the series. Martha Grimes is slowly becoming one of my favourite crime writers.
