Rating: 3/5

This book wasn't a favorite. The Campbells and Macleans hate each other, ever since a Maclean chained his Campbell wife to a rock to drown her in high tide. The Maclean's wive's were cursed from that point on.
Felicia Campbell does not want to marry the man her uncle has picked out for her. She decides to escape the keep dressed as her cousin's betrothed, but she is kidnapped by the Maclean's believing she is a Cameron. Rory Maclean has been gone for 10 years and has buried two wives that he cared for. He swears to never marry again and is reluctant to pick up the reins of leadership and be the laird. He isn't happy that his men have kidnapped him a bride, even if he is attracted to her, and is even less happy when he finds out she is a Campbell.
The middle just really seemed to drag on and it took a long time for these two to get together, I got impatient waiting on them. The book also didn't have enough romance and passion for me.
