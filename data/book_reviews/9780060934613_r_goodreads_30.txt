Rating: 4/5

After loving the books of Uther, Starbuck is a different kind of "hero". Born as a Northenern but fighting as a rebel for the South. 
As with all of Cornwells books the history part is quite accurate and he only adds some fictional characters to make a good reading story.
Found it a good start to the The Rebel series. Let's see how young Starbuck goes on to be one of the earlier US Rebels.
