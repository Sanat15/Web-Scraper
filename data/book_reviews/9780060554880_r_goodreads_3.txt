Rating: 5/5

“The Illustrator in America, 1860-2000” by Walt Reed is an ambitious book that presents more than 450 artists, both well-known and less-known. Each chapter of the book is organized by a specific decade and a short introduction to the time period is provided and provides an excellent backdrop for the artists that are covered during that time. The represented artists each have a short paragraph about them, as well as a piece that is representative of their work. An interesting and fascinating feature is that each artist also has their signature provided alongside their bio and artwork.

 All of the major players are here, Rockwell, Leyendecker, Gibson, Vargas, Frazetta, etc. as well as some lesser known artist such as Wily Pogany. There were many artists that I had never heard of and their work didn’t seem to justify them being in the book, nevertheless, it was interesting to see the range of artists presented.

 This is a book that should be in every illustrator’s library.
