Rating: 4/5

This is a Drama filled Romance. I listened to the audiobook for this book, and I found the narrator to be ok. I really got pulled into this book, and I do not want to put this book down. I found the kids to be cute and funny. I loved that there is a writer in this book. The romance is cheesey at times, but I was living for the drama and cute kids moments in this book. I received an ARC of this book. This review is my own honest opinion about the book like all my reviews are.
