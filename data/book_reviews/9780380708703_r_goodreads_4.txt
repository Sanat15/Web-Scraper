Rating: 3/5

A seemingly straightforward account of a UFO flap in Florida known as the Gulf Breeze sightings. I would probably be a UFO scoffer had I not seen highly unusual (and usually disturbing) aerial phenomena on several occasions. I have no clue what UFOs are, but I am open-minded on the subject, even though UFO literature is strewn with garbage such as Chariots of the Gods, by Erich von Daniken.
