Rating: 4/5

So a book about evil spirits, reincarnation and more military lingo than you ever cared to know but overall a great read..This book overlaps a couple of stories with a common thread of a mysterious, superhuman evil spirit that hunts and brutally murders well everyone..This spirit however is pretty selective and has a purpose to his bloody haunts and it kind of forces you to suspend reality but works..This book has some gory bloody scenes and the war descriptions, and the murders so vividly detailed will make every morbid reader like myself pretty excited..Overall I enjoyed this scary treat and now think twice about all genies and wonder if you really do get to come back in a second life...
