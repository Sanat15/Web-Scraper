Rating: 5/5

This book is an amazing resource for parents to wrestle with the holy call to be parents. How to guide our children well, as well as reflect on the ways we can become a barrier to our children so as to be conscientious of them and guard against them. I read one chapter every week for a year with a friend and that gave ample time for digestion and contemplation with God to refocus myself on what He wanted me to do with the truths shared.
