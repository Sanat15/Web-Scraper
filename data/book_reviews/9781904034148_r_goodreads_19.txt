Rating: 4/5

This book was surprisingly thoughtful. While it is filled with murder and mayhem, it also offers an intriguing look into Australia’s dark underbelly and the crime syndicate in general. Too often, people avoid examining what drives someone toward the darker side of life. Chopper Read does a remarkable job of sharing his own intellectual reasoning, at least from his perspective. The writing is sharp and humorous, laced with unexpected touches of poetry and wisdom.
