Rating: 4/5

Just the most persistently cheerful and pragmatic voice in women's history. 
"It is true, I want a great many things I haven't got, but I don't want them enough to be discontent and not enjoy the many that are mine...."
She is too sharp witted to be saccharine, too self-deprecating to be maudlin, and too full of an adventurous spirit for there ever to be a dull page.
Simple, yes, but wonderfully dear.
