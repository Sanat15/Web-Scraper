Rating: 3/5

I enjoyed this book. There were times when I laughed. It held my interest, but it was disturbing in a few ways. Written in 1956, it gives me an idea about the level of prevalence of racism and sexism in the 1950s. I liked the character of Sam, but Thrasher kinda bugged me based on how little he cared for his family or co-workers and his treatment of Alice Taggart. I would recommend a read on the basis of that alone. If you can't bear to read blatant racism, this is not the book for you.
