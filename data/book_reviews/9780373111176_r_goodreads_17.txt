Rating: 4/5

A delightful story about friendship and unexpectedly meeting your Greek god. 

Dizzy James went to help out her friend Christi who was stuck with her old fusty dusty uncle in his castle. What she did not expect to find was a Greek god swimming nude in the lake. 

Turns out that Christi's uncle isn't as fusty and dusty as she claims. He is handsome, sexy and intriguing and hiding a god like body under his oversized tweed jackets. 

The book was a fun read. I grinned like a lion throughout the entire thing. I loved Dizzy and Christi's friendship. Their loyalty to each other and the lengths they will go for each other is a beautiful thing. The ending was a little awkward but still sweet. And Claudia Laurence - who would've thunk?! That was a delightful twist!
