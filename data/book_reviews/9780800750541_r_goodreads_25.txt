Rating: 3/5

I read this as my "stiff" book (Charlotte Mason says a mother should always have 3 books going: a "stiff" book, a moderately easy book, and a novel) over the course of 2021 hoping to grow my own knowledge of early American history as I was teaching my boys at home. I found this book interesting, but not helpful for the purpose I was hoping. It assumes you know a lot about American history and it adds how God's hand was working through the events from Columbus to Washington's presidency. As interesting as that was, and I was encouraged and convicted at many times, it felt overall like we were being told God's motivations and involvement in the American story instead of just looking at the incredible story and coming to our own conclusions about God's plans. I would rather authors not try to explain why or how God was working, because it assumes that they know. Which, no one does. This was ok for me, I'm not totally regretful that I read it, but don't plan to pick it up again.
