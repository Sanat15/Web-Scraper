Rating: 2/5

2.5 stars... This could have been such a great book. But the writing felt so lacking. There were many spots where it seemed an editor should have cleaned things up before publication, and the entire book didn’t have the flow and depth of story that it should have had. The characters felt shallow and the surrounds even more so. The only things that kept this story as ‘good’ were the true facts of Mary Bryant, her fellow convicts, and the circumstances themselves.
As an aside, I don’t feel like the indecent treatment of women was done well in this book either. Even though there weren’t numerous mentions, they were a bit much for children to be able to read this, though the book otherwise seems aimed at children. I think that this is again the fault of the quality of writing however.
