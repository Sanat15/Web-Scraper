Rating: 4/5

This was a lot more interesting than I bargained for. I honestly just got the book because of the cover (love Audrey Hepburn), and I want to tear out the pictures and hang them all over my walls because they’re so beautiful! But I’ll refrain. 

Learning about the evolution of the little black dress and just women’s fashion over time was quite stimulating. Seeing its place in old Hollywood and the role it played during times of depression or war was a good historical lesson. People should definitely give it a go.
