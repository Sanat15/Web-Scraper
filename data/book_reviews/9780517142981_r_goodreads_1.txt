Rating: 3/5

This is a collection of poetry about Love from poets all before 1900. I did enjoy these poems and some of them are very beautiful and some of them are dated. They have Romantic ideals about love. 

There are the bigs like Shakespeare, Dickinson, Shelly, Wadsworth and many many more. I enjoyed Shakespeare and Dickinson the most. 

This is a beautiful pocket edition to bring anywhere.
