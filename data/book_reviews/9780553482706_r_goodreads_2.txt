Rating: 3/5

10 year old me would have given this five stars. I remember telling my best friend it was one of the best books I’d ever read. 40 year old me gives it three stars. I read this for nostalgia’s sake and it was fun and all but the story was pretty convoluted and not very well written. I feel like those aren’t factors you care about as a horse-crazy, adventure-loving pre-teen. I was just happy to read a book about a bunch of thirteen year old girls saving a herd of horses during a snow storm with a couple of twin foals thrown in for good measure. I definitely think kids would still love this series today. It’s pretty solid.
