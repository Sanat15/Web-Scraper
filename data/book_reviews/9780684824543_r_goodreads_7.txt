Rating: 4/5

A thorough and exhaustive account of the life of a great actor. Jimmy Stewart essentially played himself in so many movies but at the same time he had range and reach. A conservative World War 2 military man aside from being a celebrity, Jimmy Stewart could effortlessly pass between those worlds. By the time he had reached the age of 40 he was ready to marry and he stayed married to the same woman until she passed away in the mid-90's. Stewart never remarried but followed Gloria in death three years later. 
Jimmy Stewart was one of the originals and a classic actor.
