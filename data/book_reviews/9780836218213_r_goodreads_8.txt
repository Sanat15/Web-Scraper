Rating: 5/5

This book has a story arc of Ellie remembering when Elizabeth was a baby. Uncle Phil buys a house. Ellie gets in a car accident. Mike and his friends are experiencing crushes and hormones. 

One of the things I like about these is that often times the joke is in the pictures. Someone will say something and then there is just a picture that makes you laugh. I really like how the family encounters real situations and how everyone grows and learns through out.
