Rating: 5/5

I am completely baffled as to why all of this author's novels are not still in print. He is hands down the best writer of Appalachian fiction I have ever read, and I will say that to Ron Rash if I must. I am sure he would agree with me. He knows mountain people. How they think, how they live, their independence and code of ethics. Their sense of pride and family loyalty. Revenge, retribution, and in this book, some hard won give and take. 
Take your pick. You like an intricate plot, great characters of all types, a sense of place? You want murder, adultery, ambition, greed? It's all here. We even get taken along on a bear hunt like no other.

When I picked this off my shelf, I was rewarded with one of my favorite reads of this year.
