Rating: 4/5

This excellent book contains a plausible way to explain why man acts the way they do (the temperament model), and I actually see myself on one of the temperament blends. But Tim LaHaye never stops there. Through Bible verses and personal experience and counselling, he teaches that we can overcome our temperamental weaknesses and reach our God-given potential by living in the Spirit and obeying the Scriptures. This book can help wives understand their husbands better, and help men understand their own selves as well. A profitable read.
