Rating: 4/5

I’m starting to think the Deathlands may be the best of all worlds as the Lost Earth trilogy continues. The new nightmare world the team enters was destroyed by nuclear war in 1963 during the Cuban missle crisis. The archons are controlling a large swath of the world now, with Thrush replacing ambassadors of other countries with archon clones.

Grant and Kane on opposite sides ?? In this reality that’s the way it is, with Grant coming to as a rebel leader in the uncontrolled part of the country and having to work his onto Santa Fe ville to find Kane and Baptiste. 

Kane and Baptiste married and divorced ?? Also tuning this casement, as this Brigid betrayed Kane after discovering the secrets of the alien presence on earth. 

They also have a tougher time taking over their dopplegangers, which may point to Thrush doing something to that reality to make it harder for the team to inhabit their opposite numbers bodies, but the difficulties are not really explained. Kane flips and flops back and forth between himself and the other reality Kane, which actually saves him when under interrogation from Thrush.

Lots of action in this one, with some extended action scenes as the team tries to get to Thrush and take him out.

While all this is going on, back in the Deathlands reality, a team from Cobaltville is making their way to Cerberus, now that Cobalt knows Lakesh is really the turncoat, and Beth Li, after being refuted by Kane in a most direct manner, has troublesome machinations of her own that may spell doom for the heroes.

Another great action packed read, which is all leading to what I’m guessing is one explosive finale.
