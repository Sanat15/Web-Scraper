Rating: 3/5

Nice art and some good lore-building. I didn’t know that the Sword of Power and Skeletor’s sword were half of the same weapon that—get this—together opens the ancient secrets of Castle Grayskull!
Dialogue and plot were better than expected, though He-Man’s “bulging muscles” save the day more than once :)

I also recommend “Time Trouble” by the same author.
