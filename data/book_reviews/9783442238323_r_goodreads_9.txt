Rating: 5/5

High Fantasy book The Druid of Shannara is the second amazing book in the Heritage of Shannara series and it is a great addition to this series. Expect the unexpected from Terry and you are set.

It is an Epic fantasy tale in this magical series. Terry is a master at what he does and he is one of my all-time authors.

 I read this many, many years ago and fell in love with Terry's Shannara world and the amazing characters. I could not put it down. I am only sorry that it has taken so many years to get back to it. The pages are yellow and the smell of print brought it all back to when I first read it.

 You can never go wrong reading his book and expecting a great read.
