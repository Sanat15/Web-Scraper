Rating: 5/5

Some people are war/history buffs. I have never been one of them. That said, this book was completely fascinating from start to finish. Grant could write-- I mean really write. People are not exaggerating when they say this is one of the best autobiographies ever written. My only wish was that there was even more-- perhaps a third volume to cover his term in the presidency.

My favorite quote, if only because it is so keenly true:

"Experience proves that the man who obstructs a war in which his nation is engaged, no matter whether right or wrong, occupied no enviable place in life or history." (Volume 1, page 68)
