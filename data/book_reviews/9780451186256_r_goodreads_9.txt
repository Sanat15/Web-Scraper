Rating: 3/5

Gah. I don't like the dissolute rake, after finding love and reformation, turns back to drinking, gambling, and most aggravatingly, whoring at the first obstacle. I would have liked this so much better if he had almost taken that step but found he no longer had a desire to do It despite his hurt and anger. 

So though the characters were good, the direction the story took and some of the details were not my cup of tea.
