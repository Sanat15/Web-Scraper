Rating: 5/5

This is my third read of this book and the last time was pre-Goodreads. Interestingly, I did not enjoy this on my second read which is why it has been so long, but on this third read I marveled at Susan’s ability to present Charlotte Mason’s ideas about education concisely and beautifully. 

Of course, the first read changed the trajectory of my whole life.

I think this is still well worth reading to hear CM’s principles explained so well.
