A Silly snowy Day by Michael
This is fun and silly book. Turtle wants do things without his shell, because it's too heavy and too hot. 
He tries swimming without it and the fish want to eat him. He tries sledding without his shell. He tries sunbathing without his shell and he gets a sunburn. He also tries to run a race. He finds he needs his shell for many things. Read the book and find out what funny things he does without his shell.
