Rating: 1/5

This is a novel of short stories some of which have a mystery element. I only read to page 160 though.
 This was the last story in the book but it was also the longest and most slow moving story in the book and so I gave up because I wasn't enjoying it. The stories prior to that one were okay but none of them stick out as anything exceptional. I know lots of people like this author but this is the third one of hers I've tried and like in baseball, three strikes and you're out. Life is too short to read books I don't like.
