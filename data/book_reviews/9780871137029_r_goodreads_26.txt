Rating: 5/5

This is the definitive book on the Irish hunger strikes. The Irish Republican Movement gave David Beresford, a British reporter, all the assistance he required to write this remarkable account. The Hunger Strike and the upsurge during it and after, involving people in mass demonstrations and election campaigns that were directly tied to the movement, finally convinced Britain that they could not rule in the old way, through Loyalist thugs. Sinn Fein has become a bourgeois party, more interested in winning seats, both North and South than anything involving principled politics. But the 10 revolutionary nationalists and the many others that preceded them did not die in vain. Today it is possible to unite the working class of the North and win it to the perspective of a united Ireland. We may see it soon (both as a result of this, and incidentally as a result of Britain voting out the EU.
