Rating: 2/5

Ehhhhhhhh. The mystery wasn't very interesting, and the plot dragged. I can see why some readers say they started rooting against Rei—she seems to spend most of this book complaining about the fact that she doesn't know much about manga or journalism, rather than using her skills with antiques / art restoration in an interesting way. I have the next book out from the library, so I'll finish that, but then I think I'm done with this series.
