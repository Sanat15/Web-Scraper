Rating: 3/5

A collection of five stories, of which the first one, 'Don't Look Now', is the best.
All of them are atmospheric and creepy; the problem is, I think all of the protagonists are not just unlikeable, but irritatingly stupid, doing exactly the things they are warned not to do with very predictable results.

Story by story.
'Don't Look Now'
The film based on this one is often mentioned as the classic scary ghost story, and this is the reason I read this book. Maybe the film just didn't age well, but I found the drawn-out sex scene rather funny and then fell asleep %) As for the story, I found it quite enjoyable! Not exactly scary, but creepy and intriguing enough.

'Not after midnight' 
Nice story with a confusing ending. The main character is such an idiot, though!

'A Border-Line Case'
Freud would have a lot to say on this, I suppose. I found this quite disgusting. The main character is also an idiot, and the writing here is somehow naive.

'The Way of the Cross'
This one is not scary or supernatural in the way other stories are. I loved the idea behind it, and it was funny most of the time, but there are a couple of moments that are both repellent and rather improbable, although I might be wrong. 

'The Breakthrough'
Sci-fi from du Maurier. The atmosphere reminded me of Solaris. Another set of unlikeable characters doing something they shouldn't, with predictable results. Well... I thought this was the worst of the five and it is maybe unfortunate that it is the last one.

But all in all, it was interesting to see how versatile Du Maurier was.
