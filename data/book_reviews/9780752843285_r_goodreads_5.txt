Rating: 4/5

I found this book in a local street library and started reading it as I walked home. This was a page turner, I could not put it down! A crime mystery but not gruesome.
Intrigue and suspense.
I did not work out who the villain was until almost the end.
There was a bit of silly romance, it didn't really add much to the story.
At the end, there were still many unanswered questions for the main character but choose your own adventure, I don't think all the ends need to be tied up.
