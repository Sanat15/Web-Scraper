Rating: 5/5

2021 bk 299. One of my favorites of Lansdowne's stand-alones. I forget how good it is, and the name of the main character puts me off at first glance (how can someone named Minerva Potts be a true regency heroine after all). But I delight in this convoluted plot to save a little boy, a woman suffering a mental breakdown at the abduction of the child, a broken heart (Minerva's), and an injured hero. Lots of fun action - and the butlers are at their best.
