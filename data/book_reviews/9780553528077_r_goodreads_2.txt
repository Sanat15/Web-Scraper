Rating: 1/5

Robert Ludlum Value Collection of Little Value: 
I did not do my research before purchasing this collection of 3 Bourne abridged stories before purchasing. These are very abridged, lacking the depth of story line and characters I've come to enjoy in reading the original novels in the '80s and '90s. Overly dramatic narration &amp; presentation with distracting background music. Of no value to this listener.
