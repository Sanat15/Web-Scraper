Rating: 3/5

Having begun writing my own Western series, I have been diving into the Western genre with this being my third book.

By chance, I found this book on a shelf and (having read "The Fallon Trilogy", "Warrior of The Alatai", and "The Wheel of Time") was pleasantly surprised to see it was written by Robert Jordan.

While I know little about Native American culture in general, I was very interested to see the kinds of traditions or customs put into the story or at least see how this story may have influenced his writing.

Overall, I did like the story; I just assumed there would be a bit more conflict in regards to some story threads.
