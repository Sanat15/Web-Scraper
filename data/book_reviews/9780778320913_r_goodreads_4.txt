Rating: 5 Stars!! (Wish I could rate it 10 stars!!) 
Review: 
This was my first time reading a book by Curtiss so I was unsure of what to exspect but I loved this book so much!! It had the perfect amount of Chick-Lit Genre in it as well as Cozy Mystery Vibes that made me love the storyline so much!!

The characters were comical to read and gave me a few LOL moments as well!! 

I will definitely read more by Curtiss in the future!!!
