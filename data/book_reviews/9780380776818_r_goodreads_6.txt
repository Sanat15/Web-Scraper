Rating: 3/5

Disappointing Plot

I love this author and am reading every book of hers I can find. However, this book has been my least favorite as the plot hangs almost entirely on lack of communication, misunderstandings, and the resulting strife between the principals. Not since my twenties when I discovered Julie Garwood have I been able to enjoy a book that almost in its entirety revolves around hostility between two people who supposedly love one another. This series has this been my least favorite. I hope the author returns to plots that allow love to grow BEFORE the last pages of the book, and be enriched by the plot happening around and through it.
