Rating: 3/5

The students in my 6th grade reading group ended up liking the book more than they thought they would at the beginning. I used it because it is part of the Fountas and Pinnell curriculum for Level U readers. As I was reading the book at the same time as they were, I wondered if they would enjoy all the references to early 1990's slang and other outdated customs. For example, it uses what I considered to be outdated slang like saying something is "bad" if it is actually good and "drop him" for beat him up. It also refers to celebrities and TV shows that today's kids won't know, like Hammer and "Miami Vice." One student thought that the book ended too abruptly because she wanted to know what happened to Lincoln after he got back home. Another wished it would have kept going because she had become so invested in the characters. She also said that the book made good use of details and was sad at the end when Lincoln had to leave his host family.
