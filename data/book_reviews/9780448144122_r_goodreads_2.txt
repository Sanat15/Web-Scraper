Rating: 5/5

On my bookshelf is a large, hardback book I bought in 1977 that is a collection of black and white photos of children from around the world. I bought the book because I am an early childhood educator and the United Nations and UNICEF were celebrating "The Year of the Child." The author and editor, Jerry Mason, published the book based on photographer/artist Edward Steichen's exhibition of images from photographers who sent in 300,000 images of children. 377 photographs taken by 218 photographers in 70 countries show the circle of universality from birth to birth of the next generation. The circle shows meaningful moments and emotions of growing up, play, schools, games, religion, music, work, war, death and new life. 

This book was controversial at the time because nudity, violence and despair is shown along with joy, celebration and rights of passage. Perhaps my favorite parts of the book are the quotes from famous writers that accurately reflect the emotions exposed. Carl Sandburg said, "There is only one child in the world and the child's name is All Children."
