Got this book in a care package from the Ripped Bodice Bookstore for a dollar. I didn't choose it, they chose for me, and I have to say I'm glad they chose this one.

I really enjoyed this one. Not in a "wow this will win a literature prize kind of way" , but I genuinely do not think i stopped smiling through the whole thing. This was just a genuinely wholesome regency fake dating/ almost friends to lovers? That I just genuinely enjoyed. There were some weird terms/phrases used, but it didn't detract from my enjoyment of the book.
