Rating: 3/5

A persuasive call for recognizing chimpanzees and bonobos as legal persons. Less convincing is Wise’s theory as to why members of those species in particular are entitled to legal personhood, but which would seem to limit legal personhood to those species with the most “human-like” capacities. It is never made clear why, for example, the inability to follow a gaze might weigh against granting an animal the right to be free from torture. Whether the animal can suffer ought to be a sufficient to establish entitlement to such a right.
