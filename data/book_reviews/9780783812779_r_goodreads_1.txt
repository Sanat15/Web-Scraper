Rating: 3/5

I liked this Australian crime/mystery book. The narrator sounded wonderful with the accent. The book jacket mentioned the word "elaborate" when describing the story. I completely agree with the use of that word. The plot was elaborate...sometimes it felt a little too elaborate. There was a lot of spinning in circles with the plot, but it was creative (so extra points for that).

I liked the MC. This is the 11th book in the series so I'm sure there is some back story I was missing. I felt like I wanted more personality and emotion. Overall, I liked this one. I have another book from this series so maybe I can glean more character info. from that one.
