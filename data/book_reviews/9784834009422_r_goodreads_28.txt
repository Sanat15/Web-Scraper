Anno journeys to the United States from Japan. He crosses the U.S.A. from West to East, passing, the Pioneers, bison, Native Americans, various key national monuments and historical sites and incorporating various classic literary and pop culture icons into his illustrations, including The Little House on the Prairies, Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, The Wizard of Oz quartet, etc.

Grades 1-4.
