Emily Dodd’s father is a painter who doesn't sell many paintings. When the creditors take away the family's furniture to cover debts, Emily begins to distrust him. Emily and her father go on a seaside vacation so Emily can recover from a serious bout of the flu. One day, Mr. Dodd goes off on a mysterious errand. Enlisting the help of Kevin, the hotel manageress' son, Emily follows him. She ends up at Mallerton House where she meets a moody, fractious boy named Oliver Mallerton. Oliver calls her Sylvia because of her striking resemblance to a painting in the family gallery. Sylvia was Oliver's distant cousin who drowned a hundred years before. When Emily sees the painting, she sees herself. She remembers posing for her father while wearing the same style dress Sylvia is wearing. Sylvia is determined to find out the connection between her father's painting and this one which is signed by Renoir.
