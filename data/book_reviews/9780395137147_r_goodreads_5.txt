Rating: 5/5

I love this book it was so interesting even though I couldn't pronounce some names or cities. It was fascinating it brings back kind of a childish side of me almost like my mom reading a story to me. I loved it overall and am more interested in Greek Heroes.

This book can't be necessarily summarized because it's just a bunch of stories of different Greek Heroes in one. This book was not very challenging to read, but I did learn about more Greek Heroes because before this book I only knew Perseus, Medusa, and Hercule. Now I know about Theseus, Prometheus, and Appolo. The writing technique I would assume is fairly new except when the characters are talking in which they talk like in a more proper way if that makes sense. The book didn't really have a theme, but something that all these characters had in common was that they were brave.

I liked everything about this book it was amazing. From start to finish I stayed interested and really felt the book. This book really seeps into your imagination.

I think people who are into Greek Mythology will definitely love this book. This book compares to other books I've read because I usually read about adventures and this is all that this book is about.
