Rating: 4/5

One of the most influential and trustworthy correspondents covering Vietnam for a national network, Morley Safer returned to Vietnam before the 'normalization" of diplomatic relations with the US and provide a clear sense of how the Vietnamese were processing the war. The most important chapters, though, concern Safer's reflections on the pressures good journalists came under as they tried their damndest to tell the truth in a situation enveloped in lies.
