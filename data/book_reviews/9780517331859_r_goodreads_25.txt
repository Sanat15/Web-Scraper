Rating: 5/5

Spellbinding. Amazing. Truly remarkable. The Ninth Configuration is one of my favourite stories and was keen to read both versions but this is much more of a blueprint than the full story, it differs in many ways. However I think I might prefer it. I still need to read the book of The Ninth Configuration but as it stands this story is perfect. It's sad to think that Blatty described this as “no more than the notes for a novel—some sketches, unformed, unfinished, lacking even a plot.” because I couldn't disagree more. Everything is here and to have any more would ruin it. I think I might try my hand at adapting this version of the story into a screenplay because it deserves the film treatment.
Easily slotting itself into one of my favourite books of all time.
