Rating: 2/5

The Inspiration for Die Hard 2: Die Harder!

It bears a minor resemblance to the film for sure, but remains its own pulpy beach read self. It wraps up way too neatly for my tastes, and its villain is cartoonishly super perfect until he needs to fall apart at the end. The hero is maddeningly able to solve every complex problem with an omniscient flair far beyond his profession. The result is a bit of a Rube Goldberg machine of a story: more complex than it needs to be. You'll read worse, but you'll also read better.
