Rating: 4/5

A time traveler story. A lot of action and adventure. Every life is worth something, even if lived in virtually seclusion. 
Russia during the war was just as brutal as expected but not really any more brutal than it is now. It brings in the question which is best, a weak, ineffectual leader or a strong, merciless leader.
It all seemed a little too harsh on Molly. Always second fiddle to the adventure.
