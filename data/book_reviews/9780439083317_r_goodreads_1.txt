Rating: 5/5

This is another of my favorite Christmas books for young children. The pictures, as in all of the I Spy book, are colorful and whimsical. I love the way they just toned everything down a bit for the youngest children, a 2 year old or younger, depending on their interest level, can easily find the pictures that are shown in the rhyme on the opposite page. 

Happily this book, the 1999 version is still available on Amazon as well as another version in 2010 that looks the same but there are added "punch out ornaments".

If you love the I Spy books consider this one for your littlest :) 

P.S. There is also an I Spy little animal book, same size, also a board book
