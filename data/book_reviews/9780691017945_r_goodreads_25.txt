Rating: 5/5

I could’ve never imagined that a day will come when I will read one of Carl Jung’s books. ‘Synchronicity’ is certainly not an easy read, despite the fact that it is only a bit longer than 100 pages. The four chapters are quite diverse from one another. Firstly, the author introduces his audience to the key concepts that will later on join together into the complex idea of synchronicity, accompanied by an experiment in the second chapter, which will support the previous statements. Finally, the last two chapters have a more philosophical nature, analysing also some mesmerising Daoist writings. 
Wherever you are passionate about science or not, this book offers some great insights about the fascinating phenomena of coincidences and human’s role in the world. It is a journey between science and wonder, which certainly should be explored by each one of us.
