Rating: 2/5

I think I read these books to make sure there's not something I'm missing out on, but in reality, these books should have "For Dummies" after the title. Clearly, anyone who is willing to read an entire book on raising kids to love reading knows the basics, and while this book had a few reminders of good tricks and a few books that might be fun to add to the shelf, I found it dated and a bit obvious.
