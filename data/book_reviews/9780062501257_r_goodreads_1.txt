Rating: 5/5

This book is great! Admittedly some of it feels ever so slightly dated. The attitudes towards women being assertive are not quite so prevalent as they once were and girls are no socialized to be quite as "feminine" as in the past (no one has ever suggested to me that I should be the note taker just because I'm female). Still, I think these things linger enough in our culture and what we learn as women that this book helps get to the heart of some of things that keep us from expressing what we are really feeling. It's well worth the read.
