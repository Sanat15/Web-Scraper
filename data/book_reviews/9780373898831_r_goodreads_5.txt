Rating: 1/5

No. Just no.
I did not like Josh at all. Annette was alright, as were Marsha, Jordan and Kathleen. 
Josh was alright up till the very end, he was respectful, even when he let Annette go he did not make love to her when he could have and then broken her heart, he just stopped and said leave.
That being said, the way he manhandled her when he found her in the hotel room, and marching her in front of her father like she was a child was a complete turn off and took this book right down to one star.
The writing itself was very simple as well, so it was a miss all the way around.
