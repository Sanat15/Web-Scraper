Rating: 4/5

Despite the awful cover and title, I really enjoyed this one. Its reminiscent of early Barbara Delinsky (reminder me of her book Commitments), where it tugs at you emotionally and the plot is secondary to dialogue, which is superb. Its well written - what could have been an improbable plot comes to life realistically and his character is just so caring and tender and all those things the perfect lover should be. Plus tortured hero, just doesn't get any better! Another cool thing is that she is the rich one and he is blue collar. The baby drama is the best out of most Harlequins i have been going through lately. I am going to see what else she has written so I can get more! My kind of romance...
