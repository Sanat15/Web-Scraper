2021 bk 304. What fun - I think among the things I most like about this author is her ability to create unique characters - and that most of them have a sense of humor that tickles my funnybones. Manipulating just about everyone is the Earl of Whitshire's mother. He would best like to be left alone to solve and investigate murders and murderers. His mother wants a wife and children for him as she fears being left to the mercy of his horrible heir. Enter the children of the man who once loved the mother - what better thing can she do than introduce the son and daughter to the ton and the girl to his son. Pranks, pratfalls, and misunderstandings abound!
