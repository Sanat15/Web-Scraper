Rating: 4/5

I read The Warning and The Ultimatum this weekend, and neither disappointed. I feel as though I'd
had a mountaintop retreat experience, leaving me feeling closer to God, or at least in recognition of needig to draw nearer. Both served to point out to me, through the experience of the characters, the need to LISTEN - to be still and not give God a litany of my own wishlist but to WAIT upon the Lord and listen to what He has to say...now to put it into practice!
