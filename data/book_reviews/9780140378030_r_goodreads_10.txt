Rating: 5/5

Just when I thought I only liked Mahy books with fantasy elements, this one proved me wrong. There's a lot packed in here, which gave me plenty to chew on even as an adult, but I know this would have been one of my favorites as a kid had I read it then. The mystery, the strong characters, and the vivid imagery are hard to shake even after finishing it. I suspect there's plenty more here to unpack on a re-read.
