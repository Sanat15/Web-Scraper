Rating: 4/5

More like 4.5 stars. What a piece of history that we never get to read! Not only is this story set in the world of the U.S. Life-Saving Service--precursor of today's Coast Guard--but it also shines a spotlight on Black heroes among these brave men, who were finally honored in the 1992, 100 years after their greatest feat, and only after painstaking research and passionate reporting of the truth. I hope that a boy like the fictional protagonist, Nathan Williams, really did grow up to become a doctor for the Black community of that coastal outpost in North Carolina.
