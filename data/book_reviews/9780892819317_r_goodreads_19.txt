Rating: 4/5

I found this book very helpful when I was researching vaccinations. It's definitely an anti-vaccine book, so know that going in. The author lays out each disease and its risks/treatments, and each corresponding vaccination with its risks and/or ethical issues. It really helped me to see which diseases are a more dangerous and really weigh the pros/cons of the disease vs. the vaccine. She also talks about natural remedies to combat some of the diseases.
