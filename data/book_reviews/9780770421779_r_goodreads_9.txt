Rating: 4/5

🌳 Margaret Laurence and I share the same birth province where one of the houses she lived in is now open to the public, maintained in the manner she would have been accustomed to when it was her dwelling place.

Margaret is a strong and sensual writer and eschews the easy or predictable route to producing a work of contemporary realism. All her work was formative to my own way of creating fiction. 

Stone Angel is very much worth the read though I will say I always wished she had developed more colorful similes and metaphors like Alice Munro or Margaret Atwood.
