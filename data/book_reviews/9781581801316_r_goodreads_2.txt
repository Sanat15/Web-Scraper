Rating: 5/5

In looking for a way to use velour paper ( a paper that feels almost like very soft, short fur), I came across references to this author/artist, which led me to her oohs, detailing her methods of pastel on velour paper.
Her instructions are clear and very easy to follow. Her animals are incredible!
She shows the pastels she uses, so I can see what I need to buy. Happily, I already have quite a few sheets.

Later: I did a small painting, using her suggestions. It worked, and worked very well! I'm definitely hooked on this paper for use with soft pastels.
