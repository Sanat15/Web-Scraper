Jenna couldn't thank Duncan Fergusson enough for coming to her rescue when her car connected unexpectedly with a boulder on a quiet road in the Scottish Highlands. But did that entitle him to keep Jenna and her little sister, Suzie, virtual prisoners in his home? She simply couldn't counter his reasons for prolonging their stay. First, the local doctor -- summoned by Duncan -- prescribed a few days' rest for his two patients. Then, to make matters worse, Duncan bewitched Suzie. Why was he making it so hard for them to leave? And why did part of Jenna really want to stay...?
