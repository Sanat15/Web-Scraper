Rating: 4/5

Lots of wonderful things to find, including many references to children's books: 
Charlotte's Web, Make Way for Ducklings, The Rabbits' Wedding, The Little House, Little House in the Big Woods, Caddie Woodlawn, Angus and the Ducks, Play with Me, In the Forest, Where the Wild Things Are, My Father's Dragon, Ferdinand, Tom Sawyer, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, Little Orphan Annie, Dawn, Peanuts, Sesame Street, "The Gift of the Magi", there are surely more just in this area.

The journey is not only cross-country, but back through history as well.

If only I still had the eyes of my youth.
