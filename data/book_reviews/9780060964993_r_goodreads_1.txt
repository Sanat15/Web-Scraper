Rating: 4/5

The infamous cow creamer is back.
Aunt Dahlia, Sir Roderick Glossop, and a whole slew of Bertie's nutty friends, ex-fiancees, and frenemies show up in yet another hilarious story.

This is also known as Jeeves in the Offing. &lt;--because Jeeves takes off (ing?) for a vacation, leaving poor Bertie to fend for himself. Don't worry, he comes back at the end to sort it all out.

I know that it doesn't have a lot of Jeeves in it, and maybe that will cause some of you not to like it as much, but I really loved the story. Just the fact that Bertie &amp; Glossop bond in this one made me laugh. Loved it!

And the version I listened to had Ian Carmichael as the narrator. 
Bravo! Well done, sir!
