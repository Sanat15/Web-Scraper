Text-to-Text connection: In this story, Emily Elizabeth adopts new puppy who ends up being quite a handful to care for. This book reminds me of another book I recently read, "George's Dragon". In George's Dragon, George goes to the pet store to adopt a small lizard that grows into an adolescent dragon, and then a full-grown dragon. During the dragon's adolescence, he gets into all sorts of mischief. The dragon feels bad for accidentally burning things. He expresses the same feelings as Clifford when Clifford starts to grow too big and messes up a lot. Both sets of parents were not too pleased with their pet's growth. However, in the end they decide to love their new pets just the way they are.
