Rating: 5/5

There's something particularly special about Kurt Vonnegut. I was lucky enough to discover his works when I was a teenager and I've recently been re-reading the books I read all those years ago.
I didn't have this collection in those days, I had "Welcome to the Monkeyhouse", but I recently saw a damaged copy for a reasonable price so I snapped it up. I mean, why not? It has excellent cover and interior illustrations by Leo &amp; Diane Dillon!
