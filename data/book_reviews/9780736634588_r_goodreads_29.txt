It's honestly hard to know what to make of this massive chunk of book (with apparently lots more to come!), as the narrative tracks four friends from their school days into young adulthood, as they pass through the strictures and codes of early 20th Century British society. It's a bit too mannered for my liking, and the great rondos of that society aren't all that interesting. That being said, Powell is a master architect of character, and you really, really get to know everyone involved, and they're actually pretty damn interesting, even when they're gross. It's a bit mannered for me, but if you're more interested in Austenesque novels of intrigue, this might be your jam.
