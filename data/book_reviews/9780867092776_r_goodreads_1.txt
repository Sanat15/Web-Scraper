Rating: 4/5

This is a nice blend of theory and practice book. Organized into three units, the book begins by discussing the relationships between language, thought, and culture, drawing on research by people 
such as Shirley Brice Heath, Vygotsky, Basil Bernstein, and William Labov. Next follows "Literacy and the Learner," in which the authors turn more specifically to the traditional components of the English curriculum: grammar, reading, writing, and literature. The final unit, "Theory into Practice," turns specifically to addressing how to implement the theories and practices described earlier. In addition, the book contains a twenty-two-page section called "Strategies for the Teacher and Learner," a series of activities integrally related to the discussions in each unit.These include ethnographic observations as well as classroom activities
