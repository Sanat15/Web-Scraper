3.5 stars

I have enjoyed this first reading of the Dalemark series. 
I read the quarter's installments far apart from one another over the course of the year. Consequently, by the time I reached this one which features characters from the other books, I'd sort of forgotten who some of them were. That hampered my enjoyment a bit, but that only encourages me to read this again one day in closer succession to the previous three. I can't review this objectively, I'm such a fan. 
Reading Diana Wynne Jones is like lying on your tummy at the feet of the most experienced storyteller in your family and I'm never not gazing adoringly up at her, kicking my feet. She's someone warm and loving who holds you in her safe and capable arms. 
Since first reading the Chrestomanci books as a kid, I've trusted her to take me on adventures and bring my heart safely out the other side. Her storytelling fills me with wonder and curiosity.
