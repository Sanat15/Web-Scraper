Rating: 3/5

A sweet little story all about valentine's day Roo wants to do something for his mum for valentines day but doesn't know what to do, nobody really does actually but after a slight hiccup Roo's brain kicks in and he comes up trumps.

Next valentines day I now know what I'm doing all I need is a huge rock, a gopher, a tigger, a piglet, a pooh bear, a rabbit, loads of paint and a dictionary...... hmmm seems like a lot of hard work.... flowers it is, I think that Roo was on to something.
