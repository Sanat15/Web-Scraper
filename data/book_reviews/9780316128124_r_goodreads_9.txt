Rating: 5/5

I read it years ago...and am in the process of moving/downsizing and clearing out some of my bookshelves. Came across it and looked through it to make decision keep/or no keep. It's a keeper. 
I took it with me to read on plane to Italy...with intent of turning it in for credit at the local used book store in Florence. Read it on the plane and have decided to keep it. 

There are all the same contributing 32 authors, with all the same pieces...but on this second reading, given years between, whole new meanings emerged. So it will travel back with me and become a monthly reader. It's just that good.
