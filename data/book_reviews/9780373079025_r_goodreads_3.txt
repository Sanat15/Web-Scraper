Rating: 3/5

I borrowed this from my aunt back in high school, and the story stuck with me. Late last year, when she told me to raid her old stack of romance novels and take whatever I want, I remembered it immediately. I think it was the premise of a mother being so desperate to protect her child that she would sacrifice her own life and freedom. As well as a man who has decided that whatever feelings led to his heartbreak before were not worth experiencing again. And also that the love that opened his heart again was not the love of a woman but the love of a child. I like that spin. 

So I liked it. The grandparents being desperate for a child for all the wrong reasons was also interesting to explore.
