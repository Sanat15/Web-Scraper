Rating: 2/5

It may be due to my childhood love for the My Teacher is an Alien series, and therfore my bias, but I was expecting more from this book.
I was kind of bored. I felt like Coville was missing something in this mash up of a creature feature. Sure it was weird, but not in a "heehee" kind of way. The plot was all over the place and just seemed to be a way of introducing more monsters.
Kids might like it, but what do they know? Its definitely no MTIAA.
