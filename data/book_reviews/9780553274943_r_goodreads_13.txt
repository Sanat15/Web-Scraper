The Gunfight at the OK Corral has been the subject of so many westerns that like Custer at the LBH it makes up its own genre. What makes Estleman's take unique and worth reading is the stark realism of the style and tone. The Earp vs. Clanton feud comes off as a gang war for turf between two ruthless groups of thugs. Hard to put down, a fine western for the summer.
