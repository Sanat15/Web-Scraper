Rating: 3/5

1. This book is appropriate for Kindergarten to fourth grade.
2. This book is an information book that has facts about space. On each page it lists strange but true fact about that particular component of space. It goes into detail about the moon, stars, and planets. 
3. There is a lot of information in this book. Kids who are older would be able to read most of this book and there are enough pictures that break apart the words. Kids who are younger would be able to sit and listen as a teacher reads certain parts and look at the photos. Children in this age range will have a working knowledge of space and be able to understand and learn new concepts of space. 
4. This book would be a great tool to use when a classroom has the theme of space, the moon or the stars. A teacher can use certain components of this book to connect it to their lesson. For example, a student can look at one of the pages of a planet and recreate it for themselves. Everyone in the class can draw a different planet and together the class can make a solar system!
