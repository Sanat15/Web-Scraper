Rating: 5/5

Suspenseful thriller

Kit goes to Pratte, Vermont to gather her best friend's belongings after she was found murdered behind a grove of birch trees at a town party. The town MD struggles with an internal battle only a select few people know about. A struggle that makes him wonder if he is the one that murdered Jo. He can't remember anything that happened just before Jo's body was discovered. Kit meets the handsome doctor and the two begin to call in love. The doc tries to force himself to stay away from her unsure of his own actions anymore. Meanwhile, Kit has vowed to avenge her best friend's death. Knowing Jo was conducting a secret investigation of a murder that took place 32 years ago, intending to write a book about it revealing secrets thought to have been undiscovered. As Kit tries to investigate Jo's murder someone sensed she's getting too close to the truth, and begins stalking her looking for the only remaining copy of Jo's book determined to keep it from being published no matter what. 
This book pulled me in with a great plotline, twists, turns, and interesting multilevel characters.
