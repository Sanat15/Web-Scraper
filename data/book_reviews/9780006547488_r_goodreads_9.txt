Rating: 4/5

I thoroughly enjoyed this volume of stories of female travelers. I have to say, I was a little taken aback to have the volume start in such modern times, but I think the author made a good decision to group the travelers by their mode of travel. This way it was easier to compare and contrast the experience of the various women over time. I would, however, have liked a bit more detail on the women. It seems like we only got a few details here and there on the travelers, with more focus on how the author felt about what these women had to go through, instead of a more thorough story. I do agree that few of these women were feminists, and felt compelled to voyage from any desire to further the feminist agenda. Instead these women were compelled simply to travel, to go somewhere and see something unique and new and different, just because it was there. And that, in my opinion, is the best reason one could possibly have for a journey.
