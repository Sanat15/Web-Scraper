Rating: 4/5

It seems so silly to give this piece of classical writing a star rating, but here we are.
Did I understand this text? No, not really. Was it a worthwhile read? Maybe, I hope so. I'd definitely like to revisit it at a later date.
I will say that it was much less accessible than the Tao Te Ching, which is not necessarily a negative, just something to keep in mind when deciding to read this or not. The writing was surprisingly dense despite the somewhat simple language used in the translation. For such a small book, it took me a long time to get through. I very much enjoyed the introduction by Burton Watson, the translator.
