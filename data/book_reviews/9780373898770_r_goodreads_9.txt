Rating: 1/5

This was the most irritating couple ever. They spent most of the book having ridiculously pointless arguments, both of them supremely filled with preconceived notions and inflexible attitudes. She was such a spoiled little brat that it was difficult to think of her as a romantic figure--it gave me a squeamish feeling. And although he seemed reasonable at first, he became increasingly annoying, very caveman but not in a sexy way. The secondary characters were actually pretty nice, but the two main characters were awful. I only made it all the way through the book because it was a very quick and easy read. I will give this author one more try to see if this was just an off-day.
