Rating: 4/5

I received a free copy for a honest review

This Regency novel is set in Devon where England is under the shadow of Napoleon’s invasion &amp; the windswept coast has become a hotbed of intrigue. There are smugglers &amp; spies as well as the ordinary stuff of life too. Joanna Fineton, is expected (by her grandparents) to marry well, preferably to Lord Cholcombe's son Algernon, who's a bit of a dandy that she hasn't seen since childhood.

He'll be coming to Teignton Manor, Joanna's grandparent's home, to pay his respects soon, but we also have a certain military man, Captain Masterman, who has been showing interest in Joanna. Not to mention mysterious Captain Jackson, who breaks into the house and has Joanna totally curious about him, although his work as a double agent is spotlighted from the beginning of the book.

This is a well written enjoyable novel. Not a straightforward romance &amp; I definitely preferred the addition of spies &amp; intrigue. Written over 50 years ago, this story has stood the passage of time well. I’m now looking to read more by the author
