Rating: 4/5

I was inspired to read this book after seeing the amazing and controversial musical "The Scottsboro Boys". It was a really powerful narrative sharing the life and experiences of Clarence Norris, the only one of the "boys" who survived to be pardoned by the state (40 years later). Strong and searing depiction of the terrible racism and corruption of Alabama/ the courts/ the country. My only criticism was that the cowriter/ chronicler had done some additional research and I couldn't quite tell how much info she had added in. (The narrative is almost all in the first person except for a few trial transcripts.)
