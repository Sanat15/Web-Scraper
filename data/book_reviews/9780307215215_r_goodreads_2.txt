Rating: 5/5

I enjoyed going back to my youth &amp; reading a story from the Trixie Belden series! It was loads of fun hanging out out with Trixie, Honey, Brian, Mart, Jim, etc. At age 65, I realize how smart these teenagers were, at that time, and how polite and well-raised. I appreciated them more now for those reasons, and more, than I probably did when I was 12, or whatever. I would highly recommend for tween ages on up in reading!
