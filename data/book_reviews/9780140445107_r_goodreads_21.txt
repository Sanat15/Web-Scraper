Rating: 5/5

I had studied Aristotle before but had never read the entirety of his Rhetoric. I was fascinated to discover how much I had learned about him was contained in these few 150 pages. And not just about Aristotle specifically, either—it rather felt as if every principle which I had learned: of reading, of writing, of work, of leisure, of generally living a life of eudaimonia—could in some way be traced back to Rhetoric. This book is about far more than just rhetoric. It is a must-read for a full classical education.
