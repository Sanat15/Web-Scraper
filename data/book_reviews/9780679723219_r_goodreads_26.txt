Rating: 4/5

This was mostly slow paced with lots of character detail and was a compelling read. Mildred Pierce kicks out her out of work husband and goes into business for herself after she realises she is such a good cook she could run her own restaurant. Considering the time this was written I found Mildred to be quite a modern woman particularly in her sex life. Her big problem is her devotion to her awful, snobby and precocious daughter, Veda. Veda gets a few slaps and I think she deserved all of them and more! What a horrible girl. Interesting observations of class and attitudes to work, politics and gender.
