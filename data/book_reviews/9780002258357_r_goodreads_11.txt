Rating: 4/5

I first read this book in junior high, so like 20+ years ago, so this is technically a second read but I remembered basically nothing. If you are looking for a portal fantasy à la Narnia or Harry Potter but with beautiful writing, look no further. The writing in this book really immerses you in the whimsical, magical, and sometimes terrifying vibes of this story. It really is so different from stories we typically get today and deserves so much more love.
