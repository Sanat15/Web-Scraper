Rating: 3/5

Adventure, betrayal and bagpipes—what more could you want?

If you’ve ever thought your family was a bit shady, wait till you meet Ebenezer Balfour. Young David Balfour sets out to claim his inheritance and ends up sold into slavery by his own uncle.

A whirlwind of 18th-century Scottish drama, complete with shipwrecks, sword fights and political intrigue!! David’s journey from naive heir to hardened survivor was really a rollercoaster!!!

“I have seen wicked men and fools, a great many of both; and I believe they both get paid in the end; but the fools first.”

Alan Breck Stewart: Jacobite rebel, swagger incarnate and the kind of friend who’ll shoot first and never ask questions. Their bromance fills the story with banter, bullets and bagpipe-worthy bravado.

The historical backdrop adds a layer of gravitas to the fun. It’s a coming-of-age story wrapped in tartan and tied with a musket!!
