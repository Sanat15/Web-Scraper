Rating: 1/5

Boring as batshit. Also, I either hated or didn't care about any of the characters. Like CASTLE OF CRUSHED SHAMROCKS, this book offers an unusual gothic setting with an intriguing premise, but Karr's writing style sucks all life from the story. Literally everything that happened in the conclusion was predicable by chapter 3. Bonus: the heroine was such a holier-than-thou judgmental moron that I couldn't stand to read about her bullshit. She fails to question or even glance at the most bloody obvious suspect ever, &amp; then ends up being saved only by the work of everyone else. 

So, ladies...I guess the moral of the story is for every poor, ordinary shopgirl to be as unpleasant, unfriendly, rude, distrustful, &amp; patronizing as possible to the handsome millionaire who moves in down the street, because that will make him fall in love with you after 2 encounters. 😑 STFU. I can't even deal with those sorts of heroines. 

1.5 stars. I was gonna round up, but then I thought eh, I have nothing positive to say about it, so why should I?
