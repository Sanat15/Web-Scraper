Rating: 3/5

A striking premise — a priest shot mid-communion by someone wearing a police uniform — makes for an attention-grabbing start. Sergeant Terry Flynn takes on the case, and the Chicago setting adds grit and atmosphere to the investigation. The story moves along well, with some solid tension and a good sense of place.
I just wasn’t sold on the ending. It felt a bit forced, as if the pieces were made to fit rather than falling into place naturally. Still, an interesting read with a strong hook and a memorable setup.
