Rating: 4/5

This is a collection of stories I first read as a child. I think it may have been the first to introduce me to the idea of a book that didn't have to be a single story but could be filled with shorter compositions. I've kept it all these years out of nostalgia and I finally decided to give it another read. The only one that I really remember from all those years ago was Belinda and Belamont, and that mostly for a fragment of verse that has been stuck in my head for near enough forty years.

It's very much of its time, with lots of parents dead or away in India, leaving children in the hands of uncaring guardians, and free for magical adventures. I suspect a modern child wouldn't be able to make head nor tails of these stories, but I loved them as a kid and still retain a lot of fondness for this book.
