Rating: 4/5

Although the book is written in true Keller form, I found it a bit more difficult to absorb than his book on Psalm 23. Nevertheless, it ends in a powerful, compelling way lifting my eyes of faith to Christ - The Lamb Of God Incarnate - who is seated in glory. This fact makes all the difference in how I live day by day. This book will change how you see Christ as the Lamb of God.
