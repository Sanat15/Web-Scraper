Rating: 4/5

This was a good read. I had a lot of notes written down in my reading journal of what I thought would happen. Some I had right and some I was way off. I did figure out who the perpetrator was, but it wasn't until I was close to the end and I still wasn't expecting it. Now that I think back on it, everything made complete sense and I should have seen it coming. The ending was full of action and a real page turner.
