Rating: 4/5

A very good year for BASS. Lots of terrific pieces, and only two or three true clunkers. My favorites, in rough order of preference:
* Jhumpa Lahiri - "Nobody's Business" (amazing)
* E. L. Doctorow - "A House on the Plains"
* Richard Ford - "Puppy"
* Michael Chabon - "Along the Frontage Road"
* Jim Shepard - "Love and Hydrogen"
* Alice Munro - "Family Furnishings"
* Alice Mattison - "In Case We're Separated"

And some honorable mentions:
* Akhil Sharma - "Surrounded by Sleep"
* Arthur Miller - "Bulldog"
* Edwidge Danticat - "Seven"
* Karl Iagnemma - "Zilkowski's Theorem"
* Meg Mullins - "The Rug"
