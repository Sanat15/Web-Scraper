Rating: 4/5

This was a nice read. If it had been a bit shorter, tighter, I would have given 5 stars. The story takes its time to unfurl and introduce every character. The first part is quite interesting, as there's a bit of a mystery (although you can guess early on). Unfortunately the second half of the book is a bit slow. It could have been shortened and tightened, by restricting the details on every and each secondary character. That said, you can tell this book was supposed to be one of a series, and I really want to read more about the other characters. 
The love story is not truly centerpoint, this read more like a cozy mystery with a side of romance. Both main characters are lovable and well natured, and the supporting characters are very interesting and add a lot of comedic value. 
All in all, 5 stars for the first half and 3 stars for the second half, makes an average of 4 stars. 
I recommend this for slow burn, clean romance lovers.
