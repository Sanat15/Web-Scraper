Rating: 3/5

Shivers is probably my favorite Goosebumps knockoff series because of how dark its entries tend to be and how they explore adult themes that Goosebumps would never dare touch on. This one follows Bill and Barbara ("Babs") who go to their favorite Orlando-based theme park, Dreamland. Obviously the plot plays out like A Shocker on Shock Street and One Day at Horrorland, as you could guess. This is literally a story about human trafficking though. This was pretty entertaining but a little too predictable for me. Recommended for fans of stuff like this, but don't go in with super high expectations. This one doesn't come close to being as epic as The Curse of the New Kid.
