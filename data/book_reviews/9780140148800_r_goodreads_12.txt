Rating: 4/5

Some of the jacket blurbs misled me. "A few good belly laughs" — "often hilarious"? Not for me. "Comedy of manners" — I suppose so. But "satirical", "ultimately very sad" — definitely. 

There's a large cast of quirky characters, hard to keep track of if you read as slowly as I do. The lasting impression is the hypocrisy of some of them — especially the politicians.
