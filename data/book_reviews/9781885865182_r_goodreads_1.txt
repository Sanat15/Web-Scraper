Rating: 3/5

My absolute favorite story of this anthology was ”Drink to Me Only with Thine Eyes—” by A.R. Morlan, I think it’s worth five stars. I also enjoyed ”Moment in Time” by Deb Atwood, ”His Name Was Wade” by Gary Bowen, ”The Blood Hustle” by Raven Kaldera and ”What We Are Meant to Be” by Robert Knippenberg.
