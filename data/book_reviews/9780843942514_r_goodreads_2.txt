Rating: 3/5

This book irked me at times and at others I really enjoyed it. The author tried too hard with metaphors newly created “like her heart broke/ cracked like peanut brittle (page 350).” I get that she was trying to use unique turns of phrase, but they fell far from the mark. Other than that the story had loads of twists and turns and I truly did not know how the author would resolve the many conflicts and get the couple together in the end (as all good romances should end). Overall, I enjoyed it! But will probably not run out and try to get more romance titles by this author. It was just sitting on my shelf, likely one of my mama’s thrift store finds in later life.
