Rating: 5/5

Mary Allen read the introduction of Rooms of Heaven at Iowa Summer Writing Festival for an audience of about 100 students in July, 2014. I knew I wanted to read her book the minute she finished. This is a heart tugging memoir with lyrical prose that has the pacing of a novel and a satisfying ending. Certain books resonate with readers for a variety of reasons- place, characters, story, situation, or the writing itself. This one will stay with me for awhile. 
Thanks for the good read, Mary Allen. Good Reads rating- It was amazing with a personal opinion of "I'd read it again."
