Rating: 3/5

I didn't know...but,...I figured it out

This book was Ok. Drew and Mallory were best friends who became lovers, who went back to being best friends, who happened to end up with Mallory being pregnant.

As I'm writing this, I am starting to realize what was bothering me. I felt NO romantic chemistry between Drew and Mallory. I felt friendship. 

I could believe they loved each other. I understand that not every relationship is a grand passion. But, the uncertainty Drew and Mallory felt about the other person's feelings permeated the entire book.

Even after the realization that they loved each other happened, I really could not get past that intense feeling of uncertainty. There was nothing in the book that made me feel like it had been settled. I guess this means that the writer did an excellent job in making you experience that uncertainty through her words. I just never got that feeling of a HEA, although it did occur.

I recommend you read this book, and judge for yourself.
