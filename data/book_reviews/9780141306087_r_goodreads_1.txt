Rating: 4/5

Wonderfully drawn (except the human characters). The story was alright, although feral cats are a huge problem for Australian native wildlife (which is where the story is set).

The main antagonist, Major Mac, is an oversized fella who they call an ogre, so a bit fatphobic there. A lot of older books (and I’ve noticed this trend particularly with the Paul Jennings books) have the antagonistic characters be fat. Not necessarily because they want you to ‘dislike larger people’, but because ‘fat = bully’ tropes.
