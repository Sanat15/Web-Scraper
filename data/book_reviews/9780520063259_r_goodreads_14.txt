Rating: 5/5

I read this book for one of my Anthropology classes in college and it was not what I thought it would be. Tepilit Ole Saitoti wrote this book in such a way that it keeps you wanting to know more about him and his amazing life. His book made me want to study cultural Anthropology more than when I decided to go into the field. I was amazed by all the hardships he endured and still made it to where he ended up in life. I highly recommend this book to anyone who loves a story of perseverance, you will not regret it.
