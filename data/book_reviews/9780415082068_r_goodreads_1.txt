Rating: 5/5

This is simply the best Danish grammar book on the market. At 700 pages, this book leaves no stones unturned and no obscure rules untaught. Everything imaginable is in this book, along with ample examples of each principle and rule.

The only brief complaint I have is that some of the English translations of sentence examples are awkward. However, it's easy to tell what the correct sentence would be in each case.

As is usually the case with the Routledge comprehensive grammar books, this one is simply the best of its kind. Regardless of your level, you won't find a better Danish grammar book than this one.
