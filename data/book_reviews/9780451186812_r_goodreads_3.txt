Rating: 2/5

So I made it to chapter 11 out of 18 and I think this is the end of the road for me with this ridiculousness. 

First off, good things: FMC isn't terrible. I mean, she's not particularly attractive in any way at the beginning, but she has fairly good intentions, is a bit feminist for the time, has a good sense of right and wrong, and when she does do wrong she is aware of and regretful for it. She's got a good head on her shoulders. She's not particularly likeable really, even when she undergoes her ugly duck-to-swan transformation, but she's essentially a decent person, and she treats others according to their character and merit, isn't snobbish, and is kind to all her suitors unless they are creepy rapey pervs and in that case she very rightly knees them in the groin and stabs them with forks. 

The MMC's grandmother is cool. So is FMC's grandma, who is her second (?) cousin. 

Now, to the horrible very bad annoying AF jerkface mealy-mouthed, shallow, pervy, morals-of-a-dog rake of an MMC/male "love" "interest.

Here are all the things I hate about him and why he should be a villain not the hero:

* He's unscrupulous with only a few actual boundaries he won't cross (raping actual children, outright direct murder, and yeah I think that's it). He will deceive, manipulate, coerce, philander, seduce, and/or maneuver anyone around him who has anything he thinks he deserves. Usually money or sex. 

* He genuinely does not like the FMC in either of her guises, but thinks he's in love with her. He doesn't like that she lectures him, he doesn't like her accurate judgments of his ways, he doesn't like that she's independent, etc. Just does not like her. He lusts after her, even when she appears to be 14 and he THINKS that she's barely 16, to the point of forcing a kiss on her, while still despising her and himself for doing it. 

* He has a weird obsession/lust for his OWN GRANDMOTHER's... portrait as a young woman. It's alluded to by the FMC and himself mentally, and she assumes it's the REASON he's attracted to her after her transformation- she looks almost exactly like his OWN GRANDMOTHER as a young woman. Again, the man is in lust with the portrait of his own grandmother, i.e. just in lust with his grandmother. To the point that most of his affairs are with people who look like her, he's only really attracted to the FMC after her transformation when she looks like her, and he openly acknowledges that what draws him to the FMC is her similarity to HIS OWN GRANDMOTHER. This alone is weird and creepy AF. 

Quote from text: She had forgotten the details of that portrait since leaving Lanyard Manor and had never realized that she resembled it. He was abnormally attracted to it. 
Rumor credited him with many liaisons. Only now did Melissa realize that all those women shared characteristics with Lady Lanyard [MMC's grandmother]– tall, full-bosomed, light-haired. What did that mean for her?

* Even when acknowledging or hallucinating that he's in love with the FMC he sleeps with other women. At no point does he regret ever sleeping with married women, even when he's the guest of their husbands, even when their husbands are his friends/acquaintances, EVEN when said woman is a brand-spanking-newlywed, at all. He thinks he's just sowing his oats. 

* Just, every time he even has a little think about himself and his life, he comes across as incredibly shallow, boring, lacking in morals, and entirely self-centered and selfish at every turn. At no point does he actually do anything that isn't self serving in at least one way, sometimes even 2 ways at once. 

* He's not particularly charming in any of this, which would kinda make up for it; it would have a piquancy or something to be like, oh, he's a cad, but look how charming he is! He's not though; he just comes across as wishy washy, selfish, spineless, and blegh. 

The only reason the villain of the story is so bad is to make the alleged hero look better by comparison, and if you have to do that it's a weak plot/characterization. 

Now, someone get a time machine and get this back to the 90s to Ms. Lane's editors so she can save this book lol.
