On December 23rd, 2023. I was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease. That was my Christmas present given to me by my neurologist. It was not a surprise and I accepted it right away.

Michael J. Fox was diagnosed with early - onset Parkinson's disease in 1991. He did not go public with it until 1998. He did not accept it right away but when he did he had this to say.

" This doctor would also tell me that I could probably continue acting for" another 10 good years", and he would be right about that, almost to the day. What he did not tell me - what no one could - is that these last 10 years of coming to terms with my disease would turn out to be the best 10 years of my life - not in spite of my illness, but because of it."

The first part of the book tells about his alcoholism, and his insecurities about Parkinson's. He talks about growing up in Burnaby, a city outside of Vancouver and his move to Los Angeles to further his acting career.

When he realized his alcoholism had turned into a problem. He decided to quit with the help of AA, his friends and a therapist in New York City named Joyce.

" I've had this feeling lately, One I haven't had in years. That old feeling like I'm waiting for the other shoe to drop." Joyce says to him. Michael, you have Parkinson's disease - the other shoe dropped a long time ago."

After that, he became an advocate for Parkinson's and and testified in front of Congress several times.

And two full years of operation, the Michael J. Fox foundation awarded 25 million in Parkinson's research funding.

At times during the book I found him to be a little arrogant but then when you were 68 million and you're married to Tracy Pollan - I guess you can get away with it.
