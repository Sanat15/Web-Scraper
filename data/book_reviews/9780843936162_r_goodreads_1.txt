HR’s I will always recommend! 

Another HR I read 20 years ago that I will always re-read. I love Leigh’s writing so much I’ve paid a small fortune in shipping from the the US to AUS to now own over 20 of her books. 

The Golden Spike can be read standalone or as ‘book two’ to ‘Angel’.

A western romance that focuses on railway being laid out across America. An unyielding rancher, his fiery daughter and a southern doctor make for drama &amp; action! The love that blossoms between Rory and Thomas is a little unconventional in the best way &amp; you can’t help but fall for the Doc’s charm &amp; cheer on Rory’s spirit!
