Rating: 4/5

A major piece of scholarship and required reading for all art fans of this period of American art history. The basic thesis is that there was more in common between the American Impressionists and Realists than previously thought. There are over 300 reproductions of work from the period, many in color. There are also a fair amount of reproductions of works from private collections, always a plus in my mind.

The book can get a bit overwhelming, though, in terms of historical context for individual paintings. Great stuff!
