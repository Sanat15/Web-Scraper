Rating: 5/5

Zulfikar Ghose was my professor at the University of Texas, and I read the book when I was his students many years ago. I recall that Zulfikar's friend wrote Little Big Man that was made into a movie, and The Incredible Brazilian was almost a response his his friend's dare. I think Zufikar's story is Brazil's version of Little Big Man, a character who transcends history. I loved the story's span and of course all the sexy parts. Who could ask for a better teacher. Also, Zulfikar let me read an unpublished novel he had written that was set in Texas and was about a powerful family. It was very good, and I do not see it in his list of published books. What happened Zulfikar?
