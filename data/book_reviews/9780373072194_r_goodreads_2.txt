Rating: 2/5

This would have been a 4 stars book if not for the extremely annoying heroine.
The plot was interesting and with good fast pace. The hero was interesting and intriguing. But the heroine... OMG... I hated her! Soooo stupid!!! She was obnoxious! She ALWAYS made the wrong deduction! She ALWAYS thought that she was the one who made best decision! She NEVER took a good advise given to her! She ALWAYS knew better than all! Arghhhh!!! I wanted to strangle her! To shake her while yelling: "Idiot! Idiot! Idiot!"
I really don't know why the hero fell for her... but I pity him... poor guy...
