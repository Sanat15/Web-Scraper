Rating: 3/5

The book ¨Dont you know there’s a war on?¨ is historical fiction, and a mystery book By Edward Irving Wortis also known as AVI. The book goes over the theme of friendship, war, and adventure.			
The plot of the story follows Howie Crispers during WW2 in Brooklyn New York. Howie is a young boy whose imagination gets the best of him and his best friend Denny. Howie thinks his principal Dr. Lomister is a nazi spy. So Denny and he started developing a plan to expose their principal. During the time Howie finds out that Dr. Lomister is firing his favorite teacher Miss. Gossim, to which he forgets the plan to expose Dr. Lomister and tries to find out why Miss Gossim is getting fired.
This book isn’t one of the favorites that I read but it’s still good. It’s a coming-of-age story during wartime that takes you on a rollercoaster of emotions. The writing style most definitely makes you think Howie is talking to you. It brings you along during Howie´s life and you form a bond with him. The good part of this book is that it’s just overall a good quick read.
I recommend this book to a younger person. The book is pretty short and not a long read at all. This book does have some pros, the writing style isn’t the best but it makes you feel like you know the main characters. I enjoyed this book. If you are older you may feel like this book is immature, therefore I don’t recommend reading this book.
