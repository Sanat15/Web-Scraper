Rating: 3/5

My guilty pleasure is a regency romance book. They bring a sense of comfort that is often what I need after a long day at work. 

I’ve read quite a few of the books in this series. But this one was not my favourite. The heroine was annoying and needed to grow a backbone early on. The rake of a hero was not allowed much time to grow and so it’s a bit of a quick turnaround when he declares his love for her. 

So 3* rating because it’s ok as a book. But not the best I’ve read. An easy read (or listen) but took me a while to get into.
