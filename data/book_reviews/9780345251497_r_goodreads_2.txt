Rating: 3/5

I don't know what it is with Alice Chetwynd Ley's novels that I don't rate them higher. It was just my second one (and for sure not the last one) but I have the feeling that more or less the same will be with the rest of them. In a way, I am a bit angry at myself.

Mrs Ley books are really well-written. Like in The Jewelled Snuff Box I got all promising parts of one of my favourite genres. Historical accuracy (especially in the social rules), well-chosen characters, an intrigue (with spies), an action, a romance (although too abrupt for my taste here). There was no place to be bored. I was interested in the story the whole time. There was also a bit of humor (yet, not as much as I like). The novel was simply "a good job" - I don't know how to put it.

But... I don't know. E. g. I have read much worse written Regency romances/mysteries that (despite errors, inconsistencies) I enjoyed more. Perhaps it was a matter of small things? Too abrupt romance. Too obvious mystery. Not enough wit.

PS I have just read QNPoohBear' review. She touched the points of why I am not giving 4 or more stars.
