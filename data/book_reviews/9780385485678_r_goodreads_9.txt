Rating: 4/5

This was a nice book of short stories to read during the Christmas season. Out of all the stories, four were my favorites. Trouble at the Inn was about a school Christmas pageant where a change in lines goes incredibly right. The Gold and Ivory Tablecloth brings two people together again, taking place after Hitler’s armies devastation. A Gift from the Heart tells the tale of a Swiss girl who wants to give something to someone who does not have material wealth. Charlie’s Blanket shows the innocence of a child, and a family who has much love to give each other on Christmas.
All these stories have something in common. It’s not the presents, but the love one has during Christmas being the most important.
