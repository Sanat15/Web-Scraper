Rating: 5/5

I absolutely adored both novels and I think the comparisons to Jane Austen are apt. I think the Semi/-Attached Couple was more Austen-y and The Semi-Detached House was definitely more humorous (not that Jane Austen isn’t humorous! She is! It is a different sort of humor than in The Semi-Detached House).

I am already looking forward to a re-read. I gave them 4.5 stars, but I rounded up because I had such a great time reading them. I think I’ll enjoy them even more on a second or third read. I just wish there were additional Emily Eden novels to enjoy.
