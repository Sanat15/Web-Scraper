Rating: 3/5

I don't normally go for book that are mostly made up of quotes. But I really enjoyed this. I like the categories the quotes are sorted into for each chapter. And I like the order in which those chapters/categories are arranged. It's a really fast, easy read. I chose to read only one chapter a day and give myself the time to reflect. I've taken that approach with other books before and I really like that method of "meditating" on the contents of a book. Many of the quotes are ones we've all heard before and overall it's somewhat generic advice applied to fishing (or rather, fishing analogies used to reiterate generic life advice). But I find that it's beneficial to be reminded of that sort of advice.
