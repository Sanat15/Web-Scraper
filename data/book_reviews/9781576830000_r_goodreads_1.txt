Rating: 3/5

A friend of mine lent me this book to read. It is basically an introduction to discipleship, written in the 90s when it seems like there were dozens of these types of books being written. It's not a lot different from what I remember of those books, but I thought it presented the material fairly well. I think there are better books that cover the same information (though I honestly can't remember any at the moment) but this one is pretty good.
