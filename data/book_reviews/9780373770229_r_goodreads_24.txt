When she tried to seduce him one day (as husbands and wives should ... note that he'd been ignoring his wife never coming to her bed for weeks now), he behaved abominably; treating her badly and telling her she's not his wife but only his secretary. 
When she was about to pack to leave the country, he 'raped' her, but of course, the heroine was too soft to put him off. Only after the fact did she regret doing it, she thought he only used her when all he ever wanted was his beloved ex. So she sulked. The Hero, being as obtuse as he was, stormed off and 'divorced' her. 
So the heroine returned to the U.S. 
Only after finding out she was pregnant did he come and met her. 
There was no groveling. He barged in to her office where she worked and she left in his limousine. They talked. It was like a one sentence and she fell all over him again. Forgave him for all he'd ever done. 
This was a complete bogus pair of couple. 
So mad. 
I know D.P's Heroes were mostly a-hole, arrogant and proud but never this bad. 
And the heroine, mostly were a bit on the 'no backbone' type but never this awful.
