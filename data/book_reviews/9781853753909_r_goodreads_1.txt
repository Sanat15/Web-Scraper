Rating: 2/5

I'm actually really surprised I didn't like this.

It sounded very much up my alley. I’m generally a fan of middlebrow classics, like the stuff Persephone publishes, and I was expecting an entertaining standoff between social titans Elizabeth Mapp and Emmeline Lucas (Lucia). I love feisty women and character drama.

But, instead of juicy and scandalous, I found this very beige and dull. I know some readers didn’t like the unlikable characters, but I don’t even think it’s that. It was more that they were flat and uninteresting to me. I found all these people very silly, and with way too much time on their hands. A bit of jealousy and schadenfreude is human, I think, but I couldn’t for the life of me understand why they were being dickheads about nothing at all.

There was no explanation of their motivations, no deep dive into their personalities to make it make sense. Perhaps there are some people who are petty and awful for no reason whatsoever, but they do not interest me. The reasons WHY are what make unlikable characters fascinating.

In short: this is a book about women constantly sabotaging each other's plans for kicks.
