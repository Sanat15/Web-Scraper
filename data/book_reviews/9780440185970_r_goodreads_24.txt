Rating: 1/5

I know, I know. I should have known better than to read this one--and I knew what I was in for two pages in--but it was a recommendation, and I have a weakness for finishing things!

It was about as anachronistic, cringeworthy, and unbelievable as the title might lead you to expect. Poor Georgette Heyer would be spinning in her grave. It was short, and in a better book I would be annoyed that the story didn't have time to develop, but since this sucked it just made it a faster read.

A small thing to fixate on amidst the horror, but you never, ever need to use the phrase "wet liquid." I can promise you that your readers will assume that the liquid is wet without you having to enlighten them. You're welcome.
