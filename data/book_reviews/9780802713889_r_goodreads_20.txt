Rating: 4/5

This is similar to the Robin Heath one in the same series, indeed Mr Heath gets a mention at least once.
The author is obviously very knowledgeable about the subject, but has written a book just quirky enough to interest non-scientists. 
At times the illustrations look like products of a session with a Spirograph set (ask your parents), but highlight the beauty (and 'coincidences') of the cold clockwork of the planets.
I still marvel how our ancestors were able to deduce so much from their observations and calculations.
There is a hint or suggestion every few pages that these coincidences are perhaps nothing of the sort.
Food for thought....
