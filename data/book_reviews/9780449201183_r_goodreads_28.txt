Rating: 4/5

Love historical and regency romances. Marion Chesney always delivers. A lovely romance. Lady Lucy becomes dis-enchanted with her husband, a womanizing drunkard. Her friend keeps advising her to take a lover. When hubby embarrasses her in front of the "ton" by committing the basest "faux pas" she decides to take her friend's advise. She asks the Duke of Habard to pretend to be her lover and he surprisingly agrees. Lady Lucy for some reason is beginning to get under his skin. Things go wrong from there and end in murder and vicious gossip. Can Lucy and the Duke find a measure of happiness amid this debacle?
