Rating: 4/5

A nice semi scholarly lengthy epistolary historic novel that doesn't really go anywhere but is a good read for all that. This is the second one of her vampire books that I've read and I really liked them both, which is why I was sorry to see the end of this one and with it, I suppose, the end of the series. Olivia dies, D'Artagnan dies and the hopes I was beginning to nurture of seeing them come through the centuries until their books are written in email rather than snail mail got dashed. That kind of sucked and I wasn't prepared for it. Oh well.
