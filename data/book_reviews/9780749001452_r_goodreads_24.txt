Rating: 5/5

What a great, great book. I had read it too quickly, the first time around. A great little mystery, but like “a Greek tragedy”…; and emotionally nuanced, rich, complex…. My original review (below) was arrogant and stupid. I had read very little crime fiction at that point, and I could not appreciate the finer points of this one.

2014: 4-1/2 stars. The flaw in this book is obvious, and would have been hard to correct.

The ending is excessive and not wholly believable. A pity given how flawless the first 90% of this book had been

That said, one can see from this book why MacDonald is so highly regarded. The intricacy of the plotting is impressive; the characters rich; the writing smooth. 

Just a cut below the Galton Case.
