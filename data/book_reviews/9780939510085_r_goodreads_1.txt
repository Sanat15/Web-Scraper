Rating: 4/5

Love this cookbook! It was a gift from friends and I go back to it for several recipes. Most notably the Tipsy Carrots.

There are no photographs of the dishes, but it is spiral bound - kind of a trade off.

The focus is on seafood, but it also includes quite a few recipes for sides and desserts. A great go to with recipes - a little bit of everything.

One of the fun things about this book is that it also talks about cooking on your boat and on land. Lots of handy tips.
