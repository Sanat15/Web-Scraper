Rating: 4/5

This is a short, and admittedly old, book, but it still gives a great introduction to the world of amateur astronomy. While his advice on equipment is out of date, the principles still apply. Same with his sections on astrophotography - this was written before digital photography, but in general the rules still work. I've read that he's been working on anew edition, hopefully that will happen one day. Only four stars because it is old, but a great little book nonetheless. I've read it through twice now, separated by many years, and while I've been an amateur astronomer for decades, I still find little gems inside.
