Rating: 5/5

This book is excellent. I'm currently studying for a master in public policy &amp; have to do a course on quantitative data analysis. The course (rightly or wrongly) assumes a basic knowledge of statistics which I did not have (beyond everyday parlance). In plain English (and not scary equations!) this book uses simple everyday examples together with a wry humour. I'd imagine it was hard to cut back the jargon &amp; math heavy terms to make this accessible. Bravo Derek!
