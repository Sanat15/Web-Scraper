Rating: 2/5

This was my 2nd attempt to read a Maeve Binchy book, the first, was a DNF and this one I did finish, but really had to force myself. I just kept hoping it would get better. My main complaint about this story was that so many of the characters were grouchy, whiney, argumentative and just overall were unpleasant. I listened to the audiobook version, so I don't know if was the narrator or the way the characters were written, but so many of them seemed angry all of the time. That was the first problem.

Then it seems like so many of the main characters (with few exceptions) had very few redeeming qualities. Let's start with Helena/Lena. . .I just can't fathom a mother allowing her children to believe she was dead, just so that she could be with her lover. And then her lover, Luis, what a selfish creep! At least in the end he got what he deserved! The whole relationship between Kit &amp; Clio. . . so much drama and disagreement. I'm not sure exactly why Kit was even friends with Clio. . .the way she treated her and the mean spirited way that she interacted with her. . . who needs that kind of negativity?

The only characters with any positive qualities were Maura &amp; Stevie (though he didn't start out that way!) Oh and the Hermit Nun, but then she just kind of disappeared from the picture. I never really got what was the point of the fugitive she was harboring . . . that didn't seem to add anything to the story at all.

Anyway, I know Ms. Binchy has a lot of fans and I apologize to any of you if I have offended! But I will probably not be choosing another MB novel anytime soon. Sorry!
