Rating: 4/5

One of the only 18th c. books published by a free slave to condemn and lobby for the abolition of the Slave Trade in the West Indies. For writing by a freed slave, it is obvious that Cugoano had a robust education because his rhetoric and the methodical nature of his argument are really impressive. However, as the Penguin Classics Introduction claims, his writing does feel redundant and I did find it to be unnecessarily long (while it was only 100+ pages, even that felt too much). Overall, a good argument of why slavery in the West Indies and the Atlantic Slave Trade in general was the greatest evil known to man. He totally disproves the Christian rationalization of the institution of slavery, and I found I was convinced by his argument.
