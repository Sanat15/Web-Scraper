Rating: 2/5

Weak protagonist! Gabriella is labelled 'strong' in this book because she overcame all the obstacles but I personally feel that it made her character weak &amp; pathetic when she allowed people to kick her around. And then it ends with a happily ever after with Prince Charming. Boring...
