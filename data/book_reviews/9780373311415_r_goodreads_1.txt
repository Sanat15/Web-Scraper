Rating: 4/5

Lucy Bledsoe and her companion were guests of the dowager Countess of Silverthorne when that lady became very distressed by the tricks her son was up to, telling the women in his life that he was engaged to a Canterbury girl his mother wouldn't let him jilt. To teach him a lesson, Lucy takes the part of the nonexistent fiancée. However, Lord Roderick plays along and eventually marries Lucy.
