Rating: 5/5

Came across this book in a store discount pile in 1986. Grabbed it and giggled over the way the cartoons could perfectly tickle the funny bone. I was in boarding school in NY, and a fellow schoolmate shared that Mr. Walker was her uncle. A week later, she came back from the weekend with a personalized cartoon sketch written to me. I almost didn't believe my memory until I came across the drawing this weekend and immediately did a search to find more out about the author. So far, I haven't found much, but am enjoying seeing that his book is still available and even read by at least one other person!
