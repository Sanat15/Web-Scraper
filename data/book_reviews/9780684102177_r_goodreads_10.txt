Rating: 4/5

There are better told stories but few better stories to tell than one which poses the question why the virtuous are despised? An immigrant Jewish doctor has integrity, honesty and grit to spare, but lacks the important all-American skills of self-promotion. Despite the fact that he is a brilliant diagnostician, he remains mired as his old neighborhood decays around him. When a gang lays one of their victims on his doorsteps, the doctor lands in the morning papers and his story comes to the attention of a Madison Avenue man from the Midwest who develops a plan to spotlight Sam Ableman's quiet heroism in a national reality show. Can the world of razzmataz lift the cursed clouds that follow the doctor? I want to watch the movie to see if it has the Hollywood ending the main character deserves. But the author stays true to his story and I was wrapped up enough in the fate of Ableman to wish he’d written a happier conclusion.
