Rating: 1/5

It is really hard to rate a book about the Shoah, with a historical value and a moral meaning. Maybe I faced this book with too much expectations, and it didn't satisfy me totally. The main problem is that real and dreamlike dimensions are too much intertiwined. Therefore, sometimes I had to re-read a part, in order to better understand the meaning of author's speech. For example, I thought that some dream of the protagonist were a metaphors for the drama of the Second World War. Unfortunately, they weren't, the protagonist was simply a dreamer with a vivid immagination.
Anyway, I must say that the last pages, about the Nazi's arriving and the deportation are extremely intense and able to transmit the real impression of the degradation of humanity in the name of absurd and atrocious ideas.
