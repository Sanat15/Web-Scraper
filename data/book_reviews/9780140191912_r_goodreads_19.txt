Rating: 4/5

What an odd book. Clearly this man did not have an editor that took their job seriously, because most of it is rambling and unclear. A lot of words, very advanced words, await the reader of this circuitous, winding mess of a book. There are good parts in this book, most notably the parts that describe the specifics of scientists' histories of discoveries, and their experience with creativity. The most valuable aspect of this book to me is that the author believes that creativity is independent of many other qualities, and elegance and aesthetics has its place in scientific research. It takes a lot to get there, but hopefully hard-earned lessons stay with you longer.
