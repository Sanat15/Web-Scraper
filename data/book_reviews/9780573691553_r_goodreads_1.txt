Rating: 2/5

I had a hard time getting through this series. The problem with the Varney the Vampire series was the author was publishing the installments of the story each week as they were being published in the newspapers/periodicals. It started out okay, but just kept going aimlessly and the character development was very inconsistent.

He was nowhere near the brilliant writer Arthur Conan Doyle was, who also wrote his periodical stories of Sherlock Holmes. Bram Stoker, who wrote the brilliant novel of "Dracula" was also a genius of his time and did a much better job. All this to say I KNOW great literature came from this time period...but Varney certainly wasn't one of them.

Personally, I wouldn't recommend reading these stories. They're just very disappointing.
