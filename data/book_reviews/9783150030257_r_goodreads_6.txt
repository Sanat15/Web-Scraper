Rating: 4/5

A very funny play, basically a Satire of Viennese life during the mid 19th century, 1833. Nestroy uses a lot of local slang and tells the story of three journeymen dealing with winning the lottery. One pursues a girl and gets rich, the other drinks it all because he thinks the worlds ending in a year, and the last goes bankrupt. It's pretty good, a bit difficult to get used to the way it's written but after a few read overs and a handy Notes and Documents book from the library I was able to get through with ease.
