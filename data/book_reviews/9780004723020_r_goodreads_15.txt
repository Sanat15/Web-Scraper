Rating: 4/5

“The more we know the easier it is to survive. Knowledge dispels fear.”

I started this book with the intention to read it over time - I've recently been really interested in learning some survival skills, creating a bug out bag, etc. so I thought that I'd give it a deep read. I quickly became wrapped up in the book, struggling to even put the book down.

There's some great information in here, with excellent supplemental diagrams. I will definitely be picking up a physical copy so that I can study it over time.
