Rating: 4/5

This book, paired with my ethics and faith in the workplace class, accelerated and grew my knowledge and passion for faith and ethical integration in not only work, but in life. This book explores many aspects of how one can experience and implement their faith and ethics in many instances backed by the Bible and real-world examples. Definitely would recommend this book to everyone! It not only explores the author’s beliefs on faith and ethics, but it promotes one to analyze themselves and their beliefs, goals, and values related to said topics.
