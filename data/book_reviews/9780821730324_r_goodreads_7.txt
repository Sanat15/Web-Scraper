Rating: 5/5

Excellent book by Joe DiMaggio's brother Dom who was also a major league player who played over ten years for the Boston Red Sox. Describes the 1941 season in which Joe DiMaggio hit in a record 56 straight games - still a record - and Dom's teammate Ted Williams became the last man to hit over .400 (.406). He also tells of other players who played prominent roles during that year, all the while awaiting a call from the draft board as World War Two was approaching. Great book for any baseball fan who appreciates the players from the past who were the building blocks to today's game.
