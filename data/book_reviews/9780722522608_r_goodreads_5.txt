Rating: 5/5

Hands-down a 5-star rating for this one. Having been introduced to essential oils 6+ years ago and reading a plethora of information on them during that time, I found the book to be a very concise, very helpful reference for a multitude of issues experienced by women *and* men alike. (Men, don't let the title of the book scare you away! There may be sections that will not apply to you, but the bulk of the book is pertinent to both sexes.)

The author doesn't spend much time talking about the basics of essential oils (what they are, how they work, safety, etc), but that was not an issue for me as I have read numerous other texts that do. (That said, if you have not read such information from other sources, and/or you are new to essential oils, I strongly suggest that you do find credible sources from which to learn that prior to reading this book and using essential oils in general. E. Oils are incredibly potent, and have the potential to harm if not used properly.)

There are a small number of things I do not agree with in this book, but discerning reader will find that to be the case in anything he or she reads, so I do not discredit the author because of it. In fact, within the first few days of starting this book, I was able to test out two different recommended protocols from the author, for two different issues, and experienced far faster and greater relief than anticipated!

Again, I highly recommend this book!
