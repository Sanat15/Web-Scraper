Rating: 4/5

The last book written by Chandler with Marlowe following a redhead for a mysterious client. World weary, cynical and with a sharp dialogue this story was better than I expected after reading a few reviews. 

SPOILERS AHEAD 

Blackmail, corruption and Marlowe doing the right thing is entertaining. Betty is escaping a vindictive father-in-law after she escapes conviction of murdering his son when the honest judge discharges the guilty verdict. 

Mitchell tries to blackmail her and Brandon an ex racketeer who falls for her makes his body disappear. Marlowe deducts all this and doesn’t involve the police and on return to his office gets a phone call from Paris which makes the air full of music.
