Rating: 2/5

I usually love this author, but this book just didn’t work for me. It lacked the usual intensity I expect, and the hero felt dull and forgettable—nothing like the author’s other leading men. A big part of the story keeps the main characters apart, especially with the added complication of the hero’s brother wanting to marry the heroine, which made things feel dragged out. The plot itself didn’t hold my interest either. Overall, I found it hard to stay engaged and ended up losing interest in what was already a slow read.
