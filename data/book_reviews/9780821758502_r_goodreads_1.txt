3.5

I liked this one, it was well written and enjoyable. I thought Emily and Damon had good chemistry. The plot was good, but didn't blow me away. I thought it was a nice read and I'm glad it was clean and while Damon is supposed to have a reputation, there is never any direct talk of mistresses or things like that. It made it a lot easier to like him, although he was still a mystery to me. I wish we could have had more from his POV. My only minor dislike was the random regency slang, there was quite a bit and it didn't always flow and tended to bring me out of the story. Overall, it as a nice regency.

Content: Clean, one scene with mild innuendo.
