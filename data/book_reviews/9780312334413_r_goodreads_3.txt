Rating: 2/5

I thoroughly enjoyed Island of the Lost and thought it was very well written, so of course I couldn't wait to read historical fiction by the same author! Sadly, most of the book felt like what it is, a first attempt at fiction. I wasn't curious about who committed the crime and I didn't particularly like any of the characters. I felt that the main character's race was being shoved down my throat. But to my happy surprise, the seemingly unrelated happenings that bored me early in the book became relevant in the last few chapters! I hadn't guessed the ending after all! I'm glad I stuck with it to the end. Now I'm hopeful that the author has found her stride and the second book may be what I was looking for in this one! We'll see!
