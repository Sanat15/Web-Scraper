Honest College Student Review.

I'm a college student who reads her textbooks.  Yes, we still exist.  
This is the first textbook that I have had to settle for the e-text. McGraw-Hill only offered this version loose-leaf. 

The interactive 3D brain and its areas were my absolute favorite! Using the ReadAnywhere app, I had my textbook on the go at all times. After some chaotic life events this semester, I have been using my iPad to read my textbook, highlight, make notes, and so forth. Within the ReadAnywhere app. It helped me so much when I couldn't sit down with my laptop to read my book. 
Overall, this textbook was laid out well. Vocabulary highlighted, written well, easy to comprehend, and offers so much.
