Rating: 4/5

Read this book is two days - short and compelling! The story is set in ancient Greece and believably (accurately?) represents the historical era in which it is set - very evocative! I felt incredibly sad for slaves who had no rights at all, and the women who are also without power, in this early "democracy" - particularly the slave girl (Minta) who serves the main character (Phano). The inspiration for writing this story is found in the Afterword at the end of the book - a fascinating window into times past.
