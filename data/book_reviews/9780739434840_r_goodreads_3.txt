Rating: 4/5

The story starts harshly for the main character, Bay Butler. She's in jail for a murder she didn't commit. Her surprising release and her overturned conviction surprises her. Given her history and situation Bay is remarkably level headed. She manages to cut the stings that came with the fixed change in the murder of her partner and even find love. Surprising turns and a persistent heroine make this an enjoyable read if you like a story where a topsy-turvy world is righted by the main characters.
