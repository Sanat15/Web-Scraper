Rating: 4/5

I've been reading Baby Blues for many years and recently decided to buy and read all the books in the strip's history, and I have been really enjoying it! This volume is one of the earlier ones, and includes strips in the 2002 or so period. Almost 25 years ago, but the strips are timeless and not dated at all. Almost every page brings a smile and laugh. Zoe and Hammie are still very young and Wanda becomes pregnant with Wren during this book. Both Wanda and her neighbor Yolanda have their babies at the same time, which makes an amusing episode with the dads since the plan had been for one family to watch the other's children while that was happening! After Wren is born there are many amusing strips about her infancy. Any fan of the series will love this book!
