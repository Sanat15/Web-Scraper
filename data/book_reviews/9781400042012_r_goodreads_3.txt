Rating: 1/5

I absolutely detested this from start to finish. A soppy, syrupy, cinematic sob story that ends with a clash of drama followed by a cute paste-on conclusion. Unrealistic. Terrible lines. Fake from start to finish. A dysfunctional family, violence, physical abuse of woman and child, maiming by and cruelty of animals, drinking, drugs, profane language.....and then all of this ends sweetly. Granddaughter and grandfather are united and will live happily ever after. I knew exactly how this would end. 

My God what drivel! 

There are two narrators. The male sections are narrated by Tony Amendola. This was easy to follow and well performed. Judith Marx narrated the female sections. Her narration was way too level, subdued, calm and sweet; her intonation did not fit the lines. With such a terrible book, who cares how the narration might be?!

Sorry, I had to blow off some steam.
