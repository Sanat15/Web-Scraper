Rating: 4/5

although a bit confusing at first because of the constant switching between viewpoints, the novel gets really interesting right as Selena and Leo see each other on the beach, and it really picks up from there...with the little Spanish I know from Duolingo I managed to get through the english-translated book (with quite a bit of spanish words), but I wish I was able to read it in the original text.

as someone who knew absolutely nothing about Afro-Caribbean identity and how they relate to each other, I found the book fascinating in how it looked at the intersection of identity, race and the underlying economic current that fuels the relations between each country.
