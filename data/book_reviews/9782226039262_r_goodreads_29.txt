Rating: 2/5

Yikes

Meandering, unrestrained, uninteresting rambling. I liked the first 200 pages of this book when it was a small, focused constrained setting with Bobbi and Gard as the centerpieces. This is how it should have continued. 

I love King’s world-building abilities, but it can’t start off as a two character, narrowly focused story and suddenly explode into a large ensemble out of nowhere. Why are characters still being introduced in the third act? Why are there chapters within chapters? 

There is a great novel in here somewhere, but it needs some serious reworking. I was hoping this would help break up a recent rough stretch of reading for me, but it had the exact opposite effect. This is far from a pallet cleanser. This bad taste in my mouth lingers.
