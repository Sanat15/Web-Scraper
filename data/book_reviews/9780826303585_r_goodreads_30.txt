Rating: 3/5

Very conflicted about this one. As a writer, I loved it and as a reader I really didn't enjoy it as much as I expected. Gorgeous writing with moments of true epiphany but a lot of the times it ends up kinda just looping in on itself saying nothing and just describing things with no seeming purpose. And I kinda conceptually fuck with that style of writing but it often didn't hit for me for some reason. Maybe the mildly archaic language? Not sure. I found myself locking in more when she was writing about people or relating landscapes to people, particularly when she spoke about irrigation and talked about how it robs water of its identity. But why do I need nature writing to appeal to a human perspective? Maybe if the density and celebratory-ness of the writing was brought down just a LITTLE I could have stayed engaged during the purely descriptive sections. Either way I am very happy to have read it and I could honestly see myself revisiting it in the future and having a stronger opinion, but as of now it is a beautiful book that did not tickle my fancy! Hence why it took me nearly a month to read 75 pages hahaha.
