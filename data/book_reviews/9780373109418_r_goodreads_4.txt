Rating: 5/5

Possibly the best Mills and Boon ever written. Hero is a snooker player called Dave from Sheffield. We know Dave is international jet set because he has a tour at Pointins coming up which he can't get out of. The clincher comes when the heroine discovers Dave has his own sun bed. Naturally she marries him.

Sadly things go wrong and two years later our heroine is about to marry Sir Nigel (all round good bloke and loaded international diplomat) so she has to track up to Sheffield to see Dave again to ask for a divorce.

Inevitably Dave gets rid of trusty Nigel by taking our heroine to the local Berni Inn. Sir Nigel knows he's beaten as he he could only offer the Ritz and gamely steps down

I'm not even making any of this up. Truly magnificent :)
