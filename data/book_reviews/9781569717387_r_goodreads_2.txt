Rating: 2/5

So I'm going to have to say compared to some of the other stellar Buffy stories out there this was definitely one of my least favourite so far! 
Maybe it had something to do with the fact that Sunnydale is being invaded by bugs (ewww bugs) or better yet maybe it had something to do with the fact that it evolved human sized bugs. 

In this story Sunnydale is hit by a heat wave and with that come swarms of insects, also Giles has a new lady friend in his life that he's dating. Buffy is trying to deal with the fact that Riley is in the hospital while also trying to figure out what's going on with the human size bugs.
Oh yeah and I almost forgot to mention the bugs have a contraption that is turning regular folks into giant mutant bugs. (shudder)
