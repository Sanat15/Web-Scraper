Rating: 4/5

This was a pleasant surprise of a random forgotten paperback on my parents' bookshelves. 

Let's just get the bad out of the way. Like much of Sci Fi, even today, the women were written so badly. All descriptions of them came down to whether or not they're hot. One is hotter than the other but.... differently. Yes, there are only two women. No they do not interact. Of course a much older man gets with his ward. And yes, of course women are all daughters or wards or wives of someone. 

What's fun is how well this book wraps together political intrigue, clever engineering, pirates, medieval warfare, and space Catholicism in a book that would be a breezy read if it weren't for my smoking hot husband who keeps me distracted from reading. 

There are two weird stubby subplots, I wonder if there was an early draft that was twice as long. 

If you stumble across this book read it, but don't seek it out. 

Also, in case you were hoping, this is not about the Biblical King David owning a spaceship. A boy can dream though...
