Rating: 3/5

While Skeeve is on his mission, we join Guido and Nunzio as they try to gum up the works of Possiltum's army from the inside. Unfortunately their code of honor keeps getting in the way and they keep getting promoted until they're almost running the place. (It doesn't help when you're on a first name basis with General Badaxe's girlfriend.)

It all seems to get worse and worse, until they have to admit defeat at this termite approach and go to take the Queen on directly.
