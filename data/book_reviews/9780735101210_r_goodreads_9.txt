Rating: 4/5

I discovered the famous author W. Somerset Maugham from a radio podcast I listened to about ten years ago.

Of Human Bondage follows Philip Carey, a young man born with a clubfoot, as he navigates his long and winding journey through life.

Orphaned and lonely, he moves through childhood and adolescence with a deep sense of isolation, always longing for connection and acceptance.

It is a captivating story of growth and self-discovery, leaving readers with a quiet sense of hope and the enduring strength of the human spirit.

It almost took me four months to finish this book, thanks to my turtle-reading speed. 🐢

The book is thick, and I kept comparing it to Murakami’s 1Q84. 🤣
