Rating: 4/5

My son didn't care for this book much when we got it, but he recently pulled it out again. He's been helping more with the cooking and I think it reminded him of this book. You could almost make carrot cake with these instructions (except there are no amounts given...). The illustrations are darling, but perhaps the colors too muted or the page too busy for his 2 year old, but better now that he's almost 4?
