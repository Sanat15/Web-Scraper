Yeah, I remember not really loving this the first time I read it and I fear I should’ve trusted my gut. I would even argue that this book is 100% skippable, as it’s basically a Maggie backstory, which…is interesting, sure. If you’re into that that. But is it essential reading in relation to the series? Absolutely not. If anything, this just feels like it should’ve been treated as a separate prequel rather than part of the series, because to me, it’s basically just bonus content. 

All that said, I just found this soooo boring and so pointless. I didn’t remotely enjoy any of this. I couldn’t bring myself to care. I know it’s Maggie, but I’m sorry, I just didn’t care. This is a book you can 100% skip and I think you’ll be fine.
