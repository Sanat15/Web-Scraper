Rating: 3/5

Little Wolf has set up as a detective to support himself, and is constantly writing to his Mum and Dad to keep them informed of his progress. You don't directly hear from his parents, but it is clear that they do not expect Little Wolf o make a success of this. Indeed, he is quite hilariously inept at it - but not quite as inept as the bona fide private investigator his parents send (along with Little Wolf's little brother) to solve the cases Little Wolf has acquired. Guess who solves them in the end. (No prizes!)
A cute enough story, but not a keeper so far as I am concerned.
