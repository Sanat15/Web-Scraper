Rating: 3/5

rereading about 40 years after the first time so i don't remember the detail - it's just a solid fantasy epic that's still a easy read with a good mix of ancestral / historical thread through the books, magic, great characters and good old fashioned 'quests'.
Back in the 80's they were a random find in a bargain book shop whilst on holiday, I only recently realised that she wrote loads more books ... I may give Enola Holmes a try.
