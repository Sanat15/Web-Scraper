Rating: 5/5

While I was picking out a book, I had just randomly grabbed the novel,” Don’t You Know There's a War On?” It's a historical fiction book. When I had picked it, I didn't expect to enjoy it as much as I did. It was an easy book, vocabulary-wise, but it was also very easy to follow along, and I found myself more and more invested in the story's plot. The setting is in New York City, 1943, during World War 2. The author depicts what it was like to be a young child in the early 40’s during the war. Howie Cripers, the main character, has a family member in the military, and throughout the novel, it shows how increasingly worried he gets for his pop. This book really exceeded my expectations on whether I would enjoy it or not. I highly recommend it to anyone who is intrigued by how life was in America during WW2, anyone intrigued by history, or even to anyone who read this review and found that they would like to read it for themselves. The only criticism I have to give is that it's not long enough, and I wish it had more chapters. But I think the author did an amazing job, and I will definitely be looking into more of his books.
