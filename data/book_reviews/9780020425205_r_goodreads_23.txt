Rating: 4/5

I appreciated this as a quick and easy retelling of the Iliad and the Odyssey. It's a good way to acquaint or reacquaint yourself with some of the characters in these classics. I got a little bored with it about halfway through and took a long break, but when I picked it up this time and read the account of Odysseus' journey back home, I really enjoyed it. I was especially intrigued by the fact that the name of the Cyclops was Polyphemus, as it is also the name of a beautiful moth that is special to me. Since I hadn't ever really learned Greek mythology and there are so many references to it in our culture, there were several tidbits that made interesting connections for me. Worth my time.
