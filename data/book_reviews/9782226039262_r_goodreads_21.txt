Rating: 5/5

I think King fans consider this book to be the beginning of his decline. I discovered it in the mid-90s and loved it. Frankly, all King books with apocalyptic endings become unreadable like say a blue film with porn stars faking long boring sex scenes. 

I had a particular soft spot for the James Gardener character. His drinking binges in Las Vegas and how he lost a fortune after earning one, struck a chord with me even as a teenager. Was King inspired by films like The Hustler and The Cincinnati Kid? James' story is the best part of the book. The alcoholic James is the only character in the town unaffected by the influence of the alien spaceship. Hahaha! Love that scene where he insults Bobbi's female relative with "What perfume do you wear? Eau de piss?". Apparently, King was a raging alcoholic when he wrote this book. Not surprising that he made himself the main character. 

I have fond memories of this book. Especially because of James life and his tender feelings towards Bobbi. But I guess The Tommyknockers was the proto Needful Things and Bag of Bones - two of King's worst. He did write many other great novels during this period though.
