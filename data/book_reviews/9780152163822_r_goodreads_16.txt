For ten freaking years this book has haunted me - I’ve been googling “black and green cover, children’s chapter book” to no avail, and I even considered calling my childhood library to see if they could pull up my records from 2004. Like most things you build up in your head, finally finding and revisiting this was both wonderful and slightly disappointing. 

I don’t know why I got so attached to this story as a kid. It’s pretty bonkers plot-wise (aliens and ghosts and Medusa and werewolves and magical frogs???) and the writing leaves a lot to be desired (wtf was that communism joke???). The deja-vu was comforting, though, and the themes were compelling.

Thank god someone on r/whatsthatbook had the same request so I could put this to rest. Only 72 books to go in 2026!
