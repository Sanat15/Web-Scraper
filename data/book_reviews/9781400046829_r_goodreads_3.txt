Rating: 4/5

At first, I was put off by Breslin's masturbatory writing style, but as the book goes on it becomes more and more accommodating and acceptably lyrical, and in the end it's quite a powerful book. Though digressive as all hell, Breslin paints a spot-on portrait of New York City and municipal government corruption, but most of all he humanizes the most anonymous character on the city street, the illegal Mexican worker. With tenderness and trenchancy, he brings the abstract statistic to glorious life.
