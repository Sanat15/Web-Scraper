Rating: 3/5

I bought this book for the Cindy Holbrook story "The Rake Objects," which was just okay. I didn't really care for the sex with another woman at the beginning, but it did rather solidly cement Marcus' character as a rake (but a fair one). I found it hard to believe an absentee brother would beg his best friend to go ruin his sister to prevent her wedding. The chemistry was a good though and I liked all of the characters. I really appreciate the way Cindy Holbrook fleshes out all her characters. The footman John may have been my favorite character in this story.

I may go back and read a few of the other stories, but Holbrook's is enough for now.
