Rating: 3/5

I remember not really liking this book when I was little, and I'm not too sure why. But I think the message that cheaters never win and winners never cheat is a really important life lesson for kids to learn when they are little because it is something that carries out through the rest of your life and should not be forgotten. The illustrations are very cute and I think the choices that the author and the artist made were great to display such a simple message in a way that a child would understand.
