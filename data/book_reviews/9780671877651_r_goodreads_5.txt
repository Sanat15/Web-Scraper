Another fun adventure that spent more time exploring Jess' nature(s) and where she fits in the world. The ensemble cast was again good,  though poor Mark got left out. I was also surprised at some of the harsh reality Durgin had in the story, which was good for making it easier to relate to the characters, but a little sad if you were expecting something gentler.  It's a happy ending so it doesn't get that dark.

Once again, the e-book version needs some serious typos editing, but otherwise did not overly disrupt the experience.
