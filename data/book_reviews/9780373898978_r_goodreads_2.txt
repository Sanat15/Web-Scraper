Rating: 2/5

Okay I am glad he picked his ex-wife over the stepford wife but I really wish I hadn't read this. I hate books where the guy has moved on. Especially with a much younger woman who was just too wierd for words. I think she wasn't as nice as she tried to be. I wondered if he was still sleeping with her while he was making out with his ex-wife. So he was cheating either way to me. I just couldn't like this, but I did like the little boy Mike and for that I gave it two stars. I loved it when she poured the peas in the lap of the fiancee. That was priceless.
