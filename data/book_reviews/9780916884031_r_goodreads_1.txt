Rating: 4/5

Bill and Jill Adelman and their children were friends of the family, my younger brother having dated one of their daughters. Bill was also a founder of the Illinois Labor History Society and creator of the film Packinghouse USA in 1969. Owing to that connection, I bunch of friends and I helped the ILHS in its first move downtown in Chicago and, with help from Bill and Dad, I was able to have his first film shown at my college, Grinnell in Iowa, shortly after it appeared.
