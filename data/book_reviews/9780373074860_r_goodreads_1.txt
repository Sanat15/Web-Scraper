Rating: 4/5

3.5 stars
This was a nice read which did a good job of casting doubts through the entire book: was the heroine Laura? If not, who was she really? Our h's car breaks down in the hero's town and from the moment she arrives everyone seems to think she is this Laura, a beautiful, selfish woman who loved to use people and left town three years ago with no word since. Our hero the sheriff, is especially interested, you see he had a love-hate relationship with Laura, he disliked her but couldn't keep away from her and had a two year relationship which ended when he refused to marry her &amp; she left. The heroine of course denies she is Laura, she is Carly and has a birth certificate to prove it but things don't add up, she had a accident three years ago that left her with no memory, she seems to know things about the hero &amp; town she shouldn't and when the hero checks out her backstory as Carly things don't add up!

The book did a good job with the whole mystery. Till the very end we don't find out for sure that she is indeed not Laura and the mystery of how she has those memories is revealed. H is nasty to her in the beginning but as he spends time with her unravelling her past he seems to feel things for Carly or this new version of Laura.

However, who she actually was before becoming Carly three years ago is revealed at the very end in the epilogue.  she was a battered woman fleeing her spouse.
