Rating: 3/5

Pretty good ghost/haunted house story from Kegan. Published in 1990 I thought this may be a rip of of Rosemary's baby, but not so. Jill and Tom Douglas relocate from NYC to Northern California so Tom can help/take over his father's medical practice. Jill is pregnant and excited, but also worried. Her mother seemed quite normal until she gave birth to Jill, and then slowly mentally disintegrated, ending up in a mental hospital for life. 

The Douglas family move into Tom's aunt Dora's old house-- a beautiful old craftsman style mansion-- left to Tom after Dora died a few months back. While Tom goes off to work each day, Jill is alone, and strange things start to happen. First, a teenage girl comes to visit, but it turns out the girl has been dead for 40 years or so. Jill starts hearing things like babies crying and seeing things in mirrors. Is Jill going crazy like her mom, or is the house haunted?

This is an easy read, and it does have some surprises toward the end. It turns out (as much as I can say without spoilers) that old aunt Dora had a few skeletons in her closet that are making themselves felt once again. Kegan does a good job with character building and the story has a nice, tense feeling in it almost from page one. The 'am I crazy or am I really being haunted' meme is well done, if a little cliche. Good ghost story with some interesting twists. 3 stars.
