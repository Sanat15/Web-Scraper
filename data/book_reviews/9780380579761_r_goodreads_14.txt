Rating: 2/5

This was an abridged version that had been serialized in Boys Life 1961.

This story is set in the far future and space exploration is well established. In an area of a nebula called the Coal Sack two of earth's ships have gone missing. One of them eventually returns with an alien. This alien is called Lucifer and it's an energy cloud.

It's decided at Space Command that a mission should be set underway to journey to the Coal Sack, find the lost ship and make contact with the aliens and determine if they are good or bad.

Now I realize that this is a YA story and it was published in Boys Life, but, there were some aspects of this story that I just had a hard time swallowing.

So who do they pick to be the crew? Some war hero (makes sense) and two 17 year old cadets. Seriously?

Later a treaty is agreed upon by the only cadet left on the ship. When he gets back the world government bitches that he essentially has a lot of balls a mere cadet creating a peace agreement with the aliens. Shouldn't this have been done by a more qualified adult?

Well of course it would, but you assholes sent a War Hero and two cadets.

I know I shouldn't be too critical after all this story was published in Boys Life and their target audience was probably young boys ranging from 10 - 17.
