Rating: 5/5

This was required reading by my organization in preparation for my move overseas. It is immensely helpful - not only in providing concrete experiences by other world travelers, but in giving advice for how to adjust to living in a foreign country. Overall, the most helpful advice I received is that I must manage my expectations - often conflict arises because of what expats expect from foreigners because they are not meeting the standards that the expat has ingrained in them. That is a valuable lesson for anyone, regardless of whether they move abroad or not. Be kind, manage your expectations, and learn to listen and adapt where needed.
