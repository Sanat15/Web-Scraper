Rating: 3/5

I like this book because it deals with diversity and discrimination. We often do not look at how people from Puerto Rico may be discriminated against. Felita's family tries to move to better schools but is rejected by threats, vandalism, and violence. Felita explains her feelings and you walk through her life. I was disappointed that the book did not continue in the same path and help her overcome these hurtful realitites. Instead it moved on to her school and relationships which are equally important but not focused on this issue. I plan to keep this on hand for having children write book reports or for the reading corner.
