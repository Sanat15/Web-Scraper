Rating: 4/5

Reilly's Return was very well written and emotional and I felt captured the love between the two characters and how an adrift and grief-stricken Mandy dealt with what she perceived was the death of the man she loved along with her child.

I liked how no punches were pulled about how Mandy dealt with things, when suddenly a year after she sees him die Mandy's reaction to Reilly's resurrection is credible, she doubts him and his love. Reilly felt he belonged with Mandy, the first time after his parents died and he went into foster-care but the time he spent as an undercover cop have made him a target and to protect Mandy he left, but couldn't stay away.

He takes her when he is attacked and her house destroyed. Reilly is hurt by Mandy's words and her actions later as well. I liked how Mandy refused to apologize for what she did when he was goneshe slept with someone-else just to feel, but hey they weren't committed and she thought he was dead and loved seeing their love and how through even harsh word and doubts they loved each other.
