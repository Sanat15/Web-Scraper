Rating: 4/5

Funny Bunnies showcases examples of 30 different breeds of bunnies (though some are mixed breed, the foundation breeds are still named). For each breed, the representative bunny's name is given, as are his/her personal characteristics, alongside the more standard characteristics for the breed, and each one includes a full page color photo. For example, Horace is an examples of an American Fuzzy Lop. Listed are the possible colors for the breed, country of origin, average weight, background, and characteristics of the breed, as well as a fun fact about Horace himself. He graces a full page color photo on the left, with his breed information on the right. About every 5 breeds or so a full page spread color photo is included without a caption for your viewing pleasure. In the beginning of the book is a rundown on rabbits, their history, and their domestication.

I had a pet rabbit as a child, and my cousin bred them (working with multiple breeds), so reading this book was a walk down memory lane for me. People who love rabbits, or children interested in learning about them, will find this an easy to follow guide.
