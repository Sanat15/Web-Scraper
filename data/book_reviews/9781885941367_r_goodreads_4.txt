Rating: 4/5

Pleasant mopping up of the last of the Ellery Queen stories left unpublished, including the outline of the last unwritten novel (not bad!) and a great non-Ellery short novel. The remaining stories are simple puzzles, and the essays that make up half the book are really for Queen obsessives only, which I am. I particularly liked hearing from Manny Lee’s son and adopted son—you get an interesting view of the less public-facing of the Queens.
