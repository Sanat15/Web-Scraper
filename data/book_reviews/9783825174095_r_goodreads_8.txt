Rating: 4/5

Multiple Folktales and Folklore that come from eastern Europe including stories about inner beauty, friendship and more. The book includes stories form eastern Europe that are not as common, but are fun and enjoyable for young children. The book also includes a variety of colorful images that are incorporated with the text. The strengths of this book are that the stories are short and the pictures are fun for children to be interested in reading the book. However, the language of the book is a little more sophisticated than the children who may chose the book to read. Overall, the book is a great way to teach folktales from another country and diversity in literature.
