Rating: 5/5

Not just for kids!

I read this whole series when I was growing up and I loved it then. Now over 60, I got the bug to read them again. What I can say is that at no time reading these books do I shake my head as an adult and say “this was written for kids”. A good story is a good story, and i’m enjoying them as much today as I did at 12 as I dreamed of having my own Black stallion. My “Black” turned out to be a mare, but I loved her with all my heart just the way Alec loved his “Black and Satan”. These books are not just for kids, they are for every one of every age who ever fell in love with horses, and never fell out of love with them.
