Rating: 3/5

This book started off rough! Really rough. It got less painful as the book went on. However, this re-read has shown me that I don’t enjoy this book as much as I thought I did! Or else I’m reading it a lot differently than I did years ago. It was ‘meh’ for me. Not good. Not so bad it’s good. In my quest to re-read books I’ve been hanging onto to see if I honestly like them or if I’m hanging onto nostalgia, this was nostalgia. I am going to finish rereading the series, but it’ll likely be my last re-read for this one!
