Rating: 2/5

While this is a very helpful little book, I think it pretty disgraceful that only two of the hundred people are women, and they are both ancient novelists. I guess that tells you something about Japanese culture right there.

Who's Who of Japan: 100 Historical Personages is one of a series of pocket guidebooks aimed at English-speaking travelers to Japan. A little stiff, a little awkward, they nevertheless give a lot of useful information in a small package.

This book gives two-page biographies with illustrations of 100 worthies of Japanese history, people it would be good to be familiar with to make sense of references and allusions. It's a good little reference book, apart from the whole gender disparity thing.
