Hard to rate this type of book. I picked up in a free little library. I was interested to read a parenting book from the late 70s as I think that parenting advice tends to follow what is “trendy” and I was curious to see how something from 40 years ago stacks up. Cool time capsule I will revisit and keep on my shelf. It was particularly interesting to see how the book tackled gender roles, single parenting, race, etc. I love the fact that books (and movies) are time capsules.
