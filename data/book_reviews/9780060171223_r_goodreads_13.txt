Rating: 3/5

okay so here's the story: i found this in a local thrift store that has a fill-a-bag section where you can pay $10 and stuff a bag full of as much as you want from that section. and i looked at all the other books and dvds and found nothing for a good while. and i was like "okay. if i can't find anything else i am NOT bringing this stupid book up to the counter on its own." but luckily for me my mom was also there with me and we teamed up to fill a bag with random clothes and then this one book. so shoutout. fun enough read ! surely would have been a more fun read if i had a penis but i don't have one of those. so. also gets a bit repetitive but that makes sense considering the exercises with and without a partner are gonna look similar anyway. the writing is a little corny at times but i do respect that miss barbara keesling was willing to get a little silly with it.
