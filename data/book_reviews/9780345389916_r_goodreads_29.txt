Rating: 2/5

This book is Philip K. Dick's 'Martian Time-Slip'. 

I'm sure no other reader in the history of the world would agree, but, for me, it's about the same thing: gubble. Godwin's heroine, Jane, doesn't call the stuff by name, but she's got a bad case all the same. Interestingly, the question of whether Jane is actually mentally ill is never addressed - I'm not even sure the author intends her to be. 

I can't write a clear review because this book has me so mixed up. I don't think it's a bad book, and I think it takes some very clever turns towards the end. But, it was so incredibly dense, the focus on Jane was so myopically close, and Jane was so frustrating and so thoroughly explained that, ultimately, I didn't know whether *I* was coming or going.
