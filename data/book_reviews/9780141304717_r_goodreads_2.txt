Rating: 3/5

Another adventure with Vesper Holly and her sidekick Brinton Garrett (aka Brinnie), who is her guardian. Vesper is a brilliant teenager (16 years old) with a zeal for adventure and a bold heart. She helps the downtrodden and works toward making things right where there has been injustice. In this book, Vesper and Brinnie are up against their old enemy, Dr. Helvitius, although this time Brinnie’s wife Mary is along for the ride. They’ve been invited to celebrate the Diamond Jubilee in the small country of Drackenberg, somewhere in Europe. When they get there, they discover that neighboring Carpatia wants to annex Drackenburg, which brings unrest to the area. As usual, Vesper wastes no time in forging a relationship with the current ruler, the Grand Duchess. It’s not long before the game is afoot with exploding sausages, kidnapping, disguises, near-death experiences, clever plots and mishaps. 

I continue to dislike the narrative voice of Brinnie and wish that he had a more manly and wise presence in Vesper’s life.
