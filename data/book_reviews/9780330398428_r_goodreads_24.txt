Warning, this book contains: dangerous medical advice, racist language, sexism and negative body image.
Do not stop taking medication or other medical treatments without talking to a responsible adult, it could be highly dangerous.
This book was written in the 80’s and the language and content are of its time, and since then attitudes and knowledge have progressed.
Other themes in the book are family and belonging; other cultures - language and attitudes are of the book’s time and are now acknowledged as racist; and stereotypes and gender roles - the females in the story are portrayed as delicate and fragile, compared to the males. Even Aldofa relies on two male accomplices to carry out the physical tasks. One of these accomplices is considered less manly due to his lack of muscles. Negative body image is portrayed in both male and female characters, and is used to further the story.
