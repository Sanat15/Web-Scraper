Rating: 4/5

This book was gift from a fellow gardener, who appreciates my endless struggle against the squirrels who've made my tomato crops their personal smorgasbords. 

It's utterly delightful, playfully witty and personal and at the same time grounded in squirrel biology. The author recounts his own strategic campaign against these relentlessly resourceful and agile adversaries, and then dives into understanding the squirrel as a foe.

Much of the book is from the perspective of a birder, rather than a gardener, so the emphasis is a tick different. It's decades old now, and so the sections of the book that review and assess late 1990s anti-squirrel products are of rather less use in 2025. Still, it was a great pleasure to read, and I may apply some of its wisdom as I dive into the fray this spring.
