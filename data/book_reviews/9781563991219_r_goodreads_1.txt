Rating: 4/5

Bill Bright helps us to understand our God better by sharing it into 3 parts. Firstly, God as our great creator. In this part, he clearly defines the attributes of His ability. Secondly, he shares with us how our God is a perfect Judge by sharing the attributes of his integrity. Lastly, most importantly, God as our gracious Savior where Bill explains our relationship with God. 

This is a good book to read as part of your daily quiet time, where each chapter ends with a page on life application for us to reflect on the contents and the word of God. I recommend this book to anyone who wants to know more about God at whatever stage of your life you are in now.
