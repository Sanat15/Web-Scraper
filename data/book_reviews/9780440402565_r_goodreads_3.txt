Rating: 5/5

I’m a horse back rider my self also a horse lover when I began I book I didn’t think it would teach me more about riding that I don’t know. I enjoy learning that your friends will always be there for you and to trust them just like Jennifer trusted moonlight. Jennifer didn’t really get what she wanted but it was worth it and moonlight got another great owner. 

I prefer this book do animal lovers, if your not a big animal fan I don’t know if you’ll enjoy this book as much . Consider reading touch the moon.
