Rating: 3/5

Found this book in a note written by Peter Drucker in one of his books (Innovation and Entrepreneurship: Practice and PrinciplesInnovation and Entrepreneurship: Practice and PrinciplesInnovation and Entrepreneurship, highly recommended). I assumed that if Drucker had read it... it would be worth the try. Although I recognized some of the behaviours that project managers and bosses show in day-to-day work, the whole text didn´t resonate with me as true innovation. I think the book was written in the 80s when product management was a new thing. But nothing of that is at the core of what innovation stands for today.

In summary: read the book if you are a fresh guy to a corporation and want to know the politics of the job; don´t read this book if you are a entrepreneur or a manager who want to discover what innovation can do for you. (less)
