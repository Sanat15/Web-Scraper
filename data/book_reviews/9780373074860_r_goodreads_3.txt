This was...good? The best amnesia romance (a trope I do not care for) I've read? Gave me a lot of the same feel as some Nora Roberts mysteryish category romances from the same time period?

And yet...I don't think I'd go back to reread it, because I didn't really care for the characters. One of those weird instances where I can appreciate the quality of a book but not enjoy it (vs the much more frequent instances of knowing a book is not the highest quality and enjoying it anyway).
