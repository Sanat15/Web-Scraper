Rating: 1/5

Luscious, red-headed English Rose meets Monsieur Le Comte hero in a quaint French village in the Loire region. Turns out her grandmother was the Great Love of hero’s grandfather but he had to settle for second best after the heroine’s grandma returned to England. Not surprisingly, hero’s grandmother loathes the granddaughter of the hussy who her husband always remained in love with. So does the hero’s Trophy stepmom, who destroys heroine’s pottery studio in a fit of hysterics but gets absolutely no comeuppance. Hero likes sexing up heroine but he is swimming in OWs and it seems that the heroine is just one more mistress to add to his collection. So she takes herself off back to England where she exhibits the bust she made of the hero. Hero runs after her since grandma is dead and won’t interfere in his plans anymore. He realizes heroine loves him back because his bust wasn’t for sale. Honestly, I felt NOTHING for this one but then again I just skimmed it, plus this author is not my favorite anyway, so please take my opinion with a grain of salt.
