Rating: 5/5

I don’t think I’ve ever read a book faster. I dunno if this is because I related to the story or because I like the author or because it is just easy to read such a stream-of-conscious, almost diaristic book - or because I haven’t been able to sleep and so picked it up again and again over the past 16 hours - but I flew through it and enjoyed it. It seemed honest, interesting, and personal. I haven’t read Singer like this before and it serves to humanize the giant.
