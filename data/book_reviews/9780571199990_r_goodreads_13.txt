Rating: 5/5

this is a pretty awesome book for actors early on in their journeys and careers. it harps on the parts that nobody else does or will. the part where simply what YOU bring to the table is magical. the importance of throwing technique away. For me personally, it help me bridge the gap between what I was seeing and hearing in my scene study class and how it was working at all toward the goal of giving more compelling, exploratory scene work.
