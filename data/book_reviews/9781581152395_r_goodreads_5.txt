Rating: 5/5

This workbook helps you examine and change your relationship with money. Most people have inherited ideas about money that inhibit us from creating prosperity in our lives. Feng Shui and Money has straightforward exercises that expose false assumptions and allow you to create a new relationship with money that puts you in charge. The nine week program integrates all aspects of feng shui including clutter clearing, the bagua and the five elements. When you finish the book you will have transformed both your finances and your space.
