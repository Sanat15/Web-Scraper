Rating: 4/5

The only Tecumseh Fox book I've read, and it may not be typical; but this guy seems more normal than Nero Wolfe does--less neurotic. He takes the subway; he drives himself; he seems less elitist; and he doesn't denigrate women. Dol Bonner hooks this book into the Wolfe universe, though. The mystery hinges on a timetable, which is pretty typical of books of the time period. Fortunately, the DBRL has the other two books in the series, so I've checked out The Broken Vase &amp; may check out the first one. (I'm aware that this book was rewritten for Wolfe; look forward to comparing them.)
