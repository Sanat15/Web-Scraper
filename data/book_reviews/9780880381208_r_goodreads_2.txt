What happens in thsi one is Conan gets a quest to deliver something, and there are a couple paths between where he is and where the message belongs, and a lot of enemies along the way. There's also two or three magical places, which are barely explored. Not really much of a plot, just a short journey. The only reason I gave it 3 stars instead of 2 was because it starred Conan before any of his other adventures.
