Rating: 2/5

Easily the most confusing 100 pages I've ever struggled through. The story doesn't really have any linear flow to it, and is comprised of characters who are never described aside from name and gender. The main character is the only 3-year-old kid I've ever heard of who regularly impregnates his friends' mothers. I'm not entirely sure what happened at the end of the book, since there was no real closure or ending. The story just stopped being told without warning.
