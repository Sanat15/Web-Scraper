Rating: 4/5

Historically significant, as it was written by an eventual King of Hawaii, The Legends and Myths of Hawaii is a great way to get to know Hawaiian culture. It's a telling of their history by someone on the inside. The only issues are due to its age (published in 1888) and dry writing style of the era. But some of the stories are well-told. The story of Umi and Kulamea was very sweet and could be made into a film easily. 
If you're going to visit, I'd suggest this to read; however, it's long. So it will take a while.
