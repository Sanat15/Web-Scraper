Rating: 5/5

For me this little book is a miracle. I got it from my grandpa when I was 10 and since then I read it at least fifth times. Everytime I'm in a bad mood, everytime I'm stressed and overwhelmed I pick this book and it always helps to lighten my mood. It's interesting how everytime I read it, I find something new. It's a quick read, about 2 hours, but such a sweet one. And the illustrations are beautiful. One of my all time favorites.
