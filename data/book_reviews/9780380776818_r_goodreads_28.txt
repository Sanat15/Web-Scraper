Rating: 1/5

I just really couldn't get into this story. Christian is a bastard (literally... the result of an extramarital affair), but the general public doesn't know it. He was betrothed to Jessie, the daughter of a count, but when his father died, his brother took his inheritance leaving him essentially penniless and the count called off the betrothal. Now the count is dead and his son is trying to get Jessie married off but she's refused the suitor he's chosen so he goes to Christian with an embarrassment of a dowry for her. Her brother wants Christian to court her and then break her heart so she'll accept the man he wants her to marry. Christian agrees because somehow he wants revenge on the son for the father calling off the betrothal years ago. I don't know, it seems awful convoluted. So Christian is courting her and he's falling in love with her. That's where I gave up. I don't know where the story is headed and it might possibly be lovely, but I just can't get into it. Her brother is an asshole and his wife is a cold, heartless bitch. 

Maybe I'll come back to the story at some point in the future, but as of right now, I'm over it.
