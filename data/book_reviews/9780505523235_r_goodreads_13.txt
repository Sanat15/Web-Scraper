Rating: 1/5

This book for me had a potential to be truly great but didn’t rise to it. Midway in the book was a chance for the characters to have been written to be together while creating challenges for them without having to separate them. In this supposedly romantic story, the conflicts were disappointing to read and envision for the hero and heroine. Lil and Sarah were finely crafted and dealt with. James was given more time with Allie and, so typical of books and movies, had to die in order to give Delaney and Allie a HEA. The true villain to me and obvious from the get-go was the sickly Mother Case. She’s supposedly respectable but truly cunning, pitiful and manipulative. Even the state of the child of the H/h was a letdown. I believe the author was determined to just make the book as tedious to read and the H/h as miserable as she could write them with all her twists and turns.
