Rating: 4/5

Review by my 9 year old: 
The reason I gave this book four stars instead of five was because they left out some big parts that were in the movie. I would recomend this book to anyone because if theres a hard word it shows you how to sound it out. I also liked it because it told you the whole entire movie except for the other parts that I told you about. Its almost like a chapter book because not all pages have a picture. It does have cool pictures of the original movie. Overall it was a awesome book.
