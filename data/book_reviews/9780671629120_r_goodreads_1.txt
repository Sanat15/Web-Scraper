Rating: 4/5

An old fave we were delighted to get out this Christmas. 

The story is about prepping for Christmas and Teddy’s outing with Mummy to get see Father Christmas. Lovely little Christmas routines to read about and the illustrations are charming, detailed and give lots to pick out and talk about. 

Bud loved the colours and the sheer quantity of teddies on the page! 

A lovely book we will continue to share with him year after year!
