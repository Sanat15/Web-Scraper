Rating: 5/5

For reference, I have completed up to multivariable calculus at a university. That is Calculus 1, 2, and 3 where I am but I know that varies by institution. The math was not challenging for me, and I found the calculus concepts, like the Newtonian fluxion, extremely interesting. 

The first 369 pages are an excellent guide to the Principia. My 5 out of 5 stars are for the guide specifically, because I don’t think I’m qualified to rate Newton. 

Although I did not fully grasp all ideas in the book, I do know the concepts I caught will stay with me forever.
