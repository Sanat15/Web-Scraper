Rating: 4/5

I’m enjoying this series - the heroines are unusual and I instantly root for them. The FMC is living in poverty, rejected by her family after eloping with her late husband. She worked as a charwoman in a theater and interrupted the MMC when he was about to commit suicide. The MMC is a composer who had a brain injury after a fall from a horse that left him with tinnitus. 

Years later, they meet again and he hires her as a governess for the daughter he just found out about. Of course, they fall for each other, but the FMC resists her role as the MMC’s muse, as her late husband saw that way and was abusive. 

It sounds heavy and parts of it are, but there’s also a lot of humor because of the precocious daughter.
