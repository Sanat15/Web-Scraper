Rating: 4/5

written 1991 .... I am re reading books from my books shelves as I am now retired and have the time. 

ON my rating scale this was a 3.6 book. This book was the first book in a series of three about the brothers. I probably would not have read the other books if I had started with this one. I started with book #2, CALL ME SIN. Each of the books are stand alone but the characters do float in and out of the series. 

Characters: Holt, Texas Ranger was a definite HOT GUY but somewhat controlling.
Cory BRIGHT was a feminist but she still fell hook line and sinker for the hot guy. She says / thinks she does not want a domineering male but she does let HOLT run her life .... does the circumstances warrant it....

Description: I got a chuckle out of the description of clothing in the book .... so dated, soooo TACKY. I have always had a thing against logos, spangle and rhinestones on clothing. I prefer a tailored classic look. I also think less description allows us to use our imagination and thus does not date the story. 

PLOT: Somewhat believable
