Rating: 4/5

Very ponderous story of life in a Swiss TB ward. If you don’t know much about how tuberculosis was treated in the 1940s, you may be educated in these pages. Pneumo thorax and draining, application of asphaltum to lesions etc. All this is a regular part of daily life for these poor sufferers. 
The protagonist is a sympathetic person, but only lives in these pages as a patient, no life beyond the ward is described. Long pasages of drinking cups in the canteen, and the subdued friendships to be had. 
A lot like the experience lived by the residents. A bright, cold novel of a time and illness that no longer fills wards with doomed young men and women.
