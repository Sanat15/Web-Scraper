Rating: 4/5

It’s Jean, not Dimsie, who is appointed Head Girl at Jane’s - but Jean is too engrossed in her poetry to be on top of the problems in the school. Hilary Garth is a new junior who loves trouble, but the real challenge is the middle school girls who are getting up to something more serious. Can Dimsie put things right?

It’s so blindingly obvious that Dimsie will make a better Head Girl than Jean that the title of this book isn’t really a spoiler. Of course Dimsie takes over, and in the process has to deal with Jean’s alienation as well as the school problems. Hilary Garth is an attractive new junior, but DFB seems to have it in for poor Jean, who in so many books is the one to be in the wrong. This book is a nice balance of mischievous juniors, middles acting out, and friendship challenges, but I can’t help thinking that between them, Dimsie and Miss Yorke cause as many problems as they solve…
