Rating: 4/5

Yes it's a silly mash-up of James Bond and Harlequin Romance featuring an unabashed capitalist who somehow manages to avoid getting killed while bringing every woman he meets to a shuddering orgasm. But there's something to Bova's easygoing writing style that just makes him fun to read, like a sci-fi summer beach novel. Although in this particular addition to his "Grand Tour" series the political landscape hits uncomfortably close to home with an aggressive Soviet Union lording it over the world while an ineffectual America cowers behind a wall of isolationism.....rather like an alternate history in which Russia wins the Cold War. Still, it's nice to read the occasional book with clearly delineated heroes and villains, and Bova takes care not to make either side look ridiculously obnoxious.
