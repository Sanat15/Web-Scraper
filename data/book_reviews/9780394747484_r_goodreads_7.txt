Rating: 3/5

This book has plenty of interesting case studies, and decent logical argumentation. The history of Norway's secession from Sweden is incredibly interesting and well written, but I feel that many of the differing factors between Scandinavia and North America are neglected. This is the best case for Quebec separatism that I have read, as it is devoid of emotional appeal and instead is based in theories of development. This book would be better if it used less allegories and more actual facts. There are many comparisons between nations and organic life, which while entertaining don't make me believe her argument more. This is an interesting book, and Jacobs has fresh theories of economics, nationalism and development. It has not won me over to the cause of separatism though.
