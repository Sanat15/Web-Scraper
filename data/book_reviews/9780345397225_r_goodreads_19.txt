Rating: 3/5

A sort of sequel to the Darwath trilogy. Its main intent is to, I think, explain the unexpected cold that was the impetus to the events of the initial trilogy. The world continues getting colder, there are, I guess (mentioned for the first time halfway through the book) freak ice storms, volcanoes erupting (a thing never really well linked to what's going), and a mysterious not fungus spreading across the world and mutating folks as well as just birthing not fungus creatures (I'm not sure why both?). Oh, and some of the dooics are mages, a thing that is just entirely dropped and unexplored other than one brief scene.
Obviously, I didn't like this as much as Darwath, though the beloved characters are all still here...though I don't love what changes are done to Gil necessitated by the plot. I feel like this probably could have and should have been split into two books, both shorter than this one. Its bouncing back and forth a lot between Rudy led events at the Keep and Ingold led events elsewhere, which are only semi-related and ends up with a lot of dangling threads that don't make a lot of sense contextually. We also get a pretty oddly ambivalent ending. Probably good for completionists but not necessary? I'll still read the Icefalcon standalone when I find a copy as that appears to be a little more independent of the overall story.
