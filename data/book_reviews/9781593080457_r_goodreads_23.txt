Rating: 5/5

Third reread of this novel, one of my all-time favourites, and it still holds up pretty fine. This time 'round, there's been a few changes: one, I've enjoyed the story much more this time than in my first and second reads; two, I noticed that my favourite Brother changes per read: my favourite in the first read isn't the same as in the second read and this time; and third: I'm a bit of an outlier when it comes to translations, because I tried with five different translations for this reread, and no longer like Pevear-Volokhonsky like I did in my second and I hated the Avsey one (almost) everyone is saying is the best. 

It's a book I'm going to be rereading bits and pieces of again whenever I feel like doing so, like favourites deserve to be read.
