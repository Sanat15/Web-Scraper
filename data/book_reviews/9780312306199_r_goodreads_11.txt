Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed this book just as much as I enjoyed And On The Eight Day She Rested. This book was good and not easy to put down, although I had to. anyway I loved how the writer brought the characters together... at first I was "like" where is she going with these characters popping up here &amp; there &amp; everywhere, but then I was like okay cool although we never found out what happened to Charlotte, Shay and did anyone ever think that John was responsible for Roberta's death. Again I did like the characters and I thank God that I can't relate to their stories; however, I'm not knocking anyone that could. I gave this book 4⭐️'s, I didn't give it 5, you don't make a book over 800 pages by being overly wordy. Anyway moving on to I Don't Want No Sugar.
