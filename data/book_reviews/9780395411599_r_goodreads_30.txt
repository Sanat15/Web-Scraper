Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars, rounded down because I never would have finished it if I hadn't started skimming when I got bored. I was perfectly entertained for the whole first half of the book, then pretty darn bored from 50-80%, then totally intrigued and interested for the final 20%. I liked the ending so much I almost gave this four stars, but I couldn't forget the very soggy middle. However, this has made me want to read other books by this author.
