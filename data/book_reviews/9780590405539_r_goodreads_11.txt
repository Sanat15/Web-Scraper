Rating: 3/5

Jacob is a young Black boy who can only see the negative connotations of the word Black. When a snowman made of dingy (black) snow comes to life thanks to a magical kente cloth, he helps Jacob see the positive. Set during the Christmas season, Jacob's younger brother PeeWee wants to buy their mother a present for Christmas. Will the two boys be able to raise enough money in time to buy her a gift? Will their new friend be able to help them? Find out in this poignant story.
