Rating: 5/5

Well..........

​My current mood is shit!

​My life is falling apart!

​The darkness is encroaching at an alarming rate!

​I wake up disappointed every morning!

​I'm "self medicating" far too much!

​I'm using humor as a coping mechanism way too often!

​My therapist says it's perfectly normal to spiral a little once in a while... as long as I come back in a timely fashion.

​All that said... reading a book about the destruction of a suburban couple in the '50s was, needless to say, probably a bad decision (add it to the list of recent bad decisions).

​I won't delve into what this extremely well-written and profoundly thought-provoking (at least for me) novel is about. If you're reading this, chances are you already read the synopsis of the book and don't need me to regurgitate the life, love, and downfall of the Wheeler couple.

​What I will say is this... this novel was amazing. Extremely well-written, dark, depressing, foreboding, an absolute masterpiece of the 20th century. Time magazine was right to call it a top 100 novel of all time.

​You're going to cry. You're going to hate them. You're going to love them. You're going to be confused about your feelings, but that's OK; I think that's the point. It'll stick with you, so be warned.
