Rating: 3/5

I have often remarked that heroine's make or break a book for me and this one had a heroine whose head I wanted to bash. She was a coward and selfish. So, the heroine was married to the most selfish man ever who ignored her and her son, but she loved him. Of course hubby dumped her for his teenage secretary and 18 months after her divorce she meets the hero. The hero is a soldier who helped turn her son's destructive behaviour around. He is a nice kind 40-year old divorcee who cherishes her son and stupidly falls for her too despite all her prickliness but she treats him badly at every turn, selfishly telling him not to see her son when she thinks he is breaking through her defenses and when they do get involved intimately, refusing to marry him because he might be killed in the war. Yes she had been through a lot but she was so selfish and the hero took her back everytime. The poor man deserved better. Not this author's best.
