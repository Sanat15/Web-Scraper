Rating: 3/5

This book read more like a compendium of the treatment of women throughout the history of the subcontinent. The utopia and dream part only consisted of like 5% of the book, and it was really intriguing to see the limits to what their imagination were back in the 19th century. However, the whole book felt as if they were taking a very small aspect of the writers life and blowing it out of proportion as well, especially the selections from The Secluded Ones.

People do have to keep in mind the culture and traditions of the subcontinent in those times, and not everything was in accordance with european values back then and presently as well. And this is promiment especially in the latter half of the book, which reads as if its pushing some sort of "feminist" agenda and using such old tradtions and cultures as a sort of scapegoat.

It's a good read if you wanna get into the history of the treatment of women back during the subcontinent, especially regarding the cultures back then. However, the opinions stated in the latter half are not to be taken as seriously. You would have a much better time forming your own opinions then following the editors excerpts blindly.
