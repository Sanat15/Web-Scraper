Rating: 5/5

Intriguing and so good.
Julia Flores and Ruíz Navarro were grand characters, and I really enjoyed their story. The Flores and Navarro own a vineyard together, but it is really not a good working relationship. There are secrets and twisty turns in this engaging an intriguing book it was hard to put down. The history of Texas gaining its freedom from Mexico and its land grants from Mexico was an interesting bit of history. The cast of characters really made the story their interaction was splendid. The last part of the book will keep you on pins, and needles Lazaro was a horrible person. The ending was icing on top of an awesome cake. I highly recommend this western.
