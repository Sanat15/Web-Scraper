Rating: 4/5

I picked up this and AbraCadaver at a book fair as a joke because of the silly titles and covers, but this guy doesn't get enough credit. He writes a good medical/crime thriller. It's no Great American Novel, but you can do worse in the genre I like to call "grocery store books." He's REALLY good with analogy/simile, to the point where you want to applaud and say, "Bravo, Dr, that was a good one."
