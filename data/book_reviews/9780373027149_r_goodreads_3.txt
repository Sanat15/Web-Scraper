Rating: 2/5

Good writing but boring when it came to the romance. Heroine wins money and buys a farm in the bush so she can do her pottery full time. The H is the local it guy around town that gas money and whatever. The h wants to build up her dilapidated farm so she can live off the land, the H thinks shes a city woman who wont know how to farm and will do this as a hobby. And I dont know when but it became about the h being too independent. OW is mean trough out the story and eventually sets fire at the h farm, the H pays h off to avoid prosecution and scandal for the OW. After h big pottery exhibition they declare their love and HEA. I skipped a lot.
