Rating: 4/5

This was an unexpected little pleasure. Bought at a bargain price I did not expect much, but when I began I quickly became keen to follow the story through to its end. A clever mystery with a number of surprises in its resolution, a sophisticated and witty narrative, as well as excellent characterisation all made this a most enjoyable read.

A group on an expensive tour of Egypt find themselves marooned on an island in the middle of Lake Nasser where one of them, an exceptionally obnoxious journalist, dies suddenly. Is it murder? If so, who has done it and why? Archaeologist and part-time spy, Tamara Hoyland investigates.

I enjoyed this little novel a lot and will seek out others in the series.
