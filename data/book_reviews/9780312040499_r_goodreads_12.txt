I really enjoyed the first chapter, which followed the history of the creation of Route 66 and early weird moments like the "Bunion Derby." It gets less interesting when it starts following the road from east to west, as it is different variations of the same story: town gets founded, town sees a lot of Route 66 traffic, highway gets built and town is bypassed, town dies. Lots of kind of bitter "good ol' days" talk about how much interstates and fast food suck, and like, yeah, but I don't need to read about that for 250 pages.
