Rating: 4/5

R.F. Delderfield is well known for his set of British novels: God is a Gentleman, To Serve Them All My Days, Theirs Was the Kingdom, etc. which I read and loved. 

This historical novel, however, is not British at all but one of the Napoleonic war. Surprisingly, I had never read any books about Napoleon. The author indicates at the beginning that most of the episodes are true and that the characters are spun from real people. The story is slow going as in any war- march along slowly forever, only to have a quick battle, with a lot of presumed deaths, but some not always dead. Slow moving again, including hiding from the enemy - whichever side you are on...

I totally enjoyed the book, relatively short as it was, and would happily read anything that Delderfield wrote.
