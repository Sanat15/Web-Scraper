Rating: 3/5

Glad to put this one to bed. It has been my least favorite of the series, yet not without some endearing points. Mainly, the growth and maturity of Ralph. Grandfather was a difficult man to love and his outbursts were both tedious and cringe worthy. However, Ralph never wavered in loving his Grandfather well despite the difficulty and frustration. A valuable lesson in honoring those who have gone before us. 

Lastly, if I never have to read “Gorry sakes alive” in my life it would be alright with me.
