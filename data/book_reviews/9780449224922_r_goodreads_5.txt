Rating: 4/5

this is by far the most comical of Smith's regency romances. There is a subplot about a Mr. Swann who owns a flock of cobs, pens, and cygnets. He is the unattractive suitor of the heroine, who is in love with the usual Smith beau ideal--tall, dark and saturnine. There is a villainess, a sot, and a few older lovebirds. The heroine is a governess who has lost her post due to sexual harassment--of course it was her fault. She comes to live with her aunt, and all sorts of mésalliances occur.
