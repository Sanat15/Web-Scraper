Rating: 5/5

The romance between Americans and baseball is captured beautifully by Higgins. I found myself relating to the connections that I made with my own father and grandfather through baseball and the once tragic fandom of Red Sox nation. I consider the closing chapter of this book to be one of my favorite writings and is quite evocative each time I return to read it. If your love for the Red Sox was handed down by your family, I highly recommend sharing this book with the person that introduced you to Boston baseball.
