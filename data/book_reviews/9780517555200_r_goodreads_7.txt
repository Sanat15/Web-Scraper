Rating: 4/5

Hmm I’m left conflicted . I did not like a major aspect of this book: nearly every single romantic relationship. There was multiple occasions of assault that would result in the woman falling in love and forgiving the man who assaulted her; women who in other situations were strong and independent. However, I am a sucker for any sort of family chronicle. I liked the characters on their own, I thought the plot was paced very well and the settings were very clear.
