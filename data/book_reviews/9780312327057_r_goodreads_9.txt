Rating: 4/5

*don't actually know why Goodreads has less pages than the book, but okay. I also have about 50 pages left, but when I update my Goodreads I'm committed to leaving reviews too.

I first read Jinn when I was 13, and it was one of those books that stayed with me. Over the years I forgot the name, but vididly remember the plot line. Thank god for a book group on Facebook to grasp what I was on about with my description. 

Picked it up again after years and years, and I forgot how much I don't like books with chapters. Or at least books that take a pause between timelines. The jump back in time isn't very clear until you reread, and then it clicks. 

Once you hit "completion" on here, this is where the book starts to pick up a little with the Jinn, but also seems to become a little chaotic.

It's still a 4* book in my eyes, because the general concept of the plot line is brilliant, it's execution towards the end in the final few pages aren't a lot to shout about.
