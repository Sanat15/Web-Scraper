Rating: 4/5

This juvenile book by Ezra Jack Keats offers a solemn, yet powerful look at real life during a snowy day. The blues, grays, and purples of the skies are realistic. The trees seem colorless. The mounds of snow, and the snow on top of the buildings and streetlights give the realistic feeling of being in a smaller enclosed space. Snow on the trees remind me of their beauty shortly after and during a snowstorm takes place. It leaves the kid hoping that that he wasn’t dreaming what he had seen. It reminds me when I was kid growing up in Montreal. Good read! – Bob H.
