Rating: 4/5

Charley marries a guy named Jack, a retired naval meteorologist after a whirlwind romance in Europe. When they come home for their honeymoon, they find a dead woman in the bathtub of their hotel suite. 

It doesn't take long for Charley to figure out that handsome Jack is a hell of a lot more than a weather man.

This was a fun romp with lively dialogue, lots of action, and very little realism. If you want escapism, it's a first class ticket. I totally enjoyed it.
