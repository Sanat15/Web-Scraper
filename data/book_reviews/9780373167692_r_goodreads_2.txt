Rating: 3/5

I wanted to be able to give this book a 4 or 5, but I can't. I was ok with Dylan and Whitney hooking up. A thin line between best friends and lovers. Got it. But, then he ghosted her. 

When she saw him again, it was a MAJOR shock in more ways then one. His behavior as a man, but especially as her best friend was completely unacceptable. Dylan was not worthy of her altruism or her understanding of his situation. I just don't have words for Whitney's behavior. Trying to keep that secret from Dylan was a bad move on her part as well. On top of that, she continued to do the tailoring bit. I know is was professional behavior, but Karl couldn't have done it? Anyone else? They were rich. They could have found someone else.

I also don't understand why if Dylan was so wealthy, he had NO ONE, he could go to for advice about his situation? Cori did a disappearing act. It was obvious what was going on with her at the dress fitting. That was before we were privy to all of the details.

This drama drug on through almost the entire book. Even when it was coming to a conclusion, Whitney still didn't get what was going on.

I was interested in the story until Whitney looked up at the engagement party. I was done. Not sure why she wasn't. The book just seem a limit ridiculous after that. To spend all that time agonizing and not doing anything to address the issue.

I have read a number of Mindy Neff's books that I enjoyed. This wasn't one of them. It had the potential, but it fell short for me.
