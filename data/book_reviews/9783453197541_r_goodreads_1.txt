Rating: 3/5

Let me start off by saying that I tend to shy away from Romance novels for moral reasons, but once in awhile, I'll give one a chance if it's not an obvious romance. Obvious, I mean, by the front cover displaying a bare-chested man assailing the heroine. This book is one of the better romances I've read in a long time. It's your typical spoiled, rich, model meets Mr. Outdoorsy nature guy. It's humorous, and the man is actually nice instead of a brute like the men in most romance novels. Also, it's not laced with so many steamy, sex scenes. Whenever I read a romance novel, if the couple in the story end up in bed with one another in the second chapter, I don't bother reading the rest. I prefer the type of romance where the sexual tension is built up throughout the entire book and then they finally yield to their passion for one another. (I admit, the moral side of me prefers they get married, but those books are hard to come by.) Anyway, this book is one of those built up tension books, so it was fun to read. 
I would have liked it better if it had less bad language and no premarital sex. 
 Caution: Foul language and one fornicating scene.
