Rating: 5/5

(Am I an overly anxious perfectionist who likes to be overprepared for every worst case scenario?)

This book is a treasure trove, I desperately need a copy to keep on my person at all times (&amp; esp in the wilderness). I love his understated British "keep calm &amp; carry on" attitude, because honestly, when you're resorting to drinking fresh water from fish bladders I think the realistic chance of survival is abysmally miniscule. Though it's nice to be hopeful in any situation, if only because the alternative is rolling over and dying.
