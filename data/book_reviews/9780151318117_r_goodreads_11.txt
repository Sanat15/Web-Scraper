Rating: 4/5

The reason I love joining groups like On the Southern Literary Trail - the opportunity to read authors I am unfamiliar with and then talk about them. Katherine Anne Porter is quite a character, and I'd like to read her biography too. 

Overall, these stories often focus on dysfunctional relationships, sometimes showing both sides. Her characters are complicated and imperfect. 

My favorite two stories were Rope and The Jilting of Granny Weatherall.

A few more words on each story behind the break 

-Maria Concepcion - and her husband comes back

-Magic - "I know a charm.. In seven days they come again very happy to stay and they cannot say why."

-Rope - this is why you should make sure your wife always has coffee *smiles sweetly*

-He - less about the son and more about what the neighbors think

-Theft - "I was right not to be afraid of any thief but myself, who will end by leaving me nothing."

- That Tree - another marriage out of sync, but I had to laugh at "Hail, Hymen! What next?"

-The Jilting of Granny Weatherall - the best so far, brought tears to my eyes, the power a person can have 60 years later

-Flowering Judas - resistance to a revolutionary.... or is it betrayal?

-The Cracked Looking-Glass - a woman who has maker her life by lying and her long suffering husband.
