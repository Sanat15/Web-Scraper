Rating: 3/5

This is the fifth novel in Foster's Spellsinger series and is another good fantasy romp for Jon-Tom, Mudge, Clothahump, and company. They have to travel to Northern lands to free a perambulator who's been captured by a wizard and is causing reality to wobble and shift across the universe. Talea makes her return, and we also meet a koala, a pack mule (hinny, rather) named Dormas who's pretty spunky, and a wolverine. (Being a long-time Ohio resident, I always knew that wolverines were evil and nasty.) Jon-Tom summons a jeep to help get them started, they free a city from bad weather, and there are frequent impediments to the progress such as switching genders and turning into amoebas. It's more about the journey than the actual saving-the-universe bit, but it's another humorous adventure for the gang. Some of the music cited will be unknown to younger people but will be clear in context, though reading the first book prior to any of the others might be necessary to appreciate what's going on. The Warner first mass-market edition has a cover by Carl Lundgren that makes Jon-Tom look like David Bowie as the goblin in Labyrinth. Fun stuff all around.
