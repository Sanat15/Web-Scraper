Rating: 4/5

The harrowing portraits of men hating women, mothers loathing their children, &amp; the truly devastating absence of love. A phenomenal work of art that's raw, revolting, &amp; insidious. Owes a large debt to the dementedness of M. de Sade, though the prose--as stark and jarring, as opaque, as a broken shard of obsidian--is just damn Beautiful.

I can hear from my window some kind of Requiem suddenly coming on...
