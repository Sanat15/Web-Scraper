Rating: 2/5

The 4th in the series was entertaining if quite fluffy; this 5th entry is where the wheels apparently have come off. This was not only fluffy, but slapsticky in a bad way, and none of it was very interesting. I’ve finished it because that’s what one does when reading a series; #6 is next and I’ll soldier on through that, though I have no expectations of improvement.
After that one (Mrs. Pargeter’s Point of Honor), I’ll retire from the field - Brett took a hiatus of some almost twenty years and then essayed 3 additional volumes, but I some time ago read one of them and the memory of it even now makes me a bit sickish in my throat.
I’m still reading in the Feathering series and they’re still fairly worthwhile; the Charles Paris ouevre I read a good way back, but I’ve still got copies and intend to revisit Charles at some point. Alas, it is nigh time to bid Mrs. Pargeter “adieu.”
