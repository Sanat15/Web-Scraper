Rating: 4/5

This one brought a lot of nostalgia...
Although I'm not a fan of murder mysteries, as a child I liked watching TV series based on English detective stories - most, of course, were adaptations of Agatha Christie works. From the first page I dived into a "murder in a small English town" atmosphere with a slowly-paced investigation based on unravelling dirty little secrets of snobbish and pretentious Englishmen. 
This novel also demonstrates why Christie is "a mother/pioneer of genre". The characters, premises and classical revelation of a murderer seems to be used in practically every suchlike story. But even here author managed to surprise a reader in a way that is hard to describe if you want to avoid spoilers.
