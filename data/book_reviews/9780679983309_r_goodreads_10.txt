Rating: 2/5

This has an interesting contrast of tone. It is for the most part a fun story of a large puppy but with some dark undertones in places. This does make it hard to recommend as I feel that the target audience that love cute animal stories might be traumatised by some of the content. 
I admit I don't quite so the appeal of King-Smith's works having read a few of them recently. Obviously others do which made him successful but I can think of a lot of authors I would recommend over him.
