Rating: 3/5

"She who trusts a man is extremely foolish."

I love learning about medieval history, so this gave me some more insights into medieval literature. My favorite lai of the twelve was 'Bisclavret'; the whole werewolf aspect and the friend(??)ship between the king and Bisclavret were cool to read. Some of the other lais were also memorable, some not so much, but it was overall interesting, especially since this collection was written by a woman. A few of her female characters have certain levels of autonomy and personality that I honestly found a little surprising for the time period which makes me wonder more about what kind of life Marie led (though there isn't much information about her available).

(3.5 stars)
