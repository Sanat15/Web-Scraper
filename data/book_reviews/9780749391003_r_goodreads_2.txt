Rating: 5/5

It is the 1980s in Russia; the government under Gorbachev promises change and prosperity. "Perestroika, political reform, means that is it now legal to ask for more. But the effect is to reveal how little there is to give." Such is the irony and sad fate of the country. Christopher Hope narrates the state of affairs, introducing vivid characters such as the ambitious Vasilly, cab driver and money changer, Valentin, the poignant scholar or the hopeful Viktoria. Despondent at times, the book is an unfortunate irony in itself - describing the bleak Russian political and social setting with colourful poetry. 

While you find it hard to keep buoyant spirits through the book, I found the scenario most beautifully described, the words rolling with pleasant texture in my mouth.
