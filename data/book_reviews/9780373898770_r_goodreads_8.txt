4 stars.

opposed to the other romance books I've read, both from Janet Dailey herself, but also from other writers I can say that this is my absolute favourite. The fact that our main character is fighting for her family's ranch, but on top of that fighting against a system that's been in place for years. She believes that women are seen as unable to lead a farm and that was the general consensus in the book. I loved the fact that she was angry and fighting back against it, hence why she hated the replacement of her father, who's in the hospital and can't work at the moment, so much. Their relationship was built so naturally and I really loved that.
