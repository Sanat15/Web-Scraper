Rating: 3/5

I wanna say this is my first Edwards book to read, but then again as a teenager I read ALOT of native american books, so that could be incorrect. So, It is the first as an adult.

The writing wasn't bad, but the character development did leave alot to be desired. The female was intended to be a strong heroine, but she was also just kinda flapping around too, not that that would be incorrect of a typical female of that era. Torn between her family's revenge and her newfound love, and i use love loosely, because that was all just rushed and not developed at all, and she was so fickle about it, between her shame and generally just fighting it, so she didn't feel guilty everytime she ran away. 

I'll try the next in the series, but so far I'm not rushing out to buy and read all her books.
