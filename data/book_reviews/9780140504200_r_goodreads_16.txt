Rating: 5/5

A classic and my youngest grandson's favorite book. His mother asked me to get him a second copy for his first birthday because he had completely used up the other one. Well ... it's not exactly worn out. I could still read it, but the book was showing significant love from his sturdy little hands and numerous reads, especially the 'lift the flaps'. All children's books should be so well-loved! 🥰❤️

It is an adorable book about a dog looking everywhere for Spot, which is rather confusing because it would seem that Spot is the one doing the looking. This is explained at the end is a sweet way. 

If you haven't read this, you must. I know I read this sometime before, but I'd never recorded it.

5+ stars
