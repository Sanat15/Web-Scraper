Rating: 5/5

Wonderful, inspiring read. I read this book in 6th grade, ordered it from a school book order. It touched me deeply. Strangely, the book was a comfort to me, I read it the year after my mother passed away. I devoured every page, I loved every child she raised and adopted, and when I was through I was determined to be just like her and have and/or adopt 12 children of my own someday. It didn't turn out that way, but I am pleased with the way it did turn out. It strengthened my desire to be a mother and raise a family.
