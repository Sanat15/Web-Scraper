Rating: 4/5

Unexpectedly, I found this less gripping than the extracts in Carrera's anthology (which drew from the longer version of this work); this piece was much more religious, and almost apolitical-- certainly it didn't have the anticapitalist fervor I remembered. Very interested to read the long version now and continue to tease out the details. The included list of subscribers to the piece is also very exciting for my nascent project on abolitionist literature -- I hadn't expected each subscriber's street to be noted, which makes me wonder if I finally have a research project that calls for mapping! I was also intrigued to see that Sir Joshua Reynolds was a subscriber, one of very few who had more of a title than just "Mr." Much interesting research to be done!
