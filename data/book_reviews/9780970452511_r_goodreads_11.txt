Rating: 5/5

The CIA's MKULTRA and related projects have received so much publicity and attention by now that literature on the subject abounds, but this is the first and only book to my knowledge that approaches the issue from a mental health perspective, focusing on the doctors who actually carried out these experiments and calling them out for their unethical and grievously harmful actions. None of these people have ever apologized or owned up to their involvement in the projects.. in fact we still don't know who many of them were because much information remains classified. Colin Ross is a psychiatrist himself–quite a brilliant one–and he brings his expertise to bear on the subject, while calling those doctors and institutions that have been officially named to task for the crucial parts they played in this nefarious research. These people violated the Hippocratic Oath they took and ruined many lives for selfish reasons of personal gain, or to satisfy their own sick perversions, and it's just as important for people to hear this side of the story as it is to focus on the CIA itself. A much needed work that was long overdue! Meticulously researched, extremely interesting and skillfully written.. A winner all around.
