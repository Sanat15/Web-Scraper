Rating: 3/5

Even if you haven't read the six books in the Callahan&lt;.i&gt; series that had been published by the time this one was released, you can still enjoy the quotations, aphorisms, and epigrams in this collection (which is very reminiscent of Heinlein's The Notebooks of Lazarus Long, which author Spider Robinson freely admits). If you have read those books, though, you'll have happy memories of reading some of these quotes in the stories they're from. Some, though, are "new" and not from those tales, though many are attributed to characters from Callahan's Place or Lady Sally's. Others are from outside the Callahan mythos are presumably just ones that struck Spider Robinson's fancy, so he threw them in. All to the good.

There's also a section here with some of the most egregious puns from the series, and a few song lyrics as well. Robinson rounds off the collection with a dramatis personae which lists most of the characters from the six books (though Tommy Janssen is strangely missing), and some real-life friends of Robinson who had cameos in some of the later books.

You won't learn much of anything new about the Callahan mythos from this collection, but if this is your first exposure to that world, I hope this inspires you to read the predecessor books.
