Rating: 5/5

The premise is as bisarre as this book is charming and the story was very endearing and funny to me as a child reader, since it also has the childs perspective and is very existensial about human relations but in a light-hearted funny way. Miss Bartolotti, a chainsmoking lady with a somewhat messy life ( probably "a tad of adhd" coming from someone with adhd or just a bit with her head in the clouds, very true ringing for our modern 'sibling society' but her character and the vibe is so late70s/early 80s antics in a lovely way, such as the smoking and in the lesser details) suddenly gets a delivery, a little boy named Konrad who shows her an ikea-ish instruction manual of how to take care if a child well. Otherwise they both are as confused as anyone but need to get to know each other existensially as human beings to solve this weird project together ( also a mighty good read for both kids and parents I would say)
