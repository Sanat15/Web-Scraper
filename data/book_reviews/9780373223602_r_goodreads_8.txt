Rating: 5/5

Kit Roarke comes looking to solve the puzzle of who killed her friend Jo, which was written off as suicide. During her search she comes into contact with Doctor Noah Stewart, who has his own secrets and acts suspiciously which makes Kit question who she can trust. This story has many twists and turns that keep one guessing and is well written and well paced. I enjoyed it very much and recommend it. Thanks Pamela Burford.
