Rating: 1/5

Bleh.

I came here wanting to read about event horizons and ancient alien artifacts. Not some insipid sexcapades, involving minors and some dysfunctional family.

The last chapter (about 4 pages) was the only interesting part of this whole book.

It’s like the author tried to deliver a Clarke/Tchaikovsky/Reynolds-esque story, mash em all up into a stew but only resulted in making some inedible sludge.
