Rating: 4/5

I devoured this when it was first published in 1980. Not a huge rock fan, I was taken with Jim Morrison and his unique outlook and talent. 

Being young, the Doors music was darker than what I normally listened to and I was curious to where his inspiration originated.

I can't say much about the author and writing style, except that I was riveted by Morrison's story and the inside view of how he wrote and produced his songs.
