Rating: 4/5

Friends of mine who had read my books, were kind enough to loan me this little paperback book from a dubious publisher in San Francisco. As soon as I started reading this, I realized what people mean when they say that they could not put the book down.
This guy, Poon Lim, can barely speak English, he is thrown into the sea after his ship has been torpedoed and it sinks so fast he is sucked down to unknown depths before he pops to the surface like a cork. He is the only survivor of this act of war. The guy stumbles on a life raft just ahead of the sharks and proceeds to stay alive, and sane, for 133 days on the surface of the Pacific Ocean. It is a gripping tale, well told in the simple honest terms of a Chinese peasant who signed on as a steward aboard this British vessel.
Last night, I read over half of the book, tonight, I will finish it, so bear with me.
