Rating: 3/5

It was a good book, with lots of information, written in a memoir style, which appeals to me. However, I had a hard time following it. I think my problem was that I am not familiar enough with tank welfare nomenclature to easily follow the plot. Also, the author is British and the book was written almost 70 years ago, so some of the expressions and descriptions took a bit if untangling for me. One thing that came across is how terrifyingly disordered and disjointed the strategic plan for North Africa seems to have been, which conforms with my impression from reading other books on the subject. I settled on a rating of three stars because I can’t give 3.5 stars.
