The Skook by JP Miller 
I kept reading only because GoodReads rated it 4 stars &amp; there were no slow chapters. The main characters are not very likeable and there are some spelunking horrors. But I wanted to know what happened. 
The Skook is an imaginary figure the lead man wrote into stories for his children. 
From 1984, how lost souls find themselves. Recommended. 3.8 stars.
