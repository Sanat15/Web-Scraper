Rating: 4/5

I liked this crime novel a lot. Narrator is a forensic artist, for a change of pace. The murders took place 26 years ago. He was a child. Murder victims included one of his school teachers and a woman who was mother of two of his fellow students and patient to his psychoanalyst father. The name of the book comes from a dream she needed his father to analyze. She was a nymphomaniac, so there is a bit of eroticism involved. The crime does get solved in the end, although there is quite of list of potential suspects.
