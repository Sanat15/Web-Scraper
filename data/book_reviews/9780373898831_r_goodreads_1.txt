Rating: 1/5

"That Carolina Summer" is the story of Annette and Josh.

The little girls from The Matchmakers (Americana #8 based in Delaware) are all grown up, with the oldest one turning twenty. Vacationing in a resort in Carolina with her parents (our old MCs) and stepbrother, the older girl, our h spots the handsome H. The H owns the resort, and after a look, the h is smitten. The rest of the book is the h going desperate lengths to entice the H, with her doing the chase while the H behaves like an asshole.

The only thing likable about this book was the reappearance of the matured MCs from the previous book. I do not mind the h being the one pursuing the relationship, but she came off very desperate, bratty and immature. The ending scenes with her attempting to sleep with OM grossed me out. Also the book employs exasperating archaic views about women roles as a conclusion, leaving me utterly disappointed.

Nope.
