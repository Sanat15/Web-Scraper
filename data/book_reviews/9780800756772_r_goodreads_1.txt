Rating: 3/5

This was a fun and quick-read story, but I kept thinking good grief, do we need so many plot lines? We had 1) adoption/coming to terms/finding out about birth parents, 2)marriage rockiness with a pastor and his wife, 3) war/death/CIA/peacekeeping, 4)musician travel and performance backed with a childhood of communism, 5)art theft and kidnapping/robberies, and 5)cancer/death AND budding romance. Narrowing down a few might have made keeping up a little easier.
