Rating: 5/5

In spite of the tacky "bodice ripper" cover, this is a very good romance novel, one of the few hetero romances I can stand reading. I read the book back in 1988 before the cover morphed into the embarrassing pic here. It's about kids on the orphan trains around the Civil War era and it is very heartwarming, suspenseful, romantic and sensual. The 2 main characters have a genuine bond, not just sexual chemistry. That's why it's been a standout for me for many years.
