Rating: 3/5

I agree with much of what is said in this book, but it it very shallow. I mean that literally. There are 110 pages to the main part with large print, double-spaced lines, frequent pictures of couples in the text, and a 2-page photo spread introducing each chapter. It's kind of a coffee table book, like a bunch of blog posts together. I feel like it is too shallow to be of much benefit by itself. Maybe it works as a reminder if you've already covered all these things in depth in the past. (Shrug)
