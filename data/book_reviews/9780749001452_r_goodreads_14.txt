Rating: 4/5

Certainly an above-average mystery starring Lew Archer, the author's private detective in Los Angeles. Unfortunately it is a far cry from his predecessors in the genre: Dashiell Hammett and Raymond Chandler. Having read virtually all of the Lew Archer series decades ago, I was probably a little harsh in my dismissal of the works as being too full of pop-psychology (although I still believe this was true of the later volumes in the series). This novel, fortunately, does not fall into that category. And for anyone new to the series, this volume does stand alone, not requiring the reader to have read the prior 9 volumes.
