Rating: 5/5

Loved, loved the Odd Woman. In a way it changed my life as after reading it I dared to enrol in an English literature foundation course in London. English was not my first language and while I had a year of EASL in the Netherlands, I daydreamed my time away. What English I had, I picked up from my husband to be and patient British people. I had been devouring books since I'd been a young girl and while I'd not finished my education due to the move, I absolutely loved the course. Of course, we moved again and I became a Teacher of English as a second language. If not for this book, I'd never have the courage to take that first step. I need to re read it again.
