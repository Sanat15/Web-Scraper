Rating: 2/5

So I bought this book for 10cents in my local library under KIDS books. First off this book is NOT for kids. Ed and Lorraine Warren are REAL people for sure, I've met their nephew John Zaffis on multiple occasions and it seems like the book makes it sound Ok to contact the dead which it is not ok to do this. I suppose for the time frame when this book was written it was ok but in 2016 I could not give it more than a two star rating and hope it was not actually indeed for children. Even the cover is disturbing.
