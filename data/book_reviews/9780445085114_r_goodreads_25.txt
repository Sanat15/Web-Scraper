Rating: 4/5

"The Conquerors" is the first of four books in Thomas B. Costain's series "The Pageant of England". The version I borrowed from the library covers the Battle of Hastings through the death of King John. What a tumultuous and interesting 150 years!

This is NOT your typical history book. "The Conquerors" is not full of dry facts nor is it an avalanche of dates/tedium. Costain keeps the reader's interest with the correct(IMO) mixture of historical detail and juicy tidbit.

If you are like me, you probably learned about William the Conqueror in school. You probably did not learn that without the successful invasion of the Normans in 1066, England may never have attained superpower status she later achieved. This book splendidly makes that case. Costain does not apologize for the greedy, self-centered Norman invaders. He merely gives them credit for being the royal(pun intended) bastards that spurred(unintentionally) the English to greatness.
