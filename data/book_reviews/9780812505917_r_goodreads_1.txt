Rating: 5/5

Everybody has heard the name of Lizzie Borden and what she might have done to her parents. Well, in this book, the author did a great job with the characters. She describes how the plot goes to the ultimate tragedy: Abby, the stepmother, Andrew Borden's affair with Enid, Emma, Lizzie's sister, the British friend Beatrice and Lizzie's discovery of her own sexuality. There isn't much happening here until the last couple of pages but this is one of the best, most depressing character studies I came across for a very long time. This author knows how to write and give lasting descriptions. A bit tedious at parts but heading for the ultimate tragedy. A kind of Gothic tragedy. Highly recommended!
