Rating: 1/5

this autobiographical book drove me nuts. i had strong feelings of dislike for the 2nd half of the book as the story seemed to wallow in self pity and despair. i kept reading it because a friend knew the author and i reminded him of her? YIKES. i hope i'm not like mary allen. i enjoyed her writing, but the story was just too much. she is in love with the idea of her dead, drug addicted, dysfunctional boyfriend. she goes searching for what happened to him and ended up in an asylum for a bit after her stint of trying to communicate with him through automatic scribbling for months on end. enough already. let him go, mary.
