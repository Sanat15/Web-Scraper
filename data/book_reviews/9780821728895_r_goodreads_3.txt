Rating: 3/5

Since this book came out in 1990, I obviously can’t apply modern lens while reading the story. But I can’t seem to get past Adam, the MMC, spewing racial slurs towards Mexicans (also an obvious jab towards FMC who is a Mexican herself) and calling her a bitch a handful of times. Anita was far deserving of someone like Buck who was MMC’s comrade. He behaved appropriately towards her, giving her nothing but respect. Afterall, Anita had nursed Adam with his gaping wound and injuries. She had even helped outlining some key values in infiltrating enemies’ territories by disguising themselves as Mexicans (by dying their clothes and skin to be darker). Adam vehemently disagreed with idea, not wanting to look like a dark person his whole life. I, for the life of God, couldn’t fathom what Anita saw in him other than his gargantuan tantrums. He also kept goading her into many fights and arguments, breaking into her icy shell so she’d give him attentions, somewhat.
