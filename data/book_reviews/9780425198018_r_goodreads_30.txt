Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed reading this book. I liked the way the characters were formed and how Felicia wasn't just a helpless girl who succumbed to whatever everyone else wanted. I loved the character of Rory as well and how he struggled with his own desires, his past and his wish for peace between the clans. Even the smaller characters Lachlan and Jamie were interesting. 
I enjoyed reading the book and would recommend it to other lovers of the genre.
