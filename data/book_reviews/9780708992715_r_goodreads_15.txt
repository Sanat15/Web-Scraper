Rating: 3/5

This is a tricky book to rate and review. I picked it up off the library shelf because of the title, and the description made me decide to give it a try. I've not read anything else by the author, and contemporary romance isn't a huge category read for me, but I was in the mood for something light and fun. With gardening. :) 

I absolutely loved the first third - I smiled my way through it very quickly. Then a few things happened in the plot that I didn't love, and I started to wonder what the MCs saw in each other. The final third of the book was better, but the author never really convinced me those two belong together. In fact toward the end I wondered if she would end up with Thomas; he seemed nicer and they got along well.

I did come to see that the MCs are a good fit. She's the kind of person who focuses all her attention on one thing and doesn't cope well with the rest of life; he's the guy who wants to be in charge. She never really grew up, IMO, and he likes to have someone who needs him. So their personalities work, but I never felt like there was actual attraction between them - nothing in common, no shared sense of humor or hobbies or anything. For a book that is only focused on their relationship, that really didn't work for me. So 2.5 stars
