Rating: 3/5

Thank you Netgalley and Thistle Publishing for the eARC.
It's 1978 and 2 women are exchanged between Russia and Vienna. Things are afoot in Southeast Asia, and it's suspected Russia is heavily involved.
The story takes place in London, Vienna, Nepal, Hong Kong and the coast of Japan.
I must admit to not being a reader of spy novels and getting quite confused. My husband, a spy novel aficionado, is reading it at the moment and quite enjoying it. He will explain all to me when he's finished...
I apologize to the author for not being an intellegent reader of his intelligence novel😭, but given my husband's enjoyment I give the book 3.5 stars.
