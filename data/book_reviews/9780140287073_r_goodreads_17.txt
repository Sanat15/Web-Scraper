Rating: 4/5

The date started for this book is approximate since I don't know exactly when I began. The finished date is accurate. I put the book aside for a rather long time and only decided to finish it after reading Kerouac's Wake Up: A Life of the Buddha.

The book is certainly not a book in the traditional sense but rather a collections of bits and pieces. Many of the bits and pieces will make you think and many of them will make you go looking for a reference. Sometimes when I went searching for more info I could find nothing. I assume Kerouac simply made up a word or transposed a name in such as way as to make it unfindable even in these days of the Internet.

If you are patient, willing to deal with ambiguity, interested in Buddhism, and a fan of Kerouac I would suggest this book. Otherwise stay away. I don't know if this book is available in electronic format or not but I would not recommend trying to read it that way. Kerouac makes use of very unusual formatting so much would be lost without being able to see how the book is laid out.
