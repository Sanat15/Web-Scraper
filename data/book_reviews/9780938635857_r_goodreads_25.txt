Rating: 5/5

Genuinely one of the best books I have ever read. It has perfectly satisfied my expectation of contextualized theology and my desire to better understand how to translate eastern thought in light of Christ. Damascene did an excellent job explaining not only Lao Tzu's pre-Christian philosophy, but also expounded on the teachings and findings of Seraphim Rose in relation to ancient Chinese traditions. There were many moments I paused to weigh the lines in the wisdom/poetry section and was in awe at how beautifully Damascene presented Christ as the ultimate fulfillment of an eternal longing.
