Rating: 3/5

I actually got this after randomly buying part two first then back tracking The Fat Innkeeper

It's important to note that this one was first published in 1994 and it shows. The book is a touch misogynistic, judgmental and dismissive of people. You get the sense that it was written in the 90s but by someone still living in the 70s.

Alan Russell's writing style won me over though and the little hiccups are more products of their time than actual malice or negativity. I loved the Bob Johnson society the most.

Overall a cute and harmless book that is a little behind the times but still enjoyable as a light read.
