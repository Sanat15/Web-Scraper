Rating: 3/5

Kathy Stinson’s book, The Bare Naked Book, illustrated by Melissa Cho shows the reader the different body parts we all have. How each of our body parts differ from each other and the role they play. The characteristics of each body part that is shown in the book. Telling some of the things that our body does for instance our hands washing. 

The illustrations go along perfectly with what the book is showing the readers. As the book is describing the different body parts and how they act, the illustrations show people from different backgrounds and all with different physical characteristics from each other, showing the reader the broad diversity of bodies. The book is interesting and easy to read and comprehend is a good book to read to kids, as it opens their eyes to the idea of accepting that not everybody is the same and that’s normal. Also the illustrations are well put together with the texts which facilitates the reading.
