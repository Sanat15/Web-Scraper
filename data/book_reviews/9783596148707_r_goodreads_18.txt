Rating: 5/5

i was surprised by the 1 &amp; 2 star reviews here, the book is amazing &amp; Wolitzer's writing incredible. i felt Sara was a metaphor, for youth and desirability, fantasies of perpetual studenthood or (Broadway) fame, and attachment to our parents, all of which die before we can move to adulthood, which Wolitzer proposes to be 30. Sara is the foolish thing we do (cheating, drinking, smoking, hating parents) that each character must reconcile. i love that she hides her anger at her mother so deeply it must literally be translated. and the supposed trashing of the summer house, "which they will never return to" - it isn't trashed, it is improved by what they've been through, but they still dont get to go back. i love it. so transcendent. such a gifted writer.
