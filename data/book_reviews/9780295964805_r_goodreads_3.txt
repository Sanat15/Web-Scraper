Rating: 3/5

I read this book because I joined the Puget Sound Mycological Society and a few people there recommended it to me. It was a good introduction to mushroom identification but it left a lot to be desired and is by no means comprehensive. After a little research I found that really there is no singularly definitive mushroom identification volume for the Pacific Northwest and you have to pick up a few different guides and learn from a mentor to really gain practical foraging knowledge. Good thing I joined PSMS. I'd advise anyone thinking of picking up this book to be prepared to do the same.
