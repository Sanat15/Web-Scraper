Rating: 4/5

This book is so good—even better than I expected! As a writer myself, I thought I would mainly glean takeaways to apply to my own writing, but I learned a lot more than that. The lessons these authors share out of their journeys are relevant for anyone pursuing a dream. The chapters are ordered in such a way that they follow the stages someone goes through when they say "yes" to God and step out in faith to obey His call. The book is so well-written that it's easy to forget it's a collection of interviews; it reads like a collection of brief biographies. In addition, I learned about a number of new-to-me authors whose books I look forward to checking out. That's definitely a plus!
