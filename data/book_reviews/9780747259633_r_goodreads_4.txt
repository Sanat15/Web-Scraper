Rating: 3/5

Prim and Oz are back together almost by accident. Oz has changed and is not the likely-lad he was in the first few books.
He is also working as an actor with Prim's brother-in-law an Australian movie star/director (who is at the top of his game).
All good luck seems to fall his way, winning lottery money, falling into a plum acting job, living in a fabulous house, back with Prim- though that is not feeling right at all.

I am beginning to see a pattern of Oz's friends/lovers dying. It's like a new character is introduced and we start to really like this person,then in the next book they are dispatched.
