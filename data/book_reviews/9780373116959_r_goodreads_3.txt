Rating: 2/5

So it's Madeline's fault Dominic couldn't handle her? What an old-fashioned and negative mentality. Dominic couldn't restrain Madeline's wild nature (she wasn't that bad), so he made her pay. By spanking her. WTH. 

Although engaged, Madeline and Dominic hadn't consummated their relationship yet because he was afraid of making love to her before they were married. She was 18 to his 28 years. Madeline set out to correct that lack by seducing Dominic. He, in response, punished her via the spanking. 

Sorry, but what mature person treats their fiancé this way? I understand Dominic was feeling fearful and frustrated, but it made him look immature.

I finished this book feeling more indifferent than anything else.
