Rating: 5/5

'Anno's U.S.A.' is a wordless book appropriate to read with 2nd through 5h grade students. The book takes you through the journey traveling from the west coast to the east coast of 18th century America. The pictures are beautifully detailed and describes what life was like during that time. To use this book with my future students I would connect with our Social Studies curriculum to develop the students' understanding of colonial America. It would be best to read this book with small groups of students so they are all really able to see the little details and better understand the story.
