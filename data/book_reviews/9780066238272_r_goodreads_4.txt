Rating: 3/5

The artwork is glorious and rates 4 stars, the story rates 3 so overall 3.5 rating. 
A story of Santa losing his beautiful Snow Cat on his busiest night of the year and her looking for him and finding so many 'not the real' Santas. Made me think of so many counterfeit versions of things in the world today which we only discover by trying to find our way back to the real keeper of our heart.
