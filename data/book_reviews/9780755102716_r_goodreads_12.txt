It’s undeniable that while rummaging amongst dusty bookshelves in the local thrift store it was the beautiful jacket design and decorations by Douglas Hall that attracted me to this 1964 2nd printing of the final instalment in Lillian Beckwith’s Hebridean trilogy of warm-hearted tales of her life on a croft.
