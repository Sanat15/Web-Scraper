Rating: 2/5

I told this story with flannelboard art for a dental care-themed program for both Spanish and bilingual story times. In the Bear's Toothache, a little boy tries to help a bear loosen his bad tooth. Nothing works until the boy ties a string around the tooth and the other end to his bed post and has him JUMP out of the window. To be honest, it sounds awful and scary, but the story works since the bear is so thankful that his tooth is gone. I just fear for those kids who might try something similar. Maybe I'm paranoid. ;-)
