Rating: 4/5

This is one of Michaels's early Regencies, so there are no steamy sex scenes, just light-hearted fun. I thoroughly enjoyed it.

Kit has returned from the war, emotionally damaged in some ways. Jenny is a petted daughter that has run tame on the neighboring estate, inherited by Kit. From the passionate kiss that Kit share with her (thinking her a village lass), comes an arranged marriage.

I liked how the couple grew closer through the book. Jenny's assortment of servants that needed jobs (chose from applicants would usually be considered unsuitable people for the job) made me laugh from the incongruity of the situation, but made me happy with their passionate loyalty.

I have already gotten The Toplofty Lord Thorpe the events of which follow hard on the heels of this one.

Who would like it? Fans of traditional Regencies would probably enjoy this frothy story.
