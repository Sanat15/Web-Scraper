Rating: 4/5

A lovely little book.
Yeah that sounds patronising.......well, it is a short book, you can read it in an hour or two but it doesn't mean it doesn't have depths.
It's a coming of age story.
Of childhood innocence that I think many city kids have probably never had or will never have again....country kids (like myself) still have a chance.
The ending felt a little abrupt to me, I feel there was more scope in this story - that it could've been a much bigger book but all in all a lovely little read.
