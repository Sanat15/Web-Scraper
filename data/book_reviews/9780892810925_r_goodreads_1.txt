Rating: 4/5

this is an excellent starter guide to newer tarot readers that are struggling with the Court (which is always a problem). it also gives advanced readers a new way to look at the Court. it basis the entire Tarot Court on how they affect our lives, either in a positive or negative light. taking each of the 16 Couts individually and breaking each one down into four different (Supporter, Detractor, Resource, Challenge) possibilities of how, who, and what they could mean. 

the book itself has a removable chart with this list, there are several spreads specifically created for the book. All of them but one uses only the Court card to develop your own understanding of both the book and the Court as she lays them forth. the last spread still separates out the tarot deck (this is a big pet peeve of mine, but makes sense in the overall book) into Majors, Courts, and Minors.

overall, i do think it's worth reading, even if it wasn't exactly what i was thinking it was going to be. it does give you a very unique inside to the Court and how they could relate to you and your own life.
