Rating: 2/5

**1/2 I didn't particularly care for this book. It is a non-fiction book about John Colter &amp; his geographical discoveries he made as an early mountain man. I like the subject manner at hand but didn't care for how the book proceeded. I was expecting more of a biography type book and didn't get what I was expecting. To me it read more so as a school textbook that debated the many different theories of Colter's discoveries as there was very little written history but rather stories told by others that knew him or entries taken from the Lewis &amp; Clark expedition, with whom Colter was enlisted with at one time.
