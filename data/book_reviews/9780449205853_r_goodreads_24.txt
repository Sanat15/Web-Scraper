Rating: 4/5

I'm sad I'm done with the series! This one is probably my top three of the six. I'm glad we get to see Wentwater being punished although I wish he was publicly humiliated a little but more. In this book, we do see a bit more interaction between the love interests and I like the departure from the two-guys-competing-for-one-girl plot. This book was about Frederica finding herself.
