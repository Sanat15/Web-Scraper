Rating: 4/5

An adorable picture book about a Jewish family stranded during a sudden blizzard that leaves them deprived of potatoes and apples to make latkes and applesauce for Hanukkah. The illustrations were colorful and vivid. Overall, a great book for children to learn more about Hanukkah and miracles.
