Rating: 3/5

Started and finished date – 20.12.25 to 24.12.25. 
My rating – Three Stars. 
This book was okay, and I somewhat enjoyed but I didn't love, and I think this book needed some tweaking. I think people who like the revenge of magic by James Riley or wildwood by Colin Meloy may like is book. The cover was okay, but it could be better, and I think the colour palette at was used on the cover of book was pretty good. I think the writing was okay and the writing took some time to get used to also the ending of book was fine. I think the setting of the book was fine, but it could use some flash out and the atmosphere was okay. The plot was both predictable and full of stereotypes, but it was still enjoyable and a fun time. I think both the friendships and relationships between most the characters was fine, but it could be better. 

 I think both the world building and the magic systems was interesting and it was favourite part of the book. Most of twists in book was pretty good but some of twists in the book could some flash out. I think fans of the movie or books of golden Compass by Philip Pullman or the chronicles of Narnia by C. S. Lewis may like is book. I enjoyed learning about characters back stories, but I would like more but I found parts of book to be ludicrous. I think found the paced in the book was well structured and steady paced. The characters were okay, but they could have use some flash out bit more.
