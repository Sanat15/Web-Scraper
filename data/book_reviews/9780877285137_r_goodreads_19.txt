Rating: 3/5

My star rating is purely for this particular edition of Book 4. The Weiser/Red Wheel edition with this cover does NOT include part 3, "Magick In Theory And Practice", yet it never states that anywhere in the publisher's description. Anyone coming to this for the first time will assume this is all there ever was to Book 4 which is a shame as they're missing out on what many people think is Crowley's most important work!
So 4 or 5 stars for the FULL version of Book 4, Liber ABA but only 3 for this cut-down edition.
