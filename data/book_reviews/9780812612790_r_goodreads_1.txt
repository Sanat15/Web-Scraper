Rating: 5/5

I think I have officially found my all time favorite children's book. I wish the cover was posted on here. This little story is about a young black girl who adores spiders! I connected with this because I too love spiders and I'm a black girl. Finding this book was a true treasure and though it was short it gave very great facts that would be easier to remember for kinders. This is most certainly be in my classroom and my introduction to a few lesson plans! I can not wait to share the beautiful little prize.
