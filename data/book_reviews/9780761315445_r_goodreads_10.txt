Rating: 4/5

A lovely story about the relationship between George, a very clever baby, and his sister Layla! This is a relationship that many children can relate to, specifically those that may be adapting to having a new baby in their family. I remember reading this book as a child, and enjoyed this lighthearted book just as much as an adult. This would be a lovely read for KS2 children, as the text is large in size and does include some great illustrations, but there is still quite a bit of text to read.
