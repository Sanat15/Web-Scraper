Rating: 5/5

Um, I'm embarrassed to say I never read in the Bible in its entirety. And, I may not have, were it not for the convincing recommendation of one Savannah Guthrie, of the Today Show fame. I'm not even a huge fan of hers and haven't watched the show in years. It could be divine intervention because I ran to get an audio version. It was so clear and beautifully written. I enjoyed it and was changed by it. I never understood how comforting it is to read, a source of guidance and counsel. How many people, how many times have told you to read the Bible? I cringed and shirked that advice. Well, chalk that one up in the L column for me. So, I wish that other cringers and shirkers will find this a way to experience the Bible that eliminates the language barrier.
