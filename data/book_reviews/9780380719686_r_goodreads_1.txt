Rating: 3/5

Cute, liked how managing Georgina was and how she sweeps everyone in her path into one of her tangles. Liked how Rotham tangles himself into trouble all by himself by trying to pre-empt any trouble Georgina's visit would bring. This book is a prime example of how important communication between correct parties is 

Georgina decides her elder half-sister Lizzy needs to marry and tricks Lizzy into coming into town. Georgina write to Rotham for help. Rotham, thinking Georgina wants to be brought out, asks his female friend Alice for help...except he words his question incorrectly and ends up becoming engaged to Alice! Alice jumps at her only marriage proposal, says yes even though she loves Sir Percy. Sir Percy was rejected by Alice's father, so he never got around to asking Alice herself.  Rotham and Georgina fall in love, but due to his engagement, each starts rather destructive behavior, until finally Lizzy elopes with her love Colly. they elope because they both believe Georgina won't sanction the match, but they never actually ask her.
