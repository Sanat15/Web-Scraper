Rating: 4/5

“No other vista I have ever seen at the Canyon or elsewhere gives so much meaning to the well-worn phrase, “magnificent distances.” One may see for miles. There is the magnificent distance left and right, the magnificent distinct up and down to the river; there is also the magnificent distance up to the great bowl of the brilliant western sky. Yet there is no monotony. Space is diversified though not filled by an endless profusion of shapes and colors—sheer red cliffs, fantastic “temples” in particular-colored stones and, at the very bottom, the red Colorado whose work the whole spectacle is.”
