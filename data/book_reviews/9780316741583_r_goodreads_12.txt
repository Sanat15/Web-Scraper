Rating: 4/5

This is one of the funniest, most imaginative books I've ever read. Ostensibly about "Cadillac"- a journalist trying to describe "Soul City" for a book, this is an accurate vibe of black life. It rolls along; no, it hops, skips, jumps--it carries you along with it on a melliflous spoof/parody of everything black &amp; our attitudes toward it. Fun reading!
