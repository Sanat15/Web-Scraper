Rating: 3/5

What a strange little book. This is a darker story from Keats. It follows two brothers, Sam and Ben, as they try to figure out which apartment is playing the music they so love. The atmosphere and tone of the book are both darker, as the story is set during a rainy day and people are heard yelling. There are some scary moments where someone kind of pops out at the kids and other moments where the people who live there are a bit gruff or strange. I think he may have meant this one to be an artistic study of city life. If so, it succeeds in that. However, I prefer Pet Show and Goggles if we’re exploring that theme.
