Rating: 5/5

Eliot is a very unique voice in mythological study and this book is immensely powerful reading, albeit too short. Unlike Joseph Campbell or other Jungians, Eliot is nonessentialist in everything but the fact that myth is essential in life. Here is a writer and person who understands myth on numerous levels and while it is easy to scoff at this book's loftiness, Eliot's passion transcends the writing itself. Spend some time re-reading it and I assure you Eliot's points will sink in and you will find arguments of your own against them to create a wonderful dialogue around myth.
