Rating: 3/5

This was a sweet western romance involving outlaws and Indians, vengeance and love. It begins with Matilda, a plain spinster traveling west to meet her husband, who she married by proxy and has never met. Along the way her stagecoach is attacked by Indians and her plans are seriously derailed. 

There was a lot to like about this book, and it kept me turning pages, but though I had hopes that it would become a terrific four-star read or even an amazing five-star, I ultimately felt that it was only a good read. 

Matilda herself posed a lot of the problem. She kept letting the bad guy escape or keep the hero from tracking/killing him and frankly, it got to a point where I resented her for it. A part of me felt that she was at least somewhat responsible for the women he killed after she cut him free, knowing he had killed before, knowing he had left her to die. She took tender-hearted a bit too far, IMHO. And I wasn't all that invested in the idea that the hero should choose between his revenge and his love for her. At least not in this instance, when he was hunting an active threat. If that moral strikes a chord with you, then you may enjoy the book much more than I did. :)

I also felt that the book was a bit too long for what it was. 

This isn't a bad read if you like sweet, western-style romance.
