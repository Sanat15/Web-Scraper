Rating: 3/5

"Dear Reader -- a lady speaking to humans from the motion of her own mind is always multiple. Enough of the least. We want to be believed."

"First all belief is paradise."

This book felt procedural, like a linguistic experiment, like most of the time I have no idea why these words are here, in this order, rather than any others. Occasionally there are amazing sentences and surprising strings of syntax -- that did happen. And there's an originality, an authenticity. But most of the time I just felt: ugh, somebody's project.

Seven Walks, however, I have loved.
