Rating: 4/5

A really compelling story that in many ways feels like a series of vignettes following the main character of Korendir, a mercenary of sorts in a world that's a series of islands. But to call it that gives a disservice to the writing, which made the story flow seamlessly from scenario to scenario, following a highly enigmatic character whose journeys mirror the restlessness of the character himself. The ending packed a great punch and added weight to what proceeded it. My only criticism is I felt like Korendir and the other characters felt like they were held at arms' length from the reader throughout, and I felt a bit of emotional detachment from them.
