Rating: 3/5

I grabbed this one from the library because when I recently ordered a rare book the cover of this book was used as a protection for the smaller rare book and the cover intrigued me. I wish this series had been around when I was a kid cuz I would have loved them. And, this one was a nice palate cleanser after a book that made me frustrated. I must admit that the idea of a drive-thru funeral parlor did make me laugh out loud and I appreciated that.
