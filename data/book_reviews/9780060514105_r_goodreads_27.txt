Rating: 4/5

This, in my opinion was a good book. "Molly Moon Stops the World" by Georgia Byng is the second book of six in this series. Although, I will admit, I kind of liked the book better. In this book Molly still knows how to hypnotize people, but she prefers not to because of what happened in the last book. Her librarian friend, Lucy Logan, tells her the mysterious things going on with celebrities. After some digging, Molly and her friend Rocky find out that the culprit is a man named Primo Cell is hypnotizing his way through and is working on becoming president. Molly and Rocky head to Hollywood to catch Cell. While there, Molly finds out that a diamond that she wears gives her the power to stop time, but Primo Cell also has that power. When I got to the end of the book I was shocked. I didn't see it coming, but no spoilers. I thought it had a great ending to it. One of my favorites parts was when Molly found out she had this amazing gift and tried to tell Rocky. Of course he didn't believe her. So, she froze time and put all these weird objects on him to make him believe. When she unfroze time, Rocky was amazed, but confused at what she did. So yeah, I enjoyed reading this book.
