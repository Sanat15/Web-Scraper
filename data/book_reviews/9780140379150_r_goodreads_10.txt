4.5 stars
I'm a HUGE fan of Roald Dahl. I've been one since I was a kid and read Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, George's Marvellous Medicine, etc. So, I was absolutely delighted when, a few years ago, I discovered a collection of short stories for adults written by him! And the collection was full of twisty, ironic, and unsettling stories—a genre I hadn't explored much otherwise. (In retrospect, I later realized how even his children's stories were quite dark in nature.)

Since then, I've always been on the lookout for more of his short story collections, and this one didn't disappoint. I had read a couple of them previously but still loved re-reading them all over again. And the new ones had just the right amount of satire and irony. I especially loved how a lot of the stories didn’t spell out the outcome in words but was cleverly implied.
