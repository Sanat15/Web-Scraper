Rating: 5/5

Final Word on the American Buffalo

I always felt as if most other references to buffalo hunting only scratched the surface. This book is really only about the beginning, the middle and the end of buffalo hunting. Zane Grey tells the story through a young man who joins an expierenced scout and his partner who feel like the buffalo are a gold mine. There is nothing heroic about their hunts although there are heros in this book. Details of the chasing, the killing, the skinning, the drying and the hauling to market of buffalo skins are carefully exlained. Grey shows that there is much to learn about this mammoth slaughter and he teaches us well.
