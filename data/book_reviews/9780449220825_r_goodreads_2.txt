Rating: 3/5

I'm a little annoyed by the leaving of children and (ex-)wives behind beacuse "a man's got to do what a man's got to do" mentality that forms the basis of the voyage which leads up to the capsizing and ensuing tale of survival. I'm equally annoyed by the fact that Glennie, after the rescue, seems way more inclined to contact friends and the like than the same children and ex-wives. But hey, I know nothing about his thought process (or anything else for that matter) besides what is written in this book, so I'm not judging.
No literary masterpiece by any means, but quite interesting nonetheless.
