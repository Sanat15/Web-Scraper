Rating: 4/5

4 stars. We’re always quoting this opening paragraph! It’s a stunner. I love getting to see Benjamin + Flopsy &amp; their babies. The humour is excellent, though I would enjoy it more if Mr. &amp; Mrs. McGregor were nicer to each other, but I suppose they are supposed to be the villains! The illustrations are gorgeous and make me want a garden so much… and like all BP books, this is so much fun to read aloud!
