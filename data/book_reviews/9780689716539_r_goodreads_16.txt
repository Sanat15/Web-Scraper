Rating: 4/5

WOW. I sure learned two things while reading this roller coaster of a book: 

1) do NOT go on a bear hunt. It is sometimes illegal and also very dangerous, especially if you have no weapons and it chases you all the way back to your house. 

2) Always keep your doors locked after you come inside. The bear got in through the front door, and that was just too close of a call. 

Thankfully the family was safe and had quite the adventure together. I will plan to stay home and go on a cow hunt instead by looking for my cat, Boethius, who looks like a little cow. 

I am now able to grab his fur, and when I do, he just runs away from me and does not chase me around the house like that big bear did.
