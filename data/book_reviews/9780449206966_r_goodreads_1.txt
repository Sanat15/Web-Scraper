Rating: 4/5

This is an unimaginative title for a very good book.

At first Hadrian, Viscount Emery, reminded me of one of Georgette Heyer's characters. He soon shook those tendencies and settled into someone more familiar. 

I enjoyed the story, shifting sub-plots of the journey Ms Peters leads the reader. I'm disappointed she doesn't have any of her books on Kindle but intend to seek her out at the used bookstores.
