Rating: 4/5

Every rank and file union member should read this book. It is a great collection on stories across generations of American labor history. From the coal mines of southern Iowa to the fields of California; from the turn of the century, till the beginning exodus of labor jobs in the 70s, Rank &amp; File offers a diverse group of organizing stories from regular union workers. Perhaps most enlightening and most important is struggles of these workers within their own unions. Know your history and put it in practice.
