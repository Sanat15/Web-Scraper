Rating: 4/5

Sweet and funny as usual - I love baby April. This book is probably most well-known for the story of Lawrence's coming-out... It's crazy to think of some of the reactions Lynn Johnston had to endure for this story. (I read that she got an envelope with yellowed cuts of her comics that someone had kept on her fridge for years with a note saying how Lynn and her comic wasn't welcome in these people's home anymore. Crazy.) Especially since the reactions to the coming-out are quite harsh! It's not like the book was staying straight out "being gay is awesome!" Anyway. Of course, it was 1993. I wonder how she would do such an arc now. I do hope Lawrence's family's reaction would be better!
