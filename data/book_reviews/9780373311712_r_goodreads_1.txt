Rating: 4/5

This book starts strong with the brief meeting of the H and h, which is the perfect introduction to the characters and their personalities. I don't think the back cover describes the book very well. The h is actually very sensible and does not continue to fall into scrapes. There's an incident that happens due to the H's brother and she's not rescued as much as they make it right by her (that part of the book felt a bit strange though, as if it were part of another story). The H is a great hero but he's not perfect. After the incident, he says some harsh words to the heroine when she catches him off guard and she goes on to become a governess believing that's the end of it. The H, smart man that he is, decides to win her affection (and he does apologize for the way he spoke to her). I liked this book a lot more than I thought I would. There are the usual tropes of Regency romance but I thought the H and h were great, the story had some unique elements and I enjoyed the hours I spent reading it.
