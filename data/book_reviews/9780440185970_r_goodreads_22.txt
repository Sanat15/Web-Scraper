Rating: 2/5

I've heard so many praises for Anne Stuart so I was excited to try one.
I wasn't crazy about it.
I did like the writing style and loved the dialogue. I liked the h ok but nobody else.

Here are the things I found troubling
-I want a H to be true to the h once they've met. There are indications he's "wenching" after they've met.
-I felt no chemistry between the H and h. Why'd they fall in love?
-The H shows no remorse for trying to run off with a married woman. Then he uses his influence with the same woman later. It was weird.
-The H humiliates the h with a horrible wager. After which he doesn't even apologize. He doesn't chase after her. Nothing. I want a good grovel. He does nothing to get her back. The h goes back to him!
-The H has a friend who is a complete turd and is practically rewarded in the end. 
-The h's brother is a huge douche bag and needs to be chopped off at the knees.

Too many things bugged me about this story. Uhg.
