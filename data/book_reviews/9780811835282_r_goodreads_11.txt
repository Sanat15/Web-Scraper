Rating: 4/5

This is a nicely designed little book of soup recipes, divided into chapters that center around types of soups: creamy soups, chunky soups, chilled soups, etc. All are vegetarian, some are vegan. But most of them can be made vegan with the elimination of an ingredient, which the author indicates in a headnote. The recipes are creative, from scratch, and the instructions are easy to follow.
