Rating: 5/5

Raine is such a unique, passionate and memorable character. She is never predictable and as a reader you can't stop wondering what she'll choose to do next. You kind of want to take her under your wing, as Al tries to do, but she's a bit too prickly for that. And Al has his own problems....

This story has a pleasing mix of humor, philosophy, dramatic surprise and beautiful lyrical writing. I should confess that the writer is a personal friend of mine, but honestly, she has created a very quirky, very American, very true teen character whose journey is one you want to follow.
