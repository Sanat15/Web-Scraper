Rating: 4/5

Really a 4.5 Star Read

The Hotel Detective is a novel about Am Caulfield -the assistant manager of the Hotel California outside of San Diego. It is a detailed look at the mental abilities it takes to run an exclusive hotel and not pull your hair out.

Am uses problem solving skills to deal with the biggest headache on all - people. Whether it is staff, customers, or law enforcement, there is never a dull moment. The prima donnas, the grifters, or the corporate spies, Am has to stay on his toes. The juggling on the corporate level is crazy but realistic in today's society.

After reading this book, you'll be fascinated by all the working parts to make a hotel exclusive. And if you have common sense, you will value the joy of check out.
