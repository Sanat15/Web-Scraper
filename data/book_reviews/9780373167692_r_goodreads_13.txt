Rating: 4/5

The is the story of Dylan and Whitney. They have been best friends since they met. The line between best friends and lovers blurs and they end up sleeping together. Fast forward to Dylan's wedding where he finds out he's going to be a dad.

This story was intriguing, unpredictable, and a great romance novel. I loved the emotions and feelings. These characters were well written and entertaining.
