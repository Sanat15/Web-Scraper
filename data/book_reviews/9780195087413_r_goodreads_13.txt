I had been granted but the tiniest kiss
(On whom I've had a crush since I was nine)
If by that hottest of hotties Beatrice

After all I had experienced and done
Left—I then would have known a state of bliss
From my sad forehead until I had none

An angel on each floor erased a P
Sins (of which I’d committed every one)
Tiers rose according to the seven deadly

Tapering up into the zenith seven
If I were married to Calliope
I could never do it justice even

Of that journey up read Dante’s account
In order to behold the gates of Heaven
Near the scale of what we had to surmount

Kilimanjaro and you’ll be nowhere 
Picture Mount Everest piled on top of mount
You would have jumpéd too had you been there:

Three feet high at least oh I do not lie!
Marked a sight which made me jump in the air
That when I turned to face my guides my eye

The grandeur of God's poetic command
I do not know so absorbed was I by
What length of time on that seashore did I stand

And below them boats with their sails unfurled
Around by Zephyr’s invisible hand
How lovely to see pink clouds again whirled 

Where the stars were upside down the moon as well
And reached the underbelly of the world
So I had finally crawled out of Hell
