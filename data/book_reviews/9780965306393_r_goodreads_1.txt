Rating: 1/5

I was dismayed to learn that my county's library system only had one book on motorhome/RV travel, and even more disappointed that this was the one title that they chose to purchase. 

The comments/reviews on the back of the book are misleading - this is NOT a "reference book" or "guide." It is more of a memoir, ane one with unfortunate religious overtones. It does include a checklist at the back of the book, but when I compared it to my own, mine was actually more comprehensive. (And I've never been inside of an RV. Hence the desire for a decent how-to book!) My list would NEVER include a bible or tips for finding worship sites "for the sabbath" while on the road!

Clearly, I didn't appreciate the blessings and Christianity plugs, which made it even more disheartening that this is the only option the library had to offer.
