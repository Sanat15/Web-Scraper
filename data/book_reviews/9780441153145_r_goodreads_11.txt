From August 2026 reread:

I quite enjoyed my reread of this lovely collection of fairy tales. Just like the first time I read it, my two favorites were The Twelve Dancing Princesses (what lovely symbolism and sentiment it has!) and The Frog Prince.

“The soldier looked back at the King as the King looked at him; for a moment he wondered if he should bow, but the King’s look seemed to wish to forestall him. The soldier saw a face for whom he would be willing to carry colors into battle once more, and the memory of his colonel seemed to fail and fade nearly to oblivion.”

__
I find McKinley's work so mesmerizing. If not her most meaningful or penetrating work (in my view), this book was nevertheless a very pleasant read with several sweet, poignant moments and pockets of wisdom throughout.
