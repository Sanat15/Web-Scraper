Rating: 5/5

Shelton created one of the most intriguing natural and human history constructs. As one who visits Bisbee on a regular basis (from when I used to go with my grandparents in the 1960s before the Lavender Pit was closed), this book was a joy that I hoped would never end. I wanted to learn much more about many of the people who comprised this history—especially Molly Bendixon. One example that surprised me was that I never thought I would go back to Tombstone. However, Shelton unveiled layers of history for that tourist trap that resulted in a recent revisit to the town and its Courthouse which was more fascinating having read the book.

More histories should be written by poets!
