Rating: 2/5

I read a few Madeleine Brent books years ago, I decided to read the rest of the ones that I hadn't. This one did not do it for me. It was very slow moving and all the excitement happened at the very end of the book, it made me feel just eh about it. I'm curious to see if it was me that has changed since reading the others or if this one truly is a lesser book by this author. I'm excited to read another to test the theory!
