Living in the last decade or so, memes, gifs, vines, snapchat and brain dissolving celebrity quotes have dominated the primary form of human interaction and occupation: the internet! With these technological advances the younger generation has not only had its moral standards diminished but our attention spans too! Do you miss thinking? How about pondering things that actually matter? Well if you'd like to remember how to have your mind engaged for more than 30 seconds at a time, look no further than Jan Siegel's "Prospero's Children"! And enjoy an experience that will remind you that there are words that have more than five letters.
 While this tale will completely stimulate your mind with a grammatically seductive allure fit with a one-of-a-kind shine, but also heavily cascades the reader with adventure, passion, thrills, mystery and even a few tear jerkers all while providing appropriately placed talking boulders, woman trapped as werewolves, time travel, the lost city of Atlantis and an abundance of other wonderful suprizes that I refuse to ruin for you.
