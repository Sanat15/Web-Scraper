Rating: 4/5

Salvador Dali. An artist, a snob, an experimenter, a joker, a paranoiac, a provocateur but, above all, a genius.
I find Dali's work interesting but not appealing. Similarly, I find Dali's personality interesting but not particularly attractive. He goes over the top in his eccentricity and sometimes this eccentricity seems well calculated.
At some points, however, one must give him credit for sincerity, sense of humour and the exceptional ability to notice, enjoy and appreciate all the little things around him and generate his crazy ideas and creative energy from them. He describes this process in his diary.
In his diary, Dali focuses on the genius, that is himself. But sometimes he writes about his fellows artists, writers, poets he has known and it is interesting to read his reflections and recollections about them.
