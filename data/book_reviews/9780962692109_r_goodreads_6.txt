Rating: 5/5

Challenge your thinking and push limitations out of the nest! The book was written years ago, it is a bestseller, for good reasons.

I have read more recent books by David McNally, whose writing and wisdom has followed along with my life's path. I will buy this book for my grown children, who are out of the nest, and soaring in their life and careers. This book offers guidance and a genuine opportunity for reflection and perspective, to soar in this changing world.

It's a great read/workbook/life coach experience.
