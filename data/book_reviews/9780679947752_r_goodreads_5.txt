Rating: 4/5

This a unique fairy tale. It’s a cross between Snow White and Sleeping Beauty…except the one in the enchanted sleep is a prince with “skin as white as snow, lips as red as blood, and hair as golden as the sun”. Thankfully he doesn’t quite meet that description or he would have looked a bit creepy. The Princess, Florecita, “sat at her window sewing with a golden thimble, she pricked her finger, and a drop of blood fell on the sill.” And then a little bird comes and sings to her of the prince and off she goes in her iron shoes. The artwork is gorgeous and modest.
