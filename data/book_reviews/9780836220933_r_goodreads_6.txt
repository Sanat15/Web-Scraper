Rating: 5/5

We read many, many of Lynn Johnston's FOR BETTER OR FOR WORSE collections when Jesse was little (kindergarten and early school years... so... twenty-five years ago) so the "Date I finished..." would have been during those years.

Johnston's books were a family favorites, but they were also a valuable bridge in terms of Jesse's learning to read. She was enjoying novels like ANN OF GREEN GABLES and SWALLOWS AND AMAZONS before she could read on her own, so a lot of "easy read" books didn't satisfy her as a reader. Johnston's book had complex plots and interesting characters with a limited word count and wonderfully expressive pictures to help carry the meaning.

When I was reading my books and doing storytelling sessions in schools from the late 80's through the early O's, kids would often ask me about authors that I liked to read and authors that I particularly admired. In response I would talk about Lynn Johnston's work and her ability to tell such rich stories with a few words and a "simple" drawings.
