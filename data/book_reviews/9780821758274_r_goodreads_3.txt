Rating: 2/5

This book is sort of the anti-Emma. Charlotte works to block her brother's romantic interests, rather than play matchmaker. Charlotte is a very interesting character--wealthy, 24, in charge of her brother's estate, and a leading figure in their small gossipy town. The Knightly-esq character, Roland, is their mentor and guide but is preoccupy with his sister's and the Duke's romantic issues so the romantic drama between Char and Rowland was virtually nil. There was no 'revelation'--which I thought would come during a misunderstanding with the sister's fiance 3/4 through the book but now. 

The writing was lovely and immersive and I felt transported to the time period, but they developed Char and Roland so poorly, and focused way more on the Duke's drama torn between 2 cousins--one vivacious and scandalous the other mousy but intelligent, that the book should probably have been from a different POV
