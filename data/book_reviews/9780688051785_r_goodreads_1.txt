Rating: 1/5

For an very amature birder that labels and charts were a little confusing. A 1990's book no photos. Maps looked hand drawn. Seemed to have very complete information of where to find the birds in US and Canada. With charts of 'totals' of 'abundant, common, uncommon, occasional, and rare' which I thought was neat although do not know exactly what to think of that! Bulky book if you are only looking at your state. My state of Colorado had ONE page front and back. Hmmm...I know of a lot more detail than that!!!
