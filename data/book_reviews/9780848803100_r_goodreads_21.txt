Rating: 4/5

The story is about a 15 year old boy with a crush on a young woman whose husband has gone to fight in WWII. It’s an emotional and sometimes tragic story with many funny moments that still hold up today. 

So which came first? The book or the movie? Herman Raucher novelized his screenplay and it was released before the movie staring Jennifer O’Neill. So on this case, the movie was produced first. 

My well loved and faded copy of The Summer of ‘42 holds a special place in my heart. If you haven’t read it, I highly recommend it and I recommend watching the movie which has such a beautiful, haunting soundtrack.
