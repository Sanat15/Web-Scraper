Great book with a lot of detail information. Its not just a biographical book. The life of Vlad Tepes is mainly political interesting, thus, its interwowen with the happenings of that time. The books, so, provides a ton of historical facts about the fight against the Ottomans on the Balkan peninsula in this distinctive period.
I recommend it to everyone interested in Balkan history and/or Ottoman history.
