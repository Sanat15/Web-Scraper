Rating: 4/5

Huge fan of Knight Rider so I was excited when I found out there were books based off some of the episodes! This was a very fun read as it goes more into depth than the actual episode and has extra details that the episode doesn’t have. It also doesn’t follow the episode exactly word for word, but is overall the same which I honestly like! I liked the little differences, it was nice! The only reason it’s a four star is because there was definitely times where the book was dragging it out and parts where I had to kinda force myself to keep going as I would get bored. But overall, it’s a fun read and I recommend if you’re a fan of the show!
