Rating: 5/5

Omg this was probably one of my best reads yet! This was super fun to read once you get a general understanding of the different words they use. You don't get alot of books with an ending where the main character dies, and tbh I'm not mad about it. Larka died as a legend and the whole prophecy is just amazing. The whole concept is just out of this world. David did a wonderful job writing this. This book was my escape, when I was reading this I was right there witnessing it all. If you love fantasy then is definitely right up your alley. I can't even begin to understand David's imagination while writing this, the book felt like a whole new world. 

I cried when Kar "died" and honestly I just felt like I was there and experiencing the pain and the fear along with the wolves. I think that Kar deserves so much and Slavka is redeeming herself. Huttser and Palla have new cubs named after the family they lost and it feels like a new beginning. Fell on the other hand is like a while different story, I don't think he is going to heal, he became wolfbane and that's really hard on him, the one wolf that brought him back died in front if him and he can't get over it. He ultimately left the pack deciding that he needed to be free to hunt and to be a wolf. I don't blame him but I think he needs to let go and look at Larkas death in a way that paints her as a hero who set the wolves free.
