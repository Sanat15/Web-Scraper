Rating: 5/5

Joy, Central patron, August 2019, 5 stars:

Came across this at a used book sale. Great read. It was published in 1982, close enough to Watergate that the few stories shared about it were fresh and emotional. Exciting stories of faith and struggle shared in this book. The Hanoi Hilton church in the infamous POW camp was highlighted in one chapter. Amazing how God strengthens those that give themselves fully to Him no matter their circumstances. Great chapter on Micky Cohen, the mobster and Billy Graham. Really great read. A keeper!
