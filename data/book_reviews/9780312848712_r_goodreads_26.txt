Rating: 3/5

I wanted to like this one, but though it wasn't terrible, I felt it was too uneven. On one hand you have unicorns and 'dream colts' speaking in rhyme, and cute villains (selkies) who talk like every day is International Talk Like a Pirate Day. On the other there are very harsh adult-level violence and sexuality thrown in seemingly at random. I didn't get a sense that Wright really knew who his audience was. 
I liked the premise of the story--the dream world impinging on what we call reality--and the references to other stories (Lovecraft's 'Silver Key', for example) that Wright threw in here and there. This could have been so much better. As it is, I'm not planning to continue with the series.
