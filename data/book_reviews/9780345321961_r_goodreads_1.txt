Rating: 3/5

Well written. I find the book intriguing. The author knows how to motivate and encourage. However, the diet itself looks like another low-carb diet: high quality protein, lots of vegetables, low sugar fruit, little fat. 

I still appreciate the author’s effort to creat a positive body image. And I still want to try it out. Maybe I will revise my review once I’ve really tried the 21 days diet and exercise plan presented in the book. 

Caution: eating lots of vegetable sounds healthy. But I ate too much too soon and got a terrible stomachache. The author does warn about it and advices to ease into it.
