Rating: 4/5

I read this book as part of a book of duo novellas. This is the first story retitled “Playing for Heart” (I think that is the title). The 2nd story in the series is “Playing for Keeps”. The first story deals with Art and Maddie and how they meet and their misconceptions of who each other is before they meet. I enjoy Deborah Raney’s books and this was no exception.
