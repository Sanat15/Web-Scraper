Rating: 2/5

Now I am an Emily Hendrickson fan, and I generally enjoy her stories. That said, parts of this was good, but I fear the whole story got waterlogged with the introduction of the 'murder mystery' subplot. Ignoring that problem, I enjoyed the parts concerning Chloe and her drawings, if I could I'd give it 2 and a 1/2 stars for her character alone. There was also a whole thing going on with the grandmother that never got explained, if that had been addressed in depth this could have been a totally different read.
