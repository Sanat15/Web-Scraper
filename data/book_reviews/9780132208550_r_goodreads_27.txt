Rating: 4/5

This book won the 1968 Caldecott Medal for its illustrations.

The story is a a cumulatvie, or progressive, tale of various soldiers adding parts to a cannon before it is fired. Each page adds one more soldier and one more part. Drummer Hoff fires it off, but not without the barrell brought by Corporal Farrell, the shot brought by Major Scott, and the order given by General Border. The rhyming and tongue-twisting text will inspire laughs among children too young to read and by those who enjoy trying to read it faster and faster.

The illustrations are vibrant and intricate; they have been likened to everything from psychedelic to wood cuts and Colonial American engravings. Those that know of Ed Emberley for his simplified yet charming "how-to-draw" books will find something different in these illustrations. Although the bright colors and final picture of flowers blooming from the cannon are reminiscent of the posters and anti-war messages of the 60s, the soldiers are clearly from a much earlier historical period.
