Rating: 5/5

A perfect story of endurance and overcoming your past. Elaine is a lady hiding in plain sight from a past that still gives her nightmares. The author does a great job of building the the readers anticipation as she metes out the details of Elaine’s past. Daniel is a man who lives his life stoically trying not to repeat the mistakes made by his parents. The characters in this story are just supremely well done. The well of emotions displayed in their conversations is wonderful and allows the reader to become fully immersed. The students become allies of the couple as they work to ensure that they find the truth and true love.
