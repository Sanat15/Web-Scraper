Rating: 4/5

Much better than average multi-generational family saga about a small town in rural Georgia, complete with a serial killer, Klu Klux Klan, the Kennedys and everything else in between. Some racial stereotypes and cliches were all used to hearing, but actually fairly realistic. Starts off slowly, but quickly picks up into a fast paced thriller. One of the best aspects of the story is the unforeseen near future looms at the end of the "happy" ending. Well done by Woods. Good, solid read.
