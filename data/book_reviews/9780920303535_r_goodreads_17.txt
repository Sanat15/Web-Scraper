Rating: 5/5

I'm very impressed with the diverse array of bodies shown in this book. I've never seen some of these bodies represented in picture books before. Yes there's a drag queen face, women's bodies, men's bodies, queer bodies, transgender bodies, bodies with scars. One body has a chest scar that indicates heart surgery, another body has scars under the breasts which may or may not be a trans body. There are bodies in wheelchairs, amputees, limb differences, and yes even naked babies. 

The authors used the word "genitals" on purpose so parents can choose which words they want their kids to use and kids can describe their bodies in their own words and not be confined by sex = gender.

This one does belong in the parenting section within the children's room. It has an author's note and is written with the parents in mind. Each page has a question for parents to ask their toddlers, like "Where is your nose?"

If I were my cousins parenting toddlers, I'd grab this book for sure.
