Rating: 3/5

I loved this book when I was in middle school. So, of course, when my school closed down, I made sure to snag the same copy I read all those years ago. (Pays off when the school was owned by your church). It still sits on my shelf, next to Harry Potter and tons of history textbooks.

What can I tell you about this book? Yes, it's out dated..in fact, it's the same age as I am. But this book is a nice, light hearted read and I love its corny sense of humor. It has a fun teenage romance (a girl conjures a ghost trying to place a spell on her crush..how do YOU think it's going to go?) and it doesn't stray too far from a basic teenage book plot. I especially love the very end of the book. It makes you wonder...it makes you wonder...

This book is most definitely out of print but if you see it on the shelves and really need something nice and light to read, it's worth a peek.
