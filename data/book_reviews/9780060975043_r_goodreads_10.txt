Rating: 5/5

Now, after reading The Runaway Soul, for 20 days I feel like I need a long breath before I can even begin to process it. It wouldn’t be fair to judge it on the spot. Some books don’t end when you close it — they linger quietly, asking for time to settle in your mind and heart. I think this is one of them. I need time to absorb everything that happened, the emotions it stirred, and the silence it leaves behind.
