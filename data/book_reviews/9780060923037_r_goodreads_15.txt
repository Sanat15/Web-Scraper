Rating: 5/5

This book was really enjoyable and reflective. It opened my eyes to things I was particularly blind to. I know many people might say that this book only merely has information that everyone already knows, but as a sixteen-year-old in high school and an A student, this book made me reflect a lot. I came back to the book and saw the million highlights and writings in it. This book has really opened my mind and taught me a lot. For this, I am forever grateful.
