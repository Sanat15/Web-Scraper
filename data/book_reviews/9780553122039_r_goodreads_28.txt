Rating: 3/5

The story follows a young boy Jody Tiflin who receives a red pony from his father as a gift. He takes care of the pony,but after some time the pony dies. The narrative follows the joys and sorrows felt by the young boy. What I liked about the book was its realism,it doesn't show the kid as idealistic,nor is there any idealistic ending. Steinbeck portrayed raw and unfiltered emotions of childhood.
