Rating: 5/5

This was my take to the dr. appt book for this week. I expected a nice oh the sweet convict comes into the household and the instant attraction turns into love for Kendall and Jacob the sheriff. This was so much more. Jacob's 5 children were a handfull to start with but Kendall comes with some baggage of her own. I never expected to find myself holding my breath to see what happens next. I would recommend this story thumbs up!
