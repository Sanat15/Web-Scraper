Rating: 2/5

Summary: This book is about a wolf that lost his mate because of humans. This wolf is hunting when he stumbles across a wolf pack. One of the females joins him and they start their own pack.

This book could be considered offensive because it is anti-hunting and pro-wolf. Also some parents might be leery of the word mate in the book.

This book is alright. It has great pictures, but not much of a story line. I can see a kid enjoying it, but not loving it.
