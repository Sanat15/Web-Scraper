Rating: 5/5

This is one of those mysteries that keeps you guessing until the very end. Oh, you'll think you have it figured out, more than once, but you don't. -- Deborah O'Nair, a woman who creates crossword puzzles, is murdered, and it's up to the reader to put the clues together to solve her murder. Wonderful mystery! And after you read this one, get a copy of Death by the Reel.
