Rating: 4/5

Superman: Man of Steel was a consistently entertaining read. Sure, there is some old-school shlock in there, but not as much as many other comics from the era.

This one is fairly straightforward. We get the Superman origin story, his first meeting with Batman, a battle with Bizarro Superman, and coming to terms with his extraterrestrial origins. That being said, I do believe this benefits the comic, making it more of a timeless tale. 

I would recommend this book if you enjoy superhero comics, especially if you don't mind reading comics from the 1980s era.
