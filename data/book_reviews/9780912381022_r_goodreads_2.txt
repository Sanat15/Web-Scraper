Rating: 2/5

This was initially recommended by a naturopath I was seeing. The core message was incredibly helpful to my life, but it's encased in deeply "woo" language, meandering myths, and plenty of Walt Whitman and ee cummings quotes... I found it a bit painful and occasionally cringe worthy. I'm not sorry I read it, again the core message has been incredibly helpful, but I most definitely will not do it again.
