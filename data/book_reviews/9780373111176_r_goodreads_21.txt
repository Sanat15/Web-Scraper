Rating: 5/5

Dizzy goes to Castle Haven to help her friend, Christi. On the way, she sees a man skinny dipping and promptly labels him as a Greek god in her mind. Later that day she meets the man and it is Christi's uncle who she has described as a fusty scholar. Christi wants to show how responsible she is so she can get control of her inheritance and thinks that Dizzy's bohemian behavior will work in her favor. Unknown to Christi, Zach has every intention of giving her control of her inheritance. Through half truths and misunderstandings, Zach ends up with the wrong impressions of Dizzy despite falling for her. When Dizzy tries to explain exactly who she is, harsh words are exchanged and Dizzy leaves despite loving Zach. Zach realizes his mistake and chases after her and reveals that he is her favorite historical romance author, Claudia Laurence. They talk honestly and Zach proposes. Dizzy accepts and reveals her last secret of her name, DC (Dizzy) is for Delilah Cleopatra.
