Rating: 3/5

After reading Alcock’s ‘The Mysterious Mr. Ross’, I thought ‘The Sylvia Game’ would be as much or more interesting, going by the synopsis. But that was not the case. Emily and her thoughts are well brought out, esp. where saving money is concerned, or where she suspects her father’s actions and her irritation with him. But the other two boys weren’t likeable. The plot is about Oliver’s make-believe stories and illnesses and the main characters’ versions about the drowning and the painting related incidents. There is no mystery or any supernatural element in the story. 

Did most housemaids take a fancy for gypsies and have their illegitimate offspring?
