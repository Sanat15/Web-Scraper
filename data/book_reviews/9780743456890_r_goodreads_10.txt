Rating: 3/5

This account of an English girls affair and village life in SW France in the nineties , has an authentic feel, is very French , and tinged with sadness. The author , a fluent French speaker, was fleeing a failed marriage and encounters and falls for Luc the artist/ dentist, a classic semi bohemian figure, though quite well off.

There is very little plot, it is a memoir filled with village gossip, sometimes malicious, often very funny, mostly sexual, and bon mots about life. I laughed often, but was reminded how village life can be restrictive and unkind to those marked as outsiders.

They writing is quite lyrical in places, but the description of some of the incidents is occasionally choppy, the narrative doesn’t always flow.

I enjoyed this, it reminded me of my stays in France in the seventies, in the openness about relationships, though I never had the intense involvement she did.
