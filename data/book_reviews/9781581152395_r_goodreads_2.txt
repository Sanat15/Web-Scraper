Rating: 3/5

9-week program on feng shui for prosperity!!!

Each modules covers 9 sections of the house-the bagua. How to take care each spaces properly. 
One thing in common--don't make the space messy WHOOPS!
They includes the reasonings, exercises to excel the chi energy in the house. For example, the mantra, the piggy bank. Clear the front door space to be able to open it w/o getting struck, make your bed everyday.

Did I get any lucky check during these exercises? not really, I haven't tried much of their methods by the way beside [make a bed] and [clear my front door space]. I did get a couple of side hustles, but one was appointed 3 months ago, another one was occurred last week, hence I don't know if it has a significant correlation since the sample size was too small (can't resist my statistics head lol).

In addition, the last section kinda concluded an interesting principle with an acceptable logic. 
A good read for those who wanna jump into the art of feng-shui for prosperity.
However, if you wanna learn more on Feng-Shui, there are much more principles to this.
This book was just an entry to feng-shui for the readers who want to apply the basic principles to their houses.
