"A Season of Virtues" by Judith A. Lansdowne 1999
 The Earl of Whitshire happen to be an amateur detective. His "hobby horse" as was referred to in the book was collecting murderers. Then enters two of the daughters of Sir Loyal Virtue and his's son who become a distraction. 
 Miss Honor Virtue is intrigued by "Whit" as he is called by his "hobby" friends. 
 Then the plot thickens but humor is not forgotten. This was a very interesting read as it has a plot that has lots of twists and turns. FYI No sex. Happy Reading! ReadingGenie
