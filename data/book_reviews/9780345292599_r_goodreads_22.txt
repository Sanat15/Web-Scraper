Rating: 1/5

This must be one of the worst/most amateurishly written books i have ever come across.
Soulless infodumps, stilted dialogues where characters explain to each other the world they should actually know or retell histories as of course normal people do in casual talks (not!), childish characterisations and so on. This seems written by a 6th grade child. Who published this? Who read this and rated more then 2 stars? I suspect other children...
