What a surprise.... I received a pm from the author, Lisa Higdon! She was surfing the net and came across bookcrossing.com and decided to search for her books... When she found a copy of "Take Heart" on my bookshelf, she pm'd me... it seems we are practically neighbors! What a small world this is. Lisa grew up in this area and still lives here! I just love Bookcrossing... such a friendly, nurturing place. 

 After receiving the pm from Lisa Higdon, I just had to dig through all the boxes of romance novels and bring this one to the top of the heap!

This is story about Halley St. John, a young NYC socialite that loses all by trusting the wrong man and is saved by a little known uncle who dies and leaves his Wyoming ranch to her. I thoroughly enjoyed her adventures in adjusting from life in the New York City social whirl to the hard Wyoming ranch life. Lisa Higdon developed characters exactly as I would have imagined in the frontier.. from the no-good cattle rancher that tries to run her off and steal her land, to the ex-Texas Ranger who steals her heart.

I'll be searching for more of Lisa Higdon's books!
