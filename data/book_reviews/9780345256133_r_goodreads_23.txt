Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed it. After seeing some harsh reviews, I was resolved to read this one. I now get what the reviews were talking about the book being nothing special and I do agree but I don't think it's quite as bad as it's made out to be. While it's not on the level of authors acclaimed in this genre, it was still well done. It's a great choice for a read if you want a cool down after reading something that roused you up.

As for my rating, I have rounded it up to a 4 but I would say it's about a 3.5. It has a lot of potential though and it would do extremely well with a well rounded editing and maybe adding a few extra pages.
