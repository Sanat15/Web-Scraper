Rating: 3/5

This is the French re-issue of the Manual of Saint Germain des Prés, and it's not as good as the first editon, due to the fact that a lot of the illustrations and images were taken out - I presume for budget reasons.

My edition in English is fully illustrated and it's quit a remarkable book. The text alone is superb. Vian writes about the social life of Saint-Germain-des-Prés and the leading characters that take part in the playtime that takes place in the nighttime. Essential book regarding Paris life in 1949.
