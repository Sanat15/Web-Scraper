Rating: 4/5

Melanie Stokes was adopted after mysteriously appearing in a hospital without her memories. For twenty years she has led an idyllic life - but everything comes crashing down when it comes out that she could be the daughter of the man who murdered the Stokes's first daughter.

This was a riveting read, with strongly sketched characters and oodles of tension. I loved the characters of Melanie and David, and how they worked together to solve the mystery. There's a good amount of family drama that's balanced well with the crime elements. However, I felt the explanation to the mystery fell flat in some places when all was revealed.
