Rating: 4/5

Sabon and Pierce’s books were the best!!

I loved how Gretchen never shut up about her feelings and was so brave and honest. Plus what Sabon did to her with Brianne….my heart broke for her but she dealt with it like the queen of the desert that she is!!!

I was just a little disappointed that Sabon didn’t have to fight to much to win her back, after what happened I was expecting more but I still enjoyed.
