Rating: 4/5

A visually enchanting book of some of Norman Rockwell's greatest works including some of his covers for the Saturday Evening Post and his interpretation of The Four Freedoms, which were, rather bizarrely, rejected by the War Department even though Rockwell offered them for nothing but which were to thrill the Saturday Evening Post readers to such a degree that over 20,000 of them wrote to the artist praising his efforts.

There is also an unacknowledged brief biographical and critical essay that adds flesh to the bones.
