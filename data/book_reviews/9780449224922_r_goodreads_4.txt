I must have read this twice before I read it now, but since it's only an odd 150 pages I probably think of it and less than a real book. Lord Pargeter marries his housemaid and then after a couple of months expires leaving Lady Pargeter a very rich lady. But not far away another lady thinks something havey cavey has gone on ensuring that all that money and land was stolen from her. Then Jane Landsdown is turned off her post because the music master was caught kissing her; she shows up to visit her aunt Lady Pargeter which makes the neighbor lady even more sure the whole thing was planned. Lord Fenwick is sent to check out Lady Pargeter and Jane.
