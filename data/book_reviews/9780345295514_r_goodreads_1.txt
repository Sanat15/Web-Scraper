Rating: 5/5

This book was a wild card pick for me and man did it pay off. 

A thousand years after the modern world we know ended, people across (what was) the USA are now clustered together in small communities, towns and in some cases have become nomads. Each is so spread out that many have never heard of one another, and most societies are back in middle ages-tech.

Stel is a man born to a matriarchal society which encourages very strict/strong women ruling with iron fists- men are basically items/maids. He hates it (understandably so) even though he's married to a woman from one of the most notoriously cruel families. Her folks try to kill him, and Stel thinks "welp, time to go be an outcast then". Initially his wife is a passive watcher to this, but the idea of letting her husband abandon her is enough to make her chase after him.

This is a journey of two people. While Stel journeys to the fabled west coast, he meets a very wide array of peoples and has to think quickly and creatively to get out of sticky situations. Ahroe, his wife, meets just as many peoples and challenges, her path crossing Stels here and there, getting so close to seeing one another in a few cases! Both Stel and Ahroe grow as characters, ending off far better than they began.

I came into this book having never read anything by this author or the first book of the bunch, so I was basically blind here, and this didn't infringe much on my enjoyment. The first few chapters were hard since I didn't know some of the history/places, but after that it was smooth. The writing is very well done, the use of rhyme was fun, and I liked the characters a lot. It's become one of my top reads, and can see myself re-reading it later on. 

If you're like me and feel tired of reading thick, high-fantasy, political intrigue, trilogy/13+ book series and want a simple, fast but well done read, pick this one up.
