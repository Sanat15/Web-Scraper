Rating: 3/5

It’s funny to read a novel in which Rumpole comes out of retirement that was written in 1980, seeing as how Mortimer would continue to write Rumpole for another quarter-century or so. However, I feel that Rumpole works better in the shorter form: in a novel, even a short novel, the format gets stretched a bit beyond what it can bear. In theory, this book is a murder mystery, but Mortimer pads it out with a couple of additional cases and rather more office drama — very few of Rumpole's coworkers are excited about him coming back out of retirement — than usual. I think it would have made more sense as multiple stories, but apparently it’s based on a standalone TV special and I guess Mortimer wanted to have a standalone novel as well? At any rate, it has the usual ingredients — domestic drama, legal office drama, and mystery solving — plus Rumpole’s dry sense of humor, so it’s perfectly readable, even if the structure is a bit incoherent.
