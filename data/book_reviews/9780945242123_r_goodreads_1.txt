Rating: 5/5

The author has beautifully portrayed his life incidents of his noble Love between him (disciple) and his Master. It's a great revealing of the essence and beauty of Pure Love. The author with his soulful expressions tells us about his growing love and understanding of his Master, a uniquely simple yet spiritually profound man. 

In his narration he tells: "Here he was the Master in every sense of the term, prepared to prove his contention or point by practical demonstration rather than by verbose discussion. I had a demonstration of this trait in him when someone asked about a particular spiritual state. Master smiled and answered "I cannot explain it to you but, if your samskaras will permit it, I can give you the experience of that condition."......

While reading this book I really liked the revealing idea about love when he writes: "Love is a Godly or Divine thing, and therefore not to be spurned. Love is to be diverted to its proper and natural object, God! What the human individual is required to do is to divert his mind so that the love in the heart can be diverted to its real goal."...... Every time when I read this lovely book it reveals new and ennobling meanings!!!! A GREAT &amp; UNIQUE LOVE STORY:)
