Rating: 2/5

This was just ok. Nothing in it made me upset, except for when the H kissed the h while engaged to someone else. He didn't love his fiancee, but that shouldn't matter. The first time the MCs kissed, the h doesn't know the H is engaged, so it wasn't her fault. However the second time they kissed, she did know about his unavailable status but was a little tipsy and asked him to kiss her. In my mind that's still cheating. I know that's pretty mild in terms of cheating, but I'm a stickler for those things, I guess. The romance was slow burn and sweet, but the characterization was weak and the plot was predictable. The twist at the end did surprise me a little, but it wasn't enough for me to remember this story. However, I appreciate the author making an attempt to stay true to the language of this time period (instead of another book that shall not be named). The multiple povs were enjoyable as well, even if the author didn't indicate a change in perspective.
