Rating: 2/5

Clean Historical Mystery with romance which starts out with a Miss Phyllida Dearne being shaken awake after being drugged and this becomes a theme, because she gets drugged, knocked out and reawaken by Captain Lord Ingram a few times in this story.
Phyllida is in a bad situation but not the worse because she is a poor relation to some rich powerful titled English folks because she is too young and good looking to be hired as a companion or governess. So Phullida makes lemonade with her lemons, she is smart and good so she makes to the perfect match to our hero, Lord Ingram. There is a lot of sneaking murder with too many suspects, but it was a fun mystery to read that was well plotted. A best example of historical mystery/romance would be the The Lady Emily Omnibus (Books 1-4): And Only to Deceive, A Poisoned Season, A Fatal Waltz, Tears of Pearl or Silent on the Moor.
Benjamin Frake of Bow Street was a good detective, but I seriously doubt that the rich powerful lords would have been so respectful to a similar lowborn glorified policeman as in this story.
