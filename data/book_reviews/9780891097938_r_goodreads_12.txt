Rating: 4/5

This book is not a literal translation of Christian scripture; it is a paraphrase rendered in street language. Despite its subtitle, there’s nothing contemporary about the language of this New Testament paraphrase. First published in 1993, its language and syntax are more common to the 1970s. The idiosyncratic jargon may be a stumbling block to readers under fifty years old. 

Peterson’s folksy approach works better on the letters than the gospels and Acts of the Apostles. His direct, powerful phraseology gives new perspective on Biblical concepts. It’s an acquired taste.
