Rating: 3/5

As the "only comprehensive guide to Colorado's oldest cemeteries," From the Grave consists of almost 400 graveyards across the state and over 1000 life stories of the pioneers. Unlike most graveyard guides, it also contains maps and directions.

From the Grave is packed with information about the hard labor and wild antics of the people who stopped in the middle of the Western migration. If you have an interest in Colorado history, this book will fascinate you for hours. Unfortunately, what it lacks is more focus on the graveyards themselves, particularly descriptions of the monuments of the pioneers. The book contains very few photographs, which tend toward portraits of the deceased. However, there are a couple of memorable images, including the family gathered on the church steps around the open coffin for a Pueblo funeral and the funeral pyre of Preston Porter, burned to death by a mob in 1900.

Other than several trips flying in to Denver, I've never had an opportunity to explore the rest of Colorado. With this book in the glove box, one could make a thorough exploration of these fragile historic sites before winter or vandals destroy them. If you go, take pictures!
