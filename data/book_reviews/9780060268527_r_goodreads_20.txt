Rating: 5/5

Copyright 1960. I never realized the illustrator was married to the author! This story I bought for my kids. I had not read it as a child. I LOVE Harry!

 This is a great early reader. It will make kids feel grown up, as it's a chapter book. Harry has a problem with the lady next door with her singing. Very shrill &amp; high. He comes up with many inventive ways to get her to sing lower. This story has a good sense of humor &amp; my kids loved it!
