Rating: 4/5

Operation Excalibur by William H. Keith is the seventh book in the Saga of the Grey Death Legion and the thirty-first book in the BattleTech Universe. In this one, Grayson Death Carlye has been stripped of his command of the Grey Death Legion and his title and holdings on Glendary. Unknown except to his closest friends and family, he is going undercover in the enemies' camp and will soon be in a battle to end their nefarious plans for Katrina Steriner, Achron of the Lyran Alliance. The Grey Death Legion will also be called upon to fight this battle. A great book in this series.
