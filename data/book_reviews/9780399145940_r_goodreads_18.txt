Rating: 5/5

OUTSTANDING!

With the ability to self publish, anyone can put a book out there. Some of those are well written. Most are not. 
John Nance is a traditionally published author who not one writes superbly well, but his encyclopedic knowledge of aviation is evident throughout the novels he has written. To say it in simple terms... the man knows his stuff. 
"Blackout" is a gripping tale I found impossible to put down. I tried several times to set my kindle aside, but within ten minutes, I'd pick it up to see what happens next. 
Darn it, John, this one cost me lost sleep. It's that good!
