Rating: 3/5

I have no clue what to even put in a review because this was so off the wall! It was funny and entertaining at certain points but it was just so chaotic! Did not feel particularly romantic. I feel like labeling this as a regency romance is misleading.

One specific thing I disliked was the emphasis on how she was chubby at the beginning and then got thinner and “more beautiful”. It was mentioned multiple times and felt like unnecessary fat shaming.
