Rating: 3/5

"The Barons of Texas: Tess" is the story of Tess and Nick.

Heroine is the oldest of three Baron siblings- sisters who are fiercely competitive with one another due to their upbringing. Our heroine, the oldest, manages drilling for oil rigs. On her birthday, a mysterious stranger enters her life and there's instant attraction. He's an archeologist with an usual request, and not taking no for an answer. The heroine soon finds herself kinda kidnapped and meeting his family, and despite initially refuses, gives into his wishes. But have they fallen in love?

Sweet love story with likable MCs and explosive chemistry. Wished we would have seen more of the sibling interactions- but I really liked the hero's family.
