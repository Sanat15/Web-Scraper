*** 4.65 ***

I realize that I might have a Terry Pratchett bias. Very little this man could write that I wouldn't love. It has to do with the style, humor and those moments of self deprecation that some of the characters experience, which hit me straight in the heart every time! I loved everything about this book specifically. Our favorite Weezzard with the luggage was reunited with our foreign do-gooder who has the most earnest and inexplicable faith in him! And The Silver Horde gets involved, so Death gets to make an appearance for some of their sake... Honestly, I thought it was too short! I could have stayed with them much, much longer... ❤️😃👍
