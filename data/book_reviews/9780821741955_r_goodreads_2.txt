Rating: 5/5

This has gone straight into my favourites pile, I've loved all Clara's zebra gothics and this one was no different, this was perhaps my favourite, a wonderful heroine and a wonderful hero, love them both and their love story was beautiful with plenty of added angst due to the pain and healing they go through when falling in love due to the tragedy in Ramon's past and present. Ramon being blind adds much to the story and to the development of their bond and romance, I loved it. I loved Rebecca wanting him to have his sight back so much over how she wanted his happiness more than anything while at the same time being terrified that she would lose him if he sees how plain she really is and not the beautiful woman he thinks she is, of course we know how this will turn out and we love it. Just wonderful overall, side characters were all interesting and served their purposes. Another well wrote gothic from Clara Wimberly, the setting in mid 19th century St Augustine, Florida was lovely too, so much so that after looking up pictures of it I've put it on my travel wish list.
