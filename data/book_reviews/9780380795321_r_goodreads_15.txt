Rating: 3/5

The first time I came across Richard Montanari's books were when I read The Rosary Girls which was excellent. I liked this book but found the various characters quite hard to follow (and remember) but perhaps that was just me.
The story and writing was good and I definitely liked the little twists he put in, which I did not expect at all. I enjoy stories which do this.

Not the worst book I have read but would probably be more suitable as a holiday / pass the time read.
