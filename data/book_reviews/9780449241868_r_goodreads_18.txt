Rating: 3/5

Rough going

After reading many, many glowing reviews, I picked this book for the small middle-school class I was teaching on ancient history. Wow that was a mistake. The writing style of this book has absolutely no flow. Most of the students gave up on reading it, with only a few finishing it. I read it along with them and also really struggled with it. 

I liked the historical aspect of it, but it was incredibly hard going to get through it.
