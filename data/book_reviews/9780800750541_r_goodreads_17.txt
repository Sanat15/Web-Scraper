I was reading this with my husband aloud, and on several occasions I got very "worked up," as he would put it, because I took issue with the theological filter through which the authors viewed history. Seemed to me they were essentially saying God did really dreadful things in the name of the greater good, which I think slanders God and is inconsistent with His character in scripture. (He's GOOD, all the time!) My husband disagreed and didn't see the book that way at all, so it's possible the text never says that and I just read between the lines to arrive at this conclusion (though I don't think so!) At any rate, we haven't touched the book in months and have moved on to another one, so I think it's safe to say we won't finish this one.
