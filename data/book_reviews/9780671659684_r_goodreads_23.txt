Rating: 3/5

3rd Pike book down and still having a good time. While I would honestly say I enjoyed all the build up and character development more in the first book in this trilogy , The Dance is still a fun read. 
 The characters are all fun even tho Pike does pull the cliché cards with the two characters of color. The mystery building up still has my intrigued and I’m excited to jump into the final book in this trilogy.
