Rating: 3/5

You can still buy this 1990 "blue" hymnal new, but I'm not sure why. It was the "flagship" pew hymnal of the Presbyterian Church - USA (PC-USA) from the early 1990s to 2013, when the new GLORY TO GOD hymnal debuted. I can't honestly say I'm all that sorry to see it go: it was well-sewn with good thick pages and especially generous margin space, but the hymns themselves were almost always limited to only three or four verses and the four-part harmonies often verged on the banal. Verdict: A good book, a so-so hymnal. For about the same money consider GLORY TO GOD instead. Note also that GLORY contains over 800 hymns, this one about 600.
