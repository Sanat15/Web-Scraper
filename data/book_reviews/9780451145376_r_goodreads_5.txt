Rating: 3/5

This book is about an arranged marriage between an American plantation owner and an English Lady immediately before and during the American Revolution. Alan is American and his father insists he marries a nice English girl in order to get control of the family plantation. Barbara is the daughter of an impoverished Earl who needs to marry well in order to get her family out of debt. There is a lot of historical detail and follows the couple throughout the revolution. 

My biggest issue with the book is the hero has an affair during the war. Although we did get to hear the story from his point of view and know he did feel guilty, I did not feel he expressed his regret to Barbara. He just expected her to forgive and forget. I think he should have been more understanding and worked harder to apologize.
