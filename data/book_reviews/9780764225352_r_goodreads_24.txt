Rating: 4/5

well that was a good book!!! i wouldn't say this was a 5-star experience, but definitely a bit more than 4, maybe like 4.5!! i think the author really improved with each book because i noticed the things that killed the 5-star experience for me less and less (granted, those things are still there but the writing has improved quite a bit!) 

i really enjoyed this story, and i could find myself easily connecting to elizabeth's struggles, and as usual, she rises to the occasion with grace. although the only issue i have is right when she finds her footing in a new place, they move somewhere else and she starts over doing the same exact thing... granted im very aware this is how things worked with the "mounties" back then, but sometimes it DID feel like "oh great, we're going to do this AGAIN" so that's my only small complaint, but its still a great story and definitely worth the read!!! i cant wait to read the rest of the series but i cant believe im almost done with it, either.
