Rating: 4/5

This is a very sweet slice-of-life story about characters whose lives seem very real, due to the wonderful writing of Terry Moore. What struck me about them is that Moore manages to make the silly parts of life very real, and make the serious parts serious without making them melodramatic. 
The ad agency subplot was hysterical, and it amazes me that this series hasn't yet been adapted for a show on cable tv. It's rich and quirky, sweet and rowdy all at once.
