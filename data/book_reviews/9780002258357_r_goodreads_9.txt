"Prospero's Children" - written by Jan Siegel and published in 1999 by Del Rey. "Something was lost, long, long ago, before the beginnings of history: few remain who would recognize it, fewer still who would know the secret of its use." Fern Capel, sixteen years old through all the times and scenes in this fantasy, has been chosen to find this relic and complete a mission vital to the world. Along the way she finds valuable help from others - her younger brother Will, the Watcher Ragginbone and his wolf-dog Lougarry, and those who recognize her Gift. "Standing on the doorstep he resembled more than ever a wandering tramp, vaguely disreputable, mildly insane, a shaggy man with his shaggy mongrel roaming the countryside in search of a free supper or a lost ideal." And of course there are serious, very serious, villains, determined to bring Fern down. This was a cool, dreamy, flowing story that eventually landed in Atlantis near its downfall. Very imaginative and descriptive!
