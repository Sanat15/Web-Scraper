Rating: 5/5

I usually try to start from the beginning but I listened to "Night Life" which is book 3, years ago. A few months ago I borrowed book number 5. This is book 2 and it was way to long! Almost 25 hours on Audible. I would have liked it better if it was half as long.. I even skipped some parts when we were following "the Lion". I didn't need to know exactly how he got from Washington to California. 

I've been carrying my tablet around to listen to this book for 4 days. Since I've already listened to book 5 I thought I knew what would happen in this book but there were some unexpected things. 

After hearing how lax security was at smaller airports I really hope they have fixed that problem! Things changed 19 years ago. When I first came to the US in 2000 my boyfriend was able to walk all the way up to the gate without a ticket. 

I've got Night Life on CD so I'm going to listen to book 3 again.
