Rating: 3/5

I have been looking forward to this one for a long time. I fell in love with this story after watching the American movie...and then many years later I really enjoyed the original novel. This manga is based on the Japanese film screenplay (I have not seen that movie version of the story) and it seems like a mix between the original novel and the American movie...and I'm guessing it follows the Japanese film pretty closely. 

I'm sad to say that this was just ok for me. I didn't feel like it brought anything new or different to the story in this medium and I feel like the art was just ok...not spooky enough or creepy enough to add anything to the story. I'm still looking forward to reading the others in this series...hoping they will add something new and engaging to the story.
