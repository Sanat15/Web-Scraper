Rating: 2/5

A lost potential. Thats what the whole Sailor V series is. 
We have three volumes telling exactly the same "monster of the week" story. We have childish Minako who's falling in love again and again. She's silly, she's young, she's making the same mistakes over and over again, and there's almost no character development. This could work in one volume, not three. 
Volume two and three could have told us a different story. The story of Minako working with the police. The story of Minako finally growing up, both as a person and as a sailor guardian. I wish we could learn what made Minako the way she is when she shows up in the Sailor Moon series. She's a leader then. She acts as a guide for the rest of the girls. She knows her responsibilities. She's more serious. She's playing the role of princess Serenity to protect Usagi.
Instead there's a big gap, a whole story left unsaid. The Minako we met in the Sailor V series is not ready to be the guide or the leader. And I believe this story could be much more interesting if we could see her becoming the person she's in the Sailor Moon series.
