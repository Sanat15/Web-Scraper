Rating: 2/5

This is not my favorite Lewis story; and it might be my least favorite of his. 

But, he has some interesting and thought-provoking messages in here; one of them being on sex and the female protagonist finally understanding it and its intention. 

It’s good, but the story is a slog and you can turn to his other works, like The Abolition of Man, for some good philosophizing instead. 

Ages: 18+

Content Considerations: d*mn and h*ll are used throughout. Bl**dy might have been used once or twice. Drinking, drunkenness, and smoking are mentioned. There is a vision and a woman’s breasts are seen. There is quite a bit about the marriage bed, the feminine role, lust, sex (without using the word sex), all mentioned in a kind of teaching tone. There are a couple gruesome parts with blood, severed heads, etc.
