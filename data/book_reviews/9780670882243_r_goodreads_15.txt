Rating: 5/5

If you’ve seen the film “The Florida Project”, this is in the same vein. Heartwrenching, disturbing, and constructed of such raw and beautiful artistry that it will live in your brain forever.

I pulled this book off the shelf of my local library at a time when a card catalog still graced one of the walls. I don’t know what struck me to pull it out of its comfortable tuck on the shelf, but whatever it was I am permanently grateful. I kept it with me at all times because I would read at any opportunity, including stoplights. After I read it, I read it again. The prose was so plain yet so beautiful that I was shook. I was in awe of the ability to write this way, not just the prose but the story building. 

This story will not warm your heart. It will, however, drag you through the emotional gamut, and push your brain to question how you might be able to understand things that aren’t understandable.

I wish Lisa kept writing. She is a genius.
