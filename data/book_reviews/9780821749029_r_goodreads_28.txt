Rating: 4/5

5 stars for book 1 + 3 stars for book 2 = 4 stars total

So I'm a reader who loves dialogue and character heavy stories so the fist part (or "book") of this story was fun and exciting with the banter and the in-your-face heroine. She's that heroine who fell in love with her older neighbor and went around saying she'll marry him someday as a child, but then her childhood crush turned into a woman's love as she got older. I had a little hard time with this in the beginning because I thought she would be one of those starry-eyed girls who saw nothing but her "love" for the hero even though he hasn't been around as she grew older. But Ranney did a great job of showing me that this heroine went into it with open eyes and was ready to fight for her love- like a mature woman. The hero was lovely, even to the bitter end.. oh just swoon-worthy. We get to fall in love with the heroine's crush-turned-love right along with her.

The second part of this story  where there is a separation between the hero and heroine  was super emotional and a very interesting read, but had more inner dialogue then I normally like.  I say interesting because usually when a hero and heroine separate we don't get to go on an emotional ride with them, we're usually left to imagining what happened during that time and just accepting the character's feeling rather than feeling it with them. Whereas here we got the chance to go on the journey. I felt really sad for our heroine and felt really connected to her as she went through the worst time in her life.  . However emotional the roller coaster was though I did feel like it was dragged out a little too long. It's a double-edge sword: spend time conveying the extreme emotions of the character versus losing your audience when it comes a little to long. Ah whatever I'm being picky because book 2 meant less romance, but I guess the ending truly did make up for it:)
