Rating: 1/5

It is quite an academic book demonstrating barriers faced English learners and teachers to leap from levels to higher levels. It provides other languages experts' opinions and their psychological perspectives on how to accelerate the process of language acquisition in a scientific approach. When reading deeply and delving in the elaborate explanation of linguistic activities, it is pretty clear that nobody has found a proved academic methodology to augment English language skills of the learners.
Despite the fact that English is the most required asset in getting employed for instance; other languages teaching is more successfully applied and conducted than English.
