Rating: 3/5

Ok...well, I saw that coming at the end. Seriously?? I am normally okay with a Pappano read, but this one pushed the boundaries of even romance-book land plot lines and twists and turns.

Except for that one part that was a tad unbelievable to me-don't want to give it away-the rest was a pretty good read. I enjoyed the interaction between the two main characters, and the heroine's discovery of self. It was a long, emotional road to get there, despite the book only being 251 pages.
