Rating: 4/5

3.5 stars

Great beginning. I love a witty hero and this hero delivered on that front. Some very cute bantering took place between these two. The heroine was one of the best spinster aunts in a romance that I've read. I fell in love with how she treated her niece and nephew. What stop this book from receiving a higher rating was the secondary romance with her niece. I had to skim those pages because I had no interest in it. I've never enjoyed secondary story lines. It only takes away from the story I want to read about. This was a short book and needed depth and development to substantiate the romance. The secondary story only took away from that. It could have been a great read with just a little more substance. All in all, it was still a cute fluff read that I enjoyed for what it was.
