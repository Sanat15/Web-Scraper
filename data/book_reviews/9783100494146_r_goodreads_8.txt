Rating: 3/5

I adore Reinhold Messner and this is another good book from him, an account of his journey across Antarctica. I learned so much about this forgotten continent and it's history. It was very interesting to read about Reinhold's difficult journey through the ice, there are so many challenges and things that I had no idea about. 

My criticisms of the book are that 1) it sometimes seemed to contradict it self and 2) the pacing dragged at times.

Nevertheless I'm glad I read it and learned a lot. I recommend it to anyone who wants to learn more about Antarctica.
