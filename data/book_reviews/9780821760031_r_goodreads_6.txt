Rating: 4/5

A charming tale with Spanish influences. A man reeling from loss and war and his childhood friend whose family oversaw his plantation. It was a quick read with nice charming characters you really wanted to get to know and an adorable little girl as well, who should get her own story when she grows up within a few years. You get to learn a few things about the wine business and a few words in Spanish that you may or may not have learnt before. Ruíz Navarro and Julia made a believable couple and even though there were a few obstacles in their way, they really did belong to one another. So if you get a chance pick up a copy and enjoy.
