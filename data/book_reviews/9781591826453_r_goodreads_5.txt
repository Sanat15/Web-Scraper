Rating: 3/5

I've approached this after completing Love Hina, Vol. 01, so I compared the two as I went. The approach is more romantic and mild, less blatantly humorous/absurd (and thus less repetitive), it has this flavor of tradition and old money/Japan, which makes the random lewd illustrations in between chapters the weirder. In the end it's another harem manga where people stumble and pull down somebody's clothes, with an unusual pet running around, but I like how the protagonist usually gets motivated to grow for his partner's sake.I was curious about how the darker tones would develop, physical abuse and arranged marriages, but it's kind of left at that. I was wondering if Aoi would ever step out of her "born and forged to be a housewife" identity, but that wasn't explored, and the manga ended with the characters barely into adulthood, so good for her I guess.
