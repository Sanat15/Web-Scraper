Pohl shifts gears a bit on the sequel to Gateway. We start with a small family/crew on a mission to a Heechee food factory, as they run across a child of mysterious origin. Later we find out Rob is still around and helped finance this mission. And that’s all I want to say about the plot. I really enjoyed this sequel, even though we don’t get a resolution to the ending of Gateway yet. This book actually opens up more mystery of the Heechee and the fate Rob. Loved it, on to book 3.
