Rating: 4/5

What a potential! I loved the idea for a romance. The characters were chosen brilliantly too.

And, although Charion Chesney didn't spoil it, her books' pattern (mostly their length) didn't allow her to make much of the idea.

It wasn't a typically witty story. So I can't compare it to some charming novels by the author (e.g. from the series: The Six Sisters or Poor Relation or The School For Manners or standalone like: The Marquis Takes a Bride or Miss Fiona's Fancy).

[4-4.5 stars]
