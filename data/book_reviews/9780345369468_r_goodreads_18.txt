Rating: 5/5

This book was buried in a box in my closet, and I dug it out before Christmas. It has been a hefty and satisfying story, rich in history as it details the beginnings of Kentucky. I loved how the author followed several families through the journey from North Carolina to Fort Boonesborough. I love the amazing descriptions of the land they traveled through and all that they experienced in the course of their new lives.
For those that follow my reviews, this is not a clean Christian book. I would give it an R rating if it were a movie.
