Rating: 3/5

This book needs a proofreader to change things like "the soul of her shoe" to sole. There are verb tenses that don't match, etc. 
Although it has no explicit sex, there are descriptions that border on it. They add nothing to a Christian novel. The parts that do mention God seem to be thrown in randomly so that the author could add Christian to the descriptors. 
The plot was really good, and it flowed well.
