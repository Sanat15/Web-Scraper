Rating: 5/5

An ode to the highest quality research in the under-represented passions that have shaped Western culture but always through the lens of the Other. This collection smooths out the differences between that have for too long been considered a fetished conception of lust and desire. The overwhelming humanity should make the reader quiver in the most erotic sense. Western culture needs more of what has rumbled beneath what it has been so unwilling to reveal. Brilliant. Necessary. Black. Proud. Robust. Frequent.
