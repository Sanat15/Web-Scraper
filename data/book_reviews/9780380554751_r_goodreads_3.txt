Rating: 5/5

this is a fascinating insider’s view of a complicated artist. françoise gilot was a 21 year old painter when she met picasso who was forty years her senior. she then spent ten turbulent years with him and bore two of his children. gilot was the only woman to leave picasso and it is fascinating to read how she was intertwined with his art. she captures time and place well and her memory is remarkable as she describes interactions with picasso’s many friends: matisse, gertrude stein, giacometti, sartre, chagall, and others. she uses vivid language to describe how art brings the two of them together and how their approach to art and life informs their relationship. part of the experience of this book is looking up gilot’s paintings and learning the stories behind picasso’s well-known work. i found it difficult to read the legion of ways picasso emotionally manipulated gilot and treated his inner circle. he was casually cruel, small minded, and a petty man. the value of his work is undeniable but he mistreated the women in his life and those closest to him in terrible ways. and yet gilot somehow remains dispassionate in her telling of the years she spent with picasso. 

love, love, loved that gilot moved on to live a successful and full life after leaving him.
