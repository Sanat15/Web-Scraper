Rating: 3/5

There are a lot of reviews about this one already so I won't go into detail - but I found it quite sweet (in theory), but repetitive. At the beginning it is clear that the hero is in love with the heroine, and vice versa, but it is over half way through that they suddenly both acknowledge to themselves that they think it is more than lust. Hmm.
Also, it's very well done in a chemistry sense, but the story drags as it always seems to be the heroine putting herself out there for the hero. Yes, he had hang ups, but it just went on and on and on... She literally had to scream her love for him in front of everyone at the end for him to see it, despite bending over backwards through the whole book for him. 
3 stars (and some cringing).
