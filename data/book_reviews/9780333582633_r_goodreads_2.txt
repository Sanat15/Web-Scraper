Rating: 4/5

I finally read Seeds of Change: Six Plants That Transformed Mankind, a book I was given years ago because of my love of food history and world history. It’s incredibly well researched and detailed, and while I’m sure I didn’t retain everything, I found every chapter compelling.

It starts so positively with quinine — a plant that saved countless lives — and then moves into the much sadder histories of plants tied to human devastation: sugar and cotton, built on slavery; tea, intertwined with the British forcing opium on China; coca (need I say more?); and the potato, bound up with profound suffering in Ireland.

I’m really glad I finally read this book — enjoyed while drinking my favorite green tea!!
