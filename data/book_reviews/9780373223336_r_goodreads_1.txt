Rating: 3/5

The Eyes of Derek Archer by Vickie York is a great read. This book is well written and Ms York has supplied us with plenty of suspense, intrigue, fantastic characters and action. The Eyes of Derek Archer is a fast paced page turner and a fairly quick read. A perfect choice for those with limited time for reading. I enjoyed The Eyes of Derek Archer and look forward to reading more from Vickie York in the future. This is a complete book, not a cliff-hanger.

I bought a copy of this book at a library sale.
