Rating: 4/5

A disgusting tory whose hateful views were a less savory expression of the misanthropic, dour and essentially frightened attitude that allowed Larkin to write verse of legitimately heart-rending perceptiveness and lyrical beauty on the sour lot of living. So much of me rebels against him, but the spirit of melancholy I so often find myself attuned to had in Larkin an advocate of genius, something I can't ignore, even as I wish I could. May he rot, as we all must, more or less deservedly, and may his work at least console those who suffer life and death as he did, while rising above the sad and destructive temptations of hateful prejudice to which he succumbed.
