Rating: 4/5

Endlich! After having popped into and out of this, a widely used German harmony textbook, for well over a year, I have finally finished it. De la Motte's "Harmonielehre" is not as good as his "Kontrapunkt," but it nonetheless offers a swift, enjoyable, and honest introduction to the study of harmony. His way of naming chords is pretty goofy; and, while succinctness is usually a virtue, certain chapters really don't go into enough detail for readers to successfully complete the exercises he assigns. But we sure don't write'em like this over here, and I sure wish we did.
