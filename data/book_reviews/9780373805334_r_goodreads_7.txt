Rating: 4/5

What the heck, I was going 3.5 then decided to up it to 4 as I really liked the last third. I just liked them, cracking on with it, in spite of their respective fiance(e)s running off together. I didn't much care for this set up, I'll be honest but I warmed to calm, practical Stefanie and rather hot Quinn. Good sexual tension and, on a miserable cold dark autumn night, an idyllic Pacific island setting beautifully described without overdoing it. I like DC a lot.
