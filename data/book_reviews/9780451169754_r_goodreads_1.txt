Rating: 2/5

I should have been forewarned when I saw a spelling error in the first paragraph that there was going to be an editing issue with this romance. If misspelled words bother you stop here because this book contains numerous ones.

Elizabeth Newmarch was dismissed from a teaching position when she came upon the good fortune of becoming employed as a lady's companion to the Earl of Warminster's Aunt Honora. I empathized with the heroine trying to move among nobility. When they finally meet Warminster comes off as spoiled, rude and boring. His aunt came in second place with her meddling and shallowness. She never married but she expected her nephew to do the deed to continue the succession of heirs. Of course, Honora didn't like any of the other family members. It didn't matter how Warminster felt about the institution.

There was another gentleman who had feelings for Elizabeth long before Warminster acknowledged how he felt. In addition, there was a foppish dandy that played the villain. Though not a blueblood Elizabeth's demeanor was far above the others. I kept hoping the romance would get better and if this wasn't a Signet Regency I would have given up on it long before the end. I am giving it two stars for 'OK' and that is generous but I still can't recommend it.
