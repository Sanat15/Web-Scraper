Rating: 5/5

Rita Mae Brown at her best. A story about some memorable characters and the small and big events in their lives. While sharing their tales, Rita Mae touches on life, death, morality, philosophy, politics, humanity, prejudice, acceptance, love and loss. At times irreverent with humor and at other times solemn. The story alternates between past events and the "present". Despite knowing the characters who would not survive until the present. I still found myself falling in love with them, crying at their loss and grieving for the pain of those they left behind. That is how well she brings the characters to life. 

I am so glad there are additional books set in Runnymede so I can visit with these wonderful characters again.
