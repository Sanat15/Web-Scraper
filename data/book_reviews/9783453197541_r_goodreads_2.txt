Rating: 5/5

I loved this book. I didn't intend to read it but it happened to be in the cottage I was renting. Initially, I thought the main character, Peri, was selfish and cruel, until I discovered that she'd been so badly treated as a child, she didn't trust anyone. Douglas (the love interest) saves wild animals and puts them back in the wild. Even though he tries to help them, they are so injured and hurt, they lash out at him. Just like Peri. A great read. I laughed and I cried. I wish there was a sequel.
