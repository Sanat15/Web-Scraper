Rating: 2/5

When a little alien visits earth on Halloween it is mistaken for a trick-or-treater. At the end of the night it follows one of the boys it meets home and the young boy decides to take it to school for show and tell. When the rest of the class mistakes the alien for a robot, they aren't that impressed and the alien decides to go back to his home planet. I thought that for a science-fiction book it was fairly bland because nothing crazy happens besides the alien coming to earth. I expected more to happen but the plot just seemed to drag along. The characters weren't developed but it is a pretty short book so there isn't much time to develop them either way. There wasn't really a major dramatic question that was addressed in the book so the characters never really dealt with conflict. I can see why young children would enjoy this book though.
