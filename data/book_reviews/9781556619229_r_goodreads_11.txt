Rating: 5/5

Let me start by saying, I’m an atheist. I don’t look for books with a god or Jesus as a central theme. When characters are constantly praying for some answer or another, it’s time for me to look for a new book.
But THIS book…I LOVED this book! Ann Tatlock has a way of creating a character that fills in every corner and crevice of who they are. 
You don’t have to be a Christian to understand and enjoy this story, but believing that kindness matters will help you fall in love with it. Five full stars.
