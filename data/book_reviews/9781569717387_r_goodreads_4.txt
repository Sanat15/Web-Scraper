Rating: 1/5

Set after the fourth season of the TV series. 
As college ends for the summer, Buffy and her friends find themselves facing an infestation of demonic insects who leave Riley hospitalised and in a coma.

This is a very boring tale of the Slayer. It's not outright bad, but it's also not the least bit engaging either. 
There's lots of story potential in the concept of demonic insects, but none of it is explored here as the antagonists are basically just there for Buffy to beat up. What are their motivations and where did they come from? Well, if those questions were answered I must've missed it.

I was even less impressed with the character work for the main players here. 
Buffy spends the whole book moping about Riley, a character who I have always hated at the best of times. Tara feels a little bit uncomfortable as the new member of the gang. Giles starts dating a woman who turns out to be... just a woman with no significant role to play in the plot. Spike is... also here.
