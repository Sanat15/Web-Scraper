Rating: 4/5

For Better or For Worse was the kind of reliable comic strip that did the family thing its own way. Since its characters aged with the strip, reading an old collection is getting to catch up on events from before you might’ve started reading. For instance, I don’t remember it from before April entered, and here’s strips from that, and of course Farley (who famously died in the April era) is around, too. Today there’s Sally Forth (which actually started, with different creators, not long after Lynn Johnston began For Better, and has always been a far more bizarre look at family life), but without Johnston there’s arguably a bigger void in the comics section than the one Watterson left. Where Calvin and Hobbes defined friendship and imagination, Johnston nailed the last remnant of an adult’s perspective on life. The Pattersons, their friends and relatives, cycle through a few notable events, including a disastrous camping trip for John and Elly’s brother freaking out over finally getting married. The funniest bit for me is probably Elizabeth getting her ears pierced in the first grade, which is a huge deal for her. Elly frets about kids growing up too quickly, in 2987. Here in 2022 it’s now routine for babies to get their piercings in. Literally babies. Times are always changing…
