Rating: 5/5

Don’t take the time I took to read this book as a reflection of how good it is, it’s a good book. It opens the door to investing to the novice investor. At the same time, it refutes the notion that only the rich have the money to invest. I love the fact that it gives us a formula for investing, but works after we have finished this one. Pick up the book and get started to a new life. This book to me pairs well with The Slight Edge by Jeff Olson. 
Happy reading!
