Rating: 5/5

I loved this book. I had this marked as read, but before now I don't think I'd ever read it. I didn't remember a single thing. 

Aiken does such a great job developing realistic characters who are flawed, but also have their good points and the story is about their growth and change -- within reasonable bounds of their character.

It's so interesting to me how Aiken leaves the most dramatic bits off-stage and even at her most gothik she never gets out of hand, it fits the story, it's believable and it's a friendly nod to the type of novels her characters would have read.

There's also some lovely feminist bits and women characters you can admire and respect and who are fully developed and have something worth saying. So refreshing. I can't recommend this book enough.
