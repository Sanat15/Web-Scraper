Rating: 5/5

The descriptions are misleading. This is not the story of an older man who falls for a young girl. Fortunately, as I generally don't like those. Instead it's the story of two relationships - Sir Gareth who wishes to marry his friend Hester, and Amanda, the young girl who wants to marry Neil, who is temporarily back in England after being injured in Wellington's army. Gareth finds Amanda at an inn, having run away from her firm but doting Grandfather. She refuses to reveal her name and he can't leave her alone, so he takes her to Hester. Amanda keeps running away and getting into trouble and Sir Gareth and sometimes Hester get her out of it. 

I would have liked to see more of Gareth and Hester, as they are one of the best couples in Regency romances. But that's not the story Heyer is telling, so we don't get as much of them. Mostly it's Amanda, who is spoiled and willful but not mean. She needs a firmer hand than she's getting at home, and perhaps to be treated like a person and not a child. So she rebels. 

This is a fun story and the audio is highly recommended. The narrator does a good job with the voices and sparkles when she gets into Heyer's witty dialogue. I like this one more every time I read it. 

NB - Audio Version
I read this book a while ago, but when the audio came up on sale I didn't remember which Heyer it was. I bought it on the strength of great reviews of the narrator. Those reviews are well deserved. I think this might be more fun to listen to than read. (I did speed the narration up a bit, but I frequently do that.)
