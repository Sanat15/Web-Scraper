Rating: 5/5

Great story for the kiddos!

Cute story about habits, both good and bad. It just so happens that my right year old son bites his nails as well and he certainly could relate to Sister Bear. At the end, he mentioned he would like to try and stop, even though I have been trying to get him to stop buying his nails since I could remember. Haha.
Adorable characters, wonderful illustrations and great storyline. It's safe to say my little ones will be taking out more Berenstain Bears books at the library.
