So I've picked this book up in a paperback with beautiful little illustrations - without knowing this was originally a German book. I still read it of course and I'm glad I did. Despite some of Lorenz's claims that are certainly outdated (such as his insisting that most dogs are descendants of Golden Jackals - today a species like the Red Wolf and certainly no Jackal is assumed to be the dogs' ancestors), this book is tremendously enjoyable with many beautiful anecdotes from his life spent with all kinds of animals. This is no "How-to-train-a-dog"-book but more a kind of collection of stories grouped together wonderfully in bite-sized and thematically fitting chapters. This book made me want a dog all the more despite knowing that now is not the right time. Do give it a try if you come across it in the wild - I'll certainly do once I encounter the original text.
