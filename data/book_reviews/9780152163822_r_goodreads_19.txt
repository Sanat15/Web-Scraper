Rating: 3/5

This was a nostalgia reread; very fun! A bit too fast-paced for my adult brain, where I felt like I couldn't fully soak in some of the details, &amp; it felt like I was ping ponging from story line to story line at times, but as a kid I think this was a non-issue. Fun, creative, and a great way to kick off spooky season. Bruce Coville was my favorite author as a kid, and I can't wait to re-read more of his books!
