Rating: 5/5

There are too many great things to say about this book. Out of the three that I've read of the series so far (I skipped over Peter Duck), I feel this one is the strongest. The mixing of new characters with codes and adventure make for an exciting read that also inspired me.

If you want to introduce a friend or relative to these characters but fear the technical sailing talk in 'Swallows &amp; Amazons' is too much, you should try this book. You are sucked right into the history of these kids without repetition of the past (as in found in the Second book, Swallowdale) nor are the stories from the past book integral to the plot. This allows someone who is just introduced to this world to be enveloped into the exploits of these children without feeling they are missing anything.
