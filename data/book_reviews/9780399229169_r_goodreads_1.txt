Rating: 3/5

Alex is on his way to meet his anthropologist parents in the South American jungle. He is in a plane crash and is rescued by a native tribe. He is taken back to their village and experiences life in the rain forest firsthand: strange food, different rituals, even a celebration that turns into a war party;.

Photographs of the Yanomami tribe illustrate this story. Written in journal formats in a “handwriting” font.
