Rating: 4/5

If you can get past the 90's fashion and style , then you will really enjoy the mix of sweaters and cardigans for the whole family in this knitting pattern book.
It is split up into for groups, Baby knits, Clothes conscious kids, family favorites and fashion &amp; flair. The Fashion &amp; flair section is some one of a kind pieces that will probably take you all winter to make on sweater, but it will be original and beautiful.
Lots of mohair (hey! it is published in the 1980-1986 so what else are you expecting, the 80's are the mohair sweater years!). The baby section has a bunch or newborn to child sized sets (pants, sweater/cardi, hat, booties, etc) There is a series of cute animal bonnet hats for winter (in Mohair, of course!).
There is this 'Lightning flash' sweater for adult and child that is straight out of the 1970's disco movie wich is super fun to look at, but no one would actually make it now, but I am so tempted anyway to make a matching one for my husband and son!
Just substitute the mohair for silk, or superwash merino and the patterns can be modernized and wearable (well, maybe not the 'Lightning flash' sweater...)
You might need to change all the color schemes to natural colors or let your imagination make new color combinations to updates these fun patterns.
I loved this book and I'm going to make a few animal winter hats for my children right now.
So fun if you can get your hands on this book because it is a great addition to you 'vintage' section.
