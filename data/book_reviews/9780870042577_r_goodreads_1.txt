"Oregon is more than places. It is people, past and present, history, legend, folklore, breath and stillness in every form within its boundaries. For the storyteller, should a broken-down old cowpoke nursing a lone bottle be less important that a millionaire with a cellar full of good wine? Is Portland more fitting for the printed page than an abandoned village rotting away on the naked plain? . . .

You know better — and that is why I invite your company on this journey." In this little gem of a book the reader will find "The Saga of Silverton Bobbie" a very moving story about a dog.
