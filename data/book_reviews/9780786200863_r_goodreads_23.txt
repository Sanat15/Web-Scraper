Rating: 4/5

Aside from the multitude of suspects and some overly quirky behavior by some of the characters, this was a well written, entertaining book with numerous twists and turns. Miss Sarah Keate, the head nurse and narrator of this story, is an excellent character. The ending was a bit convoluted and the motive behind the skullduggery and murder seemed farfetched. I mean, it's not like you're going to be able to sell Radium at the local bar or flea market. Even so, I'm looking forward to the next book in the series.
