Rating: 5/5

An informative and insightful book. Definitely an eye opener, even for me, and I thought I knew quite a lot about the subject. Though probably no anti-choice/anti-abortionist would ever touch this book with a ten foot pole, if they did one might hope it would open their eyes and minds and help them to understand why it's so very important to give women control over their own bodies. I recommend this book to anyone who wants or needs an understanding on this issue as told by an OBGYN/abortion provider with over 40 years of experience in the field. Filled with real experiences and stories of patients he took care of as well as many a discussion over the politics, emotions, and opposition. Sometimes it's wise to view all sides of the issue before passing judgement. That's what this book does.
