Rating: 5/5

I read this book over a decade ago, and the story still sticks with me today. Although I've had no experience with any of the themes of this story, the author did a fantastic job of bringing the reader into the lives of this grieving family who had lost their three sons - one due to murder and the other two due to AIDS.

The author described the main characters in such a way that I felt I knew them personally by the time I finished reading the book. The emotions were very real as the parents initially had a difficult time accepting their sons temper or sexual orientation, but eventually came to love them just the way they are. 

I love how the youngest son wanted to make a difference and share his experience with others before he passed. It's a touching story that will definitely move you.
