Author/Illustrator: Molly Bang
The Paper Crane, William Morrow &amp; Company, 1985

The Paper Crane is classic collage-cut paper and print. Bang gets a real 3-D quality out of the cut outs. The characters are well rendered, consistent, and show a ton of emotion.
the Crane is rendered in white pastel on brown paper—it is a stunning effect. The story has typical folk elements—kindness to a stranger brings wealth and fame. In Grey Lady, again Bang uses torn paper and mixed media. The "lady" is rendered only on gray paper as face and hands—all else is paper or collage. the book is a wordless allegory with a strange being who follows the lady to steal her berries. The book is an amazing study of negative space.
