Rating: 5/5

This was one of my FAVORITE horsey books as a kid. I loved horses, and I would have been overjoyed to have one of my own (not having even the smallest clue how much work and expense is involved, of course). Most days my imaginary horse friends raced everywhere with me outside vehicle windows. A book about a girl who receives a horse figurine that turns into a real horse? And it talks? And they go on an adventure? SIGN ME UP FOR THIS.

The magically full-sized horse comes with a full-sized attitude and full-sized fears that Jennifer must learn to deal with. She also learns that just because you've read all kinds of books about horses doesn't mean you know the first thing about riding one! (Raise your hand if that was you, because that was totally me.) Jennifer's adventure lasts only one evening but the excitement and wonder of it lasted forever in my imagination.
