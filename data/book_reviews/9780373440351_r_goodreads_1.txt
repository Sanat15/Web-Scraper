Charming and realistic, in a good way, romance. Heading into a 4 star, but fell into a small black hole.

Gee wilikers, who wouldn’t want to give up a boyfriend of three years when you have sex on major holidays. Pretty much ONLY on major holidays.

In contrast, the H while being a widower who loved his wife, is very clear he is ready to move on and move on with the H. He’s a charmer, a sexy hunk, and the moves he puts on the heroine are a combination of too-good-too-be-true and real life. 

The h and H meet cute, kind of as he wants her to redo his house, and she ends up using his kids to get an account. The attraction is immediate between the two. The h is a cute character that falls for the H’s three madcap kids in spite of herself. The hero is very yummy and makes his attraction to the h known

Where did it fall down? The heroine has sex with the hero then goes back to Mr. Stick in the Mud. Don't get me wrong, he’s no prize, but the OM did not deserve that. If I am going to hold my fictional heroes to a moral stand, it must apply to the heroines as well. I could have looked the other way if the heroine had said a sweet sayonara to the other man, but it didn't happen that way.

  The heroine decides to marry the OM, but ends up jilting him nicely at the altar and the H steps in. I don’t find that cute or charming, but hurtful. POINTS OFF!

All in all a nice romance with some flaws.

As far as kids in a story, aces to the author for creating real kids. They were as enjoyable as the two main characters.
