Rating: 5/5

A popular history of the Plantagenets, beginning in the Norman Invasion of 1066 and ending with the signing of the Magna Carta and the death of King John in the early 13th century. The marvellous thing about this book is that it reads like a novel! This was my very first history read, and I am not disappointed. I can’t wait to read the next books in the series, and to discover other history books too.
