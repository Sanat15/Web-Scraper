Rating: 1/5

Charles is a wastrel and needs to convince his aunt that he is going to marry respectably in order to inherit.
Melissa is running away to her grandmother, who unfortunately is away. So she needs somewhere to stay for a few days - she has no money etc. otherwise.
Charles suggest to her that she come with him and pretend to be his fiancee for two weeks, she agrees. OK. That's the setup. A few months later they meet again in town. Charles' aunt has died and specifies that he has to marry his fiancee in order to inherit. Charles goes to town to find her - he doesn't know her real name, and doesn't recognize her when he does meet her. So far so good - good premise.
Unfortunately both characters were just too self-absorbed for me. And Charles, a selfish waste of space, actually matures during the run of the story, but Melissa - not really. She continuously carps about how deceitful he was to pull that stunt - but she conveniently forgets that she played along. Then, when he finds out who she is, he goes overboard. And then she goes overboard. Sheesh. They deserve each other.
