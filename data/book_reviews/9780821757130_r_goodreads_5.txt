Rating: 3/5

You have to go into reading this one knowing that it's fluff, pure and simple. Fluff from the 90's no less.
"Rutherford's Return" by Alana Clayton has a Marquess literally returning... from the dead. He has to come back and help save the reputation of a woman he ruined years ago. Well, not him, his reputation for seducing women helped two other people accuse an innocent woman of being one of his victims. He never knew, until now. And he's very interested in helping to restore her reputation, for reasons of his own.
"The Rake Objects?" by Cindy Holbrook opens with a man in bed, but on his way to ruin the wedding of a woman he doesn't know. But he knows her disgraced brother and the brother is still trying to keep an eye out for her. The sister is about to marry a man to help get funds because her brother squandered the family fortune. He ruins the wedding but also the reputation of the girl as well and he wants to make amends.
In Martha Kirkland's "The Notorious Mr. Fremont," a vicar's sister is dragged along on a country escape when her friend's rich uncle calls a family caucus. The family's black sheep is very interested in the spinster sister.
The last story, "A Halo for Mr. Devlin" by Marcy Stewart has a runaway heiress and a notorious gambler teaming up to stay out of trouble.
