Rating: 4/5

This was the most exciting book in the series.

Gen and her husband, Two Stars, can't escape the prejudice in their neighborhood. Some friends are made, but one man secretly plots revenge for something Indians did years ago.

The thing I like the most about this book is how the characters go through real feelings and struggles, yet in the end they're always pointed back to God, who is the only true hope in the world.

One of the quotes I liked from the book is: 'Elliot Leighton spoke up. "The existence of fool's gold does not mean there isn't real gold in the mountains, Mr. Picotte. And the existence of hypocrites who misuse religion for themselves does not mean there isn't a God in heaven who loves His children and sent His Son to die for them." He stood up and stretched. "Never confuse professing Christians with Christ, Mr. Picotte. The former will disappoint you every time. Christ never will."'
