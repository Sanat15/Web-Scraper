Rating: 5/5

Pro: Paul G. Hewitt is a great author and physics teacher. I used this textbook for my first physics class. In the very front is a timeline on the history of physics from 320 BC to 2012. When reading the chapters, the illustrations helped me comprehend the concepts better. After reading, I did some of the chapter review questions. The review questions are really great.

Con: None! I found this book to be quite useful for my purposes. 

Who is this for? The targeted audience would be for students studying conceptual physics/fundamentals of physics either in high school or as an undergraduate.
