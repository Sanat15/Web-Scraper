Rating: 4/5

Two brilliantly constructed short stories!

-Buchmemdel (5 stars)
An amazing story about the failure(inability?) of educated/cultured people to make a difference in the face of war and violence. An event that completely transforms every person it comes in contact with, leaving them a hollow shell of their former self (as it also happens with the hero of the story). Really enjoyed how Zweig showed how easily people forget such events and even attempt to 'sweep them under the rug' or camouflage them under a cheap disguise/decoration (as it happened with Caffe Gluck by the new owner). Loved how the writer hints at the hero's impending doom by mentioning the story with the coal. Shows how oblivious he trully was about everything that happened in front of him and in the world in general. 
A great portayal of a city's/country's/society's reaction right before and right after the 1st world war and a very emotional, heartbreaking story in general.

-The invisible collection(3-3,5 stars)
A really interesting story about Germany in the first decade after the first world war and the hyperinflation. I wasn't really familiar with that era until now but it definitely piqued my interest.
