Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed the series on BBC decades ago. This is a collection of short stories about the down to Earth barrister ( I think!) dealing with the challenges of the court system and She Who Must Be Obeyed. He loves his crummy wine. He has a certain respect for his extensive clients. He is eccentric! 
This collection includes a story about " the woman" ala Sherlock Holmes, someone stealing evidence from Rumpole's house, and a family feud involving devil worship. Highly enjoyable!
