Rating: 3/5

This is probably my least favorite Sammy Keyes book so far, but you have to give props to any middle grade book that features meth, especially still keeping a fairly light-hearted tone.

Reveal: Ben Briggs, the brother of one of Sammy's peers, set Lucinda's cabin on fire in retaliation against Dallas (one of the men who helps oversee Lucinda's vineyard) selling drugs to his brother.
