Rating: 4/5

"The Baby Track" is the story of Courtney and Connor.
Two investigative journalists team up in this one to expose a baby laundering scam- but their inquisition is filled with magnetic attraction, past secrets, jealousy, faux marriage, amnesia and a cute baby girl Sarah.
Sweet book with witty banter, good chemistry, hot sex and loads of baby love. There is minimal angst in this one, and once I started I could not stop reading this.
Would have been a higher rating if everything had not resolved spontaneously in the end.
