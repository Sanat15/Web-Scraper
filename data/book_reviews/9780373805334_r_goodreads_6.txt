Rating: 4/5

Daphne Clair is a strong writer who tends to focus most of the content on the evolving and romantic relationship between her MCs with minimal OW/OM drama. This was a winner.

Synopsis from cover: The jilted bride and groom! When Stefanie's fiance ran off with her bridesmaid, she sought an escape of her own. Quinn Branson offered one. He shared Stefanie's predicament, and her feelings, for Stefanie's bridesmaid was also his fiancee. He offered Stefanie six months on a Pacific island -- the perfect place to mend her heart. But they had to be married. And Stefanie discovered that a makeshift marriage to a man as sexy as Quinn was fraught with temptations and complex emotions!

4.5 stars. Sometimes I'm in the mood for a low-angst but very romantic, slow-burn, MOC romance reminiscent of Loving by Penny Jordan. Enjoyed the MCs who were regular people and the swoon-worthy H/insecure h both grew and evolved throughout this sexy, well-written, engaging novel. Loved that the focus was almost exclusively on MC interactions with plenty of love scenes vs ubiquitous sex scenes. The novel was a lovely look at how their love grew over time. Evocative descriptions of a paradise island a bonus.
