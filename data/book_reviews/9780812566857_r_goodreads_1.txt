Rating: 5/5

Part III of the Savage family series (one more after this book)

"The assortment of adultery, war, murder, organized crime, intrigue, glitter and gluttony swirl together to make a racy drama" - Publishers Weekly

Once again I enjoyed the story of the Savage family concentrating on the new generation. This book follows mostly the children of Johnny Savage and his current wife Rachel. The children are experiencing coming of age in the Roaring 20's a time when morals are loosing up, bootlegging is rampant, the film industry is expanding and greed for the family money if prevelant. Cesare (Rachel's son from husband #2) is the main dysfunction character and his business antics and love life leave much to be desired while his sister Beatrice is more subdued. Brook and Nick run a close second to giving their parents some gray hairs!
