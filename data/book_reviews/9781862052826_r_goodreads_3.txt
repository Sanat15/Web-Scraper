Rating: 4/5

This is a strange book - very much in the vain of the Paper Tiger books. it is the chronicles of a group of "Hairy Peruvians" and their adventures in a giant Poppykettle. as you can imagine the story is quite surreal and bizarre but at the same time very engaging - what is more the book is heavily illustrated throughout by Robert Ingpen. The book is fascinating and fun and reads like some ancient legend or historic tale. And yes this was another book unearth in one of my many changes to my book collection
