Rating: 4/5

Wow... this is me to a tee. This book really helped me understand why I have unrealistic expectations of myself, and how I allow others to carry unrealistic expectations for me to try to meet. And, most importantly, the joys of learning to live within the boundaries of reality and to recognize my needs for rest and rejuvenation. So critical for the long haul of living well. There were some references to the 80s as if the 80s are the latest thing, but ignoring those, the principles are VERY good!
