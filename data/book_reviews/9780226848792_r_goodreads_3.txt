English title: The Haunted Monastery

short review for busy readers:
A Dutch mystery set in Tang dynasty China (early medieval period). Not bad, but one of those mysteries where the devil is in the precise details and you have to pay close attention to get all of them. 

The author was a Sinologist and clearly used the setting and characters to "educate" the reader on points of Chinese thought and culture. Per se not a bad idea, but seems somewhat too didactic at times. 

Read in the original Dutch for my Foreign Language Reading Challenge 2026. #7 of 10.
