Rating: 4/5

This book was a real breakthrough when it came out in 2004. The computers were not nearly strong as today and there is a plethora of long computerish lines which are not all that necessary and useful for readers.
Kasparov was the first to utilize heavy power and he did deliver.
I wish there was more biographycal text about the players, the very first champions. Information is very sparse and the prose not really engaging. 
Apparently, it was Plysetsky, the journalist, who wrote those portions of the book.
Alekhine gets the best attention being one of Garry's idols, of course.

I can understand the hype when the book was released, but there are better collections out there with the same games and written by Capablanca, Alekhine themselves.
