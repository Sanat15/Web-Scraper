Rating: 5/5

I loved this book. From the first appearance of Lance, I was intrigued. Reading his background throughly influenced his decisions in everything he did in his life. 
Cassandra was a little girl that ended up having to grow up way to fast. Her past also influenced everyth I ng she did. Young love is so tough and you feel like you will never be old enough to make your your choices. 
This book had so many twists and surprises that I couldn't put the book down once I started to read. I could feel the pain of young live. I felt the conflict of the wanting, but the person that was wanted, just to young. Growing up can take so long. I really enjoyed the interactions between the white man and indians. Kindness does get rewarded. 
So I want more of these characters. What happened later in life? If there was a family were they raised in the white man's world, or in the Indian way, or both. 
If you enjoy a good historical book, with a little of everything, then thus is a must read.
