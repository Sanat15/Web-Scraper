Rating: 5/5

This is a really fantastic book that taught me a lot about what it means to have God involved in, and at the front and center, of everything I am. Prior to reading this book I knew more about God the Father, and Jesus the Son, but still was trying to figure out the who/what/where of the Holy Spirit. This book (based on the Bible) teaches about how the Spirit lives IN us. Therefore we can let Him feel comfortable in His home. Or we continue doing things the way the world and our human nature dictate and distance ourselves from God. It's a much easier choice than it sounds, it's all about loving God and having faith in His will for you. I had done a study on the fruits of the Spirit, but knew little about the gifts of the Spirit. Now THOSE are some amazing gifts. I cannot believe how well-equipped we are through God's power. Personal favorite: I love Joyce's expression that God is a gentleman and he's not going to push his way in without invitation. But once you invite Him in, get ready for your life to be changed in the most beautiful way : ) I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to learn about getting to know God better. Even if you're not sure you're quite ready for that commitment, this book will teach you how to prepare.
