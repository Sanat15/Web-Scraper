Rating: 5/5

"Clifford The Small Red Puppy," is the origin story of Clifford the big red dog, which is heartwarming and shows just how strong the power of compassion can be. Emily Elizabeth adopts a runt puppy, despite warnings that it is too small and weak to survive. She gives little Clifford constant love and attention, making sure he is well fed and warm. One night, Clifford looks sick and has trouble eating. Her father tells Emily that Clifford might not make it through the night. Emily lets Clifford sleep with her, and wishes that he would grow big and strong. The next day he has grown a little, and a little more, and a little more until he's so big he doesn't fit in the house!

This story is so beautiful. It demonstrates the power of love and support and the great impact believing in someone when no one else does can have. In the classroom, this book can be used as a lesson on responsibility. Students could read this story and reflect on a time where they were responsible for something else. How did they take care of this?
