Rating: 3/5

I liked this book. I like the way these romances are realistic in that they don't gloss over the smells, the lot of women, and what living in those times was like. This book was about a young girl who had seen something that changed her views of marriage. It told of conventions that prevented the young girl from taking about what she had seen to her parents. I liked how the character of Issabella developed and matured and changed her mind eventually about her views of marriage. Her friend Lucy was also a character I enjoyed reading about.

It was a good book and as usually pretty well written and it didn't go on and on.
