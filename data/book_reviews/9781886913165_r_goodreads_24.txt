Rating: 5/5

I read this stunning account of Sharon Butala's life on her husband Peter's ranch in the dry Palliser Triangle area of southwestern Saskatchewan shortly after returning to Canada. The Perfection of the Morning is the perfect introduction to this author of 22 books of fiction and nonfiction. Winner of the Governor General's Award when it was published, more than a decade later, it remains a seminal work of prairie natural history and testament to the healing power of nature.
