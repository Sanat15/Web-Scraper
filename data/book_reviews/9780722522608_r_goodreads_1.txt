A fantastic guide for all. It contained a lot more information for common illnesses like colds, flus, etc.than I was expecting, and I'm glad of that because it was all very useful, especially when I picked germs up! However, what it lacked was conditions like endometriosis, which I was really hoping was in this book (main reason for buying it). Nevertheless, I don't regret buying it and it's a great addition to my aromatherapy bookshelf.
