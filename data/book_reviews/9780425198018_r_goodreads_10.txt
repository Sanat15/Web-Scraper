Rating: 5/5

The first book about 3 half~brothers (all first~borns and born of different mothers)... the Macleans of Inverleith. This is the middle brother's story. Rory Maclean was forced to leave the sea and his ship (The Lady) when Patrick, the eldest and heir went missing for 3 years in France and Lachlan, the youngest, could not or would not take the reins of the Lairdship for his older brothers leaving the clan without its laird. Having found Rory, the 2 foremost trusted elders/retainers schemed for an heir. They had the perfect plan and the perfect bride for Rory... Janet Cameron, Jaime Campbell's intended bride. Guaranteed perfect revenge and justice delivered to a centuries old adversary... the Campbells. Except that they stole the wrong bride and they did not inform the twice widower, who vowed never to wed again, of their intentions. You see, dear reader, there was a centuries old curse ~ that all Maclean brides would never be happy wedded to a Maclean...
