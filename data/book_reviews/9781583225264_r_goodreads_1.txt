Rating: 5/5

If you ever were or are on the fence about fluoride, read this book! Fluoride is linked to numerous medical maladies that I never knew about. And the involvement of the government to cover up this fact is truly unbelievable! As a dental hygienist, I was not totally in favor of it before, but after reading this book, I really have trouble giving fluoride treatments to children when the parents request it. It's a disturbing mental struggle. However, at the same time, whether we like it or not, fluoride is everywhere and just using spring water instead of tap water and using a non-fluoride toothpaste does not help us to escape from fluoride since it's in almost all packaged foods we eat on a daily basis.
