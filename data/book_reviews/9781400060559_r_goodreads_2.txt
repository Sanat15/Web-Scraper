Rating: 3/5

This audiobook serendipitously fell off the library shelf and into my hands. These seven stories illustrate the different types of luck, including bad luck. My favorites included the stories about the lottery winner and the paleontologist. The latter finds a huge discovery by chance, which became an insightful metaphor for the archeological dig that I’ve been conducting to find my authentic self. Maybe with a little luck I will stumble upon it by chance too.
