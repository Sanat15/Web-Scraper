Rating: 4/5

Mercy, me! Someone spiked the wedding punch, and this beleaguered bridesmaid paid the price. That's what she gets for doing a favor for the bride, keeping Croft — a dashing but unwelcome guest — busy during the reception, and away from the groom. Several cups of seriously spiked punch later, they end up crashing together. At her cottage. The morning after, Mercy wears "The Letter A" and rumors abound in her tiny hamlet. No surprise when her self-righteous landlady tosses her to the curb. No surprise when her stuffy fiancé shows his true colors. But where to go? What to do??

Jessica Steele writes Harlequin Romances and some Hqn Presents. She is now about 80 years old, sad to say. Steele is one of the worst writers, in terms of syntax, grammar, etc. Her sentence construction is ... unusual. But IMO, she is among the best at Hqn in terms of storytelling and sexual tension. Her heroines are all virgins (of course), and the wanting drives the heroes nuts.

I read this eons ago, and read it again, because I love Steele's storytelling.
