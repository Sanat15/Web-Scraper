Rating: 1/5

I rarely say something so strong, but this book was absolute garbage. First of all, the title, while being almost exactly what it is, still manages to be misleading. I imagined I'd get to know about the inner life of a Brahmin family, get to know the culture and beliefs and dynamics...very little to none of that. It was literally what it says - about what the author did with the days of her life while she lived with a Brahmin family. Without adequate explanation or context (the author doesn't even mention what she's doing in India, or whether she is Hindu herself) it's entirely mundane and boring. Terms that are foreign are often left unexplained, so that only someone with a great deal of prior knowledge will learn what the author seems to want her readers to get out of the book. 
At a point, she also vanishes from her home "with a Brahmin family" and sets up with a guru. While it might have been an important part of her personal journey, it seemed out of place and inexplicably included in this random sharing of her story.

I'd be proud of myself for slogging through, but don't think I learned anything of value, or otherwise. I could have spent 5 minutes googling Brahminism and would have likely learned ten times as much.

The cover was cool, though I don't think that earns this book the one star I gave it. The "I didn't like it" of a one star rating was extremely generous, if you ask me.

This is one for the recycling bin.
