Rating: 2/5

I probably would have given this a one star if it hadn't been written by Michelle Reid, whose books I usually love to bits. 
I just wasn't expecting an Old Skool violent hero because she has never written one who spanks his h to punish and humiliate her, (because she wants to have sex with him since they are engaged and she is crazy about him - seriously wtf?). Later he also deliberately provokes, hurts and taunts her in public until she slaps him - then he slaps her right back! He does apologise, but yuck, unforgiveable, and so puzzling that MR wrote a disgusting hero like this.

Both the heroine and hero were annoying and immature. At least the hero at the end is trying to figure out why he was such a douche four years previously. Just not my cup of tea.
