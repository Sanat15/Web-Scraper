Rating: 4/5

Heart felt, amazing account of Mark Hoyle's life written by his Dad. This boy is amazing as is his family. And the town of Swansea came through for him - the school administration and the doctors spoke for Mark and provided factual information in answering parents and teachers questions and fears about AIDS. I live in Swansea and grew up here. I can't say enough how proud I am that people were able to ask the questions they needed to, honestly express their fears, and be given answers from factual evidence so reason could prevail over fear and hysteria. I am still here because this is a great place to live and raise a family. The people make it great! Mark taught us all what is important.
