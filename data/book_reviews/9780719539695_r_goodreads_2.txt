Rating: 2/5

This is a very slim book and a disappointment – the poetry is variable, with some early stuff and some ‘experimental’ (as in not buffed and finished) etc. Best part is the foreword by Bevis Hillier who describes her access to unlisted documents found in the University Library, Victoria, British Columbia. I don’t think any of the actual poems would warrant an entry in a fuller collection – certainly not in my much-thumbed ‘Collected Poems’ chosen and introduced by Lord Birkenhead.
