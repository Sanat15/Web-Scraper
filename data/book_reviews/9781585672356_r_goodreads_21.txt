this book had loads of potential to be a really good story , sadly it was very disappointing.

It began well with a trip to Rarotonga/Aitutaki places very close to my own heart, for her wedding from day 1 in fact to page 22 , she has done nothing but whinge and moan from the flowers to the locals., who are in fact warm lovely people to everything. By 22 my journey ended.

Very disappointing
