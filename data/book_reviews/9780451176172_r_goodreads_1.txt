Rating: 4/5

Very strange sort of a hero, he lacks many of the virtues of a typical Regency (historical) romance hero. He does not dance well and misses the steps, he is not conscientious about his holdings (except for his horses), he knows nothing of poetry, he is a poor card player. I’m just in the middle of the book, but so far, I don’t dislike him and there are some funny moments. He gets to redeem himaself, and a happy ending. Though actually how would their married life go?
