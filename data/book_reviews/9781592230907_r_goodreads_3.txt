Rating: 4/5

UPDATE: Now that I've taken an online ASL course, it was helpful to go through this book a second time and see the similarities and differences in signs. Or, in many cases, a sign was presented in a clearer way in this book and it (hopefully) solidified my understanding. I'm still going to need a lot of practice before I can hold a conversation or even convey my thoughts, but at least I know how to say, "Please take off your clothes"! This will never stop being funny!!! 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
I mostly liked the format of this book. It's a nice way to Signed English (as opposed to American Sign Language, the author explains the difference in the introduction). ASL is used to express whole thoughts, which is quite different from how we think in terms of spoken English. Signed English more closely corresponds to spoken English, since each gesture corresponds to a word, so the syntax is almost the same. 

The book starts with the alphabet and then goes on the phrases. After the alphabet, the book is divided into several sections, each section for a theme, such as "Nature" or "About the Body". Speaking of the latter, the one phrase that has stuck out to me and of course I have memorized is "Please take off your clothes". Hahahahaha! Why?? Just, why??? 

After completing this book, I'm certainly not an expert in Signed English, nor can I readily recall many of the words, but at least now they're familiar to me and this book has served as a good introduction to the subject, but it will also be a good reference. I'd like to find another resource so I can continue learning, but also to clear up some confusion from this book. 

Two major points of confusion here: 1) When drawing a shape with your finger, such as a "Z" or "?", the author seems to imply you're drawing that shape from your point of view. But then that's backwards for the recipient, so it makes more sense for me to draw these shapes backwards so that they appear correct to the recipient. 2) There are a few places, not a whole lot, but definitely some, where the written description and the accompanying photo do not exactly correspond. For instance, sometimes the author is doing the mirror image of what the text says. Whenever I have had some uncertainty, I follow the text. But yes, at least these two reasons will justify supplementing with another resource. 

Read this book if you too want to be able to effortlessly sign, "Please take off your clothes"!!!!
