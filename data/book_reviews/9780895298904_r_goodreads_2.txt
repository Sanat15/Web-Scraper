Rating: 5/5

Ah, "All About Green Tea"! 🍵 This book was a delightful read, and Victoria Dolby Toews truly dives deep into the world of green tea. I've always been curious about why green tea is hailed as one of the healthiest beverages, and this book answered so many of my questions...

While it's packed with information, it's presented in a way that's easy to digest. I particularly enjoyed the historical and cultural tidbits sprinkled throughout. However, I did notice that some facts seemed a bit outdated, which is understandable given its publication date. 

But overall, it's a solid read for anyone looking to expand their knowledge on green tea. It's like a mini-encyclopedia on my favorite drink! 🍵✨
