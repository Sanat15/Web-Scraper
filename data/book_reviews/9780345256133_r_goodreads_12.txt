Rating: 5/5

Another Great Read

I’ve now read most all of Ms Alice Chetaynd Ley (I’ve read ALL the ones currently available on Kindle) and my only regret is that I want more. I hope that they eventually get around to buying the rights to publish the remaining works of Ley’s so that I can devour them all. 

If you love Georgette Heyer and are frustrated by having read through her catalog and are looking for another author to fulfill your regency needs, I would highly recommend giving Alice Chetwynd Ley a try!
