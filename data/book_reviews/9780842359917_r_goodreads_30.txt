Rating: 3/5

I’ve been meaning to read this book for more than a decade, and I really, really wanted to like it. The story was good, insightful, challenging, moving at times…but the writing. Oh my goodness. It was so melodramatic. Some of the metaphors were so over the top I was literally grimacing. The endless conversations written in broken English. The vast majority of the book is just page after page of dialogue. The heavenly realm scenes were a tad strange/uncomfortable sometimes…Jesus massaging someone’s shoulders, for example. Li Quan is so spiritual all the time, and basically perfect, so I didn’t find him to be terribly believable. Poor Ben Fielding is almost exclusively referred to as Ben Fielding, never Ben. Even by Jesus. Ming giggles so much. Pretty much all the women giggle incessantly. Poor Shen’s face is always quivering. It just kinda drove me crazy. 
Three stars is as generous as I can be. Great concept, good theology, good heart…if you can get past the writing style and the weirdness that inevitably comes with trying to imagine the unimaginable.
