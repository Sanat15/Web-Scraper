Well...I'll be honest. I didn't read every essay in the back of the book. I read the ones written by people I have heard of. That was interesting. I enjoyed reading Matthew Fox &amp; Desmond Tutu. Cynthia Ozick's was my favorite response. I think my reading of this holocaust account was made more intense by my experience at the Museum of Tolerance this past summer. Wiesenthal's work is so inspiring, and reading his words helps me understand how he could do so much good after experiencing such atrocities.
