Rating: 5/5

4.5 stars. Overall, a very entertaining book.

The heroine has her faults (pride, managing) but she is also incredibly sympathetic because she is unloved by everyone, simply because she is plain and businesslike rather than pretty and charming. That made her very sympathetic to me.

In the beginning, the hero has had everything taken away from him, and he resents the heroine because she has taken advantage of that in order to "buy" him as a groom. This creates great conflict. There was only one point I didn't quite like: I thought he was being resentful and insulting to her, considering she is the only one who stood by him, and he could have walked out on her and gone to American rather than taken her offer of marriage, so it seems unreasonable for him to take his anger out on her.

However, from there the storyline progresses at a good pace, not too slow and not too fast. The ending is good.
