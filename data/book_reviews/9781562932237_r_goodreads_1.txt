Rating: 4/5

It's incredible how much my 2 year old loves animals. He knows almost all the animals in this book except the flying squirrel and loon. I'm sure he'll have these new animals memorized in no time. Though my fella is quite young, his obsession with animals means he enjoys learning these facts like how the owl hunts and how beavers build dams.

My tip: If you think a book is too old for your child, just try reading it! They will let you know if they aren't feeling it.... But they just might surprise you and really enjoy a book you didn't think they were ready for!
