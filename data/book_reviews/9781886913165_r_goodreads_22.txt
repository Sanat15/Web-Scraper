Rating: 5/5

Read this book a number of years ago, and many of the feelings I had are still with me. I highly recommend this book, which would be helpful to anyone trying to get a sense of the prairies, rural &amp; small-town life, and some of Canadian history. I have visited a number of places in Saskatchewan, and the Grasslands National Park in Saskatchewan, some of which contains the ranch that Butala moved to, following her marriage to Peter. I believe the ranch was donated via the Nature Conservancy of Canada for the Park.
