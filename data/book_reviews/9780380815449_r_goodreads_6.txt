Rating: 5/5

"Don't You Know There's A War on?" takes place in Brooklyn, New York with a family that revolves mainly around an 11 year old by the name of, Howard "Howie" Crispers. At the time, Howie was in the 5th grade while the war was taking place. He attended school along side his pal, Denny Coleman. Both Denny and Howie's fathers were in the war. Howie's teachers name was Ms. Gossim. She was in the same predicament because her husband was also in the war along with their fathers.
 I like this book because it is an inspirational book and basically talks about how no one is by themselves in a bad situation and that they have each other. The characters always have each others backs through the rough times. I also like how the class and teachers treat each other like family. My favorite part of the book was when Howie and the students tried to help their teacher during a rough time in her life. I would recommend this book to people that are having bad times and can't see good through the bad because this book is a good example of not letting anything keep you down and just follow your heart. 
Rating: 5 stars
