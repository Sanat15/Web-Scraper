Rating: 4/5

I really enjoyed this book! It is slightly outdated as It was written in 1993 and the Wolves were re-introduced into Yellowstone in 1995. Other than that the pictures are stunning and the story is interesting.

The writing is beautiful as well. "At the top, he closes his eyes, throws back his head, and howls. A wild untamed music, it seems to bounce of the moon, echoing from the mountains and filling the gullies and valleys." Very nice book!
