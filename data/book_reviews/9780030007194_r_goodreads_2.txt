Rating: 4/5

Red and Boober wander off together so the others can finish preparing for Boober's surprise birthday party, and are trapped by a cave-in. The characterization and dialogue were spot-on with my memories of the show (a favorite from childhood), and the illustrations were great. The story was surprisingly dark and scary. A happy ending is assured, of course, but there's a lot going on here. Which is what made Henson's creations so compelling: even the children's shows have layers and complexity. Add some wonderful music and you have the show distilled into book form.
