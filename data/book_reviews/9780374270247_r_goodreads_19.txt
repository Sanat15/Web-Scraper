Rating: 3/5

Brought me back to a presentation I made in college on Nan Goldin’s “Ballad of Sexual Dependency”(another drug/AIDS documenter of pre-gentrified 80’s New York - Yablonsky thanks her in the acknowledgements). Spare, bureaucratic, clinical tone. There is no bottom or top, no epiphany or attempt to find meaning. A junkie’s life is boring. I respect the horrifying honesty, would not read again.
