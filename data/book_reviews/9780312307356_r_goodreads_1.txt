Rating: 4/5

The Burnt Orange Sunrise is book 4 in the Berger and Mitry Mystery series. This installment takes place in the dead of winter and the weather creates a Christie-esque locked room, in this case a locked B &amp; B. Des and Mitch find themselves trapped along with other guests by the weather, downed trees and a power outage. Soon it becomes evident that one of their companions is a murderer. This was another good installment in the series with a side plot that is left as a cliff hanger. Very enjoyable mystery read!
