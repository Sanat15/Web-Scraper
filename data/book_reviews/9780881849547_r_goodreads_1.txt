I have a few problems with parts of this book--there is a consent issue partway through. She suggests taking advantage of your male partner while he is asleep or drunk, and that really bothered me. The fact that a man would probably not mind in most cases is not right. If you don't have consent, you don't have consent. 

I do see her point when it comes to faking, and the dishonesty there is not as worrisome, but the consent issue bothers me greatly.
