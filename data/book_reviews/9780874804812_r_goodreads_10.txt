Rating: 5/5

Such an amazing story! I first stumbled on Truman Evert’s harrowing tale in my favorite book I read from last year- Empire of Shadows by George Black (a must read for anyone who has interest in the history of National Parks, especially Yellowstone.) When I found out that Evert had published his story of survival I knew I wanted to read it in his own words, unfortunately it’s a bit hard to come by. I was able to listen to the audio version on Spotify Premium and it did not disappoint. Very much worth the quick listen/read!
