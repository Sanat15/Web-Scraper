Rating: 3/5

This is a six page pop-up book that 
was published in 1988. It was published 
by Ottenheimer Publishers, Inc. On the 
back of the edition that I have it also 
says WaldenEd Book Fairs.

The illustrations are cute and appropriate.
They are not, however, incredibly beautiful.

Even though the book is only six pages long,
the storyline in it is somewhat substantial 
and good.
