Rating: 5/5

This is a classic, and one I really like. It's really important to me that we have visible Old Guard leather folks who aren't 100% serious all the time. Baldwin is excellent at presenting the tradition along with practical (hey guess what, not all relationships are fantasy-land all the time) knowledge. This is a good volume for both newbies and those more experienced in the scene. Most chapters are stand-alone and there is no need to read the book in exact order. Recommended, especially for those who are new.
