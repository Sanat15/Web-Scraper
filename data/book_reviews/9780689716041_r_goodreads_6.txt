Rating: 4/5

The song sparrow says:
Put on the kettle, kettle, kettle ... over, and over again.

That's one of the things I like about a well-written books for kids -- I always learn something.

Unlike many Newbery titles, especially the newer ones, this is a story for young children. I like the 8-year-old boy's interest in wildlife, and I suppose that it was realistic to shoot the bear for food, especially since this was written in 1952  but I would have preferred a different ending.
