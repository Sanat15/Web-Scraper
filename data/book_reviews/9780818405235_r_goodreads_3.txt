Rating: 4/5

"Black Robes, White Justice" is an articulate and scathing review of the U.S. justice system and its racism from the eyes of Bruce Wright, a former judge in New York, notably serving as a Supreme Court Justice. He details story after story of racism both in his own experience, being denied promotions or crucified by police and media due to his race, and in his witness of defendants receiving racism from other justices. The book provides a unique glimpse behind the curtain and into the courtroom, behind the courtesies and into the consciences. The book is quite a lot to chew on, but I would highly recommend it for anyone seeking to understand the U.S. justice system and/or racism.
