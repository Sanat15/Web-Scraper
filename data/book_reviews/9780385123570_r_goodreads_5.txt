Rating: 3/5

A quick, unexpected read. Fearing military duty and starting to have some minor writing success, Singer explores what it means to be a man a thinker and a Jew in tumultuous Poland. He passes through a variety of unconventional relationships with women who are likewise trying to find some sustainable, meaningful path. Surprisingly unromantic (and with explicit but classy illustrations), this book left me a bit confused. I know Singer mostly as a short story author and am not familiar with other stages of his autobiography - this feels very much like one piece in a larger story, without a real beginning or end.
