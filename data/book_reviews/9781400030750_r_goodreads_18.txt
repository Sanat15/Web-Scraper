Rating: 3/5

A very skewed narrative from the author who at times asked thought provoking questions and forces readers to think inwards. I was able to relate to a lot of things that are still true in India inspite of 75 years of independence, like the very fast caste system is still prevalent in India or the fact that India is still okay to let go of their most intelligent people but not really bring meritocracy into their education system and beyond. People are still very fascinated with the idea of US or in general any foreign country than really feeling proud of their own roots and culture. As Hindus we have always been torn because we have been receptive of other cultures and so our own culture has diluted to the fact that sometimes people like VS Naipaul think that we don’t really value our roots.
