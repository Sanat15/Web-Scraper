Rating: 4/5

Somehow I had managed to miss Updike till now. To state the obvious, he's an amazing writer -style, smarts, humor, insight.
I really liked the juxtaposed stories of the would-be writer of a biography of Buchanan and his manuscript, all while in the Ford administration. Brilliant idea, funny too, though if you are not "into" the era of Buchanan or sleeping around it could become ponderous.
