Rating: 3/5

I liked the book and the overall story, but it’s extremely difficult to read this one in smaller chunks. If you have the patience to read about 120-150 pages per day it’s worth it. If you prefer reading in smaller bits of time, maybe not. There aren’t really chapters, just very long sections. It’s a demon story, so of course it ends with a battle against the demon (I don’t think that’s giving anything away) - but it was quite exhausting to read 130+ pages of demon battle and I went to bed much later than I should over several nights because I wanted to get to a good stopping point each time.
