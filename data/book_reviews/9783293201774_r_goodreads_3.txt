Rating: 5/5

This is the most amazing anthology of erotica I've ever stumbled across. Okay, that's not saying much at this point in my life, I'm kind of just beginning to stumble across this genre. But let me just say this. This book was put together by African-American Literature Professors. The pieces collected are by writers of EVERY genre out there, and there is poetry, prose, fiction, and more. And they are INCREDIBLE. This book is literary, beautiful, sensual, intelligent and educational. The essays throughout, written on Afro-American issues, Feminism, Inter-racial relations, and Erotica itself are incredibly well-written, poignant and intellectual. I recommend this book to everyone who loves to read!
