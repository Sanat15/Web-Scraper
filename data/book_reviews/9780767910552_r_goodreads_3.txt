Rating: 4/5

My first experience with bialys was Wegmans...my mom would get them for us on the weekends and I remember really enjoying them. 
Lo and behold I find a book all about them amongst my grandmother's books, she's a foodie and Mimi Sheraton fangirl.
If you enjoy learning about the history of certain foods, this is definitely interesting! Definitely a passion project for Sheraton, and it is fascinating how far flung Jews were from the small town of Bialystok, Poland after the Holocaust.
