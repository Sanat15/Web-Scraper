Rating: 4/5

A perfect book to read for our current economic climate, "A Plea..." outlines the damage done to the economy in colonial America prior to the foundation of the United States by the issuance of bills of credit, and why our Founding Fathers and their contemporaries were so adamantly against it (one said that any man promoting bills of credit should be considered treasonous and dealt with accordingly). This book shows that these men understood the importance of maintaining currency based on the exchange of gold and silver coin, and how once that standard was removed and was replaced with paper money, the value of that paper could be inflated/deflated to manipulate the value of it, effectively stealing from the many for the benefit of the few that understand how to reap the rewards of that manipulation. Not that that could ever happen today...
