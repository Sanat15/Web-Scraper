Rating: 4/5

3.5 stars rounded up. 
This one dragged a little, was a bit silky and had rather too many characters whose names started with the same letters. E.G. Betty, Bella, Mrs Bentley

On the upside, I chortled. Chesney had such a way with words, any of her stories are fun to read. When the world is such a mess, it is a pleasure to escape for a while with a good book.
