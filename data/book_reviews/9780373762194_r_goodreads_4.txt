Rating: 3/5

This was a sweet read.

 Jake is a little boy who believes in the Star dust cowboy his momma, Dori has been telling him stories about for most of his life. 

Riley is Jake's uncle, who owns half the ranch he's living on. Eight year old Jake owns the rest. Jake learns so much while living there!

So much more, includes woman who tries to stir up trouble, but just makes more of it for herself. 

Recommended reading.
