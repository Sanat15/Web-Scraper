Rating: 2/5

I do not have a DNA chart for Robert Sabuda, but his nationality is listed as "American" via online sources. My passport says I am American too so this is said with as much respect as I can muster. I hate when I find an illustrator and paper engineer, Sabuda, who feels entitled to teach someone else's culture to others. This book feels like an artist was interested in hieroglyphics and Egyptian culture so they wanted someone to see all their cool drawings. The work is worthy of being seen and the designs that come from the text are sure to be accurately rendered since the artist is both the author and illustrator. It just feels like someone else's story to tell and perhaps the text would have spoken to me more if it had been written by someone that identifies as Egyptian or maybe a historian. Sabuda has no doubt studied the culture but this project lacks something.
