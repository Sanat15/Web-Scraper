Rating: 3/5

Very good autobiography of Harold J. Cook the grandson of Cpt Cook the famous mariner and son of CPT James Cook the cowboy and U.S.Cavalry scout of the 1870's. This book covers his youthful years growing up on the 04 Ranch now known as the Agate Springs Ranch in Northwest Nebraska. Much of the ranch is now par of the Agate Fossel Beds National Monument. 

The fossel beds were discovered by Harolds father. 

This book is full of adventure and stories from the discovery of the beds to the leading of famous Professors and Paleontologists to the friendship with RED cloud and many other Indians.

If you like westerns, dinosours or just adventur, this is the book for you.
