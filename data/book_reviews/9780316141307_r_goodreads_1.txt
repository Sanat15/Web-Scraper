Rating: 4/5

For what this is (a &lt;130 page middle grade nonfiction from 1996) it was actually quite delightful. I 100% picked it up because it featured one of my favorites, Joe Nuxhall, on the cover; and I’m glad I did! *Super* quick and heartwarming little read. And!!!! Impressively, it featured a story or two I hadn’t previously heard. I also liked that, as someone that “cluster reads” about certain ball clubs, this ONLY included the great moments. In baseball, there are a lot of battles and wars lost; the great moments don’t always occur in a win. But this lil book did a good job keeping it uplifting and highlighting the moments themselves (not the athlete or team or season or outcome as a whole). It made me smile!
