Rating: 1/5

I have liked some of RCs books but not this one, mainly because I didn't like the way the romance developed. It seemed to me that the Hero was too manipulating and kept making sexual advances when he knew the h was there to do her job.... and the heroine s going to his place to write his biography made no sense. Moreover, I didn't like the h either because she was deceiving him and finally she takes on the same attitude she had with her first lover...as if all she really did need was good sex. I felt no respect for the heroine. Plus the sexual and romantic ambience doesn't surface as well as it usually does in RCs other books i have read.
