Rating: 3/5

Heroine: Sherry, "almost 20", poor and constantly working herself to exhaustion on the ranch her brother inherited from their grandfather

Hero: Scott, 36-year old, divorced, wealthy, owns neighboring ranch

Set-up: Scott's little sister appears to be dating Sherry's older brother. Scott demands Sherry's help to break them up. When the couple appear to elope, Scott drags Sherry along to find them. The truth is revealed, but Sherry's brother is now in London with no intention of returning. Abandoned, Sherry does her best to make ends meet. Scott tries to check in and offer help, but Sherry doesn't want his interference and mistrusts his motives. 

Thoughts: Well, Scott's a bit of an a$$hole, but more in words than action. He's threatening and overbearing, while secretly in love and desiring to take care of Sherry. Scott is bitter over his first marriage in which his wife was "disgusted" by sex and divorced him to go "work for women's rights". He tells Sherry that he wants someone who will give up everything to be with him and expect nothing in return, which is a lot to ask of a poor young virgin farm girl. Several times Scott gets Sherry naked without going all the way, respecting if she says no and also because he is holding out for proof of her love. Throw in some enemy-to-lover tension, a selfish/languid brother, other woman drama, sheep shearing, and a wildfire injury to make this a pleasant read.
