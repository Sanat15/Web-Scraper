Rating: 5/5

This was a roaring great read.

It hit a lot of my buttons: San Francisco location, bantering investigators, local theater inside information, amateur investigators working with the police.

The newlyweds arrive at their hotel to find a dead body in the bath.

Even worse news is that the cousin has been kidnapped.

Charley learns a lot about her new husband as he bonds with her overprotective guardian, Harry, whose daughter is the one who was kidnapped.

She also learns that various people at the theater are missing, namely the writer and the director.
The production is ready to audition the cast and Charley steps up to direct and lead in his absence.
His girlfriend feels something is wrong about the note left behind, and that he abandoned her without a word. The staff feel he was hired under false pretenses and he did not have the knowledge he should have based on his resume.

Charley and Jack investigate and the showdown comes on opening night.

I borrowed a copy from the public library, but will recommend this to my mystery reading group ASAP.
