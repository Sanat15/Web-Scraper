Rating: 5/5

The Oxford Dictionary of Quotations is a delight to peruse: a treasury of wit, wisdom, and the occasional curiosity that reveals the richness of language. Its pages overflow with the distilled thoughts of centuries, each quotation a polished gem waiting to be rediscovered. As Winston Churchill once remarked, “An educated man should use a dictionary of quotations,” and I fully intend to take his advice, if only to better pretend to be educated myself. I look forward to returning to its pages throughout my life, finding in it both inspiration and amusement.
