Rating: 4/5

One of the best of this series. The writing is so heartfelt and (almost) glorious. The story is bittersweet, but with a satisfactory, even hopeful, ending.
You will notice I've said nothing about the plot--it is almost impossible to talk about without spoilers.
Each reader needs to discover the clues, the hints, the twists and turns on their own.
I loved this one!
