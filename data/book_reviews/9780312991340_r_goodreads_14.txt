Rating: 5/5

Blue Moon is a great paranormal romance with some terrifying werewolves, gives some insight into Ojibwa culture and their mythology/folklore, and some steamy scenes that will have you wanting more!

I really enjoyed the author's use of various werewolf myths throughout this book. I also learned about some I didn't know about and will have to look into more. The author created this great level of suspense that will keep you out of the woods at night. I also enjoyed that the author was able to make me question constantly who was telling the truth and who was hiding a dark secret. 

I really enjoyed the author including a Native American activist as one of the secondary characters because it was a great diverse representation especially when the author highlighted the discrimination that Native Americans still deal with today. I also loved learning more about their culture!

I loved all the characters because they are all wonderfully flawed so that it is impossible not to be rooting for them. Jessie is my favorite character because she is a bigger girl and you see her doubts that I really relate to. I love that Will is an adorable nerd that is also clumsy at times. Zelda is just uniquely her and you can't help but laugh at her witty one-liners.

I highly recommend Blue Moon to my fellow werewolf and romance fans.
