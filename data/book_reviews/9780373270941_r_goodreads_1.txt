Rating: 3/5

Half of the adults in this one were dumb.
Let's start with Melanie or Melissa, our main female character. She knew her MIL was after her and it was dangerous for her to be seen in public, so what does she do on a big auction? Accepts Cara's idea of disguising her and comes out between people. I guess she was afraid of being in public only if it was anything other than auction. Oh and it's messed up that she felt she had the right to read through a dead woman's private diaries.
Now Gabe, he knows someone is after Melanie, but what does he do? He asks his BIL to find out about her past and doesn't even consider it might bring the bad guys running. (And no, the fact that it was the phonecall that did the trick doesn't absolve anyone of being stupid for taking the risk.)
Now we have Cara. Cara who was the first one Melanie told someone was after her. What does Cara do on a day of a big event where different people came? She decides Melanie can't hide to be safe, she has to mingle so she hires someone to disguise her. I fear for the women she's trying to help with the charity.
Lastly, Ian. Dude's experienced, he knows someone is after Melanie but he takes the risk and agrees to find out who she is for Gabe. 
Of course Vincent is a disgusting man, who is is an abusive henchman and a rapist, and Louise can go take a hike.
Kevin was cute, though!
