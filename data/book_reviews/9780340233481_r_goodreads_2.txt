Rating: 4/5

Easy and yet difficult to read in equal measure. The subject matter, that God will give you anything you ask is hard to Swallow for many. It feels too easy and not at all true as people's lives play out with all the ups and downs it brings. But Urquhart underpins his thinking with biblical references, real life examples of prayers answered and it gives food for thought. As a Christian, I'm wrestling with it currently.
