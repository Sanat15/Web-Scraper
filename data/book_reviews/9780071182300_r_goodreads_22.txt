 5/5 stars

I used Film Art: An Introduction (13th edition) by David Bordwell for my summer film studies course, and it was an incredibly engaging and insightful read. What stood out to me most was how accessible the writing was, even when covering complex film theory and terminology. The examples used throughout the textbook were not only helpful, but also refreshingly modern—many of them drawn from recent films I’ve actually seen, which made the material feel relevant and exciting. I especially appreciated the online textbook’s integration with Bordwell and Thompson’s blog; the links offered extra commentary and deeper dives that enhanced my understanding of film form and analysis. This is one of the few textbooks I’ve genuinely enjoyed reading from start to finish.
