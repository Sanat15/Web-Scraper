Rating: 5/5

Billy Dead was loaned to me by a colleague who said I just randomly found this book on the shelf about 20 years ago and have never been able to forget it. Let me tell you, I'm not likely to forget it either. I alternatively couldn't put this down and then just couldn't read another page because it is so disturbing and sad, really hard family stuff. I think Lisa Reardon wrote an important book that tries very hard to get at the core of how love, inspite of enmenshment in dysfunctional families that are so messed up and abusive you would expect them to run away, keeps them together. The time and setting of this novel also factored into this story (small town, Michigan in the 70's ) which sets the scene for bars, murder, domestic violence, rape.
