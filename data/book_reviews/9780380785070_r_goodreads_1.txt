Rating: 4/5

This is a story about Kenny who goes to school at Riverside Middle School.One thing I liked about the main character was that he was funny.One thing I disliked about the authors style was he had too many characters in the book.I like how the author included dialogue between the characters.I would recommend this to fast paced readers because its a short book.If you like books about middle school you should read this book.Another thing I liked about the book was that the chapters were pretty short.
