Rating: 4/5

Rita Boucher books are hit-or-miss for me. This was a hit, a big hit. Guin and the necessity of her disguises, could be a Trigger Warning for spousal abuse. She was so burden by guilt that my heart hurt for her. Then there was Lord Corvin, who she should have trusted but her PTSD made it almost impossible. He was a mess too, but he softened and became more likeable. It took some maneuvering from her aunt, his best friend, and several of the students at Guin's girls' school but they got their HEA. I really liked Lord Corvin's friend Hopley who was wise beyond his years and made me laugh.

Recommended
