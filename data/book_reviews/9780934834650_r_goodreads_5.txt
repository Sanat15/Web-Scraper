Rating: 4/5

I was deeply drawn to the haikus in the book (by Bashō and other poets)—they are so Zen and soul-cleansing. They left me eager to seek out more of Bashō’s poetry, as well as other Zen haikus, in the future.

As for the travel sketches, perhaps because I’ve read many great travel books this year (such as Travels with a Donkey in the Cévennes by R. L. Stevenson and The Shadow of the Sun by R. Kapuściński), Bashō’s vaguer accounts of his journeys didn’t keep me quite as engaged.
