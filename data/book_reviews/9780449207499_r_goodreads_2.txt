Rating: 1/5

After being amused and entertained by the first novel, this one was a blow! If I had read this book first, I would have dismissed the Author immediately! I absolutely detested the H! He acts like a deranged lecher, a real creep! He leers, smirks, manhandles the h, belittles her, unbearable. As soon as he meets her, in dire circumstances I might add, this is his what he thinks:
"Then, for the life of him unable to think of a proper way to inform the girl that she was to serve as his mistress for a short period in repayment for his generosity," 🤢
She is a 17 year-old virgin, daughter and sister of an Earl, WTF!
As if this was not enough, here is another pearl:
“How can she, who is herself a mere babe-in-arms in the ton, fire off a little country nobody without a trace of nous, a green girl who lacks either presence or beauty….”
And he is saying this to her!
And then he proceeds to do this in public, in plain view of the Ton:
"Instead he bent with a feral smile and kissed Leslie very hard on the mouth. Worse, he held the position until Daryl tugged at his arm." FYI, Daryl is her 9 year-old brother, the Earl. And again: “Drogo continued to hold her firmly against his chest, in an embrace that began to draw curious, and some frankly disapproving glances." You don't say!
One last one, when Leslie, the h, tells him that she loves him -WHY!- this is what he answers:
"“But I do not love you, child. How could I? You are scarcely out of the nursery, so green a girl that you would bore me after three days.”
Enough said, forewarned is forearmed.
