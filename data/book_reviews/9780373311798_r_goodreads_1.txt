Rating: 5/5

Oh, the thrill of the hunt! It was both enjoyable and insightful to watch these characters as their emotions unfolded and they made their true selves known—both to me, the reader, and to themselves. Especially Alys. I related to her sooo much as a woman almost "on the shelf" with a tendency to manage the lives of her family and friends. Her "meddling" was genuine and, truthfully, appreciated; but her brother-in-law was right. In many ways, women do need husbands. Not to keep them from meddling in the affairs of others, so to speak, but to draw forth their best qualities and cause them to blossom. This blossoming can happen in many ways, and a "husband" does not necessarily need to be the catalyst for growth, but so often that is the case. Real love allows us to bloom and it seeks to bloom alongside and among, rather than alone. Ms. Hoyle understands the importance of love and family in all its many aspects and interweaves these themes very well within her story. I am anxious to read more of her novels! I especially liked the addition of Egyptian mysteries in this one. So fun!
