Rating: 4/5

Viscount of Corvin, Lord Daniel Stanton, decided he needed to get married so that he can take care of his younger sister Emmaline. His father arranged for his sister to attend Morton House School for Young Ladies. He was unable to score Emmaline to the school so he got Daniel to escort her. Daniel met with Guinevere Courtney who was one of the instructors at school and one of the owners. He didn't realize that he had seen Mrs. Courtney that morning on a wild ride through Hyde Park where he had been thoroughly impressed. Ms. Boucher has written an excellent novelette with happily ever after many trials and tribulation. I highly recommend you read this book...
