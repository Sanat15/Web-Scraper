Rating: 3/5

This author is a puzzle to me. She crafts these amazing characters, who you could swear breathe, and sometimes puts them in stories worthy of them, but sometimes it something quite match up. I can't even put my finger on what doesn't work in this one, beyond the bizarre and unnecessary denouement at the end. Rhys and Alessia were great, had motivations and depths bordering on realistic, and I cared enough to want to see them survive their adventure. I think it's that last scene, when Alessia gets metaphorically dragged through mud when she'd been brave and strong and loving all the way through before that. Well, that was just dirty pool, old man, and it derailed a good story by landing it on a bumpy end note.
