Rating: 5/5

This is another fun Regency, with lots of twists and turns. However, plotting and wickedness aren't enough to overcome true love. And so, the evil house loses once again, but sits there brooding.

There was enough going on to keep me turning pages well past my bedtime. (Yes, I have a shirt that has "I read past my bedtime" on it.) It only pales in comparison to the first book in the series, since the reader now knows that some of the plot involves lusting for Mannerling.

The series is back on track! On to the next book.

Recommended for fans of traditional Regencies.
