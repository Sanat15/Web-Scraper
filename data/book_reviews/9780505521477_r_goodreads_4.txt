Rating: 4/5

The Night Before Christmas consists of 4 feel-good Christmas romances, each with a bit of Christmas magic sprinkled in:
Promises to Keep by Victoria Alexander
Naughty or Nice by Sandra Hill
Santa Reads Romance by Dara Joy
A Gift for Santa by Nelle McFather
The first story was not to my taste, but I thoroughly enjoyed the other three. This was a nice way to get into the holiday spirit.
