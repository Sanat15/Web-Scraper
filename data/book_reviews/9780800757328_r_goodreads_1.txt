Rating: 3/5

Another rather overextended plot with too many facets (Irish terrorists, an surprise inheritance, a hijacked plane, a spy in a manor house, an orphan from Kosovo, drugging an old man, a baby raised as another's son, a hideout in a vicarage) for even close to a believable reality or realistic conversations and character growth, but nonetheless I enjoyed it. I kept reading because I wanted to see how it ended, but I think I'm done with the series now.
