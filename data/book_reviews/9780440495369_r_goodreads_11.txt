Rating: 5/5

George Washington arrives in New York for his presidential inauguration, and Carrie slips away from Fraunces' Tavern to see the spectacle. While she is gone, a free-lance kidnapper captures her. Dan Arabus witnesses the kidnapping and sounds the alarm. When Dan and Mr. Fraunces rescue Carrie, they learn that the kidnapper was hired by someone. Later, Captain Ivers comes with a paper claiming that Carrie is his slave. It’s pretty obvious that the captain was behind the kidnapping. To protect Carrie, Mr. Fraunces takes her with him when he goes to work as steward at Washington’s presidential mansion. The only clues that Carrie has to her past are that Jack Arabus brought her to Sam Fraunces during the war, and that William Samuel Johnson's name was on Captain Ivers' paper. Mr. Johnson, remembers making up some papers for Jack Arabus, but he can't remember their content. Later, he does recollect that Jack got Carrie from a black woman working for the British troops on Staten Island. The child came with a paper signed by a British officer stating that she was free, and a Colonel Ledyard had something to do with the whole affair. When Carrie mentions the colonel's name to Dan, he tells her that Ledyard was Jordan Freeman's former owner. Piecing this information together, Carrie becomes convinced that she is Willy Freeman's sister.
