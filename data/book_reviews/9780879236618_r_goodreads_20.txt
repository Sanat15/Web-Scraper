Rating: 4/5

2025/6 revisit - this is one that just gets better the next time and over again. The oldest child requests it every winter, because the way everything wraps up in the end is so delightfully cozy and satisfying with a bit of chaos and hazard on the way. Highly highly recommend the Audible as a winter family listen.

2023 -We're listening to this series read by Allison Larkin and it's been the best example of a good reader making a good book better. This fourth book starts a bit more slowly but really picks up toward the end and wraps up quite satisfactorily. The addition of two new friends to the bunch felt a bit uncertain, but Ransome juggles the multiple characters well.

This series is abundantly wholesome. The best and brightest of British outdoors-loving childhood with a generous dose of adventurous imagination.
