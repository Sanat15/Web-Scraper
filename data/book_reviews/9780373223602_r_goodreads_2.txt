Rating: 5/5

Part Passion, Poison and the Paranormal

This one had me on the edge of my seat. The mystery element was handled so well that at first everyone seemed a suspect. Although there were enough hints for the observant reader to guess the killer’s identity, Noah’s secret was a doozy and kept me guessing the entire time, trying to figure out how much of a danger he was to Kit. And just as I was beginning to wonder where the romance was, the story picked up some steam. Mmm hmm. Main characters were well developed, secondary characters were handled well and you even felt you knew Kit’s deceased friend Jo a lot better as bits and pieces of her life were revealed to you. 

Since this is a renamed reprint of an older title, the only drawback was that the technology mentioned is outdated. Nevertheless, I didn’t let that stop me from enjoying an extremely well-told story.

My copy was provided by the author in exchange for an honest review.
