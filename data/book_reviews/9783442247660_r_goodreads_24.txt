Rating: 3/5

The real gamble was calling this an X-Wing novel. The flying and space combat was very minimal.
I would say this is closer to a spy thriller, which is fine, but it feels nothing like the first book. Mostly this one is setting up the next book. 

Minor spoilers:  There were also a few moments that felt like it was too well timed, like astronomical level of coincidence timing that doesnt make much sense. Stuff happened at the same time because the author needed it to it feels like.  

Thankfully I like Stackpoles writing style, and it was quick and easy to get through the book. I still like the characters and even though the plot of the book was just OK, I'm still looking forward to reading the next one.
