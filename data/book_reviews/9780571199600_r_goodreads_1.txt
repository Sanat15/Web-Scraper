Rating: 4/5

Interesting read. Knocked it out in a few days. What could, from the plot synopsis (gay ex-porn star lives across from Chassidic Yeshiva and befriends some of the devout religious boys who attend) be a predictable story of homosexuality and pedophilia turns on a dime into a gentle look at two disparate communities: gays and ultra-Orthodox Jews, and their seeming fascination with each other and their disdain for each other. Ultimately, it comes down to the age-old idea that we are more alike than we are different, and we all can learn from one another.
