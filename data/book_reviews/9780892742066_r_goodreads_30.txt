Rating: 5/5

A testimony of how Lake (one of sixteen children) who had lived his childhood with at least one invalid present in his family. Eight of his family members passed away before he heard that God heals in the present and healing did not pass away with the apostles.

After marriage, his wife became ill and alternated between fainting spells and being bedridden. After he heard that the healing power of God was available in the present through prayer, he prayed and saw four members of his family healed; including his brother who had been an invalid for 22 years; his sister who just died, but was healed and obtained life; and his wife was healed.

Lake never sought to have a healing ministry. After people heard of his wife's healing, people just showed up at their house to ask questions about her sickness and her healing, the Lake's would pray for the visiting enquirers and they would be healed.

Lake talks about having a life of consecration and a life of dominion over sickness and disease. Lake said when we read something in the Bible or hear a testimony of God working in someone now, we can walk/receive it too. God wants our soul to experience intimacy with Him and walk with Him in a present living reality.

Lake refused to be elevated above others, but considered himself equal to others and chose to refuse a hardness of heart, to walk in kindness and mercy, to be a peacemaker, to glorify God through obedience to the principles of Jesus Christ by the power of the Holy Spirit and live to demonstrate the righteousness of God in all he did.

This book is testimony after testimony of God's kindness in healing others. It also has the testimony of what he lived through with his family and how that birthed a compassion for people and a hatred for sickness and how it robbed people. His focus was God and bringing Him glory and seeing people set free from the bonds of sickness. This book was very honest, not hyperbole, and very encouraging.
