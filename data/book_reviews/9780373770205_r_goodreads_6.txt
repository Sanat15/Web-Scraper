Rating: 3/5

2.5 Stars
I’m torn how to rate this. I read Once in Paris after Lord of the Desert, (LotD) and I’m biased. (Team Gretchen) Which I admit, wasn't her fault, but I can be a bitch with a grudge.. so, sorry not sorry. On one hand, I couldn’t stand Brianne…like at all…but…on the other hand, she gets stuck being second best to an awful H... so, karma?? I’ll try to be vague but will spoiler the rest. 

Brianne is studying in Paris when she meets older widow Pierce. Pierce isn’t dealing well with his beloved Margo’s death. (2 years earlier after a long battle with cancer) Brianne is smitten; Pierce is not. The H from LotD is portrayed as a villain, and Pierce agrees to marry Brianne to deter him from pursuing her. This sets up the drama for the rest of the book with some action on the side.

I didn’t like Brianne, not only was she a thorn in Gretchen’s side, (LotD) she also acts like an immature brat. I get that she’s only 18, but if we’re to believe in a romance with a man double her age… then she needs to be somewhat mature. Also, I’m not a huge fan of the h being second best, BUT in this scenario, he married her to save her. Not because he loved her, and not because he wanted a relationship. So, it felt a little unfair when she was pouting and hating on the ex. BUT, by the end when they decided to have a real relationship… I was totally ok when she screamed “I don’t want to hear about Margo” because frankly, I didn't want to hear about her anymore either, and she was supposed to be his #1 girl. Pierce is the typical cruel DP H, but I felt he was lacking any real charisma, so he was frustrating too.

Bottom Line- I still think Brianne’s personality would have bugged me if I had read this first. I know I would still have thought Pierce was an ass. The book is ok, but proceed with caution if you have little patience with an immature h, a jerky H, or the dead ex/second best dynamic… because even by the end, I still thought she was the consolation prize.
