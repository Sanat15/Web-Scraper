Rating: 3/5

This is a short, charming, self-published book about Florence Hardesty's adventures when she--a professor of psychiatric nursing--married a nurseryman in Silverton, Oregon at age fifty. Her husband Verl grows exotic and unusual trees and plants, and has carved a niche for himself as a specialist in this market to wholesalers, selling thousands of items every year. Life around the nursery is never dull and Florence manages to cover plant propagation, Spanish-speaking workers, adventures with livestock, and her own family life with great humor and wonderful eye for detail.
