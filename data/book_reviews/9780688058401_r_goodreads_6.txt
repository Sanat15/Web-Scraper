Rating: 4/5

A better book than I've expected and somehow different from other ones from Mr. Follett: more rhythm and action, less descriptive passages and unnecessary little talk. 
The plot is interesting, things are going fast, it's a real pleasure to enjoy up to the last pages. 
All the people involved seem to escape unharmed, till the grotesque murder of Tony's mother. That breaks the spell, somehow sadly brings us to the reality and decreases the number of stars...
