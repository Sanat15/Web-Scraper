Rating: 4/5

Incredible story that's based on an interpretation of historical information, the most significant being lost logs/diaries found years after the explorers remains were found, but a fictional account none the less. The discovered photos were eventually developed and printed clearly in this book.

I really enjoyed the second half of the book when the expedition starts. The entire first half that covers the expedition preparation is completely forgettable. Overall this is a must read if you are into arctic explorations, especially those at the turn of the 19th century. Not many people know this story and is easily one of the most interesting based on the mystery and artifact evidence ultimately found.
