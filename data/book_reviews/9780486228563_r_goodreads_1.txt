Rating: 2/5

The first half had lots of detail providing some interesting historical perspective. Obviously, having been written in 1911 this history was primarily 19th century, however, and therefore of somewhat limited value at least for someone like myself will essentially only work with newer instruments. The second half (details about founders of various piano manufacturers) was SO detailed that it was quite difficult to get through - especially as many of those companies have long since vanished along with virtually all of their pianos. 

As a random side note, it was fascinating to see how quickly prognostication from this time period runs afoul of the 20th century technology explosion! Can hardly blame him, but nearly all of his speculations about the future turned out to be wrong in a shockingly short period of time.
