Rating: 3/5

Surprisingly solid for a random book I picked off the shelf. Pretty generic fantasy story, but some fun ideas.

What makes it so fun, for the unaware, is that this story is an isekai where a Minnesotan journalist in 1990 gets sucked into a fantasy world. And she's a truth-seeking, gun-toting, taekwando-knowing badass who don't take shit from nobody. 

The best parts were the culture clashes, and her convincing the main guy that his city is so crazy. It is a smidgen white-saviour, especially since there are so many Native-American references, but I forgive it for that. It isn't a dominating theme, and it's fun to see the two leads rub off on eachother.

I also bought the second book so I'll read that eventually. I hope it's incredible because this was supposed to be a trilogy but it's never gonna be finished so I'd love to be left yearning.
