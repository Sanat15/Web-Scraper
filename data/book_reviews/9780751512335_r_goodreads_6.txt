Rating: 4/5

The inspector Felse mysteries are among the best of the right sort of mysteries, the right sort being those I like, of course. There are tantalizingly few of them.
In this, there is a musical gathering and seminat on folk music at an institution housed in what was once a neogothic monstrosity of a mansion called Follymead. 
I guessed the plot and reason for the murder in chapter 1, but I didn't care because I really enjoy Ellis Peter's writing, her characters, her commentary, and in this one, the many bits and pieces about folk music.
