Rating: 4/5

I mean, it's a regency romance. There's not really any surprises here, but it's enjoyable nonetheless. I usually read a few of these a year, generally as audiobooks, because they're relaxing and don't really require a lot of brain power (and MC Beaton is a reliably good author).

One squabble I had was with the production of the audiobook. When they cut it into fifths for storage/downloading on Overdrive, they managed to chop the last couple seconds off of each section, so you would miss the last half of a sentence, or at the end of the audiobook, the website for the audiobooks themselves (all I heard was "www.&quot; and that was it).

I didn't initially think I was going to like the narrator, but I found her surprisingly enjoyable after the rough start, and she had different, distinct voices for each of the characters.

Recommended if you like the genre.
