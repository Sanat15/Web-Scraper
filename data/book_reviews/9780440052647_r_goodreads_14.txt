Rating: 2/5

This was a tough read... Things have obviously changed since the day it was published, part of the reason why I was curious about it. But I was triggered so much time and at so many levels I quickly regretted starting it. What's more, I feel like the parts with the men's letters were way too long compared to the parts where the author actually tried to analyze it. In the end, it just felt like a big porn book with no actual scientific or psychological inputs. I had the intention of checking the author's other works but I might just not.
