Rating: 5/5

"The Crimes With The Big Hotel"

This is a story you'd enjoy while on vacation at a beach-side hotel. That's where this mystery begins at a posh California hotel. When the security director of the hotel abruptly departs, assistant manager Am Caulfield steps into the job. The newbie security detective is very soon overwhelmed when the weekend turns into a murderous mystery. There is much suspense here, as well as funny situations, as the author has written a very witty novel which will have you laughing out loud. This story can't be beat. You'll be happy you read it!
