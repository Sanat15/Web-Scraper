Rating: 5/5

This one was a little rough. It took me 5-ever to read but honestly I don’t know how much was the book and how much was just me. I almost rated the book at 4 stars because of this but I really did love it. Walker Boh and his character growth is one of my all time favorites. Even when he made me want to bang my head against the wall because he kept dragging on, I still loved him and the authenticity to his character. The Maw Grint storyline wasn’t my favorite because I struggled to relate to it but it was interesting. I really liked Uhl Beck and I’m hoping we see him later on. A character made of stone is SO cool!!!! I also was really really hoping that Pe Ell had a redemption ark because I really loved his character and his sly-ness :( BUT his going mad before being killed was SO fun and I gobbled those pages up like a madman!!!!! Morgan Leah had a really cool story line and growth as well and im sad that he lost the love of his life BUT his additude about it also made me really happy and emotional. ALSO ending that Walker got turned into a Wraith?!? WHAT?!? And the King of the Silver River being sad about losing Quickening also made me really sad. I love his character and I love that we’ve gotten to see him throughout the ages and books. Also Cogline dying!!!! NO NO NO! But being back with the moor cat (sp?) at the end made me fist pump. So much happened that I don’t even remember where Par and Coll are hardly and I don’t even remember the cousin’s name OOPS but I’m on to the next!!!!
