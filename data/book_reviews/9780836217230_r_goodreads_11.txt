Rating: 5/5

Lynn Johnston's daily comic strip is one of the most lovely and true-to-life story-telling epics I have ever seen. The other ones I can name, Funky Winkerbean, Lil Abner, and Gasoline Alley are also all comic strips. They mean so much more to you if you have been following them faithfully over time almost as if it were a religion. They make you laugh. They make you cry. They make you see the ridiculous world we live in with all its injustice and stupidity with new eyes. And they make you love it. This is the story of one family as it grows, learns, lives, and loves. It becomes your family, if only you are willing to read it right.
