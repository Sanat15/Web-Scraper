The outstanding book Gulliver's Travels By Jonathan Swift is a book about a traveler that goes on island and is very big or very small. When he is big he helps with wars and captures the Belfuscudians the other war team so after 3 years he finds a boat and heads home to his family. Then when he is trying to go to Africa there is a big storm there boat gets misdirected to another island. On this island he is small and the people are 40 feet tall. He is found and taken care of by the splacknucks the tall people and in return he preforms for the people then goes home. I thought this book was very entertaining and never got boring there was always some thing going on. The author did a good job at getting your attention and keeping you to read the book. This book has great value because it is a adventure and has good ideas. The book could have had more talking so you knew what was going on it was very hard to keep on track with it. The characters voice was really good it was powerful and full of excitement. A life lesson is that if your big or small does not mean you can be mean to each other. He could have killed all of the Belfuscudians but he did not because its not the right thing to do.
