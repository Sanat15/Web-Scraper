A very interesting read. I hadn't realised how many intrepid female travellers there were, particularly in Victorian times (I love those crazy Victorians). The book was arranged by ethernet of travel rather than looking at each traveller in isolation, which did mean that some women were featured in multiple chapters. It did help by outlining some of the challenges unique to female but sometimes made keeping track of exactly what each outdraw amazing women did a bit difficult.
