Rating: 4/5

Wow, I'm speechless. This is the first time I've read a Batman graphic novel, and it has left a lasting impression on me.

The story and color-palette are truly exceptional, offering a reading experience that's vastly different from the black-and-white world of manga. The vivid illustrations and compelling narrative draw you in, making it hard to put the book down.

Kudos to m&amp;c for their excellent work in publishing and translating this comic into Indonesian. Their efforts make these graphic novels accessible to a wider audience.

However, it's unfortunate that American superhero comic books have a limited readership in Indonesia. This often forces fans to read them in English, and finding and purchasing these comics can be quite a hassle.

In this era of globalization, I'm curious if there are digital comics available for consumption like streaming services such as Netflix. Such a platform would make accessing these fantastic stories much easier.

I'll be on the lookout for digital options. This experience has certainly piqued my interest in exploring more graphic novels.
