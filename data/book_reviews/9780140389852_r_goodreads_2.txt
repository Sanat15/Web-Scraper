Rating: 3/5

This story has a clear message but doesn't take it as far as it can go. Not a negative, rather it could lead nicely into further reading/discussion with students at school. It is also quite a short story and spends a lot of time building up the plight of Rosa, the refugee from East Timor. As a result we champion her cause, but have little interest in other characters in the story, with the exception of the protagonist, Andrew. 

I would recommend this for students in grade six onwards due to the mature nature of the story - our protagonist is in year 9. It has some swearing, a drunken sister and much discussion of boyfriend/girlfriend relationships.
