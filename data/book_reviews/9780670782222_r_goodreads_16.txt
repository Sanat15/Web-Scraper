Rating: 5/5

Read this favorite many times when our girls were little. Recalled it was on the shelf and got it out to refresh my memory of the spunky little heroine. Madeline's 11 fellow friends want to get their appendices out because they visit the hospital days after her surgery and see only the upside of the traumatic event. Personal corollary: When I was in kindergarten, I broke my arm, then got chicken pox and was the recipient of much fuss by sympathetic family and friends. Hoping for similar treatment, my three-year-old sister used the bath sponge reserved for me by mom to possibly limit the spread of chicken pox in our family. My sister did get the chicken pox, but not the attention she hoped for. Rather, poor thing, she was kept home from Halloween trick-or-treating. Sibling rivalry was born! 😏
