Rating: 5/5

I really enjoyed it, read it in one sitting, I loved the gothic tones to it, it's obviously taken inspiration from Phantom Of The Opera. It's a nice story that gets straight to the point early rather than later which I like, the hero and heroine were both very likeable, I loved their interactions and could feel the tension ad chemistry through the writing. I just adore Allan Kass's art and this book cover is many reasons why, although I am suprised he didn't add Vincent's scars in the art, he normally gets every detail in but that doesn't matter, the art is stunning. Vincent is a nice hero, he's brooding and tortured but basically has the heart of a poet, and Christine is sweet and innocent but fiery in a good ladylike way, in fact both of them give me Laurence Olivier and Vivien Leigh movie characters vibes which is interesting since I always thought the two of them in the artwork look like Laurence and Vivien too. While 80s and 90s regency's are usually innocent in general which is why I read them, this one was extra innocent in a way which I liked very much, not much else to say, an enjoyable fast read, you can't really go wrong with a country mansion covered in snow with a scarred hero.
