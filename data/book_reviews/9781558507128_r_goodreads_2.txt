Rating: 5/5

Kitty Weirdness Scale revealed

Great book with brilliant art pictorial descriptions of weird behavior. I am afraid that all of mine are off the scale. However, it is difficult to find a behavior that is not in the book. Except for washing the canned food in the water dish like some sort of raccoon.
In the back of the book is an attempt to help you deal with these little (ok maybe big) weirdies.
