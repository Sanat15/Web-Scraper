Rating: 4/5

I'm reading (and re-reading) ghost stories for the Halloween season.

"The day we moved into Monkton Mills, I made an enemy of our new landlady."

Reading this made me remember why I loved Mary Downing Hahn stories so much when I was younger. It's nice to find out that not everything I liked back then sucked. (Trust me that is not always the case.) This story was more than just a ghost story. It was a story about friendship, love, death, loss, and how to carry on when you lose someone important to you. It's a story about pain, and redemption, and the power of forgiveness with a nice helping of ghosts, and time travel. What's not to like. I may or may not have cried when I read this especially during the talk Ashley has with her mother in the end.
