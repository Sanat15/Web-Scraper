Rating: 4/5

The second in Carol Drinkwater's memoir series about her changing life and her passion for her new home on the French Riviera. I do think its a pre-requisite to read the three books in series since it does follow a logical chronology although Carol does clue in readers of Book 2 with the necessary facts even if you haven't read book 1. Still, it works better in order.

The Olive Season is stronger from a purely 'craft' perspective. She moves her reader more than in book 1. She creates more empathy. She has a clear story arc. She develops her characters rather than just telling us about her friends and family. Don't get me wrong, I totally enjoy all the Olive Farm books--so much so that this is my second time reading them all. They are very entertaining. 

If you are a francophile, especially one bewitched by Aleppo pines, olive groves, the Med and its cliffside roads, you will also love this series. 4.25 stars.
