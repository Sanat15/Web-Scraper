Rating: 5/5

The kids were quick to offer up their five star rating for our latest book in the “If You...” collection. For a nonfiction book written in the Q&amp;A style, one would imagine it would be pretty dry. However, it’s really not! There are so many interesting tidbits to learn and though not a “living book” it’s written engagingly enough to keep my little group interested. It was fun to make connections with other books we have read, too.
