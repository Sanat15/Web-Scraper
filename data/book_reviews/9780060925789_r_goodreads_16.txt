Rating: 5/5

This book truly gives you the same warm and fuzzy feeling you have while eating a home cooked meal made with love. It was sprinkled throughout with wit, cozy anecdotes, and recipe gems. 

The most delightful part about More Home Cooking was Colwin’s writing voice and style. For example, here’s an excerpt from a chapter about making turkey for the holidays: 
“A turkey without seasonal angst is like…..a winter without snow, a birthday party without candles. For better and worse the exhaustion, the exhilaration, the expectations, and the complications are a kind of emotional condiment, the secret element that gives turkey its essential spirit.”

I bought this book without realizing that this was the second installment. (They each standalone - there’s no requirement to read them together or in a certain order.) So I’ve gone ahead and purchased its predecessor, Home Cooking. Looking forward to it!
