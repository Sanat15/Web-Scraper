Rating: 3/5

This artist was new to me, so very interesting to learn about. The essays in the book were informative, but I wished they had talked more about the art itself and not the different and conflicting styles in which Severini worked, although some of the politics behind it all were quite fascinating. Really liked the earlier Severini and his futurism and more abstract works. Not a fan of cubism and Severini's cubism seems (although technically proficient) so controlled, so...unnecessary, like someone trying on a fad almost. Seems like the artist's talent was limited or restricted by his uncertain artistic and art political aspirations. Nevertheless, his gift for capturing movement and his eye for color combinations are phenomenal indeed.
