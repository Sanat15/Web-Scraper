Rating: 4/5

Wow! I'm so shook. Like, all of the twists. I didn't expect majority of them. The entire book kept me on this wild ride and I loved every minute of it! So suspenseful and thrilling, truly shocked by half of the twists! And that ending! Oh that ending! If you're into page turners that you can't predict, THIS is the book for you! I plan to read many more by this author now!
