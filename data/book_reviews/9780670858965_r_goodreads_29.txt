Rating: 5/5

One of the best standalones I've ever read; I'm not sure I have the words to do this book justice.

📖 The Lions of Al-Rassan is a Historical Fantasy set in a world similar to Medieval Spain. We mainly focus on the intertwined lives of three people caught in a holy war between different cultures and religions. As their world faces conflict, they must navigate their personal loyalties versus their duty to their people.

✨ Review ✨
I was instantly drawn into this book. I knew I was going to love it, but it wasn't until I finished it that I realised just how tragically beautiful a story I had just read. This one will stick with me.

The prose itself was beautiful. Kay very quickly brought this world to life. It was captivating. This isn't a Fantasy world filled with magic, and medieval Spain isn't something I have extensive knowledge of, but I felt transported.

The character work here is phenomenal, and the way he established such individualised, nuanced characters in such a short amount was fantastic. I very quickly felt like I knew the people we were sitting with. It also surprised me to see such strong women throughout this story. 

Not only did I love them as individual people, but their relationships and dynamics with each other were incredible, complex, and so meaningful. 

I honestly just can't find the right words, but this was a beautiful and complex story of love and cross-cultural friendships versus duty to their people. 

It explores the human cost of wars fought in the name of faith, and the fragile possibility of cooperation and coexistence. How love, friendship, and humanity can flourish between people of different cultures, even as political and religious beliefs push them toward destruction.

This was a powerful cautionary tale but also a story of love and genuine respect. 

Beautifully haunting.
