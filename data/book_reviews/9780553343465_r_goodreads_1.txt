Rating: 4/5

I really, really like to read cookbooks and this is an old favorite. I had a paperback copy which fell apart after years of abuse, and I recently found a hardcover at my library used book sale. All the carbohydrate-phobics should note that the author's emphasis is on low sugar, whole grains, and vegetables. The soup recipes are excellent and easy to prepare, although cooking time is too long to make for dinner after a long day at work. Her lentil and rice soup, vegetable soup with pistou, and alpine vegetable soup are among my favorites--and I love to cook soup. A meatloaf with shredded cabbage is one of the least successful recipes in my household. I'm accustomed to low-salt cooking, and usually cut the salt in a recipe by at least a third; however, in this cookbook I follow the recipe measurements for salt. Be warned that the cookies, quickbreads and muffins use minimal sugar--look elsewhere for indulgent sweets.
