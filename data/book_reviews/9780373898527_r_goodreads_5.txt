A girl from Texas moves to Alaska to get married. Upon her arrival, she finds her fiancé has mysteriously vanished without a trace. Desperate for answers, she approaches the pilot whom her fiancé mentioned in his last letter. The H falls in love with her and promises to help her find her fiancé, declaring his intent to win her openly. The H’s father takes his role as chaperone far too seriously.

I'm disappointed that the H doesn't follow through on his promise to openly fight for the h and win her over. He kisses her while she's engaged and vows to be persistent, yet the moment she discovers her missing fiancé, the H throws a tantrum and walks away. Where's the consistency in your persistence?
