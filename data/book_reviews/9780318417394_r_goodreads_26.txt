Rating: 4/5

“Like many other much-loved humans, they believed that they owned their dogs, instead of realizing that their dogs owned them.”

I was struggling to sleep due to fibro pain, so I decided to listen to the audiobook of 101 Dalmatians. I really enjoyed listening to the story and the original was so different to the Disney version, I think most of us grew up with and had that story in our heads when we think of 101 Dalmatians. 

The main difference is that Perdita isn't Pongo's wife but brought in to help nurse the puppies. Cruella is also portrayed differently- "She had a dark skin, black eyes with a tinge of red in them, and a very pointed nose." 

Dodie also lived just outside Sudbury, which is only down the road from me and I love that she included the town she loved. "Just before midnight, they came to the market town of Sudbury." Overall, I enjoyed the read and it felt from nostalgic.
