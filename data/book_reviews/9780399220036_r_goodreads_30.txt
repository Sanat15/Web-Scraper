Rating: 4/5

Since the Skippy Jon Jones in Mummy Trouble took the entire story time during my first event, I modified my program during the second event to begin with this old stand-by by Tomie dePaola (de-POW-la). It is long, but not quite as long and easier to shorten.

A fun song to do after this story is The Crocodile Song...

She sailed away (move one arm in front of you in a wave-like motion)
On a happy summer's day (outline a smile on your face with index finger)
On the back of a crocodile (move your arms to imitate a croc's jaws)
"You see," (point to eye)
said she (curtsy)
"he's as tame as can be-- (pet the back of one hand with the other)
I'll ride him down the Nile." (with your index &amp; middle fingers, pretend to ride your other hand)

The croc winked his eye (wink &amp; point to eye)
As she bade them all good-bye (wave good-bye)
Wearing a happy smile. (outline a smile on your face with index finger)
At the end of the ride (with your index &amp; middle fingers, pretend to ride other hand)
The lady (curtsy)
was inside (rub your stomach)
And the smile (outline a smile on your face with index finger)
Was on the crocodile! (move your arms to imitate a crocodile's jaws)
