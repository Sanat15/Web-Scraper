Rating: 5/5

I'm sad because this is the last Nurse Sarah Keate book as I've read all the previous ones.

This seems to be either the first or an early Sarah Keate book because Detective Lance O'Leary is introduced and he shows up in a number of subsequent books.

This book finds Sarah working the midnight to 8am shift in a hospital on a ward where a patient is having radium treatment. Radium treatment in the 1920s and 1930s was not only novel but an expensive proposition as discussed at a dinner party before the radium was stolen.

It's tracking down the radium thief as well as a murder that keeps everyone at the hospital on their toes. And did I mention this takes place during a major rain storm? Nothing like rain, wind, and the occasional blackout to fry everyone's nerves.

Another great Sarah Keate book and well worth reading if you are into cozy mysteries set in the late 1920s.
