Rating: 4/5

What I like about these older books is they fit in during the original run of episodes. This one takes place just after the death of a major character (no spoilers here) and the fallout from that. Each character gets their own development and the story doesn't forget that it has a lot of balls to juggle.
I really enjoyed myself reading this; it capture that era into show and it felt like it could be a mini episode which is a good sign for a tie in graphic novel.
One for fans and casual readers too.
