Rating: 1/5

I always approach a prolific author who is new to me with great enthusiasm. If I like the book, there will be so many more to enjoy. But this one let me down. 

It was dreary, parochial, and each chapter had a neat ending as if the author was writing for a mildly amusing tv sitcom. It was almost Readers' Digest snippets of life, each rounded off in a satifying and positive way to give you something to talk about over dinner. People still had dinner back then. Now we eat in front of the tv. They didn't have tv dinners and sitcoms in the UK back when the author was writing.

I didn't finish it, and I'm never going to finish it. Just boring, boring, boring.

Recommended to those who read to go to sleep. It's very soporific.
