Rating: 4/5

Love the name...ronan...sigh... a older (than the average rake) a heroine older than our normal she is ..gasp... 30.

The rake has history with the heroines older brother, a scandal that sent him away for 20 years.... he has returned as a titled man...so his history is forgiven.... he rescues the heroine after a accident unknowing of her name but soon discovers it when he returns her to her pompous brother...this is where the story takes off as he places a bet on making her fall for him to get back at her brother more or less.... while escorting the lightskirts and dallying with the young women looking for marriage which he is not interested in......(for a good reason) ...and yet it was a wonderful story~

Thank you netgalley and Anne... it was wonderful!
