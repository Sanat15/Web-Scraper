Rating: 3/5

Glad I read this. It has been on my TBR for a while since it is has an extremely controversial history &amp; was very regularly challenged. Had to borrow from a private university as public libraries haven’t kept it on shelves (which I understand, with a copyright date of 1971 it contains a lot of outdated information on a very niche subject: revolution lol).

Fascinating read. I hope I never need to use any of the info though. It was chilling to read about how to make lethal weapons (including injectable darts) &amp; manufacture illegal drugs. Did you know certain toad skins can be dried &amp; smoked for a hallucinogenic effect? Banana peels can be made into drugs too. Household chemicals can be concocted into dangerous explosives. This book has the actual step-by-step for how to do all of that.

The chapter on surveillance/sabotage &amp; bugging was painfully outdated with the use of cell phones being so prevalent now. 

Pretty dark stuff. But again, fascinating nonetheless. Now I know how to make a Molotov cocktail!!! Just in case…
