Rating: 5/5

A brazen, and meticulously researched work of investigative journalism, Ms. LeBlanc writes a truly engaging story about the intricacies of urban life in Tremont, Bronx. She follows the stories of two couples caught up in the bustling and violent world of gang related crime: Of notorious drug-dealer, Boy George and Jessica; of Jessica's little brother Cesar, and his girlfriend Coco. 

Coco endures the unforgiving harshness of being a single mother to several children; especially to Mercedes, her outspoken and tough daughter; and raising them in a world of systemic poverty that is bleak as it comes. Cesar, George and Jessica all end up in prison, and the book also investigates and tells their stories behind bars: of loneliness, betrayal, and Jessica's romance with a prison guard that leaves her pregnant with twins for the second time in her life. 

With a cast of real men and women worthy of a Dickens novel, such as matriarchs Milagros and Lourdes; Frankie, Giselle, Torres, and Wishman, this book forces us to examine our own privilege, and what it truly means to live in an alternate universe where lives matter as much as our own.
