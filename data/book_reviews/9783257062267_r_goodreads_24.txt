Rating: 5/5

The narrator describes the demarcation and at times random co-existence of one's material body and immaterial thinking in a poignant and precise manner. It has resemblance with Virginia Woolf's Mrs Dalloway, even though using a different style of writing.

The narrator uses scientific language and analogies to describe existential and philosophical concepts as opposed to the more common known philosophical concepts and theories by Hobbs and Rousseau etc.
