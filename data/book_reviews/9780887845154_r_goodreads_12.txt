(Roch &amp; Carrier, 40)

Accuratley captures the emotional and cultural brevity of being colonized within ones own country. Extractionary systems expect the customs be respected, but don't afford the same liberty back. Britain and English Canadians to French Canadians. Wealthy legacy quasai-aristocrats to the modern working class. All one in the same. Not a repetition but a ryhme.
