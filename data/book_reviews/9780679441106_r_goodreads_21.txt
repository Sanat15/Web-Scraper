Rating: 5/5

This book was listed as Time magazines top ten novels of 1995. Anne Tyler wrote a story about 40 year old Delia, who up and leaves her family while they are on vacation. I know a lot of readers took issue with this mother abandoning her family, but it truly spoke to me. What woman at one time or another hasnt lost herself in family responsibilities, and doesn't even know who she is any more. Then add in a family who takes her for granted, and doesn't see her as a person in her own right. This wife and mother left, to find herself again. Ann Tyler got into her character's psyche so well. Her writing was superb.
