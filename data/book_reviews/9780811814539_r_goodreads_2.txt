Rating: 4/5

This book looks like a children's book and is published by Macmillan Children's Books. However I would not give it to a young child. It's delightfully warped for an adult or older child, but not appropriate for anyone in the age set it might - at first glance - look like it's for. 

Written in the form of an ABC book, some entries are just random:

"artichoke : artichokes have no connection at all with Santa Claus. The do not make good presents, and you cannot make a Santa out of an artichoke."

Some are bizarre:

"unicorn : the red unicorn, or Christmas unicorn, is like the Christmas zebra. It's an ordinary unicorn, but it is red, and it doesn't exist."

Some are the reason I wouldn't give it to a young child:

"steam : when Santa takes a bath, it makes so much steam that the elves get scared and run away. This makes Santa smile."

"India : in India there are little fakirs who think they are Santa Claus and try to fly. Children aren't fooled, however, and laugh at them."
