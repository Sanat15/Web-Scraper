Rating: 3/5

Reading as part of a Shakespeare project. This late period book of Shakespeare is one we don’t often see performed on stage,film and is lesser known. Shakespeare takes all he has learnt over his career and puts it into this play. A comic tragedy split into two halves. I had real problems getting through the first half. Akin to Othello in his jealousy King Leontes’ jealousy comes out of nowhere with no rationale after all his wife Queen Hermione is already pregnant and they have one son before King Polixenes arrives.
Time is even a character here and we move 16 years in Act IV and the play is much lighter. 
I was advised to watch the play at the same time as read it- I watched two separate productions on TV and fell asleep during both. It’s an intelligent play in parts but it doesn’t hold my attention like Hamlet, Macbeth, or A Midsummer Night Dream do, even throwing in a bit of Greek mythology couldn’t help me. I don’t know how to end this review as I thought the ending was terrible but technically it is a good play. Where else would you get a stage direction ‘exit followed by a bear’?
