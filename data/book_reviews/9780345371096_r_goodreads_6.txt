Rating: 2/5

(Read as part of General Practice omnibus.) Took me a looooong time to read. (In fact, I stopped and reread some stuff not recorded here. Then made myself finish it.) Do love the earnest care for individuals; didn't love the bizarre and unpleasant alien culture Lioren encounters. And then we got into looooong discussions of religon; and ... Yeah. Not my cuppa. Still, we get an outlandish operation at the book's climax; and a nice little revelation of how successful the operation was; and a satisfactory conclusion. Not a favorite, though.
