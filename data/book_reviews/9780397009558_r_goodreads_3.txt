Rating: 4/5

Another Lucy Ramsdale--Inspector MacDougal mystery. ame old Lucy trying to make everything come out her way. This book features a gay couple (back in the days when they were called only homosexuals) and is most interesting to see how the characters (and the author) handle them. Dolson is ahead of her time despite the fact that the Inspector, though he tries, has a bit of an old fashoned view. Lucy's attitude is spot on. Worth reading just to view this.
Fiance of a wealthy young woman is killed when he falls on the spikes of a modern sculpture he's purchased to be the center piece of a sculpture garden at a museum in Connecticut.
