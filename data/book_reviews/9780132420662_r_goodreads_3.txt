Rating: 3/5

Like most self-help books, some chapters are redundant. A few of the chapters could have been boiled down to two words -- Pay attention! I did like a few of the tricks. Pegging seems complicated. Visualization, linking and elaboration work better for me. The tips on simplifying math were great. (I learned them once. Now I'll remember them.) I now have an excuse for playing endless word games. (I'm doing my mental aerobics!) I was amazed by chapters with proofreading errors. Somebody's mind wasn't too sharp there!
