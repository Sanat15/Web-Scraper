Rating: 4/5

I think i will be reading more from Poe. I just ordered another full set of his stories.

Warped, gruesome and definaltly up my street for horror reading. These stories run so smoothly and i could feel the nervous crazed state some of these characters were in. The black cat had me shocked and cringing. This mans madness and erratic behaviour really had my face doing a workout of expresions while reading. 

The other stories within were just as good in different ways but its easy to see why this haunting set of tales was and is so popular.
