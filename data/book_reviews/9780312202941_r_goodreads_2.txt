Rating: 3/5

When the essays are good, they are amazingly good. When they are not, it is slow going. I felt the distinction usually rested on whether or not Codrescu had an explicit point. The writing is always great, but the pointless great writing is tiresome. I really enjoyed "Tolerance, Intolerance, Europe, &amp; America".

my favorite quote: "The problem with it is that it left out the one unpredictable element that usually renders nonsensical the best theories, namely, people's deep-seated and emotionally unassailable stupidity."
