Rating: 3/5

I love exploring these old picture books and seeing where their creators' careers eventually led. Roger McKenzie, for example, if Goodreads is to believed, went on to make a name for himself at Marvel, writing Daredevil for several years and even collaborating with Frank Miller. The plot of this book is nearly identical to another Masters of the Universe Golden Book called The Trap, which is a bit disappointing but not all that surprising. Like much of the kids' media from this era, these books were essentially toy commercials in disguise, leaving the storytelling as almost an afterthought.

What makes them interesting, though, is seeing the early incarnations of these characters before their personalities and lore were fully fleshed out. For instance, Zodac is described here as a wizard, which I found a little strange given his futuristic spaceman-like appearance. We also get to see Teela wearing her action figure's snake armor, and Man-at-Arms sans mustache.
