Rating: 5/5

While I have not read the original 1986 version, I can unreservedly attest to the simple genius of this 2021 updated edition. First and foremost, the illustrations WOW. They're the kind that, as a child, I'd've gobbled up for weeks and weeks and weeks until my eyeballs fell out of their sockets. And yet, the mastery of the simple language--the directness of the "Where are yours?" question posed at the bottom of each and every page, which explores each and every part of the human body as we currently understand it.

The page showing trans individuals AS trans individuals particularly moved me. As did the page which states, "Your genitalia belong to you." 

There is, however, a tinge of sadness for adults. Too few of us have lived so freely that to read, to understand, to comprehend fully such freedom destabilizes emotive calm. And I think that's perfectly legitimate. Feel how you feel, because this book gives it to you in as plain a language as is available, and that is its magic.
