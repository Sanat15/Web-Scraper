Rating: 3/5

I love getting to use my history degree. It was a very interesting book and I enjoyed it. Sometimes it was just a little too dense for me because I lost interest in different practices or time periods, but overall it still stands to this day!
Edit. I also wanted to say that I bought this book when I lived with a bunch of roommates from a used store down the road. I walked past this store countless times until I finally went in and the owner followed me around the entire time, but it was fun. This was the first book I saw and I wanted to dive into something that would’ve reminded me of a different study period and it definitely did. I love social history. I cannot wait to get her book on cannibalism.
