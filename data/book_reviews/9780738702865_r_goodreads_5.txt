Rating: 3/5

“Understanding the Tarot Court” by Mary K. Greer and Tom Little is a dense book.
 
In “Understanding the Tarot Court,” Mary and Tom include exercises and meditations for understanding and connecting with the court cards. However, the book is not what I was expecting because it wasn’t as straightforward as I hoped. Sections were bogged down with theory, like religious aspects. I ended up skimming the text more than reading it. I expected a nuanced breakdown of each court card, each court suit, and how the court cards relate to one another and to the major and minor arcana. There is a breakdown in the final chapter, but it’s not enjoyable to read because Mary and Tom listed each card with eight categories. Those categories are word lists that are broken up by semi-colors, not a memorable way to take and retain information.
 
“Understanding the Tarot Court” by Mary K. Greer and Tom Little is a dry read packed with information.
