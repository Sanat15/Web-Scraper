Rating: 1/5

It's 1978 and feisty 24 year old career woman Lisa is convinced that her aunt is being swindled by a golddigger gigolo, in Charleston, South Carolina. What ELSE can she do but don a green waistcoat-and skirt ensemble, tuck her silver blonde hair into the matching green turban, and traipse over by the gigolo's law office, to pretend she is the new temp secretary and find the Seekret, Incriminating Files evidencing Gigolo's Evil Swindling Schemes against her aunt, which he has been undoubtedly keeping neatly in his filing cabinet. Alas, I did not find out if she was successful as I was overcome with the strong urge to abandon the book at this point...
