Conversation with JK Rowling by Lindsey Fraser Is a book of an interview with JK Rowling. in this book JK Rowling is the narrator like she is speaking in an interview with the author of the book. JK Rowling tells us about her life and how she came up with ideas for books and a lot more. I gave this book a four star review because I like books with pictures in them. This book shows biography because there is somebody telling about their life. I would recommend this book to anybody that likes biographies.
