Rating: 3/5

Lisa thought the worst of Slade long before actually meeting him. She though he was playing her aunt a fool. Coincidence gave her a golden opportunity to find a proof that might discriminate him in the eyes of aunt when she went to face him with her accusations and he mistook her for his old secretary's replacement. 

I disagree with the other reviewers. I think this vintage novel of Janet Dailey was delightful to read. It is quite a good pass-time book. :)
