Rating: 5/5

A classic 

I had wanted to read this book when I read the synopsis and I am really glad I finally got to read it. It is a wonderful novel with very lovely hero &amp; heroine. I liked the secondary characters too. I kind of liked Caroline too, though I’d suppose I would really be in the minority here. 
I hope other books such as The conformable wife and The advantageous marriage become available too.
