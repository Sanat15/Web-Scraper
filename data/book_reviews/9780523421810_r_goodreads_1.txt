Rating: 3/5

I will be the first to admit that watching Knight Rider was a fair few years ago and so I cannot remember all the episodes or even if I got to see all the episodes but the one I do remember was the introduction of KARR.

Now this book pretty much reads like the episode I remember (like I say it has been a good few years) so I cannot be sure how faithful it is but there you go. As a good its a fun 170 odd page read but really if you knew the series (and come on who does not after all why else would you be reading this book) you can see where the story is going and how it will get there. 

This is the third (although thanks to me reading them out of order it is in fact only the second) book in the series you can already see the formula coming together of both the series and the books that were spawned from it.
