Rating: 3/5

This one was quite serious and disturbing. It deals with murder, pornography, and the question as to how far one should go in an effort to change the way people think… Anne Perry deals with it fairly well but leaves it mostly to the reader’s own idea of what is moral and right. The only thing she missed was in pointing the reader to the Truth which is Jesus. There is no morality without the word of God. Without the Word, anything and everything is open for one’s personal interpretation. There is no right or wrong only what you as an individual feel is right at one time or another. All you have then is chaos and disorder. 
God’s ways are not are ways and when we choose our own selfish way instead of his, we always go too far the wrong way and cannot imagine the damage we create and inflict on other people. Which this story does a good job at showing.

“.. but the values we adopt will be the ones the next generation will live by.” Pg 246 

Everything we do has a reaction, has a ripple, and will affect other people maybe far more than we realize. Choose your actions and those things you hold to be true with care.
