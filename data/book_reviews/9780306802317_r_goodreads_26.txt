Rating: 4/5

Dense, heavy with detail, day-by-day, sometimes hour-by-hour, account of the set-up and battle, very illuminating chapter on Vulture, Condor, and Albatross regarding the duplicitous behavior of the politicians and diplomats of the US, UK, and France, and how it guaranteed the failure of the siege of Dien Bien Phu, and how it later fed into the second IndoChina war. What a mess. But a great read. Not for the faint of heart, or the reader looking for a light hearted tale. Takes a while to get through, and some effort to keep all the details straight, but a very valuable read for those interested in the French involvement in Vietnam, and the later involvement of the US in Vietnam.
