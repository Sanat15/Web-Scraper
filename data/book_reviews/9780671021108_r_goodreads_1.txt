Rating: 3/5

This book could have been so much more, it touches on story lines but then never develops them. Think it started out to be a horror devolved to a cult and then just turned into an orgy rather than a thriller. Some of the characters turn into charactures and ends up just being shallow and hateful and even the so called good guys are not that great. Good premise but for me missed the mark. I felt sorrier for the dogs than the teenagers
