Rating: 3/5

I normally can't stand to read a play. It's more the formatting than anything else. However, I found the 1st 2 plays to be very entertaining. That was a nice surprise. Other than when Ra was speaking in the introduction, I hated Caesar and Cleopatra, but I half-expected that I would. The last play was just just as well-written as the rest, but I found the characters tedious. I rather liked the point he was making about people in general, but it didn't make those characters any less taxing.
