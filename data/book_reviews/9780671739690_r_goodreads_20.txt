Rating: 4/5

This was one of two romance novels that I read the summer my parents divorced. Prior to that I wasn't allowed to read romance. This novel was one of my of secret rebellions. 

The writing was crisp. The characters were very well developed. During their exchanges I truly felt I was there. I could feel the tension and I wanted to assuage it, very much like Maggie had wanted to do with Chase. I wondered why he was so harsh, embittered, and unrelenting. I also wondered why Maggie refused to give up on him. 

The story, was trying to impart that there is a reason for why people act the way they do. The reason may not be pretty or neatly packaged, but we can only ascertain a person's motives by being willing to listen. Some people may be born harsh and unsympathetic, but most are not. Chase was not born that way. The weight of family obligations and responsibility, as well as extenuating circumstances, had helped harden him against the outside world. 

Maggie is strong, but not harsh and this is truly a stumbling block in their communication. Their way of relating and ongoing growth from beginning to end is powerful and still relevant.
