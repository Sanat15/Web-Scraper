I consider myself a hobbyist in nutrition, so when everyone at work began drinking ionized, alkaline water I thought I should do some research to find out what it's all about. I read the first few chapters of this book and then went to do some research on-line to discover a lot of controversial information. The most useful source I found was on quackwatch.com. Basically, Robert O. Young (the author of this book and founder of an MLM marketing alkalizing products) has graduate degrees that are not creditable, has been arrested a number of times, has never published in any creditable journals, and has been highly criticized by many creditable medical researchers and doctors. His basic theory is that disease happens because the body's ph is too acidic. His critics say that changing the ph of what one eats will change the ph of your urine but that won't have any effect on the ph of your blood or other body systems. Beware people...lots of misinformation out there.
