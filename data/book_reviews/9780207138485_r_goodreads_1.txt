Rating: 4/5

Blue Willow was my mother's china pattern. I liked it as a child, and probably would have liked it even more had I known there was a story behind the intriguing pattern of trees, buildings, and birds.

Seems the design is based on a Japanese fairy tale about a pair of star-crossed lovers, OR . . . maybe it was a simply a story invented after the dinnerware was designed to promote sales. But, which is it - tale as old as time, or clever marketing scheme? It depends on which internet site you wish to believe. Either way, this retelling was pleasant enough. As an adult, I'm not particularly wild about the pattern, but I've hung onto the set all these years as I have only the dishes, and memories left of my mother.
