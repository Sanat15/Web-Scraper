Rating: 4/5

Margaret Laurence was an educated woman who was ahead of her time in many ways. She was widely criticized for not behaving like "a lady" throughout her life, but I admire her gutsiness and her commentary on life in the prairies, and much of Canada's shameful history. Some of these essays got a bit intellectual for me. Her introductions about the circumstances surrounding when and how she wrtoe the essays were perhaps one of the more enjoyable aspects of this book.
