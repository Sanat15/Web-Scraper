Rating: 1/5

This is the first book in a long time I didn’t finish. I could not bring myself to continue as it was making me angry at myself for reading it. This book is not at all what I expected. I could not read anymore political ideology. The fact that the author felt he was veiling his racist attitudes and sexual exploitation of women was disgusting. His main character is supposedly rescuing women from men who want to rape them, yet he sleeps with every single one of them, most of them much much younger than him. He is just gratifying himself and his love of sex and guns. 

I guess I just can’t subscribe to his ideology!
