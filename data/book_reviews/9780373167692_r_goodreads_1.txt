Rating: 2/5

Despite what the author wants us to believe, actions speaks more than words and they all don't add up to true love. The H suddenly starts fantasizing about h who has been his best friend and then has a one night stand with the 27 year old virginal h. Then without protesting about pursuing a relationship with her takes off and doesn't even contact her for the next several months and then they meet at his engagement party to another woman. Then he gets a note stating she is pregnant and confronts her and she denies and he is happy to leave. He doesnt break off any engagement, just follows her and continues to have sex with her and makes a declaration of love all without ever promising her anything! Then suddenly the book ends abruptly with a HEA all without addressing the elephant in the room about his out of sight, out of mind and out of contact response after the one night stand.
