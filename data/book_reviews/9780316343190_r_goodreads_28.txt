Rating: 5/5

Utterly delightful! Helene Hanff's engaging wit never disappoints. I loved every bit, but in particular the chapter "No Legs No Jokes No Chance," which was the seedling for this, her first book.

I also love that she worked in the box office of a summer theatre with former schoolteacher (later turned character actress) Reta Shaw. Neither of them could do math... with predictable results. lol
