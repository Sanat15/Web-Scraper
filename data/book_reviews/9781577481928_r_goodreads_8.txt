Rating: 4/5

I love that this book is small and easy to bring along or have sitting on your night stand. It’s laid out well and very simply for referencing subjects in scripture. I find it helpful for a quick reference or bit of the word. 

It’s 4 stars and not 5 just because it’s very old school and basic. And that’s still ok— it gets the job done. It’s just not going to “wow” you with the layout or aesthetic.
