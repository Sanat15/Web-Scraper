Rating: 4/5

This book helped me recognize the value of following my instincts - in ANY kind of potentially dangerous situation, though it was focused on the situations surrounding children. It may seem like it would plant fears into the reader, but for me, it did just the opposite. He gave me plenty of info to allow me the freedom to celebrate fear and it's purpose, in dangerous situations, while recognizing when unfounded fears can cause unnecessary spin. He also provided some games to play with kids that can help them fine-tune their sense of observation, their ability to make wise judgements and follow their own instncts.
