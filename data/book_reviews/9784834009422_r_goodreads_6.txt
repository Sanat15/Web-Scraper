Rating: 3/5

Anno's U.S.A. is a wordless picture book representing the many scenes of United State. The beautiful images tell the story of a traveler exploring and crossing the United State from West to East many years ago. In every page there are details that are typical and representative of an area including elements from the past and the present.
Hawaiian dancers next to a surfer in Hawaii, adobe homes and Native Americans from the South West, Jazz bands playing music and a boxing match in the streets from the South, Betsy Ross sewing the first American flag and the signing of the Constitution in Philadelphia are some of the features that are displayed in Anno's illustrations.
It is a excellent book to show young students how to search for details and to initiate conversations. I can see students visiting this book several time and finding every time something new to share. Every page in Anno's book becomes a dare to find out new and more exciting details.
