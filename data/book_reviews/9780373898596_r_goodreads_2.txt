"Southern Nights" is the story of Barbara and Todd.

Oh LORD! SO GOOD!!

Come along for a super passionate and angsty ride in this gem by Janet Dailey!

When our h goes to meet her new fiance's family, she is nervous but excited. What she does not expect is the unthinkable- the man who broke her heart after a brief affair is her fiance's step brother!

But instead of letting her ago, he relentlessly pursues her, declaring his intentions, branding her with his kisses and reminding her of their passionate interludes. 

The h is torn between her caring fiance and her possessive tormentor. That forms this roller coaster of a read!

I cried and thoroughly enjoyed this book! Even with the way the h described the H, we never feel any resentment towards him, and he came off as a caring but obsessed man- she on the other hand was confused but knew that she could never forget the H. She still tried to continue her relationship with OM and that's my only grievance with this book. Also enjoyed the small parallel the author made between the mother's circumstances and the h's. 
No negative or bitchy characters, just really tangled relationships in this unlikely love triangle.

Recommended!
