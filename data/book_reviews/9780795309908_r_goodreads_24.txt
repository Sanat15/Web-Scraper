Rating: 4/5

3.5 stars - I'm giving this a little bit of extra credit just because the atmosphere for the first 2/3 of this novel is just quintessential Christie in its setting and tone. It's such a delightful Christie take on a small English village where the local populace is being serially murdered. That said, the substance of the solution is fine, but its execution is far too melodramatic and over the top. All in all, an enjoyable book but not one of her greats
