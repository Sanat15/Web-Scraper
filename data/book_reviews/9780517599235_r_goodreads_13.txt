Sleep is a natural process. If your sleep is out of sync then there is something else deeper in your life out of sync. Discusses the 3 doshas and when out of balance they create issues. 
Vatta - movement
Kapha - structure
Pitta- metabolism &amp; digestion
Remember, everything that you touch, taste, smell, see or hear is metabolized. We human beings don't metabolize only food; we metabolize our sensory experiences and they become molecules of our body.
