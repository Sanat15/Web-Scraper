Rating: 3/5

It was entertaining, (and for that I gave it an extra star) but all the mistrust and jealousy between the H and h got to be silly at times. Also, the dumb wager that the H had too much pride to tell the h about (despite all the trouble it caused between them), and her gullibility in thinking the worst of the H and actually trusting a highwayman more than him was pretty ridiculous. The H being so concerned with the feelings of his ex-mistress was pretty dumb, too.

I won't give any more details, I'll just say it's worth reading, if you can overlook the storyline flaws.
