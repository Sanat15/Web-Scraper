Rating: 4/5

Madeira holiday book #5. Finished off after a return from a Sports Bar to see Liverpool v Chelsea. A 1-1 draw.

Compared to holiday book #2 ‘A Nuns Tale’ by Candace Robb this was more enjoyable. I liked the character of Roger the travelling Chapman And I had now moved from the 14th century to the 15th century with just the passing of wine, beer and grilled Octopus.

Of course this is not the first book in the series. It appears that Roger is now in his’70’s and recounting tales from his youth - 24 in this story. Clever investigative work by the young man solves the case of the disappearing brother. Enjoyable holiday read…
