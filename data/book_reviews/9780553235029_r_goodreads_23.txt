Rating: 3/5

This is a small paperback collection of interesting and strange short stories; I have carried it with me for some time. I have turned to it at conferences when there may be 5-10 min at times between speakers. 
It has come in handy when I have had short waits for appointments.
These very short short stories by well-known authors are a bit strange, and have left me thinking/puzzling over them when I should have been listening to the next speaker😂
