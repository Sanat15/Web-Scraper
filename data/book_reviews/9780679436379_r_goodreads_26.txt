Rating: 4/5

It’s the story about the animals, Mowgli and the rules of the jungle.

This is the first part of the Jungle Book and I absolutely enjoyed reading it.

The content is conveniently divided into short chapters which can be read as a short story each with some chapters as a whole poetry each.

The poetry parts took my heart. I would say the author is brilliant!

Regarding the writing, the language is fairly easy to read and understand, lives up to its young readers’ genre.
I would say I was totally engrossed in the stories and the writing got really beautiful and intense at times.

There’s revenge, friendship, found family vibes, enmity, adventure and fun.

I would say watching the adaptations of this book is not enough. The book has so much more to offer.
