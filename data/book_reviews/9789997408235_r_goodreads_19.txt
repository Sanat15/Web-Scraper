Rating: 2/5

This is supposed to be a Tristan and Iseult retelling and the main couple were extremely underdeveloped, to the extent that the first part of the book was almost entirely about a doctor and a French legal official attempting to match the above mentioned myth to historical sites in Cornwall, instead of revolving around our main couple as it should. The book continued to take unnecessary digressions until I'd finished it, and honestly, finishing it felt like a massive waste of time. The plot is foolish, ill-concieved, and feverish, and I would not pick this novel up again.
