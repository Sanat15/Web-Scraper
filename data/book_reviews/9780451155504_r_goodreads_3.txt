A few observations about the development of diets and nutrition:
1. At that time, the author already says to avoid processed foods, but now this extends to eating whole foods.
2. At that time, the author still advises to eat more fruits and whole grains, but now low carb and high fat is established. (also veganism or plat-based diet, but for me, it's compatible but less convincing)
3. At that time, less attention is paid to human evolution, but now paleo diet is one of the promising choices.
