Rating: 5/5

I love how this was not the first book in this series, but I had no trouble keeping up with the characters or the story even as a Matelli newbie. Wendi Lee twists together an intricate web of excitement, suspense, humor, and mystery full of red herrings to keep the reader turning pages. I'm an avid fan of Janet Evanovich's private investigator novels, and I found the same energy with Wendi Lee. I will definitely keep Lee on my author watchlist!
