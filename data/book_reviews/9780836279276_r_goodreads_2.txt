Rating: 5/5

Love Dad is literally a book full of notes. The book doesn't take long to read, but is well worth your time. Patrick Connolly left for work most mornings before his two boys were awake. While eating breakfast with his wife he would write them a note almost every morning. The notes were filled with challenges, whether it be school related or just to better them as people. Patrick doodled characters for almost every note also. Overall the book is very cute and touching. I would recommend this dads to read this book to their children. I think that boys who received the notes every morning highly benefited emotionally. The notes, I could tell, were all created with love.
