Rating: 3/5

This one was ok. I love Diana's tomboyishness though. The issue I have with a lot of these romance novels, not just from this series, is that they don't give enough time for the chemistry to really shine between the love interests. I did like the "hate to love" between Diana and Dantrey, but they didn't have a lot of time to enjoy each other's company. Except for the bushes, if you know what I mean.
