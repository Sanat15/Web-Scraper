Rating: 4/5

A telling account of a former slave. While he admits there are skills, and religion, that black Africans learned in their slavery, their conditions were far worse than the benefits. He argued that using the Bible and religion to support slavery not only distorts the Bible but is directly refuted by the treatment of slaves who chose to practice instead of work.

This was a tough book to get through. It is almost 250 years old and reads as such. Paragraph and sentence structure were lacking modern sensibility. There are no chapters. It is a worthy read but good luck slogging through
