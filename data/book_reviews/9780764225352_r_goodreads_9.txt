Rating: 4/5

So another book that I read in May and didn't review until now. (I own the whole series and was re-reading)
When Hope Springs New is a great book, and I like it more than the first one. I know what happens in all the books, but I can't help but wish alongside Elizabeth that she would have a child! and she never does and oh no it's awful and I hate it!
Content: hugs/kisses between a married couple. Mentions of blood, wounds. A boy is abused by family, but no details.
