Rating: 4/5

Aww love this story - it was brilliantly funny and entertaining !! What a great concept to grab the attention of children - a baby who can speak who sounds very upper class with it too!! This book reminded me a lot of the movie boss baby! Loved the relationship that the baby and his older sister had! They were a complete team who looked out for each other and supported each other with tasks that they found difficult!! I also loved how the baby grew up to be an author and made children laugh - how sweet!!
