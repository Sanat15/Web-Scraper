Rating: 3/5

Pretty standard nostalgic fun. Kind of simple and goofy but I still like these. Most of these have two major story paths with branches, but this really only had one. Also they did not use the scene cuts well, there are several times where branches lead you back to were you were before, which is typical, but in this case they did not pick a good spot. For example you rescue someone then later on the path you run into them again and they act like they never met you before, because they wrote that part from a different path and rejoined it in a weird spot. Anyway, it is what it is.
