Rating: 4/5

“Home to Harlem” gave me a vibrant, gritty look into 1920s Harlem—the good, the bad, and the real. I get why it was controversial, but McKay’s easy-flowing style made it super engaging. It didn’t paint all of Harlem, but it painted a piece of it—truthful to McKay’s world. Whether you agree with it or not, it’s life—and it’s a part of history worth reading.
