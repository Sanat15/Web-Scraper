Rating: 4/5

This sequel to Olivia and Jai chronicles the tale of the Eurasian Raventhorne family in Calutta in the late nineteenth century. Olivia and her two children live under the shadow of her deceased husband's reputed massacre during the 1857 mutiny. Determined to prove his innocence, Olivia and her children also struggle to be accepted into the racist British society in Calcutta, especially when the daughter of the family, Maya, becomes involved with one of the British civil service officers. While not offering the romance of its predecessor, The Veil of Illusion nonetheless possesses many twists and turns, figures seeking revenge and redemption, and delves into the depths of emotion felt by its characters.
