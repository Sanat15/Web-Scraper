Rating: 4/5

I thought that this was an enjoyable mystery with an exciting conclusion. The friendship between George Robinson and Wiki Coffin add so much to this tale. Who murdered Ophelia Stanton? That's the questions Wiki must answer. Once he determines who did the deed he must figure out how it was accomplished because the man has dozens of witnesses who swear that he was no where near the woman when she was murdered. To add even more mystery men commit suicide (or do they), disappear, and are washed to sea during a storm only to be returned on the next wave. It's an exciting mystery. The only thing that bothered me about this one was the author's overuse of the word meditated. It seemed as if every character used this word and I began to wonder when it would next appear.
