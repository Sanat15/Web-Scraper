Rating: 2/5

Heroine was a troubled teen, orphaned as a child, raised by a grandfather helpless to curb her rebellion. She ended up being pumped and dumped by the next door high and mighty rancher’son. Her teen pregnancy sent her Gramps over the edge and he kicked her out of his home. Heroine made her way from the country to the city and with the help of an older woman who befriended her and became her stand-in maternal figure, she gave birth to her son and scratched a living, raising him as a single mom. Seven years later, she goes back home for her grandfather’s funeral and naturally reunites with her baby daddy. I just didn’t buy the flamboyant love declaration when he dumped her so brutally after a one night stand and then did not lift a finger to find out her whereabouts for several years. If heroine hadn’t come back, he would have easily lived a contented life on his ranch with his extended family. I hate these long separation tropes, they only work if both parties were pining for each other over the years. But in these Old Skool romances, more often than not, the heroes brazenly continue sowing their wild oats while the heroines are the eternal piners.
