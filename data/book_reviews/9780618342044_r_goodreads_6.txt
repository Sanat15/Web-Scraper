Rating: 3/5

Zoo School seemed like a fun easy read aloud book as we started school this year. It took us a little longer to get through it than we should due to schedule conflicts. The end of it, for older children, is very corney. Honestly, they would appreciate Wizard of Oz a little better because the man behind the curtain isn't a speaking elephant. For younger children it's not a bad book, and seeing the children learn and grow in their weaknesses was a good thing. Younger children will find a speaking elephant fun, I think.
