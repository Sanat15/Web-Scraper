Rating: 3/5

I read this book as part of my quest to find the book I remember reading as a kid where a character remembers a license plate with MBA by reciting "mom buys apples." This didn't happen in this book, but it did have a character remembering the license plate HES by saying "hogs eat slop." This makes me wonder, did I read this book and then see a license plate with MBA in real life and remember it? I remember sitting in the car when my mom would quickly run into the store and memorizing license plates of cars in case someone kidnapped me. So maybe my would-be kidnapper had an MBA license plate?
