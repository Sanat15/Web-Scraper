Rating: 3/5

An interesting book by an interesting author, from 1960. Man has arrived on the Moon-by means of teleportation. However, triumph turns to horror, as humans discover a structure on the Moon, something that can't be comprehended by humans and also something that kills. Al Barker, super-macho man and a real 60s "Madman" type is sent to probe the thing. In a way, the story is a real disappointment--and this next should be considered a SPOILER ALERT---

 We never find out what the structure is and what its purpose is. This reminds me of Clarke's "Rendezvous with Rama," in which Earth is unable to make contact with some kind of alien spacecraft that passes through the solar system. The point seems to be--and it's applicable to Budrys' story as well-humans are TOO insignificant in the cosmos to bother about.
