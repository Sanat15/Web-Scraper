Rating: 5/5

This is the first book I read by Gardner - it was an old copy and smelled faintly of must - and has already been passed onto another. If you have time this is a must read. The book is heavy with characters - all well developed - and the full meaning of it doesn't really hit until a few days after finishing. Written in the 1970's it is most intriguing to see the way Gardner deals with issues of the time, ie Vietnam, racism, religion, etc and see how little times have changed. Oh, and the analogies are spot-on, sticking with the reader, ie, the description of a large woman walking like a domino!
