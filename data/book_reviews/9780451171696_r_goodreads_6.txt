Rating: 3/5

Overall, I enjoyed this book, but I was a bit disappointed by the second half of it. After Nicholas becomes aware of his feelings he seems to turn into a complete moron…didn’t behave that way with Belinda, so rather confused by that. 🤔

It seems that the author abandons the story of Nicholas and Joanna in favor of Dorie. Can’t see why the two stories couldn’t have progressed simultaneously. 🤷🏼‍♀️

There were some loose ends that never get resolved and, ultimately, it felt a tad unfinished. But it was entertaining, so I would recommend it.
