Rating: 5/5

A BBC Radio adaptation in three parts, dramatized by Joy Wilkinson.
This is my first audiobook ever and I loved it, the voices, the sound effects, just great.
Agatha Christie is the best when it comes to crime books and I am glad I choose her as my first audiobook.

Extraordinara "lectura", mi-am adus aminte de Teatru radiofonic pe care il ascultam seara, cand eram mica, iar Agatha Christie ramane cea mai buna.
