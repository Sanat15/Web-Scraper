I read parts of this for a book group. I love the idea of Julian. I love her visions of God as feminine. I love the comfort exhibited in much of her writing. I love that I can read a woman's voice! Some people are so shaped by the religion they live within. Would she have had these visions if she had been born a Hindu in India? The life of an anchoress is fascinating to me. I wish we knew more, about her life, and the lives of other anchorites. I've often felt so alone in life. I've read in the past about monks, nuns, and anchorites of various types looking for some consolation in my life that feels so solitary. I think the difference is, that the monks and nuns embraced and chose solitude in most cases. I don't think I would have chosen this solitude, if I'd been able to see a way out.
