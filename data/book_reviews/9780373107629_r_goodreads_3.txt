Rating: 5/5

I am totally giving this 5 Lamb Stars. Why? Because it was like a shorter, more modern version of The Tilthammer which is only my FAVORITE Lamb novel (published under Sheila Holland). I had to look up the published dates and they were approximately 9 years apart. I swear they read like they were written within months of each other. The H is truly aggressive BUT the h… That woman could kill a man at 50 paces with one lash of her tongue. If you want to read a story where the woman is very much aware of her worth, truly independent, loves her career with no intention of quitting her job for marriage and can stand toe-to-toe with any man than this is your story. Talk about a “Woman of Iron.” If you loved Tilthammer, then I think this is another that is definitely worth reading. However, I will admit that I didn’t read it for a long time because I hated the title. However, in the story, the “For Adults Only” references an argument between the H and h about love and whether you can confine it to a certain age and label it “for adults only.” Also, as a side note, there is melon and parma along with a salad eaten in the story BUT…wait for it…they also eat chicken in a basket at a pub. Fried. Food. In a Lamb novel! If there’s one thing I’ve come to rely on are Lamb h’s eating salads/melons with parma no matter how starved AND always taking showers (no seriously, no matter the time of day or how pressed for time, her h’s ALWAYS manage a shower and makeup). Just like Betty Neels’ characters eating the most heart attack inducing foods, wearing jersey dresses for travel (which seriously, knit really is the way to go when traveling) AND buying undies when shopping for clothes. And it’s always “undies.” I wonder sometimes about authors…
