Rating: 5/5

25 years of research, following 131 children and adolescents from 60 families in which the parents have separated and filed for divorce. One of the longest studies on the long term impact of divorce on children.

Divorce often affects children far longer and more deeply than parents initially expect, especially in their adult relationships, trust formation, emotional security, and view of commitment. The full impact is often delayed and becomes most visible in adulthood rather than immediately after the divorce.
