Rating: 5/5

While the writing is sparse in detail and characterization, something about the story of stone statues coming to life stuck out to me as a child in this book. They are not friendly creatures but rather heartless killers who march across the lands with cracks forming at every step which a strange rain washes away. Crushing and hurting the human children, this book brought me many a chill when I was young and I still remember it vividly.
