Rating: 1/5

I’d picked this book up from a used book sale for free, and I gave it no consideration then. On a beach trip this week, I grabbed it purely because I didn’t care if it got sandy.

I should have dropped it into the ocean- the book had a lackluster ending, and every character was forgettable and racist. Not because of the time it was written but because they were just racist. 

The only positive was that it was short enough for me to power through based on spite.
