Rating: 4/5

This was fun, I stayed up way too late finishing it. It was better than #2, though a bit of a weird read as I read it in an omnibus volume (because that's what the library had available) which I immediately forgot I was doing, and thus I was mildly confused whenever I noticed the percentages as they didn't match up with where it felt like I was in the story.

I won't say anything about the plot, it's probably best to go in without knowing anything.

I've now read three full length novels (#s 7, 2, 3) and three novellas (the collection Trouble in Triplicate) and I'm thinking I might just skip all the collections as I read through the rest of the series. Not that the full length ones are long by today's standards (see my wtf face when someone calls a 200 page novel a novella) but I enjoyed all three of the full length books, and the shorter ones... not so much.
