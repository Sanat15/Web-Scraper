Rating: 4/5

Reading a Horace Rumpole story (or listening to, in this case) is always a guilty pleasure. Familiar characters, the Chamber, and, of course, the Old Bailey. This collection of 7 stories perfectly illustrates the brilliance of Horace Rumpole. And oh, the characters - She Who Must Be Obeyed, Portia of the Chamber, Guthrie Featherston, "Soapy" Sam Bollard, and Claude Erskine-Brown who always reminds me of Frank Burns of the M*A*S*H* TV series. The cases are intricate, but Horace always prevails. Just a really fun and entertaining listen.
