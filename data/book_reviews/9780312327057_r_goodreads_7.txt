Rating: 2/5

This is such a boy book. All the important characters are male, and the women are there to be beautiful and sexy and self-sacrificing. What’s more, being a warrior is the only admirable way to be, even though it’s brutal and criminal too. It’s hard to believe this book wasn’t written in the 1940’s instead of having a major part of the story set then.

It also has nothing to do with Jinn as they appear in Arabian folklore. The bad guy could just as easily be any other kind of demon in human form.

It’s a pity, because the book uniquely blends the police procedural with the supernatural thriller, and it’s a real page-turner. But in the end it’s a retrograde understanding of what masculinity means that drives the whole plot.
