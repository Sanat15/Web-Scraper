Rating: 5/5

Wonderful historical novel of mid 1700's Scotland, when a Scottish prince starts a rebellion. Some Scottish people follow him, some don't. The young heroine of the story does a good turn for a gypsy girl, and for several years, the gypsies are doing good turns for her and her family. The gypsies tell her that if she can successfully ride a kelpie, then good fortune will come her way. When both her parents come down with typhus, she sets out to find a kelpie, so she can turn the family's fortunes.
