Rating: 5/5

4.5 -5 stars! February is Pirate, Privateer and Love on the High Seas month on Regan Walker’s historical romance review blog. This is a story she highly recommends that I own so I read it for this months theme. It didn’t disappoint and I really enjoyed the book. I’ve enjoyed all of the Cordia Byers I’ve read so far and fortunately I own most of her others. They’re older books and they have that feel. I love the adventure of these historical and action packed page turning love stories. Aside from a great hero and heroine this story contained a lot of really good secondary characters that wove some actual Caribbean pirate history into the story. The hero is a strong, proud Alpha, who really is a good man with a real love for our h. The heroine is a classic beautiful, stubborn, proud, feisty, and driven heroine, who can recognize her own mistakes. There are moments that may induce eye rolls with her actions, but she is smart and also has a very feminine side that CB balances out very well. Since there seems to have been actually very few sea faring females/pirates in history it does require a little suspended belief, but then that’s the whole point of fiction right! Just go with it and enjoy. I wish we would have had an epilogue just to get some more HEA time with our couple after they worked out all their issues. I’d definitely read this one again, so it’s a keeper I highly recommend.
