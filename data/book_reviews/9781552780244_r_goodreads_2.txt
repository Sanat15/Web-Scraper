Rating: 3/5

I was able to get hold of this book at The Book Stop Project one Wednesday afternoon as I was leaving a few books and bookmarks at the site. Admittedly, it was one of the most intact copies among the shelves. Marsha Boulton's "Just a Minute" zeroes in on the historical components of Canada, along with obscure narratives, and even reveals some facts that have been glazed over by the reading public such as the invention of the basketball, a Frenchwoman proving that what seemed to be a desolate frozen tundra can be habitable, a mayor's set of goals creating a ripple effect in the Northern hemisphere, among many others. While we may overlook some of the contributions that Canada has in the modern world, this fast read could help in building on the knowledge we have of the country.
