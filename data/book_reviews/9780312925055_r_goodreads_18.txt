Rating: 3/5

“Although he was surrounded by vigilant guards, the Night Stalker still had the ability to instill fear by nothing more than his chilling presence. He was the monster under the bed, the living nightmare of every child and of every adult”.
Un monstruo en toda la extensión de la palabra, es complejo entender la magnitud de sus asesinatos a tanto tiempo de distancia. Un mito a la fecha que malamente habrá servido de ejemplo para otros.
