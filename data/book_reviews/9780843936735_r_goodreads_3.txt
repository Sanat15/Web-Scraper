Rating: 4/5

4 - 4.5. I read this for Regan Walker’s July American patriotic month. This one is on her list and it is a very good one! Henke writes well, really enjoyed the story. Great characters although Quintin got on my nerves a little with his adamant lack of any trust and poor treatment towards the heroine. Good story line and the two romances were woven together very well. This was my first book by this author and I’ll definitely read another, which I already have. I can recommend this book.
