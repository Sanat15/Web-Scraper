Rating: 1/5

I am enamored of British cozy mysteries for relaxing reading. Here was a new series I thought I would enjoy diving into, another village with eccentric and lovable characters. Yes, there are the characters, one should expect to find, Dear Bob and poor Joe and a retired military officer now farming, etc. I was not, however, charmed by them— they came across as tedious as actual village life would quite often be. The fruitcake was also a prominent “character” in the story, apparently an object of desire, but were there nuts in it? Dried currants? Hard to connect to this inane story—even the baked goods were uninspiring in this insipid tale. Miss Seeton was certainly discussed a lot, but as for being part of the action?? I couldn’t see what all the hype was about. What uninspiring writing! Only thing more boring than watching cricket is reading endless inexplicable details about a match that didn’t advance the story. Only two hundred pages, so I slogged through, but no more will one waste her time with this series.
