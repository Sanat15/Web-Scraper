Rating: 4/5

This is tough to review because I enjoyed a lot about this, and I think Wurts is an excellent and beautiful writer, but I did have a hard time reading this throughout. I think my brain just couldn’t keep up with the elegance of Wurts’ writing style at times, so it was hard to focus on more than just one chapter every time I opened it up.

That being said, I liked the story of Korendir a lot, and enjoyed Ithariel’s addition halfway through. The book felt pretty episodic throughout in terms of the way it jumped from conflict to conflict, but I say that in a good way as I enjoyed the structure of it.

This is my first Wurts so I can’t say yet whether this is the best book of hers to start with, but I can say that I’ll definitely be reading more from her.

3.75 Stars
