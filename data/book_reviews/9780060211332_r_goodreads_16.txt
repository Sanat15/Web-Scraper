Rating: 3/5

1908, Art Atchinson Aimesworth lives with his little sister, Esther and his aunt and uncle. Art isn't very nice to his little sister - he is too busy having adventures and inventing things and eating candy. 
One night Santa sends a secret flying machine and Art, his best friend Spaulding and Esther go out on the adventure of thier lives - to the North Pole. They run into evil elves and when the elves kidnap Esther, Art realizes she means more to him than he knew. He sets out to save her! Adventure, mystery, and fun!
