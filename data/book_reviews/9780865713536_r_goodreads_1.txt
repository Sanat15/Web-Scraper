1997. A compilation of numerous authors who discuss theories, concepts, and examples of urban sustainability. The book centers on Roseland’s concept of the Eco-city, which is closely related to numerous other concepts such as Ecological Villages, Sustainable Communities, Ecological Communities, and Green Communities proposed by various authors. In exploring the subtleties of these differences, the authors cover details of ecological footprints, communities and housing, social justice, health promotion, economic development, and methods for assessment of sustainable communities. A good compilation of North American and European authors who provide varied perspectives.
