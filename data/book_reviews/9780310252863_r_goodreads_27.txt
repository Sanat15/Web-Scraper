Rating: 3/5

The personal testimony of Colson is inspiring, and it is when he tells this story that "Loving God" is at its best. Before he gave his life to Christ, Colson was in the Nixon administration and was involved in the Watergate scandal. The process of Colson understanding his sin and turning to God in his moments of darkness are written from the heart. 
However, the overall message of the book is weighed down by how Colson wrote the book. Comprising individual testimonies of different believers is interesting, but I ultimately think it makes the book disjointed. Overarching points are lost in the details of certain stories, and some of it seemed like filler. 3 stars.
