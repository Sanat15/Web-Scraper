Rating: 3/5

What a romp! Cadillac Jackson, the Big Mama's, Jigaboo Shampoo: it's just a crazy bit of black fun. It's not a serious read - but it did make me think about why some things are only racist when they come from outside the race. There would be a hit out on this guy if he wasn't black, and it would probably be justified. 
All that aside, it's a great, fun book.
