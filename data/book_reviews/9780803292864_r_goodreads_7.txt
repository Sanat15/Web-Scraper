Rating: 3/5

Check out my full, spoiler light, video review HERE. Written from 1915 to 1921 but published in 1928, so this is an old one. Cowritten with the wife of a coworker Lee Hawkins Garby. Heralded as the first interstellar ‘Space Opera’. Mostly a space adventure with intentional and unintentional humor throughout. Wow, this one is dated though, the writing, the characters, the science and especially the dialog. If you put this in the context of when it was written, then it’s still palatable but I’d suggest reading this as an SF history assignment.
