Rating: 5/5

The Eyes of Gray Wolf, by Jonathan London and illustrated by Jon Van Zyle tells the story about one lone wolf that has lost his mate and what he does on a lonely night out hunting. The illustrations are soft and beautiful as he travels through the icy world of Northern Canada. He finds another mate and as she sleeps he guards her as daylight comes.

In the back is a map showing the current habitat compared to 1700s and a list of groups that support conservation of the wolves on the North American Continent.
