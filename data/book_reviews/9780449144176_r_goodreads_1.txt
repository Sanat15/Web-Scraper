Rating: 4/5

Accurate final score probably more likely 3.5. Read this one back around 2010. Very strange book that has occasionally crossed my mind over the years, but recently I was trying to remember what it was and could not until finding some other books by the authour at a bookstore today in 2024. Don’t remember the book being absolutely amazing, but it was weird and unique and oddly memorable. It starts with a crazy time travelling through dinosaur era earth chase and eventually ends up on a planet where a weird psychic inter-dimensional worm being is causing the inhabitants of a nearby planet to go temporarily insane and commit shocking and horrible acts of violence. If you’re at a used bookstore and find this and enjoy bizarre blends of science fiction: ie. time travel and a weird cosmic worm entity causing an entire planet to go periodically insane as it screams into the void, this could be worth your time.
