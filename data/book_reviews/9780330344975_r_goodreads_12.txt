Rating: 5/5

4.5 Stars

This is the third book in the Poldark series. Book 2 ended with a tragedy for Ross and Demelza. This book starts where that one left off. The year is 1790- Ross is still in a state of grief. On top of that he is on trail for inciting a riot and murder. As well, he is on the brink of poverty. Just another day for our hero.
Demelza is trying to help Ross without his knowing. She is really the glue that holds so many together, but she never seems to realize her true worth.

I loved getting back to Cornwall and Ross and Demelza. Some new characters appear in this book- my favourite being Caroline Penvenen and her dog, Horace. Our villain, George Warleggan continues to plague the Poldarks- both Ross and Francis. I have never warmed up to Elizabeth Poldark, Francis’ wife. My opinion has not changed with this reading.

Hopefully, I will be able to get to Book 4 before long. I am anxious to see what the future holds for Ross and Demelza and now, Jeremy Poldark.

Published: 1950
