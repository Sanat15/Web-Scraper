Rating: 3/5

I had a real hard time with this book. Actually skipped ahead maybe 25 pages at one point, just to get past being annoyed, and then finished the book.
Firstly, I did not understand what the title has to do with the book.
But, more importantly, I had a real hard time with the story unfolding as, two people who are very, very interested in each other physically. They are both unattached. But they continually get in their own way, and way of the other. But, of course, in the end they end up wildly in love and talking about marriage. It is a short story, not a 600 page novel. The time of things not being perfect, could have been filled with other things, so many other things, instead of this boring, been done in so many different ways path.
