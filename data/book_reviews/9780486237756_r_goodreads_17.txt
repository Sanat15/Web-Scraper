Rating: 3/5

Another book read with Dickensian group. Wilkie Collins wrote during the same time period as Dickens and this group, happily, likes to widen its view with a variety of reads. The Dead Secret is an interesting story, though not in my list of favorite Victorian novels. It does possess a very major secret, an orphan (though not really young), a middle aged woman who is both mysterious and sometimes appears deranged. And there is a large “house” in Cornwall that is partially in ruins that will be central to the story of the titular secret. While some of the scene setting chapters did seem to go on a bit long, Collins did a great job of pacing and writing over the last several, maintaining tension while feeding out the steps of the solution slowly.
