Rating: 3/5

Set in Maine like most of Kings books this is about something strange hidden in the forest. The Tommyknockers as the people in the small town calls it. Bobbie and her friend Jim discover and alien space ship on the forest and strange things starts happen to the town. This book is more of a 2.5 - 2.7 stars. It isn't terrible, King rarely is but it wasn't great either. The ebook was about 1300 pages and I can't say that I was rushing to read it because of enjoyment, more to get it finished. The story was alright but didn't have much of the excitement as his other works.
