Rating: 2/5

Very amateurishly written, reads like poorly written fanfic. The characters are cardboard. Everything has to be told to us, nothing shown. 

The main male character is introduced as a rake, he never does anything remotely rakish apart from speaking in a languid tone of voice (the author seriously overuses the word languid). 

There is no conflict and barely any plot - now this isn't always a bad thing. It can work when the characters are vivid and believable. These are not.
