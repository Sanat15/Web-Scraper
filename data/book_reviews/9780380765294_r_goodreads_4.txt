Rating: 4/5

This story has a lot going on. Elaine Guinivere Morgaine Borne Marshall, AKA Mrs. Courtney is a schoolmistress and a lot more. She runs the Morton House School for Young Ladies or The Misfit Maidens of Morton as the ton calls them. Her girls are from upper families but don't fit in with ton society. Some are shy, most are intelligent and talented with music or painting or singing.
Guin has had anything but a wonderful life. Her coming out was a disaster. Her marriage was worst and almost broke her. She still has nightmares and fears she keeps hidden. Her goal is to teach the girls not only how to fit in but how to find husbands and marry to be happy. All the while hiding who she is and her past. She wants to keep the girls from making the mistakes she did.
Daniel, Viscount Corvin is thirty-one years old and trying to save his fifteen-year-old sister, Emmaline reputation or what is left of it. She has chased away a number of governess and been sent home from other schools for young ladies due to her misbehaving. Her new step mother is called the Courtesan Countess and hates her. She wants her gone as Emmaline is too pretty and starting to get guys attentions
Daniel sees a lady riding at dawn without anyone with her and his curiosity of who she is makes him try to find her. He can't get her out of his mind. The intrigue of her follows him always.
This story has many twists and turns, evil from Guin's past turns up to make trouble for many.
Will Daniel find out the truth about the lady in the part and if so, how will he act when he does ??
I hope you give this book a try it has lots of drama and Intrigue to keep you guessing.
