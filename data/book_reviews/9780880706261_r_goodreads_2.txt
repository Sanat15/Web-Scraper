Rating: 4/5

I read this series a couple of decades ago. I saw this book in a book sale and couldn't resist. It makes a book more interesting to me when they include actual people in the story. The Civil War leaders, spies, and places we know, mixed with fictional characters, makes a better read. It brings reality to the page.
It has a little too much romance for me but , that is okay. I survived.
A good little book in all.
