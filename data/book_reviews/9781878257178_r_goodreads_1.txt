Rating: 5/5

I still own this gem!

I knew how to braid regular braids already from a young age, but I learned other braids from this book and practiced on my sister all the time (the little brat would rip them out as soon as I was finished). 

As simple as braids are, this has been invaluable throughout my life, lol. We didn't have YouTube growing up in the 90s, and my mom couldn't braid to save her life. This book was so helpful.
