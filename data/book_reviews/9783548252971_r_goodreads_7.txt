Rating: 3/5

The House of Thunder was published in 1982 under Koontz's Leigh Nichols pseudonym. It's a suspenseful hospital romance story about a woman who wakes up from a coma with amnesia and battles the idea that she has gone mad with the help of her doctor, with whom she (of course!) has a passionate affair. Koontz's writing is quite good, and there are several twists and turns before the surprise ending, but the characters aren't too memorable or impressive. It's not the best of Ms. Nichols' books, let alone Mr. Koontz's. The plot and pacing seemed to work better in the midst of the Cold War, when the Luke &amp; Laura soap opera craze was at its apex.
