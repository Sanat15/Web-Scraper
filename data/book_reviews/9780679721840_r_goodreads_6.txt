Rating: 4/5

A really interesting book, especially when it explains Puritan beliefs and the biblical origin story around Eve. The idea that humans were created in God’s image while Eve was merely created from Adam already placed women in an inferior position from the very beginning which, honestly, is complete nonsense. Women were constantly portrayed as weaker, less faithful and morally more dangerous (“femina”).

What becomes very clear here is that the system behind witch persecutions always stays the same: fear, control and the punishment of women who step outside the accepted order.
