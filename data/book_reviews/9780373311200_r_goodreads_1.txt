Rating: 5/5

What a joy to read an old Harlequin Regency Romance and find it on level with a few of Mary Balogh’s older Signets. The heroine was the winner of this story. If not for her, the plot would have been commonplace. 

Lord Domenic Stanhope had no intention of ever marrying. He loved his carefree life and he loved women. The more, the merrier. He was a rake and made no apologies. Until his father brought him to task and convinced him that an arranged marriage would serve his son’s purposes. 

Dominec had met Miss Christina Faire when his carriage -he was driving recklessly and forced her off the road- caused an accident. Normally a sweetheart, she was a spitfire and let him know in no uncertain terms that it was his fault. So of course, he kissed her. 

The next time they met was at the altar. Needless to say, they were both shocked to see each other once again. Their wedding night didn’t go as planned and over the next few days, then what became weeks and soon months, the tides changed. Dominec kept returning home only to find his wife had not missed him. She kept busy making new friends, saving lost souls and giving away the ‘pin’ money he gave her. 

He never had a problem winning over a woman. He was the best at sports and had solid friendships. Money was never an issue and yet he found himself frustrated to no end. He was in love with The Vicar's Daughter. Now, he had to woo his wife.
