Rating: 5/5

Molly Moon Stops The World written by Georgia Byng

The book Molly Moon, written by Georgia Byng is a fantastic book. It follows the main character, Molly, and her dog Petula’s adventure when discovering that Molly has time traveling powers! This book is exciting, thrilling, and mysterious all at the same time. If you don’t read this book, you missing out. 
At first, Hollywood actress Molly Moon thinks that she is the only person in the world that can stop time, but as the book continues, Molly realizes that’s not true. When she is trying to control her powers by hypnotising objects, the world stops and Molly did nothing. This is when she realizes that she is not the only person who can stop the world. One day, Molly’s librarian friend sends her on a mission to find out who kidnapped Hollywood star, Davina Nuttel. Molly pieces together information about American Billionaire, Primo Cell. She then realizes that if there are people that can use hypnotism for good, there are people that can use it for bad. Worst of all, Cell can use his powers to permanently hypnotise people. As Cell is using his powers for bad, Molly is using hers trying to stop Primo Cell. 
Anyone who enjoys reading exciting, thrilling, mysterious books would enjoy Molly Moon Stops The World. There are many jaw-dropping moments that make you wonder what it would be like to be able to stop the world whenever you feel like it. This book is not only action packed, but it is also science fiction making the anything possible. I believe that anyone could enjoy this book, but I also believe that girls would get the most enjoyment out of it. Considering the main characters are female. Overall, this is an interesting book that just makes you want to keep reading.
