Rating: 5/5

This is just a storyteller's delight from beginning to end. I am apparently on a bit of a romance kick, having just read a few of Lisa Kleypas's Victorians, which got me thinking of Howey's books, the only romances I've kept on my shelves for years after reading -- in this case, probably 20 years ago. There's a reason for that -- although Geneva begins the novel as a tantrum-throwing diva, she soon growns on the reader, with her passion for music and her pragmatism about her career, and Kieran is a winning hero. There is romance, and also engaging adventure storytelling.

Although this one is out of print, you might be able to find a used copy online, and it's well worth the effort.
