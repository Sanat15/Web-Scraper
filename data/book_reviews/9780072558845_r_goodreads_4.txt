Rating: 4/5

This book is very informative. Raises many good questions about the teaching profession. The only reason I did not give it 5 stars is because I felt it was more on the negative side of teaching. I know many careers has both positives and negatives, but I believe because of the situations the career has been challenged with the book has a negative tone about teaching. The writer doesn’t discourage teachers from teaching but does honestly point out challenges teachers face today. Overall I do recommend this book for any up and coming teacher as it does point out a multitude of information in just 218 pages.
