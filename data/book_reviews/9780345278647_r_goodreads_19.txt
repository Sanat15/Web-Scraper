Rating: 2/5

"One good thing about rigid courtesy, Ken decided, was that females *had* to be on time."

Seriously, Anne???

This is a fantasy set on another planet -- it has no interest whatsoever in science or plausibility -- in which McCaffrey explores an unusual question: what if we met another intelligent species and tried really hard NOT to fight them?

However, despite being able to imagine a future in which something called "the Amalgamation" has given every human the same skin tone and erased all racial/ethic prejudice forever, McCaffrey, in 1969, could not imagine a future without misogyny. Only men can have jobs in a space colony. "Their women" (ugh) travel across the galaxy so that they can... cook.

There are apparently more books about Doona, but I won't be reading them.
