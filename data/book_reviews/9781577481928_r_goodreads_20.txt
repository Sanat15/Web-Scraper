Rating: 5/5

I'm still making references to this book on a weekly basis. I was hoping to actually do to it on a daily basis, but it appears that I've just brought myself to look at a verse to live by each week.
This book is great for someone who is just starting out on the bible (kinda me) and doesn't know where to begin. I love how the book is divided by the subject of the verses. 
I didn't read it from beginning to end in numerical order. I found it easier to go to the topic that I know I need help in, then the topic I'm curious about, and then I just go based on what my mother says or just pick randomly. My first section was "Courage" because I knew for a fact that courage was something I lacked. Now I refer to this book whenever I'm struggling in something, and I use it to find quotes for my section markers in my notebook. 
Some days I find it easier to just carry this book instead of the actually bible, because it's easier to find what you need. Especially since I'm still "in-training".
