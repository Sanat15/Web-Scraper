Rating: 5/5

Really enjoyed this look at the 1941 season and all the changes that were about to happen with the US's involvement in WW2 coming. All told by the center fielder of the Red Sox, Dom DiMaggio. It was the year of his brother Joe's streak and Ted Williams .406 batting average. It was a real memorable season told about by someone who had ties to both big events.

Highly recommended, was well told, and enjoyed reading more about this season by someone who was there.
