Rating: 5/5

I first read The Pathway a few years ago, but doing the full Laurel Mellin program has been life-changing for me.

This work goes far beyond surface-level self-help. It helped me understand my emotional patterns at the root and gave me actual tools to rewire the way I respond to stress, shame, overwhelm, and self-sabotage. For me, it has created real shifts in how I regulate, how I think, and how I move through life.

One thing I would say honestly is that someone has to be ready for this work. It is not just something you casually read and instantly “get.” It asks for honesty, repetition, and a willingness to look at what’s underneath your patterns.

I also think this work can be especially powerful for people who did not receive much emotional nurturing as children. If you grew up without feeling deeply seen, soothed, or emotionally supported, these techniques can help build capacities that may have never been fully developed in the first place.

For me, this has been some of the most meaningful inner work I’ve ever done. It has helped me create more self-trust, emotional safety, and real change from the inside out.
