Rating: 4/5

4* Highly descriptive richly textured family saga set before and during Russian revolution following the lives of a Russian peasant girl Galina Panova, a British doctor Simon Aylsham and the aristcratic Malinsky family. Yes, it is a long read (500 odd pages - only about half the reading time of War and Peace!) and may be considered boring by some readers because of its length yet, it is still an absorbing story, and the characters feel real to the reader. The tension and sense of danger also feels real living in Czarist Russia, then Revolutionary &amp; Post Revolutionary Russia to any citizen who spoke out or tried to change and improve the social condition which continues for the ordinary Russian before and after the Revolution.
