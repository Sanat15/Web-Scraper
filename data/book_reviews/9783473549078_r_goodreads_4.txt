Rating: 4/5

I've read this book almost every Christmas season for the past 25 years. Every time I read it, it gives me a warm feeling of nostalgia. I love horses, and Carole, Stevie, and Lisa were like my friends growing up, so I love visiting the Saddle Club and Pine Hollow each holiday season with this book. I do find that there are a few inconsistencies within the book, but it doesn't reduce my enjoyment of it. Veronica is creating trouble as usual, and of course Stevie and the Saddle Club are compelled to reciprocate. Carole is more relatable than usual in this book because we see her vulnerability. And I think everyone can relate to experiencing both holiday excitement and the mixed and sometimes painful feelings Christmas can also bring. It's a really nice story.
