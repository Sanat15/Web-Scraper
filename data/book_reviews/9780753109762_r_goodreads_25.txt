Rating: 4/5

Any kind of person can murder.

 
There is no mystery to be solved in Strangers on a Train. Almost from the beginning we know who the culprits are and how the murders are committed. 

Strangers on a Train is rather a psychological study of the people committing a crime; it delves into the darkest corners of an ordinary person’s mind and studies the effects this atrocious act has on his life and of the people close to him.
