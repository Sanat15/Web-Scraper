Rating: 3/5

It's a very lyrical read. I liked the writing style and the story itself was engaging. That being said some of this stuff was just woof and I didn't like Sappho very much. I love me a strong woman who does what she wants. But she also never considers anyone else. Also she's a massive predator. I know that attitudes were different back then but that doesn't change how she uses and manipulates the people in her care 

Also if you are triggered by young adolescents in sexual situations, stay far away from this book because yikes. 

And, I mean, I get it. Historical context and all that. But still. Yikes.
