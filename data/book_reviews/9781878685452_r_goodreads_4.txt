Rating: 5/5

This is the best illustrated Christmas book I have ever read. The story is also very cute. It is the real story of Santa as told by his elves. It answers all the questions of how Santa became Santa. You get to explore the North Pole, Santa's workshop, the post office, the candy caves etc. It is a coffee table size book with each page illustrated by 13 different artists. Scott Gustafson is one of my favorite Christmas artists and his work is included.
