Rating: 3/5

This book wasn't what I expected. I guess I was assuming the stories would be short mini-biographies of the individuals' lives, but it was actually snippets of certain parts/events in their lives, diary/journal fragments, etc. I did learn about some people I'd never heard of and learned more about some people I HAD heard of, but I would've liked to know more about each person. It has made me curious to read additional information on a few of the people and I have written down some book recommendations to further educate myself on their lives, which I suppose is a positive. Overall I thought the book was just ok and I probably wouldn't recommend it to others. At this point, it's also a little outdated with some of the people it mentions since it's been over twenty years since this was published.
