Rating: 4/5

I know this is a Hanukkah story, but to celebrate "Jewish Cultural Heritage Month," I'm reading all sorts of stories this May featuring Jewish characters, themes, stories, and histories such as this book. This is a nice picture book about a family who experience their own Hanukkah miracle when they are snowed in and unable to gather the potatoes and apples for the traditional latkes and applesauce. During the week-long celebration a stray dog and cat appear on the family's doorstep leading to several miracles. 

This is a sweet Hanukkah book and children will enjoy the colorful pictures and the story. They will especially love the dog and cat while learning more about the miracle of Hanukkah. Definitely a book to be added to people's yearly Hanukkah read.
