Brief description
Buster attends his first party only to realize that he’s not the only one feeling left out. Buster learns what he’s best at – being the best listener in the house. Buster and Phoebe find the garbage bandits – raccoons.

Identify at least 2 characteristics of this genre and subgenre and discuss how they appear in your book
•	Emergent Reader books have increasingly more lines per page.
Buster: The Very Shy Dog is no exception – going from one line (least) to eleven lines (most); the reader is slowly led to read more and more text. Once a young reader becomes familiar with this text, it is a story that they’ll want to read again and again. 
•	Emergent Reader books are less dependent on repetitive pattern and pictures.
In the three chapters of this book, I only found one repetitive phrase (in Chapter One). The pictures help to extend and enhance the text, allowing readers to make inferences. A good book for a picture walk with early emergent readers.

In what ways and how well does the book as a whole serve its intended audience?
Buster is well-suited for its intended audience because readers recognize that characters can be more than one-dimensional. In easy reader chapter books, characters (such as Buster) demonstrate that they have very distinct, multi-dimensional personalities in different situations, just like the reader does.
