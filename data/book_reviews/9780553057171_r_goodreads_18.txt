This is a modern day remake of Epictetus's Enchiridion in spirit, but not letter.
Og Mandino was a truly amazing man--his books have helped millions find purpose and direction in their lives.

"The Older I get and the longer I live, I 've discovered that the only way to conquer life, is to live each day with all of the love, gusto, and gratitude that we can muster; then look up and take a deep breath of that fresh air, and thank God for the gift of a new day; and live this day with as few frowns as possible and a basket full of smiles " Og Mandino
