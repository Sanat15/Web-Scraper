Silhouette Shadows # 2 = 3.5 stars
.
After reading a few amnesia stories, and while I know that it is fiction and most assuredly not close to reality, why do they always release someone to people claiming to know who the other is. And that is with only possibly a picture and a nice, possibly made up, story. Surely at the very least the hospital (if that is the case) should vet that the purported found identity matches (at least a little) with some kind of official documentation. It makes a nice story without this, but still having a little more authenticity in there couldn't hurt.
