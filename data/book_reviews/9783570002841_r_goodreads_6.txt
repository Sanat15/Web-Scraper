Rating: 5/5

I studied the Second World War in history at school. I was fascinated by the Third Reich, and it's hold on the German people. 

This book opens a lid on the destruction of German Youth by the Nazis. They were manipulated, abused, and tossed aside as worthless cannon fodder. It is a shameful period of German history. 

Sadly, we still see the tactics used by the Nazis in other dictatorships across the world today. I was moved and angered by the mistreatment of a generation of children, as shown in this book. 

A true warning from history.
