Rating: 4/5

When it comes to tacky 4-dollar books. I am never disappointed. I've read countless romance stories that follow very very similar plotlines and similar events (any Harlequin/Zebra Books regency romance published work ever). Despite the fact that this book is a copy and paste of like 4 other books I can think of, I still enjoyed reading it. I enjoyed the message of fate conveyed throughout the story and it was unique due to its "double edged sword" plotline. Usually, only one of the partners do a good ole switcharoo, but in "A Season for Samantha", both members had played a part in the deceit, therefore meaning both partners were to blame. It's a satisfying read if you're interested in happy endings but if you enjoyed "A Season for Samantha" and are looking for a more suspenseful read with a similar plotline, try "Impostress" by Lisa Jackson.
