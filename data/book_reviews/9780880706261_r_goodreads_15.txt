Rating: 4/5

Author Al Lacy has a distinct voice as a storyteller. His words reflect his strong personal beliefs and love of history. "Beloved Enemy" is a compelling Civil War era romance which shows the humanity behind the horror of Americans fighting each other on American soil. Jenny Jordan and her father move from VA to Washington, DC when her father receives a prestigious post in President Lincoln's cabinet. Jenny finds work as a receptionist in the White House. Loyal to her family and homeland, Jenny covers for her father as he spies for the Confederate Army. When Jenny meets handsome Buck Brownell, a Union officer, her heart is torn between her new love and the life she has always known. Pressured to assist in the cause of the Confederacy, Jenny agrees to carry important information through enemy territory. When she is caught, Buck Brownell is ordered to execute the beautiful spy--the woman he loves. How will honor and duty make peace with love and loyalty? "Beloved Enemy" is an involving and intriguing historical romance, one that brings home the powerful division among Americans which brought on the War Between the States. The numerous real life historical figures which appear throughout the story line add resonance to this tale of honor, tragedy, and hearts so true. Lovers of historical romance will be pleased to know that there are over a dozen books in Al Lacy's "Battles of Destiny" series.
