Rating: 5/5

I have been reading Melody Beattie's books since the 1990s. This is the first I've read the story of her life. I had no idea. Over the years her wisdom and lessons have helped me immensely, but I had no idea where she came from to have such wisdom. The story of her life is incredible! As usual, her writing is good, and while much of her life was hard to read about, she wrrites it so descriptively you are there with her. I heard she passed a year ago in February, and I am saddened she is no longer with us. She had a great gift, and fortunately she shared it with us. May she rest in peace. Thank you Melody!
