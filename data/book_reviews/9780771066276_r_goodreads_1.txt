Rating: 5/5

I really liked this collection of settlers being settlers (eye roll) in the Arctic. The racism was a tough truck through but there were some moments of awareness from the eyes of people interacting with Inuk, Dene, and Cree folks for the first time. 
The sled dog stuff was very well described and I enjoyed the heartbreak what the caribou herds used to be. I appreciated the honesty reflected in this book even though some of it was really awful and terrible- interesting to see the whole perspective for a change
