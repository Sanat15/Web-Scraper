Rating: 5/5

The Duke's Dilemma by  Elizabeth Chater is a clean Regency romance. It's second in the Elizabeth Chater Regency Romance Collection #1, but has also been published separately. If you are a fan of Barbara Metzger, then Elizabeth Chater will be right up your alley.

Lady Leslie Endale is the oldest of four children, including her nine-year-old brother, who recently inherited the title of earl when their father died. March Wendell is a cousin of theirs, and is next in line to inherit the earldom. He moves to the young earl's estate and becomes the guardian for the four children, but doesn't seem to have their best interests at heart. 

The Duke of Kenelm is the absentee landlord of a great neighboring estate, allowing it to fall to wrack and ruin. He returns to the area after being away for many years. He wants to investigate reports of someone buying up the surrounding land when he receives an offer to purchase his property. 

This is an excellently done Regency romance. I enjoyed it thoroughly, and happily sped through the next two books in Chater's first collection. I am chomping at the bit to get the next collection!
