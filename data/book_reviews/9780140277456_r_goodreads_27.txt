Rating: 4/5

If you were blessed by Sir Alec's previously published diary BLESSINGS IN DISGUISE, then you will be doubly blessed by this second diary. Guinness is still caring, kind, a faithful friend, too aware of his limitations, and admiring of his best colleagues. He cares about the arts and appreciates the best artists in every field. He comes across as a man to know if you can only access his circle.
