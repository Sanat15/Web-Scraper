Rating: 2/5

The setting, and idea for the characters were nice.

But there were too many repetitions about broad shoulders, arrogant attitude, etc. If you read the genre I am sure you know what I mean. Besides this, Drogo and Leslie behaved in a contradictory manner. The worst - some behaviors were not fitting to the time and place (e.g. kissing in a public place). Yes, I can accept some historical inaccuracy in funny stories (like in Chesney's romances), but not in this case.

I read through it just because I hoped it would have gotten better at some point.

[1.5 stars]
