Rating: 4/5

This is a friends to lovers romance that had humor, but kind of ticked me off. 

Dylan decides to pop in on his BFF on his way home from a meeting. He can't get his mind off of her, but she's his friend. Like many 1 night stands, drinks were flowing and things were done. Then in typical Dylan fashion, he ghosts Whitney. Months later he comes back into town with a big surprise....and Whitney has one for him as well.

Good story concept. okay characters, good dialogue, and engaging dialogue. Written as a 3rd person, multi pov with a HEA ending.

Would I recommend this book? Yes

Would I read more from this author? Yes
