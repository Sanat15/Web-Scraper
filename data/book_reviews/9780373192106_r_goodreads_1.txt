Rating: 4/5

“Mystery Man” is the story of Canton and Janine.
When Tycoon and computer wiz Canton goes on a vacation to Cancun with his daughter Karie to escape from a recent financial disaster- he never expects to have a neighbor like Janine, the exasperating redhead who drives him mad with her tongue.. and lust. She pretends to be a mother to Kurt, who he soon realizes is her younger brother- and both embark on this passionate but funny banter filled ride- not knowing she’s a best selling author.
This is mixed with loads of heated kisses, boob rubbing on hairy chests, kidnapping drama and naughty kids planning insane hoaxes. 
Really enjoyed this one, and it could have ended 50 pages sooner with the proposal rather than the last kidnapping drama. (Which made me a little mad as the heroine forgave easily).
Sweet ending.
Safe
4/5
