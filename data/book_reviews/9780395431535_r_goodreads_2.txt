Rating: 3/5

I had hoped for more analysis and philosophy about how children develop morality; instead, Coles just quotes his patients at length, in passages that seem heavily edited and rather implausible. This style gets very tedious very quickly. It is worth reading, however, just for the encounter with Ruby Bridges, whom Coles worked with during and after her harrowing and historic experience attending school in New Orleans. Ruby Bridges is an unbelievably strong, moral and memorable person, and it is a gift to hear from her directly in these pages.
