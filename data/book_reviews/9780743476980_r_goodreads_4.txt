Rating: 5/5

This book was AWESOME! Best book i've read in awhile. It is about a guy, the closer who captures serial killers and tortures and kills them to find out details that he records and leaves for the police so the families of the serial killers victims can have some closure. As the closer struggles with the idea he may be starting to enjoy killing, he locates The patron, the killer he is sure killed his family. Few things: The police are only in the book at the beginning. I think that bit should have either been edited out, or more bits of the thoughts of the police as they found each 'victim' of the closer should have been written in. there could have been more gore. At the end I wish the author would have given details of the torture, but still without that I loved the book. I definatly recommend it. I'll be looking for more of this guys books.
