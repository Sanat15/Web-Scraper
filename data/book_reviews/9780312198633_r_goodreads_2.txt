Rating: 3/5

A more successful $1 bookshop find than the last: a very '90s queer mystery set at an academic conference for Edith Wharton scholars. The gentle academic satire is the most enjoyable part here -- in contrast, the mystery is a bit too convoluted and our protagonist barely does any detective work. But I still had fun with the late '90s atmo of it all -- though I could have done without, and didn't miss, the biphobia.
