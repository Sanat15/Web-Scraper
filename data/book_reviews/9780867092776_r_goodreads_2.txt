Rating: 5/5

Last year I took a fantastic class called Foundations of Education which was basically a semester-long exploration of what public school education is for, what problems exist with its current implementation, and what can we do to fix it. This book was kind of like that course except specifically aimed at language arts teachers. It was a nice blend of theory and practice--it's got some heavy, dense parts but lots of firsthand journal accounts from student teachers. Definitely enjoyable and thought-provoking--I think there's probably not a single page in my copy that doesn't have a scribbled note or question or two in the margins.
