Rating: 4/5

I am a WWII buff, so this book appealed to me. It is a good read as it goes through a journalist, Jewish woman's experiences before and during the NAZI regime before she was able to escape to the US. Then when she came to the US, she was still hunted by the Nazis! This is more like a documentary since it is literally a woman's diary, however, it was still a very good read. I think you need to know a little about WWII to read in between her entries to understand the sequence of things and the depth/meaning of some things. She didn't get into the weeds about train loads of Jews being shipped off to concentration camps. She wrote about the parties, gatherings, banquets and more events where Nazis, supporters and non-supporters mingled, what she heard, what she was able to print and saw when they started to take over and how this occurred.
