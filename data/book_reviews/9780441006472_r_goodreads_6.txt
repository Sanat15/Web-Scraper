Rating: 5/5

A book about a man who endures through times of change. He himself changes, but at a slower pace, so that he is out of synch with the world that used to be so familiar.

This is already a great setup and the queernormative world building makes the book even better, especially as it was published in 1979. I think this is also probably the reason why the book sits at a strange spot between fantasy and literary fiction that might not be appealing to everyone. And I guess some people might also sadly have a problem with a feminist and queernormative novel written exclusively from the point of view of a man.

I still think it's a shame it only has slightly more than a thousand ratings on GR.
