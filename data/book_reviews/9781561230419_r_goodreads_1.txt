Rating: 5/5

This is a very sad yet helpful book. This book is for siblings who had a brother or sister die in the womb. It helps explain to the child what happened. It helps answer the child's question of "Why did mom not come home with my brother/sister?". It talks about the most common questions that are asked my children and try to answer those questions in the best way possible. I do not think I would use this in the hospital, but I do think this is a helpful book for those going through this. They could always read this book and monitor of the sibling is handling this situation. This is just not a book that I would have any use for. Now if a new born came in and was not going to make it and there were older siblings this book for be good to help them understand and process.
