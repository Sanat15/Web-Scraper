Rating: 4/5

Blunt and uncensored, some of these stories are so far from my realm of imagination that they must be rooted in experiences from Russell Leong's life. I read these stories in an Asian American Literature class and this is the only novel I have some recollection of, and we read at least 8 novels. There's no sugar coating the stories, full of anguish, fear, and raw emotion. These are stories you won't find discussed in any historical text that serve to open the eyes of the reader to the diversity of the human experience.
