I thought this book was just ok. I was excitied about reading it was because it was a more modern day mail-order bride romance. I was slightly disappointed. 

I was expecting them to struggle through getting to know one another since they had never met. The problem was, that they both had ben previously married, and those marriages cause both of them to have doubts and insecurities. So therefore, they were both seconnd guessing the other's motives and never cpmmunicating.

Te other problem that I had was that they slept together so fast, and they second guessed whether the other one enjoyed it or not. That part was jsut weird.

Then you add in the man's daugther and his right hand man for the ranch, and calf rustlers, and it just turns the book into a just okay read.
