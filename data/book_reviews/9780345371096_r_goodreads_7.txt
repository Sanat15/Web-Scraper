Rating: 5/5

Excellent story. This is kind of a "physician heal thyself" story. Here he gives a new meaning to diving into your work, as the patient is the size of a medium scout ship. Keeps the interest up well throughout. White does a great job in making strange, weird and totally alien characters into regular people leading interesting lives in an inter-galactic hospital. With correspondingly inter-galactic patents. If you haven't read any of this series, you are missing out.
