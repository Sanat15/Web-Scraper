Rating: 4/5

From the chapter "The Matriarch" (single mother by choice) it says "Friends were the safety net that my mother lacked." She was fifty when she was divorced and fifty when she died. She was solidly in the middle of life. But she thought her life was finished--she was no longer attractive, her children were growing up. She believed she had nothing left. She thought she was doing us all a favor by killing herself because she was in such terrible shape that she was no use to anybody. "Her suicide was an act of bravery and an act of illness. Her hope, of course was that she would get to my father, and she didn't. She didn't get to him at all. At that level it was utterly futile. But she did get to us, her children. And we were not the people she meant to get at all. What she did to us was a kind of betrayal that you never recover from." Since Kyra was born, I've never once questioned the meaning of my life. I learned that the meaning of life is that you just live it...for the people you love and who love you. 

There is one single mother and one happily married woman but not one happily married woman with a child. The majority of the examples are materially successful but emotionally lonely singles. Some men are changing in the younger generation, it is no longer viewed as an positive attribute to be a man whore.
