Rating: 5/5

This had way more depth than I expected from an 80s Harlequin superromance. It touched on very powerful issues, such as illiteracy and poverty, and the romance between the two MCs, though strange in the beginning, was very well written. This book did a great job of "show instead of tell," which I find the romance genre suffered from as a whole in the 70s/80s. I might actually have a slight book hangover, and that hasn't happened to me in quite a while. All in all, it's a great read.

Trigger warnings include discussions/situations involving animal abuse, and a few non-PC terms that were likely appropriate for the time.
