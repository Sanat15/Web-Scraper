The Last Opium Den by Nick Tosches (Bloomsbury 2000) (362.293). This is a quirky little 
read in which a late-twentieth-century American sets his sights on locating an authentic nineteenth-century opium den of the type described in Victorian literature: heavy brocaded wallpaper, gas lights, with smokers reclining about the room on couches and the floor as they imhale the fragrant fumes of the poppy gum. But does such an establishment still exist? The author's search leads him inevitably to the far east. Can he find the den he hopes for? My rating: 7/10, finished 10/1/18.
