Rating: 2/5

"Tense, engaging and utterly unpredictable"? Three qualities I haven't found from this book. It's not bad. It's just not that good either. I thought having crime mystery with a journalist would give a great new angle but it sort of plods along. The shorthand style the author uses to move across time and places should make it pacey and interesting but just kind of annoys me almost as though there's bits missing. And I did find the first third predictable. I just couldn't be bothered to finish it so definately not engaging or tense either. I also felt a bit shortchanged that what seemed like a new book was actually copyrighted 1999.
