Rating: 3/5

This was good, but it sure did get on my nerves at times! I was on the h's side the first time she wanted revenge against the H, because he was pretty crappy to her, and she was young and easily disillusioned as well as at an age where love/hate can wreak some havoc. Marrying the H's elderly rebellious relative, in order to get her hands on money the H's entitled family thought was their due (and whom she had reason to resent) and then using her new power as a wealthy widow to get some of her own back made sense, up to a point. (Being accused of old hubby's death tempered things, anyhow.)

However, when she - yet again - goes the vengeance route, this time it's wrong, as well as spiteful, not to mention ridiculous. She was older and should have been wiser! She uses her semi-intoxicated state as a (poor) excuse for getting naked with the H, and you can't blame alcohol for doing the deed several times throughout the night, as you would have sobered up by then! She then lets the most idiotic misunderstanding (all on her part) once again set her down vengeance path, only to have it backfire badly on her, and she sure deserved it!

Because of her stupidity, she and the H and the OW and the OM all suffered, each in their own way, and so much could have been avoided.

The plus side to this story was the double murder mystery, and I'm sorry to say that I didn't pick up on some clues that would have made me realize whodunit, so I didn't until the culprit was revealed and of course I said to myself, "I should have known!!!" Maybe you'll be more observant than I was.

I also thought the ending was too abrupt; after all the false starts, heartache, misunderstandings, etc., you'd think there would have been a bit more passion or at least romance. No such luck! This HEA sure fizzled.
