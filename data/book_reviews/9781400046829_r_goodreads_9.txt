I only read this book because of my English course class for college. I was already upset that I had to read it (not my taste for books) so I disliked it from he beginning. It was an interesting read. Seeing the back stories of immigration that most people don't know about but it was a little hard to keep up with. I often felt confused because i couldnt really keep up with which character was talking. It was as if they just popped out of no where. I don't know maybe I'm just not smart enough to keep up.
