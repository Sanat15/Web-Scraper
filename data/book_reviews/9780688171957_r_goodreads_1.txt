Rating: 3/5

The pop-up artwork is very well done, and the descriptions of each phobia is both clinical and accurate. I do wish there had been more obscure fears described, such as:

Arachibutyrophobia: the fear of peanut butter sticking to the roof of the mouth. (that would have made an interesting pop-up!

or

Geniophobia: an extreme fear and/or physical aversion to chins.
 
or

Pogonophobia: the extreme dislike of beards.
