Rating: 4/5

This is one of the easiest book that I have ever read. When I said easy I means it doesn’t have a complicated story line. This makes make me understand the story clearer.
	I like the way the author thinks, he try to use an animals to do what humans do. The story isn’t that boring. Even though it’s simple but it doesn’t. The main character are so dynamic which I mean the flying squirrel. At first I think he was just a jerk who seeking for love. But in conclusion he was the hero of his animal city.
	This tells me a lot about when you ever made a mistake, it’s not too late to change yourself.
