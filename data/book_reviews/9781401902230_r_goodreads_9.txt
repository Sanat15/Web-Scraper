Rating: 5/5

I thought this was going to be about disordered eating, but it’s so much more than that. It’s an acknowledgment of pain, of shared traumas that need to be voiced. Why are we so self-critical? Can we tell the difference between actual healing and cycling through the ups and downs of emotion? This is a book that fosters conversation and changed thinking. I’d recommend processing reading this with someone who has a strong skill set for emotional health.
