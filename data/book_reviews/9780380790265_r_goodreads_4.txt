Rating: 2/5

This was the only book I have read by Walker. It made me think of the exploitation movies made in the '70's and '80's. Darryl Billups, the protagonist, is a Baltimore Reporter who has successfully investigated and reported on murders in the past. Young urban professional African-Americans are being murdered by an unknown assailant who leaves no traces or obvious method of death, but who does leave a Confederate Flag sticker on the forehead of each victim. Billups investigates. There is a lot of gratuitous sex and there are moments of great clarity in the novel, but also moments of great convolution.
