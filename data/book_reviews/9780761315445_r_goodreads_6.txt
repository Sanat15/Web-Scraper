Rating: 2/5

This book is about a baby called George who can speak in full sentences-but does not share his secret with anyone but his older sister. George’s sister (Laura) is very excited that she has a baby brother who is very clever. George and Laura go on to try and reveal the secret to their parents. The story ends where George has his first birthday part and instead of inviting other babies he wants the company of adults-where he gives a speech. All the adults were very shocked with George’s vocabulary and speech. 
There are lots of speech marks in the story and this book will be good for children to begin to understand the notion of speech marks.
Overall I found the story a little boring and felt the chapters dragged on a little bit. I even found baby George to be rude and arrogant. Younger children will find this story quite interesting-a baby actually being able to speak.
