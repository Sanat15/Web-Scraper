Rating: 3/5

Rating collections is difficult. Three stars because I can't be bothered to try to sort out an average. A fun little collection of poems, all of which are European/American in origin, so...not great on the diversity level. Some of it I had read before, some of it was new poems by authors I'd read before, and some of it was new poems by someone I'd never heard of. I definitely got an idea of some new poets I want to read more from, so I'm counting it as a win.
