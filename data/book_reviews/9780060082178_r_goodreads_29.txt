Rating: 4/5

This is a superb novel and easy to read. 

This is the first book in the Raylan Givens series. Raylan is a US Marshal who seems to wear his cowboy hat a lot. The main character in the book, however, is a man called Harry Arno and the main question you will have in this book is how long will Harry last, will he get to the end of the book?

Harry has been skimming some money from his betting business that's part of an empire run by a rather large mobster called Jimmy Capotorto in Miami. Jimmy Cap has been told by rivals of Harry that Harry is taking a percentage for himself and not paying all the profits to Jimmy Cap. This is partially true but Harry is soon on the run, heading to Rapallo in Italy where he was stationed at times during WWII. He is followed there, first by Joyce his part-time girlfriend and then by Jimmy Cap's mobsters, and then by Raylan Givens, who Harry escapes from for the second time in his life, at a restaurant. Harry has been skimming money in preparation for his retirement for twenty years and has bought a substantial villa on the hillside overlooking Rapallo. 

The meetings and shootings take place at quite a speed before most of the characters return to the USA where the denouement occurs.
