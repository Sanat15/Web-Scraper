When I was just 20 years old, in 1970, I had a brief yet passionate flame named Rosalie Schreyburt. She burst upon my repessed libido with the dazzling intensity of Fourth of July Fireworks. I took her to see the film they made of this that year, for I had read the book the preceding summer.

Rosalie, if I may flesh out her personality to you, was Woke.

I was still a Kermit.

She wondered out loud to me afterward, over drinks, that it seemed likely that a virus could escape a sealed enclosure and infect humanity (and of course that did happen with COVID).

Rosalie knew the world’s dirty games. I as yet did not. So she told me there was no way she would get serious with this ingénu. 

She dumped me.

So it goes.

And yet - and yet - COVID was more than willing to get serious with me, fifty years later!

WHY, oh why, do our favourite novels’ nightmares always come HORRIBLY TRUE?!

And, Even in our love life, yet?

Four Full Stars.
