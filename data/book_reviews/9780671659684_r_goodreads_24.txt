Rating: 4/5

4.2 stars. It was an easy read. The writing is really simple. Wait, I take the “easy read back”. Polly’s narration always creeped me out, especially the part where she went insane. Of course, this guy Clark. What is his deal?! And Bubba. That guy’s weird… he calls girls sluts even when he slept with half the girls in the grade.
I love the romance between Russ and Sara. I really hated the ending (in a good way) and can’t believe my girl turned on Jessica!
