Rating: 5/5

Deep and comprehensive analysis of Khmer Rouge Cambodia based on first hand accounts and compilation of secondary sources.

This book sheds light onto one of the darkest and least explored tragedies in history that have scarred Cambodia to this day. It gives voice to a plethora of harrowing first hand accounts that humanize and intensify the brutality and tragedy of Khmer Rouge Cambodia. Also, it explores the complex legal and political maneuvering of the Khmer Rouge and their descent into paranoia. Perhaps most insightfully, it explores that factors that led to the rise of the Khmer Rouge and what removed them from power.

This is an essential read for anyone who wants to speak authoritatively about Cambodia, Vietnam, and the legacy of the US and communism in South East Asia.
