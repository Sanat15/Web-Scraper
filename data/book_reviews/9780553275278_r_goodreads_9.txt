Rating: 5/5

A great story most of us don’t know

I didn’t know much about the Aleutian campaign except for what an uncle told me of his time there as a PT boat skipper (never saw the Japanese but was terrified by the seas and weather.) The 1000 Mile War filled the massive crevasses in my knowledge and added justification for my uncle’s palpitations whenever he spoke about that part of the war. It also holds a lot of lessons learned for defense policy makers and active duty military personnel as the US contemplates war again in the Arctic.
