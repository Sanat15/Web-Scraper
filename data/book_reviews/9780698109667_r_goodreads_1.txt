Rating: 1/5

Full breasts are always straining against tight military uniforms in this slightly alternative history, and good Germans like Luftwaffe Captain Alfred Horn do not want to be lumped in with bad Germans:

"I don't really know you, she said quietly, but I think you're a good man. How can you support someone who passed the Nuremberg Laws against the Jews? How can you support someone who's turning Europe into one vast compulsory work camp?"

"Look, Kay," he said irritably. "I'm like millions of other Germans. I'm not a member of the Nazi Party. I don't go around kicking Jews or informing to the Gestapo. I'm not interested in politics. I'm interested in Germany."

If you've always wanted to read a book in which Rudolf Hess is injected with extract of feline brain tissue as truth serum and FDR enters World War II because "the New Deal has failed," [his words], pick up this one.
