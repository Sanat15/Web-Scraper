Rating: 4/5

Everyone has their thing shoes, cars, clothes, you can just about name it. This coffee table book shows how functional some styles were and how some you can see were just not functional at all. The book demonstrates how our tastes have changed and the artistry of our unique tastes in footwear goes from trendy to classic to the most unusual.

If you are interested on how Shoes have become such a fashion statement and how our tastes have drastically changed with the each era that passes this book is for you!
