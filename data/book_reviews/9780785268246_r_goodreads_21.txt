Rating: 5/5

Stephanie Grace Whitson's trilogy relates the challenges before America just after the end of the Civil War and as the western expansion began. If the country had not yet learned its lesson regarding the issue of using minorities as slaves, it was thrust in what was known as the "Indian problem" with another set of prejudices based on the color of a man's skin. Weaving the story of Genevieve LaCroix Dane Two Stars and her best beloved, Daniel Two Stars in each of the three books, Whitson did a magnificent job in keeping the story well paced with excellent continuity. Although I was initially disappointed with the lack of a happy ending with book one, it was necessary to keep the story line moving. The Christian themes are not heavy-handed at all, and creates a credible backdrop for characters who experience life with joys, disappointments and sorrow. Nothing candy coated with this series! The underlying message is that Christians don't have life any easier than anyone else...it's just they have the hope and love of their resurrected Lord that helps them get through it.
