Rating: 5/5

Lord Simon Cartwright is furious when Samantha Adamson moves into the Dower House left to her by his late father. He had planned for his future in-laws to stay there while their place was being remodeled. This rocky relationship progresses with Simon continually having to keep Samantha from getting ostracized and Samantha reminding him about the fun things he set aside when he had to save his fortune and reputation ruined by his father's debt and suicide. As these two learn to admire qualities in each other, they manage to solve all the problems thrown at them. Samantha also considers herself a match maker and makes some interesting matches while also being surprised by some matches she doesn't foresee. A well written, funny, Regency romance.
