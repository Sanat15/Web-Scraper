Rating: 3/5

Good, but split focus too much

The Starfist saga continues. As expected, it provides a level of entertainment and excitement that this series is known for while at the same time touches on many of the common hallmarks that can be found in most elite military forces around the modern world to give readers a unique connection to the story. 

My one complaint was the storyline involving Dean &amp; Claypool. From the beginning to the end, I was constantly asking myself why the entire storyline was relevant. It was entertaining, but it doesn't really do anything to significantly evolve the main characters involved. The writers could have found better uses for Dean &amp; Claypool. I'm also disappointed that Dean, at least, has not yet been promoted.
