Rating: 4/5

A quiet, somewhat meditative book by Richard Van Camp (Dogrib Nation) and beautifully illustrated by George Littlechild (Cree Nation). Richard asks family and friends what they think of when they think of horses, and he receives answers capturing the beauty and mystery of the large creatures. Interestingly, both the author’s and illustrator’s name for “horse” in their languages means “big dog”—fascinating.
