Rating: 2/5

Horrible ending! 
I liked a lot of the book but then *spoiler* the dude turned out to be a crazy kidnapper and never even told her he loved her. Maybe if he didn't steal her child, then kidnap her as well, and maybe if he professed his love, if all those things lined up, then it could have been a fun little read. Instead I was just angry that Ria went along with it all. Not a good love story!!!! I had to re-read that last page a few times thinking I missed something but nope. Just a super lame ending.
