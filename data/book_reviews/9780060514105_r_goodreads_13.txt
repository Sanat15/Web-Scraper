2.5 stars. My kids are still really into this series. POSITIVE SIDE: There is even more action than in book #1. The twists and turns play largely in this read and had one of the boys talking excitedly nonstop on this fact. NEGATIVE: The characters and their directions of thoughts I was disappointed with. I just couldn't feel much for any of the characters with this one (book 2 of the series). I'm told books 3 and 4 are better.
