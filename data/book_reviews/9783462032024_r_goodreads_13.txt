Rating: 2/5

The book dragged on more than The Lord of the Rings. Just when it was time to wrap things up, it kept on going.

The story was about Bolivar’s last journey, down the Rio Magdalena, Colombia. A historical fiction of his last days accepting his new life as a citizen but still involving himself in the political future of the places he liberated from Spain.

Too much of the story was around his deteriorating health, casanova conquests, and namedropping of historical figures who held high positions (but mostly not for long i.e. confusing). I was particularly interested in the description of the land and people as I hadn’t read about this region, and that part was great. 

Márquez wrote eloquently, if not repetitively, and many catchphrases were clever. The timeline epilogue was my favourite part, so I probably would have been better off reading a non-fiction version. I’ve read The Story of a Shipwrecked Sailor which was enjoyable and have a couple tbr but not sure if I should dabble again.
