Rating: 4/5

I read this for the first time more than 40 years ago: It was a wonderful book for young boys as it contains lots of elements of adventure. It also made me have a closer look into the Iron Curtain concept and the Hungarian drift into communism (and the drift out of communism during my recent re-read). I suspect it was reasonably close to the truth about young boy's lives in post-war Europe and countries that had vastly diminished freedom.
