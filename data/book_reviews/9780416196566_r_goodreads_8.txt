Rating: 5/5

Yet another book that makes it to the top of my favorites' list. 

I’m so grateful for this book. The Magic of Simplicity. It’s a perfect example of how all you need is heart and an idea. Seeing the original drawings of Winnie the Pooh, which are honestly simple yet efficient, was incredibly inspiring. It shows that you don’t need to be the best at illustrations; you just need the passion to share something unique and creative that comes from the heart. It shows that you don't need to be the best at anything, you simply need to just do it.
