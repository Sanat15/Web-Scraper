Rating: 5/5

Don't let the large font and glossy photos fool you: this book is not just for kids. Although it's great and accessible for a young reader, it's also fascinating and entertaining for us grown-ups. 

I purchased this book while we were visiting Big Sur, and it was so interesting to learn about the little guys we were desperately trying to spot out in the ocean. From the sad story of their near-extinction, to their rising popularity in the country's local aquarium exhibits, this book takes you on the journey of the life of the sea otter, while touching on their history and biology as well.

Did you know that sea otters are one of the only mammals who use 'tools' in their daily life? Why do sea otters do such cute little things with their paws? How do they stay warm in the chilly sea waters of the Pacific Ocean? How are they related to the river otters we see at the zoo? This book covers all of this information and more, complete with gorgeous and adorable photos of the otters.

If you're looking for a great resource to learn the basics about the sea otter (and get a chance to see them being cute and playful in full-color), this book is perfect for any age of reader and ocean-animal enthusiast!
