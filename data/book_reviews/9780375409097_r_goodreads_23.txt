Rating: 4/5

The Judges  is a fine addition to the philosophical novel genre. Wiesel's prose and voice are highlights. They both feel gentle, not in spite of but because of the subject matter. One way Wiesel achieves that effect is through the pace of his sentences. The heaps of commas slow down the reader and give great emotional weight to the words. The pace lets the words linger in your mind long enough to read between them. That is especially effective here because the ideas are the gravamen of this work. I am left wondering how close the relation is between the novel's judge and the Judge.
