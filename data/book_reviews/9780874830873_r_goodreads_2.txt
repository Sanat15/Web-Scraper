Rating: 3/5

Loyal Jones has a great ear for what’s funny and also for the local culture and language of the Southern Mountains. While a book of jokes about preachers may sound irreverent or blasphemous, this collection is neither. Jones collects jokes, stories, and mock sermons that illuminate the all-too-human frailties of ministers and their flocks, but which are never mean-spirited, off-color, or anti-religious. While some of the humor may miss people who are not from the region, most will connect to people from anywhere and some of it is laugh-out-loud funny.
