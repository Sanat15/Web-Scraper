Rating: 5/5

Although the Vietnam war was going on in my lifetime, I didn't know or really understand what it was all about. I didn't understand why our servicemen and women were not treated better when they came home. I didn't get what Jane Fonda thought she was doing. I just didn't know why any of it was as it was. This book has so many different "essays" from people who were there it becomes a little clearer but I also feel the need to understand more about the history of Vietnam. Then will probably find I need to try to figure out what our politicians where thinking. I highly recommend this book but I really think it is only the beginning to get some understanding. Views are presented from ALL sides.
