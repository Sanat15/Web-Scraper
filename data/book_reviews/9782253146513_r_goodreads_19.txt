Rating: 3/5

3.5 
I could have done without the conversations with the dead guy and when the ending happened it happened a little too fast. But this was a good book, I was never sure what to expect.

Made me wonder if I’d want to live in this “paradise.” Free of worldly concerns (for a while anyway), but even an idyllic routine would feel like drudgery after a while. After years anyway. Their utopia didn’t seem to have any plans, just live in the moment. I guess that was the appeal to them. Having found the shangri-la of backpackers, their wanderlust seemed to have been sated. I guess they knew it wouldn’t last forever but that seemed to be the goal. Just lacked a few things I’d say. Plumbing, electricity, little hotel on the beach, airport….
