Rating: 4/5

This is a story about a young girl named Beatrice and her family including 5 siblings. In a small African village in Western Uganda, Beatrice an her family will soon find out they will be one of 12 families that will be given a goat. Little does Beatrice know, this goat will be the reason her dreams of going to school will come true. This book teaches hard work and preservation. We see this family grow as a result of an organization that gives livestock to families all across the world. In this case, Beatrice and her family were lucky enough to receive a goat and bring themselves out of poverty, "Beatrice lives here with her mother and five younger brothers and sisters in a sturdy mud house with a fine steel roof. The house is new. So is the shiny blue wooden furniture inside. In fact, many things are new to Beatrice and her family lately" (p. 1).
