Rating: 4/5

I found this extremely helpful. It was set up into 12 chapter-length "treatment sessions" with commentary, questions, and exercises to do. The "stop-refocus-breathing" technique is very effective. Gave me some insight into why I developed this problem and made me recognize that my symptoms are not unique. Even though I've felt that my panic comes out of the blue (apparently a common feeling), the author challenged me to dig deeper and realize that it is not so random as I'd thought. Much more helpful than the group therapy sessions I've been attending. Highly recommended.
