This was a book of the month for my leadership organization &amp; my first exposure to Mr Bennis. It was clear from the start of the "updated" edition that he has an ideological slant. This annoyed me greatly; but it's what one would expect from someone who has spent most of his career under the comfy umbrella of academia. I'm surprised this was selected as book of the month, I'll never read another Bennis book again.
