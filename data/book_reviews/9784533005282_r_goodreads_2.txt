Rating: 5/5

This "Must-See in Kyoto" did actually help me plan my private study trip to Kyoto very well! It is pocket-size, which means you can tag along during your trip, and has all the information about history, places, people, culture, food and even basic map. It serves much more than a local tourist guide because it is written to be almost like a mini-textbook with drawings! The Japanese is very good in this, believe me (think manga.) More than that, it is from *the* official source: Japan Travel Bureau. I was so happy I could cram all the right temples within my trip. Hey, Kyoto has something like more than 400 historic temples. How do you know where to go? It also has the index and historical time-line in the back.
