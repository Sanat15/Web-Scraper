Rating: 5/5

Setting: New England, USA; 1936-1944.
I have been looking forward to reading this book for some time and finally got around to it, having read some good reviews on Goodreads recently - and I was certainly not disappointed!
This is a tale of small-town politics and the way these affect, and are affected by, the resident families. The story is packed with great characters and incidents - corruption, adultery, underage sex, drunkenness, illegal abortion, secret histories - concentrating largely on youngster Allison Mackenzie and her mother, Constance. But there are many more excellent characters in the book who really came to life for me as the story of the town and its residents unfolded and in many ways I was sad when the book ended. (Good job I've already got 'Return to Peyton Place' on my bookshelf!).
I was also intrigued to read the book as I vaguely remembered watching some of the TV series back in the 1960's although, having done some research, I am now aware that the TV series was only loosely based on the book, that several of the characters were different and it was even set in the 60's rather than the 30's-40's, as opposed to the earlier film which was much closer to the book.
All-in-all, a really great read which I thoroughly enjoyed - had to be 5 stars for me - 10/10.
