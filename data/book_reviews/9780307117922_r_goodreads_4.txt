Rating: 2/5

He-Man was a favorite when I was a kid, but I'm always nervous about revisiting it, knowing it won't hold up. I'm pretty sure I owned this book at one point.

This is very straightforward and surface level story. Skeletor, with the help of his minions, manages to get He-Man's half or the power sword and into Grayskull. He-Man, with the help of his friends, gets in and fights Skeletor. The writing and art remind me of comics at the time, with the action narrated unnecessarily.

As an adult, I see how this was designed to sell the toys. Vehicles in the background. Skeletor uses a trap door in Grayskull which was on the Castle Grayskull toy. Teela is wearing her snake outfit.
