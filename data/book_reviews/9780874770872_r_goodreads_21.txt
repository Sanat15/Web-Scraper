Rating: 4/5

Drawing what’s right in front of your nose instead of what you expect to see staring back at you sounds so much like common sense that you wonder why anyone would bother to mention it. But Betty Edwards does. And with good reason. 

Test it out for yourself. And when you do, like me, you’ll see that in plenty of cases, what you’ve been looking at all these years is suddenly open to question. Take away the lines we think surround all those objects and what’s left are assorted shadows or solid shapes. 

Learning to see them though doesn’t always morph into being able to draw them – but I for one certainly enjoyed the new experience.
