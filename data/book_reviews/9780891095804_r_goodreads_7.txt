Rating: 5/5

I read this book at the time when I needed to be reminded of God’s goodness. It helped me to see that we live in a sin filled world that comes with suffering. Does God like to see his children suffering? No! Everyone needs to read this book because it will help us to see God in the tragedy and suffering in this world. It will move you to seek God and kneel at his feet daily. You will walk away with a better understanding of God’s character. Some may not be in a place to read this but do not dismiss this book.
