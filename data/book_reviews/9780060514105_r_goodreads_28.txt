Rating: 5/5

Molly Moon Stops the World (Book 2) is one of those books that might seem for children after looking at the cover, but I would say that all teenagers should give a chance for this book. I would say that this book led me to enjoy reading. I loved how even though the main characters were around 11-years-old, they were capable to do so many things. The story and plot was very exciting and unlike any other book I have read. All the twists and turns Molly had to face kept me interested and curious to find what would happen next. This book actually made me happy to have the time to read. I would recommend this novel to anyone who wants a story that will keep them absorbed all the way from the beginning, to the end.
