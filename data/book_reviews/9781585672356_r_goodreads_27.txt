Rating: 4/5

I am a huge fan of Carol and her Olive Farm, from the books to the TV show. The Olive Season was such a beautiful tribute to her unborn daughter Carrot.

Carol’s use of description and storytelling is wonderful and transports you to the Côté d’Azur. You can smell the lemons, taste the olive oil and feel the emotions. She is able to convey the ups and downs of life so well whilst also adding facts about nature and the history of our planet. It touches so gently but importantly on child loss and the struggles with grief.

I cannot wait to read the rest of the series and enjoy more of the world through Carol’s eyes.
