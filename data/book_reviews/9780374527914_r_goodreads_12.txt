Rating: 4/5

This is an interesting book (something you say when you don’t know what else to say about a book, but it is not the case here). 

At first, I found it confusing to read — I couldn’t see the sense of the stories, the link between them, or the message behind. 

Once I got further into it, I began to appreciate its simplicity, uniqueness, and value. It’s like a life story told through episodes from here and there. Sometimes it seems simple, sometimes glamorous, other times profound.

I enjoyed it as a long read, taking in a story or two a day. I’ll definitely try another book from Jamaica Kincaid.
