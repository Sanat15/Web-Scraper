Rating: 4/5

The Ninth Annual Collection gathers 26 stories from 1991, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1991") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Kristine Kathryn Rusch's "The Gallery of His Dreams," Jack Dann's "Voices," Nancy Kress's "Beggars in Spain," Alexander Jablokov's "Living Will," Walter Jon Williams's "Prayers on the Wind," Geoffrey A. Landis's "A Walk in the Sun," Connie Willis's "Jack," and Mark L. Van Name &amp; Pat Murphy's "Desert Rain."
