Rating: 5/5

Great Fun

This book had some great old fashioned characters, or I should say older people with wonderful manners. A young woman inherits a B&amp;B. With it she gets 3 older residents that she promises to keep with the house. Only she has some concerns so she hires a private eye to investigate. How these 5 people interact is priceless. This is very much a character driven story with a healthy side of mystery.
