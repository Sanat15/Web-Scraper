Rating: 4/5

4.0 stars. This is the American Edition of this excellent Thesaurus. It is not accurate to say that I have "read" this entire book but I have been using it fairly extensively since I acquired it in 1991 as part of an 8 volume leather bound set from Easton Press called the "Complete Oxford Reference Set." I have found it to be an excellent reference tool that is both easy to use and comprehensive. 

If you need another word for "Valetudinarian" (though I can't for the life of me guess why that would be) just let me know.
