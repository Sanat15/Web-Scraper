Rating: 2/5

A combo of magic and myth = yes. Technically flowery prose = yes. Science fiction this is NOT— if you delete 2 or 3 paragraphs of explaination that the thing he is hunting is likely an alien…. This book would have ZERO aspects of scifi. 

We follow a navajo hunter in his waning years asked to hunt and find this mysterious intelligent creature— Cat. They are both the last of their kind and telepathic and full of mystical magical powers therefore must hate each other and engage in a hunt. (Yeah i was confused too— but dont worry its probably some psychology gobbldy gook deeper meaning im too dumb to get) 

Honestly, one of the tragedies of this book—- if pitched to the right audience I think there are many who would enjoy this style of obtuse yet beautiful prose and mythical meanings. People who like Tanith Lee would particularly love this author. 

I however was not the right person for this book. I want science &amp; logic or at very least character development or world building that i can escape in—- this is for literary university courses.

((You get how pushing a book about magic and myth on people who value logic and scientific explanations won’t go over well….))
