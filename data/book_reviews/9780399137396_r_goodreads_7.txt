Rating: 5/5

Tom Clancy on steriods. Once you get past the techno mumbo jumbo, an Night of the Hawk is an exciting read. I think I have learned how to construct of CV-22 PAVE HAMMER or any of the V-22 Osprey family of aircraft from the ground up. There are so many details of aircraft and armaments, I am not sure what is reality and what is fiction.
The obvious heroes of the novel are Lithuanian General Dominikas Palcikas, the V-22 Osprey and the EB-52 Megafortress. There are enough Lithuanian names thrown around to fill the Vilnius phonebook. And in an age where the F-35 is all the rage due to its stealth and avionics, it is nice to hear stories about a small rotary aircraft and a suped-up B-52. The B-52 has been around since the 1950s and the V-22 since the late 1980s, yet they are both still winning hearts.
