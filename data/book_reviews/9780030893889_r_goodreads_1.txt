Rating: 5/5

This book is part of the Rivers of America series. These books were written mostly in the 1950s and 1960s. There were written by authors who were well familiar with the region. Mr Holbrook is a writer of the northwestern area of the US and is well acquainted with that region. The book is a lively, chatty, account of the Columbia River. It is never trite but full of facts and anecdotes of the Columbia. I liken the reading of this book to sitting by a campfire on a starry night and listening to a smart and knowledgeable friend telling the story of the river. It's not a book you can fly through. Take your time and find out all about the beautiful, powerful, and scenic Columbia. Our American rivers are gems and their stories should be told.
