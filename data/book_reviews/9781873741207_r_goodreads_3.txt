Rating: 3/5

I agree with another reviewer that this doesn’t live up to the first volume. I would have preferred Colin II to maintain the ambiguity of Colin (I); instead, the second volume literalises the legend of the first Colin in a way that the previous volume had successfully avoided. In Benson’s defence, even this literalisation is performed with a certain (though not invariable) lightness of touch. Also, Colin II has much the edge over Colin when it comes to homoerotic content — which almost induced me to give it a 4 star rating.
