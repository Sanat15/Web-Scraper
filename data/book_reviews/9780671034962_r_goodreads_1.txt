Rating: 5/5

Wow! Okay, surprisingly, The London Deception was a really great Hardy Boys book. I was surprised because London isn't a particularly compelling location on its own and the brothers have done numerous theater mysteries at this point so I figured, what could be new? But I was not let down here.

The ghost angle was interesting, though I'm glad it stayed to the side. I honestly didn't know who did it for a while. I loved Frank's sneak tactics to get to the other theater guy multiple times - actually a little badass. I liked that the host family was actually under suspicion and I appreciated the number of red herrings. This was actually a pretty fun book!
