Rating: 5/5

5, 5, a million times 5, huzzah, standing ovation! 

This is by no means my first go-round with this masterpiece, which I first met in my 20s, and have had on my shelf ever since. It's probably been almost 10 years since my last re-read, but it won't be that long until I read it again. And again.

Huge desert island book. Lifetime favorite. Brilliant. Every single page loaded with jewels. 

I f*cking hate Florence King for writing at a level I'll never even approach. 

She's Erica Jong only way way way better. It's a pity for Erica that they're so close in the alphabet. I'm loving my current bathtub read of Fanny (my favorite EJ) but everyone pales in comparison to the sunbright brilliance of Flarnz.
