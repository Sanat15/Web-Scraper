Rating: 4/5

Lord Brandsley rushes off to London to rescue his young cousin from the clutches of a scheming widow. However even he cannot resist the allure of the beautiful Lady Waring. A lot of misunderstandings ensue between the couple and it is a long road to their HEA.

Pretty good book, Claire Lorel, aka Daphne Clair, did a good job and interspersed the story with a lot of actual historic events.
