Rating: 5/5

4.5

I really liked this one. I was glad that it unfolded differently than I was expecting. I thought Charlotte was going to pretend to court him, fall in love, and then break his heart. It doesn’t quite play out like that thankfully (I think that story is a little overdone). Charlotte was a very likable character, I found myself easily relating to her. I also really liked Lord Stoneleigh, even if he was supposed to be this heartless cad. He has become somewhat hardened over the years and came up with a ridiculous notion to put his potential brides through a series of tests to determine if they genuinely like him or just want his title/money. If they fail one of his tests off they go. Charlotte and Stoneleigh had a great relationship, I really enjoyed watching them fall in love. I was pleased that they were together throughout the story with no unnecessary separations. It moved along smoothly, was well written with fun banter and likable characters. This also gets bonus points for having no talk of mistresses. I loved that! 

Content: Clean!
