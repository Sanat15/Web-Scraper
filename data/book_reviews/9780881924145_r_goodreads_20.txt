Rating: 5/5

Wow, this was so thorough and practical, a fabulous reference with detailed advice on when and how to prune each plant. Helpful pictures. Did I read almost every page? Yes. Am I a serious enough gardener to imagine owning my own copy of this? Maybe yes. Is my yard wayy too small to justify purchasing it? Definitely yes. 

My only complaint is that the many excellent reference lists at the back (perennials that can be pinched, perennials that normally do not need pruning for winter, etc etc .) use the Latin/scientific names, many of which I don’t yet recognize.
