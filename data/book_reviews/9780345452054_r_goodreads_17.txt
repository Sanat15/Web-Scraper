Rating: 5/5

After 22 years and still good

I’m writing this review in 2025 and read this when it first came out in 1982. It's still humorous after all the years. We get to see Garfield having fun with Girl Scouts for our benefit. This is the first time we see John’s son who is a little furry for the waiter's comfort. What ensues is worth it for us. John is still interested in Liz which leads to some fun.
