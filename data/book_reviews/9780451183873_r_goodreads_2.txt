Rating: 3/5

When I started this book I didn’t think I was going to like it, but it turned out better than I expected. Oh, it’ll never be considered amongst the great literary accomplishments, but nobody who reads these books should expect it to.

There were definitely times when I was frustrated that William and/or Charles didn’t confess to William’s true identity (yes, the story could still have worked with Serena and Lucy knowing the truth, it just would’ve required a bit more imagination on the author’s part), but the resulting chaos wasn’t as anxiety-inducing as the plots to some of these books.
