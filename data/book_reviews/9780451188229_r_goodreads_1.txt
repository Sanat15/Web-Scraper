Rating: 2/5

Since neither the hero nor the heroine seem to care about London society, the fact that his supposedly bad reputation causes such a hurdle between their happiness, is a rather annoying plot device. Also, it is never fully explained what attracts Dunraven to Georgiana besides her youth and beauty. Absolutely loved supporting character, Buckthorne, Dunraven's hunting obsessed best friend. His obsession is so all-encompassing, so blatantly apparent, that instead viewing Buckthorne as a slimy conniving moocher, you can only shrug, laugh and go along, as Buckthorne saddles Dunraven with a hunting box, hounds, kennel master and all the bills to fund Buckthorne's obsession

 
Georgiana loves Thomas, a fortune hunter, but declares attraction to Dunraven, a rich earl with an exceedingly rakish reputation, in a ploy to make Thomas' suit more favorably viewed. By coincidence, Dunraven purchases Georgiana's neighbor's estate, and after hearing rumors he did so just to be near Georgiana, meets her, recognizes her, hears the truth and proposes. Her parents disapprove, he wins them over. Thomas, seeing Georgiana falling for Dunraven, spreads lies, uses his mistresses' brother Jeremy to pose as Dunraven's illegitimate son. Georgiana's parents disapprove again, but Jeremy reveals the truth. They marry, allowing Jeremy to live with them
