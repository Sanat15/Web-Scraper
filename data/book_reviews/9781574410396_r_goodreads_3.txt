Rating: 4/5

Incredible book. Really. As profound in its ideas and principles as it is entertaining. Can’t recall another book like this. 

- it’s not a good sale unless it’s a good buy for the customer
- all news outlets (&amp; retailers) essentially have access to the same pool of information; it’s the editing that builds the brand and reputation
- story of mink coat not sold to college freshman
- theatrics: his &amp; her airplanes
- profoundness of Martin Luther
- demanding quality from suppliers

So many more. 

A book that I would reread.
