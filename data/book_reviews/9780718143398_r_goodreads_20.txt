Rating: 5/5

Heart- pounding suspense.

A couple is framed by a US government official to take the fall for horrendous murder they did not commit. 14 years on the run and they finally get caught. Now on the run again and this time with a teenager as well the stakes are ramped up. Family first is their motto no matter what.

That government official is now in a real place of power in the government and he wants them wiped out. The only way to get to the bottom of things is to go back to the scene of the crime to try to find evidence which has been locked up for 14 years as it was in an environmental nightmare.

Although a little slow to start for me once it started going it was hang onto your hat because it was nonstop action. Heart pounding suspense with a very nicely laid out plot leading up to that ultimate finish. I found this to be a wonderful book!
