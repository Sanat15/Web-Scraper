Rating: 4/5

A very different beast from "The Bloody Chamber &amp; Other Stories". A woman takes a lover in Tokyo. A rapacious hunter journey takes him from Africa to the heart of the Amazon. A puppet acts out her desires. Innocent children are lost In Eden. 

Angela Carter's prose here is heady, sometimes florid, but always beautiful.
