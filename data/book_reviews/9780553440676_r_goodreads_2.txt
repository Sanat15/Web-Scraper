Rating: 2/5

Moonlight Man by Judy Griffith Gill starts where Dream Man ended - in Max and Jeannie wedding. 

Sharon is playing her harp again over a very long time. She has not played or composed anything since her husband left but she made an exception for her sister. 

Her son has invited a neighbor to the wedding. Marc is the reason why the boy went "missing" couple of months ago, since he likes to go to Marc's to play music but he knows his mother does not like this, so he lied and when Sharon did not find him where he said he will be she panicked. This man, this Marc, who lives in a trailer, who plays guitar and harmonica, bothers Sharon. He reminds her everything that she has kept away for years, everything she is missing but is not willing to resurrect again.

But somehow Marc still keeps getting invited to their house, by her kids, by Max parents, then they all end up going to ski chalet together and when Sharon has to come back to work between Christmas and new year, she has some alone time together with Marc and ...

Marc has wandered around the world for years, working on odd places and searching for his peace. He can not go back home, to his family, since he is not yet ready and they are not yet ready to embrace him again. At the moment he has started a cookie business in a little town in Canada and his neighbor is a wonderful single mother who is playing wonderful music if she lets herself. She has found her way to Marc's heart and Marc is ready to tell her about his past, but when Sharon discovers his story without him she get spooked and flees. When Sharon understands her error, Marc has already left.

So Sharon follows him to Vancouver. First she meets with his grandmother and then she find Marc...

An easy and sweet Christmas time romance story.
