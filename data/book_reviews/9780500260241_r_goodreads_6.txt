Rating: 3/5

I believe the photos were more interesting then the sparse biographical information. I do appreciate how the author ties out autobiographical events to specific stories and moments in novels of Fitzgerald's writing. I would like to see more and have time to read the stories and novels again. I still need to read "The Beautiful and the Damned" and about half of the author's short stories though. This books makes we read more, and definitive biographies on Fitzgerald, and the other three of the early 20th's Four Horsemen of American Literature: Hemingway, Faulkner, and Steinbeck.
