Rating: 4/5

A fun collection of vampire stories, some heavy on the horror, others more of a fantasy, but most of them are really good. Carmilla is a classic and Murgunstrumm is one of my favorite horror stories of all time, and the excerpt from Midnight Mass is fantastic. A couple of the other stories are great as well and one or two were just okay. In a collection that’s not uncommon, but the worst stories here are still decent and the best of the stories here are absolutely worth the rest of the book.
