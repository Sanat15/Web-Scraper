Rating: 4/5

This story has all the feels. I really felt for Whitney who was pregnant with her best friend Dylan’s baby only to find out he’s marrying someone else! Holy heartache!

Dylan is a great guy and Whitney so giving. Together they were either a lot of fun or full of smoldering passion. You really have to read the book to understand why Dylan would be marrying someone else when he so obviously loves Whitney - and why she is pushing him to do it. 

Overall this book gave me both chuckles and tears. I’d definitely read more books by this author after reading this one.
