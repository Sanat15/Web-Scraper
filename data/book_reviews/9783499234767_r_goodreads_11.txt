Rating: 4/5

I can always rely on Rosamunde Pilcher for a mental trip to Cornwall and the sea, a romantic relationship, family conflict and a good ending, and this novella did not let me down. The main character is an upper class young mother who has never taken day to day care of her children (I can’t imagine, but I do know that is how things were). After her husband’s death she visits a family friend in Cornwall and decides to take charge of her life, including caring for her children, standing up to her mother-in-law, determining where she lives and asserting herself in a romantic relationship. Yes, it’s typical Pilcher, but it’s always uplifting. I do think her full novels, such as the Shell Seekers and Coming Home are much better, but this one filled a perfect spot for me.
