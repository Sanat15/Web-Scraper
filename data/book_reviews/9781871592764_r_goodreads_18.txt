Rating: 4/5

Dali is the Mad Hatter of his own world. A genius with no sense of humility, he dissected everybody and everything to particles and atoms. He knows exactly what his destiny is and he will do whatever is necessary to indulge his orgasmic reveries. His brilliant mind and amusingly sickening imagination will trap you with its madness and transform your perception.
His art is consuming him as well as his industrious mind but he will not lose it for he knows one thing straight "The only difference between me and a madman is that I am not a madman."
