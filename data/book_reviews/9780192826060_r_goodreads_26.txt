Rating: 3/5

Plain, spinsterish Lisbeth has become insanely jealous of her beautiful cousin Adeline, and decides that she will finally get even with her. She knows that Adeline's husband is unable to resist feminine charm, so she forms an alliance with the gorgeous and completely amoral Madame Marneffe. I love the following quote; a slightly adapted form even found its way into the dreadful movie version.« Madame Marneffe était la hache, et Lisbeth était la main qui la manie, et la main démolissait à coup pressés cette famille qui, de jour en jour, lui devenait de plus en plus odieuse »Madame Marneffe was the axe, and Lisbeth was the hand that wielded it, and, with rapid strokes, the hand demolished the family which, every day, she came to hate more and more.
