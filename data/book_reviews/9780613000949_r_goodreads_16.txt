Rating: 3/5

I'd been looking for this book for ages. I'd read it as a kid in the late 80s but couldn't remember the name of it. After googling the main theme the titles "Sixth Grade Secrets" and "Pig City" came up. (It was re-released later on with the title "Pig City"). I ordered it off Amazon a week ago and it came today. 

I've just spent an hour reading it. I remember that the main character, Laura, had really long hair and named her secret club after the slogan on her baseball cap, Pig City. 

To make sure none of the club members talk about the club she makes them give her something embarrassing as "insurance" (underpants, a naked baby photo, love letters, etc...) The insurances are kept secret until someone tells about the club and then a war breaks out between Pig City and their rival secret club, Monkey Town. 

It ends after the secrets are revealed and Laura being humbled by a prank where her hair is cut off. 

As a 10 year old I felt I related to Laura as I was always starting my own secret clubs and also had super long hair. I always liked that she was flawed but still likeable. 

Not as good as I remembered it but still surprisingly good for a dated junior fiction.

A witty and fun read.
