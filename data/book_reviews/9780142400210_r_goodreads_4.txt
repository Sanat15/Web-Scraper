Rating: 4/5

Maybe 9 to 11 age range? My kids aren't really into dragons but there is a solid story and the thread to the next story. I thought the characters moved a bit quickly without too much depth, though it has been awhile since I've read this age range and I am looking for something my 11 yo might be interested in reading. My favorite was the connection with Lily and Queen Dragon and the story of the Golden Child. The chapters are good lengths for younger readers and define timeline changes and a good vocabulary builder.
