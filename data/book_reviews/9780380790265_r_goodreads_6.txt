Rating: 3/5

It took a while to get used to the present-tense first-person narrative of the protagonist, but it worked. He used credible street language, too, which was ok but was almost too clean at times. The sweet-talk between the protagonist and his honey didn't work for me.
The story line was what kept me reading. It has a good plot, and I liked how he switched the voices for different chapters.
I would read another of Walker's mysteries.
