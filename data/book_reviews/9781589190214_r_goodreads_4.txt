Rating: 4/5

I did not realize that this book had a Christian message. I obtained it months ago from eReaderIQ and forgot about it. As I was selecting my next book to read, I came across it and thought I would give it a try. I was hooked from the very start. 

The tie in to the Vietnam War is something that always interest those of us of that generation. I never saw "Sammy" coming. His identity was a great twist and made the plot even better.

This book proves once again that you don't need gratuitous profanity, sex or violence in order to have a good story.

There were quite a few kindle formatting issues with the ebook that I found in my version. But I assume that was fixed in subsequent versions uploaded to Amazon.
