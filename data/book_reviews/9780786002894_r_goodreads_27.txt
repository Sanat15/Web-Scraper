Rating: 3/5

Well now. A wonderful insight into how things work in government. How things are manipulated for gain.

Poor Ben...caught in this. But something is watching over him. All those stings? And no medicine? I don't see where Mr. Johnstone believes in a higher power, but he sure writes like he does. That point aside, it's pretty good. A bit difficult to follow in a couple of places, but nothing worth docking off stars for. I did knock off two stars because it seems like Ben's mission in life is t0o have as many women as possible. I mean, come on. Women out there, help me out. You're wiling to drop into bed with anything that comes your way? That's what it's made to seem like.

The part about the government not letting them be is so truthful. Let someone find something that works...and powers that be will not let it stand.

All-in-all, a good start. I will continue reading these as the potential is pretty good.
