Rating: 5/5

The psychological complexity of part one was great. And I had to reread quite a few pages/ paragraphs. But part two really just blew me away! I love Dostoevsky and his psychological studies. 
What a great study of psychological insights to human nature. And once again, the unreliable narrator with a slight twist. Because this time he tells you he is unreliable.

PS. I only read notes from an underground since I had just read the Brothers Karamazov last month. And though the grand inquisitor is well worth re- reading, my brain was a little fried from Notes.
