Rating: 4/5

Embedded in history

This very long complicated book really requires a good grounding in the modern history of China. Because of my age I remember reading in newspapers the events told in the story but I did not study the period as I have studied European history. This is a chronicle of China up to the installation of Mao but before he brought the country to the edge of self-destruction. No doubt the volumes that follow in the series will address more recent history. But for me the interest was in the story of the protagonists which is actually dwarfed by the mass of historical material. I skipped a lot in order to keep track of the characters.
