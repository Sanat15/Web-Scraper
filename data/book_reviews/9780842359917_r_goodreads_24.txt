Rating: 4/5

I rarely read fiction, except for Frank Perreti books, Alcorn's book, DEADLINE, THE SHACK and this book. SAFELY HOME was very meaningful to me because I have some friends who have actually smuggled American "greenbacks" into China in money belts to help house church families whose husbands and fathers are in prison and I have heard many stories from them of the persecution of saints in China. Also, I had spent time in the military in Asia and knew of martyrs and homeless families due to Christian persecutions in China in the 70's.

This book, though being fictional, has been very well researched and possibly based upon very real life scenarios. The "western church" needs to wake up and realize we are not exempt of the same "purifying" and "purging" through persecution here in the USA.
