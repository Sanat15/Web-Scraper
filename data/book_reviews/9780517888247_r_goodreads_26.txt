Rating: 4/5

Dinosaur in a Haystack is a collection of essays written by Stephen Jay Gould. There are 34 essays in total, which draw from a wide variety of source materials. For example, there is an essay focused on the 1993 blockbuster Jurassic Park.

The book is an older collection, but it still holds up in some cases.

Gould was a paleontologist by trade and a prolific writer.

I enjoyed the book. Thanks for reading my review, and see you next time.
