Rating: 5/5

What can I say about this massive sweeping novel. It’s beautifully written. This is a story about generational trauma, the love and bond of siblings, love lost and love made, mental illness, racism and personal prejudices. It’s about life, love, and all the ups and downs that come with both. Pat Conroy writes with such poise and beauty, I was often hypnotized by his words.

Thank you to every one who read this book with me! It was quite the journey and I’m thankful I took it with so many other book lovers!
