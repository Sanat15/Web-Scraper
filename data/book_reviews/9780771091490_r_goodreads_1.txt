Rating: 3/5

This novel tells the story of Abe Spalding, a strong and determined man who opens up a district for farming in southern Manitoba. The author shows us the many hardships and the fewer rewards of being a farmer, especially during difficult times like flooding or drought, in a simple yet effective manner.
He also shows us the struggle Abe has to adjust to the world of the 1920's and the newer generation who don't have the same values or goals as himself.
