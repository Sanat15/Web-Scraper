Rating: 5/5

As a puppy, Buster was very shy. Would he ever grow out of it?

Phoebe, Roger's other dog, was not shy but bossy. Roger's personal menagerie included many cats, too, also bossy.

In both chapters of this little book, Lisze Bechtold gives a very young child's view of life. Animals included.

The scale of drama is pitch perfect, and her illustrations are absolutely perfect for conveying the tiny but meaningful plots in the two chapters here. Well done, Lisze Bechtold. FIVE STARS.
