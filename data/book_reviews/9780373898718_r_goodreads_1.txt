"That Boston Man" is the story of Lexie and Rome.

A fiery love story set in Massachusetts between an enraged politician and a feminist reporter. Our h is a strong, independent woman who loves her job at the newspaper. When her comments about the H, a politician with a colorful image- go viral, she never expects to meet the man himself!
But that's what he does- tracks her down, challenges her to ask him out, kisses her passionately, burns her alive with jealousy, falls for her and slowly woos her to fall in love with him.

Thoroughly enjoyed it! Their chemistry was SO HOT, the envy so sexy, and their love so passionate. I liked how her past was explored and the ultimate surrender was sweet!
