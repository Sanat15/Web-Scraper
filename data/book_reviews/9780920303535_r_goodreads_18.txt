It's Pride Month and I'm reading books geared to children in support that, all people should be treated as equals and with love and respect.

This is an excellent book talking about your body. It can be "big and small, short and tall, young and old, every BODY is different!" It then goes on to explain each of the body parts like your hair, your eyes, nose, ears.......right down to your feet and what they can look like and what they can do. 

The pictures are Great! It's the first picture book I've seen with stretch marks on the belly. I love the diversity in the pictures. I love there is a child in a wheelchair and a man wearing eye shadow and a woman with hair in her armpits, and another with scars on her arm. And I love the two pages with the naked couple running after their naked son. Ok, heck I love it all!
