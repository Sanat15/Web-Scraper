Rating: 4/5

I picked up this series as a Black Friday special. Sometimes the soul just needs a little tilted love. I read this one right after The Highwayman. It fulfilled my need for a little Highland romance.

I enjoyed the addition of a curse between the McLean's and Campbell's. It was nice to have an actual reasons for the two clans to be feuding instead of just relying on "they have fought for as long as anyone can remember" line that is common. 

Felicia was extraordinarily brave the time period. She fought for the right to make her own decisions throughout the entire book. She made nothing easier for any of the males in the book. It was nice to have a heroine in a historical novel not swoon at the first sight of a charmingly heartbroken hero.

My only issue is that when the story moved to Edinburgh it began to drag. The fight to get Felicia released from her betrothal was anticlimactic. It could have cut out a solid two chapters without impacting the storyline. That being said, I thoroughly enjoyed all of the characters in this story. I can't wait for my next HR itch so I can read the next one.
