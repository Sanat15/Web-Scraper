Rating: 4/5

The second instalment of the Hern's Regency Rakes series features the most notorious rake - Jack, who by a massive bit of unfortunate events is now the heir. He has inherited massive debt and has to marry for wealth. No one knows this though, especially not Lady Mary who has decided to help him in his quest. So imagine what happens when he learns that his dear Mary actually is an heiress and what happens when she finds out about his true motives.

I was surprised with the HEA with this one. From her painful past I wasn't sure if it would turn out! An enjoyable story from Hern, as always!
