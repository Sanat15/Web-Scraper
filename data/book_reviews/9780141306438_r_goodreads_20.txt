Rating: 4/5

Mohr, N. (1999). Felita. New York: Puffin.
Genre: Fiction
Felita loves her neighborhood where she lives in New York, but her family decides they must move so Felita and her brother can have a better education. The new neighborhood is nothing like her old one, people there are rude to Felita and her family and some of her neighbors are racist. Eventually Felita’s family decides to move back to their old neighborhood, but Felita is worried that things will never be the same. 
I would use this book to help students see how racism can affect families and even neighborhoods. Students would talk about how they would feel if they were discriminated against by people in their neighborhoods.
