Rating: 1/5

There's REALLY nothing to this. I thought when I picked it up at the book store it was going to be something like Tao of Pooh - boy, was I both wrong and disappointed. It's a bunch of non-sense that drones on without end (until you give up on it at least). I'm not buying the various Pooh characters are attached and driven by astrological signs no more than I think I am. Even for a New Age believer, this book is a long stretch on having any value. I'm surprised Milne's estate hasn't stepped in on this.
