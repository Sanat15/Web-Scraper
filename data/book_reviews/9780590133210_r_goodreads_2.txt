Rating: 3/5

Robert and Rebeka are twins who died in a boating accident, along with their dog, Thatch. Fifty years later, their home has been turned into a vacation rental, and their first guests include a girl and a boy around their age. The girl, Kim, is so sure the house is haunted, and Robert and Rebeka aren’t about to disappoint her. However, haunting takes a backseat to discovering the mystery of what Kim and her brother found at the lake.

I own book eight in the series, and so when I was visiting somewhere for the night and saw they had the first book, I just had to read it. It was a quick read, and a fairly decent book. I thought the mystery was a bit bland. There really weren’t any stakes to figuring out what the treasure was, other than curiosity. I was curious too, but that was it. There was nothing else to make me care.
