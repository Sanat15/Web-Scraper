Rating: 4/5

This extended safari crossing several bodies of water and multiple continents is mandatory reading for nature documentary fanatics. David Attenborough is renowned in his role as an omnipotent wildlife narrator but if, like me, you were unfamiliar with the qualifications and experience that make his expertise and authority incontrovertible, you'll be fascinated by this eloquent account of his travels collecting specimens for the London Zoo. At times harrowing, the ordeal never seems to lessen his drive to humanely capture and transport the wonderful animals described in these pages. The diversity of techniques necessary, the required improvisation of tools and the range of different geographies would be rare for any person to encounter in a lifetime. Modern sensitivities show some of the interactions and attitudes to be less than ideal, but overall, readers will be pleasantly surprised to find that the vast majority of the journey and its operations were conducted with the best intentions to the benefit of both the wildlife and its environment, without disruption of the surrounding human communities.
