Rating: 5/5

Amazing. This one will stay with me for a while...

Ellis Peters writing is extraordinary. Period.
Plots are honed to a sharp point.
Characters care three dimensional.
More could be written about the DCI could be fleshed out a bit so could DI False as wife and Dominic False 's Mother...maybe those characters will be elucidated in future novels (Future to me! Cannot wait to read the rest...)
Only wish there had been more about the Folk Music Course in this novel...think she might have fit more of That in this one!
She is, as I continue to say, one of the best writers of Murder Mysteries around.
I, simply, love her writing.
Her descriptions of people, situations, scenes, and environments are truly comprehensive in very few words...astonishing.
