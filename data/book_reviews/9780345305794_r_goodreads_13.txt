Rating: 4/5

Story about humanity's first attempt to land on the Moon, written in twenty days, as a debut, that doesn't actually show you the landing or the Moon itself, just the preparation beforehand, sprinkled with some optimistic outlooks on space-faring about to come with the dawn of the third millennium. Now that I think about it, we haven't progressed that far since 1950s, the book is basically about Artemis II that successfully completed its fly-around a few months ago. We should have been living on Mars by now.
