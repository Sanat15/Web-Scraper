Rating: 5/5

The importance of children developing an intimate relationship with nature through free exploration, as well as the wild nature of children, which adults tend to smother.
One idea I gleaned from this book:
Unlike more primitive cultures throughout history, we have created a separate "Kidworld", even though they long to experience the real world/adult world. We need to allow them to work/play alongside us--even teach young children how to cook and use knives, etc. This is so deeply fulfilling and beneficial to both parent and child.
