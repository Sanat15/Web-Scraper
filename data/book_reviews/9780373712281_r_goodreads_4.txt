Rating: 4/5

In Mommy Said Goodbye, I didn't like Ann who was a minor character investigating the hero based on her dead cop father's notes but she redeemed herself by chasing the truth instead &amp; in this book I loved Ann, her dedication to her job, her insecurity when it came to being a woman since she was raised by a cold father &amp; spent her life trying to please him.

Juan was her partner &amp; he was someone who saw through he tough cop facade to a woman, whose mother committed suicide, who was lonely and didn't know her own beauty. Over the course of the book we see Ann change &amp; make gradual changes and her weighing them &amp; be scared about them. Like the first time she gets her eyebrows done, she is surprised to see the change &amp; even when she bought a new wardrobe &amp; started to find confidence with the help of Juan in her womanhood.

I really liked the hero as well, he was sweet, honourable &amp; after he found himself in love with Ann &amp; wanting more with her. I really liked how he was willing to make a sacrifice for her when no-one else in her life had made one for her(about the job). Even though this book features no love scene you don't feel the dearth since the romance is so well done.

I have to say I am really enjoying the books by this author.
