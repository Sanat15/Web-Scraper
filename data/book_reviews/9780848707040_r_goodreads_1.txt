Rating: 5/5

New Ideas for Lap Quilting by Georgia Bonesteel, author is one of my favorite quilting books to use as a reference. This one of the series gives ideas to use the squares in clothes, for aprons and household decorations. These projects make great gifts for family and friends and take far less time to make instead of a quilt. The book also comes with a pack of plastic templates for quilting designs to finish projects and make them individual. Lovely photos, directions and a fun book to use and encourage creativity.
