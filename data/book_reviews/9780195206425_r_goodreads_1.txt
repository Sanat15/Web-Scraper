Rating: 4/5

A fanciful children's picture book of amusing poems about 'sky people' who paint the firmament in all those ever-changing colours. Neil Canning has painted the backgrounds of different skies very atmospherically - I also like the repeated motif on each page. However, Graeme is also a very good comic artist: I would have loved to see his own take on the Skylighter people and their engineering. 

Not much more to say, really - it's a fairly short book, although of largish format - but will charm imaginative youngsters, and the celebration of a variety of weather conditions is always interesting in these Islands!
