Rating: 3/5

Jill Johnston was a literate Lesbian Separatist who never came to grips with the fact that, until we conquer the question of parthenogenisis, women will need some donations from men to keep producing children. Those of us who had boy children were outcasts by her definition at a time when we needed community. It was a rough time for everybody, and Lesbian Nation left no room for any disagreement with her theories. It is now a period piece, though it was a very hot flash in the pan back in the day.
