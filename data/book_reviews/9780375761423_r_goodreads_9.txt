Rating: 5/5

Once again, the master storyteller hits the ball out of the ballpark with this epic tale. Whether the irony and symbolism is shown to the sadistic Russian who gets his head bashed in by a coworker in the same way he killed so many baby seals, to the rigors of the Iditarod sled race with moose kills of the sled dogs, the greed and perseverance of the gold miners to the struggle for statehood. Michener keeps your interest throughout the 868 pages. This is what all writers should strive for.
