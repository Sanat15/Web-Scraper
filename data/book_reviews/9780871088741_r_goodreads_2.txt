Rating: 5/5

A boy in a U.S. military family stationed in S.E. Asia goes out to play one day with his friends. They find a grenade and his whole world changes. He is sent to his grandfather's place in rural Arkansas where he grows up. This is all the plot I will reveal and it doesn't convey how extraordinary this book is. Through his grandfather and two other elderly males, he discovers the magic of Starlight Creek and the small world around it. THE BEST BOOK I READ ALL YEAR

And I am putting on the list to re-read in 2018!
