Rating: 4/5

The cast aren't in their happy place as the book starts. Thorn is worried she can't trust Grandma Ben. The village has been attacked and rat creatures are swarming everywhere. The villagers hate Phoney Bone, but the rat creatures want to kidnap him for some sinister sacrifice. Thorn's dreams are haunted.
But at the same time we have the usual goofy interactions between the cast that keep this from being just an epic fantasy graphic novel. And we have the big reveal of why Phoney is marked by destiny and damn, that was brilliant.
