Rating: 4/5

The first of three memoirs by Arnold. This first one covers his youth, how he came to love bodybuilding, his Mr. Olympia days, and professional relationship with Joe Weider. Imo, unlike the latter two (Total Recall 3/5 stars and Be Useful 3/5 stars) here Arnold is much more candid about his own insecurities and flaws (for example how in his youth he mimicked his idols’ objectification of women). In the second half of this book Arnold distills advices about bodybuilding and weightlifting, starting with the benefits of such, followed by his top 10 irreplaceable exercises, and concluding with programming various split routines. Throughout both halves of this book Arnold intersplices his life philosophy since youth, which has the ancillary (or perhaps intended) effect of being motivational. At each stage of success, rather than resting on his laurels Arnold remained committed to the idea that the meaning of life “is not to simply exist, to survive, but to move ahead, to go up, to achieve, to conquer.”
