Rating: 5/5

For a book that came out in 1975, the info is still remarkably relevant. Every chapter held practical and down to earth advice for what to think about when mapping out your possible transition to living a country life. I appreciate how the author broke planning into pursuits that bring in income, efforts that decrease living/operational expenses, and projects that have no practical applications but can just be considered luxuries. I love how "for the joy of it" was given this equal footing.
