Rating: 4/5

I skipped a large chunk of the middle bc it seemed too depressing. i just had to know who she ended up with (was rooting for chance) but there's a lot of infidelities on his part and i didn't read the reasons why bc i skipped over those parts but i did not like how fancy took on the blame for herself. had a bad premonition based on Jewel's disapproval of her marrying chance in the beginning. sad about chance and our fmc but it is oddly realistic in a way that most novels aren't. the bad boy with the swagger that comes up empty in the end. like jason said, swagger but not substance. to some extent true if he let his business go to nothing like that, Madigan's interference or not.

still am wistful for a happy ending for chance, our fmc, and their children. though i understand why they did not work out.

reading this book was almost worth breaking my daily bar studying routine for. would've made it worth it if they had ended up together but like i said i know they couldn't.
