Rating: 5/5

Easily my favorite so far (from the Great Tales of the American West
by Harry E. Maule) is this excellent story from Zane Grey. In just a few short pages, we get a story with enough character to spark a screenplay, which I'm surprised not to have found yet. In Tappan's Burro we get desert, we get snow, we get forests, we get sunrises and sunsets, we get gunplay, we get a poetic and meaningful conclusion. Loved it.
