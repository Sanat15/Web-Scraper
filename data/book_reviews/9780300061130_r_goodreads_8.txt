Rating: 4/5

This is a great book, very detailed account of what happened in Cambodia after Pol Pot's thugs took over the country.
The Khmer Rouge was responsible for the death of (at least) 1.5 million people (in a country of 7.7 million people) in less than 5 years of regime. And that toll would have been much higher had Vietnam not invaded the country.
I'm adding this book to the:
 "But true communism was never really implemented" bookshelf.
I highly recommend this reading.
