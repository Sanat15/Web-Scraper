Rating: 5/5

Young readers of this beginning chapter book will easily identify with Buster, a big dog who is shy and feels small. Buster is bullied by the dogs and cats he lives with, and feels that wishes he could be more like them. In the two chapters of this book (Buster's First Party and Buster and Phoebe) Buster's shyness *almost* makes him miss out on cake and ice cream and he manages to find his special skill that saves the day when trying to be more like Phoebe doesn't work out so well for him. The illustrations are charming and the text is simple and the reader is sure to fall in love with Buster.
