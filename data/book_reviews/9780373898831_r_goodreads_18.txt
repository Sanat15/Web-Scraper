Rating: 2/5

These Harlequins always get a 1 or 2. They aren't good by any means, not even taking into account ALL the issues within the pages. However, I find something really fun about picking up these books you were curious about as a kid to see what's inside. The entertainment value of that alone is a solid star. It's also gives a great deal of insight into the way society was at the time, and I love historical glances in novels as they were written. From a 2023 perspective....Yikes.
