Rating: 5/5

My First Agatha Christie Novel!

One of my family members recommended this book so top of the reading list it went.

This is a Sherlock Holmes style book with the main character, Detective Hercule Poirot, methodically investigating a murder that occurred on board a train. Which of the passengers committed the crime or was it someone else altogether?

This is a very short book, and Christie writes in such a way as to compel the reader to discover what happens on the next page. Murder on the Orient Express is a joy to read!
