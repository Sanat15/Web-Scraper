Rating: 4/5

I wanted an audio book to while away long motorway hours. I looked for one read by the author and Simon Brett was brilliant. His change of voice for each character really brought them to life in the way he intended. I have read most of the books in the series and knew the sort of story to expect.

If you don't know Mrs Pargeter she is the elderly widow of the most benevolent and gentlemanly crime boss that you could imagine. He left his widow very well off not just financially but also with a most grateful network of old colleagues.

This story begins when one of these colleagues drives Mrs P to the plot of land on which another of the colleagues has just started building her new home.
When a body is found in what is going to be the wine cellar and the builder is taken into custody on suspicion, Mrs P starts to investigate.

If you like your murder mysteries with a slowly unfolding plot, larger than life characters, just enough excitement and a good measure of good natured humour, not to mention a death defying dash on a motorised lawnmower then this could be for you.
