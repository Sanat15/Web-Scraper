Rating: 4/5

My edition of the book has a really frumpy looking woman sat in a rattan chair knitting a sock. Who on earth decided on that? 

Anyway - rather an odd premise in which a reluctant country girls 'wins' a prize to go on a date with a world famous super-crooner. In a refreshing twist she isn't starry eyed and seduced by the glamour of hobnobbing about with the rich and famous, in fact she's repulsed by it all and revolted by the fans and paparazzi that dog the H's every step. She ends up telling him 'no thanks this sucks' and goes back to her nice quiet life (hence the sock cover maybe?) and he decides to wind down his superstar career so they can get married and have a normal life.

WOW - when does THAT ever happen? Normally it's the woman having to change and adjust to the man's superstar lifestyle, so I seriously LOVED how the opposite happened here.
