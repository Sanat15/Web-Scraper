Rating: 5/5

Overall, this series was a solid 4.5-star read. The first book is a bit slow because it sets up everything for the rest of the series. In the third book, Kingscote, we continue to follow Coral’s struggle to find her son. 

Without giving any spoilers, my favorite thing about this series is how God is front and center. Coral and her family go through so much, but because of their love for Jesus, they are able to keep going. Even if you don’t care for romance-heavy books (like myself), there is something in this series for everyone. Well done Linda Chaikin!
