Rating: 4/5

You know, I purchased this as a paperback when it was published in 1986. I was a young, High School girl and I loved it! I kept it with me an re-read it over the years. Now that I could purchase it electronically I did! :-)

Sighs. All right. I read it again, only this time with the viewpoint of being thirty years older. I still enjoyed the book. Eventually I became a bit annoyed with Leslie, our heroine, because she had a huge "too stupid to live" moment. But then, her character is a seventeen year old girl fresh from the country, not a jaded city girl. Regardless of that, I was still smiling when I finished reading and I always count that as a win.
