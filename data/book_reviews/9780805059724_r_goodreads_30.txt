Rating: 4/5

Haunting. 

The plot is languid. The prose ethereal and Steinbeck-ian to my ears but others say Faulkner-ian though I am not familiar with the latter's works. I thought this was a second read but it isn't, it's a first. 
At some points this was a 2 star read for slow pacing, but I am almost positive this was done on purpose.  Bahr is forgiven because there is supernatural in this book which was unexpected.
