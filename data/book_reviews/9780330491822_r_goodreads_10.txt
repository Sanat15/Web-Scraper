Rating: 4/5

This seems like writing that's on the way out. Thoughtful pieces about popular culture, written by a clever, cultured person who can put the silliest things into a context worthy of serious consideration? TL:DR much?

It's a shame. Lane is nearly peerless in his . . . lane. He hits all the notes of the scale with his thoughts, and his writing is a pleasure to read. I savored this in modest bites, and I was rewarded with wit, enlightenment, quirk and snark.
