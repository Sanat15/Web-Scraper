Rating: 4/5

This is a fun, historical romance. Sometimes predictable but easy to read and a nice "break" from reality. The overall story is a comedy of errors and coincidences. Two girls switch places in an effort to avoid unwanted matrimony to strangers. At the same time, the intended groom for one of the girls, also switches places with a friend. Of course, the "replacement" bride and groom find themselves falling in love and distraught over finally revealing the truth. But the true intended bride and groom also (coincidentally) end up meeting and falling in love and dread revealing the truth. Though the grandmother who started the betrothal didn't foresee or intend the masquerades, she realizes almost immediately what has occurred and rather than calling them out, she helps them along and enjoys the show, knowing that it will end as she wanted to begin with. The story is an obvious nod to Shakespeare and even mentions him at one point as not being able to devise a better plot. This is a very quick and enjoyable story.
