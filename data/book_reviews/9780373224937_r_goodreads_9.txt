Rating: 4/5

I wasn’t crazy about this book and I thought that Derrick was way past psychotic that is for sure. I felt the way he treated people especially his brother was awful.

Kit ran away when she was seven months pregnant. She witnessed her husband, Derrick kill a man, Jason St. John and she knew she couldn’t raise her son with that man. Kit’s been running for a while and she thought she was safe, but she was wrong.

Sander has been following his brother’s orders to find Kit and her baby. Sanders is going blind because he doesn’t know the real truth, which shocked me too.

Luke St. John has been following Sanders hoping to find Kit and the proof that Derrick killed his brother. Luke’s vengeance at first turned me off to him, but Kit’s Innocence slowly changed him for the better. It was in the last moments of this book that he found a reason not to kill in his eye for an eye justice.

I was thrilled with the big reveal near the end of and Luke was as well. B.J. Daniels did a fantastic job with that shocker. I was amazed because there wasn’t even a hint of it.

Luke has a past that Derrick tried to use to lure Kit back to him, but I was so glad it didn’t work. She knew that something had hurt him not only physically but emotionally before Jason ever died.

Luke kept his promise to Kit and by the end they both got what they needed a real chance at love.
