Rating: 3/5

A fair introduction to the ramblyness of Sholem Aleichem? Not sure.
A reminder of my favorite Kate Bush song, the Song Of Solomon, yes.
A nice stream-of-conciousnessy sadness about a young man in love that captures pretty well the personal pain, strangeness, joy of love, interlinked with the earthbound ritualness of shtetel life. Yes. Ready to read more.
