Rating: 5/5

I've read the bulk of this book, and the library has requested I return it.

It's excellent, and the insights are clearly 'hard won'. I've given it 5 stars as for me there is a light yet very wise touch. There's a knowledgeable and accessible voice, which goes beyond many self-limiting restrictions in the psychological domain.

The chapter 'The Homecoming' is a gentle and insightful synthesis and a worthwhile read to assist in making sense of many types of journey. A perhaps 'unassuming' and yet valuable investment of time.
