Rating: 4/5

This book dives into what travelers and locals want to see if only they knew about it. 

It lists the best local dining, quirky cultural tidbits, hidden attractions, unique finds, and unusual locales. 

The book leads the reader down the roads less traveled and what many other travel books don’t offer.

Also, I enjoy the book's stories about the people who live there and the history of some of these uniquely southern places. 

I turned to traveling in Mississippi and looking for what to do in non-major cities such as Hattiesburg and Meridian.
