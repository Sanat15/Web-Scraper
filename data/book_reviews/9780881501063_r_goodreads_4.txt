Rating: 4/5

This 1940 mystery is a solid addition to a great series of classic mysteries. 

Asey Mayo is "the Cape Cod Sherlock". He is a laconic old time Cape Codder. He was a sailor as a young man. Everyone on the Cape knows him now. He gets called in when a mystery happens. 

A young woman thinks she discovered a corpse in a sail loft. She runs into the street in a panic and Asey crashes his new car avoiding her. When they get back to the loft, the body is gone. An ambitious local politician disappears. Asey gets knocked on the head and tied up. The mystery gets deeper and deeper until Asey figures it out. 

This story has most of the standard pieces for this series. There is a stranger lurking around in the woods. There is an old Cape Codder who wants nothing to do with the police. Dirt roads are treacherous. People pop up to Boston and back. Asey is helped by the medical examiner, Doctor Cummings, annoyed by the State Police Inspector Hansen and surprised by the help he gets from his cousin Syl.

Like many of these stories, things get pretty complicated by the end and the ending is not completely convincing. But I don't read these for cleverly constructed puzzles. I read them for a good, humored story about old Cape Cod. This delivers that.
