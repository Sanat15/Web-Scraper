Rating: 3/5

This is the first in the series of Nathan Starbuck novels set during the American Civil War. Nate, a Boston man has run away from Yale University seminary, rebelling against his father the Reverand Elial Starbuck a hell fire and damnation, Calvinist minister, to chase a young actress. His fortunes find him in Richmond at the time of secession. Nate is initially befriended by a wealthy landowner who turns out something like the evil sergeant of the Sharpe novels and indeed there are a number of similarities
The battle at Manassas is described in chilling reminder of the horrors of war as the two armies collide in breath taking action, leaving Nate promoted but still with Washington Faulconer as an enemy.
