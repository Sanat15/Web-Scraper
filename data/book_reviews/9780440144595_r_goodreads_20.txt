Rating: 5/5

We have a rule in our house that one must read the book before watching the movie or show on which it is based. Little did I know that this novel is actually based on the film! And it’s just completely riveting! This is a true story about friendship, honor, perseverance and hope. Its history is also a painful reminder that the more things change, the more they remain the same. I can’t wait to watch the movie!
