Rating: 5/5

So this book doesn't have all the bells and whistles: it's just plain black and white text, no pictures. But it's packed with fun, and you can't beat the price, usually $5 - $10. Because it doesn't have all the features of other books, it's quicker to read. Plus, it's easy to find in many stores like Toys'R'Us and Barnes and Noble. 

I loved playing the games with my baby, and it's a great reference if you can't remember the words to favorite lullabies. It also teaches different techniques, like suspense, to get the best response out of your baby. Quick to read, easy to find, and fun to use - this book is the trifecta of parenting books.
