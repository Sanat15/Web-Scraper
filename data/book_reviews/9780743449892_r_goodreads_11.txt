Rating: 4/5

It was an absolute opposite of what I imagined! A good read, with good suspense, despite some annoying events. However, it was quite informative and educative! If we keep the perspective of the year 1195, everything that occurs in the story fits perfectly. Though at some places the author tries to justify the obviously immoral and mean behaviour of the protagonist, is what irritated me. Along with that, the otherwise "wise" coroner losing his wisdom when it mattered the most was truly annoying. Other than that, I quite enjoyed it!
