Rating: 5/5

This book left me breathless. Before posting a review, I had to ponder and write out my thoughts &amp; feelings. Patin’s writing &amp; command of languid exquisite and a delight to read. The story is one of humanity, brokenness, relationships and a desperate cry for faith to save us. It reveals the hurt of systemic evil and how people try to live good and faithful lives within it. A powerful story of family, relationships, brokenness &amp; faith.
