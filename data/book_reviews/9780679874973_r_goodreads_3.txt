the movie adaptation with pictures this is a fun quick read perfect for kids of all ages to share makes me want to re watch the movie, I LOVE Jim Carrey, such a funny man. Ace must find the missing Shikaka or there will be no wedding of the Whachai Princess to the Whachootoo Tribesman...only one problem Ace so NOT like Bats and Shikaka is a White BAT. Hi jinks and fun ensue as Ace takes the case laugh out loud funny...a sweet fast fun read.
