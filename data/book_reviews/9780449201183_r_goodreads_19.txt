Rating: 5/5

This is one of Beaton's most entertaining stories for the simple reason that I really liked Lucy. I liked that she was willing to have an affair, that in no way was she passive. When the idiot of her husband is murdered, she sells their London house and one of their Boxes and goes to the country to get their lands in order. She is practical and even though she is so young, and a bit of a goose about her Duke, she does things.
