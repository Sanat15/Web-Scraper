Rating: 5/5

In searching for a different book, I stumbled across this one. There are a handful of books from my childhood that I nearly wore out, and this book is one of them. I wanted to BE Flying Squirrel, only without the obsession about Miss Owl. (And I was always concerned Miss Owl was going to eat him. I was an interesting kid.)

I give it five stars for the memories and the joy I got from it as a child. I don't know what it would deserve if I could read it objectively.
