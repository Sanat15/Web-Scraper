Rating: 4/5

I really liked this one as the story was told more through action than anything else. The story centered around a demon looking to wreck havoc but possessing effective host bodies, one of which is a past his prime pro wrestler. The other portion of the story is of a good guy cop who knows the demon is real but does not want to believe because it all sounds so crazy. Eventually, Pierce (cop) confronts Kogan (demon) in spiritual warfare. This was a lot of fun to read and I will try more fron Griffiths as his style is action, action, action.
