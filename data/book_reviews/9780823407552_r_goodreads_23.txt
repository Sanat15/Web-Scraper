Rating: 4/5

The language was a little archaic. I found it charming, but after two pages Theo (6) announced "I didn't understand ANY of that." I set it aside and told him we could try again when he's a bit older. Well, he felt a bit older ten minutes later, we tried again, and the boys all greatly enjoyed the story! There were some phrasings that I changed on the go as I read aloud, but for the most part I tried to stick to the original text and let the boys figure it out through context.
