Rating: 3/5

3.5⭐️
I thought this one was okay. I liked it but it didn’t really feel like it fit with the rest of the series. Polly wasn’t as compelling as her mom and uncles had been as main characters and the plot didn’t grip me as much. It felt like the other books had more of a science base that this one was missing. If I didn’t have the previous books in the series to compare this to I think I would’ve rated this one a bit higher. I definitely got into it more later on in the book.
