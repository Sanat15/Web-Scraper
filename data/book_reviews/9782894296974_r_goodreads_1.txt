Rating: 5/5

found in my childhood books - nicely illustrated. beautifully illustrated, actually. great job on their part.

told quickly. not my favorite retelling. didn't really weave anything with the words but just told it like it was and let the illustrations guide the child's imagination. which i don't see as fun or interesting now, but as a kid i liked it a lot.

everyone knows beauty and the beast is based partly off of the story of eros and psyche - especially reminiscent is how eros is seen as a beast to the oracle and to the gods of olympus, and how psyche/beauty (the same thing in the story actually, psyche is known as the most beautiful mortal girl lol) is given dinner by invisible servants. it's fun to see how myths change and get into the collective consciousness of society...

cute version. glad my dad or someone got it for me as a kid.
