Learian

Of all the French poets I know
Arranged on my shelves in a row
Who brandish their wits
From Berck to Biarritz
There’s no one as fun as Queneau

Dickinsonian

O let me tell you all a tale—
About a book—I read
I laughed so hard—my crow she thought—
I had gone off—my head

Heroic

Today I read a fine book by Queneau
Which I found even funnier than Godot

Dantesque

O shouldst thou ever come across this book
Nel mezzo del cammin di nostra vita
I enjoin thee to go and have a look

Whether thy name be Mary or Saint Peter
Salome François Mohamed or Mia

Because I couldn’t relate
To the main character whose neck
Conceit did elongate
