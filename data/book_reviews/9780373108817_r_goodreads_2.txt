Rating: 3/5

A solid HP. Heroine is in love with younger brother and waits eagerly for him to return home to her. He does - with a fiance in tow. Older brother, a widower, knows how she feels and sweeps her into an engagement and whirlwind wedding because he has a young son who needs looking after. 

They have a wonderful honeymoon and heroine is in love. Only problem, hero doesn't want her to get pregnant because reasons. She does get pregnant and the rest of the novel is how they work through the hero's fear and the heroine's jealousy of first wife. Some nice scenes of the H/h really communicating.
