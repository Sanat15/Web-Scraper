Rating: 2/5

I expected something very different from this book. That's not necessarily a bad thing: As someone who usually isn't a huge fan of love stories, I should have been thrilled that this wasn't as romance heavy as I imagined. Sure, Ruth's relationship to her love interests is technically the plot, it's not what most scenes focus on. We mostly follow her being a college student and other aspects of her every day life. That was kinda nice, though not very deep, but I needed way more scenes of her and Quin talking and actually falling in love and the premise this novel was pitched to me as was rushed into the final few pages. There would have still been enough to like in this for me to rate this 3 stars - e.g. I liked the historical aspects and how the settings were utilized -but damn, the final few plot points were just frustrating and I totally disgree with their message. I wasn't the target audience to begin with, though.
