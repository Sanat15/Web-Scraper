Rating: 5/5

For cat lovers this rates a five and a possible 4 for other readers. It was well written and explanations of customs and details always add to this author's work. She's very skilled at descriptions of nature and weather also. Probably my rating is for my personal enjoyment since a cat played a major part in this regency romance and was responsible for much of the humor. MC Beaton must have been an animal lover since her two popular mystery series include cats for Agatha Raisin and dogs for Hamish MacBeth. In those, the characters more than the mystery is what entertains. I see on GR this book is listed as #6 in a regency series but so far I've read only this one . She has several regency series; there are six, each with 6 novels. One I especially liked is The Poor relations, light-hearted and humorous.
