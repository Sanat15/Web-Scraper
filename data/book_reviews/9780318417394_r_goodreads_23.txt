Rating: 5/5

How lovely! There's so much more to this than the Disney movie which is the only version I've previously known. We get a lot more of the dogs' internal dialogues. I was surprised to find that the 15 Dearly pups are actually the progeny of Pongo &amp; Missis who doesn't rate a mention in the film, among several other things. Cruella is much more devilish in the book &amp; I had to laugh about where Mr Dearly got his money from. Not that the movie isn't lovely, but it is definitely artificially sweetened. The book is a delightful read, much meatier than I expected, &amp; I intend to read The Starlight Barking as well.
