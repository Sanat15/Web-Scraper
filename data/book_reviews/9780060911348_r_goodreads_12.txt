Rating: 5/5

What a tremendous book, with very well constructed thoughts and information. It offers insights into relationships between men and women that I found fascinating. I first read it over 20-30 years ago. My copy has been read so many times by myself and people I have loaned it to that it is now held together by a rubber band. I finally did a search and now have a second copy.

If you are married or are in a relationship, I would be amazed if you are not enlightened by many of the author's theories and conclusions.
