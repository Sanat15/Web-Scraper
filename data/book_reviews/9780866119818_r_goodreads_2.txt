Rating: 4/5

If I recall correctly, this book was my gateway to the reading world! It was too hard to understand but look into illustrations on each page to understand, then one day as a youngster, I took the courage to read the book! And came my first amazing sense of finishing a good book. It wasn’t until later that I understood the symbolism and irony of this epic. Yesterday I watched the newly adapted movie by Guillermo del Toro and despite the changes in the characters, the motion picture is a masterpiece!
