Rating: 3/5

3.5

I was really looking forward to reading this one. I loved Lesley in The Unflappable Miss Fairchild and was excited that he got his own book. Maybe my expectations were too high, but I was a little disappointed in this one. I guess the Lesley I had pictured in my head from the first book turned out to be quite different in this book. In book one, I thought he was a funny sidekick with a good heart. He and his best friend Chas were always getting into mischief. In the second book, he seemed way more like a rake and womanizer. There was a lot more innuendo and his attraction to Cleo seemed to be based more on physical attraction than anything of real substance. Well, I take that back. They were good friends growing up and there probably is an underlying friendship there. I think his worldly personality made Cleo’s innocents seem all the more obvious. She was pretty dang clueless about what goes on between a man and women, like surprisingly clueless. She was totally ignorant about a huge part of Lesley’s life. It made me wonder if she really knew who he really was. She only saw the good in him and only the sides he chose to show. Anyway, maybe I’m overanalyzing it too much. I think it was your typical regency, and I’m sure others would enjoy it. There were some fun parts and for the msot part I did enjoy it. 

Content: Pretty clean. No bedroom scenes, but does contain innuendo, talk of mistresses, etc.
