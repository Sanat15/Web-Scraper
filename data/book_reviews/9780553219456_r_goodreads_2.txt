Rating: 3/5

I normally don't like this genre, but the description sounded interesting and the book was free. 
It turns out I actually do like cheesy romance books. It was a quick and easy read and the plot was fairly straight forward.
Imagine this book as an old lover that you're comfortable with, but still ignites passion in your loins...in case you're wondering I learned that description from reading "Water Witch".
The plot was predictable but still cute, and who doesn't love a book with a dog, a beauty and a hot man?

If you want a good summer read I'd recommend this book.
