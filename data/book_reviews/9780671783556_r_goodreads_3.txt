Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed this book - up until the last chapter. That last chapter left me feeling rather confused and it just seemed such an odd and abrupt ending to conclude on, with no closing remarks from Tommy or from any of the animals to round things off in a more satisfactory way. And I didn't know if that concluding chapter was meant to be'just a story', or if it was very much intended to be a parody of the Nazis in Germany - by a very perceptive and forward-thinking Hugh Lofting. Other than the last chapter or two, all the rest of the book was enjoyable and a satisfying addition to the Doctor Dolittle series.
