Rating: 2/5

This is the last Sackett novel, but it was the most disappointing of the lot. It deals with Tell, Orrin &amp; Tyrel, my favorite Sacketts, trying to move cattle to help out Logan Sackett out of some kind of unknown trouble. The switch from Tell's first person to Orrin's third person kind of pulled me out of the story and I never really got back into it. The whole thing was wrapped up in the last four pages (or the last five minutes of the audio) and it was kind of unsatisfactory - it just kind of wrapped up and left it at that.
