Rating: 4/5

I always have mixed feeling about Nero Wolfe series. The mystery is entertaining enough for a mystery lover; the sidekick, Archie while can be annoying is a charming &amp; funny man, I enjoy his many sarcastic quips; I think the revelation part is always done nicely.

But the writing isn't that good. The pace can be draggy &amp; full of unnecessary details (I don't need to know how Archie had to turn the door knob &amp; turn it again in order to open a normal door). There's a lot of misogynistic instances in the series. I have no idea what personal grudge did Mr rex stout has with women that he felt inclined to belittle &amp; stereotyped them at every chances he got. And I hate inspector cramer the most. He's very impatient, ill-tempered, ill-mannered, rely too much on Wolfe (and being extremely fussy at it too).

That being said I would still pick another book in the series purely for Archie's funny remarks.
