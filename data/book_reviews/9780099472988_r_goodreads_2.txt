Rating: 4/5

The book is a collection of 3 wonderfully written novellas: The Conjuror's Game, The Candle Man, and Fintan's Tower. . Each novella revolves around some sort of magical, supernatural or "haunted" object.

 The Conjuror's Game  is an eternal, millennia old war game between the supernatural Knights and Ravens that is inadvertently started by a "missing" game piece. The conjurer Luke and his friends must find the missing piece and return it before both sides rampage through Halcombe Great Wood. This novella has some eerie and interesting settings (not all of this world) and nicely written characters.

 The Candle Man  involves the retrieval of a lost candle that holds the life of a cursed man. The novel provides evocative descriptions of the encroaching sea who is hell bent on regaining her stolen land and tries to use the cursed man and his candle to do this. The boatman is an absolutely delightful character. 

 Fintan's Tower  involves elements of the Welsh King Arthur stories that tell of an expedition by Arthur's men to steal a magic Cauldron from the Otherworld. In this novella, a magic book guides the way to release the prisoner held in Fintan's Tower, which also holds the Cauldron. This is another portal fantasy with great characters.

The Glass Tower by Catherine Fisher&gt; is a delightfully written and entertaining trio of novellas.
