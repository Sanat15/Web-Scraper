Rating: 3/5

The Ectoplasmic Man by Daniel Stashower intertwines Harry Houdini and Sherlock Holmes in a mystery that sees the former arrested for theft and murder. Sherlock Holmes sets out with Watson to investigate and clear Houdini's name at the request of Houdini's wife. An entertaining account, this story had features that felt well grounded in reality. Houdini is proud of his accomplishments and shares some similar interests to the real life man. Stashower interweaves the fictional elements with non-fictional elements like the invention of the flying machine. Overall this was an enjoyable Sherlock Holmes pastiche. It did not stand out as an extremely remarkable novel but it was none-the-less a pleasant distraction for the approximately four hours it took to read. 3 stars.
