Rating: 1/5

There's a difference between a flawed hero and an unlikable one. Ollie seems to consistently fall under the latter category. Connor and Mia, on the other hand, are pretty cool characters. Connor gets a really cool moment against Drakon.

This contains the arc where Ollie cheats on Dinah with a younger woman... again. Serial cheater Ollie is the worst. I do appreciate Connor and Mia both giving him hell for it, especially Connor for calling him out on his behavior.
