Rating: 4/5

Scheduled as a read aloud with MFW CTG. old English, but not a problem for us. Travels back and forth in time a bit, as the actual tales of the war of 
Troy and Odysseus' adventures are told as stories to other people after the fact. Cuts out Odysseus' time in the underworld, and very little is said about his seven years with the nymph Calypso, which is great for children IMO. It also cuts out what Achilles did to Hector. The art was a neat classic style, but very similar throughout.

2021: Enjoyed it even more on our second time through. Like a true classic, Homer grows with us.
