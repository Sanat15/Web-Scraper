Rating: 3/5

Pretty good western romance. I had a little bit of a hard time with the heroine being married and having smex at 16 and the hero(28) lusting after her, even though that was not unusual for the times. Much of the conflict comes from the emotional seesaw, hero trying not to fall in love with heroine because of his past.(They had to marry to keep the ranch out of her evil uncle's clutches and it was supposed to be a marriage of convenience.)Most of their problems got solved very quickly at the end of the story, without an epilogue. 3.5/5 stars
