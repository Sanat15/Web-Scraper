Rating: 5/5

I love this book. It is very entertaining for children, involving a very strong lead character and imaginative scenarios. The story is about a little girl that is being told she must ‘eat her peas’ by her mum, a very relatable and real scenario for many children. Immediately children are excited by the story. The mother makes all kinds of promises to her daughter, if she would only eat her peas! As the story goes on, the pages are filled with vibrant images of 50 ice creams, 29 bikes, and hundreds of other things that children dream of. This book would be excellent for numeracy lessons such as counting and addition. Together the class can practise their numbers by counting the objects on the page, checking there really are 50 ice creams. Props could be used to re-enact he 29 bikes, or pupils could be counted out to bring the story to life. This book would be sure to make a very memorable numeracy lesson.
