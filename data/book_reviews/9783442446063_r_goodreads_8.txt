Rating: 3/5

Not as much fun as the author’s first novel, Miami Purity, but still a strong story of narcissism, despair, desire, and one bad decision after another. Self hatred leads to a desire for ecstatic release both by diving into the ocean and by diving into bed with Mr. Hot but (very, very) Wrong. We’ve known (or, maybe, been) people like the narrator and her lover, people driven by a desire to drive through life in a fast car, all the time headed for a cliff. It’s not a pretty story with a pretty ending, but it’s compelling.
