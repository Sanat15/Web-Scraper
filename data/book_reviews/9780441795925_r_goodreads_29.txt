Rating: 4/5

When I read this series the first time probably more than 20 years ago I had no idea that this, the last book in the series was actually published first and was originally going to be a stand alone novel. I so enjoyed reading the author's forwards to the whole series as I read them this time around and finding out how the series came to be. Cimorene will always win my heart over Daystar but I do appreciate the way the reader learns about the world and this quest he is on at the same time as he does.
