Rating: 4/5

Eternal Sailor Moon on the cover! &lt;3 I've never seen nor read the Sailor Starts arc, so this is all new material for me! Of course, through the years I've picked up some info on what happens over the course of the story, but for the most part I'm going in blind. 
 The girls are finally starting high school and Darien is about to head off to America for school. However, when Bunny goes to see him off, his Golden Crystal is stolen and he's obliterated into dust. There's also the introduction to the Three Lights, a hot new band and also the newest Scouts, the Sailor Stars! Haruka doesn't trust them however when a new enemy appears and attacks the Scouts. It was pretty dark when all the Scouts of the Inner Senshi are killed. Yupp, they're turned to dust in volume 1! 
It's a really great start to the last leg of the series...lots of intrigue and an intricate new enemy...can't wait to finish up the series!
