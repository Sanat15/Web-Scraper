Rating: 2/5

Well this was definitely my first Beverly Butler novel and most definitely will be my last. All I have to say is that after couragiously hassling through this 600+ page novel, it certainly failed to deliver. It just wasn't what I was expecting. First of all, the story line is so monotonous, with no climax or drama. Not to mention the dreariness and boredom of the story in general Second of all, the amount of sheer wordiness and incessant rambling by the author in the narrative was absolutely uncalled for and very insignificant to the story in general! Absolutely irritated me and had me skip large sections of the story just so I can get through it. Disappointing read!
