Rating: 5/5

Utterly charming. Mollie Katzen is perhaps the only person who would just decide to... illustrate an entire cookbook by hand? Who does that?? Her section on bread-making is the clearest and most darling approach I've seen, anthropomorphising the yeast and bread in a lovely way. I regularly quote her when teaching people how to make bread. I find the recipes charming and fairly simple. One thing I will say is that she and I don't have the same palate, but that's not her fault. I tend to add more spices and adjust up the fat factor. But they are all great recipes. Her challah is my go-to.
