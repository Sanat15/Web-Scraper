Rating: 3/5

Oh just another Lucy biography for me 😂❤️ For anyone that didn’t know, I am a huge I Love Lucy fan! She is a comfort I turn to when the world is sad. 

My favorite so far is still Lucille Balls own autobiography titled Love, Lucy, but this one had its merits. I’ve always found 90s biographies a little dry, but I enjoyed the parts that were personal from people they actually knew. Stories from guest stars, coworkers and family members, including their children are included in this real life story of the couple behind I Love Lucy, Lucy and Desi. 

Well researched, sometimes entertaining and as usual, I always end up crying when I get to the stories of their deaths. ❤️

Did you know? 
❤️ Lucille Ball was the first woman to own a major tv studio.
❤️Lucy was the first pregnant woman ever on tv and it was so taboo, they couldn’t actually say the word “pregnant.” 
❤️Reruns were invented for the purpose of Lucy having maternity leave after her second child. 
❤️Desi invented the three camera technique, ultimately changing the way we film tv. 
❤️You can thank Lucy for Mission Impossible and Star Trek! She started the tv shows!
❤️Desi almost wasn’t cast as Lucy’s on screen husband because the producers thought “no one would believe” they were a couple, despite being married in real life!
