Rating: 1/5

This is the worst Romeo and Juliet story I have ever read. The writing is heavy handed and redundant. 

My big question is this: Why would two teenagers get married if they wanted to watch goats more than they wanted to have sex? I don't get it. These kids barely kiss, although there is a lot of the boy clutching the girl to his chest.

I got this book out of a free pile. Once I started reading it, I had to know what happened next, but in kind of looking at a train wreck sort of way.
