Rating: 4/5

Huh, I just realised that I have had this book on my Kindle since ages ago. A definite page-turner, and pace-wise, I think I like this book better than the one I read before. I like how the author put everyone as suspects because all of them have secrets. Although I must say that there are some questionable scenes.

And somehow, the novel reminds me of the unsolved case of JonBenét Ramsey though the MO of the murder was not similar at all. I don't know... it just crossed my mind.

Most probably because in the novel the fictitious Meagan was blonde too like JonBenét; the Stokeses are influential people, as well as the Ramseys; Patricia Stokes was a former beauty queen like Patsy Ramsey (same first name too!); Brian Stokes was nine when Meagan was missing, the same age as Burke Ramsey at the time his younger sister was murdered, and there are theories that the Ramseys were involved in the murder, just like the Stokeses in the novel.

p/s: the version I read has typos.
