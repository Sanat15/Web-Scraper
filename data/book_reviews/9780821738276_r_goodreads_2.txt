Rating: 4/5

This probably doesn't deserve such a high rating, but I really liked it.
I've heard a lot of bad things about this author so I wasn't expecting a lot, but this was fairly decent.

Although I liked this, I don't think Its for everyone.

.The two main characters are extremely neurotic and illogical at certain points throughout the book. 

.One moment they're completely in love, but nearly a paragraph later they're threatening to kill eachother.

.The big plot point is based on a huge misunderstanding between the two main characters, mainly due to there inability to take a chill pill.

I really like soap opera-esque plots full of misunderstanding and disfunctional relationships, but I can see why some people don't.
