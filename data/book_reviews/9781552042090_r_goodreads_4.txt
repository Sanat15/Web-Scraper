I enjoyed this book .

Both Sebastian and Alexandra have secrets , they are really great characters that I loved seeing how they fit together.

It’s a beautiful romance, 
A story full of intrigue and adventure, with characters that pull you into their lives , you get lost in the plot.

Candace always writes great situations and throws her characters in at the deep end , wonderful.
