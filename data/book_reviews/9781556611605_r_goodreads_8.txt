Rating: 5/5

I don’t think I can adequately express just how important the message of this book is. I started this book because I was feeling the need to zoom out once again and examine what spiritual abuse really is and looks like. I wasn’t expecting it to be almost entirely about the subtleties of what legalism actually is and how it operates. The authors are both very knowledgeable, gentle, and fair, but most importantly grounded in a real, living relationship with God. 
Even if you have not experienced full or extreme spiritual abuse, this book is absolutely invaluable. It will shed light on personal, institutional, or relational patterns that are not altogether rooted in grace and it may shock you just how many are all too familiar. I feel the urge to get every Christian I know to read it. If you read this, I hope you find healing and rest in ways and places you didn’t think possible.
