Rating: 3/5

PROTAGONIST: Val Duran
SETTING: Los Angeles
RATING: 3.0
WHY: After his best friend is murdered by a drug lord named Junior in Florida, Val Duran sees the writing on the wall and flees to Los Angeles. He meets a woman at his apartment complex and their lives become intertwined. She introduces him to her family, where there's murder afoot. Meanwhile, Junior is sure to be there soon. Not bad writing, but I was expecting something a lot quirkier. The twist at the conclusion didn't quite work.
