Rating: 3/5

I "browsed" through this book more than read it, as several of the patterns I realized were some I've seen on the Accuquilt website &amp; they actually have them broke down into easier ways of putting them together (both in written directions &amp; their videos) than what I saw in this book. 

Until I actually put some squares together &amp; need to fasten batting &amp; a backing on them, then this book might be more helpful for me... For someone else it might prove more helpful than I found it.
