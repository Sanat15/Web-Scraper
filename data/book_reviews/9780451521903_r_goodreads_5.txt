Rating: 5/5

I had to grow up to fully appreciate Balzac. I've tried reading several of his books as teenager but I surrendered in frustration and boredom. Not this time. Yes, the writing is still flowery, although realistic, and there way too many lengthly descriptions. However, I was not bored and I was actually mesmerised by the prose. Also, it is hard to remain untouched by the fate of poor Father Goriot and not to be enraged by the Paris society of those times. A novel about love, indifference, the perils of wanting too much too soon, criminality an so much more. Sad and beautiful.
