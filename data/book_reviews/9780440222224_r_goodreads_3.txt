I enjoyed this one more than the first one I think. The ending of finding out what the victim had been doing on her monthly jaunts to the city had me in suspense until the end when it was revealed. I had not guessed this one at all. The bad guy was a bit of a surprise too actually. I had her husband pegged not her. I really liked Jesse, the daughter and hope she keeps progressing in her healing. When the mental health worker made the mistake of thinking Jesse was going to be living there, I took it personally just like her Mom did. She doesnt seem ill that way just takes her time processing and there is nothing wrong with that. She is a smart girl and I have taken a real liking to he and think she is one of those characters that would be cool if she was real. 
There is only one more book left in the series so far that I am aware of. I plan to order it in and look forward to reading it.
