Rating: 3/5

Alien Influences is a fix-up novel set in the far future. It takes place on Bountiful, a colony which makes most of its income from drugs they export. There's a native race called Dancers that begins to have a strong influence on the colony's children, and several of them end up killed by others. It's an episodic story, parts of which were previously published in The Magazine of F &amp; SF (which Rusch would later come to edit) and an original anthology edited by Jack Chalker. It's a thoughtful work about alien-human interaction, but I thought the original pieces were stronger separately.
