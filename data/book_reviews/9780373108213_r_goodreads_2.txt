Rating: 3/5

Review of a book read years ago. 

I loved Sherry, the sweet, kind, beautiful, poverty stricken and super hardworking heroine.
I hated Scott for 90% of the novel, but he wasn't as bad as Carl the sadist shithole from The Odds Against ( by the same author ). Scott was too snobbish and too controlling of his sister's life. He was unfair to Sherry. How the F can he blame this young girl for the actions of her lazy, mercenary and supremely selfish brother? I hated how Scott flaunted his snobbish OW slut in poor Sherry's face while simultaneously throwing jealous alpha male tantrums whenever another guy dared to watch or get close to Sherry. The irony of the situation is that Sherry has more class in the nail of her little finger than Scott and his flaky sister combined. I felt sorry for Sherry because she was too poor to afford new clothes but the girl was so beautiful that she looked hot in her cheap attire. That sure pissed off Scott's cunty bitch OW. 🤣🤣🤣🤣. 
Sherry was a veritable saint of a heroine. A real martyr. She took too much shit from everybody. I would have added an extra star to this rating if the ending had not been so rushed. Scott needed to grovel more and there should have been some bad karma for the Sherry's selfish useless brother.
