Rating: 4/5

Read as part of my "reading Colin Thiele 100 year anniversary since his birth project". 
This is the second of CT's short novels that I had not read before. Like others of his books it is set in the beautiful sandy windswept Coorong lagoon. It's a contemporary setting having been written in 2002 about a boy, the son of a Wildlife Ranger, being homeschooled by his Mum and spending as much time as he could fishing for his rescued baby Pelicans (slightly storm boy) and baby Swan. 
The Swan becomes his constant companion and when the story climaxes becomes an integral part of that climax. 
Fabulously told in the Thiele style. Another great little read.
