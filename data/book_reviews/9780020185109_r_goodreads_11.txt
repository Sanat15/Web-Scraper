Rating: 4/5

Wow GOT season 4 if a golden age murder mystery. Not until the final chapter when it is literally drawn in crayon did I know who the killer was. I was pointing fingers the entire time absolutely sure of myself and just when I was certain Fell made me feel like a “strange small man”. I was really trapped into a locked door murder mystery the last couple of reads to get out of one room and stretch my legs felt great. Phenomenal read would absolutely recommend to kids aged 1-6 and dogs aged 7-38.
