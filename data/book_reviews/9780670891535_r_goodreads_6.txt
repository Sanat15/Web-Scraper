Rating: 4/5

How to Behave So Your Children Will, Too! is one of the most practical, psychologically insightful, and emotionally balanced parenting books available. It does not simply teach parents how to stop bad behavior temporarily—it teaches how to raise emotionally healthy, responsible, self-controlled human beings.
A book about:
discipline without cruelty,
structure without emotional distance,
authority without fear,
and love without permissiveness.
For parents, teachers, counselors, or anyone interested in human behavior, this book is exceptionally valuable and deeply thought-provoking.
