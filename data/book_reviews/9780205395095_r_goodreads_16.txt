Rating: 4/5

Attended a workshop on quantitative methods at the university’s library, and the lecturer recommended this highly readable book on statistics. Was piqued, especially by its title (Statistics without Tears), so I decided to read it, and realised how helpful it is for layman readers wanting to have a better foundation in statistics. 📊

I particularly appreciated the author’s clarity of explanations, especially throughout the tougher/more abstract terrain of statistical tests and significance testing. If you ever find yourself confused about hypothesis testing and terms like “standard error”, “standard deviation” and “level of significance”, this is a great book for you! It is highly engaging and conversational; one I highly recommend to students, researchers and scientists who have to rub shoulders with the subject in their work. 💯
