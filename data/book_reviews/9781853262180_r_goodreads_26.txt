Read for my Classic Pick for September 2025 &amp; an Orilium Autumn Equinox Readathon Prompt.
These stories were written in the 1800's and I wouldn't consider them horror- there's folklore, perhaps a bit of fantasy but they're more morality tales to me than horror, there's very little blood and gore- gothic more than horror.
I appreciate the short story collection for exisiting, and for MR James' work in the 1920's for collating them.
Two months in a row with classic Irish authors and both have scored quite low- at least these make sense and are readable (throwing casual shade there at James Joyce's A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man which was nonsensical)
