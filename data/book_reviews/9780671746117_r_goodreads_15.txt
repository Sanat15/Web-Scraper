Rating: 3/5

I am way too old to be reading this series, so take my review with a pinch of salt. I re-read this book for old time's sake. I first read it when I was just a girl, and I wanted to revisit at least one book in the Nancy Drew Files series. I should have picked one that was more highly rated than Deadly Intent. 

This book was okay. I figured out one key reveal early on, but not the rest of the reveals. None of the characters are interesting, and we don't get much into the setting, rather the rock star lifestyle or New York City. That said, this was an easy, breezy read, just not that exciting. The denouement was kind of weak, but maybe the Hardy Boys books are a little more action-packed than the Nancy Drew books, relatively speaking. 

I think I will leave the rest of the Nancy Drew Case Files in the past, where they are better in my mind.
