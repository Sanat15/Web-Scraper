Rating: 3/5

Complete name of the book is: The Guinea Stamp, Spies, smuggling and romantic adventure. Spying and smuggling takes about 80% of the book, and as for the romance... ehhh, too weak. I will never believe a smart woman as Joanna (smart except when she wasn't) couldn't tell who Captain Jackson was... it was like a case of Batman or Sailor Moon, except he didn't even have the excuse of having a mask... and she could not tell?? And trusting someone when Captain Jackson insisted on the danger and to not trust anyone **facepalm**

I liked most characters, I liked that Joanna was so brave (she has some courage I would never had!); I liked Captain Jackson but I didn't like Colchombe :S there was a moment where I had my suspicions but then again I changed my mind so it didn't help :S 

The writing was superb as always. The mystery was a bit weak (could tell who was the traitor around half of the book, which made me annoyed with the heroine at her lack of awareness), and it was a bit of a mess to know who was who since 50% of the characters (spies and smugglers) do not have a face.
