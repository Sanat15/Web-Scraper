Rating: 4/5

About 30 years ago, I was working on my MBA. My father gave me a copy of Live for Success, saying that I should read it to prepare for my career. I read it and was appalled - I couldn’t believe that people would spend their entire careers worrying so much about silly office politics. I was above all of that.

Of course, I should have paid more attention. Now, after working for a few decades, I recognize the truth in what John T. Molloy (of Dress for Success fame) was trying to impart. My career has handed me many a lesson in power and politics - and I’ve chosen to learn far too many of those lessons the hard way.

In Live for Success, Molloy tells his readers how the world really works. He wants to get away from the “party line” to show you how to get ahead. He covers body language, selling (yourself), office parties, how to speak, etc. 

Live for Success is consistently interesting reading. Some of his advice (particularly on how to dress) is outdated. Also, his chapter on table manners feels out of place, even if it is relevant.

Grab a copy of Live for Success and take the lessons to heart. It will save you a lot of heartache.
