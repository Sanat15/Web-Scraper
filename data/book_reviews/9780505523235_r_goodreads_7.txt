Rating: 4/5

Once I started this book, I had a hard time getting it out of my head. Delaney and Allie faced more than enough obstacles and they deserved to find happiness. When Allie married James, I actually cried out loud "no, no, no". My husband said, "don't worry, you know they'll get together in the end." (Men....what do they know?) Anyway, turns out he was right, but I was a mess until Delaney held Allie in his arms once more.
