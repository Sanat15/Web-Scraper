Rating: 3/5

Floating Girl is a continuation of the story of Rei Shimura, who is half Japanes, she has an American mother, and a Japanese father. She was raised in California, but has strong ties to Japan, and is happy when she is in Japan working on her Antique business. I am reading these out of context, so am often confused about why characters have gone, e.g. Rei was engaged and madly in love with Hugh her Scottish love of her life, and poof, Hugh has taken off in another book, somewhere leaving Rei to continue her life without him.

In this book she has a new love interest who is Japanes, who has a twin sister that hates her. There appears to be some confusion if they care for each other enough! In this adventure Rei is still writing article for a Japanese American paper, however her boss has decided he would like it to be written comic style, as that appears to be all the rage. Rei finds she us up against a real back stubbing little witch who will lie and cheat and deceive people, to get ahead of the game on the paper. Trying to make Rei look like an idiot. Rei is taken into a different aspect of "after dark" Japan, that she has not experienced before, there are murders, and she is also under attack, and could have been killed, and not by someone she is expecting. Comic books, she finds, can be more complicated than she ever thought!
