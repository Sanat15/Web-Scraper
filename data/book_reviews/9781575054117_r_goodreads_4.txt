Rating: 3/5

This is written as 1-2 page diary entries as authors by a young wolf. Kind of dorky charming, with a few illustrations showing the shenanigans as they try to start their school and save his little brother. The illustrations are charming. As it's written by a young wolf, there are misspelled words, but there are also good new words for kids to learn.

I can't bring myself to read the whole thing though (I skimmed the last third). But I could see a younger kid loving this, maybe 2nd or 3rd grade. The short diary entries give lots of good pauses for readers getting into longer books.
