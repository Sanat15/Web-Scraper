Rating: 2/5

There's a lot in this book that's technically good. A lot of the writing is witty, the pacing is good, the characters are varied. A lot of skill went into the book. I still didn't like it.

What got to me was how cold the book was. The characters never feel any emotions very deeply. Even the would-be murderess never seems fully committed to her plot, and only seems mildly annoyed things don't go her way. And at the end of the book, she seems to have forgotten all her malevolent intentions - not repented of them, or regretted them, just filed them to the back of her mind as she turns her attention to other things. Relationships are equally shallow. There are no real friendships, as no one seems to enjoy the company of anyone they aren't in a romantic or sexual relationship with. They just spend time together out of habit, and enjoy feeling superior to one another. The romances are decidedly hollow, and even the expressions of lust don't seem move the characters much. Even the relationships built on dislike aren't very strong. And, ultimately, the plot doesn't really seem to care much about the characters either. Their actions have consequences, but no major, lasting ones. Fairly sizable actions like abuse, infidelity, neglect, repeated attempted murder all create only minor ripples in the fabric of these peoples lives. By the end of the book the characters have changed slightly, following character arcs that are really more character-bumps, and their circumstances have changed a bit, but nothing in comparison to how things should have shifted in response to their behaviour. If anyone really drifts through life, experiencing emotions and relationships and consequences in this muted, muffled manner I'm seriously concerned about them.

So, although the book had many ingredients for a good tale, it left me feeling like I'd stared into a heartless void for ~270 pages.
