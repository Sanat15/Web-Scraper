Rating: 1/5

I couldn’t get through this. It’s full of predictable tropes. Also, the author would benefit from doing research before he writes. In particular, the opening takes place at the invasion of Bougainville during world war 2. The animals found on this island according to the book are largely not native to this part of the world. Chimpanzees (Africa), sloths (Central and South America), etc. I was hoping for some fantastical explanation as to why these animals were found way outside their normal range but nothing was said. I found myself not caring about the characters. They’re flat. There is no reason to read further.
