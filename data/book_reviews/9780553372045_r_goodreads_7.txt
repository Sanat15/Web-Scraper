Rating: 3/5

2.5 stars, rounded up.

While I enjoyed re-reading the first Deverry quartet, I’m finding this second lot more of a struggle. The main problem is that I’m getting sick and tired of the main character, Rhodry. Although this is the third book in the second quartet of novels I’m going to pause here and decide if I want to continue the series. I suspect the answer might be no.

That said, I do recommend the earlier books in the series, particularly the first novel, Daggerspell.
