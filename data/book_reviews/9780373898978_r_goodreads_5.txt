Rating: 4/5

I borrowed For Mike's Sake from the local public library.

Maggie and Wade are divorced and have a ten-year-old son, Mike. He lives in Alaska and she lives in Seattle, Washington and Wade is back for the first time in the four year they have been divorced for a visit. He has a big surprise when he tells Maggie he is planning to get married to another woman within a month after kissing her passionately in her bedroom.

Maggie has red hair and flashing green eyes and a temper, which Wade sparks often. The one thing they have in common is Mike and their concern for him. The humor and conflict is often funny with Mike enjoying parts of it as only a young man can when his parents' tempers flash. This is a great second chance story where Maggie and Wade discover they still love each other before it is too late, but not For Mike's Sake but because it is true love.
