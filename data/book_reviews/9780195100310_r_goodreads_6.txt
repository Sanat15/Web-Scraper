Rating: 5/5

I really enjoyed reading the book, that is beautifully written and really engages you in the rich history of India (and also Pakistan and Bangladesh until very recent time). A great introduction to the complex yet fascinating country. You can feel the love of the author for its culture(and for some key players such as Akhbar and Nehru) as well as the sadness for the most recent times. 
The book was written before the 2004 election, but the author could see what was going to happen in India already.
