Rating: 2/5

Not going to lie, this book about William Tecumseh Sherman’s life was a hard read.

I’m a teacher, and when I plan lessons, I tend to wonder about… the story I’m going to tell. I’m starting here. How do I get them there, and keep them with me?

That clearly doesn’t happen here. Sherman really can’t tell a story. He’s like one of those kids who has a list of facts, that he likes to talk about and he tells them. In excruciating detail. Over almost 1200 pages. He’s very much “I did this, and then I did that. I needed to do this to set up that. I did it. Then I did something else.” There’s no stepping back, and giving the whole picture, or giving context, or trying to work out how to get someone to go from here to there, or overarching story. It’s just I did a then b because of c, then d, e, and f.

So no, not much fun. It shows why some people are bad autobiographers.
