Rating: 2/5

Another wonderful book. However, it does stand out for its length, but not in a good way.
I can appreciate that Beatrix Potter tried to write an "Ulysses" like story, it becomes somewhat tedious to read, as one thinks of Potter's books as small short stories.
Essentially, it's a little pig who went to market and ended up as Robinson Crusoe. There could have been shaved a great deal off the book without it losing the plot.
