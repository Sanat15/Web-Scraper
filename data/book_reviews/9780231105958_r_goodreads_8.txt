Rating: 4/5

Most odd first few paragraphs I have read from a book. It made more sense after I kept at it. 

This gave me a lot of food for thought. I have read a lot more stoic than taoist text, so I drew a lot of parallels. I was surprised by how similar they were in premises of acceptance of the universe outside one's control, but how different the conclusions were. 

Zhuangzi deserves a reread from me, but definitely not soon. This was quite dense and took 110% of my brain to try and parse.
