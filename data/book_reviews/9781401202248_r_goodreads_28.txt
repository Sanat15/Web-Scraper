Rating: 4/5

Green Lantern: "Easy... You've no cause to tell at me! I have a job... I DO it!
Green Arrow: "Seems I've heard that line before... at the NAZI WAR TRIALS!"

Golden Age of Batman aside, this is the earliest comic I've read - it's also the least subtle, most on the nose comic I've ever read, to the point that this well-meaning journey examining social issues in America that I'm sure was fresh and vital and bold and a comicbook milestone at the time, becomes, read today, an unintentional comedy (and not without its charm). Green Arrow and Green Lantern may as well be parodies of themselves, political caricatures: Green Arrow an outspoken, confrontational and patronising social justice warrior, and Green Lantern an almost childishly clueless conservative who can only ever get things wrong and whose entirely undefended values - if he even has any values beyond "respect the law"- are simply there to be torn down by Green Arrow.

It's amusing and eye-rolling to read a 1970 comic trying to be woke, e.g. Green Arrow dressed as a Native American saying "My redskin brothers find you guilty!"

Definitely one for the archives - or a time capsule.

3.5
