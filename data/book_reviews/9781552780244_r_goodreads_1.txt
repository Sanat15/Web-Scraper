Rating: 3/5

With the help of the CFB Heritage Foundation and their Heritage Minute TV vignettes, Marsha Boulton has put together a book that highlights various people and events in Canadian history in over two dozen short chapters. Some of these people and events are obscure and unknown by many. Others describe well-known events but with some information that might be new to many. This would qualify as an excellent “bathroom reader”, a book that you would pick up for ten minutes every now and then for light knowledge and enjoyment.
