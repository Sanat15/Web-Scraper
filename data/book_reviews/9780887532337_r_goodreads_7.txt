Rating: 5/5

Another of the Shakespeare Can Be Fun series. I recently watched The Globe's production of Twelfth Night so I really enjoyed seeing what the author did with the play. The kids did amazing drawings to go with it--the one with all the characters &amp; arrows showing who was in love with whom to help straighten out the confusion was priceless--&amp; all the letters they wrote to illustrate the play &amp; express the characters' feelings from their point of view were wonderful &amp; funny. While I did like the tragedies MacBeth &amp; Hamlet, they really shine when they do the comedies. Can't wait to read more.
