Rating: 2/5

This was a strange book to rate. On one hand, the world-building was amazing - so interesting and fascinating. Post-apocalyptic combined with urban fantasy! The plot itself was also very good with enjoyable characters. The problem was that the ending was horrible. There was also this weird subplot that never tied in or made any sense. (Vampires? Or something like that, zombies maybe?)
It shows its age a little in the treatment of female characters and the absence of any non-heterosexual characters. 

It looks like there are other books in this universe that I might try out, written by other (hopefully better) authors.
