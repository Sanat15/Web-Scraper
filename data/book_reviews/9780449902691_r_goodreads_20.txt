Rating: 5/5

This book is well-researched and well-written. Being a biography it is at times tedious in some non-expedition details (Shackleton's financial &amp; romantic affairs). However, in my view, these details only enhnace the suspense. And that's what's amazing about this story; you know the outcome. Yet the tale is still gripping. If you are interested both early 20th century &amp; Antarctic history, you should not pass over this Biography.
