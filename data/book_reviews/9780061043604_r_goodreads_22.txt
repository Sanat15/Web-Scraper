Rating: 4/5

*chef's kiss* 

By far the most unique murder mystery I've read in a while, due to its fascinating delivery, which is a series of letters written to and from the characters, bare dialogue from court hearings, and character statements (hence the title). I would also say that this book is all about how the death happened and proving that it wasn't an accident, since we're only presented with one option for the possible perpetrator - it's much less of a whodunit and more of a "did he actually do it?" This is definitely not your normal detective novel, and I'm not mad about it.
