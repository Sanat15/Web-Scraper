Rating: 4/5

This is the vocab book I got from my collaborating teacher. As far as vocab books go, I really like this book. The words are grouped by topic and thus focus on etamology. I gave up on using it though because my students were doing horribly; turns out they did horribly on all vocab. I did use to enjoy looking at words and seeing our vocab words. It was fun to have my mind yell out "vocab word!"
