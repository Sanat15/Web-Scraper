Rating: 5/5

I happened to see this at...the Dollar Tree. it was the one and only. I chose it because of the subject matter. I have been interested in the subject of choice ever since I was around 10? and raised Catholic. Recalling my thinking it seemed weird for so many people to have so many
kids so fast. Like stairsteps. I had one sister. Later as I learned about the Catholic viewpoint of children and procreation, I became very interested in the choice women had to make. I also felt, being aware of how abortions were performed in the worst possible fashion, coat hangers, back alley procedures, etc- I became an advocate of choice. Politically speaking, this is the epitome of scary to think of what well could be in the future of the USA. I loved this book. I also loved several of Howard Fasts' other books. I wish more people would read this novel. I wish more people would take this seriously.
