3.5

I liked the female MC, bookish but not prim, with a sense of humor and just interested in culture. She is like a smarter Catherine Morland, even her family is a less vulgar version of the Morland. And that ending was just like Mr Tilney visiting Catherine too. Luckily Alethea is more sensible, but she has to go and fall for the rake of the town **facepalm**

Not that the Beau wasn't charming, he was and it was almost inevitable for her to fall for his charms. In fact, the two girls in this book didn't have much chance; as long as the guy is a gentleman and sees her like she is the most interesting and most amazing thing in the world, said girl doesn't' stand any chance.

Alethea's cousin Lydia was a flirt at the beginning but has a very nice character development throughout the book. In fact, the book is very good and light and has a nice pace; the most "evil" in the story is Lydia's mother who doesn't think in her daughters' happiness at all, just want to see them married to dukes/lords/etc, and Lydia falls for a nice guy with money, yes, but without a title.

I think I would have liked to love Devenish. He is very gentleman, but his manners are "languid" (grrr), a terrible flirt (seems like he is courting this girl but goes to shows with a mistress) and walks in the park with his mistresses and that alone made me distrust him and not like him as a Main Love Interest :(
