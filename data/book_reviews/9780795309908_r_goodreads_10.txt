Rating: 4/5

Lunchtime Listen January 2025

A Hugh “Hastings” Fraser read story starring Supt. Battle. I say starring but in all honesty the enigmatic Battle does not actually appear until about 3/4 of the way through.
The main investigator is Luke Fitzwilliam, a military policeman recently returned from the far east who encounters an old lady on a train journey who tells him she is off to Scotland Yard to try and stop yet another murder in her small village. Luke listens to her story but feels she is being fanciful and takes it with a pinch of salt. It is only two articles in the news over the next few days, the old lady’s death and the death of a man she had mentioned that make Luke wonder if she was indeed telling the truth.
With nothing currently to occupy his time, he decides he will investigate and using a good friend to introduce him to an acquaintance who happens to live in the village the old lady named, he arrives with a cover story to explain his presence and the energy and will to find out what really happened.

I seem to remember that this had been turned into a Miss Marple story , maybe ? As my wife and I kept recalling vague facts about the case, and we both “got” the murderer quite early. All of that said, it was still a fun listen, and who doesn’t want to be read to by Captain Hastings over lunch !!
