Rating: 4/5

A Simple Story. A story for young kids. But it is equally good for adults.

Elizabeth Goudge blends skillfully fairy tales, folk songs, real life events and suspenseful episodes. Thee are wild and domestic animals that live with human beings, there are unicorns playing important roles in the novel, there are dwarf like people, there is the allegorical clash between the Sun &amp; Moon (Light) vs Darkness &amp; Night, there are moral lessons teaching one (1) to give to God what He deserves; (2) to control one's emotions and never to lose relationships for a moment of anger; (3) never to be proud always - to be humble to accept mistakes; (4) to believe in miracles. These are very wonderfully woven into an enchanting story. Liked it.
