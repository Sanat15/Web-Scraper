Rating: 5/5

I love this book. It's full of prayers to help teenagers get through some crazy times in life that they might go through. From difficult times in school all the way to first dates, it guides them through it all. The best part is, they also have prayers that can also help us as adults. This book actually helped me get through a day with things that I must face and I'm glad that those prayer are there. It's an excellent book that a lot of people in the world should read.
