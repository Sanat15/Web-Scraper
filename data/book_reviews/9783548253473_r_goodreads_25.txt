Rating: 5/5

I'm not sure why so many people are hating on this book. I thought it was wonderfully written, and the story unfolded in such a way that actually made me wonder if Byron WAS a vampire. I wish stories like this were adapted for the screen instead of endless high school vampire stories. That genre was tired in the 90s. This would actually make for a really cool anime, too.

I have a small book of Byron's poetry that I dip into from time to time, but I had absolutely no idea that his life was so controversial and strange. This book was the first glimpse I've had into the man behind the poetry. And what a man!

He was probably a really awful human and to have actually known him would probably have been to one's detriment, but there is something undeniably alluring about not giving a fuck and living life exactly the way you want to, which it seems Byron did.

I'm just so happy to have found a vampire book that wasn't conplete garbage. This was well-researched and intelligent. I really liked it. I also think it's a great introduction to Byron's life for people who don't know anything about him, obviously with a fantastical twist, but that makes him all the more fascinating.

I thought the twist at the end was pretty cool, too.
