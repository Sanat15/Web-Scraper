Rating: 2/5

I tried to read this because I love a revenge story and it had some interesting elements. The hero is 1/8th Japanese and a good portion of the story is set in Japan.

Unfortunately, I liked NOTHING about the writing from the endless mind-numbing info dump that started the book to the dubious knowledge of Japanese (a tansu IS Japanese for "chest" so writing "tansu chest" is kind of redundant) to some hilariously bad lines. Example:

“Your breasts are so beautiful I want to eat them for my lunch,” he whispered, sweeping her off her feet so that her fullness was on a level with his mouth. “Not to mention my dinner and breakfast.” 

Grade: DNF
