Rating: 5/5

"A Case of You" kept me guessing nearly all the way through this psychological romantic thriller. I love Pamela Burford's books and in that respect, this one is no different. But the similarities end there. This one is not your average rom-com romp. It is a compelling mystery with a touch of the paranormal and a bit of romance to keep it from being too dark. Kit Roarke is trying to find out what happened to her friend, Jo, whose death has gone unexplained and seemingly not even investigated. Dr Noah Stewart says he wants to help but urges Kit to leave well enough alone and let the police handle it. Problem is, the local sheriff doesn't seem to even be interested in investigating Jo's poisoning. What unfolds is a chilling tale that will keep you on the edge of your seat.
