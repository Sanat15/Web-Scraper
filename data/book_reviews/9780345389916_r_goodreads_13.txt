I got to page 260 and just couldn't continue reading for another 165 pages. I don't have a problem with character-driven novels and have finished and in some cases enjoyed books without likeable characters. But I came to the conclusion that I just didn't care any more about the protagonist. I reached the part where she met her lover in NYC and realized that I didn't care enough about learning more about him to keep reading. I suspect that if the author's style had been more succinct and the novel was shorter, I would have carried on and finished it. "Sunk costs" and all that.
