Rating: 3/5

This is the only book of Oe's that I have read. It was tedious, aimless and very much in need of editing. Some of the scenes were beautifully descriptive, but many of the key concepts were unclear or even contradictory. The title, Somersault, to me means a complete rotation, but in the book seems to be intended to mean a 180 degree turn away from something. The book was written not too long after the Aum Shin Rikyo mass sarin poisoning in a Tokyo subway. It is about religious cults and attitudes about the approaching end of humanity.

I received this book from my brother in law and would hesitate to recommend it. Some of the problem may lie with the translation which was somewhat awkward.
