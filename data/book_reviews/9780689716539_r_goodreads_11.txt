Rating: 5/5

Found one I will happily give 5 stars. What a relief! 

5/5 for relatability. There’s so much stumbling and tripping and squelching and howling through forests and mud and snow storms which the characters would prefer to go over or under or around but alas! “oh no! we have to go through it!”
5/5 for the illustration of the little dude with the stick ready to club down an entire forest. 
5/5 for appropriate length. At 17 pages this book will be over before you’ve had time to peg all of your frustrations to its pages.
