Rating: 2/5

Don’t be deceived by the covers of Joan Aiken’s “Paget Family Saga”. These are not your usual fluffy Regency novels. There is the saturnine hero, and an occasional exhaustive description of toilette, and the young innocent but spunky girl cast as the lead figure. But there is an undercurret of darkness which belies the genre – women are brutally raped, or driven to suicide, or murdered by neglect and harassment. Aiken’s books for children are well known for their Gothic elements, with the child protagonists in real danger from real evil. These are the grownup version of that danger and evil. Be warned.
