Rating: 5/5

I’ve no idea how this escaped me as a child (I would have devoured it) but extremely thankful to find it as an adult. A really wonderful &amp; uplifting read of the pre -war era in Germany as the Nazi’s started their terrifying climb to power which must have been horrendous to live through. Beautifully written from the pov of 9 year old (at the start of the book) Anna but cleverly spotlighting the tensions surrounding life for the adults. 

Best of all, it’s the first of a trilogy by the wonderful Judith Kerr so now it’s time for Bombs on Aunt Dainty!

Update February 2023- my third read of this. It won’t be the last!
