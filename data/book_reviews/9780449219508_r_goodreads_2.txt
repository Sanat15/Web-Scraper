Rating: 4/5

This was a very charming read! I have to admit I got it at my local library, gently used for free but I was enthralled by it. The characters are lively and easy to fall in love with, the writing style is simple and uplifting while still having its artistic flairs, and the plot does reel you in with its twists and turns! 
I’d recommend this to anyone who loves slow burn romances or wants a little break after reading a lot of heavy classics for a while. It’s not Shakespeare but the again not everything has to be! It’s okay to fall in love with a charming little story sometimes.
