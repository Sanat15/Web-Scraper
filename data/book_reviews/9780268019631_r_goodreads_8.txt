such a good book!!! I whole heartedly recommend to anyone and everyone who. Written over 30 years ago, it’s a testament to the power of faith and perseverance in the quest for peace and reconciliation in Palestine. Abuna Charcour’s commitment to nonviolence and love for all people is made manifest as he works to bridge chasms between people in his homeland, inviting people of all faiths and from all around the world to join in the work God is doing through him.
