Rating: 3/5

I skimmed the book, reading through a variety of sections. It enlarges on detail from the Pern stories, going through the various holds and weyrs as well as a description of the major careers on Pern. Decently written but rather dry except where there were a couple of small stories such as Felessan impressing, from his pov. Ok. Just not a story. Recommended for Pern addicts only. Note that while McCaffrey was consulted, it is not written by her.
