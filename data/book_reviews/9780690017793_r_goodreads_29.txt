Rating: 3/5

Most of the victims that experienced the horrors of Nazi Germany’s death camps did so while separated from their loved ones. Not in this account. As a young woman, Isabella Leitner along with her mother, four sisters and brother are rounded up from the ghetto in Kisvarda, Hungary and deported to Auschwitz. It is May 1944. What follows is a concise, painful recollection of Isabella’s time in Auschwitz alongside her four sisters. Their combined determination and unwavering support allowed Isabella and her sisters to triumph over inhumane destruction. The story is poetic - simple and somber without unnecessary details.
