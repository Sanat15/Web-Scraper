Rating: 3/5

5 out of 10. This is as much about the author's psyche as it is about his patients'. I found some of it insightful, but the narrative's structure was scattered, the writing at times odd, even sloppy. The psychological and psychiatric theories and approaches he shared seemed so scattershot -- this patient might have this condition, but then again might not. No doubt these field have come a long way in the past 35 years, but for it being from the late 1980s I was surprised at how much guesswork there was in this prestigious mental hospital.
