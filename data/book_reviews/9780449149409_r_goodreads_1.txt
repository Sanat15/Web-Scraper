Rating: 4/5

Psychiatrist Harry Kline is back, in another mystery thriller where a prominant man is cut down in a drive-by that leaves Kline wondering just how "random" this attack was - one of his patients is the prime suspect, and then Harry's Lover...

 What's going on?

 As usual, it's the deep and well-written characterizations that keep the story going, and you'll be sucked right in to Harry's life, his foibles (and failings), and the phenom nail-biter of a mystery that Luber has written.
