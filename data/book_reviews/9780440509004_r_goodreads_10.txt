Rating: 5/5

This was a tough read just for the content but I also blew through it because it was so validating, especially being a woman, true crime fan and mom. I haven’t read The Gift of Fear from him because the parenting aspect of this was so relevant but I’ll be picking up his other because it screams importance from every page.

While he wrote this 20 years ago 90% of it still applies, if not more so in today’s world where things seem even more intense. The statistics may be a bit off (needs to be updated from the early 2000s research) and he didn’t account for things like racial bias, I still found it helpful. 

It didn’t make me a more anxious parent, it just reminded me that vigilance is a strength.
