Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed Quest for a Maid so much, I had to give Hendry's first novel, Quest for a Kelpie, a chance. Set during the Scottish rebellion in 1745, our main character is a hard-working, brave 12-year-old girl. She tells the truth in tough situations, speaks up more than she ought, and did a good turn for a band of gypsies who have since repaid her with a prophecy and various favors over the years. Will the prophecy come true? Will Jeannie ride a Kelpie and change the course of the rebellion? 
Such a fun, well-researched, well-written book. Quite short. I had to use the glossary a lot in the beginning because of all the Scottish dialect. Some sentences looked like another language altogether!
