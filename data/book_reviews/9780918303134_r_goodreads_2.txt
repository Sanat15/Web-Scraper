Rating: 5/5

My sister gave me the adoption of Calloway (a sea otter rescued by the Marine Mammal Center) as a Xmas gift. Along with my adoption certificate, I received Vicki Leon's tribute to sea otters, which is a beautiful book with colorful pictures and great information about sea otters: how they live, mating habits, motherhood and caring for the pups and dangers they face from humans. If you think otters are cute and want to help save them, contact the Marine Mammal Center and make a donation. Then you too can have this lovely book.
