Rating: 5/5

I gave this book to my grandmother for Christmas in 1987. I actually signed the inside of it with a note. I don't remember buying it, but I assume it was something sitting out of a department store table that I noticed, looked at briefly, and purchased. As a 23 year old young man what do you buy your 84 year old grandmother. 

As best I can tell she got about halfway through the book. When she died it ended up in the procession of my parents, and when they moved and cleaned out their house, it was given to me a few years ago. 

I didn't think of it, but a couple of years ago I decided to start reading it. I suppose you could read the book straight through, but I think it is the kind of book better to be consumed in small sips. So I read a couple of pages every week during a quiet time I keep.

What a wonderful book. So full of wisdom gathered over the centuries that will never grow up. I can't wait to share this book with others.
