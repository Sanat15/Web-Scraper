Rating: 4/5

UPDATE: I came back to this book after a psychic woman told me my chakras were blocked. I read it for an hour last night. This book is dense-- definitely not a reference book. I went to look up specific chakras and how/ why they are blocked, and that information is spread throughout the entire book! every chapter is about chakras.It is designed for people who want to learn about energetic healing and the entire human spiritual experience. 

Serious, old school woo-woo. Brennan is an energetic healer who writes about BIG picture things like how spirit comes into form on earth, our calling/ work we have to do here, how we leave, the concept of time.... She tells stories of healings she has done then gives the reader a detailed "How-To" of energetic healing.
This book reminds of what I already believe: We are spirit in human form, we came here for a reason, we are pure energy, and life is painful, so energy gets stuck and blocked. 
As Matt Damon's character Will Hunting said in one of my all-time favorite films, Good Will Hunting, "Let the healing begin!"
