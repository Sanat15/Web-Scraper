Ms. Dolan is quickly becoming another favorite regency romance author. This is a companion novel to "Three Lords For Lady Anne". In this book, Maribe is generally thought to have a curse on her because so many of her suitors have either died or fallen ill. Young men are even daring each other to dance with her to test their bravery. When Demetrius comes to London, he immediately decides to prove there is no curse. I loved how anguished Maribe became over each suitor and how badly it hurt her to be treated like a pariah. I loved Demetrius' reaction to her shunning. This was a well-crafted book and so much fun to read.
