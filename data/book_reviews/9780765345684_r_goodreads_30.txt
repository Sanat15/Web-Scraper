Rating: 3/5

Love me some Tanith Lee, but this just doesn't deliver on the thrills I often (not always) get from the Princess of High Fantasy.
It does have moments where I fall delightedly into her language, and the fun she has with her worlds and characters. But not enough.
Yeah, I haven't read the preceding books, so that for sure is an issue, but every part of a series should also read well as a standalone.
Into the donation box.
