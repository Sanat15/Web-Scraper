Rating: 4/5

I love missionary biographies and this one was no exception. Dr. Foster details his experiences from growing up, to God's hand on his college years, to meeting his future wife, to his calling to Africa and then onto the many experiences he had there as a general surgeon. He is certainly from the old school of missionaries who were willing to put everything aside including family to serve God however they could. While missionaries still serve today, they strive for better balance in their lives than he did. This is compelling reading. Samaritan's Purse sent us this to read before our recent trip to Kijabe, Kenya.
