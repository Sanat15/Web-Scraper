Rating: 2/5

Religion used as societal suppression in North America. But don't be expecting the depth of The Handmaid's Tail - the focus here is mainly on action. And the clue's in the title as to the plot driver.

Sexist (it tries not to be, what with unisex fighting and such - but, really, all women ultimately want to do in Ri2100 is raise families and cook for their man), not particularly involving, and has two tacked-on stories at the back that bear little -and in the case of the last story, no- relation to the novel proper.

Certainly not as offensive as some stuff Heinlein has done, but, in the end, uninvolving... even pedestrian.
