A short review of the Plymouth Rock history although “who, if anyone, stepped on it” from the Mayflower is unknown. Originally, it was lying along the bay there in Plymouth mostly underwater at high tide but clearly visible during low tide. It was thought to be heavily used by people because of its large size, plus there were no other similar rocks around it. It was thought to have been deposited there by a glacier. Eventually, the community thought it should be honored. In moving it, they dropped it and broke it in half. Today, it has a grand memorial around it not far from the bay where it sat for thousands of years.
