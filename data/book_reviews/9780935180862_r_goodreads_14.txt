Rating: 5/5

This is a collection of myths that focuses predominantly on historical events, and only occasionally delves into the rich pantheon on Hawaiian deities and demigods. So it may not be the best introduction into this mythos for every beginner.

However, as a novice to Hawaiian mythology myself, I loved it! It was linguistically rich, deeply interesting, and showed how illustriously well-documented Hawaiian history has been, predominantly through intricate oral tradition.

It also dispelled a lot of myths about Hawaiians at the time, emphasizing that many of the stereotypes used to prove ancient Hawaiians were "savage" were also practiced by Western cultures, or were exaggerated or misunderstood.

I found it to be amazing, and a great start to my journey of learning more about Hawaii's history and folklore.
