Rating: 3/5

This is the book that never ends. Yes it goes on and on, my friend.

However, despite that, I did not hate it. I think what saved it is the very dated language and prose and it's set during Downton time. I have no idea who the main character was, and one can argue it was the Diamond Waterfall necklace, except it barely makes an appearance and is only mentioned in passing by the main family. 

I honestly did not care about a single member of this family. And my least favorite trope: Horrible people being Horrible to each other.

Secondary characters I liked: Molly the Irish nurse and Olive the farm girl..that's it. 

Totally worth skipping but if you're feeling brave...proceed..
