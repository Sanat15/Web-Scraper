Rating: 3/5

Ms. Palmer was great on setting the scene and making me feel the middle eastern surroundings and traditions, but I think the dialog could have been worked on more considering the characters intelligence and educational backgrounds. It should have been shown more through their interactions then "told". I felt as if there was too much "author voice" and not enough "character voice". Also there should have been more building time in the relationship to invoke the extent of the love that we are led to believe exsist between these two. Maybe more intensity in the meeting would have helped to make the quickness of their getting togeteher all the more believable. It was all about his needs through most of the book, when it should have been more mutual or more in her favor for her to love so hard and so quickly. With that said it was an entertainng read.
