Rating: 4/5

This is a book encouraging readers as they go through pain. Biebel and his wife lost their three year old son to a rare disease. He takes us through the stages and reminds if it is only when we allow Jesus into the pain that it can be transformed into something else. He has an interesting insight in that he says having faith makes it worse before it gets better. We go through the questions and doubts before we come through with an even greater faith in Jesus.
