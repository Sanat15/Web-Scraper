Rating: 5/5

Although I had read many Steinbeck novels throughout my life, a Stanford University Continuing Studies course allowed me to immerse myself in the world of John Steinbeck once again and I bought several anthologies of his work. Cannery Row is one of the most amusing novels you'll ever read - the story of Mack and the Boys and their frog hunt is roll-on-the-floor laughing out loud funny. 

East of Eden is one of my top 10 novels of all time; I've re-read it several times and have watched each movie adaptation. The 1980's version with Jane Seymoure as Cathy is absolutely chilling. 

A Steinbeck collection should be on your permanent shelves as each of his books is well worth reading over and over.
