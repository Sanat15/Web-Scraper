I've found this book is the kind of advice where you dip in a chapter at a time, when you need some ways to think about dealing with whatever issues you're currently facing. I read the first 5-6 chapters as a core 'here's a foundation to building oneself's mental fitness', and have been reading chapters here and there since. This book is about offering a toolkit to its readers. It's practical, with a very sensible but-not-unkind tone to it. Recommend.
