Rating: 4/5

I related to the things in this book so much. Never being best at anything, bullied in school, etc. It was all-in-all amazing, and leaves you thinking. The only thing was the ending How everything got so dark, so fast, and the way they bullied Tyson to the point if almost killing him, it was way too much for me. I was horrified! Unfortunately this kinda stuff doed happen in real life. What I feel doesn't really happen, is such people regaining their senses, apologizing, and making amends to the bullied, that's just unrealistic.
