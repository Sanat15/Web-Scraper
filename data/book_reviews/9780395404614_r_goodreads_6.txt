Rating: 4/5

Not as funny as Science Made Stupid, but then very few books are so maybe that's not quite fair.

I originally gave it 3 stars because I remember feeling vaguely disappointed because it wasn't as funny as the first one. But on rethinking it I'll give it 4 after all, because on its own I think it was funny, just suffered by comparison. I mean, yes, it was kind of a ripoff of the success of his previous book but on the other hand bits of it were also very funny and it's still a Tom Weller book. So maybe 3.5 stars? Oh, hell, why not 4. OK, 4.
