Rating: 4/5

"May dark Thial spike your eyes! May Kraan hold your living brain in acid!"

Consisting of four novels, though two of them could be aruged to be cut-ups or mosaics, this is a fine, clever, often funny, and beautifully written half of a series. I say half because the first two sections, The Dying Earth and The Eyes of the Overworld are 5-star worthy, splendidly written, weirdo scififantasy. The second two novels included here were written nearly two decades later and are simply not that great. Vance seems to have lost some of his flair and style over the years, but it is well worth at least reading the first two sections. These focus on the adventures of various sorcerers and rogues in the far-flung future of Earth and its dying sun. 
Fans of Gene Wolfe's Book of the New Sun will especially rejoice because Wolfe openly admitted to being very influenced by Vance's work, so if you like those books you'll love the first half of this volume.
