Rating: 4/5

Henry Slocum was going to speak to the big rally, in front of the governor and all. When he doesn't show up, most of Quashet sets off to look for him, including Asey Mayo, who's afraid that Henry may be the body that Jane Lennox stumbled over--and which disappeared when Asey went back to look for it. Although he didn't find the body then, Henry turns up very much alive--and then disappears again. And the medical examiner receives a C.O.D. package that turns out to be a dead body--but not Henry's. If Asey could only explain the red wig he found, maybe everything would become clear.
