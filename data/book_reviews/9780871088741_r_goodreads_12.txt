Rating: 5/5

This book ranks among my most favorite books in my library.
This is one that I will reread at least a couple more times.
Harry has such a wonderful way of taking you right at the place and moment in time and introduces such wonderful people in his works.
This is the book that has a little bit of everything I enjoy in my reading, flyfishing, the outdoors, history, exciting characters, and enchanted places. In many ways it takes me back to my years growing up on our farm. I want to find more books of this genre and caliber to read!
