What a weird book! We all know I love women going insane, which this does have to some extent, but is more about a potentially schizophrenic man and his relationships. Mostly about mental illness, but also about family and fatness. And beauty pageants. I literally had to put the book down at one point cause a scene was too gross and that says a lot since I read really strange and gross books (I guess tapeworms are the limit?)
