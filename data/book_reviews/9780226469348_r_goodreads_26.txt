Rating: 4/5

A collection of eight short stories following Vanessa, a young, observant girl living in an isolated pioneer town in Depression-era Canada. 

I was most struck by the story “The Loons”, which had themes of nature and colonization. In it Vanessa’s family brings one of her father’s patients, an indigenous girl named Piquette, to their summer cottage to improve her health. When Vanessa returns year later after learning of Piquette’s death, she also finds that all of the loons that nested on the water during her childhood have been driven away as the lakeside town has been built up.

Vanessa’s observations sharpened as the stories went on, even if they weren’t entirely chronological. Many of stories flashed back to earlier years that we had already read about but were focused on a different circumstance happening in Vanessa’s life at that time. 

Overall, these stories paint a strong-willed, yet lonely, creative girl growing up among tragedy and confusion, who uses her surroundings to escape into stories. 

I’d recommend this for anyone interested in studying the craft of writing!
