Tully Vickery was not a criminal

But it was understandable that Yate Meachem had accused her of being a thief. After all, he'd caught her at the company safe.

"Please don't call the police," she begged him. "I'll do anything you ask."

Later she regretted those words, but by then it was too late. She could only wait and wonder if Yate would really carry out his threat....
