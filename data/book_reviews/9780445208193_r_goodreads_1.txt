Rating: 3/5

This novel was written in 1989. Before cell phones, digital cameras etc.

This is a typical mystery with all the similar tropes: the independent, stubborn reporter stuck in a town she does not want to be in. The brooding, handsome detective who is a bit sexist. The hick Southern Sheriff who is determined to keep his town safe.Of course there is attraction and a smattering of action.

Yet, the tropes worked. It kept me reading because I was intrigued of who the murderer was. I figured it out about a chapter before the protagonist did.

I doubt I will be reading an sequels
