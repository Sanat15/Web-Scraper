Rating: 3/5

It was wonderful to read of Robert MacNeil's experiences growing up in Halifax, where I spent my youth. Though his was more my mother's era, the scenery, scents, street names (and the ocean!) are all things I've missed very much since I left the city thirty years ago. I also share the author's love of words, though found the references rather a shrine to his favorite poetry, and classic stories I've never had the patience to read. Actually, I would have been quite happy to do without the tributes to other writers, and just to follow his personal story.
