Rating: 5/5

This is a delightful lesbian-feminist book. Gina Covina was one of the women who published Amazon Quarterly in the early 1970s. City of Hermits has strong, interesting characters, a great framework of the lead up to and aftermath of an earthquake that rocks all of California, and a few interesting plot twists along the way. I think I'll always have a crush on Silvia Silver and will be contemplating my own hermit ness for a while. Pick this book up from a used bookseller and enjoy.
