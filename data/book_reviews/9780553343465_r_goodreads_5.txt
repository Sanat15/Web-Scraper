Rating: 5/5

My copy of this book is "tattered and torn", but not forlorn! I've used it for over 30 years and for a paperback of 700 pages it is in remarkably good shape. It is a very useful book, but so much more than that! It includes my go to Thanksgiving recipes: Cranberry Relish To Remember ( p 646), Broccoli Chinoise (p 296) and Turkey Carcass Soup (p 348-9); I often use the Menu Guide - see for example - A Week In Fall (pp 654-5) and Buffet Menus for, Summer, Winter and 4th of July (pp 657); 
 the focus on good carbs includes chapters on potatos, pasta, grains and beans dedicated to information, in addition to recipes. 

I have used this cookbook for everyday meals as well as special occasions. Highly recommend!
