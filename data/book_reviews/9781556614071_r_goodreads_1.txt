Rating: 4/5

Titus has the job of watching his neighbor's cranky dog, but is soon convinced that one day the dog has been switched with a calmer, happier dog. The three cousins find out why and Titus ends up with his very own dog. This story gives a short overview of the Sherlock Holmes' Silver Blaze story in chapter 8, so I think it would be fun to read that first for background. It also tells the story of Saint Francis of Assisi and the wolf at Gubbio.
