Rating: 3/5

“Old sins have long shadows. As we advance through life, we learn the truth of that saying.”

My first Agatha Christie book!

An innocent Halloween party leads to murder. To fully immerse myself in this read, I paired the paperback version with an audiobook because I needed to hear the British accent. (It's my favorite accent. #swoon) I don't know what it is about the British accent, but it makes me want to dress up in a posh outfit, smoke a cigarette from an opera holder, and lounge on an expensive chaise while reading. (And I don't even smoke cigs. lol) This was an enjoyable read to celebrate the Halloween season. 🎃 I also watched the 2010 film starring David Suchet as Hercule Poirot. Poirot is quite the character!
