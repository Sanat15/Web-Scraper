Rating: 4/5

All in all I loved this book. The stories are quite moving and a few even brought a tear to my eye. Among my favorites are: “the stranger who taught magic”, “the tail of the lobo”, “what’s yo name?”, “the girl at the telephone”, “when muddy creek overflowed”. 

I very much enjoyed that some of the stories are on the shorter side with some longer more hearty ones thrown in. A few of the stories were completely enthralling while a couple others I found I did have to drag through a bit.
