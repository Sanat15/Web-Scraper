Rating: 5/5

This book was way too short! 
I have no doubt there are many, many more fairy tales like these. And not only in the countries these 7 came from. 

When a book has more than one story in it, I try to pick my favourite. In this case I just couldn't, so I have 3 😁
The Twelve Months
The Shepherd King
The Chatterbox 

All tell about different kinds of dealing with hardship and/or preparing for worse times, outwitting the 'enemy'. 

I thoroughly enjoyed the book and absolutely loved the illustrations!
