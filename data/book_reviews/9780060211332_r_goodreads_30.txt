Rating: 5/5

This is a wonderful story with amazing illustrations! I love the art and the message of appreciating loved ones is timeless. I have a respectable Christmas book collection and this story will have a spot on the shelf as long as I’m around. The old-timey feel is one element of this story that inspired me to write my Christmas story, THE ARCTIC COMPASS. The theme is to try and be more present…don’t let technology take over your child’s life. SANTA CALLS, makes a terrific holiday gift for your friends or their kids.
