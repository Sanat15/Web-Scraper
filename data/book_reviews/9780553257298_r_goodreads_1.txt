Rating: 5/5

Barring series written for an older audience like Lone Wolf, this is probably the best Choose Your Own Adventure-style book I've yet come across. It's well-written, there are no items to pick at the beginning that will trap you in an infinite loop for selecting the wrong one, the choices you are given are generally quite logical, the central question of the book that you're investigating is suitably mysterious, the characters and action feel authentic, as the pseudo-educational document that all the Time Machine books are intended to be it gives the reader an entertainingly informative trip through Elizabethan England, and even the interior illustrations by Ernie Colón were well above average.

 A good job all around, Carol Gaskin! You got it right where dozens upon dozens upon dozens upon dozens of authors before you failed.
