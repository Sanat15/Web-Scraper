Rating: 2/5

Story was fluffy and full of tropes. Not what I've come to expect of Wingate's writing. Also, she references one of the characters living with her boyfriend for years and there's a healthy smattering of blasphemy and swearing (an abundance of d*mns, and several blasphemies just in the first chapter). Neither are acceptable in Christian literature and it was a shock to find in this book. I don't recall any swearing in her later books so perhaps she was convicted.
