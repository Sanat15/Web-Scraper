Rating: 3/5

Rated 3 stars. This is the 1st in a 4 book non-fiction series written in the 1940's and 1950's about the Plantagents. The author, Thomas Costain, is most likely remembered for his historical fiction novel The Black Rose.

I enjoyed this version of the events from 1066 through the death of King John in 1216. The reason for 3 stars instead of 4 is that most readers would need some knowledge of this time in history to keep track of all the minor historical people. Medieval England is one of my favorite historical periods to read and I was confused at times and used Google which slowed down my reading. I will note that the author did insert some humorous opinions which at times had me lol! 

As an example this is one of the author's observations of Henry II:
"Henry had full faith in his own judgment, no matter how quickly it might have been formed and on what slight evidence."

Two authors of fictional Medieval history that I highly recommend are Elizabeth Chadwick and the late Sharon Kay Penman.

This series isn't available for Kindle at my library so I bought the series when each title was only $0.99. Looking forward to continuing with #2 The Magnificent Century.
