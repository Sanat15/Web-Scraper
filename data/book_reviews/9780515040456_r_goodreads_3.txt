Rating: 4/5

Fun, if brief, stylish novelization of the Walt Disney TV movie! I love this. It’s actually well written but don’t expect anything expanded or developed much beyond the content and dialogue found in the show itself. Still I had a great time reading this right after watching the Disney Movie Club blu-ray of the show. Probably more comparable to a junior reader book but the story makes for a fun adventure that never overstays its welcome. Includes a few simple ink line illustrations throughout.
