Rating: 3/5

The book started off well, however it did tend to drag out towards the end. The content of the book is very much based on leadership, strategy, execution, management and global business. The book is well written with Jeffrey Garten's own ideas as well as the people he has interviewed. If you are interested in business academia then this book would suit you well. If you are more into entrepreneurship and start ups then maybe not so much. I did enjoy reading it and if Jeff brought out a new book I would probably read that too.
