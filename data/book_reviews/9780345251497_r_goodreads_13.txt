Rating: 5/5

I like the combination of romance, mystery and spying in this story. Joanna stumbles into a plot to attack the British navy by French spies. There are several suspects as to who the master spy is, but Joanna is shocked when it is revealed who he actually is. Joanna is about to become betrothed to one man, but develops love for another, she has only just met! Sorting out who she will choose is easier than she thought!
