Rating: 3/5

Reading the appendix, what these dudes wrote for the offical chronicles of the birth of the heir, you realise these guys ain’t fucking shit. Robe from far Cathay listicle head-asses. That shit is boring as fuck. Murasaki on the other hand can guide her inkstone and brush to even the most listlessly boring courtly ceremonial matters and suddenly, as if by magic, they are transformed. At once filled with fragile emotion and austere beauty. At one remove; too close to be out of reach but never to be held.
