Rating: 3/5

I love the illustrations in this book. Lately I "read" the pictures several times a week with kids. The love pointing out details and talking about the various character's reactions, but the words don't hold much interest. Many of the rhyming lines are a bit hard to speak aloud, they don't come easily, so I'm happy to enjoy the book without them. The one exception is the last page, which has the toddler version of a narrative twist. That's just got to be read.
