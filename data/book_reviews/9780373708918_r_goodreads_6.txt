Rating: 2/5

This was a lovely story, although predictable. The characters were nice and there wasn't much angst, although it was emotional at times. However, I was appalled at the amount of grammatical errors, even worse than book one in this series. There were words missing, wrong words written, dreadful punctuation with a distinct lack of full stops, commas in wrong places and words written in italics where they shouldn't have been. Another major error was where Drew (who in the book is 35 yrs old) was doing research into who his Father could be and it stated that he was born in August 1963, which means for a book written and published in 2014 he'd be 51 years old, then a bit later in the book it says late 70's which would be more like it. Without so many mistakes this book would've had a higher rating from me but it took an awful lot away from the story for my liking. I can't understand how author's allow their work to be published in such a terrible state, I'm not a writer but if I spent all the time and effort that it takes to write a book I'd make sure it reflected that time and effort, especially when people are actually paying good money for said work.
