Rating: 3/5

I am not a fan of erotic stories - most of the genre just gets very boring for me - it has nothing to do with not liking to read about sex - I just like there to be something more - and Mr. Bacchus's stories do have something more to them which means I enjoyed them and was pleasantly surprised by that enjoyment but to be honest I have no clear memory of the stories and there is no longer a copy in the library system so I have no way to reread and give a proper appreciation. Until such time I gice this book three stars on the basis of my earlier enjoyment.
