Rating: 4/5

This final entry in the main X-Wing series was a lot of fun. This was a very self contained and short story, not concerned with the big names from the other books. This is all about Wedge and his three pilot friends on an adventure to persuade a neutral and isolated planet to join the New Republic. Learning about the culture of Adumar was really unique to see, like the emphasis they place on honor and the devaluing of life. I haven't read a book by Allston since Solo Command, and I have missed his writing style. The effortless blending of good character writing, ground and air combat sequences, and especially humor makes for a fun tone throughout. This book was very funny, the interactions with Wedge and his pilots are the best, they really feel like a friend group that has been around each other too much. Like I've never laughed out loud while reading before, and this book had me laughing several times throughout. I also liked the closer look into Wedge's personal life outside of the New Republic military. I did feel like the ending was kind of rushed, my only critique.
