Rating: 3/5

I’m guilty of romanticizing the glam NYC lifestyle described. I HATE. that this is written by a man. As much as Bo had such great points I keep thinking how could a MAN write about such important and traumatic experiences we women only understand. If it was written by a woman I feel like the emotions would feel more real and less superficial and glammed up. 
This book def gave me 1999 SATC and Gossip Girl love child - and both of those are problematic in their own ways. 
It was hard reading thru the sexual stuff and rape and child abuse, I feel like it wasn’t executed in a truthful way (due to the author being a man yes). 
It did end on a better note than I expected. I expected harsher satirization of Bo. I’m glad it felt like it was almost written by a woman. 
Def trashy guilty pleasure book tho.
