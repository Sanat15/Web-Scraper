Rating: 3/5

It was the story of small boy in Sri Lanka, who lives in family of mother, father, siblings and surrounded by his extended family and how his life changes as per political scenario of the country. How they forced to leave the country and go to Canada. Here is the pattern in which this book goes :

First 1/6th of book was - childhood time games of 7 year kid.
Second 1/6th of the book was - Life of an Tamil aunt who falls in love with a Sinhalese man.
Third 1/6th of the book - Death of the mother's former lover.
Fourth 1/6th of the book - Son of father's old dead friend and letting him on job.
Fifth 1/6th of the book - teenage gay love.
Last 1/6th of the book - immigrating to Canada as increasing hostilities towards Tamils
3.5 Marks out of 5 exactly.
A good fast read book on life of kid in his growing years in Sri Lanka. I really liked the ending of the book that kept me hooked and shocked.
