Rating: 5/5

"The Damn Good Resume Guide" by Yana Parker and Beth Brown lives up to its name. This book has been around for a while and has had a chance, through multiple editions, to evolve into a useful classic for the job seeker's shelf.

This book is a bit dated but it provides a good introduction to resume writing including a short manageable list of action verbs. The book is also easily read since it only runs to 78 pages, half of which are filled with sample resumes. As such, this book is likely to not be the only book you use for your resume, but it is a very good choice as a first book to start out with.
