Rating: 3/5

Well written and with much potential. I could really 'see" the characters and there was pretty good development. However, the conflict and resolution were both shoved into the last 30 pages of the books, making it feel like everything just wrapped up in too much of a hurry. The conflict should have come much sooner, giving time for a realistic resolution instead of the old life/death/rescue scenario that is much too overused to resolve things.
