Rating: 4/5

Privacy: A small girl wanders the grounds of her grandfather’s estate and stumbles upon the eroticism of her aunt bathing naked in the river.

Backlighting: A young woman is painted nude by her aunt in the midst of an afternoon social gathering. 

Shadows: An elderly woman with a scholarly background is convinced by a youth to go exploring the Parisian catacombs. 

Raw Desire: A niece finds herself charmed by her aunt’s lover at a generationally-divided dinner party. 

Foreign Things: A reunited brother and sister share an exotic oriental fruit together. 

The Lights of Love: A brother and sister adopt each other as lovers. 

In the Garden: A focus on a gardener and his two wives: the first mauled by a tiger and his second newly pregnant. 

Presences: One sister plays nurse to her sick sister Joey who struggles with seizures, delusions, and brain cancer.

Life in the Temperate Zone: A slew of neighbors watch a storm blow into their neighborhood.

Silk: In the wake of her divorce, a scientist takes to stealing silk clothing from boutique shops. 

Borrowed Scenery: A focus on a scientist’s research in Japan.
