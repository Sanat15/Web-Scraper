Rating: 4/5

A nice little romance where the lovemaking is a work in progress....

Hero- tall, hairy chest, unattractive but appealing in a macho sort of way...he has been raised to see women as a hindrance not an asset, raised by his father while his brother was raised by the mom, sexually inexperienced in how to please a woman, (MY opinion: pretty sure his sexual experiences were at the hands of escorts who didn’t care about their own satisfaction...I wouldn’t be surprised if daddy took him into town to have his son get his first sexual experience ) Again that is me embellishing the story, there is no mention of prostitutes...

Heroine: She is a model, meets the hero when his brother brings her to the ranch, of course she is attracted to the ugly hero, she is deflowered by the hero in a rushed up bumbling make out session and the experience isn’t good...hero insults her and she leaves, she comes back 2 months later to tell hero she is pregnant, but he is such an ass she never gets it out...then proceeds to get in a serious car accident destroying her modeling career Forever!(or at least until she can be mainly healed as to cause doubt and insecurities in the hero)

OM-brother of hero...raised by the mom....seems spoiled, obsessed with heroine and makes sure to cause havoc between the hero and heroine by spreading lies about both of them....no brotherly love here, He dies and in his will he has left his half of the ranch to the heroine who wouldn’t give him the time of day with the condition that she live on the ranch...effectively sticking it to his brother(or we could do the 1/2 glass full take on this situation and say that the brother, despite his feelings, threw the heroine and hero together to work out their differences) sooooo

 6 months have passed...hero needs heroine to save his ranch....he feels guilty for her accident and also wants to make her right again(he found out about accident and baby from a letter he read that the brother had)...Anyway, she doesn’t want his pity, he doesn’t want her pity, they clear up some of the misunderstandings that the brother caused, but our hero is so down on his looks and women in general that he can’t see the heroine is in love with him...she isn’t going to say she loves him because she doesn’t want to be hurt and she is under impression hero only wants her there to keep ranch, during this time they try and figure out how to have great sex...she tells the hero how to make it more pleasurable for her(she’s heard locker talk so to speak) and directs him...I actually liked these scenes...he was not the typical experienced hero that instantly rocked his virgin heroine’s world(DP has written other inexperienced heroes similarly) They had to figure it out together...they eventually get it all worked out only to have more misunderstandings get in the way of their HEA! There is a great HEA where the heroine confronts the hero in front of his cowboy ranch hands who gleefully watch the drama unfold....we also have a nice little epilogue!
