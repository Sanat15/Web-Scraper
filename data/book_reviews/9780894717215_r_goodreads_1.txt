A collection of well-loved, well-known tales with one less common entry. The stories were told simply, with brisk pacing that resulted in a reading experience where I, as an adult, felt the already short book flew by too quickly to linger much in my memory. Not that it necessarily needed to, with how many times these stories have been retold and adapted. But I personally enjoy reading different adaptions and cultural variations of classic fairy tales, and the versions in this book did include enough small details that deviated from the 'Disney' standard to make this collection worth reading once. However, overall I found this work pleasant, but unremarkable.
Did I like it? It was okay.
Would I reread it? No.
Would I recommend it? With it's miniature size and the gorgeous cover art on the dust jacket, I'd be more inclined to use this book as a prop for an 18 inch doll or teddy bear than to actually read it.
