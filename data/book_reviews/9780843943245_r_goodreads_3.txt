A cowboy named Cole Granger is picking up some horses in town for a local rancher and is treated by the townsfolks with heavy-handed racial comments and disregard. Some racist rustlers think he'll be an easy mark, so they trail him with ill intent. Then the rancher's prize horses are driven off in the "High Prairie" (1997) and Cole sets off in pursuit to get them back where he has a cartoony adventure with gunfights, saloon standoffs, more racist outlaws, Mexican bandits and horse thieves, law men who hate blacks, lawmakers who write laws that make it illegal for black folks to carry firearms, biting rumors of pre-war interracial dalliances, racist rich white powerbrokers, and lots and lots more racists.

Verdict: A repetitive and boring karmic racial injustice revenge fantasy.
