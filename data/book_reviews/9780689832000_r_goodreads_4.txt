Rating: 2/5

This book is a historical fiction. It is about a girl named Nissa living in the south with her father and his new wife, along with their new daughter. As Nissa grows older, she realizes how many events of her past have influenced the way in which she has grown up and how with her mother leaving her at a young age, it is hard to get along. Nissa runs the local library and has to come up with the decision on if she should separate the blacks and whites in the library or keep the library as one. Nissa begins to see changes around her and decides that life is never the same. Something has to be different and constantly changing all of the time which keeps Nissa wondering if she will ever become stable. She is faced with an important decision that she has to make which could affect her life and the lives of others.
