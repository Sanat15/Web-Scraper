Rating: 5/5

I loved this. It's going in my favorite book pile. It was so real and honest. It made me FEEL something and that is always a sign of a good book. The fact that this book was published before I was born and I could still relate to these characters was also another tell-tale that this is a classic slice of life. I'm surprised that I haven't read this before and I'm surprised that I haven't stumbled across more books with a similar plot. 
I couldn't put it down. I flew through reading it. Six stars. Bravo.
