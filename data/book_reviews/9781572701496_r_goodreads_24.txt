Rating: 4/5

I wasn't sure I liked Catherine Aird after my first book of her's Harm's Way. But I loved no.1 of Seedy's cases. I forgot how much he likes his puns. And I listened to the audiobook, which always helps with mindless murder.

I inherited my love of murder mysteries from my Dad (and his Mum before him). He tells me to pick the most unlikely culprit when guessing the murderer. He's very good at it and this time I remembered to take his advice. So I jokingly decided the principal of the Ag College did it. No reason, except he was next door and not a likely killer. Guess what - he did it. My dad's advice doesn't encompass guessing the motive,
so that was a surprise. And Mr Farmer has a clever motive.
