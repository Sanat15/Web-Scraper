Rating: 5/5

David Zindell is Frank Herbert with a wilder and at times gentler flare. The Neverness saga so far has been Dune except replace Sand with Snow, the Fremen with icy Neanderthal, the spice with Yogic siddhis, and Messianic jihad with Gnostic ascension. The most important parallel: the spectacular psychedelic introspective worldbuilding that oozes off the page.

I cannot recommend this series enough, and from a “speculative fiction is an imaginative reflection of hard truths and a commentary on humanity’s could-be’s” I think this is a legitimately important work. More to come from my boy Danlo!
