Rating: 3/5

I was going to say how much I love Margaret Way's books then saw I've 2 starred about three of them. That said I really do like her stories, they are involved and well developed, great travelogues on the flora and fauna of Australia, the heroines (usually) aren't total idiots, and the heroes (usually) seem to actually love the heroines. And, hey, Hugh Jackman is from Australia so that's a golden glow there. I am concerned about MW's home life as she almost always ends up with the kind of family dynamics that Jason and Medea or the Medici's would envy.

Here it's no different, the long lost h arrives in Australia decades after her apparently charismatic alpha father left because of a tragic love affair with a married woman. As luck would have it, guess who the H is. Yep, the son of the long lost love. Toss in one of the most overtly bitchy OW/cousins, a domineering patriarch, more doormats than Bed, Bath and Beyond, some condescending relatives, and an Upstairs/Downstairs mentality for a solid angsty story. The heroine makes the statement at one point, that Australia was supposed to be a classless society and by classless I mean in the good egalitarian way.

Oh yeah, it is 1979 so there is a totally awesome and mind-blowing disco moment, man, where the H and h wow the crowd.
