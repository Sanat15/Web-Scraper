Rating: 5/5

Finally, after waiting impatiently for more than three years, I introduced my child to my favorite, most mischievous, yet sweetest and kindest little girl — Ramona!
The book is written by my favorite children’s author, Beverly Cleary. It’s a series of stories about 4-year-old Ramona and her 9-year-old sister, Beezus. Over eight books, Ramona grows up and gets into all kinds of adventures. The books are realistic family stories that capture the challenges and difficulties children face at that age.

Cleary is one of the most genuine and innocent writers — the stories were written in the 1950s, when a 4-year-old could play outside without a parent, ride a bike to first grade, or visit a friend’s house alone. The stories are full of gentle lessons, touching on everything from small, everyday issues (like getting along with friends) to serious topics (like the death of a pet) — all told through a child’s eyes.

Though Ramona can be quite mischievous, everything in the books carries a lesson. She’s imaginative and curious — and she might even give your kids a few ideas 😉 (like realizing only the first bite of an apple tastes best; after reading Hansel and Gretel, deciding to put a doll in the oven “like a witch”; or cracking eggs into dough with their shells on just to see what happens).

It’s impossible not to fall in love with this funny little rascal from the very first page. There are still seven more books to go, and I can’t wait for us to read all those adventures together!
