Rating: 4/5

If it moves, shoot it, then eat it. If it doesn't move, just eat it anyway before the dawg gits it.

I'm no vegan or animal rights activist, but I was shocked at the amount of shooting of wild animals (and Native Americans) that happens in this book. Even cute squirrels were given the "black powder and shot" treatment. Oh well, such were the times back then, I suppose. Us Aussies were no angels in our early days either.

I did like the characters and Gipson's writing style made me imagine the story was being narrated by Jed Clampett (ala Beverley Hillbillies) ...hehe. I liked Yeller, the main character, but I do have a soft spot for Labradors, which I presume him to be. This isn't my favourite dog book though; that honor belongs to The Call of the Wild or The Incredible Journey

This was certainly worth a read.
