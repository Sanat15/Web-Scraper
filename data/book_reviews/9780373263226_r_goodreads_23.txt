Rating: 2/5

"What a way to go...killed by the combined efforts of a monkey and a criminal idiot trying to teach himself how to have a sense of humor."

This book was all over the place. There were too many characters, a plot that meandered all over the place (I mostly forgot the mystery they were trying to solve) and a marmoset named Erasmus. I also get sick of Mrs. Pargeter getting so highly offended when anyone even dares talk about Mr. Pargeter's past criminal life. It's not like she isn't benefitting from his career now with all her contacts.

Of course everything is neatly tied up at the end - the crooks get caught and Mrs. Pargeter escapes with help from Truffler and Co.

The first few books in the series were good but this one reminded me of mid-series Agatha Raisin. Same old jokes. Same characters doing the same things. And lots of references to the recession.

As Fossilface might learn to say, "I lost the plot" on this one. (Har har.)

I will read some of the next, mostly because I earned a copy on NetGalley and I want to follow it through. But I can see why this series had a hiatus at one point.
