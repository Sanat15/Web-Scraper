Rating: 4/5

An excellent romance novel. This a "sweet" almost traditional regency. The Heroine is sweet and retiring, the hero thoughtful and honorable and the side characters add just the right mount of depth. Both protagonists are improved by their relationship. Lord Thorverton begins to take more responsibility for his family, specifically his brother. Meribe grows a backbone without becoming the "sharp-tongued harpy" in the mold of her sister. The mystery of why Meribe's suitors all find untimely ends is well plotted with a satisfying conclusion. The two wayward characters, Thorverton's brother and Meribe's sister all come to realize their mistakes and take action to change their behavior. Practically unheard of in a romance, very exciting. There is the usual round of "does he/she love me" misunderstandings, but all for the better. The final confrontation and declaration of love hits all the right notes.
