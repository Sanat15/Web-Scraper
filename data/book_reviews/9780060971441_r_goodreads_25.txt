Rating: 4/5

It sort of hangs together, but it somehow doesn't matter whether it does or not. Renata Adler's Pitch Dark is on one hand about the ragged relationship between newspaper reporter Kate Ennis and her married boyfriend Jake. But 99% of the book is about what happens when Kate isn't thinking about Jake, whom we never actually see. All we see are Kate's brief monologues between all the interesting things that happen in her life, such as dealing with raccoons and getting into an auto accident in Ireland. Not as good as her first book, Speedboat, but well worth reading in its own right.
