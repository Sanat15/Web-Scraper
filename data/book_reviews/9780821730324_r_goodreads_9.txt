What a gem! Even 2 generations removed from the 1941 baseball season, the names &amp; accomplishments sit in my head &amp; heart like a catechism, as they should for any deep baseball fan. Dom claims that 1941 was the last "original baseball season" and he's right. Today's game isn't the same, and is even more different than it was in 1990 when the book was written. But that history which is set upon unattainable pillars is why baseball endures.
