Rating: 3/5

Three solid mysteries. Unlike most of Nero Wolfe's adventures, however, these did not stay with me. Usually, I chew over a good Stout tale for a day or two after finishing it. The next day, I was off onto everything else that was going on. Still, they were interesting tales of murder. Each short story made for a good read before bed, which is when I seem to find my reading time these days. 

They would also be great for a rainy afternoon or when you happen to find yourself with an hour to kill. Perfect for doctor's appointments or waiting at the DMV.

Recommended for fans of Nero and Archie or lovers of a good mystery.

Find it. Buy it. Read it!
