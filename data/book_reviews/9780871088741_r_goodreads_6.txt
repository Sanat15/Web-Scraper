Rating: 5/5

This book is a gem, the same luster as "A River Runs Through It" which pays homage to similar themes of life close to nature and holds in highest esteem for her creatures foremost of which are the majestic trout and the elusive wild turkey. And geezers who live to fish and hunt and live off the land. Middleton writes deft prose with flourish when needed much like the art of fly fishing and the almost perfect cast. It's a book of love and respect for traditions knowing well time erases all in change.
