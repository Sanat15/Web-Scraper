Rating: 5/5

Powerful book! Now a favorite!
Black and white illustrations with just a simple sentence or two per page. One grandmother recounts her time living in a dugout on the prairie. Covers the journey, the building of her home, the sounds and loneliness of the prairie, success and failure of crops, the eventual “upgrades” and her final thoughts as she recounts.

This book is golden and one ai would not hesitate to give as a gift to others.
