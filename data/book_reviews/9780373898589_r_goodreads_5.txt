Rating: 3/5

A Harlequin Romance from 1977 this is a really cute one with two young girls trying to get their Dad to find a new wife. They choose a new Nanny and get their father to approve her by fudging her age on her application. The dad, Jordan Long works out of the country and meets the new nanny at the airport. She is young and beautiful just the opposite of what he wanted in his house. He is very angry thinking Kathleen lied about her age but the girls convince him to let her stay for one month. The girls do every thing they can to help them fall in love but it does not work out the way they want.
I read this one straight through without stopping. It was a lot of fun.
