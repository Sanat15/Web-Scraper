Rating: 5/5

I found this book after a Lifegroup discussion where we were talking about living on a restored Earth after Jesus’s second coming. I’ve read Randy Alcorn’s Heaven awhile ago but remembered it being a pretty long book, but this one is nice a short. And I loved it! Lawrence gives lots of biblical reasoning for his views on where we go right now when we die and also what “heaven” will be like when Jesus returns. I especially loved the end where he talks about how our view of heaven and eternity should affect our lives right now. Come, Lord Jesus!
