Rating: 1/5

The writing itself isn't the issue. It's that we have a protagonist who's been permanently maimed by her father. Who sometimes knocked her around too, but that's okay because he loved her. And it's okay that he threw acid in his daughter's face and disfigured her, because he really meant to disfigure his ex-wife. And his ex-wife is a horrible person who drove him to it and would have deserved getting acid in her face. I'm so not okay with hand-waving away child abuse, who is getting blamed in this story instead OF THE ONE WHO ACTUALLY DID the horrible thing, and how the big resolution is framed as a touching reunion between daughter and her abuser.
