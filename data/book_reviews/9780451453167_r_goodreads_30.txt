Rating: 1/5

I didnt want to DNF this book and reading it til the end made me dislike it more.

I couldn't stand the main character. She was so conceited and the author tried too hard to make her relatable and edgy. 

My biggest issue with this book was the dialogue. The issue of edginess just got too much at times and characters seemed so contradictory. 

Maybe my hopes were too high as I saw some nice reviews. But yeah, this book wasn't for me.
