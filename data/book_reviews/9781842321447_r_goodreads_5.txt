Rating: 4/5

This novel brings the story of the house – The Old Vine – to the 1950’s and completes the trilogy, which started with the construction of The Old Vine in the 1500’s. As in the previous two novels, the occupants of the house and those related to it tell their stories. Each story is in a different time period and relates to challenges and issues appropriate to the time. Lofts continues to write with great attention to the details of daily life in each applicable time period. The characters are well portrayed, but the house is the entity around which all the characters, stories and historical events revolve. I enjoyed this trilogy and was happy that after almost 500 years, The Old Vine was to have a new, ongoing life.
