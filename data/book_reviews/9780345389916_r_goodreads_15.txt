Rating: 2/5

This book started off well - I was enjoying Jane's reflections on her life, her relationships, literature. But about halfway through, the narrative lost momentum and I started skimming. The ideas Godwin is exploring are great - the motherline, the choices and compromises women make when it comes to family, marriage, career - but somehow the pacing is off, most of the characters sound alike and I found myself impatient and eager to skip to the end.
