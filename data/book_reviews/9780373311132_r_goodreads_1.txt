Rating: 2/5

It was almost page 80 when the hero and heroine of the story met. I just didn't feel the connection between Catherine and Marcus. Another thing, it grated on my last nerve that he kept calling her 'my child' even after they were married. "My child." "My child."

"My child."

Maybe it was the mood I was in when I read the book but there was no spark. My guess is that I have read so many Regency romances that there needs to be some 'oomph!' to get me to appreciate another one. But, my child, I will keep plugging along until I find something better.
