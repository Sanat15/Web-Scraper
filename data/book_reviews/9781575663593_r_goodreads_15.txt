Rating: 3/5

The story kept me interested, but something still felt disjointed even when the storylines merged. 
Also, characters, Ryan Stone and his partner, as in the previous book don’t appear until about halfway into the book with minor roles or just to take Savannah out to dinner. They are more like fairy godmothers. 
This book really wasn’t even a job that Savannah was hired for, she was just assisting Dirk.
