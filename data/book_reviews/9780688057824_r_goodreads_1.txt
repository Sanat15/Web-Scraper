Rating: 4/5

Sweet and adorable book! As with many children's books, the illustrations always win my heart. The colorful, cozy and nostalgic drawings are incredibly charming! Though the story itself is very repetitive, I think it would certainly appeal to young people. The story of Grandmother's birthday was fun to read and I'm glad I grabbed this book at my local library book sale. It's worth a read for all ages, especially if you love all things cozy and cats!
