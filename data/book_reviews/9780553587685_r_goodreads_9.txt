WOW! I have no clue how I missed this audiobook!!! I was literally glued to my iphone and then synced it to my audio night mask. I loved it! THE OTHER DAUGHTER will keep you on the edge of your seat to the very end. Twisty and shocking! 

I have read all Lisa Gardner's new books, (one of my favorite authors) but now I am quickly buying all her older audiobooks to catch up! I just found it by accident and the narrator: Brittany Pressley was superb!!! Highly recommend.
