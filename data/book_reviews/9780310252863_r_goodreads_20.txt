Rating: 5/5

My sister Cheri gave me this book years ago (like 10 or 15, I think) and I was recently (finally) drawn to it. This was written post Watergate, post prison, post starting his prison evangelism ministry and it was insightful how so many of the ills still plague our nation and church today as he discussed when it was written ('81 I think?). I strongly recommend this, and am excited to read more Colson.
