Rating: 1/5

Several well-known and popular authors here, but for the most part I couldn't get enthusiastic about their stories here.
Mr Boy was my favourite, though I kept imagining it as a cartoon-type offering, and it wasn't. But it was original and fun.
There were maybe 2 or 3 other stories that actually grabbed my interest.
One thing that popped up more than once (anthology is from 1991) was authors' confidence that the future of information storage and transmission lay in the CD. Interesting look back at the times.
