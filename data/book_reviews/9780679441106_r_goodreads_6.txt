Rating: 4/5

Catching up…

Another donation to my Little Free Library Shed, of a book read long ago. Now bringing my review to Goodreads. 

What happens when you feel invisible and unappreciated? 

Well…In this case, the main character, Delia, decides to walk away from her life. Her marriage. Her children. 

And…Simply disappear. 

For her life feels too complicated…

So…Why not just walk away from it?

And…This is the journey Tyler takes readers on. Watching Delia leave her family without a word, hitch a ride to a new town, buy a new dress in a new style, find a job and a room in a boardinghouse…

And…Begin again? As someone new?

Or…Find new complications with this new life?

The question is…Will readers enjoy this journey? An examination of a marriage, aging and independence. A book about choices. 

Or…Be annoyed by the characters momentum and decisions and the journey itself? 3.5 stars. Rounded up.
