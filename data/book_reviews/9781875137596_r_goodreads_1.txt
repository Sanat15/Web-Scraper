Rating: 5/5

Realized that I have yet to rate this book, which I had acquired some time ago. One of the most beautifully hand illustrated animal reference books I have come across, with drawings depicting the different species in various behaviors like feeding and socializing. What sets this book apart from the typical reference book is that it not only describes each species with the usual info box, but also highlights specific anatomical adaptations or behavioral traits unique to them, for instance the cooperative hunting of a pride of lions. This makes it more than a mere guide to identifying animals but also a minor discourse on their natural history. Superb and a treasured collection for sure. Oh and it is also pocket sized to boot, so you can take it everywhere with you!
