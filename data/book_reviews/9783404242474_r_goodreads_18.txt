Rating: 3/5

Although the story is more coherent than "The Antipope," this tale of allotment golf and creosote-smelling aliens isn't as funny and all I really got out of it was to use the 'London borough magically cut off' trope in my own "Long Summer of Hammersmith," although in that case it was only a few individuals who couldn't leave town. 
A comment elsewhere here gave me pause: Rankin is a fan of Flann O'Brien and all these Brentford novels, set in a non-specific past and including bicycles and Weird Science, are very reminiscent of The Third Policeman. Suppose it's a similar situation?
