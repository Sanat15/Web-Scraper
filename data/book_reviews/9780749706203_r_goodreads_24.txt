Rating: 3/5

This book was a random free one from uni. I think it’s for older-teens. It had these little stories from in this place called Zennor, they were very easy to read. My favourites were, Giants Necklace and Gone to sea. Quite a wholesome book and i really liked alot of the mythical creatures. Nice to see as you read along some of the stories link up and fit together. Time taken: 3 hours (roughly)
