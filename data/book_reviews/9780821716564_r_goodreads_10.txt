Rating: 4/5

"I'll swallow your soul! I'll swallow your soul!" 
"Swallow this." 
(blows face off with shotgun) 

Fine, you caught me. That's not a scene from this book. But when I heard the title Soul Eater, I couldn't help but assume it would be like that iconic Evil Dead II scene. Instead, the book was a lot less pulpy than I expected, going for a riff on Shirley Jackson with a house that makes people crazy. The house was kind of like the store in Needful Things only it doesn't sell anything, it just sticks a wooden spoon in your brain and gives your emotional stability a good stirring, then sets you loose on the town to see what happens.
