Rating: 4/5

"If your home has the poems of Hanshan / they're better for you to read than sutras / write them out on a screen / and look at them now and again"
a good book of poems. I've been looking to explore poems from around the world (and across time) and these are a great way to get into it. the prose is really good, but approachable. all the poems have a good flow and storyline, and are good for both introspection and the study of verse. 
my favorite of them are 20, 43, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 58, 59, 73, 117, 161, and 188.
