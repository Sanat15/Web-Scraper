Rating: 5/5

Wow! I'm really impressed with this. I've had it for 10 years and had never made it very far. Once you get through it though, you can't put it down.

Jack Standini, a local shoemaker, is a bit of a recluse. His wife mysteriously leaves him, and he's left to his own solitude, with few friends or contacts. In his spare time he works as a sculptor, carving various angel statues in a back room. Then he meets Lucille, a naïve ingenue who's life desire is to become Marilyn Monroe, and Lucille meets a man who claims that he has the gift to turn her into a veritable Monroe ... and then things get creepy from there!

The simple language used belies the insidiousness and perversity of the situation and the characters ... the texts provide veiled commentary on appearances, belief, truth v fiction, religiosity, and perhaps a host of other issues.

Read this!
