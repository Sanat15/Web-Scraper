Rating: 3/5

I really enjoyed this book. As I always found interesting how our minds react towards what we believe is correct or what we love, this book could not be any better for me.
Politics have always been , and will always be in my opinion, a dangerous field. Throughout this book, Hitler's goal was clear and he knew exactly how to achieve it. "Kids are the future" could not be better portrayed. Hitler "educated", more like trained, kids as young soldiers, giving them privileges that not even adults had, making them feel important. In that way he bought their loyalty, allowing his ideal to endure long after his death.
I found it bit hard to read because the book contains testimonies of people from that generation. I had to put the book down and process the information a couple of times, not believing the fascination those kids had for his "God".
It is easy to judge other people's decisions without putting yourself in their shoes. By reading these kind of books you can't help but reflect, learning from Hitler's generation.
