Rating: 4/5

This book is a compilation of facts, biographies, photos, recollections, statistics, and excerpts from diaries and letters.

Each new story fills you with shock, awe, and sympathy. The many pictures combined with true accounts paint a vivid picture.

The only things I knew about kinder-transport and war guests had been from fictional children's stories. This book helped to expand my view. 

I recommend this book to anyone who is learning the history of WWII. It is an essential companion to a much less talked about aspect of the war.
