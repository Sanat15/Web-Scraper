Rating: 1/5

I don't give one star reviews often but this book was atrocious. The red flags that popped up about Pohl as a writer in Gateway are far more prevalent in this sequel. Are you interested in statutory r*pe? Are you interested in minors hooking up with other minors? Are you interested in minors performing lewd acts in front of other minors? Why the hell is any of this in this book? I am not a prude but all of this sexualization of minors was so unnecessary and creepy as shit. It served no purpose. I can't help but wonder how much of it was the author's creepy wish fulfillment.
Aside from the weird sex shit, these characters are horribly uninteresting anyway. Rob Broadhead was present again but might as well have been a completely new character, as now he's just a rich tycoon with little personality of his own. At least he isn't punching women in the face in this book like in Gateway. (Seriously Mr Pohl. Wtf was wrong with you? ) The reveals about the Heechee were not worth this pain.
I will not be reading more any more of this man.
1/5
