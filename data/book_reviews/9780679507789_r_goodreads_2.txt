Rating: 4/5

I thoroughly enjoyed this book.

If you like sailing, travelogues, DIY or any of the above, you’ll also likely enjoy this real life memoir of a circumnavigation by sail.

The authors, a husband and wife team, and their apparently shockingly well behaved teen age son, completed a circumnavigation in a not nearly new, not always shipshape, square rigged motor sailer. This is not the modern cruiser of current You Tube videos - no water maker, limited electronics (no chart plotter, no GPS), limited fuel tank, limited water tanks - but plenty of salty can-do attitude take the family around the world.

Sails rip, the engine fails, lines snap, the head breaks - all multiple times. The appendix provides a list of tools brought along and they were all in consistent use. 

Written in the 1970’s, the book is also a time capsule to some well known places “just before”. Bermuda before reinsurance; Tahiti before charter flights; East Timor before the wars, Cocos Keeling before being overrun. One of the gifts the authors have is to make friends around the globe, with yachties and locals both. 

With not pretensions of being a literary masterpiece, this extremely earnest book is an extremely fun read.
