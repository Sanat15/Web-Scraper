Rating: 5/5

Very good book. Scotty is alone in the valley after her father dies. A wounded man with a gun needs her help. They are snowed in till the passes open. She finds out he works for the Governor who has made the valley and all the land around it a government land so he's in the valley to make her and her neighbors move. The trials and tribulations that Scotty and Alex face are going to be hard on all of them. Really liked this book.
