Rating: 4/5

This was so fun. Another Hannah Howell read under her pen name Anna Jennet. I can't believe it has taken me so long to discover her. I'm eating up her books.

I love her strong heroines. In this her heroine Sorcha comes from a poor clan with "gifts" or a curse if the stories are to be believed. All the women in her clan can see or communicate with spirits, and some have other gifts like premonitions. Her clan is blessed with many women, and not very many men. But they are a pretty close and loving clan. Her brother, the laird, has run off to battle and got himself caught by the English, putting Sorcha in the position of raising funds to buy him back. Which is how she hatches the plan to drag Rory, a wealthy laird, from the same battlefield and ransom him back to his own clan. 

Rory was not my favorite Hero. I came to like him, but the way he was so adamantly against Sorcha ever being his wife rubbed me the wrong way and maybe because he never truly realized how hurtful it was to Sorcha is what also bothered me. But because of this, it made his fall for her so much more satisfying. When he finally admitted that he did want her. Once again, it was a great book with fun cast of characters with their own personalities. That's one of the things I like most about her books, her characters all feel different to me.
