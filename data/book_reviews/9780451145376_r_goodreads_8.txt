Rating: 1/5

I could hardly finish the book, I was so disgusted by the so-called hero who is actually a zero. Not only did he abandon his wife and child, cheat on her, have a long,extended affair, made a child with his affair partner, paid her off and abandoned the mistress, but when his wife found out, he doubled down and blamed her! And as strong and smart as the heroine was in other circumstances, she was a jelly-spined puddle of goo when it came to him, taking him back again and again. I don’t care that this was set in a historical era with different customs and where philandering husbands and long-suffering wives were the norm, I read romance to ESCAPE from bleak reality and to admire characters who rise up above the norm and are people I can root for. This was so awful, what a waste :(
