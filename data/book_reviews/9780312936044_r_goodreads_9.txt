Rating: 3/5

Future earth speculative science fiction is so hard to get right.
This one was written in 1985 and feels like it. The Cold war was still in full swing when it was published, so naturally the Soviet Russians are still the bad guys, important recordings are still on “tape”, and the female characters aren’t any deeper than helpless damsels in distress that are easily bedded by their male saviors.
A lot of Bova’s books in this series (especially “Mars”) are really good. This early one is pretty rough.

The final act did have some good Bova-esque plot twists that kept me reading to the end.
