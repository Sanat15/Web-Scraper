Rating: 4/5

The full-length plays were great—a lot of different characters, story lines, and writing styles to challenge anyone. I wasn't as big of a fan of the one-act plays in the collection, none of them stuck to me, and most of them were farcical or comedic (which is never as fun to read as it is to see performed). It is very helpful for any actors that the selected monologues do have a bit of explanation before, sharing context, helpful hints, motives etcetera. Overall a great collection, and did a good job finding roles for women from many backgrounds and all walks of life.
