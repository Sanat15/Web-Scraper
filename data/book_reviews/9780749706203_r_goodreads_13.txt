Rating: 5/5

I thoroughly enjoyed this collection of short stories by Morpurgo. I'm currently rereading much of his work and newly reading other works of his I haven't read before and I have been disappointed in a couple (perhaps because I am older now?) but this was redemptive. 

I love how all of the stories are cleverly linked and how this is revealed at the end.

Some of the messages are very profound; a quote I took from this book was 
"The secret is to be in tune with the land, to be in rhythm with the seasons, to rise with the sun and go to bed as it sets. The land is like a god... Love the land and it will love you in return." - a most poignant message given the state of our planet today.

A great one for all ages.
