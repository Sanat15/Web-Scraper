Rating: 5/5

If you are looking for a well-written suspense with a touch of romance and a side of paranormal, then I highly recommend A Case of You. The plot is well-developed and will keep you guessing until the end. The murderer had been a blip on my radar but not seriously considered until near the end of the story. I usually have the mystery solved way before the reveal but I was only a step ahead of the author this time. Very well done.
