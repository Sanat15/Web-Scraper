Rating: 3/5

This story was about a tortoise called Shelley who couldn't understand why tortoises hibernate during the winter. Shelley's parents tell her it is ridiculous and that she should go sleep, but she waits until her parents are asleep to explore what outside is like. She meets several characters and soon realises that tortoises are not meant to be outside and that her parents were right, for instance a duck has a beak to break through the ice to get food and a dog can run around fast to keep warm. She stumbles and falls down the hill and ends up back at home.

This story reinforces the fact that parents are right about most things but also that through curiosity and exploration she learns something new. The repetition of the word "ridiculous" would be suitable for younger children to be read to in Nursery and Reception and children could read this by themselves or with support in Year 1. The story is good for talking about animals, their characteristics, different habits that they survive in and about why some animals hibernate during the winter.

Personally I think the book is average, the illustration is of good quality but there is not much variety from page to page.
