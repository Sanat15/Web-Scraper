Rating: 4/5

Enamored by the beauty of the wilderness walk in the redwoods from this morning I picked out the Ken Burns National Parks documentary to watch. It mentioned this crazy story of Truman Evert - his getting lost in Yellowstone (1870), trying to stay alive for thirty-seven days and his eventual rescue. This is an article he wrote describing his experiences of those 37 days. It is a great plot for a movie.
