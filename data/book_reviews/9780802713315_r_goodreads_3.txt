Rating: 5/5

That's an excellent history of mathematics from antiquity to the present day. The author skillfully captures the pedagogical trick of The Last Theorem. This "mathematical siren, ..., which attracted geniuses to destroy their hopes" is a common thread that compellingly guides us through anecdotes and concepts, very well explained in the maze of mathematics. The author gives his story both technical and societal dimensions, thereby maintaining the reader's attention from start to finish.
Very successful. Good reading.
