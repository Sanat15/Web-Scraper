After Diamond Dove, I wanted to continue on an Australian theme, so I reread this one—I've always liked Nicholas Jose's books. I can't remember when I first read it, but it was written in 1997, so must have been about 13 or so years ago. I remember liking it then, and still do. It's a contrast to Diamond Dove in being a white perspective—the aborigines don't sound as convincingly aboriginal—but it doesn't lack imaginative empathy. I like this book because it's about being from Adelaide, and Australia, and being out in the world with all the moral and cultural baggage that entails. Another interesting thing about reading it now, is seeing how the predictions he made about about China's changing place in the world have been realized in Australia. 

i will definitely keep this book on my shelves for a third reading in future.
