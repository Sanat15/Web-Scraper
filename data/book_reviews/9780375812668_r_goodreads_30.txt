Rating: 5/5

I loved this book. Loved it. Loved the way it was put together. Loved how infrequently the "other voices" came in. Loved the descriptions of art, and artists. 
It reminded me a lot of My Name Is Asher Lev, by Chaim Potok, even though they're nothing at all alike. But if you enjoyed "Asher", I think you'll enjoy this.
