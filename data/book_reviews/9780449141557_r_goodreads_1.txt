Rating: 3/5

I am not as familiar with this comic strip as others, but since I was killing time at my mother-in-law's residence, she has a collection of many of these older books that contain these comic strips. The two cartoons about being in the hospital reminded me of my trip at that age to the hospital as well. The one about not putting a drink in a glass from a bottle is exactly how our granddaughters act and spill. Finally, "I didn't hear you those first two times you called." Classic!
