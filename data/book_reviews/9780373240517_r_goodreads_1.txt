Rating: 5/5

Montana Passion is the second book in the Made in Montana series by American author, Jackie Merritt. Retreating home after a devastating betrayal in Washington, Serena Fanon is determined to make a success of her career as a lawyer in her hometown of Rocky Ford, Montana. When she witnesses a heroic rescue from a burning house by a passer-by, she wants to commend his behaviour, but she doesn’t have time for Travis Holden’s obvious attraction to her. Yet somehow, she finds herself accepting a date with him, and finds him physically irresistible. Travis is a successful used car salesman who isn’t used to rejection by women, but soon realises his feelings for Serena are more than the usual lust. Merritt once again gives the reader characters with appeal, dialogue that sparks and a plot with the odd twist. The mystery woman observing the Fanons is still about and there is some intrigue about the details of Charlie’s wife’s fate. An excellent romance read that will have readers anticipating the next book in the series, Montana Lovers.
