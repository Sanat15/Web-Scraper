Rating: 1/5

We have a motorhome, and we thought this 50-cent book, which we picked up at a thrift store, would be useful. And it might be, if we could read beyond the day-to-day, minute-to-minute trivia in the school of "first we drove to the airport, then we parked the car, then we walked to the check-in desk, then we waited, then we got on the plane, then we flew to Houston, then we got off the plane" that I used to see in creative writing classes--but of course, I made my students stop doing that. I didn't mind the religious stuff--chacun a son gout--but the pace drove me nuts, and I kept thinking, "okay, lady, get on with it." She apparently fancied herself a writer and spent every spare moment when she wasn't praying, jotting down minutiae, but we didn't find anything in the book we hadn't already discovered for ourselves. It was worth the 50 cents.
