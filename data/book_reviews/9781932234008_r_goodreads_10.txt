Rating: 3/5

After a very good first half I felt the book lost something towards the end. It became rather convoluted and a little bit silly. The film works so much better but maybe, as the film has such memorable visuals, this didn't help with my enjoyment. 

Also, the book ends on a cliffhanger so I now need to read Spiral. I wasn't aware of that when I started which is my own fault (I'm now aware there's 3 books in total). A good read but not as good as I was expecting
