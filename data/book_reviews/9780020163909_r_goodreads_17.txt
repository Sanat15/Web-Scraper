Rating: 4/5

Anthologies of short shorts (i.e. very short short stories) are rare. Asimov and Conklin edited two (I think) and another in Fantasy (not a great one it must be said).
In this volume you'll find the King, the God of the short short of course (Fredric Brown), but if you know him already you'll have bought all his books long ago, so is there anything else of note?
There is, there is, and plenty as well. As not everyone is equally gifted in handling such a difficult genre, some stories do not work very well, some are too predictable, some are a sort of repeat of things many times seen etc.
But there are wonderful little gems in there too, priceless texts that do more in 300 words than most novels in 300 pages.
