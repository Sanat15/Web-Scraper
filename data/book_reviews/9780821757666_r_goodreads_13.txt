Rating: 5/5

Miranda and Mushrooms 

Sensible efficient Miranda isn't so sensible when Lord Snell arrives at his Aunt's house. In the meantime Peter, another nephew, is unfavorably compared to the wealthy Lord. In the background a plot is being laid against sweet Aunt Artemis. I enjoyed the story but became somewhat agravated with Miranda's fantasizing patheticly about the Lord who gave her so little attention. She is a very bad judge of character. This was odd contrasted with her common sense efficiency. Still it was a good plot with interesting characters, and I enjoyed it.
