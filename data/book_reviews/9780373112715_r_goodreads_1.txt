Rating: 4/5

Re-read April 2020. I had to bump it down to four stars because the first half is really slow. The ending is still five stars, though. It's rare to read an HP and be convinced how much the hero really loves the heroine. 

What a sweet, angsty story with a surprising twist I did not see coming. The heroine is 21, the only daughter of an indulgent father. Hero is in the same law firm as the father and knows that his career will be enhanced if he takes out the daughter.

 Heroine is a flirt and comes across as heartless and spoiled, but eventually they do marry. Minor spoiler:  The heroine because she loves the hero - she asks him. But we're not sure of the hero. Heroine gets pregnant, has a miscarriage, and slips into a deep depression. 

The hero doesn't know what to do for her until she snaps at her father about (Major spoiler) the baby he made her give up for adoption and the boyfriend he paid off. Hero finds the boyfriend and brings him back to see the heroine so that she will know she was loved and that her father lied to both of them. When he returns, heroine has left and he thinks she went back to her true love. He's willing to accept it if she's happy, but then he finds she didn't go with her past love and he frantically looks for her. When he catches up, he finally tells he loves her and HEA. 

I do love misunderstood little rich girl stories and this was a good one. Hero was a good person. The heroine was, too. This is not high drama. It is a slow unveiling of the characters, what they're made of and how they fit together. My favorite kind of romance.
