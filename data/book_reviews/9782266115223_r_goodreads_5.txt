Rating: 4/5

This novel was a real surprise, a delightful and confusing surprise. The back cover describes a High-tech company in the United States and excavations undertaken by historians, including the professor and some members of the United States. These excavations were conducted in the Dordogne Valley, surrounding a 14th-century monastery.
Michael Crichton, well documented in the Middle Ages, produced a novel whose fluid writing and the protagonists' adventures I appreciated.
It is a book to read for those who love the Middle Ages, incorporating elements of science fiction and quantum physics. It is, above all, a great story!
