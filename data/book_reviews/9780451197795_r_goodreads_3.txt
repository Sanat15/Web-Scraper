Rating: 3/5

This book started off really well - a number of travellers are caught in a snow storm and are forced to take shelter at the country house of notorious rake, The Earl of Milford. Milford has his own guests staying already, including three women from the demi-monde. It is up to everyone to get along. Ariel is sister to a Viscount and is delighted to find herself amongst such "scandalous " people. Perhaps here is a chance for her to experience something of life. She is intrigued by Milford and suspects that he is not the rake that society has labelled him. As boredom sets in, the assembled party find themselves falling victim to a number of pranks that are becoming increasingly dangerous and it soon becomes evident that the "prankster" has a particular target in mind. Together Milford and Ariel seek to uncover who is behind it all and in the process, Ariel begins to understand the real person Milford is. As stated earlier, it started well - a great number of characters and personalities thrown together. But it soon begins to drag and you just want it to be over. All the characters are great but the story just looses its way and becomes a bit of an anti-climax.
