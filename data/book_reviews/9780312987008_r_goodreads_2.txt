Rating: 5/5

I've been reading this series one behind the other and I preferred this book over the first three. The story in this book seemed so much more realistic, having all the characters working on the same project...Evelyn's wedding for her daughter. I'm glad Bailey came back into this book, but how cleverly Janis Harrison kept him from getting too involved in Bretta's investigation.
The ending was very exciting, I couldn't put the book down until I finished it. I didn't guess who the murderer was, which made for a very good story.
Hope the next book is just as good, if not better.
