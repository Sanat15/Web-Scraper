Rating: 5/5

A powerful daily reader with quotes from TLGBQ+ authors and others framing each day’s reflection over recovery centering TLGBQ+ communities in its reflection. My favorite daily meditation I’ve found so far as a trans person in recovery. Holistic and intentional in its approach. Good for 12 step community members, sober curious folks, and all people striving for recovery in other means/spaces. I appreciate that the literature crosses many dimensions of possibilities of what it means to be in recovery, giving spiritual tools that leave interpretation open to each individual’s unique recovery journey and personal needs.
