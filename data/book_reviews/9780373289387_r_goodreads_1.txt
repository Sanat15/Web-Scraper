Rating: 2/5

Plot was intriguing, was curious about Lord Mountjoy's end goal (road? canal? rail? why?), but main villain was easy to spot. Unfortunately, the side characters (his brother Terry and her sister Angela) never emerged to be as interesting as they could have been. Perhaps the author was trying for the disjointed staccato that passed as dialogue, in order for the reader to relate to the hero, but I merely shared his headache. Evan also came off as a bit NTB so it seemed that in the future Judith would end up having to mother him.
