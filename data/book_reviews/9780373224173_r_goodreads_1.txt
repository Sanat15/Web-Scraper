Rating: 5/5

I thoroughly enjoyed this story. It started with action and continued right through with fast moving action. A young woman who sleepwalks in her sleep wake and find a bloody weapon in her hand and a dead body in her living room. The police charge her with murder but her aunt doesn't believe her niece did it so she hires a PI who believes the niece is guilty . One murder leads into another and every one thinks the young woman did it. The PI happens to be someone from her past and every thing he finds keeps proving she committed the murders. so the young girl decides to prove herself innocent. The ending has a twist that no one would expect.
