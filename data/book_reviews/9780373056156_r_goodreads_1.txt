Rating: 3/5

I liked this one because of its delicious hero and heroine. They are both very original characters, and I really liked how utterly intrigued they were by each other, even when they were so different.

I also liked how the romance progressed - they both alter their dreams a bit to fit each other in, and that's so lovely. (Although I did feel that Murphy did most of the effort on that front).

Who am I kidding, I loved this book from the moment I realized the hero wore Hawaiian shirts AND played pool. Seriously, what's not to love there?
