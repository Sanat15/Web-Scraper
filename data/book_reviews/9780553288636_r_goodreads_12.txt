A camera that's clearly being haunted by Regina's ghost helps Liz pull a Nancy Drew Files and take down a major drug ring! And not just any drug ring. That's right, this one features twins, one of whom is pretending to be the other. Which of course happens in nearly every installment in this series, but this time, it's not actually Liz and Jess who are using their twin powers for evil! Gotta love this shit.
