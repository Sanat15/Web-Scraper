Rating: 5/5

The Winter People is a gorgeous read - rich, colorful tapestry of characters, setting, and plot. This author loves words - measures them out just so, choosing for flavor and piquancy, but never density alone. You won't need a dictionary to get through this - rather, you'll be swept along poetic passages that are very like music.
Characters and plot are equally wonderful, and the early sense off foreboding is alleviated by moments of laughter and sheer beauty - and while this is a story set in North Carolina with warring clans, there's nothing hokey or cliche about it. 
Perfect start to the new year!
