Rating: 3/5

This has three plays - Arsenic and Old Lace, Detective Story, and Kind Lady. The first two take place in NYC, and the last in London.

I have to say the Arsenic and Old Lace film was where I first saw Cary Grant, and I love the movie. It was a treat to read the play. It does hold up, despite the character of Elaine.

Detective Story doesn't quite. I found it to be dull and the most dated.
