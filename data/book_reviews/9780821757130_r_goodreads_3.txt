Rating: 4/5

This gives the Bridgerton/Downton Abbey vibes that are very popular right now without the confusing dialogue. The writing is plain in a good way where you can understand the meaning and is not full of pompous talk that is hard to understand the meaning of. It is interesting to note that although women now do not need a chaperone to be alone with a man there is still the idea that a man can sleep with anyone he chooses and is just a wild thing to be tamed while a woman can still be ruined if she sleeps with someone before marriage. I did enjoy that not all the men were womanizers, some just spoke their mind, so they were deemed unfit by society; it was easier to believe in the love between the characters that way than the man just deciding that this woman was "different" than all the others he had been with. By favorite of this collection was "The Rake Objects?" for the humor of the story which I will not spoil.
