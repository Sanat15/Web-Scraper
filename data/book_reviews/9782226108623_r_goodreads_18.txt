Rating: 4/5

3.5★

Mary Higgins Clark’s We’ll Meet Again is a classic suspense read that delivers exactly what you expect from her—tight pacing, steady tension, and a cast of characters where everyone feels just suspicious enough to keep you guessing.

I found myself suspecting nearly everyone with even a hint of motive, only to be steered away from the truth in classic Clark fashion. The real resolution lands on the character who feels the most stable and trustworthy throughout, which makes the reveal satisfying rather than shocking for shock’s sake.

There’s also a sense of familiarity to the story—I may have read it years ago—but it still held up well as an easy, engaging thriller. Not overly complex, but well-constructed and consistently entertaining.

A solid, comforting suspense novel that does exactly what it sets out to do.
