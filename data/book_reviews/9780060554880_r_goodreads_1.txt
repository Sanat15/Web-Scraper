Rating: 5/5

The book is split by decades, 1860s-1870s,1870s-1880s, and so on and gives small bios on some prominent artists of those time periods. I would have liked a little more info on WHY they chose the specific artists I did (some, like Frederick Remington, and Perry of the Perry girls are self explanatory but others..Not so much) but I greatly appreciated that they found and included women in each decade, even so far back as pre-Suffrage 1860s. Most art history books ignore or leave out female artists unless she is particularly notable(and even then, many still leave them out nonetheless) so to see this book took care to mention women like Frances Palmer of the 1860s, Mary Hallock Foote of..if I remember correctly, the 1880s, rather than just lend to the impression that NO women were artists til the 1980s. This is why I gave it a five star rating. It was refreshing to see some women represented along side male artists, and I wish more art history books would follow that trend.
