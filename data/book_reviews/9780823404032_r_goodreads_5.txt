Rating: 5/5

One of the most gorgeous books I've ever encountered, with humble yet radiant illustrations bringing the story of Our Lady of Guadalupe to life in a simple and straightforward fashion without losing any of its magic. I dream of a pilgrimage to the basilica in Mexico City one day, and I hope to collect all of dePaola's books, especially the faith-based stories that he recounts with such obvious love and reverence.
