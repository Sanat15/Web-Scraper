This book reveals a history of conspiracies in the fluoride industry and fluoride's addition to are water supply and toothpaste. At the very beginning is the metal refinement, the atomic bomb and Harold Hodge, who shows up again committing Tuskegee like atrocities in The Plutonium files by Eileen Welsome. Edward Bernays (author of Propaganda) plays a major role in organizing dental cooperation for fluorides addition to the water supply. Bryson also documents a history of the Mellon Institutes science for hire policy as well as a dozen or so cases of pollution and health disasters directly related to fluoride. Remember if you don't brush your teeth with it or drink it with your water, its toxic waste. Great history, scary future.
