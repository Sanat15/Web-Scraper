Rating: 4/5

I read this upon the request of my local UU minister (I'm not UU, but you can consider me a fellow traveller). This book consists of a collection of essays about ways to integrating the spiritual and the mundane. This is very much a 101 book; not for me as I'm currently beyond that point, but for those who are just starting to develop their practice, it may be useful. Not library worthy for me, but may be for thee?
