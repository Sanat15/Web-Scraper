Rating: 5/5

Great story with a fantastic plot. Lots of twists and turns that were not solved until the last 20 ish pages of the book. This story has it all. A Laird (Rory) who has sworn off women/ marriage after the death of this first two wives and son. Two clans feuding with one partner from each clan. Felicia promised in marriage to an old friend of the king who happens to be a traitor to the crown. Rory’s clan wanting him to get married so they steal him a bride who happens to be impersonating someone else and was running away from her uncle’s planned marriage. Perfect ending. Can’t wait to see where book 2 takes this story.
