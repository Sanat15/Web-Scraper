Rating: 4/5

Read this a few years ago, but was obliquely reminded of it today. I loved this when I read it. Really made Sappho come to life -- and I've been a fan of her poetry for many years.

In the book there's one death scene that I found rather disturbing, and it should have been avoidable, but isn't. And the book is basically a tragedy; I don't much like tragedy, so it's all bittersweet.
