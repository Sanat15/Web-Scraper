Rating: 5/5

This, right here, is why no current popular science personality will ever, ever be able to replace David Attenborough. I can't even wrap my head around what he and his team encountered and handled on these expeditions (I mean, the sail to Komodo, good lord...). The adventures he had simply cannot be replicated in our mapped, managed, and regulated current world. As the his introduction notes, this is a snapshot of what an adventurous naturalist could do in another time, now lost. I'm grateful he recorded it so brilliantly. 

I always enjoy this type of book -- some crazy combination of "Jolly good, we certainly showed that croc what for, eh lads?" and "Oh my god, we seriously almost just died!". He has an incredible ability to capture the places and the people he encountered along the way, and I love that singing played such a role in his success in connecting with people. Now I must look up all the film they recorded during the expeditions, and I certainly hope that their audio recordings have been preserved and are available somewhere.
