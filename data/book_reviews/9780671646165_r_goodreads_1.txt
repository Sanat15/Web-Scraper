Rating: 1/5

I got this book because the cover is a painted illustration by Elaine Duillo (featuring two full-grown adults) and stands out to me as a lot less pulpy than her usual work. It’s gorgeous! 
Anyway…
I sped read this book as it started with the 25 year old H feeling amorous for the 13 year old h, he thinks she’s a prostitute when he wakes up in bed with her after she saved him from a thievery in an alley she frequents. He feels unmanly for his decision not to ravish her and takes her in as his ward. She has a crush on him and he sends her away to school to avoid the temptation. I’m always so disgusted by these old historicals where the man is “intrigued by a child” but they’re usually in their late teens, this is on another level. Yeah, he meets her again at 18 and then he’s 30 and that’s when the actual romance starts. Ridiculous! 
Still love the cover, though.
