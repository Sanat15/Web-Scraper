Rating: 4/5

I liked this historical novel, set in 1902, as a student. Summer 1902 is when the Russian Barshinskey family came to their small English village and forever changed the Willoughbys. The Willoughbys are skilled respected servants, but the Barshinskeys live in poverty with the children filthy and barefoot.

Mr. Barshinkey is a crude, coarse and a charming hard drinker, and his family is soon scattered by death and violence. Two of the children stay behind and keep in touch with the Willoughbys. Next tensions in Europe are ready to explode into war. 

Edwin Willoughby has a new life and good job in London, until he catches sight of Galina Barshinskey, she leads him astray into her world of theatre and rich lovers.
