Rating: 4/5

I think this one was the creepiest of all so far. Although I had a good portion of it solved, certainly not all of it. One thing I truly can appreciate about Riggs is that NEVER do I have the whole truthful accurate solution worked out before the end! That is fun. This one in the series was, I felt, much more of a "downer" than the other four. Not nearly as "enjoyable" a read for me, though well plotted and characterized, as usual!
