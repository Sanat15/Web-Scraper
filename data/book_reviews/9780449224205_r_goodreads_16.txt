Rating: 3/5

Clean Regency Romance

The second in the series, this book is not as well plotted as the first. And I'm not convinced about its title - something like Second Chances might have been more appropriate. 
Jessica's character is not as well drawn as Isabella, making me wonder what Robert saw in her besides physical beauty. Miss Trumble is a very interesting addition and I look forward to reading more about her. Barry remains his stalwart self. Mannerling continues to exude sinister magnetism.

Oh! What a cutting observation:
"And thus Mr. Devers proved himself to be a true Englishman. There was always an excuse for rape, but for wantonly stealing and losing money, none at all."
