Rating: 5/5

***Attention! This review contains spoilers. So, don't read it unless you finished the book***

This book, what to say about this book? I absolutely love all Ken Follett’s book, he is genius. 
Dangerous Fortune is not typical Follett’s book, set in 19th century , story follows the fortunes of famous and rich family Pilaster. At the beginning I thought it will be criminal book or something like that, I didn’t expect so much drama, romance and malice (all in good way).
First, my favorite character was Micky, I love that kind of smart and astute guys who knows how to care to themselves and passion between him and Augusta was really interesting. Unfortunately Micky showed that he was enormous evil, but at some way I was really sad when Augusta killed him.
Hugh is good character, I love that he is so careful to others and he loves Maisie for so long. They are meant to be together.
After reading Follett’s books I’m always so sad and want more. I give this book 5 stars because it has everything that book should have in one place and I really enjoy reading it. Way to go Follett
