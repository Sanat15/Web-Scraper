Rating: 4/5

Very intense and often upsetting book. It is a novel, but I'm sure many, if not all, of the cruel things done in the book actually happened to real living people. And from other books I've read this summer, I know the discrimination and cruelty continues to today. This summer's reading has shown me the importance of continuing to be better at showing love and kindness to ALL of God's precious sons and daughters, who are also my brothers and sisters.
