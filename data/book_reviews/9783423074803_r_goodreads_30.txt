Rating: 4/5

A great childhood book series I remember, although I never finished reading them all!
In fact, I don't know how many I finished, but in my box of junk I found I still had 20 or so paperbacks.. I had tried collecting them all as a kid - so I could read them in order, but then since I never got the full collection, I stopped reading them.. what a wierdo little kid I was.
The library always had the cool hardcover or paperback, my copies seemed like reprints, but at least the cover art was always a highlight... I have no idea why these didn't get the fame like Hardy Boys or Nancy Drew did... Hitchcock was pretty big in those days.
Anyway, they're all pretty short compared to the "adult" books I read, so what-the-hell, I'll try reading the whole series to cross off my childhood "bucket" list.
I've definitely read this one before, and it all came back as I read it. Our 3 investigators, Juipter, Peter and Bob - hanging out in a junk-yard where they'd managed to create their own little "headquarters" out of junk and a busted RV. The boys are tasked by Alfred Hitchcock to solve the mystery of a "haunted" house - which they, of course, solve- and so it's off to their next case!
A really fun read even as an adult for it's ability to transport me back to my nerdy youth. Not to mention the fun dialog.. I gotta start using the phrase WHISKERS! more!
