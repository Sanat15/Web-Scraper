Rating: 3/5

Author Kasey Michaels writes as Michelle Kasey with Moonlight Masquerade. I found the story to be an enjoyable ,no too long, way to spend an afternoon. The main characters are likeable enough. It's Beauty &amp; The Beast mixed with Phantom Of The Opera. There's a crazy aunt that I started thinking of as a 1930's dingbat character. kind of a scatterbrain with good intentions. Taken at face value she was a little annoying so I changed my perception.

Thank you to the author for making this older, 2012, e-book available. It was a nice gift for her readers.
