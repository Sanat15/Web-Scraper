Rating: 1/5

I read this book as part of my library's finding the non fiction book reading adventure. I am sorry to say that I thought this book was not my cup of tea. I thought all it did was "rip" apart a series of books using the bible. I grew up Catholic and I don't agree with anything Richard Abanes was saying. I feel that the Harry Potter series while yes there were references to magic in the books, this is one person's imagnation gone wild and wrote great books and that in some ways he was hiding behind the bible to "bash" the books and author.
