Rating: 4/5

Elaine Guinivere Morgaine Borne Marshall, AKA Mrs. Courtney is a schoolmistress who runs the Morton House School for Young Ladies or The Misfit Maidens of Morton as the ton calls them. The young women who attend are from upper class families but they don't necessarily fit in with ton society.

Guin has had an unhappy life, disastrous, and abusive marriage and she’s running from her past. She endeavors to help young women from making the mistakes she did and teaches them how to fit in and make happy matches.

When Daniel, Viscount Corvin sees Guin riding alone at dawn he’s captivated by this mysterious woman who refuses to give him her name. He doesn’t realize she is Mrs. Courtney, schoolmistress at Morton House where he’s taken his fifteen year old sister Emmaline to escape her wicked stepmother, known as the Courtesan Countess and the scandals of his family.

This sweet story has likable characters and some very dastardly villains. Guin was a wonderful, quite selfless young woman who was trying to escape her past and prevent other young girls from falling into similar situations. Daniel had many good qualities but there were times I wanted to pelt him. I just loved the girls at the school, they were delightful!

This sweet story will charm you with it characters, it’s dialogue and it’s imagery.

Thank you so much Ms. Boucher for this sweet romance.
