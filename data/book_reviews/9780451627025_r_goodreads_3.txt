Rating: 5/5

Mythological fiction is something that most of us love to read. 

  
 
People gave a warm welcome to all the mythological fiction books that were published recently. This book can be considered the best reference material for those interested in knowing more about mythology.

The author discusses the Greek, Roman, and Norse myths in detail in it. This book is divided into seven parts. They are the 
- The Gods
- The creation and the earliest heroes
- Stories of love and adventure
- The great heroes of the Trojan war
- The great families of mythology
- The less important myths
- The mythology of the Norseman 

I have this book's 75th-anniversary edition, which is simply spectacular in every way. Hamilton's brilliant writing, the cover, the layout, the printing, the illustrations, and everything else are done in an excellent manner. 

If you are someone who loves Greek and Roman mythology, this is one of the best books you can buy today.
