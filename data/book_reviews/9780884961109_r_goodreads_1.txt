Rating: 3/5

Apparently this guy is the husband of Alexandra Jacopetti, author of Native funk &amp; flash; an emerging folk art. This book is sort of a butch analog of that one, which is concerned with making clothing from recycled materials - this one is about making housing out of recycled buildings. Alexandra doesn't make much of an appearance aside from a photo of one of her looms. There are photos of people living in vats and old schoolhouses. Finally at the end Jacopetti talks about buying an old summer camp along with Alexandra's brother and sister and their spouses. Things soured for me a bit when it turns out they evicted people already living there. Anyway, it's a taste of 1970s California living.
