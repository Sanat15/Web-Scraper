Rating: 3/5

Well....I really liked the whole book, that is, until that are-you-fucking-serious wager with lord Cresing around page 297. The book went from a 4 star rating, to a miserable 2 stars. Now, I know you are thinking, but Sabrina.  Do you not see that title?  It's called a Daring Wager . Like, duh? Yes, yes I know, but one would think that to-be-determined wager would be with the hero, and not the villain. And then I have to question even calling it a wager. If agreeing to basically destroying your whole life to only be guaranteed of playing a game of cards where only if you win- will you get our hero's snuffbox back. A snuffbox, that may I mention, was stolen in the first place, and was not obtained in honorable means to begin with. Then this heroine is as dumb as a box of rocks. And most definitely not the character I read about in the first 296 pages.
