Rating: 4/5

In the Time of the Butterflies by Julia Alvarez takes place in the Dominican Republic, during the 1950s and early 1960s, under the dictatorship of Rafael Trujillo.
 The novel blends history with personal storytelling. The Mirabal sisters,Minerva, Patria, María Teresa, and Dedé—were portrayed not just as political figures, but as real women with fears, dreams, and courage. The story spans several decades but focuses on the years leading up to 1960, when three of the three sisters were assassinated for their resistance efforts. Through the remaining sister, Dedé, brings voice to their story as she reflects back on her life prior to 1960. She tells each sister’s story toward the path resistance. Alvarez’s writing is vivid and reflects on what it means to stand up for justice. This is novel is about the resilience and strength of women, the cost of freedom, and the power of memory.
