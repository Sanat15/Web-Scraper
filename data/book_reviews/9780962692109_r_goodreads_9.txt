Rating: 5/5

I received this book as a Christmas gift from my boss. I flipped through the pages a bit, but didn't think much of it. I have to say, I am honestly impressed. As soon as I finished it I handed it to my fiancé and said, "you have to read this." 
I didn't expect such a quick read to stir such a need to push harder. It made me want to strive for extraordinary and never settle for ordinary. I would recommend this to anyone, whether they are in a low point in their life or just need a little nudge to get their creative juices flowing.
