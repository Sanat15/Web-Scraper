Rating: 5/5

I remember why I loved this series so much now! 
I didn't enjoy the first book when I reread it a couple of weeks ago, but this installment is absolutely fabulous. Molly's up against Primo Cell, who has hypnotised most of the stars in Hollywood to advertise his products. He's a multi-multi-billionaire with his eyes on a much bigger prize: he wants to become President of the United States and take over the world.
Molly, Rocky and Petula team up to try and take him down, balking when they confront him and he proudly admits he's a murderer, before trying to put them all out of action. Eek! 
If you're looking for a fast-paced children's read that's not-so-light-hearted, this is the book for you. You don't even really need to read the first installment, as all of the events of the last novel are succinctly recapped throughout the story, meaning this would work beautifully as a standalone if you weren't willing to commit to a series. 
Well, at least until THAT cliffhanger, which makes it impossible to resist reading book three (which I'm hoping to get to next week!).
