Rating: 3/5

My favorite aspect of this biography is how it explained the 3 crucial compromises Clay architected throughout his life. For most part, the book is dense and tend to get lost in details, but when describing the compromises, it was easy to understand and conveyed the importance of Clay's roles in them effectively. But I feel like this book spends too much volumes talking about how people reacted to Clay's tours and speeches, which get repetitive sometimes, and the pacing was hard to get through.
