Rating: 4/5

An odd, moralizing, 17th century poetic romp through the English countryside that had made cleaning out my tackle box between reading sessions and looking at trout stream maps in nearby Decorah. A pretty cruel read for December in Iowa, but a lovely book that brought me to a hyper-bucolic time and place, as well as back to many memories I have of fishing with friends and family, and the contemplative joy of fishing alone (though alone be not the feeling).
