Rating: 4/5

If you need convincing that the frontal lobe on a teenager is not mature at the age of 17, then I suggest you read this book. Every decision these boys made was really really bad, especially our MC Duncan. Duncan was dealing with some PTSD after he was unable to save a young girl from drowning yeas ago. Based on this incident and that good ole frontal lobe telling him to do dumb shit, our boy almost got himself unalived. Don't get me wrong, I really enjoyed the book. It was written very well. A well thought out plot, lots of teenage boy humor (which I find funny because deep down I am a teenage boy), some fantastic characters, and believable situations. But you will find yourself slapping your forehead and saying, "OMG you dumb-ass, what are you thinking?"
