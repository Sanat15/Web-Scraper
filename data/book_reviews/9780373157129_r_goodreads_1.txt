Rating: 1/5

I feel sorry for the girl here. She’s such an imposter that if it weren’t for her portrayal as a sweet, innocent heroine, she could be a clone of Cinderella’s stepsisters trying too hard to catch the prince’s eye. 

At first, her attempt to capture his interest with a quick make-over was understandable. But when she joined his gym to meet him, bought ticket to the symphony, enrolled in art appreciation class, checked herself in an expensive hotel, rented a luxury car -- while neglecting her business and means of livelihood --, she appeared no better than a desperate stalker and a pathetic social climber. Even with the comical jinxes like the tennis accident and contretemps at the art gallery, it became embarrassing clear that she had  overreached. How in the world could she ever explain her deception? She must know that she was digging a hole the more she keeps pretending. 

The story line is cute and humorous. But with the premise built on make-believe (yeah, I understand we're talking about a romance novel here, not the making of an atomic bomb...), a happily-ever-after for the H/h is simply not gonna happen. I would have preferred it if her sham had been uncovered midway and then for rest of the story, they were getting to know their genuine selves.
