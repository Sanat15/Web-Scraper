Rating: 2/5

2.5 stars. Read this for an education class in school. A good, though surface level, summary of the history of the American education system, its flaws, and the policies put in place to fix it. Awfully pessimistic at times and not well-organized or well-written. Serves its purpose to illustrate American educational history but I would not recommend it as a means of encouraging prospective teachers due to its lack of encouragement / overall pessimism.
