Rating: 5/5

•	This Spanish Fairy Tale is about a young Princess who sets off to save a prince far away from a sleeping curse. She must travel far…farther…. farthest away from her home to reach the sleeping prince. She will know she is there when she wears out a pair of Iron Shoes specially made for the journey. With the help of the East Wind, the West Wind and the North Wind she is sure to make it to the prince in time to save time. Reminiscent of Snow White and Sleeping Beauty this beautifully Illustrated book is a breath of fresh air. Princess Florecita is a strong and brave heroine who is sure to inspire. 
•	1-4 grade 
•	I would use this book in a second grade English classroom.
•	Individual students who may benefit from this book include students who enjoy fairy tales, princesses or students that enjoy adventures. 
•	Small groups could use this book to create a Venn diagram that compares and contrasts between this book and sleeping beauty. 
•	I would read this book aloud to my students then have students write their own variation of the story. What would they change? How much would have to stay the same in order for it to be a different version of the same story? 
•	Students that enjoy this book should try reading sleeping beauty.
•	This book is available as an e-book.
