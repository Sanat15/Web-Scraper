Rating: 3/5

Some books don't stand up to rereading. This is one of them.

The characters are mostly a bit ridiculous (including Poirot's faithful sidekick, Hastings) &amp; too many of them are very shady.

There are too many coincidences.

The crimes are too unlikely.

Lastly not  one but two criminals are effectively let off the hook?

Poor show Christie - dashed poor show!
