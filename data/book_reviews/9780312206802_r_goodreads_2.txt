Rating: 2/5

Well, I've been an Ethan Mordden fan for a long time, but for me, he misses the mark with this one. I say "for me" because Mordden is an opera aficionado, and I certainly am not. 
"The Venice Adriana" is all about the ultimate opera diva (Maria Callas?) who lives in Venice, and a young, gay man's commission to write her biography. There are many opera references that meant nothing to me. But if you're an opera lover, they may simply enhance the story for you. This is a novel for a niche audience. You will have to judge for yourself whether you are in the niche. 
Happy reading!
