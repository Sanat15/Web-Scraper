Rating: 4/5

I've enjoyed many of Shakespeare's tragic plays but besides "Midsummer Night's Dream", "The Merry Wives of Windsor" and "The Merchant of Venice" , I haven't been a fan of his comedies. I did like the Tempest but was surprised how there was a sense of abruptness... like the interrupted wedding masque and blessing, Caliban's oath to do good, Prospero's sudden change of heart... it seemed kind of like after all this buildup there was a rush to an end.
