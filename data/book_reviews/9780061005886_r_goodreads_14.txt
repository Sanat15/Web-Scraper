Rating: 3/5

This is my first foray into Zane Grey's western world. I love the western genre but I also want/expect my historical books to be historically accurate. We all love a high noon gunfight, but rationally we know those weren't really a thing. But conning people out of their possessions was a thing. Poor Tappan is suckered by a husband and wife pair who pretend to be brother and sister. All in the background of the story is the relationship Tappan has with his remarkable burro Jennet. Jennet follows him patiently and persistently even when Tappan makes poor choices. 

The writing of Zane Grey is complicated and lyrical for a western. I've downloaded others and will give them a try.
