Rating: 5/5

Excellent daily devotional, with Kay's encouraging admonishments &amp; knowledge of Biblical context. Each month, based on a theme, is broken down into daily topics supporting that theme. Some topics carrying over for several days to completely finish the lesson she's trying to teach. I particularly liked the section on covenants - very informative! But this devotional would be nothing without a Biblical influence, &amp; it has just that. Each day includes the Biblical verses she is referring to, &amp; the encouragement to go read those verses. She also encourages the reader to record their thoughts on God, &amp; God's thoughts on them. 

Now I'm off to read Kay's next daily devotional! Thanks to Pam for lending me these books for a whole year!
