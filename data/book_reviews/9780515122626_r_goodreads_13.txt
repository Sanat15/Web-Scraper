Rating: 5/5

Time Travel romance has always been one of my favorites...
What would YOU do if you were tossed back in time?!?!?
How would you handle being a shipmate and everyone thinking you were a boy?
But of course, the Captain sees immediately this is a young woman and decides to help her in her quest...

Adventures ensue and of course they fall in love... but what happens when it's time for her to return to her time??? A surprising turn of events.
