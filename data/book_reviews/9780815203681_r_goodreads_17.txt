Rating: 4/5

While I don't share the same life views as the main character, it moved me seeing the person of Frank Algernon Cowperwood develop through life and honestly make himself remarkable as a hard working financier . Aside from his immoral behaviour with women by being disloyal(a fact that disappointed me heavely) , i could not help but appreciate his attitude towards the harsh situations in life, how even in the face of death he did not loose himself and lived till the very end making something out of it, even though in the end the final achievement was not his financial fortune , since after his death it all dissolved quickly, that all your work that you might spend a life time on might die with you, therefore I believe a part of one's purpose in life is to hope to inspire someone, to do something that contributes to another in life so that you are not forgotten.
