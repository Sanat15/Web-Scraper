Rating: 3/5

Not exactly a warts and all exposé of the TV industry but if you're a fan of Mister Ed, and why wouldn't you be, then it's a lovely cosy read with a bit of background fluff about making the show followed by a general memoir about how Young made it in showbiz. 

He started out writing radio comedy sketches in the 40s and he channels that in the writing - there's a general cheesy after dinner speech tone to all the anecdotes and you do sometimes wish he'd tone it down a bit. Overall though he just comes across as a really amiable guy who's chuffed to bits that he snagged a showbiz career and got to spend five years working with a horse.
