Rating: 4/5

For the first Green Arrow Arc that separates itself from the “I died and need to reclaim my identity” arcs, this is very good. 

A corporation in town has used immigrants from Africa to build a new commercial 15 block complex. The catch, the immigrants who were housed and paid bare minimum now are now fired and being deported back home. The ex employees protest and dismember the construction sight for revenge until Green Arrow and Black Lightning arrive. 

I enjoyed reading about Black Lightning and his niece. We don’t get to see the superhero’s team up, but we get to see Oliver and Joana…entangle (sex that’s actually written for a reason, eh hem *Kevin Smith*). I liked reading about a New Romantic fling that wasn’t subjected to him and Diana or him and Mia (which is still very gross). 

The story does get strange in the supernatural monsters and dragons kind of way for an element of the story. Not a fan of that merging with emerald archer stories, but it was fine for the first volume so I gave this a chance. The monsters don’t take away from the incredible story. 

This volume introduces a new villain, Constantine Dracon, aka Straight Shooter. A great character who I hope we can see in the future. Maybe flesh out a little more than ‘faster and stronger’ than the Green Arrow…slightly. 

I think the way they ended this arc was phenomenal. Giving Connor Hawke a spotlight to shine and Mia some more credit than what’s given to her. The one thing that did bother me is how Joana is treated. She was a great addition to the story and didn’t need to end the way she did. Black Lightning and Green Arrow didn’t seem to care about how she left so this doesn’t seem like a plot point that will carry on. Only the cheating..,
