Rating: 5/5

Grandad Bill's Song is a touching story about a boy learning the different emotions people around him feel when dealing with the loss of a loved one. The book is filled with painted pictures of activities the family use to cherish with Grandad Bill. As the reader scrolls through the small amount of verbiage on each page, a picture of the day Grandad Bill becomes visible, and also comforting. A great book for young readers, and an aesthetic one, as well.
