Rating: 4/5

Strong collection. A lot of period pieces and stories about marriage. Very few duds.

My favorites:

-The one with the roommates and the mysterious boyfriend by Jhumpa Lahiri

-The one with the cow and the pathetic husband by Melissa Hardy

-The one with the couple how's been separated for seven years by Edwidge Danticat.

-The one about memory in post-war Japan by Mary Yukari Waters

-The one with the brother in the coma and superheroes and God by Akhil Sharma
