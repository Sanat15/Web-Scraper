Rating: 4/5

This is a collection of 10 short ghost stories, all set around the Christmas period. I don't think there was one I didn't like, but some were better than others.

"Longship" was probably one of my favourites, and the first in the collection. Helen Dunmore''s "The Knock at the Door" was suitably scary and moralistic for a Xmas tale, while "The Ghost of Christmas Shopping" was kind of sad. "The Devil's Dozen" was also very, very good and I loved the long poem "Babushka" - I've never heard the tale told in such a way.

All in all, a great way to entertain yourself, especially around the Christmas tree with the cold snow blowing outside!!
