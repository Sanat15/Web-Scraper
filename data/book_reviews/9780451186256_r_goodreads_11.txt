Rating: 4/5

I found this story in a Barnes and Noble Nook collection Regency Rakes Trilogy Omnibus containing The Proper Companion, A Change of Heart, and An Affair of Honor by Candice Hern

The story
Mary is 29 and “on the shelf” but she seems to cheerfully accept her position in society. But Mary has a sad history that she hides under her cheerful smile so well that no one would suspect the pain that she has endured. When Mary sees Jack looking over the latest debs and trying unsuccessfully to find a bride, Mary offers to help him. Jack is has lost his brothers and his nephew in a terrible accident and now he finds he has suddenly inherited a title and a mountain of debt. Jack needs a bride, a wealthy bride, to help him continue the family line and save the family from the debts. Jack agrees to let Mary help him find a bride but omits his need for funds when describing what his future bride. Jack finds Mary to be so friendly and easy to talk to. He is so at ease in her company that he overlooks Mary as a potential bride, until someone tells Jack that Mary has money. Jack uses his rakish ways to convince Mary to be his bride, but of course, he is not really the rake in his heart that he seems to be. 
My Two Cents
Jack is so easy to hate and Mary is so easy to love. Jake starts to take shape as more than a fortune hunting rake that he becomes loveable. Anticipating how Mary would find out about Jack’s original motives was just the right amount of tension. I don’t know exactly what it was about this story that made it so hard to put down. I am usually annoyed by characters that can’t admit their feelings, but I didn’t feel that way with this book. I guess the reasons Jack and Mary each had for keeping their feeling to themselves seemed more real than I seem to read in most books. I like the little side romance of Olivia and Edward also- not too much written with that, but just enough that my imagination took off.
