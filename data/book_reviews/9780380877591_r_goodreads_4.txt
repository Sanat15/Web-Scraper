Rating: 5/5

This is the 2nd book in the series and continues from where the first book ends. Just as good as the first if not better with plenty of angst, sadness, romance and action to keep you hooked. The only thing I wish was a 3rd book to follow as there is never really any closure. Her brother remains lost and they still had to get the baby from the couple that have claimed him plus they themselves need to settle down, get married and find their HEA home. A pity, would have loved to see what the final outcome would be in a 3rd book.
