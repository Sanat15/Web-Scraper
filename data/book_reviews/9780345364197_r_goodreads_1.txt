Rating: 4/5

I had to request this series though my local library as an Inter-Library Loan (ILL) &amp; when this one arrived before Book #5 I the series, I decided to read it out of order so as not to leave myself open to a hefty overdue fine. 

Dang. And so it ends. It might be nice if someone were to pick up where Pedneau left off, but his settings of rural West Virginia &amp; Charleston, SC make it tough to continue the string. It would be nice to think that Whit &amp; Anna relocate to Charleston as Whit would like, but I see him moving, them continuing a long-distance relationship &amp; things finally petering out as such relationships usually do. At least, it ended on an upbeat note. I am glad that I took the time to get acquainted with them.
