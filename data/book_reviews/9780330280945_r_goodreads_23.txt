Rating: 5/5

I feel very seen by this recommendation from a friend (thanks Neil!). It’s like a mystical version of block-time ontology in which we are “slaves of that which looks out through our eyes,” and history is not becoming but being continually retroactively patterned by an interior witness that both belongs to, and destabilizes, the apparent spatial unity of time. 
So much more to research and reflect about this book! 
(Fucked up at times too for sure)
