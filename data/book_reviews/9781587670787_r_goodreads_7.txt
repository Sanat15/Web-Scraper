Rating: 5/5

This right here is my favorite Laymon short story. Laymon's strength is generally in his long-form fiction, and many of his short stories I categorize as "OK". Not terrible, but not anything to get excited about.

"Madman Stan" is an exception. This is a story which wouldn't be out of place in a "Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark"-type anthology, about two kids who wind up with one mean babysitter who scares the daylights out of them with a bedtime story about a maniac who wanders the town going from house to house, testing to see who left the door unlocked, and doing terrible things to the people he finds inside.

It has a great build up, and the ending delivers a phenomenal gut-punching twist. Even people who dislike horror in general (or Laymon's brand of it in particular) can enjoy Madman Stan.
