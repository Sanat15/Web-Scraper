Rating: 3/5

JACK ON THE BOX refers to Jack Henley, a nobleman who is given a set down by his father for extensive gambling, and is forced to drive a mail coach. In other words, he has to prove himself before he can be redeemed in his father's eyes. He drives the coach from his box and, hence, the title. This in itself was refreshing. 

Several weeks later, after learning the ropes, he is put in a shaky position in order to help a young lady. He gives her a ride on the sly and puts his job on the line. Things work out for the mysterious Cecily Wolverton but not before Jack becomes intrigued by this gentle person.

Some light suspense with a small dose of humor emerges from their relationship. A crotchety but loving grandfather, a shifty cousin and a well-meaning father are some of the secondary characters. This clean regency romance is a light piece of action between the working class and nobility. It deserves a solid 3 stars because I liked it.
