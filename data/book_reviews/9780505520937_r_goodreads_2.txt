Rating: *rolls eyes* / 5

The heroine finds herself really lacking braincells in this one.

That's the most honest thing I can say.

The sad part is that the setup of the story was pretty good.

But alas, then we entered the world of stupid people, and it became unbearable past the 100 page mark.

And I won't read books about stupid people.
_________________

Basically, after Brenda's family is killed, she runs away to the wilderness. She has no concrete plan, no sense of direction, nothing except her beauty. When she meets Striped Eagle, they fall in love (insta-love, of course), and even spend the night together. But then Brenda, still with vengeance in her heart, decides to run away from Striped Eagle rather than tell him the story behind her family's murder by an evil fort captain.

Anyway, Brenda, again with no skills in real life whatsoever, is captured by Frenchmen fur traders, who want to sell her for furs. Striped Eagle ends up buying her for furs, but this idiot (i.e. Brenda) still has vengeance in her heart and escapes again--but not before another night of sex first, of course.

If this is the pattern to anticipate for the rest of the story, I'm glad I DNFed it there and didn't waste more of my time.

Note to writers: if you make your heroines stupid, expect people to hate your stories. END.
