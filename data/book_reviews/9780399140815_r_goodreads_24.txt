Rating: 5/5

A really nice story with a good flow. Tubby loses a friend, Potter and has to find out why. He is recruited as a lawyer for the casino and everything seems to be going his way, then it's not. Threats to his family and someone else who needs revenge against the killer of their brother pull him into a dangerous situation but unexpected help saves his skin and he comes out with his virtue intact, even a bit righteous when he helps Save the Levee with his casino windfall.
