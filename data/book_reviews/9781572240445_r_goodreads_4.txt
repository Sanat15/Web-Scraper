4/5

Power of Focusing is a simple, easy-to-read book that explores exactly what the title promises: the power of focusing. It was recommended to me by my therapist, and as I read it, I realised it describes the same technique she uses in our sessions. The book helps you develop a deeper awareness of how your body feels, pay closer attention to what it needs, and learn to accept negative emotions while also recognising and appreciating positive ones - understanding how each shows up physically in the body. Despite being written in the 1990s, it has aged remarkably well. It’s a great introduction to a mindfulness-based approach, whether you’re struggling or simply curious, offering reassurance and confidence in understanding your emotions through your body.
