Rating: 2/5

It was immediately clear to me, even before I looked at the author photo, that a man wrote this. So much of this seemed to be the way a lot of men view women. 

The plot was OK, I suppose, albeit a little weak. A serviceably interesting concept. The Native American-esque names were a clever device, but there were so many I had trouble keeping them all straight after awhile. 

And, in my typical measure of whether a book was good or not, it seemed to drag on way.too.long. Several times I found myself asking, "Is this over YET?" The ending felt a little too neat and tidy for me. And the last line was pure crap.

I'd recommend this as a beach/plane read only, and don't feel bad if you want to abandon it halfway through. You aren't alone.
