Rating: 5/5

Exciting fast paced legal thriller.

This is a great book for anyone who appreciates a good legal thriller or a character driven mystery that isn't easily guessed. This book actually is like getting two mysteries for the price of one. It's fascinating the way the author parallels the prosecutor's (main character) involvement in getting a conviction in a high profile murder case alongside her belief in her son's innocence when he is the defendant in an equally high profile case being prosecuted by her co-workers. Talk about conflict of interest...the main character is stretched pretty thin and pulled in many directions. All this makes for a fast and furious plot that fully delivers on its early promise. The murders themselves are well done and will keep you guessing as to who the guilty parties are until the big reveal. You'll enjoy the ride from start to finish. It's a fun book to read
