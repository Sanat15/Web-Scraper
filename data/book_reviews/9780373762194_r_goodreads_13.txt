Rating: 5/5

I loved everything about this book, especially how the Stardust Cowboy came about and how little Jake believed and even saw proof he was the real deal. Loved how we got let in on how Riley, Dori and Jake were thinking and reacting this book is just the best. And I got a little peek at the next book in the series and cannot wait for it.
I choose to read and review an advance reader’s copy of this book.
