4.5 stars - 
  ”Speak softly, you slut; you're shaking up my whole brain” 
  act 2, scene 1 of The Imaginary Invalid 

Soooooo outrageously goofy, sharp-witted, and FUNNY…god, I can only imagine how much better they’d be to see performed live. Molière was a theatrical &amp; comic genius, and while I think every single one of these plays is wildly entertaining, my particular favorites are The Misanthrope (duh), The Learned Women and perhaps my favorite of all, The Imaginary Invalid (which, fun fact, had its stage debut on my bday, Feb 10th!!! Less fun fact, Molière died after starring in the 4th performance of it, only a week later!!! 😭) Toinette really steals the show, tbh, and if I was ever a stage actress (lmao), that would definitely be my dream role. Long live the theatre!!!! 🎭
