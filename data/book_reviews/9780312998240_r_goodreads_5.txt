Rating: 2/5

I left this book feeling like I had more questions than answers and also wondering why I kind of wasted my time. This book was off to a good start for me- we got to know some background on the characters, the suspense was building. And then, we got way too much fluff.

There was too much detail of things that did not need detail which slowed the story down to me. I felt like this was supposed to be a story about suspense and where was it? When it did happen, it felt rushed and lackluster.

I was not a fan of any of the characters as they seemed to lack depth and were rushed. Things also seemed to jump big time and I found myself just more confused. Lots of skimming to round out this novel.
