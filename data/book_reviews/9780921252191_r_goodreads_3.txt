Rating: 5/5

118 beautiful poems that resonate for different reasons, and are in most cases thought provoking, for different reasons: some are about love, others about injustice, and some are about death. People who appreciate poetry know that it is never the same, that Emily Dickinson's work is as different from Walt Whitman's as you can get, and that Charles Baudelaire's work is as different from Pablo Neruda's as is possible, and not only because they are from different backgrounds, cultures, etc. (though that is a part of it) but because good poetry is like good literature in that each piece is unique, and when it is done really well, it can be unforgettable. That's what I believe Alighieri's work is: unforgettable. Highly recommend to all poetry lovers!!!
