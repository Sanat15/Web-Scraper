Rating: 5/5

4.5 stars. Really quite entertaining book.

The heroine is very likeable right off the bat. The hero's internal arc is really good. Normally I don't like stories where one person is still in love with someone else, but this one worked very well. 

There was a weird part where the story jumped 8 or 9 months and the entire story in London is skipped over which seemed disjointed but the ending declaration of love was nice. 

A good story I'd recommend to any Regency lover.
