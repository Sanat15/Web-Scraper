Rating: 4/5

I'm hesitant to rate this or even record that I read it because the Israeli-Palestinian conflict is so complicated and there are real people on both sides. It's even more tricky theologically for Christians, who have Arab Christian brothers and sisters and yet also are related to the Jews somehow (that's a whole can of worms itself). The history of how the Church has treated Jews is horrific, and I want to be a part of the recent change in Christian attitudes toward the Jews (see Nostra Aetate). And yet we must listen to our Palestinian family, too, the first Christians. This is a good glimpse into one Palestinian community (I'd like to see what he thinks about things today, if anything has changed, one way or the other)—and, more importantly, a glimpse into the life an incredibly faithful, loving, Christ-like man of God.
