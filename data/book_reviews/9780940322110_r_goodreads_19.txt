Rating: 1/5

In my opinion, this is an account of animal abuse, and it made me angry. Even taking into account that Tulip's sixteen year life span began in the mid-1940's when attitudes about the care and treatment of animals may have been different, I see Ackerley as needlessly and stubbornly cruel, the omniscient god who controls Tulip's fate. He claims unbounded love for his dog, and I believe him; parents who abuse their children also love them. I looked over other Goodreads reviews, and while many, perhaps most, are very positive, I found one from 2015 by Justin Evans which closely mirrors my thoughts and reactions.
