Rating: 3/5

My facebook review:

Beneath Passion's Skies was actually two interesting novels and the outline of a third, unfortunately smushed up into one big mess with no sense of pacing or dramatic tension. The main couple is half-breed gunslinger Blade Masters, and "Angela, nicknamed Angel because of her angelic appearance with her pale gold hair and perfect features." The beta couple gets fake married to travel the Oregon Trail, which everybody knows you can't complete in the span of a romance novel even if you travel at a grueling pace, and they travel under the assumed names Sarah and Steve Johnson*, which was weird for me. The third couple are the standard romance novel characters with the standard courtship, but then he ends up murdering her for her money. That was different.

*My father's name.
-Mercury
