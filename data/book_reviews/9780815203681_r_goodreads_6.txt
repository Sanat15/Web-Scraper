Rating: 3/5

The last book in Dreiser's Trilogy of Desire tells of the waning days of robber baron Frank Cowperwood, again delving into his shady business dealings and equally shady romantic dealings. Not as vigorous or fresh as the first two installments, or maybe I just felt that way because Cowperwood's chief paramour Berenice didn't appeal to me at all. There are some wonderful sections, though, including Berenice's sojourn in India.
