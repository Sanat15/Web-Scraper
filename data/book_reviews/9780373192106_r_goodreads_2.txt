Rating: 2/5

I was going to cut her some slack and give her 3 stars because the story itself is actually kinda good, save for a few details. Plus the book was short and if you’re going to write a bad book, it better be short. At lease Diana respects that. But the ending was absolute GARBAGE. I would’ve liked it much better if the main couple just didn’t end up together. It was actually so dumb and not romantic at all. The writing in the book was also exhausting every other sentence it’s “she said haughtily, he asked curiously, she said helpfully”. It was like an AI wrote it. And don’t even get me started on the bullshit 2 second eye roll of a climax. I’d also like to add something that is not this authors fault alone , but I am so so tired of reading books in which the main character is an author or journalist. It’s every other book. I get that’s literally your personal background and “write what you know” but holy god please dig deeper, find anything else. So many thumbs down. Sorry Diana.
