Rating: 5/5

Loved it!

This is the 2nd Regency Romance I have read by this author.
Hers are like the Regency books I remember from years ago.
Most of those I have read lately, are just contemporary romances, in period costumes. These are so much more.
True to the language, the situations, and customs of the times; I enjoyed them both, immensely. Well done.
