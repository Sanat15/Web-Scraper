Rating: 3/5

51 Sycamore Lane or A Spy in the Neighborhood is on my favorites list because the characters are adventurous in investigating their new neighbor who they think is a spy. I can imagine the children getting sucked in to this mystery. Considered fiction, it contains the element that the setting is in real place but has a fictitious city. Designed for middle grades, it could be read individually.

In the reading classroom, teachers could use this to teach fluency, by having the readers read the dialogue read as if someone was truly speaking out loud. It also offers learners a wider vocabulary.
