Rating: 5/5

Two days ago I was reading a book is called “I am David” by Anne Holm. Yes, there’s a movie based on the book. Ben Tibber who is the role of David as the main character in the film. I’ve enjoyed reading the book which is very unusual and interesting. It is a heartwarming story. Holm wrote an unusual adventure of a 12 year old, David, who has grown up in a prison camp that he escapes from a communist death camp in 1952 in Bulgaria. He listens to the story of Johannes, and gets the help of one of the hated prison guards. David, himself question and wonders the experience about of the world outside and was self encouraged to explore his journey to find clues way home to Denmark. Through his journey he had to learn many different concepts such as languages, belonging, identity, goodness, evil, resilience, education and freedom. As a result, did he make his way home? You can decide.
