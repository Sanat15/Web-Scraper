Rating: 4/5

This story is well worth the read. I didn't read the book the way that a book should be read, as I started it a few months ago,and then got distracted for other events. Today at about mid-point in the book, I picked it up and read it to the end, and was happy that I did. This book takes a time in history, 1941, and follows the story of a wide variety of personalities as they band together to escape the Japanese under some of the most harrowing events in history. There is a great deal of sadness in the story but also a tremendous determination to survive. The author did an amazing job of describing the geographical areas covered in the story as well as describing the personalities of the people involved - some who made it to the end and some who didn't.
