Rating: 4/5

Of all of Wilkie Collins’s books, The Dead Secret, ranks just behind The Moonstone and The Woman in White. There is more focus on fiction than mystery as compared to the other two. Nevertheless, it is a satisfying read but one must be prepared for a slower pace and judge the secret by nineteenth century standards. 

Up front the secret is put into play, but its nature is only hinted at. The buildup occurs over much of the book as the characters are developed, thus suspense gathers slowly. The characterization held my attention throughout as did the gothic atmosphere. 

I thought I had the plot worked out, but I did not see that twist coming! That ramped my rating up a star immediately and made the journey through the story even more gripping. 

My advice is to avoid any reviews that reveal too much about the nature of the secret. The Dead Secret is a story that can be read at leisure.
