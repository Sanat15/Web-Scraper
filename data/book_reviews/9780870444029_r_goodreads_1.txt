Rating: 3/5

This book contains several pictures of different animal groups and some commentary regarding their behavior. Since it is aimed at young readers you won't find a lot of information, just one or two facts about each group. Although I'll say that I did learn something new. Apparently, red ants capture the pupae of other ant species (F. fusca) to keep as slaves
