Rating: 5/5

Another gift from my fiancée for valentine's day. This is a small pocket sized, easy to read, easy to digest, guide to life. The book is replete with quotes by famous anglers, sportsmen, and personalities about enjoying fishing,learning, lures, patience, conservation, silence, humility, optimism, and gratitude. My favorite quote from the book is from Washington Irving (the author of Rip Van Winkle): "There is certainly something in fishing that tends to produce a gentleness of spirit and a pure sincerity of mind".
