Rating: 3/5

It seems this audio book might have much potential. However, it is a bit disappointing. You know about the main villain already half way through the plot (and this is not intended) and you don't get an answer about the main crux of the whole story. It is even not really brutal, as the scenario might suggest. well, there are some short cruel descriptions of some torture methods, but not much of it.

So after all, this is only a mediocre thriller that tries to catch the same point of view of the tv series dexter, but fails with this attempt.
