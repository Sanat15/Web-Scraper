Rating: 2/5

When I listened to 84 Charing Cross Road, I became an immediate fan of Helene Hanff. Her snarky wit, mixed with warmth made the letters so interesting. Having said this, I was not so impressed by this book. It was about her success, or lack thereof, as a writer of plays. The first half of the book was so very boring. There were little spots where the Helene I am familiar with shone through, but not enough. At about the halfway, the book got a LITTLE more interesting. My overall experience was one of disappointment.
