Rating: 5/5

It is important for me to impress upon you that this book is a masterpiece, laser targeted at me with its meticulously descriptive locales, the carefully thought out customs of the peoples it portrays, the almost meditative exploration of being in a place so alien to you and yet slowly becoming tethered to it through human connection. And then anytime it pushes the pedal and picks up the plot it does so with the weight of a cast and a world so confidently and well constructed every narrative turn hits like a goddamn truck.

I started this book expecting a serviceable follow up to The Steerswoman (which I enjoyed), but I got something that shredded those expectations in the best possible way. This is a new favorite for sure, I can't wait to read the next entry in the series.
