Rating: 5/5

I first read this in a college pedagogy class that I audited twelve years ago with little intent of ever becoming a teacher; I think more of it stuck with me than I realized, revisiting it now at the beginning of teaching year ten and in a specifically Charlotte Mason context (!). Happy to enthusiastically endorse so much of what Macaulay advocates here and hard not to say that that her (and Mason's) fundamental assumptions about children and how they learn seem like the only right places to begin.
