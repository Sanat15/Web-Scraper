Rating: 4/5

As per the title, we know that Lord Edgware dies- murdered!! The most obvious suspect is his wife, Lady Edgware. She was seen by both the butler and the secretary entering the house. But she has an iron clad alibi- she was dining with friends. So if not her, whodunnit? Hercule Poirot’s “ little grey cells” have to work overtime to get to the bottom of this one. It is fascinating how Dame Christie’s mind works. I don’t know how she does it, but she always manages to weave an intriguing scenario- which is wholly believable!! Of course, my little grey cells are never up to the task. Stumped, again! 

Another outstanding narration by Hugh Fraser.

Published: 1933
