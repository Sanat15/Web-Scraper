Rating: 4/5

4 stars, down from 4.5 for repeated ‘koala bear’. 
This book is the lovechild of a cosy, 1970s English village story and an almost slapstick murder novel. The titular girls live and work together, then (no spoiler) events brings their son into being - but misunderstandings, overreactions, and an odd acceptance by the housekeeper of events make for a read where you never quite know where it’s going to go in the next chapter. Thrilling, but not page turning, cute, but not dull, this is one of the few books I have trouble categorising. 
But - they’re not bears, they’re marsupials.
