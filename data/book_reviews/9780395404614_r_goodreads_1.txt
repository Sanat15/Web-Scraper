My high school social sciences teacher (U.S. History in junior year, Government and Econ senior year) back in the early 1990s, Mr. Mike Gallagher, had a copy of this book on his shelf, and I, along with several of my classmates, found it hilarious.

Fast-forward to the present day, 2018, 25 years after my high school graduation (geez, where does the time go?!?!), and it's still hilarious.
