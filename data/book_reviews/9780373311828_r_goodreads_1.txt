Rating: 2/5

Viola married Richard after a short acquaintance. Right after they're married, Richard overhears Viola speaking to her aunt about the marriage and abandons her for one year. Viola, a green country girl, is able to overcome the situation with the help of the Earl's sister and friends and becomes accepted by the ton. The main plot is what happens after Richard returns.

I've not much to say about it. If you love a book that starts with a misunderstanding that could have been resolved with a conversation and continues with the same misunderstand until the last couple of pages, then you'll love this. I could not stand the two main characters misinterpreting each other every single time they came together because of this plot point. The two stars are only because I actually finished the book.
