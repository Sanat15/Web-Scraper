Rating: 5/5

Dylan and Whitney are best friends and that is so important to both of them but after they have a one impulsive night together they both decided to put it behind them and carry on being just friends only for Whitney to find out that she is pregnant and she doesn't know what to do as Dylan has a pre arranged marriage but she knows that she has to tell him. Dylan and Whitney have great chemistry but there friendship means so much to the both of them so what is Whitney going to do and how will Dylan react to her news. Wow I love this book so much and Dylan and Whitney are so great together and this book is a must read as it is so well written by Mindy Neff who is a brilliant author and writer.
