Rating: 5/5

This is a delightful story about Hanukkah. It tells, without a ton of details, the celebration of Hanukkah, with a cute kitten and puppy thrown in for fun. And it shares the spirit, being thankful for what we have, even when it doesn't seem to be enough. When we share what we have, God gives us miracles so that we have enough. Also shares a simple recipe for latkes, as well as how (and why) to play the dreidel game

A favorite for the Hanukkah season, I was a bit late reading it this year
