Rating: 2/5

I was very disappointed in this book. I love Edward Abbey's writing, and was looking forward to a glimpse into his mind -- how he came to write The Monkey Wrench Gang, what it was like to be in Arches Natl. Monument, what prompted him to write Desert Solitaire. Etc.

But no. This book is a series of excerpts from his journals, and are mainly about how his marriages failed, how he met the next wife, etc. Perhaps I'm an unusual fan, but I don't care much about authors or artists personal life -- I care about the music, or the book, and the act of creation.

If there was any of that in this book, it was minuscule, and I missed it.
