Rating: 3/5

Dinosaur Alphabet Book
Author: Patricia Whitehead
Reading Level: 4-7

Whitehead, Patricia (1985) Dinosaur Alphabet Book Mahwah, New Jersey: Troll Associates

This book is exactly what what the title suggests: an alphabet book with dinosaurs. Each letter corresponds with a dinosaur, facets of nature, and instincts.

The book is simple, written simply, and would likely captivate young readers with its depiction of dinosaurs. Some of the dinosaur names would be hard to pronounce for a young child; however, that doesn't take away from this being a good book.

Overall, I found it enjoyable. It doesn't necessarily stick out from any other ABC's book.
