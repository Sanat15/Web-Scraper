Rating: 5/5

5 stars &amp; 5/10 hearts. This book is not as funny as the first one, but it’s still quiet humorous. Belinda and Irene are hilarious! xD I love how Blyton weaves excellent humour, good morals, and interesting stories in this series. I don’t agree with everything, and there’s a bit more language than the first book (still mild—gosh, golly, blow) but it’s very good too.
