Rating: 4/5

First published in 1974, 'A Hole in Space' is a collection of 9 short sf stories and once essay - the stories were originally published over the years 1968-1974. Some of the stories have been anthologised in other collections, so a fan of Larry Niven may find themself a little short-changed, but the tales are all very good and worth a second read anyway. Enjoy.
