Rating: 5/5

I'm not usually drawn to military history, but the few times I've delved into it, I've been struck by the vivid portrayal of management, mission support, and strategic insights. This particular book is an exceptionally well-researched and relatively unbiased account of a devastating battle. It skillfully illustrates the critical roles of technology, training, arrogance, specialization, intelligent disobedience, logistics, succession planning, morale, and management in military contexts. This military example with a bit of abstraction and imagination has parallels to my everyday experiences.
