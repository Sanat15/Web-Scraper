Rating: 2/5

Logan's Bride by Elizabeth August felt too confusing to me. It was all over the place.
Katrina is a cop with shady family connections, her entire paternal side were enforcers for the mob, but since the age of 17 when her father and brother were killed she has made it a mission to rise above her family, but getting people to trust her is tough and now that her aunt, who kept the books for the mob has gone on the run all eyes are on her including her ex-godfather the mob boss. She didn't have any contact with her aunt but her aunt called her and she goes to the mob compound on her behalf. All this is watched by the FBI and Agent Boyd.

Boyd is deeply suspicious of people after his first partner stabbed him and he spends most of the book deeply suspicious of Katrina and lusting after her.
After that he falls for her and tries to convince her to marry him. He tags along with Katrina in protecting her aunt and after she gives the slip to him and Katrina is kidnapped, he rescues her. Katrina tries to run away from Boyd, saying your family won't like me, I will be a liability for your job. Honestly, I didn't care a bit. The book was totally bland for me. The reason the characters.
