Rating: 3/5

Isn't it weird how the universe works sometimes? I needed a graphic novel to complete a reading prompt but I don't have any; imagine my surprise one Monday evening when I found a battered copy of this just lying on my bed - turns out that a friend of bae loan it to him to read...

Ai Yori Aoshi follows the story of Aoi-chan, who has trained to be a wife to Kaoru-sama, since they met when they were like six years old, and she has left her family to go him and become his wife. They both come from powerful families which bring a conflict or two to the story; expect intrigue, drama and romance.

Since this is my first manga, I won't be rushing to buy this, even if I rather liked it.
