Rating: 4/5

Watchers remains my favorite Dean Koontz book, though I admit to not reading his work for the last decade or so. 

I see the seeds here of the things that have turned me off of Koontz-the formulaic good guy, bad guy, GREAT dog, but when this was written it was all fresh and new to me. It was only the repetition of this formula that eventually turned me off of Koontz for good.

Even though this is a bit dated now, it was still fun to listen to, and Edoardo Ballerini is an absolutely terrific narrator. 

Watchers inspired in me a little bit of nostalgia- when computers were first coming into American lives and cell phones did not yet exist. The idea of not being instantly available to people via call or text seems so ancient now, but at the same time I miss it. 

Anyway, this re-read was fun and it still held my interest, so my original rating of 4/5 stars stands.
