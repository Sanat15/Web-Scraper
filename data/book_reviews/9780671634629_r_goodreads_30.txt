Rating: 4/5

A brilliantly written descent into complete oblivion and a fully fleshed out (please forgive the pun which you'll get when you read it) and slow progression into evil. Disturbing and captivating enough to be hard to put down in spite of wanting to do so. I kept reading because I hoped that something would change for Beatrice. 
And yes, a powerful, societal message about the way the world works that is relevant to this day.
