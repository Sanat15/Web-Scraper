Rating: 4/5

I found this book very detailed. I realy could feel like I was in the stands at the ball game watching what happened years ago. With specific details and anomanopia's it was very easy to see what Matt Christopher envisioned.

In this book it goes from player to player and tells amazing stories. One story, which is my favorite, is of a 14 year old major league pitcher Jim Abbott. That would be me in a year!!! Although this war during world war 2 but really the major leagues. It was a dream come true, until Stan Musial got a rally started. Oh well, just to be on the field at 14 is impressive and a record.

I can relate to this book because I know the feeling of being on the field. When I'm in the moment I run harder, I get more nervous, and I can really feel the game. Just to think about these super stars is cool because I can imagine what they see.
