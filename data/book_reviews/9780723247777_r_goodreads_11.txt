Rating: 5/5

Surprised myself by laughing out loud at the mother’s response to her bedraggled kittens ruining her tea party and the ensuing chaos. Sounds exactly right! 😆 One of my favorite things about her books is how the story continues after you think it’s over (in a good way). A delight!

Really enjoying re-reading Beatrix’s books with the kids as I listen to her biography which gives all the background info you could ever want on each.
