Rating: 5/5

I worked through this book with my women’s AA group and I have to say I really enjoyed it. Of course, I have and utilize the Big Book but the language and tone is obviously geared towards men of a different time. This book uses the same principles but makes it palatable to you as a modern woman. You read the stories of other women you can actually relate to. The language is geared towards women and the way we communicate amongst each other. The advice is practical to women and the many facets we often struggle through as wives/mothers/working women, etc with addiction. It was a great read and I highlighted more of it than I left blank.
