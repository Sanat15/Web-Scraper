Rating: 4/5

I’m honestly just happy I finally read this one.

This physical copy has been sitting on my bookshelf for over ten years. If you know me, then you already know I’m a huge Tupac fan, so this book has always been one I meant to get around to. What originally pulled me in was the photos included in the center section. That alone had me curious for years.

Now that I’ve finally read it, I can say it was interesting, but it didn’t really give me much new information. After almost thirty years of discussions, documentaries, interviews, and investigations around Tupac’s death, a lot of what was covered here felt familiar.

Because of everything that has surfaced over the years about that night in September 1996, the book also felt like it left a few things out. I kept thinking about how much more we know now compared to when this was written.

Still, I’m glad I read it. More than anything, it feels good to finally check a book off my shelf that’s been sitting on my TBR for over a decade. Sometimes finishing the book is an accomplishment all by itself. (3.5/5)
