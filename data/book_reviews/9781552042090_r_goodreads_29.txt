Rating: 5/5

Excellent Book, Great characters 

With A Stolen Heart, Candace Camp continues her reign as the Queen of Regency Romance. Alexandra and Sebastian are fierce opponents, when they aren't making fierce whooped. An American woman with business which brings her to London during the Regency era, Alexandra learns that she might be the long lost daughter of an English count. The girl was feared lost to the mobs and mistaken for a Parisian aristocrat during the French Revolution. 

Sebastian disputes her claim--which she does not set forth, since she doesn’t need the estate. Only and has no interest in British titles.

Kidnappings, midnight attacks, and a runaway hot air balloon ride make the story fly high and fast. Ms. Camp knows her history, and gives us a bonus peek into East Indian culture, no extra charge.

Highly recommended.
