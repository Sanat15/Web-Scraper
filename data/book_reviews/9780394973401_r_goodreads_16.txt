Rating: 3/5

This book is fine, honestly. The story is good, but there's no real flow in the writing. I almost wished that it rhymed to make it more interesting. It is a bit outdated. There may be other reasons children do these things. It may be stress, or it could be a tic, or it could be stimming. In my case, it was that I had undiagnosed skin issues. There is some commentary too though, on stigma and how other people may mock you for your differences. That shaming people also doesn't really work either. It often backfires. This book also provides good examples of positive reinforcement and some of it's pitfalls. Rewarding somebody for good behavior may work, if they highly value the reward. I believe negative punishment however, should only be used if absolutely necessary. I can't help but wonder if instead of taking away the pennies, they should've just tried giving her the pennies first. Having the pennies serves as a reminder not to bite her nails and about the reward she will receive at the end of the day. The ending joke is funny and can also serve as an example of one of the pitfalls of positive reinforcement. People may only engage in the good behavior if they are given an external incentive for doing so.
