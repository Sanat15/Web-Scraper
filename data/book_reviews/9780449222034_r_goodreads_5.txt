Rating: 1/5

I've read a couple Michelle Martin books before and liked them, but this was disappointing. It promised to be kind of fun, but the heroine was apparently the most beautiful woman in the world ever and shockingly unconventional (but everyone loves her anyway); therefore, I found her annoying. And the hero just didn't make sense. He is Very Irate and Proper! And then he's not, but there doesn't really seem to be any reason why he changes as quickly as he does. 

Also, this is a really short book and there are a ton of characters and secondary romances going on. It kind of drove me up the wall. The ending was also too neatly wrapped up. And also kind of abrupt. I think I noticed in the other books by the author I'd read that the bit where True Love is declared was kind of rushed and didn't feel quite genuine, but I liked the rest of the story so much that I forgave that. Not this one though!
