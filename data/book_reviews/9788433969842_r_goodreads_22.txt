Rating: 3/5

This book took less than a day to read and while it was diverting, well written and pleasantly readable it wasn't exactly mind blowing and I'm not really sure why it has a place on the 1001 books list.
The story follows the lives of Gabriel, who still communicates with his dead twin Archie, and Gabriel's life which is dominated by the trial and tribulations of his parents whose 1960's peace love and music ethos is now out of step with the modern world. His father, Rex is a talented mucisian who missed the fame boat and now wallows at the bottom of a pint glass and his mother is long suffering and seeking romance and reassurance although preferably not with Gabriel's father.
