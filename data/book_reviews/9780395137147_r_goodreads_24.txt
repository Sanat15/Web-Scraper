Rating: 5/5

This book would be great for anybody wanting to learn more about greek gods and heroes. It is a great book because instead of one huge story about mainly one person it is many small stories with different characters each time. The bad thing about this however is that it makes it a little hard to take the test and remember small details of each one so I had to refer back to them many times throughout the test. One thing that did help was the author did not give too much detail or really go into depth on any of the stories. It made it a little easier to take the test and remember each story but still had to refer back to the book. 

 All of the characters in this book as you can imagine have amazing stories and very interesting events taking place. One thing I found about the book though is as i was going through it would mention characters from earlier stories and I had a hard time remembering who did what because throughout the book so many characters are mentioned. Something I really liked about all of the characters is that they always helped people and never showed off. Many heroes get a lot of attention from doing amazing things that it starts to get to them, but in these stories all of the heroes stayed the same as they were before they had a lot of attention. This book was overall very entertaining and gave me a better understanding of greek heroes and i am glad to have read it.
