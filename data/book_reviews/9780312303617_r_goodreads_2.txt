Lacking a table of contents (could somebody provide one?), I can't tell which of the Freaky Fables are in this book. The ones I have are directly from Punch. Handelsman is an underrated (almost neglected) cartoonist, who has a gift for phrasing and a wicked insight into how people's minds work. One of my favorites is an individual cartoon from the Nixon years in which a woman laments "People get the kind of government they deserve. I must be a terrible, terrible person." This is still valid if and ONLY if you accept the premise. What if people DON'T get the kind of government they deserve?
