Rating: 4/5

This was likewise a real treat, nabbed from my parents' rather ancient collection of books about plants they acquired in their education as horticulturalists back in the sixties. 

As a glimpse into previous ways of life, it is FASCINATING. The recipes from settlers, the number of times the authors talk about ''emergency rations'' like it was a normal part of life for indigenous people and the early settlers...I loved it. 

In terms of things I would go out and forage for, it was also fairly relevant. I had some trouble connecting the plants discussed with my knowledge of forest vegetation, but that's only normal. There were a few plants I was inspired to go collect next time I visit my friend at camp or next time I visit my parents. Alas, this city dweller will have to stick to backyard urban foraging for the time being.
