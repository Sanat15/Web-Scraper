Rating: 3/5

I am someone who usually adores Jennifer Greene's writing. She has this ability to pull you in with her characters and root for them. With Castle Keep, although I didn't find the characters or storylines as engaging. The h/H have to work on the restoration of an Irish castle together. They of course have chemistry from the start; but the heroine is still reeling from a broken heart and miscarriage two years earlier. The hero is sympathetic, lends her an ear and lets her fight her own battles with the crew. But somehow I found the romance to be a bit lacking. It didn't draw me in so much. I would classify this one as an average read.
