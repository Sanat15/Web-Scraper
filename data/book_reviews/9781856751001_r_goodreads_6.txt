Rating: 1/5

Be nice to the trees! The best part of this book was encouraging everyone to go and sit under a tree to watch it sway while focusing on breathing. Sitting with the trees can bring a sense of relaxation.

I am confused though because the book in the beginning claimed that people who see trees as means of harvest are of “The Farmer” archetype. This book described “The Farmer” archetype as the worst archetype you could be in your relationship to trees.

But then the second half of the book had sections about the benefits of oils you harvest from trees? I guess because they are not destroying the trees to make the oils they don’t see it in the same way?
