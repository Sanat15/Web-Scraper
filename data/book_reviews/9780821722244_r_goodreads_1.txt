Rating: 5/5

I found this book at a thrift store for $0.99 and absolutely loved it. The book starts in 1752 and follows the romance of Tamara Fleming, a lady with a proper English upbringing, and the mysterious Falcon, a proud and rugged man working at her father’s estate. Tamara Fleming has been estranged from her father, Jacob Fleming, and lives in London with her guardian, Mason Gray, who cares for Tamara like his own daughter. Following the death of Mason’s son, Tamara is summoned to live at her father’s estate, Asher Arms, in Delaware so that they may reconnect because his health is declining. Mason is grieving the death of his son, and shares with Tamara that he still grieves the death of his first wife and son, who had journeyed to America and died shortly after. Tamara reluctantly leaves her life at London and respects her father’s request. What follows is Tamara learning about her past, developing a relationship with her father, making new friends and enemies, and falling for the mysterious Falcon. The chemistry between Tamara and Falcon is great and their relationship grows through struggles and differences. Tamara and Falcon have to learn to trust each other and to overcome the secrets of Falcon’s past. The book spans over 3 years and is a beautiful story. If you find this book, you will enjoy it.
