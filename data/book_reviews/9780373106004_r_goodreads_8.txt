Rating: 2/5

They all lacked charisma. The heroine is a model aged 25, who wants to be a sculptor full time, but she behaves more like a middle aged spinster who has never attracted a male and needs to feel thankful. The hero turns 34 at the beginning of the book and is lamenting his age (!!), remarking on his lack of good looks and yet is having an affair with a glamorous widow (which could only be for his money as he has few good personality traits).
I read reviews on here before reading this, but I was won over by the normal looking hero (it's WONDERFUL when they are not all tall and handsome and women magnets), but he has the ego of an alpha, so it made no difference.
Both are so lacklustre and lacking in depth that I could not care less whether they got a HEA.
