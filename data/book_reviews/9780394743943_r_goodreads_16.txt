Rating: 3/5

Nickel Mountain I read and loved a long time ago and liked on a re-read still. I've never read Grendel. Many years ago I started the Sunlight Dialogues and bailed quickly. This time I made it through all 670 pages in about ten days. This may be the longest novel I've ever finished, only to end having not enjoyed much of the ride; in fact, not any. I was suckered in by the dust jacket's talk of moments of grace. If there were any I missed them. Gardner is a fine stylist, but there was just too much misery and loss and remorse and agenbite of inwit to suit me. Central to the story are the dialogues between the Sunlight Man and Police Chief Clumly. They are really monologues by the Sunlight Man, who is not someone you want for a friend. His orations/ravings about Babylonia are either too brilliant or too crazy for me to want to read them; I may have thus skipped a key to the novel.
