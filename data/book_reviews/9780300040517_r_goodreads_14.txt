Rating: 5/5

There are dreams too great that must still take the path of the wandering stream to reach the vastness of the sea. A young fern sapling falls in to grow into a tumbling tree.

Nobody expects when taking in hand the over a century old poem is going to reduce you to tears for 20-40 minutes at a time. I am nobody; I am a woman.

For example, she loves without thinking: that is stupid, but it is not unbridled lust. So she builds herself a hermitage of dried grass and bent willow. The waves are her emeralds, the clouds her gold. I cross out your name from the list of the Company of Sadness. I give you back your poems. Then, again unexpectedly: let me pound your magic mortar with my jade pestle. OK, I’m laughing now.
