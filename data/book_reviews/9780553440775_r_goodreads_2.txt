Rating: 5/5

I wasn't expecting much from this book,but WOW!!! This book blew me away with its fast paced story, readability, great characters and wonderful romance. I loved how the point of view flicked from her side to his within the same chapter,much the way I like to write. Denver was a pretty much perfect guy too and Courtney was very relatable. I wanted to devour the book and never wanted it to end at the same time. A new favorite for me!!! Squee!
