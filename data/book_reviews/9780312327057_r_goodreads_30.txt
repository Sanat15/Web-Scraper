Rating: 1/5

One of the worst books I ever read. I was constantly laughing to myself because everything is just so laughably bad. The plot has so many logical holes, it's ridiculous. The characters are all flat as paper and all uninteresting. In the beginning, the writing style was pretty good but starting at the middle everything becomes really hard to follow. I constantly had to re-read sentences and paragraphs because the characters jump from location to location so quickly. Also, the story could easily be told in 300 pages! It's clear that the author tried to drag it out for length and decided who/what the antagonist actually is only on the last 200 pages.
