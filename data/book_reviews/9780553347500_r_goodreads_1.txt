Rating: 5/5

I completely object to the "new age" classification that this book was stuck with! All of Emmanuel's Books share the same wisdom and guidance that all the great religions have throughout history--but without all of the mess that people have created in religion's name. Start with the original Emmanuel's Book, simply because it's an introduction to Emmanuel. There is nothing in any of the books that will not make sense if you have not read the earlier version(s), though. I can't recommend them highly enough.
