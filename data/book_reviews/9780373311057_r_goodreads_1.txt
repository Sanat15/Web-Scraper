Rating: 4/5

This is a very sweet story. Nancy Browne is an immensely likeable character &amp; Lord Selbridge is a remarkably sensible chap. I love the way she sets her family to rights &amp; makes a place of her own. Sir Reginald is a gruff old fellow but definitely no fool when he sees a treasure in his niece. There are quite a few mishaps before everyone is set to partners &amp; it is great fun to read.

(Nancy Browne, Jack Daventry, Earl of Selbridge, Sir Reginald &amp; Osbert Drakes, Lady Verena, Eleanor &amp; Gervaise Chetwynd, Sir Francis &amp; Felicity Garrard, Caroline Phillips)
