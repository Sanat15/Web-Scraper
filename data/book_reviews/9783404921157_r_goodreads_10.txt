I fear that FIFA has been deeply distracting but I have been loving every moment of it so no complaints!! 

So about this book, I picked it up because I love Alice walker but also was intrigued by the title. “The way forward is with a broken heart.” I thought to myself how ? After reading this book, I still ask how? Is a broken heart a precursor to accepting life for it? Is it a catalyst for awareness and ultimately change ? A broken heart be it from an unrequited love, deferred and lost dreams, or deeply hurtful events is such an ever changing experience but what does the way forward look like and how does the way forward lead to a more empathetic and beautiful world? I confess I continue to ponder on this even after completing this book; but as always good things take time!! Grateful to Alice Walker 💜
