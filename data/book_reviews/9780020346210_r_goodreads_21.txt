After a couple of decades of eatin' my own cooking,(and not liking it very much) I thought I would finally learn how to actually do it. No usable recipes are included. Well, some are included but they're examples from history. You wouldn't want to entertain with them. This book goes into the science behind what goes on in the kitchen. I finally found out why my "crunchy meat" tastes so good. It's the Maillard reaction. Ok, the roast was burned, but I'm still sayin' it was that frenchie reaction thing.
