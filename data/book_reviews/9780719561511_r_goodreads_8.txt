Rating: 3/5

The book was a bit piecey, but if you are really interested in the history of Tierra del Fuego, Antarctica, and Patagonia AND you want to go there, this book is probably the best around. Harrison's interest seeps through his words, but sometimes he can't hold onto his readers. I did feel that sometimes he was forgetting certain details or to make a certain point, thereby leaving me confused. But other stories were great - I liked his time on the Crusoe Islands and his research into all the great ships that rounded the Cape. Still, the book isn't for everyone - but map geeks (like me) will enjoy it!
