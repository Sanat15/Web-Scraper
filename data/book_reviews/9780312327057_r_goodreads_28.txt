Rating: 2/5

The general outline of this story is actually quite good, particularly if you are open for supernatural stories. However, I don't think I've ever actually rolled my eyes at a book while reading it like I did here. All crucial characters are male. All of them. The only female is immediately and repetitively reduced to a sexual object, highlighting the toxic masculinity in this book. She does occasionally and very conveniently find clues to reveal the backstory of the demon, but mostly I've been very annoyed at how she was displayed in this book. The story is also drawn out, particularly towards the end without any good reason. The movie rights have already been sold for 20+ years (?!) and if this movie ever gets made, I hope the writers reduce those unnecessary elements and only keep the actually interesting aspects. The author has a spectacular talent for building tension and writing the most horrific scenes (especially within the first 150 pages), but as whole this was not my favorite.
