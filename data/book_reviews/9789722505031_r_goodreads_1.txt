Rating: 5/5

The present is filled with bitterness… The past is full of sweetness… Is it so?
The old judge is placed into an asylum for the aged and incapacitated… Arbitrarily leaping through his memory he recalls his love and life, mentally sharing his reminiscences and impressions with his dead wife…
Great things occur in eternity and love is a great feeling, you, my beloved, must know this. Love is an inspiration, you must have heard of this. Or it’s like a shock. It is exactly what happened to me. It is like a collision of two celestial luminaries in the infinity – as they write in books.
He is young… She is young… The world is young… Life is wonderful… Love is wonderful… The future is bright… He rapturously sprays her hair with water and says:
I baptize you in the name of the Earth, the stars and perfection.
Perfection… In the Name of the Earth is a sad and grievous story… It’s a sorrowful human comedy…
They grow older… They face troubles and disenchantment… Their children disappoint them… Ailments and diseases… She falls seriously ill… And one fine day she says:
I have never loved you.
Hope evaporates… Beauty disintegrates… Youth and great expectations are always better than the old age and life left behind so the past is always better than the present.
