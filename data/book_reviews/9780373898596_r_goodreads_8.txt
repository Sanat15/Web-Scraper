Rating: 3/5

Our heroine, Barbara, is torn between two lovers and is feeling like a fool. Her fiancée, Todd, is a genuinely nice guy who enjoys rescuing stray pets, doesn’t begrudge Barbara her prior sexual experiences, respects her opinion, and believes in her right to choice and agency. Todd’s brother, Jock (or JR, which is how Todd refers to him and what leads Barbara into this rather contrived triangle) however has different ideas. He takes Barbara’s devotion for granted, believes that since they’ve slept together he has the right to claim her, and often talks about her as though she wasn’t there. But Jock’s mere presence drives poor, conflicted Barbara to the heights of lust. Guess which fellow she ultimately chooses?
