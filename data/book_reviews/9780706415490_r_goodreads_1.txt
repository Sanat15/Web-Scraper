Rating: 5/5

This is a collection of 20 stories, all with a horsey theme. Anna Sewell and Black Beauty, Jonathan Swift and Gulliver and Bryan Forbes and International Velvet, all make an appearance. Don't be put off by the fact this is aimed at children. I loved it and some of the stories deal with more adult emotions. So enjoy! My favourites are 'Quarter Horse Boy' and 'The Chimaera'.
