Rating: 3/5

The subtitle of this book sort of says it all. Dr. Oz is creating the medicine of the future. It's very exciting that he is a respected surgeon exploring the various "alternative" therapies out there and taking them seriously. I've read a fair amount of heart books (because of my play, fig. a: The Heart) and this one sort of cheered me up the most. I'm a sucker for the intersection of science and the more "far-out" stuff. Dr. Oz seems like a real scientist to me, in that he is really exploring all possibilities no matter how crazy his fellow doctors may think he is. 
This is a fairly personal account of how he got to his position and there are a lot of vivid surgical descriptions. If putting the words "saw" and "breastbone" in the same sentence gives you the heebie jeebies, you'll have to skip quite a few passages. 
It's an interesting read but I don't know what it would be like if you weren't wholeheartedly (ha) interested in heart medicine already. It also felt a little spare. I could have used a LOT more information and a lot more stories. I'm guessing this is just the first volume of a series. Maybe it already is and I don't know it!
