Rating: 4/5

A charming and heartwarming story about Ben Brown, his loyal dog Sancho and the kindness of Mrs Moss, Bab, Betty, Miss Celia and Thornton. I loved seeing Ben find a loving home after such a difficult life with the circus. I was especially happy when Sancho was reunited with Ben, and even more delighted when Ben’s father, Mr Brown, returned after everyone believed he was dead. A sweet story about friendship, family and finding where you belong.
