I really enjoyed this novel. It focuses mainly on the story of Walker Boh (and Morgan Leah), with only brief updates on what Wren, Par, and Coll from the previous volume were doing. Brooks does a great job in slowing the story down to allow characters to focus on their own thoughts, so the reader can understand what the characters are grappling with internally not just externally. This doesn't harm the story at all, but instead enriches it.
