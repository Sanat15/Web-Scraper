Rating: 2/5

Historical Christian Romance Fiction

Historical aspect: interesting &amp; well-researched, without sounding like a dry history book
Christian aspect: annoyingly pat
Romance aspect: cloying (*shudder*)

There is also a subtle racism throughout the book that might drop jaws, or at least merit an eye-roll &amp; a groan. I do believe the author thought she was being very "open-minded" and "radical," and certainly had no malicious intent, however.
