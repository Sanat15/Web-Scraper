Rating: 4/5

Reading Curtiss Ann Matlock's SWEET DREAMS AT THE GOODNIGHT MOTEL is like listening to Diana Krall's music; it wraps around you like a warm, comfy blanket and seeps quietly into your soul. You'll end up thinking about this book for a long time to come.

I've been a fan of Ms. Matlock's books for more than 20 years, and SWEET DREAMS AT THE GOODNIGHT MOTEL didn't disappoint. I'll admit it took me a little while to adjust to the numerous point of view shifts (not something usually encountered in books these days), but after the first few chapters I easily accepted the story's flow and enjoyed seeing things from so many different perspectives.

There are three main storylines and three female protagonists, each at a different stage in her life and each dealing with emotional landmines. Claire, the main character, who is in her forties, Vella, who's in her late sixties, and Sherilynn, a young, unwed mother-to-be coming out of a toxic relationship with a criminal, all vie for the reader's attention and effortlessly tug at our heartstrings. Then, of course, we also get into the heads of several male characters: Winston, Travis, Andrew, Larkin, Harold. It's like a thick gumbo of emotions the author manages to pull together into a satisfying whole.

This isn't a typical romance, so this isn't the book for you if that's what you're expecting. But if you're looking for something outside the ordinary, you won't be disappointed with SWEET DREAMS AT THE GOODNIGHT MOTEL.
