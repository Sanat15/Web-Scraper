Rating: 1/5

Couldn't go through this one. It was plain boring. 
I wanted to read this book mainly because I despise Dali's art. At least most of it is definitely not my type. However, I value creativity and am a passionate student of creative process in most obscure versions. 
The first chapters of the book took me on a very nice and pleasant trip. Soon, though, the concept has become less and less interesting. Author's descriptions lacked style and vividness. Finally, bored and disgusted, I was forced not to proceed with reading. It was, however, a valuable lesson. Now I know, that less than Dali's painting, I enjoy Dali's writing.
