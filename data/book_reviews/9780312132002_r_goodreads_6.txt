Rating: 3/5

This was a good sequel to "Olivia and Jai", but not as good as the first book. (I said that one should have been a miniseries, I don't think the same about this.) There were some really good moments, and I liked the character of Maya, Olivia and Jai's daughter (love the way she got back at Christian when he revealed he really was a racist), as well as Kyle, and Olivia getting a surprise visitor from the past, also the whole story of Jai and the mystery surrounding it. But - as with the first book - it was much too long. Everything could have been said and done in about 200 less pages.

Not perfect, but a good story, anyhow.
