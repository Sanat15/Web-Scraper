Rating: 4/5

i enjoyed this reread 🙂 i think i possibly enjoyed it more than the first time i read it... tho that was some years ago, and my memory is abit sketchy. i remember liking it then too tho 🙂

i was reminded of how well written it is 🙂

it was abit philosophical, and abit of a putting forward of ideas in places, but was tied together well as/into a novel 🙂

the main character is an academic, and consequently the book has a feel of that kinda echelon of society in ways, which could be alienating... but i didn't find it so overall, and that's maybe interesting to think about - why isn't it more alienating than it is, why does it work/engage despite this? 🤔

it's also broader than him/his sphere too, in both characters, circumstances and content 🙂

🌊🌍

💀 i thought the main character embodies well a sense of depression and cynicism that epitomises the state of the nation 🙂

💔 i found the observed (mass/group) Quietus scenes pretty devastating again (especially as someone considered of little worth in the society in which i live).

👥 i enjoyed the shifts, and shifting dynamics... the relationships, and the insights into each other, their situation... 

✨ it perhaps got abit allegorical towards the end... 😉🤔🙃 

🌊🌍

if you know the (2006) film, you don't know the book, and vice versa. but there is alot of overlap, and the film has a pretty faithful adherence to the overall plot of the novel, wrt the political powers of England, and the mood of the people, and especially wrt the main character/narrator of the story.

🌟 🌟 🌟🌟
