Eye of the Needle

Die Needle is a German spy who cam to England just before the 2nd World War. This is the story of his work and the people who were after him. A gripping and very believable tale.

Lie down with lions

Englishwoman Jane gets caught up between 2 spies American Ellis and French Jean-Paul. She lives in the Valley of Five Lions in Afghanistan with the latter living with the Afghans while they are fighting the Russians includes treachery and some scary journeys.
