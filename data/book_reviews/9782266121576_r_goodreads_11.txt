My brief, intermittent history with The Vampire Chronicles :

Kindergarten: happened to see a scene from Interview with the Vampire that made me think of the way the molten innards of a Pizza Roll squirt out the back end when you bite into it. 

Ninth Grade: my girlfriend at the time wrote a book report for me on Interview with the Vampire. I turned it in without reading the book or the report. I don’t remember the grade I received. I soon thereafter watched Queen of the Damned with her and all I remember is that Lestat sings a KORN song, I think…

2019: I purchased a large horror book lot which included both Interview with the Vampire  and a movie tie-in edition of Queen of the Damned. 

This Week: I found a copy of The Vampire Lestat in a little free library.

I am now finally ready to read the original trilogy of The Vampire Chronicles.
