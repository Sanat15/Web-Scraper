Rating: 3/5

I enjoyed reading about the end of the plantation era here in the lowcountry of SC. It's my backyard - and therefore it's especially fascinating to know how life was "here" - and for a woman. The book is Elizabeth Allston Pringle's journal. She wrote in a very descriptive way, and I learned much about the planting process, the slow &amp; scary travel between town and the plantations, and the joys and difficulties having slaves.
