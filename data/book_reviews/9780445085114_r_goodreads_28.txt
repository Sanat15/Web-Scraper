Rating: 4/5

History for those who hate reading history. Costain does a remarkable job of bringing to life the dry historical record of the earliest Norman kings of England. He intimately describes the character of the august personages that ruled that age and brings an immediacy to the world of the late 11th and early 12th centuries. If you are one of those (and there are a great many of you) who hate reading history this is the book for you. It is a wonderful examination of an interesting aspect of English history that is never dull or dry. It is not a text book with nothing but dates and names, but rather a living text, a Capotean non-fiction novel.
