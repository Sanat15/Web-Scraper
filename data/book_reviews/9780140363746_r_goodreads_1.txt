Rating: 4/5

I randomly found this on clearance at a bookstore and decided to grab it. Because how could I not grab 90s paranormal YA? I didn't expect much, but I was actually really impressed from the very first page. The author makes some interesting creative choices, such as switching tenses and adding in snippets of other perspectives, to broaden the scope of the novel. It's short, but surprisingly well written so that it packs a pretty good punch.

This book reminds me of The Vampire Diaries books. Lila is pretty and popular like Elena, but much nicer. Like Elena, she finds herself drawn to a mysterious hot boy and gets involved in supernatural things. There's even an all-American boyfriend like TVD's Matt, and a mean girl like Caroline.

Obviously I adored the nameless love interest. Green eyes, black hair, and rides a motorcycle. I'm going to hunt down the next volume so I can find out what happens!
