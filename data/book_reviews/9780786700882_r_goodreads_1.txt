Rating: 5/5

What a great selection of 19th and early 20th century detective tales ! I especially enjoyed the best known tales (again) there are two from A C Doyle's many Sherlock Holmes tales, a Raffles tale from E W Hornung, a Dr J L Thorndyke from R A Freeman and many others. I discovered Robert Barr's "tongue in cheek" sleuth called Luke Sharp (love that name) and one of the many Sherlockian's, such as Martin Hewitt, Ebenezer Gryce and Thorpe Hazell (love those names) a mixed bunch but all in worth the 5 stars I have bestowed on the collection.
