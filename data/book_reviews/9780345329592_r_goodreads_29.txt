Rating: 4/5

"Only in ... grace can we face our sin; only in ... healing do we dare show our wounds. Only with a single-minded attention to Christ can we give up our clinging fears and face our true nature."

Although I did not agree with everything in the book, The Way of the Heart stretched and challenged me in all the best of ways, particularly the portion pertaining to silence. Nouwen has a way of expressing his ideas that proves particularly poetic; his rhetoric permeates my soul, even when I struggle to grasp precisely what he is positing.
