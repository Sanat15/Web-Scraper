Rating: 5/5

This book is a product of it's era and take all advice with a grain of salt (probably don't take the medical advice at all unless you've done your research elsewhere) -- I delight in this book often, it's fun to thumb through and be taken back to another time. Parts of it are humorous and parts of it are relics that are best kept in the past (the "Fat and How Not to Be" chapter). The plant descriptions are what I usually will reference more than the recipes. This book is a gem. &lt;3
