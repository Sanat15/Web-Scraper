Rating: 5/5

I love the sport of hockey but hate the culture of hockey and this book explains why in disturbing detail. Despite being published more than two decades ago, the immorality within is, unfortunately, still prevalent in modern hockey.

I began reading this just before watching the 2026 documentary, Code of Misconduct (which details the sexual assault trial that eventually lead to the acquittal of current NHL goalie, Carter Hart, and four of his 2018 World Juniors teammates), and the similarities between the assault that EM described in her testimony and the various assaults detailed in this book are staggering.

Not an easy read, by any means, but an important one. The decades of rotten behavior put forth in these pages shows that the culture is dangerous for both players and those who orbit them.

While I will probably always love hockey and am so happy about the strides the sport has taken (at least at an individual level) to be more inclusive and welcoming, there will always be questions and serious concerns lingering in the back of my mind
