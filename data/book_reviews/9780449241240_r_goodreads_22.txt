Rating: 3/5

Edna Ferber was a name that I was aware of for many years but in the UK her books have been difficult to come by fro many years.
I discovered a second hand copy of The Ice Palace in the back of a bookstore in Burford, Oxfordshire last year.
I delayed reading it as for some reason I felt that it might be a difficult or boring read.

It is a fairly easy read and I found myself enjoying the book at first but I was bored by the long section of the tour of Alaska the heroine Chris Storm is taken on by her Grandfather before going to University in Seattle to finish her education.
The section in Seattle and the final chapters of the book seemed rushed and there is a rather inconclusive ending.
I can understand why some readers thought there was a plan for a second ,follow up book planned as the story of Chris Storm feels incomplete.

What surprised me was the Eco and Environmental concerns in the book ( published 1958).
One would be forgiven for thinking this book was written fairly recently when such concerns have become more widespread.

Overall an OK read but structure rather poor as the book seemed uncertain who to concentrate the story around.
Bridie , Chris Storm’s unofficial Granny, is a great character and the story always brightened up when Bridie was around.

I would not go out of my way to search for more Edna Ferber books to read but I would probably buy another Ferber if I found another of her books in second hand store.
