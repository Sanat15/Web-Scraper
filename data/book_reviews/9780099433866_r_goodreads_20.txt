Rating: 4/5

I love Tim Moore's travel books, always quirky and very funny. This was one of his earlier books and focuses on London, particularly with reference to Monopoly. It is interesting with some great London based facts. It is a bit 'Things are much better then', and he does complain about lot about his home town. It's also a period piece, written at the beginning of the 21st century, and shows how much has changed since then.
