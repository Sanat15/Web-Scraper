Rating: 5/5

Okay, I don't know how I could say I didn't miss Henrick that much. He's as much what makes this series as Peter and Elise are. So glad he's finally back in! I love his daring and his irrepressible humour. And I can't help it - I ship him and Elise so hard :D I'm not sure what age these three are when the series ends, but I'm hoping by that time they're older? :D
This one was very different to the others - firstly, the war is ended, so it had no Nazis or soldier threats of any kind. Rather, it's more of a mystery, with several twists and a slightly unexpected conclusion.
I liked the themes in this one a lot, and I think I liked it even more with the war being over. I wasn't sure what to think about Keld - the ending was very interesting.
And of course, I'm very glad to see a certain couple getting married - although wow, what a wedding! Talk about ruined XD Still, I wouldn't have traded that climax scene for anything. The climaxes in this series are seriously good. 
I think the series will probably change quite a lot now, with the war over, Henrick back, and the twins getting older. Something I love about these books is how each one stands alone but still leaves things for the next book to resolve. Henrick's father, for instance, and Mr Poulsen...
I wasn't intending to read the whole series in one go, but I'm afraid I can't help it now! This is definitely one of the best series of its kind that I've read!
