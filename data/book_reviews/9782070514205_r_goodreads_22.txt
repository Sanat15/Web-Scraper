Rating: 3/5

The above book dramatization is not actually the audio book I listened to, but was the closest thing I saw on the GR editions listed. I was looking for something short but interesting and this was an upcoming Group Read for Catching Up with the Classics. Since I have been absent from a lot of Group Reads lately, this seemed to be quick and easy way to participate. I enjoyed the story and audio format worked just fine.

The story is about the visit of one friend to another who has found a bit of treasure, a golden bug. The story takes place near Charleston, South Carolina during the times of slavery. The casual racism may offend some readers, but the basic story was good and could/should have been written without that. This is a good story for the Halloween season without being scary.

One fun aspect for me was the decoding word game which solved a mystery at the heart of the story. My mother used to solve Cryptograms and I have been known to pick up a pencil and try my hand. Truth is, I am better at Crostics.

3.5 stars rounded down, because basically this was a good story, but there wasn’t any deep content in it for me.
