Rating: 3/5

A charming historical romance, this novel is essentially a road trip. Two couple elope in the night and travel to Gretna Green to get married: Jason and Harriette; Penny and Angus. But as they proceed for four days along the same road and in the same direction and meet regularly at the roadside inns for a change of horses, the two protagonists, Penny and Jason, realize that they are going to wed a wrong person, and that what they really want is to marry each other. 
I love Carola Dunn’s Daisy Dalrymple series. This romance is an earlier book of hers, and I didn’t like it as much as most of the Daisy series, but it was still a pleasant and quick read.
