Rating: 4/5

Clearly dated but of value for historical purposes. I think the necessity of this kind of book speaks for itself. Less sensationalist than the title suggests and more one reformed gynecologist’s examination of all the different ways him/his colleagues failed to fulfill their duty of care towards women. 
The last section is very DIY/Call-to-Arms-y, where he suggests how women can take their reproductive health into your own hands (by basically putting up with less nonsense from their primary care providers).
