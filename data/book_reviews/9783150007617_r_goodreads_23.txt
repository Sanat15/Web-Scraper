Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars. An eventful, sometimes humorous, adventure novel about a young orphan who is called Simplicius Simplicissimus due to his innocence. Set in 1618 to 1648 during the thirty years war in the Holy Roman Empire. The book is told from the perspective of Simplicius. He speaks the truth, insulting people unknowingly. They take him for a jester. He begins his travels, receiving an education and then becomes a soldier, brigand, philanderer and killer.

The novel is semi autobiographical. The book is regarded as the greatest German novel of the 17th century and was first published in Germany in 1669.
