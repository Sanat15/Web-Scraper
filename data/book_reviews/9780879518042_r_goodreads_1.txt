Rating: 4/5

Inspiring book. Mostly color photographs. The text is general and vague so do not expect clear instruction. My take on the text is more of a tips and thoughts of the author. Obviously, you can't learn how to paint such detail from one how-to book anyway. If you are interested in the author's work and would like to know how she thinks, this book will be rewarding for you. It is worth the money just for the pictures alone. Definitely inspirational!
