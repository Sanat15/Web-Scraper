Rating: 2/5

I began reading this book 2 days before a college final in the middle of finals week so I was obviously a little stressed but I have never disagreed with a book as much as I have with this one. Don't get me wrong I'm biased on several fronts. The big bias is that I love the Harry Potter series but honestly I have never struggled to get through a book THIS MUCH. Many of the accusations against Harry Potter are resolved in later books (this only covers books 1-4) while the rest are unfounded and a stretch to begin with. Also I disagree with the fact that the book explicitly states that it is summarizing the Harry Potter novels so parents don't actually have to read the books they're banning their kids from reading. But in this books defense it is well written and thought provoking no matter what those thoughts are.
