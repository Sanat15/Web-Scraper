Rating: 5/5

I have taken this book back off my shelf and reread it multiple times. It covers them any pitfalls of time travel, but it is not full of technical jargon, and flashy heroes. It explores cause and consequences, and the changes that can occur from a single motion. Then the issue of personal faith is brough it in a manner that is completely human. I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to be surprised by a good read.
