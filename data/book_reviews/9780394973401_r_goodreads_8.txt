★★★★The Berenstain Bears and the Bad Habit by Stan and Jan Berenstain
Sister Bear has a habit, like most young cubs. Some scratch, some suck their thumbs, some play with their hair, but she bites her nails. The bad thing is she doesn't realize it, until it's too late, and starts to hurt. Bad Habits are hard to break, unless you have something, to remind you of them.
