I bought this Book from a shopkeeper in FATA, selling goods either sold out or left over by US/ISAF soldiers operating in Afghanistan.Infact there were dozens of such books selling for a dollar each besides other items of warfare.
This is a simple and reader friendly version of the holy Bible, divided under different subjects like anger, belief, charity, courage, hope, mercy, peace, repentence, sickness, trust, wisdom, work, worry and many others. There is absolutely no doubt that the words of this Book carry great solace and comfort and is of great help while passing through test and tribulations. These words contain no regard for the caste and creed of the people, the only consideration being addressing the pupil of God, affectionately called the childern of God herein. The message of the Book is universal and transcends time and age.This surely is the continuation of the divine guidance offerred by the Almighty since Man and Woman were created.
One of the remarkable features of the Book is the close resemblence it has with the teachings of Islam. The islamic scripture consist of two parts, the words of God as enshrined in the holy Quran as preserved in its orignal shape. The second catagory is the words of the Prophet called 'Hadith' or 'Sunnah' which are available in different books, compiled by the companions of the Prophet, these being the sayings or deeds of the prophet in the light of divine revealation. The contents of the bible promise Book in most of the cases matches with the Hadith and Sunnah but also with Quran in many many places. At numerous places they are just the same which affirms the beliefs in the authencity of the scripts in the two religions. Of course no seeker will be disappointed with the recourse to the Bible promise book.
