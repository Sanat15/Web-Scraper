Rating: 4/5

Nellie L. Mooney Is the Canadian girl who would be later become Nellie McClung, the pioneer for women’s rights. This is the first of three children novels about her life. The author, Connie Brummel Crook, relies upon Nellie’s autobiography, “Clearing in the West”, historical books, and interviews about that time period to give an informative portrayal of this influential woman beginning in her early school years. I admit to not knowing much about this amazing woman.
