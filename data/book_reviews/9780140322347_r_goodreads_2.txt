Rating: 4/5

Told in close third person, Growin’ Pains by Mary Blount Christian is a delightful middle grade read. I felt for twelve-year-old Ginny Ruth Grover and her need for “fancy” words and poetry. Enjoyed her many adventures. And I knew why she felt as she did. Blount Christian did a great job of creating backstory for her characters. I enjoyed the close-knit town she created.

Ginny Ruth and Maw were poor, like many townsfolks in Clemmons, Texas. This did not stop the folks from caring for each other and coming together when needed. Blount Christian creates a believable plot that moves along crisply. While Ginny Ruth keeps her desire to attend college throughout the novel, she comes to terms with what she doesn’t have and realizes how much she needs the family she does have and the townsfolks of Clemmons. 

If you’re looking for a good middle grade story to study, I suggest the coming-of-age story Growin’ Pains by Mary Blount Christian.
