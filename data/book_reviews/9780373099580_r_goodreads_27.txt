Rating: 5/5

Umm...ok

A different HQN. It is written around a murder. But it's also about second chances. Both main characters were likeable, there were no hateful characters aside from the murder victim.

This about a couple torn apart by infidelity. Not physical infidelity but infidelity based on intimacy and emotional feelings. The main male character could not understand why is wife wassas so angry and hurt at his relationship with a co-worker and how she did not believe he loved her. She believed he did not really love her if he could be with someone else.

After five years apart, the ex-husband came back into the main female character's life when she is arrested for murder.

While trying to clear her name, he also tries convincing her to return to him as he's always loved her. At first she is having none of it but eventually for their HEA she takes him back.

Unfortunately I'm very skeptical of this story. The problems in their marriage are still there, mainly the age difference, life experience and lack of anything in common. I agreed with our main female character all through the book, if their love was strong he would not have found someone else.

The premise of the story was a good one and I enjoyed the story. But something is not right with the HEA. I would call it a HFN because he will stray again.
