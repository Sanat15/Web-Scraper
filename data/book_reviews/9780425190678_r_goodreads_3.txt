Rating: 5/5

There is absolutely nothing funny in this western; however, there are some awfully funny situations that end up even funnier because the characters acted true to form.
This tale of a side-tracked bounty hunter and an orphan girl is a great ride, start to finish. Wild characters, wild set-ups, lots of fun.
Don’t know if Brandvold wrote any other stories with bounty hunter Prophet or not, but I will certainly look!
