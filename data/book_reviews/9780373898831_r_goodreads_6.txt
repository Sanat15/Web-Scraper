Rating: 2/5

She pursues him. I like it better when the man pursues the woman. So that was a turn-off. 

She is a virgin. One night they are making out. She tells him she loves him. He tells her he isn’t going to marry her. So they split in anger. 

The next day she plans to lose her virginity to some guy out of spite. The H comes storming in the motel room where she is with the guy. He takes her to her dad and he tells her dad that he is going to marry his daughter. 

Huh? What a difference a day makes.

Janet Dailey was of course an excellent writer, but this book isn’t it. Not romantic at all.
