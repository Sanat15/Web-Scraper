Interesting format to the illustrations to immerse the child in one color at a time. Left hand page is an illustration of a single hat and the right side is an illustration of a nameless boy slinging an activity with an illustration in various shades of the color. My two favorites are the purple as we see the young boy eating purple grapes, dressed in a purple stripped shirt and purple hat and the other is green with the boy playing with four green frogs on a green table.
Fun title for the toddler set.
