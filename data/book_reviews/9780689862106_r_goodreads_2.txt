Rating: 3/5

This is a good Angel adventure that's set within the continuity of the third year of the show. Cordelia has a vision involving Faith, who is serving her time in jail, so Angel begins to investigate and before you know it the whole gang is off on a secluded island with a big mess of complicated vampire sin-and-redemption angst orchestrated by those evil minions of evil, Wolfram &amp; Hart. It probably wouldn't make a lick of sense to a random reader unfamiliar with the show, but it's a fan-friendly excursion.
