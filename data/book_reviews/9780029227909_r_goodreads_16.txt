Rating: 4/5

This contains some incredibly in-depth reviews of various 20th century policy decision points, usually with significant firsthand information from the participants. I learned about several events that were not covered at all by school history books. In addition, much of the advice afforded is absolutely spot on, if somewhat obvious at times, with the best advice really being to take a moment and step away from urgent reactions. On the negative side, this was written in the 80s and it really needs to be updated, especially for the problems of 21st century politics, and I also wonder if Carter would benefit a bit from less proximity bias.
