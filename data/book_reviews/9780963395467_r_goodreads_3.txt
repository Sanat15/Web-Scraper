Rating: 4/5

More adventures of the best comic book starring lawyers. In this volume we find Wolff &amp; Byrd, the counselors of the macabre deal with Nosferatu gangsters, the titular "Sonovawitch!" whose mother puts a love spell on his coworker much to her chagrin, a Rashomon-by-way-of-EC-Comics telling of an encounter with the monster Gormagon and a demon possessed by a really nice guy. Batton Lash continues his classic series with pun-derful names "Dekoo Kei" and slowly simmering subplots involving the interpersonal relationships of the main cast. Always a fun read.
