Rating: 5/5

I have wanted to read this book for a long time and when I found the audio book narrated by Michael J Fox himself, well that cinched it! This book was well written and I felt I learned a lot about Parkinson's Disease. Of course he talked about his life, family, and work life as well but talking about PD so candidly was heart felt. He speaks so honestly and I could listen to him for hours! He does not speak as one would think a celebrity would speak. He is very down to earth and truly likable. An amazing book, read it if you can but better yet, listen to him tell his story. A 5 star rating from me!
