Rating: 5/5

Found on the shelves of a used book store in Maine, this book was written to be enjoyed on a deck during your summer vacation. At last, I know that I am not merely a fan of The Great Bear, but a true Ursinologist. The scales covering my eyes have been lifted, and I better understand the wondrous messages Milne shared in the guise of a children's book.

I just wonder if Williams experienced much pain with his tongue planted so firmly in his cheek?
