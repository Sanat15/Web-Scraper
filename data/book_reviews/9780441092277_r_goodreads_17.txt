Rating: 5/5

By pure chance I came across Richard McKenna's short story Fiddler's Green when first published in an Orbit anthology and later in this collected works. It's the most influential story I ever read because it's the first time I ever considered reality and structuring alternative realities. Unsurprisingly I found Philip K Dick's many stories on this later on.
It's been out of print for decades so it's interesting that so many people have kept the small flame going. Also have wondered if any of the writers in Lost encountered it or more tantalising in the TV series Life in Mars, where ithe title s even mentioned.
