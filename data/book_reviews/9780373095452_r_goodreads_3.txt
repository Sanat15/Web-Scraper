Rating: 5/5

This is the first Nancy Bush book I've read. I decided to give it a try because she is Lisa Jackson's sister and I love her books. 

This story is certainly a roller-coaster ride for April Hollis. She finds herself young, pregnant and all of a sudden very much on her own. As she struggles to forget about her first love, Jesse, April learns the hard way the differences between being rich and poor, but realizes money can't buy everything. 

Ten years have gone by and April has got her life together until Jesse walks back in and shakes things up. Will the princess and the pauper be able to live happily ever after, or will they realize they truly come from two different worlds after all?
