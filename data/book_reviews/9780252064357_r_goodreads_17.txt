Rating: 4/5

good book, maybe overly sentimental at times. compelling, if over the top main character, didn’t love the denouement but was moved regardless.

i will eat up anything set in early 20th century new york, and yezerskaia illustrates the city—esp the tenements—beautifully.

and above all, this is a love story about beauty. lots of universal truth so tied to circumstance in sonya/the author’s pursuit of beauty. 

for a class, prob more thoughts later
