There are definitely many indicators. &amp; then there is the very problem that if you know you have a dangerous case on your hands, how could you detain them if they did nothing wrong yet they were a ticking bomb as it were? Fascinating concepts and thoughts. I enjoyed now as I am totally obsessed if it were with the authors work (playing on the title, see what I did there?) I am going to read the next one.
