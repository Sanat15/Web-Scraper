Rating: 4/5

Regency Spy

Highjinks in Devon where French spys are lurking while plotting devious deeds. Our heroine meets with Captain Jackson a smuggler and spy, but for which side? Her curiosity and snooping compels Jackson to expose himself. But can he trust her with the information and his heart? 

I enjoyed this very British, clean Regency Romance. The heroine is one of those intelligent, competent types who thinks they are right. Of course this is the moment when she is definitely the wrongest.
