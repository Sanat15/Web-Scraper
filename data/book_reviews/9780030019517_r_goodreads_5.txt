Rating: 4/5

An interesting read for me who knew very little about slavery in Brazil. I was surprised at the brutality meted out by the plantation owners and their children who were born into a world of entitlement that condoned cruelty to everyone considered inferior. I was hoping that Gregorio after himself being abused would transform into a wise and kind person but no, he ended his life having learned nothing apart from how to exploit any situation to his own advantage and to hell with the consequences for anyone else.
