Rating: 5/5

The end of WW2 is just about in sight, but young men are still being called up and the flying bombs are still striking London. We see two different social groups - the street traders, factory workers and housewives of the East End and Southwark, and the moneyed lot who escaped being arrested with Moseley but still have Fascist sympathies. At this point, German agents are still abroad but the supposedly sympathetic English have a fair idea who is going to win, and they want to take power after the war ends. 

As with most Bowling stories, the cast of characters is many and varied, but don't worry too much about the walk-ons and follow the leads. The tale starts with family fortunes and young romances but develops into a thriller as the two groups of characters intertwine. 
This is an unbiased review.
