Rating: 3/5

There's not much that happened in the book but, with the help of her family, Sister Bear breaks her habit of biting her nails.
The Berenstain Bears series have always been a personal favorite. This one that i recently read happened to not be one of my favorites. It being a children's book I feel like there was a lot going on, but they were simply trying to break Sister Bear's bad habits. The colors in the book are very bright and all the colors pop out. The illustrations bring more of a bright settle setting to the book and the lines are going everywhere so I don't think it kind of sets a tone for the book or not.
I personally wasn't a fan of this particular book, but I love the Berenstain Bears series. I would recommend those books to any child only because I know how much joy those books brought me.
