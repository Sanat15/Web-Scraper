Rating: 5/5

this is not a complete history of Nicaragua, which is what i had expected. in fact, it's an exploration of key moments, events, and transformations in the Nicaraguan timeline as told through its culture and politics. like how colonization upended native ways of life. like how the Pacific and Atlantic coasts have always remained separate worlds. like how Somocistas, Sandinistas, and others have reinterpreted and appropriated the country's national heroes, Rubén Darío and Sandino. it wasn't a complete history, but it was truly superb.
