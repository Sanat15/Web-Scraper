Rating: 3/5

The story was fun, but weirdly choppy. It would flow along smoothly for a bit, then magically jump forward by many months or maybe years with awkward transitions. People were very two dimensional. And a lot of the principal action lacked coherence and cohesion.

People invented elaborate schemes that didn't seem to make sense, but were not interested enough to spend much time thinking about.

However, this was never intended to be great literature. This was meant to be a light beach or plane read. So all in all, not good, and not bad. I wouldn't go out of my way to read more by this author.
