Rating: 3/5

Miss Bindergarten is a dog in an upright standing position like a human and it's so unsettling. I know that most of the animals in her class are usually on all four but there is something just so off-putting about the dog teacher in human form. 

Nevertheless, it's an alphabet book going through the names of all the students in the class and it just so happens that every name starts with the letter of what that animal is. Eg. Adam-Alligator, Brenda-Beaver, Christopher-Cat, etc. I guess, that's a good way of introducing children to other children's names but I can't get past the drawings.
