Rating: 4/5

I didn't realise until I reached the ending that the author of this romance novel is a man! However, he makes a pretty good fist of the plot which is a traditional story of the mid-1980s.

Leith, the heroine, blessed with a reasonable lottery win, retreats to the mountains of Tasmania (and who wouldn't want to retreat there, it's gorgeous) to buy a small farm and set up a pottery.

The hero, Mac, is of course macho, rich, gorgeous - was there ever a romantic hero who wasn't, bless their little cotton socks. Of course he has a superficial girlfriend with whom he puts up for no apparent reason, and who is prepared to stop at not much to achieve her aim of marrying him.

Leith's determined efforts to achieve the very best in pottery is excellently shown and is very interesting. I particularly enjoyed the description of how the process is carried out.

I didn't enjoy her rages and stubborn independence, which also appears to be typical behaviour in the earlier romantic genre heroines and tends to make my left eye twitch. Quite frankly, depicting her still a virgin at 26 is pushing the barrow out, as far as I am concerned.

Overall, an entertaining afternoon read!
