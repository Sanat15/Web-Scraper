Rating: 3/5

This book is a very intersting study of how Tolkien approached the extended worlbuilding of Middle Earth. The short story, left unfinished by Tolikien, has been edited and contextualized by his son. 

It is clear that Tolkien had a very clearly eastblished World already Set up when he thought of many aspects of this Story, despite it being written before The Lord Of The Rings. However, this Story feels more like a friend telling you about that one amazing holiday that they had and listing down their adventures as you try (and eventually probably fail) to keep up.

Very inzeresting for some behind-the-scenes Tolkien material or just really engaging landscape descriptions, not a story to be read for the plot.
