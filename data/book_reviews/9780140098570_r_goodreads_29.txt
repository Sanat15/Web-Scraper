Rating: 3/5

This is life seen through the eyes of the stock prude character of literature, indeed probably the most self-important and socially blind individual imaginable. In one sense, it is an acidic satire against prohibitionists and moral and religious snobbery.

Trapped by his astounding disconnection from practical life and an insular worldview mostly imposed on him, you can almost feel sympathy for Augustus Carp. Almost. The slapstick and madness is tickling fun in either case.
