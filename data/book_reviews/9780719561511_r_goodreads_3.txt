Rating: 3/5

I actually had the pleasure of meeting John Harrison while studying in the UK five years ago and was very curious about this book in particular because I’d been to Argentina, but never that far south. So I bought the book and got him to sign it, but never gave it a read until now. It’s the story of a man who is clearly passionate about two things - lost tribes of natives in the southernmost parts of the world, and ships and seamen. In any case, the book has a point. It becomes clear after a while and gets spelled out at the end. Part of me felt like I had to ‘get through’ this book but overall, it was an enjoyable read and he takes on important topics. The three stars are really more three and a half.
