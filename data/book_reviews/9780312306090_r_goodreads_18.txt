Rating: 3/5

A very forward-thinking pre-9/11 work. Mass surveillance as ubiquitous, the rise of homegrown right wing terrorism (notably Ballard predicts that Muslims and non-white immigrants will be the easy targets), the superficial tolerance of neoliberalism which barely veils an unrestrained class violence, and the increasing outsourcing of culture and ethics to an isolated class of political-industrial technicians. Eden-Olympia, the novels fictional Silicone Valley-esque setting, resembles precisely the sterilized think-tank-mandated environment from which have emerged our visionary free-market despots, the Jeff Bezos', Elon Musks, Steve Jobs' and Jeffery Epsteins of the world.
