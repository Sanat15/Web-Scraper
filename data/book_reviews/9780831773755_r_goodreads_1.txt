Rating: 3/5

A several page biography on each artist compares and contrasts their
styles and gives you a good feeling of what each went through to get
their 'feel of the West'. I liked Russell's comment, 'Why aren't I as
popular as Remington? We paint the same things.'
Interesting that neither had much if any formal artistic training.

 This book is a bit over sized at 12" x 9" which is good, plus 2 facing
pages are used for 1 picture. So the larger picture allows you to
see the detail as the expressions of the horses in dramatic moments, as
in 'Ride for Timber'. Both artists had a way of capturing the attitude
of the horse.

 Many of these paintings can be viewed in Ft Worth, Texas at the 
Amon Carter Museum and the Sid Richardson Gallery.
