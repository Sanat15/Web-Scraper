Rating: 2/5

I don't know why they even put Lila's name on this thing. It's really about stupid Elizabeth and stupid Todd.

But yeah, Lila fakes a boyfriend. I don't recall if I ever bought one, but I remember there being boyfriend kits in my youth where they provided you with all the tools to create a fake boyfriend. I seriously considered this option. Oh, the 90s. They were obviously the best. But, anyway, Lila is awesome enough to craft a boyfriend all on her own. 

Meanwhile Liz is being all weird and trying to become a poet by copying the language of old poems for her stupid lovegrams that she prints in The Sixers. Spoiler alert: her poems blow. Unfortunately, the Valentine's cheers that the Boosters deliver are not much better. They are better than Liz's fraught poetry... which is maybe saying something? When Aaron finally asks Jess to the Valentine's dance via a cheer, however, the rhyming takes a new low. I really don't want to spoil the hilarity by printing it all here, but I will say that there is a line that goes something like this: You can take a joke like a boy / you look like a girl. Ummm. Thanks, Aaron?

Oh, yeah, Liz and Todd. Well, Liz writes a secret romantic poem for Todd and puts it in his locker when he isn't looking. He's so delusional that he thinks he has a secret admirer and therefore must break up with Liz in order to be available to the ladies. He's such an asshat he goes from dating Liz one second to thinking every girl he sees must be the one who is suddenly in love with him. In the words of Cher (Horowitz, that is): As if! 

Lila is so amazing that her gardener comes to her rescue to pretend to be her made up boyfriend, Gray Williams. He even goes to the same school Lila said Gray went to. What a coinkydink. She originally has disdain for him because he's "the help," but when he shows up to save her from Unicorn embarrassment, she's all smiles.
