Rating: 3/5

A cautionary tale about being careful what you wish for...
About vanity and selfishness versus being a kind person, and getting carried away with your own ego. 

"I am a forgettable girl, thought Devnee, and this time the jealousy sliced her heart into thin ragged strips of pain."

If you want pretty privilege, 90s teenage girl body image angst realism with added vampires, this has got you covered. 

I feel like I've read every single teenage poetic way of expressing self-image angst and jealousy it's possible to read. More sort of mildly depressing than it is "spooky". This is barely spooky at all really.

The ending was a bit anti-climactic and felt slightly rushed / not particularly well thought-out.
