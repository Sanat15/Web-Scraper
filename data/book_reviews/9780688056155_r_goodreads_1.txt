Rating: 2/5

Sarah's Questions is a very simple book. The story is simple, the illustrations simple, the writing simple. That's nice. I wouldn't want every book I read with Julia to be like this but that's not something I have to worry about so I was able to sit back and enjoy this. 
It would have been easy for this to be more enjoyable for me but because of Julia's love of 'I spy' she also enjoyed hearing this. 
The illustrations are quite simple, like I said, and I most certainly would not use the word "breathtaking" but I can't say anything negative. 
I'm partial to mother-daughter books but this just isn't one of the best.
