Rating: 5/5

Normally, I don’t write reviews but with all the negative reviews I saw for this I felt the need to say why I enjoyed it. We should remember how private a person Freddie was, so there most likely isn’t much or any concrete evidence as to what “drove” him. Peter Freestone has laid out his experiences with Freddie to give us a glimpse into what life was like off stage without giving away his friend’s deepest secrets. While some consider the details boring I enjoyed feeling like I had a personal tour of his home at Garden Lodge when reading Peter’s description room-to-room. I appreciated learning of how much thought Freddie put into the interior design, his love of art, antiques, and tiny boxes. If you’re interested in learning the little (probably insignificant to most) details about this music legend then I suggest giving this book a read. If you’re looking for gossip then this probably isn’t the right book for you.
