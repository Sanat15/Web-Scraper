Rating: 4/5

I borrowed this book from Aunt Annika. I love to sit down with Mama and look at the pictures. My favorite thing to do is pretend to eat the paint on the page with the three paint tins. She makes a funny face and says, "Gross." This makes me laugh and laugh. I will do it again and again, until momma tries to convince me to turn the page. When we get to the cookie page, we pretend to eat the cookies. I like the book because Mama and me pretend like the pictures in the book are real things that we can interact and play with. This is a book that I like to pull from the pile and bring to Mama and Papa to read.
