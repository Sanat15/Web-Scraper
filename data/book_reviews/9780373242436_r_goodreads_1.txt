Rating: 3/5

Again a fairly average read. The hero is a doctor who rescues the heroine and her friend after an accident and just weave them into their extended family. The heroine takes a job nannying his brother's kids, we found out that the heroine is the daughter of the dead bro of course. What annoyed me was the heroine's fear of getting involved with the hero. She feared passing on her genes and held on to her fears for way too long. The hero was a sweetheart.
