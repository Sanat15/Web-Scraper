Rating: 5/5

This series is still one of my favourites of all time. I don't usually re-read books yet I have read this particular one three times.
Ms. Lansdowne has surely had very close dealings with parrots because her descriptions of Lord Nightingale's actions and habits are perfect and delightful. 
I consider the entire cast of characters dear friends of mine who I enjoy a good visit with. Sera is wildly imaginative and Nicholas a perfect gentleman, whether he believes it or not. Their story is nothing but the best kind of sweet with a generous helping of humour and just the right amount of intrigue. 
Even Mr. Spelling, the villain, as Lord Nightingale is wont to call him, managed to secure my support. I am glad Ms. Lansdowne extended her trilogy to provide him with a happy ending. 

If you are looking for an excellent romance, look no further, you have found one of the rare gems that can claim that title.
