Rating: 5/5

Today it is forty below
in my hometown of Fort Smith
in the Northwest Territories of Canada.
It is winter and I am cold.
Not even my long johns and parka
can help me today.

It is so cold the ravens refuse to fly.
My dog, Holmes, refuses to bark.
My dad’s truck, which we call
the “Green Death,” refuses to start
and I cannot go outside.

This was a great spontaneous read. I just finished Taaqtumi: An Anthology of Arctic Horror Stories and I enjoyed a lot about that book, while not really being into the horror parts (it's not that I mind the horror parts, they just don't... idk, horror simply isn't my genre). So I looked into the authors who contributed to that collection and searched for their other - non-horror - books. This book came up and since it's really short, I just read it right away and... aaaah, this book is so nice and pretty and just... The illustrations are beautiful and the text manages to be funny, kind and (a little bit) educational at the same time. I might get this book for my aunt (horse lover) for Christmas...
