Rating: 4/5

I wanted to read this immediately after reading the previous Little Wolf story. I loved the first one and thought this one had to be just as good! And I am so glad that little me was right!

I enjoyed this book just as much as the first one! After Little wol's uncle dies he gets the school and starts teaching the cubs how to be good adventurers. Aparently there's links to scouting in this but I never picked up on it as a child.

This time it is all diary entries, still with spelling mistakes, which I think are cute but I suppose could annoy parents, but I knew that there were mistakes and that a little wolf couldn't write properly because wolves don't have the same education as people, I thought that was quite logical as a little one myself. I especially loved when there were crossings out, because it made the entries much more realistic.
