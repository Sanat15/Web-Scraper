3.5
This is a new author for me &amp; I quite enjoy it. In terms of writing, the use of metaphors &amp; figurative speech were well delivered. I did like the culture mix, it was an unique aspect. But the plot &amp; revenge wasn’t all there; the first half of the story moved fast but the second half was uneventful &amp; draggy. 
Initially, I much prefer we didn’t get hero’s POV at all, because in my opinion, it took away the intensity of the plot, the supposed revenge, his person/aura &amp; even the romance. Now we know exactly what are his intentions &amp; thoughts, leaving everything kind of dull. There was chemistry but it translates to lust rather than love. Hence, the getting into his head fully tells me of his lust for her. It was the same with her, she wanted to feel those lips &amp; arms around her when she was staring at his photo right at the beginning of the story. The ending could have been better but yeah…
