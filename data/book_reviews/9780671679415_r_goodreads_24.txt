Rating: 2/5

While his autobiography Groucho and Me is a funny and interesting read that is done so much like his trademark style you can hear his voice on every page, this book should just be called “Bitter Old Man Shouts at Sky.” Beyond the obviously outdated ideas on gender and sexuality, he’s just plain mean a good portion of the time and comes off as if he’s complaining about everything. It’s not so much a chronicle of his romantic history as you might be led to believe either, as there are sections about Hollywood and politics and the good old days vs the then current now. There was one story that was somewhat amusing about a gentleman that cheated at cards and how Harpo and Chico got revenge on him, but the remainder of the book is a slog to get through.
