Rating: 5/5

Ok, so maybe not a "real" 5 star read- but if you are looking for a cozy mystery series to read I suggest starting with this one. This is the 3rd in the series, and I've read 1 &amp; 2 - and loved it. A fun, crazy, cozy mystery read- popcorn for the brain. A great read when you want something light, fluffy, and fun. So far I am very pleased and will continue to read the series (when I need some popcorn!)
