Rating: 4/5

This is an old favorite from the 90s. I remember picking it out from the Manderley romance catalog. Jennifer's story from mines to heiress always fascinated me, but it was actually her time with the gypsies that truly captured my little teenage heart. The romance storyline is a bit simplistic but clean. The mystery provides plenty of potential suspects to consider. It's a good, old-fashioned gothic romance.
