Gift from a friend | Surface | Because I love Wales, and all related, my friend decided to loan me this series, with the understanding that she loves the books dearly and would want them back. Later she decided that she wanted to make them a gift to me, because they couldn't have a better home. So kind. I therefore wanted badly to love them. I'm reviewing each as I go, I don't know yet how they hold together as a complete series. This one didn't do much for me. The unnecessary changing of Welsh spelling did not bode well for reliability in telling the story, and that holds true. The Mabinogion is just better than this. My main difficulty here, though, is Walton choosing to bring in an ancient Mesopotamian god, and focusing so much on East vs West, with a Mary-and-Christ vs heathen-eastern-fire-gods-from-the-desert metaphor. Walton basically put bits of Arthurian tales, Welsh legend, and random bits of Celtic myth into a sack, stirred them up with a Christian stick, and plucked out what came to a hand based in 1930s prejudices. I'm desperately hoping the rest of the series will either be more true to the source or more filled out as a narrative, else what will I say to my friend?!
