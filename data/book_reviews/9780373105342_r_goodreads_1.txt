Rating: 4/5

This romance might be problematic for some readers because both protagonists are cheating on their respective girlfriend/boyfriend. The heroine tries to fight her desire but succumbs. The morning after, she is guilt-ridden. The hero’s reaction is to make a cold-blooded proposal that sounds more like duty than love. 

Of course, KT writes with such depth, such complex character development, such a hot chemistry without using lewd or purple prose, such tension, such a wonderful sense of setting (the beautifully wild but claustrophobic island of Sculla, off the coast of Cornwall) that it makes for an unputdownable read. If you don’t mind flawed characters who live, breathe and behave like real and not altogether likable people instead of idealized romantic leads, and if you enjoy an intense level of angst and an author who can manage to keep you on the edge of your seat despite the predictable tropes of this genre, you will enjoy this book like I did.

And I must add that the concluding declaration from both hero and heroine but especially hero (who tells her the minute he met her, she lit a flame inside him, and it has never been put out...awwwwwwww) was absolutely romantic AND believable. I had no trouble believing in their HEA despite their troubled beginnings. As many turkeys as I have read from KT, this is one of her books that reminds me what an amazing, compelling story-teller she is. Really in awe of her talent as a writer. They sure don’t make Harlequin writers like her anymore.

Thank you to StMargarets for sharing this book with me!!!
