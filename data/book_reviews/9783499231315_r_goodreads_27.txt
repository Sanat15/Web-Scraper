Rating: 5/5

Another great novel from Jelinek which I loved a lot. The story of a policeman who enters relationships with women in order to exploit them and steal their property, and ends up killing one of them. The novel is either narrated by one of the victims or several (I couldn't understand it) and it's extremely confusing and difficult to read emotionally too, but it’s worth it in my opinion. The policeman is an entirely repulsive, unimaginative, and uninteresting person, and I believe him to be intentionally written that way: because most scammers killers are boring assholes in reality. The book is hard to read but it's a top notch portrayal of the worst of humanity and I think it's a great novel because of it.
