Rating: 3/5

Another unlikely story not very true to the times, but wonderfully entertaining nevertheless. I thoroughly enjoyed Lord Nightingale, Stanley Blithe, and Sweetpea and only wish that they might have existed or pets like them other than in the author's imagination. I can believe in a kitten that thinks it's a dog, since I once had a dog who thought it was a cat. That's the problem with raising a kitten and puppy at the same time. One of them tends to copy the behavior of the other.
