Rating: 5/5

"Passionate Scandal" is the story of Madeline and Dominic.
Firstly, a round of applause for Michelle *claps*
Very rarely we read a HQN romance where all aspects of our reading experience are satisfied- this book is one of them.
Four years ago, lovebirds Madeline and Dominic broke up due to her impulsive behavior, something that led him to publicly humiliate her and Madeline flees to her mother in Boston!
Now as her beloved stepsister Nina is getting married, Madeline returns to confront her old demons- only to realize her and Dom's families have become enemies, so much so that her best friend and Dominic's youngest sister Vicky cannot attend Nina's wedding!
How the new sophisiticated Madeline deals with the remaining embers of her love and reunites the feuding families is the story.
Both hero and heroine have loads of depth here. Both accept their mistakes about the past, and neither are unnecessarily mean in the present. They acknowledge the attraction, but the heroine fights it due to her old broken heart. The hero is kinda obsessive, the heroine has a backbone, there is some throwing of OW/OM on each other's faces as expected but their reunion is pretty damn sweet and I felt every secondary character successfully added to the plot.
A pretty damn enjoyable read.
