Rating: 4/5

I really enjoyed this historical true crime story. Set in 1799-1800 New York City, Levi Weeks is accused of murdering Gulielma ("Julie-elma" -called Elma by her friends) Sands and dumping her body in a well. His all-star defense team includes Alexander Hamilton and Aaron Burr (yes, working together - but for those who know - foreshadowing!).

Not only is the trial fascinating, but the underlying attitude toward pre-marital sex is also very enlightening - and not what you would expect. 

Anyone writing a historical novel in this time period would benefit from reading this as research into customs and attitudes of the time.
