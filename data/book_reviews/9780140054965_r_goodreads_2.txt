Rating: 3/5

Paul Theroux was one of my father’s favorite authors, and I had just been to Malawi (or was about to go maybe?) when I picked up this book that I’m pretty sure I found on the side of the road a few years ago. It…has not aged well. While it was interesting to read a (farcical) account of an American trying to navigate the political whirlwind that was Africa in the 60s and 70s, the plot is bizarre and the way Africans are portrayed is squarely awful. In all fairness, the American and other European/American characters are also pretty absurd and awful people, but it’s still tough to read. I’d still like to try some of Theroux’s more well-regarded novels and travelogues, but it’s going to be a hard sell after this one.
