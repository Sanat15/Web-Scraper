Rating: 5/5

I read this one, the first of the author's "Skylark" novels, in one of the summers between college semesters, if I remember correctly. Smith is given credit by many as the inventor of the space opera subgenre of science fiction, and this is his first instance. He began writing it, I have read, around 100 years ago. The copy I read was a 1958 Pyramid, revised edition - exactly how it was revised, I don't know. Regardless, it was a good romp of a read with the heroes, Richard Seaton &amp; Martin Crane making the discoveries which allow them to build a space ship. And there is a villain, "Blackie" DuQuesne, who they have to contend with. The book is a product of its era and should be read and enjoyed as such.
