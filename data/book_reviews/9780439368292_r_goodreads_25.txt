The young man and the sea by Rodman Philbrickis a realistic fiction book. ”Before I tell you about the biggest fish in the sea” the main character is Skiff. He had a boat in his backyard and his want to fix it and take it out to sea. And he convinces his dad to stop drinking and go fishing.Internal Conflict: Character vs his bully 
Explain: this affects his problems because he tried to raise money to fix his dad's boat but his bully destroys his crab trap External. Conflict: Character vs. his dad because his dad drinks a lot of beer and just watches tv and does nothing but tv and beer this affects Skiff because he has no one to look up to except his dad since his mom is dead. I like the book because it has a lot of action. And the ending is really good. I think people that would like this book would have to like action packed books. And any age
