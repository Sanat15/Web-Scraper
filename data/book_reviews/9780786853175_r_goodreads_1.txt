Rating: 5/5

The Birnbaum 2001 guide to WDW is a great basic book for your WDW Vacation needs.. It has nothing to detract you from any special goings-on at the park.. Nevermind that 2001 is supposed to be the 100 Years of Magic Celebration.. Hopefully you can find out about that when you get there..
Birnbaum's is a cute handy color-filled guide of all the basic info you will need for the park.. It breaks it up into sections on getting ready to go.. including accomodations.. the separate theme parks.. the water parks.. and other recreational activities (such as the many golf courses)..It also lets you know about the different rides.. and incredibley, they are ALL highly recommended!!!
I am being ironic here.. as of course, many Disneyphiles know that this guide is the party line.. The writers of the book no matter what they say, are in debted to Disney to speak highly of everything as much as possible without coloring the facts overly much.
If you've ever noticed the wonderful color photos, this is because they have permission by Disney to use images of the parks.. that no one else does, as well, the guide is published by Disney-owned Hyperion.. So you see the guide is looked after by Disney from start to finish.
Does this mean you shouldn't get this book?? NO! By all means, get this book.. if this is your first trip to WDW, or you like the color pictures.. or maybe your last copy of Birnbaum's got worn out.. (which is what happened to my multi-used 2000 edition)
If you want a comprehensive guide though, and can forgo the shiny pictures.. My top-knotch reccommend would go to Rita Aero's WDW guide.. She's got it all &amp; with an honest reccommendation for the enjoyment level of all ages of each ride... Another book I reccommend is the Passporter Walt Disney World...It goes way beyond handy &amp; useful!! But those two are for another review.. =)
