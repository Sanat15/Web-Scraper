Rating: 5/5

I really liked both main characters and especially Sharon even though she was immature compared to Ferguson. I have wondered about the notes sent to Sharon about her husband's Who sent them? Did Elaine send them? I think so as it was a way to break up a marriage since she wanted the husband. 

Ferguson loved Sharon but it was based on lust that he had for his young bride, who was a student, as they had little in common. Because Elaine was a lawyer and the two of them worked closely together, an emotional relationship developed. I truly feel that Elaine sent the notes to Sharon. Elaine was about Fergus' age so that was another thing they had in common.

Ferguson handled the situation among the three of them very badly as Sharon asks about his feelings for Elaine. He seems unable to use his lawyer skills to let Sharon know that she is loved by him and wants to remain her husband. 
He getsElaine but Sharon is rooted deeply between them. Ferguson is liked by the reader most of the time. 
How would he have handled Sharon's murder trial if Elaine had not died and they were living as a married couple?
Even as I read this book a second time I was always on pins and needles about how Sharon would treat Ferguson.
I diid not like Sharon giving up on her marriage to Ferguson and was rooting for a positive relationship during and 
after the trial.
I am always left feeling sad and pleased after reading Truly Married. Even so I give it 5 stars.
