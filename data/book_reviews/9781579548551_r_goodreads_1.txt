Rating: 4/5

When I took this book from the library, I had not heard of Joey Green. I merely wanted a book to introduce me to some gardening techniques. So I looked at the title and took it out based on that. I had some initial misgivings with the brand name products, but this book is informative.

Gardening Magic is alphabetical by topic. The book discusses things that come up when dealing with gardens. We get trivia and pieces of pop culture along with the advice.
