Rating: 4/5

This is a fantastic book for anyone interested in where American public education has been, is, and is going. 

This is the 18th edition of the book and Spring has done a fantastic job keeping the information relevant to the current educational and political climate. He takes into consideration both political parties stances on education during the 2016 presidential election as well as the current president's educational initiatives. 

However, where this book really shines is in the look back at education and how the past has effected and determined where we are now.
