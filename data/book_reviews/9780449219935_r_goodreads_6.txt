Rating: 2/5

Wish I'd seen the description of this book, where it describes it as "scintillating," before I read it. I liked the idea of the story, and have read other books by this author that were fun, light, regency love stories -which had a certain innocence while hinting at the idea of passion (but not going into it). This one stepped over the line right near the end and ruined the story for me.
