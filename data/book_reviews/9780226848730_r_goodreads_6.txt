Rating: 5/5

After The Haunted Monastery, this one is quite possibly my favorite.

 We find our hero, Tang-dynasty magistrate Dee on the pleasure capital called Paradise Island, where, as usual, he must deal with murder &amp; mayhem. As usual, one crime leads to the uncovering of others, and I love to watch the magistrate pick up and unravel every strand of mystery. It is also cool to read these stories &amp; begin to get a bit of a feel for everyday life in ancient China.

#9 in the series, so don't start with this one. Start with the first one. Recommended for people who want something different in their mystery reading; also, I think anyone who likes historical mystery should enjoy this series as well.
