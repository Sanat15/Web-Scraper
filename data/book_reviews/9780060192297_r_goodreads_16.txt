Rating: 3/5

This is mainly a detailed intro into the world of manga comics, illustrators, publishers, and the underworld of manga enthusiasts in Japan with side plots about strip clubs patronized by young Japanese women, who are not as innocent and demure as we think, and the workings of Japanese yakuza gangsters. Too many tangents and sidetracks make the story line herky-jerky and overlong - could have been 50 pages less. Not as good as the early entries in this series with Rei and boyfriend, Hugh; now Hugh has been written out and Takeo is her boyfriend, and Rei is more irritating and irritable instead of comedic.
