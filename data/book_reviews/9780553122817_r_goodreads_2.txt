Rating: 3/5

Major Rex Daviot enters a marriage of convenience with Quenella to ensure her escape from the German Princeling who tried to taker her for his own. But it’s not completely selfless. Quenella comes with enough money to help pay for Rex to take a position in India that will allow him to continue his spy work for England. Rex has no trouble at first with Quenella’s frosty attitude toward him, understanding that she needs time to recover from what the princeling tried to do to her, but he’s worried that she may always remain cold and closed off because of the trauma. However, travel heals many wounds, and it’s through exploring India and studying its culture and religion, Quenella realizes that she has a lot of love and (dangerous) adventures awaiting her in her marriage to Rex. 

The romance was actually pretty good, but the bulk of the book drowned in political intrigue. It was hard to get through at times.
