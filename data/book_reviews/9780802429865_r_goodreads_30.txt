Rating: 5/5

This missionary memoir by Gladys Aylward was short and to the point, but it packed a punch. Reading about her relentless and courageous faith was both moving and convicting.

My biggest takeaway was how incredible the Lord was in the ways He answered her prayers and moved in mind-blowing ways. Also, it was inspiring to see how truly she believed what she was praying, asking her petitions with conviction and faith and resolution to His will, whatever His answers would be. My constant thoughts throughout were, “Would I believe those prayers she prayed if I were in her shoes? How can I pray more like that? How can I produce abundant fruit for the kingdom of God?” So deeply convicting and eye-opening.

I’m so thankful for missionaries like Gladys who have paved the way for so many and who are a reflection of faithfulness and fervency for the gospel of Jesus Christ. If it weren’t for missionaries following God’s call to difficult places, my family may have never heard the gospel.

Loved this book, would reread and would recommend!
