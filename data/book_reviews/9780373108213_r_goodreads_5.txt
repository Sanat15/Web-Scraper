Rating: 3/5

Another old skool goodie where the couple can look back on when they were first falling for each other by reminiscing how he slapped her a couple of times for her own good...and to give him satisfaction. First of the story is saturated with him making sure she knows her and her shanky brother are far beneath him. Later, he faults her for not believing in him. Heh. Has a little of all the good stuff: wrist-gripping alpha hero; strong but easily melted heroine; catty but pathetic OW. Kept my interest.
