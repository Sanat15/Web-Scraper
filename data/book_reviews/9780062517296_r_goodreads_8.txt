This book was good. With helpful ideas. One book that I liked even better is called, "I'm a Widow, What Now? Embracing Life after Loss" by author Patricia N. Muscari. This is one of the first grief books to challenge the unfortunate &amp; totally unnecessary ‘5 steps’ of grief. I’m a Widow, What Now? is unique; it can move you through your grief and into contentment much faster! This is the real life experience of the author; nothing is ‘manufactured’ or borrowed. http://www.yourfuturewithin.com
