Rating: 3/5

He was, I learn, a good friend of PG Wodehouse, and Plum frequently namechecks him in his Jeeves books. Bertie has read a few of his books, and Aunt Dahlia to use one as a missile for ticking off young Bertie on more than 1 occasion. In honour of that, I read 1 of Stout's books, though not a hardback that Aunt Dahlia might have wielded. He is still in print, amazingly, a niche following for his faithful recurring detective. This isn’t 1 of those, however. It’s excellent fun; fairly direct stuff, high pace, spare prose; not over-serious, but not mocking or cynical. The crime in question seems highly improbable, but the book is carried off with vim and energy, so you’re swept along perfectly willingly
