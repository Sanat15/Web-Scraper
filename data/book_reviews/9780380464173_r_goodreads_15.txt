I particularly liked the bit about Shakespeare, the story of Lord Mortimor (who believed himself a herring), and the Wimsey family political policy, which held there should be a Wimsey on either side in any political upheaval, so as to make sure of the family's survival, as well as help out any fathers or sons who may have become political prisoners. Extremely cynical, and eminently sensible. 10.12pm
