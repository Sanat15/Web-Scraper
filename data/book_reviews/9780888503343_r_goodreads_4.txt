Weeding my gardening books. This is a big, heavy book, listing over 1000 plants with color photos of each. There's also general information on designing an herb garden, and the history and usage of herbs. I bought it as a gardening reference, but the listings don't include information on cultivation (sun, when to plant, soil type, etc.)

Still, this seems a useful reference and is beautifully designed. 

Verdict: Keep.
