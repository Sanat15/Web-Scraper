Rating: 4/5

I'm having trouble deciding if it's a 3 or 4 stars book. On one hand, the author is not a writer and it shows. It felt as though I was reading a diary,in no particular chronological order. It's a little chaotic and too many details (I get it, he loved interior design, I liked the description of his house, but I didn't want to know about each and every door knob and small object). Also, it took me around 100 pages to really start to enjoy it. On the other hand, I really liked the fact that he portrayed Freddie as he was, from a very personal point of view, with ups and downs, the bad and the good. This was my first Freddie Mercury biography and I really feel I got to know him a little bit, from Peter Freestone's perspective. The last two chapters are hard to read, I cried almost the entire time reading them.
