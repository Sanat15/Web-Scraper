Rating: 5/5

Phoolan Devi, referred to as the queen of dacoits by her companion.

This book narrates the tale of a girl who, like any other girl in her village, was born into a low-caste family but exhibited a greater spirit of rebellion than her peers. She stood up against the family disputes and the oppression inflicted by upper-caste men in a patriarchal society. This defiance led to her suffering torture and abduction, ultimately resulting in her joining Vikram Mallah's gang as a dacoit.

Moreover, it illustrates her fight within the dacoit world, her quest for revenge following her husband's murder, and her journey to establish her own gang. Even after her surrender, her struggles continued.

This book highlights Phoolan Devi's tenacity, rebellious spirit, desire for vengeance, and unwavering determination.
