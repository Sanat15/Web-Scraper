Rating: 3/5

Though I did find this story quite interesting, it was much harder to get into than her usual books. With 7 kingdoms and so many characters, I really could have used some sort of map and/or character index to figure out who was who in the beginning. Towards the end, I really thought this was going to be the start of a series because it seemed like there was so much left to do, but then somehow it all got wrapped up pretty well.
