Rating: 1/5

DNF at 82%

This is the most horrible piece of literature I have ever read!! This review is mostly rant about this terrible book! 

Now, don't get me wrong. I absolutely loved the first book, "A Wrinkle In Time", it's one of my favourites. But this one, this one brought me torture more than my period cramps do!! 💔

Now let's talk about the story, I loved Charles Wallace in the first two books, he was my favourite character. But you know how they say, too much of everything is bad? Well, that was the case with Charles, I liked him when he was given to me in small quantities but this book was all about him and I started hating him! And I missed Meg so much!! 😭😭

This was also the most complicated piece of literature I have ever read. Tell me, while reading a 304 pages long book, will you be able to remember the difference between Maddox, Mad Dog, Madoc and Maddock? What about Zyll, Zylle and Zillo? And what about Gwen, Gwynn, Gedder and Gwydyr?! NO RIGHT?!! Then why did the author do it?!!! And trust me when I say this wasn't the only complicated thing. 

The only interesting and exciting part of this book was the stories of the people from the past I just named above. There was nothing exciting about the present characters or time. Other than Charles, we barely got to know about the other characters and what was going on in their lives. Hence, the bad things outweighed the good things. 

You wanna know about the writing? I'll tell about the writing! In one word it was... Awful! It was impossible to understand!! 😫😖
