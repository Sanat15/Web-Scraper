Rating: 2/5

Well, I was bored. Something about these two being together turned me off. She was an ice-queen, but why is he as dry as a stick on the side? Good grief, they both like to smoke but no smokes at the scene, ha, pun intended! No chemistry and no tension. She smokes and he smokes, and I don’t know what the author was smoking because it all fell through the cracks. Get it? Got a lighter? Because cigarettes are props. She waves one around and he offers one like a prize. They smoke to flirt, to grab attention, and for sophistication. Okay, I exaggerate, but it was just lousy and poorly executed. Definitely not attractive or a good look in my book. Not to mention the gambling opening scene did nothing for me. It felt lame and uncool. More like an (unclaimed) addiction.

Normally, I ignore the smoking and such lifestyles in these vintage as it supposed to scream he/she is very sophisticated but like I said, these two together wasn’t fire. They were like “Dumb and Dumber” and “Ash and Ashtray.” So, not this time, Janet Dailey! Oh, not this time.
