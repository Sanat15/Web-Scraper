Rating: 5/5

My, my... what a great final book to The Texas Star Trilogy! 
 I loved the entire set up of this book! Everything was perfect! It was a little bit over the top with so much love, but I liked it. It made it feel like a fairy tale. 

 Having Serita and Giddeon (main character's from Texas Gamble, and Marina's grandparents) in this book teaching their grand daughter about love, was absolute genius. I loved seeing how involved they were in Marina's life. I do wish that Stella and Logan (main characters from Texas Dawn and Marina's parents) were in this book as well, but I guess I could live with them only being mentioned. 

 The amount of history in this novel was an overload of information. There wasn't too much, but I was so overwhelmed, I couldn't take it all in, so I am probably going to reread this book again just to understand it all a bit better. It did make me feel so grateful that I live in this day and age, not in the Aztec era when they sacrificed people in disturbing ways for very stupid reasons. 

 A very satisfying historical romance read that I definitely would recommend.
