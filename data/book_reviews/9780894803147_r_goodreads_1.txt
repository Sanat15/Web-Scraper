Rating: 5/5

This little book I'm sure made its way into my home when my children were young. Filled with color illustrations, it describes 24 different bugs by showing an illustration of each and describing its behavior, what it eats, etc. There are also many activities given for young people to try, along with capturing and observing some in a bug box.

It impressed me that this book, which bills as for ages 10 and under, would make a good starting point for a science fair project.
