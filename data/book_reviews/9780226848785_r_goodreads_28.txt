Rating: 4/5

Two related stories, both taking place in Lan-Fang. The first is quite elaborate, the second a lot shorter. Both stories contain 3 cases, as usual. These cases are intertwined and the conclusion is as unpredictable as always. For the first time ever, I started taking notes to better understand and remember all characters and clues. This provided me with more insight, but I was still unable to unravel the mysteries at hand.
