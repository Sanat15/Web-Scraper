Rating: 5/5

Its too bad that this book is so hard to find. I had to get it through inter library loan and there was only one copy in all of Alberta. 
If you like animals in your fantasy stories then you won't find many authors who do animal communication better than Doranna Durgin. Changespell is a continuation of the story of Jess, a woman who started life as a horse, Dun's Lady Jess, and now spends time as both horse and woman. If that isn't enough of a trial to deal with, there is still a forest full of evil magic wielders, some mysterious connection between mundane and magical realities, and a some very challenging romantic possibilities. 
I enjoyed this book from start to finish.
