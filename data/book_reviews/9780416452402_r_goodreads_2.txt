Rating: 3/5

The Bears Toothache is about a bear that wanders upon a little boy at bedtime. The bear has a painful toothache, and is trying everything to relieve it. With the boys help, the bears toothache is soon relieved and the bear leaves to continue his life. 
The fairy-tale aspect of this book lets children react and use their own personal experience to extend their imagination and put themselves into the little boys shoes. What would you do if a bear wandered into your room?
