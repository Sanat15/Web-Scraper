Rating: 4/5

SPOILERS AHEAD 

Four short stories. 

Smart Aleck Kill. A corrupt politician. Johnny Delmas the P.I is working for a for a film maker but quits. He then finds the filmmaker dead. It was made to look like suicide but all wrong. Sutro a politician being blackmailed killed the film maker and tried to make it look like suicide or a smart aleck kill. In the end Sutro is killed by his wife. 

Pick-up on Noon Street. Pete Angllich an undercover narcotics agent finds a film star called Vidaury hires a crook to set up a fake acid attack on him to generate publicity. The crook Fritz Waltz plans to blackmail him but is killed with Vidaury wounded but now able to fet genuine publicity. 

Nevada Gas. De Ruse a smooth gambler is set up by his girlfriend. A lawyer is murdered using cyanide gas in a sealed rear seat in a limousine. De Ruse escapes the same fate and finds out the lawyer and his wife were behind the murder. De Ruse is saved by a hotel detective with a bulletproof vest who kills man and wounds the woman. De Ruse goes back to his girlfriend and forgives her. 

Spanish Blood: Delaguerra a detective investigates the murder of his friend. He gets suspended and uncovers a conspiracy but it not as it appears. The victim’s wife murdered him for having an affair. A shootout at the end results in the conspirators both dead and the corrupt Police Commissioner surviving.
