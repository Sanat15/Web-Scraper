This isn't really a book you can review, nor is it one you should let negative reviews sway you on. It has to be read to see if it's for you. I definitely went into this thinking it might be ridiculous. But the more I read the more I appreciated it and had some lightbulb moments brought on by it. There are some great connections in here. There were also some things addressed in this book (like the story from the Bible [Judges 19], here called The One Who Was Cut into Pieces) that I've never seen addressed anywhere else though they have bothered me for ages.
This book is one of the first spiritual books I've read that I felt justified the page count: it definitely made use of the length without too much repetition of old information like others in the genre. A lot of books can feel like the author had only a handful of things to say and tried to stretch into a book's length, but Patricia has absolutely plenty to fill it out.
