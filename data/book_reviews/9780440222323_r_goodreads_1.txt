Rating: 3/5

Bryson LeFort has been incarcerated in Revolutionary France because of an illness at 18 that affected his memory. When all 7 of his brothers and sisters are dead of various means, his mother has him brought back and arranges a contract with a local girl to marry him, be his duchess, and produce an heir. Not knowing his true state, Annalise agrees. When she meets him, they're attracted, but he refuses to speak - trying to recoup his memories and figure out what's going on. She's scared, especially after her virginity is taken after she's drugged by his mother. She escapes to home, but when she discovers she's pregnant, her father gets them married. All appears to be getting better until she's kidnapped by the French Revolutionaries who have been systematically killing the family. Bryson conquers his fears to rescue her and expose the plot. All ends well!
