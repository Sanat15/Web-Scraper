Rating: 1/5

I read this book that I found at my grandma’s house and I was curious if it was actually good. Spoiler alert: it’s not great. The description was intricate in parts that it didn’t need to be, and I couldn’t really get a full visual of what the Delta Queen would look like and it’s pretty prominent as a setting device. Also, the twist that Leslie is dead? That was probably the most interesting thing in the story. Chance and Selena’s relationship was also quite weird and it was just not for me.
