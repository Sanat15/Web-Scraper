Rating: 4/5

I’ve read a few of this author’s books at this point. They all have pretty similar themes but are generally quick and well written. 

This one had themes of grief/loss. The concept was neat. Some of the imagery was a little scary? There’s a little girl dying of consumption, so maybe just something to be aware of since it’s a book for younger kids.

But the length and pacing were right and I liked it the whole way through.
