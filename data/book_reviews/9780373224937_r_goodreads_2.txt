A Father for Her Baby (Lost &amp; Found)

Kit was running for her life and for her son. She had seen something that wasn't supposed to be seen by anyone, a murder. She knows if she is found that she'll not only loose her son, but her life. So far she thought that she had a safe haven, for the time being, the thing is ,you can't stay anywhere very long. Then she got into the wrong limo, and ran right into the man who just might save her life. This was a fast paced, keep you on your toes, great read!!! Great Read!!!
