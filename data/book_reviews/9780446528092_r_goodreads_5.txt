Rating: 5/5

I really love this book so far. I've been searching for a book that has some common sense ways of helping me to deal with some of my medical issues. The authors ( one of which is a doctor) spent many years of trial and error, using themselves as genie pigs, before they updated the current version of this book. Having worked in toxicology, chemistry labs and in Hazmat I understand the importance of maintaining a proper PH balances with all things. This book goes into how you need to alkalize your body in order to maintain a healthy Ph Balance, where by you will have good health, loose weight, wart off diseases ect... In fact diseases can only live in an acidic environment. So if you alkalize your blood and your body, then you create an environment in which all known diseases cannot live in. 
The authors identify that what we eat is the main determining factor of the PH in our blood and bodies. Sometimes the environment can also effect our ph. 
I could really relate to one of the opening statements by Shelly when she said "I'm so sick and tired of being sick and tired." 
Although I've lost a lot of weight on my own in the last couple of months,especially by working out with my friend Ashli at the gym, going on walks with my daughter and eating smarter.I still think there's something missing that I'm not quite doing right.This book seems to have some unique, unconventional answers as I scan through the future chapters I haven't read yet, like Alkalizing supplements, alkalizing exercises, alkalizing whole body cleanses, along with alkalizing our bodies through better diet choices.

I think If I practice some of the concepts in this book, in combination with my other book, "Low Fat Living Today"...That I could really get my health back under control once and for all.
