Rating: 3/5

This novel? Not Updike’s best as it’s erratic. Featuring two unfinished novels inside one, the story is a traditional Updike sex romp set in the mid-1970s (Ford). The randy college professor Alf is writing a biography of a virgin president (Buchanan) while carrying on affairs with several women on and about campus. Memories of the Ford Administration is at times clever and well-intentioned, but ultimately a bit absurd and pedestrian.
