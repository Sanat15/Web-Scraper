Rating: 4/5

This is where a 1/2 star would come in handy. I rated one of her other books 3 stars and I like this one better but I'm not sure it's quite a 4-star book for me. 

Nickie suffers from post traumatic stress from an incident with a "funny uncle" when she was fourteen. She is now grown up but still having issues and because of a loan taken out by her late mother, can not get away from the man. When Nickie's father dies, he leaves her a bed &amp; breakfast in Maine and that means that Nickie must arrange for a leave of absence from her New York City publishing job and go to Maine to sort things out. Her father's lawyer, Clay Parnell, helps her to settle the estate and then some. 

I have liked what the author does with the detail and atmosphere of the setting in each of her books and I wouldn't have been the least bit disappointed with the setting in this one except that I have vacationed in Bar Harbor myself and have read all of Mary Roberts Rhinehart's books.
