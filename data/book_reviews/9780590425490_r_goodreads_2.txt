Rating: 5/5

I'm kind of shocked my toddler managed to sit through this whole book. Again, maybe not the best kids book unless they're into baseball but even though I grew up around baseball I didn't know most of these terms so that was fun. I also like the modeling of "I don't know what this means -&gt; let's guess -&gt; let's look it up to check if our guess was right -&gt; let's practice using this term."
