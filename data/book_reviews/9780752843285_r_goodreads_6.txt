Dodie Blackstock was only eight years old when her mother was kidnapped, and her body was never found. Dodie is the daughter of a multimillionaire and became estranged from her father, but when she is 29 she is informed her mother’s body has just been found. Dodie returns to the family home and tries to piece together the fragments of her mother’s life and come to terms with her own troubled past. An interesting read, with interesting characters, and a fascinating plot.
