Rating: 4/5

Times are tough in Bugville. Spider and his friends can't afford a grand Thanksgiving feast. At first, they can think of nothing but how to get a turkey on their dinner table. However, a chance encounter with a scared turkey fleeing a butcher shop teaches Spider, Ladybug, and Fly a valuable lesson about the true meaning of the holiday. Agreeing that the stuffing is the best part of the dinner anyway, they celebrate with the very thankful and relieved turkey as their guest of honor and newest friend. 

Silly, childlike illustrations and a madcap storyline make this one a winner, with an upbeat humane message that won't turn off mainstream readers.
