Rating: 5/5

Anyone who talks about alien abductions is considered loopy, not a serious person. For this reason I have avoided books like this. 

I mean really, people who are abducted claim to be abducted not just once, but over and over and over again. 

This book by Professor Jacobs is well-organized and well-written. And he convinced me that yes, alien abductions are probably happening. But this wasn't a book just to make that point. That is the starting point of the book. 

Why are they doing it? Professor Jacobs hypnotized over 700 people who had abductions, and through hypnosis noticed some common themes. Let's see: the aliens have a breeding program. They take sperm from men, but mostly are more interested in women, and inserting fetuses in women short term, and then they take them out, and put them in jars. I know this sounds crazy.
Many women abductees spend quite a lot of time holding hybrid babies, part alien, part human. Many children abductees spend a lot of time playing with this hybrid babies. These hybrids are starving for affection, for families (as they have none). 

I won't take it further. I don't want to spoil it for you.

But the author has a section on how hypnosis, bad hypnosis, along with media representations of aliens can affect what is gleaned from a session. He goes over the arguments of skeptics of recall from hypnotic states. He also has a chapter, which I found fascinating on the history of the UFO researchers' themes regarding aliens. Either they are here to help us from killing ourselves, or maybe they are gods and leading us to a promised land. That is the common one we hear now. The author doesn't buy these threads at all.

If you want a relatively easy read that will bring some turmoil to your ordered mind and worldview, read this. It is a great book.
