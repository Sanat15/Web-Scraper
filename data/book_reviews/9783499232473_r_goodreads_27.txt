Rating: 2/5

Updated review 12/4/24

Disappointing. Proof positive that a Maine setting is not always enough to save a so-so book. 

The sentences are often lovely, but so are the colors of insubstantial cotton candy, which disintegrates quickly to reveal nothing but a paper stick at its core. 

I just couldn’t engage with the characters or plot. And I was annoyed by the lack of quotation marks for dialogue, which is pretty easy to parse in some novels but definitely often ambiguous in Minot’s style. (Where does the speaking end and the narration begin? Often hard to tell. Some folks will think it’s clever, but I become irritated with this kind of authorial game-playing with the reader. “Read my mind,” the author seems to say.) 

But I didn’t finish it, so what do I know? DNF ~ 40%
