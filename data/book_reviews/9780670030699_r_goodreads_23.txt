Rating: 3/5

I appreciate this book for putting me on to 5-HTP, which has really helped my depression. But I think I also would have gotten there with my naturopath eventually, so if you can afford to see a naturopath doctor I'd recommend that instead (or if you're in WA state where some insurance plans cover naturopaths as primary care physicians!).

This is a good book to recommend for people who are struggling with mental health issues so much that they can't bring themselves to get professional help yet. Supplements (done RESPONSIBLY) can help bridge that gap to make things better enough that someone could then start seeing a therapist and/or doctor/naturopath. Depression too often convinces us we're not worth that kind of care, so that's the big scenario where I think people can benefit from this.

The quiz is also great for highlighting the things that probably affect us more than we realize. Since reading this book I've been able to notice that most of my mood stuff can be traced back to my estrogen dominance, and it's good to know that so I can eat liver-supportive foods and such. So overall a useful resource but not the be-all-end-all.
