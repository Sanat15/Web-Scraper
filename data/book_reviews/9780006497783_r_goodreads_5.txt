Rating: 3/5

It was readable &amp; overall enjoyable to do so. Not much in the way of structure, plot or red herrings, but since it seems someone wants to dynamite the State Visit of a Russian nobleman to the Cairo of 1908 but no one knows who, it qualifies as a mystery. Pearce loves this era and it shows, as he has a humorous writing style, the light touch evident throughout makes this a pleasant read.
