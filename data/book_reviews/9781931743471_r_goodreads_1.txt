Rating: 4/5

Mark is a new professor at a private college where some of their rules are different than from public schools. The students are accusing him of harassment even though they don't have proof. Although Lillian is retired, she is using her professor title to her advantage in working with Mark. Because of this accusation against Mark, Lillian's granddaughter (Mark's girlfriend) Sandra kicked him out from their living arrangements from her condo. Again, without proof of these allegations. 

Fortunately he has Lillian who believes him and goes to sleuthing to prove his innocence. 

The ending of this one could have been better.
