Rating: 4/5

This collection of essays was an illuminating exploration of poetry from a number of different angles: the broad purposes and philosophy of poetry, the historical development of several poetic styles and schools, biographies of several individual poets, line-by-line analyses of poems that broke down the poetic and linguistic elements to a more granular level than I've seen, and finally the backstory behind one of the author's own works. While it took me a while to work my way through the book, and some chapters were farther afield from what I was looking for initially, it was all rather fascinating and felt like being back in grad school (in a good way). 4.5 stars and I'm glad I already picked up his Next Word, Better Word.
