Rating: 4/5

Not quite as good as Book 1 ("City of Ice") but still
exceptional. I was not fond of a long section early
on in the book that focused on the 'criminals'; I
enjoy following the police detectives and how they
investigate and figure out the crimes. Cinq-Mays
and Mathers are a good team and the unusual group
of Canadian bad guys (the mob, "Hell's Angels",
and the indian groups) give this a unique flavor
of reading.
Highly recommended.
