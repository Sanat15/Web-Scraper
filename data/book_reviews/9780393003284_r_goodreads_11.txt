The five stars is not about the writing style or lack of it. It is about a topic rarely written about. You can say whatever you like, but I would ask you which other history analyst are you quoting to compare with as to dismiss its analysis. It is a early work, but when you read all the following books, you can acknowledge the importance of this one. I read all his books, but specially this one I wanted to reread, and only for one reason; you can understand the present by understanding the past. I appreciate Owen`s Prologue to this edition.
