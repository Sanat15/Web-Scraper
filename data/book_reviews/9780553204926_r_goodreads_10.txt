(Read in ebook, which isn't listed.) Interesting combination of mysteries, but I'm realizing that I'm not that fond of Tecumseh Fox. Also that Stout got better at interesting characters as he wrote more books, because the characters around Fox are bland and dull and tend to blend together. Every time he mentioned the names of some of them, I had to remember that, yeah, they were characters in this book, and I couldn't remember a thing about them.
