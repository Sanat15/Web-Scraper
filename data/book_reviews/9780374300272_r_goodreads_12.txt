Rating: 1/5

By far the worst in the series. The book contains all the flaws of the previous books (repetitive dialogue, indecisively bland two-dimensional characters, and characters that act alternatively both much younger and older than their ages) but fails to have any of the concepts or ideas. Another issue is the fact that some of the characters, the grandparents here seem to have forgotten all the events in the previous books and don't even feel like the same characters.
