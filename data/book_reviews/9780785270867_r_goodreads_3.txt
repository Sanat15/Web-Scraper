Rating: 1/5

For me this story really fell flat. This book started out really interesting and I was eagerly waiting for the climax, and then when it came it really didn't feel like a climax at all. I felt like all the characters were stressed and worried for no real reason because there wasn't really anything wrong. There was so much written about the characters inner turmoil and not enough about what was actually going on. I found myself not really sure what was even going on in this book.
