Rating: 3/5

Best friends Dylan and Whitney had a one night stand and unfortunately the next time Whitney hears from him, she is pregnant and her is set to marry someone else. Out of all the Mindy Neff books this one is my least favorite, there are so many things just off, not right. It was still a decent read, but it could have been so much more enjoyable to read.
