Rating: 5/5

A most moving lecture, this wee book packs a powerful punch to the brain. How can you truly help a starving person if you yourself are well fed? Is it possible to even come close to achieving empathy? All it takes is effort, active participation, intimate participation. Ms Hellwig relates this concept to the Passion of Christ: how can we truly live through the Passion if we didn't experience crucifixion ourselves? One didn't have to have done so. All it takes is the act of allegiance, commitment, and the effort to include one's self in participating. Don't experience the Eucharist because you have to, do it because you want to. If you ask me (and Monika Hellwig, for that matter...well, in order to ask her, you'd need a ouija board), passivity is far too widespread in church-goers these days. Aside from having memorized all the replies and lyrics to the hymns, how many devotees really listen to the words anymore? Next time you enter church, vow to not sing, only listen...I mean really listen to the words. I promise you, you'll learn something new (and you didn't have to memorize it). If only I could have met Monika Hellwig. She is such an inspiring author. I envy my professor of theology for having been friends with her.
