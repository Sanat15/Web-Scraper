Rating: 4/5

I was surprised at how the lives of closeted American servicemen before the turn of the millenium can still offer startling parallels to my experiences a quarter-century hence and a continent away. Had been in a few highly unique encounters and I was baffled to come across mirrors of them here. I guess it's just as our French bros say: plus ça change, plus c'est la même chose. And yup, I googled that shit up lol. 

Even if the interviews turned explicit and raunchy, they never became downright vulgar. Didn't feel like Zeeland disrespected or objectified his respondents so kudos to him. I also picked up a few tidbits that I reckon I'll be exploring soon heh. Wish me luck.
