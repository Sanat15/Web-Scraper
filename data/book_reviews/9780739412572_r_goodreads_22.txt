Rating: 2/5

This was just okay. I read this as a buddy read so it's not the type of story I would normally pick up, but still, I was hoping it would be better than it was. The author told the story at a snail's pace, and Sarah came off as cardboard. Then, even though the author teased that there may be some intrigue  Sarah observed Levi and Lydia together and had suspicions that Levi was more "experienced" than he was letting on and might NOT be a virtuous suitor for Lydia after all. I thought that this might develop into a side story and introduce a plot twist and a more interesting story line for Lydia. Wrong. The author never went anywhere with it; she just dropped it altogether. Why even bring it up then, if you are not going to follow up on it? , everything is predictable and at the end, is tied up with the tidiest little bow you ever did see.
