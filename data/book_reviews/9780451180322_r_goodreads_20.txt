Rating: 3/5

I usually love Nancy Thayer but I did not like this book. It did make me glad that I did not have children. It's three stories- a child, Julia who wants to be a wife and mother and not have an accomplished career as her mother did. Diane is an artist who creates beautiful Jewelry- but she's married to a man who manages to dump all the responsibility for the children on her through passive aggressive refusal to take on the household work. He works in his laboratory and that is what matters to him. The dialogue between his wife and him goes on and on and started to irritate me- which the author may have intended because it certainly irritated Diane! But there was too much of it. Jeanne, the grandmother, had a romance with a Russian spy who had her steal a formula from her father's safe. This formula was hunted for by an FBI agent who Diane falls in love with - and there is clearly an affair in the works here. I felt the book set women up for romance scams- the man who Jeanne fell in love with was still in love with her 2 generations later. And of course his wife had died, so they can have a real relationship. Same with the FBI agent and Diane. The FBI agent's wife had died. Although the book showed the inter generational relationship of the daughter, mother, and grandmother, it was too filled with cliches and coincidences for me. On the other hand, it helped me through a boring day and I think it was an OK read.
