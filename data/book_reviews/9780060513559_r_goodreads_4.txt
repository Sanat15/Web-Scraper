Rating: 5/5

I bought this book when I was 10 in 2003, It amazed me then and I still love it now that I'm 22. If you wonder what you'll see inside basically it shows you around 3 pages of the work of an artist(each represented by the faces on the cover) and then a step-by-step section with pictures of their proccess making one of the works featured. I think is great for anyone, but I feel its ideal for teens and pre-teens who are thinking of pursuing art as a career or just enjoy it very much as a hobby.
