Rating: 4/5

Despite its size and technicality, I devoured this exceptional book over the past week. It’s maybe the most interesting and approachable reference book I’ve ever read, and I can see how it was *the* resource for runners before the internet. My edition was 2002, which I think is one of the more recent publishings, so I had to take a point off for some sections being heavily out of date; chief among these are the chapters on shoes and the necessity of orthotics.
