Rating: 5/5

The history of Vietnam is long, bloody, courageous and still being written as we near the 2030s. Written through her own relocation and the memories of her relatives, starting with her great grandmother and grandfather, Duong Van Maj Elliot retells the epic story of Vietnam from the 1800s through the early 1990s; the colonization done by the French, Japan’s influence and brief ruling, the rise of the Viet Minh (Cong) and America’s influence… and lack there of. 

The reader will learn about how the French suppressed the Vietnamese, the mandarins, the Japanese influence, the rise of communism and those who joined to liberate Vietnam as well as the corruption of leaders, the fall of Saigon and more through the eyes and memories of Mai’s family.

A long but worth while read 🇻🇳
