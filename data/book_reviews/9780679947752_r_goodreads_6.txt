Rating: 5/5

This book: A bit of a treasure.

Almost a reverse sleeping beauty, with great scenes, fairy-tale characters, and a fantastic journey. Many picture books can't compare with this plot or the fine artist, and I would recommend it to anyone who wants to bring home something wonderful and extraordinary for little girls. 

I looked for this book for what felt like AGES over the internet, trying to type in key words from my memory and not ever knowing the title or the author. I remembered the illustrations and a bit of the plot, but that was not enough to help me find this bit of treasure from my past. I visited the libraries and the bookstores in search of it, and reaped absolutely *nothing* -well, maybe a few new books, but when I *still* couldn't find this particular one it drove me crazy! So finally, when my family made the 9 hour trip to visit my childhood friends and family I made the short stop (while we were there) to my old elementary school. The library looked the same...except smaller, as I had grown much, much taller. I cannot describe to you in the right words the utter sense of joy that I felt overwhelming me when I pulled it out of it's hiding spot after hunting through the section I had remembered it originally being in, pulling out books one by one in steady search of it. I was so lucky that it was even still *there*, let alone in the same exact section. I immediately bought it over the internet that night, while I was on my vacation. If you collect beautifully illustrated children's books, or enjoy fairy tales, as I always have, then this is a book I would highly recommend. It may not be worth a nine hour drive and an endless search to you, but it held a lot of love and memory for me, and that is why I took the compassion I had for it with me on the year-long hunt. It would make an excellent movie, and why it is out of print, I do not know. It is a gem buried among today's rocks.
