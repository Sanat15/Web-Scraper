Rating: 4/5

Helpful Book on Purpose of Evil and Overcoming Through Prayer

Several years ago, I read his book entitled Destined For The Throne. It was very thought-provoking and helpful. This book in the first 2/3 or so is a reprise of that book. The author is heavily influenced by E.M. Bounds and that is a good thing. The shared premise is that God does nothing apart from the prayers of His people. Billheimer carries it a step further by affirming that the purpose of prayer is to train Christians in conquering and overcoming evil which prepares the Christian to rule with Christ in eternity, ruling and judging with Christ. It also has helpful insights on spiritual warfare. 

The last part has to do with his views on healing and more spiritual warfare. However, the last section on spiritual warfare is primarily a biography of Rev Billheimer written by his wife. There is some insight to be gleaned, but I was somewhat disappointed and felt the section was misnamed.

Overall, I enjoyed the book and thought it was very helpful, especially the first part. I recommend.
