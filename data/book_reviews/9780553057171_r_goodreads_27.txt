Rating: 5/5

Mr. Mandino’s book, A Better Way to Live, is an Intimate, honest and inspirational view of a man who looked at life and made hard choices. His turnaround, motivational and inspirational books, and view of life are amazing.

his book is full of anecdotal evidence showing that we can initiate positive changes to live in better ways The Profund thought to love others as if today is their last day on earth and living as if it is our last day, awakens the heart. The author is a great storyteller and has a flair similar to Mr. Zig Ziglar. Great quotes, side stories, maxims, and life lessons are a beautiful gift to the reader.
