Rating: 3/5

This book follows the dual plot-lines of an elderly couple living out their last days in the desert, and a pack of coyotes seeking a new home due to a drought. The couple have named the coyotes, with the main canine character being called Brand X. I found this a silly name that made it difficult to take him seriously.

The animals do not talk, yet seem to have a kind of instinctual spirituality. Brand X is seeking the legendary Skywater, a sort of coyote paradise that he simply knows about without having learned about it in any way. This results in a kind of partial anthropomorphism – these are not just mindless beasts, but neither are they fully sapient beings. I would definitely have enjoyed it more if they had talked, as this would have fleshed out their personalities more and made the spiritual elements seem less jarring and unlikely.

The book is mostly well written, though there are a couple of grammatical mistakes, and at one point “sluff” is used instead of “slough.” It was a decent read, but it had potential that it didn’t live up to.
