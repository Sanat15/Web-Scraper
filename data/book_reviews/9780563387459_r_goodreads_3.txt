Rating: 4/5

Allison Hargreaves was a mountain climber. She summited Everest and was killed attempting a summit on K2. What makes this book so interesting in the mountaineering collection of books is that Hargreaves was also a mother of two small children. The book is about her husband taking their children to "mum's last mountain". It brings all of the controversy and double standard of perceptions of women as mountaineers and can they be mothers at the same time. Heartfelt and thorough, an interesting read. I am fascinated by mountaineering, and collect books regarding the subject and this is one worthy of adding to my library.
