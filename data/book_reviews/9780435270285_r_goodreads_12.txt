Rating: 4/5

A boy and a cheetah living lives along parallel lines, both runaways, both rebels, both prone to getting into trouble…
It took me a while to get into this, probably because I imagined it was a boy meets wild animal, they become best buddies etc, which it is not. Cheetah and boy coincide, affect each other, even help each other, but mostly they live out their own story lines independent of each other. 
Good, but slow and subtle rather than heart poundingly exciting. I liked the descriptions of the area, the way of life and the people. 
First book in a trilogy following the further adventures of its human hero Smiler.
