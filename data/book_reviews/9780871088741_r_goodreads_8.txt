Rating: 4/5

I wish I could tell the author that I loved his story but he has “already (become) something else, something new.” He’s probably already come back. “…not as (himself), of course, but as life, the life (he had) led.” This would be worth reading again for a lesson in needing less, grounding myself more in the land, and being patient with others who have experienced things that may never be known. I hope his boys have inherited his love for the land, trout, turkeys, and old men.
