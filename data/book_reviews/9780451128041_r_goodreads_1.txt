Rating: 3/5

This was entertaining, if a bit over the top, as the h, Sarina, goes a bit overboard in keeping distance between herself and the H, Janson, (believing he'll never commit) even seeming to prefer a middle-aged Chinese man over him! (Long story there, involving other characters.) 

Sarina has some mega melodrama, as she's kidnapped, escapes, hides her identity, works in a brothel (but only as a hostess), gets addicted to opium, nearly gets addicted to alcohol, gets pregnant by Janson, thought her baby son was stillborn, discovers he's alive and has been adopted, stalks the adoptive mom and the baby's nanny, gets a job as the nanny when both the adopted mom and nanny die (how convenient), FINALLY gets convinced that Janson cares, tells him about their son, refuses to give up the charade in case the adoptive father (a grieving widower) gets angry, then he, too conveniently dies, and Sarina, Janson and their son get ready for a HEA!

Way too unbelievable, but still entertaining.
