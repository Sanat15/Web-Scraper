Rating: 3/5

I think i prefer Janet Dailey`s Silhuette romances more than her Harlequin stories. "The Bride of the Delta Queen" did start off interesting with the red-haired heroine Selena Merick joking to a bunch of men that she is "expensive" in her night services. The man sitting near that table was none other than the cynical hero Chance Barkley who takes her words seriously and forces her into his arms. Selena nearly didn`t manage to escape him, but when she does she thinks she never will see him agan. Alas she does, and feels trapped when she knows he is aboard on the steamboat to Mississippi.

This could have been a lot better. I mean the start was quite dramatic and Chance had no reason whatsoever to treat Selena like that, even if she was "a lady of the night". He had none right whatsoever to judge when she gave him NO apparent reason for it since she never even said anything remotely rude to him. In many other stories there usually is a solid reason for heroes to be so darn pissed and vengeful especially when heroines insult them to their faces at the start of the story. But in this story,none, zero. Chance had no special characteristics to him either that truly sets him apart from many other heroes, there was something cold about him i can`t describe. In that way i felt there was no reason for Selina to actually fall in love with him (although it took quite long time for her to fall for him). I don`t know, this was a average read.
