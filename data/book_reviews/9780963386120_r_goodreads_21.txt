Rating: 4/5

It’s nice to hear someone articulate the challenging behavior I observe everyday in my kiddos. It makes me feel validated and empowered. I’m glad my husband and I read together so that we can back each other up when our kids act a fool. Didn’t love the section on eating &amp; rewarding with dessert only once food is eaten but I’ve also read more about that than this book dips into so - like any parenting advice - we’ll take the parts that work for us and leave the rest. I can see us revisiting this as the kids get bigger, too, bc some issues (like getting kids to do homework) just don’t apply… yet :)
