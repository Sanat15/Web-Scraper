Rating: 5/5

This was an excellent book. It held your interest and kept you wondering what would happen next. There's humor, mystery,horror, intrigue, and drama all through the book. The Dakotas had a very hard life in 1862 which caused hatred between the white people and the Dakota Indians. For Ellen, Simon, Genevieve and Daniel, their life was very rough, but they still lived for God and won others for him.
