Rating: 3/5

When I first picked up this book I was thrilled with the information it contained. But I've since found that it really helps to have a teacher or mentor who can help teach you the various techniques (leaving the body, scrying, using a pendulum, working with the aura, etc.). I understood the book much more once I began clairvoyant training. The information in the book is good, but I found it difficult to apply/try the exercises as a complete novice working alone.
