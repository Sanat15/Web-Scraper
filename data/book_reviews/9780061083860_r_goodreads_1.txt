Rating: 4/5

Madeline Smythe, at the age of four and twenty, became an orphan. Raised in Canada, her father's last wishes is that she returns to England. When she arrives, she finds herself a 'lady' with an inheritance and is expected to marry Jaimie, the impoverished Earl of Hampden. Instead, she is attracted to his older half-brother, Captain Titus Evan Rhodes.

Here starts the matchmaking game. Jaimie secretly wants young Della. Almira, her mother, thinks Madeline will suit him better. But Titus covets Madeline. And Madeline has strong feelings for the Captain. Except, initially, they don't let each other know this. 

Wow, there were so many twists and turns in this story but it was written in a fun way. Witty with some refreshing dialog, Madeline and Titus develop an unusual 'friendship'. 

"A serious argument is what you want? All right, I'll oblige."

"Your mother is too young and lively to be stuffed away in a dower house."

It was true. Grudgingly, he admitted, "You are right on that score."

"Thank you."

Moments go by when she says, "You have had far too much to drink."

"Not half as much as I plan."

She set her jaw, and he braced himself for a barrage of insults. Instead, in a swirl of green silk that matched her angry green eyes, she left him standing there in the drawing room. Madeline gives as good as she gets.

It is a shame but the best that I can tell is that Ms. Morrow only wrote this one romance. I would have enjoyed reading more from this author. AN INNOCENT RUSE is an enjoyable love story with two very likable main characters.
