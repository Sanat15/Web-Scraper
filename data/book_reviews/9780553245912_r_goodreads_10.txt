Rating: 3/5

3.5 Stars

This is the first book I have read by Georgette Heyer. A lot of bloggers I follow, including Nancy Pearl, recommend her books as comfort reads- so I decided to try this one on audio.

This is the first book she wrote and I can’t say I found it exceptional, but it interested me enough to finish. I gather some of her others are far superior. She did write this one when she was 19 years old.

This book is set in the 18th century- in the 1750’s. We meet Lord jack Carstairs, who was banished by his father, the Earl, 7 years prior, for a cheating scandal. We find out pretty quickly that he took the blame for his younger brother. He has become a highwayman, but one with principles- think Robin Hood. One day he saves the pretty Diana from being kidnapped and of course love follows.

There are sword fights, duels, ladies swooning and being coquettish, family interplay, balls- just what you would expect from a Regency Romance. I will say it is not heavy on the romance- that is almost secondary in this book.

I did listen to this one and I can’t say I loved some of the voices the narrator used for some of the characters. I may try some of her later books at some point, but I won’t be making them a priority.

Published: 1921
