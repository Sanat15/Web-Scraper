Rating: 4/5

Daniel, who is a Viscount sends his sister to the school which is run by Guin/Elaine and her aunt. When Daniel and Guin meet he does not realize she the person in charge of the school. Guin has a scandalous past which she has kept hidden. This book has an evil villain, and enjoyable secondary characters and a hero who made me mad sometimes. But the book was very enjoyable.
