Rating: 3/5

The book I’m reading is called “The Night Journey” by Kathryn Lasky. The book is based on her aunt's own story. The author wrote a beautiful tribute to everyone who survived through hardships and shown great courage during a very dark period in human history. With rich details about the people, places, and animals that Nana Sashie saw during the Russian pogroms, Kathry learns about the history of her family. In the book, it tells the past and present Nana Sashie tells about her time when she was Rachel's age, showing the close tie between a great-grandmother and her great-child. For me, there is too much character in the plot, and the writing is rather flat and off track. It fails to express any authentic feeling that you could connect with. Nonetheless, this book is an excellent starting point for educating young children about Russia's pogrom past.
