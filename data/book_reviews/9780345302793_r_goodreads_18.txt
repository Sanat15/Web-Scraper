Rating: 3/5

The edition I read was, I think, the first paperback edition. It was the first book I read by her &amp; I'd call it a SF Romance. I really liked it, although it is kind of a hackneyed plot. Alien abduction &amp; a Prince riding to the rescue. I can't really give it 4 stars, although I'd like to because of the place it holds in my reading - an early step to her work.
