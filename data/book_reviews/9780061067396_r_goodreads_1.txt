Rating: 5/5

Now this has to be my ABSOLUTE favorite book read as a kid. I read this book so many times, of course crying each time I read it. Although looking back, I would've given the book thumbs up just for being about a girl named "Star". She was a memorable, loveable character. I wonder if it still exists somewhere in a box or the back of a closet, I would love to read it again and see what I think of it now.
