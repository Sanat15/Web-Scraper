Rating: 4/5

The Fifth Annual Collection gathers 28 stories from 1987, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1987") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Pat Murphy's "Rachel in Love," Bruce McAllister's "Dream Baby," Robert Silverberg's "The Pardoner's Tale," Octavia E. Butler's "The Evening and the Morning and the Night," Howard Waldrop's "Night of the Cooters," with strong appreciation for Michael Flynn's "The Forest of Time" and Dean Whitlock's "The Million-Dollar Wound."
