3.5 Stars
 rounded up to 4 Stars. The ending, for me is what saved the story. The chapters concerning Mariah Ellison were way too drawn out. She is an unsympathetic character even after the reveal of her past since she retaliated with verbal and mental cruelty towards others. Two long chapters devoted to her plight were in essence just a bit too much and in no way changed my opinion of her. She still remains an unsympathetic character.
