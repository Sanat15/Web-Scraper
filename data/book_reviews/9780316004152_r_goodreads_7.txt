Rating: 4/5

Fascinating look into the unique individual who loved the desert and southwest canyon country probably more than anyone is history. Has some offensive views and controversial opinions, but overall, it's a great read and has some good wisdom and humor. If you like Paul Theroux stuff, you'll enjoy. I just don't understand how he kept getting women and how he got away with just going off of 100 mile walks whenever he wanted leaving newborn kids to be tended to girlfriends and grandparent, but I guess he figured the ladies knew what they are buying when they signed on. The fact that he decries the tourism in Arches back in the 1950s makes me laugh; he is probably glad he's not alive for the selfie era.
