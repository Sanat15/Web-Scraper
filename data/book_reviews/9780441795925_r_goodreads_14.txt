Rating: 5/5

Talking to dragons

It was sad to not have Cimorene but the book is full of adventurous and funny moments for Daystar, Shiara and the dragon, when they find the princess and the knight, that was especially funny.

SPOILERS

16 years have passed, and Cimorene baby is now a 16 year old young man called Daystar, so when Antorrell the wizard shows at their house, Cimorene gives Daystar a sword and send him to the enchanted forest to find his destiny, there he meets a young fire witch girl called Shiara, a young Dragon, and in ther path met Morwen, Telemain, several wizard who they fight, to finally reach the Castle of the King of the forest guarded by Kazul and the dragons, where the final battle between Wizads, and Dragons and the enchanted forest is to happen.

So the final chapter is called “Which contains a wedding, a feast and a happy ending”, and I was so sure it was going to be Daystar marrying Shiara, kind of mirror like to Cimorene and Mendabar, but oh surprise its someone else getting married.

5 stars
