Rating: 3/5

If you like Georgette Heyer, you'll like this one. Witty, humorous and a good narration style, what more can you want in a light romance?
Taken under the wings of their aunt, Kat and Jacko, the twin, and their sister Maria are expected to make a good catch on the marriage market, which, due do a scandal in their past, they will need. Unfortunately, Kat is not your usual young lady, behaving as society expects. Things just happen to her...
I loved this story and will definitely try to see more of this author.
