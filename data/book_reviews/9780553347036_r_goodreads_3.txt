Rating: 3/5

Nope - not a self-help book but a novel about a teenager building dreams that she almost believes. Her father is absent and her mother lives with her 'current knight in shining armor' leaving Esme with no model for what her life should be. Her dreams become her reality until reality shatters them.

Moves through about 8 years though often it's hard to figure out her age since she lives in her dreams as a young woman even though at the beginning she is a 12 year old. Comments about school - she often drops out - and jobs - she often quits - give some indication. While not a great read, the story kept interest - wondering who or what would happen to help her.
