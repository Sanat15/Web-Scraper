Rating: 3/5

This is one of those romances where if the characters would only communicate, talk to each other, so much unhappiness and jealousy could have been avoided. Jennifer and her sister are supposedly close, so why did Jennifer just assume that Sheila and Logan had something going on, even while Logan was obviously pursuing Jennifer? Why wouldn't Jennifer ask her sister, as soon as she met Logan and saw how close Sheila and Logan were, why wouldn't she ask her sister about him? But no, she had to wallow in her love and misery until literally the last three pages of the book. But then I guess if she and her sister had ever actually talked, the book would have been a short story instead of 183 pages.
