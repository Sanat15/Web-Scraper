Rating: 3/5

As a biography this was okay. The aspect of this book that shocked me was the extent of segregation in America prior to the civil rights moments of the '60s. I always thought the Jim Crow laws were relegated to the Southeastern part of the US but this book showed it to be the law of the land. What a dark time for our country and it was only two generations ago. Sad to see racism rearing its ugly head again in America.
