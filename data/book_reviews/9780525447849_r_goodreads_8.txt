Rating: 4/5

Pooh, Piglet, Owl and Christopher Robin experience an endless downpour. 

The four of them interact to reach Piglet in his home during a rain storm. 

Piglet had been worried about the weather and felt that he needed to be rescued.

He starts out by sending a help message in a bottle in the hopes of it reaching his friends.

A male centered children's book from times past.
