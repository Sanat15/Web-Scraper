Rating: 3/5

This is a great resource for new parents that are overwhelmed with the idea that their kids are going to need to read and hopefully enjoy it. Reading books together and sharing a love for language with your child is essential for academic success and an enriched life. 

My hesitation with this book is the titles it provides. The titles are out of date. The books they mentioned are great, don't get me wrong, but check with your child's teacher or local librarian for more current titles to hook your kids. Bring these out after they're hooked.
