Rating: 5/5

For every Star Trek fan, a must. The trials and tribulations (yes, even with Tribbles) of getting the show on air in an era of TV with few avenues. The book is all about TV production with all its' challenges of budgets, deadlines, and censors (no, not sensors). The things you couldn't show or say on TV then may make us scoff today but maybe that's not such a bad thing. The original series had many educated and articulate fans and although it only lasted three seasons, it was enough to spin off generations (pun) of TV shows and movies.
