Rating: 4/5

Imagine being one daughter among a family of four girls and three of your sisters are secretly plotting to overthrow the dictator of the Dominican Republic in 1960. In the Time of the Butterflies is inspired by the true story of the Mirabal sisters (Minverva, Patria, and Maria Teresa) who were assassinated after visiting their jailed husbands.

This powerful book opens with the recollections of Dede, the fourth and sole surviving sister. Alvarez's masterful storytelling combines fact and fiction as she weaves a tapestry of each of the sister's lives and advocacy. 

Alvarez is a prolific author: 6 novels, 3 nonfiction books, 3 books of poetry, and 11 books for children and young teens. In 2013, President Obama awarded Alvarez with the National Medal of Arts for her extraordinary storytelling.
