I - the apology of socrates -- august 2021
currently trying to get through a review-writing spree -- it is very tough on my nerves.
this book was a compulsory summer read for my humanities class, and - although i did not particularly enjoy it - it proved to be one of the most thought-provoking essays on the list. 
despite the fact that socrates' points and his explanations were sometimes arguably comical -- "you NEED me", "i am as right as mélétos is WRONG", ... -- and the parallels he established (particularly the one about the illiad) had a questionable consistency, he still made his point and defended himself gloriously. 
and yes, he was still executed. but think of this way: it allowed plato to steal his words and financially benefit from this final apology. what a legendary friend.
