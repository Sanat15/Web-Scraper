Rating: 5/5

What a delightful read! The art looks like fridge gallery work from a class of kindergarten students, and details of the story are crowd sourced. It feels like many hands took part in helping this book come to be, along with a lot of love. 

If we're to keep it going: The most beautiful thing I know about horses is that they have the ability to PLOP down on the ground and ROLL around! I've seen it once in person (years ago!), and the memory still fills me with amazement and glee.
