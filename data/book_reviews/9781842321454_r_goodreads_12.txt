Rating: 5/5

This is the kind of historical fiction I loved as a teenager. I was invested in the story from page one. Although the time, late 14th early 15th century was far more brutal than this book suggests, I appreciated the details of the religious orders, the guilds and the social culture of the day.
The characters are wonderful. The pacing is excellent, no soggy middle. I look forward to the rest of the series. 
Thanks to my Goodreads friend Diane for the inspiration to pick this up off my TBR shelf.
