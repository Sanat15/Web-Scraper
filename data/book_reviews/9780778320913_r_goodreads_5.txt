Rating: 4/5

This is the first book I’ve read by this author. It was very good with
Down to earth characters in a small town setting. Claire Wilder comes 
To the Goodnight Motel and town to find the graves of her grandparents.
She leaves her old life and job behind in the Hope of finding herself,
A life and something she like to do. Claire is a helper and gives of herself
To others and falls for the Motel and people she meets and befriends.
Recommended
