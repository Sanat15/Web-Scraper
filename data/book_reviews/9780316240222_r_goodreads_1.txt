Rating: 3/5

This is a fun and colorful book about the circus. About halfway through the book, I started wondering what the title had to do with the story, but at the end, it makes sense. It's a cumulative tale that is rather simple, but the illustrations make it fun. The characters reminded me of a fun website for little ones that I used to go to when our girls were little, called http://www.kneebouncers.com/
