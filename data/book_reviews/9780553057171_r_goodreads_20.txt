Rating: 4/5

Who would've thought that at one point in his life, one of the world's leading inspirational authors, nearly spent his last few dollars on a suicide gun? 

 A Better Way to Live is Og Mandino's own personal story of success, and for the first time ever he describes the joyously redemptive process that turned a down-and-out alcoholic into a millionaire and a happy man within 10 years. In this book he shares his 17 "Rules to Live By" that helped turn his life around.

One of the factors that gave Og the idea to share his backstory with his readers came in the form of a young woman asking him to sign one of his books for her after one of his lectures. The young lady said to him that he made achieving success seem too easy, this was probably because he didn't endure any hardships or trials along the way. This struck Og deep, and he immediately worked his backstory into his succeeding lectures so that he will be more relatable, and has now found it's way into a book.

It has been said, that when you read a book, it's akin to being in the same room as the author. All the information you get, is like the author personally teaching and sharing this information with you. This is what makes this book special. Og personally gives you a tour of his house and invites you into his private work space where he will tell you all about the 17 principles to a Better Way to Live, and in the classic Og Mandino style: you read 1 teaching/lesson for a prescribed period (1 lesson per day for this book) before moving onto the next, while integrating what you learned prrviously to the current lesson. Thus building good habits and replacing bad ones.

I love Og's writting style and how he is able to weave a tale (fiction or non-fiction) so immersive and still gets to teach you valuable lessons. It's always a pleasure reading his works. It's just that this book is not suitable for very young readers due to certain themes. Parental guidance is advised.
