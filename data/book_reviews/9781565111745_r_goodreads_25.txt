Rating: 4/5

When I was growing up as a kid in the country, Babe was probably my favorite movie. I must have watched the VHS in our family room dozens of times. It really was a lovely movie, with a truly outstanding cast (James Cromwell! Magda Szubanski! Miriam Margolyes!). Dick King-Smith's children's novel, which the film was based on, was equally as lovely. A short and sweet tale of perseverance and the value of difference and respect and dignity towards others.
