Rating: 3/5

3 stars is perhaps a bit generous - I don't know that this story has aged well. I don't know how or why but this ended up on my Kindle (and had the dubious honor of being the book on the TBR Kindle list the longest so it got picked up recently). It's a very stereotypical romance in many ways - love at first sight, lots of barriers between the couple even once married etc. My biggest issue with this type of romance is a lot of the conflict wouldn't exist if the couple would just have a five minute conversation with each other. Also, the heroine, who every one calls brave every five pages, falls apart into tears about every five pages too so...it got old after awhile.
