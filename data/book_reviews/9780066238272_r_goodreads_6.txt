Rating: 5/5

Santa's favorite cat is a beautiful white one who follows him around. She loves to snuggle in his lap and hide away in his pocket. One Christmas Eve she slips into his pocket as he leaves in his sleigh.
All is new and beautiful to the cat. She hangs farther and farther out of the pocket until she falls to the ground. Now she is lost on the streets of New York City depending on Santa's final cry that he will find her.
Every Santa she encounters isn't her Santa. She is lost and tired. Finally she curls up beneath a Christmas tree.
Although all of the illustrations are excellent, the cat is gorgeous. Some of the city scenes show New York landmarks such as the Chrysler Building.
