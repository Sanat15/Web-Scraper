Rating: 4/5

Very much a book of its time. Specially in the newer mystery genre. Although you'd think the characters would be hard to keep up with, having such non-English names, I found even small characters had a flaw or mannerism that set them apart. I didn't figure out the killer but I only try to solve mildly but at the end see if all the pieces fit.
My only real problem is treatment of women. This one wasn't as bad as some others of this period in the 60's. But the snipes about women drivers and such weren't really unnecessary for the story
