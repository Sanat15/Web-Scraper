Rating: 3/5

Believable 

I enjoyed the character Lady Mary. She went through her early life and survived despite her father. Her fault if any was being a trusting soul who thought the best of people and suffered extreme betrayal by this belief. Now we come to old Jack the supposed hero character in this book. Jack was and is a complete degenerate with not a single idea in his whole body of any goodness. He is the type of person whose outlook on life is only for himself and will never change. He will profess love to Mary, marry her then go his merry old way. Mary's only hope is to deny through the marriage settlement any use of her money without her express say so. That's the only to keep a snake and pervert like Jack in check. Jack ruined the entire book for me and will for any reader. Therefore I cannot recommend this book to anyone.
