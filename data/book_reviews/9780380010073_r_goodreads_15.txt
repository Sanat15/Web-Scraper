Rating: 3/5

25 percent gripping drama shoehorned into 75 percent CSPAN. Long stretches of this book include parliamentary procedure and endless lists of names, political offices, and minority and state affiliations and they often don’t add much to the otherwise interesting plot. This just might be me. Some other reader might find that gripping and not repetitive and unnecessary as I did.

The book was at times also infuriating to me as it portrays a government with honor that takes to task its few dishonorable elements. Today’s Washington would embrace the scandals and nastiness, probably brag about it. It shows even with its underlying disfunction, how government should work. Unlike our current situation.

Read it for its Pulitzer-ness. But not for entertainment.
