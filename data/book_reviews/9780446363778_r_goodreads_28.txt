Rating: 4/5

This book was very deep and insightful dive into the life of Jim Morrison . I enjoyed learning about him as a person and getting an idea of what was going on in his head that inspired him to write his poetry and songs. Unfortunately you will also learn that Morrison was a terrible person and seemed to getaway with a lot of the egregious things because of his looks and later his fame. You also learn that his parents that he also treats very poorly sent him money all the time before he became famous so he could live a carefree beatnik style life.
