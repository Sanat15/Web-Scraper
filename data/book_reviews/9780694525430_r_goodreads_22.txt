Rating: 5/5

The fourth Baudelaire book is good following one of the weakest in the series the wide window. 

The orphans are sent to work at a lumber mill. Yes, I said work! their new guardian who is only know as boss. Has made a deal with Mr. Poe that he will keep count Olaf away in exchange for labor from the kids. Lumber mills are dangerous especially if there are kids working in them. Well there was accidents and a death. Klaus is hypnotized by the town optometrist. Of course the awful boss is unable to keep away count Olaf who has a great disguise that I won't reveal. Sonny almost dies in a swordfight. Klaus has to build a invention. Violet has to read for research. will the kids succeed or will count Olaf gain the Baudelaire fortune. Read the miserable mill and find out.
