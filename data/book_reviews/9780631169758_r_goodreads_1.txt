Lewis begins by asking whether there was more change or continuity in women's psotions.

3 important social trends:
1)increased % of married women in employment, but substantial wage gap, and no increase in male unpaid work (i.e. helping with home and kids)
2)huge increase in divorce (especially in 1970s and 80s), mostly petitioned by women.
3)huge increase in illegitimacy, and also high female and child poverty.
Were these changes a result of women's choices or constraints?

A very highlighted points:
-Public expenditure levels had risen steadily, except on education and housing.
-1949 granting of Legal Aid meant a spate of divorce, where prior couple separated informally.
-1980s committment to "walking back" the State.
-The rise in abortions after 1967 had no effect on illegitimacy rates.
