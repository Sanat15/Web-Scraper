Rating: 3/5

3.5, rounded up

My toxic trait is that I love a super trashy epic sweep mass market publication. The Lucky Santangelo series by Jackie Collins is an all-time fave. I got this book for 50 cents at my library's used book rack and well, it was worth 50 cents! Maybe even two dollars. It was written in the early 80s so it's a little racist and misogynist but so were the eras of history being written about. It was the perfect palate cleanser after several serious memoirs.
