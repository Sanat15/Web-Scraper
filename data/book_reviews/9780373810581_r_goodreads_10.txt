Rating: 5/5

Excellent story that engaged. The book itself is old, written during the 1980s or 1990s, and there are moments when it shows. But it does not diminish from the underlying story. There are a few more typos than should be excused, but I assume that is part of the process that turned it into an ebook and none were truly disrupting the flow of the story.

The story itself was exciting and yet also plausible. I'd pass it along.
