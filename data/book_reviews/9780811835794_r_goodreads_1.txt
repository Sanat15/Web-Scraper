Rating: 3/5

American art in the 1930s and 1940s was some of the best, stylistically and thematically, depicting those industrious Americans seriously hard at work: making steel, tires, assembling pumps and motors, building dams and schools, stringing telephone wires, drilling oil wells, firefighting, mining coal, running railroads, building airplanes, shipping tanks and fighting Fascists—you name it, we did it! 
 
Those were great days, and I certainly would like to make America great like that again. 
 
The murals shown in this book were part of those saved by people who cared in the greater Chicago area. Although some themes are repeated in different forms, there are many unique and timeless depictions here, which one hopes Americans still have a taste for. Do they? I know I do.
