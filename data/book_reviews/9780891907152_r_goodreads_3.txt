Rating: 2/5

Frank G. Slaughter wrote this. The man lived from 1908-2001, seemingly a pretty good span of history to live through as a physician and medical novelist. I struggled to find any effective tension or particular interest in the characters of this book. It truly shocked me to learn that Dr. Slaughter published over 50 novels and that this was one of his last. I figured that Plague Ship was a one-off. 

What did shine through was Frank's awe towards medical advancements, and a desire to communicate these advancements with a general public. He must've been a noble sort of guy:
 

I will say that anyone who reads this and likens the “plague” in this story to the COVID-19 pandemic is likely doing so only because they have lived through a pandemic. Beyond the simple fact that a pandemic occurs, very little in this book resembles COVID-19.
