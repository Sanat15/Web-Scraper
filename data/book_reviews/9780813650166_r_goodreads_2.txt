Rating: 3/5

If you love the Dick and Jane books I think you'll enjoy this book as well. It's definitely written in the same style. It would be a great book for beginning readers because it repeats so much. This book is a retelling of The Ugly Duckling.

Talking Points: How are you different from others? Has anyone made fun of you for those differences? Tell us about it. How did you handle the situation? How would you handle it now? Who are the people in your life that accept you exactly as you are?

Essential Oil Pairing Tip: I'd diffuse bergamot essential oil when reading this book - because bergamot oil is helps promote self-esteem and self-love. It's helps to make us happy with what makes us uniquely us.
