Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars.  

 Brady  by  Jean Fritz  was  a surprisingly mature and thought-provoking read  that I ended up enjoying a lot. 

 Brady  was an incredibly relatable main character.  It was easy to sympathize with his feelings and struggles,  even if they weren't exactly things that I had ever had to live through. Seeing his growth through the book was really rewarding, and it made me emotional. 

The topics touched in the book,  the hot topic of slavery the 1800s   makes for a very intense setting; and leaves for an almost unresolved feeling to the story.  Yes, we know the ending of history as a whole, but for the purpose of the characters in particular it felt very much like their story is still up in the air. Of course, that feeling is precisely the point, this is not a topic that was solved easily, but it brought much strife, and the book makes a perfect appeal to those real emotions that many lived through for many years. 

All in all, I enjoyed this read, and while it took a little bit for me to warm up to it, once I finally did I could scarcely put it down.
