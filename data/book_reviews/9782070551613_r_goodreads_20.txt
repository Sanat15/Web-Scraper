Rating: 5/5

The Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants by Ann Brashares is a young adult contemporary novel set in the United States, mainly in Maryland and various summer locations, during the late 1990s to early 2000s. The story follows four best friends spending their first summer apart while sharing one pair of jeans that somehow fits them all. It’s about friendship, growing up, love, and finding yourself. This was a 5-star read for me — heartfelt, nostalgic, and one of those books that perfectly captures that moment in life when everything is changing but your friendships still ground you.
