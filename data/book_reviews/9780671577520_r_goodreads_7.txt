Rating: 3/5

3 Stars for Bitter Harvest (audiobook) by Ann Rule read by Mary Beth Hurt. 

I’m not sure why I gave this book a try. Somehow it got recommended and it ended up on my TBR. And no matter how well written the story is I still prefer my horrific stories to fictional. I just don’t enjoy reading about a man that married a monster that kept trying to kill him and then ultimately killed two of his children.
