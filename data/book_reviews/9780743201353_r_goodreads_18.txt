Rating: 4/5

Another good story by Ivan Doig. There are always unexpected twists throughout his books and this one is no exception. We get to learn more about Susan Duff (little schoolgirl in an earlier book "Dancing at the Rascal Fair"). She is now a grown woman teaching music lessons when Wes Williamson brings her a student that he wants her to teach... his chauffeur, Montgomery Rathbun. She's a strict teacher but he's not a child she can push around either.... watch the sparks fly! 
Oh, and we say goodbye to other characters in this book. I'll not ruin it by divulging whom.
