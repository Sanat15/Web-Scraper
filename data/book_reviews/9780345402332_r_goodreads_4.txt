Rating: 1/5

Years ago, I was out of town when this book was read and discussed with a women’s book group from my church. It was sitting unread on my bookshelf, and I decided to pick it up now and see what the book was about. It did not resonate with me as the notion of the divine having a gender isn’t limiting my spiritual experience. Perhaps this is the result of 25+ years of change in thinking about gender, growth of my confidence in myself, and my personal beliefs. I did trudge through the book although I considered more than once putting it in the dnf list. The divine as Creator, Redeemer and Sustainer is language that fits well for me.
