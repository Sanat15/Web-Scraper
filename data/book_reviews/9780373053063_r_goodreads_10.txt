The book is great!
And it has one of the best closing statements i have read. Not something mundane like they always had their sunshine or something. Here it is........

"Out by the fence, the prickly pear cacti were in full bloom. Sometimes, he thought,
the ugliest plants put out the most beautiful flowers. He guessed there was a man back the road a piece
who wouldn't argue with that statement one bit. And neither would the woman who'd put the bloom
there."
