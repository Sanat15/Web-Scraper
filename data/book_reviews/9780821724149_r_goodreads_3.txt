Rating: 4/5

It never ceases to annoy me when reading 60s to 80s gothic romances when lines like “you don’t need your own finances” are said by supposed hero’s. Reading these stories are a fantastic way of highlighting how far women have come in independence and how concepts of romance have changed. 
That being said Beverly C Warren is a favourite gothic romance author for me because her books are always fun and a bit of a wild ride. Surprise death, out of the blue shooting, crazy betrayal, omen-like children. Servants casually watching a sword fight to the death (seriously). They always have me laughing at what these poor heroines are put through for plot.
