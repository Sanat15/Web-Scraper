Rating: 4/5

A darker move for Takeuchi - Tuxedo Mask and Sailors Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Pluto are all killed, disintegrated, by a crazed Sailor Scout from a far part of the galaxy, apparently killing other Scouts to consolidate their power. Sailor Moon's only clue and allies are three intergalactic Sailor Scouts also from deep space, the Sailor Starlights. I'm enjoying it - there's an easy door to bring back the deceased, but Takeuchi's doing a good job putting Sailor Moon through a ringer.
