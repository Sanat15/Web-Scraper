Rating: 5/5

I want to start of by saying this book was fantastic. The sroty of Allie and Delaney moved and touched me in so many ways from beginning to end. The characters were well developed and the story was beautifully written. I loved the fact that the hero and heroine actually shared a true and unique bond that trascended the usual plain physical attraction in most romance novels. Since I started reading Wings of a Dove I knew it would stay with me forever. Definitely one of my top so far. 
For all the above reasons and the simple fact that I enjoyed reading it so much I gave it 5 starts, though I do have one complaint. After so many misunderstandings between the main characters, I really feel the author could've taken more time throughout the end into putting all the loose ends and small pieces together, and I would've so much liked to read an epilogue in the end.
