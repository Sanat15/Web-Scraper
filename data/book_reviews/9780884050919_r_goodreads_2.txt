Rating: 4/5

This is not the best biography I’ve read, but it’s still pretty good. It would serve as a good introduction that’s not too overwhelming. The full-colored portraits and other pictures are gorgeous, informative, and fascinating. The analysis doesn’t go as deep as I would care for, but some areas are golden nuggets worthy of holding onto. Some of the memoirs are not treated with as much skepticism as they should, which perpetuates misinformation. Overall, it’s a decent biography with some passages I’ll treasure forever.
