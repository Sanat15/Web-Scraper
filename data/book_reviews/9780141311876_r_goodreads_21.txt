Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars. Great parts of this book read like non-fiction, so the parts that begged belief kinda sat wrong with me. An enjoyable, quick read, I chose this for a reading challenge bc my husband enjoyed it when he was ten (and wanted to run away from home, as most of us have, from time to time). George made this a believable (for the most part) prospect. I enjoyed the way the main character adapted so well to his surroundings. Most people, myself included, would benefit by becoming more attuned to nature and spending time in the woods. The smells and sounds alone make the woods more than worthwhile 😀
