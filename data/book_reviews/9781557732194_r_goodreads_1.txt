Rating: 4/5

One of the better "RoboCop" rip-offs. In the 21st century, the book was printed in 1989, so, a virus, along with nuclear war has made America, in parts, a no mans land run by criminal gangs and street thugs. That's where Donovan Steele comes in. A member of the dangerous Strike Force team. His job is to fight a loosing battle with them in the city.

When the leader of The Skulls wants to have a parley with the Strike Force to finger the criminal leader of the city, he is shot and blown up. He is put back together and made into a Cyborg. He becomes the perfect crime fighter. Even if he would rather of died. Pretty good read.
