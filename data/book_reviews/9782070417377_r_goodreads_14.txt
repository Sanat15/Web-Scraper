Rating: 3/5

Really interesting book. I had to read it for my French classes, and at first, I thought it would be a boring book, but turns out I was wrong !
The only problem is that all the names, and all the murders confused me; I sometimes couldn't remember who was who, and who was dead and who wasn't.
I've also learnt more about painting, and this book made a lot of references to my French course, which was really useful.
