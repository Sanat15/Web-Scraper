Rating: 3/5

Shane Holiday has been tracking Lillian Smith for 7 years. He has fallen in love with her 6 1/2 years ago. Now he has to approach her and try to worm his way into her trust, so that he can find out who killed his uncle. Only Lillian has this info locked into her head. She had to see who was there when she fled her home, her husband, and her life, so long ago.

Neither one wanted, nor was ready for love, marriage, and a family. But Shane has gone and done it... he's done something he told himself he'd never do. . . Find out and read the book. 3.5 stars.
