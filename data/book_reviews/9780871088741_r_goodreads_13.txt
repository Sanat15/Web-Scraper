Rating: 4/5

A book I was dreading reading - fishing is certainly not my thing, and neither is men's worlds. But the author's style is so beautiful, his command of language so thorough, that I was pulled in. I cared for the author, I cared for the old men; I cried when they died. A moving work of how real life can be more about God than church life; about God being in the world he created more than he is in the world man's created.
