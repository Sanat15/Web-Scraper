Rating: 5/5

Wow. This was a very moving book and it really shined light on the genocide I didn’t know happened. It was incredibly well-written and I felt like I got to really know the characters. Vahan’s story is so remarkable and needs a lot more recognition. The author did an outstanding job of inserting the reader into the horrors of this time period. 
This book inspired me to research this forgotten genocide and other untold horrors of history. It was extremely eye-opening to read this, and I’ve really learned to appreciate everything I have in life. 
I def recommend this book for anyone interested in WW1 or the forgotten parts of history. This book will def stay with me for a long time.
