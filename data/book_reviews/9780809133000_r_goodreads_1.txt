Rating: 4/5

I read this book as preparation to present this same retreat this year for several different large groups. It's a pretty standard method to timeline your faith journey. I think it would be very helpful for someone to read the book on their own to help them retrace their journey and to help them vocalize (either to themselves or others) how they came to this point in their faith life. I really believe that until you actually ponder and even put into words what your feelings are you may not understand or even believe them. It's a great tool and I would recommend this book to certain people.
