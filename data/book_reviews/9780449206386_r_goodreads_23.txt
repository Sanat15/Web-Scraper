Rating: 4/5

I put this book down a few times before I decided to finish it. I'm glad I did. Intriguing mystery that involves marriage to people that may have regrets. An ex-wife arrives in Belle Island to identify the remains of her ex-husband. She stays with her brother's family with another family that have interest in the development of Belle Island that is different that her ex brother in law. Add to this a string of arson fires and the mystery deepens. Good mystery
