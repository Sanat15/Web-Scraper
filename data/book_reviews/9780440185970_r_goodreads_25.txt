Rating: 4/5

Enjoyable story !

I was curious to read one of Anne Stuart's earliest Regency novels. This was an entertaining storyline about Gillian, the youngest of the Redfern siblings who is single and is busy raising her nieces and nephews. Throughout the novel, her inner thoughts express how unhappy she is going to one sibling home to another only to take care of her nieces and nephews and how she dreams of having a family of her own. The hero was hard to warm up to . He was easily influenced by his friend participate in a bet and when he finally realized his feelings, it was to late. At the end, Gilly decided to take matters on her own and convinced the foolish rake that she is willing to be with him.
