Rating: 3/5

A good read that makes me want to read others of Spring's books. I learned a lot of facts about English laws concerning wealthy people immigrating to England and bringing their servants with them. I enjoyed Laura our PI who has an interesting life seems to be a good detective yet had some major flaws. She is quite shallow as a person and doesn't seem to be too concerned with the dates of those who seem to Love her. I tend to like my main characters who have some flaws. I can identify with that.
