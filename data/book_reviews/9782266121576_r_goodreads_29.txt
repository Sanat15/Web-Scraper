Rating: 4/5

So, this is the story of Lestat. The story of how he was turned, the people he turned, the creation of The Theatre De Vampire, Armand and the children of darkness, Marius, The Keltoi, The Ancient Ones, finally ending up in New Orleans where he is thrown into the swamp and left for dead by Bradley Pitts &amp; Kirsten Dunst. Fast forward a few years and Lestat rises again, reads a few detective novels, and then becomes a famous rock star. =(I might have left a few key moments out for dramatic purposes) 

I’ve read a few Anne Rice books so I knew I would be diving headfirst into Auntie Connie’s velvet sack of word syrup. And yep, the sack was full, full of densest most luxurious word syrup I have ever forced my way through. And here I am, I survived, and it was worth it. Hugely entertaining, and wonderfully camp.

4 stars. The syrup had a few lumps in it.
