Rating: 5/5

Do we dare review the greatest scientific work of the smartest man that ever lived? Can we as mere mortals even comprehend his genius? Touched by divinity, Newton writes, privately, the sum of all human knowledge that came before him and more. For the sum of all human knowledge before him, becomes smaller than what he would create, discover and pen on his own. 

Newton shows that words like 'autodidact' and 'polymath' should be defined simply as 'Isaac Newton' in the dictionary. They were created to represent him. 

No other man, before or after him, has achieved more for humanity.
