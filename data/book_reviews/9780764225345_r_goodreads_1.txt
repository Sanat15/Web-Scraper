Rating: 3/5

After the disappointment that was book two, I wasn't sure if I wanted to continue the series, but there's only six books, and they're short so I said why not, they can't all be that bad. 

Truth be told, book three was a little better than book two. Do I think the rest of the series will be as good as book one? No, but if it's around book three's level, I can work with that.

So this time around, Elizabeth and Wynn have settled into their routine a bit, but there's absolutely no romance to speak of. None. Zilch. Except like, one cute scene during their anniversary.

The rest of the time, Wynn is gone (wow, who would've thought?), and Elizabeth is whining about not having a baby. And when I say whining...day in and day out it's all "God has given everyone BUT ME babies". 

I feel bad for her, I do, especially after what happens later on in the book, which managed to bring a tear to my eye, but it was just frustrating to read about the same thing over and over again.

At least she started teaching again, those parts were fun. And she's less lonely (minus the baby issue), she has friends now, sort of. But she's still ignorant, even after years of living among Native people...again, it's better than in book two, but like...

For example, the whole witch-doctor witchcraft thing, I read that and I sat staring at the page for a minute afterwards, because it was so, so ignorant. And yes yes, different time and what not, but I can't, I just can't, okay?

Hopefully book four will manage to do something/be better in all aspects.
