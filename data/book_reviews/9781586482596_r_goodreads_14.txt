Rating: 3/5

A middle aged woman leaves her job as a legal secretary to become a teacher in NYC by joining the Teaching Fellows program. This book recounts her experiences during her first year in a Brooklyn elementary school. As a former Fellow, I enjoyed reading about the issues she faced (the same that all face) working in a failing school. 
I like that the book was written by a news reporter (the story was originally told through a series of front page articles in the NYT), someone who visited her classroom throughout the year, rather than being written from Ms. Moffet's point of view.
