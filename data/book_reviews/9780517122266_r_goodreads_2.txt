Rating: 5/5

This book is an insane wealth of information -- what's great about it is that it's easy to flip through, doesn't require tons of sorting through to get a simple answer, and keeps it super basic, but extremely informative. The copy I have is from 1959, and it has a really pleasing look to it. Vintage pictures, and lovely binding. I know there's a more up to date version, but if you're hunting around for a copy, try to get an old one. It's just sort of cool!
