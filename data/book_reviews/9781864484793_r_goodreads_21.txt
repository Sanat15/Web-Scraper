Rating: 1/5

I’ve never in my life seen such romanticism of colonialism and I wish I could have never seen it. Disgustingly white supremacist. I’ve never read such direct racism in my life ever before. I was only able to get so far because the writing was smooth. Otherwise, the overemphasis AND the supremacist lenses of analysing the history of SOUTHEAST ASIA is absolutely wild. You would forget that you’re reading about SEA and more so reading about Colonialism. I would better title this book as “Romanticising colonialism ft. Southeast Asia’s history.
