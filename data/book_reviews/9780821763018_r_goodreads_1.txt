Rating: 2/5

I had high hopes for this when I bought this second chance romance but between the h's vacillating over whether she could trust the H or not and the whole fairy godmother/angel thing this had going, it was just a stretch to far. Don't get me wrong the h had reason to question whether to trust the H or not given their history but she just came across as a weak willed idiot most times, even without the angel smoothing things over. 

There is a secondary romance but it's more a filler than anything else.
