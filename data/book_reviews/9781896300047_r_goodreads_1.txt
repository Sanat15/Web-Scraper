Rating: 5/5

I was in a production of "...Remains..." by Raleigh Ensemble Players directed by C. Glen Matthews in 1999. It's a difficult play to read, but amazing to experience when it's staged by a talented company. It's viscerally violent and sexy. We did the show in downtown Raleigh, NC at Legends, a gay nightclub. This was one of my favorite experiences as an actor. Our cast was close and the experience was unforgettable. 
The movie screenplay/ film version reeeeally misses the tone Fraser captured in the stage version.
