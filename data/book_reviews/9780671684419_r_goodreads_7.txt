Rating: 4/5

Abbie Scott is traveling back to Eureka, Arkansas when her car, Mabel, blows a hose and starts shooting stem from under the hood. Up drives a green sports car with the hood down and a dashing Good Samaritan leaps from it to assist. A knight in shining armor? 
Seth Talbot is Eureka’s new young, handsome minister arriving the week the previous pastor is retiring. Tongues wag, church attendance improves.
Abbie’s knight in shining armor wear a clerical collar!
