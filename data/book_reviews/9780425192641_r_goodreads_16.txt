Rating: 4/5

This series is always interesting - blending as it does fact with fictional characters. This instalment is set in 1901 in the real life home of the family of the late Queen Mother and involves a convoluted story of a member of the Royal family presumed dead but actually living in the castle who goes missing. Charles &amp; Kate are called by the King himself to investigate the missing mans disappearance. It was an enjoyable read, though there were several coincidences which stretched credence somewhat.
