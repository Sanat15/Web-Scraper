Rating: 5/5

Work book club. 

The finest example of the power of tonal restraint I’ve ever read aside from Ishiguro. Those Brits, huh. As I type I want to underline and add exclamation marks and even more hyperbole but that’s bc I’m no Iris Origo who maximizes the drama with her marked absence of emotion. 

And still! What she produces is a vivid portrait of surviving war from a very unique perch, that of a wealthy and well-connected, morally righteous woman determined to help her neighbors and passersby survive a war too. 

The scene of them running for the hills, babies in arms, pram stuffed with diapers, will stay with me for a long time. Of her crouched in a cornfield while shells rain down. Of the heroism and generosity and decency of everyday people!
