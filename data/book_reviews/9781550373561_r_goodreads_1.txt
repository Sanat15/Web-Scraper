Rating: 4/5

Account of a young Finnish-Canadian girl in rural Ontario during the Great Depression. This is by no means a thriller or a page-turner, it's a slice of life (and a short one). I was given it in 1994 and liked it enough to hold onto for thirty years apparently! 

On a reread I enjoyed the depictions of the Finnish community hall and the political undertones, as hoboes move through the narrative, leaping aboard cross-country trains and asking for a meal, and union organizers in the logging industry are murdered and not found until the thaw.
