Rating: 2/5

I wasn’t going to continue the trilogy, but ... 

Middle books tend to be fillers, bridging to the end. And they are usually the weakest of a triple. Pohl and Williamson took a mildly fantastic and serviceable opener, threw in a few wild detractors for spice, and then proceeded to dumb it down for the short attention span pulp fans. Worse, they bizarrely have three characters an East Ender personality overhaul that seemed like they spent too much time holding Tolkien’s ring and way too much time watching B thug movies from the 1940s. 

Now I have to read the last to see where this goes.
