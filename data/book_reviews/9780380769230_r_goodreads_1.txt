Rating: 4/5

I've read a few Overland Regencies and really enjoy them. I was excited when I saw that her books have become available for Kindle. 

Anyway, I thought this was a fun, traditional Regency. It was a tad predictable, but with likable character, good writing and a fun plot I didn't mind that I knew how things would play out. Belle was a fun heroine. She has been dubbed the Golden Icicle for her unapproachable ways, she is also filthy rich and because of her hard upbringing is leery of fortune hunters. Marcus is an Earl and on the brink of bankruptcy. He is also the one that gave her the nickname and they have been butting heads for years. Now that her ward and his heir are forming an attachment they are thrown into each others company much more than they would like. They become unlikely allies as they try and keep the happy couple apart. Of course, spending all that time together they realize they have misjudged one another and surprise surprise they start to have feelings for each other. 

Overall, I did really enjoy this one.
