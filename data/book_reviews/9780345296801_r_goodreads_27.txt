Rating: 2/5

A promising sci-fi world but the conveyance of it is always either too dense (lots of proper nouns and weird ways to describe a typical action) or frustratingly pandering (the typical “I know everything about the reader’s world and will explain it to you dumb sci-fi world characters for the reader’s benefit” type character is in this in full force). None of the characters in here are much more than sketches, and there isn’t much sense of adventure or discovery. Narrator Rogers is fine. [LIBRARY AUDIOBOOK]
