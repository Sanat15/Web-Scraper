Rating: 3/5

In this one the Hero and heroine have a marriage of convenience.

The heroine is very beautiful and lives with her uncle who works in the foreign office. Unfortunately because of her beauty, the heroine attracts the lascivious attentions of a German Prince. He even tried to rape her. The uncle can do nothing without inciting a diplomatic incident for the country.

The uncle knows the Hero and proposes a marriage of convenience to get his niece out of the country. The Hero agrees, because he is eager to take the position of Governor in India, and he needs a wife. The heroine is not only beautiful, but intelligent and rich, so she is an ideal candidate.

The Hero also feels compassion for her and her plight. She has been very traumatized by her experience.

They get married and move to India. The Hero knows he has to treat her gently so she will start trusting him. Luckily the heroine is very interested in India. So the Hero starts gaining her trust by teaching her about the country and he even teaches her the language.

They get along well and the heroine is starting to trust the Hero. By now he is in love with her and realizes that she is the ideal woman he has been looking for all along.

The heroine uncovers an assassination attempt and is able to warn the Hero, so he can go look for he bad guys and save the day. This is also finally when she realizes that she is no longer afraid of men, and that she loves and trusts the Hero. 

Of course the Hero comes back safe and sound, they declare their love for each other and have a belated honeymoon.
