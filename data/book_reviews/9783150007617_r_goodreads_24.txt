The story of Simplicius Simplicissimus is one of lengthy adventure and mischievous activities. The protagonist (Simplicius) comes from humble beginnings but soon finds himself the center of attention in imperial courts, armies, and bedrooms around Europe. This book very much reminds me of the travels of Forest Gump, just 300 years prior. The historical aspect of the novel is also fascinating as it gives a glimpse into what German life was like during the Thirty Years’ War of the 17th century. While it can be a tough read sometimes due to the lack of punctuation and old writing style, it is certainly worth getting to the end to see the circle of life complete itself. This book is humorous throughout while still giving a vivid depiction of what life was like for the author to be born into a war and live not only long enough to see the end, but long enough to reflect upon and draw light to the events of his life.
