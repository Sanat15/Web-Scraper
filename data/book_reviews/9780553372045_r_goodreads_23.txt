Rating: 2/5

My goodness, Rhodry gets himself another love interest/person to sleep with? How many has he had?

We see events coming together now - the siege starts, Rhodry finds his dragon, and we're heading to the conclusion of the defeat of some evil. I liked that we learn more about history - i.e. the displacement of Man onto the country led to the displacement of the Horde, which in turn affected the elves and the dwarves. Even the minor subplots - Yraen and Carra - are full of tension (in part due to the few glimpses we get of them).
