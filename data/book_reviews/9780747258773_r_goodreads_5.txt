Rating: 5/5

#7 in the 13th Century English Roger the Chapman mystery series. It is 1522 and 70 year old Roger is recounting his life starting with leaving the life of a monk at Glastonbury at age 19 in 1471, to set off into the world as a wandering peddler or chapman and finds his true calling in solving mysteries.

August, 1476, and Roger is returning home to Bristol after a nice summer's peddling. He is sidetracked by a request from his duke to escort a bride en route to her betrothed in Glastonbury where they discover that the groom has recently disappeared. Roger offers to stay and find out what happened. He links the disappearances to the discovery of an ancient scroll written in a strange language that the groom thought was the secret to something valuable. Then a few days later, the groom's younger brother also disappears.
