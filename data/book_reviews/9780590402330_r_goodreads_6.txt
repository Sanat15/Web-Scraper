Catching Up…

Most popular book amongst grandkids – Check
Most popular book amongst neighbors – Check
Book that comes and goes quickest in Little Free Library Shed – Check

So… What is it about this series that is so appealing?

Before I answer that question, let’s just say this is the first in a beloved series by the author, that is illustrated by Felicia Bond. 

The idea is that a mouse happily jumps from activity to activity with the help of a child, only to end up right back where it all started. 

The repetition helps young readers, and it makes for a great read-aloud book. 

As an aside, the book was adapted into a TV series in 2017, and a 30-minute holiday special, “If You Give a Mouse a Christmas Cookie,” in 2016. The book is also available in Spanish. 

Back to the appeal of the book…

It allows kids to follow their interests and look for fun in surprising places. As well as, make a mess and clean it up. 

Because…It is showing kids all the steps for cleaning up after making a mess, basic hygiene and responsibility…

In a fun way. 

The child gets to support the mouse’s interest in the story, and take care of the mouse.

The mouse shows curiosity, creativity, enthusiasm and energy. 

It is a positive and gentle story, imaginative and creative with fun messaging. 

Ages 4+

The question is, who hasn’t read this book, yet?
