Rating: 4/5

I was in the mood for some Harlequin WTFery and Mortimer didn’t disappoint. Don’t read if you aren’t into stupid heroines and menacing Greek billionaires bent on revenge. The plot is utterly bizarre.

Young girl fresh out of university is tricked into accepting the position of a companion to a young girl she’s never met. When Regan arrives to Cornwall she realises she’s been lured there by the man she’s never met but heard and feared of.
Many years ago the hero’s wife ran away with the heroine’s disreputable father after the hero was left blind after the motor track accident caused by the same disreputable parent. The hero swore revenge and put the ten-year-old heroine UNDER SURVEILLANCE. She was to become his wife, so he kept her surrounded by his employees. 
Now he demands marriage and sons.
