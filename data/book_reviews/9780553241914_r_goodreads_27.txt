Rating: 4/5

Pretty good later entry to the Nero Wolfe series. A three novella volume.

The first tale: "Kill Now - Pay Later" was up to the usual high standard, until the dramatic finish, where unknown facts were introduced that helped point to the murderer. Still, an entertaining read.

The second tale: "Murder is Corny" was first rate from start to finish by my reckoning. There were adequate clues mixed in with red herrings right along. I didn't guess the murdered until late, but once Wolfe rolled out the details, it was one of those smack-your-head moments where you realize you should have spotted them early on. Well done Mr Stout!

The third tale: "Blood Will Tell" was more complex. A bit soapy and psychological at times and resolution came via motive, which I didn't spot in the pointer clues until Wolfe laid them out. Another good one.
