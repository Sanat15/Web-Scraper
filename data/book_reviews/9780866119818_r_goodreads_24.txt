I remember just having turned 12, just having moved into a big empty house and getting ready to sleep there for the first time. 

I sat down in my bedroom full of boxes to pass the time with the first book that I found--this one....

 It was already almost dark, but it got later and later and my family had neglected to tell me that they all left to run errands. 

I finished the book a bit shaken up and wandered off to find a better-lit, more populated area of the house, but it was all dark and no one was there. 

That seriously scared the crap out of me and made me think this was a much scarier book than it was for years afterwards.
