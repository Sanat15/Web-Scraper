Rating: 5/5

Despite the somewhat frantic glimpse into critical discourses that the book sheds light upon, Bressler’s work can serve as a complementary source of study for each and every school of criticism included, ranging from ancient times (albeit briefly) to the late twentieth century.
As good news, reading this work does not require prior knowledge of critical theories, as it pursues a thorough and accessible study of the discourses.
It would definitely be suggested to anyone with a passion for learning about literary criticism who does not know where to start.
