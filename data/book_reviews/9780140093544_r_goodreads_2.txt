Rating: 3/5

This is an interesting portrait of Vita as gardener (I highly reccommend her gardening writing). I feel like so many biographers of Vita allow her bisexuality and, most particularly, her relationship with Virginia Woolf, to eclipse all her other accomplishments. This biography is a welcome change from all that-- not to mention the fact that there are so few biographies of gardeners in general. I feel like I learned a lot about Vita, about her garden at Sissinghurst, and about gardening in general from this book.
