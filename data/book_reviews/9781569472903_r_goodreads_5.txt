Rating: 3/5

There are lies aplenty in this book, and you spend it trying to work out who the murderer is … if you can be bothered.
My problem was that I didn’t like any of the characters and didn’t really care what the outcome was.
For me, it was a book to read as I didn’t have anything else (picked from a bookshelf in a holiday cottage). So, if you haven’t got anything else, give it a go, otherwise I wouldn’t bother.
