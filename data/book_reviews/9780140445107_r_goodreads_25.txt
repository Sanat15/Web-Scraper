Rating: 3/5

Wow….takes some endurance to read this. I’ve never read Aristotle, so I feel like I’ve now completed a rite of passage. That said, I struggled with the applicability of this for my current situation. He wrote much of this for use in judicial or political settings - not mine. His terms were very different than I’m used to, so I felt like I was constantly trying to understand what he was saying. And many of his examples were so distant from today that it was hard to fully appreciate them. George Kennedy did a masterful job at translating the Greek and keeping the readers oriented as to “what’s was coming next and how it relates to other parts of the book.” He even highlighted where Aristotle contradicts himself, since this volume is actually his lecture notes written over many years. I’m sure I’ve changed and contradicted myself over the years - it made me feel good to know I share something with Aristotle!
