Rating: 5/5

We all love a big, strong hero who comes in to save the day. 💕 

Logan Sackett may describe himself as a hard man, but his actions tell a deeper story.
He values women, honors family, and lives with a strong sense of responsibility. He is fiercely protective and willing to sacrifice his own wellbeing &amp; desires to care for others. He never backs down from a fight.
As we experience Logan, the book highlights what it means to be a good man. It emphasizes Biblical values like responsibility, respect, personal property, and ownership- It pushes against the socialistic idea that everything should just be shared and it stands for defending your land, livelihood, and labor. 

The story itself is straightforward with light &amp; simple characters and an engaging plot. A tiny twist at the end had my jaw on the floor.

I actually read this because one of my 5th grade students insisted I borrow his copy over spring break. 
I was super sceptical and genuinely did not expect to enjoy a “boy book” set in the 1800s American West. But I honored my student's request and, y'all, I’m so glad I did!!! It was refreshing, easy to follow, and genuinely fun. I learned a lot. 

Louis L’Amour’s writing is immersive and keeps you hooked from beginning to end. Definitely glad I gave it a chance!
