Rating: 5/5

When you see the publication date of 1372, you would expect literature dry as dust. In rhymes. And language none understands. Well, bless “translators” who put works like this into more modern words. Because as it turns out, they are actually quite enjoyable. This one in particular reaches Shakespearean levels of sass and clever word play. I am pretty sure I did not catch all the sexual innuendo, either. But it is humorous and quick to read, the melody of the rhymes is very easy to follow. The biggest surprise was the content, though: that much feminism and female independence and empowerment is a book that a lot of modern writers and politicians could take a leaf out of. Very entertaining and surprisingly modern, overall.
