Rating: 5/5

I haven't done a ton of research, but I can't find much on the internet about how much these books are truly based on the author's real experiences as a priest and how much he takes some true stories and elaborates them for the purposes of his writing. I have to assume it is more the latter than the former, but I love reading these books. I would put them right there with the James Herriot books as ones that I will keep reading over and over for just their genuine humanity. Both of them have laugh out loud funny elements, but both of them also deal with terrible tragedies in a very real way. Life is seen in its true, full, poignant reality, but with a brightness of outlook ultimately that leaves me uplifted. These are right up there with the books that most of my friends probably have never even heard of that I would recommend the highest. And I've got one more to go.
