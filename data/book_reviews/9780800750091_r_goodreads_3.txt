Rating: 5/5

Tim LaHaye really knows the temperaments, as shown in this book. As I read, I found myself categorizing the men that I know, saying, "Oh, that's why he acts like that!", and "Oh, that's why he did that when I said that". 
 I learned so much about guys and how they think from this book. Would recommend to wives, girlfriends, and even to the guys themselves...
