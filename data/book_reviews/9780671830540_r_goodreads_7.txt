Rating: 4/5

A rich business man is murdered with a poisoned tea. Then two more murders occur. The police can’t make heads or tails of this puzzling case, but Miss Marple thinks they were murdered in a way that’s reminiscent of a nursery rhyme.

The story starts off with Agatha Christie’s knowledge about poison firmly on display. The nursery rhyme combined with murder is something Agatha Christie has done well multiples times in her career as an author, and this is not an exception. It’s an excellently crafted mystery plot with plenty of twists that can keep you guessing.

The characters in particular are a delight. This allows Miss Marple to be used sparingly, giving the other characters more opportunities to shine. Inspector Neele and the Fortescue family dominate the pages, and the story is all the better for it.

This reads a bit more like a detective mystery than a typical cozy mystery, though I think that actually benefits the story as a whole. It’s a story with a strong mystery plot that can grab you from the start and the characters are interesting enough to keep you hooked. One of the better Miss Marple books in my opinion.
