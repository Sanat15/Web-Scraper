Rating: 2/5

I wanted to like this book. I loved the idea of reading about a friendship, between two women, that started as happenstance and turned into a decades long relationship. Their lives becoming parallel to the point that became widows within months of each other. BUT, I didn't like it. It was boring; Sarah was boring. The details that the author chose to write about, really did nothing for the overall theme. Sarah and Katie's wallpaper business...who cares? The fact that her mom was an arachnid "expert" travelling around the globe...again, who cares? There were too many detours into these benign details and they become distractions, that pulled me away from the story of Harriet and Sarah. I found myself wondering if I had gotten the theme of the book all wrong and it was a book about simply Sarah.
Normally I would have given up on a book that didn't catch me in the first 50 pages, but I had made a promise to myself that I would savage such books out. Unfortunately this was the first book I read after this promise.
