Rating: 2/5

Horrible Harry a zero hero

Even in farce there needs to be some chemistry or connection between the main couple. There was none. If I were in Isabella's shoes I would have ran off to America with Biddle the old drunk servant before I would have married Harry. Horrible Harry treats the h like dirt leaves her open to insult and wouldn't have taken the time to save her from a man he knows is a rapist if the old drunk hadn't pressured him to save her.

Isabella a 19 year old girl is thrown under the bus because in two years she hasn't agreed to marry. Her punishment. Marriage to Harry. No one deserves that. She really should have given America a try.
