Rating: 3/5

This book, originally published in 1977, is, apparently, the 12th book in a series about a London couple relocating to Cornwall to grow daffodils commercially. 

I thought I would enjoy it more than I did. The story rambled quite a bit, to the point where, when the little side trips ended, it was hard to even remember what had been happening beforehand. 

 Also, relatively little happens - the author spends a lot of time either reminiscing about incidents from earlier books or ruminating on the meaning of it all.
