Rating: 4/5

Graham in 1897 on a walk in Cornwall is saved from suicide by a man who takes him to his hotel. There he goes into a trance. 203 years later he awakens in 22nd century London. There he finds that he is the richest man in the world. 

The world Graham awakes in a futuristic dystopian world with cities of conveyors and towering buildings, flying machines and a society governed by class. 

Graham is known as the Sleeper and a Council takes him prisoner to maintain the status quo of a class driven capitalist society. Ostrog rescues him but is as bad as the White Council. The story has lots of action with Graham learning to fly and in the end saves the day freeing them from slavery.
