Rating: 5/5

A lovely Lenten read. I’m so glad I finally picked this up now, when I have spent more time contemplating art and artists than I had when I first bought this in 2018. The book has more of a conversational style than a building-to-a-conclusion definitive statement. I felt at times as if I was sitting for tea with Madeleine at Crosswicks and listening to her talk freely, in that way that we do when there’s not a point to be gained but an idea to explore. It took a bit to realize this and to settle into the style. I used my pencil liberally to underline and make notes, so I look forward to flipping back through and remembering.
