Rating: 4/5

Koestler spent a lot of his career interviewing and reading scientists and thinkers. This led to his recounting of creativity in people with this book. He primarily views it as the ability to link different subjects to develop something sharing those parts, either directly from nature or from more abstract ideas. This is also why he speaks a fair bit on humour and jokes because they depend on having unexpected twists. Overall, it is a useful book to have on hand if you are wondering about how ideas develop over time.

The Picador version unfortunately leaves out an entire other part of the book, so I recommend getting a more complete version which should be over 700 pages.
