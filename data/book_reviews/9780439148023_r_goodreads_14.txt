Rating: 5/5

Somehow, even though I read and enjoyed other Beverly Cleary books as a kid, I'd never read any of the Ramona books until this week. Now I've just finished reading Beezus &amp; Ramona with my 7-year-old, and it was such a revelation! We both loved it so much, and we were laughing hysterically in some parts. (The fabulous party scene!!!) It also had the loveliest and most nuanced conclusion about sibling relationships of any book I've read in a long time. We will definitely be devouring the rest of the series!
