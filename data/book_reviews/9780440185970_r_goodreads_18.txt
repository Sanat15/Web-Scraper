Rating: 3/5

I liked the author's narrative. She did a good job creating her characters and presenting vivid scenes, dialogs between them.

What didn't work for me was the romance and the conclusion of it. I think the relationship between hero and heroin should be developed better. l am willing to be convinced that heroin was half-smitten, but for the hero, l needed more persuasion to be happy for the end.

Overall l am glad that l read it. It is still better than most of the historical romance books.
