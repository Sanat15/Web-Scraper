Rating: 4/5

I will always and forever love Daniel Two Stars and Genevieve LaCroix. Such a good story with very good quotes and scriptures. Finishing this book is very sad. It's like leaving an old friend. The end of this trilogy is so good I will miss it dearly. 

There were a few loose ends. We never find out what happens to Jane, Meg, and Hope. I can assume they lived happy lives together, but I don't know. I wish that the names of the children at the end were given.
