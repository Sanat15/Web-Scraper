Rating: 4/5

I should be honest: I hate nonfiction. Especially war stories. That said, I ordered this book because my dad served in the Coast Guard in the Aleutians in World War 2. He said that nothing went on and he just froze there. I no longer believe that. The book rarely mentions the Coast Guard but I doubt that he didn’t see action. It was an engrossing account of a 439-day campaign against the Japanese invasion of American soil.
