Rating: 3/5

The story was a bit much. 
This sudden relationship came out of "nowhere" and was very unrealistic to me. The ending was also dramatic and a bit ridiculous to me. 
Actually, I found it a bit boring in some parts. It would just go on and on about how bad someone was feeling etc for the second or third time. 
This was just not my type of book - too much eye rolling from me. 
However, there was one quote at the end of the book that I did enjoy:
"There comes a time in life when one's obligation to other people must take a back seat to one's duty to oneself. Too often, in order to fit in to the world, one forgets the true shape of one's own spirit and forces it like square peg into a round hole, gradually eroding it until there is no self left to encounter."
I thought that was a clever way to put it and different from the usual "be yourself" pep talks. 
Regardless, this book was only okay. I have not read this author before and I doubt I will again.
