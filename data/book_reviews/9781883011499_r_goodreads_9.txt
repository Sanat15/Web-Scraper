Rating: 4/5

Five novellas, including "The Killer Inside Me", "The Talented Mr. Ripley", "Pick-Up", "Down There", and "The Real Cool Killers". American Noir of the 1950s, indeed. A mixed bag: one was down right creepy, all were gritty and told tales of shabby bars, lives gone wrong. "Down There" was the basis for Francois Truffaut's movie, Don't Kill the Piano Player. A connection with France is shown in several tales (the American authors lived in France).
