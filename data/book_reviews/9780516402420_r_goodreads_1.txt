Rating: 4/5

The amazing quarter horse can do almost anything. He is just as comfortable chasing cattle or riding for pleasure as he is playing polo, racing or jumping. By mixing the blood of the stocky Spanish jennet and the English thoroughbred in the 1700s, early American colonists created a truly astounding animal. This new breed of horse was muscular and very strong. However, his most surprising aspect was his speed. The quarter horse is fully capable of outrunning a noisy motorcycle in a quarter mile race.

As settlers pushed west following the Civil War, the quarter horse became well known as being a good “cow pony”. Even when herding cattle for dozens of miles a day or racing alongside a stampeding herd of longhorns spooked by a violent thunderstorm, the quarter horse never shied or faltered. When there was very little water and only unappetizing thin blades of grass to eat, the quarter horse still did the best he could with the meager conditions. Quarter horses often waited patiently in driving rain or dust storms until the cattle drive was ready to proceed. 

The quarter horse’s intelligence and gentleness has allowed him to become one of the most favorite breeds around the world. Ms. Stewart has provided the young reader with tantalizing facts about this wonderful horse and Mr. Munoz’s photos literally leap off the page, endearing the reader to the style and rare beauty of this marvelous creature.
