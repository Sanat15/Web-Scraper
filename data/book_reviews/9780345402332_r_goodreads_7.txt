Rating: 2/5

Patricia Lyn Reilly reveals the desires of many in early twenty-first century America. We seem to want a God who closely resembles us -- in detail. She wants a "She." And she shows us through her emotional journey just how much we want God to take on specific gender, racial characteristics, even our specific values. As such her journey into mysticism reads as deep longing -- and emptiness. God seems to become no more than a super self-portrait of the author. And as such A God Who Looks Like Me serves as a powerful warning of what fruits human imagination can produce.
