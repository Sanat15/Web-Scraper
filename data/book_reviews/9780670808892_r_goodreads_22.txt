Rating: 2/5

Have you ever wondered how long the first transcontinental railroad was? Well, this book was even longer! 711 eleven pages! The details were beyond numerous. All the builders, all the business people, all the politicians, all the squabbles, all the backstory. It was too much, even for me (and I thought I can generally handle a long history). 

There's so much to it, doubly complicated because it details the story of two railroads being build simultaneously (one heading West from Omaha; and the other heading East from Sacramento). And while parts of the story are certainly exciting, it's impossible to keep energized while reading this because of how bogged down I got with all the 'in the weeds' details. 

Amazing for all the interesting factoids and important history. But upon finishing this, I was very UNINTERESTED in reading anything more along the same lines.
