Rating: 4/5

This was a fun, lighthearted read. I actually ended up liking it more than I thought I would. Bethany was easy to like. I also liked Eric, even though he is a bit of a sap. I guess that’s a bit harsh, but whenever he was with Bethany he turned into a romantic poet or something. While I thought they had good chemistry, I would have liked a little more substance to their relationship. I did like that Eric was a gentleman and treated Bethany well. There are a few twists in the story and with one of them I thought Bethany was way too forgiving. I think the author missed an opportunity for some great tension with that story angle. The last part of the book was a bit more exciting than the rest and a few twists were thrown in, although they weren’t all that surprising. I would probably give this four stars, but since it was clean and also entertaining I’m going with a five. (I wonder if all of this author’s books are as clean??) Overall, I did really enjoy the story and I think it is one I would probably enjoy more the second time around and when I wasn't as distracted like I was this time. 

Content: Clean romance, some clean passionate kissing. There also may have been a bit of swearing, I can’t remember…
