Rating: 3/5

True story: the author's boyfriend committed suicide -- and she wasn't able to let him go. She searches high and low for ways to communicate with his ghost/soul or whatever form he was "in" now -- well-written, somewhat compelling, though in the large portion of the book devoted to their short time together, I often thought, "These are pretty much the thoughts every person has when in love, when in a relationship". So at times it felt narcissistic -- and naive. In the end it's about the author's own personality, and how her tough childhood shaped her ability to process life and relationships -- though this connection is only lightly touched upon.
