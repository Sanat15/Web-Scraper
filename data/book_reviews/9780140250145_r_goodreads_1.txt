Rating: 4/5

Deeeelightful!!! This book in the series is such a joy as the author (who is also a barrister) gives us six humorous short tales of the lovable, irreverent, poetry spouting, plonk drinking barrister, Horace Rumpole of the Bailey. He is the man who takes cases of petty crooks which nobody else in the Chambers would touch and wins them through sometimes less than straightforward methods.
He debunks judges and has a habit of calling them "old darling"...............and gets away with it.

He lives with his wife of many years, Hilda, who he calls "She Who Must Be Obeyed" but certainly not so she can hear him. She has great plans for Rumpole's career but he is having none of it. He is satisfied with his career defending some strange, and often guilty, people who live on the edges of society.

Someone said of him that he belonged in the great gallery of English oddballs from Pickwick to Sherlock Holmes to Jeeves and Bertie Wooster. This book and the series are just plain fun!
