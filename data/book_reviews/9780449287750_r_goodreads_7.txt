Rating: 2/5

So much emphasis on the mysterious house and the various other mysteries (Did evil Minerva kill her husband? What will she do to win Charles' love? What is the true story behind Miss Trent? Is there really a ghost? What is the weird plantation owner planning?), plus way too many viewpoints in use, meant that the romance was decidedly an afterthought. None of the characters got enough screen-time to be compelling.
