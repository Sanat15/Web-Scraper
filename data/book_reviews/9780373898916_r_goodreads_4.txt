Rating: 4/5

~D~ in my A-Z Mills &amp; Boon Readathon.

I almost gave this one five stars because I *adored* the heroine and the close, loving relationship she had with her adult children. I *loved* that the heroine was an 'older' woman (37 I think) and how she was so determined and positive and full of laughter. I was so caught up in how much I enjoyed her following her dream.

The hero, however, was an underhanded, rude twat and the 'romance' sort of came out of nowhere in the last few pages. Since this is supposed to be a romance book, I had to dock it a star for having such a lame romance.
