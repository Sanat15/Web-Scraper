1913, Washington DC. 13 year old Francesca's parents do not approve of suffragists or women working outside the home, but Francesca dreams of being a reporter and is curious about the suffragists. When she meets Katherine Sullivan, a famous suffragist, she finds someone who takes her dreams of a reporter seriously. Francesca wants to see the suffragist parade, but she's sure her parents would never let her go or will they?

Great story!
