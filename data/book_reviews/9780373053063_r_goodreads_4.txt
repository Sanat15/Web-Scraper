Rating: 1/5

I didn't like this one at all. The H was so cruel and since we skipped over the months of their separation when the h hated him, it seemed like she forgave him too quickly. They both made assumptions about each other and almost never tried to communicate. Anytime the h would want to talk, the H would get all mad and defensive and that was the end of it. He was completely unwilling to put himself out there and continued to act like a total jerk until the end of the book . Guess who had to do the chasing? You guessed it. The h. There was no grovel on the part of the H. He was an idiot, and I guess by association, so was she. The only reason this received one star was because they were both celibate during their separation and because he wasn't a manho.
