Rating: 5/5

Lady Francesca Wilde is a well-known folklore writer. She researched many of Ireland's folktales herself, going straight to the sources and asking people the specifics on stories they had heard. This is what made her one of the authorities on the subject. Many people go straight to Yeats when they want to read about faerie folklore, but Wilde is another goto on the subject. She is well known for some of her other writing and for her famous son, but as a folklorist, she went beyond others and managed to collect folklore and put them into readable stories. This is a must read for anyone interested in faeries or folklore enthusiasts.
