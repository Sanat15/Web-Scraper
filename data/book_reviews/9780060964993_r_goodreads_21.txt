Rating: 5/5

All the Jeeves &amp; Wooster books are good, but this is one of the best. We get Aunt Dahlia, Sir Roderick Glossip playing an undercover butler, scheming Bobby Wickham... and the cow creamer makes a reappearance! This despite the fact that Jeeves is on vacation for much of the story, only popping in now and then to lend his magnificent brain to Bertie's and friends' problems. His final solution is especially hilarious. :)

These books are great to read or listen to. I feel sorry for people who haven't discovered Bertie and Jeeves yet - they're missing out on a load of fun!
