Rating: 2/5

I discovered this author in my local library and have read two of his other books prior to this that were amazing, 5 star reads. 

This started off like that, the first half was great, the story had me sucked in. The second half however, absolute let-down, slow, repetitive, nothing happened. 

AND THEN?!!! The book just ends. Without giving away anything or spoiling it the book just fucking ends without even addressing the main premise or answering the most important fucking questions raised in the first half of the book. So now I’m just sat here wondering why? There’s too many why’s. But two of them being why do authors do this? Leave books on shitty endings with no answers? It’s not cool, it’s not clever, it’s not ‘quirky’. It’s bullshit and it winds me up. Which leads me onto a second ‘why’ - why have I wasted my time reading this? I’ve invested my time into this book, based on the blurb on the back - only to find that none of it gets answered. The whole story was building up for this big reveal, the answers to WHY everything has happened, but noooo we don’t need to know any of that do we? Let’s just fuck it all off on some stupid ‘mysterious’ ending. I’m fucking fuming and have knocked a star off while writing this - it could have been so good. 

Fucking ridiculous - don’t even bother.
