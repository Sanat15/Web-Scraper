Rating: 5/5

What an amazing cat Varjak Paw is!

Varjak Paw is a Mesopotamian Blue (a fictional breed) who has lived his whole life sheltered inside the home of an elderly lady, surrounded by a large family with countless cousins and uncles. But Varjak is different, and his family keeps reminding him about that. Everything changes when a mysterious Gentleman and his two sinister cats arrive at the house. To save the family, Varjak’s grandfather—the only one who truly believes in him—urges him to venture Outside and return with a dog.
Told entirely from Varjak’s point of view, the story is gripping from start to finish.

This was my child’s class book at school, read aloud in the classroom over the past month and a half.
