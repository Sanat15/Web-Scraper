In 1993, the daily For Better or For Worse newspaper (look it up, kids) strip ran a storyline in which Michael's now-teenaged friend Lawrence comes out as gay. I read the strip regularly and I remember feeling surprised and pleased. I was a teenager then, the same age as the characters. I didn't know it was a historic moment in comic strips, but I knew it was unusual. 

I also remember being shocked at some of the public response that followed. The same newspaper covered the backlash from some quarters, with some readers cancelling their subscriptions and some newspapers cancelling their inclusion of the long-running syndicated strip. I thought, what the hell was wrong with people? 

In 33 years, society has changed little, as ongoing book bans demonstrate. 

Johnston wasn't being an activist. She was simply being an honest storyteller.
