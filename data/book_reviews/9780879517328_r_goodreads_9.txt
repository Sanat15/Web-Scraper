Rating: 4/5

As with most of my books these days, I stumbled upon Bending the Landscape: Science Fiction at a thrift store. I was actually looking for Star Trek novels, but this was a hidden gem I couldn't pass up.

Bending the Landscape--as it turns out--is a series of short story anthologies, with each story focusing on queer characters and queer issues, and most being written by queer authors. Queer science fiction is right up my alley, and I enjoyed a lot of the stories in this book. My favorite is called "Time Gypsy," which is about lesbian time travelling physicists. I also really liked "Silent Passion," "Dance at the Edge," and "On Vacation." 

Some of the stories do drag on a bit, but of course not all 21 stories can be to everyone's taste. I definitely recommend checking out this collection if you're into scifi, and/or if you're interested in queer perspectives on the world circa 1998. There's also a fantasy version of Bending the Landscape, which I hope I can get my hands on soon!
