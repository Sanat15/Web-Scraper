Rating: 5/5

I once was blind but now I see.

I have a new reverence for the supply cabinet. I had no idea what the supplies were used for, and I now notice a communication style all around me that I thought was just idiosyncrasies. This book has changed my whole outlook. There are over one hundred pages on how to obtain a good position in the office. And for those that are challenged by the written word there are many colorful diagrams.

By itself, this book is amusing but combines it with the knowledge of the traditional Kama Sutra and it takes on a greater dimension.
