Rating: 4/5

Despite initial apprehension (based, in large part, upon the truly horrific cover), I rather liked this book.

A warning for anyone reading this- technically, the heroine cheats on her husband, but it didn't really bother me. The husband more or less told her to (on their wedding night, no less), and was more a father to her than a husband.

I liked that the hero realized he was in love pretty early in the story, and he stuck to it. Lots of angst and emotional upheaval can be good, but they can also get somewhat tiring. So most of the excitement came from the adventure plot.

I had a problem with the disposal of the villain at the end, though. The hero spares his life once, in the process thinking that he didn't want to leave the villain's pregnant wife to have a fatherless child. I would have been fine if the book left it at that, cruel as the villain was. But then he attacked one last time, in the process somehow figuring out where the hero and heroine were, when even they hadn't known where they were going, giving the hero one last chance to kill him. Actually, a lot of what the villain did in the end was perhaps convenient plot-wise if the goal was to kill off the villain, but otherwise made little sense.

But overall this was a pretty fun book, and I liked it.
