I will quote directly from page 205
"are there conscious dark or evil forces at work in opposition to God?"
(answer) no. no. no. there is only ignorance, which breeds fear.

That answer may be true for some people as part of their experience, meaning they have not experienced evil or dark forces. When you experience those things, it means it is possible, and invalidates that answer being true in an absolute sense. It's not different from an atheist to proclaim that there is no God, because he has had no spiritual experiences.

To me there is nothing worse than spiritual teachings that ignore the existence of evil forces, and pass it off as some form of innocent ignorance and fear. I have experienced dark spirit entities, for several years, and they are as pure in terms of malice and ill intent as any being that is good. I would not wish them upon the worst human being. Half baked spiritual teachings based on contrived idealism that deny the existence of dark beings or forces are themselves a form of ignorance.
