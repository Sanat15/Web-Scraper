Rating: 4/5

This is a book that made me smile. The illustrations are colorful, simple and bold.

Richard is half white, half Indian - his mom is a Dogrib Indian.

Dogrib are not horse people, they have a great respect for dogs. 
One cold day in Northern Canada, he wonders about horses and asks his family 
and friends the question,
"What is the most beautiful thing you know about horses?"
This is a question one could ask about anything!

Richard shares that the Dogrib's word for horse means 'big dog.'
One of his friends is George Littlechild, a Cree. Cree are horse people.
The Cree word for horse means 'big dog'
