Rating: 3/5

Okay. I found it a touch hard to follow the latter part of the book, I could not quite understand the motivation for the central conflict at that point. Plus, a sort of sudden insertion of a third party to the conflict seemed very out of the blue.

Further, this book seems to suffer from the same problem that burdens a number of books by authors of the 1950s and 1960s - especially in regards to authors that are accustomed to writing short stories to pay the bills. When these authors try to expand a short story into a full size novel - the story seems to run out of steam and seem incomplete. This book sems to fall into that category, it seems like it might have been a nifty short story, but the author did not seem to quite know what to do with it in trying to turn it into a heftier novel.
