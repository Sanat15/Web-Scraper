Rating: 1/5

This one was a difficult read for me. It has an interesting premise for sure, even if it's a little messy at times. I really do feel like either the author was actually experiencing hallucinations, or put great effort into recreating the feeling for just about the entirety of the book.
 Without going into spoiler territory I think it's worth pointing out that there is plenty of SA in this book, possibly more than I've ever seen in such a short book.
