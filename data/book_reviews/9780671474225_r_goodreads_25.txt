Rating: 4/5

Reading The Pale Horse made me realize I only have a few Poirot stories left before I’ve finished all of Agatha Christie’s works, and that thought makes me both excited and a little sad!

This book is classic Christie brilliance. It blends her knowledge of nursing and chemistry with her unmatched talent for crafting a gripping mystery. The best part? A touch of the supernatural that somehow still made perfect sense, adding a refreshing twist to the story.

It’s both a whodunit and a howdunit, and while I managed to figure out the "how," Christie kept me guessing the "who" right up until the end. With every book, I’m more in awe of her mind. She was truly one of a kind.
