I accidentally fell into a rabbit hole and read all of Helene Hanff's books in the first few weeks of 2025. I finished up reading through her body of work with her first published title - Underfoot in Show Business, and what I consider her tightest memoir. Underfoot recounts Hanff's experiences as a struggling playwright in the 40s-50s in NYC. Hanff never actually successfully "made it" as a playwright - this book chronicles the challenges (the shift from theatre to TV and onwards based on the fashions of the time; the budget and time limitations in producing stories on stage and television; the limitations based on physical space and time - living in tiny apartments, having to work side-jobs to earn a living) and also her evolution as a writer.
