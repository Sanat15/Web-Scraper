Rating: 2/5

New York PI Mike Hammer is gunned down and assumed dead when witnesses see his bullet-riddled body fall into the river. Chance has it that a drunk unlicensed doctor actually pulls him to safety and treats his wounds in secret. After Mike comes to, he hears that an old war buddy of his is on his deathbed asking for him. The old guy mentions knowing of a generational financial conflict within the New York mafia and that he is the only one who knows where $89 billion of mob money is hidden.

Verdict: An okay mob mystery and romantic adventure. 1996 Mike Hammer just isn't as fun as 1949 Mike Hammer but "Back Alley" is still a decent read.
