Rating: 2/5

Not one of her best. This is full of the author's stock characters and bad writing ticks. It feels infinitely padded. Characters are constantly stating out loud that they know a secret but they can't tell it now but maybe they will in the future or that they can't tell but they can show it, but not now, maybe in a couple of days. It's a form of narrative edging that never pays off in a satisfying way. There is a big twist at the end but it's so stupid and so late that one can only shrug. It doesn't help that there are so many red herrings and they all reek to high heaven so that the ending feels almost arbitrary. This is definitely Whitney on autopilot.
