Rating: 4/5

Published in 1980 as a follow-up memoir to ‘I Heard The Owl Call My Name’, Margaret Craven takes us through her life growing up during the Depression - carving out a unique career for a woman at that time working for various papers. Her dream was to be a writer, and write she did. Through her brother, she heard of an Indigenous community in British Columbia, Canada called Kingcombe. After spending a few months within the community, she headed back home full of ideas and inspiration. Having already read and loved the corresponding novel, I’ve been quite excited to read this ever since my grandmother gave it to me. I quite enjoyed it, definitely put me in the mood to re-read The Owl.
