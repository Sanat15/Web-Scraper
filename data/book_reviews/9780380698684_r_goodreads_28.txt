Rating: 4/5

(#40 in my Year of Reading Women)
Short review because I'm catching up. Even a middle level du Maurier is a class above most writers and this was surprising for me. A story I had no idea about, set in contemporary 1960s Italy, lots of flashbacks to both renaissance Italy (a fictional city somewhere like Ravenna) and the years around WW2. Two brothers separated by the war, both thinking the other dead until a chance death in Rome leads the younger brother on a trip back to his home town. Not a murder mystery, more the psychological study power and rejection have on a young man's mind. Very interesting indeed and - as you'd expect, beautifully written.
