Rating: 2/5

i’ve been in the process of reading this for a while now, getting through a little chapter every once in a while before bed. I love the show, so it was very pleasant to “rewatch” the first three seasons by reading about them! It’s a nice episode overview, with fun recurring sections commenting on the same things in the various episodes - for example, the “Logic, let me introduce you to this window” sections remark on continuity errors or impossible things the characters imply have happened within dialogue etc.

I could have done without the author’s constant comments about whether a given dress, skirt, or tight top worn by the actresses were sexy, however.
