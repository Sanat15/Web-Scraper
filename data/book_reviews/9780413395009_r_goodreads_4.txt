Rating: 4/5

It’s turn of the Twentieth Century London and the Deptford Strangler is lurking about and making short work of seemingly unrelated people around the City. An uncle and nephew writing a history of an unsolved series of murders in Fifteenth Century Italy notices some interesting parallels between the historic crimes and the present day ones, and then it’s off to the races. Heretical cults, the Inquisition, reincarnation, astral projection and Nostradamus all play a part in the story as it unfolds, and it unfolds in a fun way. Recommended. Thanks to Valancourt Books for bringing out a new edition of this book of which I would not otherwise have known.
