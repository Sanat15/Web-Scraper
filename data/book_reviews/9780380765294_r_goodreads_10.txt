Rating: 5/5

Your past comes calling 

Elaine aka Guin has suffered much at the hands of an abusive husband! Daniel still harbors resentment from his mothers actions that he saw as betrayal as she took his younger sister to live with her! These two souls shared grief and never really grieved. 
Daniel slowly realizes he has feelings and passion only with the right woman and Guin realizes she is better than her past and shame she deserves love and hope in her future! You live again when you face your past let it go and look and love present and future! They also had help from some mischievous misses aka student from her school!
