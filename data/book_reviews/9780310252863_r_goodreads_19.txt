Rating: 5/5

Through personal accounts, stories we know well, and stories we have never encountered Charles Colson brings poignant illustrations about how we are to love God to life. Some of his points I knew well through growing up in the church; however, this book brought those points, and others, into a clearer perspective than I've ever had. I have struggled with how to love God. How could I love the One engough, considering how much He loves me. I didn't know how I could draw closer to Him. This book brought convinction and clarity to those questions. This quote really sums up what the book discusses and the main point to take away: "To believe, to repent, to obey, to be holy, to bind up the broken hearted, and to serve" is how we truly love God. Obedience in prayer and reading the Bible is what begins our path to loving God, following His path for our lives, and making a difference in lives around us.
