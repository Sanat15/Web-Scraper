Rating: 4/5

This was a fun little book that my kids and I enjoyed. A flying saucer/robot comes happens to come to Earth on Halloween. He’s taken in and befriended by a young boy after being mistaken for a trick or treater. It’s pretty humorous and a bit nostalgic for the 80s/90s crowd. In a way it even reminded me of Stranger Things (misfit with weird powers taken in by group of friends at Halloween time. Hidden from parents and teachers, has to learn about our customs and how to communicate). Anyways it was cute and my kids liked it.
