Rating: 4/5

All three stories were entertaining, but I have to say I liked the last one, Fintan's Tower, the best (as an Arthurian scholar, I would). And as I have a weakness for Kay, I really liked Fisher's portrayal of him, and that she went with his characterisation of the early Welsh stories, instead of the later one, in which he is a bully (though there were some elements of that in there). Anyway, leaving the analysation for another day, I quite enjoyed the book.
