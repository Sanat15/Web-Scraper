Rating: 3/5

The book is divided into two parts -
I really enjoyed the first part, focused on The Key. It is well-written, with rich vocabulary and beautiful phrasing, great pacing and suspense. The first half would get a 4.
The second half, focused on The Door, felt less... real. Fern conveniently just knows or senses where to go, what to do. Intense, emotion-ridden events like Fern &amp; Raf's relationship are glossed over. It was not nearly as gripping or intriguing as the first half.
