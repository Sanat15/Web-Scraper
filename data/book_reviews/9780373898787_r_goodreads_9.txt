Rating: 1/5

I was intrigued by this because I love survival romances like this, a couple relying on each other after a disaster, and falling for each other as they get to know each other during that. But this is one of the worst written books I’ve ever read. I know Janet Dailey is a popular author, and this was written really early into her career, but it genuinely baffles me that it was even published.

On top of the awful writing, this is so incredibly racist. Leah is a horrible person, throughout the entire book. She throws tantrums, only cares about herself, is so conceited, abuses Reilly, is a cheater, etc etc. And basically every time she threw a tantrum, or hit Reilly, he’d kiss her. It was disgusting.

I’m aware this was written almost 50 years ago, but I listened to the audiobook which just came out in 2024 and nothing was changed so obviously the publisher stands behind the words. There’s also multiple references to rape in very problematic ways - like he asks if she’s a virgin and instead of saying yes she says he could find out and no one would be around to hear. I don’t want to just keep calling this disgusting but I’m not sure what other word describes it so well.

I will say that the narrator was pretty good, but that’s the only not-terrible thing I can say about this. 1 star. Bummed I bought this audiobook, but at least it was cheap during an audible sale.
