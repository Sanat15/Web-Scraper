Rating: 3/5

I was curious to read this translation, but I also read it along with a NKJV translation. Having studied different translations, I knew that this would be more of a paraphrased interpretation by modern theologians. That is exactly what this is. I would not recommend this for a detailed bible study, but if someone needed a more modern day explanation of a parable, it may suffice. I give it three stars because, at the core, it is the divine Word of God, but a deeper study of God's Word should be done than what is provided in this translation.
