Rating: 3/5

This author has a pithy sense of humor and has taken a serious topic and made light of it. He has had 3 heart valve replacement surgeries and the third one almost killed him. After the first two he kept smoking and drinking and had a “devil may care” attitude. Looks like he might have finally gotten the message after the third. I just didn’t care for the flippancy, but the details of the surgeries were interesting.
