Rating: 5/5

Who was the man in the rainbow beard who gave a letter to Inspector Thumm and paid him a thousand dollars to keep it safe? Who was the man wearing a blue hat who bribed a bus driver to join a tour group of Indiana school-teachers? Where has the security guard of a museum vanished? Why would someone break into that same museum to steal a book, only to replace it with an even rarer one? What is the meaning of the strange characters "3HS wM"? These are just some of the questions that the heroes face in Drury Lane's Last Case. I loved the bibliomystery at the heart of the first like two-thirds or so, which is just about things getting stranger and stranger until you have no idea what's going on, but eventually we get to a murder, which has some very simple and yet exciting clues that lead to a very memorable solution. Yes, the murderer is very easy to meta-guess, but I don't think the book is any weaker for it. Queen anticipates another author's work in this one. And some great closing lines here as well. I'm very glad that I set out to reread Queen because this is one work which I've appreciated much more looking back on it.
