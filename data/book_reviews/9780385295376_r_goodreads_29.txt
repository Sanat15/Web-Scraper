Rating: 2/5

I cannot finish this book. Although I love some of the characters already, a story has to be believable to me. I’m 150 pages in and can’t do it. I do not believe they traveled so far with her skin painted “black” pretending she was mulatto. I cannot believe that boy brought paint and canvas and easel into a mining camp, and never seemed to run out of paint in 1860 something when they had no money. I cannot believe that an enslaved woman would have been able to get a spice straight from Africa, again, in 1860 something. Too much nonsense. Fantasy is ok, if it’s believable. This is just nonsense.
