Rating: 3/5

"Treat yourself to a frustrating experience"

Your colors are not going to be enough. You have a huge crayons box? There is another bodypart. There is always another bodypart. The book is trying to tell you which types of color to use. Excuse me? How dare you? I use the colors I want. Then what does it even mean to use "light colors"? And how do you think I will waste my yellow for one stupid small spot? Ok so you start to color the different body parts following what you THINK is a good combination, then bam! There is a part you haven't seen before and the only color left is too similar to the color beside. Oh and which is the letter for Esophagus? F, of course. Not E. No. E is vena cava. Ileopsoas? H. I is for psoas. Makes sense? Right. RIGHT. I'll just have to scratch the color out of the paper, leaving a brown stain all over the place. When you are sure you have colored all of a letter, SURPRISE! There is another one you didn't see. Which was the crayon you had used now? WRONG. It was the slightly similar one. Honey, dinner is ready! No. JUST NO. I need to finish coloring this stupid page. Was light green C1 and dark green C2, or the other way around? And what about C3? I HATE THIS BOOK.
