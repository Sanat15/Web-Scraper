Rating: 5/5

The Description does nothing for the impact of this story.
What if we were nuked? The story takes place in 1984, so you have to remember that this was during the height of the Cold War, where everywhere there were threats of Nuclear/Biological attack.
Survivor, Veteran and author Ben Raines, who misses out on the war because he was stung by a nest of wasps, and pretty much sleeps through WWIII. 
Upon going out into the world, he finds that survivors are fighting warlords and evil across the country.
 And the Rebels, rumored warriors that are trying to pull the country from the ashes of the war, have been told to contact him, as he is their leader.
After meeting many people along the way, he intended to sit back and write and document the war and the survivors. but eventually decides to join the rebels and command their army.
They strip down what they can from across the country to unite in the Tri-states of Montana, Wyoming and Idaho to set up their own country within a country.
A heavily militarized society where every resident is fully willing and capable to defend it with their lives.
This is my favorite book of all time, as it's the type of society I would love to live in.
Where criminals are executed, and people helped each other.
Eventually, the US Government regains enough strength, to try to invade the Tri-States. 
And "Destroy" it. Meanwhile, Raines and his Rebels have escaped, and return in Fire In The Ashes.
