Finally, I finished this book! The premise was very good, you kill my father and I kill your wife and no one will no because we have no motive. It promised to be an interesting reading but, for me, it wasn't. I thought it would be a thriller with the killings and the fear of being discovered, but it was more about the fixation Bruno had about Guy and his life.

I know no one should get away with murder but I kind of sympathized with Guy and I was hoping he wouldn't get caught, but he put in his head that he had to talk with Owen, dumb!
