Rating: 5/5

Reading Robert Rankin's books is like having an adventure with crazy friends.
 Jim Pooley and his friend John Omally, frequent The Flying Swan pub, run by part-time bar man Neville, and it's here that most of their adventures start, (and usually end). Fighting unknown powers of Darkness, putting a stop to evil aliens, with help from Professor Slocombe and Edgar Allan Poe, and Sherlock Holmes, yes uou read it correctly. The Brentford Trilogy is one madcap read, that will have you laughing out loud. I love Rankin just as much as I love Sir Terry Pratchett .
