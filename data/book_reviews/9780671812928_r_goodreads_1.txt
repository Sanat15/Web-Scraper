Rating: 3/5

I bought this used. It’s a trashy ‘70s novel and suited the intended purpose of finding a light read and was worth the little time that I invested in it. The author’s absurdly repetitive use of parentheses was disruptive to the flow of the story. Parentheses we’re used on every page. There were also instances when an entire paragraph was framed with parentheses! I would expect that this annoying tool would have been excised by the editor. Despite this distraction, the story and the authors thoughts were clearly communicated.
