Rating: 5/5

A delightful book about urban wildlife, this book contains a great deal of factual information that is conveyed in an accessible, story-book-type of fashion that engages children and adults alike. The book highlights not just the cute and cuddly (e.g. squirrels and rabbits) but also those animals that tend not to endear themselves readily to people (e.g. rats and snakes) and gives reasons why all of them have something special about them. 

The beautifully rendered drawings by Douglas Penhale bring a whole new dimension of wonder to the book.
