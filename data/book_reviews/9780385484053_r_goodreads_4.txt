Rating: 3/5

Hm. Two and a half stars, actually. This book tells more about the author than anything else. The certain slant on the stories, what is emphasized, what is downplayed, the nature of the stories chosen ... they all create an interesting portrait of the woman who wrote it. She has an interesting view on what it would mean to be a man that rings false for me. She's also very interested in the sexual aspects of the stories, which gets awkward, burdensome, but at times insightful. I don't think I'd really recommend this collection to anyone. It didn't quite work. The concept was interesting, and like I said, it tells a lot about the author ... but it wasn't a great read.
