Rating: 4/5

Now this was a story of twists &amp; a bit of turns! Valentine Denning is an experience photojournalist who delves into the harshness of war torn countries, showing the world the mayhem and death that have been challenging us for centuries. Val is in her prime and has fallen in love with a colleague who upon his untimely death finds out that he has been leading a double life! At Tony's funeral she encounters not his ex, but his widow and so the insecurities of Val's whole life come back to haunt her! Val loves Europe, as it has been her home since she was a teenager, with special ties to France and England; but her enigmatic mother and baby brother still live in America. Will Valentine ever find the security of a loving relationship? Well maybe with Jake and definitely not with her 'cold' mother! Ms Bradford certainly has a way with the English language, especially occasionally I was glad to have my olde trusty dictionary to look up the meanings of words I haven't heard used in a very long time and all in all, a very good read!
