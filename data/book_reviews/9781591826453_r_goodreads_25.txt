Rating: 5/5

It might be nostalgia. It might be my hopeless romantic side. It might even be that I am just so content with so many things right now but I still love this series. Reading this sent me right back into my gleeful fangirl squeeing that is so rare now to me that when it happens I feel like I have to celebrate. While Ai Yori Aoshi is very... risqué the story and plot fluffs it around so much that you entirely forget that, oh hey, she's totally naked right now. If you're looking for sappy lovey dovey this is a great manga for it.
