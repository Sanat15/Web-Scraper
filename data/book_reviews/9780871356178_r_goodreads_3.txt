Rating: 3/5

Stan Lee and Keith Pollard deliver a fun, epic, large-scale Silver Surfer graphic novel that is beautifully drawn with the funky dialogue and character names you expect from Stan Lee. I believe this was the last time Lee wrote the Silver Surfer, a character of whom he was particularly protective. I wanted to like this more than I did. It is a good Silver Surfer comic but unfortunately it wasn't a great Silver Surfer comic.
