Rating: 5/5

A book I read as a young teen that has haunted me for a lifetime. Kate's work had a profound effect on my life, becoming feminist early and helping understand how our gender is not valued.

I went to see Kate Millett's art installation about Sylvia Lykins, tapes from the trial of her murder running in the background, a life sized twin bed with a paper mached Sylvia chained to the bed, cigarette burns on her body. A basement dark and eerie. How her life degenerated after her parents let her stay with a family they didn't know. Abused to her death in what was a murder. We must not forget. I could never, she is part of my psyche.
