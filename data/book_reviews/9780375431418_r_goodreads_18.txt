Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed learning more about Fox's life in his memoir. It's a good mix between his childhood, career beginnings, ascent to success and fame, his struggles with alcoholism and mental health, and his experience with Parkinson's Disease. My one minor issue with the book is the chapter organization could have been a little more streamlined. I am definitely interested in picking up one of his other books at some point since this book is over 20 years old.
