Rating: 3/5

Raced to read the prequel novella to put off starting the last Cadfael book for just that little bit longer, but it only took me a night of listening to get through all three short stories. Alas!! I did like the one where Cadfael decides to become a monk although tbh I feel like his thought process was still pretty opaque to me.... but I think that was also the point, that it was this feeling which came over him. The other two were actually not prequels, I think they just took place sometime during the series?? Anyway the second short was veryy good and I loved the couple even though we only met one of them!! And the third made somewhat less sense to me, like why did they know it would be safe to bait their trap like that... but I do like Cadfael enough that I just enjoyed reading more of him. WHAT am I going to do when I finish the series, I ask, having already started the last book.... omg
