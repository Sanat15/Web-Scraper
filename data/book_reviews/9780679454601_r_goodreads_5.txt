Rating: 5/5

I can't say enough about this book. Allen's writing is so deceptively basic, so clear, that one would almost think there is no room for nuance there. And yet every sentence speaks volumes, and leads you deeper and deeper into her story, a very difficult one to read at times, but very rewarding for those who can stick with it. This is a nonfiction account of how she moved to Iowa City and met and fell in love with the man of her dreams, only to discover that he was a cocaine addict and an alcoholic. Six months after they met, he committed suicide. How all of that went down is half the book---the other half is where she went in her grief, trying to reconnect with him again, using the Ouija board, automatic writing, etc. A very level-headed look at what might or might not happen after physical death, and also at the psychology of addiction and co-dependence. Allen doesn't pretend to know "the truth" about any of it, and that's one reason the book is so gripping. It's a fascinating story, beautifully explicated. I can't help but think that it would make a terrific movie, in the hands of the right director and actors, and I also hope she will come out with another book soon---I've been waiting ten years!
