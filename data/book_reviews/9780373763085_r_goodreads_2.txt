Rating: 3/5

This book tell the story of Conner recently being retrenched and replaced by Richard and Dallas who was previously engaged to Richard. Conner, is back to the ranch and help in training mustangs while looking for a new job as a system analyst and he found himself helping Dallas in taking photos of the surrounding for her books. He have feeling for Dallas but as Richard has taking the initiative to asked her out first, he took a step back on his feeling for her. And now that she is free and single, he found the seed of love that he have for her started to blossom again. But, Dallas is pregnant with Richard’s child and she only get to know about it after they broke out. Dallas wanted to remain single and raise her child on her own but there is attraction between her and Conner which she is struggling to deny. 

This story is about second chance in love. Both the main characters are single at the moment and is free to commit to each other. The only issue is that Dallas is pregnant and whether Conner is able to overlook the fact that the Baby is not his biological child. To complicate the matter more, Richard seems to be in the picture and is trying hard to reconcile with Dallas. Reading thru these complications is rather exciting and I could not stop myself from reading to get to the ending of the book.
