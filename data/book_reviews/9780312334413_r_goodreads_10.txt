Rating: 4/5

Druett has been on my to-read list since I interned at a maritime museum but I somehow haven't read any of her books until this one. I'm so glad that I started and found the beginning of a series. A Watery Grave is set before the 1838 exploring expedition and begins with Wiki Coffin, a half Maori linguist arrested for finding a dead body, then the ripples from the murder end up making the start of the voyage complicated. Druett's writing reminded me of O'Brian's as her knowledge of the era is incredible and the characters feel real and complicated. I look forward to reading more. Fair warning, this book does contain period appropriate racism and sexism but as a nice balance since Druett is from New Zealand, Wiki's point of view is well represented. There's racism but the ship crews have the representation they did in the era.
