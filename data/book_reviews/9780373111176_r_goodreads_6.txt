Rating: 4/5

"One Chance at Love" is the story of Dizzy and Zach.

When Dizzy James is asked a huge favor by her close friend Claire- she never expects it would include travelling up to her uncle's castle, encountering a nude Adonis, and later finding out that it is Claire's uncle Zach! In order to portray responsibility, Claire hatches the plan to show Dizzy as a wild, uninhibited belle- something that would surely put her scholarly uncle off- but as sparks fly between Dizzy and Zach, soon Claire's plan transforms into subtle matchmaking.

A sweet geeky hero, a scarred-from-past nerdy heroine, some sardines and animal love mixed with nude bathing and humor, this was just a pleasant read overall.

I liked how kind Dizzy was with Kate, and how understanding Zach was. Adorable epilogue!
