Rating: 4/5

4 1/2 stars. Just short of terrific. The start is my least favorite part but the book has some of the best scenes I’ve ever read from a Golden Age of Mystery book (1952). Tension, darkness and some humor too.

Some of the characters are great—mostly bad guys, the awful tiger and his colorful cronies plus one very good canon. Ones I expected to be better such as Allingham’s usual detective, Campion, are sideline figures. Some scenes are like Dickens only a hundred years later.

Most of the story takes place in an actual event in London in the deadly smog that killed thousands in a week’s time. The setting is ideal for this story with its evil murderer.
