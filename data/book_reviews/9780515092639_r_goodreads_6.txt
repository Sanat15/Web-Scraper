Rating: 3/5

I picked this up off of Laurie's shelf today...and when I started reading it, it didn't seem familiar to me. I thought I had read all of Elizabeth Mansfield's books, but I guess I haven't. It was a good read...easy one too as I finished it in all of a couple of hours. I like Mansfield, she is in the vein of Georgette Heyer. This one did not disappoint, with a likeable hero and heroine and everyone lives happily ever after....AFTER they get over the bumps and obstacles thrown in their paths, of course.
