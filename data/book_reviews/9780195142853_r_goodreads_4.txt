Rating: 5/5

An excellent account of gravity (and cosmology) from Newton to Einstein and beyond, but this will probably only appeal to nerds who still know how to work a slide rule, nerds who run around with pocket protectors and bad haircuts. Written in 1989, with an afterword updated in 2000, so one wonders (if one is a nerd) what progress has been made in the past 19 years. [Not recommended if your reading typically includes car chases, gunshots, love affairs, and stuff like that.]
