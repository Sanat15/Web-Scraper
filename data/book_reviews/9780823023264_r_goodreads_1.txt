Rating: 4/5

Winslow Homer’s art always resonates with me, so I couldn’t resist this book which focuses upon his somewhat lesser-known watercolors. One of the reasons I admire Homer is because he never bowed down to European influences. He was determined to stay truly American, which is why his work stands apart from other American artists of his time. At the time when Homer was painting, the medium of watercolors wasn’t considered to be very cool. Critics considered watercoloring to be something young ladies learned in finishing schools.. In this book, one can see why Winslow Homer tackled this form of art, especially showing the influence of the sea upon his pictures.

FISHER GIRLS ON THE BEACH

  

GLASS WINDOWS, BAHAMAS

  

SHORE AT BERMUDA

  

The book is very easy to peruse. Each watercolor is on the right side with the text and history of the piece on the left side. This makes it a nice way to learn about Winslow Homer’s art while admiring his use of colour. Some coffee table books are too big or too convoluted, but this one is just right.

Book Season = Spring (sunlight and breezes)
