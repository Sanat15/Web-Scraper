Rating: 5/5

I won A Hint of Seduction by Amelia Grey from Goodreads.
A story about a young woman who, during the Victorian era, journeys to London in search of her biological father, A Hint of Seduction is a wonderful read for those who love historical romance. Grey does a truly fantastic job of weaving into the story the hindrances caused by the societal rules of the time. Modern women of Western societies may be appalled by the limitations put on the main character, Catherine Reynolds, but they will also admire her determination. Further, for a woman of her time, Catherine is shockingly forward. In appreciating the profound force and stifling rules of the society of the novel, the reader is richly blessed with the story of a heroine who uses all the tools available to her to accomplish a nearly impossible task.
The second protagonist provides a very different challenge. John Fines, Earl of Chatwick, is a privileged male who has not only power and influence, but also impunity. He has neither responsibilities nor tribulations. His days are filled with entertainments and indulgences. While he is very good at maintaining the necessary façades of society, he has never considered taking on the mantle of duty. Until he meets Catherine.
The story follows Catherine and John as they navigate Victorian society and their hearts. I always enjoy stories in which suppressed women hold secret power. In Victorian society, women were clearly suppressed. In A Touch of Seduction by Amelia Grey, Catherine's power is in being true to herself despite all attempts to mold her otherwise. It is a satisfying read.
