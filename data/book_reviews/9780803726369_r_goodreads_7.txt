Rating: 3/5

Interesting novel about an entitled teen sent unwillingly by her parents to a boarding school where they feel she will get a better education than at Julliard, where she wants to try to get in as a pianist. She bears witness to a runaway and the pressure for her to confess escalates, as does her panic about trying to escape herself. The lies build up and she's not fooling anyone. Wells' writing is excellent as ever, but I found the very ending of this book a bit sudden and confusing.
