Rating: 5/5

Enchanting story, full of hope &amp; love!

Marjorie, adopted as an infant, is now seeing her birth parents &amp; family for the very first time! Emotions run strong as preconceived ideas are righted &amp; everyone comes to love this new sister &amp; the sister, them.
This is my third reading of this precious book. It's so full of the most important things in life! Like family, faith, strong convictions &amp; of course, love. So many lessons learned by all. It's one ill read again!
