Rating: 3/5

Dr. Oz shares some of his heart surgery anecdotes. Without getting too graphic, Oz puts heart surgery in simple terms we can all understand. This is an informative book infused with narrative of personal experience and reflection. Oz explores a blending of Western and Eastern medicines and shares how he has brought some elements of the Eastern traditions into the operating room. This is a great book for anyone interested in health and medicine, but of special interest to those who suffer from heart disease as well as the family members of those inflicted.
