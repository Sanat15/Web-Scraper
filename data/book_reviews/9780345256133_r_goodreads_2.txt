Rating: 5/5

Pure romance pleasure.

One of the rare novels I can call Heyer-ish, or as QNPoohBear wrote a decent Heyer copycat.

There was a place and time for growing love (despite how short the book was). There were interesting secondary characters.

The charm is worth 5 stars. Of course, there aren't the same 5 stars as for Heyer's books. I am rating this romance comparing it to most of the genre (and others, read by me, Alice Chetwynd Ley's books).
