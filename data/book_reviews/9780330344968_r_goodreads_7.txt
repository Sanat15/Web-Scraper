Rating: 5/5

This is the fourth book in the Poldark series and it was absolutely riveting. For Ross Poldark, it starts on a positive note as he and his cousin, Francis, are reconciled and working together. But the mining business is a dangerous one and tragedy strikes. Life is not easy for Ross and Demelza- besides the poverty they are dealing with, there is turmoil in their relationship. Something I never tire of is Demelza’s homespun wisdom. 

Dwight Enys and Caroline Penvanen continue to delight me with their ongoing romance. Caroline has fast become a favourite of mine. 

George Warleggan figures more prominently in this novel and I will admit to hating him more than ever. 

I loved being back in Cornwall with people I have grown to care about. The ending was perfect but left me wanting more. Since there are still 8 in this series, I am in luck! 

Published: 1953
