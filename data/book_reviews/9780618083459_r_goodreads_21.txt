Rating: 3/5

This guy is famous in education circles--love him or hate him. This was a pretty good book that presented the argument against the traditional model of education in our country, with evidence to back it up. Pretty convincing, particularly his arguments against the mass use of standardized testing &amp; all of the pitfalls along with that. Interesting that this came out before No Child Left Behind made mass standardized testing even more prevalent... I'm sure he has a lot to say about that &amp; probably has more current books on the topic. Anyway, I'm not necessarily on the Alfie Kohn bandwagon, but he not only presents the argument against the way we're doing things, but gives some solid ideas of more effective practices. Don't think they'll be happening everywhere until the federal policies change, but we'll see.
