Rating: 5/5

Why did I never read any Beverly Cleary's works before? Yes, this is actually the very first book I've read by her, and it was a hoot! Except for one moment when I winced at one of Beezus' ideas (a girl can't like mechanical things), this was a fun little story. Ramona is a handful! And poor Beezus, trying so hard to be patient and appreciate her inventive and mischievous four-year-old sister. Typical exploits that had me laughing and laughing: inviting all the kids in her nursery school to a party, shutting Rigsy the dog in the bathroom, defacing a library book (I know, awful!!!), dumping an entire carton of eggs, shells and all, in the cake batter.....) 
The family dynamics were pretty much what I expected for the time period in which the story was written, but I loved the relationships between Beezus and her aunt and mother. A sweet story, and I'm curious about Ramona's other adventures.
