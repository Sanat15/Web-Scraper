Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars

There are quite a few Dick Francis novels in the old Reader's Digest Condensed Books series. This was my first.

Reflex tells the story of Philip Nore, a jockey and photographer, who becomes embroiled in shady dealings at both his workplace and in his personal life. The story has a bit too much plot going on: the search for a long lost relative, the mysterious death of a friend's father, Philip being pressured to throw races, two affairs with a young lady, multiple characters with money troubles, bad guys burning buildings to get rid of blackmail evidence, a drug pusher, Philip's love life, and more! The writing is fine, though you won't find beauty in it. Francis sticks with very short sentences. (I always find it odd in books when a friend or relative is beaten nearly to death and no one calls the police.)

The ending of Reflex was a roller coaster ride, though, and made the read worthwhile.
