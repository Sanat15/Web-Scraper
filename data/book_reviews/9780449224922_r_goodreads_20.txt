Rating: 4/5

I've read this book several times and really enjoy it. There aren't crazy conflicts, just likeable characters and a sweet story of two nice people falling in love. It's quite, easy to read, and not a lot happens other than people hanging out in drawing rooms and enjoying a quiet country estate. The supporting cast is enjoyable as well... a little kooky, a little funny. The battle of wills between Lady Sykes and Jane's aunt is quite a bit of fun. Both hero and heroine are nice. I enjoy this one every time I read it. It makes me smile.
