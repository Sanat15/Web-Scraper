Rating: 2/5

Published in 1901, this book has elements that haven’t aged well and some that did. The structure of the story is compelling. An orphan boy missing a hand, sets off to find his way in the world, knowing nothing of his family, or what left him maimed. He becomes the most trusted employee of a lumber baron. He guards and cares for the timber rich Limberlost forest. He meets and is enthralled with a young woman, who is content to be known as The Angel. There is danger and beauty all around. The characters could be interesting, if only they could be a bit more nuanced. It seems everyone is either stalwart or evil. It got too nauseating to listen about how both Freckles and Angel were beyond compare, they could do no wrong. The was a lot of contrivance in the ending, full of so much social class superiority it mostly undid and respect I had built for the story. I do realize this is to be expected for the time, but I do think it show both the best and worst of the new world.
