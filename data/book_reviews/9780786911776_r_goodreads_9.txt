Rating: 3/5

This entry in the Harpers series, billed also as part of the Songs &amp; Swords series, delivers another competent but less than surprising adventure that unfortunately keeps Danilo Thann relegated to the sidelines and Arilyn Moonblade offstage in favor of Bronwyn, a young Harper merchant with secret family ties involving the Zhentarim &amp;c. Entertaining if not exactly for what I hoped.
