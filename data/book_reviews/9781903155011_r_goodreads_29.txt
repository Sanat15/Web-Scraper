Rating: 4/5

Her style of poetry is eclectic, funny, sad, inspiring, and relatable, all at once. Picked this up in a bookstore in Bath and it made me fall in love with poetry all over again. 

My favourite bit of the book goes: 

So sometimes I try
My mother's technique
Which is silence for a week, A brooding stare into the ruined future, And no rouge for that look of
You are making me so miserable you are giving me
A fatal illness.
 
It occasionally works.
