Rating: 5/5

This was a great story! It was full of mystery, lust and a growing romance that was full of surprises and suspense. I started reading it and couldn’t put it down. The story draws you in and has you on the edge of your seat, with tissues by your side. The writing was so real you could “feel” the longing and aching of her heart, but you could also “feel” the love. 
A must read for any mystery romance lover. Great read!
