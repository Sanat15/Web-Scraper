Rating: 2/5

Apologies to the people also in the book group, but I just CANNOT read 11 more books of this. I understand it's an examination of the English upper middle class, but stuff has to HAPPEN. It takes a great skill to talk about a character's day to day life, and make it worth reading, but I personally do not feel Powell has this skill. That being said I only read Book 1 out of 12, but to force myself to read 11 more when I really got no enjoyment out of the first, would be unfair to myself I think. Hopefully other reads enjoy it more, and maybe one day I'll pick Book 2 up, but not this year.
