Rating: 3/5

Babies are being born all over the country with red hair, green eyes, terrible diseases, and hideous wounds. Dr Heller and his wife, a reporter, discover the secret behind these babies dubbed "the Christmas babies" due to the color of their hair and eyes. 

It involves secret illegal genetic testing, mad scientists, high ranking Government officials, and plenty of murder and sex appeal as well. 

It was featured in the book Paperbacks from Hell, but it definitely is not a horror book. It's mainly a medical thriller, but it's still good. It skips along at a quick pace and that chapters are short and sweet. There is plenty going on with our characters and some mystery. It has basically no Christmas charm, so it's not a great read in that aspect for this time of year, but I still had an ok time with it.
