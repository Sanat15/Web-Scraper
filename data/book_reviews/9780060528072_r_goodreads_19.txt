Rating: 3/5

I thought I’d read all the D&amp;P books months ago, and I was surprised to find this one on a shelf. I might have liked it better if I was still in a Dalziel and Pascoe mood, but my universes have changed since. And apart from the detectives, I didn’t like most of the characters. And then again, it takes place shortly after 9/11 and that was an unhappy time. Oh, well.
