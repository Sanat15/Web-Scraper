Rating: 3/5

“Were all the jewels of Golconda awaiting me on my solution of this enigma, I am quite sure that I should be unable to earn them.”

Exactly my attitude upon exposure to most puzzles, and due to my inabilities, I am not a mystery reader or word puzzler, and this is a story for those folks.

But, Poe writes in layers of complexity, and there were other things I enjoyed here. 

The build up to the mystery was good: a remote island, a question of madness, an unusual and creepy bug.

The dog played a key role in the plot, so that’s always good. 

And the character of Jupiter was intriguing. A racist, offensive depiction, but the most likable character. He is loyal, funny, and essential to the success of the undertaking. And Poe gives him a name that means king of the gods.

But this is a howdunit, not a horror story, which for me was disappointing.
