Rating: 2/5

When I read a Regency romance I like it to follow some of the conventions. Samantha was so heedless of the dictates of society that I would have liked to see her stewing in her own juices a bit more - she certainly deserved some grief for her thoughtless actions. Samantha was certainly no Grand Sophy (I suspect Sophy was the model), &amp; Martin isn't a patch on Heyer. It seems as if the author read a few Regencies &amp; then knocked out her version without doing any research whatsoever.

The book was a bit too silly for my liking but, for all that, it was a light, amusing, escapist couple of hours I spent with this book.
