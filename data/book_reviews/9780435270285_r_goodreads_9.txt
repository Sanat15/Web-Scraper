Rating: 4/5

Samuel Miles aka Smiler is on the run from the police. He has been found guilty of a crime he did not commit and sent to a reform school which he escaped from. Whilst hiding out he comes across a cheetah named Yarra who has escaped from Longlet. He thinks that he must help Yarra and not turn her in when they share their hiding places. Smiler soon gets a job working with animals and is happy. He still has a long wait for his fathers return but they in the meantime things go wrong and he had to make a run for it. Will he manage to evade the police until his father returns. 
I liked this book and recommend it. It was easy reading and I am looking forward to readying the next books in the series. Thank you to netgalley and the author for the chance to read this book in exchange for my honest review.
