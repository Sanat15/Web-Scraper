Probably one of my most successful Booksale finds in HS. Although, I would mark this as older middle-grade for the content and character ages instead of young adult, so that's one to take into consideration. An intriguing read, Sorcerers of the Nightwing follows Devon March, as he steps into Misery Point with more questions in his backpack than a fifteen year old should carry. The book has a simplistic writing style, but that is not a deterrent to creating a creepy atmosphere and exciting action scenes. Rereading this as an adult transports me back to the exact moment I first read this - a little cringe but nevertheless endearing.
