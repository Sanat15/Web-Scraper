Rating: 4/5

This was my fourth Pilcher novel and I knew what I’d get for 600+ pages of my time: lovely setting (Scotland), well drawn characters, family drama (in the most genteel British sense), and a story in which to lose myself while drinking hot tea and biscuits on a cold and snowy day. This one had a fun connection with The Shell Seekers - an overlapping minor character. While I loved this quintessential reading experience, I struggled just a bit to keep all the main characters and their relationships to each other completely straight. Still, a completely delightful read. I will continue to seek out her books, at a rate of about one per year ;-)
