Rating: 3/5

WHAT HAPPENS SHOULDN’T HAPPEN TO A DOG. BUT IT DOES. AND IT’S SENSATIONAL!

Snoopy’s off on a daring escapade while never leaving the safety of his kennel… This book comprises about 50% of my WWI knowledge. The humour generally arises from Snoopy’s dramatisations, which sometimes appear in third person in the manner of an old Pathé newsreel, and through the sudden intrusions of the real world. My favourite is the description of a ‘small French farm house’… we know it’s French, Snoopy, you’ve been shot down in France!
