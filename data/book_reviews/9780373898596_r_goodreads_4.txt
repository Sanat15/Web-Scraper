Rating: 5/5

I love Janet Dailey books. Reading or re-reading one of her books is nostalgic as teen I devoured so many of her books. I loved the Calder series.

This is a new book for me, about the time she started publishing the Americana series, with school and working part time I didn't have the same amount of reading time.

I so enjoyed this book! The main characters all had unique qualities and strengths and weaknesses. The author tried to keep secret which man was going to marry the female main character but it was obvious to me.

This story sucked you in from the beginning and you were rooting for all three main characters even though you knew only one male could win.

I would recommend this to everyone to read if you want to escape for a little while.
