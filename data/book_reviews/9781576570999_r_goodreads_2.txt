Rating: 5/5

One of my new favourites. I can't remember if I read this in the late-90s, but as an adult I can appreciate its sense of fun and great atmosphere. The details of the natural environment abound and do much to balance out Harry's portrayal, which isn't quite as in-depth as I would've liked. Note: if you enjoy reading accounts of Carter's unearthing of Tut's tomb, you'll really get a kick out of this one. Also if you've ever walked in on anyone in the bathroom...

One more thought. Anyone else think Buddy Nevins could or should have been an antagonist?
