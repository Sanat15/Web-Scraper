Rating: 4/5

Look, this is one for the Niven completist. It isn't a collection of short stories (though it does contain some, and the new ones are actually very good). Nor is it an autobiography, but does have some autobiographical stories.
Basically if you're forwarned and a fan of Niven's you'll enjoy it, but if you're not forwarned you will be annoyed. If you're not already a fan, this won't make you one.
Read it if you get it very cheap and like Larry Niven's work.
