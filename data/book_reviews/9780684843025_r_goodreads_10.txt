Rating: 4/5

Readable without ever quite becoming absolutely essential. A limpid moment every dozen or so pages. "Comprehending my Cat Jeoffry" asks the very serious question, "How does it happen that our bodies get all the necessary things done for us, or most of them anyway, without our conscious minds being required to intervene or even supervise the process?" The answer - which is implicit, rather than expressed - punctures our hubris, and should shape our designs and intentions far, far more than it does.
