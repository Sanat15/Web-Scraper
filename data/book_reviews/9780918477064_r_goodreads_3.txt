Rating: 5/5

Although not a bride myself (my wife and I read it together), I enjoyed the book very much. Mrs. Hildebrand has her husband's wit and a woman's heart. Rooted in the philosophy of personalism, her advices are well-argued, profound, and in accordance with the Catholic faith.
A recurring themes in her letters are the "Tabor vision" of one's beloved (recalling a moment with your partner when you were amazed by him — like apostles on Tabor), and the recognition of love as the only means that allows you to see clearly who the beloved truly is.
