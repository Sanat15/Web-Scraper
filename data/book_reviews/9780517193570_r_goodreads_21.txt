Rating: 4/5

Edward is an aspiring writer living in London. The narrator, a teacher and University friend of Edward, tells the story looking back on events. After some success early in his career, Edward is invited to the house of a famous author in the South of France after writing a criticism of him. After the author dies Edward is bequeathed a mysterious manuscript which plays a role in his becoming successful. 

It’s a dark tale that gets darker. Its an intriguing take on the role of literature, and a sparse but haunting tale of possession and entrapment.
