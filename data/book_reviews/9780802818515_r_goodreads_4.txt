Rating: 3/5

Quite a different book about marriage. I do like how short it is, but I do like how gospel-centered the book is. And while yes, I found it hard to concentrate on it fully b/c of some other things going on in my life right now, I see the huge payoff. Now looking at my relationship, I definitely want to keep it Christ-centered. And it is through the Holy Spirit that is changing and transforming us to be more God-fearing, and as a result, seek the benefit of each other. One message that honestly scares me is the fact that in the New Heavens and the New Earth, there will be no more marriage except that all of us will be married to Christ. That scares me because I love my girlfriend, but with this in mind, knowing that our future marriage is only a reflection (a "copy" according to Bromiley) of the marital union with Christ, I know that, as the head of the household, I must always strive to succeed in my husband's duties and responsibilities. But most importantly, love her with all my being, not just because of infatuation or intimacy, but also because of a loving commitment to grow more Christlike and to show to others Christ through our marriage.
