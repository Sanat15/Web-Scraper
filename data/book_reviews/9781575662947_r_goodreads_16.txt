Rating: 1/5

A gay love story, cats, and Catholic theological drama. What could go wrong? With this book...everything.

Look, I know it's hard to write a good novel. I don't bother panning books that are just poorly written. But when they're as arrogant and mean-spirited as this one, this Abyssinian's claws come out.

This author never misses a chance to show his revulsion and contempt for overweight people, women, and effeminate men. He's projecting all his internalized sissy-phobia onto his antagonist characters so that his protagonist and boring love interest can display their perfectly assimilated, straight-acting, upper-class minimalist good taste. Which is ironic because the dramatic dialogue in "Flight Dreams" is as unintentionally campy as Madeline Kahn in "Clue" shrieking "Flames on the side of my face!"
