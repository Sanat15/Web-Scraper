Rating: 5/5

Prior to reading this, I have read upwards of 40 Vietnam War accounts and memoirs. This collection of players and periphery participants in the war is rich, unbiased and detailed. I loved it, and have a better understanding of the US presidency, military strategy, the communist movement, media's portrayal of the war, and the Vietnamese accounts of both the North and South. Apparent through this reading is the obvious importance of international politics and the need for bi-partisan efforts.
