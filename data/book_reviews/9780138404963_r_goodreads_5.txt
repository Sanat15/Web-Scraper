THE BIBLE OF STAGE MAKEUP.

Of all the stage make up artistry books and "how-tos" I have read, this book is by far the best. Richard Corson does a fantastic job demonstrating different makeup trends ranging from the production of "Cats" to "Phantom of the Opera." He also elaborates on the many different processes for aging makeup and and the use of prosthetic. I (unfortunately) had to self-teach myself many tricks of the trade by using this book as my guide, and was able to get free-lance gigs due to the knowledge I gained from Richard Corson.
