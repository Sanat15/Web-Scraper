Rating: 4/5

This book took me nearly a whole year to finish reading but not because it was boring or bad, the story itself is actually quite compelling but in a kind of meandering type of way. I could not read it for 3 months then pick it up again and it was like visiting an old friend. 

This book is propelled solely on vibes alone, despite ostensibly revolving around a murder mystery that was probably the part of the book that was least interesting. Living inside Ray's head and hearing him recall all these horrific memories in such a matter of fact, almost languid tone is somehow hypnotic despite some of the truly awful things he recounts.

The dull Americana backdrop with a sinister underbelly has the tone of both something like Sharp Objects by Gillian Flynn or a JT Leroy story, incredibly evocative that you feel sweaty and grimy just reading it. 

Happy that I finally finished it, a very heartfelt and bittersweet book that I'll definitely be thinking about for a long time to come.
