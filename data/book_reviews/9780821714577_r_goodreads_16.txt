Rating: 4/5

4 1/2 stars.

This book was absolutely beautiful.

I loved the hero—he was nearly perfect. My only issue was how harsh he became when the heroine tried to leave him. Due to his carelessness and anger, she temporarily loses her eyesight. While he never intended to hurt her, the fact remains that his actions contributed to what happened. Thankfully, she eventually regains her vision, but it was heartbreaking to watch her go through that ordeal. To be fair, the hero suffers deeply for his mistake, which made the situation easier to accept.

Apart from that, I truly loved the story. The romance between the hero and heroine was wonderful, and the hero’s love for the heroine was especially touching and heartfelt.

I do have one more complaint, though. It seems to be the author’s trademark to keep the main couple apart for long periods. Once again, there are months of separation before they finally reunite for good. While the payoff is satisfying, the prolonged separation can be frustrating.

Overall, I thoroughly enjoyed the book and would definitely recommend it.
