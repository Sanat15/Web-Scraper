Rating: 3/5

i had to keep on re-starting this book as it didn't catch my interest at first, but when i was left with nothing else to read, i finished it. It's set in the famine in Ireland. Brother and sister, Sean and Annie, have a chance to escape the famine, but they have to leave their dying mother behind and search for their father.
Micheal Morrpurgo was my favourite author for a very long time when i was younger, and still have a lot of love for a few of his books but this one would not be one of my favourites.
