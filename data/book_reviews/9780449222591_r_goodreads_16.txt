Rating: 2/5

2.2 stars. If you think of this more as a spiteful comedy romp that is trying and failing to be clever rather than a romance, you might like it. I lost my patience with it. The heroine was a farce of a spinster, a so called feminist bluestocking who was really a judgemental prude and was such a letdown. Was the author jeering at bluestocking feminists?

The Second main character was not the hero, but a gluttonous beauty who scoffed food and never bathed, and then graduated from gluttony for food to gluttony for sex. Wtf?

I didn't find it amusing. There was no real romance here. Just a bunch of people being judgemental and falling out and a couple of caricature villains being villainous. Meh.
