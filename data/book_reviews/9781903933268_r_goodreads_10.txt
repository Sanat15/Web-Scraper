Rating: 5/5

Once I debated with an author I look after about humans' descent from other animals. He showed me the ultrasound scan of a human fetus growing in the womb through its various phases. I saw the fetus passing through fish, through frogs, through other mammals before shaping into a human being. Then he lent me this book from his library. Darwin had proved what my eyes saw for a human fetus. Some cultures see animals as our ancestors. This book scientifically proves that truth.

This book also pushed me to ask the following: Among other animals, bulls are also our ancestors. While killing an animal painlessly for food seems reasonable and acceptable, how can we torture a fighting bull in arenas, humiliate it by turning in circles, and then inflict it a slow, agonizing death? Only for our entertainment? Could we do this to our human ancestors? Or to our enemies? In full public view? In civilized nations that see public bloodshed as violent, yet consider it legal?

Didn't Victor Hugo say: Corrida tortures not only the bull, but also the human conscience?

What does such shows of public torture do for our children?
