Rating: 4/5

The '80s and '90s really glamorized institutionalization. 

Marina (name not revealed until the last page) was in a mental hospital after the accident, but that's not the best place to keep a teen forever and her mom and the new husband don't want to deal with her, so she's sent to boarding school, where she is gradually accepted into a dorm of girls despite her scarred face and not talking. Marina is wrapped up in her own, very valid, issues, but comes to understand that everyone in her group has their own fourteen-year-old girl problems. It's a nice book, it's short, it's a first novel, and the English teacher is such a Mary Sue for Marsden himself. It's not brilliant like the Tomorrow books but it's worth reading if you're a Marsden fan or you want something quick and angsty.
