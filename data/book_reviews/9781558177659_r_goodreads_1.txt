Rating: 3/5

The Highwayman by Nadine Crenshaw (1993) is a typical highwayman HR with story of adventure, escape and love. It begins with a mysterious highwayman on the Yorkshire moors… very atmospheric, he’s robbing folks. He survived prison.. this is now his life. 

Then a bit later the story moves to the heroine,18 year old Jane who works as a domestic servant in York for the elderly Mr Welby. She’s been saving her money for two years… she wants to emigrate to the Georgia Colony. Her horrible father unexpectedly shows up, wants her money and takes her away against her will. Disaster continues for Jane … and at her lowest moment she’s rescued by a mysterious highwayman… Keith Grayson. 

This is not a slow burn romance.., but it’s a slow moving story. Lots of descriptions and lots of introspection by Jane and Keith overwhelm the plot. I had to skim read paragraphs to get to the good bits. There are passionate scenes … but the descriptions are brief. Keith was done wrong early in life and suffered mightily, and Jane was wrongly accused of a crime… together they forge a new life together thru their love. 

Overall, a middle of the road romantic story with likeable characters, a bit of danger and a dab of passion. ⭐️⭐️⭐️
