Rating: 5/5

This book has a lot of excellent information. It combines psychology with Feng Shui principles. There are exercises that help you dig deep into your subconscious mind; which in turn help you to become more aware of yourself and your actions. The exercises help you become aware of what is keeping you from having abundance in your life. And abundance doesn't necessarily mean money. It can be abundance in relationships, job fulfillment, ideas, joy, etc. 
It is suppose to be a nine week program, but in my personal opinion I don't think most people can finish this program in 9 weeks as it is very detailed and involved. More like 9 months I would say. But it is worth the effort for sure.
