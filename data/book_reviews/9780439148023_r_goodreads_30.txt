Rating: 4/5

Another read aloud for my six year old. I had never read this before! Charming and silly. Beverly Clearly is so skilled at depicting children. Their innocence, their wonder, what delights and bugs them. My six year old seemed to relate to both the pesky 4 year old and the mature 9 year old. Fitting as he has younger siblings that drive him nuts AND is also a pest himself quite often. I think he felt seen listening to me read this which was cute. My 11 year old listened in occasionally and was totally into it too, although he probably never admit it.

Reread it in summer 2023–William is 7.5 and the twins are 5. This books really is so cute and perfect for this age range. They laughed and related. The audio version is cute.
