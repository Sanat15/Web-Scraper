Rating: 4/5

This is one of the best books I have read by Phyllis A. Whitney, written of the true story of the San Francisco earthquake and fire in the early 1900's. It begins when a mother and daughter travel from Chicago to make a new home in San Francisco with a wealthy family living on Nob Hill. This book carries the reader through a romance or two as well as the earthquake and the fire, as well as much manipulation by the owner of one large home.
