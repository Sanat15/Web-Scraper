An excellent read. As usual, I prefer an old school paperback, and this, like the others is over an inch thick. So lots of story. A couple of issues jarred a little a couple of inconsistencies may have been missed except when they fall in the same paragraph. Also, the use of language in dialogue. Everyone speaks with the same style no matter if they are human, elf, dwarf or other. I find that odd, but not odd enough that it spoils the book. 

Loved the Jahdo and Meer story. Lots more characters and lots of adventure. Best of all. A dragon. 

Although a series, I think it would be possible to read this one as a standalone.
