Rating: 5/5

Another enjoyable book by this author. The characters are well drawn and I particularly like Alethea as she is clever and witty. James is the town flirt who isn’t usually serious about anyone. However, what starts out as a game for him, soon becomes more serious, but how does he prove to Alethea that he is a serious contender for her hand? No hot sex scenes, but good old fashioned quality story writing!
