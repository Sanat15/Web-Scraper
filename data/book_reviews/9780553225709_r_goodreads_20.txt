Rating: 5/5

instant favorite, especially for the last book (titular coming into the country). i feel like i know the entire town of Eagle circa the 1970s at this point. mcphee's descriptions of people is so good, and the metaphor game here was off the charts. as is tradition, my iowa-brain still can't comprehend any sort of mountain formations described, but compared to Annals this was barely a problem here. painted about as good of a picture as i think you possibly could of Eagle.

"the encircled river" (book 1) also incredibly good, but overshadowed by talking to the locals later on. wasn't as enamored with "what they were hunting for", but still good.

the real highlight is it gave me plenty of fodder regarding the alaska native claims settlement act to bug people with who may or may not have to deal with it frequently.
