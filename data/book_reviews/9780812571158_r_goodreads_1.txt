Rating: 5/5

Review: Coyote Summer By w. Michael Gear
One of the first books I shared with the avid reader known as my mother, I loved the story of the wild west, the familiar Native American tribes and the interactions between Richard Hamilton, and Willow, inspires the spirit that there is a way to solve any problem. The rogue that is Richard has created an avid fan of many of the Fansite group. Willow is a character that is impressive in the magic that she brings to the story.
