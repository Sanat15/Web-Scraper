Rating: 3/5

Our oldest loves making crafts and she loves nature, so this was a great find for her at her elementary school library. She's borrowed the book a couple of times and has made a couple of the crafts already. 

The instructions are clear and the photographs are helpful for showing the different stages of the projects. We both thought this was a good book to inspire children to make things out of natural materials. Some of the projects are simple enough for any child to make, but most are fairly complex and would require adult supervision and/or assistance.
