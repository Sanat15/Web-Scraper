Rating: 2/5

Well that was slow and painful.
Apparently all can be solved with a Public-Private Partnership, being the right person for the job and a $1,000,000 gift to "kick things off". I jest but only sort of.

This is a thorough personal and family history and it's about what you'd expect from one. It's interesting to get his view of the various world leaders and historic events Mr. Rockefeller met and was involved in and there a good deal of candor he brings to "not everything is perfect in Rockefeller-land". Nonetheless, the writing is dry and lacks the passion necessary to get the reader to set aside the feeling of 560 pages of humble-bragging.
