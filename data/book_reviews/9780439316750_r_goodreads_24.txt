Rating: 3/5

3 stars - An older middle-grade/teen novel (and also a Disney movie five years ago) about the hazards &amp; fleeting nature of popularity and being an outsider. I quite liked StarGirl herself, less so the narrator Leo here. Couldn’t help but make comparisons to current events, sigh… This story might help some kid but I think that school is quite different now with social media, at least from this dated book. Got a few challenge prompts done with this one though!💃🏻
