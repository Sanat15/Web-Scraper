Rating: 3/5

This is 2nd book in the series. So read the 1st book before you start on this one otherwise you wont be able to get the background. Also the events in the 1st book are important to understand this book.

Again a build up to the previous book.
Really looking forward to the 3rd book on this series where all the mystery will be solved.

 The whole thing with Clark has me thinking this is a paranormal book. Reading the 1st book I got the impression it is just a murder mystery. 
Makes things interesting.

Also, I hate what Jessie does to Clair. It was horrible and I just felt like punching her. I found most of the characters in the book very superficial. Their friend is dead and they are worried about Homecoming!!

Also, Bubba is one strange fellow. Don't know where to place him. Good Friend or selfish jerk

Love Michael. The only person who is not selfish.

Also, The Rock and his other side had me surprised.
