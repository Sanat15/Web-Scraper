Rating: 5/5

If you think you know Jesus, this book will make you look at him through a completely different lens. Nolan strips away the "stained-glass" image we see in churches and gives us a raw, historical portrait of a man who was deeply embedded in the social conflicts of his time.
What I loved about this book is how it highlights Jesus as an outsider who stood up for the marginalized and challenged the status quo. Even though it was written decades ago, it feels incredibly relevant today—it’s about justice, human dignity, and the courage to act in the face of inequality. It’s not just a "religious book"; it’s an inspiring look at a protagonist who chose to get his hands dirty to change the world. A must-read if you're interested in the intersection of history, social justice, and personal conviction.
