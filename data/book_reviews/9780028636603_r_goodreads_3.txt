Rating: 4/5

I'd given it a flying five stars if it fleshed out more how to reliably put people in trance.
Otherwise the book is brilliant, filled with delicious tidbits of historical and therapeutic insight, TCIGT Hypnosis provides the essential base of knowledge everyone should have about hypnosis both as a hypnotist and as a subject.

I cannot recommend this enough and wish you start thinking hypnotically about how minds work.
