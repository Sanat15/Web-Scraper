Rating: 4/5

If you like your murder mysteries with a bit of humor - this is your series.

I started to listen to book 2 first, but stopped and listened to book one - glad I did as there are spoilers in book 2.

This series is set in San Diego at a world famous and revered hotel on the beach. It is lovingly monitored by Am Cauldwell, the Assistant Manager. He has an unpleasant boss who does not give praise, only negativity.

During the course of the story we hear about the vagaries of the hotel business. Since guests are just passing through they are not always on their best behavior. In addition, there are the quirky hotel employees that a manager has to deal with.

In this first episode there are accidental deaths, intentional deaths and a possible suicide. Adding to the chaos is a convention of Bob Johnsons.

I thoroughly enjoyed this first in the series. I own both the book and the audio book. You should join in the fun.
