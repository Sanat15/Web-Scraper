Rating: 3/5

This is a great primer to getting involved in animal-assisted therapy. Whether you have a dog, a cat, a horse, a llama, or a gerbil, Dr. Burch has something to say to you! Her work is inspiring and her stories of the value animals give to people never fail to astound me. The book itself is rather old (published in 1995), but its content is still relevant for anyone who has considered volunteering with their pet. I hope to return to this book one day and train our (future) dog for therapy work.
