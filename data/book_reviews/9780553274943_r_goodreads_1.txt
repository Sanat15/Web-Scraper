Rating: 3/5

Mr. Estleman always turns out competent readable product and he likes to cross genres.In many ways he reminds me of Elmore Leonard.  Bloody Season was the first book of his that I read. It's a well written fictional account of the events surrounding the infamous gunfight at the O.K. Corral in Tombstone, Arizona in October 1881. The novel came out just a few years before the release of the movies Tombstone and Wyatt Earp and I have no doubt this novel served as an inspiration for those two movies. The book does not portray either side as being all good or all bad. It shows that violence begets violence and the issues that led up to the violence are rather murky at best. This is a well researched and solidly written novel that is more of a historical drama than a western. I recommend it for fans of westerns and those who aren't fans of westerns, but enjoy intelligent fiction.
