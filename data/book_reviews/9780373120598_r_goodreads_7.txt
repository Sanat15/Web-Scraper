Rating: 2/5

This story chugs along nicely until the heroine returns home, then it slams to a halt. Just repetitive nonsense to drag out the story. A lot of cousins forced couples, ew. Hero is a lot, and after he finds out the heroine isn’t his cousin(she’s his OTHER cousin 🙄) he is insufferable. So many pointless side characters, that need to be trimmed. The long lost family member angle wasn’t bad but it really comes outta nowhere. Read it I guess.
