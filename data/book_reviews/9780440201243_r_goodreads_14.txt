Rating: 5/5

I picked this one up because of my love of her previous works, and am fascinated by cheetahs.
Usually if someone puts a little personal spin on history, I find it needs a delicate balance between believable and outright silly. I found that Clare Bell did a good job in adding her own flair to an existing story in history (and I say story because of how old the characters are in our history).

And of course, she again does a wonderful job of creating a main character that you can feel a twinge of empathy for.
