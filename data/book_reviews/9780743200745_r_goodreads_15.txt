Rating: 5/5

57 is my favorite number and for that reason I try to make the 57th book I read in a year about a subject that is very dear to me. It just so happens that male friendship is one of my absolute favorite subjects, so I was happy to make this my 57th book for 2026. It wasn’t just about friendship but also family relationships on several occasions, which we of interest too. Still, my favorite chapters where when the author wrote about friendships, his own and notable ones in history. It’s a shame that in a world obsessed with romances that books about male friendship are rather rare.
