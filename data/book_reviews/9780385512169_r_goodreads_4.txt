Rating: 3/5

This is a very literate collection of nine short stories. In this instance, I define literate as meaning that the author doesn't come out and tell you what he wants to say, but rather talks around it and makes you guess, and if you come up with something good then you get a feeling of profound accomplishment... but you're never really sure. It's just like some of those offbeat New Yorker cartoons, which is where my favorite of the stories collected here originally appeared, Super Goat Man, and "cartoons" is part of the title of the collection, and so I think I must be on to something. Super Goat Man is one of the several stories that has an element of fantasy to it and is about a B-list superhero who retires to the academic life. Access Fantasy is about a traffic jam. The Vision is about comics and cosplay and growing up. They're all very nicely written stories, full of charm and wit and clever phrases. I enjoyed reading it, but doubt that I'll remember any of the details for very long.
