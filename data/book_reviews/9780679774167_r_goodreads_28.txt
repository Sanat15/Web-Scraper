Rating: 4/5

i absolutely could not stop reading this book from the very start. a very strong four if not four and a half stars (maybe five?!) 

tony lord and his best friend sam are the standout athletes in their small ohio town, full of the bravura and ego that only youth can bring. when tony’s girlfriend is found dead with him standing over her body, he is immediately cast in the eyes of the whole town as a murder. but he is never charged, and manages to get out of dodge, go to harvard and become a renowned defense attorney married to a beautiful actress. 

twenty eight years later, tony is called back to the town by sam’s wife. sam, now the local high school vice principal, is suspected of the murder of a young student with whom he may have been having an inappropriate relationship. 

what follows is an absolutely brilliant portrayal of friendship in its forms of both love and loathing, the nuances of the human psyche and the stories we tell ourselves, and ultimately just a riveting legal drama and mystery. 

i could not tear myself away from this story or its flawed characters. this is everything a good thriller should be, thought provoking, suspenseful, and deeply compelling. i couldn’t get enough.
