Rating: 4/5

The Nonexistent Knight is the third part of a series by Calvino called Our Ancestors. 
1. The Cloven Viscount ◑ 4★
2. The Baron in the Trees 𐂷 5★ (⚘Not to be missed!❀)
3. The Nonexistent Knight ♞ 4★

Rimbault is a knight who has no body or form but exists inside his armor. It causes lots of confusion for him and others. This is a humorous story of the nonexistent knight navigating a solid world of form, and yearning for love and connection in the process. The humor reminded me a little of reading my beloved Terry Pratchett because Calvino crafts absurdity in the most unexpected and delightful ways. I laugh, giggle, and think about the preposterousness. He is navigating the world as best he can, but is rather misunderstood. 

♘"Well, for someone who doesn’t exist, you seem in fine form!”

Being nonexistent obviously impedes one's ability to connect with others. Calvino explores what this feeling of isolation and alienation does to the psyche without ever becoming heavy-handed. 
♘"Take off or put on has no meaning for me.”
♘"Don’t you see you get more out of life than I do, corpsey?”

Taking off and putting on develops a new meaning for "corpsey" LOL. 

At one point he goes 'missing' and when his colleagues are looking for him they say well if you haven't seen him around, that means he must be here. Rimbault is looking for a "certainty of existing that only a woman can give." Of course this also leads to the question, "isn't he a bit of a lightweight in bed?". He does find love and together they work out the complexities of this rather existential disembodied dilemma. 

My favorite of Our Ancestors is definitely Baron in the Trees, but this whole series of novellas is delightful, dealing with some similar themes always in a playful way.

4.25/5🌟
