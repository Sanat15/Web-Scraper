Rating: 4/5

When Dizzy was persuaded by her best friend, Christi to go to her Uncle Zach's (a fusty-dusty professor) castle to make Christi appeared responsible and level-headed, she didn't expect to encounter a "Greek god", swimming naked. And when she reached the castle and meet the professor, she had a shock for it was her "Greek god"!

The story started with that cute opening chapter and it was a nice read. Not too dramatic which I like. It was a light romance. Sweet and sexy without being too mushy and too explicit in sex.

I find this a very nice read!
