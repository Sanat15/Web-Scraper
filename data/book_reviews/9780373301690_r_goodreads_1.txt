Rating: 2/5

I was expecting some steamy adult romance, and i guess i got what i was looking for but also was more annoyed by other things. 

I just could not bring myself to like Jake. He was showing Anger Issues and Rape Tendencies. 
Maybe these things were ok and were the norm in the 80s when this book was published, who knows.But I just could not stand the author romanticizing him over and over. 

I really liked Amber's determination to be independent and her starting her own business from the scratch! Real girl power there. You go, girl!
