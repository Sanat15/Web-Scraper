Rating: 5/5

Fun and fast, silly and stupid. I picked up my Grandpa's Stepdad's copy of this book and was pleasantly surprised. This is the first Western that I've read -- now I see why there were stacks on stacks of old Westerns in his library. 

Between scenes of action and surprise, there was a good amount of what my Professor used to call "philosophical white sugar." Meaning: small, usually unnoticed lessons on ethics and morals within our modes of entertainment. In creating an exceptional protagonist, L'Amour also created a role model and set of expectations for his audience of young men. Even if some thoughts are outdated, I'm charmed, maybe inspired by the author's notion of what a good man should be like.

But it's just a silly little book. It's a great lighthearted read.
