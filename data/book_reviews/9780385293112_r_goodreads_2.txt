Rating: 1/5

How I adored this book in my youth, recommending it as the best guide to the New Age at the time (over and above say Marilyn Ferguson's Aquarian Conspiracy). It was all part of the dynamic that led me to living in Findhorn and devouring what Spangler wrote and said . . . What can I say? The author is not without a noble spirit. Yet in time, I would see there was so much Findhorn could never give me, nor give the world. For it tries to leapfrog over the greatest Mystery of all ... Or as Rudolf Steiner said bypass it. But from Steiner I would go even further in directions that bewildered my old friends at Findhorn. More about that here:
