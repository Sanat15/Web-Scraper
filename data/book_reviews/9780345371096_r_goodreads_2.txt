Rating: 3/5

March 2021 reread in Kindle omnibus "General Practice": Adjusting my rating from 3 to 3.5*
------------------------
This 8th book of the Sector General series is the first one to not feature Dr. Conway since the very first book. While I found it interesting to have a non-human main character for the first time, Lorien (a Tarlan) was a more frustrating protagonist than Conway ever was. Plus the many discussions about religion, though appropriate, were of less interest to me than other types of ethical debates. However, I would be interested in reading more about Lorien in a future book to see how his "punishment" ends up! White made a small but significant change in his writing which enhanced the 'alien-ness' of the narrator - all masculine &amp; feminine pronouns were replaced by neuter ones (it instead of he or she).

Oh, and just in case anyone is horrified by the title - Lorien isn't a mad killer or even a mercy killer, just an arrogant doctor who made a mistake due to insufficient information and impatience. This is not a spoiler as these facts come out in the very beginning of the book.
