Rating: 4/5

Although medieval times are not my primary interest, from time to time I enjoy a good novel set in that time, and this one was very well done. The novel follows a man (Martin) who escapes from feudalism and after much suffering and some luck becomes wealthy. His wives, children and grandchildren are the main characters in the story. The relevant part of the life of each character is narrated by that character, so the reader sees different perspectives of life at that time (male, female, rich and poor). From knights and castles to abysmal poverty, all is portrayed in magnificent detail. It is clear that the author has done very extensive research, because the very detailed picture she paints of medieval life is absolutely tangible, even though it is so foreign to the life we live today. If you are looking for a wonderful vision of life in the 1400’s and an enjoyable family saga, this is a good one.
