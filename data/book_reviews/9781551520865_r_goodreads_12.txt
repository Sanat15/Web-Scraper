Rating: 3/5

”Holiday meals were extravagant, yet conservative, served in heavy eighties handmade pottery bowls on matching woven placemats, cooked with great care but no love, cooked in reverence to obligation, not art.”

An interesting little collection, with very short snippets of what it was like to be a queer person growing up in the Yukon in the 90's. I connected with the themes of these pieces, and I liked some of the sentences very much, but overall, I felt like the works were quite short, and many of them were tied up in a neat bow at the end. I would have preferred more ambiguity, and less moralization at the end of each story. Favorite stories: "She Comes Home a Moth," "This, That and the Other Thing," "There Goes the Bride"
