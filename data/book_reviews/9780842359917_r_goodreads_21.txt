Rating: 5/5

Both to Christians who are passionate about sharing the Good News and to distracted American softies who forget about the persecuted church — read this book!” Safely Home challenged me to pray for the persecuted church. It also convicted me to be bold in the Gospel while I am in this “trial of prosperity” in America where I can place more importance on my comfort than on sharing the Good News. 

Safely Home gives the reader a perspective into the author’s depiction of the spiritual powers at play behind the scenes of the story. It convicted me to live this present moment as if it eternity depends on it — because it does. 

But if you just want a good story, moral aside, I still recommend it — it’s interesting and engaging. I found it hard to put down.
