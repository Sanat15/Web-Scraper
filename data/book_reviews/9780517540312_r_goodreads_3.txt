Rating: 5/5

I've read many biographies of the Louisa May Alcott and her family, but this one is one of the best. Incredibly detailed in its research. Beautifully written (Bedell's style is creative, full of imagery, even humor). It also includes intimate details and analyses not included in any other biographies, creating a sharper image of the family members and their contemporaries. My only critique is that by the end, the biography is primarily focused on Bronson, with hardly a mention of the other members of the family, even though it covers the 1850s when the daughters were young women working in Boston and New York. However, since Bedell had planned a second volume and died before finishing it, it's possible she meant to have that open the second volume.
