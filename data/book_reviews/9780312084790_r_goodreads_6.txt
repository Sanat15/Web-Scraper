Rating: 3/5

I really liked the class I read this book for, Informal Fallacies, but I believe that the book could have been better. The examples were mediocre and in some cases, had more than one answer though it was not specified. We discussed it in full in my class about the pros and cons of the book and I believe it is an excellent book but that there is one we have not yet found that would be a better alternative. I enjoyed the comics the book had in every chapter, the writing was engaging and the pictures kept one captivated by the examples. 
Overall: 6.7/10
