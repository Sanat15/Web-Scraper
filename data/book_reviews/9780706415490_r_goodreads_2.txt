Rating: 3/5

I've been working through this book for nearly two months - to be honest I put it aside and only just came back to it. It's like with most anthologies, some of the stories you really enjoy and some you don't. And what I didn't realise was that a lot of the stories are excerpts from full length books.

A few of the stories gave me that classic 70s/80s pony story feel, which I love since I read a lot of those growing up. A few were weird! But it does go to show how horses can permeate our lives in so many different ways, for those of us who choose to love and/or live with them.
