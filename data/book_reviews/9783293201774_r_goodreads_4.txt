Rating: 5/5

I read this book at the end of junior high school when I was really into works of erotic fiction. My goal at that time was to become a erotic novelist so I poured myself into books of the genre. Black Erotica is a collection of sensual works of writing, from poetry to short stories written by a wonderful mosaic of people representing all parts of black culture. It is a great example of how sensual, free, loving black eroticism can be. I owned this book and this was my very first foray into erotica written by black people. I was use to reading books by white writers and their views of eroticism so it was very eye opening for me as an African-American woman. The current erotica in black novels can't hold a candle in my opinion to the way the words in this book were strung together. Not demeaning, far from overly masochist but very sensual.
