Rating: 3/5

Captain Horatio Hornblower of the English ship, Sutherland, emerges as a short-tempered warmonger. He seems to be happiest when anticipating battles. His saving grace is his love for a certain unborn child. In the end he gets his comeuppance, more or less.

I do wish these novels were not so violent, but that's war, and this is a tale of life in the English navy during the Napoleonic wars. 

Hornblower is also unfortunately caught between two women: (1) his pregnant wife, Maria, to whom he is physically faithful while not 100% appreciating her, and (2) Lady Barbara, a considerably more attractive woman who he fell in love with during the sixth novel of the series. She has married someone else, however, so things aren't looking great for any real relationship with her.

I'm reading these books with a few other Booktubers... because I was curious and wanted to know what was in them. This is the seventh of eleven in the series.

I'm giving this one only three stars because of the level of violence. One battle after another. Hundreds of men killed. No, thousands.
