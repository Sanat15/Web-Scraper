Rating: 4/5

A teenage boy lives and explores the Amazon rainforest with his archaeologist parents- and finds buried treasure which may be too good to be true..

Exploring the jungle through MD Spenser's eyes was a unique experience and one I really enjoyed and connected with. The description of tropical animals and plants in the forest really gave me the idea of travelling through the jungle in search of ancient secrets.

He finds some buried treasure, keeps it and somehow bad karma befalls his family. He endeavours to find out the secret of the Myan king to stop the curse.
