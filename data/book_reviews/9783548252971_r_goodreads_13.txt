The first half was *chefs kiss.

The last half was just dumb. I can’t even say it without spoiling. It came out of nowhere. Had me saying. “Really? That’s the pay off?”

I listened to this on audible because it was free. The audio book was terrific. The narrations were perfect. 

Screw it, I’m going to spoil it for those that want to know what happens. So if you don’t want to be spoiled, don’t read further.. 

Starting NOW! 

It was a KGB experiment… yup she was part of a KGB experiment. 

The haunting isn’t supernatural.

Susan’s doctors and caretakers — specifically Dr. Hamilton — are manipulating her as part of a psychological experiment.

Dr. Hamilton:
	•	Is obsessed with proving that trauma and suggestion can completely reconstruct a person’s reality.
	•	Believes he can “cure” her brain trauma by recreating the original trauma in controlled stages.
	•	Is essentially gaslighting her into believing she’s being stalked by dead men.

The four boys?
	•	They weren’t dead.
	•	Hamilton tracked them down.
	•	He used them as actors in his experiment.
	•	He staged the entire haunting scenario.

The goal: break her psychologically and then “rebuild” her mind.

After Susan escapes and the plot is exposed…

Tony is revealed to be alive.

He never died in the original assault.

He survived — but had undergone surgery and physical reconstruction. His altered appearance is why Susan didn’t recognize him earlier.

He has been in the story under a different identity.

So:
	•	The haunting wasn’t supernatural.
	•	The doctors were manipulating her.
	•	The bullies were tools in the experiment.
	•	Tony survived and ultimately reunites with her.
