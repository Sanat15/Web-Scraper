Rating: 5/5

4.5 stars
This was my first time reading Camus and I really enjoyed his writing style and appreciate the way his brain worked. There are so many quotes I wrote down or had to reread a few times just to let them sink in. He also touched on a lot of varied topics and i agreed with nearly everything he said. Everything he wrote was handled with such tact. For example, his section on Christianity. It's so fascinating for me to hear unbelievers' opinions on Christianity as a Christian myself, though sometimes it's also frustrating. But what he wrote was very respectful and showed he had a clear understanding of our beliefs, and many of the things he wrote still ring true in modern Christianity. I think his take on the death penalty was the most interesting part of this book. I've always been very against the death penalty, but I never even considered the reasons he gave against it. After reading this, I don't know how anyone could support the death penalty. And the final section on artists, I didn't always understand fully what he was saying in certain parts, but overall, I loved his general concept of what art should be. He also had a very wide vocabulary and I had to look up many definitions while reading hahah
