Rating: 4/5

This is a what if history, although there are those who would say that there was a failed underground sort of attempt. In any event who couldn't love Adrienne Clarkson (Flowersong) as an exotic koi housed in a fishbowl and carried from event to event. Or Judy LaSwamp calling Peter Waterhole a batard (frog for "a large loaf of bread") but it's the phrases that just slip smoothly past like "the building of the Great Canal...(which) made it possible for the Beavers to move the good things from the Land of the Otters and the Land of the Gophers back home to be enjoyed behind the dam." Goodness knows that was often the way we felt about the role of Ontario.
