Rating: 4/5

Want something different? This is it.

We have some more or less ordinary people in what appears to be an ordinary world, but we are told right away that reality distortions are occurring and we may be / probably are in an alternate world.

Everything we go with our adventurers we see weirdness, as if our narrator was on LSD. But Pickover keeps us grounded by having our heroes cling to the core of reality. 
 
One of the adventurers is an eccentric android, in case we weren't having enough fun. 
 
So our heroes fight their way from A to B, and eventually we get a just-as-weird explanation for what's been going on. When I put the book down it was like getting off a carnival ride. 

There's a very good chance you won't like this book, but I recommend you give it a try.
