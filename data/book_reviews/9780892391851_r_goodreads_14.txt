Rating: 5/5

What's the Most Beautiful Thing You Know About Horses? by Richard Van Camp art by George Littlechild
🐴🐴🐴🐴🐴

What's the most beautiful thing I know about horses? Their friendship is earned and reciprocated 10 fold. Also, mares will negotiate with you. I love the fact that I have to communicate with Claire and Rose, not just tell them what to do.

I came across this book when I was ordering my @indigenousreadingcircle challenge books and just needed to read it. It is a children's book and would be an excellent book to read with small children to excite their imaginations. Lots of questions are asked and the art is vivid in a way that will have children itching to try the colors and forms themselves.

Van Camp weaves in cultural references and language into this fun little book in a way that is very easy for a child to connect to. He also includes photos of his family and friends that he interviews.

Now that I've read it I will be donating the book to my local library.
