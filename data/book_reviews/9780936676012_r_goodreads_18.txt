Rating: 5/5

I loved this book. 
For context— I’m beginning to grow as a reader. I’m trying to find what interests me, and overall I’ve been trying to read more often. 
With that said, I burned through this book. I’ve always been interested in early childhood education/development (ECE) and this was perfect. 

The author gives instructions for how to teach young children to read, and he also explains why it is so important. As someone who knows a decent amount about ECE, I was surprised and delighted to learn how important it is for children to read early on. 

This book filled me with an immense amount of excitement not only for my future children, but also for myself. 
I know I’m an adult, who is far past her “early learning” days, but knowing how important it is to read, has given me more motivation towards my own reading goals. 

One negative I will point out about the book, however, is that the information continuously repeats throughout the book. If you choose to read this, know that you won’t miss much if you skip through parts. 

Amazing book! I encourage all parents, and those soon to be, to give this a read!
