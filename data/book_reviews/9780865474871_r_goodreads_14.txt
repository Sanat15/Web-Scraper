Rating: 5/5

What a fantastic read! I really enjoyed L'Engle's perspective on art and faith, the nature of "Christian art", and sharing stories from poets and storytellers of decades past. I was underlining every other page with inspiring quotes. It was like reading a devotional, or better yet, having a coffee with another writing friend who feels like a rebel too because the establishment can't put us in their boxes. 

Some favorite quotes: 
"We don't have to be right! We do have to love, to be vulnerable, to accept joy and pain, and to grow through them." 

"Art is an affirmation of life." 

"It is the ability to choose which makes us human."
