Rating: 3/5

Snow White is my favourite fairy tale, and the Disney version has had many written versions (I have the original little Golden Book that included the illustration of Snow White's mother sewing by the window, which never made it to film). I tracked this one down because it's the 50th anniversary novelization, and written by Suzanne Weyn. I have read some of Suzanne Weyn's fairy-tale retellings in the "Once Upon a Time" YA series. I liked this one quite well. I've also read Jim Razzi's novelization, and both are good. I was struck by the illustration on the cover: I've never seen Snow White in a pink dress before. It's an interesting look, different from her usual yellow and blue.
