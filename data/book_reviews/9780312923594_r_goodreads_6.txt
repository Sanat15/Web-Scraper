Rating: 4/5

Novella. I'm not sure what I would have thought if it was a full novel, but at least at this length I appreciated the silly humor. The (spoofed, older) main crew members of the Star Trek Original Series join the (spoofed) Next Generation crew and are pulled to a nearby planet by s (John Deer) Tractor Beam. There they accidentally cause the opening of suspended animation chambers which are being used to cause the bodies to lose weight over time. Once out - and especially after seeing a snack treat - they're ravenous for food. This situation doesn't satisfy the Original cast's taste for adventures, so the Original crew goes off to have more fun...

I haven't watched Star Trek universe things for quite a while. Perhaps, some Star Trek fans would not appreciate this kind of satire. But if you're looking for humor you might give it a try.
