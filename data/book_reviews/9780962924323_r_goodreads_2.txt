Rating: 4/5

This book was sort of frustrating to me, I felt like the author spent too much time talking about the runner's appearance, but he was a reflection of how men viewed women distance runners I think. But I had to roll my eyes when he had comments like "she was every boy's dream" or something like that (not an exact quote). For me, it was interesting since I started running in 1979, when women's distance running was still in its beginning stages, and he did do a great job of following the trailblazers in competitive running at that time, and the pressures that were put on them and that they put on themselves.
