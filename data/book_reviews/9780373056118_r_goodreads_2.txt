Rating: 3/5

Read in HIS CHRISTMAS PRESENT, though I’ve read this book twice before, many, many years ago.
Enjoyable even though it’s showing its age. But the ethos of growing and progressing, especially in the workplace, remains pertinent today.
Loved Sam and how he learnt to open up and accept love. He obviously had a troubled childhood and didn’t know how to enjoy the little things.
Dallas annoyed me with the way she clung to her ex marriage troubles. He ex-husband was a lazy, good for nothing and she just hadn’t moved past getting rid of him and his attitude.
Sam with the children was so sweet, I adored his interactions with Nikki and Billy.
All ends very nicely, though having a child at their ages (Sam 46, Dallas late 30’s) felt odd.
