Rating: 5/5

The h has known the OM only for a month and she’s already engaged to be married to him. She goes with the OM to meet his mother and then finds out that his halfbrother is the H. 6 months before she was in a relationship with the H. She hasn’t slept with the OM, but she did sleep with the H 6 months before. 

The H tells his brother (the OM) that the h is his and that he wants her back. The OM says he has found the h and he won’t let her go. 

The tension in this book and the chemistry with this manly man H is what makes it a 5 stars read. The H challenges the h to see if she can stay neutral to him. When he asks her to come and comfort him, that’s sexy. Even I felt that, lol. 

What I didn’t like was that the h stayed in a relationship with the OM after she found out that he is the H’s half-brother. And I didn’t like the fact that the OM is his half-brother. That was a bit too The Bold and the Beautiful. Even though she hadn’t slept with the OM, she kissed him and I assume she also made out with him since they are engaged. And I also didn’t like that the OM was too much of a doormat.

But even with the dislikes, it is still a 5 star read in my opinion. Janet Dailey is a legend regarding her writing skills.
