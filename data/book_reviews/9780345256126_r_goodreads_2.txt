Rating: 3/5

Alice Chetwynd Ley had a charming style of writing, perfectly Regency. Taking one of her novels I am sure to be in the Regency (romance) world. She didn't make errors considering historical accuracy, etc. And her pen was very Heyer-ish.

But, Chetwynd Ley's books sometimes lack something. In plot, or wit, or spark. And it was such a case this time.

Don't get me wrong - Alice Chetwynd Ley's Regency novels are one of the best in the group of authors inspired by Georgette Heyer. Still, some of Alice Chetwynd Ley's stories are great, some just nice.

[3-3.5 stars]
