Rating: 4/5

Another complex plotted D&amp;D novel from one of this franchise's more reliable authors. Most things here are done right save that far from satisfying conclusion, a conclusion apparently, that will remain unresolved. We've also swapped one feisty female character in Arilyn to Bronwyn, but her motivations aren't that dissimilar. Taken out of the restrictions of the Forgotten Realms world, I wonder what the author could've done with her...

In any case, this novel is a page turner. The bad guys are done well - among the better D&amp;D efforts - and the various threads of the plot come together effectively (mostly). but it's set up for a sequel, and this hasn't materialised yet, and allegedly never will, so be warned.
