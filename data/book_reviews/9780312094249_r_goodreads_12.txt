Rating: 3/5

The Tenth Annual Collection gathers 24 stories from 1992, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1992") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Maureen F. McHugh's "Protection," Kate Wilhelm's "Naming the Flowers," and Frederik Pohl's "Outnumbering the Dead." I also appreciated Joe Haldeman's "Graves," Pat Cadigan's "Naming Names," and Bradley Denton's "The Territory."

Despite the great stories I listed above, this was one of the more disappointing volumes of the series--too many stories at the level of 3 stars or fewer.
