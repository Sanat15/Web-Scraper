Rating: 5/5

Holden checks a dead drop he recognized from his past with Lynch. He finds a note from former agent Sir Malcolm Jones who claims he was sent by Lynch to get Holden out. Holden is forced to believe him, but it's not going to be easy to extract him from the organization of a genius-level villain. He needs complete focus and plenty of luck when he finds himself alone and sought by both sides.

Malcolm starts inquiries which draw the attention of Peter Grimm who works for Tao. They capture Malcolm at the extraction point and Holden's attempt to rescue him fails.

The goal now is to find a file on Holden that Malcolm hid away. Holden is lucky enough to find it first, but is forced to destroy to keep himself hidden and alive in Tao's organization.

While recovering an item for one of the rulers of the world, Holden gets captured by the FBI. They use the most effective interrogation and torture techniques, including threatening Miss Misery's welfare and getting his ex to speak to him. He never gives in and gets saved by Tao's men.

Holden runs away from them as well and becomes a hunted man. Months later Tao finds him and offers to take him back into the fold. It's not really an option when every alternative is impossible to attain.
