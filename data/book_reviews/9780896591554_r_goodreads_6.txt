Rating: 1/5

As an art history student with a primary focus on female artists, I was excited when I picked this book up at the AGO last summer. However, after now reading it cover to cover, I can say for certain that it was probably the most disappointing read yet. It has incredible potential due to the layout, progress, and pictures but the writings by Frank Getlein are simply unacceptable. He is ill-informed on many matters concerning female artists and what they were allowed to paint and not. He is historically inaccurate on events concerning Mary Cassatt's life and, the comparisons made between her and not only past artists but, also her male counterparts are extremely degrading. There are many other books I would pick up again before this one.
