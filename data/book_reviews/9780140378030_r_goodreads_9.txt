Rating: 5/5

My best friend got me this book for my birthday and I've avoiding it for more than half a year because she got me a translation in Macedonian and I have a bad experiance with English-Macedonian book translations. I finally decided to read it today, for the last day of the year, as the only book ony shelf I haven't read, and I'm pleased to say that I really enjoyed it. It was so beautifully written, although it was a translation, so I can only imagine what the original is like. I don't usually read books dealing with such issues, but I have to say that this was just plain fantastic.
