Rating: 5/5

Wow it has been a long time since I seen this book. I was a child in the library and saw the title of the book and picked it up. After reading a few pages I decided to check it out and was in love with the story. It has been 12 years so I don't remember most of the story at all and reading it today might only bring fond memories. A book not for my age today but for the 7 year old child back then, was an amazing story of little wolf's adventure.
