Rating: 2/5

I liked it well enough, I suppose. It's a bit formulaic, boy meets girl, boy likes girl, boy loses girl, boy gets girl back. There wasn't ever any doubt (is there ever in these books?) that the characters would live happily ever after. I had a few problems with the book though. 

The heroine. Jessica Seton is an heiress who must marry a husband that her Uncle approves of in order to receive her inheritance in the form of a library. I loved her love of books, and I warmed up to her unromantic nature. I found her a bit frustrating though. She spends the entire book one way...then at the end, she does a 360 and turns into a frustrating, overly romantic, almost simpering girl. Had I been the hero, who had just gone through quite an ordeal to get back to her and discovered what had transpired in my absence...I'm not sure I would be all that excited to be with her. 

The pacing. I got to 80% of the book and there was no ending in sight. Then, the next 20% FLEW by. It was like someone had opened a floodgate. 

Ultimately I would say I really enjoyed the book until I got to about 75-80%. Then to me, it just went downhill. 

However, I did enjoy the hero. Dryden is an excellent character and I enjoyed the chapters from his viewpoint a lot more than those from Jessica's. While this is a sequel, it's an entirely stand-alone novel. Which is nice as this one was free on Amazon while the other book is still at $2.99. For a free book, though, it was an enjoyable enough read. Had the ending been different, I would have given it 5 stars.
