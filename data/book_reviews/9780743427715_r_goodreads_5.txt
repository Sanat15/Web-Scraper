Rating: 4/5

Not bad. Characters were well captured but a few things in the book irritated me. Firstly, set in season 7, not season 6. 
Some of Willow’s spells sound like Harry Potter spells. Excrucio?? Come on. That doesn’t sound like Buffyverse witchcraft. 

Overall, I enjoyed it and I actually liked the length, but wouldn’t reach for it again.
