Rating: 4/5

I re-read this for my End of Life reading project and just realized that it wasn't yet listed as one of my books. Somehow it got left out in the past but glad to add it now. Published in 1983, it predicts the 1989 earthquake in San Francisco. It's the story of a group of people in Guerneville, family members and chosen family members, who help each other survive and thrive. If you liked Armistead Maupin's Tales of the City, you'll probably enjoy this too. This one focuses more on women...
