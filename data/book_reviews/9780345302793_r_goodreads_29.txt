Rating: 3/5

2.5 stars

This was not what I thought it would be. It has quite a complicated world and situations happening with a lot of politics. If you are only looking for a horror/sci fi you might have more luck, but the emphasis certainly isn’t on the romance. It isn’t paced like anything I’ve read before, that’s for sure. I found the female mc needing to be in a “more beautiful” body and being viewed really on in terms of her womb frustrating.
