Rating: 3/5

It's hard for me to give this a rating as I originally read this in high school and LOVED it. Classic D&amp;D player fiction. Crossing over into the world of your game. It gets 5 stars for all the memories it brought back. Is it a good book? It's fine .. a good fantasy novel. The female characters are OK, not great. But there are a few glimpses of interesting world-building. And some interesting (albeit brief) observations of quirks of character traits and personality - of what would happen if you became your D&amp;D character - what part of self would you retain.
