Rating: 4/5

London = The Smoke. 
A Tiger = a tough, confident, streetwise person

This story has one of the best names for an antagonist: Jack Havoc. [I love the assonance at the end of that sentence.] Another character is named Tiddy Doll!

It took me a bit to become invested in the story. Such is often the case of audiobooks. I began again. Once I had the characters straight in my head, I sailed right through to the end.
