Rating: 3/5

This is a two star story, but CL kept my attention with her special CL touches. The plot is simple: hero meets heroine when his much younger sister (17) runs off with the heroine's younger brother. He thinks the heroine is in on helping the young couple and subjects her to a few punishing kisses after a surprise visit on a Sunday morning. Heroine slaps him in retaliation.

The rest of the story concerns the younger brother/sister moping around and causing their siblings concern, the heroine going out with an OM and then running into the H with his OW, and the hero finally deciding he's in love and pursuing the heroine. The younger brother/sister pairing doesn't work out while the H/h does because true love is for adults only. Hence the title.

So the romance is okay - too much time with the OM for my tastes. But CL's touches are there:

Hero has never eaten in a pub and is amazed to be enjoying "chicken in a basket" ( I looked it up - it's fried chicken on top of chips and was very popular in the 1970s)

The hero's grandmother's pink house (inside and out) with the praying cherub doorknocker

The heroine's career as a graphic artist and her penny-pinching publisher

The heroine's country cottage and all of the lovely flowers in the garden

Extensive conversations about Thomas Hardy, George Bernard Shaw and family dynamics.

And like the cherry on top of the Charlotte Lamb sundae: melon for a starter.
