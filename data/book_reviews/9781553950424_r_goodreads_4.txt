Rating: 1/5

The story here could have been better than it was. Mr. Holsclaw could have done more research on sharks than he did, because he got a lot of misinformation into the story. No sharks would ever exclusively attack humans and boats!! He also portrayed them as inherently evil. Sharks are NOT evil!!! As for the rest of the story, the characters are shallow, and the ending was downright lame. They wanted to kill the sharks with atomic warheads?!! Be serious!!!! The story ending was left open allowing for a sequel, but fortunately none has surfaced, at least yet.
