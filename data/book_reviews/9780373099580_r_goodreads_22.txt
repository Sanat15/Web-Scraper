Rating: 2/5

2 Stars - generous rating
I should have listened to my friends' reviews. What was the point of this "romance" book? Perhaps I'm being harsh, but seriously, this H never fought for the h (Sharon). This beta-hero bullcrap didn't work for me. And he was exasperatingly confusing! I'm not even sure who the OW was here, it was a toss up. Who held second place, the h or the OW (Elaine)? I know he said he loved the h and admitted to having "deep feelings" for the OW, but at several points he also mentioned loving the OW. Based on his contradicting actions and feelings, it was like he only loved parts of each one, but never all of one. I don't even consider him a bad person. He was flawed but he was never mean...just extremely disappointing. Ugh. If the OW hadn't died, the H would have remained with her forever. Even after she died--which by the way, left him devastated--he didn't go the h. She was just a pesky memory. This was an epic fail. Next!
