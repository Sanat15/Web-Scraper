Rating: 4/5

The whole story of Plymouth Rock is pretty amusing I suppose, since in all likelihood, the pilgrims never set foot on it. It's funny because no one ever mentioned it or cared about it for 100 years and then the history of Plymouth Rock was sort of invented. If you are looking strictly for a history of Plymouth Rock, this is your book and it covers it well. 

I got it thinking it'd be more of a history of the Pilgrims, and that it is not. So in that regard, I was disappointed, but I'll go ahead and rate it for what it is... a kids book about Plymouth Rock. And for someone who is looking for that, I'll give it 4-5 stars. Easy. Full of information. Cute illustrations.
