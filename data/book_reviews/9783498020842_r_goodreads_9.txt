Rating: 2/5

This was a weird book for me. Beginning of the summer I had nothing to read an picked up this book in hopes of a good story. I was taken aback by the tale of Bo a high class call girl basically. Although the book was interesting and some times very witty it was also very confusing. The book can best be describe as a year in the life of Bo as the reader learns the ends and outs of the Millennium Girl. Often I found myself wondering what the point was of all of Bo's escapades and while entertaining at time the book left me wanting more closure.
