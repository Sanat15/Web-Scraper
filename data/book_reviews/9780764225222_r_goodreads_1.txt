Rating: 4/5

The sequel to Saint Ben, which I quite enjoyed. This one was good - followed up on the characters and kept them in character. So that was good. Some really fun writing moments in there - one whole paragraph using heart disease language to describe a tangle of freeways! This one was a little more hokey than Saint Ben so I found myself skimming in sections. But overall, a great read. A beach read - quick and enjoyable and nothing too absorbing. 

Very enjoyable.
