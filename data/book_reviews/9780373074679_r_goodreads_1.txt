Rating: 3/5

This was a very average category. Our heroine is 35 and rebuilding her life after her divorce two years earlier when a husband to whom she devoted her life, left her for his much younger secretary. The heroine has now started working and is slowly enjoying life. At a conference for work, she is kidnapped and held captive by the hero. Our hero is a desperate archaeologist running from thugs who want to kill him so he takes refuge in heroine's room scaring her. From here after initial hostility the heroine is sympathetic to hero's plight and offers to help him evade said thugs as well as capture the evidence required to break an artifact smuggling ring in Peru. Obviously h/H go on the run hiding in a B&amp;B and get physically involved. But the hero doesn't think his nomadic life has anything to offer h. We do eventually get an HEA. The book was as I said earlier average and ever forgettable but it kept me occupied the few hours I spent reading it.
