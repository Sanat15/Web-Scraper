Rating: 3/5

Sweet bordering on cheesy. A television documentary maker and his production assistant investigate the story of a WWII pilot whose posthumously published poems have increasingly gained a following amongst the public.

There is a lot of fashion and food porn in Paris, followed by a trek to the Scottish countryside.

The hero is a rakish commitment-phobe with mommy issues who succumbs very early to the virginal, feisty heroine. I almost miss a dash of CL's cruelty that she oversaturates most of her male characters with. The hero here was too nice and the heroine pretty much had him wrapped around her little finger, which lessened the tension and intensity I have come to expect from CL.

The background story of the poet/pilot and the woman who was his muse was very sweet and wistful so that it kept my interest going in an otherwise not very inspiring story.
