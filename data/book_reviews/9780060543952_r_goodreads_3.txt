Rating: 4/5

I can't believe I waited to read this series. Read #1 yesterday, and will read # 3 today. The year is 1844 andv3 British spies have returned from Afganistan. There is a traitor on the loose and they believe they have captured her. Meanwhile, Charlotte, the daughter of a Viscount who she learns from his journals is a spy master. At a ball she follows a suspicious fellow and gets captured. She of course believes he is a villain . He kidnaps her because she heard too much, and the book is the rest of the story. What I loved about the book is that it overlaps with Will and Jane's story, so we have some of the same scenes. I don't think that will happen with book 3, which I am going to start right now.
