Rating: 4/5

This reads kind of like a chapter book, much longer than I expected it to be considering it’s a children’s book. The art work matched the moodiness of the story, its artful watercolor touches added to the whimsy of the mc’s, Sam, imagination, disguised as lies. The use of moonshine being the description of “lies” was a clever touch. It reminded me of the phrase that goes something like “drunk on lies”. A soft-hearted story.
