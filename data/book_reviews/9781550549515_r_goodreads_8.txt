Rating: 3/5

The author's father travelled down the Yukon in 1898 during the Gold Rush. Over 70 years later, Berton retraces the journey with his wife and children, travelling back to Dawson, the town where he grew up.

It's a pleasant read, although one does have to work hard to keep track of the various members of the party, not helped by Berton's habit of giving all his children names beginning with P - Penny, Pamela, Patsie, Peter, Paul, Peggy Anne and Perri! I think I would have appreciated it more if I had been familiar with the areas Berton is describing though.
