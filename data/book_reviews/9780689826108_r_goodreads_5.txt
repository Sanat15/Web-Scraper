Rating: 4/5

Female Huck Finn or the modern Scout Finch either way this book has a strong voice in southern fiction. Nissa struggles with her parent's divorce.
I loved this book personally. I could appreciate it at the adult level- the mother is struggle with independence during the 1940s. All the characters are well-developed and endearing. Upper elementary students could connect with the realistic view of family dynamics. Nissa is a great fictional character that students will relate with.
