Rating: 3/5

August 2015
A filler because the next chapter in the book Rebekah and I were reading tonight was too long to finish before bedtime, but there was still some time left. This was a book that Jenna got when she was not quite one from her grandparents. We've read it numerous times to each of the girls. I love getting to do the voices of all the characters. 

Poor Roo doesn't know what to do for his mom for Valentine's Day. So all the gang get together to help him prepare the greatest valentine ever. But it ends up being Eeyore's advice and Roo's own creativity that saves the day. A fun snuggle book!
