Rating: 5/5

I read this as a kid and found it to be just as delightful as the Disney film, which I still adore as well. If you have children who love the movie (cartoon or live action, really), or who love dogs, this is a very fun book. The one big difference that I recall is that the adult dogs are named Pongo and Missis, and Missis has always felt sensitive about her boring name, and so calls herself Missis Pongo (since her marriage), and lets other dogs imagine that she just doesn't choose to use her other name. Perdy, or Perdita, is another adult dog that they take in who has also had her puppies stolen. She's actually a liver-spotted Dalmation, which I found interesting, and her previous owner neglected her and called her Spots.

Update 2026: I remembered almost nothing about this except for the above mentioned details about the names. This edition that I just bought in England is illustrated so delightfully that I just can't stand it! Also, there's a lot going on here, and it has a lot more peril, as well as some veiled details (calling it "a marriage" instead of saying, "mating," etc) that are very old-fashioned and yet still work so well!
