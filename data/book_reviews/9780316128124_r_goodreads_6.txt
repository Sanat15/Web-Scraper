Rating: 5/5

This book turned out to be one of my favorite, and most recommended books, about the soul. It truly is a "Handbook for the Soul." Some of the stories contained within will affect you in ways that others will not. Some of them are meant to be understood (I believe) in a way only you will understand them. I found this to be a fine book, very inspirational, and very meaningful. It will stay in my personal library for frequent review (should I remember to look at it again! :L)
