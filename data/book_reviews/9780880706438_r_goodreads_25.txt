Rating: 5/5

Better than 'Man in the mirror' (Morely) but not betterer than 'Disciplines of a Godly man' (Hughes). This book is great, though having read a lot in this sector, I dont think I learned too much new in the personal disciplines category, however I picked up some excellent advice around parenting and speaking to your kids about sensitive issues etc. Excellent book.

One encouragement to all, is that almost every respected book on these topics encourages a man to read the bible in full every year, and I agree whole heartedly agree.
