Rating: 3/5

Snowfall at twilight,
Yet a mandarin drake and duck
Still linger
On the ancient lake
– Shiki

*

Under the willow
With a leaf stuck in his mouth
The puppy sleeps
– Issa

*

The little duck looks very wise
When he pops up fresh from the sight
Of what lies far below the water
–Jōsō

*

The silence!
The voice of the cicada
Penetrates the rock!
–Bashō

*

Faraway and faint
The wild goose gives his cry.
Since then my thoughts have been fixed
On the middle of the sky
–Misune
Haiku selected and illustrated by children’s book author Demi, and translated by her husband, Tze-si Huang.
