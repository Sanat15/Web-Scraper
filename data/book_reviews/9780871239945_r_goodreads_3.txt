Rating: 3/5

So when I was most of the way through this book I described it to my husband as "solidly average," which was making me on the fence about reading the rest of the series. I realized it was neither boring nor exciting and I didn't find myself caring about the characters, but it wasn't torturous to read either. But the climax and ending were interesting, so I've decided I'll progress on to book #2 to see what happens.
