Rating: 3/5

I would have to say overall, I was not a huge fan of this book, especially compared to some of her other books. First, it sure skirted the line of being a 'closed' door romance, especially towards the end with Major Cutter. I feel like this book was just one innuendo after another, which put me off. And it might be because I haven't gotten that from any of her other books. Second, I feel like the manner in which both characters come to realize their love for each other is undeserving of the characters themselves and comes rather late. It felt rushed and the pacing was off. Lady Agnes was really the most enjoyable thing about this book.
