Rating: 3/5

I knew going in that this was going to be extreme, and because of that, it had the possibility of being redundant. It is. Very well-written depravity with some interesting characters, some wit, and a bit of social commentary, but the story is very thin and comes with an anticlimactic ending.

A bit of interesting history; Platt was an Englishman who had just moved to the U.S. and was practicing his newfound “freedom” from the oppression of U.K. censors when he wrote this.
