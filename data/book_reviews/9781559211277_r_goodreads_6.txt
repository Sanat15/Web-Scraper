Rating: 4/5

Wow—now I understand the hype around Poe. After reading two of his most famous stories consecutively, it’s clear that Poe is a master of crafting chilling, unforgettable horror.

The Black Cat is a brief yet chilling and gruesome tale that explores the dark animosity between a man and two black cats. The man is a walking paradox, especially with his interpretation of freedom and tranquility—which reflects his perverse, psychological nature.

This is one of those stories where events spiral out of control, getting worse by the minute. Its thrilling and horror elements also weaves seamlessly with Poe’s prose—richly descriptive and psychologically intense. For a short story, this packs a punch. 

4.2 stars
