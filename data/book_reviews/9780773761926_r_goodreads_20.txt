Rating: 3/5

2.7 stars (weird rating... I know) 
I read this book at school, and though it was a nice story, it got boring at times (especially in the beginning). It started getting a little more interesting when we got to part 2, but, I still didn't find myself loving the book. It was a nice story about the struggles of slaves during the civil war, but I think that the author could've done a lot more with the story. I also think that she could've added more to the end, instead of ending the book off where she did, leaving me a little confused. A lot more could have been done with this story (which is why I only rated it 2.7 stars)
