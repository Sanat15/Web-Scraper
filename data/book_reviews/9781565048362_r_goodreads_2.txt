Rating: 1/5

When it was published this anthology may have read better than it did when I read in the early millennium years. Like many 'gay' themed anthologies there is plenty of 'gay' characters, themes or sex but they are simply grated onto old and dreary si-fi or fantasy tropes. Another tale of ye oldie fantasy land ripped off from Tolkien isn't redeemed by a gay character or plot line.

If I'd read and reviewed when published I might have been kinder but not especially honest.
