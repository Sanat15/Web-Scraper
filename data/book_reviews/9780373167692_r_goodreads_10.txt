Rating: 5/5

This is a really sweet story. Dylan and Whitney have a long history of friendship. They had the perfect friendship both challenging and complimenting each other. They have one night together and their lives are changed. But Dylan gets engaged to another due to a business deal, despite the fact he loves Whitney. I found myself both laughing at times and sometimes shedding a few tears. This book definitely had some good emotions. I enjoyed this book and am looking forward to reading more by this author.
