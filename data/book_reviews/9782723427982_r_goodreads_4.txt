Rating: 1/5

So I couldn't stand anime Minako because she's kinda ditzy and annoying and we got enough of that with anime Usagi but I liked her in the manga because she was kind of a badass leader. That being said, these books were pure torture for me. It was all pointless fluff much like 90% of the anime (by anime I am talking about the original series, not Crystal which is based on the manga, just FYI). 

Fans of the original anime will probably like this mini-series but those that prefer the more serious drama that's in the manga will probably hate it as much as I did.
