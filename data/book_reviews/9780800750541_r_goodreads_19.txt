Rating: 4/5

“The Light and the Glory

By Peter Marshall and David Manuel

Have you ever wondered what the early American history was like from a Christian point of view? Why did some things happen as they did? What was God's plan for America? And why are we so ungodly today? I really enjoyed this book. It is refreshing to read something about the history of our nation that doesn't cloak the fact that our founders indeed had a strong Christian faith. Four stars. ”
