Rating: 5/5

“She was my best thing.”

During The American Novel Since 1945, a free online lecture series, Professor Amy Hungerford commented that The Bluest Eye was selected, because most people would have already read Beloved. Of course, I madly wrote down in my notes: “Read Beloved ASAP.”

Well….the 2025 audiobook and eBook tacked on an introduction. This was a mistake.

Because the introduction contains major spoilers, explaining the setting and almost all of the characters. Part of the reading experience is discovering the characters—who they are and their relationship with one another.

Toni Morrison was inspired to write Beloved after she read about an escaped slave who ended her daughter’s life upon recapture.

In Beloved, Sethe similarly ends her daughter’s life upon being recaptured. Many years later, Sethe is haunted by her decision.

Morrison does an excellent job portraying how one decision can alter one’s life, how some decisions will be thought about every single day until one’s last breath.

While Morrison is revealing the characters’ backstories, she does an incredible job creating a sense of urgency, letting the reader know that the sand in the hourglass is almost up.

And the ending. Wow! It was so shockingly beautiful. That quote. Oh! I will remember that for a long time to come.
