Rating: 4/5

Charley Van Leeuwen is a commitment-phobe, so no one is more surprised than she is when she returns from England with a husband. Jack Fairfax is retired from the navy (meteorologist). Unfortunately, Charley discovers a body in the bathroom of their hotel room when they arrive from England. Inspector Yahata is plainly suspicious of her and Jack. Charley's uncle Harry is very suspicious of Jack. He does his usual background check on Jack and tries to convince Charley to annul the marriage and send Jack on his way, but she's furious with him and not about to send Jack off. Charley has a repertory theater group (in fact, it was to do with the theater group that she has been in England for a year). Charley is very wealthy. Her parents died when she was young and Harry has been her guardian and still acts like he runs her life. Charley has lots of friends in the theater and that she went to school with that are all surprised about Jack. Then her cousin Cece is kidnapped with a six hundred thousand ransom demand. Jack gets involved in the ransoming of Cece and Charley refuses to stay at the hotel waiting. Then she learns Jack is having her followed for her safety. She is furious, so they come to an understanding that she will have a bodyguard for her protection, but he won't be following her - he will be with her. It seems the bodies that keep turning up have something to do with a man that Jack put in prison for treason. But the man is dead, so someone on the outside is carrying on with his plan. And they need to find out who soon to keep the body count down.
This is the first in a series that seems that it will be quite interesting I suspected who the guilty party was, but had no idea of the whole plot.
