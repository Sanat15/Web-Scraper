Rating: 4/5

“She should have let it be. Everyone deserves their walls.”

First Lisa Gardner book for me and I’m impressed! As of September, It is the best mystery/thriller I’ve read this year, we’ll see if it will hold until the year ends. 

I really enjoyed the audiobook as well, somehow it just got released last year even though the book has been published since 1999 but I’m happy it did - another terrific performance from Brittany Pressley.
