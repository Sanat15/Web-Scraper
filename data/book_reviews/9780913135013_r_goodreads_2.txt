Rating: 5/5

Ken Fulton takes you, a newbie to amateur astronomy, by the hand and walks you through the "jungle", showing how you can avoid the pitfalls of "trash scopes", spending too much, and getting in over your head into accessories or astrophotography.

With light-hearted good humor, he expounds upon the best way to get started on stargazing and an appreciation of the wonders of the night sky. His offbeat descriptions of past foibles and future "perils" will evoke a smile or a chuckle as you read.
