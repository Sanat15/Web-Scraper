Rating: 3/5

Another tale of an ailing monarch/all-powerful leader and the residents* of his town; animal corpses drowned in water or left baked in the everlasting heat. But, for the caliber we see LATER in One Hundred Years of Solitude, it remains both underdeveloped and unremarkable. It is Magical Realism, sans the magic. That certain Latin American special ingredient is not present here, &amp; boy is it missed.

In Evil Hour absolutely nothing happens. Communication is impossible--even complicity in the conspiracy doesn't occur or mean a thing. And we must suppose that that is G.G.M.'s point.

*Judge, dentist, lieutenant, priest, impresario, cops, wives, mothers, boys... y mas!
