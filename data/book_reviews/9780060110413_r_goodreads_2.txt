Rating: 4/5

Not What You’d Expect

An unusual offering from Dickinson, although to be fair, most of his books are not main-stream detective novels.
In this late entry in the Detective Pibble series, he has arrived in Greece after being dismissed from British police under murky circumstances. Somehow he gets involved with a wealthy man who doesn’t mind breaking the law. There’s a convoluted plot involving drugs, stolen art, and a criminal on the run. Interspersed with this plot are some lovely and moving descriptions of Greece, and philosophical ponderings by Pibble. The book is well-written, and I recognize its value. But I didn’t really like it because it’s a sort of adventure/spy thriller. But if that’s your thing, by all means read this well-done, thoughtful look at international criminal enterprise.
