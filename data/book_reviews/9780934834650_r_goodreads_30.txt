Rating: 5/5

On a Friday early afternoon, a time I usually refer to as ‘my morning’, 32 degree heat seeps through me. Staring into space for a long time, the headphones guide me through vastness. Cracking voice and over-exposed guitars place both my feet in the approaching ocean. So many places to travel to, even if only within the confines of my skull. We will do this again and again, oftentimes over the same ground, till old bones stretch weathered skin and the last journey holds only farewells and no greetings.
