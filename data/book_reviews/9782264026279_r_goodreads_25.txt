Rating: 4/5

Set around 1970 this may seem a bit dated to younger readers - younger than say 50 years that is.
A enjoyable book, a re-read after many years. George Felse leads his police team in investigating the murder of a journalistic photographer. I'm not going to add spoilers so it's hard to say much about the story. Suffice to say the village feel as described woul make many wish to move there.
It's not an outstanding book but this is a solid mystery that gives enjoyable reading.
