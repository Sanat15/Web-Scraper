Rating: 4/5

I read this book in high school for myself not assigned for any class, and enjoyed it. I remember connecting with the character's emotions and experiences at the time, even though I'm white and from the Midwest. Lots of the coming of age themes resonated with me at the time. I am, however, just learning about the controversy surrounding the author; though that doesn't color my memory of a good, not great, book that spoke to me as a teenager
