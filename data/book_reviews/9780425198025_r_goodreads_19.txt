Rating: 3/5

A low three. I enjoyed this, but there were many little things that bothered me. To me, the heroine came across as very naive; the hero kept describing her as witty and independent, but that was not how she seemed to me- though I also felt some of the writing sounded like it was written by a teenager, so perhaps it was just poor characterization by the author. I did like the heroine overall, she was very straightforward (despite sneaking around behind her sister's back). I had a hard time liking the hero wholeheartedly. I liked his overall actions, but not his internal thoughts- several times, he thought of himself as a "lover of all women" - a phrase I came to hate, since other characters also used the same phrase about him, as well. I think what kept this book enjoyable for me was the mild intrigue of the truth about Catherine's father, as well as their piqued interest in each other.
