Rating: 4/5

I got the opportunity to see Captain Michael present on the topic of It’s Your Ship at a leadership conference earlier this year. There’s a lot to learn from this book about building a team and changing the culture - especially within an organization as bureaucratic as the US Navy. 

I’ve seen some reviews that he comes across as arrogant. I genuinely don’t think he’s trying to be and it’s more his straightforward way of writing, but I’ve been turned off from books where I get that vibe from the author. Maybe having seen him in person, it wasn’t very abrasive for me in that sense. I would recommend this one.
