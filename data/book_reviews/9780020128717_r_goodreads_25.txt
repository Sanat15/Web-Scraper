Rating: 4/5

Far more entertaining than I expected. The author has an idiosyncratic prose style that is entertaining to read even when the subject is "French Symbolist Poets you never got around to reading." As a onetime student of French fin-de-siecle literature I should have read more of Verlaine, Rimbaud, Valery, etc. and more of Flaubert than just Trois Contes. Oh well, it was an abortive career and I moved back to good old English lit. My knowledge of Eliot, Joyce, Yeats, etc. is much stronger but still pretty dated. Despite all this I enjoyed the book for its style, its discriminating judgements, and the memories of those days when I cared far more about literature than I do now (though I was always pretty cynical even by Gen X standards).
