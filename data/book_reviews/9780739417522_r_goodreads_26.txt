Rating: 3/5

I started this some time ago and must not have been very into it bc I stopped reading around the 40% mark and left it for months. I have been in a book slump and randomly picked this back up. The second half was better than the first. The heroine grew a bit of a backbone and thr hero groveled and felt horrible for his mistakes. Of course there are misunderstandings, an evil half sister and her evil lover, plus some murder attempts. All par for the course in HR of this era. But I did finish it finally so that's something. Giving this 3.5 stars.
