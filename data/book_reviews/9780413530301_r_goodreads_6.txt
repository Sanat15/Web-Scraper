Includes 'The Play's the Thing', 'Good Morning, Bill', 'Leave it to Psmith', &amp; 'Come On,Jeeves'.
The last two plays are adapted from Wodehouse's own books.
However the first two are adaptations of Hungarian plays for an American audience.'Spiel in Schloss' became 'The Play's the Thing'so later 'Good Morning, Bill'was also based on Ladislaus Fodor's work. Wodehouse did not read the original plays but received a plot synopsis from his agent so he could create and embellish them to fit their new settings.
