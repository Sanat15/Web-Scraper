Rating: 3/5

This was a good reference book to start my journeywith poetry.
I enjoyed ch 1-8 the most. 
Ch 9-13 studied individual poets (Rilke, Mandelstam, Ritsos...)
..interesting, but not what I was looking for. I skimmed these chapters.
The last 3 chapters dealt with Time, Participant in a poem and Beauty.
Conclusion: Prepare yourself for a long read. 
Sift the information to your own needs.
Lesson learned: 
Don't read a poem and go after it as if it were an item on a final exam.
Poem: it is the experience...not the final exam that counts.
