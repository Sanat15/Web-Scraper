Rating: 2/5

I have the movie and like it a lot. This book had some of the exact same lines but the story was a bit different. Some of the characters names were the same but in different roles. Some of the situations presented were so far fetched and very goofy….hard to believe actually. I did manage to get through the entire book but it wasn’t exactly a real page turner. If you can find the movie, it’s well worth watching, but It’s really hard to find. I believe it was a made for TV movie.
