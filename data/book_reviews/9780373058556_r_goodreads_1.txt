Rating: 4/5

Another great winner from this author featuring two vulnerable characters falling in love with the help of a ghost. The hero is a carpenter still smarting from a break-up with a woman who cheated on him that has left him without doubts about his masculinity and ability as a lover (how novel to see a hero who has such insecurities!). h/H meet when hero comes to do repairs on the house they inherited and the heroine comes to said house to see if there truly is a ghost there; she ends up bunking there. The heroine is taking a break from her life and has dealt with men who always want to use her for her family's name. These two never figured that they would fall in love but they do and their journey is wonderful. There is a cute dog too! I ordered a paperback off Amazon as I really wanted to read it.
