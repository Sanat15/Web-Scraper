Rating: 4/5

One of the great aspects about this Goosebumps book is that it's set in Egypt.
It's just a shame that the main protagonist Gabe is so annoyingly unappreciated of visiting a cultural hotspot.

Stine quickly establishes the hot conditions as Gabe constantly complaining about being thirsty highlights the heat.

Goosebumps books always work with a formula of removing the parents and the fact that they're called away whilst on their business trip is a neat movie.
It also allows a different dynamic as Gable is left with his cousin Sari and Uncle Ben.

Much of the plot is a run-around, though there is that sense of Gabe navigating a strange place - whilst an interaction with a taxi driver is also amusing.

The early books in the series does carry a much more darker threat and there are times that you feel that the kids could really be harmed.

I like how this feels different compared to the other releases and really establishes the anthology nature of the series.
More of an action adventure type of story, though does have some genuine intense moments.
