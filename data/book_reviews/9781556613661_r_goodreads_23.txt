Rating: 5/5

Wow, wow, wow… this book was so much better than the first, not that Silk was a bad book! Chaikin does a fantastic job immersing the reader in the jungles of India. From dangerous crocodiles and tigers, to athletic monkeys swinging through the trees, and even some creepy spiders, the setting truly feels alive. There was plenty of action and strong faith woven throughout, and I couldn’t put it down during certain parts. Right now I’m sitting at a solid 4 stars, but after some time to reflect, it just might jump up to 5. I can’t wait to finish the series!!

Edit: I am bumping it to 5 stars!!
