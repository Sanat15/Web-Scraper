Rating: 5/5

Stout at his best.

This is by far the best of Stout 's novella compilations. It was late in his career. Earlier novellas often felt like they had been cut short. At least one was in effect a rehearsal for, or draft of a later novel. These novellas work on all levels: they're perfectly paced, written with clarity and with characterizations of great economy, yet delivering a full sense of the relationship that make Stout 's novels such a pleasure, that between the genius Nero Wolfe and his arms and legs and our narrator, Archie Goodwin. The whodoneits are also are on a relatively high level. 3 small gems.
