Rating: 2/5

While there is no doubt that Andre Gide was greatly influenced in his life and writing by Oscar Wilde, the two only met a handful of times. Therefore, I wondered how Jonathon Fryer would turn their relationship into a 200+ page book. It turns out most of it is a rehash of Wilde’s and Gide’s biographies, which have been documented much more skillfully by other writers. This book is for the general reader, filled with slang and cliché, and has little to offer to our understanding of either writer. A big disappointment.
