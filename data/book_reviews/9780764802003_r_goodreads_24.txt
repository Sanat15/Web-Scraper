Rating: 5/5

a deeply impactful book. i feel the need to examine myself closely and my soul to determine what part ails in the forgetfulness our society elicits regarding the poor. no longer can we ignore the poor, whether materially and/or spiritually. i’m deeply convinced that this should be our number one priority: that of human connection through the binding thread of Christ. this book has deeply affected me in an extremely positive way and i cannot turn away from it. a must read.
