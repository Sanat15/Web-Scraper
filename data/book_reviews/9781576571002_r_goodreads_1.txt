Rating: 4/5

At 9:30 am on a Saturday morning, 12 year old Martin is woken up by his mom yelling for him - her tone implying he is in trouble for something. Someone made a mess all over the kitchen with peanut butter, and broke some dishes - but Martin swears up and down it wasn't him - he hates being blamed for stuff he didn't do!
Some type of destruction becomes a nightly occurrence over the following week. Martin is convinced an intruder is to blame, but when Martin's brother finds brown fur stuck to the floor, and a huge animal print too, he's not so sure. 
Can Martin find out who - or what is targeting his family before it hurts them?
