Rating: 4/5

Mildly entertaining children's read. A little light and unrealistic for an older reader perhaps, but the characters and their dynamics with one another still feel real. Ally's observation of his mum and 'being told yes, feeling no' - how he would ask to paint, and being told 'I suppose so', followed by endless orders of setting up the paints and being careful with everything and not to do it in that place as she'll need to use it soon, with the result that he'd give up and do something else. Dynamics such as this make me appreciate that turning point in children's literature from when parents were simply cardboard cut-outs of a character, with a few standard parent-y sounding lines, to becoming more and more often people (with flaws too) in their own right.
