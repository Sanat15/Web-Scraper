Rating: 4/5

I felt a nudge to check this out on February 1st. As I read it aloud to Vanessa, it brought my Auntie to mind. Later that day, my mother called to let me know she had passed away. My Auntie's middle name was Guadalupe and Our Lady of Guadalupe is important in my maternal family.

The book is lovely, despite some unlikeable visual/ text depictions of Indigenous peoples. It can be really difficult to reckon with the dissonance of Catholicism's role in my own Pueblo world as both a force of genocide and balm for my family members' spirits.

I ended up ordering a copy for my mom, who also enjoyed it.
