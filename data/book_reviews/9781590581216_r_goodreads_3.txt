Rating: 5/5

I would have to say this is the best book I’ve ever read. Kept me intrigued, and got me back into reading. There are crazy plot twist, this book really makes you think. Charley, the main character, shows lots of character development throughout the story, becoming more responsible and smarter as it goes on. Totally reading the sequel when I can get it. I would say the age for this book is really anything over 14 or 15. Would totally recommend, and if you are looking at this to know if you should read it or not, do it!!!
