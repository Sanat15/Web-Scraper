Rating: 5/5

I have a 4 year old who likes to asks questions (“why is she so fat?” “Why do his eyes look funny?” “What’s wrong with his hair?”) at some inconvenient times. They are all normal, inquisitive questions for a 4 year old, but this book helped give him a more appropriate space to ask those questions and learn more about people and our different, amazing bodies. I thought this book was perfect for a toddler and preschooler.
