Rating: 2/5

I bought this book years ago and reference it occasionally when I’m not feeling well or just to look things up. Recently I came up on a chapter on weight loss and the language used was horrific. And I mean truly horrific. The author says “maybe more disgusting than a fat adult is a fat child.” And this is the least offensive thing that she says about people who struggle with weight.

I have never read such things written about people who struggle with weight, whether they’re fat or chubby or whatever you wanna call them. I am truly shocked by this language. She also says things like “measure the thickness of your flat by rolling it between your fingers – this should disgust you enough to want to do something about it.”

No I do this myself because I am slender in many places but my stomach just has rolls. But to have language like that used about the body? Untenable. This is extremely damaging. So while the book might be useful in many ways this language is just well the language is what disgusting, not fat people.
