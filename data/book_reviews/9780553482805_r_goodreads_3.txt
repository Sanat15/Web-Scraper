Rating: 4/5

This is basically SVH's "Love Letters", except that Lila never has to admit that Gray isn't real. Plus, Lila is awesome so it's automatically better.

That being said, the subplot with Todd is absolutely hilarious. He dumps Elizabeth after she writes him an anonymous love letter, because he thinks it's from someone else and wants to be with them instead. He spends the entire book trying to figure out who it's from, and he uses rationale such as "Brooke asked to borrow ten cents from me the other day - you'd only do that if you like someone!" and "It must be Maria, because she was once at Liz's house when I called Liz. That can't be a coincidence." Seriously. Why is Todd such a goon?
