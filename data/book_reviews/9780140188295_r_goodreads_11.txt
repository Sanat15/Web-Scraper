Just finished reading😭,It was a masterfully crafted, though short, novella. While reading, I felt a similar resonance to 'The Adventures of Tom Sawyer' by Mark Twain. However, the stark contrast lies in the focus on adult struggles, not childhood innocence. It centers on George and Lennie, a small, astute man and a large, gentle soul, less intellectually gifted.

" 𝗗𝗼𝗻’𝘁 𝗸𝗻𝗼𝘄 𝘄𝗵𝘆. 𝗠𝗮𝘆𝗯𝗲 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿’𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘄𝗵𝗼𝗹𝗲 𝗱𝗮𝗺𝗻 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗹𝗱 𝗶𝘀 𝘀𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗼𝗳 𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵 𝗼𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿."

 George perpetually strives to shield Lennie from harm, particularly when they secure work on a ranch. He promises Lennie rabbits, aware of his tendency to inadvertently harm small creatures, such as mice, during affectionate petting. On the ranch, Curley's wife drifts about, seeking attention under the guise of searching for her husband. Her presence casts a foreboding shadow, inevitably culminating in the story's tragic denouement.

→it is an imperative read. I could not relinquish it. The narrative unfolds seamlessly, and the supporting characters imbue the story with profound depth. A palpable sense of unity and cohesion renders the entirety exceptionally potent.
→ The conclusion... it is utterly devastating, yet perfectly fitting. It is a narrative that endures, lingering long after the final page is turned.
