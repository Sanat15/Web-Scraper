Rating: 4/5

This charming little book was a good introduction to all that makes the Swiss, "Swiss." I am of Swiss ancestry, and anxious always to learn about my ancestral homeland, especially after having met some distant cousins there. I admire the national commitment to the environment, their national pride, and their orderly society. The book is almost 30 years old, but much of what the ex-pat, Paul Bilton, describes is still very true. It isn't inexpensive to live in Switzerland, but the quality of life is worth the cost.
