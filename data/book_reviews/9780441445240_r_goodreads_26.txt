Rating: 5/5

I love this series so much and have learned from stepper’s skills as a writer. The series starts with a bang with the book, which draws us into the Trie Game with fascinating detail, not failing to show us the cruelties and flaws of the Gamesmen and the Game itself.
My only regret is that there seems to be no electronic version of the series available. I have had to resort to my old paperback editions to reread it.
