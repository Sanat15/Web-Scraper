Rating: 5/5

I love this book! It is so quirky and unusual and fun. It's a gothic and a paranormal and a mystery/suspense, and a romance and...just plain weird. I wish that the author would write more books for me to read.

Peregrine's Rest is completely unlike these books, but for some odd reason brings them to my mind: "The Graveyard Book" by Neil Gaiman, "She-Crab Stew" by Dawn Langley Simmons, and "A Fine and Private Place" by Peter S. Beagle.

10/20/13 Reread once again. It's almost Halloween, and this is a fun one for this time of year.
