haven't read the essays included in this (yet) but even though they seem interesting the works presented in this book talk by themselves. Samaras is an artist with insane habilities to render his vision in various mediums while also having one of the wildest most astonishing imagination. from his boxes, to the photo transformations and the darker matrix drawings he exhibits an insane range on his work but all share the same vibrancy and sense of movement. one of my fav plastic artists oat and only with the work exhibited in this book
