Rating: 5/5

This book is hilarious and absolutely delightful. Not sure I should count as having read it, 'cause it takes less than a minute to go through. But then, of course, I went slower to admire and examine the artistry of the mechanisms and the clever spot-on illustrations by Balvis Rubess. They're hilarious if you don't have the phobia!

Each page gives you a word, the pronunciation, the definition, and a bit of info on the definition. 

If you haven't checked out the genius behind the pop-up mechanisms, check out Matthew Reinhart's youtube how-to videos. Straight-up awesome dude.
