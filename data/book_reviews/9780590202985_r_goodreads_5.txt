Rating: 3/5

Part of this did take place at Nightmare Hall. Things don't look good for one of the residents.

* Rachel is looking at some art student's work when she sees something odd. There's a painting, which is a seascape, and in the waves it looks like someone is drowning. None of her friends can see it though.
* That night Rachel has a dream that a student is hit on the head with a baseball bat whilst fishing and drowns. She wakes up to her roommate telling her that it has actually happened.
* Rachel then sees another painting, and this one looks like someone falling down the stairs. She later dreams that Milo at Nightmare Hall is pushed down the fire escape and seriously injured.
* Rachel then dreams about a girl being killed by having plaster poured down her nose while a student is making a plaster cast of her face.
* Rachel then gets injured and trapped in an art store room and has to escape by using a dumb waiter.
* Her purse is stolen and then returned to her with a message saying that she won't live to see monday (which is the next day).
* She's then chased down some stairs, goes to a mall and nearly gets a heavy plant pot on her head, and ends up a bit freaked out.
* She then gets hit on the back of the head and wakes up with cotton pads on her face and some nutter mixing up plaster to kill her with. She gets away by kicking the plaster jug over, slicing her attacker, and knocking them over. She discovers the person trying to kill her is Samantha, who was pissed off because nobody appreciated her art work. So she decided to make her art matter by painting people's deaths into it. Bizzare.
