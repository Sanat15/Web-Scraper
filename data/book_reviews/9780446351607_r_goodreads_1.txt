Rating: 4/5

I was pleasantly surprised by the time I finished Racing Hearts by the unknown-to-me writer, Eileen Witton. Her name turned out to be a pseudonym of animal-lover and author Janice Bennett. And yes, I had read a couple of her romances but nothing that I was overly fond of.

First off, this is an American Regency romance. The widowed heroine, Charlotte Deane, was as American as apple pie. She was proud of who she was and her country. Her much-older husband had left their farm in a bit of a fiasco when he died, leaving her to salvage what was left. Plain and simple, Charlotte loved horses. 

On the other hand, Kevin Marlowe was an English nabob with a working horse farm in Ireland. He had loads of money but no title. He was traveling the colonies -his words- searching for his younger brother, Tony. He had arranged a marriage between his sibling and a young lady of the ton. The lack of entitlement was a sore spot for Kevin and Tony was going to be made to help him.

Tony was attracted to Charlotte’s younger sister, Bella. Kevin thought she was no good. ‘Charly’ and Kevin seemed to butt heads every time they were near each other. In the meantime, there was a bit of suspense. Someone was trying to keep Charly from paying off her deceased husband’s mortgage so she could finally own her homestead. 

 Kevin would do something nice and just when Charly thought he might be improving, he would tick her off once again. This clean romance is for those of you that love ‘opposites-attract’ trope. Just be prepared to wait for that HEA.
