Rating: 1/5

Don’t waste your time. A total snooze fest. Nothing like the prequel, “fly girl”. Had to force myself to keep reading. The opposite of a “page turner”. Unless you count the fact that I kept reading &amp; reading, waiting for something to happen, but it never did. Very tedious &amp; boring. Way too much inane dialogue with no action or real drama. Very anticlimactic. This book is going in the trash. I wouldn’t even give it away to my worst enemy.
