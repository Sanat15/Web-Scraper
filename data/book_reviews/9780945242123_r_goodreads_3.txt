Rating: 3/5

This book is divided into two parts:

Part One deals with P. Rajagopalachari's Master and his ways of living with respect to Our Personal Environment, Tolerance, Duty, and Love. This was a very nice section to re-read, having read this before about a decade ago, and the stuff of it being extremely useful in daily application. 5-star to this one, no doubt about that!

Part Two deals with P. Rajagopalachari's Master's Teachings and Works. This section dealt with "The Way of the Spirit", "Approaching Reality", the Role of the Guru (Teacher, Master), on Spiritual Experiences, and "The Gift of Liberation". Although having some interesting and valid points, I could not at many times come to agree with what was written here, and finally seemed to me that this part was however, wonderful for a non-intellectual personality of sorts, the common-man of the masses.

The book is written in extremely plain and lucid style, easy to understand, and worth a read at least once. And especially moreso for the First Part.
