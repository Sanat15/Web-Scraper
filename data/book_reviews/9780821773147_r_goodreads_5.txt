Rating: 3/5

I give this book 3 stars because it took a good minute to get into it. The male protagonist was really unlikeable at first and I was not sure where the author was going with it. Fast forward and Treasure wins him over. The fact that she was 17 and God knows how old he was made it icky for a bit too. 

My favorite part of the book though was Treasure being a thinker and plotting ways to survive with her temperamental husband.

The fun side story of Lorenda and Wyatt was good and I wish there was more to it!

The ending was random as hell which was disappointing.
