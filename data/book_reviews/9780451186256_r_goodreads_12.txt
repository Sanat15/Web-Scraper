Rating: 3/5

3,5 stars

I liked the book, I just didn’t love it. 

The review has some spoilers, so be warned: 
Jack, Lord Pemerton, is a cynical rake forced by family circumstances to seek a bride. Lady Mary Haviland is a plain, charming, but firmly on-the-shelf spinster who offers to help Jack by presenting him with suitable candidates. Once Jack learns that Mary is very wealthy he realizes that he won't need to look elsewhere for a bride. He believes that Mary should be very happy and thankful for the opportunity he's giving her by marrying her. Mary is verbally and physically abused by her father in all of her childhood so she grows up thinking that she is very ugly and the only way someone is going to marry her, is through her money. When Jack proposes Mary thinks that he doesn't know about her money so it must be their easy friendship that makes him propose so she accepts.
From the beginning of the book through somewhere towards the end Jack acts like the fortune hunter we know he is, he doesn't do anything to show us he is changed so it wasn't easy for me to like him. I didn't. 
I also didn't realize when they felt in love, IF they ever did. 
The storyline was nice but their relationship seemed shallow.. hence the 3,5 stars.
