Rating: 5/5

I finished this book in total awe of Elizabeth Strout. This was her debut novel and for me it ranks right up there with my favourite- Olive Kitteridge.

Amy and Isabelle- daughter and mother- the two were so well drawn out I felt like I knew them personally. When we are first introduced to them, we immediately know that something has happened to cause a major rift between them. Very slowly we are made privy to what has caused this animosity.

This mother/ daughter relationship is explored so perfectly. Isabelle is a single mother who worries incessantly. She is shy and insecure. She wants to have friends, but doesn’t know how to put herself out there. Amy, too, is shy. But she is starting to stretch herself. Bothe seem to live in their own thoughts- having difficulty sharing with the other. It seems that we are definitely products of our parents and our upbringing, no matter how hard we try not to be. It’s near impossible to shed all that has been instilled in us.

I thought this book was pretty near perfect. The exploration of mother- daughter relationships is one that I am always drawn to. I loved the way Strout had this underlying tension throughout especially as we got closer to finding out what happened. 

I’m so glad I finally backtracked and read this book. Highly recommended. Going on my favourites list!

Published: 1998
