This is the second book in this series, about the brother we met in the first.

Cameron has lost his faith in human-kind, he lost his parents in a crash, his bounty-hunter partner betrayed him with the woman he thought he loved, someone stole his trust-fund etc. So, now he's moved to Mustang, trying to start up a ranch.

His place is not in good straits and Alicia comes looking for a job as a housekeeper, he refuses but when he sees her daughter Rebecca he agrees. He knows Alicia has secrets.

Alicia ran with her daughter after her husband died and her cold in-laws threatened to take her daughter away. With Cameron she finds a safe place, and he arouses a lot of feelings in her that her husband didn't but Cameron is not willing to open up.

I liked Cameron over coming his past through the confrontation he really needed and Alicia finally deciding to take control.

The book was pretty enjoyable.
