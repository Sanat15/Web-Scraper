Rating: 4/5

Read in anticipation of my journey to Morocco, this is a fascinating book, although written in 1966 and thus a bit dated in style and presentation (small print, lots of lines on a page...). I learned lots about the tribal nature of the Moroccan culture and the information about how the Sultan was a true demi-god to the people. Lots of heads being lopped off, and torture and such. But explained in a context that makes it more understandable. I’m glad I worked my way through it.
