In between intense, thoughtful, meaningful writing, I need some palate refreshers. This is a silly story with characters named Ampersand (&amp;), Diggit, Skillet, Cave, Sutch. Hidden treasure, literary and otherwise, skulking, ne'er-do-wells, skullduggery, ocean-cliff towers, old spinsters, and moldering gentry all abound. Fun, funny, and easy.
