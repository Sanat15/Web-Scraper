Rating: 5/5

Love This Western!

I for one have never read a book about a sheriff with a 200-pound dog, specifically a mastiff, as a sidekick. I was trying to picture the look on the faces of the lawbreakers as Sheriff Jonas Culpepper strides up to them with such a huge dog! 

This is the second book I’ve read by this author. I enjoy this author’s writing style and unique plot ideas. This story has a train robbery, a damsel in distress, and a hero. All the elements of a good read!
