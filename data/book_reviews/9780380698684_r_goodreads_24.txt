Rating: 4/5

This story is multilayered and a mystery. Armino a tour guide in Italy is confronted by an old woman in a doorway who he recognizes later when she is found murdered. She is Marta a woman who looked after him when he was a child during WWII. He goes back to his home university town of Ruffano. There the Donati brothers, separated for 20 years by WWII, each believing the other to be dead, are reunited. 

Aldo directs an annual festival which appears to be heading towards a confrontation between two group of students. Secret societies, love affairs and mistaken motivations lead to a mad chariot ride with 18 horses and the flight of the falcon.
