Rating: 2/5

Not anywhere near the caliber of writing in Jackson's To Die series...Lackluster and syrupy tale of Skye Donahue and Max McKee. Rich man falls for poor gal and powerful Jonah McKee forbids the relationship because, surprise-surprise, Skye is not good enough for his boy! This type of story has been done to death and Jackson adds absolutely nothing new to this one to make it remotely interesting. And lucky for us *sigh*...(NOT!), Jackson decided to take the Love Letters trilogy ("B" is for Baby and "C" is for Cowboy in addition to this one) and combine them into a new release titled Revenge. Pass on this one. It's basically written "white noise". But if you must read it, save your cash and go to your local public library and check it out.
