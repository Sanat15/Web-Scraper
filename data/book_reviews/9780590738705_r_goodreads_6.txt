Rating: 5/5

I was very excited to see that this was republished
in 2019, as it was one of the key texts, 90s classics all, (along with the Usborne Book of
Castles and A Street Through Time) that inspired me to learn to read, rather late, so that I
could get the most out of these beautifully illustrated books. The illustrations are very
detailed and clear, holding the attention while you examine the text. I spent literally hours
poring over the pictures, especially the castle, the underground station, the galleon and the
cathedral. Stephen Biesty’s books are the best for drawing the eye, conveying a great deal of
information very palatably, and creating a totally absorbing experience. 7+
