Rating: 5/5

I just recently found out there’s a sequel to The Illustrated Mum so I decided to reread the original before diving into the new one. This is one of the books my mom bought me, and I probably first read it when I was around ten? Picking it up again now fills me with so so much nostalgia, worn cover and slightly yellowed pages🥹

The story follows Dolphin and her sister Star as they navigate life with their unpredictable mum, Marigold. When Star moves out to live with her dad, Dolphin is left to face the highs and lows of her mum on her own. I just adored Dolphin so much, she’s such a relatable and a heartfelt character.
