Rating: 2/5

SHORT science fiction and I mean short. No singular story went beyond 5 pages. Which makes for a quick read but it takes a special author to make something special with so little words.

Average of the stories = 2.12

Most stories were perfectly fine, mostly 2's and 3's with only a couple standouts and even more real stinkers. But I'll just highlight the highs.

Robert Heinlein - The more I read from him the more I understand how legendary he was. "Columbus Was a Dope" is a retelling of the timeless debate 'Why explore any further?'. With the slightest of twists to make it fresh yet again.

Jack Finney - Wonderful little story about "The Third Level" above Grand Central Station and the secret within. Can't wait to see read more from him.
