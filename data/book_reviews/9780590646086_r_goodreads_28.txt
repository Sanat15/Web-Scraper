  plot
 ⋆˚𝜗𝜚˚⋆
I was actually a little unsure going into this. I’ve read that this book holds a very feminist message, and I, as you should know, am not a feminist. Of course, I will not abstain from reading books with light feminist themes, partly because it’s hard to find books (even Christian ones) without messages like that.

The plot was slow-going. I’m usually one to enjoy books with slower plots because that means I get to enjoy the book more, it has more depth than just being a “page-turner” but this was more “I just didn’t feel like going on.” I was tempted to dnf multiple times, but I hate not finishing books. 

Though it was quite a unique take on Cinderella, it did take away the whole message of Cinderella being an example of loving one’s enemies, as the Grimm brothers wrote it to be, and more, “I have no choice but to work for you.” 

Also, this was a retelling of a retelling. Very much based on the Disney movie, and not much the original Grimm fairytale. Many might not know this, but in the Grimm fairytale, the stepsisters were actually very beautiful, but ugly in heart. I’m wanting to find Cinderella books that are based off of the original fairytale, not the Disney movie. 

Positive note: I did find a biblical message in this, and I’ll elaborate in my “personal thoughts” section

  personal thoughts
 ⋆˚𝜗𝜚˚⋆
I think the theme of this book (obedience) perfectly illustrates what Christians mean by obeying God. There is nothing wrong with obedience, but when it becomes mandatory, where you have no choice but to obey, then it is not willful. God wants us to obey willfully, He wants us to choose to follow Him. 

That is why He put the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil. If God did not give us a choice to obey Him, we would be robots, just obeying Him because we didn’t have any other choice. But Adam and Eve chose to disobey Him. The devil did not force them to, nobody shoved the fruit down their throats, they chose it, they knew well that God forbade it, and yet they disobeyed.

As Christians, we choose to obey God and His law. There is always a choice to disobey, to not follow His law. But like a parent punishes their child of disobedience, our disobedience against God will be punished if we do not repent of it. That’s just the little snippet of the gospel I found in this.
