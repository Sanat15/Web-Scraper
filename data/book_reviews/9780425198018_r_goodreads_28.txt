Rating: 4/5

(This review is based on ARC e-book provided via NetGalley in exchange for an honest opinion.)

Having read Beloved Stranger, book #2 of Patricia Potter’s Beloved Trilogy, first, I can vouch Beloved Imposter (book #1) is a standalone with its own HEA. As with most series, the plots and character development are more established and enjoyable when these three books are read in serial order, but certainly not necessary. As an avid fan of Scottish romance, I did enjoy Beloved Imposter. Ms. Potter’s writing style flows, and she adds enough historical details to give her intriguing romantic tales and characters credibility. 

My biggest concern regarding this book is the amount of time spent waiting for something imminent to happen. Perhaps I am unfairly comparing it to Beloved Stranger, which moved at a faster, more suspenseful pace. Also, I tend to dislike a romance where a former first love overshadows the main couple’s. Therefore, was not a fan of Rory’s first wife, Maggie, as being portrayed so perfect all the time. The dead can do no wrong adage kept coming to mind. 

Overall, I know once you read one book you will find yourself wanting to read the HEA’s for each of these Maclean brothers. It truly is a well-written, thoroughly entertaining series.
