Rating: 1/5

A disappointing Harlequin presents, that really had potential. Unfortunately, the heroine, who is a model, receives a threat against her life. Except, her father does not deem this worthy enough to tell her, so he assigns her a body guard which she tries to evade at every turn. The father and body guard are condescending towards her, and she acts immature. The father is truly terrible, the heroine is unlikable. I don't see them lasting long term.
