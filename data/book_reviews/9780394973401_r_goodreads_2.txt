Rating: 2/5

I am so over the Berenstain Bears books. Thank God Julia never got into these like I know some kids are. We've read them when we've come across them but other than that we tend to pass them by. 
I can see where this book would be helpful for a kid who bites their nails but man, this family is so annoying. Why don't they have names? Everyone has names. Even some rocks have names for the love of God. Yet members of this family of bears are known by "brother" and "sister"? 
Listen, I'm sorry, I was going to review this like I normally do but I can't. I no longer want to think about any of these damn bears. Ever again. And, just so people don't get the wrong idea, I'm rating this two stars because.... hell, I don't even know why. I just don't want to give it one star. But I still hate it. 
I almost forgot - the stories are far too long - you could cut out half the book - any of the books - and still get the same thing and maybe no fall asleep too.
