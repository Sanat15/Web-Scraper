Rating: 4/5

Farce in plot and play

Charles, playing in a farce on tour, and doing some voice recording on the side, finds himself entangled with four women, a promise to lay off the booze, a thesaurus, and a death. There’s as much confusion in his own life as in the play. You’ll be cluey to predict whodunnit. Note: in this book, written and set in the late 1990s, Brett draws on the BBC scene in the 1970s, when the first Charles Paris was published — but funnily, Charles (like the long-lived Poirot) has aged very little.
