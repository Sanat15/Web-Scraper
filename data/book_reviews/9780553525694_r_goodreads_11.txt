Rating: 4/5

Many people seem to have this as their least, or one of their least, favourite X-Wing book, and since I didn't enjoy the other X-Wing books by Aaron Allston I didn't have any high hopes for this one. But I was pleasantly surprised as to how much I actually enjoyed Starfighters of Adumar. At least the first half of the book. The last half consisted mostly of space battles, and I don't really enjoy reading about that very much, so it brought my overall enjoyment down a bit.

3.75/5 stars
