Rating: 4/5

I read this the other day while watching the snow fly. I'm not sure this has every plant, but I was able to identify some of the plants I've come across before in Southern Montana. Some of the pictures don't really capture how the plants look universally, but this would be a lot of fun to bring hiking/camping/fishing/insert-outdoor-activity-here. I got my laminated edition at the thrift shop and I'll be taking it on nature walks since my mother and I usually only speculate which plants are which.
