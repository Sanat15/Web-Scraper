Rating: 4/5

Summer vacation is here and it is pretty hot here in Sunnydale, in fact it feels hotter than it's ever been. The insect population has exploded, and all of a sudden a new threat other than vampires and ghosts has emerged from the darkness - giant insectoid creatures who are kidnapping and turning hapless victims into more of their colony's worker drones! It's up to Buffy and a distracted Giles to come up with a plan to stop this new terror, but an unexpected tragedy is about to upset the status quo. Fassbender and Pascoe come together to deliver a great Buffy story that is as far removed from vampires as what you can get. Buffy and Giles are on edge, there is love in the air, there is drama, action, and even a well placed misdirect or two. Penciller Richards and inker Pimentel continue their streak of delivering great looking, clean artwork that brings the Scoobies gang to life. Another decent addition to classic Buffy the Vampire Slayer line of comic books, and sure to be enjoyed by fans of the series.
