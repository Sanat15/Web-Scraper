Rating: 3/5

It read quite well, like a novel in parts. There were some sections which focused on detail, such as all the detail of the people on a ship, which became a bit tedious. Amazing detail of Cook interacting with native populations - hard to imagine what is must’ve been like for Māoris to have seen guns and an emormous ship for the first time. Also amazing how the ships function like a moving town. Overall I enjoyed but would find it a tough recommend unless someone has a particular interest.
