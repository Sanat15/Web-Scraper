Rating: 3/5

Purchased for $3 because friends dared me. After avoiding eye contact with the cashier, I quickly skimmed the book to unlock its secrets. I regret to inform you that I have not yet practiced the techniques described, so I cannot confirm their effectiveness. If I later become a sexpert, review will be updated. This lukewarm review focuses instead on the mediocre writing style and a major flaw in the writer’s assumptions. I’m certainly in favor of men having a more pleasurable experience. But Barb writes as if a man lasting longer is the ONLY possible way for a woman to be satisfied. If that’s what you believe, you may have been watching too many internet “documentaries.” I’d like to think they author knows better, but you wouldn’t get that from this book. But as I said, if I later find her advice to be effective, that might earn another star.
3/5 🍆
