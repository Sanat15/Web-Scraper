Rating: 5/5

What kind of Emergency Room or Urgent Care can make this story end well? Fact is (at least, a fact in the fictional world where stuffed animals walk and talk), terrifying accidents happen. 

However, this appears to be a picture book world. Which means that most likely, all endings are happy. And those walking-talking animals produce their own money, can buy whatever they like, and never have a parent. Come to think of it, no wonder animal books like this are so popular. 

Scary-ish but true, Elliot -- the stuffed moose -- catches his leg on a nail. Soon his stuffing threatens to come out. Oh, horrors!

However, Elliot gets by with a little help from his friends.

FIVE STARS for this tale of friendship and ingenuity, a picture book with fantastically-beautifully-cuddly pictures.
