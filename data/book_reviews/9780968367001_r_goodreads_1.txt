Rating: 5/5

Randomly picked the book. The time management techniques that are split into chapters are actually based on one simple principle that you don’t want to waste your time or even others. 

The methods that the authors mentions may not applicable in the digital age of emails, SMS, WhatsApp, x, time saving tools and discord. The guiding principles remain the same and following them may stop all doomscrolling. One thing he mentions on entertainment, we can choose the programs, we want to watch that is way ahead of its time. 

There is opportunity to write a book with the digital tools in mind. What he ends by saying is most relevant - time management is not an art or science, it’s a way of life.
