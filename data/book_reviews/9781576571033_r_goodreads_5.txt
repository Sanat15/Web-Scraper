Rating: 2/5

The Book starts with a promising idea, but sadly never manages to deliver on it.
The first half had a decent atmosphere and a bit of intrigue, but the further the story went, the more everything weakened. By the last third, I felt my interest fading completely.

The characters were quite underdeveloped, and the villains especially felt flat and superficial. The writing style is very simple, easy to follow, yes, but not memorable or engaging. The plot begins with some excitement, but the second half feels rushed, as if it was wrapped up quickly without proper buildup.

Even though the pacing is fast, it didn’t help much; I never felt truly attached to the story. The book had good potential, but the flaws overshadowed it, fast read with a good concept, but ultimately too shallow and rushed to leave a real impact.
