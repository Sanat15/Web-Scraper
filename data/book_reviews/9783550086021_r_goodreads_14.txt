Rating: 4/5

DeMille can tell a story, but do not expect a quick one! The sequel to Plum Island takes place a year or so after, but with Corey with a new job. After he retired from the NYPD, he taught at John Jay for a bit, and then got talked into joining the "Antiterrorist Task Force" located in NYC, which consists of primarily FBI types and NYPD folks (retired and active). Corey has mixed feelings about the job, and that he works in the Middle East section, but so it goes. Things change rapidly one day when a Libyan 'terrorist', who defected in Paris, is due to arrive at JFK. Corey and his team await the arrival of the plane, but we quickly know something is very wrong. 

As the title suggests, the Libyan known as the Lion has come to the US to play a game, a very deadly one. The long backstory to the Lion dragged, and the lessons on Islam via DeMille I am not sure I buy, but they do set the stage. The Lion has come to the US for some serious payback, and he is sharp, well trained and deadly. He also has had some serious help from Libya to set this up. Pretty much a classic thriller, and given that this was published a year before 9/11, almost prophetic. Yes, this felt bloated at times, yes I found the pacing erratic, especially at the start (e.g., the first 300 pages!), but it really settles down after that to be a nail biter. 3.5 deadly stars!!
