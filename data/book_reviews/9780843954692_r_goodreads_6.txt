Rating: 4/5

Fast-paced legal thriller

If you like legal thrillers, this one is not to be missed. Jordan La Fontaine is an assistant prosecuting attorney in Portland, Oregon. She is assigned a case involving an apparent wife-killer, but information is uncovered that raises doubt whether she is prosecuting the right man.

At the same time, her son has been charged with the murders of two college students parked in the local lovers' lane.

If I had proofread this book, I would have made 20 corrections.
