Rating: 4/5

"Entoverse" marks a strong comeback for James P. Hogan with his fourth entry in the "Giants" series. Written in the '90s more than a decade after its predecessors, Hogan's heroes tackle another interstellar (and interdimensional) mystery. Scores of characters traverse multiple worlds and planes of existence. "Entoverse" returns the sense of awe readers enjoyed in Hogan's 1978 "Inherit the Stars." 
Protagonist scientist sleuths Victor Hunt and Chris Danchekker are joined by (finally) strong female characters. Dr Sandy Holmes, journalist Gina Marin, and goth virtual reality surfer Nixia enhance the usual cast members Hunt, Danchekker &amp; their Ganymean cronies.
Detailed world building abounds. Scientific postulates and theories challenge readers in this long but rewarding sci fic odyssey. Highly recommended.
