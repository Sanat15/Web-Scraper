Rating: 4/5

The Fourth Annual Collection gathers 27 stories from 1986, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1986") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Pat Cadigan's "Pretty Boy Crossover," Richard Kearns's "Grave Angels," Harry Turtledove's "And So to Bed," and Lewis Shiner's "Jeff Beck," with a slightly more mixed appreciation for Judith Moffett's "Surviving."
