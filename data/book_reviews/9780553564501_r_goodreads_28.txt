Rating: 1/5

Pretentious, moi? This book is, and though it's not the author's first it might as well be. The main character is impossibly talented and virtuous, secondary characters orbit around him, and one by one are dismissed. 
The author's rather juvenile prejudices against certain races, all lower social classes and the one religion it's predictable for him to hate, make this a very tedious book. The words utter and bilge spring to mind. If not for a lockdown, 100+ days and still continuing, I wouldn't have wasted weeks!
