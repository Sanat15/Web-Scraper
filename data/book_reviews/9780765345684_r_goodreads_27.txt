Rating: 5/5

OK, first of all if you have read the Goodreads description it is dead wrong. So wobbly it was clearly written by someone who had either NEVER read the book, or had not read it for fifty years and could not quite remember it.

This IS the final book to the Unicorn Series, it is the final one and follows Tanaquil's adventures in yet another world. There is one alternative world per book and per unicorn, each is an awesome fantasy novel as well as many other things. Each colour of unicorn, each world represents different things about humanity and the inner landscape of a human being, as seen through our protagonist.

This one was unexpectedly different, it was charming and gave an extremely satisfying end to the series. I will say no more, because I will be reviewing it online.
