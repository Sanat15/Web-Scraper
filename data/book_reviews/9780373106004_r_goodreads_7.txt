Rating: 2/5

Where has the drama gone? Kinda boring!

After her grandmother's death, the heroine finds out about inheritance in France. She decides to go exploring. On her way to Bellevigne she meets a charismatic Frenchman. Of course, he's the owner of the said house and invites her to stay and use his grandfather's studio (she is a famous sculptor). His grandmother loathes the heroine, his stepmother hates her guts and yet the girl agrees to stay.
There's a chance the heroes are related (they may share the grandfather) and it's too close for me.
