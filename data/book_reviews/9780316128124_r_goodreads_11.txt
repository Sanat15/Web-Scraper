It amazes me how many individual convictions exist concerning spiritual matters. I have come to the conclusion that what we each choose to believe is a very personal journey; a compilation of various religions and beliefs that we adopt and hold to be true, either by first hand experience or what we learn from others. There were some theories I didn't connect with at all. But others that touched me deeply, adding new dimensions to my own conclusions and examples of how to go even deeper into this most intimate realm of inner examination.
