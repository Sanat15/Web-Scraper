Rating: 3/5

It was okay, I read it. I think Edwards is kind of pretentious with all of this talk about "left and right hemispheres" but it does an excellent job at trying to explain how to actually draw, namely to learn how to "see" in a certain way than to capture the soul or essence of whatever subject you're drawing.

 I felt like a lot of the props that were essential for the exercises were just a way to jack up the price of the this book which contributed to my cynical reading of it.
