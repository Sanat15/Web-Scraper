Rating: 4/5

Maybe a 3.75, but I'll round up for GR

This is interesting because while it is in the X-Wing series, instead of either Rogue or Wraith squadrons we narrow back to Wedge and a small group of close compatriots as he's sent on a diplomatic mission to Adumar (n.b. I felt weird commenting in the readalong as Daumari about the Adumari lol). After being the longsuffering dad of two groups of loveable found families, it's nice to get Wedge alone with the thoughts in his head as he's put in an unfamiliar environment. Can you dogfight your way out of court intrigue?? Kiiiiinda, especially when everyone wants to duel and Wedge wonders if this culture can be less wasteful with lives.
