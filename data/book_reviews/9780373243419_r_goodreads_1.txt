Rating: 2/5

Ugh. What an awful book. Ms. Carey's favorite words seem to be lovemaking/making love/make love, and she uses them continuously throughout the book. It's like she took what would be normally just a love story and tried to spice it up in a Harlequin Romance way by forcing those words in, instead of just letting the book flow naturally. I loved her 1982 book "Kiss And Tell" and that's why I bought this book way back in about 2001, but I had forgotten just how bad it really is. I bought three more of her books as well and if I remember right, I didn't care for any of them. But I'm still going to give them one last read before I decide whether to toss them or not.
