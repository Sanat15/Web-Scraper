Rating: 3/5

I'm torn. I love the setting. However, the love story itself is lacking the chemistry.

The characters meet and go to bed. The hero is furious because the heroine's reaction is not what he wanted. What did he expect? Did he want her to start writing the wedding guests list?
And why is everybody trying to make the heroine feel guilty? Her loving mother calls her heartless. Really?
The hero is really placid. And they spend the most part of the book apart.
