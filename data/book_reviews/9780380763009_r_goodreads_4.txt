Rating: 2/5

This one is hard to get into. So many Italian Cliches. The opening is a mess of dialog and random things happening. Especially in the first few pages the author used words like 'lusty' and 'bosom' to describe the main love interest. 
I have read two others from this series, this being the third one I have read I would say this series has its moments and then it’s phone-in moments. The execution and flow of the story just isn't there. 
Once the characters are in Brighton the story picks up slightly better than the start in Italy. It’s still full of cliches but the pacing is better towards the middle. Plus the relationship with Valerian and Allegra turns more helper than rogue. The ending feels rushed and cheesy. I ended up skimming some of the last few chapters.
