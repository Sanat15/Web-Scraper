Rating: 4/5

This book was one of the first story book for my little angel. We enjoyed reading this book together from very early age, even today we reread this occasionally. I remember that my daughter instantly recognised Winnie's friend Piglet when we visited a departmental store for soft toys.
Now what can children get from this book?
They can learn colours while listening to the story; wonderful way to teach colour. Event they can feel the sense of counting.
Ultimately the celebration of friendship irrespective of win and loss among friends.
