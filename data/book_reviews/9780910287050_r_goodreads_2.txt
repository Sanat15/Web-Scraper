Quite useful for a new parent. It discusses the different stages (newborn - 3 month), (3 month - 6 month), (6 month - 9 month), (1 year - 2 years), (2 year - 3 years), (3 year - 6 years), (6 year - 9 years). Behavior may look similar on the surface, but they could have entirely different reasons -- child may cry for discomfort during first 3 months, later their cry could imply they want attention, et cetera. The parent's responses need to be different, based on the age and developmental stages. 
I would want to read again, to familiarize myself with these stages.
