Rating: 4/5

4½ Stars

This was refreshingly light and fun, a change from the more intense romance I've read by Ms. Palmer. Canton and Janine are fabulous characters who have sizzling chemistry and bounce witty barbs off each other. Canton's teen daughter and Janine's teen brother are perfect supporting characters. I enjoyed the intrigue they found themselves embroiled in, and the resolution. I'm sure I'll be reaching to read Canton and Janine's journey to HEA again.
