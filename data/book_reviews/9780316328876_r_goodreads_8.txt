Rating: 3/5

I'm happy to say that Brain Macalvie is back. 'I Am The Only Running Footman' is another book in the Richard Jury series.
 I love the supporting characters in this series. From Racer, Cyril the cat, and Fiona at Scotland Yard to Carol Ann and Mrs. Wassermann at Jury apartments to Agatha, Trueblood, and Vivian in Long Piddleton.
They have such distinct voices and personalities. I sometimes think I love these books for them alone.
That being said let tell you how much I love Richard Jury. He has such an intelligent persona that he just makes you believe he will solve the case. 
More great characters in this book were the Warboys family that ran the inn Melrose was staying in. They brought great comic moments with their pent-up violence.
All in all another great mystery for Martha Grimes, and a great treat for her fans.
