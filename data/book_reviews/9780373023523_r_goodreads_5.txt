Rating: 3/5

I dislike it when plucky heroines fall in love and then become weepy and incapable of rational thought. 
Heroine (22) is blackmailed by an initially quite rabid hero (35ish) to become his “mistress” (foul term) for a family weekend. He is soon in lust, then love and at this point becomes more human and likeable. 
It’s a decent story and I’ve always enjoyed Jessica Steele novels, but it’s not a favourite. 3 stars.
