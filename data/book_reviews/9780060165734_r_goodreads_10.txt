I got this from a friend who was moving and getting rid of books -the name was familiar and as I'd never read anything by her I picked it up. 
I don't know what I was expecting, but it wasn't necessarily a story about an (ex-?) alcoholic detective obsessing about a suspect. 
There was quite a bit of humor and it made for an entertaining read, but it felt a bit dated and Steve's obsession became annoying at some point. There is an explanation for it, but it was a while before that became clear.
