Rating: 5/5

Unpublished portions of some of Freud’s letters from his estate prove that he knew “hysteria” in the young women (mostly, but his research also included boys) that were brought to him was a trauma response to sexual abuse, particularly incest. And yet, when none of his colleagues wanted to accept that (or purposely didn’t want it brought to light - nobody apparently bothered to seriously critique his conclusions), Freud decided to gaslight these young women into believing it was all in their heads. “Fantasy”

The author, having been trained in Freud’s psychoanalysis techniques, shows his bias and stops at calling Freud a coward. I think that’s being way too generous and there’s reason to believe that there were darker things at play here given Freud’s occult connections.

Interesting to read about how children were viewed and treated at this point in history. It gives certain education and reform movements around this time a proper context. But overall a graphic read. Wouldn’t recommend unless actually doing research on a related topic.
