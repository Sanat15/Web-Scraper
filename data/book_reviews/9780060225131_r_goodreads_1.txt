Rating: 4/5

"My name is Julio de la Vega Medina." For the young reader interested in the story of Columbus' first voyage to the New World, Miriam Schlein's short novel "I Sailed with Columbus" is an easy to read and to understand introduction based on Columbus' written log. Our narrator, Julio, jumps at the chance to accompany Cristobal Colon who hopes to find a westerly route to the Indies, with funding from Queen Isabella and King Ferdinand of Spain. 

The story is told diary style and serves as a neatly written lesson spiced up with interesting nautical facts - one of Julio's duties is to turn the ampoletta (hourglass) each half hour and sing out a short verse so all aboard can mark the passing of time. Again I noted the importance of hymns and prayers - "Sailors are among the most religious of men. They are also the most superstitious." 

A neat summary of the goals of Columbus can be compared to those of other explorers as they conquered faraway lands and people: (1) Claim new lands for Spain, (2) Teach the natives (called Indians because he thought he was in the Indies) to work for Spain, and (3) FIND GOLD!
