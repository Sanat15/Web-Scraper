Rating: 3/5

Sarah has been sent to her Great Grandmother's to get them out of her parent's hair and find a husband so she can permanently be out. She is a great organizer; they are tired of being organized. On the way she meets up with Stanhope who dies at one of the coach stops of tainted food. Sarah was in her bed and survived the food because she didn't each much, but they mistook her for Stanhope, a corpulant woman, and she assumed the role. She got to organize a house party for Anderson to find him a wife before he was thirty in a month and before he lost his property because of the conditions of the will it would revert to his 4 year old nephew if he didn't marry. It all worked out; all the ladies invited except Caroline found husbands just not Anderson. He found out he had another year to go, but he had fallen for Sarah/Stanhope. He has married her as a last resort and was going to annul the marriage when he found out he was only 29 and had a year to go. He couldn't imagine being without Sarah so he consummated the marriage.
