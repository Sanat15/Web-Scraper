Rating: 5/5

After wanting to read this for years (but not having the many hundreds of pounds for a copy) I'm so happy that it's been released in ebook format!

This is *the* essential book for Disneyland Paris fans. A bonus of the ebook is that it's up to date (even including the 2019 Phantom Manor refurb) and contains hyperlinks to interesting videos further illustrating the attractions described. An absolute joy to read!
