Rating: 4/5

John Corey is back, and now he's working for the Man as part of the ATTF, an anti-terrorism division comprised of CIA, FBI, and former NYPD. Of course, Corey is still full of piss and vinegar, tossing around the sarcasm and snarky one-liners as always. And as always, hooking up with his female cohorts. A true Alpha-male! 

I won't bog down this review with a summary of the story, you can find that on the book jacket or here on Goodreads. I do have to say, the start of the book is FANTASTIC. The whole scene with the airplane and the air-traffic controllers was so gripping, I knew I was hooked to the story. I am NOT a fast reader by any means, and I have to admit, I could not put this book down. Nelson DeMille is rapidly becoming one of my favorite authors. 

The story is very realistic and draws you into the scenes, almost like an eye-witness. The only drawback to DeMille's writing is that sometimes he can be a tad verbose. I mean, sometimes he can take the most mundane activities and stretch them out over 3-4 pages. It can get a little slow sometimes and I found myself saying "Ok, we get it, he's brushing his teeth, Let's move on!" But the rest of the writing is so captivating, it was easy to overlook the excess minutia.

All in all, I really enjoyed this book and it is my favorite DeMille book so far. I've added the rest of the John Corey series to my 'Want To Read' shelf and look forward to them all. If you enjoy a good thriller with a wise-ass protagonist, this is a read for you. I give this one a solid 4 stars. It would have been 5, but I subtracted one for the long-winded sections and the lame romantic interest storyline in this one. Just my two-cents.
