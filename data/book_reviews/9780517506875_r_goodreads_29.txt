Rating: 3/5

It’s a compelling story that calls out for your sympathy until the very last word. But it’s overwrought and at times almost impossible to keep track of all the characters. So I’m torn because I was cheering for David: for his success and for his happiness. But then he seemed foolishly fixated on being there alongside his southern brethren, instead of using his enormous advantages as a Harvard trained lawyer to institute broader, more far reaching changes. And it sometimes felt like the good characters were too good and loyal and the bad characters too evil. 

All in all I kept thinking of ways to tighten the story and wishing it had been told by a black man himself.
