Rating: 5/5

Incredible work and research put into this book. Finished it up right after beginning an archaeology course in Irish Studies and I feel. Whole. 
Walking in this place and trying to imagine the Neolithic people who lived here, the tomb builders, of portal tombs, passage tombs…walking in the same steps thousands of years ago. Did they feel the same about the way the air smells? How the wind feels? All the millions of shades of green that somehow exist here? It is really quite something.
