Rating: 4/5

Loved this book. A vividly written and powerful story, really makes you feel like you are living through the depression right alongside its characters. My one critique is that it ends in a strange, abrupt way, with Rosasharn breastfeeding a starving old man. Maybe I just don't get it, like is does that symbolize the creation of the welfare state with the New Deal? The poor of America suckling the teet of the government? I don't know. Overall very good. Loved the Joad family, especially Ma and Tom who seemed to be the ones holding everything together.
