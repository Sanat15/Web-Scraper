Rating: 4/5

This is a great story in the old tradition of historical romances - a saga of adventure, mishaps, sea voyages, lost inheritances and of course, the romance between the two main characters.
The book was very long but never felt bogged down, with the plot continuing to develop at a good pace. Brandy and Keir are thrown together in circumstances not of their own making and contained on board Keir's ship, with no where to go, their romance is able to develop over time, as it would in real life. I especially like the fact that Keir has to work at ensuring their happy ever after.
The cast of supporting characters is full and interesting making this a well rounded story.
