Rating: 5/5

I know I read this book at school when I was a little kid, right when it was newly released, because I still have the drawing I did as a 7-year-old, copying the cover! (Here it is on Twitter if you want to see it, ha ha https://twitter.com/StudioMikarts/status/1316401096408080384) I had dug up that drawing recently for a university project where my team wanted to show my art progression from kid to adult, and that inspired me to get a copy of the book it was based on for my home library! I was very happy to find a good used copy for incredibly cheap, especially since it was an original copy featuring the cover I used as reference for that drawing. This evening I read the book again for the first time in about 25 years!

It's still a lovely book, telling the story of a wolf who lost his pack. It's a positive story, realistic, not saccharine, and naturalistic, not fantastic. The illustrations are still beautiful, as well. Definitely a children's picture book worth reading even a quarter of a century later!
