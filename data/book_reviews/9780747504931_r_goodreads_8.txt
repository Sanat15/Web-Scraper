Rating: 4/5

Ben Wicks did a good job of compiling the memories of children who were evacuated from London to the countryside during WW2. The story is written in their own words. The book is also illustrated with personal mementoes and other historical pictures. I had heard about this project, but had never really thought about it. What an undertaking. Children as young as 5 were sent to live with foster parents in the country to keep them safe. Another consequence of the ravages of war.
