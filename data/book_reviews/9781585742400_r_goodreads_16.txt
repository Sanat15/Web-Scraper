Rating: 5/5

I truly enjoyed this book. I chose it as my February book, a book with a color in the title, for the Massachusetts Center for the Book 2024 reading challenge. So far I am very happy that I joined the challenge. I have read two books that I would not have chosen to read otherwise. 
I wasn't sure about it when I started it, but it definitely got better as I kept reading. The very descriptive style of writing made me feel as if I were right there. 
I would highly recommend this book to anyone!!
