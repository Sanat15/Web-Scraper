Rating: 5/5

I have a few books that I return to frequently and this is one of them. Because my copy was so worn and coming apart, I recently ordered a new (used) one from an online bookstore. "Rules for Prayer" brings together teachings from classic Christians throughout the centuries concerning how they scheduled their spiritual lives. While reading how great teachers, theologians, and writers ordered their daily devotional time, I am always inspired to incorporate something new into my own prayer life.
