Rating: 2/5

not one i'll read again. i hate the number of ellipses used (i stopped reading the harry potter series for the same reason) and you could tell this book was written either late 70s or early 80s because the euphemisms for genitalia were just awful... a penis is not a sheath, for one. there was a lot of 'love buds', 'love channels' and 'love seeds'. the sex scenes weren't very imaginative. it also wasn't a very believable story, for that time period. after two meetings (one in which no word was spoken and the 'heroine' shot at the 'hero', the second time actually speaking), the next time they met they had sex and were deeply in love. just not realistic. i won't be reading this again. the only reason i gave it two stars was for the fact that there WERE sex scenes with some sort of detail.
