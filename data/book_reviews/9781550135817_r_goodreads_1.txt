Rating: 1/5

The subtitle describes this as “An irreverent history of the tattoo.” Staid, outdated, and boring might have been better descriptors. 

While there are some interesting bits of trivia, the overall work is too concerned with religious and sexual connotations to get out of its own way. And there are weird “narrative” chapters that serve no purpose except as filler. Surely there are better volumes on the topic.
