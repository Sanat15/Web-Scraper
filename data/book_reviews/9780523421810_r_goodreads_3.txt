Rating: 5/5

Loved this one! I found this more exciting than the first one! Just like the first book, it goes more into depth on certain things and has extra details that the episode didn’t have. I also love the different twists the book has, while still having the same overall outcome! I really like that the books add their own unique twists to them instead of following the episodes word for word as I do think it would be boring. This was a super fun read and I definitely recommend if you’re a fan of the show and the antagonist, Karr!
