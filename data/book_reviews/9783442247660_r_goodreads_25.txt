Rating: 4/5

I had a good time with this one! I actually enjoyed it more than the previous book.

It leans heavily into a spy-thriller vibe, giving the story a faster pace while allowing the established cast to shine. The villains felt more compelling this time around, and the introduction of the Krytos virus was an interesting idea.

The worldbuilding on Coruscant was the major highlight. Considering this was written before the Prequels, it’s impressive how well it aligns with what we later saw on screen while still adding new layers.

My only criticism is that it clearly feels like a middle chapter, setting up future plotlines, and some of the romantic subplots didn’t fully land for me.
