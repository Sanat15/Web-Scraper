My favorite aspect of the traditional stories I have read is that they tend to be centered around child characters who are less fortunate than others. The same goes for this story, I just think it is nice for representation and so that students living in poverty can see themselves as characters who are about to have something amazing happen to them. This story moved a little fast for me. I'm not sure about stories that have a quick and easy resolution. However, I liked that the harp and hen that Jack stole were once his families belongings. I think that's the author's attempt to justify his stealing and to wrap the story up.
