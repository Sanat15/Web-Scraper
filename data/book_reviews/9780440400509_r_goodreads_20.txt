Rating: 5/5

Gene Stratton-Porter is one of those classic authors that I somehow missed in my youth, much like L.M. Montgomery and Betty Smith. But better late than never! I enjoyed Stratton-Porter’s prose every bit as much as Montgomery’s and Smith’s, and Freckles grabbed my heart just as quickly as Anne Shirley and Frances Nolan. And he rooted himself just as deeply. This is one of those books in which the characters are dripping with personality and goodness. And the nature writing was fantastic, drawing me deeply into the swamp with Freckles and wooing me along with him as he fell in love with every creature that populated it. I loved the prose, which managed to be easy and light while maintaining a depth and beauty that charmed me and made me think. My introduction to Stratton-Porter was lovely from start to finish, and I look forward to reading more of her work.
