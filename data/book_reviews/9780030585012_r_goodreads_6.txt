Rating: 2/5

Having finished all of the works of Raymond Chandler and Dashiell Hammett, I was looking for other writing is this genre. This collection certainly demonstrates that James Cain does not deserve to be ranked in the first tier of hard-boiled noir writers, probably not even in the second tier. He is a one trick pony, and not a particularly clever one at that. After reading most of the stories, I tired of this and abandoned it because of the mediocre writing.

The now largely forgotten Paul Cain (no relation) was a considerably better writer. His Fast One is probably the ultimate hard-boiled novel.
