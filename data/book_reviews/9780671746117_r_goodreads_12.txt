Rating: 3/5

This is the second of the Nancy Drew Files series of the 1980’s. Nancy and her friends are invited backstage to a famous rock and roll group when the lead singer goes missing. I found the simple storyline enjoyable and relaxing as I do when I tag along with this sleuth. I am watching the short lived drama series starring Tracy Ryan as Nancy. It was made in the nineties and was based on these files. 
It is all fun and innocent stuff.
