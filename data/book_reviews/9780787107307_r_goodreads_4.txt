Rating: 3/5

I appreciate Faye wanting to have her voice be heard. I mean almost everyone involved in OJ Simpsons case had a book deal. Which, I’m not complaining because I think of Nicole Brown a lot. But Faye, she had two. The first, about Nicole Brown Simpsons private life that Faye says she published to spread awareness about domestic violence and paint Nicole as a person rather than the murdered ex wife of famous football star, OJ Simpson. However…the only thing I know about Faye’s first book is Nicole’s sexual endeavors with Faye and without Faye. Only knowing that about private diary (Faye’s first book about Nicole), it felt purely exploitative. I will read private diary one day, and hopefully my opinion will change that it is what Faye says it is, a book purely about spreading awareness of domestic violence. 

Faye’s second book, is her letting the public know she isn’t just a rich, Beverly Hills, blonde, former coke addict, white woman that the media, public, prosecution, and defense treated her as. And she is all of those things. She knows it. But she like Nicole, is also a person outside of OJ Simpson, and the OJ Simpson trial. 

I did really like this. I liked Faye.
