Rating: 2/5

I didn't care much for this book. Inspector Troy seems like an interesting character, but he was barely in it. Most of the book was spent with an Army Intelligence officer, Capt. Cal Cormack, and Scotland Yard Special Branch detective Walter Stilton. Much was made of Cormack being baffled by Stilton's London slang and colloquial and literary references. It quickly became tiresome.

As for the mystery element, there barely was one. It was more of a spy-catcher novel, but not exactly a thriller. However, a good bit of the procedural action was interesting. It was at its best when it was being a detective/spy-catcher novel. Also, there are some pop culture references to the likes of Al Bowley and Flanagan &amp; Allen that I enjoyed. I love their music.

Unfortunately, there was a tedious love triangle between Walter's daughter Kitty and Cormack and Troy. I didn't care for that part at all and it dragged the overall tone of the book down. I won't be reading any more in this series.
