Rating: 3/5

Started out not loving this, since it was plodding along a bit at the beginning, but I stuck with it and I'm glad I did. A fun saga set in the "dawn of time" on the Asian steppes, at some point after people had domesticated horses but before they had metal tools &amp; weapons. Similar in feel to Auel's "Clan of the Cave Bear" series. I'll read more by this author.
