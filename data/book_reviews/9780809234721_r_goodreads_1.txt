Rating: 4/5

As I said in a progress report on here, although I am not pregnant I found this book to be very informative for those who need a break from baby brain overload. Friends, and family could get some use out of reading this book. I laughed till I had tears running down my face. As it was written in 1995, most of the tales about how the birthing process goes can be outdated depending on the person. However, if you just need a good laugh go for it.
