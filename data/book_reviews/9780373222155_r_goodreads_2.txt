Rating: 4/5

3.5 stars rounded up. CW for Native American stereotypes and some homophobia.

Good mystery, though it took me a while to warm up to Adam. Do we ever get to meet Familiar's girlfriend Clotilde?

The text for this new edition seems to have been created via OCR as there are some odd typos, like what should be the word 'burn' showing up as 'bum' in a couple of spots. It's distracting and I wish someone had done a quick copy edit.
