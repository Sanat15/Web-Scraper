Rating: 4/5

I grew up in the Appalachian area and Irish is part of my background. 
We were taught about our heritage in school and of course, in family gatherings.
So much was not even known in the generations I was exposed to. Many had been here since the 1600s, so much local and family history and of course, Irish home history did not come down the years.
I found it amazing how strong the Irish were, the Viking past is still alive and well.
