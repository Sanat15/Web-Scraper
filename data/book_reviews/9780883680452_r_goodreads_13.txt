Rating: 2/5

I couldn't even finish the book. It's really a 2.5. I read it halfway. 

What I liked about the book:
- The emphasis on how as Christian parents we have a huge responsibility of teach our children good vs. bad, manners &amp; respect, but most of all who Christ is &amp; how we can live for him. 
- The only chapter I liked was the chapter on why parents fail and how to avoid failure 
- Full of scripture! 

What I didn't like: 
- The way it was written. It was written in 1887
- I found some chapters repetitive 
- It was mainly focused on what a father should do. Towards the end it got into mothers, but I didn't even read it 😂
