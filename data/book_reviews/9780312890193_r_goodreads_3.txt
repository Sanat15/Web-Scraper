Rating: 5/5

This was a awesome story about Olivia. I was very happy that she found someone to love until the day she died the true death. I was sad that it had to end. I wanted her to find Saint Germain and just be friends and see each other. But of course that doesn't happen and it made me sad that she never saw him again. 
 
Spolier alert!!!!!
The man that was trying to kill her I think he found her there in Roma and that is who killed her. The last letter with Saint Germain to Niklos says that they believe there was a explosion that took the floor and the heater down and crushed her. Then says they found a eldery man on the side of the road dead that had a limp. That had to be Nino. Who else could it be. 

End of spolier......

If you love Saint Germain then you will love this book as well as the other two about Olivia just as much. Keep Reading!
