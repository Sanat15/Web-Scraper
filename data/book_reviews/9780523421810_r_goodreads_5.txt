Rating: 4/5

When I was a young teen, maybe 23 or 24 years ago, I went through a phase where I was overcome by a deep desire to read a Knight Rider novel. I didn't know if such a thing existed and had no easy way to find out, so I decided to write one myself: I remember selecting an episode and hammering out a few pages of an attempted novelization on a typer-writer, then abandoning the project and forgetting all about it. Fast forward to January 2016; a strange dream made me remember that aborted project, and after a quick search online I discovered that some Knight Rider novelizations had indeed been published over the years. This one is based on a "classic" episode, the 9th of season 1, where KITT meets his match in the form of the earlier prototype KARR, who doesn't care about protecting human life but is interested only in self-preservation (just like Lore and Data in ST:TNG). The novelization is of a single episode and so fills in a lot of character background and provides lengthy descriptions, some of it padding. The writing is clear and surprisingly professional for what must have been a rush job. There are, surprisingly, plenty of science fiction references throughout, and quite a bit of humor, courtesy of the two numbskull criminals that set the plot in motion. The violence and sexual innuendo are also a factor or two more intense than the actual aired episode. At long last my desire to experience Knight Rider in prose form has been satisfied--and it may be again!
