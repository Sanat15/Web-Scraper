The Magic Circle is based on the Brothers' Grimm fairy tale Hansel &amp; Gretel, but with a twist.  This story is told from the perspective of the child-eating witch, who wasn't always a witch, didn't always eat children, or live a lonely existence in a candy house in the middle of nowhere.  This is a story about a single mother trying to do the best for her child, with an appearance and talents that make the rest of the village uncomfortable.  Unfortunately, life doesn't always turn out as planned... especially if their are demons involved.  Napoli has written a somewhat dark story, but it's beautifully told, if a bit short.
