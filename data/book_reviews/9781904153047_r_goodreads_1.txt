Rating: 4/5

The part of London we see today is just the tip of a huge underground world. Beneath it are lost villages, streets, rivers and fields, not to mention the network of tunnels, cables, pipes and - very importantly - sewers, which make it possible for millions of people to live here.

Divided into 8 chapters, the book touches on the London Tube, the Thames Tunnel and it's construction, the drain network and the interceptory drains made by Bazalgette, old rivers of London, period of London's bombing (The Blitz) during WWII and people hiding underground and in caves, London's graves and crypts, etc.

The book will tell you about the London that you can't see overground. Written partly as a city guide, the author also offers where to walk to trace the underground city and a list of some great museums to visit.
