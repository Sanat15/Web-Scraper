Rating: 3/5

This manga was fairly disappointing. Only half of this volume is part of the actual storyline, the other half being two short chapters of sidestories. While this volume focused mostly on Lita and Mina and their dreams, a big chunk was also about Bunny and Darrien and Rini and Helios. &lt;3 We get a nice backstory on Elysion and who Helios is and how he fits into the greater scheme of things...plus Rini's first kiss!!! And Helios also explains what's happening to Darien's heart. Lita's chapter was full of her dreams to own a bakery and be a wife...also her desire to grow stronger to protect her friends. Mina is upset because she's suppose to be the leader but feels like a failure plus she wants to be a star and worries her time as a Scout puts that dream in jeopardy. Her story ends on a to be continued however. I love all the Scout's new moves! And I do like Naoko Takeuchi finally taking the time to give them some spotlight. It just doesn't seem like enough though this late in the series to learn such insightful things. The Dead Moon Circus and the Amzoness Quartet could have been featured more, as this is only a 4-volume series. Perhaps the next 2 volumes will give them some more screen time.
