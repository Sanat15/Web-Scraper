Rating: 4/5

Lush. Beautiful. Horrific. 

It’s books like this that make me realize how much garbage I read, all the while marveling that a human can put words together in such a way. Creme brûlée after eating Skittles for dessert every night. An aged scotch after nothing but Bud Lite. Of course not everyone likes creme brûlée and Scotch, and not everyone will like The Prince of Tides. 

I’m embarrassed to admit this is the first fiction by Pat Conroy I’ve read. It will not be my last.
