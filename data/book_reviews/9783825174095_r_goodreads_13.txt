Rating: 5/5

This charming collection of little-known folktales is a find for those looking for Eastern/Central European background. The tales include:
"The Twelve Months" from Slovakia (a seasonal twist on the same themes as Diamonds and Toads, the Talking Eggs, etc.-- where being nice to those you meet when set on an impossible task earns you good results)
"Misery" from Poland (similar to devil or brownie tales: Misery lives with an old couple, but the old man is able to lure him away and trap him; the rich neighbor sees how happy and prosperous his neighbor becomes and...
"The Shepherd King" from Serbia, a tale so twisted it probably belongs in Andrew Lang, where shipwrecked but worthy king and his daughter prosper
"The Most Beautiful Flower" from Slovenia, a challenge-to-get-the-king's-daughter with added wise advisor tale
"The Chatterer" from Russia, how a wise man manipulated gossip for his own ends (with the usual badmouthing of women talking, though)
"The Happy Man" from Croatia: the king is dying but won't accept it. His doctors tell him if he can obtain the shirt of a completely happy man...
"The Hundred Children" from Romania, where a childless couple is suddenly overblessed by a wish granting, and have to use a certain amount of tall-tale heroism to gather money to support them.
