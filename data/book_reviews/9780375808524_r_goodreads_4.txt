Rating: 3/5

Just when I was so happy reading the Junie B Jones books with my son he comes home with this-complete with photographs! He's ridiculously proud to have picked out such a gross book. 

The ice mummy was found in the Alps in 1991. In the pictures he looks like an overcooked chicken wing. Seriously, this book is disturbingly gross. Reading it was like watching Fear Factor, I kept flip flopping between gagging and laughing until I couldn't speak. I realize that makes me sound insane but that's how I would get watching people eat maggoty cheese too. My reaction pleased my five year old son to no end and now I'm sure he'll be bringing home lots more books like this to torture me.
