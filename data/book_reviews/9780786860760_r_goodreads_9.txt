Rating: 3/5

I skipped the third book in this series because a lot of Goodreads folks said the series really picks up with the 4th book. I really liked the first two books, but this one was kind of a drag. It took forever to get to the meat of the story, with a ridiculous “mystery” in the first half. Even though we’ve entered the 90s publication era, there’s still a lot of problematic stuff here, anti-Semitic nonsense, n***er tossed in for no reason whatsoever, spic, etc. I really hope Crais grows as a person as the series moves along in time.

For the kids who weren’t around in the 90s there’s also lots fun stuff, like mimeographs, microfiche, card catalogs and cell phones that came attached to your car. It’s totally bizarre to me that 1990 wasn’t ten years ago and there are generations of people who have no clue what these commonplace things were.

There’s also a wholly unnecessary romantic subplot, we get it, the MC is a stud, women can’t help but love his studly ways, that takes up another huge chunk of the book.

Meh.
