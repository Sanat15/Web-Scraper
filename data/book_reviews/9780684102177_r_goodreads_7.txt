Rating: 5/5

This story is based on the author's father, who died the year before I was born. Green paints an excellent portrayal of the experiences of a newly immigrated family who must endure overwhelming poverty and the struggles they faced in order to succeed. His description of late 19th and early 20th century life in New York tenements is superb. However, the development of the main character, Dr. Abelman, is what propels this book to the highest rating. You can't help but love this character, even with all of his tirades, anger and general view of life around him.
