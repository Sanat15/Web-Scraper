Rating: 3/5

An unconventional story about a retired woman who rebels against a boring retirement and against prevailing prejudices against older people and fulfills her dream of finding her own small island. She travels to many countries that are unknown to her and experiences wonderful moments and discovers great places, but also poverty and run-down living conditions. A nice little story that can be read well in one go, which motivates you to be active even in old age and to travel the world if possible.
