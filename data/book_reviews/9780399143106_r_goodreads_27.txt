Rating: 5/5

Beautiful, dark, realistic, gripping. I loved it. Of course I was already in love with the book starting with the quote from Dante's ''Inferno'' at the beginning, and the reference to Dante recurs in the whole book. The evil character is really evil and not a stereotype of evilness. I liked the fact that Nava didn't go for the easy happy ending in Rod's story. Also the analysis of the mechanism of power in Hollywood and in L.A. is brilliant and not delivered as a sermon.

I'm really sorry I still have only one Henry Rios' book to read.
