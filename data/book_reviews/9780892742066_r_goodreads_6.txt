Rating: 4/5

I was reading God's Generals when I stumbled on this much shorter book. I found out that John G. Lake is one of the names that will be discussed in the former book so I read his own accounts first.

All I can say is: this book will encourage a Christian to desire more to walk in the supernatural - miracles, healing, divine provisions and more...

P.S.: I believe in miracles, and in the moments I have doubted their authenticity, may the Almighty help my unbelief!
