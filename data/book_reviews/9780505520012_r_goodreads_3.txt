Rating: 4/5

Thea possesses the ancient power of the Sphere of Light. She’s been given the gift to protect her people from evil conqueror Berezan. She feels inadequate to the task especially when her mentor is killed. She still doesn’t know how the power works, although she can use it to heal. She helps a handsome warrior who has been found collapsed in the frozen tundra of her homeland. Little does she know that he’s the ruler of Borderland, and they’ll need to unite their forces to succeed in her mission. They both withhold truths about themselves at first but cannot deny their attraction. Can they come to terms with their feelings before Berezan’s army overtakes them?
