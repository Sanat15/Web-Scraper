Rating: 5/5

Gary Provost, a seventh son of a seventh son, was known as the "Writer's Writer" for all the advice and help he gave would-be writers. His writing books remain some of the best ever written. I read Beyond Style when it came out in 1988 and it has remained one of my favorite writing books of all time. I still suggest it when I talk at writer's conferences and it isn't even one of the books my company, The Write Thought, publishes under our Classic Wisdom on Writing series.

Baffled in Boston is a breezy who-done-it? and a delight to read on a spring vacation--Big Sur, are you envious?--as i just did.. It was to be the first in the series of humorous mysteries starring Scotty Scotland, a struggling writer. Alas, it was published a few months after Gary's untimely death, and, of course, no more were to follow.

Pick up a copy for the beach.
