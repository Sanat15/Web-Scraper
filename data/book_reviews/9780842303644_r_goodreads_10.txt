Rating: 3/5

Having only read a few books by Grace Livingston Hill, I wasn't sure what to expect since my mom had liked it, but my sister thought it was just okay. I did enjoy it. There were some missing words and commas that bugged me, but I liked the characters and had to keep reading. While the message of Christ was shared, it didn't come until the second half of the book which left the first half almost completely devoid of prayer, or anything Christian.
There is one chapter with several drunk, course men, and one of the main characters in a night club which I didn't really like, but the rest was good.
