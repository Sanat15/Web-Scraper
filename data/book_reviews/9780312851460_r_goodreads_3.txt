Rating: 4/5

The story is imaginative and inviting, with an interesting and compelling narrative that has you practically begging for more. This is high fantasy, but in a much more ethereal way. Instead of grand magic and high adventure, it focuses on intrigue and character.

Our main protagonist is introduced to us still in ‘our’ world, and through newspaper articles she herself has written. As such, we are immediately drawn into the character.

It can also be said the writer has a good knowledge of Native American oral traditions, as well as their heritage, which is subtlety peppered throughout.

The ending itself is admittedly a bit disappointing, but this is still only the first in the series, so it’s not a true ending anyways.

I would recommend this book wholeheartedly to anyone a good fantasy setting with memorable characters.
