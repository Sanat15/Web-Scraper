Rating: 5/5

Loved this book!

Winston spins a tale in this 2nd book that brings heartache, happiness, and real historic problems that evolved in Minnesota even while the Civil War raged elsewhere. I did read book 1 first, and I recommend others do. Since it is based on sweeping historic events, reading chronologically makes sense.
If chronology isn't important to you, this could be a fascinating story, just alone.
No more writing...buying book 3 right now!
