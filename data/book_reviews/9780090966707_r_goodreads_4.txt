Rating: 3/5

This is one of my favorite Edgar Wallace books. It is about horse racing and betting. The beautiful young heiress meets the Scotland yard detective on the ship from South America to England. She is determined to move into the house she has inherited, and the detective is trying to chase down some illegal racing folks. The trainer is working on property leased from the heiress's estate. Other than this coincidence, there are fewer hard to believe coincidences than usual, and the story moves right along.
