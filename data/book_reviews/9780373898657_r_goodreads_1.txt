Rating: 4/5

"The Homeplace" is the story of Cathie and Rob.

A beautiful story set in Iowa, this one is all about having a home. Our h is a young school teacher, who is utterly heartbroken when her family has to sell their ancestral land and house. She is engaged to her childhood sweetheart, and has dreamt of having her children play the land she grew up on. Alas!

Soon a new boy comes to her class, and she realizes that he is the son of the new landowner. Her empathy for him does not shadow the resentment she has for his father.

But when the H begins inviting her into the home she has loved all along, making her question her comfortable relationship, will things change?

A lovely story between a single father and a school teacher. I love single dad tropes, and this was pretty nostalgic. Was there great chemistry in this book? No. In fact, the h spends most of the book denying her feelings. But why four stars? Because I loved the descriptions and the heartfelt longing she had for her land. Because she was so kind with the son Tad. Because the dog Duchess knew where she belonged. And because the H made the sweetest gesture at the end, which also contains a beautiful afterword by the author.

Safe for me
4/5
