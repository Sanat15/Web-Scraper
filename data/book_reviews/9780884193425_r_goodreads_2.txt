Rating: 4/5

Finished this book. This is a practical book about generosity. The more I learn about the "prosperity movement" the more I realize that the reason people react to it is because it has been tained by a fringe who say that God wants to make you excessively rich. Yet the "prosperity movement" that I am familiar with really should be called the "generosity movement" because that is what it is about. And Jack Hayford makes the point that Jesus is the greatest giver because of what He gave us. Great book!
