Rating: 4/5

This book gives a good (if dated) overview of the Voynich Manuscript and the mysteries it represents. I especially liked how much you learn about different kinds of areas (codebreaking, history, alchemy, outsider art) while the authors are trying to approach the manuscript from different angles.

I've encountered the manuscript in different media at different times in my life, and I am always fascinated by it. This was a good and enlightening read.
