Rating: 1/5

I think the look on my face while I zipped through this was a combination of "Huh?," "That is stupid, not scary," and "Someone picked these stories on purpose?" Two of the stories were decent, and I'll be tracking down the original collections they appeared in to see if they're any better. I want to get more spooky story collections for the students, because they can't get enough of elementary-school-level horror, but I will not be getting this one.
