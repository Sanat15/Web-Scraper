Rating: 5/5

Surely every growing child will benefit from this recounting of "The Ugly Duckling." Why do I think so? Because have you ever looked at four-year-olds? They're all weird!

Something that I observe appreciatively.

And speaking of appreciatively, this story flows beautifully, even using preprimer vocabulary. Such is the verbal adroitness and narrative talent of Margaret Hillert.

For an example of her fabulous writing, here's a quote from the low point of the story, when the dejected "duckling" goes away from the other ducks. Picture the tear in his eye, Goodreader, as he says:

"Away I go.
Away, away, away."

Yet again, FIVE STARS of admiration to Margaret Hillert.
