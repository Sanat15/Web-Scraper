I was interested in the essay, "Summoning Somebody - The Flesh Made Word in Toni Morrison's Fiction" by Vanessa Dickerson

I was excited when I saw the title of this essay discusses Toni Morrison's fiction. In particular, the novel "Beloved"and the relationship between Black female bodies and the trauma that they have undergone and the ways that healing must take place in order to alleviate said trauma. 

These essays speak to the "recovering" of Black Female bodies from the neglect, erasure, abuse, reticule, etc have endured and how they go about mending and healing.
