Rating: 3/5

Not a bad book considering it was written in 1928. Was able to overlook how the protagonist said Jesus had blue eyes as did John. Also said Mary Magdalene had blond hair while John's was tawny.
I believe back then most people went along with the depictions of Jesus painted in the middle ages. 
Artists in Europe had absolutely no idea what a Middle Eastern person looked like. I doubt any of them ever went there. 
Their idea of beauty was blond and blue eyed probably because it was rare.
The author also got the method of the assassination of Roman emperor Heliogabalus incorrect. Again, allowing for the time it was written it might have been what was recorded as there was no Google. 
I'd never heard of this emperor and Google had some interesting information. 
The premise is interesting. The protagonist knows Jesus and mocks him for his slave mentality and preaching peace while Jesus is dying on the cross. Jesus tells him, "thou shalt tarry until I return". The protagonist stops aging and begins wandering for hundreds of years. The book is about his adventures many of which were sexual, not all just quite a few. He also acquires a servant named Kotikokura who also seems to be immortal, no explanation of this has been given so far. Fortunately there are no specific details regarding his sexual exploits.
The author does tend to do a lot of nonsensical blabbering, especially at the end. However I'm giving him a pass because of the year it was written.
