Rating: 3/5

Debut Tessier and the weakest of what I’ve read from him so far.

It’s not bad. Just… kind of there.

You’ve got the eerie setup: strange lights over Millville, mutilated livestock, and a town slowly unraveling as everyone projects their own meaning onto whatever’s happening. There’s a solid idea here, almost that creeping, cosmic dread, but it never really builds. Things happen… then more of the same happens. Lights, deaths, unease on repeat.

It coasts like that for most of the book, then suddenly remembers it needs an ending in the last 30 pages. There’s a brief spike, like "oh, here we go" and then it just… fades out.

Frustrating, because the bones are good. The atmosphere is there in flashes. It just never escalates the way you hope it will. 

If you’re already into Tessier, it’s still worth checking out. But this one feels more like a warm-up than a knockout.
