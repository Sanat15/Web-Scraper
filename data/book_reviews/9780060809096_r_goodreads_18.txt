2024 Review - 4 stars
The code breaking still eluded me but I enjoyed the plot much more this re-read. It is a bit bloated, but the dynamic between Lord Peter and Harriet is my favorite. 

2014 Review - 3 stars 
While I totally love Lord Peter Wimsey and Harriet Vane, this book got tedious. It's too long. I skimmed through the code breaking portion because it made my head swim (it was also late at night, not the wisest time to solve a mystery)
A great mystery, though, fun to read and track. I look forward to more!
