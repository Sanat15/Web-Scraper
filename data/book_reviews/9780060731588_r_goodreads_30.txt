Rating: 4/5

A well written but self endorsed book. An amazing story of how an enlisted man moved through the Army ladder to reach 4-Star rank. Franks was not a chair borne soldier, he was in Vietnam as a artillery forward observer, Afghanistan as a commanding General and Iraq as overall commander.

He took the war in Afghanistan and Iraq out of Washington D.C. and kept the second guesser at bay which made more sense. A lesson he learned from Vietnam.
