Rating: 4/5

I'm usually very wary of men writing sapphic characters but this one was really good; their relationship was lifelike without any fetishisation. What I liked best was the women's unwavering devotion to each other. If you can trust your partner to immediately help you deal with the body of a man you just killed, they're the one.

It's described as a 1980s English countryside cosy murder mystery/thriller but that's not accurate because the murder happens only around the 50% mark, being not the point. The subtitle, "A Story of Village Life," is a major understatement, but accurate because a lot of the book is about two women and their life in a village. They run a gift shop and have been together for seven years. They booth at fairs to sell their hand-sewn smocks or homemade cheese/honey. As they live on farmland, there are hilarious incidents like a pig barrelling into their shop, or bullocks breaking into their garden. There is nothing of the city here. 

After the women have a short break, Jan gets accidentally pregnant and gives birth to a son. Jan and Sue very happily raise him together, but then one day, the biological father arrives, says something that can be misconstrued, and Sue unintentionally kills him. They dump in the septic tank, but then the man's best friend shows up to doggedly investigate the disappearance, possibly out of love as well. I'd say that the ending is fair and a lot more compassionate than contemporary murder-thrillers.
