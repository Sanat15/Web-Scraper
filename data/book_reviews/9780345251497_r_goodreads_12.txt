Rating: 4/5

A good read. A mixture of adventure mixed with romance, more emphasis based on adventure. A story of French spies and their activities on the south coast of England. Ley's inspiration probably derived from Robert Fulton's the invention of the nautilus submarine and the flushing out if a proactive spy, although the events in England are fictional (re the Nautilus) as far as I can research. I really enjoyed this story and could never be sure who the antagonist was until the end. The ID of Jackson was also a nice twist. Would recommend.
