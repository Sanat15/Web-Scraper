Rating: 5/5

The story gets a pretty good start and keeps you on the edge. The main character's thoughts are in the first person, making the experience intimate for the reader. He is a good guy, but must risk his life many times over to bring down the criminal organization.

Holden is a post-human with superpowers who was tasked by Lynch with infiltrating and dismantling Tao's criminal organization which employs post-humans. Lynch, the only one who knows of the mission, is now in a coma, so Holden's is naturally worried. The only thing he can do is focus on the mission to prevent being killed by either side.

Tao suspects that he has a spy on the inside. While investigating the matter, Holden's partner Genocide kills one of Tao's generals or Prodigals named Steeleye. Instead of getting killed, the two are promoted and given Steeleye's task list.

Holden's position as a Prodigal gets him close to his target Tao. He also learns who runs the world. Tao's influence and supterfuge creares a danferous rift in the group that he wants to profit from.

A suitcase black hole puts Holden in the same place with a former girlfriend. When she was told that he betrayed the good guys, she wanted to lead the hunt for him. Their encounter risks blowing his cover, but Tao might still be convinced of Holden's loyalty.
