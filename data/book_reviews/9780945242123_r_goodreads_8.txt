Rating: 5/5

First half gives ethical and moral direction to life. Especially, the chapter named 'Duty' changed my whole perception of social responsibilities. Although, fourth chapter is named 'Tolerance', it teaches cheerful acceptance.
Looking at master's way of life, it brings a feel that we should inculcate the same in ours and become like them.

Second half mostly deals with higher aspects of the abhayasa or sahaj marg.
