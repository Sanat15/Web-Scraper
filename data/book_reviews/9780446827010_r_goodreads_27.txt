Rating: 4/5

I strongly suspect if I were to read this again it would pall very quickly. But to a young teenaged boy, this was exciting stuff, especially since, as a young eventually-gay teenaged boy, there was just as much homosexual action as otherwise (or at least so it seemed). 

The movie itself seemed pretty terrible to me, especially after the hardcore sex scenes were added. So much more interesting to read a little bit about the acts than to have to sit and watch. (Not that as saw it as a teenager, I saw it in my 50s, not impressed). But at the time I enjoyed the plentiful novelizations out there, so that I could (a) read the stories about films I hadn't seen, or (b) read the stories about films I had seen and loved, at a time when VCRs didn't exist so much. I probably read the novelization of the 1970s King Kong five times! And I certainly read this racy volume at least twice.

(5* = amazing, terrific book, one of my all-time favourites, 4* = very good book, 3* = good book, but nothing to particularly rave about, 2* = disappointing book, and 1* = awful, just awful. As a statistician I know most books are 3s, but I am biased in my selection and end up mostly with 4s, thank goodness.)
