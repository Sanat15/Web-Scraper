Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed this book because it touches many of my areas of interest: the internment camps, the sierras, Japanese-American bay area history. I feel that Mitchell did a decent job of conveying the complexities of life for his characters and their surroundings. For me, having driven the roads he uses to build his plot, gave reality &amp; depth to his story. Unfortunately, this is not an easy book to find, so if you're interested, you'll have to find it used.
