Rating: 2/5

Maria and David Fenwick arrive in Florida from Cuba where David had been serving with the British military. The British and the Spaniards have just swapped land and the Fenwicks are among the first to settle in the now British colony in the years before the American Revolution. Together they seek to build a life until a tragic accident takes David. Can Maria move on?
 For me, the least favorite of her fiction. I felt this one read more like a biography than historical fiction for some reason.
