Rating: 2/5

Slow, Boring, and Ultimately Unsatisfying

Firstly, the title doesn't even make sense. Our rake, Simon, is in fact very repentant. He dislikes what he is and believes it makes him unfit for "good" people. That doesn't sound unrepentant to me. 

The story centers around an attempted murder-mystery that is just convoluted and unrealistic. When you finally discover who the guilty party is and why he did it (I won't spoil it), you will roll your eyes and wonder why you ever cared to know. 

Like far too many regency novels, this one ends on a silly, down-right ridiculous note. Suddenly, magically, all the characters who should be angry are not...everyone who should raise objections simple doesn't...and so on. I don't care for serious stories that suddenly turn into sitcoms in the last five pages. It smacks of someone not knowing how to wrap things up.
