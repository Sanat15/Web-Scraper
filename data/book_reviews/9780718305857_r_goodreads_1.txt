Rating: 3/5

"Over the years Denys ValBaker has written amusingly and lovingly of his family life in an old Cornish mill house and of his adventures at sea in the family's former MFV Sanu. Now he writes about the children and the special place each child has in his heart; he writes of them as very young ones living in an old cottage inSt.Ives, as teenagers moving to the Old Vicarage at St Hilary and then to a beautiful new home by the creek, The Old Sawmills. Gradually the Val Baker children leave home one by one, only to return home one by one, drawn back to Cornwall and to their family.

"Jess Val Baker has added her own touching tribute to her husband, who died a short time ago, and to their life together."
~~front flap

A charming book, but definitely a memoir of the Val Baker family and the various houses they lived in over the years, and the various children. I suppose my taste runs more to books written about the land -- I would have found that a bit more enjoyable.
