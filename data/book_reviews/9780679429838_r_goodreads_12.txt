Rating: 5/5

Started very strong: here is a guide to a world we all have visited but few truly know. Wavers a bit chapters by chapter but not too much. Then, the last part comes roaring back at you, and you trust this man to explain anything and everything to you about not just flight but human fallibility. 

I have had a (free) copy of this book for years. We interviewed him on The Savvy Traveler. Finally reading this helps me examine (my) life. 

Bonus points for making me feel surer than ever or maybe sure as ever that passengers on airplanes should stfu and plane tickets should be more expensive.
