Rating: 3/5

On the lighter side of Gothic Zebra fare...the first half felt a bit like a cross between something YA and a cozy historical mystery...it really reminded me a lot of the Sunfire Historical Romance series from the late 1980s. So it was probably the unique setting - Altlantic City right before the turn of the century - that provided the most interest for me in the beginning. Our Heroines behavior (I am independent female hear me ROAR) bordered on the ridiculous, especially in the beginning. The second half definitely picked up though, and finally about 2/3 of the way through we made it to the namesake “moontide manor” and some more familiar gothic scenes and elements, including a decent hide and seek chase scene through an unfinished amusement park at night. There were several potential villains and layers of villainous activity (those damn gamblers!), and a couple of red herrings, but no great shock who actual murderer was (but the villain was suitably villainous and creepy in their final showdown with the heroine).

Basically this is no Blackmaddie (Cray Cray in all the best ways possible) or Crystal Shadows (elegantly written and plotted with a great twist)...its more on the “cute” side of things, better for those who want cleaner (PG) simpler stories, or for YA audiences.
