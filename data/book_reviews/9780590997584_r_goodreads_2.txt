Rating: 4/5

I bought this book for my kids through Scholastic Bookclubs. My kids absolutely love it. It is silly and clever. They sing along all the way up through Day 12 and then beg me to sing it again. 

It is exactly what it looks like, a rewrite of The 12 Days of Christmas. I enjoyed the illustrations and thought they were pretty well done. My kids enjoyed seeing the number of cats grow on each page.
