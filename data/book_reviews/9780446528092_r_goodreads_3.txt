Rating: 1/5

One of the more absurd books I've ever read. I decided to read it because I knew people who held it in high regard. Dr. Young is a quack and a complete pseudoscientist. The book reduces all health questions to a matter of PH balance. He believes that are diets are far too acidic, and if we return to diets of vegetables, no meat, and little fruits, we would restore the proper balance. Cancer, almost every sickness, and even HIV come from excessive acid in the bloodstream.

Dr. Young's absurdities abound. He believes in "pleomorphism"—the idea that cells can change forms from bacteria, to healthy cells, to fungus, to cancer cells, or anything in between. They can also conveniently vanish, because the root of all cells is little seeds (I think he calls them "zysmas" or something like that.)

What makes the book persuasive for some people is that it's filled with testimonials. Most of them give only the first name, but the only testimonial with the full name is a woman named Kim Tinkham. She was on Oprah and testified about how she had been healed from cancer by following Dr. Young's diet. The book records the complete story of how the diet changed her life. Ironically, the only testimonial you can actually google (because of the full name) leads you to the truth that she is now dead—only 3 years later.

This book is highly redundant and full of absurdities. There is very little information (if any) that is helpful in any way. Don't waste your time.
