Rating: 4/5

Not many people can be a captain of a ship because it demands that you have a keen mind and a strong will. A ship of the line in the Napoleonic era was a complex system that needed someone to keep it and the crew functioning. Jack Aubrey is such a commander in this book and he overcomes both the enemy within and without as he sails a privateer sloop off the coast of Spain. The saying that war involves long periods of boredom interrupted by intense action describes this book. Some parts about the sailing technology of the day were incomprehensible to me but the battle scenes were great. A privateer was basically a pirate with a license to plunder and share in the plunder as long as it was enemy vessels seized. This was the whole point of the mission of the sloop Sophie as it cruised through this book and will be the purpose of other voyages. I have read some of these books and the later ones get better and better.
