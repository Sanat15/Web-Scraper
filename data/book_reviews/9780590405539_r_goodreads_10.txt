Rating: 4/5

Jacob hates being black. “Everything black is bad.” His mama tries to make things better, but Jacob is angry. His younger brother PeeWee suggests they build a snowman. Made out of slush, the snowman is black. PeeWee finds an old blanket to wrap around its shoulders. But this is the Magic Kente that has once belonged to the African storyteller that uses its magic powers to tell stories. With this Kente, the snowman comes to life and shows Jacob his inner strength and proud heritage.
