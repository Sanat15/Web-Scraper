Rating: 4/5

If you have a more than passing acquaintance with, and great fondness for, both British history and Lord Peter Wimsey, you will find this book to be irresistibly delightful. From the arrival of Roger de Guimsey with William the Conqueror in 1066 to the unknown fate of Viscount St. George in World War II, Scott-Giles weaves the Wimseys throughout many major episodes of English history. Combining his correspondence with Sayers with her own private-press Wimsey stories (originally distributed only to her friends), Scott-Giles creates a highly readable account of the fictional family in their full historical context. Scott-Giles' own expertise in English heraldry and history contributes significantly to the success of this little volume. This is more than fan fiction; it is the product of an attentive reader and a responsive author in close correspondence over many years. The result not only enriches further reading of Sayers' mysteries, but is a rewarding read in its own right.
