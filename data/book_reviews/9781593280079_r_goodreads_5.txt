Rating: 5/5

This book was fabulous. Hearing stories from the Bible in combination with real life testimonies made it really easy to connect specific messages god wanted to convey. The giants of fear, discouragement, anxiety, loneliness, worry, guilt, temptation, etc. are there and have always been for everyone. It was helpful to learn how we should deal with these giants and it definitely gives peace. I would 100% recommend this book to anyone.
