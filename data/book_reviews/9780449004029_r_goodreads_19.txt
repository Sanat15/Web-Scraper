I feel like a lot of parenting books are desperate to reassure parents that they're doing a great job. Um... you don't know me. You don't know that I'm doing a good job. Anyway, I'm here for information, not therapy.

That is not this book. You won't find any of that "Your sweet bundle of joy is blah blah blah" here. Weissbluth is here to give you information, not coddle you. Similarly, you won't find any "Studies show..." When Weissbluth cites a study, he cites a study. Bam. Here's the lead researcher. BAM. Here's their affiliation. B-A-M! Here's the year and also whether other studies support or refute their findings. This is exactly what I want.

Because of the above, it is a dense book. It's not a fast track program to a better night sleep. But there is a ton of useful, practical information backed up by research to help your child get good rest, plus all of the benefits a good rest brings along. I've made changes in my own sleep because of the information in the book. It's not a quick or easy read, but I think it's worthwhile.
