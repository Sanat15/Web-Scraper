Rating: 3/5

This book was decent, but nothing amazing. The Documents in the Case is Dorothy L. Sayers' only novel (co-written with Robert Eustace) that is not part of the Lord Peter Wimsey series. I was mostly a fan of that series, but even those books were hit or miss for me. I loved about half of them, and found some of the others to be mediocre or just meh. It's been I think at least a decade since I read any of her books, and for some reason I had a desire to come back to her. This book is told in an epistolary format, and the tone is very similar to the Wimsey books- humorous and perhaps a bit satirical. The book is pretty short, but it took quite a while for me to get into it- maybe about 1/3 of the way in. I don't regret reading it or anything, but I would say I don't think you're missing much if you're a Wimsey fan and choose to skip it.
