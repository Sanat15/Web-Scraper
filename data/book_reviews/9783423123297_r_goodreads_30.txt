Rating: 3/5

Somewhat different than his later works, but everyone starts somewhere. I appreciate his consistent style. It is true that these stories feel collegiate (as supposably that's when these were written by Boyle), almost poetic in some cases, but having read his later works, that was expected. A few were too fluttery for my taste, but every story was thoughtfully written and had something to laugh at (the Lassie story was uber clever). There were a couple fantastic ones, ones that I digested easier than the rest:

Very nice: 'A Women's Restaurant' (my favorite), 'Green Hell', 'Descent of Man',

Good: 'Bloodfall' , 'Quetzalcoatl Lite' , 'The Champ'
