Rating: 4/5

Very good.
For both newbies to libertarian ideas as well as folks like me who have been in &amp; around the libertarian movement for many, many years.

Great background on what the ideas are all about. Who are the key people. Why the ideas and movement are so important. What the ideas promise for beneficial changes in the world. What vast improvements have already taken place in the world as a result of libertarian ideas already being adopted. How well grounded they are in reality, not some fantasy.

Highly recommended.

I co-hosted with the Federalist Society of Silicon Valley a lunch with David Boaz on 31 March 2015 in Palo Alto, CA. About 50-60 people enjoyed meeting David, meeting others interested in hearing more about libertarian ideas, hearing David's remarks on his book, asking questions and having fun in the newly resurgent move toward more liberty.
