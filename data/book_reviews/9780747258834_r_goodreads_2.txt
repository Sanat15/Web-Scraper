Rating: 4/5

Londoners in 1944 live under the threat of the flying bombs that kill some and traumatise others. The residents of Quay Street strive to make ends meet, while drinking and finding romance when they can. Their speech is rendered with plenty of dropped aitches. In the same city, Nazi sympathisers make plans to overthrow the government. But when the conspirators kidnap a youngster from Quay Street, the local community put aside their differences and stand together. I would have given this book five stars, were it not for the overwhelming number of named characters. I had to keep a page of notes to remind me who was who. Even so, it is a very good tale.
