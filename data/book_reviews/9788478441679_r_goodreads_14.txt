Rating: 4/5

Cees Nooteboom's *Roads to Santiago* is a captivating travelogue that explores Spain’s landscapes, history, and culture through the lens of a curious Dutch writer journeying along ancient pilgrimage routes. 
His eloquent prose, rich with vivid descriptions and philosophical reflections, transforms encounters with villages, cathedrals, and ruins into profound meditations on Spanish identity. 
The book blends personal narrative with witty, insightful commentary, making history feel alive and engaging.
