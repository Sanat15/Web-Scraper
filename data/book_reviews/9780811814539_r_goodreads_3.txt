Rating: 5/5

This is one of the funniest books I've ever read. It is difficult to explain why the po-faced, dry delivery triggers all my laugh buttons, but it does. I've know people with a good sense of humour not "getting" it, so don't feel left out. But I think you should try to "get" it - it "gets" you! And when it does, joy ensues.

Die droë woordeboekerigheid van hierdie woordeboek oor Vader Kersfees is moeilik om te verduidelik. Nie almal snap die humor nie - maar o liewe wêreld, dit WERK vir my! Ure se giggels gewaarborg as dit vir jou ook werk!
