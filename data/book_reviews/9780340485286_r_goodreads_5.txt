Rating: 4/5

I bought my copy in 1989 and returned to it after reading an excerpt in Mariella Frostrup’s anthology of women travellers. Christina Dodwell is a bold and adventurous traveller and writes beautifully of the landscapes and people she encounters. Keyif, her horse, plays a key part in the journey and you are taken with them to a world that has likely disappeared give the recent history of the region. Determined to read more of Christina’s travels.
