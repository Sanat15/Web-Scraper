Rating: 4/5

I found this a really interesting read that had me gripped with suspense for much of it's duration. It's an extremely clever and spine-chilling written book and the disturbing scenes involving the Patron's method of murder is extremely disturbing. However, the final conclusion of the book was disappointing with a number of the issues raised earlier in the text failing to be resolved by the conclusion (such as Nikki's life and the Closer's descent into madness and what they will do after the death of the main villain). As these are not resolved, a potentially exceptional book becomes weakened as a result. A greatly disappointing end to the novel.
