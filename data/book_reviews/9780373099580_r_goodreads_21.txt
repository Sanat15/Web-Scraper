Rating: 3/5

Its a nice book, I liked the murder plot element more than i liked the love story but it was fine. There is cheating, no sex for her during separation but he gets married to OW, there is a HEA.

H cheats but doesnt see it as cheating cause it was just emotional and kissing(its the 90s so kissing doesnt count apparently), the h finds out and puts an ultimatum that he fails so they divorce, the story starts 5 years later. They are both older, he married the OW and she passed away, h finished school and got a job with some life experiences.
They both say that during their marriage they only had sex and fought and the H couldnt talk to her beyond that because she was young and immature and the OW satisfied that part for him, she was to him his intellectual equal.

Both H and h had flaws, the H thought the grass was greener with the OW and when he got there he realised that it was fine, boring and the way I read it bad sex (he was surprised the h got wet already), basically he had a roommate to live with and had boring sex with but he couldnt complain cause he Fed up. The H did not really fight, he was very non confrontational with the women in his life, it feels like he knew he was manipulated by the OW but he couldnt prove it and he resigned himself to a life with her. She died so no one will know what would have happened, but I think after remodeling a house and realising that he stil wants kids to fill it with a period of no sex he would have been gone, but she died so no one will ever know.
The h wanted a perfect love and a guaranty, but sadly life gives no guaranties. I dont mind that she took him back, in 5 years she couldnt move on, she admitted that, it was either stay lonely and single or take him back and get the life she always wanted, so she took him back. In those 5 years she dated and tried, made an effort to move on with other men but couldnt so I dont mind that she didnt get any during that time.
