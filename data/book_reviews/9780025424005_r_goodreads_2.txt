Rating: 4/5

This book had been sitting on my shelf for over thirty years, even though I love books by Ernest K Gann. I guess I just overlooked it somehow.
In my experience, no author has ever described flying quite as good as he has.
Flying Circus is a collection of short chapters, each telling a short tale of the author’s personal experiences in Aviation, along with some background on the individual aircraft involved.
Some are very interesting, some less so. If you find yourself in a chapter that’s a bit dry, just press on and you’ll probably be rewarded in the next one.
Overall, it’s a good read, but no Fate is the Hunter or The High and the Mighty.
