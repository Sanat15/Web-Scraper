Rating: 5/5

When I brought home my Magimix over 35 years ago, a copy of this cookbook became a loving companion that still influences how I work in my kitchen. Aside from preparing salads, doughs, cakes, sauces and more, when my (4) kids were growing up I must have made a fresh batch of mayonnaise recipe nearly every other day. (My son tells me that when their last Nanny finished her year in the States, she was explaining the ropes to the new Nanny and he overheard her explain that in the U.S. they add mayonnaise to everything!) I'm still using the same Magimix, although I've had to replace the blade once and the bowl three times, and I'm still using Norene's recipes❤️ 

Norene Gilletz (May 29, 1940 – February 23, 2020) ❤️
