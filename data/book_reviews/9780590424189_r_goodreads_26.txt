Rating: 4/5

Read this with my kids as a read aloud along with our homeschool history. The kids (6&amp;9)loved it and got really into it. I myself enjoyed it and looked forward to our read aloud time each day, we couldn’t wait to find out if Laura would have a change of heart and if Martin would make it to freedom. It was a great read and matched up with a lot of the other books we have been reading about the Underground Railroad.
