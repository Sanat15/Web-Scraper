Rating: 3/5

I like the Bernie McGruder books by Phyllis Reynolds Naylor. Bernie and his friends Georgene and Weasel are always have to solve a mystery. In this book, the vice president of Higgins Roofing Company has disappeared along with the money. At first, everyone thinks the v.p. was assaulted and kidnapped--until they find the blood in his office was that of a pot roast. Now the whole town is looking for him and Bernie and his friends are sure they can find him.
