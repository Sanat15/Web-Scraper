Rating: 4/5

Absolutely Charming, again.

Another, set in similar times as the Cazalet Chronicles, with such in-depth descriptions of what is surely the mundane, but sweeps the reader along into the story. This is the author’s debut novel, and a sign of things to come with her eye over the day to day, and relationships , whether parental, sibling, friend, or marriage. Starting and finishing with Christmas at The Lancing’s (place), wealthier distant relations, our unnamed MC grows up, tries to find her place in life, falls in love, and realises her dream that she is bigger than the place and circumstances she grew up in.
