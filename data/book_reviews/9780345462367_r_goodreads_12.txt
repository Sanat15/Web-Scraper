Rating: 5/5

There is something about the words written by Donna Boyd that encapsulates pure beauty and finesse.

I read her Devoncroix Dynasty series years ago in my early 20’s and was besotted by her exquisite writing and story telling, quite comparable to the early works of Anne Rice at her best.

I was able to get my hands on The Alchemist recently, and ate it up. There was alchemy &amp; magic, ancient world romanticism, elements of horror… all the good stuff. 
Remininiscent to her Devoncroix series, this story goes back in time and takes you on a journey through ancient times leading up to present day in the perspective of one of three Alchemist main characters.

Ms Boyd’s imagination and ability to build a world enriched with lush and vibrant scenery and romanticism is such a pleasure to get lost within.
