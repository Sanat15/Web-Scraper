Rating: 2/5

Visually overwhelming domestic interiors from inner city London, accompanied by close up details for full impact (a fridge door, a bathroom basin, even a dog) and preceded by black and white exteriors of largely well-known London streets; eg Brick Lane and Portobello Road.

I searched for ideas for our home and came up with nothing, for these interiors are full of furniture I cannot afford and the designers have scant regard for practicality. They look fantastic though, even if they’re not for me, places to visit rather than live in, although I was taken by milk crates being used as movable walls on pp62-65.

PS: The book has Taschen’s usual irritating layout quirk: the captions are lumped at the end of each section, so you have to go through two steps to find out what you are looking at.
