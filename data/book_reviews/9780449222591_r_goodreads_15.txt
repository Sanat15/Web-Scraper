Rating: 4/5

There’s nothing better to me than seeing 'old maid' heroines, who prize morals and proper manners, break those damn rules and surrender to passion. Unfortunately, the book doesn't go into great detail about it, but the little it showed was enough. It has hints of a farce, but it still succeeded in its goal of entertaining me. I laughed so much at Susan and her addiction to chocolates and kisses. I saw people criticizing Harriet for being so attached to her values, but that’s how it was back then—in fact, there are still people like that today, so I don't get why they're so offended. Oh, and I find it fascinating when the hero is obsessed with a specific detail about the heroine; in this case, Robert has a total fixation on Harriet's mouth
