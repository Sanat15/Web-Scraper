Rating: 4/5

Fascinating novel about Henry James. An ambitious novel, written in the style of Henry James (sort of, it's evocative of James but more pleasant and modern) and readers will get out of it as much as they bring to it.

Toibin tackles his subject only in late career; therefore this is not really a biographical work. However, James has always been a mysterious creature, well hidden, and this novel does offer some insight.

Now I look forward to The Magician, which is indeed more of a biographical novel on the life of Thomas Mann.
