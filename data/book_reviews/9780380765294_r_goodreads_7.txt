Rating: 4/5

Enjoyable - Try It!
An interesting story that moves at a fast pace with plenty of action and delightful secondary characters. I loved Guin and how she had turned her life around and sought to help other young girls. Not sure how I felt about Daniel believing his mystery woman was a down on her luck and mistreated gentlewoman, virtuous (ie. not a lightskirt) but still determinedly (almost reprehensibly in the face of her objections) pursues her with the object of making her his mistress. I guess I found it slightly distasteful and disturbing. It was a good thing that you knew she was attracted to him also or it could have been termed abusive.
