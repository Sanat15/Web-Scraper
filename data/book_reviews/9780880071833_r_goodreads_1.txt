I've given up trying to review Brilliant's books alphabetically by title. He didn't write 'em in alphabetical order, and trying to sort them into some sort of chronological order is even more confusing.

So I'm just taking them as they come to my hand--a literal grab bag. This one came up next, when I rummaged in the bag again.

I'm having fun trying to remember why I marked certain pages. Sometimes it seems to have been happenstance: or I was interested in more than one on some pages, perhaps. Plus what I used for bookmarks has sometimes more than passing interest, as well.
