Rating: 2/5

Unfortunately nothing new here. I had hopes, it started well Lady Mary and Jack forming a friendship. But it all goes downhill when he finds Mary will suit his requirement for a wife and she naively thinks yayy, he likes me for myself. Of course he falls in love with her and she finds out the real reason he had asked for her hand. This is all very by the book but it went off the rails for me.  she is very upset about him wanting her for her money and runs away (I had no sympathy for her as she decided to wed him after a couple hours deliberation. Anyone with a grain of common sense would have done a little digging and thinking. Then she ignores the last couple months of his happy company and wonderful treatment of her. Since she's run away without talking to Jack a couple days before their wedding he is incredibly hurt and runs to london where he sleeps with everything in sight. What???? And he supposedly loved Mary. Maybe this was done back then but I don't want to read it in my romance novel where I'm escaping from reality!! Then he heads off to find Mary and Love conquers all.
