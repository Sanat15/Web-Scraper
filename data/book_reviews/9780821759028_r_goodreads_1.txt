Rating: 3/5

A day late and a dollar short with this month’s TBR Challenge, one after my own heart: “Favorite Trope.” A favorite trope of mine is a Regency from the good old days, and an always enjoyable subset, a British aristo falling for a forthright America. Lord Montjoy’s Country Inn was published in 1998, not a Signet but a Zebra Regency Romance. I liked the colorful cast of characters and how much they all had to learn about each other. The earl turned reluctant innkeeper was delightful but his American cousin (by marriage) was sometimes tiresome. Samantha Edwards was not a wealthy American heiress but she had a wealth of energy and ideas. In particular, fill the earl's castle with wealthy Cits and soak them for enough money to get the estate on a solid financial footing. Clare's description of Cits was all over the map, sometimes sympathetic, sometimes unnecessarily insulting. 

Her bobcat was quite tiresome. I definitely could have lived without it. Also, an earl called Kevin? Really… 

A fun quick read but don’t spend more than I did at a UBS ($1.50).
