Rating: 2/5

This feels like a series shaping up to be primarily about Rhodry (just as the last one was about Jill). It feels like there's more at stake in this series - i.e. we're finally seeing the fate of Elidd hinted at in the previous quartet. It's nice to see Perryn and his family make a reappearance, although Nedd's death felt unnecessary. 

The main difficulty is that the cast of characters is getting huge enough that I can't quite remember who they are any more (especially the significance of their various incarnations).
