Rating: 4/5

This book is written in simple but effective words. I have never thought to inspect my relationships through a boundary-focused lens, so I feel that I gained insight into my life as well as further affirmation (that I didn’t know I needed) that it really is good and healthy to listen to my gut. I also realized that there are areas in my life where I can do a better job adhering to the boundaries of others, and I’m especially grateful that I read this in my pre-kid days so I can keep this in mind when my time comes to raise some young’uns! I have always tried to be conscientious of people’s boundaries because I’ve historically struggled to hold my own, but this book really helps me to pinpoint boundaries in more contexts beyond trying to figure out how to politely exit a conversation, for instance, so I do feel better equipped to recognize limits of others. There is also valuable content in here for improving &amp; honoring the boundaries in one’s marriage!
