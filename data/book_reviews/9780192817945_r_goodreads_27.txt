Rating: 5/5

5.0 Stars. First, I’ll say from the start that Dickens has emerged as one of my all time favorite writers. This is only my 5th Dickens book, so I have a long way to go still, but I have enjoyed everything I’ve read so far, and have many of his most popular works still ahead of me. I love Dickens voice and humor and I’m definitely in my happy place reading his novels, even though they are nearly all really long books that take me a while to get thru (this novel was 843 pages). 

As is usual, the protagonist character and his loved ones are very poor when the book starts. The book is about their struggles against poverty and the many unscrupulous villains who all try to harm the various family members with ill intended schemes. But as is usual for Dickens, good wins out and the villains get their just deserts. One great thing about Dickens is that he never leaves characters hanging. Everyone is awarded according to their merit as people. 

My favorite characters in this book are Nicholas (of course), Mrs Nickleby (the comic relief of this novel), the Cheeryble brothers, Tim Linkenwater, and poor good Smike. Nicholas himself is among my favorite Dickens protagonists so far. 

Well worth reading. And after 2 months together, I shall miss these characters.
