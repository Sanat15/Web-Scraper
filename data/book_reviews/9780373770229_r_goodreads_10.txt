Rating: 4/5

You'd think that after reading 50 or so Diana Palmers, I'd be used to her awesome - well- versed formula..
Apparently not! This book had all the classic elements..
Hairy sheikh (not a cowboy this time) with a tortured past
A virgin but plain looking heroine who is strong yet innocent 
And some glitches before their deserved HEA. He does f up in a traditional way too- but what surprised me was the accurate Arab descriptions, the humor and the amount of sex in it!
Totally loved it(even with the minor OW, again classic scenes)
Safe-ish read
4/5
