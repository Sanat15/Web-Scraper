Rating: 5/5

I really enjoyed this book. I like listening to Alan Young on the radio in his many roles and this book tells a lot of the story behind that as well as his on screen roles. The main focus of the book is about Mr. Ed, but it really tells the story of Alan Young. He pays reverence to Mr. Ed in a way that you really understand that he and Mr. Ed were truly friends.
 This book more than tells a factual story; it shows the character of the people involved. Mr. Ed had character as well; he was just not a prop or animal. Lots of pictures adorned the book; I really enjoyed them. My only criticism is that Mr. Ed's pictures were throughout the book. The other pictures were in the middle. I would have liked the pictures germane to the chapter to be there and all of the other wonderful photos in the middle. 
It is a great book and feels friendly.
