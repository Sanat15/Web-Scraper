Rating: 4/5

The 3rd installment of Cameron Judd's historical fiction series - Tennesse Frontier Trilogy. An excellent series that was both entertaining and educational. Educational in the sense that historical events in the pre-state era of eastern Tennessee were depicted through the fictional eyes or the protagonist, Joshua Colter. I knew very little about this era of US history (post Revolutionary War and pre Tennessee statehood) but learned many details of the frontier life, church/community activities, battles with both the British militia and the native Indians of the region. Totally absorbing reading and made me yearn to learn more actual history from this era and this geographical area.
