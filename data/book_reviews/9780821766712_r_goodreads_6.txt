Rating: 4/5

Lord Nightingale is not a lord nor a nightingale. He is a parrot/macaw who has been in the family for decades.
This is a charming regency tale in the style of Georgette Heyer. The characters are clever and endearing. Plus you have family pets help in the action. This is a clean tale about an un-polished earl who thinks he is a frog and the girl with the overactive immagination who falls for him. Throw in a scarlet macaw, an over-sized puppy, a kitten, a clumsy horse named Grace and a bunch of other family, friends and foes and you have a rollicking tale of romance and a comedy of errors. Sadly this book is not available on Kindle and is probably gone from most libraries. But this series is fun, clean, and quite humerous. I recommend it to regency enthusiasts and animal lovers especially if you like old fashioned, innocent, clean romances.
