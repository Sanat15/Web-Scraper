Rating: 4/5

The actual rating is probably closer to a 4.58 star rating, since I had a few slight problems with the story. Overall, I thoroughly enjoyed reading this book, despite how long it took me to read. It took a bit of time to get into the book, and I'm glad that I read through the book and didn't put it down like I might have. 
So like most 'children's books' that I've read, the two main characters, John and Philippa, where more mature for their age, but I think that aspect fit in nicely with the rest of the story. Also, I loved how well thought out the world was (basically, the world building was fabulous). The plot was well-thought-out, but the scene at the end with the twins and Groanin flying to the North Pole and conveniently finding a guide and all the right flights in what seemed like a very short amount of time seemed a bit too convenient.As did the 'trapping Akhenaten' scene. Maybe I'm just used to death and mayhem and expected more death and mayhem. That's a morbid thought. I will be picking up the other books in the series, but probably not that soon, with school about to start and everything.
