Rating: 4/5

This book is just page after wordless page of simple black and white cartoon drawings, with the only splash of color coming form the vibrant yellow the yolks of the eggs that make an appearance on every page in all kinds of scenarios and settings ranging fro the strange to the totally mundane (but with egg). It's so hyper-specific in a way that's both weird but also exudes confidence in what it's doing. I don't understand it, but I still love its bizarreness, and I will continue to faithfully hold onto the copy I found in a used bookstore years and years ago.
