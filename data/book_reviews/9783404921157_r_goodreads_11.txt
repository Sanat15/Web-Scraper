I read the Color Purple a long time ago and thought I should dip back into some Classic US authors. This set of short stories was compelling and charming. Because so many are autobiographical, I ended up doing a deep dive into Alice Walker and was fascinated. The stories reflect compassionate views of an older person looking back on their life. There’s a lot of sexual openness even within marriage and a causality about affairs that I didn’t find compelling, but I took them to be her own experience and part of fiction is to try to understand people who are different. I appreciated the complexity around interracial couples, especially in the mid-20th century, and interracial love and friendship in general.
