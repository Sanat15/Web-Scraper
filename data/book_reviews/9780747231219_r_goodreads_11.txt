Rating: 2/5

This felt a little more phoned in than the Brother Cadfael mysteries I've read, somehow. I never got as invested in the story or characters (though the Jantar Mantar scene was fun). I was also confused and annoyed that Tossa became a non-entity as she ceded all agency to Dominic, but I realized later that this is part of a Felse family series. 

I found this on the library's giveaway shelf after we had come back from India, and many of the things Peters wrote about 1970s India still felt familiar. The women were wearing saris but the men more western clothes. Basically all their interactions were with light-skinned men, except for when they were beset by urchins. Delhi itself sounded much smaller. The embassy area was new and on the outskirts. There were more bicycle rickshaws than motor-rickshaws (maybe they'll go back with the new oil crisis!).
