Rating: 4/5

A delightful foray into literature from my childhood after my son found this copy in a local charity shop. Mrs Pepperpot (‘Teskjekkinga’ - The a teaspoon Lady in Alf Prøysen’s Norwegian original but more lyrical in English as Mrs Pepperpot). As find as I remembered, the audacious Mrs Pepperpot uses her cunning and wisdom to get herself out of all sorts of scrapes that the inconvenience of randomly shrinking to the size of a teaspoon lands her in.

Each chapter a self contained story, fun bedtime reading or independent reading for those beginning on chapter books. It is a book of its time so expect to enjoy the quirks of late 20th century Scandinavian life along the way.
