Rating: 5/5

William Mann writes carefully researched but colorfully rendered histories and biographies of Hollywood. In this volume be recounts the 20th. century careers of those before and behind the camera. Some played the closeted charade demanded by the studios. Others refused to play that game, living with same sex partners to the handicap and detriment of their careers. A fan of Turner Classic?
