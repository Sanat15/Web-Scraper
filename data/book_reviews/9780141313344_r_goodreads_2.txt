Rating: 2/5

Read for year eight english, semester one 

I probably should have enjoyed this book more than I did, being of Italian heritage and all. It was definitely a quick and entertaining read, with a fantastic message and heartwarming ending. However, when you analyze a book in depth for too long (when there really isnt a lot to analyze) the novelty wares of, and you start to hate a book. 
I will need to re-read this, one day.
