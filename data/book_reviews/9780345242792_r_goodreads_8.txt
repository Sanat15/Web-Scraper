Rating: 1/5

So didn’t know this book was a sequel but that didn’t matter much. Writing style is childish and the plot extremely simple. Would it be a film, I would laugh at all the illogical stuff that went on. Also the “what will it be? o the clue is obvious but no, I won’t ask or say it” chapters made me laugh.

I did read into the writers and their lives, much respect for what they achieved. Missed them both a decade, pity.
