Rating: 4/5

Almost a Major American Writer

I first read some of Shaw's short stories in the New Yorker when I was an adolescent. He was one the Jew Wave of postwar writers. Mailer, Roth, Salinger, Malamud. I hope he made some serious money from "Rich Man, Poor Man." It was one of the first Network Mini Series, and was quite the thing in the seventies. I regularly taught "The 80 Yard Run" in Freshman Comp, and perhapa a few dollars of royalties came his way.
He never got over his ranking. The few who reas are still mad at Roth, still, unaware, follow Mailer, and a few might consider Malamud.
But nobody understands or remembers Shaw. His fate was to be a QB on a minor College football team, where such passing as was done was by the Tailback.
He played as he wrote, well, useful, a part of the now receding 20th century Jewish writers.
