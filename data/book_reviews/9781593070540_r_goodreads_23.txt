Rating: 4/5

I can't make up my mind how successful this is as an adaptation and whether my slight feeling of nodding along is just familiarity with the source material. Certain scenes have a lot of power, the opening in particular but also the photographs and the discovery of the body - pacing is commensurate with the movie and as a manga a lot of it is fairly standard sitting and talking that lacks visual flair - whereas Nakata's movie uses the unfolding narrative to really build tension through superior tension, that aspect is slightly missing here. Nevertheless it still feels like a take on the material that has its own style and ... it's Ring ... it still has power to suck you into its orbit.
