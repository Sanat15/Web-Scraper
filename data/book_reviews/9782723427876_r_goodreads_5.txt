Rating: 3/5

After reading the Sailor Venus prequels, it’s a huge let down knowing how she’s used in the Sailor Moon manga. In her standalone, she’s an incredibly strong guardian with so many tools and moves at her disposal. It makes no sense that she’s a background character and has 1 move in Sailor Moon. Building her up as the leader and then making her weak in Sailor Moon is annoying. 

I did have some issues with the content in her 2 books too. I know it was the 90’s, but the following content is harmful for young readers: excessive fat shaming, normalizing relationships with older men, making a joke out of a man lifting up Sailor V’s skirt (she’s 13), Sailor V asking an adult ‘man’ to take all of her for a full body check while she’s in a bra- I’m sure there’s more, but these were the most jarring to me.
