Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars. there were some fun ones in here, and I'm always impressed by just how spooky the ghosts in these old stories can be. the fair folk stories in this were great as well. faves were 'The Child that went with the Fairies' (what it says on the tin), 'The Sexton's Adventure' (part of the larger story 'Ghost Stories of Chapelizod'; about a sexton prone to drinking and a man who indulges in the same vice - the ghost in this one is particularly great), and 'Sir Dominicks Bargain: A Legend of Dunoran' (a classic 'deal with the devil' story).
