Rating: 5/5

Fascinating reading on auras and healing

I read this book to understand energy healing, how that works and why. This book was a deep detailed description with examples, illustrations, and a guide on how to become a healer. The author presented many thought provoking concepts that are helpful to anyone who wants to release negativity and grow to a stronger self. I think the book is more of a launch for those who want to become an energy healer. The path to becoming an energy healer is a much more complex process than just reading a book.
