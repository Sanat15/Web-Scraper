Rating: 5/5

I absolutely loved this book. I first read a part of it (like the first three chapters) when I borrowed it from a stranger to pass time at a class and then it took me three more years to finally track a copy down and read it cover to cover all in one go. A great book for players of Dungeons &amp; Dragons who ever wondered what it would be like to be transformed into their characters into a fantasy world- dark, dangerous and with no traditional health care.
