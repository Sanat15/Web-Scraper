Useful story only if you want to learn the slang of the early 19th century:

tie-mates (male friends)
a convenient/prime article (mistress)
hurricane (packed reception)
cork-brained air dreamer (a fool)
addle-cove "
caper-witted, want-witted (foolish)
nanny shop (brothel)
tarradiddle (lies)
grassville (rural area)

But the heroine is so undomestic or brainless that she can't figure out how to wield a broom (it's not a Dyson Supervac 10, lady) and the hero is a standard issue chauvinistic toff who plans to shuttle between mistress and wife while the less said about the hole-filled plot the better.
