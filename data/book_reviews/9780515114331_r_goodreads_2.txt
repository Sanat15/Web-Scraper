Rating: 3/5

this was a fast read it wasnt a carzy off the wall kinda book. more of a kidnaped in the woods kinda book. alex, a cop and his wife, jenny move to Virgina from California to get away from the city life and live in the country with fresh air, trees and privacy. they never imagined that this small town they wanted so badly to get away to would the reason they would never want to see the woods again. not long after they move in to their new country home and alex settled in to his position at the county jail as a officer, he gos missing. in plain day the empty cop car was found with one in site and no witness along the long abandoned road. not happy with how the chief is handling things jenny starts her long journey to find the truth and hopefully alex along with it. as unsafe as the city life has proven to be the country is out right deadly.
