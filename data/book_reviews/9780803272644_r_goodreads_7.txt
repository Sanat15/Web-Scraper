Rating: 3/5

3.5 ⭐️ Because John Colter did not document any of his travels, this book depends on the documentation of those who traveled with Colter or, more often, what people were told by Colter. So, the book is a lot of speculation. It is more about about William Clark, George Drouillard, and Manuel Lewis than Colter. Still, I enjoyed reading about these early 1800s trapping, navigational discovery, and harsh life in the mountains tales. It is quite dry in places, but at under 160 pages it isn’t hard to stick with for the good stuff.
