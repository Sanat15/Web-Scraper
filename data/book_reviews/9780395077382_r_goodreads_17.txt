Rating: 2/5

Horribly dated now with as one reviewer pointed out, it's frankly abhorrent portrayal of First Nations.
Written in the mid 50's, it's to be commended for it's independent, stalwart female character, Hannah Fowler who beset by no end of frontier challengers, handles them all with fortitude and aplomb so 5 stars for that aspect of this novel. Giles sometimes hints at a momentary concern that the settlers have for their actions settling a land that is not theirs and in their behavior and attitudes to the Shawnee people but generally dismisses any brief thoughts of conscience pretty promptly.
