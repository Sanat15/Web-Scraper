Rating: 4/5

The book is well written. It is a Western love story and is a sad tale. Jay Grobart is an ex-soldier who married an Indian squaw. He had two children by her and went to jail for killing the three men that supposedly had sex with her. His wife died. He returns after his sentence is over and goes about recovering his children. The story is told from the perspective of another woman that falls in love with him. It is not a happy story. I will end the review here to avoid revealing too much.
