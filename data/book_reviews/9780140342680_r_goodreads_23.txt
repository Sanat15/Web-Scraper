* No is Yes (OH MY GOODNESS, MY FAVOURITE PAUL JENNINGS STORY OF ALL TIME. Not kidding here. This one stuck with me so much and STILL trips me out so completely at the end!)

Okay, to get out of my brackets. Basically, this girl has been kept locked away by her father, who has not let her speak to anyone else - she's like 14 or so I think? It's been a while. And he's gone and taught her different words for things... tree is lamppost, salt is sugar, coffee is tea, father is mother, no is yes. It's twisted. Anyway, right at the end, there's a fire in her house and the fireman asks if anyone else is inside, is her father inside? And she just looks at him and answers "no" and I EXPLODE FROM THE AWESOME. Because no means YES, but father means mother, and her mother is long dead... aaahhh. It's incredible.
