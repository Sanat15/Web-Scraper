Rating: 3/5

This was an interesting start, with a potential murder mystery set-up, before it veered off into a bit of a confusing plot line. I did enjoy the references to the time period it is set in and also the humour. Dougall and Amanda were quite interesting characters, before the twists made it a little unbelievable towards the end, although some of it was fairly obviously going to happen. I would be interested in reading more by the author.
