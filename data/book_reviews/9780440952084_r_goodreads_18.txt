Rating: 3/5

Did you know it is possible to write a tale with an endearing, magical, eye-covered seraphim? Or that mammoths, shape-shifting angels, and teleporting unicorns could belong together in a retelling of Noah’s Ark? Madeleine L’Engle’s creativity is inspiring. When reading her, I often find myself asking whether I agree with her; but she gives me things to think about, and imaginative stories to muse over.
