Rating: 3/5

Just finished reading this one and I have to say it was not bad.

In the first part of the story we find out that Buffy's mum has passed away and Buffy is trying to deal with it all now that the distractions of planning a funeral are over. However in the mist of all this, a grieving mother is seeking revenge on the slayer for killing her daughter (flash back to "Autumnal", Verena the voodoo girl), and has concocted quite the hair brain scheme. 

In the second part of the story emotions are all running hay wire as accusations of jealousy and theft are thrown back and forth between team Xander / Anya and team Willow / Tara. Three little Demons who have been compared to gremlins in personality are on the loose and feeding off of all the negative emotions. Top that all off with a dose of Spike just trying to help the slayer and getting nothing but grief back for his efforts and you are in the middle of a Buffy episode.

I would recommend this one to anyone who enjoyed the Buffy TV show.
