Rating: 3/5

This is more swordsmen politics, about leading the tryst. We finally get an explanation: after Wallie's exploits (such as crawling in the mud), it would be difficult for him to lead. Shonsu also led a doomed expedition against the sorcerers. Wallie figures out that Nnanji is to be the leader of the Empire. He resists this at first, but then learns to accept it.

Wallie also figures out that the sorcerers were previously scribes, and their "magic" is actually technology / sleight of the hand / lip reading / use of carrier pigeons. He brokers a peace with the swordsmen. We end with him settling down with Jja, who has (by miracle) become a seamstress.

I liked the messenger's explanation of the other paths that could have been taken / the alternative kings to Nnanji as well.
