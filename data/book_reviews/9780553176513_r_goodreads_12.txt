Rating: 5/5

I had a bunch of Saddle Club books as a kid. I think possibly #1, #3, and #4, because I don't remember the contents of this at all - I do remember that Cobalt died, but I probably gleaned that from book 3. Anyway, it was lovely to fill in the blank, even if the story was a bit tragic, but no one can argue that this doesn't teach kids good wholesome life lessons, and is pretty realistic about horse life.
