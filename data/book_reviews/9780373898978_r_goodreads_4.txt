Rating: 5/5

Janet Dailey is an outstanding writer. Sparks fly between the man and woman. She writes so well that it is as if you are there in the room with them. 

The man and woman have been divorced for 5 years and they have a son together. He now lives in another state and their son visits him in the school holidays. The man and woman haven’t seen each other in 5 years. The man has now come back to tell her and his son that he is going to be married to another woman. Years ago both man and woman have agreed to move on and that’s what he is wanting to do. Until after all those years he sees her again of course. 

This could well have been a horrible book, but the excellent writer that Janet Dailey is, makes it believable that the man still loves the woman, but has separated from her because they always fight and just can’t get along. 

This book has romance, passion, chemistry. Some scenes are even funny. The dialogue between the man and woman is so good. I love this book and I will keep it.
