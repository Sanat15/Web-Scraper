Rating: 3/5

Loved the short stories, recognised the areas of Ireland he talked about.. it was so observant of human nature.. witty and poignant... his understanding as an adult about his parent's strained relationship as well as so many people in general. It's an unusual way of writing but I enjoyed it and I could absolutely see the characters he was portraying.. timeless; and, because of that, informative in a rather frank way, that life doesnt change whenever we were born, humans will be humans.
