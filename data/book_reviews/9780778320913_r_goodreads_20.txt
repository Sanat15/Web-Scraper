Rating: 5/5

I really loved this series and the people living in Valentine,OK and I hope its not done yet. Ms. Matlock's characters are so real, and her stories just flows with life in the many situations, and humorous outcome of each and everyone connected in the little town of Valentine. You won't want it to end, and it made me wish I lived there. If you haven't read Curtiss's books, then your missing out!
