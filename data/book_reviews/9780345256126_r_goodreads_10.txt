Rating: 4/5

Similar to Letters For A Spy I didn't find anything unique in Alice Chetwynd Ley's 'Tenant of Chesdene Manor' but the author has a certain something (more 'Heyeresque' than most perhaps) when it comes to evocative atmosphere. 

I preferred this novel to 'Letters for a Spy' because its heroine, Diana Chalfont, is considerably more spirited and memorable, and likewise the serious-minded, secretive hero, 'Mr. Richmond', leaves an impression. 

Again, the author's whodunit doesn't take a Hercule Poirot to unravel (actually I think Captain Hastings could have managed this one!), but it's a pleasant enough 'cosy mystery'.

In summary - the best I've read so far from the author, and I'm rating it a three-and-three quarter star vintage regency romance read.
