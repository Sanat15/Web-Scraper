Rating: 5/5

*Happy Sigh*. I love these books. I will say it over and over again. And more clearly, I love Jace. I adore Jace. I would like to marry Jace. Could that please happen??
But yes. I thoroughly enjoyed reading "Under Eastern Stars" for the second time. I think the first book in this series, "Silk" will remain my favourite, but I still adore this one. Though sometimes I wanted to kind of...kindly shout at Coral. I guess maybe I am more over-cautious than she is...but sometimes it just felt like she really needed to think just a tiny bit more. XD

Still though, I think she's a good character and love her and Jace. &lt;3

As I said in my review of "Silk" I can "properly" review these because I hold such fondness for them.
