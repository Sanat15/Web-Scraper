Rating: 5/5

This book gets a 5-star rating from me as it was a timely reminder that the Lord, my God, in whom I put my trust is amazing (:

Received it as a gift decades ago, when my best buddy died, but only reading it now, after my father has gone Home. The thoughts on (certain specific days) were spot on, and so meaningful. The Lord truly reaches out to meet us at our point of need, even going so far as to set things up 20 years ahead of time, before we even have a clue we'd have (that particular need).
