Rating: 2/5

The first book in the Sector General series, Hospital Station, was basically a handful of short stories strung together with very little narrative thread. 

Star Surgeon is also episodic, but with a somewhat stronger narrative throughline. Some parts of it are OK, but overall it's quite weak. The ending felt particularly rushed.

I know it makes no sense, but as Conway &amp; co work undercover on the alien planet Etla I could not stop thinking about SovietWomble's clips from Arma 3 where you play as undercover freedom fighters of the island of Altis.

Not sure I will read the rest of the books in the series. Also not sure if this deserves to be labeled science fiction or not.
