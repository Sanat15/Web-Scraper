Rating: 5/5

I find books that detail the writing life and speak to an author’s process fascinating, and this book did both well. Diary of a Novel is an absorbing read detailing one author’s journey through researching and writing the rough draft of a novel. I enjoyed learning about Ms. Price’s process for cresting her historical fiction novels even though I don’t write historical fiction and have never read one of her books. I liked the pop culture references to the time, seeing her opinions on current events and hearing about her many friends. I could easily picture and relate to the concept of feeling as if you are straddling to worlds (in her case, two centuries), trying to keep a foot in each. Most of all, Ms. Price had me longing to take take a retreat to St. Augustine myself, and to visit St. Simon’s Island where she worked.
