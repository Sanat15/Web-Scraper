Rating: 4/5

Summary:
 This book is a A-Z rhyming book for kindergarten children. In the book it matches names to rhyming words. 

Evaluation:
 Honestly, I feel as if some of the rhyming words are not your typical normal rhyming words. They are t words you would hear in kindergarten or 1st grade. 

Teaching idea:
 I would use everyone’s name and see if I could come up with a rhyming word to match. Then I would have them write it down and say it. Maybe even have a generic song that we could all sing and put out name in.
