Rating: 2/5

I want to be fair to the illustrator because the drawings are beautiful. The story on the other hand is rather a rip off from Snow White and Sleeping Beauty. Her mother's thoughts of having a daughter is identical to Snow White and the plot of the story is that of Sleeping Beauty. I just kept thinking that it was rather odd that she had to travel walking half across the world to find her blonde prince THAT SHE HAD TO SAVE because God forbid you find a blonde in Iberia, ridiculous.
