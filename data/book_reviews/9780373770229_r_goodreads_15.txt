Rating: 1/5

Didn’t work out for me. DP was rather lose in her writing of this one. The potency of the relationship between the h and H was missing. Also my utter absolute Uber pet peeve: easy, needy h. Gretchen is barely 23, in a foreign African/Muslim/Jewish/Christian land (yeah DP tried but failed to make it sound neautral). Her friend who was supposed to be with her had to leave in an urgency and now Gretchen is by herself in a place where she can’t even read/ speak the language. 

And yet: Gretchen is easy talking, probing, incessantly asking unpleasant questions of a seemingly wealthy stranger. Despite her qualms on being a prude and only to be sleeping with her husband, she was extremely available to hang out and chat with this stranger. She had absolute no reservations or bashfulness and I could not connect with her at all. The H was alright but if I were him, I’d run in the other direction from Gretchen and her questions and lack of humor. Fail.
