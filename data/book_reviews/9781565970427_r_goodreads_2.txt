Rating: 5/5

When Slater McGuire received a call from a friend asking for his help finding a kidnapped child, he could not refuse to help. A security expert, he knew he was out of his league but he had to try. He never imagined he'd wind up teaming with the leading authority on finding kidnapped children, J.M. Clarebridge... Joy. Or that he'd fall head over heels for this secretive, emotionally withdrawn lady whose darkest memories had made her retreat from the world &amp; live in the darkness. He is determined to find out her secrets &amp; draw her back into the land of the living. What follows is a harrowing tale of intrigue, danger, loss &amp; ultimately love.

You won't be able to put this book down. I've read it at least 100 times &amp; it still moves me. Sometimes to tears, sometimes to laughter. This is definitely a must have for your book collection. It has drama, suspense, comedy &amp; romance. It's well written &amp; so engaging you will be drawn into this world &amp; come to feel like you are a part of it. You'll come to love these characters &amp; want to read their story over and over again. If you read this book, you won't regret it!
