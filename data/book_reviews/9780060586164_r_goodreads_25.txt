I don’t know how Elmore Leonard does it. I don’t normally like short stories but I devoured this whole collection. It even had western stories, something I’m not really into, but I even loved those! The eponymous short story is what got me to read this because it’s the first appearance of Raylan Givens, the main character in Justified. That short story was almost captured exactly in the pilot episode of the series. Still, even as good of a job as they did with that, the short story is still stellar. The real gem of the whole collection is the final one, Tenkiller, which makes me wish I could make it into its own movie or series. Absolutely amazing collection.

One correction: Fire in the Hole isn’t the first appearance of Raylan, the book Pronto was. My bad.
