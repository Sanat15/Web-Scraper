Rating: 3/5

I saw the movie to this novel many years ago. It was a tearjerker movie that really was touching and emotional. I read the book and I wished I hadn't bothered with it. It was a good story, but it was aaaallll over the place for me. Going back and forth between past and present, hallucinations in the present, talking to the main character's lost love in her dreams, to her kids having conversations in the present, etc. I was able to follow along, but it took some getting used to. Not my favorite writing style. Too much going on. The story itself was great though. The movie was 5 stars, the novel is 3 stars for me.
