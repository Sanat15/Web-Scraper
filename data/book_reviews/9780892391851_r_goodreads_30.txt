Rating: 3/5

I picked this book up because the illustrations were so bold and vibrant and the title seemed kind of silly. Then, I was pleasantly surprised when this book took me on a journey to learn more about parts of the world that are different from my own through curiosity and connection. I think it would be a great book for any child in a remote place who is fascinated by things or people they may never encounter in their own communities. It helps show how to be open to embracing new things and not scared of differences.
