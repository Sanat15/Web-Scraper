Rating: 5/5

This last volume took me by surprise and you can see Minako change at the end. She accepts her duties and decides to seek out her other comrades. I must say Kaitou Ace was very much obsessed with her, even if he did love her, he had a twisted way of showing it. I loved the series because it gave me an insight on who Minako was before encountering the rest of the senshi. As you follow her story you see how they always in some way or form ran into each other or were nearby. I really loved this last volume, I only wish that even as short as it is that it would have been turned into an anime or OVA. Other longer series have been condensed to one season, so maybe this one as short as it is may one day be turned into one.
