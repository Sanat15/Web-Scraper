Rating: 5/5

Clean Regency/Georgian Romance

A very sweet story with a wonderful hero in Pam - a rare character who is gentle in all the ways that matter. I absolutely adored him. Katie, the heroine, is an impetuous young lady who keeps landing in trouble from which she's rescued aided by her own wits and of course Pam. 

The author's thorough research of the period lends credibility as she blends in places and persons from history into the telling. 

If you like Georgette Heyer then the conversations in this quick read will enchant you.
