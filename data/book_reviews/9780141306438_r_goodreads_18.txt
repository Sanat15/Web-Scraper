Rating: 4/5

It's a very realistic portrayal of a Puerto Rican family living in New York as seen through the eyes of Felita. Her parents are both Puerto Rican but her mother is dark like a black person and her dad is very light like a 'gringo'. Felita has two older brothers. One looks like her mother, the other brother looks like her father and she is a mix of both of her parents.

 Some of the things she experiences are very mean and sad. It is shameful that parents teach their kids to be so hateful to others because of the color of their skin instead of the content of their character. I'm surprised that this book made me cry. It was probably because it hit a little close to home.

This is a great book for middle school kids. The back says ages 7-11.
