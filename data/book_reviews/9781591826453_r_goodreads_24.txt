Rating: 4/5

This particular graphic novel quite surprised me if I am to be honest I just happen to see it on the shelf and thought why not broaden my horizons, I know some feel that this is full of too many predictable paradoxes and find it can get quite boring but if you stick with it you find yourself feeling the story with the characters. It is a very sweet story of remembrance and friendship and I agree the ending is a little anticlimactic but it helps end the story on a gentle note. For life can be both gentle and painful and I think this saga catches a glimpse of reality.
