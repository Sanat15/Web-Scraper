Rating: 4/5

This is the first in a historical fantasy trilogy published in the 90's. It follows an English apothecary's apprentice named Thomas Chinnery who's journey to China by ship is interrupted off the coast of Goa. He soon finds himself mixed up with a Scottish mountebank, Portuguese inquisitors, a sorcerer, and a mysterious Indian priestess, all concerning a mysterious red powder with miraculous powers said to be the blood of a goddess.

The series reminds me somewhat of various Tim Powers novels which have that same historical-fiction-with-a-magic-element quality, only I think I prefer Dalkey's prose to anything I've read from Powers so far. It's good fun. Looking forward to reading the rest.
