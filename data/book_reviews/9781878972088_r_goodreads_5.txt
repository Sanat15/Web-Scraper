Rating: 3/5

Stein was not amused by the success of The Autobiography of Alice B. Toklas, which she felt was not her best work. 

However, this did not prevent Stein from attempting to cash in with a follow-up bestseller, "Everybody's Autobiography". 

This follow-up fails to recapture some of the lighthearted magic of the earlier work. It plods at times. Stein's grandiose ambitions are never on better display than in the title of this book.
