Rating: 3/5

The true story of parents who lose all three of their boys, One is murdered , and the other two die of AIDS. IT is well written, but the boy the story focuses on wants to shop, blame others, and make his life mean something by speaking about AIDS. The problem is that he does not want to admit that he is gay and that a promiscuous life style had anything to do with his disease, which makes his message less effective.
