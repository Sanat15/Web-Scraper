"..... Those who had remained were dying of poverty, others had gone in search of new wars to survive or had become highwaymen, like countless veterans of the liberating army everywhere in the nation. one of them summed up their feelings in a phrase: 'We have independence, General, so now tell us what to do with it.'

In the euphoria of victory he had taught them to speak to him this way, with the truth in their mouths. But now truth had changed masters...."
