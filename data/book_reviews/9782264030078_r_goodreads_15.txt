Rating: 5/5

Yes!

Finally a 5 stars for this month of February!

The last 6 months I’ve been (slowly) reading books that have been sitting on my shelves for years.
This one I had since 1995 (published in 1994).
When what a terrific debut this one is. 
It’s simply marvellous!
I loved the writing, although simple, but beautiful, which captivated me right away.
Although I have the original paperback, I did borrowed an especial edition for its 25th anniversary, which opens with a new introduction by the author.
This is a historical fiction set in a divided and tumultuous Sri Lanka, between 1976 and 1983, about a boy going through his own conflict of gender identity.
The story is told through his yes and the book is divided into 6 related stories.
It is really touching, sad and beautiful at the same time. So unforgettable,
It has been adapted for the screen and directed by Deepa Mehta, which was released in 2020 (Netflix).

PS. I also simultaneously listened to the audiobook narrated by Rahoul Bhaneja, which I highly recommend (the audiobook is currently on sale for $3.59 (Kobo, Canada - no subscription required).
