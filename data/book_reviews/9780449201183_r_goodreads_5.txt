Rating: 4/5

Lucy, Marchioness of Standish is becoming disillusioned with her handsome husband. He gambles, comes home drunk at all hours and has liaisons with other women. She knows that fashionable society sees nothing wrong with such behaviour and when her friend, Ann, suggests she takes a lover she wonders whether that might be the best way to go. When she meets the handsome Simon, Duke of Haberd she thinks her prayers might have been answered.

But there will be more unpleasant surprises for Lucy before her uncomfortable situation is finally resolved. This is light entertainment for those occasions when you want to get away from real life and read something that doesn’t tax your brain. The goodies come out of it well and the baddies get their come-uppance. 

Lucy gradually develops into a resourceful heroine who refuses to let things get her down. The characters are well drawn and the book is well written and if you are suffering withdrawal system from Georgette Heyer’s Regency romances this could be a good substitute.
