Rating: 5/5

I liked this one quite a lot. It's told from varying viewpoints rather than just the main character which tends to be the norm. I could identify with the main character's love of collectibles and antiques. She loves the hunt and discovery of items at garage and estate sales. She's still trying to discern why she loves what she collects, but she's getting there. Her family is reasonably understanding for the most part. 
Jane has a good eye for detail which helps her investigate the deaths that occur throughout the book. She's a bit quirky, but a very appealing character. I could see being friends with her. 
The mystery and investigating are well done, I like the setting and the characters. I look forward to finding more of this series to read.
Definite recommend
