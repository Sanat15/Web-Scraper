Rating: 4/5

I would have given this book four stars if Manzarek, who is obviously a very intelligent man, toned town his worshipping of Jim Morrison at least a notch. That was kinda irritating.
Other than that, it is a great musical autobiography telling a lot about the composing process, the inspirations behind the songs - all down to the chosen chord progressions. And this is what I am looking for in these books being a composer myself.
