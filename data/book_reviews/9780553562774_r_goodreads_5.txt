Rating: 4/5

Why, I do believe I read this out of order... but no matter. It was a good follow-on book to The Overmountain Men, I just need to go back and read The Border Men. I wondered where the actual Battle of Kings Mountain went. The Canebrake Men started out with a violent start, and continued with a good deal of war and wanton killing spread throughout the book. I feel it is a pretty good representation of how safe one would have felt along the frontier in late 1700s America. I enjoy the attention to family life and relationships in this series, making it more than just a catalog of struggle and horror in the western North Carolina/would-be Franklin mountains.
