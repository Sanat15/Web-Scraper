Rating: 4/5

Sweet and readable closed-door regency romance, of the old-school type that reads like regency fanfiction - our heroine, Alethea, was raised in a household that believed in education for girls as well as boys, has come to London to be "polished," and gets to meet all of the luminaries of regency-era Britain (her favorite authors, scholars, etc.). The romance is fairly low-key and characters are fairly three-dimensional; even the obnoxious cousins have some depth to them.
