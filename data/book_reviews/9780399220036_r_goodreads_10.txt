Rating: 4/5

This one is a bit special for me.

I first heard about Bill and Pete Go Down the Nile on an episode of Reading Rainbow that my class watched in elementary school. Unfortunately, neither my school library nor the town library had a copy. Eventually I forgot the title, and in those pre-Google, pre-streaming days, there wasn't an easy way to track it down. As a result, my seven-year-old self never got the chance to read it.

My forty-year-old self, however, was able to find the old episode, identify the book, borrow a digital copy through Libby, and finally read it.

And honestly, it's a charming story. For young children interested in Egypt, it introduces fascinating cultural and historical elements without becoming overwhelming or overly detailed.

Young readers will enjoy the humor, the friendship between Bill and Pete, and the engaging adventure. It's an excellent choice for the classroom or for the home library of any curious young reader.
