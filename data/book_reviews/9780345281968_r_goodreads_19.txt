Rating: 4/5

A collection of shorts. My favorite two were:

 For a Foggy Night- about a world in which fog changes the world around you if you enter it, possibly by changing which universe you are in (out of infinite) and you soon forget about the differences when out of the fog making your new universe your new life.

And

Exercise in Speculation - on the differences and viability of psychic vs mechanical teleportation. Niven references The Stars My Destination by Bestor (my next read actually) so slight spoilers for me (and you if you are reading All the Myriad Ways before Stars My Destination)
