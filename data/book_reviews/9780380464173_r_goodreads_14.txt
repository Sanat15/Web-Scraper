Rating: 4/5

While she was writing the Lord Peter Wimsey mysteries, Dorothy Sayers and her friends made a game of inventing the history of the Wimsey family. Scott-Giles garners information from the Lord Peter novels, Sayers' private correspondence, and various other sources to pull together this amusing volume. It's of absolutely zero interest to any but the most avid Sayers fans, but for the very small portion of the population it's intended for, it's entertaining and a lot of fun.
