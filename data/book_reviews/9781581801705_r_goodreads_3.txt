Rating: 5/5

Such a pleasant and inspiring read! I did not go in to this book expecting it to be a thorough "how to" so I was pleasantly surprised to see that it was full of examples from the artist's own journals as well as helpful tips. I have always had my own "artist's journal" where I create pieces of art that are "journalistic" in nature. This book focuses more on drawing and documenting your surroundings. I appreciated this lens since most of what I journal about are my life events and emotions. This helped me learn how to have an eye for the beauty of nature around me!
