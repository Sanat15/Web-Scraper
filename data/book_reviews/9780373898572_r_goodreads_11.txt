Rating: 3/5

Oddly I didn’t hate this book or the adultery. I’m not advocating for it however humans are complex and no one’s perfect. My irritation comes from the authors clear bias; in previous books she made her internalized misogyny blatant. Feminists, who are the only reason she was able to blaze the trail, are bad but somehow love even if it’s with a married man is ok? Seeing how Dailey was her husband’s receptionist I have a suspicion parts of this book were biographical.
