Rating: 3/5

At the beginning of the story, it starts out with the curious little monkey, George, and the man with the yellow hat. They are leaving to the airport to meet with a friend who is from Africa. The monkey, George, goes exploring at the luggage area to look around at his surroundings. He ends up getting lost and cannot find his friend. After much exploring at the airport, he is able to find the man with the yellow hat who is with their friend from Africa.

In the story, the overall mood is curiosity. The author presents the text in a way that makes the reader curious as well. It includes surprise and mystery when the monkey, George, is searching for his friend. The illustrations show George as very expressive. He will be drawn with a face showing interest, sometimes excitement, and also being scared. This gives the reader insight into how the character feels ad what can be done to change those feelings.

It is simple to explain the plot by just viewing the pictures. The text does not play a vital role in the comprehension of the story. The pictures take up a large percentage of the pages with very little written text. It is friendly towards younger readers or those whom are just beginners. The conquest set out at the start of the book is completed by the end of the book, giving the reader a sense of accomplishment in the plot of the story.
