Rating: 5/5

This book about Brazil's history, people and cultural life also has a "fast facts" section, maps, glossary, index and photos.

The book is more of a browsing sort of book than an in-depth, read it book. The country and people seem "alive and real". The focus is more on how people live while still presenting the reader with lots of information. 

The food section even includes three recipes from Brazil. It is a great overview of Brazil that makes you want to find out more. There is a good balance showing the different cultural contributions to Brazil. The map, while fairly simple (it shows the states and a few large cities), is good. The author grew up in Brazil and lived there at the time of publication.
