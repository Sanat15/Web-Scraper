Rating: 1/5

Definitely not worth the time it took to read -- and it was a fairly quick book. An almost skeletal plot, with paper-thin characters. Granddaughter of a long-time US Congressman is set to run for his old seat, but her husband is opposed to her running, when earlier he had seemed in favor. They fight, and the next day he is killed when his yacht blows up. He of course, turns out not to be what (or even WHO) he has claimed, and has not only planted the bomb to blow up some business associates who know too much, but also has escaped the blast, possibly to prey on another victim.
