Rating: 4/5

This is a clever little book and it's got some surprisingly "modern" discussion - and some quite old-fashioned concerns as well - with the occasional bit of humour (although not really a funny book) it was also a bit slow-paced at times for me and it became quite obvious quite early on "who'dunit" (I was actually expecting a twist proving it wasn't the guilty party for much of the second half, but no, that didn't happen) so I'm rounding up to a 4 star but it's more that some of it is really a 2 and some of it is a 4... and I'd give it 3.5 if I could, perhaps a whole half star for the delightfully vivid cover art on the 1980s copy that I was able to pick up at a bookstore while on holidays!
