Rating: 5/5

Gorry sakes!! We made it through this one. While it’s not my favorite in the series, it has a charm, all on its own. Set in Maine with Ralph’s grandfather, housekeeper and appearances from Uncle Levi. Each book has different phrases that seem to repeat through the book. I never have to hear gorry sakes, tarnal fool boy and time flies again. I know I will though, as these little sayings weave their way into our family life. Ralph is growing up and his character development in this book shines.
