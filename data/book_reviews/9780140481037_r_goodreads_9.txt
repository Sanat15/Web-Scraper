Rating: 3/5

I read Candida by George Bernard Shaw in 11th or 12th standard. 

The story revolves around Candida, a strong-willed woman torn between two men—her husband, who’s a preacher, and a passionate young poet. 

Shaw's sharp wit and clever dialogue kept me engaged, and the humor isn’t too shabby either. I really liked how Shaw tackled the complexities of relationships and gender roles, with a refreshing dose of irony. The characters are all so vivid, each with their own quirks. 

After reading it, though, the one thing that stuck with me was how Shaw pushes you to question expectations in love and life.
