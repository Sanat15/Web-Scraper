Rating: 3/5

2024 Review - 3 stars
It is a very different vibe from Three Lords for Lady Anne but I enjoyed this re-read. It is a fun and cute read about a woman whose “bad luck” has left 4 suitors dead. When a visiting lord decides to disprove the curse, he finds himself getting more than he bargained for…

2018 Review - 2 Stars
Though this booked lacked the initial vulgarity of Three Lords for Lady Anne, it also lacked some of that book's more redeeming elements. The heroine gains some gumption as the book goes on, but for the most part is a wet noodle. The villain is extremely predictable. The plot barely bothers to disguise who it is and reveals the motivation almost in the first chapter. The hero's gallantry lacked motivation. 
Heck, everyone lacked motivation. 
This was one of those books where there was nothing particularly wrong with it, but neither was there anything particularly right. The couple worked well enough together but I never felt the romantic tension of the first book. 
I was particularly puzzled by Hester's former relationship with the hero. They once had a thing? They didn't? Hester's POVs in general did nothing but muddle the story. 
All in all, a good reminder that it is time to set this Regency phase aside and read something that takes more brain power.
