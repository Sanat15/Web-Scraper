Rating: 3/5

Reading this book, I notice that their is much humor, but the book wasn't very clear to me. Paul Stewart, and Chris Riddell's, Lake of Skulls is a book that was fun to read, with many dangers that Free Lance has to deal with. Free Lance goes around and is surrounded by dangers, like witches and evil lords. All of these are in his path of trying to get the enchanted crown. One think I disliked about this book was how short it was, but one thing I liked about it was how easily you could read it, so I guess the shortness sort of makes up for it. Free Lance is in a forest, and he is with his horse constantly going through life threatening situations just to get a crown. Throughout the book there is some humor, but its more childish. I don't necessarily recommend the book if because I did read it pretty quickly, and was kind of shocked by how fast I did, but it is still filled with Free Lances obstacles he has to face.
