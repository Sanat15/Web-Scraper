Rating: 4/5

Another great read

Tony Dunbar, has taken his charters and used the excitement of the Big Easy to put together an enjoyable novel. Having once lived there , the color and of the city and the quirkiness of some of the characters bring back memories of the city.
I still have beads wrapped around another souvenir on book shelf. So the novel brought back memories. I think this finishes up the series on Mr. Tubby. Although I did not read them in order. They where all enjoyable.
