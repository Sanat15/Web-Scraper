Rating: 4/5

It started off slow to other novels I have read, but once it picked up pace, it held interest. Several red herrings along the way, for both characters and us the reader. I did however have a suspicion of who it was before the end... which didn't spoil it, as I was just please I was able to work it out and have it confirmed..... I am not going to spoil if for future readers though, so not giving anything away. A good, enjoyable read, well worth your time and effort.
