It has its amusing bits but there are quite a few unsympathetic characters here. I got a little tired of the xenophobic winks. It is frequently implied that Lord Howard's Indian souvenirs and the loan words in his speech make him ridiculous. But the primary problem with him is that he's a lecherous creep. The MMC appears a slowtop because it takes a lecher courting her before he realises he likes the FMC. She encourages the lecher mostly out of pique at the MMC.
