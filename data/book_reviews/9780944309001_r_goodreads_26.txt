Rating: 5/5

4.5 stars, rounded up

I can't believe I'd never heard of this one before! Such a cozy story, and the perfect read-aloud to do at Christmas time as a family. My only beef was that the dialogue tags (Nancy said, Plum said) were unvarying and repetitive, nearly always at the beginning of the sentence. It got a bit clunky to read aloud sometimes. 

But the story was positively delightful. This was a magical read for my 10-year-old daughter.
