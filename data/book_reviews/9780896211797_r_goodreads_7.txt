Rating: 5/5

I have stumbled upon an author I really like and happily he was a very prolific writer. The books are of the old west à la Zane Grey except Max Brand has a much better command of both language and technique. He has an excellent knowledge of horsemanship which especially appeals to me as well as human nature; Max is an exquisite story teller. Since I use the format of audiobooks, this book was amazingly appealing due to the narrator Robert Keiper who is absolutely spot on in capturing the drama, the character voices, the ever-building suspense. And best of all I found it for free on the LibriVox Ap which I just discovered due to my book club’s ever-restrictive and annoying new rules. What a great way to start out the New Year- a great and truly thrilling story read in perfection and all for free!
