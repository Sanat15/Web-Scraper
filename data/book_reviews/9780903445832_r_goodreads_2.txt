Rating: 3/5

Revisiting a book from my past. A school story, with a lot of emphasis on things like the role of the prefect. It makes me realise how very authoritarian the world was, and maybe still is. I can remember always feeling that the things the girls did which were against the rules were all pretty mild - a midnight picnic in a cave hardly compares with the fags and drugs some of my contemporaries at school were consuming, and the one thing which I thought was really bad, the theft of a kitten from a poor area, seems not to be condemned in the same way. (The theft was inadvertent, but no attempt was made to return the kitten to its owner once the mistake was understood and the owner's identity known). Dimsie, of course, is a great character.
