Rating: 4/5

Excellent world-building and characterization in this romance set in the ballet world. The ballerina heroine is devoted to her career which means that she has no personal life. Feeling burnt out, she takes a holiday, wherein she meets the handsome hero. When he does not recognize her as a famous prima ballerina, she can't resist having a fantasy holiday fling with him under a fake name. But it turns out she was not equipped with the emotional fallout of their affair, so she runs.

For those who get triggered, be warned there is a devastating scene where heroine, who got pregnant from her affair with hero, gives premature birth to a baby who tragically does not survive. The author very poignantly described the ensuing depression and grief.. 

I don't remember why I had initially shelved this book on my avoid list, probably because the hero uses an OW to try and make the heroine jealous but I can say without a doubt that I don't regret reading this story. I totally believed in the eventual HEA of the hero and heroine, not an easy feat in a genre that is littered with wishy-washy characters and dubious "happy for now" endings.
