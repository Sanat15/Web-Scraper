Rating: 2/5

"Low Country Liar" is the story of Lisa and Slade.

A exasperating comedy of errors, this book is based in South Carolina. Our h has a distant aunt, who is an absent minded writer. She comes to know that her aunt has a male friend, and immediately suspects that he might be cheating her when some money gets involved. She goes to investigate and meets the tiger like hero. However, the heroine then takes the opportunity to investigate, and pretends to be his new secretary, complete with a wig and change of clothes. The plot then proceeds into this absurd double life the heroine then begins to lead, pretending to be his very married secretary during the day and his very antagonistic and attractive nemesis at night. All this culminates into a very anti climactic and predictable ending. 

Totally let me down.
