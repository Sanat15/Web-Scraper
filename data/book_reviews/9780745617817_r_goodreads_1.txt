Just what the doctor ordered. Hegel frequently took swipes at those who are "incapable of pure thought." Welp consider me guilty as charged. To understand such dense and convoluted thinkers as Hegel I need them grounded in the concrete. I absolutely have to start with a biography. I need to study how Hegel's apartment was occupied by French troops as Napoleon marched through his town. If I don't have such material historical facts to work with then the ideas don't spin for me. This book does a great job weaving Hegel's ideas into his personal life and the historic context of the Prussian state. I've never felt "A=non-A" so vividly before!
