Rating: 5/5

I'm still in awe that I managed to even get my hands ON this manga, let alone have the honor to read it. I've watched the anime portion of StarS several times through, but the original storyline is so much more raw. The names are localized to their English versions here, but from what I can tell the rest of the story is intact as it originally was.

It's incredibly fast paced, but that's the glory of not having to deal with filler episodes. The story and artwork in this manga is amazing- a must read for any Sailor Moon fan!
