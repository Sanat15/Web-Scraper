Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed the numerous lengthy descriptive passages of the jungle environment in which the characters find themselves, contrasted with the descriptions of the apocalyptic society they must return to, leaving the audience not sure of which one they'd prefer. 
I also found the gradual and implicit sensation of devastation brought on by the wars and conflict, and I found the dynamic between Brainard and Wilding very interesting.
I think they should also hurry up and admit their love for each other.
