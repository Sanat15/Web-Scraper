Rating: 2/5

(Abridged version, year 2)

Lissa, dealing with the struggles of having a jailbird for a dad, starts making the wrong kind of decisions, making the wrong friends. When she realises she's taking a very harmful path, she's already in it too deeply. At the end of the book, there's even some excitement with the mafia. 

I don't consider this to be one of my best reads. It starts off with little excitement. The final ten pages try to make up for that, but it's only mediocre. The story mainly focuses on the concept of friendship and peer pressure, but I'm not sure the message with get across.
