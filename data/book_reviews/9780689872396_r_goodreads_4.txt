A free lance knight is hired by an ambitious lord to get him the crown from the Lake of Skulls. There, he finds lots of dangers, but for the price he would be paid, it was worth it.
The conclusion of his adventure, though, was not what he expected!
I was bored at the beginning but suddenly it got very interesting! Short engaging story for older readers. Detailed description and delightful illustrations by Chris Riddell!
