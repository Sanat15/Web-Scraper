Rating: 4/5

Short but very sweet book. Once again another partner in crime gets a character into some serious trouble full of lies. Then you all know how the story goes, she or he fall in love, the truth surfaces, queue major angst and then conclusion/happy ending.

It was a delightful book to read. I can't honestly say too much bad about it, expect it was way too short. The ending was a plus though, it ended rather nicely.
