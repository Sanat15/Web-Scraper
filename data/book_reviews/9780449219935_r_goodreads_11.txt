Rating: 2/5

A badly done, melodramatic farce

Melodrama alternates with farce in this improbably plotted Regency romance. It is tall on obnoxious protagonist behavior and very short on actual romance. The transition from hate to love between Harry and Isabella happens quite suddenly in the very last few pages of the novel. It is poorly motivated and, therefore, painful to endure for fans of historical romance who prefer well written novels.

My experience with Marion Chesney’s historical romances are that they are quite hit or miss. I’ve really enjoyed some of her romantic comedies, but I rarely enjoy her melodramas, such as this book. 

I experienced this novel as an audiobook narrated by Charlotte Anne Dore. She does a quite adequate job acting out all the parts.
