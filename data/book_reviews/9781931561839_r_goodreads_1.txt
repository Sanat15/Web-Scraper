Rating: 3/5

I'd tried to start this over a year ago and just couldn't get past the first few pages... And last night I read it in one sitting. I don't know what'd changed, whether it was just my concentration, but what I'd previously found dull and slow had become compelling and entrancing. The relationship between Jack and Anne-Marie was a pleasure to witness, its ups and its downs. I found their life together a very realistic portrayal of love and its hardships.
Some of the police scenes were fairly clichéd, and perhaps Manlow intended this, but if that is the case he should've made it more apparent. If not, then he could've done with consulting more police officers as well as physicists.
