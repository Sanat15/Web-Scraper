Rating: 5/5

"Mount Misery" is part II of a four (4) volume series which Joseph Bergman has written under the penname "Samuel Shem"- this four volume series began with "House of God," this series continues with "Mount Misery" (1997), "Man's 4th Best Hospital" (2019), and most recently "Our Hospital" (2023).

"Mount Misery" is written as a first person narrative, the narrator/ protagonist is a young psychiatrist who is in his first year beginning to work at a hospital in Massachusetts. Throughout the course of this book, the narrator/ protagonist continues to encounter gross incompetence and corruption at the hospital that he's working at. 
Stephen Joseph Bergman had been a practicing psychiatrist for many years, so it isn't really possible to determine how much of the events that he's described in "Mount Misery" were partially inspired by real events which he'd witnessed when he'd been working as a psychiatrist, and how much of the events that he describes in this book are entirely fictional. 
Regardless of whether the events that Samuel Shem writes about in "Mount Misery" were partially based on real events that the author had witnessed, or whether these stories are entirely fictional, the story does seem entirely believable to me. If you enjoy reading books which illustrate how some psychiatric hospitals are staffed with psychiatrists who are every bit as unstable, delusional and unhinged as the patients, how the legislation which regulates the health insurance industry often creates an environment in which corrupt and incompetent doctors are far likelier to become successful than those who are interested in making a serious effort to offer useful assistance to the people who are the most in need of assistance, and what this implies about American society and culture, then you will enjoy reading "Mount Misery. "

I should point out that as of Jan. of 2026, I have not yet read "Man's Fourth Best Hospital" and "Our Hospital," I do intend to read those 2 books at some point during the second half of the 2020's.
