Rating: 5/5

This western historical romance focusing on the Alamo period in Texas history is masterfully written.I love when an author can immerse the reader in a time period and setting this way. Her research and expertise show through but never overwhelm this passionate story. Ruiz and Julia have a long tangled past. Julia loves him but he married another woman. Now he is a widower with a child who Julia cares for but fighting in the Texas War for Independence has left Ruiz a bitter man with no intention of marrying again for love or any other reason. Yet Julia and Ruiz must work together to ensure the survival of their families' vineyard. No spoilers. Check this beauty out.
