Rating: 4/5

This is a must read. It goes into the health risks and symptoms of over exposure to fluoride. The fluoride Deception looks a the roots of the movement to fluoridate water, the scientific data that was published as far back as the early 1900s, and the marketing reasons why a substance that was once used as rat poison is now marketed as a dental wonder mineral. The author also shows top scientists and researchers that were once advocates of fluoride who were expelled from their careers when they honestly looked at the affects of fluoride.
