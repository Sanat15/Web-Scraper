Rating: 5/5

I read this book back in 2014, and it was timely to read it once again as a morning, devotional guide. It is extremely well done, covering so many things that can mess up our lives, and so much scriptural advice which can help to keep us on the right path. The stories and examples the author uses are great examples of how God works within our lives when we let him, and also how the devil works when we give him a foothold. 
 
It is truly an amazing, helpful, movtivating read! It is a keeper and I will read it over and over again.
