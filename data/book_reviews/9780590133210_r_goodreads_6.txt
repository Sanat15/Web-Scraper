Rating: 3/5

Another book in the new series called “books from elementary school i remember only vague details about and am now trying to find them again”. This memory is of a book picked off the shelf of a third grade classroom. The protagonist was a ghost girl. She was helping solve a mystery. It introduced me to the word “phasing” as a means to describe a ghost going through a door or wall, and she talks about how she hates the feeling of it.

I don’t think this is the right book….mostly because the main narrator is Robbie and not Beka and there’s no mention of “phasing”. But i’m wondering if my memory 1) is slightly wrong (they call it “smooshing” in this book but describe it the way i remember) and 2) if maybe it was a later book in the series? I ILL’d the 4th book in the series because the image on the cover seemed the most familiar to me.
