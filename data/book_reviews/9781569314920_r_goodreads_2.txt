Rating: 3/5

One of the stronger Ranma volumes. The main story deals with a goumet martial arts competition - Soun Tendo promised one of his daughters to French aristocrat Picolet Chardin II - they substitute in Female Ranma who has to train in the art of speed eating. The other story in the book focuses on the rivalry between Akanae and her sister as Nabiki reveals she's in love with Ranma.

This is far less disjointed and daft than many other volumes and there's some nice development in the Akane/Ranma relationship. Great fun.
