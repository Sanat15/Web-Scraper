Rating: 3/5

A re-read of this Regency era romance. I liked this better 
on previous read. This time 3.5 stars. 

Gabriella ("Brie") was dtr of a viscount's 2nd son employ-
ed as a veterinary surgeon. She assisted her late dad in his
work. Her mom &amp; beautiful sis (wed to a baronet) scorned 
this as an unladylike avocation. Brie's younger bro sought
to take his dad's place one day. 

Naïve Brie jumped to conclusions IE she assumed Dexter 
(later revealed as a duke) had starved &amp; beaten his horse! 
Brie's sister Angela came across as tactless and immature. 
Flirty Ang + insincere baddie suited one anther and should
have made for the Continent or elsewhere together? 

Brie &amp; Dex were a couple from different worlds. This social
and experience gap seemed insurmountable at times. Dex 
made some sly comments about her lack of dowry, etc. 
They both needed to listen more closely to the subtext of 
their convos. 

Brie &amp; Ang: both too plain-spoken, but at least Brie softened 
her approaches &amp; reactions over time. 

Something seemed missing this time around? 

Revised.
