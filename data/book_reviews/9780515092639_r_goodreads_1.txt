Rating: 3/5

It took a few chapters for me to get into this one- I thought I was going to hate the "bubbleheaded" heroine, but it was very sweetly done. I think Mansfield was going for the "love you against my better judgement" thing that Mr. Darcy had going on in P&amp;P, but it wasn't quite endearing enough; I thought Griff was an insensitive, selfish jerk, and he didn't really have a softening of character by the end of the novel. Ada redeemed the book for me- a charming love story.
