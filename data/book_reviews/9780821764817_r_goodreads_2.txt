Rating: 2/5

DNF

I was lured by the other reviews here and thought this would be a fun read...I was mistaken. The plot - if one cares to call it that - does not reveal itself until late in the story, and the characters are merely cardboard figures. Everything was just so nonsensical that I could not bring myself to finish beyond half of the book. 

The hero is a plain nice guy with a secret job, which is stereotypical but easily overlooked. The heroine, on the other hand, is an idiot who sometimes act like a lady of her social position, but more often not - which land her into scrapes from which the hero has to extract her. 

I've read stories featuring intrepid heroines who land themselves into trouble and also dangerous adventures, but this is not even up to that mark, seeing as how utterly boring the story is. 

The writing is not good at all. That is to say, it is not necessarily bad - depending on one's standards - but it certainly pales in comparison with those of other Signet Regency/Zebra authors such as Nadine Miller, Martha Kirkland, Jeanne Savery...and even those authors who have bad plotting but very gripping writing in their books!

I would not recommend this now, nor ever, unless you want to be confused and provoked into anger.
