Rating: 4/5

As an aspiring author, I find that familiarity with those ancient stories lying sleeping within the collective human consciousness lends an air of credibility to one's characters, who otherwise might be rendered one dimensional. So too do legends and fairy tales ignite the imagination and lead down paths we may otherwise never have noticed; the tick tack tick tack of the leprechaun mending shoes under a dock leaf breaking on our ears. Should we be curious enough to seek him out, and he be of a happy mood, we may come away with a pot of gold for our efforts.
