Rating: 5/5

Enchanted Attic is the best of the Shivers books that I've read. The family curse, the haunted dolls, the secret attic, chef's kiss. I didn't remember the mc being quite such a turd, but the first time I read this I was probably a little turd too. I wish there had been a little bit more interaction with the Fowler family, but at least Spenser wrote in breadcrumbs to their big reveal. When I first read this, I lived in double-wide trailer in the middle of the Daniel Boone National Forest, I knew how dark a night with no stars could be and was not at all surprised when the Fowler boys began to play again.
