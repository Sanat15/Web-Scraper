The afterwords of this book gives a great review:

"This book is a handbook. It is meant ... to be read and reread, ... that some of the thoughts, voices, and wisdom in these pages may remain with you."

"...a relationship with the soul can be cultivated through understanding, insight, care, attentiveness,and love. ... nourishing our relationship with our soul isn't something merely to read about and forget, or to think about when it's convenient. Nourishing the soul is a lifelong journey, traveled day by day, ... "

"Do things that touch your soul, from the silly to the profound. We can judge the quality of our experience by the degree to which it touches or soul."
