Rating: 3/5

Lady Lissa's Liaison is a free read from Barnes &amp; Noble, but is better than many high priced books. Lissa, who is still grieving the death of her father, is besieged by all the males of Society looking to wed a fat purse. Horrified by all the attention, and just wanting to be left in peace, she concocts a scheme to rid herself of all suitors. Lissa's new neighbor has a shady, and questionable, past and she intents to use it to her advantage. Too bad he doesn't appreciate her ingenuity. An adorable illegitimate son and a crazed suitor help to assist the romance along.
