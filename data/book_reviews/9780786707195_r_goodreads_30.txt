Rating: 3/5

This is one of the later Albert Campion stories - both is style and in the characters life - here you meet a number of characters who you have been introduced to and seem develop on and off throughout the series - (but there are far more new ones you are introduced to I hasten to add). 

This story was fascinating in how the story not only twisted and turned but also how the ultimate crime is revealed - I will not provide any spoilers but enough to say that the crime you are initially given is nothing of the whole story which is revealed slowly and in pieces throughout the book. 

I will admit I struggled at times as the narrative did tend (at least for me) to change speeds and directions at time but still the story is fascinating so that these issues are quickly over looked.
