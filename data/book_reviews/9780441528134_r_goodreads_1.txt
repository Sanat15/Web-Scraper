Rating: 4/5

The plot is a chaotic mess. You are stumbling alongside Jonny, the „hero“ that is either severely wounded or drugged out of his mind. Or drunk, mustn’t forget the alcohol. Everything feels filthy and gross and dark. 
A disturbing book, with no real purpose, very difficult to read—definitely no good entertainment. And yet, I liked it?
I'm not really sure. But it almost seems that way.

In an interview, the author commented:
I had no idea how to write a novel when I started Metrophage and didn’t trust that I’d learned anything along the way. What do you do when you want to hold a reader’s attention for ninety thousand words and have no idea how to do it? You shout and run around setting things on fire.

And that's exactly what you get.
The author's talent really shines through here, and I definitely need to finally read Sandman Slim...
