Rating: 3/5

This isn't a "great" book but it is a vivid and literate one. The premise is original - Sir Philip Sidney dispatched on a covert mission to Poland. That's something you don't see every day.

The novel itself is an atmospheric concoction of historical fiction and mystery, with a touch of the uncanny. There are incidents sharply delineated in the narrative that remain with me like snapshots and come floating back in random memory long after I finished reading.

I recommend it to those who enjoy a brief, evocatively written sojourn in another time and place. I think it deserves to be better known than, perhaps, it is.
