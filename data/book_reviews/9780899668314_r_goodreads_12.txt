Rating: 5/5

This book has been the least enjoyable so far in the series. While there are some interesting parts like learning about bees, most of the book consists of the grandfather calling Ralph a “tarnal fool boy.” There is character growth like Ralph learning to deal with his grandfather and not reciprocating anger. It is not until roughly 60% through the book that Ralph and his great Uncle Levi learn why the grandfather is being extremely mean to everyone around. It was a painful journey to get to that point in the book but that last 40% is so much more enjoyable and just as pleasant as the previous books in the series.
