Rating: 2/5

This was a fun book until the hero turned into a villain. There was no reason for the ML to kidnap the FL. The reason he gave in the book was because the FL wouldn't believe it was her brother who was after her. Why would he even need to say who he thought it was? He could have just said he thought someone was after her life and invited her to stay at his place or some such. 
Or he could have shared his thoughts with her and let her believe what she would and protected her whether she decided to stay at his place or risk being at hers. 
Also, if he suspected the brother, why did he believe leave the brother at FL's house without so much as having him or the house watched? 

There were a few saving graves in this book as it was funny at parts and had some rather amusing misunderstandings. Alas, not my favorite by MC Beaton. I'm still going to give the next book in this series a go as I think it will be different set of characters and a whole new story and I enjoy this author's writing style.
