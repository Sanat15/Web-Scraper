Rating: 5/5

Historical Fiction at its best!

Great news for fans of Georgian/Regency fiction: A.C.Ley is the real thing! After stumbling upon one of her books by chance, I am now in the process of reading her entire collection. The writing is excellent, the settings are accurate, the villains get their due, and the heroines and heroes are interesting and very likable.
Thanks to her children for bringing back their mother's intriguing stories, and a thumbs up for the cover art that drew me into the book before I read the opening chapter!
