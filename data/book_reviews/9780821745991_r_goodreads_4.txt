Rating: 5/5

If you like Regency Romances this is good one. It has substance and is beautifully written. I liked that the heroine was independent, smart and had a purpose. The story centered a round a collection of books that Jessica would inherit when she marries by her 23rd birthday. This becomes harder when the uncle turns down all of her suitors and a librarian that has his own designs on part of the collection. Jessica secures the aid of Sir John to thwart the librarian, who has his own designs on the library collection.
