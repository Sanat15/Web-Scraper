Rating: 3/5

i’m just a little lady reading this and not practicing any of the techniques sooo….

the first 3 chapters read as one long introduction just scaring the reader into thinking their partners wont enjoy sex unless they can last forever. like there aren’t other ways to please each other and enjoy sex? it feels like it tries to say that women can only cum from intercourse which is just weird to read in a book by a female sex therapist.

but then later on she sorta fixes this by having the partners swap positions in exercises and focus on the woman’s pleasure and all her sensations.

despite missing the mark in female pleasure, i appreciate how often this book reinforces safe sex practices and prioritizes consent and comfort of both partners in every single exercise

the writing also picks up n becomes sillier in the later chapters. i did have a few giggles and found some things to be cute n wholesome.
