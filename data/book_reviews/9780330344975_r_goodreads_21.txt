Rating: 4/5

I love this series! I'm sure some of this is because, having watched the excellent tv series, I can see the story play out before my eyes. With that said, I think the writing is so cinematic that it would be easily pictured, just not with the characters from the show. I kept wondering who Jeremy Poldark was, but that is answered before the final pages. I will note that these books are great in audio if you are so inclined. I'm going to have to prevent myself from moving on immediately to book four as I have several books cued up, but soon I hope to be back in the late 18th Century Cornwall. 

Why I'm reading this: I'm really enjoying this series, so on to book three!
