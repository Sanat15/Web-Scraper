Rating: 1/5

I thought this book was going to be about how to travel in your motorhome. Instead it is a diary of a very religious housewife and her husband ,who has a temper, who take to the road on vacations. 

The book is awful. When they decide to buy the motorhome, she feels guilty because "there are so many people out there who have no homes." Are you kidding me? Retirement is for doing for yourself and enjoying life! You earned it! 

And all of her whining about being a woman and having to drive the motorhome because of her husband's bursitis? I wanted to gag. There are millions of women who drive trucks and motorhomes every day. Wake up and get out more! 

The religious crap in this book drove me crazy and I took it back to the library and chose a different book. If you are into feeling guilty about retiring and enjoying life - this is the book for you. 

Yuck.
