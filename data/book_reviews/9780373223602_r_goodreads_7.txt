Rating: 5/5

I adored A Case Of You. It’s probably the best Pamela Burford book I’ve ever read and an absolutely rip-snortingly good romantic suspense to boot. Packed with twists and turns and intrigue, this story had me on the edge of my seat throughout. At first I thought it was just a murder mystery and really the romance is very much a background thing for much of the book but it is there nonetheless and even that relationship has twists and turns to it. I honestly had no idea who the murderer was until the end of the story and I simply couldn’t put the book down until I found out.
