Rating: 4/5

The Eighth Annual Collection gathers 25 stories from 1990, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1990") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Charles Sheffield's "A Braver Thing," Terry Bisson's "Bears Discover Fire," Ted Chiang's "Tower of Babylon," Pat Murphy's "Love and Sex Among the Inverterbrates," and Joe Haldeman's "The Hemingway Hoax." I also appreciated Molly Gloss's "Personal Silence," James Patrick Kelly's "Mr. Boy," and Dafydd ab Hugh's "The Coon Rolled Down and Ruptured His Larinks, A Squeezed Novel by Mr. Skunk."
