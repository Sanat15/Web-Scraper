Rating: 3/5

It is always hard to fully implement the parenting techniques given in any book, because your situation never seems to quite fit the mold. We have had some success using 1-2-3 magic, but there was certainly no "magic" to it. I am sure the author would accuse us of not following it as exact as we should, but trust me, you cannot always send your child to "take 5" or give them another equally as impacting discipline when you are supposed to. Further, our child tested this method (i.e., let us get to the count of three despite repeated time-outs and other lost privileges) much longer than is warned about in the book. It has been a few months and he is finally reacting positively to the 1-2 count. Another issue with the 1-2-3 method, is our son knows he just got a pass when we say 1, and almost always repeats the offense up to the count of 2... So annoying! I have started to tell him "that's one AND two!" and then he stops.
