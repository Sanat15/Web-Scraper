Rating: 3/5

I finished this book with relief and a “so what”. The book suffers from repetition upon repetition with trivial and inconsequential details that fill paragraphs. When a famous author with a reputation for important writing submits a new manuscript for publication, it is never seriously questioned. This book could have been far better and more important, had the editor actually read the entire manuscript and honestly suggested necessary editing. I rated the book 3 stars, mainly because, even after getting bored with the characters and their peculiar struggles with the coming apocalypse and whether to wait for it or bring it on, I did want a conclusion. And I was already at three hundred pages. By the time the conclusive fifty pages rolled around, I actually didn’t care.
