Rating: 3/5

Probably the best biography you are going to get of the infamous outlaw. Utley makes a point of really trying to bring forward only things he can know for sure, and assumptions that make the most sense. Billy the kid is one of the most enigmatic, interesting characters in American History. The problem lies in how short his life was and how little is really known about him. This biography suffers in that manner, there's not a lot. A great bit of this book is about the Lincoln County War which in my opinion is not all that interesting and fairly ridiculous.
