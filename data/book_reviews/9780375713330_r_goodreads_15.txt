Rating: 3/5

My brother gave me this book after being unable to finish it. (I did finish it.)
We were interested in the setting and time period, because our mother was born in 1926 and raised in the French concession in Shanghai and Harbin. Her family was Russian but had come to China as construction engineers on the trans-Siberian railroad - so as far as I know they did not flee the revolution so much as eastern Russian pogroms. My mother's father worked for a British bank in Shanghai and they had polo ponies and servants and - at least according to the photos we have - lived a very comfortable colonial life. My mother attended an English school in Shanghai and learned some gorgeous cursive script and a vast amount of Romantic poetry by heart.
Interesting book, but the plot was a tad cumbersome with an especially implausible plot twist at the end. It was difficult to get engaged in the characters. So an okay read but not riveting. Maybe if you are stuck somewhere with nothing else to read....
