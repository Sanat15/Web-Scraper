Rating: 2/5

This was book 9 in the Myth Adventure series, but actually follows on from book 7 (MYTH Inc Link). It is not as illogical as it sounds as it runs parallel to book 8 (MYTH Nomers and Impervections). Book 8 dealt with Skeeve in the Dimension of Perv, where he had gone to search for Aahz. Aahz going missing was only one of the crisis which happened, the other being Queen Hemlock's expansion plans in the Dimension of Klah. So when Skeeve goes one way, he sends the rest of the team to Klah to see what they can do.
Much of it is told from Guido's point of view, as he and Nunzio have been persuaded to enlist in the Possiltum Army, with the hopes of throwing a spanner in the works. The other team members are almost bit players in this. I didn't find as much humour in this book, but perhaps Guido's speech patterns didn't help. There were a few threads left hanging, but previously similar events have been dealt with in subsequent books.
