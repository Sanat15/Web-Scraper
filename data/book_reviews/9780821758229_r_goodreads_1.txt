Rating: 4/5

Another good read by author, Martha Hix. This last book in the Magic series is about the youngest of the three brothers, Jon Marc O'Brien. He didn't want a magic lamp picking out his wife on his 30th birthday like it did for his two older brothers. He planned who his wife would be and after corresponding with her for two years she was to arrive on his 30th birthday. When that day came however, he met Beth but not the Beth who he'd being writing to. His Beth had died on the journey to him. She convinced the other Beth to take her place. Beth Todd is fleeing from her past with nothing to her name so she becomes Jon Marc's wife. What will happen when Jon Marc finds out? Their adventure begins. Enjoy!
a small note: Not to give away any spoiler alerts... I do wish the author wouldn't have thrown a crazy event in the 2nd to last chapter and then ended the story without expounding on the after effects of that event. I feel that the author just added it in at the last minute and didn't really need to do that. Otherwise, it’s a nice story.
