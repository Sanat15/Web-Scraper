Rating: 4/5

Adorable. 

Note to self: this is the one where the heroine glimpses the hero skinny-dipping and is immediately smitten with his Greek God physique. Imagine her surprise when he turns out to be the “stuffy” history professor uncle of her bff. The “stuffy” professor she is supposed to impress with her irresponsibility so her bff will look responsible enough to access her inheritance early.

It’s a stupid plan and none of it works out because these two soulmates zoom past the “stuffy” and “irresponsible” masks to head-over heels-in no time. 

Half the fun of this story is discovering just *how* perfect they are for each other. Sexual desire is one thing – but hearts and minds is so often overlooked. The author drips bits of backstory into the narrative so that it dawns on the heroine (and the reader) just what a perfect fit this hero is. I think my favorite moment is when we find how the heroine’s career and the hero’s dovetail. It’s a delightful scene.

This is a very open and talky hero, with the heroine keeping a lot of pain and secrets close to the vest. It's a dynamic you don't see often, but I enjoyed it. 

I don’t want to give too much away because I had forgotten all about Boogenhagen’s review (which prompted me to the waiting list at Open Library) and went into this one cold. I loved all the characters, the setting and the romance. Warning: this is fluff – good fluff – but very low angst.

Edited to add: Good lord, look at that cover. It's awful - and ironic.
