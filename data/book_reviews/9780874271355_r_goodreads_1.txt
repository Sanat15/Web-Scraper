Rating: 5/5

Outstanding exhibit, wonderful book with great reproductions. I loved this show and had to go through it twice to soak up Gorky's tragic genius. Don't buy this expecting to be illuminated as to what his art is about. For that kind of information there are several very readable biographies: "The Black Angel"by Nouritza Matossian, Matthew Spender's "From A High Place: A Life of Arshile Gorky" and I think the best of the three "Arshile Gorky: His Life and Work" by Hayden Herrera who also wrote a biography of Frida Kahlo.
