Rating: 4/5

This was one of my favorites as a kid so I was excited when my son picked it out last night. Donald sends his three lazy nephews off to work, each works for a year and heads for home with a magic item. One of the nephews gets a magic table, another gets a magic donkey and another gets a magic stick. A shifty innkeeper steals from two of the nephews and gets his ass handed to him by a third. Fun stuff.

I didn't know as a kid that this is based on a Brothers Grimm tale. My wife and I took a weekend vacation recently and listened to an audio book of the Brothers Grimm on the way. There were at least three different versions of this story on it.
