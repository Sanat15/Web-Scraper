Rating: 3/5

This book is funny. It's basically a deluxe version of the Xenophobe's book. If you can find the normal softback version, that's probably a better/cheaper bet.

Here's a quote from the first page. "The population of Switzerland is made up of 4.5 million German-speakers, 1.25 million French-speakers, 700,000 Italian-speakers, 70,000 Romansh-speakers and 500,000 who say very little."

- Paul Bilton

Since I'm living here in Switzerland, this book is full of useful information and dangerous stereotypes that are sure to help me and get me in trouble.
