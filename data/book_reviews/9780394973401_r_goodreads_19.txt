Rating: 2/5

I don't know how effective a solution the Bears' penny system would actually be for stopping habits. I don't care too much either way since I'm reading these books for no other reason than the fact that I am a nostalgic adult with no shame. But this is really just one of the lesson-learning books with limited entertainment value. The Berenstain Bears are at their best when they are going on quaint or goofy little adventures, with morals only tangential to the main action.
