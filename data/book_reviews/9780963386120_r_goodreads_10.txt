Rating: 4/5

Like a lot of parents, I wanted to find an easy to follow discipline technique that erred on the side of gentle. So far, I am extremely pleased with the 1-2-3 Magic method and Phelan’s adjoining start techniques. 1-2-3 does not solve all of my two year old’s sharing skirmishes, but it works quite well for “don’t go in there” and “don’t touch that” incidents with my free-spirit kid. 

While my child is a bit young for some start strategies, I am very impressed by the effectiveness of the timer option. Obviously, my daughter cannot tell time yet (hearing her count along with my iPhone is a trip), but telling her an end time has made for faster marker pick ups and less tears when we head back from the playground. Those are huge parenting wins over here.

I took a point off for some organizational issues. It felt weird to read a chapter about getting your child to get ready for school on her own and get to school by herself followed by one on potty training. That said, I loved the chill out approach of the potty training section. I also really disliked all the half-assed cartoons. What is it with parenting books and stupid cartoons?! All that said, I will keep this book on my reference shelf for many years and challenges to come.
