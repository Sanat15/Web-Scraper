Rating: 3/5

I absolutely loved the way Socrates "defended" himself however I am not a huge fan of commenting on the actual text and trying to explain the many points of it WITHOUT having read the ACTUAL apology first. I had no idea what the writer was referring to for the best of 155 pages. However once I got into the actual text things became way clearer.
The same can be said for Crito. Nevertheless, the intro was way shorter and the ideas were beautifully conveyed. The main theme of the entire book is the historical accuracy of Socrates as presented in the works of Plato. The book ended with yet another commentary on the said accuracy issue. It is a book definitely worth reading.
