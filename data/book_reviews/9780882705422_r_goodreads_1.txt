Rating: 4/5

I purposely waited before reading this book until I finished the writing of my latest book 'True Worshipers'. I wanted to see whether the revelation I received, would come somewhere close to a classic like 'let us worship'.
Well, I was pleasantly surprised. What a marvelous book, well written, clearly explained, but so full of power. I enjoyed it thoroughly and I recommend it especially for a time like this, because the religious climate as we know it is changing. We need to know where we stand with God!
