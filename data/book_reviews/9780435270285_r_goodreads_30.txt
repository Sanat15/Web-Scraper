Rating: 4/5

This is in my opinion half a story about a runaway boy called Smiler, and Yarra, a runaway cheetah. Smiler is a good kid that feels he has been wronged and has no way of proving himself until his dad comes back from the sea. Smiler was a very relatable character and through out his whole journey and escapades you end up rooting for him. I enjoyed the story but the pace was a little slow to my liking. 
What I did not like was the ending. WHAT HAPPENED? It somehow feels like there should be an epilogue or an extra chapter.
12/05/21
The story ages well in my mind. Changes rating from 3.5 to 4
