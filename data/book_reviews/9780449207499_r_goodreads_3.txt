Rating: 1/5

Ugh. I know that Regency heroes tend to be a bit on the overbearing side, but this one slid right over the edge and is a domineering bully, with few redeeming features. He browbeats the heroine and kisses her against her will, and his helpful acts are mostly done from selfish motives - To amuse himself or to defend what he considers his. In many Regency stories, this ghastly man would be the villain. He doesn't deserve the heroine and she doesn't deserve having him inflicted on her.

One of the main reasons I bothered finishing the book was that the actual villain was even worse, and I wanted to be sure that he got his comeuppance. Which he does, but offstage and almost as an aside.

Not impressed.
