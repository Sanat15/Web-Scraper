Rating: 5/5

I'm a fan of strong women and men. As a writer of erotica, they're the fuel that drive my stories. Mind you - I write fiction, but it's based on some of the very principles expressed in Dr. Susan Block's "The 10 Commandments of Pleasure."

Dr. Susan clearly presents men with the 10 things women want AND she tells women 10 things that men want too. Both sexes play key roles in having a good sex life, and her style of writing is non-judgemental, informative, and sometimes downright funny. Her chapter for women called "Thou Shalt Adore his Penis" was priceless! Ladies - No matter what you think you already know about a man's penis - you'll learn something after you read this!

As a woman, I was of course, interested to read Dr. Susan's commandments for men on how to please women, and she didn't disappoint. Her chapter "Thou Shalt Make her Laugh" was one of my favorites, and you realize that for a woman, it's never about just stimulating the sex organs.

No...these are NOT the commandments that Mozes gave, but they will go a long way to helping you have a better sex life, and isn't that worth it?
