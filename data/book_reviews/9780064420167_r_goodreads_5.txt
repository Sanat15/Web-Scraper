Rating: 3/5

3 stars &amp; 3/10 hearts. I appreciated how this book showed me the wonder and importance of the tropical rain forest—although the ants gave me the shivers. There was hardly any plot to the story, but I did like the way it was set up. It was very laid-back, probably a good read aloud for kids. Kind of a boring story, at the time, but looking back I find it not so bad.
