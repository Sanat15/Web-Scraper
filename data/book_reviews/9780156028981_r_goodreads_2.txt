Rating: 5/5

This is a hilarious satire about America's first invasion of Iraq. The narration gets a little irksome--as the narrator is a surfer dude and uses 'like' and 'dude' a little too frequently for my taste. However, I can overlook that for his witty juxtaposition of a blind love for freedom and all things american--like jeans, sex and rock and roll--and the cultural realities of a modern Islamic nations.
