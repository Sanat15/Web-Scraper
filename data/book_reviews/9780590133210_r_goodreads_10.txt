Rating: 5/5

The story revolves around Robbie and Rebeka twins of age eleven. They die in a storm misjudging the weather of Kickingbird Lake. After fifty years they become ghosts. But, their age doesn't change. They remain age eleven for fifty years. Their house is later (after 50 years) known as the "Old Zuffel" house. However they couldn't live in their house, as they were many workers turning the house into a vacation rental. So, they decided to live in their attic with bunk beds a dresser and many other items which makes the place more comfortable to live. The first people to come for a vacation there even after knowing that it is haunted by the twins were the Shooks family...... Kim Shook, Scot Shook and Mr.Shook. During one of their hiking trips in Kickingbird Lake they discover some old possessions of the Zuffel twins. A golden watch, a siver locket and a dog's chain. The golden watch belongs to Robbie, the silver locket belongs to Rebeka and the dog's chain belongs to their pet dog Thatch who is also turned into a ghost after the big and unexpected accident. Kim knowing this story of the strange, unepected accident is off to investigate. At the end she realizes that Robbie, Rebeka and Thatch are the twins and the dog who are the ghosts. She also realizes that they want their possessions back. She thinks, the ghosts are angry because of their possessions been taken away from them and that is the cause of the late distractions in the family. But actually the ghosts weren't angry at all, it was just one of their way for them to practise their ghost powers. So, she decides to return it, but, without Scot's notice. Will the ghosts receive their possessions? It is up to Kim.
Read this fascinating book and find out yourselves.
