Rating: 4/5

I was looking for an author with my initials for a Book Club challenge, when a friend suggested Francesca Simon and her Horrid Henry.

Henry is a regular horrid boy in a regular British school. And this book tells his 4 horrible adventures(?) with his school mates.

It was quite amusing to read of Henry's horridness. I hope he grows up well! But also look forward to read more of his horrid stories. :D
