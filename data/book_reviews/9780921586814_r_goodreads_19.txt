Rating: 2/5

I wanted to like this book, since my creative writing professor so kindly gifted us all books at the end of the semester, but unfortunately, I wasn't super thrilled with this poetry.

The concept behind it makes sense. You can definitely see how "weather" as a theme impacts not only the word choice, but also the meandering permutations and ever-changing rhythms of the text. Sometimes two-word phrases and other times long, run-on sentences. Occasionally, certain sentence structures would recur, but in general there was a chaoticness and randomness that interfered with my ability to understand what the poem was actually getting at-- maybe femininity? But that's a complete shot in the dark.

For the sake of analogy, it's June in the Midwest, and it was beautiful yesterday. Shockingly not very humid, the temperature was in the mid 70's, and partly cloudly. Flash forward to today: rainy, cold, miserable. The unpredictability is less than desired. I want to make some sort of sense out of it, but I can't.
