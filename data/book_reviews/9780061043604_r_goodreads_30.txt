Rating: 4/5

After reading this book I feel like there are no smart people left in the world. I also feel like the internet has killed my (and everyone elses) attention spans and we no longer sit around having actual conversations. This is the first of Sayer's work that I have read and it was enthralling even though I had to concentrate extra hard to focus because the dialogue wasn't super fast and choppy and the reader has to actually think hard about what is happening. Great book and I'm looking forward to reading more of her work.
