Rating: 4/5

French/English heroine travels to her deceased father’s valley in wine-country Australia to meet her grandfather, aunt and uncle, and two cousins. 

Her father self-deported after he had an affair with a married woman, the hero’s mother. Don’t worry, hero was born long after heroine’s father left. 

 Heroine instantly bonds with her autocratic grandfather and then proceeds to take the valley by storm. She engineers matches between her widowed aunt and an antiques dealer, her cousin and a “lower class” neighbor girl, and blows the doors off an illicit affair her other cousin is having with a neighbor. 

The hero remains elusive until a bush fire burns down his family estate, his mother dies and he spends time in the Outback in contemplation. It is only in the last three pages of the book that they get together, so be warned.

I really enjoyed this one. It had a different tone than most MW’s. Oh, the cousin OW was crazy pants and the family dysfunctional, but this Australia was green and populated and had frivolous pursuits. The H/h spend time in night clubs and even disco down at a party. 1979 is alive and well within these pages.
