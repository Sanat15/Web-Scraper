Rating: 5/5

I found this book at a used book and record store, and I believe it has probably been one of my most cherished possessions. It’s a book that helped me through a difficult time in my life, and it’s a book that can be re-read multiple times. It contains a collection of different authors, many of which I was unfamiliar with before getting this book, but I have started to look into their other works as well. It has so many different unique perspectives and ideas that really make you look inward.
