Rating: 5/5

Loved this book! An experienced newspaper reporter, the author interviewed people on all sides of the very complex logging &amp; spotted owl issues. He wisely wove all of this into an intricate and compelling book. As an outsider who only recently visited the region for the first time, I found this book enlightening, helping me understand what I saw and why. I wish I could return to the area; my appreciation would go much deeper.
