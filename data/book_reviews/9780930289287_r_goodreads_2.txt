Rating: 4/5

Somewhat dated, especially the women's clothing and hairstyles, but I still really enjoyed it. I like Byrne's classic take on Supes, that he just wants to help people because he's an altruistic person. His Lois is more difficult to like, but I'm guessing her icy demeanor will defrost as times goes on. This version of Lex Luthor has since become the norm, the rich, powerful businessman jealous of Superman. Up to this point, Luthor had just been a mad scientist who sent giant robots and the like after Superman. I also liked how they made him older and a childhood friend of Perry White instead of growing up with Superman. Kudos to Marv Wolfman for the Lex revamp. I found it interesting that large gaps in time occur between each issue, covering about the first 10 years of Clark Kent's adult life. Bryne's art is fantastic. He brings his classic 80's polish to it that he applied to most of the Marvel universe over the decade.
