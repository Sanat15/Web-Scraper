Rating: 4/5

I found this novel an intriguing blend of politics, religion, Elves, and vengeance. I am somewhat bothered that it suggest Roger, a major villain, has some type of special powers: he runs off with a sword in his back and recovers; he throws a table across the room. However, I did not find any explanation for these events. 
Miriam is a slim, young girl who has healing powers. Her body demands that she uses these powers to cure others, although it causes her pain. Her parents disown her, because they firmly believe in the Church that insists such powers ate evil. She is arrested and tortured, but manages to escape. Her ability leads to several rescues, which don’t always have positive endings. 
It is a fairly violent novel.
