Rating: 4/5

Update: Finished. Bumping up a star because I feel a bit wistful thinking about it.

I read about half of this. It’s a sound, workmanlike, unspectacular book. There’s hardly any poetry or excitement, nor is there intended to be. It’s told as a series of autobiographical essays written by Zeno for his psychoanalyst. They (the “chapters”) are very long, the middle ones averaging over a hundred pages.

I really liked the chapter about the death of his father.
