Rating: 2/5

Somewhat related to Three Lords for Lady Anne, in that Anne and Bronson appear intermittently. I didn't find this story as charming as that previous story, though, because it was hard to see the romantic connection between the hero and heroine. Thorverton is a nice guy, I guess, but Meribe didn't have a ton of gumption for most of the book. 

(Side note: "Meribe" seems a very strange choice of name for a young English lady, especially when her sister was named Hester...)
