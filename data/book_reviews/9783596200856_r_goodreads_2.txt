Rating: 3/5

I found this a mostly charming account of a man's life with his dog, Bashan, and their adventures on walks through meadows and woods in Bavaria, where Mann summered in the early years of the 20th century. The descriptions of nature are sometimes beautiful and I generally was amused by the anthropomorphic discussions of Bashan's feelings and the give and take of the relationship between dog and, Mann's word, master. Although they escaped my notice as I listened to the LibriVox audiobook while I was out walking, I've read reviews that reminded me that there are brief incidents of the master whipping Bashan and hitting him with a cane, so you should avoid if this would disturb you.
