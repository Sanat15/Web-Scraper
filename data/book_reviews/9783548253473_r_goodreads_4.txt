Rating: 5/5

What an interview with a vampire. Rebecca, a descendant, meets her ancestor in a spooky location in London. Lord Byron never died. Inside you'll hear his story about how he became a vampire, his debaucheries, his relationship to women (even his incestuous one), the places he visited (Greece, Italy and Little Asia), his open attitude to men. You'll also meet up with Shelley (Mary too), Polidori (who wrote The Vampire). Incredibly good and literary portrait of "The Napoleon of Words". The horror isn't missing either. This book combines some old school narration and 19th century flair with classic Gothic elements. Highly recommended!
