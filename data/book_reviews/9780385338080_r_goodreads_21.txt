Rating: 3/5

as a marketing baddie i am OBSESESD with emma going from assistant to executive after simply asking her boss in front of the whole company, while also fucking the owner of said company. who may be 5 years her senior or 40 years. we're never told. and honestly? none of my business &amp; happy for her.

this book was so fucking silly and awful, it kind of came full circle to being enjoyable. not a single plot point in this could or did ever in the history of the universe happen, but i was just along for the ride

this was not complete without a bitchy roommate side character obsessed with clothes and anti-woman sentiment. and i need a whole spinoff book about the lawyer roommate who is also a ballerina in her free time. this book has plenty of hits on 2000s buzzwords, like g spot and being a size 8 and thinking you're fat.

it feels like a book that was written to fill the void between kids books and Adult Books before YA/new adult came on the scene, because i can't imagine an adult ever being the target audience of this. it read like kids playing pretend of what they thought adulthood would be like

rest in peace, queen sophie kinsella. she sure could write a damn entertaining and nostalgic book
