Rating: 4/5

I thought I knew my own mother tongue well until I read this mind-boggling "poetry-fest" of archaic words, rhymes and metaphors. At first, I was ready to put down the book and leave it unfinished because I couldn't understand what the heck I was reading - until I eventually noticed the footnotes. (Thankfully, they explain exactly what the poet was trying to say!) A few pages later, I was completely hooked by the tragic story of Thúy Kiều. The ending was satisfying, and I can surely say I enjoyed this one-of-a-kind poetry book.

My score: 4⭐
Reading diff.: Difficult
