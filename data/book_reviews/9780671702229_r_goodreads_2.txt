I read 100/300 pages of the version I got my hands on, but man... it's terrible, really. I usually try the first 50 and in case it's real sh*t, like A Clockwork Orange was, then I simply give up, and yet I told myself that I'm gonna give this one a try. Useless.

There are so many contexts, concepts, situations that are simply not explained at all. How is the reader supposed to know about all religious factions in Jerusalem or important places if they've never been there? I was really expecting to learn something new reading this book. There are too many stupid dialogues, which could simply be replaced by narration intertwined with backstories and contextualization of all the mess created in the first chapter.

This first chapter I'm talking about is an absolute disaster. I don't even remember sh*t, cuz it was so badly contextualized that it really felt like a hurricane of riots, actions, and unrest. It was not well-written at all. I couldn't even follow the sense of all that meaningless vomit (unless it was going to be explained, needless to say). But I guess I didn't dedicate enough patience trying to decipher and solve mysteries on my own. Or perhaps I wasn't focused enough. Quite a boring reading experience to be fair. I had had better.

And then the story itself... I couldn't care or get attached/involved in it and it felt annoying too many times. I ain't reading any other page and I'm sorry I had to waste time just to give this book a bad review.
