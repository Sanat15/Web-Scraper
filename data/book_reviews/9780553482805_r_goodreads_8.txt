Rating: 2/5

I recently bought this book at a 2nd hand store and gave it to a friend who missed out on the joys of a main stream American childhood. Of course, I had to re-read it before I gave it to her. I read many of the Sweet Valley Twins stories in my childhood though I didn't follow them through high school and college (I didn't like the more romance-heavy feel of these stories.) I found myself with similar feelings - relating more to the goody two-shoes Elizabeth than the annoying Jessica and her stuck up Unicorn friends. Overall, the stories get a little redundant but are still a fluff of fun.
