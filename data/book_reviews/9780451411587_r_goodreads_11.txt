Rating: 3/5

This was a fast-paced, nice, average reading. I should have read the first one but it was fine as stand alone. I didn't find the villain believable at the end, but he provided suspense to the plot throughout the book and kept me interested. I liked the heroine very much, a tomboy cop, straightforward, honest, in love with the hero for many years but believing he's out of her league. I liked the hero but there was no plausible explanation why he has avoided the heroine for so long if he's attracted to her, or much insight as to what type of person he is. And anyone who's ridden a motorcycle at 50 mph (not even the 70 mph used in the story) will know the harley love scene is sci-fi stuff.
HERO is a Warrior, a widely known and recognized fire marshall, from a high class family, forced to work with his brother's long-time partner, a police woman he has lusted after for a long time against his will.
SCENES/CONTENT: few/hot
GENRE/TONE: contemporary/action
LENGHT: 294 pages
