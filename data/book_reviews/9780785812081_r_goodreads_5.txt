Rating: 5/5

Fighter has been on my ‘to read’ list for a while and Len Deighton’s recent death finally prompted me to download the Kindle edition. Best known for his fiction, Deighton was also an excellent historian as this compelling account of the Battle of Britain shows.

As well as covering the key points of the battle itself the book looks at the main commanders, at the technological developments – in aeronautics, engines, radar and more – and the command and control systems that made it possible.

It doesn’t shy away from the failings and mistakes by both the British and the Germans. A final chapter looks at the lessons learned, or not, on both sides.

The are plenty of books on the Battle of Britain but few offer the comprehensive warts and all portrayal that Deighton provides here.
