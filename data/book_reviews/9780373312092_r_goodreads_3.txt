Rating: 3/5

3 1/2 Stars! ~ This was a charming read. It's been awhile since I've read a Regency, and it was a lovely diversion for the afternoon. This most disreputable Rake gently rubs off on the very Prim Miss Jane; and the Rake finds himself wanting to be respectable. There are a few laugh-out-loud moments and some rather serious ones as well. All in all, a very fun and light read.
