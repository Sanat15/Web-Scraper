Rating: 4/5

Lovely as ever.

You wouldn't think it from how mellow the books are, but Ransome is a really strong plotter. The way he splits the characters up and ties the plot threads together in the last ~75 pages, creating just enough jeopardy to create some real tension without breaking the gentle tone, is remarkable.

The last pages of this one may be the most perfect distillation yet of what I love about these books.

Also – Dorothea and Dick are wonderful additions to the cast, and in my headcanon (sic) their parents, "digging up remains in Egypt," are Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz.
