Rating: 2/5

“Raines story” is the second book in the Pearls of Sharath series, and the story of Michael and Raine.
Walking on a road during a dark night, Raine suspects someone is following her, only to bump across Michael, who promises to protect her and delivers her to her destiny. There are instant sparks, but when Michael sees the stolen pearls of Sharah adorning Raine’s neck, he is compelled to follow her.
What follows is a couple divided by their responsibilities and promises- Raine to her family and Michael to his job. As expected, there is mild angst and hot lovemaking entwined with a sinister plot.
Average read overall.
