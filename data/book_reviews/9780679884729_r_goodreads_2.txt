Rating: 5/5

Adorable board book for emerging readers! Mom or dad may have to help little ones as this one has lift-the-flap surprises and instructions about where to place the (included) stickers. Arthur and his family are getting ready for Christmas, so there are scenes with card-writing, decorating &amp; baking. 
I've loved Arthur ever since my adult son was a little boy! I saw this in the little free library in my building's lobby and just couldn't resist...
