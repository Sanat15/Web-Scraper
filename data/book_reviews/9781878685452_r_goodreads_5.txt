Rating: 4/5

I've had this book for as long as I can remember but I had never read it. Considering how the Christmas spirit seemed so scarce right now where I'm standing, I figured it was a good time as any to read this.

The drawings are beautiful and all have different styles. The story in itself is super adorable.

To me the best part (and a surprise at the same time) was Clement C. Moore's poem. I had never read "The Night Before Christmas" so finding it in this book and being able to read it made me happy.
