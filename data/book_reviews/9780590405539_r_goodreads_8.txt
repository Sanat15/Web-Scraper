Rating: 5/5

The Black Snowman was one of my childhood favorite books. It tells the story of a boy named Jacob and his little brother who find a magical Kente cloth that brings their black snowman to life and gently teaches Jacob of the beauty and strength and goodness in being black. 
It’s a sweet, sad, and poignant story that ends with a note of hope and continuity. The artwork is gorgeous and sticks me, it draws emotion out of me, specifically when talking about the ancestors at the beginning of the book or when the snowman calls them forth. 
It may be an older children’s book, but it’s still very precious and lovely and may or may not make you cry.
