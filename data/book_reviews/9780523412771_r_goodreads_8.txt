Rating: 5/5

I can't explain why, but this book absolutely mesmerized me. I made the mistake of reading reviews on Amazon before I finished the first book, The Westerby Inheritance, and as a result, I needed to see if there was a happy ending in this book so I could feel better. I could not get Lord Charles and Lady Jane out of my mind throughout this book. 
I can’t say I felt good after reading this, but it was so absorbing, entertaining, and haunting. I don’t think I will forget these two books for a very long time.
I loved ALL of the characters, except the evil ones, and I would include the house as one of these “evil” characters.
