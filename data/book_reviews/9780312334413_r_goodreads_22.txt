Rating: 3/5

The United States Exploring Expedition set sail from Hampton Roads, Virginia in 1838. The author uses the expedition as a cornerstone for a series of 'whodunnits at sea'. She has conjured a seventh vessel, peopled with fictitious mariners (one of whom being Wiki Coffin - our translator and sleuth) then set it off with the fleet - Wikis first task is to identify a murderer from among the crew and scientists. For some time I have had a NF title by the same author on my TBR Island of the Lost: Shipwrecked at the Edge of the World, she has written a number of maritime books, several of which interest me as well.I'm pleased to say I enjoyed it, possibly more so as I'm Kiwi with a smattering of Maori - I'll be reading the remaining stories for sure.
