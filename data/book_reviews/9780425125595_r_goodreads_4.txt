Rating: 2/5

This book started out a very well written story. I hadn't read any older Westerns, and didn't know what to expect with this 80 year old book. I recently finished "Pillars of the Earth", and thought that this was better writing - the characters more vivid, the story less like a movie, more complex. Then I got to the end. This story just ends. The characters get in quite a pickle over dozens of pages, and get out of it in a couple of sentences. Seems to have surprised the author, too. The last page of the book is jarring -- Max must have gotten paid by the page, when he met his quota going on to something else. I listened to this on a Book on Tape - and they left 20 minutes as an ad for another book. I was expecting this story to continue, so that didn't help. I enjoyed the story and the suspense, just don't expect a satisfactory ending.
