Rating: 2/5

I tried another Vivien Alcock book, since I was impressed by A Kind of Thief. I liked the opening of Stonewalkers, but the book as a whole struck me as abrupt and off the mark. Statues come to life, but are veritable Frankensteins, and not even as likable. The main character, who has a problem with lying, is apparently cured of lying after her encounter with living statues??
