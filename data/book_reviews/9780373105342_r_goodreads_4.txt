Rating: 4/5

3.5 to 4. Quite good but a very vintage book. 

Shoutout thanks to my friend Naksed! 👍🏻👍🏻🥰

The Hero in this one is very dominant, verging on harassment. He is immediately attracted to the heroine, although he knows she has a boyfriend yet he practically bullies her into a sexual relationship with himself. The Hero is much more experienced, and he easily overwhelms the young, unsophisticated heroine, who gives in eventually. It made me a bit uncomfortable since they were cheating on the boyfriend.

As it turns out. The Hero has a kind of understanding with another woman from his home town, so he was cheating on her as well. She shows up much later in the book, so not much drama from this sector.

The author succeeds in showing us that the couple, although the heroine is way more conflicted than the Hero, are just too in lust with each other that they just cannot help themselves. 

In the end, the Hero also succeeds in bullying the heroine into agreeing to marry him ASAP because he refuses to wait a few weeks for her. This could have been seen as romantic, but I just thought it was inconsiderate of him. He first put the heroine and her parents in an embarrassing position when the whole town gets to know that she split up with her fiance only to take up with him soon after. He also deprives her parents of seeing their only daughter married properly in the church, in their town.

The heroine had wanted to wait because she wanted to spare her parents gossip since they would have to continue living in the town, but the Hero is rhe kind of guy who just had to have his own way.

I say good luck to her in the future. 👍🏻
