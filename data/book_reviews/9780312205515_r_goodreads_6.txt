Rating: 5/5

A discovery is made deep in the ice of Antarctica. Shane Schofield and his team of US marines are immediately sent there to protect it. But other countries want it too, and they’re willing to go to war over it.

This is Matthew Reilly at his best. It’s like a big budget Hollywood action movie, but it’s action scene after action scene at a blistering pace. Once the story gets going, it barely gives you the time to catch your breath, keeping you on the edge of your seat until the end. This style might not be for everyone, but if this is something you can enjoy, you will absolutely love it. This is pure adrenaline disguised as a book.

In terms of characters, Shane Schofield could be described as an action hero. Though it’s not his brawn but his brain that stands out most. It’s his quick strategic thinking that can get him out of impossible or dangerous situations. Another thing that stands out about the characters is how much certain characters trust and care about each other. And the shared respect they have for one another.

This is an action-packed story to say the least. And there’s so many cool twists that keep your eyes glued to the pages. If I have to compare it to something, I’d say it’s like a mix of MacGyver’s clever problem-solving with the relentless action of Die Hard, though at a more intense pace. Definitely recommended for the right kind of reader.
