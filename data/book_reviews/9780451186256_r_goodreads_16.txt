Rating: 2/5

Hern did not develop her characters well in A Change of Heart. Jack was actually one of the most despicable love interests I have ever encountered and Mary’s giggling turned her into a brainless teen. The plot was interesting but maybe the details of Mary’s past were introduced too late for her to be sympathetic. Good literature should show the reader rather than tell them. Other characters repeatedly told us how likable and good Mary was but we were never shown a good side. Jack was self indulgent and never redeemed himself. Jack and Mary’s relationship was doomed from the beginning.
