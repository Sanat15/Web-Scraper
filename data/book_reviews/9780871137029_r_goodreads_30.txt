Rating: 4/5

A heartbreaking account of the experience of a Irish prisoners of the Anglo-Irish conflict of the 1970’s to 1983, specifically those who chose to go on hunger strike in 1981 to demand recognition as political prisoners rather than common criminals. The author’s use of actual messages and letters to and from the prisoners paints a vivid image of the struggles and injustices they faced day-in-and-day-out. Beresford makes sure to paint the humanity of each hunger striker as he shares the life history of each man. It was an eye-opening and heart-wrenching testament to the lack of humanity in some
Government leaders out to flex their power rather than serve and recognize their people.
