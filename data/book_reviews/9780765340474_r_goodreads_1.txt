Rating: 3/5

This is mostly a retrospective volume collecting excerpts from novels and reprinting short stories from other collections along with some essays and curiosities and a few newer things. It's the third such omnibus that Tor released, following N-Space and Playgrounds of the Mind. I didn't think the excerpts worked on their own (you should just read the novel), and I already had read almost all of the shorter stories. I thought they should have just reprinted the existing collections. On the other hand, it did have some enjoyable material new to me, and it's good that they put Niven's name back out on the shelves with a new title. The content itself is of mostly high quality though I grew tired of email exchanges disguised as essays. Niven was the best hard-sf writer in the field for a long time, though the title of this one is proved by the diverse contents. I just thought Tor overdid the re-packaging.
