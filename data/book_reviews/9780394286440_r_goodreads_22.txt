Rating: 4/5

This was a great sequel to the classic Gateway in which we learn more about the Heechee without actually meeting them. Robinette is still a wacky protagonist, both charming and annoying. He and his AI-pal Albert exploit the Food Factory and Heechee Heaven to benefit mankind (but especially to Robin's benefit). We are given some more background on the Heechee's and (here it becomes suspiciously close to the story in The Expanse) what they were fleeing when they left all their artifacts all over the Solar System and beyond. We are also introduced to Wan, the strange adolescent they meet in the Food Factory. 

The outdated sexism/pedo fantasy aspect of the book (the oversexed 14-year-old) fortunately is forgotten once we meet Wan, otherwise, I would have enjoyed this far less. I thought there were moments that Pohl was seeing far beyond his time, particularly when describing the AI constructs of Albert and Sigmund.

A great book and worth the read!
