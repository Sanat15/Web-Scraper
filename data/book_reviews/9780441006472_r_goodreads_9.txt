Fantasy without the magic, which I don't hate? I generally love sparse writing but this was a little too austere in its detail even for me. But! 70s feminist fantasy featuring explicitly queer and trans characters goes a long way to keep me interested. I would have liked Ryke to be any more compelling than he is - seriously the man has no personality other than Soldier Who Is Madly In Love With His Prince - but this was good escapism right when I needed it.
