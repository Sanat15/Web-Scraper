Rating: 5/5

Amazing book, not stuffed full of facts and people you don't care about. No speculation, well researched. When there are conflicting reports, the author makes it known. Exclusively about the Kid and his short life with good bits about Pat in there too. It is surprising to me how he is one of the most popular outlaws of all time knowing what his real story is. It seems like most of the murders he committed were done attempting to escape or flee except for sheriff Brady in Lincoln, which he was only a part of a group. He wasn't the gunslinging Robin Hood or the bloodthirsty outlaw like i thought he would be. The press can do crazy things to make someone famous.
