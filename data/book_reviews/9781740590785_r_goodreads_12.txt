Rating: 4/5

Quite good as usual with LP. 
It's a big book though, if you only plan to visit some part of Germany, I think regional or city book would be more appropriate, and may give more details on smaller but still nice places. 
The historic and cultural section at the end of the book is really great, and worth a read during a train trip :) 
Prices rises quickly it seems, and there is a lot of construction going on in Hamburg and Berlin, so these cities are changing quite fast ! 
Even Cologne is actually building a new museum and a new waterfront, so I guess better look for the latest edition.
