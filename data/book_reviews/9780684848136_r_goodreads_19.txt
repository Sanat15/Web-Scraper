Rating: 4/5

Along with Budd Hopkins, Dr David M. Jacobs is one of the leading proponents of the alien abduction phenomenon and its sinister nature - namely the breeding programme to create alien-human hybrids through decades-long generational abductions. 

In turns fascinating and terrifying, Jacobs’ exposé of the true nature of the alien abduction phenomenon requires the reader’s attention. He is fully serious and demands the reader to be likewise. Jacobs writes authoritatively and backs up his hypotheses with vast amounts of witness testimony. 

Never preachy or veering into New Age cultish nonsense, Jacobs ensures that, no matter where you stand on this issue, by the end, you are much more of aware of its true nature.
