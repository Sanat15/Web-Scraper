Rating: 3/5

Ally (short for Alistair) gets roped into visiting his Aunt Chloe in hospital; a current patient dealing with an illness of the mind. The weekly visits with both mom and dad are extremely boring, Aunt Chloe acts like they're not even there. While on a visit, Ally looks out the window and sees the name 'Flora' planted out in the gardens at the back of the hospital. Intrigued to see who planted it, he sets off to investigate. but to get to the place involves crossing the lake, a difficulty in itself. He finds two people living quite a primitive life absorbed in nature. Flora and Riley are building a life together and are soon to be parents. Flora's spiritual character has funny moments in this book. Her happy outlook helps Ally look at life a little differently. Riley provides a contrast, still with some humour, because he is rather grumpy and competes for Flora's attention with Ally.

There were some lovely bits throughout this book. However, for me, I finished the book and felt a little unfulfilled. It was just ok and I wanted it to be so much more after reading the blurb.
