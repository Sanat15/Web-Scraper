Rating: 5/5

A great introduction to pre-Meiji Japanese literature. As someone who's never read pre-modern Japanese literature before, I was struck by the raw evocativeness of wakas and Noh plays. Quite a number of the stories in this anthology were also surprisingly relatable to modern-day readers and didn't read off as simply a time capsule of the ancients, with stories of girls with strange interests, lovers eloping, gender benders, and a hilarious story of two travelers who try to sleep with the same girl, who unwittingly end up sleeping with each other. So, all in all, this was a great read, with a perfect curation of stories and plays, even for those who are new to Japanese literature.
