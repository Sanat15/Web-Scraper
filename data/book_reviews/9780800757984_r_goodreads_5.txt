Rating: 5/5

I don't read much for Westerns but I'm glad I picked this book up because it reminded me how much I love reading books centered around ranches and horses. 
This was an easy and engaging read that I found was much more enjoyable than I expected and I loved the bond that Lee has with her horses!
Full of West Texas in the late 1800s, a smart and determined woman, loyal friends and Christian Faith, I'm looking forward to continuing this series soon!
