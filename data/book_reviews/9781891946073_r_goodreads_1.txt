Rating: 3/5

This book started off strong and promising but towards the middle started to go downhill. A couple of stories that kept me breathless had terrible endings leaving me disappointed and almost angry that I'd wasted my time getting so caught up in them.
A couple of the stories were also way too long and can only be called boring such as the one set in a blues club.
I had looked forward to reading this book for a long time and it was much better in anticipating than the actual reading.
In summation, its ok to kill time with for a couple of nights but not a book I'd ever read again.
