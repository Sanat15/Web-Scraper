Rating: 4/5

I first read this when I was 12 or 13 in a Reader's Digest Condensed Book version. Didn't remember much of it and had no idea it was the first in a trilogy. I loved it back then, read it several times, then promptly forgot about it for 40 plus years.

Read it again and loved it just as much. It's a fabulous book and I'm excited to read the rest of the trilogy!
