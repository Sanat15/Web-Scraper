Rating: 1/5

Don't read the description of the book...because that's the entire plot.

A weird (and I mean really strange) girl comes to school. Students like her because she makes them happy. Students then begin to hate her because she's different (as if that was not apparent). Randomly, Stargirl and pseudo-main-character Leo start dating. They meditate; she tries (unsuccessfully) to fit it with the school; then she leaves. End of story.

The biggest problem I encountered while reading this book was that I hated Stargirl.
No, Stargirl, creeping on little kids is not socially acceptable.
No, Stargirl, embarrassing people will usually end poorly.
No, Stargirl, of course people will think that stalking others is strange.
No, Stargirl, you have to observe people's reactions lest you hurt feelings by overlooking others' emotions.
No, Stargirl, please don't bring a rat to school. That's unsanitary and risky.

I did enjoy that the book touched on aspects of peer pressure; however, I think that Stargirl was just too bizarre to sympathize with. Also, the ending appears to be stealing the spotlight with its message of relaxing, true meditation and of people coming from stardust.

What did I just read?
