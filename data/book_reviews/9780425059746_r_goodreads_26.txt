Rating: 3/5

It is not Arthur C. Clarke’s best work, but that doesn’t mean it is not worth reading. This is fun and short and I would recommend for anyone who enjoys Clarke’s work or more generally, science fiction. It is light on characters and doesn’t have the bookkeeping that great Space Operas do. Clarke is a world builder. In Against the Fall of Night, he hints here and there at great mysteries which are not always revealed. It shows a world of what could be, should we rise and fall as a species. The book reminds us that there are many important lessons in history and we would be wise to never forget them, no matter how far we climb beyond our origins. It makes me want to go and read The City and the Stars, and Rendezvous with Rama, again.
