Rating: 3/5

Turns out this is the last book in a series that I haven't read, so I've got no sense of knowing the character and her struggles previously.

Even so, I love Tanith Lee's writing, and this book was a type of absurdist fantasy that was just... fun, but also a little bewildering.

Underlying theme was essentially facing one's own unresolved emotional issues, which happens to be a little too relatable to me!
