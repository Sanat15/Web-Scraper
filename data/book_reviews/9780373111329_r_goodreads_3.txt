Rating: 2/5

Who would have thought it would take an aging rock star to get this couple on the right track? That was unusual (not tm mention entertaining), and I couldn't help wishing he had been able to work his own love life out so well, he sure was the picture of pathos crying and boozing over his lost love, though a free-spirited girl half his age was probably a mistake from the start.

But he did get the h (and also the H) to realize that for a marriage to work it can't be "my way or the highway" and that was something they both had to learn. Compromise is the name of the game!

Also, the h should have realized just how manipulative her sister was (not to mention jealous), and how she used her "illnesses" to manipulate people and keep her from anything she didn't want to deal with. The h should have told her to "shut the F up" and stop badmouthing the H! It was easy to understand why she was alone; no guy could ever be that desperate!

The scenes where the H was getting acquainted with the responsibilities of being a parent were entertaining, and it shows how having a baby may be blessing, but at the same time, it's no day at the beach!

But I do think the natives on the island were exaggerated, as natives often are, more's the pity.

Not bad, but a bit annoying in spots.
