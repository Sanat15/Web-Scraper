Rating: 5/5

This 23 year old book has been on my to read list for like forever. It was part of my school library collection and I have no idea why I overlooked reading it then. First, George Littlechild's art is spectacular. The text is equally stunning. A boy asks his family the question, What's the Most Beautiful Thing You Know About Horses? Their responses tell us as much about them as they do about horses. I appreciate how much we learn about Van Camp's family and culture and their relationship to dogs.
