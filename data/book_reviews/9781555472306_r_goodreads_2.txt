Rating: 3/5

Set in the early 19thc, the h, Marianna, married Christopher St. Jules, hoping for a HEA, only to discover there was a dark, sinister side to her husband beneath his phony charm. After he goes one step too far, resulting in the death of their baby, she escapes, and starts a new life (as Maria Melton) with the help of some aristocratic friends, keeping her past hidden. When she meets the H, Prince Radu, she finds her true soulmate, but how can she tell him of her past? In a time when royal marriages have so many rules, Radu determines to break them and marry the woman of his dreams. But how can she marry him, when she's still Christopher's wife? Even if divorce weren't a scandal, how could she risk Christopher finding her and refusing to let her go? 

Having distanced herself - both physically and emotionally - from her former life, Marianna decides to push it aside altogether, and - as Maria - marry the man she loves.

And then the troubles begin!

a good story, but a bit too dark in spots, and unfortunately, it includes rape.
