Ah, Lucien Charon, or should I say Monsieur le Comte, like our h Troy (Victoria) how susceptible I am to your gallic charms. This was a delicious recherche du temps perdu story ie the kind of old fashioned, out of time type that takes you back to innocent days where you had to pre book a phone call and service was politely rendered. A little French fantasyland, although unlike the modern HP billionaire, this chap seemed to have a level of busyness, travel and preoccupation more in line with the actual work of being a banker/vigneron/landholder. Mind, our h is also a photographic model and rising star of sculpture with a refreshingly honest appreciation for a bit of alright. None of your fake struggling and flouncing here. The sexual tension is intermittent but good, despite the on page going no further than kisses (ah, the impudent thumb down the spine. Monsieur has all the right moves without being pepe le pew). Low angst, dreamy and just what I needed for a palate cleanse. The youngsters will find this one passes them by, I suspect. Nice cover.
