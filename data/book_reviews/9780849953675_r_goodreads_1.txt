Rating: 5/5

It was the lovely cover of this book that caught my eye while browsing at the bookstore. But since my bookshelf space is limited I replaced it on the shelf and moved on. Then a quiet inner voice whispered, There are valuable lessons for you in that book. I try to listen to these kinds of inner directives so I retraced my steps and picked the book up once more, quickly flipping through it again. 

I followed that gentle prompting and purchased the book, and over the next four months I read small portions of the book at a time, easy because the book is formatted into small essays, liberally interspersed with beautiful artwork and illustrations. The author, Joni Tada, is a quadriplegic and has been for over 30 years, yet all the beautiful artwork in the book is hers—lovingly and carefully painted with her mouth. Tada's insights and spiritual writings are personal and profound. I'm so grateful this book came into my life when it did. It's not only lovely to look at, it's also thought-provoking and very moving. 

 4.5 stars
