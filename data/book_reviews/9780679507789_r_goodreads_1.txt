Rating: 4/5

If you love sailing or plan to sail out of your harbor or bay, this is a wonderful read. It is not a romantic story but is a personal account of this man, Gordon and his wife Nina's sailing trip around the world back in the early 1970s. You will learn a bit about each port they put into and what you might expect even to this day along your own voyage. You can still purchase the book on Amazon for about a buck.
