Rating: 1/5

What a terrible story this was! I tried to make allowances for it being dated but that didn't make up for the fact that the heroine was the most obnoxious non-villainous character I've read in a long time! She was immature, bitchy, and rude with the hero turning every single encounter into a major misunderstanding. She was petty, jealous and underhanded with her sister, upset because of her own mistaken assumptions. Here's an idea: why don't you talk to your sister and ask her what's going on? 
She didn't seem to have one single redeeming feature and we are supposed to believe that the hero and heroine fell in love! When!?! Over a sleigh ride (which she ended up ruining - surprise, surprise) or maybe it was the fruit basket? All I know is that whiny, crazy, brat didn't deserve jade, take it back and run for your life, and if your thinking of reading this book I'd advise you to do the same! I wish I could...
