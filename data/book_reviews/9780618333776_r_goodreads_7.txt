Rating: 5/5

I thoroughly enjoyed this novel. It came across my way because my son was assigned to read it for school. Perhaps one of the reasons I liked it so much is that we live at the edge of Shenandoah and have trekked through many of the locations described in the book and have read about the history of SNP and the people who lived there. You can still easily find traces all through the park of fence rows and foundations. 
Regardless, we found the characters to be real and well drawn out. So much so that my son began asking about motives and connecting with the main character. (truly a groundbreaking moment) 
It's a quick read and a different insight into our collective national history, so I would definitely recommend it.
