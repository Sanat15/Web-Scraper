Rating: 3/5

I happen to be an E.L. Doctorow fan and believe he is one of the very few authors who has ever written truly compelling historical fiction.

Unfortunately this book lacks authenticity. I have no problem with the plot and the drama but I did not feel that this book was grounded to a place. It did not seem that a good deal of research went into it either when it was published in 1960 at the height of Western mania. 

Instead read Doctorow's World's Fair or Ragtime which are two of the best books that I've read by anyone. 

2.5 stars
