Rating: 1/5

The initial encounter between Catherine and John was quite irritating. Her groom has a broken leg and his horse possibly injured but instead of being rational &amp; revealing the situation and acquiring help from John, she dallies around and then only thinks of her reputation. I think the author could have chosen a much more reasonable method for the H/H to meet without making the Heroine look like a vexing idiot!

Yeesh, all of this trivial dialogue and misunderstandings just because the heroine refuses to be truthful. Then to contradict her own moral obligations, she then meets up with the hero unchaperoned to return the horse.

Plot-hole: Why is everyone overly concerned about Catherine being alone with John...when later, she is allowed to go on a picnic UNCHAPERONED? Eh?? 

Did not like the insta-romance either.
