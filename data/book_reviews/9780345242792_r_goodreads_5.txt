Rating: 2/5

This is a tale about underdog heroes who do not deserve to win. Seriously: they're under-prepared, unarmed ill-informed, and easily duped, yet these three (four? five?) cadets from the Undersea Fleet Academy think they can take on an army of militarized mermen and sea serpents and come out on top. They do everything wrong every step of the way, and are saved from multiple deaths only by the author's heavy hand.

Dumbed down a little more, it might be a good Young Adventure book for the broodlings. As it stands, though, it's just a prime example of why people used to sneer at science fiction.
