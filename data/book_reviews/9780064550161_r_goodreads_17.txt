Rating: 4/5

I bought this anatomy coloring book and the Netter's Anatomy Coloring Book. The Netter's book has the edge (imo) because of the much nicer/clean drawings and the addition of clinical notes. The text is also better organized (imo) and easier to read. BUT, having said that - I think this one has the benefit of coloring the words associated with the drawings (to help draw connections). I just wish the illustrations weren't so "muddy" and busy.
