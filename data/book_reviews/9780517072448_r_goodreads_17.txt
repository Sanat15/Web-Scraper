Rating: 4/5

I will concede that poetry has never occupied a place in my soul. But I tried then when I discovered “Selected Poems of Carl Sandburg”. 
Arrangements, he wrote about the practical battles of the toiling class. 
Having read and appreciated Sandburg’s profile of Abe Lincoln and “Rock” the sole tale he focused on, I reveled in his style of writing.
The accounts of his poetry express strong emotions and vigorous passions addressed out of a sentiment for the struggling class.
