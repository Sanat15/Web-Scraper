Rating: 5/5

One of the...

Best western I have ever read if not one of the best books I have read. The telling of the battle at the Alamo made me think I was actually there. You could feel the story as well as read it. 
This is not the first book I have read by this author by any means.
Just the first one that left feeling better informed about the fight for freedom from Mexico. I have been to the Alamo before but now I have to go back again.
