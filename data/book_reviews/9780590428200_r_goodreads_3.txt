Rating: 3/5

Darlene decides to whip the Pinecones into shape because they're too into goofing off and giving in to their defeatist attitudes. I did quite like this one, although their "trick" on Coach Miller and the Atomic Amazons at the end pretending Darlene's great grandmother was their spiritual coach was a bit strange. But it's a good story with some good lessons about trying your best and not giving up and having pride in your team and so on.
