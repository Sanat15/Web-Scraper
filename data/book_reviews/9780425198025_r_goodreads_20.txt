Rating: 5/5

My Kindle had to be charged so I grabbed a book that had been sitting for sometime on my night stand yesterday evening, it was an autographed copy of Amelia Grey's Hint Of Seduction. This book has a very charming avant guard heroine! I read through the night as I fell in love and was captivated by the story! This is an excellent light summer read, perfect for pool or beach! If you haven't read any of Amelia's books you are sorely missing out! I give this one 5 stars!
