Rating: 4/5

Found this book for $1 at a secondhand store. Never read a book so fast, until the end. I find the author repeated soo many things over and over again. I would have liked a bit more perspectives from the survivors. However, the book is quite old and maybe hasnt been written for our modern culture for True Crime. Side Note: for someone who is diehard true crime reader, I have actually never heard of this serial killer for some reason so I had absolutely no background information on this story so it was like I was learning about it for the first time so that may sway my opinion for the intrigue on this story. Nevertheless, lots of detail and loved it
