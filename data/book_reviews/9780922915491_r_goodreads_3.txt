Rating: 5/5

An excellent and important book that has only gained importance post-9/11. As big and shocking as the OKC bombing was back in 1995, in retrospect it looks more like a warm-up, a prelude to bigger things to come. To look back at OKC now is chilling, as one sees the 9/11 before 9/11, complete with railroaded "anti-terror" legislation, shadowy military/intelligence connections, a blatant coverup, etc, etc. 

This book is a must-read for anyone researching 9/11, and obviously for anyone researching OKC. It's well-researched, well-written, informative, and enlightening.
