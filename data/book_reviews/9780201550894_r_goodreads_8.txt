Rating: 5/5

Positives:

- Well grounded book.
- Good selection of topics.
- Added value to my understanding of procrastination. (!)
- Some relations of procrastination are interesting. (e.g. procrastination vs. time, procrastination vs. childhood)
- "Living and Working with Procrastinators" chapter is a good contribution imo. Especially the part named "Summary of suggestions for parents".
- And very very specifically, i liked this compact definition of happiness:
"Happiness comes from living well, according to your values. It comes from being connected to other people and to your innermost self. It comes from being able to accept that you are who you are -- and that what is, is, whether procrastination is part of your life or not, like being able to enjoy your garden - even with a few dandelions."

Negatives:

- Discussions might be shorter.
- More practical tips or examples can be given.
