Rating: 3/5

I so admire writers. The devotion, desire and discipline it takes to put out even one novel is amazing. And yet M. C. Beaton has written so many novels that she uses five pseudonyms. I can barely find the time to write short reviews. 🤷🏼‍♀️
 So as a fan, it pains me to not give this book 5 stars. But, although it is an adorable story, it felt a bit forced. Anyway, I still liked it and will keep going with the rest of the series! 😘
