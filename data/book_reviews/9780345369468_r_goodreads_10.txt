The same place and time of my ancestors, John "Renta Dan" Baker, a
long hunter with Daniel Boone, Richard Wooldridge, part of the troops under the command of George Rogers Clark, and his payroll and other records were documentation for making him one of my DAR patriots.
William McCollum, was one of the early settlers in Kentucky. He
married Angeline Baker, daughter of John Baker, and they were part of the group who left in the train of over 103 wagons from Russell County
Kentucky for Chariton County Missouri where my mother was born about
100 years later. Russell County broke off from Lincoln County, which
was one of the original three counties in Kentucky in early statehood.
