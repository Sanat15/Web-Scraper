Rating: 5/5

A friend loaned my husband and I this book back when it was first published in the ‘90s, recently I re-read it for Ladies Summer Bible Study at church. With a Biblical foundation it explores what life after death will be like. Though there is much we don’t know, the Bible does give some clear clues. First, when a believer that dies, their soul/spirit goes to heaven where God, the angles and other believes are. When Christ comes again there will be a new heaven and a new earth. Believers will be given physical bodies to live on the new earth, there will be work to do but it will be better than the Garden of Eden, there will be no sin as we interact with others, animals and nature. Most of all we will be with our Lord and Savior.
