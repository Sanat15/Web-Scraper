Rating: 3/5

I remember enjoying this book, but very little else about it. I've read a number of books by Campbell and have enjoyed them all. I come away from them knowing things I would never have even thought were things one might know. This one is about our perception of time and our various body clocks. The best part of the book, for me, was the story of the man who spent months in the bottom of a mine while scientists stuffed around with his various body clocks. Still suffering years later from the effects of this - it just goes to show that maybe there are some things we ought not to play around with.
