Rating: 5/5

So in the aftermath of Vol. 6, the village is in ruins, the Lord of the Locusts has his hooks in both Rose and Bone, Rose's creepy aunt is a living dead scythe-wielding killer, and the rat creatures are running rampant. Team Bone is racing to the ancient imperial capital for help but reality's unstable and the odds are not good.
Smith pulls it off again. Creepy and terrifying while keeping his characters on a human scale.
