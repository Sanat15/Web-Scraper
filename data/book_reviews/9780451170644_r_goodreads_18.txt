Rating: 3/5

This book wasn't bad, but I liked Anne's relationship with the twins far more than the romance between her and their guardian. It wasn't bad, but the only development of it seemed to be the fact that he kissed her twice and that he actually got to know the children. It wasn't terrible, but kind of bland overall. I did enjoy the meddlesome old lady frame story, though, even if it meant there were a ton of names that were hard to keep straight.
