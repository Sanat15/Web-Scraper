Rating: 5/5

AH! THIS WAS POSSIBLY THE BEST VOLUME YET!
 Volume 9 is also slightly longer than the other books in the series, but so many thing happen in this one! It was a mega-action-packed volume as we gear up for the finale of this arc and the main Sailor Moon Series. This volume provides the background information on the Outer Senshi and what their duties were for the Moon Kingdom and why they've been reborn in this era. Sailor Pluto/Setsuna bears some ominous news on Earth's future warning of the Sovereign's awakening. Uranus and Neptune decide they must kill Hotaru in order to stop her from destroying the Earth. In Sailor Moon's attempts to save Hotaru, she calls on the love of all the Scouts and transforms into Super Sailor Moon. Professor Tomoe is hard at work as well, and when Hotaru manages to snatch the Imperium Crystal from Rini, Mistress 9 is born. Drawing from the seemingly endless power of the Imperium Crystal, Mistress 9 threatens to blow up the planet...and Rini continues to grow weaker. Super Sailor Moon must decide if she has the strength to destroy what was once Hotaru's body in order to save the planet. 
 It was an amazing volume, one of the strongest in the series by far. Loved the background info on the Outer Senshi! Can't wait to read the close of this arc. :)
