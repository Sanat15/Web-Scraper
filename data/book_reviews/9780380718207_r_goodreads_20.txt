Rating: 5/5

Maybe his best

Neither the characters nor the events were dinky. All rang true. The people were "human" some of whom struggled with recognisable questions of morality while others were to cynical or hardened to even consider them. The sexual tension was classy &amp; satisfied. All the actors played well together as they crossed the world plotting nefarious deeds. Solve succeeded, some fell apart, no one was a super wo/man. All had classy feet. A thoroughly enjoyed romp in the shadows of the CIA.
