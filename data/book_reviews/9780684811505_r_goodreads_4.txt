Rating: 2/5

I wish this book was better researched. The research maybe fine for newspaper standards but not historical standards. 

I got this book as part of my research for a Historical novel I am writing. The absence of dates and time groups has been frustrating. Claims that certain people testified before congress would be in the congressional record but because he doesn't give dates the accounts can't be verified. 

I was turned off by the second paragraph when it was clear this book had an agenda. Yes John Lehman was no boy scout and there were and are problems in the military. But I got that this was not going to be an even handed account of Lehman's time as SECNAV. 

I would take this with a large grain of salt.
