The Black Snowman by Phil Mendez is about a family of two boys living with their single mom in an urban area. The older brother, Jacob, is sad and angry about being black. He believes that “everything black is bad.” His mother and younger brother, Peewee always try to remain hopeful and encouraging. 

This story holds a very important message for many young black kids who, similar to Jacob, are negatively impacted by poverty and lack of self-esteem. Jacob learns to believe in himself and feel good about his racial identity. Rating: 3.9
