Rating: 5/5

I have attempted on several occasions to read Whitman, only to fail. This time, I succeeded. I suspect that has much to do with our current political situation (the Trump debacle) as anything else. Whitman's overwhelming positivity and cheerleading for the Democratic experiment was precisely what i needed at this time; a reminder that we once nearly destroyed the country in a war of secession, and were surrounded by pandering and incompetent politicians as well as great ones. I can't recommend it highly enough. If you're looking for something bright and shining to remind you that progress isn't always easy, this book may well be it.
