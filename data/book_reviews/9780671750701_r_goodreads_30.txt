Rating: 4/5

A good, fun read. As always, Goddard delivers on suspense and twists, with the past coming back to complicate the present. For me, Goddard sometimes goes wrong in two ways: the plot twists can spin out of control, and his main characters can be merely pawns in a complicated game. In this book, the twists were under control (assuming you can suspend your disbelief, maybe a lot of it). As for the main characters, I realized they were lacking when my reaction to their romance was "OK, sure." 

When Goddard mixes his formula well, with the right amount of twists and good characters, we get Past Caring, Painting the Darkness, and Into the Blue.
