Rating: 3/5

Book 401 - Philip Hinchcliffe - Doctor Who and the Masque of Mandragora

Another historical tale… this time one that takes the 4th Doctor and Sarah-Jane Smith to 15th century Italy. It is a pseudo historical tale that mixes cults… gods and an energy that could mean the end of the world itself.

More of a romp than a thrill…unfortunately filled with stereotypical characters … the stand outs include…the first viewing of the alternative control room… the first of many times the Doctor looked as if he would get decapitated… and the most beautiful description of a near historical event that you can get.

Just ok…not spectacular with a mid of a messy ending. Unfortunate as Hinchcliffe was one of the great Doctor Who writers.
