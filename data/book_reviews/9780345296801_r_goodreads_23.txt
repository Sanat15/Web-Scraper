Rating: 2/5

Philip Jose Farmer is a brilliant writer - but this book is not it. The writing is a bit bland and weirdly paced. There's no poetry, no gut-punch moments of which he's so capable, instead there's meandering plot and repetition. The entire thing is 'We're chasing a baddie' -&gt; 'We got them' -&gt; 'Oh, maybe they're a goodie after all' -&gt; 'We're chasing a new baddie'  over and over again, until their rag-tag bunch is a huge crowd and people need killing off in great unceremonious batches.  If you removed the repetitive plot cycles, you could delete three quarters of the pages and still have basically the same book.

Good overall concept and a reasonably escapist read, but Consider Phlebas / Ringworld are more involving for 'the journey', and Tau Zero / Schild's Ladder are better alternatives for pure scifi concepts.
