Rating: 5/5

I loved this! It is told so well from a child’s perspective but with the underlying story that an adult can pick up on easily. I loved reading about Anna’s resilience as she adapts to Switzerland and then to Paris. I especially loved the Paris section because Anna and her brother Max have to learn a whole new language. Their struggles and how they eventually feel at home is so well told. And then the abrupt moving again to England at the end — thank God they moved when they did (1935)! I wish the story had gone on but by then you know Max and Anna will adapt yet again. It will be hard but they have done it before and they get to be together as a family. Such a moving story and funny too!
