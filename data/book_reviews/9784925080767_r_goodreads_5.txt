2.5

A blood-soaked tragedy and a reader left disorientated and stupefied.

I have to confess… may the gods forgive me…halfway through the book I was strongly tempted to commit some atrocities towards all the characters of the book. 
The plot was so puzzling, I doubt the murderer/s knew themselves why or how or when they were committing the crimes. 

The patriarch of the Inugami family has died and left an extremely complicated will. 
A will so convoluted that I felt sorry for the deceased’s attorney trying to explain its multiple clauses to the hostile family members. 

Needless to say, the will sets in motion a series of murders that based on the long and boring narrative, I bet even the writer himself had a hard time explaining and solving. 

This is the case of the movie would be much better than the book.
