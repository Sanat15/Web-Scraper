Rating: 4/5

I actually never thought of reading this book. What prompted me to read it was that I met someone mentioned in the cast of characters. I was curious to know more about this person. I have read Seale's other book on Assad and thought it was well written and very detailed.He certainly exposed the dark side of human nature. Seale is very knowledgeable and his research is thorough. I believe he also speaks Arabic and his wife is an Arab. This is unusual for authors writing about the Middle East. Apparently this is the one area of the world where you need no expertise and can write whatever no matter how racist or incorrect and get away with it. Seale does not fall into this category.
