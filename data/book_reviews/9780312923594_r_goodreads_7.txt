Rating: 4/5

The "Star Wreck" parody novels at times haven't been the best, but "The Generation Gap" by Leah Rewolinski is actually one of the better ones. In this edition, the crew of the USS Endocrine is on a mission to figure out a way to handle a race known as the Cellulites who were put under due to issues w/ weight loss. However, the original crew of the Endocrine under James Smirk decides that Ricardo's plan isn't something they want a part of causing them to do a saucer separation and take their part of the crew elsewhere. Rewolinski's take on this parody is at times almost comedic genius as she literally does things with both crews that at times we the reader could only dream of. The book is clearly designed to be a farce, and is a good one at that.
