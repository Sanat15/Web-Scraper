Rating: 3/5

One of the craziest lost in the woods tales ever. Near-sighted assessor of internal revenue from Montana, for whatever reasons, joins an expedition to explore Yellowstone. One day, he gets off his horse to check out the trail and his horse wanders off leaving him stranded and alone without supplies. Spends the next 37 days lost in the Wyoming wilderness. How this man survives is beyond me. He seriously burns himself sleeping above a hot springs, starts a forest fire, and practically starves to death. While he recounts his rescue and praises God and the rescuers’ generosity for saving him, he fails to disclose that he refused to pay the rescuers the promised reward, claiming he could have found his own way out (despite confessing that he was near death when they found him and they had to nurse him back to health before they could move him to Bozeman). Honestly, God should have just smote him on the spot after that. Where were those mountain lions when you needed them.
