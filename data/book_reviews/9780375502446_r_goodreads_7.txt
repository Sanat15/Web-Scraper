Rating: 5/5

To me this book is a Must Read for anyone who has faced cancer, is interested in medicine, or research. 
This is a history of why we have medical treatments for many dieseas today, including most cancers. 

I enjoyed watching young Judah, grow up while living in a very traditional Jewish home with his father, the Rabbi. Young Juda was inspired when he accompanied his father on his father's many visits with patients in hospitals. Judah went on to change the world.
