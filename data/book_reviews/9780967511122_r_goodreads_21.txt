Rating: 5/5

Star rating: ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

This is the second time I've read this book, and I still haven't read the first book in the series. As a big fan of talking creatures, ancient mysteries, and clocks, I love this book. Hermux Tantamoq is one of the cutest main characters I've ever read, and I consider myself something of a talking-rodent-in-clothes connoisseur. I hope to read the first book at some point, but it feels like no library on earth has a copy.
