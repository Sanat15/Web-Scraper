Rating: 4/5

Love this book as a glimpse into a bygone era of Central Asia. Murphy underwent her epic journey at the perfect time to capture the polarization of life in this part of the world. While some areas were gripped in the wheels of progress, others were blissfully unaware and going about life as it had been for centuries. 

My only qualms are the occasional confusions that arise from the fact that this novel is a compilation of her journals. I found myself wishing there were maps included and maybe the occasional foot note to explain her connection to some of the reoccurring characters or themes.
