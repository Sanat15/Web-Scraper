Rating: 1/5

I’ve been chewing this over for a couple of days because I acknowledge that we all have different tastes and opinions and, hey, who’s to say I could do any better..... But the overriding message that this book left me with was that the dad actually meant to pour acid over the mum and she was a bitch who probably deserved it. 

Whether the writing was any good or not has become a moot point because of the sympathetic stance towards a perpetrator of domestic violence. This wasn’t written long enough ago to be forgiven for place and time. 

It’s a big no from me.
