Rating: 4/5

Reading this 1948 book now in our times is bound to be flavored with rear view mirror perspectives…. The most interesting facet to me is the categorization of personality types into 4 groups, a tone nowadays transformed into a more balanced and holistic view of the human being through neuro-scientific research. It also describes jealousy of people more talented than you, irrational ire of the masses fueled by a lying demagogue…. It’s set in the aftermath of smaller nuclear disaster and avoids Cold War politics.
