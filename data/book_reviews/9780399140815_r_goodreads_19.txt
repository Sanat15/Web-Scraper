Rating: 3/5

Long read. I finished the first book in the series and started this right away. Mistake. While the book wasn’t bad, it just wasn’t great. I really like New Orleans, which was probably why I started reading. There wasn’t that much local flavor though. It’s a good read, I guess, but just not for me. Maybe I had too much of Tubby too quickly. After awhile I will try book three and see if I change my mind.
