Rating: 4/5

Playing For Time has Ryder O'Neil who is a genius and works for a very top secret anti-terrorist agency. When Ryder first meets Joanna Stratton she is testing her skills as a make up artist by making herself appear to be a 70 year old woman. He is very kind to this "elderly" lady which Joanna thinks is a wonderful character trait of his.

I really enjoyed this story right from the beginning and wanted to find out what happened next. There was humor, mystery and romance in Playing For Time. A perfect book for me.

Even though I truely enjoyed this book, there were times that I was confused about Ryder's job. I would have liked more details on what he did and for who.

This is the first book I've read by Barbara Bretton and it was quite easy to get into. I will definitely be reading more of her work.
