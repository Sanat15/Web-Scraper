Rating: 5/5

A fantastic read, each chapter tackles many topics of faith and personal secular-world struggles. Mother Angelica answers a ton of core questions to the best of her ability with assistance from Bible Scripture to describe and interpret God's message. 

For anyone seeking to strengthen their beliefs or desire to go to ground zero on reflection and growth on their own spiritual state, then this is a top read for you! 

Mother Angelica gives common sense, compassion, and encouragement to help the everyday Christian/Catholic. This book reveals the beautiful things available to you at the end of the road--if you chose to love God back over the alternative. We learn in this book to humble ourselves through acknowledging/tackling our flaws; we also begin to see in this text why sorrow, pain, and joy are necessary to lean into our desire for God. 

With our personal trials, repentance, and trust in God, we can all strive to be better to one another and get to Heaven! Have faith and be strong, don't make excuses, or get comfy like the Devil wants you to. Challenge your mind and soul, and you will feel whole.
