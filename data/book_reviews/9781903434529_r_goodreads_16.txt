Rating: 3/5

The first thing I want to say is: the cover of this book is beautiful. It’s what made me buy it without even knowing what it was about.
The Witch Trade follows four main characters: Abby (who is looking for her missing parents), Spike (who doesn't remember who he is or how he arrived in Abby's town), Captain Starlight (who is on a quest to find Ice Dust), and Benbow (a giant albatross). Together, they are drawn into a world of magic and must battle evil witches.
If you have children or know any young readers, I highly recommend reading this series to them or buying it for them to read themselves. I honestly don't know why it isn't more popular; it truly deserves more hype. The story is easy to follow and the world-building isn't complicated at all, making it easy to visualize the setting. I’ve added this to the list of books I want to read to my baby cousins, because I know they are going to love it!
