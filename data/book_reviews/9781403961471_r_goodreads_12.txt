Rating: 5/5

A committed set of lessons on the vital art of poetry - craft, technique, purpose, value - by a great poet. His long essay on the history and craft of free verse is unmatched. Even when discussing the details of prosody which many might find too "technical" the true life of the artist is always behind it. It's a gift to the reader to overhear Dobyns' appreciation and understanding of the practitioners he returns to the most as his heroes/heroines. His in depth knowledge spans a lot of territory across the years across geography and his compassion is noble.
