Rating: 4/5

Theroux’s bite-sized double novella concerning the double lives of two unwitting transgressors fed me exactly what I ordered: a seedy, guilty-pleasure read that was textured and tantalizing as I consumed it… but a bit insubstantial and devoid of anything actually nourishing upon digestion. &amp; that’s ok! It was provocative &amp; cunning enough to keep me interested in either protagonist’s foray into the underbelly of the Western world. A swift thriller overall, even if it was a bit of an eye roll at times.
