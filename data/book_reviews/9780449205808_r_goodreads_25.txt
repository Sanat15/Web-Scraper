Rating: 4/5

What an absolutely delightful story. It was hilarious in some parts, frustrating in others, but well crafted and fun. 

I have to say that I listened to this as an audiobook and the narrator did a good job of characterizing each voice, but almost seemed as if she hadn't read the material beforehand. Odd stops and starts as if she were reading it on the fly. This only slightly dampened the appeal of the book and because she did such a good job with the voices, I can forgive the odd rhythm of her reading. 

A fun read and a great romance of the time. Can't wait to go on to the other sisters.
