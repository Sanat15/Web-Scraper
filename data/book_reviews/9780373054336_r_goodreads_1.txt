3.5 stars
This was a nice sweet oldie. The heroine's older sister enters her into a contest to shake the heroine out of her boring and staid life. She wins a date with the hero, and they connect. She also forms a bond with his daughter who suddenly lands back into his life when his ex enters rehab. They quickly form a bond, exchange words of love and the hero asks her to marry him but she is unsure. There is a minor misunderstanding which is heroine's lack of faith in herself but that gets quickly resolved and HEA.
