Rating: 4/5

I'm not sure I can describe this. The authorial voices are luminous and get under your skin. Each sentence is beautifully crafted and filled with feeling. Each story is a unique and painful rendering of a woman's love for one man. It felt like I was being carried along in a dream. It's certainly not a page-turner, but I did keep thinking about the characters and wondering what would happen to them. I would not reread this straight through again. But I think it's the perfect book to dip into on a cold and quiet afternoon and just relish what words on a page can convey.
