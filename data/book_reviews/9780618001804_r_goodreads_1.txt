Rating: 4/5

I’m surprised there aren’t more reviews on this book. I picked it up at a local library, and what caught my eye is it didn’t have the bold tones of “Change your life!” screaming at me on the cover. It felt more like an invitation to read, and was enjoyable. One of the quotes at the beginning of the book resonated with me. 
“One of the saddest lines in this world is, ‘oh come on now - be realistic’. The best parts of this world were not fashioned by those who dared to look hard at their wishes and gave them horses to ride.” - Richard N. Bolles 
This book, at the very least, gave me a good dose of inspiration. There is even a list of inspiring things to do to get your inner self motivated and moving. 4/5 stars.
