Rating: 4/5

I've been looking for this book - or a book like it - for ages. I often wondered what might happen if you turned the juice up to eleven and an half. Now I know. 
This text is so densely packed, it’s a zip file of word play and abstract violence; a comic book without pictures. It's fractal in depth - just about every sentence is worth a second read. More like a very long poem, or at least a chaos engine connected to a typewriter. The work!
One star removed for being a smart arse.
