Rating: 5/5

I remember picking this up at the English bookshop in Zürich when I was nine or ten. It was around Christmas, and I have a distinct memory of reading the dark, folklore-y stories in front of the fire, and of it being my favorite book for quite some time. The book consists of three novellas - a mysterious game that comes to life, a person whose soul is tethered to a candle, a magical book that transports you to another world - and I don't think they have anything to do with one another beyond all of them beginning in the real world and slowly shifting into eldritch, frightening territory. I especially liked The Candle Man with its waterlogged imagery and something about a drowned town and a spectral figure, and just the delightful coziness of reading a scary story in supreme comfort while the outside world is snowy and dark.

I don't know if I'd ever want to read it again, as it probably wouldn't live up to my memories. But it also doesn't really matter what I would think of it now, because for the age when I read it it was a perfect, perfect book.
