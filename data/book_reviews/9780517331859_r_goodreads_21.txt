Rating: 4/5

If you are looking for a Blatty novel that rivals Exorcist or Legion, sorry folks, but you will have to keep looking. If you want a quick but engaging read with some odd twists and a thought provoking ending, well this is your book. Here lay the story, character, and concept seeds of the Exorcist. It's almost as if Blatty used this book as a testing ground for the ideas he would later explore in his best work. How can a just God and evil coexist? Can a person be good if they have committed bad deeds? Are we disgusting base animals or angels waiting to get our wings? Who decides all this and when?.
Hudson Kane is an obvious precursor to the stoic Father Karras and his actions at the books end echo the resolve of the boxer-priest.There are no other analogs to Exorcist characters, but we do get an asylum full of Air Force officers that may or may not be faking insanity, a Gothic mansion owned by the widow of a horror movie star, and a character in the form of Cutshaw that is deserving of his own book.
If you are a fan of the Exorcist, Blatty, or just interesting novels in general, do yourself a favor and give this one a read.
