This book gives a mafia slant to who done it regarding the JFK Aassasination. I read his Pearl Street Mafia book too, same thing.

Despite the fact I see other players playing a bigger role, I am still glad to get detailed dirt on J Edgar and Billy Sol Estes and most of the LBJ circle of corruption.

You get a day to day summary of what was happening 1961-1963. It gets repetitive, and seems sort of robotic at times, but the nuggets of truth/ info keeps you going.
