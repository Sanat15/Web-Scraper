Rating: 4/5

Overall, I enjoyed a lot of these stories. Naturally, as a collection of twenty different short stories, you get a pretty wide spread of subject matters and styles. Many of these focus on a grounded, setting, with most tending towards historical fiction. Not every story will be your favorite, and you might not even like every story here, but I feel like there's enough stories that the typical reader will find at least one author that speaks to them.

I especially recommend this book for any aspiring writers trying to figure out their own style, voice, sense of narrative structure, and more. You'll have plenty of inspiration to inform what you could do through your own writing
