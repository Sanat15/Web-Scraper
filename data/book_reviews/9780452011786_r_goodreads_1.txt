Rating: 4/5

I really like this book. It gives excellent information for people considering whether to go to law school: What do lawyers do? How do you pay for law school? What's the typical law school classroom like? There are chapters with special notes for minorities, LGBT students, women, and parents. The second half of the book has chapters introducing the standard first-year courses, plus professional responsibility and jurisprudence.

I still recommend it, even though the latest edition is 1997.
