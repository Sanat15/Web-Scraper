Rating: 5/5

This was the best book I’ve read - or at least easily in the top 3! Madeleine L’Engle is brilliant and a masterful narrator - she seamlessly weaves an insightful framework for the intersection of Christianity and art in a way that I’ve never heard but at the same time have known my entire life.

She always was realistic and practical which I found helpful and endearing

Without a doubt, this is a book I will be revisiting frequently for a long time
