Rating: 4/5

History is about what happened not what might have happened so the author emphatically repeats because it is difficult to abstract SEA’s history into neat patterns for generalisation. The book carefully navigates ‘classical’ period, pre and post war changes, focusing on the developments due to colonisation and revolution, mainly from a political perspective. Extremely useful for anyone wanting a more accurate context that informs the why of the present and its future trajectory for the region.
