Rating: 3/5

1. None
2. Grades 2-4
3. Shortly after his grandfather dies, a little boy asks his family members what they were doing the day he died. Everyone remembers Grandad Bill in different ways and reminisce to get them through the very difficult time.
4. I though this was a very sweet book. It is quite sad, so it is not something I would keep in my classroom library year-round, however, if there is a death in one of the student's families, this could be a beneficial book to read to them or the class to explain what they are going through and how they are feeling. 
5. This could be a good book to read to a class if one of the students had a death in the family. If it is not appropriate to read to the actual student, then it could be read to the rest of the class so they have a better understanding of what is going on in their classmate's life and how they are likely feeling.
