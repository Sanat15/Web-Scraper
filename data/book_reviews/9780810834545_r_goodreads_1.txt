Rating: 4/5

I'm not exactly sure I'd call this a biography as opposed to the history of 19th century baseball with some personal details thrown in. That's not to state it's not interesting, because it is. In fact, there are very many stories about the history of National, American, Players and Federal leagues and why certain leagues succeeded or failed. Also about an early (failed) version of the player union. Owners and players have been "going at it" for 150 years now...the more things change the more they stay the same. Either way, John Montgomery Ward seems to have had a very impressive life inside and outside baseball.
