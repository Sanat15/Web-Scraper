4 Stars! ~ Kit witnesses her husband, Derrick, murder a man and she runs; goes into hiding. Luke's sure that Kit knows what happened to his brother, they disappeared at the same time. When a news camera catches Kit's face on film, Luke can now find her and her new baby. Lucky for Kit, Luke reaches her before her husband. Together they try to unearth the evidence needed to put Derrick away. This was a good suspense. Kit and Luke make a great pair.
