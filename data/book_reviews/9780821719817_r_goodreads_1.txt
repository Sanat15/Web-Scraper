Rating: 3/5

According to my kindle, I put this down at 66%. Can't imagine why. I seem to remember liking what I read up to that point. The beautiful heroine was sold into slavery, the hero buys her, drunkenly rapes her (I think he believes she is coming on to him, but she isn't), then feels bad and gives her a cushy job working for him at his mill (or mine). There are some bad guys out to get her, and the hero and heroine begin to fall in love. Lots of sexual tension. I definitely need to pick this one back up soon. And don't pass it by just because of it's dismal kindle edition cover!!!
