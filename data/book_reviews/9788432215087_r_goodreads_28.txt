Rating: 1/5

I read this years ago,so can't review it in detail.
But I didn't like it,a very unpleasant book.

An American woman marries an Iranian man,and then goes to Iran,with him.According to her,she then lands in a living nightmare.

What spoiled it for me was how much fault she finds with everything Iranian.(I'm no fan of the Iranian revolution,and the repression it has unleashed).

However,this book looked like a hate filled propaganda piece to me.
