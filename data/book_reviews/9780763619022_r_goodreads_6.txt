Rating: 5/5

Loved reading this book to my little girls every night (because this is a children's book). Super confused by the negative reviews by grown adults critiquing this book as though it was claiming to be literature. 

You will have a wonderful time reading this book to your kids. The rythm and prose of the writer help your kids fall asleep while the story remains exciting and thought provoking. It teaches lessons in simple enough form for 5 to 12 year olds to understand. 

Highly recommend.
