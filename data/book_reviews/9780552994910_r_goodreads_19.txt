Rating: 3/5

It’s a long time since I read anything by Mary Wesley- reading this I remembered why (insert gritted teeth emoji). The beginning reads very much like Elizabeth Jane Howard’s (superior imo) Cazalet Chronicles but after a couple of chapters things unravel at quite an alarming rate. I wasn’t keen on reading about the repeated abuse of young Sophy (spelt Sophie on the back jacket) or the behaviour of most of the cousins &amp; of course, Grumpy Richard. I loved the Cornish setting &amp; much of the banter but the rest just came across as a little seedy and depressing.
