Rating: 5/5

This was one of my favourite books as a very young child! I didn't even know what it was called because I didn't know French at the time. (My version was in French) I don't even know of there is an English version. I just discovered now at 37 years old that the film company Topcraft, who crafted one of my favourite films as a child, The Last Unicorn, helped to create the first film in a series that this book is based off of. It's a shame that this was never reprinted! It was such a great book that I still remember how much I loved it &amp; would make up the story on each page because I couldn't read the French at the time. I didn't start learning French until Grade 3.
