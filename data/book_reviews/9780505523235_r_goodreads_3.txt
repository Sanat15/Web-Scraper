Rating: 3/5

This story has the potential for greatness. It transcends many years, the main characters are fleshed out and their love is beautiful. It was very frustrating when the misunderstandings started happening. They happened one after another resulting in the hero and herione to kept apart for a very large portion of the story. Absurdities and the abrupt ending has me knocking off and a few stars to what could have been such a great book.
