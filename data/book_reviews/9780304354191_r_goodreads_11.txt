Rating: 3/5

I unevenly enjoyed this book. First half was much stronger than back half , propped up primarily by quotations by Walter Harris’ journal when living within the makhzen. Read kinda game of thrones-y , liked learning more about feudal Morocco. Second half was quite sensationalist sacrificing historical accuracy for flashy unsourced inferences. Meh. Also one of the brothers names was madani and everytime I read it a picture of zohran mamdani came in my head
