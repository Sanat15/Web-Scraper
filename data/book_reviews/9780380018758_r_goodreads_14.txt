Rating: 5/5

July 2026: The second book I’ve finished today that ends with a glorious wedding! Such a delightful ending to a delightful story. I so enjoyed rereading this with some lovely friends. I think the time is right to give this to my friends’ kids for Christmas this year too. Can I just say that Marmaduke Scarlet is my favorite?! 

Absolutely adored this. Read as part of the @elizabethgoudgebook club readalong in July 2019. I can see parallels to Harry Potter in this, and I wonder if C.S. Lewis read it too because I can also see echoes of this book in Narnia. Really lovely imagery of heaven and a world alive with imagination and wonder and delight and beauty.
