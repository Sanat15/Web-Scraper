Into the Porcupine Cave and Other Odysseys: Adventures of an Occasional Naturalist by William W. Warner (National Geographic Society 1999)(508.0) is a great collection of tales by one who was a budding naturalist in the 1930's and 1940's. The stories feature snorkeling on reefs in the South Pacific during World War II, howler monkeys in the Amazon basin, porcupines as pets in a boarding school dormitory, and whale watching before whale watching was cool. I enjoyed every word of this! My rating: 7.5/10, finished 8/19/13.
