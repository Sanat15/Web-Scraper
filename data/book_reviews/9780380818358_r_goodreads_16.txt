Rating: 3/5

Although I really enjoyed the first 3 books of the King's Blades series - - this one reminded me too much of the first book.
With the exception of who joined Beaumont to fight the evil Czar at the end of the book, this one was just okay. Too many similarities to the first book. Beaumont (our Paragon) was interesting - very similar to Durendal in the first 3 books. More Sex, some gore, and it took 350 pages to tell the back story. But I do enjoy the Blades.
