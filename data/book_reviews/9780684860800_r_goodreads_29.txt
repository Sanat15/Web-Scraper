Rating: 2/5

while i've seen his books everywhere, i've never read this author before. 
I can understand why he's so popular. I also see the point some make about him being "too formulaic" but as this was my first of his, i didn't have much of a frame of reference.
apparently, this was a story with a spin-off character from his more popular Dirk Pitt series. (which i've never read)

the characters were likable. good story that kept my interest.
lots of action. moved at a good pace. 
and like a lot of action-adventure thrillers, there are a lot of characters &amp; a lot going on. (ie. multiple locations around the world...) which was my negative.

i would read another of his books but not in ebook format. It just took too long that way. (the page count increases by about 3X)
