Rating: 4/5

Daniel Stanton, Lord Corvin mason to Earl of Sinclair, has tried to live down the family heritage. Womanizing, abuse, pirates, and thievery. He is know as Saint Corvin due to his opinions on the sanctity of marriage. When he married it will be to a scandal free woman of virtue.

Elaine Guinivere Morgaine Borne Marshall Courtney, Guin, is the head school mistress at Morgan House. Hiding from her scandalous past, she teaches both girls how to over one the obstacles to avoid her fate.

Lady Emmaline Henrietta Caroline Stanton, Daniel's sister was raise away from her father and brother due to the meanness and cruelty the Earl was famous for. Now with her stepmother wanting so run of everything, Norah the Courtesan Countess as she is known, has begun a campaign to bring the downfall of Emmaline.

Norah is also mistress to our two time villain Sir Mortimer Septimus. He was the devil behind all the rumors of Guin when she was married to Sir Alfred Marshall. Now with no money and thinking he could bully his way to Emmaline's money they skeem their way thru escapades and fobbles. 

Meanwhile, Daniel is so taken with the mysterious woman rider that will not give him her name. No matter what he tries she stays just out of reach until her horse panics and she is unseated. Saint Daniel to the rescue. 

Will his mystery woman be his? Will she ever give him a name? Does she learn to trust him? Will she ever love him? Does Sir Mortimer get to wed Lady Emmaline? Will the money be his? Will Norah get what she deserves or will she be forever known as the Corteaean Countess? Will earl Stanton ever see past his own greedy wants and get to know his children?

Find out in the laugh out loud romp through Hyde Park, Vauxhall Gardens, and London streets and rookeries. You will be entertained with great characters, thrilling plotlines and witty banter. So come along as this wild adventure will enthralled you to the end.
