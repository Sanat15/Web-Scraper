Rating: 2/5

I gave the novel 2 stars because I don't normally read genre books, so it may have more appeal to those who do read mystery novels. I don't recommend it generally.

The story begins well with one professor chasing another professor car-chasing another professor on account of a stolen object. There's a fight and both professors disappear. One of them, badly beaten is taken into the "witch's house" - that is, the house of an old woman who is a bit insane. That's how the book got it's name, but there's nothing witchy in the novel other than that so the title is manipulative. 

The crime and who did it is made clear by the author at the beginning, but there's a subplot involving twins in the story. The author hints that they have something to hide as one of them talks about "fingerprints" and seems oily. At the end of the book I expected to find out what the twin actually did and were hiding, but nope. One of the characters asks if the twins might have been involved in a murder and a detective says they are "looking into that." No resolution. Along with the title, this also is manipulative because I read the book waiting to find out what the twins were hiding.
