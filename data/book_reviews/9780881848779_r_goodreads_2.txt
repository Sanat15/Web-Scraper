Rating: 4/5

I picked this up at the Amarynth Bookstore in Evanston during a break from work and read it soon after purchase.

The Kennedy assassination was a major event of my childhood and questions about it started becoming apparent to me thanks to the radio talkshow I listened to a few years later during the sophomore year of high school. I believe it was Mark Lane that I heard interviewed, but soon others followed.

Since then I've maintained some interest in the possibility that the Warren Commission Report was a whitewash, an interest heightened when my friend Michael Miley started doing research on the matter in order to write an article about it around the time of the release of Oliver Stone's JFK film. Since then I've read quite a few books on the matter.

This book seems to demonstrate that Hoover and his FBI failed to prevent the crime, failed to responsibly investigate it and participated in the concoction of the substsantially falacious Warren report. That Hooever was active in planning or executing the planned assassination is not proved, but his motives for doing so are amply demonstrated.
