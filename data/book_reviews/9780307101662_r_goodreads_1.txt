This book is by, Linda Apolzon. The illustrations to this book are by, Kathy Wilburn. This book was published in 1986. The book "I'll Share With You" is about a brother and sister (Kelly and Carl) who take turns on the swing and share a wagon, but when it comes to Kelly's dolls, she won't share with Carl. She has to learn that it's lonely when no one is playing dolls with her. I would recommend this book to children in kindergarten and the first grade.
