Rating: 4/5

Ahh! To be a wee red-haired Scottish Lass in the mid-1700's who works like a drudge and knits while she walks, but still has time for adventures. She has a bit of a tongue, our Jeannie does, when provoked and she'll tell the honest truth and do the hard thing. And all this at the time of- and near- the Battle of Culloden. The ultimate escape for me. And also (though I have no right to) when I read conversations in the dialect, I feel I've gone home.
