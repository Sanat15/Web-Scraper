Rating: 4/5

The House that Jack built... not what I expected it to be, it was much better!!! This is the first book by Catherine Barry that I have read and I really loved it.
I loved the characters and how she told us Jack's story, how she brought Jack to life. Not everyone has a smooth ride in life and this is especially true of Jack, this book takes us on a journey, Jack's journey in fighting her demons and coming to terms with herself and her life.

i would recommend this book to readers of woman's fiction, a very well written story that will stay with me for some time.

Thank you to Endeavour Press and Netgalley for the copy.
