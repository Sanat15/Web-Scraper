Rating: 5/5

This book has everything. A brother and sister twins where sister dresses in men’s clothing and hates men but falls in love with and marries an Earl. A younger sister who steals her older sister’s fiancé. Two older women looking to marry and are much on the prowl and an older woman who is desperate to marry well now that her money has run out and she’s wearing dresses cut so low in the front that half her nipples show. Then there’s Harriett Pym playing matchmaker.

All in all a thoroughly delightful read!
