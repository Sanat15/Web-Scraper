Rating: 5/5

The standard poetry collection that everyone who studies German uses. I'm pretty sure I've read every poem in it, many of them over and over again. I have notes in my edition from discussions in several classes and also from later reading. I've owned it since 1982, but I'm pretty sure I started reading poems from it in high school several years earlier. I've used it so much that the spine is taped up. And it looks like the tape needs to be replaced. It's one of my bedside books.
