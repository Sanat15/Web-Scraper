Super good book. I dunno why it took me so long to read this author. I can't remember the last time I was so invested in a character that when it looked like someone dear to them was about to die, I actually had to stop reading. It's probably been at least five years And the ending was so great that I couldn't stop smiling. I hope all this author's books are this good cuz there are quite a few and I'd love to have dozens of books this good in front of me.
