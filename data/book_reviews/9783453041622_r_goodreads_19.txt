Rating: 5/5

This was my first John Saul book and it certainly will not be my last. Sir, how DARE you play with my emotions like that. From the constant gaslighting, to the evil scientist, to the DRAMA. I loved this whole book besides one part. And even then that part was necessary to show how evolved Mark’s character became. This book made me rage most of the time and I still loved it. It kept me enraptured and I could hardly put it down. This is your typical 80’s book and I’m fucking here for it.
