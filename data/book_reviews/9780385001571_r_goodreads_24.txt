Rating: 4/5

I learned so much about the Adamses. I’ve come to really admire John’s intellect, perseverance, and strong convictions, along with his willingness to stand by them—even when it meant disagreeing with others, including close friends. I’m just as struck by Abigail’s faithfulness, her respect and support for her husband, her deep love for her family, and her generosity toward everyone she encountered. Reading this made me want to grow—to be a better wife and mom.

The last 100 pages leaned heavily into politics and policy, which I didn’t enjoy as much. I understand its importance, but it wasn’t as engaging for me—that’s the only reason I landed at 4 stars.
