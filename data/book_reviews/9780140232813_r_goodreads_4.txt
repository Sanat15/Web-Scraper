Rating: 5/5

After being blown away by Waiting to Surface I was anxious to read more of Emily Listfield's work and Best Intentions didn't disappoint. The characters were likable and believable. Nothing could be more realistic than Lisa's ongoing trauma as her company is bought out, that's for sure.
The twists and turns were effectively woven into the ongoing everyday life we all have to deal with, even in extraordinary times. It is a thoroughly satisfying read; the only problem is the urge to skim in the race to find out what happens next.
