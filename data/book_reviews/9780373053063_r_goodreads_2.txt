Out by the fence, the prickly pear cacti were in full bloom. Sometimes, he thought, the ugliest plants put out the most beautiful flowers. He guessed there was a man back the road a piece who wouldn't argue with that statement one bit. And neither would the woman who'd put the bloom there.  

One of the sweetest and emotional DPs I've ever read.
This is the story of Erin and Ty.
Erin is a model. When she visits her good friend Bruce on the ranch, she doesn't expect to meet the glaring eyes of his brother Ty. Ty is judgmental of the sweet belle who shows up on his ranch.
Soon the resentment leads to a night filled with passion- but the jealous brother Bruce intervenes- creating misunderstanding between the couple. 
Months later, Ty's cruel words send Erin running- to a life threatening accident and a miscarriage. 
When half an year hence, Bruce dies and leaves his heritage to Erin, and the truth about his lies is exposed.
Ty is heartbroken over his treatment of Erin and spends his time wooing her in his own ways.
It's a complex love story. Unlike the usual trope, the hero has a couple of insecurities and they are exposed gradually. It's rarely in romance where you see a hero confessing his inexperience- and the hero and heroine discovering their passions together. 
The heroine is strong and lovable. 
There is a heavy dose of angst. Some confessions are made, some hidden. A small separation follows by a sweet HEA. 
I really enjoyed this story. 
Safe
4.5/5
