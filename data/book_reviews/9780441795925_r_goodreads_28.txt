Rating: 3/5

Don't effing read this. Excuse my French, but just like the last book, this one jumps around like a rabbit on crack. Except this rabbit is also on a trampoline! I realize it's a series, and you may want to read it, but my job is to discourage you as much as possible, cause it's.... eh. This story is narrated by Daystar, the 16 year old son of Queen Cimorene and King Mendanbar. However, I don't want to write more about the plot, because the continuity is so horrible, I thought I'd finished the book halfway through and it felt like the beginning at the end. Yes, I'm exaggerating. No, I don't care that I'm exaggerating, because that truly is how the book felt to me. Don't read it. Not unless you happen to enjoy going through a portal back to the beginning at random points throughout the book.
