Rating: 4/5

Overall, this was enjoyable and a really easy read. That works out great for me since all my books lately feel heavy. And I know I’m an adult, but something about this book was so intense. It was the fate of a defenseless creature, and I spent the entire time so worried about the fate of said creature that I really couldn’t enjoy all the action. I’m good to read books about adults dying or in danger (even though sometimes this is heavy too), but I think I’m just too soft about living things that are so young and in trouble.
