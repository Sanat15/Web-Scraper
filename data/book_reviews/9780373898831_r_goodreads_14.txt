Rating: 2/5

I first read this book about 20 years ago when I was 16. I remember really liking the book and wishing I could be Annette. I decided to reread the book this weekend. It’s a fast read but so cringey. Whether Annette was 17 or 20 she was still too young for 33 year old Josh. Also, I could barely get past the ending of Josh tattling on Annette to her father. They placed such importance on her virginity. As if people didn’t have sex before marriage in the 80’s or 90’s. Also, it took him mere minutes to decide that he in fact wanted to marry her…after telling her he wasn’t interested….what?!?
