Rating: 3/5

The writing is still strong in the series, but this one didn't grab us quite as much as the first did. One thing about the plot was a slight annoyance to my kids, but I don't want to mention it because of spoilers.

Maybe the fact that we read it over the holidays and were more distracted by activities and visits had something to do with it, but the kids didn't ask me to keep reading as much as they did with volumes one and two. They did ask me if I had any more of the series, though, but we are going to move on to something else for a while.
