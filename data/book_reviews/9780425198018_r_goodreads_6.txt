Rating: 5/5

BELOVED IMPOSTOR by Partrica Potter is an interesting Scottish Historical Romance set in 1511 Scotland. #1 in the "Beloved" Trilogy. Intriguing, and interesting. While, a bit slow, in the beginning it picks up nicely. A century-old curse, danger, treason, a dark legacy and romance, makes for an intriguing story. The Clan MacLean has been cursed by the Campbell clan for centuries, but can true love, friendship and the coming together to find a traitor, mend the clans and finally bring peace and break the dreaded curse. An enjoyable and satisfying read. Fans of Scottish legends, Highlanders, and Historical Romance are sure to enjoy the MacLean Brothers. A new author to me, and I will be following. I can't wait read the next installment in this compelling and dangerous Trilogy.
