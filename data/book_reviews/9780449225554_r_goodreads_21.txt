Rating: 5/5

Brilliant satire and serious theological debates, allowing all the clergy to be real human beings, with unusual jobs. Susan Howatch's Starbridge Series of novels follows a fascinating set of clergy in England, each book taking us deeper into characters who may have been tangential in another book, so that they build on each other in moving ways. Set in the UK decades ago, it could play as easily in the US today when it comes to the preacher's life. I was shocked to learn that my favorite author on the interior life is not clergy herself, but she really gets the life.
