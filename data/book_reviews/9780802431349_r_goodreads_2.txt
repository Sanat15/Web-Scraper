Rating: 5/5

Spirit-filled compilation of preachings by the late E.V. Hill. Reading it comforted me, challenged me, made me repent, and ignited a desire within me to love God more. If these preachings had that much impact written, what more if they were spoken? E.V. Hill's writings are not "fire and brimstone" preachings, but they are a mirror to your soul and God's heart for His people. I cried reading the last essay where E.V. Hill recounts God's work in his life when his wife died. Every Christian should read this book.
