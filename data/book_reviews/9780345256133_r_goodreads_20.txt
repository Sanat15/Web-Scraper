Rating: 4/5

Alethea was the cigars daughter. His only daughter, with six brothers. She was educated by her father as well as her brothers. Not a traditional way to educate a lady. She was considered a bluestocking because of her intelligence and interest in things other than fashion and fripperies. 
 James Devenish known to his friends as Beau because he was a perfect beau. The ladies fell at his feet. His dress was impeccable as any dandy should be. He was a shameless flirt who quickly became bored with the ladies and moved on.
When Beau met Alethea she could have been less impressed. Although he was definitely handsome, his reputation proceeded him. She wanted to see the sights in London and visit the literary and art centers. 
Her aunt whom she was staying with was determined to find her a suitable husband.
