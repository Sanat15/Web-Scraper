Rating: 5/5

I have to say i was amazed at what some of the members of Goodreads had to say about this book. flagrant misunderstandings are the words that come to mind. there were some mighty mean stones cast, by her family and now by some of the readers. i know that many people cant even begin to fathom what it would be like to go through a childhood like this! it is not uncommon for an abused child to lie to protect an abusive parent this is a fact. I couldnt put this book down, it made me want to cry for the child not to mention the adult Adeline!
(its remarkable what you became in life. I am glad you had your aunt Ba Ba. My heart goes out to you and your sorrow) I would reccommend this book to you if you are a very compassionate person, otherwise you might be like some of the other readers and project all kinds of crap into it.
i will read more of Adeline Mah's books in the future!
