Rating: 4/5

Marnie Montgomery wants independence. Her father is her boss, owns her condo, bought her BMW. On top of it he is pushing her to marry Kent, the cheating jerk. Not able to take anymore, she resigns her position at Montgomery who runs hotels. 

Adam Drake was the star of Montgomery hotels until he was accused of embezzling a half million dollars. He needed to find out who framed him so he can go on with his life. He is ready to build the first Hotel Drake but until he clears his name, he can't get the financing he needs. 

Adam need someone on the inside who can help him with his research and finds an unlikely companion in Marnie.

This the second time I have read this book. It is an enjoyable read and I liked the fact that the villain is not easily detected early in the story. I also liked the Adam made some blunders - unlike most book heroes. Story is plausible and dialogue is smooth, not choppy.
