Rating: 5/5

"We thought that we would never see
"A suit to compensate a tree...."

Can you sue the Devil in federal court? What happens when a man tries to sue HIMSELF? What judgement do the magistrates give over the Golden Books when there is a new publisher who knew not Zim?
 What about liability for a church where "running in the spirit" takes place? And can you be sued for writing about someone who grooms dogs with a blowtorch?

 This "lay edition" tells of weird and whacky cases in the annals of the judiciary... and unlike the "misleading cases" of A.P. Herbert, these are all REAL.

 I was particularly impressed by the savoir-faire of Justice Musmano. The bench's gain was literature's loss.
