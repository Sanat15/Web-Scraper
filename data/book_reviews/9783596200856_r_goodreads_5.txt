Rating: 3/5

David Wales narrates at Librivox, here-- https://librivox.org/bashan-and-i-by-...

Both good nature writing and an amusing description of the relationship between a man and his dog. Set in Bavaria, Germany.

The book covers both canine and human psychology. The ending will make you laugh. 

The narration by David Wales is not of a professional caliber, but the words are easy to hear. The recording is scratchy. The voice is hoarse. On the other hand, its totally free! I have given the narration two stars; it’s not bad. It’s better than nothing, and I am glad it exists.
