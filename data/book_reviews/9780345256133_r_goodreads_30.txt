What a delight.

Alice Chetwynd Ley is one of my favourite authors, and like certain others, I return to her books again and again. 
I could prose on here about plot, characters, and pace, however what I want to say is read this author’s books. 
If you love Heyer, then read these books. 
If you love Georgian and Regency romance, then put this author’s work on your reading list. 
Finally, read this book!
