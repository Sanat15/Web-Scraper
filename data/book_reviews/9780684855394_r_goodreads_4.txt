Rating: 2/5

I found this book to be disappointing as the author appears to have great bias in her thoughts regarding computers, and technology in general. With very little academic study, she makes a case against computers. I would be interested to see updated studies that are current, with the current programs and technologies available to children today. This is the post-digital revolution era, I'd like to see more on this topic as everyone is inclined to put a laptop in the hands of every school child. It really needs to be addressed.
