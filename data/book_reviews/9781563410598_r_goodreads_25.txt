Rating: 4/5

A little hard to follow at times due to the fact it’s very much prose poetry rather than a structured memoir (which I didn’t know going in, but was pleasantly surprised!) but incredibly, and sometimes uncomfortably, beautiful and honest and real and it really was a wonderful glimpse into the struggles and pleasures of a butch/femme relationship and how they’re perceived in a heteronormative society. As a femme lesbian, this is a very important book to me and while it wasn’t necessarily what I expected (certainly more erotic) it was a very enjoyable read.
