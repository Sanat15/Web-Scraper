Rating: 2/5

I gave this book a retry, but it was still meh.
The hero kidnaps the heroine at her wedding because her husband physically assaulted his wife, which ultimately led to her suicide. While the premise is intense, the execution didn’t work for me.
After the kidnapping, the scenes where they ride through the jungle on horseback were especially uncomfortable. The hero was extremely harsh, showed no understanding or care, and kept touching and manhandling the heroine in a way that felt creepy rather than romantic. He came across as the biggest creep, and there was absolutely no chemistry between them.
The hero seeking revenge for the other woman—because he loved her—is not my trope at all. Still, I gave this book a fair chance. Actually, a double chance. I abandoned it quickly the first time, then picked it up again and read further, but it still failed to hook me.
