Rating: 4/5

The Seventh Annual Collection gathers 25 stories from 1989, though as always, one of my favorite bits here is actually his introduction ("Summation: 1989") where he summarizes the state of the short fiction and the SF/F publishing industry as a whole. My favorite stories were Judith Moffett's "Tiny Tango," Michael Swanwick's "The Edge of the World," Megan Lindholm's "Silver Lady and the Fortyish Man," John Varley's "Just Another Perfect Day," and Janet Kagan's "The Loch Moose Monster," and some appreciation for Robert Silverberg's "Tales from the Venia Woods" and Alan Brennert's "The Third Sex."
