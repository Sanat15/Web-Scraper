Rating: 4/5

Quantum Leap!

The world is on the edge of nuclear war.
Some pissant little country nobody has ever heard of has a dictator with his finger on the button. And for some reason, the President of the United States calls Mr. Murray to "warn" him of this imminent disaster. &lt;--roll with it, stranger things have happened

Basically, the plot calls for Charles Wallace to enter the bodies of different people throughout time and somehow (it's never really explained) tilt the lives of the people around them toward a better outcome. Meg, now married to Calvin and pregnant with their first child, is with Charles Wallace through their mental connection and helps him by running around doing research in the present.
Did I mention Charles Wallace is time-traveling with a unicorn?
Because he is.

Anyway. The point of all this is to make sure this evil dictator is never born. Replaced, in fact, with a peace-loving leader who will NOT destroy the world with a fat nuke.

Discovering these books as an adult might skew my perception of how good they are but I'm still enjoying L'Engle's writing.
