Rating: 5/5

An Elvis Presley-themed attraction seems a great idea--until an Elvis impersonator winds up dead.

Add to that death threats to the woman portraying Priscilla Presley (the repugnant Crawford Buchanan's beautiful daughter, no less), and remodeling being held up in another venue because the workers have seen Elvis's ghost, and you have plenty of mystery and mayhem to keep Midnight Louie, his roommate, Temple Barr, and talk show counselor Matt Devine busy until the mysteries are solved.
