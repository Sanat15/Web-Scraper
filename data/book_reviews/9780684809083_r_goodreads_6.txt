Rating: 4/5

A well-done look at Tyson post-prison and what a mess the heavyweight division had become by then. I enjoyed it, but probably partly because it was the boxing I remember during probably the last years I followed the sport that closely and like everybody else at that time, I was fascinated by Tyson and his power. As expected, no one really looks that good in this book as there are seedy characters everywhere, but it is an interesting and engaging read.
