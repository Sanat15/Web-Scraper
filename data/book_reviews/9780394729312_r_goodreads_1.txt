Rating: 5/5

Norwood is a simple man… He used to take care of his sister who isn’t quite all there… Suddenly she gets married… So he becomes somewhat redundant… So now he is on a special assignment delivering a car and a girl to New York…
Norwood brought the cars to a skidding stop. Miss Phillips was thrown forward and her head slammed against the windshield. “You can’t stop here, you fool!” A white station wagon smashed into the back of the Pontiac. Miss Phillips was thrown backward this time, from the neck up, and her head slammed against the center post. The driver of the station wagon had jumped out and he was running for Norwood and shouting. He had a bloody head.
Nothing goes as planned however… But the journey continues… Then he starts adventuring in New York… He hates the city and goes away… His life turns into a road movie…
That night a suicidal owl flew into the windshield but didn’t break it and later they saw a house or a barn burning out in an open field. No one seemed to be around it trying to put it out.
If one road ends another road begins – roads are endless.
