Rating: 2/5

I love Wolfe and Archie, but I always have trouble with anything Rex Stout wrote that doesn't include them. I'd read this book before and forgot to list it on Goodreads so I thought I'd try it again. It has some potential, but so many characters and no central hero... And writing women just isn't Stout's super power. There is a rich, beautiful woman with serious business acumen and style in this one, but she has the nickname "mountain cat" (cougar!) and elliptical pupils in her predatory kitty cat eyes. (I'm rolling my kitty cat eyes as I type.)
A little note on something I thought was interesting: there is a semiliterate old prospector character who says he can read printing, but not cursive writing. Perhaps we are returning to that?
