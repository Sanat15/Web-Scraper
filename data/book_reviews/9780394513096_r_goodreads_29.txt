Rating: 3/5

I enjoyed parts but this book has no narrative flow. The author jumps around all over the place with no rhyme or reason. The book was supposed to be about a whole season in the NBA for one team, namely 79-80 Portland, but he uses that as just a framework for his stream of consciousness writing. He talks more about Bill Walton and his feet than anything else in the book and Walton wasn't on that team that year. When he stuck to the topic it was interesting. Some of his random writing was interesting as well but having no flow hurt it.

Can't really recommend, again some of it was interesting but a lot was a slog to get through.
