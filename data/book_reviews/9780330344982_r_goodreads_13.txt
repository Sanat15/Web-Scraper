Rating: 4/5

I loved returning back to Cornwall and the world of Ross Poldark and Demelza. Having learned that there was a 20 year hiatus between book 4 and this one, I felt grateful that I came to these late and did not have to wait that long for the continuation. Was it a seamless interruption? For the most part, I thought so. Yes, it may have started out slowly, but I still quickly fell back into the narrative.

This go round we are also in France, watching the French Revolution evolve. Dr. Dwight Enys, when last seen in book 4, had signed up to go as a ship surgeon. At that time, he thought all was over with Caroline Penvenen. His ship has been destroyed and Dwight is missing.

Elizabeth Warleggan has her baby on the night of the Black Moon, which portends bad luck. George is overjoyed to have a son. Does this make him a nicer, kinder person. No, he is becoming more villainous with each book.

New characters are introduced. Demelza’s brothers, Sam and Drake arrive as does Elizabeth’s cousin, Morwenna. All added interest to the story.

Winston Graham continues to delight me with this saga. He also ended this one with quite a bang- what will the future hold for these people that I have become invested in?
As Demelza said to Ross, “Just by living we are all-what you call it-hostages to fate.”

Published: 1973
