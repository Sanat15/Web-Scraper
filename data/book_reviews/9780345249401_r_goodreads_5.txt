Rating: 3/5

A light regency romance with a quintessentially English feel to it. The story follows the exploits of a trio of sisters on a visit to Brighton, focusing particularly on Catherine Denham, a sprightly young lady with a penchant for getting herself into trouble.

Though not on par with the likes of Heyer, this still made for an easy, pleasant read. Quite funny in parts, and I particularly liked the initial set up of Catherine and Pamyngton's meeting, which was rather amusing. I did however find Catherine's behaviour a trifle too thoughtless at times.

Overall a refreshingly 'clean' read compared to others in the genre, told with a gentle charm.
