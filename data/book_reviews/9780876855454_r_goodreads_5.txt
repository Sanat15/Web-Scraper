Rating: 1/5

This is not the book to read before a trip to Morocco. Bowles' portrayal of the politics and character of the Moroccan people during their fight for independence from French is bleak. All of his characters, including the foreign tourists, are calculating, deceitful, and distrusting. Their actions are often perplexing, and the novel can be difficult to follow as an audiobook. Bowles has some interesting cultural observations. However, he overindulges in lengthy debates on what Morocco should be.
