Rating: 4/5

This book was so pleasant a read and since I know the story behind it, I thought, "if only they had let Virginia write the book she wanted to," I think Virginia was one of the first historical romance writers who really wanted to put Celtic fantasy in her novel. Of course, at the time this was written, she just had to take it all out and leave "the sight." We have often talked about what we wanted and what we got in the world of publishing. Virginia is one of the most naturally gifted writers I know. She could write a book in a month if she had to, and well she did. She and I wrote one in 9 weeks, reading it to each other over the telephone. She is still writing to this day, 2025. Her first books were written on a typewriter which she burned up. This story was way ahead of its time and limited by editorial input. I love the idea of it. Always have.
