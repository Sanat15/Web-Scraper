Rating: 4/5

Very cute little book of poems. Skip "J.T. Will Never Be Ten" and "The Cry Guy" unless you need a good cry 😭😭😭

Favorites (in no particular order):

1. "A Day In School"
This poem reminded me of a page in Poison For Breakfast where Snickett talks about our minds be yanked from one place to another in school. 

2. "You Can Do Better"
Every teacher should have this printed and taped to their desks. Your best will change from day to day, and that's ok. ❤

3. "Teacher, Could You?"
Haha. No. No I could not sit next to my friend and not speak to them.
