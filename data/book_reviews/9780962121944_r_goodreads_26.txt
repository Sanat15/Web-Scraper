Rating: 5/5

A clever story to relate the principles of The Law of Attraction and teachings of Abraham. Written for anyone who is young at heart - and of course there is an wise Owl! It's easy to see the parallels between Esther/Sara and Abraham/Solomon. The character and challenges represented by Sara will be relatable to most readers. Quite delightful and informative, this tale offers a new perspective on life.

The printed book contains illustrations by Caroline S. Garrett. The audio book is narrated by Jerry Hicks and features exceptional music composed &amp; performed by Stewart St. John.
