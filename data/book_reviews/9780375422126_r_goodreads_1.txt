Rating: 4/5

Theology. A gimmicky point of view. Bible quotes.

This book has all the ingredients for the recipe of bad book. Somehow Mike Bryan took the fixuns and made a tasty dish that is not only clever and entertaining, but insightful and meaningful too.

In The Afterword, author Mike Bryan tells the story in the form of an Afterword to his bestselling novel, The Deity Next Door. But The Deity Next Door doesn’t actually exist.

Bryan shows us the interesting back story to a story that doesn’t really exist. The Deity Next Door becomes a bestselling book almost overnight and the author has a lot of questions to answer to his fans.

Bryan’s non-existant novel follow the story of a modern-day messiah named Blaine – a secular computer nerd from NYC who figures out that he has diving powers. Not just I-can-see-through-walls powers like Superman. He can control objects and cure cancer…serious shit.

This book, written through the eyes of a journalist, explains the theological questions that come to mind when encountering a modern-day messiah.

In ten words: I wish more authors created unique ways of storytelling like this.
