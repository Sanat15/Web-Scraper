Rating: 5/5

Read this book in 2007, and its the 1st volume of the wonderful "Aubrey/Maturin" series.

This first outing is set in the year AD 1800, during the Napoleonic Wars, fought on Sea and Land, and this book will see the close friendship and bond between Captain Aubrey, and his ship's Surgeon and Intelligence Agent, Stephen Maturin.

Storytelling is absolutely wonderful, and all figures featuring in this amazing seafaring tale come all vividly to life whether on the upper or below decks aboard ship, and the hostile atmosphere of the warlike times against the forces of Napoleon are very much in attendance.

The story feels very authentic in its writing style by the author, and what the dialogue is concerned.

This story starts in the year AD 1800, and we see our main protagonists, Aubrey and Maturin, and the rest of the crew on board the ship, The Sophie, and they will encounter many engaging and dangerous missions.

What is to come is a most entertaining seafaring tale, in which Aubrey in his nautical way will keep the Sophie afloat and working, with his crew of course, while Maturin with his Surgical and Spy work will do everything possible to assist England, in his endeavour to gain as much information as he can that can be used in the War against Napoleon.

Highly recommended, for this is a very worthy opener of a terrific series, and that's why I like to call this first episode: "A Masterly Aubrey &amp; Maturin Opening Scene"!
