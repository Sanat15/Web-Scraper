Rating: 5/5

The most moving of the three Sector General novel(la)s I’ve read thus far. Some readers will chafe at the extensive religious conversations, but I was sufficiently invested in Lioren’s growth arc, including how that intersected with the religious beliefs and concerns of other struggling alien beings. 

At the outset, Lioren isn’t terribly likable. But as in other stories in this series, it’s satisfying to untangle what is his personality and what is an artifact of his species’ expected social behaviors. That's one terrifically interesting aspect of these books: well-developed alien biologies and cultures that clash in unexpected and often ultimately fruitful ways. 

Lioren’s story is fundamentally about emerging from depression and embracing friendship. But I love that along the way we get to explore different ways of honoring one’s culture, taking responsibility, and delivering justice.
