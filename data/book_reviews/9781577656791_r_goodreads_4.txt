Rating: 5/5

Beautifully written prose through and through...nothing short of an excellent narrative of an adventure. This was a highly enjoyable read and perhaps not to be taken as an example for the way a youth should be raised. Nevertheless, despite the bad habits and mischievous acts, Tom Sawyer was also a hero (in and out of the book). I highly recommend to all ages but will advise on a PG-13 rating.
