Rating: 3/5

I got this book in college, but only read The Awakening. I just finished reading Ethan Frome and O Pioneers! Ethan Frome is a terribly depressing story, reminiscent of Jane Eyre. I've read a couple other novels by Wharton that I enjoyed much more than this one.

I enjoyed O Pioneers! much more than Ethan Frome, though not nearly as much as My Antonia, which I recently read as well. 

These are classics, and students of American literature should be sure to read these.
