Rating: 5/5

This book has everything that I love to read about all in one storyline; tribal life, prisons, decent spicy scenes, and a putrid villain that only knows hate. 
This book will also teaches readers about invertebrate life in Africa, specifically about the use of flea beetle pupae to poison arrow heads and the tsetse fly which causes rinderpest in humans. This may not be of any interest to other book worms, but as an individual that has bugs on the brain, any book that obtains correct knowledge about insects will always be a good book in my eyes.
