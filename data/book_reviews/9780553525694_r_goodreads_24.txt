Rating: 5/5

This was a whole lot of fun. I've not had so much fun just reading a book in a while. This is a great "bottle" book, which means that it's fairly self contained. The first four X-Wing books and the next 3 books were each in a mini arc. "Isaard's Revenge" and "Starfighters of Adumar" were both pretty good standalone books. This one really could even be considered not a part of the X-Wing series because it only uses 4 of the X-Wing pilots. Still, very fun.

Aaron Allston was known for his humor in his books, and this one's no different. Even though I liked "Wraith Squadron", I felt like this one's humor resonated more and was more organic. I think this comes from the fact that this was Aaron's 10th novel and he was a more experienced writer.

He solved a lot of plot inconsistnecy's revolved around Wedge in the "Jedi Academy" trilogy and "I Jedi". I liked Iella Wessiri and was super happy by how that thread ended.

The plot itself kind of reminded me of standalone Star Trek plots. The explorers find a planet, realize that there is something odd/different about the planet that causes a problem and they have a big climax in order to resolve the problem. This just felt like Star Wars though, because of the way Allston handled the politics and the battle designs.

Overall, for being a short(er) book, this one stand out among the Legends/EU books for me now. 9.3 out of 10!
