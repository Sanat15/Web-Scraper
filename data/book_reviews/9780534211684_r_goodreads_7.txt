Rating: 2/5

The 10th edition of the book by the originator of counseling &amp; interviewing microskill theory. Like many textbooks, after this many editions, it needs a drastic overhaul. 
Far too much filler in many chapters that does nothing to advance a students’ understanding or application of the skills and my classmates and I *hated* Ivey’s anti-medication “depression can always be cured with exercise and and sunny walk” rhetoric.
Much of the content feels unnecessarily gendered and the exams provided from the publisher based on this text were *rubbish.*
