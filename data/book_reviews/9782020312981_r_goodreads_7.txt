Rating: 5/5

Cracker of a read. I remember the tv series adaptation with Kenneth Branagh not the original Swedish guy Krister Henriksson.

Starts with a young girl burning herself to death in a Rape field to a series of brutal axe murders and scalp taking.

Wallender gets initially sidetracked in his pursuit but sorts it in the end. Wallenders father is diagnosed with Alzheimer’s which makes the relationship between them more difficult.

The story is perfectly told. Great characters. Can see why this book won a literary award.

Like reading a John Connolly book I began to get sad as the pages left decreased.
