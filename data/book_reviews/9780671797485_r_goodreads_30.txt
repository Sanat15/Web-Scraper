Rating: 3/5

I found it quite intriguing, earning a solid 3.5 out of 5 from me. The book splits into two distinct parts; the first chronicles his journey to becoming one of, if not the most, renowned bodybuilders ever. This section captivated me with Arnold’s unwavering determination and his unique mindset—his certainty not in whether he would achieve his goals, but simply when. His perspective simplifies life’s decisions, a lesson in the power of assured ambition.

The latter part of the book, which I’ve left for future reference, dives into his specific training routines.
The biographical content truly resonated with me, portraying Arnold as not just a bodybuilder but a man driven by an undeniable self-belief.
