Rating: 4/5

Delia Grinstead, wife, mother of 3 older children, just walks away one day without a backward glance or to a thought as to where and why she was going. Once she thought about it…..”for I’m here because I just like the thought of beginning again from scratch.” Was that the real reason? Was she tired of feeling like part of the furniture by her husband and her children? Was she tired of feeling like she had no voice? Was she tired of feeling under appreciated? Maybe, any of those could be possible. But once away, she set about starting a new life- she got a job, then changed jobs, she found a place of her own, she made friends, she did as she pleased with no one to account to. She moved on like her past did not exist.

Her circumstance bothered me. It made me feel sad for her and her family. She seemed to not give one thought to them and they did not reach out to her. Once they knew where she was, other than one sister, no one made an attempt to see her or talk to her. That broke my heart, it really did. Ok, as a wife and mother myself, I truthfully could understand being fed up and needing a break from them all, but to leave and not call or seem to care, I couldn’t accept. But this book was not about me, it was about Delia and Delia finding herself and figuring out her own mind.

A thoughtful book true to Anne Tyler’s style. Her people and their problems always seem real to me. I did appreciate the ending, yet I would have liked a bit more.

Published: 1995
