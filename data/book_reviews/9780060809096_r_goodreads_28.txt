Rating: 4/5

More like 3.5 stars but rounded up for the delightful banter between Harriet and Lord Peter- not to mention his persistent marriage offers and the inevitable “certainly not!” Harriet reply! The murder mystery plot is one of the most convoluted ones I have come across in some time- I truly despaired it ever being solved. But solved it is- and in a quite unique manner. Sayers is a great mystery writer but this could’ve used some editing.
