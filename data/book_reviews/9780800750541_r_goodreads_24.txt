Rating: 5/5

A new favorite, yet a book written in 1977.

This is the type of history that many attempt to either rewrite, or cover up.

The faith of the discoverers, founders, and early American heroes shown, yet also failures and lack of faith...all put on public display.

One thing lacking is the scourge of chattel slavery and how America gave in to the temptation of being double-minded in its stated ideals outlined in the Declaration and Constitution, yet intentionally obfuscate and redirect those rights away from the black man.

That said, the author does not hold back any other punches, and rightly speculates how God’s providential hand is in all things, and if His people seek him and repent, He will guide them to His glory.
