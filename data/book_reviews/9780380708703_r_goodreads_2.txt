Rating: 1/5

Unlike the previous "alien" book I read, Strieber's Communion, this one asks the reader to get on board with a ton of assertions, nearly ALL of which are just backed with a "trust me" and nothing else. Walters paints himself as a kind of misunderstood hero standing up to those goddamn alien invaders and taking Polaroid after Polaroid as a kind of "proof." His bland diary/reporting style get boring after about 150 pages, and the biggest revelation to me in this book was a possible cameo by my old realtor.

Strieber's book at least gave you options (if you believe he's not simply lying): if you don't accept alien life, you don't have to, he could be crazy, suffering from PTSD, or lots of other things. Walters throws so much at the reader it's either hoax or nothing, and it's a hoax. When I got bored, a quick web search revealed some of the particulars, and I recommend that rather than this ponderously dull book.
