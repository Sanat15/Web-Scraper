Reason number 37 Why I Still Call Myself A Catholic. 

Years later (I read this in its first edition), I married a wonderful girl from Texas who taught me the intimate connection between food, cooking, meals, and communion. Coming as I did from the "stand in the kitchen and eat from the bag" school of nutrition, I suddenly remembered this book. When that dinner bell rings, children, it's the Lord calling us all to share a meal. And, yes, there's enough for all.
