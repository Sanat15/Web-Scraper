Rating: 5/5

Former Boston Red Sox Centerfielder Dom DiMaggio with Bill Gilbert released in 1990 “Real Grass, Real Heroes”. This wonderful book profiles the legendary 1941 Major League Baseball season, and how the players were impacted by the American entry into World War 2. The book describes Ted Williams’ .407 batting average season, Dom’ brother Joe DiMaggio’s 56 continuous game hitting streak record, and Vander Meer’s back-to-back no hitters, plus many more records set in 1941. I especially enjoyed the book’s descriptions of the ‘41 Yankee/Dodger World Series. This book is written in the first person by a player who has an amazing “insider” storyteller perspective. Dom put me in the present moment of a baseball season I will long remember. (p)
