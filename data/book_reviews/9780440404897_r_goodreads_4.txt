Rating: 5/5

Excellent easy reader about a town in Minnesota that literally moved - all 200 or more buildings and homes - so that the town could exploit the iron ore which lay underneath it.

A fascinating true story that I never knew anything about. As with many children's books, it makes me want to learn more about the history, events and politics which must have surrounded this huge decision.

Great for the young audience the book is aiming at, or as an introduction to a piece of Americana for older readers.
