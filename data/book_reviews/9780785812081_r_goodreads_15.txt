Rating: 5/5

Fantastic, succinct and fulsome account of the Battle. My first Deighton book (certainly not last). He says more in a page than most authors say in two or three. Repeatedly demolishes myths that have sprung up since the summer of 1940. Particularly good on the personalities behind the battle, eg Dowding, Park, Mallory on the British side and Goring, Kesselring and to a lesser extent Hitler. Highly recommend.
