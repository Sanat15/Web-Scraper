Rating: 4/5

What a great series. Totally underrated.

This is the fifth book and it started well, got slow in the middle, and then ended well.

There are 3 separate story lines going on, but really the main one about the murder mystery was the best one. 

The one with Incarnadine had promise, but got a little weird and I think feel a little flat. Although, he's always a great character.

The one with Linda and Gene really wasn't that great and seems like it was added as an afterthought.
