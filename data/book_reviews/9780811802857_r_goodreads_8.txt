Rating: 4/5

Beautiful illustrations, evocative of the great frozen north. Gray wolf is restless. His mate has died in a trap. He is lonely and hunting by himself. He meets up with a wolf pack late at night, lurking in the dark. There is a page at the end on the threats to wolf survival, and an interesting map showing the wolf's range in the 1700s (basically all of North America except Florida) and the range today (mainly Canada).
