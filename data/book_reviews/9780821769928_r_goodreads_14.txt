Rating: 3/5

I was disappointed in this book. Her other books I have read I have really enjoyed, but this one I did not. I had really liked Leslie in the Miss Fairchild book, and thought his character was really weak in this book. I thought the "plan" was ridiculous in the first place; and that Cleo would come up with something like that and that Leslie would keep on going along with her was double ridiculous. I was glad they finally woke up in the end, but had a hard time reading about all their foibles. So disappointed. Oh well, I guess you can't win them all.
