A little wibbly-wobbly-Anglican-wanglican, as much as I do truly love me some Anglicans. The broad usage of faith here (in God, good; in "art," hm; in your work as a distinct entity which is better than you and thereby makes you a Christian, nope nope nope) didn't land. I struggled to lock onto her thesis--maybe a me problem, but unhelped by the anecdote-to-uncited-quote-to-argument-for-appreciation-of-the-*old*-BoCP format. Redemptive star for some really good quotes.

Read instead: Mystery and Manners (O'Connor), The War of Art (Pressfield, I think?), the 1928 BoCP
