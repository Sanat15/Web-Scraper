Rating: 1/5

Just couldn't finish although I got through 145 pages before giving up. I have read and loved at least 8 of Ms. Godwin's novels and as I was struggling through this one, I checked the publication date - ah, this was one of her earliest novels, right after Glass People, which I did finish but didn't really like. Characters were dull, tedious, not people you could care about. There was little plot, story didn't go anywhere. But after this book, it seems Ms. Godwin got her wings and the characters and stories bloomed.
