Rating: 2/5

my red flag for this should have been knowing better than to read a book with roots in feminism that hasn't been updated since 1992. because this was a doozy at some points. very overwritten and meandering to several chapters that were barely even tangentially about sex. some factoids were interesting and i do like the composition of this book being laid out by region and culture rather than focusing on just europeans or the western world. i definitely learned stuff reading this which was the goal, but it was a struggle getting through some sections enough that my impression of the entire book is just a general uggghhhhhh.
