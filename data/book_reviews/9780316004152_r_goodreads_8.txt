Rating: 4/5

Mix of articles, diaries, and miscellaneous writings. Really interesting guy. An environmentalist, explorer, journalist, and anarchist. His diaries include very beautiful descriptions of the places he visits, and some interesting tidbits of philosophy here and there. I don't agree with many of his political views, although I do appreciate the sentiment and see how such views could develop. I have become rather skeptical of relentless growth as of late, economic, industrial, technological, and he might have influenced me on that. Do not look up his views on immigrants, rather racist. Somewhat misogynistic views on women, but not super glaring. Overall good read, with varying quality depending on the piece.
