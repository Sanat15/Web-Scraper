Rating: 4/5

As a parent-fan of Raffi's music, I just had to read his autobiography. The book was just as authentic as his public persona - you can feel his commitment to the good of the world through his writing. I really enjoyed reading about his life journey to become the children's musician that we all know and love. One of the surprising parts of this book was Raffi's interjections about television, the spread of the internet, technology, leaving the reader with eerily poignant quotes - he knew back in 1998 just how the internet would affect children today. I did get a little lost reading during his environmental activism era, but I would still highly recommend this book to anyone, especially "Beluga grads" and those who share Raffi's music with their children.
