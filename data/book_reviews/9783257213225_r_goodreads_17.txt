Rating: 1/5

Passion - secret, forbidden, rejected - murder, suicide - unspoken is made boring. Not a titillating touch. Meant to be historical methinks? Moral that country ways are honest, best? When meanest person (of many) ends up happy-ever-after marrying? Realistic no-point narrative like true life?

1800s Petworth near Chichester Cathedral, innocent released convict Matthew Bilbo heads home to herd sheep, fearful for cellmate "Simmie" p 11, forgives judge Luke Paget. Beginning connects later when Simon Pike flees jail and hides with Bilbo, befriended by Luke's son. 

Ellen Paget 22 is whisked by godmother from Brussels girls' boarding school from too-willing Directrice. The young teacher's heart beats faster when her older Professor blatantly glances, compliments "angel' "muse", and students predict proposal. Her new charge Menispe 4 will not sit still and throws tantrums willy nilly. Ellen's half-brother/ cousin/ something Benedict follows and chivvies her everywhere; from their quick tempered retorts the two are lifelong childhood sweethearts. 

Raoul still loves his wife; his ancient fine family insist on a male heir. Cold Louise ignores her daughter, hosts literary salons with her lesbian lover Germaine in male attire. Germaine insults "self-centred English prig" p 258 Ellen, yet makes passes at her. Boring name-dropping - George Sands, Baudelaire - blah blah - attempt to be historical? 

Luke's heir Gerard befriends Bilbo, alienates political ambitions of father with musical bent. Sneaky housekeeper Mrs Pike gets Luke's will in her favor. Her son Simon soon connects overly convoluted roundabout plot. 

A fortune-teller p 164 and Luke's nightmares both predict a fatal stone crush. Author's pre-note says 21 Feb 1861 "spire of Chichester Cathedral did fall straight down". Luke sees his first wife Matilda in visions. Yawn. Supernatural spirits do not improve story. Also library hold on Aiken's most popular Wolves of Willoughby Chase means author gets one more chance. 

 After family insists Louise retire to country, she uses gun from Germaine's garrett to shoot Menispe dead in the head, then herself. How did she learn about gun hidden under floorboards? After recounting the horrific scene to Ellen, Germaine, always disguised as man, runs away to countryside. Ellen maybe translates for adult and intellectual challenge, but why fetch funds to flee from publisher? 

Disinherited sisters lock up Luke to force will change. After minor stroke, he deteriorates badly. Ellen, recuperating from coma, forgotten smash by Simon, asks Benedict to help rescue her papa. Stone crushes Bilbo, Simon, and Gerard, bent on redemption by raising the carved Doomstone match to Resurrection megolith his father is obsessed with. 

Professor Patrice Bosschchère, Ellen's crush, has been decades married to Brussels headmistress Directrice, against his seminary rules. Why should Mrs Pike be rewarded by marriage to stuttering younger lawyer for her money? Presumably Luke changes his will favorably before he dies at the end.
