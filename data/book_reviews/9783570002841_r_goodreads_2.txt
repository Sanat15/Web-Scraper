As is well known, children in the Third Reich were brainwashed with Nazi ideology starting from the kindergarten. They were taught to love Hitler more than anyone; after the Fall of Berlin, a file cabinet was found in the Reich Chancellery with letters to Hitler from ordinary Germans; many were from women who asked the Fuehrer to marry them or offered to have a child by him. There was much brainwashing in ordinary schools, but even more in special elite boarding schools, which concentrated on building the character and leadership skills of the children at the expense of the academics; many of their graduates went on to have excellent careers in the Federal Republic of Germany, both in the Bundeswehr and in the private sector. In addition to schooling, National Socialist consciousness of the youths was upheld by the Hitler Youth; a phrase "I don't want to join the Hitler Youth because I want to be a free human being" could land a young man in a concentration camp. In Hamburg, there were some "swing kids": children of rich parents who wore hats, scarves, vests and neckties, played jazz music, danced and kissed girls; after they were caught, they were tortured by the Gestapo and sent to concentration camps or penal battalions. And of course much effort was expended on making good soldiers from German youths: as the war wore on and Germany was losing it, the draft age was progressively lowered, so by the end many 16-year-olds and 17-year-olds fought. They fought bravely, being too young to fear death. In February 1945, two Hitler Youths, a 16-year-old and a 17-year-old, were dropped behind the American lines with a radio; the next day the Americans captured them and in June 1945, executed them.

When I was a kid, we called the Soviet Young Pioneers "Breschnewjugend"; I wonder if, had Germany won the war, as she very well could have, the Hitler Youth would have degenerated into something as insincere as the Soviet Young Pioneers of the 1980s.
