Rating: 3/5

Some good stories in the second half, but nothing close to "The Trials of Rumpole." There were also some uncomfortable moments in the first half where you could definitely tell the humor was from the 1980s and earlier. Rumpole is a product of his time, even if he shows himself (somewhat) more progressive - or rebellious - than his peers, so keep that in mind if you want to check out these books or the TV show.
