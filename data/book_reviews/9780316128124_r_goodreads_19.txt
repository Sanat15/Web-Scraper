Rating: 5/5

Handbook for the Soul resonated with me deeply. It took a few days to finish it. For some reason, I put it down and picked it back up. It is a great selection of essays and stories that literally and emotionally refuel and remind us how special we are in the big scheme of things. It reminded me of the little things and how we are all connected. It validated and reaffirmed how life is precious, to live in the moment and find the miracles in everyday life. Life is a miracle, no doubt about it.
