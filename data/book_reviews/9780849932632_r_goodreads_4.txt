Rating: 4/5

Required reading for my DMin program. I can only imagine how ground breaking this must have been in 1995: an integration of physiology, psychology and theology all pointing towards the pace at which we live our lives and experience adrenaline and stress. 

It is a credit to work like this of Dr Hart’s that, reading this 31 years later, it was somewhat pedestrian. I am grateful that knowledge about sleep, vulnerability, sabbath practices, the importance of breathing practices etc are all pieces of wisdom readily available now. So: don’t want to knock the book as boring because I knew most of this already. Rather - I’m grateful to be three decades downstream of work like this. We are the better for it.
