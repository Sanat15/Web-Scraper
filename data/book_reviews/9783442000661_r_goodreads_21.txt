Rating: 4/5

Twisty 

This one was a true rollercoaster. I enjoyed how Poirot stumbled into several incorrect conclusions before uncovering the truth—it added a welcome touch of humanity to his usually flawless persona. Christie gave the characters more complexity this time, with layered backstories that made the mystery richer.

I especially appreciated how small, seemingly insignificant details—like a character’s manner of speaking—became the thread that unraveled the entire case. The subtle undertone of sycophancy woven into the plot was another clever touch, adding a faint but telling social critique.

Overall, this felt like a more nuanced and confident piece of writing from Christie, balancing misdirection, character depth, and sharp observation.
