Rating: 5/5

I read this book to my son, Camden, who is 8. I decided to read it to him because we are studying early American history. This is a beautifully written living book for any kid! Camden rated it five stars! He was easily able to retell the story to me after taking several days to read the book. That in my opinion is a well written book! I, myself, learned a lot and found the history of Plymouth Rock to be fascinating!
