Long ago I had seen the movie WAR HORSE and the movie was the adaptation from the novel WAR HORSE by the award 🏆 winning former Children's Laureate Michael Morpurgo. At that time I wasn't much into reading and so I never read the book until now.

The story revolves around a young boy Albert and his love for his horse Joey who his father accidentally buys instead of a Farm horse. Joey was a young Colt.

Joey was not liked by Albert's father and one fine day after a rough incident with Joey, Albert's father threatened Albert that he will shoot Joey if he misbehaves ever again.

Since Albert loved Joey he promised his father he will tame him and make him a fine farm horse, and so he did.

Barely a couple of years since Joey settled in the farm in 1914 The Great War broke out and the British Army were looking for Horses to be inducted into the Cavalry, Since Alberts father had loans to pay one fine day when Albert wasn't around he went and sold Joey to a Captain Nicholls of the British Army. When Albert came to know about this he was devastated and felt betrayed by his father who promised him Joey was here to stay. 

Albert tried enlisting himself in the army but because at that time he wasn't of the required age of being enlisted he couldn't be joining the army with Joey. However Albert had made up his mind to pursue Joey and get him back home from The Great War.

To know more read the book and watch the movie as well, all in all a powerful story of the truest of friendships in the worst of wars.
