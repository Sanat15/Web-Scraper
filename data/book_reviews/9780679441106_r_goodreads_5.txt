Rating: 4/5

I have read quite a few books by Anne Tyler now and I have really enjoyed nearly all of them. This was no exception. 
I like the way this author writes, calmly, even placidly, she steadily builds up a picture of life the way her characters live it. There are no huge dramas, no histrionics, just the minutiae of daily life and the ordinariness of everyday people. Along the way we develop an understanding of our characters and start to sympathise with them and even worry about them.
Our main character in this book is Delia, an average middle aged house wife who one day just keeps walking away from her life. It is an intriguing idea that one could just walk off and start somewhere fresh with a whole different lifestyle and different people. Of course there are repercussions and people are hurt along the way. The ending was the opposite of what I had been hoping for but it was true to life and not hopelessly romantic.
Quite a long book but very easy to read and very hard to put down. Recommended:)
