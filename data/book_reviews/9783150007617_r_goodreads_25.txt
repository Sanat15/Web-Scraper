The way some people (Herr Thomas Mann) talk about this you'd be forgiven for thinking it's the German literary version of COME AND SEE so effectively does it underline the futility and misery of war. In fact, it depicts the 30 years war as a rollicking series of delightful, Roald Dahl-esque misadventures. There are bawdy sex and fart jokes in here that would be totally at home in DUMB AND DUMBER or AMERICAN PIE.
