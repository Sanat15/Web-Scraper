Rating: 3/5

This is the first book I have read by Lois Menzel. Honestly, I have been reading a lot of Regency romances lately and most of them have been flat. CELIA is not that way. 

Celia was the youngest of five daughters. Her mother doted on her and expected the best attachment. Along comes Anthony Graydon and he was mesmerized by Celia's gentleness and looks. He was heir to a title that would eventually make her the Countess of Walsh. 

Celia and her mother take an extended visit to Anthony's estate. She met his parents and relatives. She also became friends with Ursula Browne, a minister's daughter, and helped her at the local orphanage. Her relationship with Anthony grows and he asks for her hand in marriage. All is well until Robert, Anthony's older brother, returns from the war with Napoleon. He was both injured and blind. At first turned off because of his injuries, Celia ever-so-slowly saw beyond material possessions. Anthony encouraged her assistance with his brother because he loved him. 

It was a no-brainer that you know Robert and Celia were fated by reading the book description. I found Ursula’s story just as interesting. But 4 stars as I rated it in 2011? Alas, no.
