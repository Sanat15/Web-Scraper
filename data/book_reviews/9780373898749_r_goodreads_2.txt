Rating: 2/5

I read this in 2019 and unfortunately this book doesn't stand the test of time. The writing is excellent and I really liked the starting point of a heroine who is already married, as this is so unusual for M&amp;B. Unfortunately this book has serious consent issues; particularly one scene where the heroine tries to fend off the hero with a pitchfork and he overpowers her to kiss her against her will. Read only if you are interested in how much the romance genre has moved on since this book was published.
