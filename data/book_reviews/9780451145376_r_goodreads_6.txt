Rating: 1/5

Joan Wolf loves a cheatin’ hero, and this guy takes the cake. Don't expect any grovelling, instead you'll get self-justification, whining, and some sexual manipulation. On top of that he’s an enslaver obsessed with ‘liberty’ — which is historically accurate but still totally ick. The most romantic relationship in this book was the mother’s long-term adulterous affair. I want to read that book. I’m still giving it 3 stars tho cuz I find Wolf’s books (especially the old ones) emotionally compelling and well-written, and I'm so glad they're back in print. But by the end I was wishing the heroine would run off with her handsome cousin. The hero had me rooting for the brits :)

Edit: I dipped my feet back into this and my god this is really the worst worst MMC ever. I’m downgrading to a one.
