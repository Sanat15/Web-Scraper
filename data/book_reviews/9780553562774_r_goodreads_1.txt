Rating: 3/5

Final book in trilogy about the settlement of western Carolina and TN mountains. This is an area and time period I am very interested in. The first book started with the main character as a young boy with a Cherokee trader father, well before the Revolutionary War. Now, in third book, he’s a man with a family who by the final pages is considering moving on further west because by 1790, the country is getting all settled. Author has done a lot of research and has a deep knowledge of history. Sometimes the characters (especially the women) are a bit sparsely drawn but hard now to understand the difficulties and dangers the settlers faced. Book also tries to portray the Cherokee fairly, too, as they fought hard to keep their lands.
