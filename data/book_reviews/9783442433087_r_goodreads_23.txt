Rating: 5/5

This is a strange mixture - novella length stories bookend the collection, while in the middle are a bunch of short pieces (one very short) that come from that very grim vein that turns up in most of Rendell's non-Wexford titles, the vein in which vicious and almost-insane characters take the lead. 
All the stories are superbly written - Rendell is one of the best stylists in the world of murder mysteries - and all are immensely readable, as even her most grim books are. And, thankfully, at the end, there's The Raspberry Tree in which there's no truly nasty character at all, and which has a much more upbeat ending than might be expected.
