Rating: 1/5

How can you create two out-and-out annoying central characters in one single book? This book did that; One is a spoilt brat caught with two grams of heroine in Bali, and another is an arrogant brat of a sister of the first spoilt brat rescuing her and getting annoyed at each and everything coming at her way. Usually, when a character is a victim of a corrupt system, I feel sympathy for him or her, but these two people are so annoying that I almost lost my reverence for the entire Japanese people. Can't give the book more than 1, probably less.
