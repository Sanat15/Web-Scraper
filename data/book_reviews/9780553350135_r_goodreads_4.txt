Rating: 5/5

Dark 

This book was brilliant. Honestly, when I first read what the story was about, I thought I’d be bored—but it turned out to be a pleasant surprise. From the very start, I couldn’t put it down. It’s packed with events, multiple murders, and an atmosphere thick with tension.

The characters are complex and deeply flawed, tangled in emotions of hate, jealousy, envy, desire, and pure malice. What struck me most was how masks slowly slipped away—people who once seemed as delicate as blooming flowers revealed themselves to be rotten at the core, with their corruption festering for years until one trigger brought it all spilling out.

And then, the plot twist—absolute perfection. I suspected the murderer at one point, but the way events unfolded made me dismiss them, only for the truth to snap back at the end. It was so well played and masterfully crafted. A truly gripping and unforgettable read.
