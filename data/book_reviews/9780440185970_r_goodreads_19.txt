Rating: 4/5

It is exactly what the title describes. The spinster is thirty years old, pragmatic. She's the one who took care of the parents and sacrificed herself--in other words, she chose to remain single. The story opens with her visiting her oldest brother, who is quite a bit older than her. She is there to watch her niece and basically do the things her sister in law won't do.

On the way to older brother's house in London, her carriage breaks down and she gets a ride from a rake and his drinking buddy. He's an older, world weary rake but he's still gorgeous. Of course he is. 

This is not difficult reading. Of course there's the obligatory sexual content. I don't care for that at all; just a preference. I also incredibly annoyed when the sex is so great the first time around. Isn't that special. But I digress.

Sex aside, I enjoyed it for what it is. Entertaining. Light. There's a really cute secondary story too. 

4 1/2 stars, which includes less a half star because of the splendid, first time, (eye rolling) sex. 

Please note, I read through my local online library.
