Rating: 4/5

This was an intense read. The H/h are at odds at first because of a building lease, but the hero pursues the heroine with all he's got. There's some jealousy, etc. . . but that isn't the real problem for this couple.

 After he wears her down and they marry, she balks at having sex. He is confused and hurt until she explains that she had been gang raped as a teenager and she still felt judged by the town and defiled and soiled in her own eyes. She asks for a divorce when she sees disgust in his eyes. They're apart for awhile and then he comes back for a second chance and he explains it was disgust at himself for not being more patient, rather than disgust at her. 

The attitudes toward rape seem a bit dated, but not the emotions a rape victim might go through. Well worth a read. HEA
