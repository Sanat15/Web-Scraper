Rating: 5/5

I waited and hoped for this book to become available in my favorite second-hand bookstore for about a year since I live abroad where shipping charges are best avoided. I finally broke down and ordered it. It was well worth the shipping charges. I kept thinking that O'Reilley had written this book especially for me.
Are there really others out there who have a daily yoga and meditation practice but think that a retreat with the amazing and wonderful Thich Naht Hahn in Plum Island is among life's most boring offerings? That opinion alone made O'Reilley my new best friend.
I relished every page of this well-written, thoughtful book.
