Rating: 5/5

I laughed and cried while reading this book. It took me back down memory lane, growing up and visiting Mt.Zion. I did not know his daughter but grew up with E.V. Jr. because we had the love of choirs in common. I read the book in his voice. He was a fire cracker preacher with a lot of impact. 
The book is a combination of messages as well as poignant stories about his life that he wove in to make this an arresting book about A Savior Worth having. I am so glad I found this book at a Good Will store. Whoever had it didn't know what they had. I am especially grateful for this find given the recent passing of his son, E. V. Jr. 
I will read this book over and over. As I read it, my spirit was stirred and rejuvenated. We have this treasure in earthen vessels, my Lord!
