Rating: 5/5

I think when a story is a comedic romance, the reader has to suspend belief at some more outlandish antics. The author did a very good job of setting up the situation where the heroine ends up as the hostess to the bankrupt hero's house party. If all those things happened, the situation would occur. Since the heroine shared many of my own traits, I had a lot of sympathy for Sarah Greenwood who assumed the dead Miss Grimsley's identity and took on the enjoyable (for her) task of putting the household to rights for the party. Although I never laughed out loud, there were many amused smiles along the way to the Happily Ever After. 

Although I never laughed out loud, there were many amused smiles along the way to the Happily Ever After. If you're in the mood for a a fun read, you will enjoy Charade of Hearts.
