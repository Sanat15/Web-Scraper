Rating: 3/5

This is an OK little photo book. John Fielder is a good photographer -- but a *terrible* writer. His doggerel "poems" opposite his photos are, well, embarrassing. The book shows signs of hasty, careless composition (captions out of place, wild radish captioned as mustard, etc.). The color printing is just OK. Printing has gotten much better since 1986, but Dai Nippon of Tokyo was doing better work then. Another corner cut in production. In summary, not a keeper. 2.5 stars, courtesy round-up.
