Rating: 5/5

This book was written in the 1980s and doesn't claim to explain anything to everyone's satisfaction. But, it is an interesting book and in the light of what has happened since the 80s, has proven to foretell the future. I found it quite informative with a short history of forensic medicine and investigative work. I had to look up some of the cases that had commentary on them and found the insights into several cases quite fascinating. I wonder if the writer of the forensics TV shows had read this book.
