Rating: 1/5

Not the book for me. I wanted to like this one, it had promise, but I stopped early on when it got a little too steamy and I worried the rest of the book would only get worse. I liked out heroine Aurora. She's naive, but endearing and has a good heart. I wanted to like Kenyon and maybe I would have if I had continued reading. But, I didn't like how he handled certain things.  Kenyon rescues Aurora on her wedding day. Thinking she is pregnant he offers to marry her to prevent scandal. Even though she has had a rough day and made it clear she want to wait to consummate their marriage he ignores that and gets her drunk. He starts to seduce her and when he realizes she is a virgin he suddenly stops and then gets mad at her thinking she tricked him into marriage. First, he showed up and stopped her wedding. He then proposed to her and only have her an hour to decide. So it's ludicrous to think she tricked him. Second, he gets this innocent girl drunk and tries to seduce her without a thought to her feelings. I'm sure it wasn't meant to be icky, but I thought it showed him to be selfish and only interested in one thing. Later he does say he wants to woo her, but after their second night together I decided to stop. Nothing happened that night, but their was enough innuendo and with their first night for me to realize it would probably only get more steamy.  

The plot reminded me of Georgette Heyers Arabella (if I remember right) and I'd recommend reading that one instead. 

Content 
Romance: Not clean. Wedding night is fairly steamy, although does stop early on and not overly descriptive. Innuendo and I'm not sure after the first few chapters.
