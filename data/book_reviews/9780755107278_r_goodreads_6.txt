Rating: 3/5

First of all, may I say I am puzzled by the main précis above given for this book. It doesn't just give us an idea of what the book is about, it is a complete disclosure of the entire book including the ending. The entire thing is one big spoiler; whoever writes these things please do not do this. 
In any case, I give this book 3.5 Stars. Not one of Johns' best efforts, but a comfortable and easy read. The book seemed fairly short to me, and the plot was not particularly complex. As the entire plot has already been shown to prospective readers above, I'll not bother explaining what the book is about. I will say though at one point Air Commodore Raymond does say this about Lord Langdon to Biggles: 
"He seems to think we have nothing to do but run around looking for his confounded rubies".
Well, Lord Langdon would be well justified in thinking so, for all he had to do was snap his aristocratic fingers, and Scotland Yard was sending it's best air agents on a life-threatening mission to a desert in another continent. I'll bet the mission cost more than the rubies! LOL! 
But, that's just quibbling. Without these plots we would never get to see Biggles and Company travel the world. 
A decent read, if not Johns' best.
