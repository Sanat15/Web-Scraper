Rating: 2/5

A middling collection of short-stories, the main connection being that they all feature characters delivering far too many lines of expository dialogue. Anderson obviously had a lot more conversations recounting the basic history of his civilization than I do on a daily basis!
There was also some very heavy-handed metafiction, a supergenius far too smart to be surrounded by humans, and a number of tedious diplomats. None particularly endearing qualities, and the final story in particular, Among Thieves, despite its short length I had to force myself through.
For me, Duel On Syrtis was the standout, a vivid science-fantasy thriller in which a game hunter from Earth hunts a (sentient) native Martian.
Gypsy was also a good read, a pleasant exploration of grass-is-always-greener-syndrome.
