Rating: 3/5

Hard to write a review about this. The Cadfael chronicles are altogether more like one single long story to me than 20 separate ones. And I love the whole thing. 
This instalment is not a very memorable one - a very unpleasant man getting his just deserts, the obligatory romance story, the civil war - King Stephen vs. Empress Maud - continuing to tear England apart.
Still a charming outing to mediaeval Shrewsbury in wintertime.
