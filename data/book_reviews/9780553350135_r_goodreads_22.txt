Rating: 4/5

This Agatha Christie novel stands apart from her usual work with its intriguing setting; ancient Egypt, around 2000 B.C. The characters bear names like Imhotep, Nofret, Renisenb, and Yahmose, which took a little getting used to, but once I settled into the world, the story unfolded with Christie’s signature flair.

Despite the unfamiliar backdrop and historical context, the plot delivers everything you’d expect from Christie: a tangled web of motives, multiple murders, and plenty of suspense. Bodies drop left and right, and the mystery kept me guessing right up until the final reveal. A refreshing departure from her typical English countryside mysteries, but just as enjoyable.
