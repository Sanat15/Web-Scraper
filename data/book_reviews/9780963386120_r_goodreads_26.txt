Rating: 3/5

This book makes a lot of good points and shows the “arguing” cycle you can easily spiral into with you “little adults” and how to get out of that and define who is the parent and who is the child. I find it really good to read a parenting book about every other month to keep discipline and instruction fresh with my kids. This book was more unique and effective than a lot of the books I’ve read. I would definitely recommend it.
