Rating: 3/5

I am thankful my first Barbara Pym book was 
  Excellent Women
 because I loved it! I loved the story and the protagonist’s sharp wit, and the narrator, Jayne Entwistle. When I started Jane and Prudence, I expected the humor and engaging characters as I found in Excellent Women. Sadly, I did not like anything about the 65% of this book that I finished. 

The two books share the same general premise, setting, and were written within a year of each other. However, Jane and Prudence pales in comparison to Excellent Women. While I would not say the story was bad, it wasn't interesting either. The humor was missing. The women were weak and boring. And the men were either condescending or reprobates. I do not have to like characters to enjoy the story. In fact, I have encountered characters that I have hated yet could not wait to find out what they would do next. This book was so bland, I didn’t care about the story. 

In addition to the bland story, the audio was worse. Mary Sarah’s narration had misplaced inflections that sounded weird, and all the male voices sounded the same. 

After enjoying the first Pym book I read, I won’t let this failure warn me off her books. Hopefully, my next selection will deliver that high comedy I missed here.
