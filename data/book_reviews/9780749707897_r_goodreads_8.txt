Rating: 4/5

Maria is an only child whose parents have rented a Victorian summer house in Lyme Regis. She finds a half-finished sampler made by a girl named Harriet who lived in the house a century earlier. She meets an energetic family renting nearby. 

The book suggests that places hold remnants of their previous inhabitants. Maria hears a dog barking, a swing creaking, voices that the adults around her cannot hear. These moments hint at the supernatural but could just as easily be the imagination of a daydreaming child.

It is partly a mystery, and partly a quiet story about a child who goes on holiday and returns home changed. The book leaves Maria, and the reader, with a sense of how the past lingers. It belongs in that rare category of children's books with enough depth to appeal equally to adults.
