Rating: 1/5

Ugh, so much to hate about this book, most notably the OW, who was clearly top choice no matter what the H said at the end. Also, the OW is an h in another Palmer book and she didn't seem to have one ounce of compassion or concern for the h, even though the H was treating her very rudely and dismissively. She barely acknowledged her, or found the H's behavior funny. I hope Palmer made the OW suffer when she was the h in the other book, because she really deserves it. 

Also, all of this adventure/mercenary stuff is nonsense. I hate the way new male characters are introduced as being good-looking alphas and it becomes painfully obvious that these new men will be H's in later books. She doesn't seem to realize that if you've met one hot alpha mercenary, you've met them all. There are only superficial differences, and that makes it very tedious.
