Rating: 4/5

This was an interesting take on the King Arthur story cycle starting before Arthur knows that he is Arthur and tied into the Robin Hood legends as well. Apparently, Tolkein himself took inspiration from White's book for the Ring series. I thought that the pace was plodding at times, but seeing the world as a bird was super cool and the battlescenes were fun as well. There are three more novellas and books in the The Once and Future King and I think I might be tempted to go all the way with this series.
