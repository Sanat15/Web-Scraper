Rating: 4/5

This depiction of 1930s medicine is Something Else. It's almost a joke, and the serious way it's discussed is laughable.

Otherwise, though, this is a great book. There are some plot holes, but the character stuff is really great - the relationships and fights are superbly managed - and in general, Brent-Dyer is on top of her game here. Joyce has more of a reformation than I expected, too. 

Maybe I like this more than is justified, but even so: I really like this.
