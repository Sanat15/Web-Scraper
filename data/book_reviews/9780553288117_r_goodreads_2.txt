Rating: 4/5

Fascinating book about how lawsuits affect safety measures. This novel is about an airline accident. I found it difficult to put down, as I couldn't wait to find out how it turned out. I also liked the characters and found the plot ingenious, not common. It is one of (I think) only 2 novels he wrote that are not part of the Marsh Tanner detective novels, which turn out to be very good, also. Overall, I recommend this author in general.
