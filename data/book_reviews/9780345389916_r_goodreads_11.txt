i was bored to LITERAL tears and could not bring myself to finish this book fully. part of my hatred is just fueled by my horrible professor who made me read this, but mostly, i just could not find a single redeeming quality of this book. 

your message means nothing if it isn't communicated well. and im not gonna sit here and defend a homewrecker because she's supposed to be this "new woman" and watch as she upholds the idea that feminism must be immoral to be "true feminism." this book feels, in fact, very ANTI-feminist in the sense that Jane is not a girls girl and also seems to look down upon the way her grandmother and mother lived their lives (as wives and stay-at-home mothers). 

women. listen. you can be a stay-at-home mother, educated, career-focused, a wife, traditional, progressive, etc. AND STILL SUPPORT WOMEN WHO DO NOT MAKE THE SAME CHOICES FOR THEIR LIFE THAT YOU HAVE. AND WE NEED TO STOP THIS IDEA THAT THERE IS A SINGLE WAY TO EXIST AS A WOMAN CORRECTLY. (i think this is what this book was trying to get at, but it just was executed poorly)
