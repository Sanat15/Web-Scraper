Rating: 2/5

2.5 stars. I wanted to like this book more than I did. I freely admit that I'm not the kind of person who is really into cooking &amp; reading about chefs. Although I really enjoyed Marcus Samuelsson's "Yes, Chef." However, the main problem was that the audiobook narrator was not good - he &amp; the stop &amp; start nature of the narration really detracted from the book. I'd likely give it 3.5 stars if I had read it rather than listening. I did enjoy some of the stories about the CIA, the students, &amp; the instructors. Honestly, I would have enjoyed it more if it had focused more in that direction, although I know that was never the intent. Please don't let this review detract from your interest. If you are someone who is fascinated by cooking, methods, ingredients, &amp; skills, you would likely enjoy this book. Just don't try the audiobook. Another reason is that you would likely wish to go back &amp; re-read or make notes.
