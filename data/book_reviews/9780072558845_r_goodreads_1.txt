Rating: 4/5

This book was super interesting and informative! This was particularly helpful for the literature review portion of my thesis, but is also just an interesting read about the state of education. Sadly, because of the RAPID pace at which educational policy and related issues changes, this book is slightly out of date. However, between this and Dana Goldstein’s book I have a more comprehensive framework through which I can understand current education news, issues, and policy changes!
