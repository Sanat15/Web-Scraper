Rating: 5/5

This book is a mix of a fiction and an (auto)biography. Diego Rivera was a classic mythomane. 
I enjoyed his impossible-to-confirm stories and ‘lies’ like a kid listening stories about Santa Clause. 
Book is a beautiful collection of short stories and anecdotes from his life and many other people, like Frida Kahlo, Picasso, Hitler, Stalin, Charlie Chaplin, Ford, Rockefeller, Mussolini, ....
Pure fun and pleasure with some art and a hint of history and delivered thru a really unique character.
