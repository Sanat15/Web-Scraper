Rating: 3/5

This slight volume is a highly opinionated, rather localized exploration of the Santa Lucia legend and Swedish (and Swedish-American) celebrations and traditions. The viewpoint is Christian and more anecdotal than really well-researched and I am personally more interested in the pagan roots, folklore, and sources for the legends. Nonetheless, it gives an interesting if brief overview of the history and current (as of the late 1980s) observations of the Lucia pageantry. With recipes, which is always appreciated.
