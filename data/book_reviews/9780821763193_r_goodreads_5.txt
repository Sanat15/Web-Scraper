Rating: 5/5

I found The Scent of Roses to be a delightful read. I'm one who loves a bit of humor mixed throughout the story line, and I found myself chuckling out loud through out this read. The earlier book To Have and To Hold in the series makes for a complete unit. I prefer to read books in the order they're written as a series or follow-up in a story line. However each book can stand on its own quite well. I look forward to reading more of Charlene Raddon's work.
