Rating: 4/5

I'd decided to give this book a chance after l happened to read a discussion between two reviewers who were drawing parallels with this Hero, Scott and the Hero in " The odds against " Carl.
Well, Scott pale in comparison with Carl, in cruelty, nastiness, violence and stupidity. 
Don't get me wrong, Scott ( Born of the wind ) did have a strong and imposing personality and he could be nasty and insulting if he wanted to, but he was mild compare to Carl ( who was dreadful).

It was obvious to me that Scott was fascinated with the heroine from the get go and he fell in love with her halfway of the book only the reader did not know that only suspected. 
He had a sharp tongue but his actions showed that he cared for the heroine and tried to help and protect her.
The heroine was wonderful. Beautiful, good hearted, hard-working and had guts, no wonder she won the Hero's heart.
