Rating: 5/5

This an oldie but a good historical romance. It is one of my favourite ones with a lot of emotional depth and romance.
Andre, an aristocrat, and archaeologist/historian is travelling through Turkey when he encounters an orphan, homeless young "boy", Ali, and hires him as his servant. He has no idea that Ali is the daughter of his English mentor left stranded in Turkey. The book is set in Turkey and later in England. I found the story sweet, engaging, heartstring tugging and very romantic.
It is part of a trilogy, but I found this book the best of the 3. I truly love this story and tend to re-read it every now and then. This book has a very special place in my heart.
5 Star solid read, I highly recommend this book!
