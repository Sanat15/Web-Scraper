Rating: 4/5

I read On Wings of Eagles with great interest, since during my having lived in Iran for two years, I was intensely drawn to what had happened in 1979 before the Islamists took over. I lived very near Rezaiyeh and visited there often. By the description in the book of how Perot's men made their escape I could tell it was most likely through the routes that I had traversed in the Zagros mountains.

It was thrilling to follow the escape routes and the dangers that Perot's team faced.
