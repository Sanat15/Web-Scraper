Rating: 4/5

At first I thought I was being dropped into a game of Clue with Colonel Mustard, Miss Peacock, and Miss Scarlet (those are not actually the names of the three permanent residents of Peregrine Manor, but one is indeed a colonel, and the two women fit the roles if not the names). There is an isolated house on the cliffs, and strange goings-on plaguing Maggie Gladstone after her aunt dies (was it murder?) and leaves her the big B&amp;B and its three elderly residents. She calls in Josh January, PI, who's really after a little R&amp;R after an on-the-job injury, to investigate. Piece of cake, he thinks. Of course it's not quite that easy, and I guessed the culprit and the motive very early, but the endearing characters and the romantic sparks made this book a very satisfying read.
