Rating: 4/5

“The Spy Wore Red,” “The Spy Went Dancing” and “The Spy Wore Silk” is a trilogy written by Aline Griffith, Countess of Romanones. Doña Aline was a graduate of College of Mount St. Vincent (my alma mater!) who was recruited after graduation to be a spy. She was sent to Madrid, Spain in 1943. There she met her future husband, the Duke of Romanones. 
These books chronicle her exploits and are highly entertaining reading. The author moved in high society and was a valuable asset for America and its allies.
