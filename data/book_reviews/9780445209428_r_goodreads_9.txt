Rating: 4/5

A marked improvement over Firedrake in almost every way. Knaak's writing has improved and the worldbuilding, dialogue, characters, and pacing are much better this time around. The biggest gripe I have with this book is the lack of action scenes as most of the book is buildup to a showdown with the Ice Dragon (which was pretty awesome). Despite that everything moves along at a good pace and Knaak seems to be getting better at managing all the moving parts in the story. I'll definitely keep reading these as each one is better than the last and they're quick enjoyable fantasy reads. 3.7 stars rounded up.
