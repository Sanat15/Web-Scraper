Rating: 2/5

So... Elaine jilted Mark 8 years ago because her father is apparently a zealous religious freak and arranged her marriage to a guy who had no use for a wife and let his mother control everything (even though he knew that she was bad at it)... I don't blame her for making a mad dash stage right...

Fast forward 8 years and Elaine just happens to be living near one of her jilted guys estate and his daughter and invalid nurse just happen to arrive then things happen in town that the guy has to flee to this particular estate... Followed by rumors and weird happenings intending harm on Mark but he doesn't believe the worst of people... 

It was a cute fluff read with some intrigue but the villain was over the top bat shit crazy and I still have a seriously hard time believing that in just one week, Mark the guy who basically swore to get a wife knocked up then abandon her has pulled a complete 180 and now can't live without Elaine and all of his high handed, libertine ways were just a front.... RIGHT... sure ...

I'm really starting to see why this anthology was so cheap... SIGH... I guess I get what I paid for!
