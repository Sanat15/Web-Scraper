Rating: 5/5

Loved this, I love when the heroine is actually authentic to the time period, it's all I want and this delivered, the heroine was lovely, sweet and understanding among other things, enough to get the enigmatic Michael to fall in love with her, she was such a good wife which is another thing I look for, I've been reading too many books with unlikeable and/or stupid heroines lately so really enjoyed Elizabeth. I loved their relationship throughout the book, it was beautiful, of course there are scenes when you just wish they would talk something out but it wouldn't be a gothic book if there was no suspicions! Another thing, many of these books can be disrespectful towards Ireland and the Irish but thankfully this one was respectful! Overall it was just a lovely book, and unusual in the sense that they're married for the whole book and it's actually a real marriage rather than convivence so it was different and I liked it. The mystery isn't really deep, the main mystery is Michael's distance and behaviour which was fine but it wasn't really a big reveal or anything.
It was a good book with a good characters and a decent storyline, the romance and love scenes were all good, the love scenes were so romantic, so i rate it 5 stars.
