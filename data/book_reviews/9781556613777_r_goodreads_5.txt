Rating: 4/5

WWII is officially over for the Danish people. That didn't mean that there were not pockets of resistance wanting to get revenge, pull off mischievous deeds, and those on the German side trying to avoid capture.

This book is much about forgiving and letting go of the anger the children, especially Peter, who were angry for many things. However, the anger is only hurting the one who holds it.

I definitely recommend this read. My daughter has loved all the Robert Elmer books that she has read.
