Rating: 3/5

A piecemeal biography of Scrope Davies, written after the momentous discovery in the 1970s of his trunk in an old vault of Barclay's. A rake, wit, dandy, gambler, drunk, tennis player, and loyal friend. Seems like Scrope was one of those people who are loads of fun to hang out with at parties, but who never manage to actually DO anything in real life beyond succeed at hanging-on.
