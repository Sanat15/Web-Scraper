Rating: 3/5

I found this early Ken Follett (before he became KEN FOLLETT) caper novel in a Little Free Library box while on vacation. (I love those boxes.)
There are three seemingly unrelated storylines that gradually come together.
The ending was anticlimactic, and the book could have gone on longer (only 253 pages) to better explain what happened to all involved.
I felt let down after reading such a good story.
