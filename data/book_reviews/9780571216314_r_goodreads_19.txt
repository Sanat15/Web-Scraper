Rating: 5/5

I loved this book.
It is gentle because it is set in a time when life was slow paced and people were polite and spent their time drawing botanical pictures. 
It is interesting because of the slowly evolving realisation that Dr Cake might be someone else.
It is easy to read and well written.
I have just bought myself a copy (after reading it twice) and intend to read it again when it turns up in the post (from abebooks).
