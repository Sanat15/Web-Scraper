Rating: 4/5

Admittedly I’m being a bit generous with the rating, but I did enjoy reading this and plan to reread it again in the future. Imho, poems always require rereading and this book is chock-full of both poetry and prose from all centuries of Irish history. It’s the perfect coffee table book for someone like me. Also, the photographs and paintings are very beautiful and excellent writing inspiration (if only every other fantasy and speculative fiction writer out there hadn’t already used Ireland as their source of inspiration).

Note that this book is a highly subjective history of Ireland and its notable writers, but I liked that it touched on mythology, nature, religion, architecture, and important historical events. The author even mentions the Troubles, which were still going on at the time of the book’s publication and undoubtedly affected the creative works made in Ireland during those decades. Would recommend if you want to read a very personal take on Ireland’s literary history, especially poetry.
