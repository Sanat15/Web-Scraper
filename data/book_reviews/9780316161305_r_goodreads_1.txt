Rating: 3/5

By today's eyes this book looks dated with its black and white illustrations, but it is full of useful information for new gardeners who have not yet purchased all the tools by which to conquer their surroundings. This book's title is deceiving as it has nothing to do with garden sheds only their contents covering tools like rakes, lawn mowers, heating pads for germination. My rating considers the fact that I was disappointed as I was looking for some useful ideas for my new garden shed!
