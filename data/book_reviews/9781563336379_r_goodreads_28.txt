Rating: 4/5

A fascinating insight into the late 1970s/early 1980s world of part of the gay BDSM community. This will not be a novel for everyone because it is quite hardcore and therefore confronting in places, but at the same time it is a very compelling exploration of one young man's education and experience in submission. I think one of the things I most enjoyed about it was the epilogue which gave you a brief glimpse into Mr Benson's POV. All in all an interesting read, despite the rather odd mystery sub-plot.
