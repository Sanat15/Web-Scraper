Rating: 3/5

The Home of Dark Butterflies is simultaneously a gritty dive into the lives of kids labelled as "problem children" as well as a beautiful coming-of-age story of a young boy who is forced to adopt a rather analytical perspective from a very early age. The strongest point of this novel are definitely its characters and their descriptions. Simultaneously exaggerated and believable the different characters the protagonist meets are rather fleshed out as well as incredibly human in their actions. After reading The Home of Dark Butterflies many people around the reader may start to make more sense. While the psychological side of the storytelling is incredible some might find the pacing and structure of this novel a bit sluggish and dense. While this heavy and lingering style of writing has its own appeal some might find it undesirable.
