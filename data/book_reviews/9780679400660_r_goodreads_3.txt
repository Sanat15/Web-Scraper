Rating: 4/5

If what this book hints at is true, it is sad, apalling, disgusting and disgraceful. The one unproven, but possible theory goes like this. Right-wing Israeli hawks used the Mossad to covertly help Abu Nidal kill moderate Palestinians who were in favour of peace and trying to work towards a two-state solution.
So the idea is that Israel boosted Palestinian extremists to demonise the Palestinian people in the eyes of the international community.
This just goes to show that I have no idea what the rest of us in the world should think or do about the Middle East.
I unfortunately get so exasperated sometimes that I just feel like walking away and leave them to sort their own mess out, as if I just don’t care anymore. I know that isn’t the right attitude though, but am really tired of behaviours on both sides.
