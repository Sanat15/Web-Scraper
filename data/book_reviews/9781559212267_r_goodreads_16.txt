Rating: 4/5

Another goodie by Ms. Pym! I do like this type of book, its not action packed but rather focuses on the characters. It's like a slice of life about 10 years post WWII in England. It's full of people you know and situations you've encountered, from the coworkers, friends, lovers and spouses. We've all known them, this book shows their reality, the good, the bad, quirks, warts and all. 

Pym is funny, ironic, satirical, bitingly honest, and perceptive. She was a fan of Jane Austen and I see some parallels in this book which I quite like. Jane is compared to Emma Woodehouse, and I can see many similarities in their characters.

What I think I liked most of all in this was the honesty of her characters. None of them were perfect, they weren't scathingly horrible, just everyday people, but she shows us the little cracks that appear, that we all have, in everyday life. 

I'm so happy to know some lovely ladies that introduced me to her, and look forward to reading more Pym.
