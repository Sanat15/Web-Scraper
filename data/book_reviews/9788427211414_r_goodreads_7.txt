Rating: 2/5

I'm a huge fan of Zane Grey's works and this was definitely not one of his better ones. 

Grey captures the feel of the West, as the characters go after buffaloes, but the drama is really forced, even for Grey, as two young people have a chance meeting that leads to instant love. 

There's not much conflict in this outing, as the villains of the piece are dealt with too quickly.

I will say a huge bonus to this edition of the book is the Foreword by Jon Tuska that details why Grey wrote the book and the huge edits that were made to it. This helped me better understand why this book is so different from Grey's other works.
