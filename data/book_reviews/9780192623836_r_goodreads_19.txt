Rating: 5/5

This review is for the 3rd edition.

I've tried to read similar "improving your mind" books, however this is the first one that stuck with me. For me, I very much liked that it was written (essentially) like a science textbook - the sort of thing I would've read at Uni. This, more formal, approach helped me in two ways

1) It was reassuring to see that some mental health issues (say anxiety and attachment in my case) are so common that there is sufficient material to put together a text book. In the best possible way, its nice to see these issues can also be presented just as one would with any other illness. Helped removed some of the feelings of stigma and weakness.

2) In the past I would have, as a result of my own ignorance and preconceived notions, been quite dismissive of concepts such as mindfulness. But seeing them presented here in the format I am used to (that of a scientific document) helped me engage with the ideas properly and in good faith.

I don't think this book is a magic bullet, reading it didn't "solve" anything for me but it definitely helped a great deal and I'd recommend it to anyone "sciencey" that is interested in their own mental health.
