Sarah and Jacob travel down to the market to where Jesus is speaking. Both children become very curious and make their way to see Jesus face to face. Throughout the story, readers learn what we must do in order to reach the kingdom of Heaven. In the end, every child is blessed, including Sarah's.

I liked this book and thought it is a cute easy read for kids. The illustrations are really good, and I especially like the illustration when Jesus is blessing the children at the end.

If allowed to have in the classroom, I think this would be a good book in simply teaching students a little bit about Jesus and the kingdom of Heaven.
