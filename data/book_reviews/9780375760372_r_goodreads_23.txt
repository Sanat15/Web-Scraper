Rating: 5/5

When I started this book I thought I was in over my head. Michener starts with a detailed description of the science behind the creation of the islands. When the “fiction” began, the story took me on an unexpected journey into the culture and through a progression from the first inhabitants to what was modern day when he finished the book (late 1950s). He supposedly spent five years in the islands and completely immersed himself into life in Hawaii. After reading this book it is hard to dispute that fact.
