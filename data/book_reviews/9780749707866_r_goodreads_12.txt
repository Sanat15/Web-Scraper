Rating: 2/5

Interesting, lovely prose, beautifully atmospheric, but disappontingly anti-climactic; I think she had not quite decided where upon the genre spectrum she would fall, so  in the end there is nothing to the hunt really at all, it is just a few pages of tension but no grand revelation, no climax, just -- oh well, this happened, now let's go back to our lives. Her later books are willing to slip out of realism more.
