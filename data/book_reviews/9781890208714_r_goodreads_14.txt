Rating: 3/5

This was apparently Andrew Taylor's first novel and is much less accomplished than his later works, in particular the Marwood and Lovett series. However, it was a good holiday read. William Dougal is a bit of an anti-hero and the ending was somewhat predictable but on the whole I enjoyed it.
I see that there are 8 books in the series; I'm not sure whether I will be interested enough to read all of them but will probably give the next one a go to see where it takes us.
