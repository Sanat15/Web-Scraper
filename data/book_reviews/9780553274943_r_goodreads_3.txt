Rating: 4/5

Thoroughly enjoyed it as Tombstone was based on this novel on the Earps vs The Cowboy Gang. It offers a very close historic accuracy and while it is not my favorite work of fiction on these events (that being the wonderful Doc and Epitaph by Mary Doria Russell) it is up there and it WILL make you want to pursue other works both fiction and non-fiction about the history of this still hotly debated feud.
