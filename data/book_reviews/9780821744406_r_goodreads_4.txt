Rating: 5/5

Sheriff Culpepper, and his dog, goes about their job with gusto, and his pistols control things!

Most definitely five stars worthy and the only downside to me, was the continuous use of God's name in vain, throughout the entire series of unending adventures! Sorry, but I am of the opinion theory degraded by this continuous misuse! The story, otherwise, was an ongoing series of unending gunfighting excursions and adventures, that successfully maintains anyone's undivided attention from beginning to end!
The Author's outstandingly descriptive storylines successfully maintains anyone's undivided attention, as the Sheriff, and his associates, pull no punches when it comes to defending right respective areas of responsibility! Too, the character selections could not have been any better portrayed, as they continuously fulfilled their commanding assignments throughout this compelling western folklore masterpiece!
Definitely recommend this compassionately motivated story to all readers, but especially to you dyed in the wool western folklore enthusiasts, as it will fulfil your innermost desires! You will find yourselves riding along with the Sheriff and his associates throughout the entire series of unending gunfighting adventures! Even you non-western enthusiasts will be completely engrossed within the first few pages of this fast moving series of realistically renditions of how our old American West was probably finally tamed!!!😃
