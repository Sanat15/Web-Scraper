Rating: 3/5

Oh man, these books are turning into a more wholesome Sweet Valley. Best friends fighting over a boy. A double love triangle! And it just kind of ends on a "to be continued" note. Kind of addicting. I'm not sure if these books feel different because I'm old enough that I could have my own kid Stacey's age (I think I would have loved them if they came out when I was 11 rather than 17ish), or if this series truly has taken a strange, hormonal right turn (and might have worked better if the girls were all allowed to move onto ninth grade rather than eighth for the zillionth year). Maybe it's the lack of babysitting stories, and the sense that the girls are growing apart (and not just because two of them are fighting); I recall the original series they relied so much on each other, and now if they're not telling the story, their roles are relegated to mere cameos. Why isn't Stacey talking to Kristy and Mary Anne (or using them as mediators) and confiding instead in the new girl (who hates Claudia and might have her own alterior motive)? As much as I'd like to believe Stacey couldn't help what happened, she wasn't making it any easier on herself by being such a flighty headcase. Ethan deserves better.
