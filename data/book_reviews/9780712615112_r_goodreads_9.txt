Rating: 2/5

Historical romance set during Bonnie Prince Charlie’s ill-fated rebellion in 1745. The protagonists are star-crossed lovers. Not only have their illustrious Scottish Clans been feuding since the middle-ages but they are now on the opposite sides of the political conflict. In fact, it is the heroine’s brother’s refusal to take up arms for the Prince that precipitates his murder at the hands of hero’s brother. After that, the two lovers are separated for 90% of the story, each finding solace in new partners, before being reunited a couple of pages from the end in the most contrived of conclusions. As a historical fiction, it did its job. As a romance, it left me cold. The hero in particular was so callous as to become unintentionally comical. He asks the heroine to forgive him for his part in her brother’s murder and when she doesn’t, he spends the rest of the story pouting at her perfidy. Her not forgiving him instantly for the murder of her brother means that she never loved him and now he feels sorry for himself lol. Talk about gaslighting!
