Rating: 3/5

3 Stars ~ Dana's a divorced mom who battles constantly with her ex-husband on how their deaf 8 yr. old daughter should be raised. Rob wants to deny his child's deafness and insists on her attending a residential school where the kids are taught to appear normal. At first Callie had been taught to read lips, and now she was adept at signing, but Dana was light years behind in signing and was finding it difficult communicating with her daughter that she only saw on weekends. At the urging of the school, Dana had enrolled Callie in riding lessons, and this is where they met Will who trains horses while he's not competing in the rodeo. Much to Dana's surprise Will easily converses in sign with Callie, admitting that he had a family member who was deaf. Callie clearly hero worships Will, and Dana enlists him to teach her to sign. Will can't refuse mother or daughter and quickly finds himself getting too close. Will promised himself to stay clear of women who wanted happy ever afters with a brood of babies, because he knew that deafness was hereditary in his family. Dana was the happy ever after kind, and no matter how strong their attraction to each other was, they had no future.

Ms. Gold's debut is a heartwarming story of two soul mates who find each other through the needs of a special child. Will is hero material all the way, with his sexy cowboy looks and his honorable heart. Dana is a woman struggling to give her daughter a happy childhood, one where her deafness isn't a disability but just a part of who she is. Callie is a delightful child who captures hearts on contact, and almost stole the story. Of course, the evil ex-husband threatens any happiness, until Will steps up to protect the woman and child he's come to love. An enjoyable first for Ms. Gold.
