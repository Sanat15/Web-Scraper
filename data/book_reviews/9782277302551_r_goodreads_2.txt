Rating: 4/5

A classic of erotic literature, *Manuel de civilité pour les petites filles à l'usage des maisons d'éducation* parodies the strict educational manuals of the time and takes the form of a long series of one- or two-sentence tips that teach young girls how to conceal the fact that they know far more about sexuality than their teachers.
The book is a full-blown attack on the hypocrisy of social conventions; the author doesn't hesitate to flout all taboos. The tone is deliberately crude and outrageous. Once the initial shock of the first few pages has worn off, reading this manual becomes irresistibly funny.
