Rating: 4/5

3.75? The first half of reading this I would’ve thought it’d turn out to be one of my favorite psychology books. And I did get a lot out of it—the first half really explored the person-centered stance which I really appreciated and identified with. I also was inspired reading about Roger’s view of how the theory could be applied to settings outside of therapy, environmental and governmental levels. 

The second half focused a lot on applying PCT to education, which just didn’t interest me as much. I also kept being continuously surprised by him throwing ideas about psychic capabilities and the paranormal in there without a lot of exposition. I think it’s also important to recognize that the book was written in the 70s, so some of his predictions or commentary were outdated, of course. I’d be interested in reading more modern works on PCT too.
