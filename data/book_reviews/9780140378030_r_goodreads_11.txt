Rating: 4/5

I picked up this book very randomly in the library while searching for books to read with my kids. The synopsis sounded intriguing but I was unprepared for how surprising the story was; I definitely did not anticipate the major story plot twist toward the end. Excellent build-up and character development! I enjoyed the allegorical references and the parallel stories running alongside Hero's. Mahy's illustrative writing style was a bit challenging for my younger listener but I prefer that over the watered-down juvenile fiction books we've come across recently. 

The setting was a bit confusing at first and when they mentioned a character moving to Australia I thought that was a bit dramatic of them until I realized they're located in New Zealand. It doesn't take long to realize that a lot of details are withheld in the beginning and are drawn out cautiously, but the payoff is well worth the intial confusion as you try to figure out who's who. Truthfully, it matches the organized chaos of the Rapper family in contrast to Hero's selective mutism. 

I read aloud to my kids every night and had to skip a couple of amusing choice words, but overall it was a great book. The suspenseful and sensitive scenes are done very well and are age-appropriate. I understand the ending was symbolic but I honestly didn't care much for it and thought it would have ended just as well a couple of chapters prior. Either way, I'd recommend this book to age 12 and up, although my 8 year-old enjoyed it as well.
