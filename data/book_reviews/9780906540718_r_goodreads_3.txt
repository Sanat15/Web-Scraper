Rating: 4/5

Interesting book.
This is the clearest explanation I have come across yet about the conflict between the early Jewish christians and the gentile christians. This conflict is hinted at in the battles between Paul and the leaders in Jerusalem: Peter, James and others.

A big revelation to me was the impact of the Jewish revolt and the destruction of Jerusalem in the war of 66-70 C.E. Up until then the leaders in Jerusalem had exerted fairly strong control over Jewish-Christian synagogues/churches in the diaspora. With the destruction of the temple and Jerusalem, power transferred to the church at Rome which began to usurp authority and exert control over the gentile churches. It is the breaking point at which Christianity left Judaism and the birth of the Roman Catholic Church.

There is also a clear outline of how Pauline Christianity set the tone for what is believed by christians today; and it is quite different from what Jesus and his Jewish followers believed.
