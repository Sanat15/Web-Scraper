Contemporary (Pre-smartphone) second chance romance.
WOW! This was a complicated plot line with secret pregnancies, lies and more lies.
Nicholas and Amy where in love as early adults (20 to his 23) when a younger sister decides to break everything up with some bad choices. We get a cute 7 year old, 'Triple' who has a medical problem which brings everyone together to hash out the lies and secrets and save a marriage.
HEA.
No one in this story is innocent because there is a lot of lies and mistakes. Maybe Nicholas is the only one that never lies but he doesn't dig deep to find out the truth either.
Frustrated with this crazy car crash of a story.
