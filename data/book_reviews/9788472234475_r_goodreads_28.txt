Rating: 3/5

"It's not you it's me" is what I feel like saying to this book!

It hit a couple of tender spots for me at the start because of my own mother/daughter relationship. I put aside for a while because I found the switch in POV to the mother a bit hard to handle. It was rhetorical whining really &amp; the story was uniformly depressing. I had to keep taking breaks &amp; read something else.

I'm unlikely to read anything else by this author.
