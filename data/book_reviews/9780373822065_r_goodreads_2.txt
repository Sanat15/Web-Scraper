I grabbed this one out of a box of books a friend brought me ages ago. I decided it was time I started going through it. I picked this one because it had a sporty theme and with the Olympics going on, I thought why not? You'll definitely know it was written during the Cold War. I still enjoyed it very much. The danger, secrets and wonderful writing all combine to engage you in the character's world and relationship. Glad I decided to open that box of books. ~ Kim
