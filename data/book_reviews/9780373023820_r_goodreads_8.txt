Rating: 4/5

This is a hard book to rate. I'm not crazy about two of the plot elements, the very young (in years and experience) h with older H, and the H's "you're a tart" attitude based on her conman stepfather. On the plus side it is intense, readable and I liked it. I find Marjorie Lewty tends to build in ambiguities, especially related to the H and h's relationship, and that's true here.

St. Margarets has spoilerized this so I won't. 

The ambiguities lie in the nature of "want" vs. "love" and whether either feels lasting love or not. And how their feelings about Tim, her husband/his brother, who died the day after their runaway wedding, may interfere with their feelings and relationship. 

The h did not mourn Tim as much as I would have liked to see. Instead she somehow transferred her growing care for Tim to his brother, the H. Will it last? Who knows. The HEA is realistic and the fact H stopped making love when he did gives it more credence, but I think they have or will have some guilt about Tim.
