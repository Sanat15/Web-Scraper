Rating: 4/5

I saw the new Met Opera adaptation today. The magic is lost with the modern stage settings and costumes which leave us with just a flute and a cannoli.

UPDATE:
I enjoyed Opera Tampa's traditional staging much more today than the modern Met adaptation so I'm bumping my rating up a star to four. Also, I'm hopeful that since the only death in the opera is that of the monstrous snake, and since the ending is a perfectly happy one, I'll be graced with a respite this evening from the persistent haunts of my recurring late night primal screams. A good night's sleep is just what the piper ordered!
