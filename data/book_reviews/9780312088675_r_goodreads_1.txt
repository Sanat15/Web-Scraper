Rating: 5/5

I picked up this book for a dollar at a table sale at Quatrefoil Library, and I'm so glad I did, and so astonished that I hadn't heard of Madelyn Arnold before. She is a truly wonderful writer, and this anthology of stories moved me more than any collection of stories has in ages. The voices of the various protagonists are SO vivid and distinct, it is difficult to believe that they all came from the same author; Arnold's sympathetic imagination is astounding. Recommended for any lover of short stories in general, and certainly for anyone interested in LGBTQ literature.
