Rating: 3/5

Karpov's Brain isn't as cheesy as its title suggests, but gets close. It also isn't as cheesy as the cover art suggests, but certainly tries. 

Set in the Soviet Union, Karpov's Brain focuses on some heady themes: politics, antisemitism, and neuroscience (though the science tends toward the pseudo variety. There's a lot of talk about the sinister aspects of one particular hemisphere.) Our titular Karpov is a brutally efficient KGB agent who, in a risky gamble to calm his paralyzing seizures, agrees to an experimental brain procedure from his former lover, Dr. Irina Tashenko. 

How will the surgery affect the already unstable Karpov? Will the Jewish dissidents survive the KGB's schemes? Will the beautiful but cold Tashenko escape Karpov's lecherous grasp? And what other plans does she have for... 

  Karpov's Brain
 

And yes, this spy thriller is exactly as fun and ridiculous as these questions make it sound. So grab a slice of your favorite cheddar or brie and enjoy yourself.
