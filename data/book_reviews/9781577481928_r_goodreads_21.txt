My 1st Copy -This book was presented to me on my first time attending the United Methodist Women Meeting at Seminole, OK on 28 Oct 2008.

My 2nd Copy - I found this on Mom’s bookshelves in her “Family Room” where she had photo/posters of all her kids, grand, and great-grands and their partners after she passed away on 01 November 2025. It was purchased at Haslam’s Bookstore in St. Pete, FL (one of my favorite bookstores in all the world - it was so huge.
