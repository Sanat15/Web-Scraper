Rating: 3/5

A well researched biography and a difficult task at that, considering the layers of fiction within the subject herself. Anais portrays a paradoxical character in her own life and her self edited diaries reflect the construct she has built around herself; one full of deception and compartmentalising.
The writing itself was a tad bland and flat, considering the subject matter. It became tedious and clunky at times as Fitch stitched a patchwork of a life into a readable account.
