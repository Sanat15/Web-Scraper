Rating: 4/5

A very high four star rating goes out to Mr. Grey from this reader. For a good western novel some authors are just a given and Zane Grey is one of them. When I spied this on our libraries shelf I knew I would have to give it a shot. So, a young man signs up with a buffalo hunting outfit just at the beginning of the great buffalo slaughter. Meeting a young lady just before lighting out on the trail will just add that much more to the story. The research Grey did must have been amazing. From my understanding he actually spent time getting the real story of the buffalo hunt from someone who was actually there. So for a great western give this one a spin and I am sure you will not walk away disappointed.
