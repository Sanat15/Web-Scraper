Rating: 4/5

I really actually quite enjoyed reading this. Before you hold up your hands saying "Whoaaa! Feminism alert!", the majority of the women travellers mentioned in this book aren't bra-burners, but are as the author says "women whose travels were or are the result of a compulsion".

As someone who could be likened somewhat to Bilbo Baggins, who likes home comforts and thinks there is plenty of fun and exploring to be had in my own country, I found myself as I progressed through this book, starting to dream of adventure and felt foreign lands beckon. In reality, the countries explored and travelled through back in the 19th century have changed from what Margaret Fountaine and Mary Kingsley would have experienced. However for many of these women it was a chance to escape social strictures of the day and I think that is something that is certainly still relatable to us today.
