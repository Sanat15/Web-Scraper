Rating: 4/5

"I'm not used to playing these games, of being satisfied with kisses. In the past, I've always taken what I wanted with few exceptions. Then you come along with your damned red hair and green eyes - and the menacing specter of your father, the reverend. And I get the awful feeling I'm being reformed."

I like it. It reminded me why I used to love Janet Dailey.

The story follows the minister’s daughter from Iowa who goes on vacation alone. In New Orleans, she’s propositioned by an elderly man who, after drinking too much, mistakes her for a prostitute.
The good-humored h finds it hilarious. Imagine her being mistaken for a prostitute! She quickly names a large sum to get rid of him and rushes back to her hotel. Her trouble begins when a handsome stranger shows up, offering to take her on. Evidently he can afford her services.
The hero attempts some kissing and groping, but the heroine outsmarts him and manages to escape. A few days later, she befriends a kind woman who invites her on a cruise.

Throughout the story, I kept wanting to warn the h not to be so trusting of strangers.

But then the hero reappears, so everything ends well. The older woman is apparently his aunt.

There’s a mystery involving the aunt, though the hero refuses to discuss family matters with a stranger (no matter kissing and groping the said stranger).
Why 4 stars? I wanted the H to make a grander gesture in the end.
