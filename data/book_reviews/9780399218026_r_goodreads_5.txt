Rating: 3/5

Couldn't find this book at any library closer than 80 miles away, so thanks YouTube! The story is sad about Grandad Bill's passing. However, it doesn't offer much comfort and doesn't provide any real answers about what it means to mourn, or die, or even to live. It does start me thinking about my own family and those that have passed away. I am reminded of what they mean to me and how much I love them. It also makes me think about what I am doing each day to try and make a difference in someone's life, especially my family.
