Rating: 5/5

This was a really good story about Mallory and Drew. Mallory finds out she's pregnant with Drew's baby and doesn't want to ruin the friendship between them. At the same time they find out that Drew's mom died and she kept her cancer a secret from everyone. Drew has to decide if he wants to give up his job as a traveling correspondent and settle down in his hometown. Mallory doesn't think Drew will ever see her as more than a friend. These two have some hard decisions to make. Drew learns a lot about himself. Mallory will do whatever she can to provide for her and her baby. These two have some good friends that are their for them.
