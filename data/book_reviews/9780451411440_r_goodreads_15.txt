Rating: 4/5

Laurie Draper (36) is in Austin, TX setting up a magazine. Her relationship with bf Dale is over. She's a mess. But finds friends and a new life at The Crossroads, and the cafe. She meets Graham and bonds with him but he has ugly secrets. There are issues with asbestos and her depressed father. She has to make choices re family, work, and decide what will bring her happiness. Many of us are going through similar issues as we deal with life on a daily basis. Well written.
