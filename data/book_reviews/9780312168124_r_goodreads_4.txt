Rating: 2/5

This wasn't a bad book. The main character, Angela Matelli, is a Boston P.I. cut from the same cloth as Sue Grafton's Kinsey Millhone. I thought it was well written. The plot revolves around a "true name theft". OK. These days we call it "identity theft". I know. There was a time when it wasn't so common. This book would have been more interesting at that time. Now, however, it's a little dated.
