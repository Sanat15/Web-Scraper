Rating: 3/5

Arsenic and Old Lace
Such a funny story about murder! This play definitely makes the audience take to heart the saying, "Don't judge a book by its cover."

Detective Story
I love that this play takes place only in the police station. Because the location is static, the audience is able to learn much more about the various characters and in a more interesting way. I also love the intertwining of the professional and the personal in this play.

Kind Lady
This story is kind of a horror play. I think what makes it just as scary today as when it premiered is that this still happens today, albeit perhaps a little differently.
