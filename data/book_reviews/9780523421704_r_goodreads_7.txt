Rating: 4/5

Does an excellent job at capturing the feeling of the TV show, but raises the stakes and makes the action more gritty and exciting. Many minor details differ from the show, which is fine as there is no set canon and even the show often contradicted itself in that regard.

Despite numerous typos, it was very fun to read and I loved the character interactions. Even the typos have something of the show's cheesy spirit in them~
