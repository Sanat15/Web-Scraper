Rating: 5/5

This book surveys the life cycle of businesses, organizations, and even nations. From the entrepreneurial prophet, through the barbarian who conquers the territory, through the builder who builds the capacity of the business, to the administrator who organizes it, to the bureaucrat who presides over its decline. This is a tremendously practical guide, with applications on almost every page. How to avoid decline and maintain the vitality of the organization is the purpose of the book. The answer is in a synergy between the aforementioned characters, leading to an ever-renewing cycle that resurrects the failing organization or takes a thriving one on to new heights. I highly recommend it as a guide to the leader of a business, organization, or even a government.
