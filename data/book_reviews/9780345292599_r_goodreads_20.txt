Rating: 3/5

The kindle version of this book appears to have been loaded with OCR, and not fully read after doing so. 
It needs some formatting work. The transitions between places should have a blank space between them. There are a few mistakes in the OCR conversion (or typing) where letters were incorrectly converted, and a simple scan for misspellings would have caught those.

The rating is solely based on the formatting because it makes the book harder to read and understand. Fortunately, I have read the books and liked them, and I still have my paperbacks so I can read them. It is nice having them on the Kindle, but the formatting makes them harder to read and will likely push people to drop the book before they finish it.
