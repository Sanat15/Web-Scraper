Rating: 4/5

"Nothing is ever buried, the earth rolls in its tracks, it never goes anywhere, it never changes, only the hope changes like morning and night, only the expectations rise and set. Why does there have to be promise before destruction?"

A western noir. An allegorical storytelling of the battle between good and evil with nobody winning. A story of false hope that life will get better. A story of people not able to face the truth. Do we run? Do we bury our heads in the sand? Or do we face the enemy regardless of the outcome? 

Gripping and dark through and through. The last third of the book felt a little too long and belabored, like a very slow death, but if you can take it, I recommend Welcome to Hard Times.
