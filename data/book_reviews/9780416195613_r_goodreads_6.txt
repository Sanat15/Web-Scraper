Rating: 3/5

If Cuckoo in the Nest is the story of post-war masculinity and the coming back of a generation, then this is the story of the girls and womenfolk around them. It focuses on young Elsie Hollis who is grappling with life as a scholarship girl at the local grammar school and suffering from the bullying of some of her peers. It is a dark book at points as it touches on child-abuse, parental neglect, and post-traumatic stress in a side-plot which is handled with delicacy, and it reads a lot younger than Cuckoo In The Nest which perhaps reflects the age of the protagonist herself.
