Rating: 3/5

With Open Hands by Henri Nouwen is a gentle, poetic invitation to prayer- less about technique and more about how we think about prayer and approach it: with surrender, vulnerability, and presence before God. 
I found moments of beauty and quiet challenge throughout.

That said, parts of the book drifted into territory that felt at odds with my beliefs. Perhaps I simply didn’t understand him, but at times the language blurred the line between biblical truth and a more generalized spirituality. 
There was a distinct lack of grounding in Scripture and an absence of a clear expression of the gospel. 

Still, there’s a tenderness to Nouwen’s writing that stirs reflection, even when I don’t fully align with his theology.
