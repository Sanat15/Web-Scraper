Rating: 4/5

This book is utterly delightful. I first listened to it as an audiobook with my 11-year-old son, and I have re-read it several times in the intervening two decades (so has he). We also read everything else in the series and attended one of the few talks by an author that I have ever gone to with my children. Charming, funny, but also able to make serious points. Highly recommended.
