Rating: 4/5

Marcia Davenport strikes again!!! With the polar opposite of The Valley of Decision, which spanned 70 years and 4 generations -- this was about one woman over a single dramatic week. Typically gorgeous writing as we have come to expect from Marcia, wonderful descriptions of New York and Jessie's lifestyle and her relationships with the people around her. I did find the ending a little tough to understand or square away with what had come before but I do think it was the right way to end it...
