Rating: 5/5

Eight years ago they were once engaged and when the wedding day came, she jilted him at the altar and was promptly disowned by her warply religious father and was never heard of again at least by her known name as Mary Thompson. She has re~invented herself and found her calling as a children's books illustrator in Cornwall where she took sanctuary at her former governess' cottage. He is now the Earl of Bridgeport and sinister forces are stalking him so he also took refuge (after a duel was forced upon him in London) in his lesser known property at Treselyan Manor in Cornwall. As fate would have it, they met and they were at war. He planned on revenge and she does not want anything to do with him except for his starved for love and learning endearing daughter. Unknown to both of them, they have a business connection ~ their creative pleasures, his secret poet identity and her secret artistic illustrator persona collaboration via her agent and his publisher. A brilliant and entertaining read.
