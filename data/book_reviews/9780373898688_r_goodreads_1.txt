Rating: 4/5

"The Bride of the Delta Queen" is the story of Selena and Chance.

Wow!

The book begins with our h vacationing in New Orleans and waiting for her friend. She is a minister's daughter from Iowa, and finally exploring the city. She gets mistaken by a bunch of men as the women of the night, and mockingly names her price. The H overhears the conversation and moves on to proposition her. The h tries to deny him and escapes..
Soon she meets an older lady who is waiting for her lover, and decides to board the famous boat called Delta Queen across the Mississippi river as her companion. She realizes that the H is her nephew, and has come aboard to break the engagement, and battle for her heart..

I definitely did NOT see the twist coming- and it totally did make me cry. Cynical H, confused h aboard a ship filled with memories and history. Their chemistry was average, the H did come on a bit too strong- a trait that I've come to realize in Ms Dailey's heroes.

Enjoyed it!
