Oh my, my, my. This was precious. "Professor"-style hero...yum. Heroine with very interesting backstory, who is instantly smitten with the hero. She is also loving and loyal. I loved this couple!

Low angst, high on the adorable rating.

One-of-a-kind "meet-cute"...She sees hero skinny-dipping in the lake! Hee hee!

There is also a fun twist at the end.

Grade: A

Look forward to rereading this one!
