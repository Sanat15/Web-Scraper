Rating: 4/5

A posse led by a legendary lawman goes after a clever gang of robbers. It’s 1906 and the real theme is the end of the mythic west. Both posse and bad men are misfits and cast offs (the posse has a child molestor who is worse than the robbers) men out of place and time - no one gets out alive. The bad men find the legendary Hole in the Wall by using a map in a dime novel about Butch Cassidy. Estleman has a nice line in slightly ornate prose in his westerns.
