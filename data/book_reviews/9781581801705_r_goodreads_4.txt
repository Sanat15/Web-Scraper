Rating: 3/5

Lovely book, the illustrations are gorgeous and it's worth reading just for that, but it lacks in substance as far as actually making your own journal. Oh, it goes over it but it's exactly one page. I guess I was hoping for a lot more. Mostly the book is advice about getting out there and sketching or painting in public or in nature. It's worth taking a look at but not worth keeping for my library. Obviously, this is just my opinion and others might have found it more useful!
