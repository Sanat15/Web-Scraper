Another collection of 3 Nero Wolfe mysteries. 1. The death of the top man responsible for increasing the bobbins company's profits was blamed on Wolfe's and Archie's bootblack apparently he does service for the company male bosses, too... plus his daughter works there. Then the bootblack apparently jumped off a cliff because of his guilt? in doing away with the wiz boss for debauching his daughter according to police reports. The orphaned bootblack's daughter, Elma Vassos (apparently Vassos was a great admirer of Wolfe's for the great detective's cut~out picture was framed in a place of honor at his house according to Archie) took sanctuary at Wolfe's house and she became their client and guest until her father's funeral. So to flush out the murderer, Wolfe sued the Board and Cramer for $1M each for slander. 2. Another case of a person spreading vile rumors against an innocent young woman's reputation to force her into marriage. This is about having the correct moral values? and killing to protect a loved one vis~a~vis killing to silence someone. With the correct moral value, one should own what one did even it it means going to jail and not pile in the body count. 3. This deals with a crime of passion and unrequited lust... that the murderer have been kept hanging and teased long enough by the victim, specially if he also has pretensions and exaggerated opinions about himself and what is due him... what some would call his Consequence... very bad and deadly combination when a man's ego has been battered and when such a man is slightly off... either he may charge it to one of life's disappointments or he may take revenge... in this instance, he killed the cause of his misery.
