Rating: 5/5

I bought this book at the bookstore within Cambodia’s Killing Fields memorial centre, following the shopkeeper’s recommendation that this was the best account of the Khmer Rouge regime by far. I can see why she thought this. Kiernan, the author, interviewed more the 500 people who lived through the regime, ranging from members of the regime itself to normal people caught up in the so-called revolution. This allows him to write a deeply personal account not just of political trends and activities, but of what normal Cambodians went through on a day-to-day basis under the Khmer Rouge - which was often so horrific that at several points I had to stop reading. Despite how hard it was to get through at times, this was an excellent account, and I’m glad to have learnt more about a part of history I knew so little about.
