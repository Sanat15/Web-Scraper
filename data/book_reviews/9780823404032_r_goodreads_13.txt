Rating: 4/5

Oh Tomie, you take my breath away with your visions of Mary and rose-filled tilmas. Sigh. This book set me on the verge of tears, as your stories often do.. You really know how to visually represent both the importance and intimate nature of well-loved, well-worn stories, and make them new and yours, and also all of ours. Hooray, hooray, hooray.
