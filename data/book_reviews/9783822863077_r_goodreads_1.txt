Rating: 5/5

An excellent overview of a fantastic and enormous collection.

The format is a full page reproduction facing a page of information about the artist. In some cases where they own multiple works by an important artist, additional smaller repros are surrounded by another page or two of explanation. By and large the focus is on technique and art-historical influence; gossip and adulation are avoided (sometimes to a degree which is almost disingenuous, as when they imply that Caravaggio was scandalous because of his stylistic innovations and leave out his wild behavior). 

Contents range from medieval to modern, so recommended whatever your art preferences are.
