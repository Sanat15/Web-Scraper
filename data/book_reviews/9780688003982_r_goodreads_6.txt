Rating: 5/5

Oh my how I loved this book. I read it so long ago during a summer break. And I got lost inside the story. Back then, there were authors who had a great ability to craft tales that took readers down a rabbit hole of the connected relationships of the characters. They were Harold Robbins, Sidney Sheldon, Belva Plain, Judith Krantz, William Goldman and Jackie Collins. And even Monique Van Dooren's book "Night Sanctuary." But Century still lives inside me like a fantasy and reading it was like a magical lucid dream. Those lazy days of summer and reading a book in a backyard hammock are long gone but the memory of this book still lives inside me.
