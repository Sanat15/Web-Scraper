Rating: 4/5

For all that I loved The Doors ( I remember that some of my classmates went to see them), this book seemed to lack the passion of the time. I remember that Ray Manzarek didn't much like the book either (although he disliked Oliver Stone's film even more, as I recall) but I found it interesting, at least, if a little cold. I'm looking forward to reading Greil Marcus's book, even if it's more from the point of view of a fan (And frequent concertgoer with the group) telling how a relatively few intense years of performance left a mark that will last a lifetime. In short, I enjoyed this book but not as much as I thought I would.
