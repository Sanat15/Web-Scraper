Rating: 4/5

I really liked this book. I was trying to find books that were informative about the Mormon religion and what it's all about. This is a story about a Christian girl that receives a scholarship to BYU and decides to go to the college and experience the Mormon way of life. 

She learns a lot along the way about what Mormons think and their way of life and really tries to see life, God and religion from their standpoint. I won't ruin the ending but I will say it's a fun and easy read and quite interesting.
