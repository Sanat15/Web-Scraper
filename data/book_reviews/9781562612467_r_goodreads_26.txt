Rating: 5/5

This is a great book, a great resource on European art and history. Rick Steves has done a great job keeping it interesting and funny all along. To those who find reading history boring, this book will certainly be a nice surprise for you. 

Though it is advised to finish the book cover to cover before starting on your trip through museums and historic ruins &amp; castles of Europe, if forced to do otherwise please make sure you read at least last 3 chapters Art Appreciation, Music Appreciation &amp; Thirty-Two Millennia in Six Pages (total 40 pages) at least.
