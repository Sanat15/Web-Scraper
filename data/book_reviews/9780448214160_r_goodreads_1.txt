Rating: 4/5

An interesting book of short stories with wonderful illustrations. The book starts off with the author, Elizabeth Coatsworth, telling readers that some of the stories were made up by her husband, and some by her, and challenging readers to guess which one is which. (She tells which ones are hers upside down afterwards.) 

The Snow Parlor, The Other Side of the Hill, and The Toymaker’s Housekeeper were the standouts for me. 

3.5 Stars
