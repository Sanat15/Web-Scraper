Rating: 1/5

I did not like this book. I picked it up hoping for a historical novel about anchoresses and women in the Middle Ages. What I found was a deranged fantasy set from a modern male priest's imagination - NOT historical, not interested in the actual story of women or Christine, the (real) Anchoress of Shere. Honestly, I only got about 20 pages in - there's a  brutal medieval rape of a young woman by a priest, in a "prima noctis" fashion  that I found extremely infuriating.
