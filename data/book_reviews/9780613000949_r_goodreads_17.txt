Rating: 3/5

I read this last night when I couldn't sleep. It has always been a childhood favourite of mine but after re-reading it I have downgraded it to 3 stars (it was 4 before). When I read this as a child (probably around 6th grade), I loved the whole club thing and if I remember correctly, I tried to start my own club (no insurance required) but it was a spectacular failure. Reading now though, I can't believe how horrible these girls are! It is still quite funny and I always loved the idea of the dictionary pages punishment but I just can't get over these kids. Perhaps my adult mind is getting in the way of the innocence of reading a book from my childhood or maybe I was just more tired than I thought last night and should have just gone to bed.

I will always have fond memories of this book. I don't even have my orginial copy (it had the cover with the 2 girls and 2 boys) anymore, it had been read so many times it literally fell apart but by that time Sixth Grade Secrets was no longer available - you could only buy Pig City. I was really disappointed about this. So I have a copy of Pig City and hopefully one day, I can get an old copy of Sixth Grade Secrets back on my shelf.
