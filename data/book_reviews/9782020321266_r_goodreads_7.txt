Rating: 4/5

I'm a total sucker for time-travel novels, and Replay is a damn clever one filled with unexpected twists and traumatic experiences.After a fatal heart attack at age 43, Jeff Winston wakes up baffled to learn he is not dead, but a young college student again back in 1963, (no spoiler here) and as he begins to relive his life over and over and over again, he becomes a bit more prepared and curious each time. Wanting to know the cause of this unusual phenomena, he finally stumbles across an interesting clue resulting in an outcome that gives special meaning to his tumultuous recurring lives.Replay is a fast moving 300 pages that can in no way compare to my two favorites in the world of time-travel, (Outlander and 11/22/63) but I still found it to be a unique and enjoyable read I'm glad I did not miss. Life IS So Short!
