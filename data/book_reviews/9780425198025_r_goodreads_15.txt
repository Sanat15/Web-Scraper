Rating: 4/5

Amelia Grey’s A Hint of Seduction is the second romance about one of the Terrible Threesome. As the story begins, our heroine, Catherine is riding to get help for her injured groom when she literally almost collides with the Earl of Chatwin’s horse. Catherine is unseated and John (Chatwin) attempts to make sure she is uninjured. As he places her upon his horse to walk her back to get help, Catherine steals his horse and flees to get the help needed. The ensuing farce of why John lost his horse and how a woman (or ghost) came to be on that horse is quite amusing.
Catherine’s character is a bit naïve and occasionally annoying, but overall very refreshing. She has the confidence to own her feelings, her behavior, and her relationships. Her goal for her season is not to find a husband, but rather to discover the identity of her biological father. John, however, has no goals and really does not understand or accept his own feelings. His saving character trait, is that he adores women of all ages, shapes, and sizes. He enjoys dancing with all the women at a ball including the less popular ones. He makes each one feel special.
The novel is a pleasant read. I enjoyed some of the secondary characters particularly Lady Lynnette’s tendency to gather gossip and Victoria’s belief in her match making brilliance. I always enjoy a fun romance and this was certainly that.
