Rating: 4/5

This is the first of two Zagwitz stories, suitable for junior readers. It's an adventure story set in the World of Creativity with the hero Zagwitz a potty inventor, his helpers to mention a few -Flewy, Awoo, Eggbert, Lucy &amp; William- and a host of baddies, my favourite being the mogster pirates.
There are illustrations throughout the book which help young readers to picture the characters.
It is funny, well written and full of imagination.
