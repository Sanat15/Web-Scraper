Rating: 5/5

A lovely read! A collection of photos of mothers/daughters, sisters, friends, interspersed with poetry, quotations, paragraphs create a "celebration of enduring friendship." The need for and delight in friendships of all kinds at all ages is a part of the DNA of women. We crave the depth and comraderie of relationships with women--Ross captures that in this book which can easily be read in an hour or so. Food for thought and a feast for the heart and soul! I wish I had multiple copies to share.
