Rating: 5/5

On YouTube and Instagram a young man recently recreated a channel @becomingahusband. Each day he does a task he thinks will assist him to be a kind loving spouse and attract his future bride and mom of their children. 

Today, If You Give a Mouse a Cookie is a book he chose to read because he said he doesn’t consider himself a good out loud reader, but he wants to practice for his future children. 

How could I pass up the opportunity to hear the story I had never read before. 

The story theme about our choices lead to consequences and new decisions is super important for children to learn. Awesome illustrations of the journey the mouse goes through as he lives the decisions he’s made. Not to give away the ending but it has a great full circle moment I do not predict. 

Great story. Great idea to practice reading to children when confidence is not so good. And, if you’re not following this young man recommend his channel. To me, this is why the internet exists. So much fun.

Random read and hey, if you’ve got littles this may be the story for you. 

The theme is a great way to explore decisions making skills. Cause and effect. So teachers to parents head over to the library or see if Libby has a copy you can borrow with your digital option using your library card. Check this book out for a great life lesson and awesome story about a little mouse who likes cookies.

Plus, you could have a milk and cookie party after the story. And with art supplies do a similar activity as our mouse friend from the story. You’ll have to read to find out what this is :)
