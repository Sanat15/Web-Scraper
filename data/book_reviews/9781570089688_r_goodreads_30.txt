Rating: 3/5

It's a short book, fairly decent. Having read Napoleon Hill's Think and Grow Rich, this book seems like just a primer to the aspect of positive thoughts influencing a person's life positively.

I disagree with the author on 2 points:

1. All disease is a result of negative thoughts. I strongly disagree. I have seen some of the most positive, cheerful and happy people falling prey to deadly diseases(cancer) and just living the rest of their days in pain. The author's statement might be true in 75% of cases, but definitely not all of them.

2. There is no thing as luck. What I mentioned in point 1 is a good example of bad luck. Children born to billionaire industrialists and other privileged people, have a significantly easier childhood than 99% of other similar-aged people. They are free to choose their own path without worrying about money or about feeding their families. You can't choose where you are born. There is definitely an element of luck.
