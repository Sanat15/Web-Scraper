Rating: 3/5

3 stars. this one was okay but had no central conflict. the H is an absentminded and mild mannered surgeon and RBB who falls for the h at around 40% and then bides his time waiting for her to fall for him. for me, this plot formula of Betty's is a bit boring.

h (27?) has become a housekeeper chef to a horribly selfish and cheap old lady after her dear father died. however, she is saving up to set up a catering business while also supporting her mum. H is the horrid old lady's nephew. at first he watches out for the h out of mild concern for her wellbeing and a sense of fairness because he knows his aunt is horribly inconsiderate and he is mostly annoyed that he keeps putting himself out for doing so. then he realises he is on love but she doesn't love him yet, so he keeps helping her through various misfortunes until she finally falls in love with him.

it was interesting enough to keep me reading to the end but it didn't have as much depth of plot or romantic chemistry as I like, but then again I do like the angsty books and this hand almost none.
