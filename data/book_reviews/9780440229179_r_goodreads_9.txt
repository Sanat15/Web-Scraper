Rating: 5/5

This book was written abt the Armenian Genocide during WWI. We read this book for school and it was very powerful, moving, and heartbreaking. This book will break u down and build you back up as a more grateful and appreciative person. Even though this was a sadder read I do think everyone should read this book because the Armenian Genocide is not as well-known as the Holocaust but I feel that both were equally horrific.
