Rating: 4/5

A decent Shivers novel. Probably being a bit generous with 4 stars but the last section of the book makes up for the somewhat mediocre first 2/3'rds of the book. The atmosphere is a bit lacking in this one but there are some decent genuine scares near the end that save it. Give it a go if you're a fan but there are better Shivers books out there. 3.5 stars rounded up.
