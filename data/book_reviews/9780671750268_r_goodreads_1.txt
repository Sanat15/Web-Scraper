Rating: 4/5

I very much enjoyed this. Much of this is well before my life, but I’m very familiar with Julia Child, and some famous books like Fannie Farmer and Joy of Cooking. I wish the book did a better job with detailing how Americans ate before this period to contrast with changes that took place. The numerous stories about meals were great. Lots of recipes. My main disappointment with the book is the endless introductions of people who popped into and out of his life. I found it too dense to keep track of.
