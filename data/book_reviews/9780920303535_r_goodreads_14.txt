Rating: 5/5

Love this book and I'm happy to say it includes an illustration of a mother breastfeeding her child. Each page is dedicated to a part of the body which is described in different ways. There is a page on Nipples where one of the illustrations is a mother breastfeeding her baby while she reads a story to an older child. Underneath this illustration it says "Milky nipples". Overall it is fun book to read to kids, while at the same time teaching them about various parts of the body. As well, there are lots of details in the illusrations which are fun to talk about with your kids. One extra point I have to make - although there is only one breastfeeding image, it's enough to teach children that breastfeeding is a normal way to feed a child.
