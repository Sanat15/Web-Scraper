Mixed blessings

She was married to a police officer that was killed in action. Then she learns that the baby she has been raising was switched at birth and is not her son. There were two serious deliveries at the same time at a small hospital. Peter finds out this same information at the same time. Peter decides to go meet the other mother. They continue to interact and there is drama trying to decide how to handle this information. It is a special story
