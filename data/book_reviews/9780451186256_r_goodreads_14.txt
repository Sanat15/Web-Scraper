Rating: 4/5

I realize that it's not easy to write story about a plain-looking heroine and a handsome, rogue-ish hero. To me, the hardest part is how to convince reader about the hero's change of heart. Most of the stories I've read so far relies on sexual attraction between hero and heroine, which - after some time - doesn't work very well with me. However, this book DID IT. True, we will always need the sexual chemistry to prove a point, but this book focuses more on other aspects, yet still believable.

Another thing is, the writer managed to make turn me from wanting to smack Jack on the head for being heartless in his seduction to Mary to rooting for him as my champion hero of the book. 

Good job, Ms Hern! :)
