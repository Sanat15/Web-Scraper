****DNFed on page 194****

Okay, this is gonna be a difficult one…

I’ve ultimately decided to not rate this book for a variety of reasons, the main one being that I liked it the first time I read it (gave it 4 stars) but had mixed opinions this time around, mainly based on the fact that for me this doesn’t have the type of rereading quality that I look for in my books to determine if I want to keep and/or read them ever again.

So while this book being the author’s first IS very rough, especially in the first couple of chapters, I still recommend people check it out as I thoroughly enjoyed them the first time I read this series and its continuing two others; it just doesn’t work for my rereading tastes, sadly.

So to avoid tarnishing that image I’ve decided to not reread further and keep them as a happy memory.
