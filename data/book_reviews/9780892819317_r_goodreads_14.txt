Rating: 4/5

Much deeper information about vaccinations and surprisingly very unbiased and very research-based. The main downside to this book is that it was published several years ago, and doesn't include some of the newest vaccines. On the positive side, it spends several chapters discussing the history of disease, the efficacy of vaccinations, and alternative ways of keeping your children healthy throughout childhood. Parts of it were too in depth for me, and had little relevance, but may be interesting to some. TONS of info, but needs to be updated.
