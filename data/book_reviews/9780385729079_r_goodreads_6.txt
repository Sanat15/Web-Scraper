Rating: 2/5

I picked this up while perusing the library, it's title intrigued me and I like ancient civilizations. I was disappointed. The main character, Phano, has all the attributes of a strong character but because of the way she was written came across as a whiny child to me. When she hurt I did not feel as a reader should towards a heroine. This story seem like it would be an interesting one, it is even based on true lives to an extent. The book lacked details, it felt very bland, the details it did contain were more like ramblings about facts of Greek culture that sounded akin to a history book. Facts do help the validity of historical fiction but not if the story itself has little glimmer. There is a great villain who does very evil things but he's drawn out too long. The story seem to have a couple different small climaxes than a large one which also made the tale dull. Around page fifty I wanted to stop reading but I'm a person who likes to give the book the benefit of the doubt and see what happens at the end. At page one hundred I desperately hoped the book would get better or end soon. It got slightly better but not enough; I regretted not starting a different book. Perhaps the writer is better suited to non-fiction, she seemed more comfortable in that area.
