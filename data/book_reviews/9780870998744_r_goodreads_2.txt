Rating: 3/5

While erudite, this book is mostly of reference interest. The book contains one chapter describing the mechanics and purpose of Islamic calligraphy, as well as a bit about its evolution. This is followed by the very dry catalogue that contains biographies of individual calligraphers and little additional explanation or analysis to add to the general reader’s understanding. The calligraphy is beautiful, but the images are mediocre and we could do with a few more close-up images to show details.

If you are interested, it is a free PDF download on the Met’s website, but I wouldn’t say I recommend it for casual reading.
