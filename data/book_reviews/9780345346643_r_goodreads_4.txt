Rating: 4/5

A blurb on the jacket of this novel calls it "a luminous example of the crime novel." It is that, for while it focuses on crime and its detection (and is in this edition part of the Dell Great Mystery Library), it very adroitly explores the social milieu of a small New York industrial community that, like the novel's protagonist, has an inferiority complex and is its own kind of "black sheep." The counterplot to the murder investigation is the determination of a young couple to make their life in the town, despite its reputation.

In the midst of much interesting character development I was disappointed that the murderer (whose identity is known from the beginning) is presented as a hopeless case who never reaches a point of questioning himself, and thus a possibility for redemption.
