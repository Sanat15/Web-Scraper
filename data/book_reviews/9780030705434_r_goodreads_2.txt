Rating: 2/5

I have always found it difficult to like a book if I don't like at least one of the characters. In this book, I don't like any of the characters. This book is written in a hard edged, noir style, but it doesn't work because it doesn't have someone to cheer for in the darkness. It feels dark for its own sake. The profanity is pretty intense in places, and the dialogue doesn't actually sound very genuine in a lot of places. Finally, the author seemed to be saying that religion is for the week, the crooked, or the ambitious seeking power. He's entitled to his opinion, but it did detract from the book as I was reading it.
