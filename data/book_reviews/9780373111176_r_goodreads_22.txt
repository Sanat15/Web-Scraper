Rating: 4/5

Dizzy James agrees to help her friend Christi convince her guardian uncle that she is ready to receive/handle her inheritance. The plan is for Dizzy to act as dizzy as her name suggests so Christi will look responsible by comparison. But when Dizzy catches sight of Uncle Zachariah Bennett skinny dipping, she discovers the 35-year-old bachelor is not at all “fusty-dusty” like her friend claimed and regrets having agreed to the plan. 

Originally published in 1988, One Chance at Love is a classic Harlequin romance, worth a read for its nostalgic value. I enjoyed the story. Zachariah is pretty hot!
