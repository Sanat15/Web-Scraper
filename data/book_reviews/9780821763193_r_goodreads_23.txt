Rating: 5/5

I was captivated by this enticing story. The Scent of Roses is written with such titillating finesse, that you are drawn immediatly into the mystery and intrigue. I found I experienced each twist that Rosyln experienced. Trust me... there is a twist or two! Sparks fly between her and Whip that refuse to be denied. The question is ... whether Whip is as dangerous as people believe he is! Throw in a murdered husband with so many secrets and I found I felt a lot like Roslyn... not sure what to do next! Charlene Raddon has a clear writing style that brings the reader into the story. You experience detailed vivid reality and you are quickly wrapped up in this exciting storyline! I invite you to feel the characters and experience a sizzling mystery. A 5 star read and well worth it!
