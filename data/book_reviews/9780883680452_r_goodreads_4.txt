Rating: 4/5

50 chapters, goes through scripture relating to parenting/family/children from Gen to New Testament. great for reading devotionally and meditatively, especially with the prayers at the end of each chapter. 

the good:
- very helpful and encouraging content. prayers especially helped posture me towards God in humility and praise as a parent
- loved discussion of training the will of the child to love to do the Fathers will (more than simply obedience, delight in the law of the lord)
- appreciated the hard truths of coming to terms with the need to be humbly aware of my own sin and failings as a parent and bring those to the Lord in confession and prayer
- wonderful emphasis on the parents need to demonstrate/model all things to the child: heart for the Lord, faith in Him that results in obedience, delight in his law, spirit of joyfulness, love and patience, godly discipline, consistency and steadfastness in judgement , repentance and humility
- ch 49 and 50 are the best (50 is a good summary/conclusion)

the stuff i didn’t love:
- heavy emphasis on expecting the faith of children (almost as a result of parents obedience? although he’d say a result of Gods promise, he also seems to say the parents disobedience or lack of faith negates/abrogates/limits that promise’s effectiveness)
- heavy on disqualification of elders/deacons who have unbelieving children (definitely made me think through this more, not sure i’d go as far as he does making a blanket statement disqualifying in all instances)
- format of going through the bible picking verses relating to the topic is cool in theory, but at times felt like just a tool to talk about topics and less actual exegesis of the referenced verses.

overall, i took a long time to read this mostly because each chapter was convicting and worth pondering and trying to apply in what i do personally, how i love my kids, and how i pray to God. read it.
