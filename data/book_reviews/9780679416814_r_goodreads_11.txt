Rating: 3/5

i think i could explain this novel in its conceptual interests in a way that makes it sound much more compelling than the execution really is. The micro history of Alf juxtaposed with the macro history of the president, specifically James Buchanan, and the general changing of social and sexual culture of the 70s, Ford's 70s. i will say that i think Updike is a lot weirder than a lot of people might think he is, or at least his obsessions are stranger than others. 
Also the narrative frame here is so funny. I love when a 400+ page novel is supposed to be like, a letter to an editor
