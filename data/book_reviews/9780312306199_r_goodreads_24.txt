Rating: 4/5

4.5⭐️

It's rare that a book gets better the second time around, but this one was like experiencing pure magic all over again. J.D. Mason’s One Day I Saw A Black King delivered an emotionally intense journey that had me captivated from start to finish. The characters were so vivid that they leapt off the pages and drew me into their world as if I were living alongside them. The depth of character development is simply extraordinary. I definitely felt a deeper connection to the past narrative, but the way the two stories intertwined made me cherish the book as a whole. This exceptional tale, with its unforgettable characters, will stay with readers long after they turn the final page!
