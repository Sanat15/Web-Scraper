Rating: 5/5

I am so pleased I read this book. And you can't often say that about a book. Of course you can say, 'Oh I enjoyed that book'. But how often can you say 'I'm so pleased I read that book'?
It was funny, entertaining, thoughtful, sensitive, educational. It told the story of a complex, intelligent man, who was much, much more than just the creator of Sherlock Holmes.
If you love the Sherlock Holmes canon, then this is required reading. If you love reading about how authors work, how they get their their ideas, then this is required reading.
The man wasn't perfect, but in many ways he was a visionary, and had ideas well before his time. Ideas which we're still struggling over in places.
Loved it. A definite five star read.
