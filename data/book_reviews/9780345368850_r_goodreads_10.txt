Rating: 5/5

The Outskirter's Secret is a shockingly good book. It's devastating to see the low number of ratings here on Goodreads.

It's hard to write a review without spoilering a lot of the interesting turns the story takes. 

I will say that the writing is a sharp tool in Kirstein's hand, poetic when appropriate, neutral in tone, almost scientific for most of the book.
I give this book five stars even though there's quite a bit of violence and some scenes that I found very drastic. It's that good.

More people should read this.
