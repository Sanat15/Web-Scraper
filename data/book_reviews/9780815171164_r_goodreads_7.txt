4.5 stars
This book did a great job presenting the available research in a way that wasn’t too dense or difficult to read. I think there were some sections that lacked some information, and I found only one assertion that is false - the idea that “[antipsychotics] do not produce drug dependence”(168). The alternative is not yet supported by extensive research, but based on speaking to many medical professionals, research on available literature, and personal experiences I do know that it’s actually not too difficult to become dependent on an antipsychotic.
