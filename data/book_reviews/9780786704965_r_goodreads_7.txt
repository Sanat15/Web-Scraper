Rating: 4/5

As much as I don’t usually like short stories, I couldn’t resist one by my favorite author, Rex Stout. If I died and could come back as anyone I would want to be Archie Goodwin. (Even though I am a woman)
I wondered about the first few stories and then I realized they were written for a magazine. That explains the cadence and possibly the odd writing symmetry.
The stories later in the book are, to me, more his normal writing. 

Looove Nero Wolfe books. I have replaced them with hard covers and reread them every few years.
