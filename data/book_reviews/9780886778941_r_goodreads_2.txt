"It may be because this is a collection of short stories or due to the calibre of those involved, but this has the greatest density of ideas of almost anything else I've ever read.
Some of the sections are more just describing a situation or concept at the slight expense of narrative but I can live with that in doses of only 25-ish pages per place.
Reading this inspired me to look up whether or not warp travel had any basis in fact, which is either good or bad depending on who you are, I suppose?"
