Interesting

Mark Lane wrote this book in the mid sixties, before the death of Jack Ruby in January of 1967.
My impression of this early work is that Mr Lane had an amazing grasp of the strengths and weaknesses of this case against Lee Oswald in an amazingly rapid fashion and also was a quick study of the Warren Commission documents.

While a lot of the information in this work has stood true for approximately 60 years, I read this work to see if early researchers such as Lane, Meagher and Thompson had a firm grasp of what we know today as facts. To this point, I was not disappointed.

Again, this is an older book, but it is amazing how pertinent and relevant this information is to the present day body of knowledge.
A truly impressive work.
