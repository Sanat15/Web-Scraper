Rating: 5/5

This is another of my December book club reads about celebrations of faith and culture at this time of year.

This is a beautiful little story about a Hanukkah miracle. And while the story itself doesn't dwell on all the details of Hanukkah it highlights the eight days of feasting and celebration. Following the story is a couple of pages on the history of Hanukkah, latkes and applesauce and an explanation about the dreidel.

A lovely enjoyable book!
