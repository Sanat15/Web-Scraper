Rating: 4/5

This is the second novel I’ve read by Arlene Hale, and it did not disappoint. Love’s Sweet Surrender is an exemplary member of its genre—the mass-produced paperback romance novel.

The characters are likable, and Hale does a great job of building out the small world of Bella Grande Island. At many points in the novel, the intimate setting and small cast of characters create almost the feeling of a stage play. While the plot progresses formulaically, a few of my predictions still turned out to be wrong, which I appreciated.

One of the things I especially appreciate about this author is her aversion to including explicit sex scenes. She still manages to whip up plenty of passion without taking the reader into the bedroom.

All in all, I would definitely recommend this book for an afternoon read or a plane ride.
