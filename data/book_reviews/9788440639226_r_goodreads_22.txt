I’ve been slowly savoring Our Prince of Scribes: Writers Remember Pat Conroy, which I warn any fan, will make you want to reread all his books.
No author assuages my reader’s soul like Pat Conroy. So far, this is the second of his treasured novels I’ve revisited recently and I am thoroughly baptized in the saltwater tides of his beloved lowcountry. How I miss the man and those books he didn’t get to write. 
“The only word for goodness is goodness, and it is not enough.”
