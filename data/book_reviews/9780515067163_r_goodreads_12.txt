4.5 stars

One of my favorite entries in the series so far. 

I kept wondering how Marsh was going to turn this tale of a happy-go-lucky family into a murder mystery, but I needn’t have worried. The murders do happen, and their gruesomeness is in stark contrast to the fun-loving Lampreys.

As always, I love Marsh's good writing, the nods to great literature, and the surprise ending.
