Rating: 4/5

I especially enjoyed this book because its unquiness. I mean, a story about an indentered servant isn't the type of book I read often. Despite Cole Sloan's rotten life, I found it was hard not to like a him a little. I think especially when he tries to protect Samantha toward the end that I was really hoping he'd get his life turned around. A good book to read...definitely not the typical one!
