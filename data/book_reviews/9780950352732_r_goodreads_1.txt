Rating: 2/5

I bought this book and another by the same author in a charity shop and I'm sorry to say they will be making their way back soon. 
I found the book to be pretty tedious and disappointing. It's about an ill assorted bunch of cricketers on tour in the West Country. For me the characters were uninspiring and their 'adventures' more Bunter than Botham. 
It is a slim volume and hence a relatively painless read.
