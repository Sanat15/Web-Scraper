Rating: 3/5

Good book about a bygone era in MLB. DiMaggio tells about the 1941 season where his teammate Ted Williams was bidding to be a .400 hitter and his brother Joe was on his way to a 56 game hitting streak. There's a bit of old man yelling at a cloud in there but I think he tried to show how great the players of his own era were and how much fun they had playing the game.
