Rating: 4/5

Sister Rose is called upon by the Eldress of Hancock, the Shaker village near Pittsfield MA, because of her prior experience dealing with murder. There are a number of possibly dubious novitiates in the village...and then murder. And of course, after a number of incidents, Rose is able to get to the bottom of things.

This book allows a reader to see a different Shaker community, and to see a New England village from the perspective of a Kentuckian. There are of course many similarities, but one become aware of the different character of each village.
