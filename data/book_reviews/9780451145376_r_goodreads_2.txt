Rating: 3/5

Read: 8/27/25
Setting: Revolutionary War, England, Virginia 
Trope: class difference 

plot: British aristocrat Barbara is wed to wealthy Virginian Alan in order to pay her father's debt. For Alan, Barbara is the prize. Unfortunately for her, Alan is a strong patriot. Barbara just doesn't understand why he feels the way he does. This causes many problems for the couple.

This story takes its readers through the events leading up to the war to Cornwallis surrender at Yorktown. Through it all, can this couple's love survive their political differences?

dislike:
1. I love JW, but I prefer her shorter books. The longer the story, the more the flaws in her writing become obvious.
2. All her characters are one-dimensional. We never experience these characters with any real depth. I felt like I was on the outside of the story. The characters have some bad things happen, but the story moves on without ever really addressing any emotional toll that the event had on the character.
3. The H cheats. This didn't bother, but I know it is a problem for other readers. The MC fights, and after 2 years, he finally cheats while at war. He regrets it immediately, and the h makes him pay for his transgression. 

liked:
1. I did like the MC and the historical aspect. It was like a quick review of the Revolutionary War.
2. the author's pacing is what makes her books so easy to read. Hate or love her books, you'll still finish them.

Conclusion:
While not a bad book, it's not great either. It was just okay. I think I'll stick to this author's shorter books.
