Rating: 2/5

This is overall a fine book, it has a believable magical-post-apocalyptic story line. The characters, while not all well-developed and a bit flighty, are fine. The reason I give it only two stars is for the lengthy last chapter where the author feels he needs to spell out all the nuances in his plot that were not clearly developed. Pushing that content back to the story line instead of a long recitation would have made it much more enjoyable at the end.
