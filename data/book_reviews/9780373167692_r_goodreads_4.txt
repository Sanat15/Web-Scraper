Rating: 4/5

Best friends Dylan and Whitney have a one night stand and a few months later he’s back in town engaged to someone else. She was a virgin; he doesn’t use a condom even though he’s the one with experience, and she acts like it’s no big deal and makes him feel like she was just using him and keeps pushing him away throughout the book. Dylan and Whitney have an affair before his wedding. I don’t know how that makes it right to Whitney that at least he wasn’t married yet. Dylan loves Whitney but is financially tied to business deal with his fiancé Cori’s family. They both made me shake my head over their actions. I loved Whitney’s tuxedo designer Uncle Karl who doesn’t miss a trick and is not above doing what it takes to make his niece happy.
