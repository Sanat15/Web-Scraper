Rating: 5/5

"Jesus promised that the Last Days would be marked by greater works than He Himself had wrought." (John G. Lake, Adventures in God)

I heard about this book while I was reading another book and decided to find it online. The copyright expired, so I knew it would be in the public domain. Luckily it was, I found a version here: https://daniellovett.files.wordpress.... 

Unfortunately, this version had many spelling errors in some places, so I am thinking that the person who transcribed it didn't have their coffee before doing so. Nevertheless, this book was awesome. John G. Lake is one of the greatest healing evangelists ever known. I said, "one of the greatest" as God has raised up many more healing evangelists who came before and after him. He was very unique in that he was a doctor and was a Canadian who later moved to the United States. He began his ministry in Spokane Washington. You can find more information about him by doing a google search. 

God used John G. Lake to bring healing to many. He opened healing rooms and was a missionary to Africa. He tells of how God supernaturally supplied his and his families needs during his ministry. He believed in the provision of God. That God provides provision for the vision. He recounts in this book how whenever a need arised in his ministry, he would pray and God would supernaturally supply. Praise God.

One of the greats of the faith and worthy to be studied and imitated.
