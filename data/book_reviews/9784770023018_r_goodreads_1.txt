Rating: 2/5

Brian Moeran "Buchan" is an anthropologist who lived in Japan for four years until personal tragedy cut the time short. This memoir details his time living in a country village and the people he met there. I only made it to around page 85 of this 250 page book. While it was nice to read what life was like for him, it seemed to focus on all of the negative qualities of the people he encountered. I'm not sure if he's bitter because of the accident that his young son had there and how it was handled, or if this is really how he sees most of the people there. Even though one of the reviews said "suffused with wit and humor" I didn't really see that. This book gave me no desire to want to visit the country-side in Japan, expressly because of the supposedly dour attitudes of all of the people there.
