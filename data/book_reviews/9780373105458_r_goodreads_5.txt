Rating: 4/5

4 Stars! ~ A book written by Charlotte Lamb is always enjoyable. Our heroine has unknowingly won a contest for a glamourous week in London for a makeover and fabulous concert and date with a popular singer. Our hero is the singer whose concerts attract hoards of women of all ages. Quincy gets an eye opening look into Joe's professional life and it takes away some of her innocence. Her heart tells her to trust Joe but her head tells her that he's just another celebrity phony. Though the HEA was kind of sudden, I thoroughly enjoyed this love story.
