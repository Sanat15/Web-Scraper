Rating: 4/5

A book for children that takes them into the life of a young jackrabbit whether dealing with environmental issues, weather and predators.

 The bright lifelike illustrations will immerse your child in this wild world. They will meet some of the prisoner's neighbors who are later elaborated on in the back of the book.

 I would like to get this particular book new with the audio parts to see if it really does include natural animal sounds. But with or without the audio I would still highly recommend it.
