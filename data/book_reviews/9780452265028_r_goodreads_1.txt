Rating: 4/5

This is a summer read. It isn't the Great American Novel. Its a small story about small people and the issues they have. Its a simple straightforward examination of what I would describe as a middle-class suburban white guy. They aren't wrapped up in world affairs. He isn't rich. He isn't poor. He seems to just be trying to get through life. George isn't going to change the world, his worlds is what is around him.

Its a good summer read, doesn't demand anything from the reader.
