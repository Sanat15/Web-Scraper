Rating: 4/5

If you enjoy books like Scarlet Pimpernel that are about spying and romance at the same time, this book would be a perfect joy to read. 
This book is set in the period of Bonaparte's reign.

Captain Jackson is a spy for England who has taken up smuggling to get hold of French dispatches. His actual identity is known to very few people. During the course of his spying he comes across the wealthy and well-bred Miss Joanna Feniton. 

Miss Feniton knows she should not get mixed up with spies. She has to do her duty and marry well as her grandma has taught her. She is expecting to be betrothed to Algernon Cholcombe, son of a wealthy and powerful man.

However she can't resist helping the mysterious Captain Jackson. She gets caught up in the series of events and starts suspecting that someone in her house party may be the local leader of the French spies.

There is a very nice combination of thrills, mystery and romance. Characters are well-drawn especially her grandparents, and friend Kitty. 
The language and setting are perfect for the time too. No quibbles on that front.

A clean historical romance that I thoroughly enjoyed reading. 4 stars!
