Rating: 5/5

I blazed through Miami Purity and absolutely loved it- I think I’ll read everything Vicki Hendricks has ever written &amp; ever will write. Noir black as the void.

The protagonist of Iguana Love is less charming, but this is about storytelling …. There are some parts I had to push through (no spoilers) and yet ... I’ve found myself thinking about this book long after finishing it.

The major arc, the minor arcs. The final line! 

Bravo, lady. I cant wait for more of your stories. 

Miami Purity is a must read, and if you cant get enough of it, come try this one.
