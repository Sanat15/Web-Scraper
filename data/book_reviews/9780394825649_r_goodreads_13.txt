Rating: 4/5

A classic from my childhood. The book should be called Donald's Nephews and the magic stick, because Donald is only a minor character in the book. 

growing up poor, Huey, Dewey, and Louie set out to get jobs to make money to help out their uncle Donald. In turn, each one receives a magic item while also running into an evil Inn Keeper. The book is a bit longer than a normal children's book but it's not a hard read.

It's a fun story with solid Disney art. the perfect book to read to children before bed.
