Rating: 5/5

Review #1 (2024): Book number 4 is a beauty. Absolutely loved this one. It had action, plot progression, introduction to Bartleby, and escalation regarding the war. Also, the battle of Kingdok and Thorn cutting his arm off was such a badass moment. I also enjoyed learning more about Thorn’s “awakening” abilities, along with the hooded individual trying to lure her through her dreams.

Such a great story, by far the most action filled one yet! 

5/5
