Rating: 4/5

A broad, and at times, overwhelming, look at the impact gays and lesbians had on Hollywood. And spoiler alert, it was pretty much everyone. If you like them, they were gay. The book is heavily slanted towards men, but that is a product of it being easier to discern gay men from a sociological perspective. The author's methodology is meticulous and the conclusions he draws about the way homophobia ebbed and flowed are solid and fascinating. A must-read for any classic film fan, really.
