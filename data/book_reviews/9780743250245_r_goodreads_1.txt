Rating: 4/5

These poems/songs were all silly, and, to be honest, a little trippy. You could probably invent your own cadence when reading these, but once you hear the songs on the CD you will find yourself singing these as songs. The final one in the book - Bed, Bed, Bed - was my children's favorite, and the only one they listened to over and over. The others were pretty esoteric, but bedtime translates across all languages.
