Rating: 4/5

I checked this book out from the library constantly whenever I needed a quick book fix. The story is cute and full of humor with a happy ending. As a teen I always wanted my own invisible boyfriend that no one could see or hear but me whom I could always count on to be there for me and understand everything I was going through. Then I grew up. 
But many books are like old friends, sometimes we have to drop in and visit every so often just to see how they're doing. This is one of those books.
