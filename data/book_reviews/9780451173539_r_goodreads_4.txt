Rating: 3/5

I started out liking this one, but it lost a lot of its steam as the story progressed. I liked our H/h, I think eventually they would be good for each other. While I liked Demetrius, I would we could have seen him "fall in love" a bit more. Meribe was a likable heroine and you can't help rooting for her, especially after everything she's been through. I liked that Demetrius goes to London to prevent his brother from getting caught in the Black Widows web, and instead turns into her protector. 

Overall, I liked it, would have liked a few things to be tweaked, but it was an interesting and unique read.
