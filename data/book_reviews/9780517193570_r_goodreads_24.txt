Rating: 3/5

I almost gave this two stars because it really was just ok. Although, it wasn't bad and I am a softie so it ends up as three stars. I don't really have much to say about it but I did want to include a few quotes:

"It is a commonplace that children so weaken friendships that many parents cease to be social beings at all."

"Only once or twice did he come to our flat; the presence of children, even signs of their existence such as toys or the push-chair in the hall, discomforted him."

"Parents with young children are not often welcome - at least, there is usually more genuine pleasure at their departure than their arrival - but these cousins had enough children for two more to make little difference."

I thought these were hilarious and read them out loud to my wife. She was less amused than I seemed to be. Kids are the best, right?
