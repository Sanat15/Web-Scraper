Rating: 4/5

An interesting young adult (late middle grade?) dystopian novel about what might happen if global warming caused the ocean to continue to rise and cut off more and more of the world's land and connections. There are multiple small islands barely eking out an existence after being cut off by flooding, and life gets pretty desperate. Zoe, the main character, gets separated from her parents when trying to leave on a supply ship, then later manages to get off of her island with her own boat based on skills her father has taught her. She ends up on another island and learns more about what else has been happening in the world and on the islands around her, but still wants to make it to the mainland to try and find her parents, even though she is losing hope in them even being alive. I think what I found the most interesting about the book were the "stories" about the past (as in our current reality) and how their present came to be...others think these stories about the world getting warmer and people doing nothing to stop their contributions to that problem are crazy and unrealistic...and if humanity ever survives to that point, I can see things actually happening that way. The end was less satisfying and almost too much of a pretty little bow, but I enjoyed the story overall and could see this used pretty effectively in a middle school classroom for a variety of purposes.
