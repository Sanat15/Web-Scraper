Rating: 1/5

The main character of Mystery in the Night Woods is a Trump-esque shitshow. He's a landlord squirrel who likes to give lectures about how working hard will bring you success. But then he sees a beautiful owl, and after she turns him down for marriage, he kidnaps her. Then when he gets in trouble and goes to jail (the only sign in the book that kidnapping women who turn you down isn't actually okay in this world), he's just keeps telling himself that he's so successful because of all his hard work and how he's always worked hard for what he gets. Kidnapping another being counts as "hard work" and, therefore, he deserves her. At no point in the book is there a suggestion that this guy is the bad guy. He's undoubtedly the good guy and frequently contrasted with "real" criminals. At one point, he does regret kidnapping the owl, but he spends no time dwelling on it and gets her forgiveness immediately.

Even outside of this horrible plot point, this book is awful. Something inside of it died a long time ago and has been rotting ever since.
