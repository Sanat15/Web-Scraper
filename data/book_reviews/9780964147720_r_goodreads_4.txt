Rating: 4/5

A cute little book of advice for grandparents. Many are something just about anyone would think of, but others are just plain good advice. My one gripe - suggestions of making a first toolbox for your GRANDSON. Excuse me? What about your granddaughter? Back in the 50s my brother and I had our own little saw, given to us by our grandfather who showed no partiality in encouraging Scott's and my woodworking talents. Other than that, a sweet little gem to refer to often.
