Rating: 5/5

Poor Faye. She wrote a book about Nicole Brown Simpson, because she knew her and the public wasn't really hearing about all the good about Nicole and what she put up with from O.J. Then Faye takes all the backlash, and personal attacks and just has to take them. She writes this book because she realizes there are lots of women in abusive relationships unable to leave and she wants to acknowledge that and help to the extent she can.
