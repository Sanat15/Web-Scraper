Rating: 1/5

The Star Fox was a disappointing entry from Poul Anderson. After being impressed with some of his previous novels, this one was a bit of a let down. Overly long, kinda boring, and a bit ick, the book is a basic swashbuckling space adventure where not much happens but the hero outsmarts his enemies, builds his dream political entity, and...sends for a woman who adores him and who he is willing to settle for? A consolation prize for missing the boat on being able to bed a lovely 19-year-old, even though his daughter is similar in age. It's just...all a bit gross and boring.
