Rating: 4/5

WOW!

I've read it many times as a teen and yet I was surprised while I finished it. I mean, I know some of the books I read as a kid were edited to fit a younger audience and I wouldn't be able to pinpoint what was edited here but, boy, this book was something. 

Hero was one of my favorite characters growing up because she chooses not to speak with anyone but her older brother. She goes to school, as a job in a neighboring house but she doesn't speak. Her family is a bit on the famous side and she probably suffered from it, especially because she has a mother who wrote a book about child care and a sister who wrecks cars for a job, despite being a math genius. 

Enter her neighbor, which is a tad weird. And Hero's world gets shocked.

Very good! I even found out what a soap shaker is...
