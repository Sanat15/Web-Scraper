Rating: 5/5

Whitney and Dylan have been friends forever. Unexpectedly, they got together a few months ago and went a little too far... now she is pregnant. Now she finds out he is getting married to save his company and more. The characters are strong in their ideals and very protective. Tension comes as two couples who love each other are supposed to marry the wrong one. Uncle Karl is behind the scenes trying to set things right. The action is a roller coaster ride that keeps you drawn into the story. the ending is so worth it.
