Take me to your reader!

This is a rather unbelievable story, but does challenge the logical mind, in several ways. First, much of the world seems to dealing with illusions in politics, medicine and religion. We seek witnesses to things like talking burning bushes, diabolical leaders who lie to hold power over the masses, and men who believe God has Devine control and influence over the world, but is weak against free will. I gave the book 3stars, because it failed to do anything more than give us more questions. Is God real, are certain humans all powerful and do UFOs exist? The interrupted journey was another literary illusion that should be in fiction. But, hey, I'm sure this was the blueprint for every sci-fi attempt since. Let the buyer be skeptical.
