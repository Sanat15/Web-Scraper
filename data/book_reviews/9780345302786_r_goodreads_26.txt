Rating: 2/5

Bad things happen, then everyone ends up ok but depressed. I really should donate this series to the library, I'd forgotten how corny it is. Very standard, very cardboard, but something I know I enjoyed as a kid.

The biggest problem with the last book is the unveiling of an idiotic plot. Coram is close to evil king Wencit (sounds like an xmas song) for six years, and he can only kill the bad guy after battles where large numbers of people have died? This really powerful Dernyi is a moron.
