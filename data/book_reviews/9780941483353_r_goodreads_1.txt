3.5 or so

Content warnings- child abuse, sexual assault, sexual harassment, animal cruelty and killing, homophobia, poverty, violence against women, arson, alcoholism, self harm, attempted suicide. 

There was a lot of sweetness, but more cruelty. I would not read it again, or suggest it to anyone.

Lesbians lesbians lesbians lesbians! A good smattering of representation, and only the idea of steamy relations. I expected it to be spicier, honestly. I've read YA books with more heat than this.

I don't typically go in for Love at first sight, so Arliss and Irene didn't really make sense to me. I feel like the blurb on the book jacket lied to me. The book tried to do too much, and you never really got the chance to see any of the characters grow. A lot of things HAPPENED, but too many stories were being told. I wanted to know more about Grace, Alta, Frankie, Sophie, Margaret, Irene's family, Arliss' mom. 

Furosa should have been the absolute star, but was relegated to a side character. I would read a whole book about what happens next for Wonderland. And the Raging Mother Mountain was entirely not prominent enough. I think the use of the poison oak as a weapon was quite a nice touch, though.
