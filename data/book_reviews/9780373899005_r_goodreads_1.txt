Rating: 2/5

"Darling Jenny" is the story of Jenny and Logan.

Firstly, it's been a pleasure to virtually travel through the USA via this wonderful Americana series. These have been my constant companion in these times, especially in the last two months when things have gone bonkers worldwide. I've been working non stop and coming back to this series has saved me from insanity, allowing me to relax after 40 hour shifts- thank you Ms Dailey!

That being said, I did not like this book because of the heroine. A whiny, judgmental shrew who makes a wrong decision, and then suddenly becomes a know it all about the male gender. She decides that her widowed sister is being "misused" by a close friend, and takes it upon herself to discourage him, despite him doing NOTHING wrong. She suddenly yearns for him, he tries to make her jealous using her sister, another man likes her sister- but she rejects him, her ex returns, then there's this weird foursome between them, the heroine assumes a lot and spends all the time being a shrill crybaby who acts like a bitch 24x7, while simultaneously crying for the hero's "love". Thank god, the torture ended.

I did not enjoy this but will definitely miss the series.
