When I travel, I love to buy local cookbooks compiled by the Junior League. I have never belonged to the Junior League. I am too old to belong now, and when I was a young woman I was not wealthy enough to be asked to join.

So now when entertaining, this San Francisco cookbook is a lifesaver. 

I recently had the joy of entertaining my aunt and uncle from Malvern, Iowa. They enjoyed my dish, Shrimp in black bean Sauce.

Here is the recipe, for my cousin Audrey:

Shrimp in Black Bean Sauce (serves 6)

1 T. dry sherry
2 T. minced fresh ginger (I used more)
1 T. soy sauce
1/2 t. white pepper
1 pound raw shrimp, peeled and deveined (I used a pound and a half)
1/4 c. peanut oil, divided
1 c. diced onions (I used scallions)
1 10-ounce pkg. frozen tiny peas, thawed
1 small green pepper, diced (I used a yellow pepper, don't love the green peppers)
2 cloves garlic, sliced
2 T. Chinese fermented black beans, mashed
2 t. cornstarch, dissolved in 1/4 c. water

Combine sherry, ginger, soy sauce and white pepper.
Pour over shrimp, marinade 15 minutes, drain.

Heat 2 T. oil in a heavy skillet or wok and stir fry onions, peas and diced pepper for 2 minute, remove with slotted spoon and set aside.
Add remaining oil to wok and stir fry garlic and black beans 10 seconds. Add drained shrimp to wok, stir-fry until pink.
Return vegetables to wok and stir in cornstarch mixture. Stir well and heat until sauce thickens.

Serve with white rice. Enjoy!
