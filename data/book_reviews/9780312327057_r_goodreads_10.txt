Rating: 3/5

This will be a tricky one to comment about without breaking my own "no spoilers" rule (not that I don't mind it rather there are better reviewers out there who would do it far greater justice.

So what can I say - well personally I struggled to connect with the story - hence why it took longer than I expected to read it - there are a lot of fun dialogue between the characters and yes there are some very well worn tropes here too meaning that there is either a hell of a lot of coincidence or its just very convenient for the story 

That all said there is plenty of action - I do not think there are many wasted pages (as if I would know) and certainly every chapter is needed even if at 600 pages it did take its time. So yes an interesting action packed read thank you Mr Delaney.
