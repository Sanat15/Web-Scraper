Rating: 3/5

Overall good book. I have never read this author before and really enjoyed seeing both sides of a topic that I only see one way. I think every woman should have a choice, and it should be up to her what she decides to do and not the community that she lives in. I wonder, if this would have been set in a different location, if the community would have felt the same way. Is it all small towns that have the same beliefs, or is it simply the south.

Can't wait to find another book by him and see what other topics he covers.
