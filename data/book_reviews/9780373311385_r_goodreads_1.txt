Rating: 5/5

What a satisfying read! A young jackanapes being taught a lesson by his father, Sir Geoffrey Henley, by being sent to work as a mail coachman finds himself maturing and benefitting from his change of station. He encounters Miss Cecily Wolverton and almost forgets his place. The ensuing storyline is riveting and moves along at a good pace. A month after meeting Cecily, Jack Henley is injured in a coaching accident, breaks his leg and ends up convalescing at Cecily's grandfather, Sir Waldo's, manor. He is reacquainted with Cecily while trying to hide his true status as a gentleman but his secret is eventually revealed. Cecily's cousin, Alfred, Lord Stourport, enters the scene offering marriage to Cecily. Well, thus the adventure begins as Jack helps Cecily recover her father's missing will. Will Cecily be restored as the rightful heiress? Will Alfred's former valet, Sudbury, offer a clue as to where the missing will could be? Will Jack Henley be restored as his father's heir? Will Jack and Cecily be able to finally follow their hearts? Only by reading this novel will all these questions be satisfyingly answered! Two enthusiastic thumbs up for this one!
