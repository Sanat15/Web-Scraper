Rating: 5/5

This is an old Presents novel from 1982, and its a really excellent one. The heroine is Quincy an assistant in her father's veterinary practice. Her young brother enters her in a contest with a grand prize of dating Joe Aldonez a top recording artist, without her knowledge and she wins!

Much to her chagrin, she is whisked off to London for her date and all too soon she finds herself succumbing to Joe's charms, but his lifestyle doesn't appeal to her at all.

This is a book that kinda hits home for any woman that ever crushed over a rock star. Its a little cheesy, but fangirl dreams are always that way, and this played out exactly the way I've daydreamed getting together with Simon Lebon, Elvis Costello or Justin Hayward and it comes complete with the fairy tale ending too.

Great book and one I'll probably revisit too!
