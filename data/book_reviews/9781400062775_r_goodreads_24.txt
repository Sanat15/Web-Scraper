This author has published 10 books per Goodreads, and based on the number of ratings, this one is the most well known. 

The storyline is that of a 14 year old girl who loses her two moms in a freak accident and ends up living with her biological father whom she has never met. He was the sperm donor for the gay couple who wished to have a family. 

This an epistolary novel. The entire story is told through letters and emails. Rosalyn, the child, writes letters to an imaginary friend (“Fluffy”) per her therapist’s suggestion. Sean, the dad, and his best friend email back and forth as the friend encourages Sean. There is also an “Aunt Karen,” best friend to Rosalyn’s two moms, who would have liked to raise the girl when her mom’s died. 

All in all it’s a story about grief and overcoming grief, better stated learning to live with grief and finding a way to want to continue living after the unthinkable happens. I believe this book would be considered young adult fiction.
