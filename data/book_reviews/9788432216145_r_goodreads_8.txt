Rating: 5/5

Everyone in love with the movies, a good love story, and a simple story of goodness and friendship ought to read this masterpiece. Valentin and Molina make a pair of unlikely lovers whose love, respect, and kindness towards one another and hope make this a powerful and heartbreakingly comic read. 

Then there’s the alluring film star and actress of technicolor dreams, haunting and making life magical, bearable and dangerous. 

It will definitely make you want to turn back to the beginning and start reading once again. Also, its for anyone who loves literature that deals with multicultural and sexual politics, and for any history buff who loves historical fiction.

As a kid, I also saw the now classic film starring Raul Julia, William Hurt in his Oscar winning performance (1985) as Molina and Sonia Braga. 

Postscript:

1994. I was little kid at this time. But I was so privileged to have seen Vanessa Williams as The Spider Woman on Broadway. 

It was adapted by the legendary musical collaborators, Kander and Ebb, directed by Harold Prince. I had no inkling of the novel, or the film then. 

As a kid my little theater loving heart was entranced by Williams’ mesmerizing performance. No wonder she’s become a Broadway stalwart, and how lucky am I to have seen her become one of Broadway’s most respected and consistent performers. 

Also, the musical costarred Howard McGillin and the now, equally legendary Broadway legend, Brian Stokes Mitchell. 

These three replaced Chita Rivera, Anthony Crivello, and Brent Carver. If anyone loves theater as well as the novel, listen to both the cast albums with Rivera and Williams.

I am also curious to see the 2025 film version of the musical starring Jennifer Lopez and Diego Luna.
