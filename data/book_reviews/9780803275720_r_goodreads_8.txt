Rating: 5/5

Originally written in 1976 &amp; republished in 1990, Basil Johnson's "Ojibway Heritage" is a rather unique book that gets into the mythology of the Ojibway people. The book itself is a fascinating read as it is told in the form of a story that would be passed down from generation to generation. Each chapter is as fascinating as the one prior &amp; is definitely a book that anyone with an interest in these peoples will enjoy.
