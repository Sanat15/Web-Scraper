Rating: 2/5

It was, um, cute? Maybe? 
As it becomes clear quite quickly that the husband is not a redeemable character, it was just a matter of waiting to find out how he would be eliminated. (For as much fault as I find in these stories, you have to give them credit, they don't promote adultery, whatever the title of the book might imply.) 
Highly dramatic and ridiculous, but fluffy and fun if looking for something mindless.
