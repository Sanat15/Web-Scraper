Rating: 3/5

There was a ton of great information in here. The author made efforts to organize the content, but I would have appreciated more reminders throughout the text that tactics in chapter 3 only work in the pre-rut, four in the main rut, etc. I think each chapter would be improved by including a list summarizing the stands to hunt, deer behavior to be aware of, etc. for that portion of the season. 

I enjoyed the stories of the author's hunts. I always wonder when I read this author, is he sponsored by Ambush Saddles and Scent-Lok? I have no doubt he truly believes in the gear, but the sheer number of times he says "Ambush Saddle" and "Scent-Lok" is a little crazy.
