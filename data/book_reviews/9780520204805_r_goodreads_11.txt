Rating: 4/5

A great read. Rae Yang's prose is beautiful, and you can easily place yourself in her shoes as she speaks about her dreams and what she loves. I think there are some parts you might need some prior knowledge surounding the Cultural Revolution. Although it is a memoir and doesn't necessarily need to reflect the political state of China because Rae wouldn't have known these things at the time, there are certain references that might be lost without further research. Overall, a heartbreaking read that exposes the realities of Red Guard experiences without valorizing the violence of the revolution. I liked this book a lot.
