Rating: 5/5

Not everything in this book was relatable to my life but it is absolutely necessary reading for parents. Main takeaways are: listen to your intuition (it is a gift to you!), ask questions in situations where there are unknowns (potential babysitters, daycares, schools, programs, etc.) so you know what dangers you are facing rather than worrying about unnecessary things, train your children to be rude to tricky people, educate them on listening to their own guts and knowing how to respond to weird behavior, have your eyes OPEN to the reality of violence and face it head on. 

This book met a real need for me this week and I'm thankful to the wiser and more veteran mothers who read it before me and recommended it. One said she wants to give it to people as a baby gift. Me too. (Also in that basket: Hold on to Your Kids, Parenting from the Inside Out)
