Rating: 5/5

I read this book in high school and deeply appreciated it. Returning to it several years later now, I’ve seen again how it deeply impacted my desire to “keep open house for the universe”. I also find Oswald’s complete dependence on the Lord for provision and direction inspiring, and challenging me to live more like that. And the way God used his and Biddy’s marriage for His glory…ahhh. God’s plans and ways are so glorious and beyond our human understanding in the thick of everyday life.

Additionally, I found myself deeply appreciating the flow of his poetry at the end…very much relating to his turbulent and angsty early poems subsiding into sweeter, more cheerful pieces as God refined him.

In short: I love this book. I will return to it again. And I can’t wait to meet Oswald someday. He was definitely a kindred spirit.
