Rating: 4/5

I picked up this book almost at random, not having a special interest in the Australian subject matter, but found it surprisingly good for a fairly obscure writer, quite versatile and multi-faceted.
A sincere well-informed look at difficult Australian issues, and also quite a good interwoven story of a diverse group of associates. By the end however I found it rather schematic and to an extent predictable and contrived by its messages. Nevertheless, it is well-written and has its moments and quite informative about Australia to an outsider.
