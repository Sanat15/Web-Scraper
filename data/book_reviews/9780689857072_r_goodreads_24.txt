Rating: 3/5

TW: racism, miscarriage 

I really liked the conversations that took place in this story. We follow a young girl who has been told a certain group of people are bad because of their race, and she spends the book questioning this. A lot of people in her town use religion, so she decided to read the Bible for herself to see what it’s all about and why others think that. I also liked that she doesn’t follow blindly, she really questions everything that is going on. She may not always act on these thoughts, but you can tell she feels bad and knows certain things are wrong. 

I liked the conversations about religion and our main character thinking for herself. It is quite a short story, but I do think it is the perfect length. It’s made for younger readers, so the themes and morals in this book can be really obvious at times. But I think that’s pretty normal for stories written for younger readers. Overall I liked it, and I’m glad it exists.
