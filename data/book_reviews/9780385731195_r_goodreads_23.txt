Rating: 4/5

Acceleration by Graham McNamee hit me harder than I expected. It’s not just a thriller it’s a story about fear, choice, and the way ordinary people handle extraordinary danger. I kept thinking about Duncan’s recklessness and bravery at the same time, and it made me question what I might do in a similar situation. 
The tension doesn’t just make your heart race; it forces you to confront ideas about responsibility, courage, and how quickly life can spiral out of control. Even after finishing, the story lingered, making me reflect on how fear and morality are tangled in ways we rarely consider. This book isn’t just entertaining it leaves a mark.
