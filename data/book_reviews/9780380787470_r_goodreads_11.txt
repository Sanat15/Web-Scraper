Rating: 4/5

I really really liked this book,i din't rate it 5 stars only for the reason it reminded me of the movie ''terminator''and its sequels.Whoever reads this please don't get put off by that fact,if u read it u'll understand why i said that.
I stayed up until late and today morning i woke up early to finish it.Towards the end at some point my heart missed a beat and tears came to my eyes..I related to Annie b/c i love my husband just as much as she does hers and i know that i ,too,would recognise him under whatever disguise spell he would may have been.I just know i would.
