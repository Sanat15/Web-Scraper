Rating: 5/5

This is a beautifully written book about a horrible, horrible thing.

The author's sister, Sheila, is raped and murdered. 
Her disappearance, the discovery of the body, the trial, the aftermath of grief. We go on the journey via Miseck's potent use of language and her willingness to be real. She speaks directly to her sister in these pages.

The unusual format is journal-like with short snippets and random thoughts that cut to the bone and chill the heart. 

Honest, poignant, and so, so sad. A reverent tribute
