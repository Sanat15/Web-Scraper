Rating: 3/5

I thought that the book was made too early in David Beckham’s career just because there was not much to really talk about in my opinion. And also with the limited range of topics Beck did not really go into too much detail about what happened behind the scenes so the reader does it really learn much more from this. Unless you are such a die hard Beck fan that you do not mind a very short book with mostly pictures of him then you will just feel that this book is just okay and that is why I give it 3 stars, it is not bad, it is just simply okay.
