Rating: 5/5

Catton not only possesses the gift of eloquence: also the gift of letting us have the battles &amp; feel them to. Amidst the tableau of carnage, he deftly picks out the moment that wins or loses the day. With equal clarity he maps out which hill or field unlocks the next mountain pass, the next railroad, the next strech of Mississippi, the next state.

1955 was the year of Rosa Parks &amp; there is definitely a Civil Rights Movement flavor whenever slavery is discussed. It's always an unspoken assumption coming to the fore as an archaic society is inevitably swept away by a more broadly defined Land of the Free.
