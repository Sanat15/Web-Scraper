Rating: 4/5

"La Neige en Deuil" is how a novel should be; the right length for the story, with a coherent plot and engaging characters, and brought to life with wonderful description of the landscape and the white-knuckle adventure that takes place within it. Then there's the ending, which is perfect. It will leave you thinking about right and wrong, desire, obsession, delusion, and above all, about brotherhood.
