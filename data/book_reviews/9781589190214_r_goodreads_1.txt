Rating: 4/5

I snagged this book for my Kindle because, frankly, it was free. The storyline sounded intriguing, but aside from that, I didn't know what I was getting myself into. 

Joe Box is a born-again Christian who finds that his past is not only haunting him, but following him in an effort to destroy Box and the rest of the men in his platoon. 

It has been said that this book is a "man's book". I would have to agree. While the language isn't raw (it's meant to be a Christian book), some of the scenes are pretty brutal. 

While there are times when dialogue and language is choppy, overall, it is a solid read. Like most stories, it cleans up perfectly. For someone who likes happy ending, that's not entirely a bad thing.
