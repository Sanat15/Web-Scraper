Rating: 5/5

The Zebra-Striped Hearse by Ross MacDonald (1962) is the best of the Lew Archer series imho...I guess this is why I've read the book three times now. Hearse contains all elements of an Archer novel...The wealthy but splintered L.A. family with the missing daughter, many characters all with a past, Archer constantly on the move through-out California, Nevada and Mexico and a heart-felt but brutal ending. Nothing but the best of P.I. literature...Excellent &amp; Highly recommended...5.0 outta 5.0....
