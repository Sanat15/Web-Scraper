I finished this book on the way to a funeral in Georgia. I'm having a hard time separating my thoughts on the admittedly excellent book from the loss I'm feeling and will try to give this a more thorough review later. Or maybe this raw expression is just going to suffice for my review as it's honest, if unrelated to Rumpole's exploits. 
Alright, maybe a little bit of book review:
These books seem to have hit on one of the wittiest characters I've ever seen portrayed. His irreverence and style are unmatched, and he is as quick and precise with words as a fencer is with a rapier. Truly brilliant portrayal of British humor and courtroom drama, mixed beautifully.
