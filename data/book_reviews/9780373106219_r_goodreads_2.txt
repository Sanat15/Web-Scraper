Rating: 2/5

This could have been a fun ride given the premise of the story, but the ending was just too saccharine for my taste.

The heroine gets drunk at her best friend's wedding. It seem's the bride's younger brother decides to play a prank by slipping "vodka" into the heroine's lemonade. Before she knows it, she is out for the count and hero has to deliver her safely home. Given that he really is a hero, he decides to stay the night with her to make sure she is all right. There is just one slight flaw with the logic. It seems the heroine has a fiancé who happens to walk in on them the next morning. Needless to say, the "sh#$" hits the fan and the whole town is talking about the heroine's demise. 

The heroine bolts to the heroes getaway hideaway to lick her wounds and figure out how to get life back on track. It was kind of funny and JS did a great job in building up the mentality of small town life. The hero was kind of sweet and there were a few OW thrown into the mix to liven things up. 

Again, this could have been a terrific read but fell short of the mark at the end.
