Rating: 4/5

I love this setting. One of my favorite genres is Historical Romance. Throw in a good mystery and then you have a winner.
It was a wonderful story that will keep you wondering until the end. I didn't read To Have and To Hold but will definitely go and read it now. Even though it is the first book I'm curious to see what was going on before this story.
A very good book that has everything that I love in it.
I was given this by Story Cartel for an honest review and I honestly think it was a wonderful, loving, on edge, what will happen next story filled with lots of twists and turns to keep you turning the pages.
It was a 4.5 star but since they won't let me to the half star it shows as a 4. Believe me it is 4.5 or maybe better.
