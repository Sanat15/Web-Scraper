Rating: 5/5

Another good read

After reading the first few chapters, it dawned on me that I read this book about 25 years ago. Still it was an excellent read for someone who enjoys historic fiction with a little bit of history and a lot of fiction. The time period has always interested me, I guess that's why I enjoyed it. I would recommend it to anyone, particularly those who enjoy the flash and boom of a flintlock.😊
