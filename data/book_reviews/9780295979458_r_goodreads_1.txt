Rating: 4/5

A subtle collection with a wild array of different storylines, ranging from the adventures of a bathhouse performer, a performance artist determined to continue his work even in the midst of a strange triangulation, a writer and filmmaker thinking about donating his sperm to one of his friends, among others. Phoenix Eyes and Other Stories reminds me why I simultaneously love and hate Los Angeles, precisely because its amazing kaleidoscopic concentration of peoples, cultures, geographies, among other things.
