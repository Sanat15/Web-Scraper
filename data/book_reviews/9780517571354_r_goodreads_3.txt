Rating: 2/5

I must acknowledge that at first I liked it, but the moment it began making references to Spanish history from the biased perspective of Spanish Black history, it lost all my interest. The further you progress in the book, the worse the references and exemplifications become. I don't understand how it can make claims about history that don't come close to reality. The climax of this review is when it talks about Hernán Cortés. I strongly recommend to the author to educate on what he'is talking about. Furthermore, what's the need for so many references to Spain in a book about "corporate life" written by a north merican?
