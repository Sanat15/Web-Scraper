Rating: 4/5

3.5 Stars ~ This is a fun read. Dizzy is a loveable young woman, who to her friends, appears to be as free-spirited as her name. Being the first to jump to help someone and with her genuinely kind heart, she's earned her title of godmother six times. Only her best friend, Christi, knows the deep pain she keeps buried and that drives her to live her life as an orphan-by-choice, if not in fact.

When Christi calls out an SOS, Dizzy can't refuse to answer. With Dizzy to play the role of irresponsible impulsive practically homeless friend, Christi will appear mature and confident in contrast. Just what Christi needs to prove to her guardian Uncle for him to release her inheritance on her 21st birthday. There's one hitch, the fusty, dusty professor that Dizzy is expecting Uncle Zach to be, turns out to be a golden Apollo in disguise. 

Though only 35, Zach has two loves -- his castle, in dire need of renovation, and history. He's not sure what the two girls are up to, but it doesn't take him long to realize Dizzy is not the girl trying to live up to her name. She completely enchants him and he finds himself falling hard and fast. When Dizzy owns up to Christi's scheme and confides in him who her parents are, Zach feels that he's been taken for a fool. It take's Christi in a rage to protect her loyal friend, to point out that Dizzy's not the only one with secrets about identity.
