Rating: 3/5

Okay, I think I liked this. I picked it up because I read a novel by this author last year and enjoyed her style. While her style is there it is obvious that this was a very early work.
I liked the story, but found it horribly confusing. I kept forgetting who the characters were. Also there were a lot of words for a fairly slender storyline.
It hasn't put me off, she writes really well, but her later stuff is definitely better. This was written by someone still honing their skills.
