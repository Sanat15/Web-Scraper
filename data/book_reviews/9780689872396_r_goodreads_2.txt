Rating: 4/5

It was an interesting choice made by the creators of the beloved THE EDGE CHRONICLES books to create books less steeped in the mystical and more in the real world (Knights of the Round Table times) geared towards an even younger audience. But it paid off.

Paul Stewart's writing is tighter, stronger, and more dynamic here; Chris Riddell's illustrations are darker, more gritty, and less chiseled--in some places they become rough sketches--and together they form a story more mature than any of their other works, and with a stronger voice. It thrills me that they created such a dark and gripping tale for such a young audience.

My favorite line of LAKE OF SKULLS: "I was on a plateau of rock, as flat as an out-of-tune minstrel."
