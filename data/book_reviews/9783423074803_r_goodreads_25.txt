Rating: 5/5

I've basically grown up on old mystery series XD

Seriously though.

My nine - twelve year self survived solely on Nancy Drew, The Hardy Boys, The Dana Girls, Beverly Gray, The Bobbsey Twins, The Boxcar Children, Agatha Christie books, and:

The Three Investigators.

Five stars for good mysteries, great memories, and wonderful hours of daydreaming myself into the stories that proceed throughout this series.

Thank you to my Grandmother for lending me (and then letting me keep) all her The Three Investigators books &lt;3

And thank you to that giant book sale I went to recently which produced another volume of this series that I immediately grabbed and brought home :)
