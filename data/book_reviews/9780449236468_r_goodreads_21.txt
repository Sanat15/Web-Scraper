Rating: 3/5

Interesting. I used to read a lot of Andre Norton, while I was in my twenties, and they were vintage even then. But I had never read this or Star Rangers, which must be among her earliest published works. So when I saw them for free for my Kindle, I snapped them up, in the omnibus volume Star Soldiers. Given their publication dates (1953 &amp; 1955)--ie, several years before Sputnik--their naivete about what it takes to achieve lift-off (which she, naturally, terms blast-off) is understandable. Nevertheless, the story has a certain antiquated charm.
