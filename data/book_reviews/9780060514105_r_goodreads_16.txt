Rating: 4/5

Going into this I didn’t realize it was the second book in a series. I realized about a third of the way through when I felt like I was missing key pieces of information. At that point I was already committed so I figured it out from there. It was a really cute story. The imagery and overall descriptive writing were really strong. I was totally able to visualize the characters, and the story as it was moving along. There were a lot more twists than I was expecting. It’s supposed to be a book for ages 8-12 but some parts felt a little too violent and scary (?) for an 8 year old to read. With that said, overall a 4 star read. (Also I learned how the presidential election works because of this book so thanks Molly moon)
