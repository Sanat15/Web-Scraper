An excellent and extensive overview of the Cambodian Genocide. It's supplemented with many oral histories showing just how brutal those years of 75 to 79 were for the people of Cambodia.

I just wish the author had written in more detail about the ideology of the Khmer Rouge. They seem to have this bastardized mix of Extreme Maoism, Communism and ethno-nationalism. Would have liked to hear more about the development of the party's ideas. Although the Khmer Rouge (or Angkar as they styled their authority) was notoriously secretive, so maybe difficult to decipher much from their inner workings.
