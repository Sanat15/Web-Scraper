Rating: 3/5

Since we're studying the Pilgrims right now, and this was on my shelf, we read it today. It is a fun look at the history of the rock. Not so much about Pilgrims, but the rock and what's happened to it over the course of the years.

My boys thought it was very entertaining and it held their attention. I had no idea it had been moved repeatedly (and broken).

While very interesting and well done, I'm not sure it's a "must read". If you can find it, it makes for an enjoyable few minutes though.
