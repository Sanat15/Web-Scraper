Rating: 3/5

This is a hard one to review. It's not the type of book you pick up and not put it down. You have to digest it and think about what you just read. This book goes into the faith that Elvis has with God and the woman who makes it her mission to pray for him. Do I believe what I read....I believe some of it, but some I'm not sure about. I guess you'll have to read it and make up your own mind!
