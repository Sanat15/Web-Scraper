Cold and stiff mannered Miss Joanna Feniton

last of the Fenitons who came over with the Conqueror is to be married to Mr. Algernon Cholcombe, heir to an earl. Well, he hasn’t asked yet but he is coming to ask for her hand in marriage. They have met many years ago when they were children, Joanna cannot remember whether he was fair or dark.
