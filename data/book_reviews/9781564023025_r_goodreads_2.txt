Rating: 4/5

Something about the illustrations in this book felt very British to me from the start, so I did have to look up the illustrator and was not surprised to find he was British. 
Still the illustrations are well done and I really like them, enough to be considering buying the book (I do love horses). 
I also liked the way the book was written, simple over all with a child's observations of a field of horses and how they feel about horses, and written so that the reader is drawn in to the book. However, it also contains horse facts on some of the pages, written in a different font from the rest of the story. 
Well done over all and a great early horse book for young horse lovers.
