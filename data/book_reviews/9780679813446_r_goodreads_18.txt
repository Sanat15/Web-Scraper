Rating: 1/5

We fell in love with The Black in the first book. We were expecting this sequel to be mostly about The Black Stallion like the first one was- but we were wrong. This book was more about the government and hierarchy rankings in Arabia, sandwiched between mentions of The Black. My kids were disappointed and lost through all the names and different dialogues. I was lost myself and it was hard to help them through it. 
Just stop reading after the first book. 😂
