Curious George x Don Quixote, mash-up
This was so bananas I'm going to need my potassium levels checked. 
Marcovaldo is full of wonder, curiosity, and meddlesome absurdity. These episodic shorts are connected by the main character, Marcovaldo and the setting, 1950s Italy, and so can be read as a novelette. 
This was an absurd journey through the seasons. From mushrooms in the city, treating patients with wasps, eating fried brains on a motorbike, and learning the difference between public and human relations Santa, this had me laughing and highlighting. Calvino's sentences are incredibly crafted. There are some long sentences that drape like a beautiful train of a designer dress (that I immerse myself in re-reading for pleasure) and these are interspersed with lovingly simple and poignant stand-out lines. The prose is constantly balancing on a comically swaying motorbike as it teeters over cobblestones. It's an unexpected ride, with beautiful scenery, a humorous narrator, and some unexpected wisdom. Oh and did I mention! There is an island with a hidden cat realm 😻 which brings Marcovaldo to a shining 5/5 ⭐️
