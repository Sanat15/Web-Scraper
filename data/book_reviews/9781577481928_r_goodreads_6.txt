Rating: 5/5

This is one of my favorite books when I want to find scriptures according to subject matter. I always keep one in my car, so I can use my time effectively when I am stuck in traffic, it is a great way to get your focus off your frustration on to what really matters.

I also try keep a new one handy to give away, if I meet someone needing encouragement. Many homeless people don't have a Bible, so this is lightweight for them to carry and I keep them enclose it in an individually sealed ziploc bag, so if they get rained on the book doesn't get damaged. I can't tell you the appreciation I've been shown from those wanting one. It is a great way to witness and lead people to Christ or repent and rededicate their life!
