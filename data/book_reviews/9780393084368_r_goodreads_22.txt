Rating: 5/5

I remember reading this when I was in college and thinking it was amazing at the time. I also had just received a letter from a friend who had gotten married right out of high school that was enumerating her daily life and I was struck by how far our lives were diverged r/t our life choices. I felt extremely lucky to having this adventure in life &amp; learning at college and I hoped my life would not turn out like many of the ones that Friedan analyzed. Through her efforts and many of the early feminists who fought for opportunities for women, I can say that I have had a wonderful, fulfilling life. I'm sure this work would be an eye-opener for today's young women who are unaware of the restrictions placed on women only 40-50 years ago!!! As a college graduate I couldn't even get a credit card without my father or spouse co-signature!!!!!!
