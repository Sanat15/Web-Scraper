Rating: 5/5

A slim volume on the life and work of Marcel Proust: a supposed invalid who reclined in bed and yet became a prolific and enigmatic writer. 

Of course, one can easily spend one's entire life studying Proust and wallowing in the related Proustian industries, so a slim volume such as this one is all the more appealing. It has everything without being too much. One can duck in and read all about it and still have time left over for the most important things in life — such as some chamomile tea, a gluten-free pastry, and the time to daydream.
