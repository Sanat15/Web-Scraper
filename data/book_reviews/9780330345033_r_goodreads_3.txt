Rating: 5/5

With a setting during the Napoleonic Wars the Poldark saga continues with the main focus being on the teenage children of Ross and Demelza Poldark.

First I want to say how much I've enjoyed learning about the Napoleonic Wars as told by the ever fascinating author, Winston Graham. I actually felt like I were sharing this stressful time with the Poldark clan.

The ever snotty Cuby's rejection of Jeremy Poldark has sent him into a tailspin causing him to run away to join the army; buying a commission with his ill-gotten gains. Hopefully he can finally forget Cuby.

I'm delighted that Clowance has broken her engagement with the ne'er do well Stephen Carrington. However, Stephen is not taking this very well at all and his huge ego is nearly shattered. Now he regrets not 'raping' the innocent Clowance when he had the chance as that would've taught her a lesson or two. UGH! I hope this guy goes down with his ship.

This book ends with Napoleon being exiled and Jeremy Poldark returning to his regiment in Belgium - very close to Waterloo - where the escaped Napoleon seems to be heading! This is not good at all and I'm worried sick about Jeremy surviving the Battle of Waterloo.

So I'm rushing off to read the next book in the series to discover Jeremy's fate with fingers crossed.
