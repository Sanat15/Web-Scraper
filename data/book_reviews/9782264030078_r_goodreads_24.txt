Rating: 5/5

This has been described as a coming-of-age story or sometimes as the story of a boy's sexual awakening, but it is about so much more.

It is told in six parts, that cover Arjie's life from the age of 7 to about 14, with a background of the simmering tensions between Tamils and Sinhalese in Sri Lanka, tensions that finally break out into a savage and brutal civil war. His family is afraid that Arjie is "funny" and we all know what that word means. His father is especially concerned and force him to change schools. It doesn't help (hint: don't send a "funny" boy to an all boys school).

In this novel, we follow not only Arjie's maturation, but it is also about racism, family secrets, love, and of course the violence of the war.

It is hard for me to believe that this novel, one that I can't say is exactly entertaining--it is too sad and violent for that--is a debut novel. Absolutely wonderful.

4.5
