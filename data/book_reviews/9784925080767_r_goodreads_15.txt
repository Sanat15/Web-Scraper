Rating: 4/5

Series still going strong!

How do the twists KEEP COMING???

That said, the opening of this story was so similar to one of the previous books I was starting to worry it was the same book! 

Still, this delivered another fun mystery that kept me guessing. A few bits and pieces were easy to put together but otherwise I was stumped right up til the end.

I love this genre so much - no time wasted on dumb romance between detectives, just plain old fashioned crime/crime solving.

One more in this series to go, but hopefully more will be translated!
