Rating: 4/5

"The Dead Secret" is an atmospheric Gothic novel about family secrets and a hidden identity. There are well-drawn characters in a spirited heroine, her blind husband with strong principles, an extremely anxious servant, and a kind uncle.

Some of the book is set in the haunted wing of a mansion in a room that has been closed up for years. The plot involves several moral questions, and is full of suspense.

This is an enjoyable 1857 work of Wilkie Collins where he shows his early talent in writing sensation novels. Thanks to Lori for leading a wonderful discussion in the Dickensian group. 3.5 stars.
