Rating: 3/5

This book can be used for so many purposes. Here are a few good uses for this story. 
1. Introducing origami
2. Discuss how the things people do can affect others
3. Introducing a different culture (Asian culture in particular)
4. Discussing the importance of sharing and helping others in need
I particularly like the illustrations Bang provides in the book. Some pictures look three deminsional; one looks like it was hand drawn. The simplicity of some of the pictures looks easy enough for children to draw. People who are into art will enjoy the illustrations.
