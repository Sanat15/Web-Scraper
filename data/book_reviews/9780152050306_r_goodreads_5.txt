Rating: 3/5

A suspense novel with a dark and scary atmosphere,The characters are well written and the way they interact with each other and their sense of fear was the best thing about this book
The plot was average for me, and there was a feeling of boredom in some chapters, and the ending also left me with important questions, the most important of which is why, until the end, we did not discuss what is in the last room. The book was good and intriguing but wasn't as good as the other books i read for nixon.

(VERDICT: 7.2/10)
