Rating: 3/5

This novel is set in 1962 in Dortmund and is told by Jurgen Fohrmann, a miner, who is confronted by changes in the work environment as the end of the German Economic Miracle is approaching. The cast of characters are all having to come to terms with their past and what they really want for the future. Initially I wasn't particularly interested, but as the novel continued the portrayal of ordinary people at that extraordinary time was thought-provoking and made the novel well worth reading.
