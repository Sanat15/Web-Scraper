Rating: 2/5

I enjoyed only one of the four short stories, that is Marcy Stewart’s A Halo for Mr Devlin. It’s old school - a road trip for a rebellious runaway and cynical gamester - and nothing new, but well written, amusing and somewhat charming (especially the last page). I’d rate it at 4 stars but the other stories left me cold. I borrowed this from Open Library, and read it online, skimming the ones that didn’t interest me, so one out of four didn’t worry me.
