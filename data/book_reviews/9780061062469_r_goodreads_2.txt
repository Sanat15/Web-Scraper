Rating: 3/5

A continuation of the Vampire Twins series. 

This book finds Paul turned. He is bitten and becomes a vampire. Ami is upset but there is nothing she can do for her brother now. Together they are still trying to piece together their family history and find out more about what is means to have a vampire as a father. While trying to keep friends and others alike from finding out their long buried secrets. 

Janice Harrell is an impressive YA author. I liked this book and will continue the series and look for more by her and authors similar.
