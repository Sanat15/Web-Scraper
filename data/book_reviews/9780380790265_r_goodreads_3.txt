Rating: 3/5

Baltimore homicide detective, Phillip Garner, is puzzled by the recent string of murders. He calls his friend, Darryl Billips, reporter for The Herald, to get his feed back. Darryl, who was recently named editor, was eager to from behind the desk and back on the streets. In Hidden In Plain View, author Blair S. Walker gives readers a reporters view of crime solving/reporting. 

Walker's story began with all the makings of a great thriller but it sort of fizzled half way through. The characters were predictable and the mystery was easily solved within the first 80 pages. I enjoyed the story despite it lack of excitement. I would rate this as a 2 1/2 but bumped it up to three for home town appeal.
