Rating: 4/5

I used this book to help prepare for my Natchez Trace trips. It is incredibly useful despite being incredibly out of date. There are sections of the trace that were "proposed" and not finished in their current form when Lori wrote it. It's too bad there is not a more up to date version.

But you can easily ferret out the current and useful info from the other. For instance, the historical info is the same, as are the side trips you can take off of the trace. If you are depending on the book for b&amp;bs or campsites make sure to check and see if they are still in operation before setting out. I used only the 3 NPS campsites on the Trace and even some of that info is out of date (Busby no longer has a gas station, even though even the NPS maps say it does. It doesn't.)
