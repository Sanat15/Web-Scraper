Rating: 3/5

I'm so glad to be finishing this book because I fell asleep whilst reading it more often than any other. On the bright side, I now know what keeps me awake while reading (grapes) and so thanks M. Atwood for bestowing this gift on me.

I now know more about the nature of Canadian writing and writers than I did before. I also stongly suspect that I know more M. Atwood than I did before. It's all good.

Canadian literature (and writers) seem a bit down at the moment. If you meet any, please give them a hug from me.

Read this if you have to. It's good but it's also a tad boring if you're not keen on reading about someone's opinions on Canadianness.
