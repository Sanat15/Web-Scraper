Rating: 3/5

Anna Lee Waldo's book Sacajawea was one of my all-time most favorite books so I was sure I'd love this one too, but I didn't. The first 2/3 was pretty dry reading other than spots here and there that kept me interested in it. I think there was too much intricate and irrelevant detail for me like descriptions of clothing, descriptions of meals, including how they were eaten, etc. The last third of the book kept my full interest though. I'm glad I read it but give it 3.5 stars.
