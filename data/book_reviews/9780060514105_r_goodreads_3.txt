Rating: 5/5

This is the second book in the Molly Moon series.

This time, Molly has promised to stop using her hypnotic powers because they are too "dangerous", but she misses the sensation that she gets when hypnotizing people and the fusion feeling, so she attemps to hypnotize objects instead of people one time when she is wearing a diamond around her neck and it freezes time! And Molly is summoned by Lucy Logan, a librarian and the sister of Cornelius Logan (the author of Molly's hypnotism book), to stop this evil American billionare and hypnotist named Primo Cell who has all the celebrities under his power and is plotting to become President and take over the world!

Ohhh, this is cool. Just like the previous book, Molly Moon's Incredible Book of Hypnotism, it features an excellent plot, colourful and likeable characters, lots of humour, and a positive message. Except it's even more exciting!

I'd recommend this book to just about anyone, but you should definitely read the first book first. There are some confusing parts, but that's the fun of it.

GO MOLLY MOON!
