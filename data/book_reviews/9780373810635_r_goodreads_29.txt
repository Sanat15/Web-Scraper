Rating: 5/5

A Mutual Liking

There's always that one relative who thinks they're better than anyone else and, unfortunately, Claudia's uncle left the house to one such cousin. I don't really understand why, but he did think he had provided for Claudia and her mother. Thomas was the consultant who saw her uncle. I think Claudia fell in love at first sight. Of course, she didn't know that. 

Thomas made a snap decision to ask Claudia to marry him. I think he knew that she would make him a really good wife. He knew he liked her, but it took him a while to realize he was in love with her. Unlike Betty Neels' other books though, Thomas is the 1st to realize he's in love. Normally the lady realizes she's in love 1st. Claudia is still under the mistaken impression that they are just friends, which leaves her open for one of Thomas' exes to cause problems.... and for Claudia to finally realize that she is in love with Thomas. 

There isn't a whole lot of conflict in this story. It's still a really great love story.
