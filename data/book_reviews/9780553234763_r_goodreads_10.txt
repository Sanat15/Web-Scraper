Rating: 4/5

"...real joy is God in the marrow of our bones." Reading this book made me feel close to my Grandma again, which was so amazing. It was special to her &amp; my Great Aunt Mary, as was Saint Augustine, the setting of Maria. This book will be treasured by me. 
Maria is written in the 70's &amp; that seemed obvious in the writing style. That wasn't exactly my favorite, but I loved everything else about this book. I loved the character of Maria, I loved learning more about the history of Florida (my new state) &amp; St. Augustine. I loved that Maria found true joy even after so much hardship &amp; heartache. And I loved that this is all based on true people &amp; true happenings &amp; that I can go to St. Augustine &amp; see Maria's house. 
The story of Maria begins before the War of Independence &amp; follows her through that turmoil &amp; also the turmoil of losing three husbands. She is strong &amp; talented &amp; resourceful. Such a good book!
