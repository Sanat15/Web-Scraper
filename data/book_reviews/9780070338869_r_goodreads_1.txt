Rating: 2/5

Squatters and landowners tussle over an abandoned house slated for renovation.

The squatters happen to be a packrat, a squirrel, a skunk, a snake, some wasps, and an owl, but they are willing to play dirty, even recruiting outside help from a friend with a nuclear option.

I know we are supposed to be on the side of the critters, but they weren't nearly cute and cuddly enough to forgive their transgressions. They're just pests.

Side note: This is an old book from my childhood home, first purchased about fifty years ago by my mother through a mail order subscription to the Weekly Reader Children's Book Club. I think this is the first time I've ever read it because I often have memories of dwelling over the pictures in these old books, but not this time.
