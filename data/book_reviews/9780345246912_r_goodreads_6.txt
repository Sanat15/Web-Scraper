Rating: 5/5

I obtained two additional vintage books about Star Trek from my aunt who has just passed away, and when I found this one I had just about assembled the perfect backstory of the franchise, so with her having died about a week ago it was perfect timing, if not perfect circumstances, as well as it being the 60th year since first broadcast, to finally read them…Whitfield’s book was first published as production was still occurring, early in the third season. It’s a literal (great) bird’s eye view of the series, including hilarious memos passed by production staff. What I most take away from all this is summed up, actually, by Richard Donner’s approach to the 1978 Superman, which he famously described with one word: verisimilitude. For Gene, it really was about keeping everything as authentic as it could be, taking it all very seriously. With his military background, he led the ship in the direction of as much real science as it could handle and still be entertaining. Very invaluable.
