Rating: 5/5

My son gave me this book several Christmases ago and it's a real lump-in-the-throat kind of book. There is much wisdom, fishing, hunting, living, and learning in this book and I recommend it highly.

There are lots of sensei/kempai books and films around and this is one of the best. I never read one that I do not think of my father and I and my son and I and how everything comes around.
