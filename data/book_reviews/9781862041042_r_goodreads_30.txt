Been re-reading this, now that my older kids are teens. I've been rather unschooling in our approach the whole time we've homeschooled, even before I knew it. 

I forgot, however, how "ANARCHY!" this book was! 

I love it, though, because this choice, these possibilities, deserve a voice that's intelligent. Grace Llewellyn does that. The revised version goes into greater detail about legalities. There's just so much in this book; it's packed. I love all the snippets from the Growing Without Schooling magazine, which unfortunately is not being published anymore. 

Anyone considering homeschooling teens will glean something valuable from this book, whether you unschool all the way, or use some of these ideas to supplement a more traditional approach.
