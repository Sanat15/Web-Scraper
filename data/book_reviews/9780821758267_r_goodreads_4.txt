Rating: 4/5

4 STARS. This was actually a lot better than I was expecting. I’ve had it on my tbr for a few years because this is an author I really like’s favorite author. 

Since it was written a while ago I thought it might be boring—but I couldn’t have been more mistaken! It was full of adventure and humor, and no junk. You can’t go wrong with ducks, butlers, and gossip flying about at every second. (And no stupid misunderstandings between the main characters—LOVED that.) 

Seriously, this book did such a good job of always keeping you on your toes and keeping it exciting. She also wasn’t afraid to let good things happen to the main characters, and for them to win in a lot of little fights and information, which is probably an odd observation, but sometimes I think writers exclusively let bad things happen and the main characters be kind of stupid. But I cases like this, you truly feel the danger because you actually don’t know who will win because you’ve observed both—not just the main characters being bested constantly.

But the butlers and servants were literally probably my favorite part of the whole book. Lesson: Get your butler flowers.

I loved the many POVs and main characters we get in this book and also I’m a SUCKER for when literally everybody is paired off and happy by the end. It makes my heart happy—sue me.
