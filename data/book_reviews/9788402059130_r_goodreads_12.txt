Rating: 5/5

Perhaps the greatest spy novel ever, it is chilling (as cold as the title) and realistic and hair-raising. A true classic!

Smiley takes a back seat in this extraordinary Cold War spy thriller hitch shows the true hypocrisy on both sides of the Berlin Wall. There are so many twists and turns in the story that it reads very quickly. It is a fantastic novel, one of Le Carré’s best. 

What Le Carré really excels at is getting into the psychology of each actor in the story and building up the pressure up to the climax which happens on the very final pages. He was never one for a long denouement. This one features a beautiful love story with a double (at least) agent and is set in the Cold War on both sides of the Berlin Wall. For those in the back of the room with the red hats, Russia was and still is the bad guy on the other side of the wall, even if the name has changed and even if we are not perfect on our side.
I am at pains to say much more because the book is so short (and breathtaking on nearly every page) that more details or analysis would inevitably lead to spoilers.

So, just take a long afternoon on a cold weekend before the end of the winter, and curl up on the couch with this one. Let me know what you think!
