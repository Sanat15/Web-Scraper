Rating: 4/5

A standalone novel by Agatha Christie. Ariadne Oliver (mystery author) makes a very brief appearance only.
Our main amateur sleuth is Mark Easterbrook. He decides to investigate the murder of a much beloved priest after a few coincidences come together for him. He is helped in his endeavours by Ginger Corrigan.
To be honest, when I first started listening to this a book, I thought a few of these coincidences were far fetched, but I went along with it. It did come together nicely as the story progressed.
There are elements of the occult and witchery in this book, which is not a typical element in her books.
At one point, one of the characters wonders if the “machines” will take over from people someday. This book was published in 1961- I guess Dame Christie could see what lay in our future, much like the fortune teller in this book.
I have listened to many Christie’s over the past year. I must be getting the knack of it because I figured out who the “mastermind” was. I’ve never done that before.

Hugh Fraser, as always, does a fantastic job with the audio.

Definitely an enjoyable read/ listen. 

Published: 1961
