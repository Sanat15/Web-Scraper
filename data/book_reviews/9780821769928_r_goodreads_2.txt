2.5
Was this one written by the same author as the book I recently enjoyed and gave almost five stars to? This book was kind of on the lines of trashy. I couldn't stand the scheme they came up with, I couldn't stand the other men outside of Les, and I couldn't stand how the whole thing ended. Why 2.5 stars? Because it did keep me reading and it wasn't really dirty. It just had an undertone of less morals which I am not a huge fan of. Also, this is more a critique for the time period I guess, but I really don't like the fact that it is okay for men to sleep around with "loose" women but a moral women can't even be alone with a "gentleman." Which I approve of no relations before marriage I just don't like reading about loose men anymore then I like it with loose women.

This book was just okay. Pity, because it makes me not want to read anymore of this series.
