Rating: 4/5

Irregardless of Peoples Opinion. This is and will always be one of my most favourite Books. I have read this Book countless times and still not bored with it. I don't give much, if any, Spoilers in my Reviews because I feel like it takes away interest in reading for oneself and coming to an opinion of your own, that said, read but go in knowing that the H and h relationship was borderline crazy, push and pull. Sometimes fighting like Tom and Jerry, sometimes loving on each other. Generally I liked it.
