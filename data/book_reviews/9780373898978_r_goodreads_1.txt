Rating: 1/5

The "H" is a deadbeat dad who isn't there in his son's life but just turns up when the novel starts because he's getting married again and has to introduce his ex-wife ( the heroine ) and his son to his fiancee. The heroine is left to raise her son all alone after the bastard H moves to Alaska when they divorce. The son is a spoilt brat whose mommy over compensates for his lack of a daddy by making him her entitled little prince. The heroine has a chance to finally move on with a nice guy who is also sexy but she ends up succumbing to the dumbo H again. The H has had a very active sex life since the divorce while the heroine has been celibate and bitter. The H's fiancee is a bratty rich girl who acts like she's going to be the model stepmom by trying to tell the heroine how to raise her kid. I wanted to tell her to stfu and mind her own business because the heroine was a bit too polite and tolerant. I got to the halfway point in this novel, kept wanting the H to get knocked down by a truck started skimming through the rest and then decided that my blood pressure would thank me for not reading all the way to the end. NOPE. This was not romantic at all...
