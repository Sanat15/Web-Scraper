Rating: 4/5

Book 7 in the Deverry Cycle, and the third book in the story of The Westlands. Kerr continues to impress! The world-building keeps on expanding - now with dragons and armies of creatures - and everything feels seamless and well written. Familiar and new characters intermingle, and as a reader you only want more!

This is the first book in the long series that ends in the middle of ongoing events and with a bit of a cliffhanger. I recommend having the next book ready at hand.
