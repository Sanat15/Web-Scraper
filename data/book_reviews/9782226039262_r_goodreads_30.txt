Rating: 3/5

At one point in the story, The Tommyknockers features a typewriter that operates telepathically, and the second half of this book reads as though it were written exactly that way. In other words, messy word vomit with no edits, revisions or omissions, just everything that popped into King's mind spewed out onto the page. I did enjoy the first half, however, when the characters and the town of Haven were being set up. It's too bad it went off the rails.
