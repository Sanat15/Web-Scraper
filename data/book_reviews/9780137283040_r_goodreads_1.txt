Rating: 4/5

Ah, the joys and pains of loving a particular sports team, especially one fated to an inordinate amount of title-seeking failure (as I have experiences as a deep-garnet follower of all things Gamecock, especially on the gridiron). Herein we have the excellent writer Higgins explaining his love for the Red Sox. A humorous account, well written, insightful and often humorous. Followers of that team and the sport as a whole will likely enjoy these pages, even if they disagree with some of his pronouncements. Higgins was a friend of my boss, and I picked this up on a whim, and I enjoyed it.
