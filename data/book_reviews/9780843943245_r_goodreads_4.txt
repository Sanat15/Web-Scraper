Rating: 5/5

Hiram King tells the story of a former slave by the name of Cole Granger now living out west who has been tasked to deliver some prize horses by his white boss and he is determined to complete the task no matter what, be it indians, mexican bandits or the oppressive racism he has to deal with constantly. And also there is the political battle between the Democrats and Republicans fighting for control of the territory and also personal battles between men who want to be bigger and more powerful even though they have more than most around them.
