Rating: 1/5

This book got thrown away after I read it. Although it claimed to be clean church humor that could be used in sermons, not many pastors would keep their jobs if they did. Quite a lot had profanity needlessly inserted and others were about pastors having affairs. 
Besides there were only a handful of decent jokes Needless to say it was not the type of joke book that I wanted around for guests or children to look in hence it is in the trash.
