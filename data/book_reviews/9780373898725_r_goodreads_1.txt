"Enemy in Camp" is the story of Victoria and Dirk.

SO GOOD!

A sexy, banter filled battle of wills between the H and h. 

The h is a rich heiress, the H a revered reporter. His articles about their family enrage her, and when her father invites him to their island to spend two weeks, she is mighty mad! 
They start off on a feisty note, with them challenging each other at every turn. But their chemistry is too hot to deny. Soon love blooms, but will their differences overpower their emerging feelings?

This was awesome! A strong h and H who complement each other, and totally are a match for each other's wits. I liked how the H dealt with her family, and especially her smitten sister. The h on the other hand took time to understand the H's perspective, and the book ends on a super sweet note.

100% recommended and SO HOT!
