Rating: 4/5

Not a terrible textbook by any means, but each section contained a list of relevant people, often without any more than a name. That was fairly useless. But, overall, it does a solid job as an overview. 

Past editions had an incredibly useful student essay in every chapter, which showed readers what kind of work a student could actually do with these theories. That seems like a loss to cut that in this 5th edition, but there are expanding sections on African-American and Queer criticism and a new section on Ecocritisism.
