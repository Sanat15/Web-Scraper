Rating: 3/5

Clemens Brentano's best known story. Very well told, and I believe there is a an English translation available for those interested. It tells of the tragic fates of two people wronged in different ways as told by Kasperl's grandmother, who also happens to be the Godmother of Annerl. I would say it does not have the ethical complexity of Kleist, Goethe, or Schiller, but still worth reading
