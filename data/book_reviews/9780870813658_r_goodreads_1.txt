Rating: 5/5

This is a great book for spending a week in Yellowstone with. My cousin and I worked our way through all the accessible geyser basins, and we had the book with us every day. It helped a lot in knowing how likely it was that any particular geyser was likely to erupt soon. It pointed out places we might otherwise have missed, that have trails/boardwalks but are not in the main traffic areas, so we saw White Dome and Great Fountain erupt. 

It also saved us a lot of time - the Park Service has closed many trails to back-country geysers due to damage done, and the book notes all of these also, so we didn't try to go where we would not have been allowed anyway.

The descriptions of the geysers, spouters, and pools are very helpful and informative. It's well-written and interesting; there is a lot of park history included - just the right guide if you like geysers. The 5th edition also includes other geysers around the world.
