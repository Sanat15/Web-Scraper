Rating: 4/5

How did the witch in Hansel and Gretel end up alone in the woods in a candy house with an appetite for children?

Well, let's find out.

As so many witches do, she started out as a midwife who called on demons for information. Totally innocently and with the best intentions at first. And then she slowly lost control over her own desires, got greedy and a little too cocky.  And then she lost everything she ever loved, made a candy house because it reminded her of her daughter (I think), and had demons shouting in her head to EAT THE CHILDREN. But being a quality midwife and all-around good person underneath it all, she throws herself into the fire assisted by Gretel without succumbing to the temptation to eat a child. Except for the finger she bit off that baby because it had one too many. So she dies in flames after much suffering.

In other words, this books is fantastic and amazing, and I would have loved it as a teen.
