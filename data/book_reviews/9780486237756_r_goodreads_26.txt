Rating: 5/5

I wavered between four and five stars, but I went with five because I really enjoyed this story and the characters and the mystery. It was well written and compact. All the details mattered. It had a moral to the story, but also moral complexity. I’m so glad I read this.

Oh, and one part of this I loved was Uncle Joseph (just loved him in general!) and his music box that plays two Mozart melodies. I can hardly wrap my mind around the idea that that was the only way he could hear music without a live performance. Seriously I just can’t even. We are so blessed with ready access to music. Joseph was so deeply moved by his music box and kept it all the time for comfort and consolation. It was given to his brother Max in Germany by Mozart himself!
