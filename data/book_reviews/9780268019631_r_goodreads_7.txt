Rating: 4/5

Elias Chacour is the Archbishop of Galilee of the Melkite Greek Catholic Church. This is the second book of his that I have read. I found this book to cover much of the same ground that his first book, Blood Brothers talks about. That did not detract from this book, but readers should be aware of this fact.

This book will expose the reader to a point of view that we rarely hear about in the United States. Chacour considers himself to be a descendant of Jesus' disciples. His message is that the Israelis and the Palestinians need to live on the same land, in peace.

I wish that I could believe that his desire will ever come true.
