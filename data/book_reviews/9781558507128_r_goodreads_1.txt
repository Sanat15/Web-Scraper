Rating: 3/5

I likely picked-up my pair of miniscule comic joke books by Missy Camp Dizick at a garage sale. Even though I recall that it must have been over ten years ago, it is seldom that I do know retain precisely whence the contents of our bountiful book collection come. We who thoroughly research bargains by mail and make special driving trips, are committed to physical books to a degree that creates stories about the quest to obtain them. I wonder if this artist’s unusual and literally campy three names are a pseudonym meant to sound dizzy or ditsy, or if they are her actual given names.

I imparted the information in my previous review this year, that she is a gallery visual artist; which I found incredible, going by the lumpy generic shapes in these comic strip joke books. I say again that I am curious to see what she produced that got accepted into New York and California art galleries. For her notecard product line as well as these tiny joke books, these are cute caricatures in a cartoon style with sweet, colourful backgrounds that are pretty. It is nice that Missy depicts and discusses in her little text scenarios; general, plain, home life. Excepting her particularly strange imaginations, everything is familiar to all of us.

I gave “Test Your Cat’s Psychic Powers” , her 1998 joke book, two stars because it was more odd than amusing. This 1997 predecessor, however, “Test Your Cat’s Mental Health” comprises common, relatable cat descriptions in the main and numerous warm moments of friendship, love, comfort, and security. It is all too obvious that all of these drawings replicate people and cats in the same shape blueprints; only switching the colours of fur, hair, and clothing. Nonetheless, this suite of cat activities is funny.
