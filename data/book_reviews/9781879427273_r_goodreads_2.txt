Rating: 3/5

I enjoy short story anthologies. Ones used to come out every month a few years ago. Now they seem to be a bit more difficult to find.

This collection of short stories are all by women. Some stories have a decidedly lesbian point of view. Nevertheless, there are a few gems in this collection. There is a little ghost story that will stay with you long after you finish this collection.

If you would like a little change of pace, pick this up if you can find it.
