Rating: 4/5

This lovely book was filled with the author's experiences during the years of her life as a quadriplegic and the lessons God had taught her through them. Her amazing pictures and sketches (all done by holding the brush or pen in her mouth) graced every other page. Reading her meditations reminded me to view my life with the right perspective. Hopefully I can remember to do that. Here is a favorite quote from the book: "Suffering doesn't teach me about myself from a textbook, it teaches me from my heart. It will always show me what I love -- either the God of all comfort or the comfort that can become my god."
