Rating: 2/5

This started out to be a very promising read. A woman on the run from her abusive husband is in the wrong place at the wrong time and winds up getting kidnapped by a gang of train robbers. Their leader is your typical Western hero who was robbing the train only to get enough money to buy back his two children from a local Indigenous tribe, so he seems to be a sympathetic character. (Cat Dancing was the name of his deceased wife). 

Sparks fly, although the eventual outcome of this whole scenario looks grim. However, the theme of rape is brought up repeatedly to the point where I didn't want to keep reading. And finally, there was a plot twist that I found utterly revolting and most readers will be turned off by it, as I was. I haven't seen the movie with Burt Reynolds, but the ending was thankfully changed for the film.
