Rating: 4/5

I'm surprised this book isn't better known. The first of a seven book series, it's a really good beginning and a solid tale in its own right. Lots of colorful characters populate a well realized world. Set long after an apocalyptic event, along the rivers of what was Mississippi, it tells the story of tribes, separated by culture and tradition, but with more in common than they realize. A bold adventurer, willing to look beyond tradition, beyond cultural differences, into the hearts of people, thinks life could be more than conflict and suffering. 
I hope this gets reprinted. It deserves to be wider read.
