Rating: 5/5

When Kit Roarke goes to the town of Pratte to investigate her best friend's death she finds more than she bargained for.
Jo's death was in fact cold blooded murder!
In a town where everyone is a suspect and not knowing who to trust reluctantly she picks up with the enigmatic and handsome town doctor Noah Stewart but is even he who he seems and what secrets is he hiding?
Can Kit solve the puzzle of Jo's death and save her own life into the bargain as the web closes tighter.
Full of excitement and suspense this is a page turning little romance that has all the ingredients for a great read.
