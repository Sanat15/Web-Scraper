Rating: 5/5

When you're ready to use Bach Flower Remedies (BFRs) seriously for yourself or others, this is the reference book to have. It is not a beginner's book nor a book for those who are familiar with BFRs but who don't need extended treatment. It's such a book of depth that it's the book you'll refer to over and over as you improve your life or assist others who may have questions.

My copy is annotated, highlighted, and pages gem-clipped. I refer to this book regularly, weekly wouldn't be an exaggeration. Worth the investment to buy if you're serious about BFRs.
