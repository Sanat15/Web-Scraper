Rating: 4/5

I must confess... unlike Sean Hannity, I don't really have any interest in reading anything less than 4-5 star books. I'll leave the Dick &amp; Jane stuff to him and Ted Nugent. Anyway, this is more than a typical, boring recitation of facts. Mr. Mak traces the history of this cosmopolitan city with a lot of 1st person accounts. Great information about the 17th and 18th century. It gave my trip there a great deal more depth.
