Rating: 2/5

Boogenhagen saved me from writing a long review. Be sure to check out hers to get the complete plot.
Just know that in this second chance romance:

*2/3 of the book is a flashback

*The hero spanks the heroine for not telling him she's a journalist.

*The hero is from a working class Northern England family. His mother is a stereotype. Period. From the cigarette hanging out of her mouth while ironing to her dyed hair and grasping attitude. 

*The heroine is beyond immature - the way she leaves him, the way she enters an engagement with an older man while still married, the way she gives in all the time. *sigh*

*Hero is a cheater with anger issues.

They belong together.
