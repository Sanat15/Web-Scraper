Rating: 4/5

"You will observe that the stories told are all about money-seekers, not about money-finders."
 Edgar Allan Poe, The Gold-Bug 

This was so different than anything I have ever read by Poe.

A treasure hunt such as one has never experienced. Sensational! It's Poe though so should one really expect anything else?

Anyway, I had no idea this book even existed. I was made aware of it by another Poe fan who remarked that it was her favorite by him so I decided to settle in for a read and I'm sure glad that I did.

This is not a five minute read, like much of his poetry and short stories. But it is not full length either. Take your time with it and have fun. 

As I said books on treasure hunts? Usually, they're not for me. However, I have a rule. That rule is there are a few, but a few, authors whose works I will read no matter WHAT they're about and Poe is one of them.

To date, I do not think I've ever rated anything by him under a four. And he has so much work I still haven't read. The Gold Bug was, until recently, one of those books.

My eyes sparkle, thinking of all the Poe ahead of me, waiting for me to read. Such a writer he was!

I must also comment that the woman who made me aware of this story has since passed.
She was an activist besides having exceptional reading taste. RIP Miranda.

As you are likely expecting, I am recommending this!
