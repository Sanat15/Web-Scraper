Rating: 4/5

This is a beautiful book with beautiful color and black and white photos. Author Kondo explains the history of this Japanese drink--its history, brewing and its role in present culture. For the consumer, he explains the types of sake how to drink, how to serve, and how to taste. It is an excellent intro to the world of Japanese rice wine. 

The one shortcoming that prevented me from rating this higher is that it is a bit dated. It was written in 1985, and much has changed since. Breweries in the United States have changed; sake with additives are available as are jizake or local brands of sake (at least in Hawaii).
