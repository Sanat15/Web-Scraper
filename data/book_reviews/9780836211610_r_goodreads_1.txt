I didn't realize that Ziggy was one of those comic strips that leaned towards biting social commentary. However, the bulk of the comics featured in this book, showcasing observations on the commercialization of the Christmas season, reminded me of the more cynical moments from the Charlie Brown Christmas television special.
Did I like it? It wasn't what I was expecting, so no.
Would I reread it? No.
Would I recommend it? For readers who treat Christmas with a heavy dose of pessimism and jaded Grinchiness, this might be right up their alley. To readers looking for a feel-good Christmas book, this is not that book.
