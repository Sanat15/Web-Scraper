Rating: 5/5

I have an older edition of Conceptual Physics by Paul G. Hewitt. This is a great book, it will teach you physics in a different way than other textbooks. It's a conceptually driven textbook, full of words pictures and explanations. If you understand the concepts clearly, it will be much easier to understand equations, and if you understand equations, you can surely solve physics problems. The content is not hard actually, it's very friendly compared to other first-year physics textbooks such as Halliday.
