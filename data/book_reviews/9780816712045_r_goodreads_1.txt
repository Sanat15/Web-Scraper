Rating: 2/5

This was the first time I had read the book, although I enjoyed the movie when I was younger. The book didn't incite nearly as much awe or intrigue as I was hoping for. I didn't love the little girl or the garden the way I expected to. I remember there being much more mystery and magic in the movie. Of course, I can't say any of this is the fault of the book. Its certainly possible that the movie wouldn't be able to stand up to the power of my memory if I were to watch it again.
