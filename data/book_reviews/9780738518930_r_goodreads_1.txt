Rating: 4/5

Max Grinnell is a writer and lecturer, known as the Urbanologist. This is one of his earlier works, which focuses on the Hyde Park community in Chicago, Illinois. 

Hyde Park is best known as the neighborhood, housing the prestigious University of Chicago, which Mr. Grinnell is an alum of, as well as the Museum of Science and Industry, and was the site of the Columbian Exposition of 1893, and is an interesting subject. Those who have read Erik Larson's Devil in the White City will be particularly interested in the photographs and maps that show the reader what Hyde Park looked like in the years following the events described by Larson. 

In Hyde Park, Mr. Grinnell shares his obvious deep knowledge of the Hyde Park community offering the reader a good overview of the area's history. For those of you familiar with Mr. Grinnell's lectures and other works, this does not show his witty and quirky nature, nor would it be appropriate for a the Images of America series, that being said this is a wonderful book for those with an inkling for history and city development.
