Rating: 2/5

This one just didn't hold my interest. There was a lot of repetition, with the characters discussing the same events several times, which got a bit annoying. As for the actual mystery, it turned out to be the most boring murder ever.  No one killed him. He fell down and hit his head. I know, exciting stuff.

I will grant this book one positive nod--Brother Cadfael's a sympathetic character. I know this is a popular series. Maybe the other books are better.
