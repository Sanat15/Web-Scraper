Rating: 5/5

I read this book for the first time when I was about 15/16, and for one so sheltered, the rawness of its stories and the way they twined together resonated with me. I didn't know the name of the book then, since the first pages were torn off, and I had this mistaken impression that it was written by Victoria Holt. I'm so glad I finally found it, even though it seems somewhat anti-climatic. 
I love this book. I love the characters, laughed with them in their triumphs and cried when they lost or died. For me, the book wasn't about the Diamond Waterfall, it was about three generations of women and their search for a deep love which always seemed to elude them.
