Rating: 3/5

I'd like to give this Four Stars because—even as Tessier's first novel—it's cleanly and economically written, a solid work of characterising the town and its inhabitants, who are all complacent and/or stymied by life when the inexplicable enters the equation, in the weird nature of Ballard or Wyndham cosy catastrophe. The slow reaction and inaction leads inevitably to an open ending that may infuriate, but probably worked better in the societal and environmental upheaval of its time.
