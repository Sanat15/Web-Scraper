Rating: 3/5

I recommend this small poetry book if you are new to love poems. It's a nice book to read before going to bed or bring with you on the train. I have read poems from the famous Shakespeare and Dickinson before, so I felt like I was introduced to a lot poems that I have read in the past. However, I was pleasantly surprised to read the poems of Christina Rossetti and William Butler Yeats which I had never read previously. Overall, some of the poems and the language used are somewhat dated and very old-fashion, which makes some of the poems hard to understand. I will say I do admire this book for what it represents.
