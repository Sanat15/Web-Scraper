Rating: 3/5

Due to the editor's wish to present the original work in its entirety as written, it was a bit of a disjointed read to move from the text to the footnotes, thus the 3. Had it been the original text only, it would likely have been a 4. The text was quite interesting, filled with comparisons that still ring true all these years later. Although I did not find this enjoyable, I did find it to enlightening on the topics I had forgotten from World History all those years ago in high school and Western Civ in early college. If nothing else, it demonstrates that things do change, but they also stay the same with different players in different roles. The relevance of the text today surprised me.
