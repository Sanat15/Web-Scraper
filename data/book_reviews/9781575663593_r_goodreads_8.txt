Rating: 3/5

Darker-toned mystery than usual for P. I. Savannah Reid. She's assisting her former partner Dirk Coulter in his attempt to apprehend a serial rapist who disguises himself as Santa Claus.
The storyline involving Savannah's sister Vidalia and her red-neck, low-class family seemed jarring and out of place in a book featuring such serious subject matters as serial rape and cop-killing - totally inappropriate.

I think I'll take a break from this series.
