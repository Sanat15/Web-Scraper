Rating: 3/5

Marla investigating the murders in the first two books is almost understandable - one occurred in her salon and in book two the fundraiser she volunteers to help her cousin organize is being sabotaged.
This time, though, there is no reason other than she is a nosy, meddling woman.
At least I got a much clearer picture of Marla - 34 years old, 5' 6" and 120 pounds.
Det. Dalton Vail still comes off as a very wooden character.
