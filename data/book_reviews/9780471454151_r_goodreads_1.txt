Rating: 4/5

This book is apparently a "best seller" and it does a good job of explaining nutrition, food and the rationale for a low-carb diet. It is also packed with recipes for their 30-day "solution". So in this sense, it is self-contained. 

I don’t believe that anyone would be harmed by following their 30-day diet, but of course "check with your doctor first". I found some of their claims a little far-fetched, that's because they are selling a book. For example, "eating a potato is the metabolic equivalent of eating a quarter of a cup of sugar". That's nonsensical and crafted to suit their own ends.

There are useful quizzes and plenty of insight which can help you assess how your current diet is serving you.
