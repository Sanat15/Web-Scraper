Rating: 4/5

A collection of short stories/novellas that won a Nebula award in 1969 and it is still as good now as it was 56 years ago. I liked them. They are all different, some of them of course better than others, but generally the overall standard is very high. There are even some unexpected twists at the end of some of the stories. For the length of the story the characters are well written and formed and the story lines suitable for their short length.
