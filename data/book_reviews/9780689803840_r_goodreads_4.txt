1651, Salem, Massachusetts. 12 year old Sarah didn't mean to laugh and play in the snow with her friend Elizabeth. The punishment for such behavior on the Sabbath is harsh. When one of the villagers mistakenly identifies Elizabeth and her brother Roger (Roger had lent Sarah his coat to wear on the walk home) as the Sabbath breakers. Sarah faces a difficult choice. Should she tell the truth or let Roger take the blame?
