Rating: 5/5

If you like books with suspenseful cliff-hangers this is definitely the book for you! It all starts when Katie Osborne moves to a new town. On her first day she meets a girl named Shannon Lindsrtom when they begin to become friends Shannon asked for Katie to go shopping later that day with her. It all falls apart when Katie gets stuck babysitting her little brother. When Katie goes over to Shannon's to tell her she cant go shopping some guy answers the door.. Katie does remember Shannon talking about her older brother who is about 10 years of age apart. But when Katie gets told Shannon went to piano lessons now she is definitely confused. Katie hands the guy her name, number and address. The next day when Katie goes into school she hears the horrible news of Shannon's disappearance.. On Katie's way home from school she hears an approaching green car who seems to have turns just to hit Katie. Now with Katie in the hospital with a broken leg. On the other hand, Shannon is in the middle of nowhere in a barn trying to think of an escape plan. Finally she comes up with one.. Does it work or not? Now with Katie at home alone with a broken leg does Shannon's kidnapper come for Katie or not? Find out when you read this story in which you can't put the book down!
