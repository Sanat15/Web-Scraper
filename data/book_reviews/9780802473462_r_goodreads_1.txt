Rating: 4/5

From my own personal library. I sure did take a lot of notes in the back of the book. Contains some very practical advice for parents and adults in whatever stage of life you are in primarily drawn from the book of Proverbs as well as other parts of the Bible. For example, he lists the nine values in life worth more than money (wisdom, righteousness, etc.). One note reads this: "The internal values of a Dad and Mom (not just what we say but who we are) are the most powerful human forces shaping our kid's commitments. He goes on to say that our kids must see that our values are worth standing up for in public. Finally, God wants us to base our lives on these 3 commitments to Him: reverence, trust and intimacy. All good stuff that is timeless in application to our lives.
