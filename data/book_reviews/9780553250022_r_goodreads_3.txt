Rating: 4/5

“Who Rides With Wyatt” is a well-written, mildly dated historical novel concerning Wyatt Earp, Johnny Ringo and Tombstone, Arizona, in the days of the silver rush. Author Will Henry blends fiction with history to create a very plausible picture of time and place. 

It’s important to remember that Henry wrote his novel before historians had thoroughly researched Wyatt Earp, the Clantons, Johnny Behan, John Clum and others. Oral history is the basis for the story. There’s a certain amount of conjecture and speculation that fills in the blanks. 

The novel was published in 1955 but it is still very readable, entertaining and enjoyable. Reading it made me want to saddle up and drift... West!
