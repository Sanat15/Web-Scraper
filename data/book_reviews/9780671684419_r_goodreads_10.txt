Rating: 3/5

Along time ago in a galaxy far far away, I became a romance fan by reading Janet Dailey. Some of her books stayed with me over the years, and this is one of them. Romance novels gave changed over the years, but this one held up surprisingly well. I stumbled on to an old box of books and several of her titles were there packed away and forgotten. I plan on going on a nostalgia binge read soon.
