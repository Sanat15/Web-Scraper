Rating: 4/5

I only read this because I wanted to know more about these two after listening to the musical NAPOLEON with Jerome Pradon. The book held my interest, which is saying a lot, because I'm not really a history buff, per se. Of course, this book was primarily about the relationship between Napoleon and Josephine, and not about Napoleon's military stuff (the military stuff was talked about when relevant, but not in great detail). I was very fascinated by the letters and quotes from memoirs of people who knew them, as well as from Napoleon and Josephine themselves.
