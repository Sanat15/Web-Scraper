Rating: 2/5

I picked this up at a thrift store because I thought the cover looked pulpy and rad, and used it as a bathroom reader. It served that purpose reasonably well, I suppose.

It's full of "strange but true" accounts of bizarre happenings, with no sources. They're often written like 1-2 page fiction stories, usually are based on one individual's testimony, and I assume they are generally either highly exaggerated or complete nonsense. That's not the case for everything, though; as some pages are dedicated to famous legends like the Loch Ness Monster, while others seem like actual documented accounts of unexplained events (rather than just one person making a bold claim), and a couple are just basic fact sheets on things like electric eels or killer bees. Each page is accompanied by a crude-yet-kinda-charming illustration.
