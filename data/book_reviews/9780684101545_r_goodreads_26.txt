3.5 ⭐️

This was my classic read for March.

What can I say that hasn’t already been said? It was a solid novella. I didn’t hate it, but I didn’t love it either. Now that I’ve finished it, I do wonder if it’s one I might appreciate more on a reread.

A few things are certain. The characters were very unlikable. I couldn’t stand Daisy, and I’m not convinced Gatsby was truly in love with her so much as what she represented. 

At its core, this is a story about chasing the American Dream and the illusion of it. Overall, it’s a sad, bleak story that really drives home the idea that money can’t buy happiness.
