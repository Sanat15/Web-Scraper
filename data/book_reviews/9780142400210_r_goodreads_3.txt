Rating: 3/5

The second book was definitely a little stranger than the first, and it seemed like mostly because there wasn't a super strong throughline for the plot. Is it supposed to be a book about the quest for the blue lily? About the history and politics of the Black Empire? About Dragon's Downfall and its strange statue? it's a little bit of all of that, and so never really feels like it has a true climax or resolution like the first book did. We get to see our favorite characters again, and meet some new ones that seem like they will continue to be important going forward, but that's really it. That being said, one of the things I really appreciate about the series is its ability to deal with big adult topics (like sovereignty and nature vs nurture) in very kid-friendly and appropriate ways. We will see what the future books bring!
