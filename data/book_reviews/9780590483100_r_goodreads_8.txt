Rating: 3/5

I needed something mindless and distracting to read, so what better to time to rejoin my favorite anachronistic teens? 

Let me tell you, Top Chef has NOTHING on Mrs. Goode’s Cookware Battle of the Bakers. The rules of this competition make very little sense, which is perfect because it’s judged purely on vibes and sex. Claudia and Mary Anne are nearly driven to insanity by the task of finding and following recipes. And the contest is plagued with sabotage as extreme as fire and Claudia being accused of poisoning her rivals with insecticide. Welcome back to the BSC indeed! 

All the adults in Stoneybrook abandon their lives and careers to participate in their own version of the competition, so there’s of course a massively boring B-plot, where Kristy turns a basic daycare gig into a capitalist youth camp. 

The conclusion to the mystery is pretty much what you’d expect. I was personally rooting for one of the troubled non-Stoneybrook children of divorce to be the saboteur.
