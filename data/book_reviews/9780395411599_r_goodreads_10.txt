Re-read for Retro Reads book pool, May 2026

It had been quite a while since I last reread this one and I had forgotten vast swatches of the plot. However, I had not forgotten just how much I enjoyed it. This is the best kind of historical fiction--character driven, with small, relatively unimportant people caught up in tumultuous times. My original 5 star rating stands. It was so nice to get to know Charis all over again.

Quickie review from 2009 
Marvelous historical fiction romance set in the days of the Byzantine/Roman Empire. Our heroine wants to be a doctor and disguises herself as a eunuch in order to accomplish this. Her disguise holds until she is on the frontier and has to interact with the barbarians. My favorite of Bradshaw's.
