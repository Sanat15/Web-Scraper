Rating: 5/5

Young Kerry O'Connell is the only survivor of a shipwreck in the Caribbean that also killed her parents. She's taken in and raised by a resident of St. Dennis where her wild ways get her into trouble. Privateer Nick Lawton discovers Kerry on deserted shore and claims her as his prize, sets out to tame her. Dragging Kerry along as he chases more fortune, his ship crisscrosses the Caribbean and later, Ireland. Along the way, they discover the meaning of love.

I really enjoyed this book.
