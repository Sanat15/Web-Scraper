Rating: 4/5

A fine selection of SF very short stories. Most collections of this general type, a themed group, are usually very mixed bag with me. In this book there was only one story I didn't care for, the one by Theodore Sturgeon, who has always been a hit or miss author for me. Definitely recommended. Also a bit of dumb luck, one of the stories was one I'd been trying to remember who wrote for a while.
