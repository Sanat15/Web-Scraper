Rating: 4/5

In my opinion, this is one of Jean Fritz's more charming biographies. There is something quintessentially appealing about preferring to walk when it serves better than riding, over all entreaties and implorations.  Yet even more beguiling is the line of reasoning that finally gets him on a horse, especially coming on the heels of so many more persuasive arguments.  My daughter and I particularly enjoyed the illustrations by Trina Schart Hyman. They manage to feel both historically accurate and whimsically casual, an interesting visual contradiction.
