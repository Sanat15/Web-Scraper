Rating: 2/5

Golden Fires is the second book in a California based trilogy and continues the story of the Coulter family begun in . The year is 1856 and the story is centered around Samantha (Sam) Coulter and the new Federal Marshal Clint Rawlins, who Sam hates from the get go. The Coulters fared well during the early days of the Gold Rush and left Sam and brother Lucas very well off. Sam still works at running the family laundry business, and she's also the major shareholder in the local water company - a business that the ebil baddies want to control for their very own. The head evil baddie even sends one of his flunkies to sweet talk Sam into marrying him so they can control those shares.

*Yawn*

This storyline got old very fast for me. I didn't care much for either Sam or Clint, and really didn't care if they got together or not. No chemistry here. Zip. Nada. The baddies were just too black, let alone all those snarls and curling lips to remind us how bad they were. I'm not quite sure what type of reader the publisher had in mind for this book, as the romance (such as it was) really didn't happen until the very end. Despite what the cover might imply, I wouldn't call this a bodice ripper and there isn't enough solid historical detail to satisfy historical readers like myself. Oh, and do be warned - there are events and language in this book that is most definitely not PC, to the point that I won't even quote or go into greater detail. *shudders* Passion's Trail
