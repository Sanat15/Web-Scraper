4.5 Stars.

This was a "Library $2 Bag Sale find" and I picked it up to read this weekend. An interesting concept when residents of St Paul, MN gathered at a dozen public libraries sharing memories with 4 authors who collaborated to put together mostly true stories into mostly fictional ones. It truly does capture the soul of the city. Authors notes mention that 500 people were involved in this project. Short stories I think anyone would appreciate.
