Rating: 4/5

Amazing! It’s a beautiful testimony to the power of God’s Word to bring Salvation those who are willing to listen. There is nothing more convicting and powerful than the Prophecies in the Old Testament when paired with the fulfillments in the New Testament. 
 This is a very good collection of personal testimonies. Everyone has a different story. There are stories of atheist, agnostic, deist, Orthodox, and Reformed Jews. It covers the full spectrum, but Christ’s love reaches them all. 
 At the very end is a section that explains the most pointed Messianic Prophecies, and answers the charge of Anti-Semitism in the New Testament.
