I've been keeping an eye out for a copy of this book for years. When I stumbled across one in a used bookstore downtown, I was surprised how skinny the book was. The hyperbole used on the front cover stating that the book contains 'all the inside jokes, gags, pranks, secret messages, cameos, code words, double meanings and other hidden references' is a bit of an overstatement and led me to expect a much thicker volume. I bought the book anyway, and while it did have some interesting entries, it is more of an overview of the subject than an in depth study. The book also reprinted some well known stories, like the origins of the character names for Sesame Street's Bert and Ernie, that The Urban Legends Reference Pages have argued over the authenticity of.
Did I like it? It was ok.
Would I reread it? Probably not.
Would I recommend it? If you can borrow this book for free it's worth a look. If you have to pay more than 50 cents for it don't bother.
