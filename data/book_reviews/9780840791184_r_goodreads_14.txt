Rating: 5/5

If I knew how good this book was it probably wouldn’t have sat on my shelf for years before I picked it up. Thank you Maria for the original recommendation. Elisabeth Elliot has a way of writing that is simple yet profound. “It is this simple matter of seeing loneliness as a gift—to be received, and to be offered back to God for His use”. God has never promised His people a life on this earth without suffering-in fact it’s a promise of a life of sanctification-of walking through a refining fire. And a promise of eternal rest in Him. It is what we do with that suffering, with that loneliness that matters. We offer it back to Him for Him to use it for His glory &amp; the furthering of His kingdom. May my response to His work in my life be that of Elisabeth’s-that of surrender, be that of George Matheson’s-in the words of his song O love that wilt not let me go. This book was full of reminders of how God works through the broken, that strength is only found in Him.
