Rating: 4/5

Madaline loved Dominic with all her heart but she was way too young for the marriage he wanted. There was a fight and a huge blow up involved. Too many people were witnesses to the ending. She was devastated and left for several years. Now she is back for her step sisters wedding. Will there be any healing? Will there be forgiveness? There was so much pride that was hurt back then. Does Dominic still want revenge? I have read this book several times and still feel the angst that she felt. Have read several books by Michelle Reid and will do so again.
