Rating: 5/5

To be fair, I haven’t read this book from cover to cover but I’ve now read a good chunk from all the sections and I always find myself coming back to this book for quick research. The best basic guide to film with plenty of historical background, explanations of terms and techniques and analysis examples. I have the twelfth edition and I’m surprised by how up to date it is. Everything is written in a digestible way with lots and lots of pictures to illustrate what is being explained to you. Simply a must have for film students and enthusiasts.
