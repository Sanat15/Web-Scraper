Rating: 4/5

A new undead threat seems to be lurking around Sunnydale, targeting corpses instead of live prey, making for a tricky mystery to try and solve. Meanwhile, a foe from Buffy's past that just doesn't seem to quit is leaving a trail of bodies behind her as she seeks to restore her looks... Featuring two separate story tracks that are woven together, Watson does a great job here in delivering a good Buffy adventure with all the necessary quips, teenage drama, and social commentary. The horror aspect is also ever present, with plenty of blood, death and violence, and all of this is wrapped up in some great artwork courtesy of team Bennet and Ketcham. A short, enjoyable read that leaves you looking forward to the next collection.
