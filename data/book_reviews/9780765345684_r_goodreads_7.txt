Rating: 3/5

Once again it's enjoyable to read about Tanaquil and her familiar, the peeve, but this time it feels as if the whole story is unnecessary. The world Tanaquil finds herself in is just too silly and the fact she can do anything makes the whole plot null and void. It's as if Lee threw all the stray fantasy ideas she didn't know what to do with into one story. The whole point is that Tanaquil must come to the decision to go in search of Honj, but surely she could have done that at the end of the previous book? To postpone the happily ever after isn't really a wise decision as this story doesn't contribute anything to the tale. It's also a mistake for the chemistry between Tanaquil and Honj to be mostly absent. But the ending where they find each other again, the peeve and his mate and Jaive and Worabex make up for the silliness in the middle section.
