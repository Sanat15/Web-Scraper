Rating: 1/5

If I could give this book 0 stars, I would. 

During a hectic shopping trip with my five year old, she unfortunately picked this book up and begged me for it. I barely took a second to analyse the cover or title before throwing it on my basket. 

It wasn’t until we got home that I realised what this dreadful book was about.

I refused to read it to my daughter and made up my own nicer version instead about a family of elephants enjoying their cake.

Incredibly toxic and dangerous to have such a book sold to children.
