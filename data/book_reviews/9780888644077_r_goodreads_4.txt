Rating: 4/5

When I was reading this collection of essays I often had the feeling that Margaret Laurence was chatting with me. Although sometimes the conversation felt a little flat--when she was talking about her first experiences travelling by air--most of the stories were informative, thought-provoking and, although dated, relevant to my life experiences. Whether she is writing about small-town life in Ontario or Manitoba, her first experience travelling as a tourist, or the life of a Somali poet and independence-fighter, Laurence demonstrates her profound understanding of, and sympathy for humanity.
