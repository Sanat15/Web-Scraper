Rating: 2/5

I mean, this wasn't a bad book, though I can't see myself wanting to re-read it. It took me about... less than an hour to finish it. I think this is the kind of book I'd have liked a couple of years ago, as I probably could've related to it more than now, when the childishness and oblivion of the characters was annoying at times (though completely appropriate to the story--). The plot twist at the end was great, however! Have to admit I didn't see it coming (the one with her father--the other one was obvious).
