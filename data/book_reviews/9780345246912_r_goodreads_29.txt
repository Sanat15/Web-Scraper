Rating: 5/5

From 1968 comes a rather interesting look at just how Star Trek not only got onto the airwaves, but also how it survived. Stephen Whitfield's novel is one not only just for the fans of this series but also for anyone who is interested in seeing just what goes into putting a television show onto the airwaves. From start to finish we the reader see how the various pilots &amp; characters evolved from the mind of Roddenberry to the finished product. Overall, a fun read &amp; a treasure for any fan of this beloved series.
