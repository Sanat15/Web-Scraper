Rating: 5/5

There are many stories written about that day in Tombstone, Arizona. This is one of those stories and it is a good one. The story is actually an interview with an old man in a retirement home about the events leading to the Gunfight at the O.K. Corral. Now you may find that this story doesn't match the facts, but that is what makes it interesting. How true was the story teller in telling his tale and how does he know the small details. Well you just have to read it to find the true of the story. So do you ride with Wyatt?
