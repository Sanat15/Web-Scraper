Rating: 3/5

I was fascinated by a journey like this - very uncomercial, extremely challenging and deep into wildest part of the earth. And I am amazed how they managed to walk it finish the journey. That being said I felt like it focused more on the constant repetition on the puller / follower roles (at the end of the book I understand why Messner felt the need to keep on repeating that he was the one in front, the on with heavier load and greater motivation). Even if he seemed annoyed with this conflict he felt way too much time writing about it. Also the story seemed to repeat the same thought quite a lot. And I missed the promised 'activist' position, then just fell to the background even if at the intro he stated that this was one of the main PR messages.
