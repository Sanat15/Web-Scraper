Rating: 2/5

I would give this book two and a half stars.

 The Stonewalkers is a pleasant surprise. Vivien Alcock has an original style; the story doesn't unfold in any of the ways I expected, so at crucial junctures of the plot I had little or no idea what might happen. Would the author allow a kid to be mashed by a gigantic statue? Might Poppy Brown, the lead protagonist, not survive to the end?

 On top of this, The Stonewalkers is superbly funny, with a touch of darkness to keep the humor from becoming stale or predictable. Substantial family issues and dynamics are also explored with a good level of insight.

 I have heard Vivien Alcock's writing described as offbeat, and it certainly is that. To me, The Stonewalkers is a distinctly British creation, and one I definitely enjoyed.
