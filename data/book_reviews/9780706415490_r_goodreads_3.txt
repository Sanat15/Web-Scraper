Rating: 1/5

EDIT: The editor was actually Janet Sachs, not Reginald Gray. He was just the illustrator.

This was a product tie-in to the short lived television series, The New Adventures of Black Beauty. If I remember correctly, there is a chapter from Black Beauty In it. One of the more depressing chapters.

A more accurate title for the book would've been Horses and Ponies Die All Over the Place. Just how depressing did this get? There's a Guy de Maupassant story in it, "On Horseback". And that was one of the few stories where the horse or pony doesn't die.

There's also a chapter from Anna Karenina which foreshadows what the rider will do to the title character.

Another downer called "Silver Blue" about a very unusually colored pony stallion. 

It's also annoying that most of the selections were not complete short stories in their own right. They were chapters from books. There are some "up" chapters from Fly-By-Night, Gulliver's Travels and International Velvet, but they are few and far between.

The illustrations are, quite frankly, fugly.

I last read my copy sometime in the 1990s. Although there are many lost books from my youth that I miss, this is not one of them.
