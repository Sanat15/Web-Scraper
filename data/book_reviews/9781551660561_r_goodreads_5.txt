Rating: 4/5

“Cattlemans Choice” is the story of Mandelyn and Carson.
Two neighbors live on ranches side by side- polar opposites. While Carson is wild and free, Mandelyn is prim and proper. But when Carson gets into brawls, only Mandelyn calms him..
Soon Carson employs Mandelyn to teach him how to be a gentleman, to woo someone he greatly cares for, and our story begins..
A non typical DP book with all her classic elements
-hairy cowboys 
-sweet innocent heroine
-a past which makes one of them wary
-hidden attraction
-sweet kisses
-jealousy involving other people
But mixed with this we had the sweet plot of a hero head over heels over the heroine, a heroine who realizes her feelings and a sweet reverse seduction with surprising ending. Also glad minor characters got their HEA!
Safe
3.5/5
