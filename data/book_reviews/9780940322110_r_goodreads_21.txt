Rating: 1/5

I've had this book on my TBR list for eons and kept forgetting it because it was at the end. This book takes place before spaying and neutering, proper education and responsibility of pets wasn't as common as today (the author passed away in 1967), which I did take into consideration. But my low rating (too bad there's not an X rating for XtraAwful) is because this guy is just too, too obsessed with little Tulip's pooping (and NEVER picking up after her and gloating when she leaves her business at the door of a business, a grocery!) and so preoccupied with her having sex that it's almost a perversion. My heart ached for the little dog. All I wanted to do when I finished the book was throttle him for being such an irresponsible creepy individual. 😡
