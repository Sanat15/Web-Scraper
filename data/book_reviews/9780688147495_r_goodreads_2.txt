Rating: 3/5

This concept book describes big and little objects and is appropriate for kindergarteners. There are pictures of a big foot/little foot, big dog/little dog, big hat/little hat, big truck/little truck, etc. This book includes objects that would be of interest to both girls and boys.

Activities
1. My first activity would help to reinforce the concept of big and little. I would have the students sitting in a circle and after reading the book, I would allow several students at a time to walk around the room and find something that is big and little. If the students are struggling, I would give them clues such as “we cut with these.” The student could then go get the teacher’s pair of scissors and their own scissors.
2. I would have each student make a page to form a classroom book titled, “Big and Little.” At the top of the page, the student will write Big________, little__________ and then they will draw a picture of their big and little objects.
