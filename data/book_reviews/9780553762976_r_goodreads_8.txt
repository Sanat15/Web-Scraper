Rating: 3/5

Amy Denovo meets Archie at Lily Rowan's, where she is helping to gather material for a book on Lily's father. She instinctively trusts him, and knowing of his association with Nero Wolfe, wants his help to find out who her father is. Amy suspects that her mother 'started over' at some point, taking on the name of Elinor Denovo. But they were never very close and she can't ask for more information because her mother is now dead (hit-and-run). The only lead is the $1000 checks that Elinor received every month beginning shortly after Amy was born. It's a complicated case where Wolfe, Archie, and all the usual helpers spend lots of time and lots of the client's money to track down leads. Maybe not the most interesting plot, but for me it's always the interactions between the familiar and beloved characters that makes a Nero Wolfe story worth reading.
