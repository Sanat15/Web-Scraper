Rating: 2/5

The first two stories started incredibly strong: they were both incredible sci-fi that deserved entire books. However, every story after that is a steady drive downhill to the point that it frankly becomes hard to follow and even to finish by the last story. The titular event of the collection doesn't even get a story! It's simply referenced briefly in the second story as something that happened offscreen, then the narrative goes through a timeskip and it's never brought up again. The first collection in this series intrigued me, but this one killed that interest.
