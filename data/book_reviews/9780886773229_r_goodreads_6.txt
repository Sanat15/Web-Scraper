"Thee will not appoint thyself my conscience, Nhi Vanye. Thee is not qualified. And thee is not entitled."First of all, "thee" is an accusative "you," i.e., "I will tell thee." The subject pronoun is "thou," i.e., "I know thou art a man of honor; I'm so sorry to bother thee at home." This should read as follows:"Thou wilt not appoint thyself my conscience, Nhi Vanye. Thou art not qualified. And thou art not entitled."There we go! All better.
