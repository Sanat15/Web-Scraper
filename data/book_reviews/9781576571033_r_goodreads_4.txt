Rating: 3/5

5/10

Lost In Dreamland is a book that I have been wanting to read for a very long time, and it was alright. The book starts off pretty strong but fails to be a consistently good book. Dreamland was an amazing setting but M. D. Spenser did not expand on it at all, which is disappointing as it had a lot of potential. The second half of the book feels pretty repetitive too and drags on a little bit. Despite being flawed I actually really enjoyed reading it, hence why I gave it three stars. Going into this read I was expecting a story similar to One Day At Horrorland however it was significantly different which I respected.
