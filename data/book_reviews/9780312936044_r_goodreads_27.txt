Rating: 5/5

This installment it The Grand Tour series was very good, took a while to build but it eventually took off. Considering the political times we are going through in 2026, it is an interesting take on Russia/ The Soviet Union’s effect on the world post Cold War. Anyway, the plot pulls you in. The characters are likable. The science fiction part of the story is like an action adventure novel from 40 years ago which is about how old the book is. I’m looking forward to the next installment in the series (Empire Builders).
