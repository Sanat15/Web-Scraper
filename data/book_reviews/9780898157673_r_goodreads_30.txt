Rating: 4/5

Interesting research on maximizing production from farming with a long-range view: It is not about what the garden will yield this season but how sustainable it is over time. Much thought is given to composting, to recover nutrients that go into growing, and minimizing fertilization from outside that circular system. The focus is therefore on the soil. 

This book was a bit beyond me, a newbie to backyard gardening, and I would imagine most casual gardeners would feel similar. The techniques are for serious to professional growers, but that does not mean the discussion is not interesting and inspirational. Detailed charts are given for space usage and density, etc.
