Rating: 5/5

"The Scent of Roses" is an engrossing romance/mystery/paranormal/historical book. Very hard to categorize in a single genre. And that's the way I like it!

Rosalyn Delaney's husband, Josiah, has disappeared. Their marriage is a marriage of convenience to save Rosalyn from the clutches of a radical Mormon who wants to make Rosalyn his third wife. Rosalyn is fond of Josiah, and so she tracks him down in Arizona, only to find he had thought her dead and had remarried...and has been murdered.

The suspects include Whip Kincaid, Josiah's friend and business partner; Lucinda, Josiah's new wife; Tony, Lucinda's brother; and according to Whip, Rosalyn herself. 

Naturally, Rosalyn and Whip find themselves reluctantly drawn together. The sexual tension is high, the scenes hot. Will Whip get lynched? Will Rosalyn give in to her feelings for Whip? Will the murder be solved?

Charlene Raddon has written just the kind of book I love most...full of subplots that twist and turn together, keeping me on the edge of my seat, wondering what's next. The mixture of genres blends together seamlessly to produce a cohesive, fascinating tale. The glimpse into Mormon culture adds an additional facet to the plot. If you like romance...mystery...paranormal...and historical stories, you'll love "The Scent of Roses." Pick it up today!
