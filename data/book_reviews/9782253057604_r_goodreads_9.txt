Rating: 3/5

i never would have picked this up on my own, but list videos have a way of sneaking books that you've never heard of onto your tbr.

this is the book that the beloved sapphic movie Carol is based on. and while i can't say that i adored my reading experience, i really did enjoy what this book was in theory. it's one of the first lesbian pulp novels to give its heroines a happy ending. it's also one of the first lesbian novels to have two femmes in love.

and it's a book that i know i'll be thinking of years later. the age gap, the frank conversations about sexuality, the sacrifice that Carol makes. definitely worth a read.
