Rating: 4/5

I just recently finished a non-fiction book about one of the most infamous shootouts of the old west titled The last Gunfight. So when I saw that this book here was a fictional account I thought I would give it a whirl. I really thought I would be placing this on my western shelf and have read myself a good old yarn of some rooting tooting and shooting. And how wrong I was. This author has done as much research as the author of the non-fiction account The fiction come in mainly as the conversation between the players of this high western drama. Definitely fits in on the historical fiction shelf and a good recommend for anyone looking for a short novel packed with the details of the shootout that occurred in a small alleyway next to a boardinghouse just ninety feet from the O.K. Corral.
