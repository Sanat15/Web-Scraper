Rating: 4/5

You could dogsled the Iditarod Trail or read this book, both take about the same amount of time and have their challenges, like endurance and either way, you’ll feel frozen. Another great trademark tome of historical fiction by James Michener, heavily based in history, with his own character’s stories to carry the tale along. 

He starts with tectonic plates and ends in contemporary times (1980s), from brutal Russian colonization to Gold Rushes, Aleutian whale hunts, Japanese invasions, Oil, and maybe more than you ever wanted to know about Salmon canneries. Always didactic, sometimes the lesson being taught is a little too obvious in his simple dialogs, ’Should we kill all the otters?’ No Johnny that would be bad wouldn’t it ?” 

We just completed a cruise to Alaska, visiting a few cities. Usually I do my reading before a trip, but I’m glad I got it in now, and I’m ready to return, head North, to Alaska.
