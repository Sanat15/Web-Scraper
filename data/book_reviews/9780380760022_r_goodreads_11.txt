Rating: 3/5

Years ago I read many Zelazny novels. He has written some of my favourite books - including Lord of Light and the Nine Princes Amber in particular and also Damnation Alley and Jack of Shadows. Also the links with Hawkwind. 

This was on my TBR shelf from a charity shop. It’s later than his “core” novels from the 60s and 70s. 

It took a little time to zone back in. In a sense it’s classic Zelazny matching future sci-fi with a mystical mythical world. We’re in the 22 Century as envisaged in 1983 - sort of Blade Runner with hover cars, matter transporters, and telepaths. 

The book is actually in two parts. In the first part we’re tracking an alien assassin after a long build up; then we’re are in world wide hunter/hunted chase all of the world. 

For a relatively short novella it took me a while to read. However the pace picks up in the last quarter with a world of shadows that is pretty Zelazny-esque. 

From a completist point of view I’m glad a read it, and sadly we will see no more from the great man. However definitely would not recommend as the first book to read for a new reader.
