Star Hatchling is a story that involves unrealistic and science fiction characters an settings with very realistic topics. Shelum, the main character one day finds a weird hatchling within her village. Her sister Cheko and Shelum have no choice but to investigate what this scary hatchling may be. They soon learn that this hatchling is an "other", a creature that looks just like a human but from another planet. Shem and Cheko, go on the mission to help return this hatchling to her home star.
my one critique for this book is that i found it to be slow in the progression of the plot. i felt as a reader i could have potentially lost interest because the pass was slower then i would have wished.
