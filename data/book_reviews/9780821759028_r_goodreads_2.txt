Rating: 3/5

Definitely a very cute, witty book. Sam hails from Ohio with her pet bobcat "Bob" in tow, and realizes that her aunt lives with a very handsome English lord. She opens up a country inn, which is funny and gives us all sorts of characters, and is determined to sabotage his impending marriage. My main issues is that the ending is rushed and we never find out what happens to her land in Ohio.
