Rating: 3/5

“We read a long, detailed story of stone age people who lived 9,000 years ago. We call these people "Aleuts" today, but that is a name given them by Russians in the 18th century; they call themselves Unangan and they still exist on the Aleutian Islands, if in much reduced numbers. If you're looking for a readable tale with a very well-researched background, this is it.” 
Goodreads reviewer Bob Newman
