Rating: 1/5

I thought this was going to be a Gremlins-esque tale of crazy babies with creepy eyes. Actually it was about mad scientists and creepy genetic engineering. Either way, it was pure trash. Can't say I hated it, though. I liked it in the way I like watching MST3K. Co-written by a doctor, it also has some oddly specific medical references, which I suppose is better than being inaccurate. Two thumbs.
