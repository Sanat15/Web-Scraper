Rating: 5/5

I never would have believed that a text book could deserve a five-star rating, but this book changed my mind. 

Over the past six or seven months I have been studying this book on a daily basis – just a few pages at a time. All the derivations of formulas and the extensive mathematical applications of theories, principles, and formulas (the things that make most physics textbooks so scary) are left out of this one. Instead, it explains, in mostly easy-to-understand language, the concepts of physics. And every principle of physics that I can imagine is covered – motion, gravity, electricity, magnetism, fluids, light, thermodynamics, radioactivity, fission, fusion, relativity, and much much more.

The book's purpose is to enlighten young minds and inspire the truly well-suited minds to want to learn more. In my opinion it does a great job.
