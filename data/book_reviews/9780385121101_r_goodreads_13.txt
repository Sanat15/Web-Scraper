Rating: 5/5

This, without doubt is my favourite Rendell novel. She rightly won the Gold Dagger Award in 1976 for this wonderful story.

It tells the life of Arthur Johnson, a psychopath who lives in a house of flats with other renters. Every so often, he has to strangle a mannequin hidden in the basement to feed his desire. Complications arise when a new person with a slightly similar name to the psychopath rents one of the flat rooms and the mannequin vanishes.

One of the most edge-of-seat page turners. Really creepy.
