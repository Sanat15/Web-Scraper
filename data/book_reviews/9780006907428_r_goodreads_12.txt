Rating: 3/5

Again, like Linton’s at the Chalet School, I’m not a fan of this book. It’s a strange addition and I wonder sometimes what it would have been like to read this as a full book with Linton not as two separate books. Book 12 in a reread for the first time since I went to Pertisau (don’t @ at me I started with Exile then went back to School at the Chalet) (book 11 in series). Joyce is unlikeable Thekla is a nightmare and the ending is nigh on ridiculous even for EBD.
