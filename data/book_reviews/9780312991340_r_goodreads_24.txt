Rating: 4/5

Jessie McQuade is a police officer in a small town and is called to the scene of a car accident where a woman was bitten by a wolf. Not 5 hours later, that same woman has attacked and killed another and is shot herself. Then the bodies disappear. And suddenly, evidence of a new strain of rabies is showing up. A strange federal officer, Mandanauer shows up and suddenly Jessie finds herself hunting werewolves in an attempt to stop the rise of a Werewolf God. And also involved is a local professor and naturalist, Will Cardotte. He's sexy, he's attracted to Jessie and he may be falling in love with her, but is he a werewolf?

I really enjoyed reading this story. I was engaged and hooked from the start. The mystery in finding out who the werwolves are and who the leader is just kept me going. I suspected everyone. So I had to be right about at least one of them. Will was super hot. I would have liked to know a bit more about him, maybe gotten a little bit more background about him or understand why he was as insecure as he was. Jessie was my kind of girl - sarcastic, a loner and tough as nails hiding insecurity. I wasn't too much a fan of the first person narrative, as it sometimes felt like a bit too much sarcasm at times. But the first person narrative is part of the reason this was such a great suspense/mystery - because we only knew what the heroine did. BTW, awesome love scenes!
