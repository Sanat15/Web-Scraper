Rating: 3/5

Long time newsman Douglas Kiker wrote three of these Mac McFarland mysteries back in the mid-80’s and I read then back then and enjoyed them. Recently thought about them and found copies of all three paperbacks, and have now read this 1st one. I enjoyed it just as much as before, maybe more, as it reminded me of our long-ago annual trips to Cape Cod.
Here McFarland blows into North Walpole (fictional, but to me it reads like Chatham, a lovely area of the Cape) after his marriage and his newspaperman career in Chicago fall apart. Cape Cod is more an escape rather than a destination, but it’s winter and he’s resigned to just waiting it out. But one evening when he lets out the dog Mou Mou (not a favorite, but rescued from his ex-wife’s plan to put the animal down) to do her “business” she alerts him to something wrong and when he goes out to look, the next door neighbor is lying dead on her patio. He calls the police, but his newsman instincts kick in, and we’re off!
May just go ahead and start the 2nd today - it was that much fun to visit Cape Cod again!
