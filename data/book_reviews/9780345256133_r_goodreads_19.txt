Rating: 4/5

A 4-star book I would recommend to any Georgette Heyer fan looking for more! Alice Chetwyn Ley has been a real find.

The main characters of the story are country mouse Alethea and her cousin, the town bred Lydia. Lydia's mother (Mrs Manbury) is the quintessential ambitious mother who wants a Duke for her beautiful daughter. For her niece Alethea, though, she believes a poet will do. 
The cousins do not get along at first. However they band together against Mrs Marbury's schemes. 

The characters are well-drawn and a pleasure to read about. They all behave consistently and the changes in a person as they fall is love is shown with a masterful hand. 
While there is a slight feel of reading a Heyer book, there are no characters or conversations drawn from her books. 

Historical accuracy is spot-on and so are the mannerisms and speech. There are small insights into well-known real people of the day (Hannah Moore, Frances Burney, Mrs Siddons etc.) that make the book even more delightful to read. 

Am extremely happy to know that this authors books are back and in e-book format. Go ahead and give it a go if clean well-written, well-researched historical romances are your thing.
