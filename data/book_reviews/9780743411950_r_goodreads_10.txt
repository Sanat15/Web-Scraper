Rating: 5/5

A beautifully worded story for a thoroughly undeserving subject - Willy has had a bit of everything in his life. Accused of murdering his wife (drunk, accident???), he serves his time and writes a book about it. Later, he’s in the USA, feted by those who wish to make a movie of his story, but there’s one thing the reader notices: Willy is not a very nice person. Estranged from his daughters, a terrible partner to his girlfriend, unmotivated to work, and scathing of everyone in his life, we’re taken on a roundabout journey to his past with the death of his mother, and interspersed with extracts from his late daughter’s diary, this is an engrossing and entertaining read.
