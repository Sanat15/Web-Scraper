Rating: 2/5

2.75..........I feel like I've been much more critical of books this year. Let me preface this review by saying I listened to the audio so I'm not sure if it had a negative effect or not. (Although I recently listened to another Koontz book and really liked it). As for this one, I don't know what it was but it did nothing for me. It's basically a story of a religious fanatic who sets out to kill a little boy because she thought him to be the anti-Christ. That alone made me think it would be a little scary........it wasn't. I also wasn't thrilled with the insta-love of these characters. The boy's mother hires a private detective to help her, they spend days on the run and he's already in love with her and wants to be a dad to the son? ---Anyways.......sad to say I was disappointed with this book but that won't stop me from reading more Koontz. I'm still a fan.
