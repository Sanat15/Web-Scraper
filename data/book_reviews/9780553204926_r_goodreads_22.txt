Rating: 1/5

I absolutely abhor this book. It's terrible, the only reason why I didn't abandoned it was because I hate not giving things a fair chance. 

SPOILERS:
Here are some of my notes while reading, they may not be objective but they are honest.
- For a huge portion of the book it was difficult for me to keep up with the large cast of characters. In a 159 page novel there are 19 characters that I can name off the top of my head, although I know there are many more than that. Out of that number 14 are important and are directly involved in the investigation.
- A chase scene that amounts to literally nothing but frustration lasting 3 pages.
- Tecumseh Fox (in my opinion) is an intensely unlikeable character, and I suspect it's due to the author's not so subtle misogyny.
- Chapters almost always stop on some sort of cliffhanger which reads like a serial and a bit too formulaic for my taste.
- In the later chapters Fox is overly violent with Ms. Garda Tusar, even though she is found to be an accomplice (unknowingly). It only distressed me not because I like her, I didn't enjoy a single character in this book, but because he was not violent with Diego who was also an accomplice nor the actual murderer. 
-Hebe Heath is just stupid, no literally Wolfe writes her as just a dumb woman who has no motivations or reasoning for any of her actions. Just a dumb international movie actress that does incriminating things cause she's stupid. Huge disappointment.
-Perry Dunham was a wildly dislikable character whose poor behavior, manipulation and gaslighting made him noble in the end. A sentiment that was mainly reserved for male characters in this novel. That and the benefit of the doubt.
- The conclusion was just as terrible. Fox somehow had more patience and restraint for a man who murdered 3 people, and attempted to kill Fox's own friend, but somehow had to physically assault a woman in her own home who happened to be an accomplice. A woman who was just worldly NOT a murder accomplice, simply put a mistress who was involved with a psychopath who killed a family friend, her brother, his son-in-law, and almost killed her admirer.

Sorry it was so long, and that wasn't even half of what made me upset. All in all I would never recommend this book to anyone it is poorly written, poorly paced, and most of all boring.
