Rating: 1/5

When his parents and people are murdered the hero strikes out on his own to avenge their deaths only to be caught and put in prison. 

The heroine has lost her father and brother and because a woman can't rule her people are threatened. She hatches a plan to take a prisoner who resembles her brother enough to pass him off to the elders to buy her time to think of another solution. Convinced the hero has neither his memories or his sight, the heroine brings him home. But the hero does remember who he is and regains his vision quickly enough but decides to let no one in on that fact. A lot was wrong with this book. 

First off, the hero and heroine are from completely different parts of the planet yet they speak the same language, have no different accents and fit in completely with each other’s customs? Secondly, despite the fact that each privately know they aren't related, they don't know that the other person knows. The guy gets a hard on in front of his 'sister' and the heroine seduces the man she's made to believe is her brother! I'm sorry but the whole thing was a little more than disturbing. I didn't like this book and will give away in a second.
