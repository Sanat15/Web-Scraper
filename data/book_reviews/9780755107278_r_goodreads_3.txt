Rating: 3/5

All you need to know - no Algy (still recovering from his ordeal in the Terai), no mention of Ginger (presumably nurse-maiding Algy in Mount Street), nothing resembling a decent plot, no cigarettes smoked until Ch 10, mention of the usual biscuits and tinned sardines (because Biggles is never properly equipped), a rhinoceros (briefly and - *spoilers* - tragically), the local authorities are helpful and the villain brutal, a reasonable amount of flying, the unavoidable info dumps, and a great setting (the Kalahari). I read it for the setting and because I'm a Biggles completist. I wouldn't say that late Biggles isn't worth reading (Biggles and the Plot That Failed from 1965 is enormously entertaining) but Johns made little effort with this one.
