Rating: 5/5

Don’t read this book unless you want to cry, feel pure, unadulterated rage about the injustice of the world, have a huge wake-up call, feel deep sadness and grief, learn more than you thought you could, and cry some more. That being said: everyone should read this book. It is essential knowledge and an honest, firsthand account of the AIDS epidemic in Africa. I never would have came across this book if it hadn’t been for my biology class about disease in modern society, and it has completely changed my worldview (I hope my professor liked my “tear-counts” in the notes I took). 
Seriously, I want to scream the name of this book from the mountaintops. It is a call for change. A call for action. If you are thinking about reading this book, just do it.
