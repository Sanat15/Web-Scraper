Rating: 3/5

Picked up a good tip or two from this book but most of it was pretty common sense in my opinion.

Would’ve appreciated some more detail on train logistics. It seems that some things have changed in the internet age. Gone are the days of buying tickets to fast trains on your scheduled day of travel. Most Barcelona —&gt; Paris trains were sold out 3+ months in advance… The remaining slots were limited and $400+ each regardless of whether or not you had a Eurail pass.

If you have the time to travel indirectly by train it may make sense, otherwise air travel has become superior in a lot of cases. A new section on the scams run by RyanAir and other budget airlines could be helpful as well since those are becoming more and more clever.
