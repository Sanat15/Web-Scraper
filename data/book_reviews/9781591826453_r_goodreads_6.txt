Rating: 4/5

4/5 Ai Yori Aoshi and Love Hina were my introduction to harem anime back in the early 2000s. While more romantic and less slapstick in scope than Love Hina it really is a male driven fantasy series. A young woman “Aoi” shows up out of Kauru’s (protagonist) past and says she’s his wife and promptly moves in. 

It’s a cute story and the art is great. The plot itself is a little weird from a modern American perspective but it’s a bit about a young couple bucking their traditional rich Japanese upbringing and pursuing love. 

The harem hasn’t really formed yet as only the 2 leads are introduced this time around but it’s coming.

I don’t remember how much of the series I’ve read back in the day but I know I never finished it. So I figured I’d give it another go. It’s cute and heart warming with a bit of fan-service which is right down my alley.

Our heroine Aoi Sakuraba on the cover though I’m pretty sure she’s prominent on all of them.
