Rating: 3/5

Really ⭐⭐1/2, but the final twist bumps it to 3 for GR. Once again, Foster jumps forward in time as the war between the Weave and the Amplitur continues and introduces an entirely new set of characters. Our main protagonist, Lalelelang, is a Wais scholar; the Wais being the most 'civilized' race of the Weave (and represented on the cover as a white chicken or something with blue eyes). Most Wais become catatonic when faced with any violence but Lalelelang has her heart set on constructing the history of humans and alien races; as such, she needs to study humans! While the war still rages, Lalelelang heads out the front line and eventually meets Colonel Straat-ien, a human but the offspring of the humans the Amplitur tampered with.

Now, an unforeseen result of the Amplitur's tampering gives these humans the ability to 'push' others (although not humans) to do certain things, just like the Amplitur offer 'suggestions' to their client races. These affected humans call themselves the "Core" and keep a low profile. 

Lalelelang, during her studies, formulated a hypothesis that when the war is over, humans will not be able to stop their war-like ways. The entire story centers on this hypothesis, albeit Foster takes his time putting it forth. What will humans do with the titular Spoils of War? Decent ending, but plodding pacing made this seem more like a chore to finish than a really good read.
