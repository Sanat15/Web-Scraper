Rating: 3/5

⭐️Plot: 
Fever Devilin has decided to go home to Appalachia when he starts having problems as a college professor. He declares that he is retiring early. Well, retirement does not start out very relaxing when he arrives at his childhood home to find a dead body on his porch and someone shooting at him from the woods. 
⭐️ My thoughts: 
I really liked Booked for Murder. As I was reading it I wondered if PJ Nelson wasn’t maybe a nom de plume for a better known author. Maybe they were writing something completely different and didn’t want prejudgment from readers. Well, I was right. The author is a well known writer and academic by the name of Phillip DePoy. 
Being the curious person I am I decided to try one of the (many! a prolific writer!) books this author had written previously. I settled on the first book of the Fever Devilin. series. This book was bad. It was wordy for the sake of being pompous. Really a turn off. There were some beautiful descriptions. I could see the things I had liked in Booked for Murder ~ I just could not get past the literally and academic showing off. It seems like many authors are trying their hands at cozy murders lately. They are popular, for sure, but deceptively hard to write. The formula seems easy but I can tell you that few authors actually get it right. I’ll give DePoy credit that he seems to have nailed the cozy mystery his new series.
