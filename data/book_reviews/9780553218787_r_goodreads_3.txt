Rating: 4/5

I started reading this book expecting a modern day fairy tale so I was a bit apprehensive as this is not usually books I enjoy. What a pleasant surprise. Like Winters Daughter the characters rang true and were well rounded. Their actions were logical and well founded, although in the beginning I struggled a little to grasp why the return of the jewels was such an important issue for Willa, but it became clear soon enough. I thoroughly enjoyed reading this book and will never see a kite dancing in the wind without thinking about Willa and Nicholas. The author succeeded in weaving an enjoyable read from the first chapter to the last. This is a book I will recommend to any lover of romantic fiction.
