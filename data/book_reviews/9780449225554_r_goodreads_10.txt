Rating: 4/5

It is a fitting end to this sextet of books, which started with Charles Ashworth and now ends with him as Bishop of Starbridge. His past comes back to haunt him in that place, so pivotal to both his personal life and relationship with God. We see, however, as readers that as Bishop, he has fallen back in to the habit of projecting a glittering image to outsiders. He relies on Lyle, his wife, as the one person he can be himself with. Unfortunately, by doing this, he doesn’t recognize that Lyle also has need for a refuge. And her marriage is not providing it. Luckily Jon Darrow is still around to help steer him straight after a tragic loss. But it is quite a journey. I frequently wanted to slap Charles silly for his obtuseness. 

Re-read. Through my library account connected to Hoopla, I was able to listen to this narrated by Robert Whitfield. Unfortunately, none of my libraries have hard copies available. I read this first back in the late 90s. If I were younger and had a library to build, I would definitely add the Starbridge series to my collection. I love this series, even though I am not religious. I wonder, however, how much my familiarity with Christian doctrine helps the reader understand these books?
