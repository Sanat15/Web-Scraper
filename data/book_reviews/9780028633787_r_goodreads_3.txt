Rating: 1/5

When I write a new class, I like to pick up any easily-available books on the subject, aimed at as wide a variety of audiences as possible, just to see what else is out there. I didn't have particularly high expectations (I can't remember ever using any of the Idiot's Guides before, but my impression of them has never been particularly positive), and I barely made it through the Puritans before giving up. It's trying too hard to be snarky and edgy and appealing to people who don't want to actually put in any effort to read and understand these works.
