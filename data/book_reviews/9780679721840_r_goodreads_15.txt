Rating: 4/5

Really good read, the sort of thing that really hits when you read it on a whim. In the end, it took me a while to get through the whole thing as I got busy with a thousand other things as usual, but I still thought this was a very compelling read, especially because of how well researched it is. I will admit that despite not being exactly what I was looking for (which is totally my fault), I still got a lot out of this, particularly in its last chapter where its conclusions are best established. I found the book's introspections into what witchcraft actually looked like the most interesting, and I would have loved to see more of this, even when I know that this information is harder to come by and far more limited.
