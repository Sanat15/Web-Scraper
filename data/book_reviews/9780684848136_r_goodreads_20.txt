Rating: 2/5

Middling. Another academic writes a sensationalist book on aliens for an easy buck. This one is characteristically exploitative and insincere in its journalism, recycling the interviews of abductees and the views of more sincere but less respectable Ufologists. It reads more like a pulpy sci-fi novel warning of the hybrid agenda you can find covered quickly online these days. But it comes off as disinfo or even misinfo because it doesn't speculate on any human involvement at all in its UFO apocalypticism. I'd prefer a skeptic's book that holds fraudsters or the CIA solely accountable for UFOs.
