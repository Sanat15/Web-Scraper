Rating: 4/5

Today I continued exploring famous classics, and what better choice than reading something by Edgar Allan Poe? 📚

I had no previous experience with Poe’s work, so this became my first introduction to his literary world. Considering how famous he is within the genre, he felt like an author I simply had to try. So why not dive headfirst into one of his most well-known stories?

In The Black Cat, we follow what initially seems like a loving family man who gradually loses his sanity throughout the story, eventually committing increasingly violent acts. The main character is essentially the definition of madness — raw, brutal, and deeply disturbing in his behavior. 🖤

The story itself is morbid and written with strong emotional intensity, which I definitely see as a strength of the author. However, my creative brain — which very easily turns everything I read into vivid mental images — isn’t quite as grateful! 🤣

But honestly, that just means he did a great job. 🫶 It’s something he absolutely deserves respect for.

This is classic horror, so you should definitely go in prepared for things to get bloody and potentially disturbing for some readers. 🩸

Even though my mind creates mental images of everything I read almost as naturally as breathing, I still find it fairly easy to distance myself from the events in the story. Because of that, the book didn’t trigger me as strongly as I know it can for some people.

That said… I was definitely disgusted by the cat scene. 😬🐈‍⬛

Still, I can shake those feelings off pretty easily and move on. But if you’re sensitive to darker themes — ahem — TW: animal cruelty — this might not be the best starting point for exploring Poe’s literary world.

In my profile there’s a more detailed explanation of how I rate the books I read. After some consideration, I’ll be giving this 3 stars, which in my rating system means:
“Liked it and would recommend it.”

That said, I honestly could have given it 4 stars because it’s very well written and detailed in a way that clearly shows the author’s talent.

Curse my indecisiveness! 😅

The rating might change in the future, but I definitely feel tempted to read more of Poe’s work later on. ☺️📖
