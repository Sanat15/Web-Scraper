This was a good novel, fortunately, the audiobook version has an excellent reader, and he kept me on board, I tend to miss when it comes into that form – my mates say they cannot listen to audiobooks, worse, my daughter is not a fan of the medium, but then a good work deserves reading on paper, or kindle 

The Mask of Dimitrious https://realinibarzoi.blogspot.com/20... would be the third presence on that list, but this last is also in The Top 100 Crime Novels of All Time, which is a list published in 1990 by the Crime Writers' Association (CWA) of Great Britain, where it is ranked 24th 
The Mystery Writers of America (MWA) published a similar list named The Top 100 Mystery Novels of All Time, and on this one, The Mask of Dimitrios is 17th, though on The GOAT, these books have a lower ranking

What is interesting at Journey Into Fear is that we have a local character, one called Banat, and Eric Ambler even explains that this is the name of a province in my country, he delves a little into the history of World War II here, the Iron Guard – a fascist outfit, in some ways like MAGA – was very powerful, and Banat was associated with it
Graham is the hero, he is a British engineer, working in Istanbul at some canons, which are crucial for fighting in World War II, ergo, the enemies want to sabotage and what better way than eliminate the engineer who would delay the work for long enough to make it an advantage for Germany, in the games of the conflict 

Banat is a hired killer, enters the hotel room of the main personage, and shoots at him, only managing to hit the target in the hand, nevertheless, they will try later, the assassin will get on board a ship that is supposed to take Graham to safety, first in Italy, and then back home, under a false name, with fake passport
Worse still, the mastermind, Moeller, is on the same ship and they are determined to eliminate the hero on this second attempt, Graham tries to convince the second in command on the (quite small) vessel that he needs to get off fast, but he is derided, and his story ridiculed, some hope comes from a…Turkish agent

Kuvetli was meant to protect the British asset, but will he manage it?
