Rating: 5/5

A wonderful adventure in a magnificent series. It is not the first time for me, and I will read through this whole series more than once again, I'm sure. For the average working-stiff American male to read through a romance series that is not a long Western, may be a bit strange, but Children of the Lion grabs your attention at several levels, with at least acceptable research into the archeology of the nations and times it describes. A yarn, to be sure, but a wonderful, hopeful yarn.
