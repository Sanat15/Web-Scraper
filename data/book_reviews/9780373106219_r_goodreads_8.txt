Rating: 2/5

2/12 stars.
The heroine (22) is too good to be true, too dull to read about and too dim to see what is right before her.
The hero (30s) is clearly a bit of a Man About Town, with a girlfriend, but willing to follow his hormones with the heroine. 
She’s so vapid and makes such a fuss about everything, and seriously, why would this rich guy who normally dates cold blondes suddenly fall for this virtuous dimwit? She has no conversation whatsoever.
Urgh.
I like sweet innocent heroines by Jessica Steele, but innocent doesn’t have to mean brain dead. He’s not such a treasure either.
