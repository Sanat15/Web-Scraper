Rating: 5/5

The story of Allie and Delaney has stayed with me since I first read this book as a teenager. I read it every couple of years and fall back in love with it every time. In fact, I lost my original copy of the book during a move and had to buy a used copy to replace it...that's how much I love this story! 

The story transcends time and I can honestly say it is my absolute favorite romance story ever. I absolutely love the way the author has written their story, the way it spans years and how it captures life. Misunderstandings, feelings of loyalty, feelings of misplaced loyalty - it's all part of anyone's life. I felt as if I could put myself in those characters shoes. 

And now that I'm reviewing this book, I feel the need to pick it up again and read it. :)
