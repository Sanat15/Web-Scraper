Rating: 5/5

A WONDERFUL AND INTRIGUING READ

Even tho this book started out slow,it is a wonderful and intriguing book about love that started out when the hero and heroine were children. Ruiz Navarro was married to Alicia Estrada in an arranged marriage. It was a loveliness marriage that we see nothing of because she dies before the beginning of the story between Ruiz and Julía Flores. Julía's father, Felipe is part owner of Los Montes Verdes vineyard with the Navarro family. 

The vineyard is in trouble and Ruiz only finds this out after the death of his father during the war to free Texas from Mexico. Doña Eugenia, Ruiz's mother never cared for Los Montes Verdes, nor the Flores family, which is a deep dark secret that Pilar Flores keeps close to her breast, which Julía questions a lot and, eventually Pilar finally reveals. There are several other plots in this book... we have the plot of Don Estrada's estranged son, Ruiz's feeling of sense if duty to everyone for everything, the Flores' family's saving of the vineyard and Felipe's wanting to take the reins but needing Ruiz's approval, the need of Doña Eugenia's need to sell and or get away from Los Montes Verdes, the secret love affair of Teresa Navarro and a lowly vaquero (Allende Soto), the secret deranged planning of Lazáro Estrada of his father's estate and a secret treasure, the love between Paco Flores and Luz, and finally the love that Julía and Ruiz have for one another... oh and the love everyone has for Conchita, Ruiz's daughter from his first marriage.

This book is full of a lot of twist and turns of the heart, greed, duty, and love. This is a must read, especially if you enjoy Historical Western Romance and especially part of the history of Texas gaining its freedom from Mexico and its land grants from Mexico and what was promised to the men who fought in the war. This is an awesome read!
