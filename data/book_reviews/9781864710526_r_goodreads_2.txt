Rating: 4/5

I was surprised how small this book is - I finished it in less then an hour. First I want to tell that if you are a fan of attached parenting you might not like the book. In some parts it is kind of old school. Still there is a lot of good and useful information about why babies cry, how often to feed them, how much sleep they need, how to settle and have fun with the baby and finally how to take care about the new parent.
