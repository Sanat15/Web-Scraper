Rating: 3/5

Aging gay porn stars are fascinating. Once lionized by a culture that feasts on male beauty, what becomes of these cinematic fantasies after the cameras stop rolling and younger stars take over?

Sandford was featured in a few films from the late 70s and early 80s, and afterward was diagnosed with AIDS. During a writing workshop for those with the virus, he introduced a few chapters from his experiences living across the street from a Hassidic Boy's School. The boys were as intrigued by Sandford's existence and he was of theirs.

I never felt the story ever take off into storytelling, and it seemed more a series of loosely stitched chapters played out as vignettes. Sadly, Sandford passed away like so many others during those early years of the epidemic.
