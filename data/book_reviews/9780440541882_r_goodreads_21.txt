Rating: 2/5

I was really amazed at the horrible recipes in this thing, but keeping in mind that it was written in 1966 I'm guessing you cooked with what you had. I know eating habits change but this is a good glimpse into how much they change--for example, readers are cautioned to buy commercial sour cream and not use cream that has been allowed to go sour. Reason? In 1966 it was difficult to find sour cream in a grocery store. 
So unless you're a historian, I'd avoid this as a cookbook.
