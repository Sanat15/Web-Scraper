Rating: 4/5

This has to be the briefest retelling of "The Ugly Duckling" on record. Hillert uses a grand total of 40 different words, all of them ones a beginning reader would be able to understand, in the entire book. The effect has a Dick-and-Jane sort of feel, and while the story greatly abbreviated, we still have the duckling being teased (though no use of the word ugly) and running away, being helped by one man and frightened by children, and realizing he's a swan. The illustrations are charmingly vintage at this point, but colorful and help to flesh out the text. While older children will roll their eyes, the book would work well with very little ones just learning to read.
