Rating: 3/5

Too many gory murders for my taste

Plot twists galore is a strength of this author. Only a few spelling errors/typos. But the multiple murders were just too graphic as well as the sexualization and physical assets descriptions of every female character at almost every opportunity. Thx for the opportunity to read this book, which I received for free, but just not for me. Probably more to a lust filled man's taste than an older woman's.
