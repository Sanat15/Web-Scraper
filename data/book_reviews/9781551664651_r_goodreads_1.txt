Rating: 4/5

I must admit I had difficulty getting through this novel. I started it back in January, would put it down for a few weeks, pick it up again, down again, etc., and finally finishing it in September. Author Elizabeth Gage puts her characters through wild and tumultuous rides, with consequences for actions showing up at unexpected times. And it's those hooks that make you keep coming back, simply to see what was going to happen. Ms. Gage is a gifted writer and storyteller. I don't know if this novel was ever made into a movie, but it certainly has the elements that would make it interesting.
