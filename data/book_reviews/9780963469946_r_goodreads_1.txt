Rating: 3/5

Nothing is simple, especially marketing. It is a marketing ploy to name something simple. But there is good information in the book, more for authors who already have a publisher, less for self-published authors, but all was relevant to getting the word out about your book. Marketing is a massively time consuming activity. I did save a lot of money however, as he does not recommend buying advertising until a base demand is built for your book with publicity, which is free.
