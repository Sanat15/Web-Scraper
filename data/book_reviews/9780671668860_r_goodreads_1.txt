Rating: 5/5

Claire has made the decision to leave her controlling husband Bennet and move to UK with her lover. She goes to school to get her daughters and leave that very day...unfortunately Bennet has anticipated her move and is at the school in the heads office, she can see him through the window. She has one daughter the younger one Meredith and waves to Liza to come quickly, Liza waves back, loses her holding on the play equipment and falls to the ground hitting her head... Claire has to make a quick decision... go with just one daughter, or stay. This decision has consequences which impact their lives forever...
