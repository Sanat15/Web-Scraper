Rating: 3/5

Amazing and disappointing. Some really interesting insights into the man, he's mostly very honest and yet sometimes you almost wish he wasn't as for all his ideals he's revealed to be insightful, sometimes brilliantly inspired, brutally honest, funny and also, selfish and somehow lacking in imagination for the thoughts and emotions of others. An interesting read but to say it's a bit 'mixed' is an understatement. I'm predisposed to like his general stance on many things but this book was a reality check.
