Rating: 4/5

4.25 stars 
This was an unexpected and sweet treat. I've never read a HP like this before. The hero, Joe, is an American pop star (with Spanish heritage) and the heroine won a contest in which she won a date with him. They spend a few days together in London amidst his busy tour. It was a really lighthearted and cute story. I'll definitely re-read it. Easily one of my favorite books by Charlotte Lamb. 

Angst Factor: None
OW/OM: A smidge of OM drama 
Steam Factor: No love scenes--disappointing! Rated PG.
