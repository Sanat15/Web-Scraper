Rating: 4/5

An exciting read! Reilly's Return chronicles Reilly O'Neill and Mandy Edwards's epic adventure ;) In short, Reilly's a good guy being hotly pursued by baddies who want him dead. As they catch wind of Mandy, she's endangered as well.

Lots of slightly old school alpha male "I've got to protect my woman" reasoning, but Ms Autin captures Reilly and Mandy's heartfelt emotions and passion, and ultimately allows the characters to reveal what's in their hearts to each other in a meaningful way. While Mandy is female, she's not just the "little woman"--she's smart, can handle a gun, and not exactly a pushover. And while Reilly's definitely the (over)protective sort, he's thankfully no fool either.

Ms Autin kept me turning the pages as the combination of action and emotion wouldn't let me turn away :)

It was a pleasure to read Reilly's Return and to see these two find their way to a happy ending! Looking forward to reading more of Ms Autin's work!
