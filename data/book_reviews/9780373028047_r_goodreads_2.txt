Rating: 2/5

This is a nice hero. With Charlotte Lamb, I am used to cruel heroes. This hero is different. 

He tells her quite soon he is in love with her, but he is afraid to marry. She wants marriage or else he should just move on. 

She kisses and cuddles another man after that. I wouldn’t find it okay if the hero had kissed another woman, so I don’t think it’s okay that she kisses other men. 

Anyway, the whole romance is actually about her getting him to the point that he does want to marry her. 

She already knew he loves her, which didn’t prevent her from kissing another guy. The H was a bit of a doormat for the h.
