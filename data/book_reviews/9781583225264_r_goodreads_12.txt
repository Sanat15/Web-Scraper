Rating: 5/5

A timely book to read with Portland's upcoming public vote about whether to add fluoride to local drinking water. I picked it up with some concerns about water fluoridation, and thought I might get some recent &amp; relevant data on the subject. Instead, I got a roller-coaster ride that at times felt like I'd stepped into a Hollywood conspiracy story! I eyed certain quotes &amp; claims with a fair amount of suspicion initially... I was not expecting the connection between fluoride and the Manhattan Project, Cold War governmental needs, and big-business industry. But the more I read, the less I could deny the outrageous facts that were being presented. Covered-up studies, lawsuits, and huge propaganda campaigns are all presented by Bryson with an effective and chilling precision that will leave you looking at fluoride in a completely different way.
