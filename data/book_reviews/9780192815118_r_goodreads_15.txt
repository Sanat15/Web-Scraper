Rating: 4/5

Izaak Walton's 1653 treatise on fly fishing and the contemplative life. 
This later edition contains other writings too, compiled by Walton, but not his.
The treatise is mainly a conversation between The Angler (Piscator) and The Huntsman (Venator), concerning which activity is the best to devote oneself to. 
Spoiler: The book is not only about fishing, but about a way to live life simply while staying in touch with the natural world.
