Rating: 5/5

This was my very first romance book! Mine was the white Harlequin paperback version though.  That Carolina Summer Such the innocent reader I was then. I was completely swept away by the characters and romance. Guy sees girl, likes what he sees, attempts to tame, girl indignantly rebels though secretly desiring to be conquered, girl plays her own kind of game, eventually both of them open their eyes and realize they can't live without each other. Of course, I was only about 17 when I read this book...~lol~

Compared to more current storylines and romance novels, I've certainly read better but its reminiscent value merits 5 stars from me.
