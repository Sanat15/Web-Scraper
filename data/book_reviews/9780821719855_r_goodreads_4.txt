Rating: 4/5

I loved reading a book with an FMC who shares my name! I also loved how Mariana's parentage secret and the tension between her and her adoptive father added to the story. I'd hate it if my parents tried to force me to marry someone I didn't love, though this happened often back in the 1700s in which this book is set.

However, the frequent use of the word squ*w bothered me. I can't imagine Native Americans would ever refer to their own women like that, as what happens in this book. That was enough to make me knock one star off my rating here.
