Rating: 3/5

In several hospitals around the country, a strange phenomena is taking place: difficult births, the babies born with red hair and green eyes, babies that die soon after. Josh Heller has dealt with a few of these, so when a new computer system comes along promising to solve some of the problems, he gets it installed. But the red-haired woman in green who arrives to install it tries to seduce him, putting more strain on Josh's marriage. His wife, Pat, is working hard as a reporter to uncover a senator's ties to genetic experimentation, not knowing that she might just become the mother of one of the Christmas Babies...

This book looked so amazingly cheesy that I just had to buy it from a library book sale. I figured this was a good time of year to read it - but, disappointingly, most of the story takes place in Florida and the "Christmas Babies" aren't actually the scary part of the story. The scary part is how much Pat drinks even after finding out she's pregnant! (Seriously, three mixed drinks in one sitting!). Or this description of an orgasm: "It came in rolling waves until she thought her head would blow off, and then she collapsed." 

It was a fun read if you enjoyed Paperbacks from Hell and want to read something that is not especially well-written, but written in a way that is easy and fast. I couldn't quite figure out why Dr. Burns had decided to use random women as his test subjects, when he had a whole stable full of them back at the lab (none of whom were rescued, BTW), especially since the babies kept dying? But as a holiday read it didn't really hold up.
