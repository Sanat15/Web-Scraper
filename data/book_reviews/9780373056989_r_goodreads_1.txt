Rating: 3/5

Cassie has come to Colorado to stop her wayward younger sister from marrying a man that she barely knows. Slade, cousin to Cassie's sister's fiancé, thinks Cassie needs to butt out, but he agrees to take her to see her sister. Only one problem: it's a two-day hike, and Cassie's only hope of getting there is to stick with the tough, abrasive Slade...

I'm not sure whether I enjoyed this book or not. It was a very average 80s romance (despite being written in the 90s; it just has an 80s feel to it) with a somewhat-irritating hero and heroine who I nevertheless quite liked. Slade is moody and often quite rude, Cassie is a little entitled and bossy, but neither tip over the edge into being impossible to root for. I probably preferred the second half, when Cassie is introducing Slade to Boston (I am NOT a wilderness type of girl, and think that the trick he played on her to make her hike for two days was atrocious), although it would have been nice if Slade had made a bit more effort to enjoy her world the way she made an effort to enjoy his. I did feel that the book felt a bit balanced in Slade's favour - it's Cassie who ends up giving up her life to go live in his world, rather than compromising, which probably adds to the 1980s feelings I get from it.

All in all, quite readable, but I wouldn't care much if I didn't read it again. 3 stars.
