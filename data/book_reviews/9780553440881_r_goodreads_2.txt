Rating: 5/5

It had been awhile since I'd read this book, but I still love it. Cory and Holt are a great couple. Austin makes a great backdrop for the story. I like that Cory stands up for herself She even ends up capturing the robber in the woman's restroom while 2 Texas Rangers are in the bar but realizes what is important in life. Holt is a somewhat overprotective and a bit of a chauvinist, but Cory won't let him get away with it.
