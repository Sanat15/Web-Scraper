I would love to read The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich someday, but in the meantime this novella-length biography of the benchmark for all poop stain humans will do me just fine. True, I can appreciate a 1000 page tome in the right time and place. But once in a while I also enjoy a slim sketch of a biography to gather a rough outline for future use. The coloring in comes later, with expert psychoanalysis and a fair review of historical context. 

This title was an effortless read (or listen, in my case). Even a vague outline of Hitler's life brings out a wealth of facts worth knowing for a history enthusiast like myself. I couldn't help but filter some of Shirer's observation through the recency of the events. He seems to take sources like Mein Kampf or the words and saying of Albert Speer at face value. Nonetheless, he is a valuable source for having witnessed many of these events firsthand, even if his own experience does not weigh heavily into the narrative. There seems to be a wealth of options to choose from for biographies of Adolf Hitler, but this one was an easy first choice before I think deeper about the next one.
