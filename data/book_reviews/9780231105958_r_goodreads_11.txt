Rating: 3/5

A corrupted text far too obfuscated with the intentions of it’s scribes and rewriters passed through the millennia . Some stories are in the spirit of Tao: the stories involving trees and uselessness, the story of the bird and offerings of men, of Confucius’s student attempting to help a corrupt king, of the butcher cleaving livestock, of the White Sea turtle appearing as an envoy in dream, and some discourses on the limited expectations of smaller men, but that’s it.

The exaggerated singing at the event of their wive’s deaths, the logical word play of moism , the intellectualism of filial piety values (loving parents, following the brother’s will, etc.) of Confucius mores adds confusion and contradictory messages relative to the experience of Tao. The contradiction and change of voices is startling and far too often. This supports the theory of multiple authors having rewritten the text with their own bias in each successive generation. Sadly these corruptions represents the larger parts of the text.
