Rating: 5/5

This series had been published in 1998 ish... and they are still relevant now. I have read, a re-read a lot of these, and now I am in my 40's and still enjoy them. It seems on a re-read I am understanding some of the punch lines that didn't apply to me then but do now. I love the Canadian elements, I love the fact that our characters age, people pass away, hardships happen... because life does happen but we see that they can make it through ... Long story short, some make me cry but they ALWAYS make me laugh
