Rating: 5/5

This book by a classic writer has a strong heroine written long before it was “cool” to write about strong women. She knows her own mind, makes her own way, and yet still has a heart! I wish I had read this as a young woman, I highly enjoyed it . I have been a fan of Andre Norton for years and this book is nothing like any of her other books I remember reading, but it has been a while, I must go back and read some more!
