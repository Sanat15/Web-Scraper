Rating: 4/5

This one is probably closer to 3 1/2 stars. A weaker entry in to the Cool &amp; Lam series of novels. It suffers a bit from over-plotting, weak characterization of the secondary characters and the ending strays WAY in to the "cozy mystery" tradition. But given the fact that the clues really weren't there, it almost has to do so as Lam mystically figures it all out. 

Readable, but a disappointment compared to the other Cool &amp; Lam books that I've read.
