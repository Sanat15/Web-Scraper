Rating: 3/5

Cooking and romance, what's not to love?

When pastry chef heroine sees an article on CEO hero that claims one of her recipes as his own, she's understandably upset. With her sister's help, she enters him into a charity cooking contest, planing to unmask this imposter. But the more they run into each other the less she thinks of revenge and the more they fall for each other.

This was a nice story with likable characters and a surprise at the end that wrapped it up with a great bow.
