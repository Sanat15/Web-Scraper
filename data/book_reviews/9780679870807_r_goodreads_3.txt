Rating: 3/5

A bit of a weakling, for Shadow Zone standards. Funny enough, this is one of two kids’ horror books from the 90s with the title Scream Around The Campfire, which is a really bizarre fact. I enjoyed this SZ but it ultimately wound up being the least impressive of the series by far. The main character’s arc is enjoyable and feels like the correct version of the shitty arc we had with the chick from Camp Cold Lake. A polar-opposite quality. The book is well paced and wraps up everything in good time, and there’s some fun to be had with this volume, but it’s ultimately a flawed piece. The Bigfoot ordeal is disappointing and doesn’t really crescendo too much in the third act, leaving me dissatisfied with them as the creature feature of the story. The resolution is convoluted and honestly poorly written, with things oddly lining up leaving things resolved without much of an actual climax to the story, and Gina feels stolen of good character moments with that. There’s even a really obvious solution to the central conflict that would’ve worked much simpler, which is just to scare the antagonist… and I mean, we got there, just not at all the I wanted, even if it does deliver on some exposition. If you couldn’t tell, I’m pretty mixed on the third act as a whole. Overall, 7/10. It’s good, but turned up a bit deflating by the end. Bellow Encasing Le OutdoorGatheringFlame (fucking end me).
