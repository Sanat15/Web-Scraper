Rating: 3/5

I have known about the Gulf Breeze UFO sightings practically my entire life. In 1990, some evidence surfaced that suggested that Ed Walters' photos were hoaxed. I really had not heard anything new about the case since the early 90s. Then, this last Thanksgiving weekend, I found a copy of this book, which I didn't know existed. 

I want to believe. To me, these sightings and accounts are as iconic as any other to be found and told in UFOlogy lore. I do believe that UFOs were seen in Gulf Breeze Florida in the late 80s and early 90s. I'm just not sure that the authors' accounts and photos are real. For 25 years, something about those photos has bothered me. Something has just not sat quite right.

Now I am no more convinced of Mr. Walters' credibility, than when I first started reading this book. If the purpose of the book was to convince me that the author has been truthful, it failed. Furthermore, the end of the position statement by Mr. Andruws, of MUFON, is weakened by bizarre assumption and speculation. Putting everything aside, it was, at the very least, a fun read and entertaining.
