Rating: 4/5

This is a story if three young African American men from Newark, NJ that were noticed by their teachers as very intelligent, and encouraged in middle school to make plans to go to college. They were frequently faced with peer pressure to make choices that weren't in their best interest. Sometimes they resisted, and sometimes they made a bad choice. Sometimes there were consequences, including jail time, and sometimes they were let free. They made a pact to continue their education and encourage each other throughout their journey. They knew if one quit, the others would most likely follow. They were all able to complete medical/dental school and become successful leaders in their community. Granted there was a huge community supporting them, but their struggles were real.
The quality of writing wasn't 5 star, but the story was well told. Hopefully their story and the work they do with their foundation helps encourage other youth to dream big and achieve them.
