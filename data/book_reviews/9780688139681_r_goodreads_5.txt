Rating: 5/5

It is well written. Jordan gives us his story of living as a African-American 13 year old boy who becomes a "black man". I was interested in reading it because I only have a vague idea of what Kwanzaa is. I didn't know how it is celebrated and how Jordan applies it to his every day life.
It is a short read and moving and inspirational. I was impressed. I encourage anyone to read it.
