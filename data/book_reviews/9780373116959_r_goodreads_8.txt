Rating: 5/5

Ohhhhhhhhhhh my goodness.
I am madly in love with this book! Def. in the top 5 HP list now. Why have I never read this one?

It was perfect for me. Loved both the H/h, all the 2ndary characters, the family feud, the sexual chemistry, the setting. The pacing for the reawakening of the romance was just right, and I loved the little unexpected twist at the end.

I also loved how the house was part of the resolution.

This book is a truly original and unique HP. ADORE IT!
