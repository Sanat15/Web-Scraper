Rating: 3/5

Well, this book wasn't great, like the whole entire series. The concept was an interesting one, but the execution was mediocre. I found myself pretty bored by the whole story in this last book. It had a couple of exciting parts but overall the story was boring and the characters were flat. There was a lot of twisting and turning that happened at the end that felt very forced and didn't really leave the ending satisfying. There was no real conclusion to the war and the machinations of the Amplitur. It was certainly a let down after reading this whole boring trilogy. Overall I would give this book and the series 3/5 stars. The first book is by far the best book and it was super frustrating to read because of the theme and the main character. I can't say that reading this trilogy was a total waste of time but I wouldn't recommend it to anyone when there are many better sci-fi books out there.
