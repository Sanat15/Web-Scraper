Rating: 4/5

Rex Stout always delivers the most amazing character interaction. Comprising three short stories: Kill Now, Pay Later; Murder is Corny; and Blood Will Tell. The middle story was brilliantly brought to screen with Maury Chakin and Timothy Hutton... but in my opinion Blood Will Tell is my favorite of the lot. Nero, Archie, Fritz and the gang never fail to bring a familiar smile to my face in the brilliant crime series more about how they most entertainingly and playfully work together than about the mysteries themselves.
