Rating: 3/5

I haven't read a Babysitters Club for years and I got this one recently to try the series again.

In this Mystery special there is a baking contest in the town and Claudia and her BSC friends are taking part in both the baking contest and the day care and babysitting duties.
But when someone begins tampering with the teams ingredients, Claudia and her friends have to find the culprit before they lose their place in the contest.

This was a fun read and I definitely got a lot of nostalgia from this as I remembered bits of the series whilst reading.

It was an enjoyable one for me, and I'm looking forward to trying more books in this series again.
