Rating: 3/5

‘gigi’ i feel loses a lot of its charm &amp; humour from a modern reading perspective. what i assume to be a strong foot into adulthood for gigi/gilberte (don’t get me wrong she is clearly presented as being a very modern, headstrong character, that comes through clearly in the text) instead comes off just a bit too weird! it could of aged worse at least, it’s not so completely offensive to read and the writing is great throughout! 

‘the cat’ is definitely where this little collection shined for me!! i can see why these two novellas got paired up in this book as the cat also hits on moving on into adulthood through alain. frankly this has just aged much better which allows some of the to humour really land. i also found the writing to be far superior compared to gigi (even tho gigi was very well-written!) lots of beautiful little segments throughout that adds a nice contrast to the growing tension that bubbles along nicely!
