Rating: 2/5

When I was a kid, I liked to read about unexplained events and phenomena. These things increased my sense of mystery in the world, and I loved a good scary story. This book is a collection of "reports" (very short narratives) about everything from precognition to UFOs to ghosts and monsters. For some odd reason, data about unusual creatures like the electric eel are also included. Most of the stories are so short (under 100 words) as to be completely uninformative, but they can ignite a spark of imagination. There are no sources cited, and in cases which are not common knoweldge, it is hard to say if the authors simply made them up. Some, which are well-known, such as Betty and Barney Hill, are annoyingly incomplete. This book is for entertainment, not information, and its entertainment value is frankly limited.
