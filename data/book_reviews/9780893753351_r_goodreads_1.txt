Rating: 1/5

Copyright 1980, this is a Troll associates book. There are only 3 stories in this book. The theme is haunted houses.

 So, this is a book for elementary school age &amp; up. Saying that, I have to say the first story is completely inappropriate for kids. The illustrations show kids that look like middle school age. This is an "I dare you to spend the night in a haunted house" story. The thing is... one of his friends gives the kid a gun. For protection. Against ghosts. Hmmmmmm. Spoiler here ----the kid shoots himself. Fortunately in the toe. Still, this is not a good story.

 The other 2 are ok, the last being the best. I still can't recommend this to anyone wanting to read it to their kids or have their kids read it.
