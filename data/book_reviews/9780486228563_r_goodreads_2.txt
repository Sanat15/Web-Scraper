Rating: 5/5

Well, I read almost all the book (some chapters are so detailed, that I just didn't have time to read them completely. But I wish I had...). Anyway, I read it almost entirely and since it was written in 1911 by an author who knew the European and American piano industry from first hand, it is a very valuable document. I was surprised by the lack of distortion of this book (most of the books and articles on music history written in the first decades of the XX century that I have read are certainly "stained" by a nationalistic and too romantic approach... But it seems that Dolge's mind was very practical and down-to-earth. Maybe that was always typical in US. Who knows?).
