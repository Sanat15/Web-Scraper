Rating: 2/5

The H just had a thin layer of oiliness that never washed off. I also didn't like that at the end  he admitted that he had slept with a lot of women and didn't remember most of their names, and that the heroine was the first time he ever told a girl he loved her and meant it with every fiber of his being. So that means that he apparently had no problem lying and telling women he loved them when he didn't. To me, that's what separates a hoorish-but-redeemable H from a complete slimeball -- the former is always honest that he doesn't love the women he's with, so when he says he loves the h, it's something transformational for him. An H who throws the term around hasn't been transformed, since the term never meant anything to him in the first place.
