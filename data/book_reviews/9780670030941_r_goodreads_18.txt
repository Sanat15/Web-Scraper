Rating: 4/5

This was a very interesting book that discusses a topic that is less commonly covered. A really nice job of tying in the science to haunting imagery of the author’s experiences in the field. 
There were a number of personal loose ends that were interesting and added an emotional component in places but were the kind of thing you might see in a work of fiction so when they end up going nowhere kind of fall flat. Also the scientific conclusion is interesting but seems a bit lightly covered. Recommended despite the criticisms for fans of science and foreign travel.
