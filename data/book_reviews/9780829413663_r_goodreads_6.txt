Rating: 4/5

Usually I read books like these too fast for them to be useful but I really tried to slow down and do the suggested practices (not all of them but the ones that resonated with me). This book is sort of like the Ignatian starter kit with a look at consolation and desolation, disordered and rightly ordered desires and imaginative prayer practices.

Silf describes the self as concentric circles with the outer rim being where we are, a little deeper how we navigate the world and near our center is who we are, whom God made us to be and where we meet God in our true self.

There were lots of images and metaphors I picked up along the way and pieces of Ignatian discernment I thought I knew but now understand a little better.
