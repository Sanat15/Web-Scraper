Rating: 4/5

Um, wow. There's a lot going on in this story. 

The death of a longed for baby was hard to take. Both for the characters in the story and for myself, who lived through such a thing. 

Small town politics and secrets and gossip.

The "othering" of the Chinese immigrants. 

Post-partum depression. 

Anger with god. 

And yet, it all comes together and the only way out is just to keep living forward as much as possible.
