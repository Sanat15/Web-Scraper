Rating: 3/5

After reading “Nothing Like it in the World”, by Stephen Ambrose years ago I picked this book up after a friend recommended it. First, I must applaud the Author for doing an incredible amount of research. Unfortunately, I felt the “flowery” writings quoted from newspaper reporters of the time was unnecessary, the continuous commentary and personnel thoughts of all parties involved was just too much for me. Reading the complete backgrounds of even the smallest “players” was overwhelming, just too much minutiae for me. I understand that all of his information lends itself to giving the reader a true feel/view of the times and in that respect the book was very well done but it was too much for me. At page 300 I started skipping sections and basically gave up.
