Rating: 4/5

There’s been much more research done on the Plantagenets in the 70+ years since The Conquerors was first published, but I find Costain’s quaint mid-century storytelling style right up my alley. It reminds me of a 1950s Disney documentary. This first of four Plantagenet volumes ends with the death of King John, so Henry III awaits in The Magnificent Century.
