Rating: 3/5

A mixed bag, this. At times he has an almost Ivor Cutler-ish sense of urban life, but without Cutler's poise or control. Periodically - perhaps not often enough - you hear a voice that echoes the sharp oddness of Joe Orton or Viv Stanshall. The first part of the book is more straightforwardly (sometimes very) short stories, which are variable. (The title story hits it dead on, but as the first piece in the collection sets a difficult standard to maintain). The second part is better. Subtitled 'Incidents from an Endless Dream', it gives a stronger sense of how the unfolding content reads, and allows Coyne space to pick at that itch driving him to write.
