Rating: 2/5

Very happy with my life choices.

This is just as much about how to get along with your siblings as it is to not have children. I suppose kids are fine in their own way. But one brat and one whinger…those poor parents. But then again, that’s what they wanted.

The brat monopolised the whinger’s time, ruining all her fun. As a reader, I felt exhausted reading about it. The parents were very lenient. 

A good message at the end, but still. Nice illustrations.
