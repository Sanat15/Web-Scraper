Rating: 1/5

If the book had ended at chapter 48 it would have been a great ending and satisfying bringing all the elements of Gershwin's travels and planning. 
Instead we get 16 chapters of hermit Gershwin "retiring" on Earth who apparently is now a rapist in response to threat or to "teach a woman a lesson". I felt like it was a complete betrayal of the character and ruined the series for me.
