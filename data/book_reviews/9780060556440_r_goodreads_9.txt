Rating: 5/5

A Blockbuster Legal Thriller ... I wish every mystery I read would have a character like Phil “friggin” Skink. He all but stole the show. Phil talks like an underworld thug and he is ugly – really ugly – “With a face like a battered hardball.” As the story progressed, my revulsion for Phil Skink turned to admiration and even endearment. At times I felt the tale was a little too long and convoluted, but I stuck with it regardless of that. It was a page-turner. I couldn’t put it down, and if I had bailed out of it, I wouldn’t have gotten to know Phil “figgin” Skink – one of the best fictional characters I have encountered in my reading. – David B. Crawley, M.D. – Author of “Steep Turn: A Physician's Journey From Clinic to Cockpit” and “A Mile of String: A Boy's Recollection of His Midwest Childhood.”
