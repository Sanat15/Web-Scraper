Rating: 5/5

This was one of a projected series of twelve factual books I believe, that Deighton intended to write about the history of WW 2.
As he now appears to have retired from writing we are left with just some.

One of the problems of reviewing non fiction works is whether you rate on accuracy, or as a good read, or both.
On the basis of ' both ' I based my rating.
Again his capabilities for research into his subject shows through, excellent.
