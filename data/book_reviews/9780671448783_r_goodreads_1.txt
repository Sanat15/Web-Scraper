Rating: 4/5

This epic story spans from just before WWI until the second world war begins. As Elizabeth Wingate makes her way through life, she discovers that it isn't always cut and dry, and that the choices she makes affect not only her, but the people around her for years to come. Finding herself in a marriage that struggles with fidelity and understanding, Elizabeth is determined to live her own life. Watching her daughter, Jennifer, floundering with her own demons, Elizabeth is forced to become the one person to help her daughter stand on her own two feet. With the help of Elizabeth's oldest and dearest friend, Ginny, this dramatic novel comes to a satisfying conclusion.
