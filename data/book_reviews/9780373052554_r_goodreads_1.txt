Rating: 3/5

Imagine if you met your favorite romance author, only to discover that he's a man who doesn't believe in love! I think that alone was enough for me to download this book! The heroine is a woman many of us could identify with ~ a school teacher who loves to read romance novels. I thought this was an easy read and hooked my interest. Please keep in mind that this is a re-release of her early work. I've been reading Ms. James's books for years and I can tell she has really grown as a writer. I'm looking forward to reading her other early contemporaries when I get the chance.
