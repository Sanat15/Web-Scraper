Rating: 3/5

I was upset and disappointed when I realized that this book was originally published in 1991 and rereleased with a new updated cover but not content. Cal's grandmother, Caroline, who actively runs one of Wyoming's largest, oldest, and most profitable beef ranch calls a 'computer' a "new fangled " gadget...Really in the Wyoming, where livestock and farms are mechanized/modernized if they are to survive in 2016. The content should be redacted and modernized to reflect the times .Also readers/purchasers of the book should have been made aware that it was a rerelease novel.
The novel was short, the plot and characters' behavior predictable, and dated. Tsk, tsk, Ms.Woods!! 2.5 /*****
