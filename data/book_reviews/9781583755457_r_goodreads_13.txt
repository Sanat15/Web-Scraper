Rating: 5/5

I first read this book when I was 11 and I absolutely loved this book!
This is probably my favourite non fiction book and this book is a lot better than the teenage one
But if I could change some of this book I would change some of the stories in the tough stuff chapter because it is not very appropriate for an 8-12 year old to read!
For example "Get Help Now!" and "I love her more than ever" were a bit questioning
But I would definitely put those stories in the teenage soul book if it were up to me!
But they are all so good! Especially the family and friendship category!
OVERALL: 1000/10
