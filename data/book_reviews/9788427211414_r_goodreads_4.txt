Rating: 4/5

I loves Zane Grey and his western stories.
I always found myself picturing the amazing scenery and characters of cowboys with their hats and guns. This story paints Texas buffalo hunters in a beautiful inspiring way. I keep dreaming of the buffalo stampede; thousands and thousands of buffalo running and grazing in the open. 
Also, I was surprised to learn that Zane Grey was a dentist in his early life.
