Rating: 3/5

A clear and accessible explanation of the challenges of working and traveling abroad. As someone who has lived abroad in central African, post-Soviet, and east Asian countries for the past ten yeas, I affirm the messages of this book. I also felt that this was a good book as an introduction for those about to embark on or early in their experiences overseas, but less useful for someone who has already spent a decade abroad.

The author admits that he exaggerates at times to underscore his points; and for diplomats, there is an additional tension between adapting to the local culture and professionally obligations to represent your own culture abroad that is not present for those who are merely expats.

The excepts from travel writers added a layer of richness to the otherwise fairly general commentary, and the short case studies were, for me, perhaps the most interesting part of the author's own work -- I found myself wanting more of them,
