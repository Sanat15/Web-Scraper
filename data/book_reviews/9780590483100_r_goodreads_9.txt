Rating: 5/5

This took me way too long to read for a BSC book, especially for one that I enjoyed. But I liked the bake-off (though I don't understand why Claudia created (terrible-tasting) recipes when they could have just found some in cookbooks?) and that this was a mystery that was plausible. I also like that Grace was tolerable when Cokie was sick - reminds me of the Sleepover Friends book  Kate the Boss, which had a similar plot point with their nemesis getting sick and them realizing the sidekick is a decent person. In summary, this book made me want cake. (But not Alma's Memory Cake, I don't like cherries.)
