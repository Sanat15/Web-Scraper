Rating: 2/5

Enjoyed meeting characters in first book. However, this book is full of hints about the main character’s feelings for each other (wouldn’t happen with any nun I grew up with) and I could have done without the graphic ‘ceremony’ scene in the woods.
Having said that, I’m going to give book 3 a go but the first sign of any ‘lingering looks’ or ‘touching of fingers’ and I won’t be trying book 4.
