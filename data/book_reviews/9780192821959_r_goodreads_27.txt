Rating: 5/5

Yes, it's obvious that Wilkie Collins wrote this when very young, but I think it lends accuracy to his portrayal of the young man, Basil. I agree with readers who find Basil somewhat annoying, and the main two females are just caricatures-- one blond and angelic, and other dark and devilish. But you know what? I couldn't stop reading it! It's really a great example of the Victorian sensation novel.
