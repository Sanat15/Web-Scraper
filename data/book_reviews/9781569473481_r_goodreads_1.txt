Rating: 2/5

I've really enjoyed Patricia Carlon's novels in the past, especially The Whispering Wall and The Running Woman. This one was just not up to par. Although the description of the fire was excellent, the mystery itself was predictable (perhaps I've just read too many of this type, though), and Carlon's circular style not as well suited to this particular type of story. There are a lot of characters, and not only are they difficult to keep separate, they also have the same conversation over and over. While that is Carlon's point--how wearing this experience, and these conversations, are on the title character--it's also wearing on the reader.
