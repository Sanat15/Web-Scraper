Rating: 3/5

If you’re looking for a good read set during the U.S. Revolutionary War try The Rebel and the Rose by Joan Wolf (1986). It’s begins with an arranged marriage between an Englishwoman, Lady Barbara and a wealthy American, Alan Maxwell who lives in Virginia. They begin on different sides of the conflict .., he rises to become a general under George Washington and she manages the family home while he is away at war. 

Plenty of historical events and details including Bunker Hill, Valley Forge, British occupation of Philadelphia and Yorktown drive the storyline. Bedroom scenes are written briefly and there is a romance that develops slowly as they get to know each other. This book is about the history and how the split from England affected a family. Most importantly, the book brings to life in vivid detail the personal hardships this six year war of independence caused. Includes infidelity as part of the drama.
