Rating: 4/5

Between my lackadaisical end-of-summer reading and the high-pressure start of another school year at work, my reading goal for the year is under serious threat of non-accomplishment. Cue random-ass books that are both easy and delightful to get through. That's why I read all my photo-tacular vacation purchases this past weekend (who me? weep to be on vacation again? *sob* yes!), and that's why I read this today.

While I can't deny I have various fears that drive some serious aspects of my behavior, I don't think I'm particularly plagued by phobias at this point in my life. So, yeah, I loved the dental visit (dontophobia) and the top of the skyscraper (acrophobia) pop-ups in particular. So much fun in the detail! And I can only wonder if my friends think I have mysophobia (pathological fear of exposure to unsanitary or disease-producing substances.... including filthy bathrooms, judging by the pop up) due to my general unwillingness to "go" in random bathrooms and port-o-lets. Nope, not a phobia, just a low revulsion reflex.

3.5 stars
