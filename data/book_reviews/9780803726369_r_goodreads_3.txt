Rating: 1/5

Rachel, the first-person narrator of this story is annoying. Her parents seem a bit clueless. Why wouldn't they try to find a school where she could have time to practice the piano even if it isn't a music school. Either Rachel's mother, or Rosemary Wells, does not understand the correct use of pronouns - when to use "me" and when to use "I". Although set in a boarding school it isn't much of a boarding school story.
