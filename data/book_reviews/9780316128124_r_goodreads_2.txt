Rating: 5/5

I got this book as a present five years ago. I started reading it back then, and for some reason I stoped half way through it. I picked it up again, and again I stoped. This time I read it from start to finish in just a couple of days. I couldn't get enough of it, words really resonated with me, and everything finally made sense. The difference between then and now is that I was finally ready to nourish my own Soul. I have been working on my own spiritual path for years, but I have been more committed to it for the last two years. It has been great to read about other's approach to Soul, how they nourish and honor their own souls, how they live their own spiritual lives. As the title suggests, this is a handbook, you can take this as a starting point if you want to get in touch with your own Soul, your Higher Self. You'll find lots of advice and suggestions on how to do this. You might even discover that you have been inadvertently nourishing your Soul by reading about all these personal experiences. Either way, this is a great read to dip into your Soul.
