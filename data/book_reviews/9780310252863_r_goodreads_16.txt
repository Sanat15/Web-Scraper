Rating: 5/5

Wonderful read. There is nothing quite like reading the accounts of those whose lives have been radically changed by Jesus. I’m not sure why I’ve waited so long to read a book by Chuck Colson, but this won’t be my last. God did amazing things through Colson’s imprisonment for Watergate crimes and the Prison Fellowship ministry he began as a result. The stories in this book are inspiring, and they are also beautifully written. Definitely recommend
