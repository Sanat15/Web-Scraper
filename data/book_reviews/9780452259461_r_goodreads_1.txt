Rating: 5/5

Alzheimer's disease is an erosion of the self. As it progresses, it robs you of your abilities to think and perform tasks. The copy I found is hardcover and a rerelease issued in 2001. The authors intended the book to work as an aid to a person going through Alzheimer's.

Although the book is a phenomenal resource and deserves all the accolades it receives, it hits a little too close to home at the moment. I have a family member with Alzheimer's disease, and I thought I could handle reading about it. I was wrong. I will have to wait until I can deal with the emotional impact. Just reading it makes me misty-eyed.

While I enjoyed the book and what it said, I couldn't finish it. Thanks for reading my review, and see you next time.
