Rating: 4/5

"The Sands of Time" is a novel with an interesting concept. In this alternate world, mice are civilized and live just as humans would. The plot follows Hermux Tanatamoq, a watchmaker who is one day confronted by a mysterious chipmunk asking for his father. Throughout the book, cats are not something that should be casually mentioned in public, but the idea of felines play a big part in our main character's adventure. I think Michael did a very good job at portraying Tucka as an antagonist. The bit about her saying that only ugly people would say that beauty lies on the inside was a bit infuriating, and it served its purpose well. Overall, this book was light-hearted and an enjoyable read!
