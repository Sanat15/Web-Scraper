Rating: 5/5

Heartwarming story

This is the first time I have read Brentwood even though I have been a fan of Grace Livingston Hills books for many years. Brentwood is a wonderful story of Marjorie who was adopted by a rich family as a baby when her birth family was under great stress. Now her adoptive parents have died and the mother left Marjorie a letter encouraging her to look up the birth family and apologize for having kept her away from them so long. She goes, falls in love with them, and finds a relationship with Jesus as well.
