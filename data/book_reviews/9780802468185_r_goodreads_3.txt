Rating: 4/5

I am a fan of Tony Evans. I love to hear him preach. I have read some of his books and have found them very useful. In ‘The Best is Yet to Come’, Evans walks thru the Bible exploring references to the end times. Much of the book is very helpful: connecting Old Testament and New Testament passages; exploring what scripture says about heaven and hell; correcting misunderstandings about God. 

But in this we differ: I read a Revelation a little differently. Where Evans promises the Church a rapture before suffering, I see continual encouragement to stand firm, to endure, to overcome. There must be something we are challenged to overcome. In fact many believers have endured severe persecution and intense suffering. Many continue to do so. Whatever we face before the new Heaven and the new Earth, we face with Spirit-power. John the Revelator is diligent to prepare us for the challenges. We should read with eyes and minds and hearts open. 

Ah but indeed, the Best is yet to come. And I will be delighted to stand before the throne with my brother in Christ Tony Evans, whenever and however we get there!
