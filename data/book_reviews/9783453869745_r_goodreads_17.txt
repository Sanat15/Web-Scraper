Rating: 2/5

I had hoped to like this book more than I did. Teddys family wins the lottery and after that a ragtag group of people come to live with him. The lottery plot felt like it faded into the background at times. In fact, at one point we learn how some money was spent in a passing conversation. I wasn’t really a fan of Teddy’s uncle Frank or the random vampire guy that showed up. This book was also pretty dated.
