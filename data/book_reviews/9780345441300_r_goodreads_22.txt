Rating: 5/5

It was unique to learn about a the movie making process from the writer's viewpoint rather than that of the director or actors. This also was unique in that Irving was the original god to these characters in novel form. It was shocking how much say he had over the film. Kudos to Lasse Hallstrom for allowing it. This was a short read with many detours away from The Cider House Rules. It's not necessarily a comprehensive view of how the movie was made. However it offered a glimpse into Irving's working mind and his hold over the story, as well as the story's hold over him. A worthwhile read.
