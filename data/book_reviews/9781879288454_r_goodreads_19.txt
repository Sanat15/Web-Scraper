Rating: 3/5

This is primarily a devotional work. Part emphasis on the physicality of Christ on the cross (I had shots from Passion of the Christ in my mind as I read). Part Erasmus-style urging Christians to be better, do more, try harder---the usual "Do your best, and God will do the rest" nonsense. Part comfort of the gospel: a real, tangible forgiveness for you, bought by Christ. Part focus on God as a tender and loving Mother who protects and provides for believers as children.

The Penguin edition contains the short text and long text versions.
