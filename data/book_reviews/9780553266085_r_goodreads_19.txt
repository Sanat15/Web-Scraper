Rating: 3/5

3.5 stars. 

This is a Christian romance originally published in 1902. At first it started out kind of preachy and I wasn't sure that I would like it. However, as soon as I allowed myself to be drawn into the world I enjoyed it very much!

There are some religious ideas presented that I don't see in the Bible (why do I always feel the need to say this about religious themed books?) but watching Evelyn's gradual and complete transformation through the book was sweet and satisfying.
