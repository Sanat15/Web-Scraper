1815 Battle of New Orleans. 12 year old twins Pierre and Andrew, with their help of their free father, have escaped their cruel master. Hidden in the swamp, they wait for their father who has promised to come back with their mother and baby sister. When he doesn't return, the twins decide they must find a way to rescue them before they are sold. But they are not alone in the swamp. The swamp is filled with alligators, pirates, and troops both British and American. Pierre and Andrew know they need help to succeed, but who can they trust?

A little slow moving, but an interesting adventure story.
