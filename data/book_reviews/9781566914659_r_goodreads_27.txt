Rating: 5/5

While I was a frequent traveler 10-30 years ago, things have changed. I gathered a lot of good notes from this book. Things as varied as how to download offline city maps, using an inexpensive SIM chip, buying quick-dry cloth washcloths for countries that don't usually have them, suggestions to buy tickets to save time standing in LONG lines (even if it costs more), loading a VPN, using Google Earth to "walk down your street" before actually booking your B&amp;B or guest house, and so forth. Great ideas! 

I put together a multipage check list before heading off to Budapest soon, so I am appreciative of this book's great advice. It is good even for seasoned travelers, if their travels are a bit dated.
