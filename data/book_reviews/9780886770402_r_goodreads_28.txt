Rating: 2/5

This is probably my least favorite Elric 'novel' so far. 

It is hard to call it a 'novel', as there is really just a couple of stories, one more of a novella, and some musings by the author about how he got where he is. 

The largest portion of this book is a story about 'Sojan the Swordsman' and has nothing to do with Elric. 

Feels like this might have been a marketing ploy ack in the day.
