Rating: 5/5

This seems like a must read for parents of both boys and girls... has practical tips for keeping children/youth safe (from sexual abuse, injury or death from guns, stranger kidnapping, and more). I wish I could find the book to note some other specifics for future reference... so more to come later...

A few I can think of now:
1) Don't emphasize not talking to strangers as much as teaching children who they should talk to if they need help (a woman, not a man).

2)Teach them what to say and do if someone tries to molest or abuse them... "Don't do that or I will tell." Also advises that if anyone ever tells them not to tell... then tell. If anyone tells them not to scream... scream.

3) Listen to intuition and don't worry about hurting someone's feelings when it comes to children and their safety.

4) Teach your children about the 2 factors that make sexual assualt or abuse easy to carry out: privacy and control (PC). 

5) I liked the discussion about worrying as well... it made me feel that I have less reason to worry if I proactively carry out steps to help my children be safe.
