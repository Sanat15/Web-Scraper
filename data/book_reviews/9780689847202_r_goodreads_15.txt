Rating: 2/5

This is a traditional Dora the Explorer book that goes hand in hand with the television episode. I usually do not like these type of books as I believe storybooks should be books and not an interactive television show. this does have interactions asking the child to do things or find things (which is nice to help teach children BUT shouldn't be listed as a storybook but as a teaching book.) So reading this out loud to a special needs child or a child not old enough to do the interactions is kind of 'lost' within the story. But it was an okay book.
