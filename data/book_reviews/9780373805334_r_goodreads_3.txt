Rating: 2/5

Yep, boring.

The heroine left at the altar or cheated on who is then rescued by the H is one of my favorite tropes. Of course, if this had happened to me I might be waiting for Johnny Cash to come sing Folsom Prison Blues to me in the pokey.

The h is left the day before the wedding when her fiancee runs off with what is now her ex-best friend. It takes a few months, but the H asks her to go into a MOC so he can install a computer system on a small island in the Pacific. Slooooowwwwwww buildup to sex more than romance. The couple come back to NZ where they end up seeing the bad couple. Awkward, but the h and H manage to maintain the high road which discomfits just about everyone especially the bad couple. Nice hints that huge social consequences are lying in wait for the bad duo as they live in a small town and ALL sympathies lie with the H.

I think this is boring is it's so realistic. There are no OW swooping in to leer at the H and sneer at the h. The H gets a little bossy when he thinks someone is interested in his new wife which is irritating but understandable considering what happened to his first fiancee. We don't even get much parental outrage or interference and the irritating bad guys are around too minimally to generate much angst.
