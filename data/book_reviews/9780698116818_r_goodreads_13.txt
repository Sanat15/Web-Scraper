Rating: 2/5

This was written by the same author as the Ben Franklin biography I wrote. I liked this book slightly better because it talks about a part of history that is barely mentioned and yet has been built up into a monumental event - Plymouth Rock. When I was growing up, I thought it was an important rock that explained the first people on the Mayflower. But it isn't. It's just like any other rock. Definitely an interesting foresight into social studies classes.
