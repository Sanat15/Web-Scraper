Rating: 1/5

DNF. Already dropped. Sorry, Nora Roberts. This was the first book by you that I’ve attempted to read and I was like “wat is this….”

There were some funny lines. The narration switched between the two main characters so frequently and very randomly. I knew what was going on and I could follow it, but it was overdone and therefore became annoying. I’d like to just read what’s going on without constantly having to know what the other character thinks/sees. How can a story progress like that?

Also just 8% in and the characters were already being flirty with each other despite their claims of hating the other. Seemed like Roberts was trying too hard to create a flirty environment. It felt forced
