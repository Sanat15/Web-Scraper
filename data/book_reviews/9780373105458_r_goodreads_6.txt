Rating: 3/5

I quite liked this story by CL it’s really very sweet. When Quincy’s brother enters her into a competition to go on a date with a famous pop star Joe Aldonez without her permission. Quincy is in total shock when Joe plus his manager and people from the magazine turn up on her family’s doorstep. She’s furious with her brother, but agrees to go to London and participate as she doesn’t want her brother to get into trouble. Joe flirts with her and there are some kisses thrown in but Quincy is determined not to be another in what she’s sure is a long line of conquests.

While I liked this offering from this author, I’m not sure she should have made the hero a famous pop star supposedly living the accompanying lifestyle, as there was no evidence of him living that wild lifestyle, no women throwing them selves at his feet, there were no parties or booze, in fact it was ridiculous how he was always out and about on his own and without any security guards. Plus he just didn’t behave like you would expect from a pop star in fact he was just too nice. Plus the author resorted to telling instead of showing the very few mentions of him performing, and his interaction with his groupies. But the chemistry between the two leads was easy to believe. As I said before this was quite sweet but should not have been set in the music business.
