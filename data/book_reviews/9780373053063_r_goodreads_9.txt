Rating: 3/5

I love DP when I’m in the mood for soapy angst. This book delivered on both fronts at the beginning and sucked me right in. The H really wrongs the h and he realizes it right off the bat. He goes about atoning, but then things get iffy. The H got on my nerves with his whining and in insecurities. He really needed his ego stroked and he ran hot and cold. I liked the h a lot more as she had a backbone and some conviction. They fall into a marriage and the H is supposed to be making everything up to the h but he’s mostly just projecting his self-esteem issues onto the marriage and being unnecessarily cruel. All we hear about is how ugly he is and what a terrible lover he is! Would’ve been 4 stars if not for that. Nonetheless, it was super entertaining.

Triggers: pregnancy loss, slut shaming
