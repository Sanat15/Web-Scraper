Rating: 5/5

These are beautiful stories! I am always impressed by a collection that seems themed (not that it's necessary for my enjoyment) and, here, each story is linked to the next one, if only, for example, by a mention of mangosteen and durian. (While reading such well-written stories, it's a bonus to learn new things, like these southeast Asian fruits I'd not heard of before.) 

A sense of place also reels me in and that element is strong in each story, from the first, set in the south of France, to the last, on a southern island of Japan. In between we get Paris (including the tunnels of its underground sewer system), Singapore, the south of England and Cambridge, Massachusetts. Each place feels authentic and indulges all the senses.

Several stories feature recurring characters and each reveals more of that particular woman, though each story can stand on its own. Most impressively, perhaps, every story ends with a paragraph that is beautiful and fitting in relation to what came before.

Tara L. Masih was right about the stories getting better as you read on! I thought "Presences" was my favorite until I read "Life in the Temperate Zone," and even now I'm not sure if maybe the last story might not be my favorite. It's wonderful not to have to choose.
