Rating: 3/5

This is the first book I've read in the Claire Camden series by Audrey Peterson. Unfortunately, it seems that our local library does not have copies of the other two books in the series, so I will have to keep my eyes open for those books at used bookstores and library book sales and such. 

The story was engaging and I loved the art and literary references. In fact, I might just have to read more books by Wilkie Collins. And I did a brief search on J.M.W. Turner, but I should try to learn more. Overall, I thought this was an entertaining and quick read, perfect for reading poolside on a hot summer day.
