Rating: 5/5

This is a tremendous book. I taught it in our adult Sunday School class at church and the people loved it. I would highly recommend this book to any Christian as it will help in the areas of life that are often struggled with. I cannot think of anyone that does not struggle with fear, discouragement, loneliness, worry, guilt, temptation, anger, resentment, doubts, procrastination, failure, or jealousy. Not only does Dr. Jeremiah describe the dangers each of these giants produce but he also gives biblical as well as practical advice on how to overcome them.
