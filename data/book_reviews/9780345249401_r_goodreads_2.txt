Rating: 4/5

This is a very enjoyable old fashioned clean novel. I only recently discovered this author. On the blog Word Wenches they were talking about old romances they used to love and Nicola Cornick posted The Beau and The Bluestocking by this author. I immediately purchased it as I love Nicola's books also. Now I'm on a bit of a run of these as I'm currently just looking to read easy uncomplicated books. 
As I said easy quick and sweet. Having said that, I did want to slap Catherine, the main character a couple of times. Was quite indulgent and selfish at times. But all's well that ends well!!
