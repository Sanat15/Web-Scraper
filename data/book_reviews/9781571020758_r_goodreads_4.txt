Rating: 5/5

I love this book. It's vintage and the elves are called brownies like the little elves that have a need to serve and do chores for their household master, in this case Santa Claus and Mrs. Claus at the North Pole. The little brownies smoke pipes and act like little sprites. They are dressed like Gnomes and not in the typical red and green elf outfit. The narration gives you a tour of the North Pole on Christmas Eve.
