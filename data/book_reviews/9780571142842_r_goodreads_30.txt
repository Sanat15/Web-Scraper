Rating: 5/5

A very well told story of the lives of an ordinary rural family in Ireland. It's about a widower, his two sons and three daughters, mostly focussing on Moran, the ex soldier now in his fifties. The author vividly and concisely portrays memorable characters, their relationships and the way they live. Moran is a quiet, proud, stubborn man who is fixed in his ways. A memorable read. Highly recommended.

I have read two of McGahern's other novels, That They May Face the Rising Sun (about a small rural community) and The Dark, (a coming of age story, semi autobiographical), both of which I also highly recommend. I will endeavour to read his other books as I enjoy McGahern's writing style and the way he develops his characters.
