The story of two lost people who find each other. Uh-huh.
 
Actually, the premise is good. A former government agent is persuaded to take one last case and find a kidnapped young girl. While tracking the kidnappers he runs into a mysterious woman and her three search dogs, also looking for the same girl. So far, so good. Then it starts to fall apart.

The kidnapping is solved almost as an after-thought. She is wounded in the retrieval of the kidnap victim, he takes her away to a secluded place where she can begin to heal and he falls in love with her. The dogs somehow disappear into thin air, she claims she is cursed because everyone she loves dies, he insists that he won't, and then he is wounded in another plot-line, and she learns to loves again. Oh brother.

I would like to know what happened to those dogs, though.
