Rating: 3/5

Jane is dismissed from her teaching position when the music master kisses her and joins her aunt who’s just inherited a fortune after marrying her employer who passed. The entire neighbourhood comes to investigate this most unusual will and this new lady.

This was fine but definitely not special. I enjoyed the romance between Fenwick and Jane based on mutual tastes (books) and forged in country pursuits. Jane’s unhappy suitor Mr Swann owns a flock of birds and those scenes were hilarious. This one has a lot to do with morality (Jane’s unhappy circumstances, her aunt’s sudden money and condition) which wasn’t quite as enjoyable as her other books.
