Rating: 3/5

One thing I love about storytelling is that when you tell me a story, even if it's a story I know well, I'll see something new about the story. Another thing I love about storytelling is that when you tell me a story, I see something new about you.

Richard Van Camp, whose people live too far north to have horses, uses the question "What's the most beautiful thing you know about horses?" to learn "some odd and sweet things" about horses. "And I also learned more about my family and friends. I found my curiosity was very contagious and soon I had the whole town talking and wondering about horses."
