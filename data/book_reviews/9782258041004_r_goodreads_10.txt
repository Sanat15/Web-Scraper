Rating: 1/5

As one of the children in this book might say, "Yucky!" Vonda should've stuck with Star Trek novelizations (I'm guessing; never have read any of her novelizations - never having read any of the Star Trek novelizations - I can only make guesses as to her writing talent in that world). None of the characters behave in character, except for perhaps Chewbacca; even C-3PO isn't nearly as annoyingly pedantic. And the story? Besides being, as others have pointed out, generic, it doesn't at all fit in with the Star Wars universe. Especially once you take out the standard "remnants of the Dark Side/Empire are left over and are trying to rebuild" plot points, you're left with the bit revolving around the character of Waru and its plan to "gain energy by annihilating the Force of our universe with the anti-Force of its own."

Anti-Force?!

Bless you Martin Freeman

I gave up about halfway through and skimmed the rest of the book just to finish it because I couldn't take the inanity or the mediocrity any longer.
