Rating: 4/5

This was a nice surprise! Great detective story about a jockey/photographer who learns about backroom dealings and crimes at his jockey club. He’s also been asked to find a long lost granddaughter. There is a lot going on but it never feels overwhelming. This book takes its time. A good number of clever surprises. The characters are well thought out. Dialogue and actions felt real and sensible. 

I had never heard of Dick Francis before reading this. Turns out he was a successful jockey, besides being a successful, prolific crime writer. Will be reading more of his. 

4.5 ⭐️
