Rating: 4/5

I read the series out of order, but it didn't really matter. This is more goofy fun and an easy read which makes it a really nice book to read in-between my more "challenging" books.
You can follow the story fine without reading the previous novels. Sure you're not going to know the history behind all the characters, but as you're meeting new characters as well as becoming more aquainted with the old ones, it's ok.
