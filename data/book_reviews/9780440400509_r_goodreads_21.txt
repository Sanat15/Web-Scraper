Rating: 3/5

25 years ago, I gave this book a 4-star rating. After all, what’s not to love about a boy who is starved for love and beauty and who finds it all in the wilderness of the Limberlost? 

This time through, my thoughts were mixed. I listened to the audiobook, which I thoroughly enjoyed for the added suspense it brought to the story (in spite of the narrator’s mispronunciation of almost every old-fashioned word!) The last few chapters, however, when they were “read out loud” to me, seemed too maudlin and too pat. I winced at how easily all troubles were smoothed over. 

I guess I’ve read too much Elizabeth Goudge in the intervening years to enjoy that like I used to.
