Rating: 4/5

Carrie didn't want anyone to recognize her for her fame as a ballet dancer, so she took the name Boonie Hughes (her friend's name) when she went in a two-day vacation in Florida. She met Alex Taylor - a mysterious attractive man - and soon he became her first lover! Even though he touched her like no other man, Carrie had to go back to her ballet life. She didn't correct Alex's information about her name and the other things she lied about because she thought that any man could not figure out in her career life, and any way she wouldn't meet Alex ever again because he was too angry of her rejection of him in their last day together. But, she was totally wrong! 

I enjoyed this book immensely. Different with greet depth and intensity. A book to keep and a recommended read.
