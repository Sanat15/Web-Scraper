Rating: 3/5

This is my first book by Linda Chaikin, and I really enjoyed it. I’m giving it 3 stars because it felt more like a setup for the rest of the series than a fully standalone story. Even so, I enjoyed reading it and getting to know the characters. One thing I especially appreciated was how strongly some of the characters’ faith in Jesus was portrayed. Coral’s mom, in particular, stood out to me as someone who was very grounded and steady in her faith.
