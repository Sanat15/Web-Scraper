Rating: 5/5

I cannot be certain of the name Martha Grimes. For
It fits so well in this story of crime, people, mystery.
The drawings are pretty, and the curtains are drawn
I'd certainly be wrong if I was not to fawn
Over this excellent book of poetry

I bought it for six bucks, read it in two sittings and really had a great time. I wonder if there is a solution to this puzzle? Its wildly confusing if you are looking for one, but wildly fun if you are somewhere in the middle. Makes me want to read more easy-to-read poetry
