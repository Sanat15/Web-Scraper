Rating: 4/5

Landmark rocks are important. They should be preserved at almost any cost. It's easy to blast them out of the way, but they can't be made in any factory. Once they're gone, they're gone forever.

I've been keeping a notebook of landmark rocks, with the emphasis being on those in Grand County, Colorado. The Plymouth rock is the most recognized rock in the Unites States ... and it's been broken at least twice, and chipped away by souvenir collectors. (And I'd be surprised if even a single one of those rocks is still around.

Fritz acknowledges the age of the rock, but she never says that it's a type of granite. If she had included a couple of sentences about its geologic history, I might have kicked this up to 5 stars.
