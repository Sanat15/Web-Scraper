Rating: 3/5

This series is my total guilty pleasure. And before anyone gets on me - yes, Anita is a total Mary-Sue and sits very comfortably on her high horse. But I've come to realize that I don't read this series for her, I read it for the other characters.

Incubus Dreams has Anita knee deep in a vampire serial killer case. Some vampire group is killing female strippers and everything seems to point to the Church of Eternal Life and their master, Malcolm. On top of the case, Anita is trying to balance her personal life and the emerging of new powers between her, Richard and Jean-Claude. 

What I liked was learning more about the pasts of some of our characters - mostly Nathaniel and Damien. The supporting cast really makes up this book series because, let's be honest, if it was just about Anita I would have chucked it so long ago. And let's not forget my boy Jason - he's still here and still going strong as my favorite character.

But I've also noticed a shift in Hamilton's writing here. It seems as if the plot is becoming more 'how much sex can I include and with how many different partners' as opposed to what made the books good in the first place - Anita's actual work (both zombie raising and police work) and the intimate (not sexual) relationships with our core group. I hope that Hamilton starts to reign it in and get back to what it began with before it just becomes a series about sex.
