Rating: 2/5

The book that brings such difficult and ambiguous subject should be written with care. While it's obvious it was an ambitious project, the accomplishment didn't go that smoothly.

The writer tries to show different aspects and different characters in the reporting-like stories. She travelled around the globe to write some of them, kudos for that. You can see the emotional involvement of the author and while it only shows how good of a person she is, it stops me from feeling the story myself. We are served the author's opinions and conclusions and it bothers me a lot. The book itself is a bit chaotic and although it's short, it took me a bit to get through it.
