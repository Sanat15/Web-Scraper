Rating: 3/5

I've read some Vivian Alcock in the past, and really loved them. This one was not my favorite of hers; the set up was great, and I enjoyed the complexity of the characters, particularly some "good" adult characters with definite flaws, such as the dad in the book. The three main children in the book reminded me of a citified variation of the characters from The Secret Garden: the spoiled rich kid, convinced he is ill, the practical kid with a great disposition, and the main character, grumpy, in this case, from a recent illness. The mystery of the painting and the "Sylvia game" itself were interesting, but the resolution seemed a little pat. I still enjoyed, just seemed a little routine and derivative compared to Alcock's other books.
