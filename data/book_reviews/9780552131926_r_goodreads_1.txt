Rating: 5/5

One of my favourite read again books by S Tepper, it's very hard to find now and very expensive too, so if you come across it , snap it up. 
It's again a S Tepper strong female lead character who is trying to live her life but has a lot of hardships and obstacles to face. It delves into the realms of mysticism ,magic and romance as Marianne a good person; encounters strange adventures in different worlds to survive against dark forces.
Highly Recommended
