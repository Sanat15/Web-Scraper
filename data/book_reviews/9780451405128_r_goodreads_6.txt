Rating: 4/5

A Bit of Some Fantasy

The story line is very engaging. Ali and Andre have a great deal of depth to their characters. There is some "wokeness" with the frequent use of "Allah" by Ali. The whole area covered by how different religions have different names for God is completely unnecessary to the rest of this HR. 
Andre's father is portrayed as a "miracle worker" of a physician/surgeon. He operates successfully on Ali for an ovarian cyst. This being done in 1870, beggars believe. That really is a fantasy event imagined by the author.
