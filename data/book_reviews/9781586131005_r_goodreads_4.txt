Rating: 1/5

I've met a lot of people who follow Falun Gong and they seem really nice so I wanted to give it a try. But I didn't like anything about this book. It's talk about supernatural abilities, about suffering to pay for karma, etc. When the book started talking about flowers floating on your head and 3 pillars touching the sky and rotating and... Well it just wasn't for me. I didn't like the way Li Hongzhi talked about it as if it is the only right and true practice and everything else like Buddhism and Doaism is not good enough, and only Falun dafa can bring you true enlightenment. Started feeling a bit cult-ish to be very honest. Irrespective, if believing in all this makes people happy and live a good life, than I support it! It just wasn't for me.
