Rating: 2/5

"A Tradition of Pride" is the story of Lara and Rans. 

A sad story based in Mississippi, in which the h is trapped in an unhappy marriage to a philandering husband, until the H enters her life. Her snooty family does not want divorce to tarnish their legacy, hence she turns a blind eye to the skirt chaser. The H on the other hand can see what she is going through, but all he does is taunt her and blame her for not "responding" to her creepy husband- even after the latter attempts to rape her.

This was terrible and absolutely revolting. Poor h. She was hungry for love and affection, and I felt really bad for her. The ending was the only redeeming factor.
