Rating: 5/5

I really enjoyed the deep dive in the human psyche. Faced with impending death, the characters are forced to surrender to their deepest thoughts, fears, and longings. The depth of each character is astounding for a 200 pages book. 
The author gives us a glimpse into a 24 hours ordeal of 5 people and a lifetime of unhappiness of 1 person. Ironically, the so called “Judge”, the author chooses to keep as a mysterious psychopathic figure. 
I really recommend this books be read with time and understanding of how complicated we are as humans.
