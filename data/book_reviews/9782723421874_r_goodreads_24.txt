Rating: 4/5

The Infinity College story's intensity increases as we get closer to the final battle. We learn more about tragic Hotaru's story and why her father is the way he is. We also learn about the solitary lives of the outer scouts, their talismen, and why they are on Earth at this time. We discover the purpose of the formidable Sailor Saturn, the most deadly of the scouts, and Moon gets a power boost thanks to the power of &lt;3.

Will the scouts be able to take down Pharoah 90 and the newly introduced Mistress 9?!?! Will Saturn awaken and bring the end of all things?!?! Well, you won't find out in this book. Maybe the next.
