Rating: 4/5

Donald Duck and the Magic Stick is about 3 nephews, who are ducks, who leave their uncles tailor business to make it out on their own. They each go their separate ways and when it's time to go home, they each get a magical item from their boss. Each of them were told to always watch it, so no one would steal it. The first two nephews go to an innkeeper and they get their items stolen when they were sleeping after they told the innkeeper what the items do. Then, they figure out the innkeepers trick, they devise a plan to catch the innkeeper using the third nephew’s magic beating stick. They catch the thief and get their items back. There are many themes to this story. The main theme is to not be a thief or be greedy. The innkeeper was very greedy and wanted the magical items, and in the end, he got in a lot of trouble and got beaten up by the magic stick. I honestly really liked this book because the plot was developed well and it really had a lot of depth especially for a children's book.
