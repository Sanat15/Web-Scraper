Rating: 4/5

This was Patricia Highsmith’s first novel, initially published in 1950, and most often credited as being the first “Murder Swap” novel! 

It inspired the Alfred Hitchcock movie, by the same name but if you THINK you know this story, because you watched the movie, you don’t!

Mr. Hitchcock actually changed quite a bit after the initial conversation between two strangers on the train, where a suggestion was made that the men “swap murders”.

“Some people are better off dead”, Bruno remarks, (to Guy) “like your wife and my father, for instance.”

Guy doesn’t take his train companion seriously, until his wife Miriam ends up dead.

Bruno, feels a plan WAS agreed to that day, and he will apply RELENTLESS pressure on Guy, to follow through on his end of the deal, as he becomes more and more OBSESSED with Guy. 

And, a DOGGED detective name Arthur Gerard is convinced he has got it all figured out. 

I figured it was about time that I actually read this CLASSIC which inspired my favorite thriller of all time-“THE KIND WORTH KILLING” by Peter Swanson. 

I am definitely GLAD that I did!
