Rating: 4/5

Summary from the back cover:
"On the very eve of their heartrending divorce, Annie and John miller forged anew the fierce, unshakable bond of love that joined them together, body and soul.But, come morning, Annie was mysteriously gone- for good- leaving her husband shattered, her daughters shaken, her entire family far less than whole.
Now, six years later, Annie just as mysteriously reappeared, eager to fill the aching void in her husband's heart, at her family's core. But the wounds went deep.The scars were thick.Could John and the girls make room for Annie....and her astonishing gift of love?"

My review:
This was actually a good book, Annie left to protect her family, even though they were already divorced but the Hero thought that she left with another man.
She made lots of sacrifice and upon her return, wants to see her family she haven't seen for six years. By this time, the children are all grown up and her elder child at first resents her for having left.
The hero decided to give a chance to the heroine but this time, the whole family is in danger.
