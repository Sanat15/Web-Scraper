Rating: 5/5

I co-edited this book. I love it of course. Also I promise you of this date that there is not a better document out there on Paris night clubs circ. 1949/1950.

Boris Vian himself wrote the text as a tongue-in-cheek insider's look at the Saint Germain des-Prés scene that took place right after the war. Here you get images &amp; text regarding his legendary clubs - Tabou and Club St. Germain. It was the perfect location for the arts at that time to meet and dance. Miles Davis, Jean-Paul Sartre, Jean Cocteau, Buster Keaton, Simon DeBeavour, Raymond Queneau, Duke Ellington and the list goes on and on. I am very proud of this book!
