Rating: 5/5

This book is perfect if investing has ever felt intimidating or out of reach. Nancy Dunnan breaks everything down in a clear, no pressure way and shows that you don’t need a lot of money to get started. The advice is practical, realistic, and focused on steady, low-risk growth, not hype or shortcuts. It feels like a friendly guide walking you through your first steps into investing with confidence. A great read for beginners who want to start smart and stay consistent.
