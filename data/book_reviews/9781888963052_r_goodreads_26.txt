Pretty high action in this volume!

The war is on! And our heroes are kind of strewn about the valley. I like that the characters keep getting split up and then meeting again always under dire circumstances. The villains plan was stopped right at the last second and do to pretty funny means. However, getting rid of our first antagonist (who was pretty bad) means that we're going to finally meet the big bad soon!
