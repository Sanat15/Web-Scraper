Rating: 3/5

A little slow through the first half of the book but the accident site scene really made up for it. I only dock it for being slow albeit it's short length, which I feel should have been extended to really flesh out the relationship between Isaie and Marcellin. Entertaining novel overall though which leaves some room for interpretation on Isaie's mental state by the end of the book, closer to a high 6/10 than 3 stars out of 5
