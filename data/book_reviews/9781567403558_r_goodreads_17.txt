Rating: 3/5

This is a strong 3.5 or 3.6. I didn't give it a 4 only because it's sort of light-weight. But oh so charming and satisfying! It's chick lit at its best. Great characters, good writing, and a fun twist on the Romeo and Juliet theme. I enjoyed Calling Invisible Women, also by Jeanne Ray (Ann Patchett's mother), and I plan to read Jeanne Ray's other romps. They're quick reads and great fun!
