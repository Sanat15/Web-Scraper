Rating: 4/5

I'm kinda surprised to only see seven reviews for this book. This is the lovely story of five children who find themselves enrolled into a new sort of school, a Zoo School. The desks have animals in them and their teachers are zoo keepers. With the motto of "Let the animals be your textbooks" what's not to love? I liked how each child in the story had something unique or different about them. Drake was scared all the time of everything, Kitty loved animals, Robin had multiple illnesses and sicknesses making her frequent the school nurse, Leo constantly slept and Ursula just wanted to go back to the routine of her old familiar school. Each of the children learn valuable lessons and make fun transformations by the end of the story. I most enjoyed the wise expressions of the missing principal Ms. Font throughout the story. I think it would make a lovely read aloud for first or second graders. Third graders may not like the ending as much.
