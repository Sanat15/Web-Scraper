Rating: 5/5

For a kid's book this was pretty nuanced and for a kid's book from the early 1970's it was forward thinking. 

The protagonist is described as having "the gloom" and often wants to be on her own with peace, quiet and books and nature, I mean basically she's a depressed kid who loves reading (this speaks volumes to me!) She lives with her dad as her mother has fucked off to South America and has had a kid to another man! How many children's books of the 70's dared to have anything other than a nuclear family or a widow/widower as parent. Anyway, these little nuances set the tone for the story. Lively has come up with a great folkore-led tale of a dangerous ancient hunt being reignited by a vicar who wants to put on a display for his money-raising fete by getting the kids of the village to perform a representation of the hunt in antlers and costumes. 

Very good storytelling. Not only does Lively conjure up a sense of atmosphere and local setting, she perfectly captures the awkwardness of being a kid and the isolation when you don't feel accepted, either by adults or other kids. 

5 stars
