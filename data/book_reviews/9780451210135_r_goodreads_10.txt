Rating: 5/5

HEA, yay

They say people don't change, but in romance they do. You'll smile to read the wonderful changes that Louise has dreamed up. When you step into her world, you are transported back in time and immersed in the Recency period as if you were living right there with her characters, feeling their feelings and walking beside them. Her characters are each unique and filled with emotion. The plot unfolds taking you with it swiftly, hoping it won't end too soon.
