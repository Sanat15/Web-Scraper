2.5
Beaton may be good with prose, but this book was so fuckin creepy wtaf. The love interest kept defending her from being assaulted but also assaulted her MULTIPLE TIMES. Also he kept insisting she was too young and emphasising how he viewed her as a child but then threw that all away right at the end to marry her.
The historical accuracy of the world building was actually good though. Also, Minerva was (until the end) a fun take on a regency heroine.

Got this as the first short available book on borrowbox because I needed to listen to something and here we are.
