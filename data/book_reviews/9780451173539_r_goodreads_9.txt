Rating: 5/5

So delightful. Well-developed characters whose journey to love is rocky but inevitable and whose chemistry is obvious from first sight. I did find it annoying that the heroine fell victim to that horridly weak of all plot devices—the unshakable belief that the hero really doesn’t love her or want to marry her but is somehow shamming it even after he kisses her passionately and tells her he can’t live without her. But she didn’t allow this belief to lead her to utter stupidity and didn’t even entertain it long so it was ok. The rest was so fun—a mystery and much adventure in figuring it out, a surprising twist at the end and a satisfying HEA—though the epilogue really should have at least mentioned Hester and how the fortune played out.
