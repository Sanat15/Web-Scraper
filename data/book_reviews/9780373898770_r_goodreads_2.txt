Rating: 3/5

This one was pretty good. A spoiled brat rancher’s daughter bristles at the Alpha man who is temporarily subbing for her dad while he is recuperating from an accident. The heroine begins as insufferably rude, mule-headed and just plain unlikable until the hero gives her a well-deserved set down for letting her father down with such behavior. This is when we finally see a turn around in the heroine’s personality. It isn’t that she transforms overnight from a spoiled brat to a perfect angel. But the hero’s words manage to pierce through her hard shell so we can finally see to her warmer, softer inside. The heroine manages to redeem herself beautifully, even if she still has fits of temper and sometimes makes dumb decisions. Her love for her old horse and what happens to them during a storm is a particularly sad moment but one that allowed me, as a reader, to emotionally connect with her. There are a few more minor misunderstandings before these two get their HEA and it was sweet and very well-deserved :)
