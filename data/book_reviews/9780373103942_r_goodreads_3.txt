Rating: 4/5

Claire would let nothing stop her

Once Claire had decided she was going to marry Rod McGilbray, the rugged engineer, nothing would change her mind. 

It didn't matter to Claire that their meeting in Africa had been the result of a rather mean trick. Or that her father advised caution. It didn't even matter that Rod was dead set against marrying her. All that mattered was that Claire loved him. 

But was love enough? Claire soon discovered she was facing far more than she had bargained for..
