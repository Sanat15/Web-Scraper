Rating: 3/5

Danny visits the museum and wishes that he could play with one. One is living and he makes friends with the Dinosaur that makes things fun in the city as Danny rides him out the museum doors. They do some fun things like going to a baseball game. Danny and his friends play Hide N Seek with him and when it is his turn to hide....well how do you hide a Dinosaur?

Another fun read from my childhood. My Mom purchased several of these books that I had from a traveling door to door salesman. Makes me think now of how lucky Mom was that it wasnt this day and age!

The illustrations are remarkable for a children's book.
