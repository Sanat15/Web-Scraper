Rating: 5/5

Vegan Virgin Valentine is a book written by Carolyn Mackler, and its about Mara Valentine, who is a good girl. She gets good grades, shes a strict vegan, and shes running against her womanizing ex-boyfriend for valedictorian. She even got into Yale. Shes all her parents could have ever wanted, and everything was going perfectly.. until she got the news from her parents that her sixteen year old neice, V, was coming to live with her. She wasn't exactly V's biggest fan, and she didn't want her to come and ruin everything for her. Which is basically what she did. Now, Maras incredibly stressed, and it doesn't help that shes falling for her twenty two year old boss, who one of her friends happens to be in love with. Shes even begun to dream about grilled cheese sandwiches. 
I really liked this book, and i would recommend it to anyone.
