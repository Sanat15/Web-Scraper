Rating: 5/5

"If by the age of forty a man is still disliked there is no hope for him."
Finally somebody just came out and said it! :)
Jokes aside, The Analects is full of wonderful sayings, stories, and advice from Confucius and his disciples from around 2500 years ago. Even though the introduction and appendices were tiresome at moments, they were well-researched and worth exploring, as they attempted to separate fact from fiction and provide clarity for readers of all different levels of familiarity. Recommend for anyone interested in philosophy, religion, and generally becoming a cooler person.
