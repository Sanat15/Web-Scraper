Rating: 3/5

I liked the book up until after they were kidnapped then it just seemed like not much happened for a long time. Like it could’ve summed up everything within 4 pages but it was drawn out and not a lot happened in that time. I was hoping most of it would be in the theme park and honestly I thought from the name of the book they were going to be in the land of dreams which would’ve been way cool. The animatronics and how they made them was cool and shown well in the first half of the book. Wish the second half was better but other than that it was a good read
