Rating: 4/5

This is such a cool rustic collection. The tone is quite different from the other Best American Short Stories I've read, there's a heavy undertone of loss, not deprivation, but desolation and boy do these stories sting, even the most hopeful of them. My best in the collection are Along The Frontage Road - Michael Chabon, Seven - Edwidge Danticat, Puppy - Richard Ford, The Heiffer - Melissa Hardy, Watermelon Days - Tom McNeal, Family Furnishings - Alice Munroe &amp; Surrounded by Sleep - Akhil Sharma
