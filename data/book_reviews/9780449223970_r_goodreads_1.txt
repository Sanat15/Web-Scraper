Rating: 3/5

Mistress Mischief is the better of the two books. Max and the things that happened to him because of Freddie were so humorous. I loved them both. Four stars. The Lady Who Hated Shakespeare was another story entirely. To the very end I did not like Cordelia; she was bad tempered, opinionated, and impulsive. She did not deserve Miles and I have no idea what he saw in her. Two stars. Averaged to three stars.
