Rating: 5/5

I was looking for some good old school science fiction to cleanse my palate. As much as I love modern fiction and historical non fiction, I selected this after feeling in a rut. This is an excellent collection and includes short stories by the masters of science fiction, Isaac Asimov, Arthur C. Clarke, Robert Heinlein, etc. Also, it's immensely satisfying to read a short story and put the book aside for a while. 

Right now I'm trying to find some current science fiction writers that match up to the standard set by the old masters. One which may be promising (I've ordered it) is The Windup Girl by Paolo Bacigalupi.
