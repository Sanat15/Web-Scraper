Rating: 4/5

To be truthful, at this point, getting to dip back into the world of Nero Wolfe &amp; Archie Goodwin has become a true comfort to me - like returning to Hogwarts. The consistent world-building over so many books &amp; decades has given me a great oasis in which to retreat when the world gets a little much.

That being said; the compilation books, like this one, are often much more satisfying for the world than they are for the mystery.

In this case; the 3rd story ends pretty satisfyingly, the 2nd story’s end is a bit deus ex machina, and the 1st story’s ending is pretty absurd.
But again, I was just happy for 3 new (to me) Nero Wolfe stories to read and each easily had more than enough charm to keep me from dwelling too much on any plot-shortcomings.
