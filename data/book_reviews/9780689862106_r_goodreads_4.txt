Rating: 4/5

Excellent! Really enjoyed that! A very good Angel tie-in novel. Both of the authors have previously written Buffyverse novels and it shows! The characters were spot on to the show, I could hear and see them in my head. The references to the show were right and there were even some mentions of events from previous tie-in novels which were also good. I especially loved the interaction between Faith and Wesley it was very well done. The storyline was good and well thought out really enjoyable you couldn't' ask for more really. If you enjoy Buffy and Angel there is nothing not to like about this book.
