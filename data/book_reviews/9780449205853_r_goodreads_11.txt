Fun story, loved Freddie and the duke

Sorry to see this series end. It was so amusing even if the stories were formulaic and similar. Lots of jealousy and attempts at revenge, a young miss and a titled gentleman. The unreligious vicar. The surprising curate. Lady G and her malapropisms. A comedy of manners and mishaps while the six sisters sorted out their futures. Full of errors, but who cares? Too late to fix them now, and the stories still entertained me, especially this last one.

I think what makes this family work is how even the vicar and Lady G know what's what and do the right thing when it matters.
