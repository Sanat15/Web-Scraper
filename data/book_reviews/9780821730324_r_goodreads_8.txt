Rating: 4/5

This book is an account of the 1941 baseball season from Dom Dimaggio (and actual author, Bill Gilbert.) This is an interesting season as it was the year of the last .400 hitter (Ted Williams) and it was the year of Joe Dimaggio's 56 game hitting streak. Of course Dom was Joe's brother, and he was Williams' teammate in the Red Sox outfield so he had a ringside seat for both accomplishments. The 1941 season was also the last before America's entry into WW II.
