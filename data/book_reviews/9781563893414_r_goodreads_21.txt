Rating: 5/5

My friend Tom gave me this to read because of the plotting of the story included in this collected work trade. Once again I was amazed by how Frank Miller (FM) was able to take Batman (BM) and put him in a no win situation, a situation anyone else would just quit and run away from. But that is what makes BM so unique; his ability to see his weakness before his enemies, and find a way to get stronger in spite of himself.
