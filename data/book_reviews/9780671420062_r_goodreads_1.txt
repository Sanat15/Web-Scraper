Rating: 4/5

A great little book to carry around in your bag, whether you're studying Shakespeare, working on a play, or just enjoy great written works. It has a brief synopsis of every one of his plays, in chronological order (as of 1999), a brief description on the characters, and a brief recounting of the plays staging history (which, didn't really make sense to me, maybe because I'm not familiar with most of the productions, especially as you go farther back in history for them), as well as a brief biography of Shakespeare's life, discussions of his poems and sonnets, the history of theatre in Shakespeare's day, and a description of the major Shakespeare festivals. In short, it's a little bit of everything, in brief.
