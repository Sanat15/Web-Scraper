Rating: 3/5

My friend took this out of the library, since she had had experience with choose your own adventure, she expected there to be more "unfortunate" endings in this one. I think, she said that the endings didn't make sense and you can' loose in any of them...which assumed was good.
SO....she gave me the book and dared me to die in one of the endings :) And, reading one of these with the single goal of loosing was actually quite fun. I think, in the end, I got crushed by the ceiling and floor. Then I read it again, and ....the creepy vampire village people got me. :D Fantastic read, I havn't read these types of books in awhile, they are quite fun.
