Rating: 2/5

This book was well written and well researched but the story just didn't work for me.
Jessica has to fulfill the conditions of her father's will before her birthday or she doesn't get her inheritance. Her inheritance is an extensive collection of books. John Dryden tries to help Jessica secure her inheritance.
I liked Jessica and John, and I am book crazy, but for me the story was way too much about the particulars of the book collection and not enough about John and Jessica.
