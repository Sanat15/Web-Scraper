Rating: 3/5

This book had potential. Very inspired by hard boiled novels and with clear parallelisms with “Vertigo” (which I think is even playing in the movie theater), the author can create an asfixiating atmosphere that is very effective. But the plot is flimsy and under developed. The reveal of the bad guy is not surprising or that interesting. The female characters are so flat and stereotypical they become a caricature. He attempts the genre femme fatale/troubled damsel but even giving allowances for a supposed genre novel it falls as flat as the characters are. The main character, Darman is also without depth, his passive and nihilistic attitude makes him boring and his view of women and life is between sad and disgusting.
