Rating: 5/5

I have lots of respect towards John Douglas and Dr Robert Hare. If you want to understand psychopaths you have to read both writers books. I have read 'Mindhunter' and currently I am reading the 'Obsession'. 
I totally agree with Mr Douglas that psychopaths are dangerous people (I refer them as different species) and should never be freed, they are not fit to live amongst us. They are not normal.
All they now is destroy and bring chaos to where ever they live. 
If you are one of the lucky people on earth who never met or had a relationship with a psychopath you will never understand the message John Douglas wants to give. I know naive people who believe in goodness in every person I am afraid to say No! not everyone has goodness in them, trust me I was with a psychopath and I saw evil on this earth and I am extremely lucky to be alive.
