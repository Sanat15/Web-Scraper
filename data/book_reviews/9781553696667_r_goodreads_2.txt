Rating: 5/5

If this were a movie, it would be rated R. It’s an autobiography of an Ethiopian girl who runs away from home after attempted genital mutilation at the hands of her mother and aunt. She is adopted off the street by Emperor Haile Selassie, but is banished from court after falling pregnant from a general’s son. She is kidnapped by the general’s wife, but escapes the kidnapping and many subsequent near-death experiences, including a mass murder following student protest. As a single teenage mother with no family and no money, she struggles to provide for her child amidst the political upheaval following the emperor's assassination.
