Rating: 4/5

synopsis:
teresa and cort shared a night of passion several months ago and have been avoiding each other since. when a bomb in a nightclub kills 15 people, cort is there as part of the arson investigation squad, and teresa catches the call. teresa is in the process of temporarily transferring to another division while her partner (who is also cort's brother) is on his honeymoon, so she figures that she won't have to be around cort for that long. however, once she does get her transfer, her new supervisor believes that cort is on the take for the mob and wants her to dig up the information on him.

what i liked: i liked cort and teresa. they were each strong characters and fully realized. the developement of their relationship was honest to their characters. they were also very protective of each other, and teresa didn't believe that cort would be on the take, not even for a second. i liked that the reveal of the big bad was a little off centre; not the norm.

what i didn't like: not much. the story drew me in and held my attention. there was a little too much of pov from other characters, other than teresa and cort (such as teresa's new supervisor), but other than that, it was an enjoyable read.
