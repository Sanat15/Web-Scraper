Rating: 5/5

Jokes about rationed toilet paper and firing squads abound in this unflinching poke in the eye of the foolish superpower that was the Soviet Union. 

The cover announces: Uproarious enough to crack up the KGB!

Was it ever!

Like, the one about the Russian actress who went to her doctor who asked if she'd ever had a check up there. And she purred, "Nyet, dahlink, but quite a few Poles."
