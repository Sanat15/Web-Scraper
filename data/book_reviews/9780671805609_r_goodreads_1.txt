Yesterday I came out to Park Ridge from Chicago to help a high school friend prepare his natal home for eventual sale, his parents having died. Last evening we collected all the books in the house and organized them so that his brothers and sisters could determine which they wanted to keep, which they would be willing to discard. Among them were several books I used in the public schools, books I'd neither seen nor thought of since leaving my parents' home in Park Ridge.

One especially uninteresting book was Larousse's compact dictionary, pictured here. The local schools required French so when I moved here in fifth grade and until I graduated from junior high, I carried a copy around. Upon entering high school and being given a choice in the matter, I switched to Latin, never having learned much French.
