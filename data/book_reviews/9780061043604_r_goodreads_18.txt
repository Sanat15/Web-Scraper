Rating: 3/5

2.5 stars- Wow, this is such a change from other selections by Ms. Sayers. I was expecting another story involving her main character, Lord Peter Wimsey. He is a clever, somewhat unconventional amateur sleuth. This book however did not have anything to do with the Wimsey series, at all.
This was a story told through the use of letters written by several of the characters in the book. They are used to document how a death came about, and the victim's son is putting them together to try to come up with some proof that his father was actually murdered.
I did not care for this style of story-telling and only continued hoping somewhere Wimsey would step in and solve the mystery as only he knows how.
Very disappointing read IMO
