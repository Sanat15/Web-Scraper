Rating: 4/5

Set in 1965, this is the final book in the Church of England series, by Susan Howatch.

This novel covers a lot of issues that are still in play in the 21st century C of E. 

Howatch’s writing style is an enjoyable combination of academia and fiction, with a lot of P.G. Wodehouse-style humour thrown in for good measure. 

An enjoyable read, though perhaps a touch long.
