Rating: 4/5

Initially was turned off by the “wokeness” of the 2024 updated version of this book. Tried to find the prior version on audiobook thru the app I use but was not available. Because I thought the content would be good for me, I persevered. If you can get by the unnecessary wokeness like I did, there’s a lot of good stuff. I’ve been sober 7+ years, strongly believe in and am thankful for the power of AA and the steps. This book was a good immersion back into the steps from a female perspective.
