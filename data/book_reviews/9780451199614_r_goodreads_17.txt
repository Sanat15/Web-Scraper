Absolute delight of a farce. It's Heyersque in some ways: fluff-for-brains heroine and dorky honorable hero are besotted with each other but don't believe the other is; the ensemble around them is hilarious as they all engage in their own screwball plots and hijinks; I had to look up new-to-me vocabulary. The prose is witty ("He prayed the good Lord’s rod and His staff to comfort him, for his own sure as Hades did not.") and the hijinks are peak silly, and I had a great time.
