Rating: 3/5

Sweet, feel-good read.
Full of coincidence and hope this tale of two children looking for home is endearing. A well paved and easy read that despite the happy ending and twists of fate manages to avoid being twee or forced. Believable despite the good fortune and coincidence that holds it together.

I read as my daughter had to read the War Horse for school...she enjoyed so I felt we should try more by the same author. This is not as emotionally packed as War Horse but equally well written with engaging characters.
