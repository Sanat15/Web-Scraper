Rating: 5/5

Goodness me! The best one yet!

Setting off to northern France to capture some mutinous sailors eventually leads to Hornblower becoming governor of Le Havre, and eventually the overthrowing of Bonaparte. 
With some sad deaths of beloved friends along the way, he finds himself caught in France's interior when Napoleon makes his escapes from Elba and reasserts himself in the country. 

Hornblower's valiant attempt to escape to the coast ends with him being hunted down like a dog, court martialled and sentenced to death...
