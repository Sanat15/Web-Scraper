Rating: 4/5

Hazelden produces many wonderful daily devotional resources for people in Recovery. And, like all others, this is a great daily source of inspiration and direction. This particular volume is aimed specifically toward those who are members of the LGBTQ community as well as the 12-step Recovery community. It does a wonderful job of connecting these two realities for people who find themselves both queer and addicted. A great resource toward health and healing.
