Rating: 3/5

Preeti's review inspired me to read this. It's very typical Marion Chesney, but one of the better ones. I found one particular bit very funny:

The H arrives to see the h after a separation. He is furious that she appears to be unaffected by the passionate love letters he sent her. She is bewildered, and finally they go through the letters together. He re-reads them and recognizes that they are actually very formal letters, and that while he thought he was conveying his profound feelings, she was reading letters that were unusual only in that he seemed to inquire frequently about her health.
