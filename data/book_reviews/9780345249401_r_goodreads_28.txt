Rating: 4/5

The last in the Eversley family trilogy, Freddy does make an appearance but this is about the adventures of Viscount Pamyngton and Catherine Denham one of six sisters and who honestly is a bit naive and a bit wayward at the same time.

Thankfully on this occasion Pamyngton appears to have grown up a bit and in the process of dealing with Catherine and her sisters scrapes and problems finds himself I think. I've never known a heroine quite so prone to being kidnapped as her!

A satisfying conclusion.
