Rating: 4/5

I LOVED the array of bodies show in this book, along with the frankness that accompanied it (I have NEVER read a children's book that mentions nipples and then asks the readers where theirs are!).

My only (tiny) quibble is some some of the text. They talk about arms then ask, "Where are your arms?" Like I said, they have many bodies of different abilities and configurations, but what about the child that doesn't have one of those body parts at all? I'd have preferred a more general question like, "Do you have arms? Where are they?" Or "Can you see someone's arms? Where are they?" Minor, and it won't stop me from recommending the book, but it could have brought it from a 95% to a 100%.
