Rating: 4/5

Whispers was an irregularly published semi-professional magazine of horror and dark fantasy that Stuart David Schiff published and edited in the 1970s. It was named after a fictitious magazine that H.P. Lovecraft named in one of his early stories in Weird Tales. This fourth anthology appeared from Doubleday in 1983, containing stories that had first appeared in the magazine along with some new material. It has a nice cover by Stephen Fabian that captures a true maritime horror feel, and would be at home on a William Hope Hodgson novel. The book contains sixteen stories, many of them from the biggest names in the genre of the time, including good ones by Hugh B. Cave, Tanith Lee, Charles L. Grant, David Drake, Frank Belknap Long, William F. Nolan, etc. My favorites were Into Whose Hands by Karl Edward Wagner and Creative Coverage, Inc. by Michael Shea.
