Rating: 2/5

certain parts were VERY dated but some of it was helpful. i'd say skip the 3rd chapter (at least pages 48-58) unless you are interested in reading about some crazy biological/physiological determinism that no longer is validated. overall, i'd say it still has something to offer parents trying to help understand and better "parent" their children. the breakdown of what to expect from children all the way to age 10 was interesting, as it reminds one that parenting is a long-term occupation and growing-up is not something that is complete at a certain stage.
