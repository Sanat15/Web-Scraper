Rating: 3/5

‘A Rebel at the Chalet School’ is best described as a novella and somewhat of an oddity, at that. Published as a stand-alone title, it’s actually the second part of ‘The Chalet School and the Lintons,’ (which I haven’t read) and the follow-on in the series after ‘Exploits.’

Part-potboiler, part fill-in, ‘Rebel’ still manages to retain something of its original splendour with good characterisation and compelling writing throughout.
