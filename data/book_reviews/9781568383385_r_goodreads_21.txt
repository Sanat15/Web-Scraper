Rating: 5/5

This books does something to me. It opens cracks in my heart I didn’t even know existed and lets the light in. It’s the second time I’ve read this book. It’s one of my favorite books of all time. ♥️ It’s one of my “desert island” books for sure. Literally Melody speaks from a perspective, outlook, and heart posture I never ever hear anyone else speaking from. How are we so lucky and blessed that she opened her heart wide open to tell us this story? Thank God for Melody Beattie!
