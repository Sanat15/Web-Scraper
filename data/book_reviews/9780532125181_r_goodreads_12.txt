Rating: 4/5

A strong recommend for this novel from the very beginning of the Golden Age of Science Fiction.

Is this an old book? Yes (1939). Could this have inspired later authors? Absolutely. The About The Author notes that the first encounter here partly inspired Alien. My reading of this book leads me to believe that it is partly responsible for Star Trek. And who knows how much later work was influenced by this pre-WW2 novel.

It is a good story in four acts. It has some early SF tropes of the elite scientist saving the day, the all-male starship crew exploring the universe, and science coming to the rescue in the end. But these are tropes partly because of this book and others like it from the 30s and 40s.
