Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed Fred Mustard Stewart’s transatlantic historical fiction epic, “Century.” The novel focuses on a two-branched family, documenting their early beginnings in Italy in 1880 and then to their eventual crossing of the Atlantic Ocean to New York City. There, the author gradually follows and explores their lives as they establish themselves in the U.S. as Americans, penetrating Fifth Avenue’s early mansions and to the ghettos of Brooklyn where we witness the birth of the Mafia. 

The book then slowly progresses to Hollywood in the 1920s, spawning film moguls, sexy sirens, the silver screen, celebrity romances, and Hollywood dreams. Spanning four generations and two world wars, Stewart’s saga chronicles the triumphs, accomplishments, aspirations, fortunes, and tragedies of this turbulent clan.
