Rating: 2/5

Tom Chantry has come out west to buy cattle for a company back east. He is a man who does not believe in guns, which gets him into trouble the minute he steps off the train.

He buys a herd and then hires a manager for the drive to market. The manager is a known thief.

Chantry believes that reason solves problems, better than a gun. He spends a great deal of time trying to figure out how French Williams is going to steal his herd from him, and when one of the hired hands is going to kill him.

He is shot. His men abandon him. He keeps the herd moving by himself until he runs into a Kiowa war party.

The action really heats up when he arrives at the railhead.

There is a woman named Sarah who wants to see Tom dead. We do not learn who she is or where she is from or why she’s so hates Tom. The last action scene is confusing.

This was not L’Amour’s best.
