Rating: 3/5

People on the US planning on starting a war against Russia and China. A nuke fired from a US sub so close to Russian soil when it hit the Chinese cities, China would respond with their powerful arsenal. The world in chaos, all leaders, all militaries ready to complete their mission until there was nothing left. The launches, people instantly wiped out, panic spread the ones that survived. Disease, pillaging, raping, children separated from.parents a complete breakdown of humanity. The World went berserk, the weapons left everything to dust and the survivors did the rest. Ben Raines ex military, writer decides to capture the destruction in words driving around the country interviewing people. He will become General of the Rebels and a fight against the US President will happen. Different societies against each other. The political diarrhoea is filthy. Johnstone went over to top. Should of just cut back of this and it could of been a good survival series.
