Rating: 5/5

I read this book after getting to know someone who had gone through a divorce. As I moved forward, I kept wishing I had discovered it earlier, because I could clearly recognize many of that person’s behaviors reflected in the book. It felt almost as if the author was describing patterns I had personally witnessed.

In the end, however, I came to an important realization: while empathy is valuable and even necessary in a relationship, it cannot replace personal responsibility. A divorced individual must also be willing to change, to reflect on their experiences, and to reach at least a basic level of self-awareness regarding their own emotional wounds. Empathy should not mean carrying the entire weight of another person’s unresolved issues alone.

Moreover, social and family circumstances often play a significant role in this process. External pressures, expectations, and unresolved family dynamics can slow down or complicate the healing journey, making personal growth even more challenging.
