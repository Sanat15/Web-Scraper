If this was a soap opera, I suspect a script editor would have rejected the drafts as overly fantastical and one has to assume that Rivera was winding Gladys March up with a lot of this. Here's a few highlights of what I mean:

 - Finding himself suspicious of his mother's account of how babies are made, the young Rivera decides to dissect a pregnant mouse in order to find out for himself, thereby driving his mother into a fit after confronting her with his findings. 
 - As a teenager, Rivera gives a sermon on the non-existence of god to an outraged congregation, causing the Priest to flee from the demonic youth. 
 - Practising human cannibalism for a few years after seeing how well it worked for some cats who later escaped from their dubious residence before invading a lunatic asylum. 
 - Watching President Hindenburg perform druidic rituals at a Grunewald ceremony. 
 - Being suspect of attempting to assassinate Trotsky. Twice. 
 - Making failed attempts to assassinate Porfirio Diaz and Hitler, the latter due to being restrained by some rather complacent German communists. 
 - The Kahlo family seem to have handled assassination attempts on their lives rather well, as Frida's sister apprehends two Nazi assassins at gunpoint and Frida goes one better by doing the same to four assassins sent by General Cedillo.
  - There are also appearances by Sorolla, Picasso, Chaplin, Rockefeller and Stalin.
