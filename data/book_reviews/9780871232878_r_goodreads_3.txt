Rating: 5/5

A BRILLIANT READ

I have only positive thoughts on this book and a very high regard for the author and his dear wife. If it wasn't for their persistency in prayer to do God's will through their battle of spiritual warfare and illness God's voice may never have been heard by the thousands that have read his book and had their lives turned around. I only wish I could have bought the book 'Destiny for the Throne' myself but sadly it must be out of print. I will buy all of Paul Billheimer's books because they read differently from other books somehow and encouraging too - just what I was searching for.
