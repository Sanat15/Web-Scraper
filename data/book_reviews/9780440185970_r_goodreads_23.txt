Rating: 2/5

I had this book on my list for a while and started reading it with great hope. All my hopes were crushed and I'm sure there was a lesson to be learned, only I don't know what it is.
The despair started with the use of the word "fat" to describe a character flaw and utter undesirability at the very beginning. Then there was the Persuasion/Cinderella trope of the old maid being exploited by her nasty family (which I don't get because she was supposed to be also the "beloved aunt", cannot she be both and NOT be remorseful and resentful?) which to me it came across quite toxic. Also, the hero is a heartless gambler, player and womanizer who really has no morals or limits and it toying with the heroine for 90% of the book. The cherry on top was a horrible humiliation the heroine goes through at the end, followed by no grovelling at all, so she straight up goes to him and forgives him.
The story itself was not badly written or edited, but the logics of it were all wrong, character's actions were not suited for the time period, very over the top, and not in a nice, funny way.
All characters were annoying and there were no redeeming qualities to it.
The writer is from a different period, which should make me forgive some stylistic choices, but then I remember some fantastic 80s reads and remain set on my low rating this time.
