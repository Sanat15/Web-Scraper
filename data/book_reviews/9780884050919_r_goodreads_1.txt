Rating: 1/5

Ugh. Not a long book but very detailed into the chaotic life and times of Napoleon and Josephine through to her death. A bit tedious yet I found myself fascinated to learn more about this Emperor whose home I toured in Elba. He was clearly mentally unstable, narcissistic and addicted to receiving “adoration” from Josephine. Josephine, although seemingly in love with Napoleon’s devotion, behaved as an abused partner unable to free herself from his emotional grip. This read like a sad and wearily unhealthy love story with a bad ending. Money cannot buy happiness or love. And the power seized through narcissistic overtures will always come to an end when you stumble in your own doo-doo.

I forced myself to finish this book and now it’s finally over.
