Rating: 4/5

Teo Sandoval possesses an otherworldly power that unleashes storms and lightning. With his dark and mysterious demeanor, he is often feared and labeled as the devil's spawn. Disconnected from society and lacking humor, Teo keeps his distance. However, everything changes when Melanie Daniels enters his life, desperately seeking his assistance.

I found the story to be enjoyable, and the characters were particularly appealing to me.
