Rating: 5/5

Namako: Sea Cucumber caught my attention with its beautiful title and cover. Immediately upon reading, I’m drawn to Ellen’s innocent perspective during her visit to Japan. In a short period of time, she encounters some of life’s challenging lessons, such as cultural differences, dangerous/complex people, injustices, and illness/death. I love Linda Watanabe McFerrin’s stunning prose, which captures many memorable details of Ellen's journey to find her place in the family and world. A wonderful novel for all ages.
