Rating: 3/5

Read while traveling so here are my quick notes:

Nice! I do like forced proximity and mtb.

Leads have excellent chemistry and neither apologizes for who they are, nor worries about the instant connection. They accept it and are indeed a good fit. 

Grandpa is peppery and helps things along in the ways that need it without being too convenient.

Idea to send Jack to drive the mail to learn hard lessons -- and the daughter to inherit on special dispensation -- interesting twists on the norm.

Oft I complain about "it happened off, bad actually" with big scenes of import, but Jack hieing off to figure out the will, and right the situation, while leaving Cecily wondering and fretting worked well.

And then just enough uncertainty and in the aftermath to provide some extra pangs and good moments of emotion before the big HEA. A very well-earned one, too.

Nothing of the ton or the usual ball-then ride-then let's get to Bath-then visiting Regency trappings and that was just fine! I like a different spin on a usual setting.
