Tut was young when his father passed and his older brother took over. But when his brother passed, there was no one left, but Tut to take over. He was still young, but he stepped up and took his brother's place.

This is based on the story of King Tut's life, but I can't say how accurate it is. However, it is a nice story and it should at least interest young children into learning more about King Tut and Egypt.
