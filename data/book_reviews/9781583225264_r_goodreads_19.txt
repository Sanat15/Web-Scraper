Rating: 4/5

This might be the scariest book I've read all year. Or decade. That being said, I am finding myself a little skeptical that the government would wipe all of these negative health problems and their direct cause under the rug and then go ahead and put fluoride into our drinking water &amp; toothpaste. It's just seem ridiculous... Or would they? I can see why they wanted to hide the facts and go forward with the atomic and industrial advances to help win the war and make our lives easier in the 40's-50's. And not to mention how much the big business spends to lobby the government to ensure that their profits keep coming in and to keep the cost of production down-- sometimes at the cost of safety. 

But it also scares the crap out of me it's all very true and still going on. Because as Bryson points out we do seem to have a lot of current mental, physical and environmental problems today, and the long term effects of even small amounts of fluorine have never been studied. All the evidence laid out covering the worker's and direct neighbors to factories health and environmental problems that can be directly linked to the use of fluorine was shocking. How we as a people seem to be getting continually sicker as our exposure to fluorine is now in everything - carpet, plastic, teflon, water seems very logical and obvious! This is also, sadly, another prime example of how money and power make the world go round and that the "little people" are disposable and even silenced when they don't go along with it - even when they are getting hurt.
