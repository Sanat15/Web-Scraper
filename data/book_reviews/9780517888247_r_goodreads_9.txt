Rating: 4/5

Lovely collection of essays covering a broad range of topics adjacent to natural history and evolution. Each essay was thoughtful and distinct. Some of my favorite essays include - different types of eclipses, darwin's father's misogynistic plant poetry, and how whales evolved. Some essays were exhausting to read. Gould takes his time on explaining topics which I did love, but occasionally goes on tangents that, while related and interesting, take away from the overall point and don't stick to memory.

Very worth a read and will teach you something new with every essay
