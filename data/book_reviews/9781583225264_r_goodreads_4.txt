Wow.
This book may not be what you are expecting.
You will be schooled on the cold war, the creation of the atomic bomb, and national security.
The book focuses mainly on the dangers of fluorides in respect to the environment, and the workers exposed to them while working for big industries. You will learn about the strange circumstances surrounding the decision to add fluoride to our drinking water and the problems that it may (or may not) be causing.
You will not hear as much about toothpaste as you might expect. You will learn of the many different ways you are exposed to fluoride.
If you thought that you didn't trust big business and the government before, you will be shocked to find the extent of the shady business deals, human experimentation and cover ups that have been going on for decades......
A must read for any US citizen.
