Rating: 4/5

In life there are 2 sorts of travel guide style books, one that feels instructional (the read something like "Go here... do this eat here, visit there..."), while the other feels more gossipy. These books would have some facts about a place, but they feel more like they are trying to give you a flavour of a place, and pique your fancy. Lonely Planet books fall very much into the former category, while "Off the Beaten Path" books focus more on the second.

This is a good book, it feels less facty that other travel guides that I've read, but it is different, feeling less like they're giving you direction. This isn't a bad thing, if you go in reading the book with the idea that you're looking for guidance as to where to go next, rather than trying to identify places, once you've arrived.
