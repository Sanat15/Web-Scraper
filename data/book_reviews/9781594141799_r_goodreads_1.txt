Rating: 2/5

The story was so-so. Not bad, but not great. There was some good scenes and I liked the scene when Robyn finds the kitten and when she tells Killian that she named him S.A.M. (Secret Agent Man). I will say this book is the perfect example of why you shouldn't judge a book by its' cover. The story wasn't bad, but the cover is cheesy....puhhhllleeeezzzzeeee.
