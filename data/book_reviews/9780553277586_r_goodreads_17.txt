Rating: 5/5

I am a huge fan of WWII historic fiction and this novel set in Norway was so good. Johanna has been living the life of a 21 year old in Oslo when the Nazis invade. During the initial bombing she meets Steffen who ends up working for the Resistance. Soon she is drawn into working for the Resistance and she takes on a much bigger role when she moves back home to the west coast of Norway. Her love for Steffen and her country is at risk as she plays a dangerous game. Loved it!
