Rating: 4/5

I am currently finished with the book “Suicide Squads” by Richard O'Neill. In this book it takes you through the Developpement of submarines and planes that the Allies and Axis powers used in WW2. It tells amazing stories of people who risk their lives to complete a mission for their country. Next it also tells you about how most of these missions were suicide missions. Last it tells you what the weapons on the submarines and plane were and a little about what they were made of and how they were built. If you like to read and learn about WW2 like me, I recommend this book for you, it has action and great war stories that I have never heard of. Although I did lose interest in some parts where it would just talk about the making of machines, the stories definitely made up for it. The book is only 251 pages long, it is quite easy to read and exciting. One thing I think the author could have changed was the length of the pages. Overall, I really liked the book, I held my interest most of the time and was an exciting read. Lastly if you like learning about submarines or WW2 stories and missions this book is for you.
