Rating: 3/5

Again a trio of murder mysteries. All three involving some sort of bludgeoning during the commission of the murder. Generally this was a pleasant read, the first and third stories are well done Rex Stout books. The middle one bugged me because this was one of the occasions where Rex hides a clue from the reader. Usually he does this by hiding it from Archie, the POV character, and while it works to convey Archie's frustration at the situation it does feel like cheating. Still, this book is good and worth reading.
