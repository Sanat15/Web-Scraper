Rating: 2/5

There were things I liked about this book, namely that the style was consistent throughout and that the voice of the protagonist was refreshing and hardly ever annoying. Someone called it a Huckleberry Finn in Kuwait. Truly, it is an adventure, I guess, but it's more like Alice of Wonderland in that everything was so lunatic that I almost expected the narrator to ask if I was actually still reading it. Many a times I considered leaving it, but a book has to be really bad for me to do that. I was hoping to actually learn something about Kuwait and Desert Storm but, in the end, the story was so far-fetched that I couldn't confidently believe anything about it.
