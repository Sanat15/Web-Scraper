Rating: 3/5

It's difficult not to compare this book with Three Lords for Lady Anne since it follows up on characters from the previous book and the hero references Lady Anne as his ideal of a wife. The book is okay on its own but it lacks the humor of the previous book and it suffers from the lack of a strong heroine. I loved that the H was a good guy: no mention of previous dalliances and he treated the h gallantly from the beginning. The h was a nice person but I wish she had developed more gumption. That said, her behavior was consistent throughout the story and I could see how the H and h would suit and have a happy marriage. I also enjoyed the secondary male characters (H's uncle, best friend and brother) but I think the book would have benefitted from a strong and likable female secondary character (the h's sister and aunt were no fun at all and Lady Anne appears only briefly). Overall, the book was nice enough, just not great.
