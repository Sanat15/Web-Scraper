"Boss Man from Ogallala" is the story of Casey and Flint.

A sweet love story set in a cattle ranch in Nebraska. 

Our h is a wild tomboy, and a devoted daughter to the owner of Gilmore ranch. When her father has an accident and is bed ridden, the bank forces them to hire a man to run the ranch in the meanwhile. The h is infuriated and disappointed. When the H, a famous rancher arrives to temporarily takes over, she does not hesitate in expressing her displeasure. She behaves like a loud shrew and shrill brat, taking joy in verbal banters and retorts, until she is chastised by her family, as well as the H for her attitude. She soon has a change of heart and finds herself falling head over heels in love with the H. But ofcourse, it wouldn't be a romance novel if the h did a logical thing of confessing her feelings. Instead she spends all her time pushing the H away, and fighting with him. Looooads of drama later, they confess their feelings and the book ends on a sweet note.

Enjoyable read! This series is addicting.
