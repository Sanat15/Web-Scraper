Rating: 3/5

This children's book falls right in the middle of ratings for me. A story that was not written by the original Pooh author, it lacks the humor and feel of the originals. Furthermore, the illustrations are too smooth and resemble computer imaging more than art. But the lessons taught are wonderful, and it is Pooh and Roo together, so that is pretty darn cute...which is all to show why this books falls right in the middle at 3 stars for me.
