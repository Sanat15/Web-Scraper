Rating: 3/5

Civilization has grown complacent after mankind has evolved for millions of years, creating an utopia of sorts were humans live for leisure and nothing more. Alvin, the youngest member of said civilization, yearns to discover what is outside of this seemingly idyllic city. 

The world building and story about Alvin discovering what is earth outside of Diaspar is interesting enough, and Arthur C. Clark had a very direct, but immersive way of writing in this book that made imagining the world and background very easy to me. 

The characters were paper thin, though, and it made it hard for me to invest in a person like Alvin, that seemingly had no personality of his own besides having a desire to see more of the world for his own reasons. 

3/5 stars
