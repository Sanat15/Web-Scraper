Rating: 3/5

These are very silly. The repetition of foreign language endearments is tedious, but the evil villain is satisfyingly evil and the pride of the Philadelphian heroes is amusing. Plus, you get passages like this:
"'Vile being! To attempt our destruction by means of exploding edibles. What could be more reprehensible! To employ a harmless Drackenbergerwurst as an instrument of murder!'"
