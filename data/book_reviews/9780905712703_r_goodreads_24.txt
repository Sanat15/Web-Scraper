Rating: 4/5

Anthology containing five books; I have reviewed each book individually under their own entries.

The Grapes of Wrath
The Moon Is Down
Cannery Row
East of Eden
Of Mice and Men

I can say that while the texts within this anthology are complete, the Table of Contents is incomplete, page numbering crazy, Chapter links missing, and even links to the start of each book completely missing! Thus, this ebook is readable if you want to read all five books straight through, but navigation can only be done once you make your own bookmarks for the five books and their internal chapters.

Overall, a good anthology, despite the table of contents issue. Several typographical or transcription errors, and most of those were minor enough not to render the text unreadable.
