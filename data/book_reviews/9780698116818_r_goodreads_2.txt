Rating: 5/5

This book is awesome! I am thoroughly prepping for a Cape Cod vacation this summer! It is unbelievable--this rock was carted around to five different places, broken in half, than that piece broke in half again. For dozens of years in any century it was walked on, climbed on, muddy, dirty, and ignored. There is probably no chance a pilgrim on Dec 22, 1620 stepped on this rock. The ship didn't go near the rock, they were half dead and frozen, and nobody was hanging out on the beach in MASSACHUSETTS in December!
I still can't wait to go see it ;)
