Rating: 5/5

The third of 3 editions (that I know of), presenting some of Bateman's best on high quality paper in a high quality book.

The large pages and the printing quality make these books must-have for any art-lover. Even animal-lovers would be well served with these beautiful paintings.

My only regret is that I can't bring myself to tear out and have some of the pages framed to display on my walls. Not only would that damage the books, but it would also mean that the image appearing on the back of the page I had framed would be lost to sight.

Always an immense pleasure to take these volumes out and just casually go thru admiring the depth and details of each painting.

Should I ever win the lotery, I would absolutely get a Bateman original!
