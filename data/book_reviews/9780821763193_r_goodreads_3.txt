Rating: 5/5

The first 30% of the book (roughly the first 1/3) was slow. I honestly was not sure I would make it through the book. It was very dry.

But, oh but…

At that 30% (or 1/3) part, I couldn’t get enough.

I couldn’t put the book down.

There was not enough time in one day to allow me to continue reading!

I loved it! I haven’t read a good love story in quite some time and this was not only a great love story, I loved this love story!

This book made me hurt, made me anxious, made me sad and made me laugh out loud! (Literally, there were times my husband stopped me and asked what was so funny because I was laughing out loud!)

This was a fabulous story that is mixed with mystery and love.

A must-read for any romantic!
