3.5 stars
The eldest Inugami passes away leaving behind a will that can be cruel and spiteful. One the will cannot be read until unless the eldest grandson has returned from war. One thing is for sure, the eldest inugami has taken care of all if else conditions. The contents of the will after it was read triggers a series of murders. Who is murdering the heirs of inugami ?
Well I would call the events surrounding this book and the ending quite bizarre . I feel as if Kindaichi never did anything much, just that evidence keeps popping up now and then and he collates them at the end of the book to come to a conclusion. 
Overall a fast paced thriller with a peculiar sequence of events
