Rating: 3/5

The story is a lot like Alice in the wonderland and through the looking glass

some characters:
Tanaquil -sorceress
Tanakil- Tanaquil’s ‘evil twin’ 
Lizra- Tanaquil half sister, empress
Honj-Lizra’s fiancé, Tanaquil’s love 

notes to remember story:
Tanaquil follows a red unicorn into a mirror (really just her own mind and or consciousness)
she steps into a world the exact opposite of her own 
meets her mirror image self Tanakil and has to work through troubles that are really her own 
she returns home on the red unicorn (her hearts desire)
and Finally with all resolved she gets her HEA with Honj
