Rating: 4/5

Larry Baldwin decides to move back to Georgia from New York. He is hired as a regional salesman for an office supply firm and he's good at his job; you could say ruthless to the point where the owner is grooming him to take over the firm. As the boss is showing him around town, a young woman comes out of a shop and Vic is quick to let Larry know what a "bad girl" she is. Larry meets her in a bar a couple weeks later and although she's a full generation younger, he's struck by her. He realizes Vic's badmouthing is not accurate for who Cody really is. Despite the age difference, love is kindled but can it survive?
 Re-read in 2021. This is another book that I associate with my grandmother recommending. It was a quick read over Memorial Day weekend. I'm glad I was able to find a copy in the state library system to be able to read this again.
