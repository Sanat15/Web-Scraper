Rating: 5/5

The Gift of Fear is a book that I feel every person (especially women) should read. Mandatory. Full stop. 
This book should be required reading for parents. 
Politeness can kill you. Teaching your kids to be polite no matter what could cost them everything. Humans are animals and we were created with survival instincts and incredible intuition that can keep us alive in dangerous situations. We just need to stop letting denial, false reasoning, and politeness get in the way of the animal instinct. Humans are the only animals that will actively cooperate with their own victimization. And why? Because we hate being impolite. 
I love the way this book gives you permission to let that inner mama bear loose. Trust your intuition about bad situations and people. Being a jerk is preferable to ending up dead or injured/ignorantly placing your children into the hands of predators.
