Rating: 5/5

I loved Kishon´s satire on domestic and wild animals, pets and their human owners or humans and their pet owners, depending on the way you look at it, accompanied by witty illustrations by Ninoslav Kunc in its Croatian translation. I especially liked a new explanation of Pavlov´s conditioned reflex theory, explained by a dog and the story of an African grey parrot named Whowho. If you want to laugh your head off, this is the right book for you!
