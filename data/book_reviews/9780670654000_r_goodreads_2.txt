Rating: 4/5

This beautiful story captures the fun and magic of a city kid’s day in the snow.

Young Peter wakes up to a landscape completely transformed by a fresh fall of snow - all the cars are covered, as are the roads and footpaths. This is definitely a day for adventure - tree branches to shake, snow angels to make, and so it turns out that Peter did have a wonderful day, even taking a snowball home in his pocket to save for later!

I have to admit that I’ve only recently become aware of this magical book, as I hadn’t heard of it here in the UK. 

Anyway it was a pleasure to read, because in many ways, Peter’s excitement brings back so many wonderful memories of times past, and actually if I’m being totally honest, I still feel excited myself when I open the curtains to a fresh fall of snow! Delightful. Thanks Tina for putting this on my radar.
