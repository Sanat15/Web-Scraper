Rating: 3/5

my parents were married the weekend of woodstock. this details what they were missing, but from the organizers' standpoint. little snippits from such folks as wavy gravy and tom and lisa law from the hog farm give the story some life. some of the photos are pretty nice, too. typos run throughout the book. reading this oral history made me remember the causterphopia i felt at woodstock in 1994 and how much of a hassle it was to try to leave when I was sick. it is pieced together very well,like a collage, by taking transcriptions from various participants and organizing them into coherent and corresponding thematic time lines.
