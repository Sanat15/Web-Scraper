Louis L'Amour, eyes glowing red, typewriter on fire: what if set in the 1600s and the hero canonically has second sight and learned astronomy from a Muslim from Samarkand and goes around saying things like "man was born to die. do not hasten the time"

Genuinely felt like I was reading a fantasy novel at times, and I loved every second of it. Features a thoughtful interracial romance and some fascinating musings on cultural mixing and how it can broaden our thinking. And he assimilates to HER culture, not the other way around.
