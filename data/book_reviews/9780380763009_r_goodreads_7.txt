Rating: 4/5

Finally, after reading "The Anonymous Miss Addams" and "The Questioning Miss Quinton," I was about give up on finding more of my type of romance novels among Ms. Michaels' alphabet series. "The Chaotic Miss Crispino" had a marvelous, feisty little Italian gal as the heroine - Miss Allegra Crispino, whose parents had passed, leaving her all by her lonesome in Italy. Valerian Fitzhugh who had been traveling abroad for nearly 3 years, receives a missive from one of his father's friends requesting that he travel to Italy and bring his granddaughter, Allegra, to England.

Allegra's English grandfather had disowned her mother when she married an Italian, so Allegra has never met any of her English relatives. When Valerian finally tracks Allegra down after searching for a month, he finds she has been on the run from a beautiful young Italian man who has fallen in love with her. Since Allegra has no family, no money and no home in Italy, Valerian easily persuades Allegra to travel back to England with him. It seems that Allegra has a major talent - she has a beautiful voice and had hoped to have a career as an opera singer.

Allegra is everything I love in my heroines. Beautiful, feisty, knows how to "get some of her own" without doing damage to others. Valerian is somewhat of a slowtop but eventually he finds his way past his brain to his heart. This book had all the minor characters needed to serve as the backdrop for this sweet romance. Thank you Ms. Michaels, or whatever your name is.
