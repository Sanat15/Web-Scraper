Rating: 4/5

This should be a compulsory read for all scientists. I wish I had known about this book sooner.

In Statistics Without Tears, Derek Rowntree explains statistical concepts in an engaging, conversational manner. I really appreciate how the book dissects technical terms such as “standard deviation”, “variance”, “standard error”, and “regression” into clear, digestible explanations. He couples these explanations with numerous visual diagrams and figures, something a visual learner like me finds especially helpful.

Statistics is not as daunting with the help of this book!
