Rating: 5/5

I read this just a couple of months ago, but enjoyed it so much this 3rd time, I upped the number of stars. Monteith was a perfect hero, Samantha a perfect heroine and it is beautiful to see them falling in love without even noticing that they are. The other characters are also extremely well done and I enjoyed this book just as I had 3 months ago. 

A good regency with 3 love stories. The main one is just right: Samantha has been a little in love with nearby neighbor, Lord Monteith, for years. But he is just a near neighbor with no plans for getting married. One day they are informed that Lord Howard, Monteith's uncle is returning to England a very rich man. Once he does he proves to be a rake, very loud and very forward. He tells Samantha that he wants her for his wife but she neither accepts or turns down his offer. Monteith cannot stand that Samantha will marry such an old man. So what does he do? That's the question. I enjoyed this Joan Smith very much.
