Rating: 4/5

Not much Felse action in this one. It mostly just happens in George's vicinity.

The resolution felt rather unsatisfying. I dislike when the killer commits suicide, and Robert getting off without any consequences felt wrong. I get that his life kinda sucked, and he did his best to take care of his rather undeserving mother, but he 1) covered up a murder and 2) didn't do anything to prevent the second murder. Would it really have been so hard to call the inn Gerry Bracewell was staying at to say, "Hey, you're in danger?" Give the guy a heads-up, and there are half as many murders in this book!

Audiobook narrator Simon Prebble is excellent. Listened at 1.1x.
