Rating: 4/5

This is a clever little Historical Romance. I really enjoyed the characters and the plot. It's refreshing that a book isn't revolving around finding a husband. Catherine and John are wonderful characters. The love that evolves really makes the story fantastic. It's not really love at first sight. You see them change, each in their own way, but for the better.

The only thing keeping it from 5 stardom is the part of the plot about finding Catherine's real father. For it being pivitol to the story and so well played up, I feel that in finding the answer, it was sort of anti-climatic. I was hoping for a little more excitement to it. Maybe some drama surrounding it. Not that it ruins the story, in fact it certainly helps enhance it to be more than just parties and balls and what not. 

I think that the dialogue is great. Witty, fun, engaging. This story certainly held my interest from beginning to end!
