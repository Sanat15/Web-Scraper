Rating: 2/5

I used to love Choose Your Own Adventure Books. The summer before sixth grade, these were the only thing I read. Checked them out of the library four at a time, returning every couple of days for the fresh batch. (This was before you got rewards for reading. I would have earned my "Summer Reading Award" the week after school got out.)

Now, though...? I wanted to like it but just wasn't really into the story. Of course, I've been reading a lot of deep stuff lately. Maybe I just wasn’t in the right mindset. But I think 10 year old me would have enjoyed it.
