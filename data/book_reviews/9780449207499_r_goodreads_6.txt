Rating: 3/5

A quick, amusing read that I found entertaining. Drogo Trevelyan, Duke of Kenelm, is an arrogant, autocratic, aristocrat, but he acted pretty well towards the four young people who burst upon his life, unannounced &amp; unwanted. The Endales are an likeable family: 9-year-old Daryl, the Earl, mature for his years; 10-year-old Meredith, the scientist; 14-year-old Hilary, the drama queen; &amp; 17-year-old Leslie, wavering on the verge of adulthood, practical eldest sister one moment, woman experiencing first love the next. Except for one too-stupid-to-live moment, Leslie naivete was quite endearing, &amp; I like that she spoke her mind. A light, fairly superficial tale, but fun to read.

With an Earldom at stake, her brother's mishaps were no accident! It took two attempts on her nine-year-old brother's life to alert Lady Leslie Endale that their greedy guardian March Wardell meant to have the family fortune.

But transporting the young earl and her two little sisters to the safety of London and Great-Aunt Endale was no small feat. It seemed all was lost when, hiding in the disused mansion of the notorious Duke of Kenelm, the rahehell himself arrived unexpectedly.

And that was but the beginning. For the dissolute Duke had taken a secret liking to the ragged runaways - and their eldest sister. Could Lady Leslie enlist the Duke in eluding her pursuers and win his heart as well?
