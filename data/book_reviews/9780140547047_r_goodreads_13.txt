Rating: 4/5

I have loved James Marshall's work ever since a small library patron of mine in the 70s read Yummers over and over again. Then, 30-some years ago, one of my high school library pages (who is understandably still a friend) introduced me to his favorite, the George and Martha series. In all this time, I never realized that Edward and James Marshall are the same person! Live and learn.

I only just this week bought my own copy of Space Case, which I read when it was new. I'll be reading it to my husband...after all, he's a science fiction fan. And I was reminded of this story when I saw a review of The Stray, which I also bought.

My only objection to the illustrations is the Halloween costume depicting an American Indian. I suppose we have to consider the time in which the book was written, illustrated, and published. I myself was not very sensitive to the issue until I was enlightened by my newly acquired grad school friend who had been married to a Seneca man. She set me straight and is still doing so, 40-some years later!
