Rating: 4/5

This book really is beautiful- the illustrations are amazing, even as an adult I can marvel and admire them. My students also loved the images. I teach art- and I like to use a read aloud to kick-off each unit and help build my students historical and contextual understandings of the art we'll be studying but giving them a memorable story to help them latch onto. I use this for my Egypt unit every year- my older students (I'm an elementary teacher) enjoy it the most, the story is a bit too detailed for my younger students, but they love to admire the pictures and tell their own story based on the images.
