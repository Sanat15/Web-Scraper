Rating: 1/5

Like other reviewers have noted, Theroux's fiction does not hold up well compared to his famous travel narratives. Looking on my previous reviews, I never gave more than 2 stars, and compared to those, this one was worse. Perhaps the setting itself was too exotic and far-removed to sustain more interest from me. I plodded through this book in multiple very short readings, which likely contributed to the incoherence of the whole story I'll admit. The protagonist drunkenly stumbles from one rain sodden cesspit to another in this banana republic, while indifferent locals extort, yell at and set fire to each other and their ramshackle huts. The outcome was a real dud of a novel (thankfully a short one) just like the half-witted coup or mini-revolution if one can even call it that, that fizzles out even before it began.

Save your time for the more insightful and useful accounts of his many travels.
