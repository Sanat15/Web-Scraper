Rating: 5/5

There aren't enough stars for this book! I have had this book on my shelf for over 10 years and, having read it , I don't know what to me so long. 
This story takes place during WW2 and covers the life of a performing dog that has a chance meeting with a wounded soldier trying to get to an evacuation point out of France. The experiences he goes through and the people he encounters are unforgetable. 
I won't be forgetting Bel Ria
