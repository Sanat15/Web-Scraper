Rating: 4/5

An excellent continuation of the first novel. Now grounding the story and making the villain - a still unknown - yet deadly assassin. This story reconnects the relationship of Oliver with a past lover Dinah and reintroduces himself to other heroes like Hawkman, Mr. Terrific, and Stargirl. 

My issue is the same problem I had with the last one. Not sure why this is so over sexualized; especially the new Speedy. Feels very out of character for a sex worker escapee to want to have sex so much…even when she’s underaged. For Oliver and Dinah it makes sense but it goes too far once more. What is Kevin Smith up to???
