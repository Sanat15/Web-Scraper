Rating: 4/5

An interesting read chronicling three of Attenborough's trips around the world to film and collect animals in the same vein as Gerald Durrell. The attitude towards animals and conservation and their collection is dated and there are some analyses of indigenous peoples that make me cringe, however once you remember that this is written in a zetigeist for which British imperialism and the 'might' of the British Empire is still fresh, it is an enjoyable read that makes you want to head off into the uncharted wilderness and try and wrestle an giant python out of a tree.
