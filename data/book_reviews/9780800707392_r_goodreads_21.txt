Rating: 5/5

I loved reading "The Hiding Place", the first book that Corrie Ten Boom wrote. These prison letters are very revealing about her prison stay with her sister Betsy Ten Boom. It's a sad story, but it gives the reader the despair she felt in isolation. And how hard it was to find out her Father died 9 days after he arrived at the prison. It was really sad when her sister died. Read about her Christian Faith and how her and her sister helped people to "Come Unto Christ".
