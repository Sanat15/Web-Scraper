Rating: 5/5

While "On Food and Cooking" was not the type of book that I typically read, I found that I thoroughly enjoyed reading it. Despite the fact that it took me nearly three months to read, I found just about every single page deeply informative and interesting. At times, the book feels like a textbook that one would read for some sort of chemistry or cooking class, but I wouldn't say that the book as a whole is so dry as a typical textbook. It certainly did a fine job of walking the line between informative and entertaining.

There is so much information packed into this book. Throughout "On Food and Cooking", McGee manages to cover a huge range of foods, from raw ingredients like fruits, vegetables, and eggs, to more complex creations such as sauces, breads, pastries, and candies. While discussing each subject, McGee does not limit himself to the physical properties of the food at hand, or even how to prepare it. Instead, there are entire pages of historical and scientific context, giving a complete picture of each topic being covered.

I truly wish that all educational books would take a page from "On Food and Cooking". The breadth and depth of topics covered was fantastic, and all of the accompanying information made this book absolutely fascinating.
