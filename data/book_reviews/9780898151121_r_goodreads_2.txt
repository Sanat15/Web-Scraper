Rating: 3/5

My first question about this book is can it help you get a job. The answer to that is a yes. My second question is does it have all that you need. The answer to that is a no.

The first part of the book outlines a simple 10-step process to identify the type of job you're seeking and how to bring together all the information you need to write a targeted resume. The remainder of the book is samples of resumes and cover letters.

While it is a useful guide, you would need a lot more information than what's in the book to write interview-winning resumes. It will help you put together a draft of your resume, but it fails to show you how to turn your resume into a sales document.

It describes itself as a "crash course", which it is. It's certainly not a full curriculum.
