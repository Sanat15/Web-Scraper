Rating: 3/5

I'm really torn about what rating to give to this particular novel in the Rei Shimura series. I agree with another reader's assessment that it's not as good as the other novels in the series thus far, but so many aspects of the story were so entertaining and eye-opening about different aspects of modern Japanese culture in the early 2000s that I really enjoyed reading the book anyway. It's too bad that Massey doesn't seem to have put much energy into characterization, for either her series-long characters or the characters particular to this novel. But wow did I learn some interesting stuff about Japanese anime fan culture, doujinshi publishing, different types of anime (broadly), types of hangouts for anime fans and teenagers, themed strip clubs for women, and the supposed kinky sexual nature of otherwise sedate Japanese homemakers and office women. I'm not entirely sure whether to trust the book's portrayal of the Japanese yakuza (gangsters/mafia) or not, but you have to admit that if you throw all of those plot elements together, you're going to have a really entertaining story. If I were rating this The Floating Girl based solely on how entertaining it was for me, I would give it four stars. But since I know Sujata Massey is quite capable of developing her characters better and giving them more understandable motivations (which seemed somewhat weak for her protagonist Rei in this novel), I'm giving it 3 stars.
