Rating: 4/5

It's 1905 in Australia. Samantha Connolly and her sisters, Margaret "Meg", and Linnet are unable to find husbands in Ireland and times are tough. The three girls decided they will emigrate to Australia as indentured servants. Little did they know that they would enter a world totally different from what they were used to and be treated like slaves even through slavery had been abolished. 

This first book in the Australian Destiny series started out a bit slowly; but as the story developed, I was pulled into the beautifully described scenery and the events taking place especially in Samantha's life. Samantha was very much a forthright, "tell it like it is" person. Cole Sloan, the sugar cane plantation owner, is a mix of contrasts making it hard to tell if he is a hero or a villain. His treatment of other people adds to this. Once the story started moving, I found myself entranced and look forward to reading book 2 in this series.
