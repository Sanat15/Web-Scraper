Rating: 4/5

You know how sometimes you pick up a book and it just transports you to your own childhood. My wife picked this out for a library read with our son and this book completely brought me back so loving and reading this book in kindergarten with my teacher Mrs.Snell. The color, the simplistic yet pleasing storyline, and the curious nature of every child just seeps from the pages and I truly feel like that’s a major reason this book has thankfully withstood the test of time. I love winter.
