Rating: 5/5

I read this book as a teen. I enjoyed it very much. Its a continuation of Minako adventures as a Sailor V , and her life as a school girl and after school life. The illustrations are pretty. In whole 3 volumes of Sailor V manga books You can find Sailor Senshi and Usagi in school uniforms, so Minako met them somewhere in Tokyo by accident. What is very nice while reading the books :)
