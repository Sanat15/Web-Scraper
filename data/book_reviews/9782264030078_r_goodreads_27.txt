Rating: 2/5

I wanted to like this but I simply didn't&lt;3
The writing was not good, and it's all I could notice for the entirety of the reading experience. I kept with it only because I'd already given up on Gone with the Wind right before I started this one, and I didn't want to be a double loser. But, yeah. This was just a set of six sequential short stories, which promised to be about Arjie's coming of age, but really just used him as a vessel for commentary on the political riots in Sri Lanka at the time of his sexual awakening. The one upside about this book (and really, about reading in general) is that I learned a new thing. Had no idea there was such a tumultuous history in Sri Lanka, and that there was so much conflict between the Sinhala and Tamil people there. 
Anywayyyyy. Onto the next. C u then!
