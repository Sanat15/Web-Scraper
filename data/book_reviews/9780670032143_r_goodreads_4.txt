Rating: 5/5

Perhaps the best book I have read on the Vietnam War. All interviews with all sorts of people involved in some way. I hope to add more notes in the future. 

Duong Tuong left this poem at the Vietnam Memorial: 

At the Vietnam Wall 
by Duong Tuong 

because i never knew you 
nor did you me 
       i come 

because you left behind mother, father, and betrothed 
and i wife and children 
       i come 

because love is stronger than enmity 
and can bridge oceans 
       i come 

because you never return 
and i do 
       i come
