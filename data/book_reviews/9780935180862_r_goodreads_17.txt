a princess in manoa

This story used emotions to destroy the main character. She never listened to good advice through the whole story. All her suffering is because of that. I’m sorry she left no children. The foreword of this book is not accurate. This land was not empty when the Hawaiians arrived. They conquered the people living here. The medium sized people were raised to be human sacrifices in the temples. The very little people (menehune) were hunted and killed until few were left and they hid in caves in the mountains. They did build things of stone for several chiefs in order to get food. They still live among us today on the different islands.
