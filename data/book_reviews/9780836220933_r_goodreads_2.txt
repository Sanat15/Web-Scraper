Rating: 3/5

Typical For Better or For Worse collection--several short continuities, generally marked by light, occasionally sentimental humour (though Johnson is generally good at avoiding a descent into the saccharine). Johnson likes puns, as do I, though hers occasionally fele forced to me. She also likes irony, on which a great many strips depend--not that that's unusual for comic strips, I guess, but it is very much a go-to for Johnson. Lovely art, of course.
