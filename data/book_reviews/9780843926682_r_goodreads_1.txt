Rating: 5/5

A enchanting story of passion and hope in the times of MacBeth and Lady Gruoch. Patric and Elen live a great love, but like Lady Gruoch's cousin, she is married with a MacBeth's chieftain, Talcoran, the Pict. Although the marriage for convenience, they find a loyal and passionate relationship until the Talcoran's death in war. Then widowed Elen became a prize in the hands of her ancient love, the victorious Patric, a powerful commander in the forces of new king Malcolm. It's a painful tale about the several ways of love. Elen loves Patrick with the first flame of her youth, after she loves Talcoran with the pleasure of their companionship. She also loves her sons and her country and lives every pain and sadness of a cruel age, the tumultuous Scotland of early 11th century. Interesting also the historical characters, a MacBeth and Lady Gruoch ambitious but humans, a different portrait of Shakespeare's monsters. For fans of medieval romance novel, a great choice of reading.
