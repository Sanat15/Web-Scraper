Rating: 3/5

In Which Pooh is revealed to be Magus, Druid, and Esoteric Spiritual Leader.

This book is fascinating for anyone who loves Pooh as I do. At many times, it seemed as though the author was so desiring the story to display esoteric traits that he almost plucked them from thin air. It reads like a persuasive essay where the author will prove his point at all costs. A very entertaining and clever essay, to be sure, but simple persuasion all the same. Throughout, one wonders whether Milne consciously placed all the symbols that the author expounds upon. I do plan to investigate this possibility, but I believe that the symbolism simply comes from the inherent classicism and mythology of Pooh's world, not purposeful esoteric teaching from Milne.
