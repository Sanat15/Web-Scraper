Rating: 4/5

In 6 short stories, Jean Giono brings us back into the presence of Martial L'Anglais, captain of the half brigade of Saint Pons in Haute Provence.
We are in 1830. Louis Philippe is in power; The Napoleonic epic is still in everyone's minds. Martial is a veteran of the Napoleonic army.
In these lands of Haute Provence, highway robbery runs with stagecoaches in the Pays d'Aix, Sainte Baume, Rians, and the borders of the Cevennes.
Martial had confronted with this brigandism but, above all, with himself.
He has a disenchanted look, and at the same time, he has certainties about men's honor, horses, and nature.
Giono's writing magnifies his six short stories with his description of Haute Provence under the snow and his turns of phrase, which imprint us with Provençal nature.
