Rating: 2/5

As much as I am a fan of Elizabeth Chater, I found this to be lacking in so many ways. I couldn’t get a hook on either the hero or the heroine. I rather wanted to slap them both silly. Kenelm quite frankly was a prick, too busy being mean to think of the consequences of what he did or said. Leslie was too busy tearing her self in pieces to be really tell Kenelm to do one! 
Their personalities were rather thin with little for the reader to get their teeth into, and that isn’t something I expect from this author.
Read but only if you e nothing better. 😞
