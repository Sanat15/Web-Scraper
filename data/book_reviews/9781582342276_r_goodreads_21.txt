Rating: 5/5

Spurred by romantic dreams of brocade curtains and supine limbs of exotic beauties, also by less ethereal rumors of a possible palliative for type-2 diabetes, Tosches searches through southeast Asia for the title establishment. Features some of the author's best writing, and an interesting companion piece of sorts to his masterpiece Where Dead Voices Gather, which was written around the same time. Both mark the passing of time, or time passed, in the curling wisp of fragrant smoke or the cigarette burn of a faceless picture-recording; both emerge even as they disappear as rapidly vanishing evidence of a lost world.
