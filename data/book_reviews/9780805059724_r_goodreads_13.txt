Rating: 5/5

Beautiful novel and so much more than just a story of the Civil War. I loved all the characters. After turning the last page, I immediately returned to page 1 and started again while I await the arrival of 
Bahr's third book. 

Amazingly talented writer. I would read his grocery list!!

Second time around was just as enjoyable: Stribling, Old Hundred and Eleven, Gawain, Gault, Molochi and all the fascinating characters.
