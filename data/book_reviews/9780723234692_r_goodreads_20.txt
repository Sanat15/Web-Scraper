Rating: 3/5

I can recognize as an adult that this a classic story and the artwork is beautiful, but is probably not a story I’ll keep in my daughter’s library. Me personally, I just don’t want to read her a story about rabbits marrying their cousin and talks of baby bunnies heads being cut off.
To add, the edition I have of this story is quite nice. It’s a large book with a squishy cover and the pages are large and thick. The pages are a good mix between a board book and regular paper. The pages also have a matte finish. My baby thoroughly enjoyed feeling the cover and pages while admiring the gorgeous watercolor artwork.
