Rating: 2/5

I'm starting to get tired of these similar story lines: Mr. Armitage keeps buying dogs, forces his daughter to marry, she plots to not marry, and all the men plot around her and she is a silly girl for trying to not marry him! Despite that, I will finish off the series because I feel like I owe that to all the girls. I'm also curious if a more interesting plot line opens up. I also want to see Guy Wentwater dead or utterly humiliated.
