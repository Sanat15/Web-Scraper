Rating: 3/5

A kind of a sequel to Survival in Auschwitz and The Reawakening. It's the story of Jewish partisans at the end of World War II that wander from Russia all the way to Italy.
Smooth story, rather realistic but not a literary high flyer. Especially weak towards the end. Levi elaborates on existentialist problems, and the character drawing is nicely balanced for some of the protagonists. Not his best, though.
