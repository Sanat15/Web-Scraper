Rating: 4/5

This unusual novel, found on a remainders table in a GLBTI bookshop in Sydney, Australia is full of surprising charms: opera; food; sex (mostly gay); humour; love; tragedy; Venice; and, presiding over all, la Divina. Maria Callas, here refashioned (fairly transparently) as Adriana Grafanas. The central character's first name offers the author the opportunity to model the plot loosely around Cilea's verismo opera 'Adriana Lecouvreur', as much as Callas' own life and career. His narrator constantly seeks his 'Maltese Falcon': a bootleg tale of an early performance of Adriana by Adriana at Venice's La Fenice opera house. This is also something of an in joke for operaphiles: Callas never actually performed the opera, in Venice or anywhere else, but her version of its two most famous arias was a staple of her recital repertoire. I loved it.
