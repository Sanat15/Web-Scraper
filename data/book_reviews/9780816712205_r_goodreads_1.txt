Rating: 3/5

Those Amazing Dinosaurs &amp; Other Prehistoric Animals ~ A Book of Mazes! is a small coloring book for young children. The animals have mazes for the children to follow in the middle of the animals. It is pretty simple but too difficult for children that have trouble seeing lines (a skill they accquire between age 4 and 7), so some of the kids loved and some were not too interested. It is a good book for a rainy afternoon activity.
