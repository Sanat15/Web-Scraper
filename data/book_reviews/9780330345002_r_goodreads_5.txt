Rating: 5/5

There were some uneven patches, but at its best, this book was as good as the first three &amp; the sixth Poldark book, so I'm going to give it the full 5★. 

At times I had trouble putting this book down - &amp; snapped at my husband when he tried to interrupt my reading - always the sign of a good book! :D

As always Graham has created a complete world, so I was fully invested in Ross &amp; Demelza's adventures &amp; marital difficulties. Little details were fascinating to me, such as early mouth to mouth resuscitation use &amp; the poor state of much of the country's roads. I hadn't thought of it before, but Demelza until  her trip to London had probably never been more than 10 miles from where she was born!

I had always thought of Regency women's dress starting from 1800, but no - late 1790's. An example from 1798 (public domain)

The Poldark TV series finished with this book - sounds like the ratings were declining &amp; there is a considerable time gap before #8 in the series. &amp; I've also been told that the next book The Stranger from the Sea is the weakest in the series. Should I carry on with this series or just enjoy the memories of the satisfying reads I've had until now?
