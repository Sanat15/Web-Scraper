Rating: 2/5

Locked inside is about a girl named Marnie who is kidnapped by her chemistry teacher. Marnie is 16 years old and is addicted to a computer game where she is a sorcoress and there is an elf, who she becomes friends with throughout the book. Her teacher claims to also be the daughter of Marnie's mother, Skye who was a famous Gospel singer. Then later on a boy who she learns goes to a near by school comes to her rescue but he gets shot in the leg by the teacher, and is being held hostage just like Marnie. At the end, Marnie and the boy finally get out of the teacher's basement, by shaking the half drunken seltzer bottle and spray it in her face, and then they run up the stairs or limp in the elf's case, run away from the teacher's house and they go to the police.
This book wasn't the bet one that I have ever read, but it has intrigued me to want to read more books by this author, she writes pretty intense books and I like that kind of stuff.
