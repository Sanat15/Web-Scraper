Rating: 4/5

I wish these books were back in print. It's one of my three year old's favorites and we read it every night. Basically, the Barbapapas house gets too small, so they find a fixer-upper and make it beautiful, but their neighborhood gets developed and they get kicked out to live in an apartment. They hate apartment life, so they move to the country and make their signature barbapapa houses using barbaplastic. But, trouble comes again. . . !
