Rating: 3/5

This book would honestly work better as a bunch of essays. It’s full of interesting ideas—about philosophy, psychology, time, religion—you name it. The writing is beautiful, and Eco clearly knows what he’s talking about. But the story itself just doesn’t make much sense.
It feels like Eco just throws Roberto into random situations so he can talk about whatever topic he wants. It doesn’t always fit together, and after a while, it’s hard to care about what’s actually happening in the story. There’s barely any real plot, and the whole thing gets dragged out by long, complicated reflections.
Eco is a great thinker and writer, but not a great storyteller. The ideas are cool, but I think the book would’ve been better if he just wrote essays about them instead of trying to force them into a confusing novel.
