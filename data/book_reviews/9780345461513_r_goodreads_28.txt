Rating: 5/5

A fantastic collection of the original Conan stories by Robert E. Howard, published in Weird Tales Magazine, way back in the early 1930s. 

These are not edited to fit modern times, nor are they expanded to add some sort of chronology to them. 

This is the real deal. 

Contains a bunch of early drafts and alternate stories towards the end, which completionists will enjoy, along with wonderful illustrations by Mark Schultz, sprinkled throughout!

Highly recommended
