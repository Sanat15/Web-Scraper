The mother, the daughter, and the holy ghost, Joan Nestle. 

I walked away from the persistent desire to be in love with you and knew that a collection of your own was something I wanted. And holy fuck. Did not disappoint. 

This is a shittily brief review because... no notes 🙌

I love the way Nestle writes. Just her prose and how tender spiritual moments are woven into history and theory. It really conveys the profoundness of the history and lives. Nestle can so seamlessly slip these moments in. Nestle places so much importance on the lives of lesbians, butch and femme, sexual women, and sex workers. I always walk away from Nestle feeling like I am profound. That my life is profound. That my histories, our histories are worth of such profound value.
