Rating: 3/5

Whip Kincade doesn't understand why his partner is so concerned with money all of a sudden. Just as he's about to discover why, there's a mine cave-in. Later that evening when he goes to talk to Josiah, he finds him dead of a gun shot wound. He also finds several other people around Rose House. One is Josiah's polygamous wife Rosalyn, who had just arrived on the stage to find him. Another is the man out to find her, Zephaniah Strong, an extremist splinter group head. Whip is forced to hide in hidden passages in his own house while he tries to clear himself of the murder. In the process, several secondary characters, including a ghost, make their appearance. Rosalyn and Whip also fall in love and eventually all of the pieces come together.

Some semi-accurate LDS facts, some sex.
