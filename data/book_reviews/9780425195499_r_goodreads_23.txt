Rating: 4/5

Anna Abbott is ready to move out of her Daddy's house. It's past time to, given that she's raised here college aged daughter under his roof while she became a hair stylist and established her clientele in Harriet's House of Hair. Finding her dream cottage in the Isle of Palms is just the first of many changes for Anna, and she meets them all with joie de vive.

Dorothea Benton Frank's books have a low country charm that brings me back again and again. Comic and sweet, dramatic and engaging, this novel about a woman finding her place is entertaining and heartwarming.
