Rating: 3/5

Genre: Picture Book - Baby/board book
Awards: None.
Audience: Ages 1 to 3.

A) This book is made from boards so that it can be held by a baby. The writing also follows a pattern and has little thought behind it.
B) The author uses color to connect it with the animals in the story. Each color is associated with the animal that it would normally be associated with in real-life. This allows for clarity and growth in understanding for such young readers.
C) I would use this book for one-on-one story time or for whole group-read aloud with young babies. I would make the sounds of each animal so that they could make that association. 
D) What color was the fish? Rainbow.
