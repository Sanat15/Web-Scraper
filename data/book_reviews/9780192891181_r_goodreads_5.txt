Rating: 4/5

This book is a good snapshot of Aristotle's philosophy. Whether it is logic, metaphysics, science, philosophy of mind or ethics, J.L Ackrill's book gives a good overview of Aristotle's work. Ackrill gives different points to Aristotle and gives good critiques of Aristotle's ideas. The book may be a book from the 1980s, but it has aged relatively well given that views on Aristotle haven't changed particularly. 

Aristotle's views on logic and science outlined centuries of development and thinking within the West and Islamic world. JL Ackrill outlines the developments of logic particularly with syllogisms and shows important features within the philosophy of science that allowed scientific thinking to evolve within the Western world. Whilst Aristotle had some deplorable views on slavery and women, his developments were crucial for how we have progressed as a civilisation. And I would argue that the JL Ackrill book is a go to for those who want to understand Aristotle's ideas.
