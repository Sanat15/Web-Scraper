Rating: 5/5

This totally met all my gothic needs. The atmosphere and mood are dark and there was a thrilling ending. The story kept me on edge, but I do think it was a little long there in about Volume 3. Le Fanu has wonderful writing and I enjoyed that it was in first person. I got all the creepy vibes from the governess and kept wondering if Uncle Silas was sinister or slimy or what. This is a great gothic mystery!
