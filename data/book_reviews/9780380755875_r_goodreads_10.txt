Rating: 4/5

A straightforward classic type of haunted house story. Social worker Doreen Addison moves into an old and longtime abandoned mansion, deeming it the new “Addison House” - a group home for kids with shitty parents. Soon strange accidents, disappearances, and deaths start occurring. It’s painfully obvious to the reader that it is in fact ghosts wreaking havoc upon the new Addison House but the MC remains oblivious to this throughout the book which was a little frustrating. Overall this was an enjoyable read &amp; would be a great option for younger readers just getting into the horror genre
