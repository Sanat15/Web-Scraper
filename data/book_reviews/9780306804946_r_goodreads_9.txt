Rating: 4/5

Chris Heath knows how to write about 'Pop stars'
His 1st Robbie Williams book is probably the best of its genre, and he even made our whacky Robster entertaining in his mature years in the recent follow-up.

This, though, is where he cut his teeth, charting the first Pet Shop Boys tour of the late 80s. He captures their mannerisms and character to a tee, and we really get to know the people behind the faces only a few short years into their new life of fame. It's a time capsule with cameos by other stars, including Bros and Janet Street Porter. 
Entertaining and a must-read for a Pet Shop Boys fan.
