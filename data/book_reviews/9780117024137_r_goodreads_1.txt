Rating: 5/5

A stunning, historical document... 

In 1979 there was a highly interesting debate concerning UFOs in the British House of Lords. The debate was initiated by the Earl of Clancarty, otherwise known as Brinsley Le Poer Trench, a ufologist that held wild ideas concerning ancient aliens theories and UFOs hiding in “hollow earth”. But Trench also held that the governments in the world were keeping a lid on knowledge concerning UFOs, and he managed eventually to organize a debate in the House of Lords to at least force the British government towards enclosure. 

The debate took place on 18 January 1979, and was extremely well-attended. The book contains the complete transcription of the debate. It is clear that the now famous UFO sightings in New Zealand (google: “Kaikoura Lights sightings” for more info) have had a lot of impact, since they were mentioned quite frequently during the debate. Interesting also is that of the twenty-so speakers, only two or three are hesitant about UFOs and only one is outright hostile towards the whole idea. All other speakers are in favor of or at least sympathetic to Clancarty’s plea. There are also discussions concerning the Loch Ness monster, and a couple of mentions of personal UFO sightings. 

It’s a remarkable document, well worth a read, and a historical document as well. Very interesting, though ultimately – as was to be expected – Clancarty’s plea for openness was dismissed by the British government on the basis that “there is nothing to convince the Government that there has ever been a single visit by an alien space craft, let alone the numbers of visits which the noble Earl, Lord Clancarty, claims are increasing all the time” (114). 

It’s up to the reader to make up their own minds concerning this issue…
