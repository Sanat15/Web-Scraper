Rating: 1/5

Maybe I'm in a bad mental space because I was bored by this beloved book. J.R. Ackerley, the author and guardian of Tulip, over-writes, and is overly fascinated with his dog's sexual needs. There is a certain scientific care in how closely he observes her, but I feel like I'm invading Tulip's privacy reading it. 

If Ackerley had had Tulip spayed, would he have had enough material for a book? Probably not.

Confession: I did not finish this book. I got about 100 pages in, and when I discovered that there was yet ANOTHER chapter about breeding Tulip, I was done. Life is too short.
