Rating: 4/5

thought you can definitely tell it was written in the 70s, I’d say it holds up well for the most part. My favorite story was Ulysses Star is Still Alive! Lantern and Arrow try helping these natives fight against encroachment on their land rights, more specifically, their rights to the lumber on their territory “protected” by a deal their chief Ulysses Star made with Washington a century prior. (Minor spoilers) Green Arrow dresses as the ghost of Ulysses to try and unite this native group and make them more militant, whilr Green Lantern tries finding the documents of the treaty which were held by a member of the tribe who left the reservation some decades prior, in an attempt to then meet up with a congress member. The pink tone on the native folks can be a little much and also the use of the word redskins, but O’Neil’s intentions were good no doubt, decently woke for the 70s, not just the Ulysses issue but all the issues in this series; it’s understandable why this series is admired and recommended as much as it is compared to other silver age comics.
