Rating: 4/5

To be honest, I did not read every single story in this book. I skipped some of the longer works, or those that had a writing style or setting that didn't resonate with me. I discovered two things: 1) I'm not a huge fan of short stories because many are unsatisfying, have a sad or bad ending, or just seem kind of pointless, and 2) I much prefer the older stories written before 1900.

I was not surprised to see that many it my favorites were those written by men and had rather adventuresome or exciting themes. Some of my most memorable from this collection were:
The Legend of Sleepy Hollow
The Lady, or the Tiger? 
The Luck of Roaring Camp
Ah Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge
A White Heron
The Return of a Private
The Lost Phoebe
To Build A Fire
The Golden Honeymoon
The Devil and Daniel Webster (the whole reason I picked up this giant tome)
The Red Pony
