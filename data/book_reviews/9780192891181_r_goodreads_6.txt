Rating: 4/5

Ackrill does a great job at putting forth the principal components of Aristotle’s scientific, theological, and philosophical thinking. To me he represents the non-dogmatic type of thinking that welcomes complexity. The idea that the simple is always explained by the complex and never vice versa, I think this is where he moves away from his teacher Plato. Will be diving into the categories, I see this as a precursor to Leibniz characteristica universalis and the promethean knowledge culture of encyclopedias and more recently AI.
