Rating: 4/5

Alright, now that I've read all of Ellen Meloy's books I actually want to review them all. This book is probably the easiest introduction to her as a writer, which makes sense because it was her first book, but it also has the least strong voice. The writing is still very well done although it is less surprising than in later works, which is why I think first time readers would enjoy it more. Last Cheater's Waltz is where I would recommend starting for reading Ellen Meloy, not least of all because it is a lot easier to find copies of.

Raven's Exile loosely follows a river ranger's season on the Green, giving useful interpretive insights about the river that you would expect to hear from a ranger. The notable digression is their trip to Las Vegas, my least favorite chapter for fairly obvious reasons, but it fits the narrative given that a lot of the Green's water eventually ends up there. I wish the concept were a little tighter and the focus on ravens more prevalent, but it is still a very good book worth your time to learn about Desolation Canyon, rafting, ravens, and how we interact with the world around us.
