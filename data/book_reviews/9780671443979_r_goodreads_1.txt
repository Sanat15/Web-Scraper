Rating: 4/5

Marian Burros, the books author, is a published opponent to much of America's fast food industry. An alternative--gather recipes that help busy people cook entire meals quickly. Here, then, is a compilation of tasty, generally healthy, easy every-day foods of a happy variety. In a world of an endless supply of cookbooks I've decided this is one that is worth the self space. Its out of print, but that's where amazon.com comes in most useful.
