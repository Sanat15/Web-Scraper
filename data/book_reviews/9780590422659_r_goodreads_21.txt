Rating: 4/5

*This edition features the original cover art as the image from 1992. However, I read the updated illustrations by Kris Easler for the 2022 edition, which on Goodreads lists it as the Kindle Edition.

A family has just begun their traditions for Hanukkah, but a terrible snowstorm keeps them indoors. This prevents them from gardening their potatoes and apples. As food begins to dwindle, the family has two unexpected visitors. How can they feed two more mouths? 

Find out in this very cute story of family, togetherness, hope and maybe miracles. 

The illustrations by Kris Easler are engaging and cute. Fran Manushkin's text makes for a quick read.
