Rating: 2/5

Intrigue is the second book in the Daughters of Mannerling series which my cover said was written by MC Beaton (is she the same person as Chesney?) anyway it has about as much intrigue as to quote an old Australian commercial a gum boot filled with warm sago.
Really a struggle to get through all these prancing prunes pouncing about the place with their proper ways and perplexing postures, bunch of old plonkers. Dull! (And yet I continue on because you know I've borrowed them all so....)
