Rating: 5/5

Tense and frankly inspiring account of surviving the occupation of one's country by fascists. Can't imagine why this might feel relevant to me!!! But reading about all ways Origo, her husband, and her friends and neighbors resist, most without going so far as to join the partisans and shooting people, illustrate the varied types of resistance that are possible -- and effective. And in the end, they are freed.
