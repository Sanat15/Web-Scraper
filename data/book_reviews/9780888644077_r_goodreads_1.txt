Rating: 3/5

Heart of a Stranger was the January selection chosen by one of my book club members. A good choice over Christmas, because it is easy to put down, and pick up without losing track of a story.
It's hard to rate a book of essays - some I loved, some I hated, so I've given this book 3 stars to balance off the good and the bad.
I enjoyed Sayonara Agamemnon (travel essay), Captain Pilot Shawkat and Kipling's Ghost (Suez Canal), and The Wild Blue Yonder (humourous)
When reading the book, the reader needs to remember that the essays were written in the late 60's and early 70's, and put the essays in that context.
