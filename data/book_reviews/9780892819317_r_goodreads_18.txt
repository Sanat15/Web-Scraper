Rating: 3/5

This book is fairly well-balanced and provides a lot of background information on the history of vaccinations, how vaccinations work, and the risks/benefits of vaccinating your child. I did feel that even though Romm tries to provide both sides, she obviously leans towards not vaccinating. This is such a difficult issue and one that I mulled over for a long time. Ultimately we've taken a middle road and have selectively vaccinated our son.
