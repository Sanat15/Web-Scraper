Rating: 4/5

Pat O'Shea sees the professor, Everett Addams, in the opposite office slip a supposedly stolen objective lens into his pocket in the doorway opposite. Pat follows Everett to the parking lot and then off campus all the way to a long dead end street and an unofficial dump at the end of the road. When Pat confronts Everett, Everett's car, which was not turned off, slips over the edge into the dump; Everett hits Pat on the head with a stone; takes Pat's wallet, and drives off in Pat's car believing he has killed Pat. Just before a terrible storm, Pat is found by Mrs. Pryde,the crazy old woman in the nearby house who is known by all as a witch. She thinks Pat is her son Johnny, who was put to death for rape, etc. many years before. Pat is not only wounded on his head, but has a broken leg and a wound on his arm. Pat's wife, Anabel, worried that Pat hasn't returned home, begins a long search to find what happened and where he is. The Provost and police do not seem helpful, believing he has a personal reason he wouldn't want known. Everett also disappears. His second wife, Celia, calls her twin brother. They are both very strange and probably criminals and appear not to want either missing man found. Everett's daughter, Vee, appears to be neglected by both her father and stepmother, and moves in with Anabel.
