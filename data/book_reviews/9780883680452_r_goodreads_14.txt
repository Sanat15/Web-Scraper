Rating: 5/5

Provoking to good works in parenting

Murray does an excellent job of exhorting Christian parents to view their earthly duties of raising children in light of its eternal value. I found the writings tremendously encouraging and challenging, as may be evidenced by the copious number of highlighted passages I've shared. Although I am still reading and praying through some bits that I'm not sure I agree are biblical, I'd overall recommend the book for the prayers that end each chapter if nothing else - and there's a good deal else from which to learn!
