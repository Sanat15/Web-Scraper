Rating: 5/5

This book covers a gamut of erotic delights from plays, prose, astrology to poetry. It includes beautiful works of arts for gay and straight, from decent to smutty, written by people of color from all over the world and essayists, novelists, and poets. I've read this book so much, the binder is coming off and I haven't completed reading the entire book yet. If you love erotica, you must have this book. And if you're not sure, but want to play with your sensual side, you must have this book.
