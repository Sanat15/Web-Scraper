3.5 stars

This one wasn't too bad. It was a pretty good story. Of course there are some trademark DP touches - but they are fairly light in this one.

H thought he was impotent for nine years, the result of a landmine explosion. h makes him feel for the first time. his longtime love for an OW makes him a little bitter - she married another man and had a child. H/h marry to give H cover for his impotency. H/h only married a short while when they arrive home to find OW - H basically drops h like a hot coal to spend all of his time with OW and her son. OW loves him like a dear friend. Danger and bad guys enter the picture and the h represents herself well. Of course, there is a HEA.
