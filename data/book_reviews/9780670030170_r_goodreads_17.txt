Rating: 4/5

This was a book my mom gave me, highly recommended, but it languished on my tbr until my mood for nonfiction returned. This is one of those rare Unicorns of a book which is full of information, but does not read like a textbook. Meticulously researched, engagingly written, and highly recommended if you're interested in the history of the film industry, Old Hollywood and the studio system, and the impact and experiences of the gays and lesbians who have helped shape the film industry since its inception.
