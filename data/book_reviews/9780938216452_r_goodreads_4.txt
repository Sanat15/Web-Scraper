Rating: 4/5

Originally published in 1980, this biography is journalistic in its style and content. The focus was on her accomplishments and many there were! I picked up the because she was responsible for iconic buildings at the Grand Canyon but was totally impressed with the wide range of other buildings she designed. Particularly interesting was the interior decorating she did. She pioneered a new movement with the successful integration of authentic Native American art, architectural designs and building methods with newer "southwest" art. She went to great lengths to use and replicate authentic furniture, indigenous art and functional interior decorations such as lighting fixtures, hand-painted glass panes, and even the china designs were based on mexican and native motifs. But then her unique design for standing ashtray - a jackrabbit holding an ashtray - added the whimsical. A quick read, this book left this reader wanting to investigate her life more thoroughly.
