Rating: 5/5

I received this book as a late Christmas gift, I’m reading it for Easter (hence the egg theming), and it actually turns out to be a Thanksgiving story… crazy correlations. When I read the first book in this trilogy back in November, I saw the potential. It was truly a great read in itself and the writing was spectacular, and I am over the moon to report that the sequel is not just perfect (in my eyes), but easily one of my favorite pieces of kids’ literature AKA my special interest. Seriously: Kathy Markel is a truly talented children’s author and has some of the best proust and bizarre creativity I have ever seen, and she conveyed this story’s themes, arcs, and tone wonderfully. I love the plot itself a bunch: it follows an antagonistic character from the first book as they not only undergo a massive reformation over the course of the story, but discover a lot more about themself. The character work is superb and everyone had a function, whether it be Mike’s genius or Stacia and Scott’s unique abilities. The humor is very well executed, the climax and the following aftershock close the book very nicely, and I had an absolute blast eating this book up. It’s barely even in the realm of kids’ horror, but still, one of my favorite reads, period. Overall, 10/10. It’s riveting Sci-Fi for kids, with plenty of aliens. Made for me atp. Anticipating reading book three, whenever I get my grubby hands on it. Until then… Happy Easter.
