Rating: 2/5

Well, I am of two minds whether to try another one of her books. This is my first one of hers. It was just...silly to me. It actually made me really appreciate Charlotte Lamb's works even more. In fact, I am pretty sure Lamb did a storyline very similar to this (except it was the h in that one that inadvertently caused the death of the H's wife and unborn child). I honestly can't be bothered to hunt down which story that was specifically. Not because I don't care about Lamb so much as I don't care about this one. The biggest problem I had with it was that it felt very choppy. Like watching a movie that jerks from scene to scene so that you're like "wait, where was the transition? was there supposed to be something inbetween to ensure a smoother transition from point A to point B and it just got axed on the cutting room floor?"

So. I read it. I finished it. I saw that I had another one of her books marked as "want to read." I'll give it one more shot. 

Carole Mortimer. You're on notice.
