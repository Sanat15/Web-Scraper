Rating: 2/5

I like the rustic, off the cuff turns of phrase. The gradually revealed plot features good attempts at garden-path narrative blossoming into alternative explanations. Plot mechanics hinge on special powers, and in a post apocalyptic world that's fine... But the illusion is spoilt by their inconsistent deployment; always unleashed to a degree just sufficient to get out of some close scrape. The result is a carefree romp through diabolical events that seem to be the first that spring into the author's head – even if used already in the book! Unfortunately the overall effect of this sloppily built world, depends upon the reader omitting to conduct the most elementary scrutiny. It's not clear how the book was published in its current form.
