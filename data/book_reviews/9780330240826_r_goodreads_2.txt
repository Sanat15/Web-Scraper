Rating: 5/5

One of all time favorite books when I was a little kid. I used to take it with me everywhere. 

The Barbapapas (don't you just love saying that word?) are candy colored blobs that can shape-shift. In this book, their house was torn down so they built their own by pouring cement over themselves and creating a tiered, white, bubble-shaped house--think of lots of little pods--that I thought was the coolest. There was also a house in my town that kindof looked like it, so I would always yell out, "There's the Barbapapa house!" when we went by. I think it must have driven my mother nuts.
