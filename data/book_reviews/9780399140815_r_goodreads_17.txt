A mystery filled with political endeavours resulting in greed, murder and "family".

New Orleans, the place of tourism, gambling, and political financial gain is all inclusive in this murder mystery. Friendship runs deep and the murder of a friend must be resolved. But the story involves more than one murder related to the first. Vengeance is mine says the Lord, but when a family member is murdered should vengeance be taken by a family member? And how are the murderers rewarded for their deed? A good read.
