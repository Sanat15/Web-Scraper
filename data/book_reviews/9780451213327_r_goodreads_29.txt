Rating: 3/5

The story's heroine is a telemarketer who is convinced she hears someone murdered as she made a call. Unfortunately, no one believes her and so she decides to investigate for herself.
The story drifted a bit at times, but there were some surprises along the way.
I promise, after reading how these telemarketers are so abused, that I will always be polite, even when saying a firm no.
