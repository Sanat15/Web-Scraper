Rating: 4/5

This novel is quite good and comes from a different era all together. A man watches his son play baseball and while the game unfolds he reminisces about his life and 50 years unfold during the baseball game. At first I thought it was just going to be a man reliving his conquests of various women throughout his life but it is a deeper than that and Shaw writes with both an eye for the times that he writes about but also the underlying emotional upheaval that follows all of us throughout our lives. I enjoyed reading this novel and it reminded me of why I really like Irwin Shaw stories, they are packed with humanity and there is always something that reminds us of ourselves in his works. He was an outstanding writer and I hope his works will continue to be read. Great read especially on a late spring/early summer afternoon.
