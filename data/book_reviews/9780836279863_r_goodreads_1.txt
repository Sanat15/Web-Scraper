Rating: 4/5

After having read what seems like million of books about cats, cat quotes and including cat art and/or photography it is nice to find one that most definitely seems to stand out from the rest of the crowd. A sheer treasure this one has been laying around the house for who knows how long as it has been misidentified to have been another such book that I have had.

 The book starts off like any other cat compilation with a bit of a history although this one also focuses on how much the cat appears in literature and as much a companion to authors. And to what surprise should I see the name of one of my most favorite little known author Cleveland Amory included within this roster - most definitely a change from all the usual unknowns and Classic authors who appear.

 What makes this book special to me is the fact that instead of using all time trivia and poetry that has been recycled twenty times over, In the Company of Cats uses mostly excerpts from books focused on cats. The selections are perfect to the T in capturing the beautiful essence of catness while showcasing a wide range of genres as well as writing styles, which at the same time encourages me to look up some of these works for myself.

 And then the illustration-style is a combination. The majority of illustrations are colored photographs capturing the right cat and background to be used or in a few cases providing some older artwork as well. And to complement each of these artwork if the picture doesn't cover the whole page are beautiful embellishments added to the side of the page such as the phases of the moon, stars or even the deck suite symbols or even a nicer background to help offset the medium.

 Definitely a new treasured cat compilation book for my collection as well as one I would recommend to other cat lovers out there. And most definitely approved by my own puss who has chewed up the ribbon bookcase to leave her own mark.
