Rating: 3/5

A slightly dissapointing read. Of the two den experiences described in the book, the first is to be expected - a primitive Laotian style opium smoking session, whereas the second is a more upmarket Chinese affair, which any contemporary opium antique collector would dream of. The book was much shorter than I expected, some of the vocabulary too complicated (for me anyway) and some of the text was obviously pinched from Peter Lee's 'Big Smoke'..
