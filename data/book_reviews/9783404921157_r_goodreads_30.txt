Rating: 5/5

4.5 rounded up — beautiful writing, honest portraits of relationships complicated by infidelity, sexual identities, racism. did not mind the repetition of these themes due to the autobiographical nature of most of the stories which walker is up front about in the intro (and imo is incredibly powerful and unique). started in the middle in a bookstore w the stories of orelia and john &amp; couldn’t put down, enjoyed reading slightly out of order though the beginning &amp; end of the book complement each other beautifully. “olive oil” maybe my favorite
