Rating: 4/5

Nancy and her friends George and Bess attend a rock concert. Shortly before it's supposed to start,
one of the band members disappears. Nobody knew why even though he had disappeared previously.
Later Bess disappears. Nancy, George and some new friends head to Chinatown in New York City to a restaurant believed to be a hub of criminal activity.

The story's suspense kept me reading. My only caveat is the solution came too easily.
