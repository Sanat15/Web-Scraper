Rating: 3/5

I didn't really like this story as much as some of her other ones. Ty Wade is one of the meanest men in her books yet. It is explained why he is the way he is but it makes him hard to like, at least to me. 

Erin is sweet and kind and loves him, warts and all but his brother has been pitting one against the other and in the ultimate act of revenge he all but forces them together again under tragic circumstances.

Ty has been so jaded by life and a father that didn't know how to love and thought that any strong man could refuse sex that he raise Ty that way. 

Erin returns to the ranch to spare a lot of people their jobs and she is not the same woman she was when he last saw her. Tragedy steps in and when Ty sees Erin again she really isn't the spitfire he can't admit that he wants. 

There is a lot of guilt and misunderstandings between the two of them and it's exhausting at times.
