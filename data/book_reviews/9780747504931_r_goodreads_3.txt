Rating: 5/5

I always knew about the evacuation of children at the beginning of the Second World War, but I never realised that whole schools descended on 'safe' areas along with their teachers, all of whom needed to be accommodated and cared for once they reached their destinations. It was heartening and distressing to hear about the experiences of the evacuees, their parents and of course their host families. Ben Wicks has sensitively captured the human side of evacuation richly illustrated with photographs and witness accounts.
