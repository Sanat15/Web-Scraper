Rating: 5/5

oh, stops the world, what a lovable mess you are. 

this book is my cosmic pleasure. i got this book for my sixth birthday, and now that i'm two months out from my twenty - sixth birthday, and about to start my library sciences degree, i decided to give it a reread. this book is what ignited my lifelong love of reading. it's still my favorite book ever written, even with its glaring flaws. finding the original molly moon in the wrong shelf in my school library set off a chain reaction that has now led to me becoming a sort of lucy logan myself. thank you for this book, georgia. and thank you for 20 years of my favorite book. 

TW. child abuse, child endangerment, hypnotism / loss of autonomy, brownface.
