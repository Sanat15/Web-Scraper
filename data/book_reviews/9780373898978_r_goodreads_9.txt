Rating: 5/5

🎸 When your past comes calling with a guitar and a broken heart…

 Fore Mike’s Sake hit me right in the feels 🎸💔. Janet Dailey crafts a story full of lyrical heartache and healing, all wrapped in a romance that makes you believe in second chances. The musical threads were beautifully written, the emotional beats real and earned.
Mike is the kind of book boyfriend you’ll root for—and possibly write fanfic about afterward. 😏 And the heroine? Fierce, flawed, and unforgettable.

🎤 Spice level: Medium with rhythm

 📖 Final thoughts: The perfect harmony of angst, love, and late-night serenades.
