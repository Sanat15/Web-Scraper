Rating: 3/5

While this was better than the first novel of the series, I'm just having trouble getting into this. The first novel seemed to use graphic violence to make sure everyone knew this wasn't a kid's novel. This wasn't quite as violent, but it never came across as a children's book either.

Overall it seemed to wrap up the story, but there is a cliffhanger ending that leads into the final volume of the trilogy. 

This series was based on the continuity of the Dreamwave comic series, but I would have preferred more of a fresh start for the novel series.
