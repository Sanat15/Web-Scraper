Rating: 4/5

Stunning. I must admit, it took me a couple of pages to get used to the art... but then I became enchanted by it. The text is poetically written and provocative. Lots to think about. Lots for families with a certain predisposition to talk about. 

You might want to read the back cover note first. 
I enjoyed this on openlibrary.org and will continue to read more by this Canadian Dogrib author, and more by the Cree illustrator.
