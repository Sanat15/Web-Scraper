Rating: 4/5

What a fascinating way to write a mystery through letters, statements and without a detective. No Wimsey in this unusual story. 

Two young men move into a house in the maisonette upstairs. Harrison the owner has a young wife who is bored. Munting’s the author shares the flat with Lathom an artist. Harrison is an engineer, amateur painter and collector and cook of exotic mushrooms. I learnt a lot about muscarine. The story follows the events leading up to the apparent accidental death of Harrison in his shack from a poisoned mushrooms. 

I think Lathom was a little mad and Harrison’s wife through her ambiguous letters influenced his actions. Munting also said while they were at school together he did bizarre things. Morale of the story do not keep love letters from your mistress. 

SPOILERS AHEAD 

In a nutshell Lathom and Harrison’s wife were having an affair. She refused to divorce him and Lathom poisoned Harrison. His mistake was he used synthetic muscarine which can be identified as synthetic and so he was caught and hanged.
