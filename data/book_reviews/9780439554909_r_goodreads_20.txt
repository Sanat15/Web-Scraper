Rating: 5/5

Ahhhh...the last happy Harry Potter (I know, I know, not 100% happy but the last truly 'happy' one for a while.)

When I first read this book, I was so engrossed in the Quidditch World Cup that I actually forgot there was going to be a plot for the rest of the story. It was so fun to follow the wizarding world in this sport-related tangent (and I am NOT a sport person, though maybe if we had flying brooms, I could get with the sports).

Then there was the triwizard tournament where I was so invested in the outcome that I forgot to be focused on what sort of evil stalked Harry in this book - it was just such a fun twist on the series!

And of course, the maze ending had me absolutely SHOCKED cause like I said, I was having so much fun bopping around to the story that WHOOPS there's actual serious consequences out there. 

But despite the ending, this is still one of my favorite books in the world.
