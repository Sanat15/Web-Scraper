Rating: 4/5

Margaret Wise Brown sure liked bunnies, didn't she?
Here we have her paired with Garth Williams instead of Clement Hurd from "Goodnight Moon" and "The Runaway Bunny".
The illustrations make it a beautiful story, though it's not very suspenseful. Protagonist bunny moves into a warren with a white bunny. Neither's gender is obvious, but the moving in sure comes across as how baby bunnies are made.
