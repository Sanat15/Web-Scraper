Rating: 4/5

They are both haunted by their pasts

Now the head mistress at a school for young ladies, Mrs. Guen Courtney is hiding a secret that could ruin all she has worked to achieve. 

After years of living a discrete life,Viscount Corvin now enjoys a good reputation in spite of his family's scandalous past. 

The Scandalous School Mistress was a surprisingly good read with complex and flawed characters. I liked the good qualities in both the hero and heroine, but the hero did step close to the line of becoming a bully. Each scene was well described and there was a nice balance between thoughts and dialogue. Several of the students provided a bit of comic relief and the plot twists kept me interested. The ending, though not surprising, was happy and smile-worthy.

Content 411: Other than some veiled sexual tension and none specific innuendo, this book is a clean read.
