Rating: 5/5

Damn, if this isn't just the real deal. A no-BS, hard hitting novel taking place during the dying days of the wild frontier. It's darn edgy, and consider, it was published in 1983, before Lonesome Dove and Blood Meridian. The fantastic, descriptive prose makes the reader pay attention.

The novel aims to put an emphatic end to the era of the outlaw. It's subversive and pretty bleak, but it is very good. If you like stories that take unexpected turns and lean towards the poignant, check this out.
