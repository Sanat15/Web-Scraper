3.5 stars

It is a good read on the Alcott family, but is places more of a focus on Bronson Alcott and Abby Alcott, not Louisa. It has a lot of interesting stories and aspects of their family life. It gives a lot of interesting background into the people that raised Louisa and what living with her parents must have been like. 

That being said if I ever met Bronson Alcott in person I may punch him in the face.
