Rating: 5/5

I found this to be a creative work that helps persons grow into better writers. I always recommend it along with additional titles. Others can find a great deal of hope in its pages too, of course, especially strong readers and those with an artistic bent - dancers, painters, sculptors, nature lovers, photographers, pianists, guitar players, musicians of all sorts, persons who create by means of sewing and knitting, those those create by means of custom automotive work - the list is endless. Madeleine’s words will nourish the spirit of everyone who connects with them.
