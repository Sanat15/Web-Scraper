Rating: 2/5

“Nothing like the sea. I must go down to the sea again, to the lonely sea and sky.”
“You’re never lonely at sea.”
-
This book took me forever to finish. I have a habit of starting books and never making it to the end, 
but I also struggled to get into this one.

But I had to finish this one because it’s special to me. It was a gift from my niece. How thoughtful &lt;3

I learned a lot of new vocabulary, but what stayed with me the most was its message about forgiveness. Sometimes we forgive not because we’re weak, but because it’s the only way we can truly move forward and keep living.
