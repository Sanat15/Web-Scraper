Rating: 2/5

2.5? 
 As a rule of thumb, I never expect much from Janet Dailey, Diana Palmer or Sandra Brown. The first two authors are the most lacking for me. I see potential but they usually never fully deliver leaving me unsatisfied every single time! I only bother because sometimes I’m in the mood for that certain plot.  
It was a quick read. I thought I like bickering, battle of the will trope but when Dailey does it, it’s like teenagers fighting. Cats &amp; dogs. No chemistry, no tension. Characters were flat. Don’t even know how she came to love him. He sounded immature for a grown man, she was just annoying. Gaaaad! What more can go wrong with this book!
