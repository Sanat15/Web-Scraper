Rating: 3/5

Charlotte is attending her first event since coming out of mourning and she's recently discovered her father is a spymaster by reading his journals, so when she sees a man at the ball that doesn't blend in she follows him. She winds up putting herself in danger and then getting herself kidnapped because her other option is being possibly raped and murdered. 
When Nick finds the woman hiding in the wardrobe he volunteers to be the one to get rid of her, once his mark leaves he binds the woman and passes her off the balcony to his partner. Turns out they are government spies trying to catch a traitor. 
For a spy adventure their isn't to much in the way of thrills or suspense, but the emotional damage the characters have previously suffered was interesting and the steam was decent.

TW: previous sexual abuse
