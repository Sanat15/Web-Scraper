Rating: 4/5

Fancy Kingston is escaping her home after her parents died to live with relatives. On the road, she's kidnapped by two bored bad-boys. Lord Robert Torington is touched by her situation; however, he has his own problems and sails for the Continent to serve under Wellington. Fanny follows him in search for her brother. With war looming, will she find him in time? Is there a future for her and Torington?

This book was published in 1990 and is written in that style. Still, it's a captivating story.
