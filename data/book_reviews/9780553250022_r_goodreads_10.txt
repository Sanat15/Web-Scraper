Rating: 2/5

Emphasis on events leading up to the gunfight at the OK Corral: corruption, robbery, and politics in Tombstone AZ. Wyatt punishes all the wrong doers, but nothing special about the story. No in-depth analysis of what made the characters do what they did and why. I became interested in Wyatt Earp after seeing the movie, Tombstone, starring Kurt Russell - now that was a great movie!
