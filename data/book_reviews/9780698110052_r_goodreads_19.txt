Rating: 3/5

I first read this book in the 1980s and thought I would reread it to see how it stands the test of time. While it is an enjoyable read, research into Anne Boleyn has uncovered far more details than this book contains. Norah Lofts seems to spend a fair amount of time talking about the outdated notion that Anne Boleyn was a supposed witch and this was a factor in her "bewitching" Henry VIII.
