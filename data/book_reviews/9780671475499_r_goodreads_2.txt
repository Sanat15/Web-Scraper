Rating: 5/5

I read this when I was in high school, and was immediately drawn in. The story is about Fiona, and orphaned teenage girl- full of angst and a fiery spirit. One day she sees a star fall down into the forest, and she chases it, and her world soon changes. 

I don't know if Tim (whom I had the very good fortune of meeting in NYC many years ago) ever managed to follow up on this book, but if he ever does, I'll be the first in line to buy it!
