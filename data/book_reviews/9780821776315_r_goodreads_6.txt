Rating: 3/5

I started this story because I wanted to read a RS and I had never heard of Lauren Bach until now. Plus, this was rated the highest of all her books. My three stars are based on the suspense.

The Good

The suspense was fairly decent. Adam, his captive doctor, Renata, and Lyle were on the run. Outside forces indicated Adam was being set up. When the author followed that line of thinking, the pacing was good. 

 The Bad

For Adam, it was insta-lust. Whenever Adam and Renata were in the shower. I found what happened hard to believe. 

The Ugly Lyle: he was irritating to no end but I know he was written that way for a reason. I just didn't like his character. Also, one minute he was shot with an infected wound and 103 fever and, in the next moment, all he thought of was a BJ. So much so, he had one of his ladies bring a box of condoms to one of their mysterious meeting spots. 

And the epilogue? I saw no purpose to it.
