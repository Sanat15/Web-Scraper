Rating: 4/5

as a brubaker glazer, it brings me great joy to finally get my mitts on their origin series (we could count  scene of the crime  but phillips as the artist here is zoo wee mama) - i knew my local comic shop was selling these but for a shorter first vol i debated just ordering a trade or the omni + they were so thin i knew i was setting myself up to struggle with shelf shuffles

this was like if brubaker wrote ennis'  the boys  except there's more of a plot focus and less dark humour however, i anticipate it will ramp up given that this was made in 2004 and one of the characters is  literally  named "genocide jones" 

brubaker does a stellar job at giving the reader enough to go on whilst not revealing too much in holden's internal monologue. i absolutely would read more and small doses of this will save me from a reading block i guarantee 

but team game so 

 3.5/5 stars
