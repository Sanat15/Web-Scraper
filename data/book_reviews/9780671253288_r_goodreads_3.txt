Rating: 4/5

A Spaceship for the King was serialized in Analog magazine December 1971 - February 1972 and appeared in book form for the first time when DAW issued it in mass market paperback in 1973. It was the cover story with a terrific Kelly Freas cover for the first Analog installment, and the paperback had a lesser but still good Freas cover. Pournelle revised it and it was published in 1981 with a new title, King David's Spaceship, but I only read the original. It's a very good first novel, about a planet that's fallen into non-technological feudalism after dropping out of the interstellar empire but is gradually regaining lost technology. The galactic empire revives and takes the planet over as a colony, and they have to prove they can build their own spaceship to regain their freedom. Nathan MacKinnie has to recover the lost blueprints. It's the first book of his CoDominium sequence, and a surprisingly well-done and intriguing space opera. It's military sf with political philosophy, but also a rousing adventure.
