Rating: 4/5

An interesting book on the level of James Bond. The book revolves around a man (Jonathon) seeking revenge. He can not get over the fact that his wife and child are killed by the Russians when they shoot down a commercial airliner that had accidently wandered into Russian air space. He is a wealthy man who sells everything and turns all his attention to seeking the man he believes gave the order to shoot down the airliner. He obtains some Surface to air missiles to assist the Afghanistan rebels in the fight against the Russians. He runs across the Cartel in purchasing the missiles and somehow escapes then travels to Afghanistan and meets with the Rebels and help shoot down a few helicopters and planes. He learns to fly a Mig-25 then steels one and has partial revenge. The story is really hokey and unbelievable but a fun read and gets the woman in the end just like James Bond would do.
