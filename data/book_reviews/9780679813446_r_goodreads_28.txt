Rating: 5/5

Once again, Alec Ramsey has taught me new lessons.

How to evade torture:
A. Pass out at the slightest bit of pain

How to survive getting lost in the desert:
1. Find distant landmark
2. Walk toward that 
3. Hope you don't die of thirst before you get there

How to compete in a desert race:
i. Have giant black stallion
ii. "Call on him" about a quarter mile from the finish line

I am the wiser for reading this book.
