Rating: 4/5

3 1/2 Stars, rounded up.
I had to keep going back to check, once again, when this was written. It's so close to what is happening now, but was written in 1993.
A great book? No. But lots of food for thought and I did enjoy the legal wrangling, much of which is different than usually found in books about trials. Did not like the way it ended, though. Kind of a dud, like the author needed to be somewhere else.
