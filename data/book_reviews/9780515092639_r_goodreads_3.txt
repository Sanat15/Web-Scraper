Rating: 2/5

if Amelia Bedelia were a regency heroine this would be her book. However I didn't find her charming as a child and I don't really find her charming now. Someone as scatterbrained as Ada would drive me clear up a wall, and I thought the arguments the hero made against marrying her were very sound. It's obviously meant to be silly and fun but I just couldn't get into the spirit of it.
