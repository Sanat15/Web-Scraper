Rating: 3/5

Graded on a curve because Michelle Reid and because I liked it better the second time I read it. 
 
This story has a Regency plot stuck in a contemporary setting. H/h were engaged to delight of their families and everyone in the district. H/h were wildly attracted to one another but neither seemed to trust that feeling. Hero kept trying to make heroine jealous for his own amusement. At a vulnerable moment he withheld sex since heroine was so young. Heroine felt rejected and broke off the engagement. The final straw came when the hero left the heroine in the middle of the dance floor at a ball. 

The H/h’s families have been feuding ever since while heroine lived with her mother in Boston. She’s finally returning for her stepsister’s wedding and thus begins the H/h’s second chance.

The heroine was rather silly, but the hero was ridiculous. He was ten years older than the heroine, but still had to play mind games. This is a case of being happy the heroine is happy. Hero is no prize. 

Boogenhagen has all the details in her excellent review.
