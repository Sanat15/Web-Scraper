All of a sudden, in the good-natured child, the woman stood revealed, a disturbing woman with all the impulsive madness of her sex, opening the gates of the unknown world of desire. Nana was still smiling, but with the deadly smile of a man-eater.

In Nana, the scandalous 9th book in the Rougon-Macquart series Zola focuses on the Théâtre des Variétés, the high society of Paris, its prostitutes and its ruined 'gentlemen', recounting the rise and fall of the impertinent and immoral Anna Coupeau known as Nana, the daughter of Gervaise Macquart.
