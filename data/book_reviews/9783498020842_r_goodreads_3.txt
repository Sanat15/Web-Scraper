Rating: 1/5

I thought I would be able to write a humorous review about this book being a stupid premise and terribly written, but I cannot. I am affronted and nauseated that the author discusses subjects like child abuse, sexual harassment, sexual abuse, and rape in such a cavalier manner. If none of that bothers you, by all means, read this book. The characters have no redeeming qualities and it is rife with slurs against homosexuals, other cultures, Christians, and even Ohio. At least half the time the writing feels the like someone puked words onto a page. The only thing I don't regret about this book is that we found it at a thrift store so none of that dollar went to this legitimately terrible author.
