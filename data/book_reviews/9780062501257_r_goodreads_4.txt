Rating: 5/5

This was an incredibly transformative book. The author expanded my views on what it meant to be “assertive”. Which she defines as living your authentic life/ self, spontaneously, in all situations. Truly life changing. She addresses the reader in a friendly, but professional way. You can tell that her information comes from research and study without overwhelming you with a bunch of facts and figures. The writing was direct and I didn’t feel that it was “wordy” or too conversational as these types of books often are.
