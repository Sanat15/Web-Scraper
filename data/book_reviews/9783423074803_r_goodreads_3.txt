Rating: 5/5

How can you not love The Three Investigators? When I was a youngster, I discovered the series at my local library. The Secret of Terror Castle was the first book in the series that I read. I was hooked. From that day forward, some of my best memories were plunking down in my dad's recliner on a rainy day and being lost in the boy detective's latest case. I wanted to be a fourth investigator. That secret hideout of theirs captured my imagination. I could picture what it would be like to open a secret door here, a secret passage there. Even after all of these years, when I stumbled upon a collection of Alfred Hitchcock and The Three Investigators, all of those wonderful memories came flooding back. I had to share them with my own children. And so it begins. I started them off with The Secret of Terror Castle. We read one chapter a night. Then we follow up with a question and answer session to see how much they've been paying attention. They love it and get so excited to be the one to answer my question. Tonight, we came towards the end and they begged me to read, "just one more". One more turned into finishing the book. They are now officially hooked and I'm all smiles writing this review. What about the book, you say? It's a classic. Enough said.

5 Gold Plated Rolls Royces out of 5
