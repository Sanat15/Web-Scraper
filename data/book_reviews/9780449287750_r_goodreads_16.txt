Rating: 4/5

Favorite book in the series, along with The Homecoming. Yet again, a strong heroine and hero, lovely supporting characters, and plenty of humour to make this a fun, lighthearted read. However, I would have preferred less of the gothic 'haunted house' nonsense. Will recommend to those who need cheering up with a simple yet enjoyable book!
I actually like the covers of these retro Regencies, they're better than my own copy which was rather cartoony and childish. :)
