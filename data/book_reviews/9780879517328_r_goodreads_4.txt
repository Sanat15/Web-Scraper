Rating: 5/5

Sheer brilliance. The problem is, well-written gay characters make me cry. Happy stories make me cry with joy and sad ones make me cry in pain. So this book involved a lot of crying. I was amazed to see how many stories could be made from the same beginning prompt. I adored that none of the characters follow the trend I call "gangst" (gay-angst) where they are defined entirely by their sexuality. This anthology did a wonderful job of acknowledging and exploring the characters' sexualities while avoiding the common coming out narratives that make up so much of other gay literature. I laughed, I cried, and I highly recommend this book.
