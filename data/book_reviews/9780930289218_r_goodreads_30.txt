What kind of fallacy is it, when you assume something is all but forgotten, just because you've all but forgotten it yourself?

This was Frank Miller's first project for D.C., about a year before The Dark Knight Returns, and I remember following it avidly at the time, but I hadn't thought much about it since 1984.

Epoch making, of course, just like "Rocket 88." That doesn't make "Rocket 88" a great song per se.
