Rating: 3/5

3 Stars for Hawaii (audiobook) by James A. Michener read by Larry McKeever. 

It feels like after listening to this audiobook for over fifty hours I still just got a small part of the history of Hawaii. This book really focuses on several important families and how they struggled and ultimately they benefited by their perseverance. But there was generation after generation of brutality that took place. For some reason I thought that the history of this beautiful island would have been more pleasant and a lot less cringe worthy.
