Rating: 3/5

By 1986, Ladybird books had gone some way downhill when compared to their rather brilliant non-fiction books from the 1960s. That said, for a children's book, Galvatron's Air Attack is quite a lengthy read with decent 2000AD-style illustrations. I always found the whole idea of Transformers rather ridiculous anyway, but the writer here has woven something of a plot out of this lame premise, with plenty of characters getting a mention. I found it rather wordy and slow-going though, so I'd be surprised if many kids would go the distance with it. 3/5
