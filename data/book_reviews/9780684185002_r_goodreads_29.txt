Rating: 5/5

I did not know about this novel of Paton's until I spied it on my in-law's bookshelf and borrowed it. The phalarope is a bird that reverses gender roles - in this case, a racial metaphor about the "iron law" of the Immorality Act. A powerful book by a writer whom I have held in high regard since I first read Cry, the Beloved Country in high school over 40 years ago ...
