Rating: 4/5

EDIT 2022: I was supposed to only read the books I'd never read as a kid, but I've now decided to just read the entire series in order. Some of these will be re-read and maybe get their ratings adjusted, others I'll be reading for the first time. Cheers!
Now this one was a lot funnier than I remembered, and also pretty spooky, even if everything was later explained Scooby Doo style. Just fun all around.

Original review:
I have read almost all of the books in this series, I will slowly add them all to my list. "Slowly" because I don't want to spam anyone by adding them all at once.

These books made my childhood. I really think that they shaped me into what I am today, so I'm very thankful to my local library for carrying these when I, in the tender age of 10, decided to start borrowing books (using my dad's card, no less; of course, he knew I was using it)
