 Short, originally published in Scribner's Magazine in 1871. Part of the Washburn Expedition, Langford's journals spends some time regarding their search for him. Well written. Surviving on a diet of roots, he almost died, after being found, from constipation! For early Yellowstone fans mostly, but a good adventure story of survival on its own.
