Rating: 5/5

What I like about this children's book is the bright colorful illustrations and the way the book is shaped. It will sit on a shelf normally but the animals on the right side of the novel are cut out so children can grab one of the 5 night animals and go directly to that page. This is a board book so it is will hold up nicely in repeated readings. There are more animals than just those five animals inside the novel. When you open the pages, the illustrations pop out with great realistic pictures of the animals and one or two text box(es) which includes information about the animal. The font is nice and bold also making it easy to read. Nice book.
