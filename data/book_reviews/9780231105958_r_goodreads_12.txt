Rating: 5/5

The central theme of Zhuangzi is to free yourself from the conventional values of the society you live in, and wander freely by becoming one with Heaven and Earth. For me, the concepts of 无用之用 (the use of uselessness) and 无为 (translated as "inaction" in this book, can also be translated as "non-forcing") were very enlightening. 因为无用之用，方为大用；无为而无不所为，命也。

Here are my favorite quotes from this book:

1. 天地与我并生，万物与我为一。
Heaven and earth were born at the same time I was, and the ten thousand things are one with me. 

2. 至人无己，神人无功，圣人无名。
The perfect man has no self; the holy man has no merit; the sage has no fame.

3. 不知周之梦为胡蝶与，胡蝶之梦为周与？
He didn't know if he was Zhuang Zhou who had dreamt he was a butterfly, or a butterfly dreaming he was Zhuang Zhou.

4. 人皆知有用之用，莫知无用之用。
All men know the use of the useful, but nobody knows the use of the useless!

5. 吾生也有涯，而知也无涯。以有涯随无涯，殆已！
Your life has a limit but knowledge has none. If you use what is limited to pursue what has no limit, you will be in danger. 

6. 大知闲闲，小知间间；大言炎炎，小言詹詹。
Great understanding is broad and unhurried; little understanding is cramped and busy. Great words are clear and limpid; little words are shrill and quarrelsome. 

7. 泉涸，鱼相与处于陆，相呴以湿，相濡以沫，不如相忘于江湖。
When the springs dry up and the fish are left stranded on the ground, they spew each other with moisture and wet each other down with spit — but it would be much better if they could forget each other in the rivers and lakes. 

8. 夏虫不可以语于冰。
You can't discuss ice with a summer insect. 

9. 惠子曰：“子非鱼，安知鱼之乐？” 庄子曰：“子非我，安知我不知鱼之乐？”。
Huizi said, "You're not a fish — how do you know what fish enjoy?"
Zhuangzi said, "You're not I, so how do you know I don't know what fish enjoy?"

10. 至乐无乐，至誉无誉。
The highest happiness has no happiness, the highest praise has no praise. 

11. 天地无为也而无不为也。
Heaven and earth do nothing and there is nothing that is not done.

12. 不知吾所以然而然，命也。
I don't know why I do what I do — that's fate.
