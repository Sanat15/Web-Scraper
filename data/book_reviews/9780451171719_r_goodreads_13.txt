Rating: 3/5

This was a good Georgette Heyer book, but it's predictable. From the moment Miss Taverner and her brother almost collide with a stranger's curricle in the beginning chapters to the final pages, readers will have everything figured out.

MMC Lord Worth's demeanor reminded me a lot of Justin's from the first book in the series. Julian is an intelligent and responsible guardian who works to keep his feelings in check regarding Perry and Judith. Especially Judith.

FMC Judith Taverner is a strong willed young woman with a desire to be more in control of her personal relationship decisions. (She's a very wealthy heiress and has many suitors.) When Lord Worth communicates that no betrothal request will be granted to anyone during his guardianship, it infuriates Judith. Their relationship is sometimes more stormy than normal. And on occasion, they purposely enjoyed antagonizing each other.

I liked this book - just not as much as "These Old Shades."
