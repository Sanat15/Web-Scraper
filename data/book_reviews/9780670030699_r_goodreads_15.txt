Rating: 5/5

I was hesitant to mark this as “finished” because I plan to refer to it often! 

I have read a lot of books about depression and anxiety. I can already say this is one of my top two as far as actually being helpful. 

If you take antidepressants, or you feel like you might need them, read this book! It isn’t 100% against taking prescription drugs but it has suggestions for what to do if the drugs are no longer working well for you or if you want to avoid them altogether. 

A lot of the negative reviews are from vegans and I understand that. The book pretty much says you should eat a lot of meat. I don’t plan to start eating meat but I’ve already felt a huge difference from adding some of the supplements the book recommends. 

If you are feeling sad or anxious or lethargic this book is definitely worth reading!
