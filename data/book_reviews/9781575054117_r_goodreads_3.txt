Rating: 3/5

I stumbled upon this when I was trying to find a different book, and I guess you can call me a tough grader unless it's a book that touches my heart or has very well developed characters. In this case, I would've given this a 3 1/2, but I have it a 3 because I wasn't really wanting to read this in the first part, and really just wanted to read it so I could read the other thing. If you like this type of book, go for it, but I wasn't too into it, I just read it because It wouldn't go away from my "currently reading" list. I mean, I've been in my own little world of lesbian romance novels and fantasy novels for so long I forgot things like this existed, so give it a try, I thought it was okay.
