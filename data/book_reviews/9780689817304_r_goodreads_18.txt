Rating: 4/5

This is a fictional account of King Tut's childhood and how he came to be Pharaoh. It tells a story that is based on facts but also adds in some details that may or may not be true but help bring the story to life. It is the story of a great Pharaoh who dies, a son who destroys much of the good that his father had done, and another son who undoes this destruction and brings joy and communion with the Gods back to the people of Egypt. A nice narrative to go along with the more fact-based books we read while studying the topic. 

Around fourth grade reading level, I read it aloud to my five year old and she enjoyed it.
