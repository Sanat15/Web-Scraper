Rating: 5/5

Truly enjoyable read! Sam and her sisters take a chance at a new future by indenturing themselves to Cole Sloan. Moving from the lush green hills of Ireland to the rugged, hard lands of Australia proves trying enough, but throw in the volatile personalities of Sloan, owner of Sugarlea plantation, Luke Vinson, the young headstrong preacher, and an aborigine or two and life truly is exciting. Sandy Dengler brings Australia and its people to life in Code of Honor Australian Destiny Book #1. Action, adventure, and excitement abound within the pages of Code of Honor.
