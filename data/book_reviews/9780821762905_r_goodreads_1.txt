Rating: 3/5

Zebra Regency books are really fast reads. I have just finished the third one in just as many days. This was the third one I read. I found them as I was unpacking and thought they would do until I got some books I am waiting on to finish another series. I like Paula Tanner Girard. She tells a good story without all of the lust and angst. To quote her "I love a fast-paced light-hearted comedy." I do, too. I loved all of Barbara Metzger books and she writes in the same style. I do recommend her books if you just want a nice book to curl up with for an afternoon.

First: The Sister Season
Second: Lord Wakeford's Gold Watch
Third: The Reluctant Groom
