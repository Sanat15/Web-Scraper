Rating: 2/5

This is probably my least favorite Catherine George book. I kept thinking "ew". The heroine is masquerading as a friend, and is falling for her friend's cousin. I'm not crazy about lookalike-and-can-pass-for-each-other plot lines to begin with, but the "ew" factor comes from said cousin, the hero, having the hots for the heroine. Who he thinks is his cousin. So, ew.
