Rating: 5/5

The fact that I own this book and love it and actually use it fairly often shows what a name geek I really am! As much as I love it, however, isn't quite as complete as I would like it to be. I'd say that about half of the surnames that I look up are not covered in here. Those have been surnames of English, French, Spanish and Italian origin. Overall, this is the best book of it's kind.
