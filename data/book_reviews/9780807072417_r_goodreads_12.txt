Rating: 3/5

I enjoyed this book because of its explicit illustration of the connection between food and identity. Shange does an amazing job of weaving all the food influences into her explanation of the diaspora, and her own background. I look forward to trying a couple of the recipes, and her colloquial/friendly tone throughout the book was really enjoyable. It took me a long time to read though, because the snippets and asides distracted me and I had to really focus on completing the book.
