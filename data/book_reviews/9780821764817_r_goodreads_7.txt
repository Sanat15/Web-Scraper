Rating: 5/5

This book is a delight from start to finish. Every character is full and deep. The various twists and turns keep you guessing and anxious to know what happens next. I think it's even better the second time around since there are so many little things I missed the first time (not knowing how it all plays out). The love between Bethany and Eric is beautifully portrayed and written exquisitely. I must say that chapter eight is my favorite and so powerful that I re-read it every time just to enjoy it again. All in all, a fantastic book.
