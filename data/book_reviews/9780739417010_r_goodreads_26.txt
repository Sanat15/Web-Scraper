Rating: 5/5

A Laugh Out Loud Romance

I really needed a good read &amp; this gave me one. I've been unfortunately stuck in a rut with the books I've chosen to read Every book seems to be filled with so much angst that I find myself wanting to throw my Kindle due to the absurdity of the hero\heroine problems. This book gave me a funny, sexy, humorous story that gifted me (&amp; my Kindle) a reprieve from that. Also absolutely loved the Hero Thomas. What can I say....I have a soft spot for rakes. Now I'm off in search of more Victoria Alexander books.
