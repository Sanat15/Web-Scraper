Rating: 4/5

Definitely a book that has the potential to change your life if you put it into practice. Lots of useful information, but there was some not needed writing in between. There was lots of writing to try and justify why you should use the tools suggested. The examples were good though.
Some of the information I have tried to put into practice in my own life. I realized that I do not always act the way that I have portrayed myself to be upon meeting people. I do not try to market myself to people very often, which is why I have a few close friends. 
"I can control the messages I send and my life will be better for it."
This is a self help book that can really help you transform yourself for the better. If you are going to read it, be prepared to try practicing it.
