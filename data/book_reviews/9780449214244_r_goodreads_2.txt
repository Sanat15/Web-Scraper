Rating: 3/5

This one was cute enough.

As with many of Joan Smith’s novel, its a madcap romp of misunderstandings and lost opportunities until the very end.

The heroine is practically on the shelf, she has been in love with the young lord of the manor since forever. The young lord is busy having fun in London and hardly comes home. Their families have been friends for years, so they know each other well.

The young lordling is home now, because his prodigal uncle has returned to England and is coming home. The uncle was the irresponsible younger brother of his father and was known to be a rake. However he is coming home from India now as a nabob! He has amassed a million pounds, so everyone is at pains to curry his favor.

The rich uncle immediately sets his sights on the heroine, since she is the only fresh face in the group of old widows and spinsters. The heroine doesn’t really like him, but she is miffed at the young lord since he doesn’t seem to see her potential. So she plays along with the rich uncle, not exactly discouraging his suit.

It works and the young lord is quite jealous although he doesn’t realize it yet. So they have a lot of rows and misunderstandings among themselves, neither one wanting to admit any feelings for rhe other, until all is almost lost.

Of course its a romance so everything works out for everyone in the end.
