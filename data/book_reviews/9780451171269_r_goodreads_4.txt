Rating: 3/5

This one wasn't too bad, I enjoyed reading it. Definitely better than many of the romance novels I've read, but still just doesn't compare well to other books of different genres. At least this one had a fairly plausible plot and characters. I liked the internal struggle that Kate had to retain her willpower and not allow Knowlton to ruin her life for a brief fling. I think the only thing that really bothered me was the spelling and grammar errors.
