Rating: 4/5

So, Georgette Heyer's brother was sick, so she cheered him up by . . . writing this complex swashbuckling romance . . . as a teenager . . . and then beginning her prolific career. What an icon! I love her, I love her books. This one was great, not as frothy as some of her later romances, but actually more romantic in its way. The whole premise of it hangs on a rather flimsy hook . . . which several of the characters acknowledge! It's quite good, and I am very pleased with my sudden impulse to read/listen to her entire oeuvre.
