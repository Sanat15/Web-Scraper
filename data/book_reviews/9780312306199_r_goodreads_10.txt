Rating: 3/5

3.25 Stars

This was a doozy to say the least. This book very much gave black trauma from the late 90s/early 2000s. Both Connie and John’s stories were super traumatic from the literal beginning and at some parts it was really hard to digest. Especially the time period where Connie was just adopted by Earl and Pauline. Her and John were both terribly failed by the adults in their lives which directly affected how both of them lead their lives. It was sweet that at the end of the day both of them were able to get closure on their situations, but John’s encounter with Fool (Adam) was really strange. I really didn’t like that the “truth” was that Fool and Mattie were actually “in love” because that’s just not the truth no matter how you flip it. Fool (disability or not) was a grown man who took advantage of Mattie and we know that for a fact she didn’t like it because of how Agnes found her the day she conceived John. It definitely bothered me that there was a prospective given where John had to have some sort of empathy towards the situation. To me, the letter was not really enough to conclude that they were “in love”, especially when all other prospectives of the story told us that Mattie did not consent to Fool doing this to her. This book was something to say the least.
