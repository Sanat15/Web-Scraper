We did see and it's not a bad novel(la) at all. You can read it in an afternoon. A topical piece about the kinds of violence our policy makers supported back in the '80s, this one set in El Salvador. It's pretty brutal. But what you read is a Rising Up. And a crushing down. And no kidding, it's the kind of stuff RURD is simply built for. As topical, those of a certain political stripe will call it propaganda, which it is, but propaganda for the good guys. Aesthetically, the prose is more or less that of the illiterate uneducated peasants who variously narrate the novel ; the passing of the 'I' from one character to another provides for adequate structural interest. Even if you see it absolutely everywhere, don't hesitate to pick up a copy. You know, tolle, lege.
