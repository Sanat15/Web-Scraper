Rating: 4/5

“Confessions” starts slow and then it picks up quickly in an unrelenting series of hilarity. I’d say, flawed as he was as a human, and aren’t we all, he is one great writer. His words come from an ape soul. We’re talking basics here - relationships with women being primary. His exchanges with a therapist in a group encounter session was a take on “woke” way, way before I heard that term. The pages turn fast, each prompting good belly laughs. The wording, the pace, the humor, the primal insights, the saying of what mostly lies unsaid - all good.
