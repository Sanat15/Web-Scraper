Rating: 4/5

My mom is in the process of clearing out her house. Every time we visit she gives me something. Often I take it just so she'll have it out of her hair even if I know I will throw it away. Last visit I scored a bag of book. This little gem was among the mess. It is a gift sized book of poems by famous poets...T.S. Eliot, Ogden Nash, Oscar Wilde, Emily Dickinson, to name a few... but the stars we not the poets or their poems. The stars of the book were the wonderful linocuts of cats by Martha Paulos. I like them so much I'd consider framing a few if/when the book falls apart.
