Rating: 3/5

I just finished the very first book in the Shivers series.I figured it would be appropriate because this is the one that started this series and it's January as of the typing of this review.The story follows our main protagonist Nicole and her little sister Casey.They just moved from a big city that I don't remember where from.They move into this old house with a cemetery next to it.Nicole begins hearing noises while trying to sleep in the house,but blames it on rats. Nicole is more bookish then her sister and enjoys staying inside.Nicole ends up exploring the house and finds this library.She begins moving books around and finds this secret passage that leads to the attic.She hears voices in the attic.She can't fit so she goes and gets her sister.One thing I'll point out is Nicole claims her sister is a terror of sorts,but Nicole is super mean,even when she wants Casey to do something.They end up sneaking in the attic and they see this door  and it needs a key.They climb back down and look for a key at this desk.The first half of this book is somewhat intresting.It falls apart in the middle.After this Nicole begins to hear Cello music durring the night,but nobody else seems to.They climb the attic agian and finds the door.They go inside and find this trunk.They break open the trunk and inside are these dolls.I'm not going to reveal anything about the dolls,but they end up being pretty creepy,especially when Nicole finds one that looks alot like Casey.This scene is actually really good and saves this book in my opinion.I give The Enchanted Attic a three out of give stars.
