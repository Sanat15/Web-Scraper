Rating: 3/5

Too many typos!

The thing about re-reading these books is that every man depicted would be considered an abusive and controlling man by anyone’s standard. 

The typos in every on of these books is pathetically unprofessional and distract from readability. What the hell happened to proofreading and editors? 

There simply isn’t any excuse for the word *Party* to ever be substituted for the name ‘Patty.’

Otherwise, this is one of my favorite Dailey books.
