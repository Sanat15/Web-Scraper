Rating: 3/5

A fun read, although I do agree with another reviewer that Simon is rather a repentant rake by the end. Given Ariel's behaviour, it's rather a good thing that Milford wasn't unrepentant, or she would have found herself ruined &amp; repenting at leisure. I would have liked to have shaken Ariel by the lapels until her teeth rattled when she put herself, &amp; others, in danger by leaving her room when shots were fired, but at least she peppered the right person. 

An easy, enjoyable read.
