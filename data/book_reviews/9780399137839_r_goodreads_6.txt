Rating: 4/5

16th century England, Spain, and France.
Another excellent Jean Plaidy/Victoria Holt historical fiction.
The Inquisition is a sad subject, and, interestingly, it was an uncomfortable re-read in today's world. Catholics torturing, beheading, and/or burning Protestants. Protestants ditto to Catholics. Religious fanatics on both sides, hiding behind politics. Still not sure how the title comes into play -- I must have missed the reference to a scarlet cloak when I skipped the bloody parts...
