2 1/2 stars
Kinda dull, and I didn’t really understand what the issue was that the rector couldn’t admit his feelings and marry, as far as his cousin was concerned. I guess that’s just how the times were back then, but for how progressive he seemed with other stuff, it seemed kinda weak that the only thing stopping him was he didn’t want his wife to be the breadwinner essentially. Good thing the cousin just up and died out of nowhere? That plot element seemed really weak, especially since the cousin is never seen in the book.
