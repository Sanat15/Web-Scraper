First of all, Demi is one of my favorite illustrators and the pictures in this little book are among her very best: pathos, sweetness, and utter accuracy. The page that follows the "ladybird's" unfolding and flight so that all four of her wings are observable is especially exquisite.

Secondly, these are just the kind of texts - little rhymes, little bright vivid contained (but unrhymed) translations of haiku and similar poems - to place writing poems within the the reach of primary school children, before they're ready to explore the strictures of formal verse. The 5-7-5 count isn't kept to in the translations. Not a problem for me but something to be aware of.

Little Japanese nature poems are an acquired taste. But they're a taste I've acquired: for life.

4.5 stars.
