Rating: 4/5

I think many people who've formed negative opinions about this story are missing the point. A common criticism is that Chris McCandless was selfish, careless, and foolish - duh. It's not a secret, and I feel that John Krakauer makes that clear repeatedly, describing him as “heedless” and “overconfident.” At no point did I feel that Krakauer idolized him or tried to frame him as a hero. He was clearly fascinated by McCandless and may have seen parts of himself in him, but he never gushes or portrays him as someone to admire.

Despite how reckless and arrogant he seemed to be (and who can say for sure, since none of us knew him), he left a profound impression on nearly everyone he encountered. It surprises me how quickly so many people seemed to fall head over heels for him. It’s hard to believe his flaws completely overshadowed his character, but the people he met all seemed to care for him deeply. And yet, he was so resistant to being loved!

I can only hope his parents and sister were eventually able to find some peace after his death. It’s incredibly difficult to keep offering love to someone who can't (or won't) accept it.
