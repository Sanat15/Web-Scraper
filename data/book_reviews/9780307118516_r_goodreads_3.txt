Rating: 5/5

A magic mailbox! Donald's three nephews take turns using it and receive extraordinary pieces of mail that send them to distant lands and grand adventures. Donald, of course, gets screwed.

+ this is the first book i remember reading by myself. the first time is always magic
+ Donald Duck gets screwed. somehow i knew that he deserved it, despite knowing nothing about him prior to the book. that's quality writing, it is!
+ i don't have this book anymore, and there's only one page i remember: one of the nephews gleefully swimming underwater in the ocean and finding a treasure chest. maybe that's not much, but if the memory has lasted for almost my entire life, it must be pretty special

This is incidental to the book, but a big thumbs up to The Electric Company, the television program that apparently taught me to read. How ironic.
