Rating: 4/5

Heart wrenching, for how the peasants live, try to survive, raise their children in spite of the exploitation by the landowners, the Church, and the state. Horrifying how the soldiers are brainwashed to turn against their own people and even families and justify the abuse and injustice the inflict without understanding how they are merely perpetuating the grievous exploitation by those in power. Lastly, encouraging to see the bravery of those at all ages who seek to help others understand what exploitation is, how unfair it is, and demand that things change. 

A portrait not just of the day in a life of the underclass of El Salvador, but society's had nots the world over.
