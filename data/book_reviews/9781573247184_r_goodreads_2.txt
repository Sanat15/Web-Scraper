Rating: 3/5

Although I am not a lawyer or a law student, I still found this book entertaining. I like 'tidbit/trivia' books, no matter what the theme or subject...well, almost no matter....and if there is humour mixed in, all the better. 
There was a lot of interesting law related stuff in this book, but I did find that it was very much biased towards the U.S. legal system, in some of the chapters.
