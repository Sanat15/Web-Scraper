Rating: 5/5

This is the ninth in the series of Victorian mystery novels by Robin Paige and featuring the characters of Kathryn Ardleigh and her husband, Lord Charles Sheridan. (I read this one slightly out of order, after I had read numbers 8 and 10, which did not present any continuity problem.) 
This could well have been my favorite of the series. The plot unfolds nicely, keeping us interested as events proceed. There are several actual historical figures among the characters, including one (King Edward VII) who has appeared multiple times, which I enjoyed. The setting is Glamis Castle, which is an actual place, one of the oldest and largest castles in the UK (and open for visitors). The book does a nice job of describing the setting, its relationship to the royal family and to the nearby village, and provides suitably colorful stories of ghosts and their links to past events. 
There are, of course, some deaths resulting from criminal activity, providing the basis for Kate and Charles to investigate. We see a reprise of one colorful character, a German spy, from a previous book. Although a spy and therefore in the story for Germany's benefit and not for England's, he is enjoyable for his cleverness at disguises and languages, and we get surprised by how he is treated by Lord Charles at the very end. 
It seemed easier than it sometimes is to keep track of the various characters (despite the fact that I heard the audio version). 
Altogether a quite satisfactory and enjoyable, book. If I ever get back to Scotland, it would be fun to visit the scenes of this set of crimes at Glamis castle!
