Rating: 1/5

I am a kindergarten teacher. Upon cleaning out the storage closet in my new classroom, I came across this book today, January 2019. I would never read this book to my students. First of all, the title is offensive in itself. The fact that the snowman is made of out dirty snow and a rag implies that black is dirty, but yet, the boy is supposed to be proud of this. 

This book needs to be filed away in the recesses of the past. We have so many better books that do not placate and patronize African American children. This book is stereotypical of books in that decade and does not need to be passed on to kids. I would only use this as a learning tool for teachers and professional authors to compare and contrast better books for children of this decade, to make better choices and write better material.
