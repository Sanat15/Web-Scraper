Rating: 2/5

Well read again by Adams Morgan, whose voice fits Bernie Rhodenbarr perfectly &amp; captures the other New Yorkers well, too. (I love Mrs. Hesch. Reminds me of my Aunt Anna.) Bernie is again suspected of murder &amp; must find the real killer before the cops find him. There were so many similarities to the first in the overall plot that I just couldn't give this another star, although it wasn't bad. The end worked out about how I expected, but there was a bit of twist that I thought was over the top. Bernie thinking that Craig the dentist was the one making love to his ex-wife while he was hiding in the closet. Other than that, it was another fun romp, but I'm hoping the next book will break the mold a bit more.
