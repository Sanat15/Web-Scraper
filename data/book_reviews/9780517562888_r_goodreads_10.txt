Rating: 4/5

Rossetti's poetry may not be my favorite, but the poems included in this (short) collection are not so bad. She favors religious subjects and the end-rhyme is heavy, but that's typical of this time period. Some poems are abbreviated and there's no way to tell exactly which parts of the poems have been removed, so that's irritating, but that only happens in a couple instances.

What I like about this collection is that a few pieces of art coincide with some of the poems, and as they are from the same artistic period as the poems (Pre-Raphaelite) they give nice examples of the way literature and art were heading in England during Rossetti's lifetime. 

The introduction by Peter Porter is also helpful for those who don't know much about Rossetti. It's brief but covers the basic points that are pertinent to poems included. This is a good introduction for those who wish to explore some of Rossetti's poems before buying a large collection of her work.
