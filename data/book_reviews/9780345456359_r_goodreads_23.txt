Rating: 5/5

Wonderful short stories that are part of the world of P.E.R.N, Parallel Earth Resources Negligible, by Anne McCaffrey. Anne was an amazing writer and she pushed boundaries, won awards that had never been given to Sci-fi before, and so much more. 

This is a book I'd love to own in paper form, hardback or paperback. I am buying 

The stories in the book. 

The Smallest Dragonboy. 
Keevan is very small. He is searched and comes to the weyr. His story is about overcoming big obstacles and being true to what is right. 

The Girl Who Heard Dragons.
Aramina can hear the dragons, when they converse with each other, and the leader of the renegades wants to capture her. She wants that skill so she can hide better when the dragons are near. Aramina and her family escape and hide, but thread is coming, quickly. They are at risk so Aramina tries to talk to the dragons, they hear her. That's just the first few pages of this short story. 

The Runner of Pern. 
Tenna is a younger runner, her genes have been carried from many generations when they ran across the continent to carry messages back and forth. This is Tenna's first cross continental run. She has a run in with someone, and that is what the story is about. 

Ever The Twain. 
Twins Nian and Neru are very close, Neru has dreams of being a dragon rider someday. Nian is happy for her brother, but if he is searched and not her, she'll be left behind.
