Rating: 4/5

I bow to you my queen. The queen of meandering words that somehow turn into a novel that I love each time. Even though it doesn’t always make sense and I often lose the thread, somehow I find it again and every-time I marvel at your talent. You make me love your books, although they contain most of the things that I do not usually like. Pretentious writing, metafiction, loose plot, lots of metaphors and so on. 

This is not her best work, the writing feels hurried, but it was interesting in its subject. A man turns from man to woman, while traveling through centuries. Confusing but fascinating. 

For anyone who needs it, here is my guide to Not Being Afraid of Virginia Woolf, like I was for a long time
