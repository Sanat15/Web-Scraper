Rating: 5/5

I saw the movie when it came out and just recently I rewatched it for the first time in 20 years. Now I've finally gotten around to reading the book. 
Annie Proulx documents the hard scrabble lives of the ranchers of Wyoming. Constantly broke, worked to the bone with very little to show for it, the men get drunk and the women get pregnant, it doesn't sound like a life to envy yet something keeps them there. Something more than lack of money. 
This is perhaps why Proulx's work to tell these stories succeeds. She understands that for all the hardship, poverty and brutality there is still beauty, love and family. In other words even poor white trash have poetic moments. 
This is a brilliant collection and deserves recognition as an American classic.
