Rating: 4/5

The small town in Germany is Bonn, the capital of West Germany at the time the book was written.

There's no George Smiley and no 'circus' involvement in this book.

Leo Harting has disappeared from the British Embassy with some secret files. Turner is a security officer who's given the task of finding Harting before he does something with the information he has.

This happens in the context of the rise in popularity of a character called Karfeld who is a political figure who feels that West Germany should align with Eastern Europe rather than the 'West'. 

The files that Harting has indicate Karfeld may have lied about his WWII record - fighting at Stalingrad for example - and that instead he might have been involved with experiments where poison gases were used on humans. Turner realises that Harting will probably try to assassinate Karfeld at a rally attended by his supporters, but how to stop him?
