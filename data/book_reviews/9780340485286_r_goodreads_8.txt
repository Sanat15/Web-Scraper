Rating: 5/5

Amazing account of a female who sets off on horseback in the late 1980s in order to explore foreign lands. Dodwell’s courage astounds me! Traveling alone, Dodwell explores regions of Eastern Turkey and ventures into Iran. 
I am an avid traveler and I have spent time in Cappadocia (which captivated me). I would personally love to travel further into eastern Turkey and Kurdistan but safety concerns and political entry restrictions prevent me from doing so. Dodwell’s book captured my imagination by allowing me to experience an area of the world which will most likely always be inaccessible to me.
Highly recommend!
