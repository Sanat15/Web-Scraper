Rating: 3/5

Peck is one of my favorite actors, so I was looking forward to reading this book. It’s not a terrible biography, actually an easy read. There’s a lot of information I didn’t know about, especially when covering his childhood. It appeared to have been a lonely childhood, which caused him to be rather remote, though not unfriendly, as an adult.

I wish there had been more of his personal life, but once he became an actor the focus switched over to his career. Not bad on the face of it, I enjoyed reading about his movies, but it also meant that all his stage work was covered, too. Since most of the plays were little known, at least to me, the information about them was rather boring. I found myself skipping through a lot of it.

Later, he tried producing, which didn’t go well. As he reached his 80s, Peck stepped away from acting. The book ends a couple of years before his death.
