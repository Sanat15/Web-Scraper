Rating: 5/5

Another book about Billy Graham that I found at the library. This is a short book all about his marriage to Ruth, and how the people who knew them always considered them as much of a "model couple" as anyone could hope to see between two sinful, imperfect human beings.
 Describing their childhoods, Robbins carries the reader into the couple's first meeting at Wheaton College. After that, he details a different aspect of their relationship in each chapter.
 The way that Ruth raised five children while her husband toured all over the world preaching the gospel.
 The way that Ruth would never hesitate to pull an innocent prank on one of the evangelistic team whenever they were all together.
 The way that the two were able to disagree and critique one another without making the other angry.
 One good example is when the couple were at the White House together with the president. When asked what his ideas were for a good vice-presidential candidate, Billy started to answer when Ruth kicked him under the table.
 Shocked, Billy asked her why the violent intrusion. Embarrassed, she said, "Well, I just needed to remind you that your main priorities were in spiritual matters rather than politics". 
 Instead of growing angry with his wife, Billy thanked her for the reminder and took her hand.
