Rating: 5/5

I finished this book in about two days and will be immediately jumping into the second. While the cover is spicy, the content is real life experiences of out and closeted athletes and coaches and the obstacles they face in the world of sports. It was published during my junior year of high school while I was still a closeted athlete myself. Today, I am an out and proud therapist and am disturbed at how many of these stories match the experiences of my clients today. Things have not changed enough in the thirty years since the interviews in this book took place.
