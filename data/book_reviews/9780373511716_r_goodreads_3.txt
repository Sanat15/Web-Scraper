Rating: 3/5

Zach Christopher, captivated by her beauty, desires Anya Valorian to be the face of his family's cosmetics company. However, Anya lives in seclusion and resists their undeniable passion. She carries a burden that forbids her from indulging in a mortal's love. Their struggle between desire and consequences forms the heart of this tale.

Although I found this book to be an enjoyable read, I must admit that I have liked other books by Amanda Stevens even more.
