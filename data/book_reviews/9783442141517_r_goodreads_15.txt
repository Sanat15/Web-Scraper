3.5 
I must admit I started this book skeptical. I was always curious to read it but was pulled towards other modalities such as Reiki first. Years later I borrowed the book from my friend to finally give it my attention, and the more I read it, the more I enjoyed it! Near the end of this book I felt inspired and connected to my own higher self, my guides and the unity within us all.

Barbara Ann Brennan channels a Spiritual Guide called Heyoan. Heyoan is a word from Kenya that means The Wind Whispering Truth Through the Centuries. Heyoan's message was my favorite thing about this book! Deep and Powerful, even in the first 14 pages Heyoan asks "Where is the discomfort in your body/life? How long have you known about it? What is it saying to you? What have you done about it?" These questions, when answered honestly can reverse any and all discomfort within, if we only do the work needed to change. 

I docked some stars because I found the specific directions for healing, the chapter on pendulums and the Character Structures, far to rigid for my liking. The Aura's in motion/therapy pictures also seemed flawed as it is all relative towards the person who is channeling. More impressions and information needs to be added/recorded by varying practitioners to fully validate these findings. 

What I can gather is that this form of healing is done through opening ones chakras and heightening personal frequency to a place where you can receive high amounts of energy to heal the self and others. Most of the techniques are intention, visualization, and Spiritual Guides working through a balanced high energy/frequency healer.

Heal through Unity! I appreciated the emphasis on Unified Physics that was much ahead of its time (I don't think it was a term yet discovered when this book was first published.) Many of the exercises are helpful and part IV, V, VI of the book were the most interesting to me.
