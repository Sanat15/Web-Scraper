Rating: 4/5

I should get credit for actually reading this six times, as I’ve read it in my own, then had to reread for all five of my ELA classes! For what it is, as tale of various youthful adventures, it’s good. Good pacing and lively characters and drama to keep the kids occupied, while Twain’s criticisms of the church and civil society brings a depth that makes it thought provoking for all ages.
