Rating: 4/5

So far,I've read three books by Pat Conroy.There are some common elements in each :
Dysfunctional family,abusive father,difficult childhood,traumatic events and even suicide. 

This is the story of the Wingo family of South Carolina.
Henry Wingo is a fisherman,who squanders whatever little money he has on farcical business schemes.His wife is Lila,who is his victim but also a manipulator.

Tom Wingo,their child is the narrator.He has had a nervous breakdown,while his sister Savannah is recovering from a suicide attempt.

The family has one particularly tragic day in their past,when they were all traumatised.They would all like to forget what happened that day.

And if that were enough,Tom's older brother Luke is dead.The circumstances of his death are gradually revealed.

So,there is a lot of doom and gloom.But Pat Conroy also has a fine sense of humour,and it makes an appearance every now and then,to lighten things up.At times,the prose is lyrical.

It became a movie as well,with Barbara Streisand and Nick Nolte.Having seen some youtube clips,the choice of Nick Nolte as Tom Wingo didn't appeal to me.But it's good to see Barbara Streisand.

It is indeed "a huge thunderstorm of a novel",as the publisher's introduction states.
Pat Conroy writes beautifully,he has a way with words and this is a terrific book.
