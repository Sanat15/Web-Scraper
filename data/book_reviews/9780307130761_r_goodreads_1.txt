Rating: 4/5

This is such a lovely book! I first came across this when my daughter aged 1 chose this for the summer reading challenge at the library. She absolutely loved it, although of course she couldn't read she talked the volunteer listener's ears off describing the story!

A ghost invites some ghost friends round for dinner at the castle. As they eat different foods their 'sheets' change colour. When they eat something magic they disappear altogether!

A wonderful and simple story with such friendly ghosts you could never be afraid of a ghost again!

Read on open library.
