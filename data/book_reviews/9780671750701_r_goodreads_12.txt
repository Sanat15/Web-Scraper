Rating: 3/5

Are we nearly there yet?
This novel had good characterization, excellent historical research and an intriguing plot. BUT, it screams out for a ruthless editor who will cut to the quick.
In addition, Mr. Goddard's vocabulary can at times infuriate; "...autumnal refulgence...", ..."an hotel...", ..."traduced...", "...susurrious...".
I did enjoy it but couldn't resist skimming several pages in order to get to the point.
Verdict, good, but flabby, but some could say that about me.
