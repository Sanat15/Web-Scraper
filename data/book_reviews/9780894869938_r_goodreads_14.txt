Rating: 5/5

Wonderful book. Highly recommend for any woman working 12 steps for any kind of addiction or compulsive behavior. Very inclusive of all types of problems (i.e. alcohol, drugs, food, spending, etc.) Deep discussion of the steps; helps to see them in different light. Looks at ways to reinterpret the old-fashioned, male-focused language of the original AA big book and traditional ways of envisioning a Higher Power.
