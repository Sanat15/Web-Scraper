Rating: 4/5

Partition is a complicated event. What is not complicated is the root of the violence surrounding it, which is communal differences. Bhishma Sahni depicts the communal violence in depth. The narrative is slow burn even though the catalyst is quite explosive. The violence builds up and Sahni shows us every piece of the story quite well, highlighting the hypocrisy as well as break down of personal relationships based on religious strife. He calls out Hindu Fundamentalism quite blatantly as well. He also highlights the gender based violence in depth which i did not expect to see. I just wish he hadn't been skewed in his view of men of a particular religion (Muslims) being part of gender based crime. It was harrowing to imagine the levels of violence even if experienced through a fictional piece of work.
