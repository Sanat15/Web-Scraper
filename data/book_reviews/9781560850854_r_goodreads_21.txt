Rating: 4/5

This took me a long time to read because I could only do it in small sections in between moments of digesting the history. It was a hard read, but informative and incredibly well researched. At times the author filled in the gaps where written history lacked, but it never felt like he was trying to push any kind of a narrative or making any unreasonable claims. I'd recommend it for anyone who is prepared for a detailed deep dive into a very uncomfortable topic.
