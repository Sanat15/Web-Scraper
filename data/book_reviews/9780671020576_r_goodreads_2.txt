Rating: 3/5

That is not a mummy on the cover. That is a burn victim. Did you think it was a mummy? Because I sure did.

Not a mummy. 

Doesn’t matter because fire has very little to do with this story. 

This is more of a…post-apocalyptic, sci-fi leaning cautionary tale that’ll trigger some major existential angst, if anything. 

Depressing? Yes. But I didn’t hate it.

I don’t know what Pike was going through in the late 90s but I have concerns. And questions.
