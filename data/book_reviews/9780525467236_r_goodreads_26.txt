Rating: 1/5

This book just wasn’t for me. The pacing was way too slow. There were chunks of text explaining the history of the area, but not advancing the plot. The abundance of the wolves’ terminology quickly became confusing because if you miss the definition it the words make little sense. The wolf behavior is very off and nonsensical as human characteristics were added to the wolf behavior. I really wanted to enjoy this book, but I was ultimately confused, and the characters didn’t feel very developed. The setting was the most developed part of the book, but a lot of the setting was explained in info dumps.
