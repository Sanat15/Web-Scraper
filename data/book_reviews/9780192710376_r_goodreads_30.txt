Rating: 4/5

A good adventure story set in Britain under the Roman domination. Sutcliff is a very talented storyteller and paints a vivid landscape of Roman forts and Celtic moors. I appreciated the easy flow of the text, a real page turner without excessive descriptions or political infights.
For readers searching the modern "gritty" feel, foul language and geysers of blood this is not that kind of story. There is a certain YA vibe, of an epic in the style of Karl May or Alexandre Dumas. There is war, and danger, and high risk, but there is also friendship, honor, duty, romance and hope. The heroes are not blessed with superpowers or incredible talent at weapons of war, nor are they interested in bad-ass posturing to see who can **** farther than the other. For me it worked better this way.

I have not seen the recent movie adaptation, and I'm glad I have started with the book. I am tempted to try more from Rosemary Sutcliff
