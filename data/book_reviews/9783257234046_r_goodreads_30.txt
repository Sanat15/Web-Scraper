Rating: 4/5

When I first met Tom Ripley, I did not think him too talented- he seemed to be a petty scam artist. He definitely blossomed into the man he was meant to be after going to Italy and meeting Dickie Greenleaf.

Luck was with Tom the day Herbert Greenleaf came into his life and asked for his help. Tom heads to Italy in hopes of persuading Dickie to go back home. Instead he decides he wants to be just like Dickie. He resents Marge, the woman in Dickie’s life. He is infatuated with Dickie, so when Dickie seems to want him out of his life, Tom becomes angry and kills him.

“To Tom, Dickie’s polite cheerfulness on the train was like the cheerfulness of a host who has loathed his guest and is afraid the guest realizes it, and who tries to make it up at the last minute.”

How is Tom able to maneuver out of this situation- that is when his talents come alive. He connives brilliantly. How did Patricia Highsmith manage to make me want Tom to succeed? She is definitely the Talented Ms. Highsmith!

Published: 1955
