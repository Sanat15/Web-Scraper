Picture Book #15

Funny Baby was just like the Ugly Duckling. It was about a duck family and the mom had babies, one egg was much larger and a different color. Finally, the egg hatched and it was much different than the rest. Growing up, it was always made fun of and not liked by the rest of the ducks. Finally, a swan told the 'duck' to look in its reflection in the pond, and the funny baby found out that it was a beautiful swan all along.
