Rating: 5/5

If I could give this book 10 out of 5 stars, I would. There is no better name in the world for the kid, and what kid doesn’t like dinosaurs 🦕🦖??? This book made my childhood. It is a literary classic and should be up there with the greats. Everybody knows Tom Sawyer, but why doesn’t the world know about Danny and his dino friend. It is unfortunate to say the least, but it must be known that this book may be the best literary piece I have ever encountered throughout my search through the depths of literature. I’m hoping I get to read this for my AP Literature class next semester. I love this book!!!
