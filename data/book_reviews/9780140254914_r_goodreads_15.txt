Rating: 2/5

What a quirky little book. Set in the late 1800s and based around some historical events, such as massacre by Mormons on a wagon train near Salt Lake City, author Clyde Edgerton weaves a tale of characters around the Mesa Largo and newly discovered Native American Cliff Dwellings. To be fair 2 1/2 stars would be more fair. Just didn't move me to 3 stars.
