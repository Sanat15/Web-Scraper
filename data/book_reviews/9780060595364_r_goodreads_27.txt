Rating: 5/5

I gotta admit the title drew me in !!!!

This is labeled as a romance but I found it to be more a mystery romance. I absolutely loved this and cannot wait to read the rest in the series. 

Debbie Sue is determined to win the reward money for solving the murder of the nastiest woman in town.

She is not going to let her ex husband Buddy, the town sheriff, stop her. 

With the help of friends, she solves it but almost becomes a victim. She and Buddy realize they are meant to be together and get remarried.
