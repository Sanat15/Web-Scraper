Rating: 2/5

This book has so little information about John Colter that much of it is actually about other people instead - Lewis &amp; Clark, Manuel Lisa, and others that knew Colter. When the author does discuss Colter himself, much of what he says is obvious conjecture: 'Colter very likely...'; 'he might well have...', 'he must have...', 'Colter would not have...', 'There is no reason for believing that Colter...', etc. It looks like there are several newer books on Colter out there. Maybe one of those will have more insight into the mystery.
