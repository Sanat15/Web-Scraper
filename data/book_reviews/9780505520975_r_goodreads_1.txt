Rating: 4/5

In the Kentucky wilderness, Pamela found love and passion in the arms of a Miami Indian. But when one of her brother is murdered and the other one abducted, she blamed the same Indian, Strong Bear, for the tragedy because before that fateful day, her older brother had discovered her with the Indian and harsh words were spoken between him and Strong Bear. 

Strong Bear decided to take Pamela to his village and people after the tragedy.he planned to make her his wife but there is one white man who wants her and who will stop at nothing to get her. After all, he is the one that killed her older brother and got the younger one abducted. 

This story is filled with love, ecstasy and passion between strong bear and Pamela. Although strong bear already had an Indian wife, it was not uncommon for chiefs to have more than one wife. I do not like this custom but it is what it is. And I respect that. 
The fight between the whites and indian is neverending. So many senseless killings and lives lost but that was how life was at that time. 
Strong bear is a strong chief looking out for his people. Pamela is a strong heroine more than suited to be the wife of such a chief. She met with a lot of tragedies but those tragedies shaped her and strong bear's lives. 
The story was a bit drawn out however. Too many attempts were written and wasted by the white man who wanted to steal her away from strong bear. Those were unnecessary. It seems every time, the white man has something up his sleeve to steal pamela and his attempts always end in vain. Also the ending was boring. I find myself rushing through it. Pamela left strong bear to go to Louisville to tend to her younger brother after he was found. There she spent countless days, nights and hours wishing for strong bear. All of this was time wasted. The author could have made the ending much shorter without drawing it out so much that I end up losing interest.
