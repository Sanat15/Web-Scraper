Rating: 5/5

Another installment in the story of the folks who live in Valentine, OK. All the regulars are there; Vella and Belinda from the drug store, Winston, and a new character, Claire Wilder, who stops at the Goodnight Motel and ends up buying and restoring it to its former glory. In between, there is the story of Claire's broken up marriage, a little bit of romance, a theft, a competetion to see whether a father or a son will win Claire's heart. The characters are down to earth, and the book is a gentle tale of small town life.
