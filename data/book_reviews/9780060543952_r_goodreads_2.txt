Rating: 4/5

Nick is just someone I had difficulty figuring out. Nick is a spy and has done his duty for England for years! Nick Wright aka Earl of Folkestone! We have Charlotte Whittington aka Mrs Sinclair read her father’s journals and his spy adventures and she was intrigued! 
Life in a journal is far different from real life! Charlotte attempted to emulate her father got her kidnapped by Nick and his other friends to secure the safety of England against a traitor. 
Nick has secrets but I disliked his treatment of Charlotte even after she shared secrets of her horrible marriage! 
Nick could have and should have handled things much differently! Charlotte was just like someone who has slept and was just waking up from a very bad dream after mourning! 
Nick kept blowing hot and cold and blaming his past and his care and concern for all women!? What! The more I learned about Nick’s past the more I understood he was not ready for a future! 
Charlotte fell first and fast and more so because he showed her some kindness after a lifetime without! 
Do these two get a HEA? After many adventures yes! I did like how these two stories overlap! Bravo
