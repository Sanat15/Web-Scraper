Rating: 5/5

Excellent book. Colin Fletcher writes beautifully about the outdoors. Written after he hiked the length of California in 1958, the book is more than a travel log. He captures what it is like to spend time outdoors with only what you carry on your back. It is something that can change your perspective about the world we live as
well as you as a person. He was ahead of his time. Definitely worth the read. I immediately passed to book along to a friend.
