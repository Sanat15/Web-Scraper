Rating: 5/5

This book was definitely not what I expected. There was a point throughout the book where I couldn't figure out who the main characters was. I loved reading from Wolfe's and Storm's perspective and how they got their happy ending. I can't wait to read more about Quinn &amp; Morgan's story because I felt like I read snippets of their story throughout the book. I loved the mysteries during this book, kept me really curious about what was going to happen. Can't wait to learn more about Quinn.
