Rating: 5/5

A story of a young woman and man trying to get past duty to others to love.

A young woman has loved only one person all her life but can he put aside his duty to everyone but his love to finally tell her of his love. I loved the way they sniped at each other one minute and then fell into each others arms the next. The secondary characters were great and added so much to the story. I was hooked from page one to the very end. Go ahead buy this book to read and I hope you love it as much as I did.
