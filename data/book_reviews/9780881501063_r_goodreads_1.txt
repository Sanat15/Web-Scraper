Rating: 5/5

The Criminal C.O.D. (Asey Mayo, #15) by Phoebe Atwood Taylor.

This delightful Cape Cos mystery kept me on task throughout the story. Our Cape cod sleuth brings his brother, Syl, into the picture as well as Doc cummings. The Lennox family, Charles &amp; Kate &amp; Jane, play a centrical part in this diabolical feast of unending clues. A mystery that forces Asey into following suspects no matter how difficult the task. 
So well-plotted and interesting that it's listed under My Favorites in this series. Don't pass up this book or this series.
