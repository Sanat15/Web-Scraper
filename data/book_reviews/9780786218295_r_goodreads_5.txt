Rating: 4/5

it's 1141, there's still conflict... Hugh Beringar is involved. Cadfael visits with Hugh's wife Aline, and his young godson Giles, and they wait for Hugh to come home... 

the Benedictine Sister Magdalen 😁 brings news of a Welsh prisoner they have from the conflict...

and Brother Cadfael goes to Wales to organise a hostage exchange... 

⚔🎚🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿🌿💚 

i enjoyed this Brother Cadfael novel 😃 especially because alot of it was set in Wales, and it included alot of Welshmen 😁 

there's also a concurrent story (or two) of young love, as frequently there is - plucky and passionate, and occasionally misguided (tho not by Cadfael who tries to guide all as well as possible). 

there's Welsh princedoms, borders, feuding neighbours, jostles for power and/or plunder.

and a mystery at its centre, sometimes eclipsed by the rest of the action, but never forgotten... especially by Brother Cadfael 😉🙂

🌿💚 "but even God, when he intends mercy, needs tools to his hand" 💚🌿

🌟 🌟 🌟 🌟 

accessed as a library audiobook, well read by Roe Kendall.
