Rating: 5/5

This is my favorite JD book and probably the best story she has ever written. And I know Simon &amp; Schuster think so too, as they set the price of the kindle version at over eleven dollars. (Originally published as a Silhouette romance in 1981 for well under two dollars) This quibbling aside, it is a beautiful little love story set in a picturesque town in the Quark Mountains. The characters, more cosmopoliton than townish, are wonderfully rendered, as the budding romance emerges between the very handsome minister and the lovely legal secretary. Having a religious theme might put some readers off,(no bodice ripping) but the theme is deftly handled and by the end of the book,(maybe even at the beginning) you will fall in love with Seth as much as Abbie does.
