Did his suit actually contain the ghost of the unfortunate Bernie Sanders, (not the American politician who is currently president of the US, in the far preferable alternative reality where logic and truth beat prejudice and propaganda in the last election). Or does that strange sound and worse, those intermittent touches, mean that there is an alien life-form trapped inside his space suit and perhaps preparing to breakfast on astronaut?
