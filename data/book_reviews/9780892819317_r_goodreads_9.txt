Rating: 4/5

I think this is a very helpful book, particularly the first half of the book. Written by a Yale trained MD and midwife, Dr. Romm presents a very fair and balanced guide to understanding the current vaccine schedule. I can’t say definitively whether or not Dr. Romm is pro or anti vaccine because she really doesn’t insert her opinion or push an agenda - she states facts and gives data. I don’t recommend this book in audio form, I think it’s nice to have a physical copy to be able to highlight and refer back to several studies presented and I’m glad I took the extra time to leaf through the physical pages. The second half of the book lost me. Dr. Romm guides the reader through treating common childhood illnesses with a more natural, herbal approach. Unfortunately, I lack access to an enchanted garden where I can pick fresh schisandra, perilla, notopterygii, and cnidium plants (roots? seeds? Not sure) so I’m probably going to stick with Tylenol when my kids get a fever for the time being. That aside, great book.
