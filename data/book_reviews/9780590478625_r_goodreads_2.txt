Rating: 3/5

This book is a charming, cozy, and heartwarming story that’s perfect for the holiday season. The wintery christmas atmosphere is beautifully captured, I could genuinely feel the spirit of December throughout the story.

The characters are truly lovable, Zander, the kind hearted vampire is the standout character and his friendship with Ben is the highlight of the story, their interactions are warm, funny, and heartwarming. While the villain’s role felt weak and some characters like marly thorn could have had a bigger part, the main characters more than make up for it and make the story engaging.

The plot itself is enjoyable and unique, though slightly cliché in places.
The final confrontation with the villain felt a bit simplistic and naive, but it doesn’t take away from the overall charm of the story.

Despite its flaws, I genuinely enjoyed reading this book, the ending is heartwarming and satisfying, leaving a lasting impression. The combination of lovable characters, a festive atmosphere, and a feel good story make it one of my favorites for this holiday season.
