Rating: 3/5

Very informative and contains the brilliant line “mosquitoes, which are essentially flying needles…” 

The author is an epidemiologist and maybe a mosquito expert, but sadly not a decolonial theorist. One thing this book is missing is a more comprehensive look at the interplay between colonialism/global development/foreign aid with mosquitos and disease transmission. A good companion to this book would be Robert S. Desowitz’s How the Wise Men Brought Malaria to Africa: and other cautionary tales of human dreams and opportunistic mosquitoes.
