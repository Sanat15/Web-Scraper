Rating: 5/5

A treasure of a book, containing a whole series of fresh insights into God's Word, particularly on the subject of God desiring to indeed give us everything we ask for - and continual scriptural proof of such a truth. It is honest and raw and compassionate and encouraging, and it's just a shame it hasn't seemed to reach a large audience. I praise God for the author of this book and their dedication in writing it, it has been a real blessing to me. Definitely worth a place on any self- respecting, dedicated Christian's bookshelf.
