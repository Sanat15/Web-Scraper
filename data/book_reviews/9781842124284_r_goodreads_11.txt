Rating: 4/5

Grim stuff. It's amazing to think how religion can justify setting people on fire to proclaim God's glory.
And of course Oldenbourg correctly points out that Abbot Arnald-Almaric probably never actually said of the citizens of Beziers "Kill them all, God will look after his own": there wouldn't have been time, since Beziers fell because a few of the citizens decided to sally forth in front of the town and taunt the armies besieging them, as in Monty Python and the Holy Grail; whereupon the mercenaries chivied the citizens, stormed the castle, and put all the citizens to the sword, and for good measure burned the town down before the knights and nobles could plunder the town's wealth.
