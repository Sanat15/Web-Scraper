This was okay - I really enjoyed the commentary from Dr. Imad-ad-Dean Ahmad since it was full of important References (back of book) and scriptural/hadith references where necessary; the latter was especially important when he was clarifying any matters related to Islamic teaching or Prophetic history. The commentary corrected many important inaccuracies from Lane's original work overall. I feel that this alone made the reading worthwhile and interesting. Though it is by no means comprehensive - but nice and brief, and if you're looking for something in-depth, the combination of this and the Reference list will give a good starting point.
