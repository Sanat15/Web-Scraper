What makes an author stand the test of time? Why is it that some authors have such prolific careers and others simply write a few books and are never heard from again? I believe that there are certain keys that answer those questions and the Anne Stuart has them firmly in hand. The Spinster and the Rake is a really good example as to why. This is a re-issue of an older Regency Romance and yet it stands the test of time very well. It was written at a time when historical-romance was the thing and yet Anne Stuart was able to move with the times and become one of the very best in romantic-suspense too. She has a formula that I just love: wicked heroes, wonderful heroines with grit and a this veneer of humor and mystery to bind it all together. No one writes a bad boy like Anne Stuart.

Gillian Redfern (the Spinster) has spent her life tending her siblings' children. She is now 30 years old and is basically an unpaid nanny. She has financial means, bur society and her family, frowns on her being independent. 

Ronan Patrick Blakely, Lord Marlow (the Rake) was banished from the country 20 years ago for trifling with the wrong married woman. He has returned a more mature man but his past indiscretions are still dogging him. 

When Gillian's coach overturns, Ronan and a friend, Vivian, rescue her. The sparks fly and after Gillian is delivered to her home, Ronan and Vivian enter into a friendly "gentlemen's" wager. Ronan is to prove he is still an irresistible hunk by seducing Gillian.
