Rating: 5/5

When I first started this book I wasn't sure I was going to like the way it was written. But I looked at the other reviews and felt I needed to give it a try. 

The author and survivor of the Holocaust tells her story a little differently than most; the language is raw, very real and her anger is visceral, years after she has emigrated to the US. That is what makes this story so unique. At the end of the book, she vacations with her family in Europe and encounters a group of older Germans who are also on vacation. Her reaction is one of terror since she is sure they were Nazis or at least complicit Germans. 

This would be an excellent discussion book.
