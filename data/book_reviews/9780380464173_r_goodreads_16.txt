Rating: 4/5

The conceit behind this book, namely that the Wimsey family actually existed, is brilliantly maintained throughout, with 'anecdotes' about particular members of the family and their antics over the centuries. Although the product of leisurely correspondence and 'whimsical' (ha, ha) consideration of certain points, the whole is satisfyingly well-constructed. Perhaps only for devoted fans of the author/ character, however.
