Rating: 4/5

Even though this book is called Aromatherapy for Women, it's not exclusively about women, just mostly is. The book covers things like the female system, pregnancy, childbirth, after the birth and remedies for children, as well as a bit on the immune system. It's written in a conversational style, with personal experiences on using essential oils for various reasons, and that's enjoyable - you feel like a friend is talking to you. 
The recipes are good, I've used various ones and found them helpful. It's a kind of small book, only twelve chapters, 162 pages, so it's not as extensive as another book I have, but I would recommend it as an addition to your aromatherapy books.
