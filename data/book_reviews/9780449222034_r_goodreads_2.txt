Rating: 3/5

This book was entertaining enough to keep me reading to the end, but I disliked the premise that the heroine - a young woman of breeding and every social advantage - would sing bawdy songs, vandalize bakeries, and do all the other "scandalous" things the author could think of, for no reason at all. I kept wondering when the story would reveal the heroine's motivation for "flouting every rule of polite society" but I never got an explanation. For me, her behavior just seemed like a device to generate conflict between the H and h. On the other hand, the story did have some amusing moments I enjoyed for the most part.
