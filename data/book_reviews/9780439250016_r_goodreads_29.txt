Rating: 5/5

Preschool age children love to learn new 'big' words and A Silly Snowy Day offers them one, 'ridiculous'. The book also promotes per-reading skills with the repetition in the text. It is a good book to read often until the preschool child learns the story and can pretend to read it to themselves, another pre-reading skill. Pre-reading skills are good predictor points for the child to have success in learning to read in kindergarten. The other plus is my preschool class loves the story and it took us to a discussion of hibernation and how animals do this in winter, how hibernation is different from regular sleep and what animals do this in winter. Each year I try to cover lessons in hibernating animals and A Silly Snowy Day is now part of my collection of children's book on the subject.
