Rating: 5/5

Loved this story! I don't know when I enjoyed a book so much! Maybe it was the title itself!! "sweet Dreams At The Goodnight Motel"! Doesn't that sound intriguing? Claire , who's looking for a different life , divorced, lonely , happens onto this small Oklahoma town... I have read a lot of this author's books... she always has good taste!! Lots of fascinating characters involved!!! Great read ....absolutely entertaining book.....
