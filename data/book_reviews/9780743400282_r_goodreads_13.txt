Rating: 5/5

This is the first Elizabeth Chandler book I read.

The story is about Megan, Her Grandmother and Cousin.

I dont want to spoil this book for you guys.But It was one hell of a read. 
I couldn't put the book down. Such a terrific book with all the elements to make it 
a successful mystery book .. The book is also a YA book .. and anyone who loves YA genre .. will LOVE this book .. Give it a try .. you wont regret it.
It didn't have a slow plot ., it was a perfect page turner. With good amount of witty replies, spookiness and drama !

I am looking forward to Elizabeth's new books .. I'm keeping an eye on this talented writer.

I hope you guys enjoy it as much as I did !
