Rating: 4/5

Nice little novel set in South Carolina. Janet Dailey's primary character, Lisa, a young television producer, is taking her first vacation in several years. She decides to visit her elderly aunt in the low country of South Carolina. But, she has an ulterior motivation as well. She wants to save her aunt from a supposedly criminal investment manager. In trying to expose him, she pretends to be someone else. She is unable to complete her mission. The ensuing story shows off Dailey's penchant for misdirection and unique story-telling ability. 
I enjoyed the story and recommend it to anyone looking for a fun read with interesting characters and a little bit of discrete deception. The hallmarks of Dailey's writing.
