Rating: 2/5

So disappointing. The first book felt like some kind of textbook about Lovecraftian monsters. But the second was even worse. It didn't even feel related to anything Lovecraft wrote. I really disliked the 'prehistoric' sequence. And, in regard to said sequence, why wouldn't the 'Old Ones' have struck at Crow while he was trapped back there? They had to have existed at the time and should have been aware of him. But the worst was the insipid 'love story' and visit to some kind of weird paradise. Two words sums up the insanity. Lisping dragons. Yep. It even contains the dialogue, "You thmelly?" I kid you not. But what I can't get over is the book claiming that magic is just 'another kind of science'. I really hate books that take that angle. It's like supernatural books that give a mundane explanation for every single haunting. What is even the point?
