Rating: 4/5

The Beauty and the Spy by Gayle Callen was a cute story, with a well-deserved Happily Ever After!

Nicholas (Nick) Wright, the "H", and Charlotte Whittington Sinclair, the "h", were both good characters. I really enjoyed Charlotte, and the way she improvised.

I chuckled a few times, and I am now also interested in reading Sam's story.
