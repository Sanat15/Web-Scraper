Rating: 3/5

I read this when it very first came out and adored it. Now I'd recommend it with some cautions.

Lonely Land by Denise Lopes Heald would not (I hope) be accepted now. Man buys slave, they "fall in love" and find a solution. A modern reader is going to question (I hope) the whole premise. 

And I'm not sure we'd be in a hurry to recruit straight people to a volume such as this either. 

But there are some stand out stories: 

Ellen Klages' Time Gypsy' (the title makes me wince) is lovely: as a science historian goes back into the past to meet the scientist she admired growing up.

L. Timmel DuChamp's Dance at the Edge pays for the entire volume. You'll just have to read that one because I don't want to give things away.
