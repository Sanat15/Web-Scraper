Rating: 3/5

An Assistant Professor at Columbia University, Rodger is left a Chinese lacquered box after the death of his wife. The box is supposed to provide clues to a huge treasure in Chinese art hidden by a GAO ancestor.

In need a cash to sustain himself Roger begins to look into the secret of the box seeking help from people knowledgeable about Chinese art. Then people begin to die and Roger realizes that he needs not only help in interpreting the box but in staying alive. 

I found the story to be somewhat far- fetched but interesting and it gives the reader a lot of information on both old and new China.
