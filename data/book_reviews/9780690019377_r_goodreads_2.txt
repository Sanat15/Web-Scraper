Rating: 3/5

Mostly of interest because I now live on Fleming Island. I have been to the cemetery and have seen St. Margaret's church, but have never had the opportunity to go inside. Obviously the area is much more developed than when the author came across it in 1979. There is a large Baptist church called Hibernia just up the road and a gated neighborhood called "Margaret's Walk". Fun to hear her take on the area, but hard to keep up w/ all her dear friends and research helpers!! Of course today, much of her research would have only been a click away.
