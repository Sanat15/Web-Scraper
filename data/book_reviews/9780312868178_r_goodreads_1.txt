Rating: 2/5

Cats- of both the furry feline and the cool dude persuasions- lead us into a mayhem fest of an Elvis impersonator attraction, à la Vegas hotel style. Whole lotta shakin', murdering, second-guessing, and quirkiness goin' on. Glittery flamboyance abounds in sizes S thru XXX-L. Adding in a few chuckles, deviant behaviors and curled lip smirks, plus several well-developed characters, a fun read develops. So... kick off your blue suedes, kick your mind outta gear, and allow the zany animal magnetism of all things Elvi (apparently the plural of Elvis) roll you away from reality. Author's favorite word, sprinkled as heavily as filler in a litter box, is "ersatz." I mentally filed it under "ignore mode" after the first half-dozen repeats, because the compensating Elvis factoids are sprinkled in liberally too. It would be remiss of me to not mention by name the sleuthing feline, since he's the star of approx. a dozen books in this series: billed as Midnight Louie, cat with attitude. As a cat aficionado, I found his demeanor somewhat "meh" ... sorry, Louie ... but you didn't rock my world; well, perhaps I appreciated you a slim whisker more toward the final chapters. Enough redeeming qualities in the storyline pushed me through to The End.
