Rating: 3/5

Middle school fast paced friendship drama, would go down well with the Raina Telgemeier readers in my school I think. Dangerous idea for kids to pretty much against their will give Laura something they wouldn’t want to be shared with the rest of the class in order to keep their position and the club a secret. Laura is a confident individual who thinks she can put one over anyone else and control the group, until Gabriel takes a stand and starts an opposing club. Good fun.
