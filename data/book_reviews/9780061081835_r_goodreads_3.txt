Rating: 2/5

When Jon Leighton enters her father’s bar, Elizabeth Jones is immediately taken aback by how handsome he is. He also has a kind disposition that is nothing like the other men she’s encountered, certainly not like any in redcoats. 
Poor Jon was once a great military man, but, due to being kicked in the head by a horse (no, I’m not kidding) he is really only used for his brute strength. According to his commanding officer any brains he had were scrambled from his encounter. 
Elizabeth, who is less than conventional, is intrigued by him nonetheless. They keep meeting each other unexpectedly, and each time Elizabeth sees some side of Jon that leads her to believe there is more than meets the eye. 
Well, turns out, there is. Lieutenant Jonathan Leighton buries himself in oafish stupidity intentionally. But why, you may ask. That is a very good question, which I won’t reveal! 

Writing was competent. Honestly, the most frustrating bit of this book was that it seemed our heroine was falling for someone, how shall I say it, with special needs. It was just off-putting. 

I didn’t even bother to rate it because I found it so odd. 🤷🏽‍♀️
