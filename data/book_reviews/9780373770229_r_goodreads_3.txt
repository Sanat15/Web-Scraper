Rating: 1/5

This book had one of my major pet peeves inconsistency in a series. In the previous book Once in Paris Philippe Sabon was introduced and he was a Muslim complete with praises to Allah and his country was referred to as a simple Muslim country. Now in this book he has somehow changed from Muslim to Christian and his country in now a country of mainly mixed religion. Now I don't care what religion he is but I hate inconsistency. Was the author unable to write a story with the hero a Muslim? and had to change it I don't know, but the whole thing grated after I enjoyed the previous book and was looking forward to this one.

The Author also went back to her trope that she does in some of her books of heroine smugly announcing how old fashioned she is throwing in her overly religious parents as an excuse while roundly denouncing modern society Blah!!
