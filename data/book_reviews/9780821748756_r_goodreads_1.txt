Rating: 4/5

I really enjoyed this one. It had such an interesting feel. The magic mixed in with the regency romance. There was lots of talk of The druids and gods and goddesses and even some Christianity thrown in. If you don't like your Christianity mixed in with talk of a lot of other gods and religions this would not be your kind of book. It was well written and the way that you found out about the characters lives unfolded so well. This was such a sweet romance, a little beauty and the beast. It had less morals than proper regency romance but I would still call this book clean, but it is for adults.
