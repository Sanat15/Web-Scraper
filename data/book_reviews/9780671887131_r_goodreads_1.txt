Rating: 4/5

Augusta McKenderick is a Southerner teaching at a girls school. Spending the war at the school, moving from student to staffer along with her sister, they struggle against the Union army in their way.

Major Jackson St. Charles has come to the school to interrogate Augusta, and to find out what exactly has happened to a missing senator who's last plans were to visit the school. Of course, it also lends well to try and find the missing McKenderick brother that he feels is responsible for the death of his men and his own injured leg.

Twists and turns, but nothing can keep true love apart.

A very nice story.
