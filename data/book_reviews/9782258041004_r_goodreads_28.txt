Rating: 3/5

This is a pretty early legends story, with not a ton of prior works to build off of. As a result, this is a very unique and weird story, similar to the last couple of books I've read. The story is told through three points of views. We follow Leia and Chewie, Han and Luke, and the three Solo kids. Following the Solo kids were my favorite parts of the story. Since being born in The Last Command and Dark Empire, they have showed up or have been mentioned in most proceeding works, but there hasn't really been an effort to explore them as actual characters. This story is the first major instance of spending a lot of time with the kids. Jaina and Jacen are five, and Anakin is three, almost four. These ages are great for the story, it shows what their interests are and all three's developing force abilities. The other point of views aren't anything too special, and I felt like a lot of coincidences kept happening for eveyone to be in the right place to meet up at the end. Luke was acting wildly out of character and I wasn't a fan of that at all, but there is a bit of a reason for it, which gets cleared up at the end. The love Han and Leia have for their kids is really apparent, and I appreciated the strong emphasis on it. The villian was extremely bizarre, but had a satisfying conclusion.
