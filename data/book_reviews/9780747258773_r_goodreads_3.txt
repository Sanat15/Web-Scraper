Rating: 5/5

A complex man with many talents!

This is first Roger the Chapman book I’ve read, but it will not be the last! Though Roger appears to be but a simple peddler, he is so much more! A man of letters &amp; great curiosity, his talent for resolving mysteries is recognized by the noble houses of his land. He has a well developed sense of justice, along with great powers of discernment. He is like a bulldog when faced with a crime or mystery. He won’t rest until he ferrets out the truth! Truly, a man of many talents.
