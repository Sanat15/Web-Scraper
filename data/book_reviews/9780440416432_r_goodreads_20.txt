This is one of a middle-grade mystery series about a spunky tomboy named Sammy who always seems to land in the middle of trouble. I was *obsessed* with this series as a kid (probably around 7th grade), and when I found a copy in our school library, I wanted to see if it held up. 

I think yes! I probably wouldn't have enjoyed it as much reading it for the first time as an adult, but it brought back so many intense memories/emotions that I felt reading it as a kid. This book was especially fun to revisit because it's the book where Sammy meets Casey, her eventual love-interest and one of my favorite characters in the series. The series tackles pretty adult themes but in an accessible way for kids. The world Van Draanen builds is so full and real. I want to go back and re-read more from the series, which I aged out of before it fully concluded.
