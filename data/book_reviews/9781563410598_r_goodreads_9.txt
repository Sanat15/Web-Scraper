Rating: 5/5

an ode to butch-femme culture with such beautiful prose! what a blessing to get insights into the tender love shared between her and leslie feinberg. the other reviews weren't lying...there are indeed brazen descriptions of desire, sex, and love. i feel like i've really learned to appreciate femininity and womanhood through the words of minnie bruce pratt: "i don't want woman to be a fortress that has to be defended. i want it to be a life we constantly braid together from the threads of our existence, a rope we make, a flexible weapon stronger than steel, that we use to pull down walls that imprison us at the borders." central to the book is allyship not only to her own femme identity but also to others. i appreciate how she took the time to delve into intersectionality and queer identities. perfect way to end femmeruary - i love femmes and i love their active decision to be feminine despite it all &lt;3
