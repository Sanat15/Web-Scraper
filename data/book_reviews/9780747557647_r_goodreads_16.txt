Rating: 4/5

Here's the thing.

I know this book isn't the most well written. I know the dialogue and the characters and the romance can be cheesy, and boy, are there plot holes. But I first read this book when I was 14 and loved it so much it actually made me interested in history class. It's also one of the only books I reread on a consistent basis (every few years), and so quickly at that. I still own my very tattered copy, unable to depart from it. And that has to say something, even if my English major guilt makes me ashamed that I enjoy this book series so much.
