Rating: 4/5

This is the book on which one of my favorite Harrison Ford movies was based. I wasn't aware it was based on a book until recently, and now I'm at a loss as to how I discovered that. Regardless, I think I appreciated the character changes the film adapters made because having the main character be an internal affairs investigator just fit more with how it was pieced together that his wife was missing and then the connection to the crashed flight and later finding the apartment. I'm unsure about making the woman a senator. It did create more drama, but as a SAHM, there was a greater impact to the quiet moments that may have been harder to create in a blockbuster/action/drama as opposed to maybe an artsier film. There was a lot of introspection in the book that took up pages and pages. That was filled with other things in the movie. I found the book to be more of a case study in grief in betrayal and was more focused on how the characters were making their decisions, and it forced me to consider how I would respond in a similar situation. I enjoyed this book and am glad I was able to read it. It is not a beach book. It was good for the train and car. Probably the airplane, too, if youre not a nervous flier.
