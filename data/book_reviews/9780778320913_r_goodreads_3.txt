Rating: 3/5

My first time reading this author and I was not disappointed. Vivid, interesting characters with some situations that sometimes seemed a bit contrived, but you know what it had a nice feel good factor and the author tied up all the loose ends by the time the book was finished. If you are looking for an interesting town, quirky characters and a woman deciding to up stakes and move home then this is what you need to be reading.

My thanks to Hidden Gems for a copy of this book, which I reviewed.
