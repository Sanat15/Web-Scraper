Rating: 5/5

Action packed book w/ fun facts and adventure

This story was filled with fun and adventure, as well with tons of historical facts. This book makes you fall in love with characters as well as dislike some of the others. This book makes you want to get into a time machine, go back to the 1830s and head westward into the wilderness. This book shows that women are just as courageous as the men unfortunately it also shows women can be just as evil and ruthless, if not more. This book definitely shows Hero &amp; Villain can be either Man or Woman and in some cases both genders can hold either title, of good or bad.
