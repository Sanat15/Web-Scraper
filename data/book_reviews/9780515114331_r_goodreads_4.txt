Rating: 5/5

I found this book used on a dusty shelf in an old book store well over 10 years and gave it a try. I'm glad I did because it is one of my favorite books eve. I've read it many times and will always recommend it to anyone that enjoys this kind of book. What kind of book is it? I've heard it described as Backwoods Horror and that fits the bill. This book is NOT for young readers, it has a lot of four language, sexual violence, and detailed gore.

Quick rundown since I don't see an official one. A new cop in a small town in the backwoods of West Virginia has a run-in with some mountain people, kidnapped and forced to help run an illegal moonshine operation. Horrible shenanigans occur that you wouldn't believe. The latter half of the books slows down a bit as the author suddenly includes a subplot of the wife trying to find her husband which isn't very interesting. But it picks up back at the end. This book is not for everyone. You probably won't even like it. But I love it and I am sure I will read it many more times in the future.
