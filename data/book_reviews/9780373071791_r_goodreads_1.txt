NOTHING MEANT MORE THAN LIFE -- AND LOVE.

Their marriage had ended, but not the love they felt for their son. And in five days one summer they discovered that their love for each other was still a powerful force.

When their son was kidnapped, the years of separation fell away as Ben and Maggie Altman drew together to fight for their child. Suddenly they were closer than they'd ever been, thinking--and loving as one. Side by side they fought to save one life so that three lives could be spent together.
