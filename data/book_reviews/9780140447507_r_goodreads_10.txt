Rating: 4/5

This was a set text for one of my university history modules, and has been really informative and thought provoking. I feel I have a much better understanding for abolitionist arguments during the late 18th century, particularly from a Christian / religious standpoint. I think its really intelligent how Cugoano goes through biblical lineage in order to disprove the arguments of the time (i.e that there was a hierarchy within the human species, or that Africans were somehow lesser to their european counterparts (if they were supposedly descended from Cain)). I think this text is fundamental to understanding anything about abolitionism, as well an insightful memoir / history.
