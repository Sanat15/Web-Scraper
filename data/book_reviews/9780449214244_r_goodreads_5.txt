Rating: 4/5

Not my favorite Joan Smith but still very entertaining! It’s definitely one of those regencies that has a lot going on, lots of misunderstandings and side characters causing shenanigans. Sometimes this type also still manages to be very romantic, but my romance-meter was not satisfied by this one. The ending, in particular, fell flat for the main couple. I did enjoy the book overall though! It was very funny and engaging. Personally, for a more romance-focused Joan Smith, I recommend Escapade.
