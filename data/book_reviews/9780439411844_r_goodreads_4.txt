Rating: 5/5

Beautifully done pop-up book, with amazing details. The moving parts, lift-the-flaps, and other elements were incredibly well thought out and spread quite well throughout the book. You could probably read this 20 times and notice something new every time. Many of the pop-ups are interesting from every side. The text, just bits from the actual book, is not very easy to follow, but who really cares with so much else going on?
