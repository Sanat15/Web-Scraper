Rating: 4/5

I enjoyed this one, a LOT!!! The CONTENTS confused me --- three years: 1952, 1925, 1965 and when I finished it --- the fact that it was divided up by years really put it all together! And by hearing each story individually: Etta, Dan Ferris and little Sarah ---there was a lovely amount of suspense, characters were very real, interesting ---just enjoyed a fast moving, quick (maybe 4 hours) read!
