Rating: 3/5

Reread. After reread a lot of DP's novels, I found whenever there were all the sweetness and tenderness at the beginning of a book, something bad would happen. And that's also the case of this book. h should let H do more to gain her forgiveness. 

This book had sth new: H got injured several years ago so he couldn't bed a woman. Of course h "cured" him afterwards.

H was charming at first, but then he turned into a jerk after he married h, esp when he thought he was still in love with another woman, who had a husband and a child already. Felt deeply for h. Wished H grovel more because he didn't deserve forgiveness so soon considering the way he treated h. Who cared if he was a Sheikh or not?
