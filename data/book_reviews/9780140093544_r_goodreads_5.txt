Rating: 4/5

“How I wish I had another 50 years to look forward to and 10 gardeners and 10 thousand pounds”. 

Vita was cheated out of inheriting Knole where she had grown up, but she and her husband bought Sissinghurst for only 12 thousand pounds because it was a ruin that reminded her of her previous home. She and her husband created one of the great gardens of the UK. The story of her life, her homes, and her growth as a gardener are all chronicled here. She knew and visited many of the great gardens of her time. Even the Queen Mum came to see Sissinghurst. Vita was prepared. She always wore pearls when she gardened. Not bad for an amateur.
