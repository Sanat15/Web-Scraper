Rating: 4/5

Read this until the binding fell apart (in the late 90's, before the poker boom in the early 2000's). 

Had multiple copies, read it over and over. 

Not about No Limit Texas Hold'em (re:Rounders, and later televised Poker), it is strictly about LIMIT Hold'em which is way more boring and mathematical, and less psychological. 

(why a 30 year old review? Was tasked with picking 10 books that had a huge impact on my life (or that I read over and over until the pages fell out), and this is one of those.
