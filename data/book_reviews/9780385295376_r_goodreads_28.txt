Rating: 3/5

I remember reading this 30 plus years ago and remembered it as a good book about the westward movement (gold rush, Indians, etc.). The re-read didn't go quite as well as I remembered. I found that I really disliked the character creation in the novel. All characters were super great at whatever they chose to do as well as all being beautiful. This combined with them all making some of the most stupid decisions possible made it a frustrating read. The time period descriptions in the book were very good and enjoyable (mining camps, small towns, saloons, etc.). I am not sure I would discourage others from reading the book, but it wasn't really my cup of tea.
