Rating: 5/5

Want to be mad? Want to be really really mad? This book is perfect for you then. 

I went to court when I was a kid against my step father. But my step father’s lawyer insisted I was lying because I didn’t like my step father. I just wanted to get rid of him so I made up stories of abuse. He got off. Never went to jail. And I know I wasn’t the only kid he abused. We are still living in a world that doesn’t believe children.
