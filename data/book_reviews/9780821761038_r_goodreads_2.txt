Rating: 4/5

A solid 4 star read. Years ago, I read two books by this author and loved them. I am thrilled to have found this one at a used bookstore some months ago. The Earl of Whitshire loves to solve murders, partially because he wants to understand why they are the way they are. He cares not a whit for traditional London fashion and takes everything that comes his way with humor and a practical attitude. Honor Virtue is brought to his home by his mother for the London season. The lady turns out to have a sense of curiosity to match his own. And the Earl's mother is quite determined to get them together. This story is full of wonderful characters, hilarious dialogue and moments of pure zaniness.
