Rating: 3/5

this book is almost word for word the same as her other Crone's book of charms &amp; spells"(in my opinion, charms and spells is better.). i did not care for this book to much i felt as if it read a bit to fast and threw together. i have to say if you had her charms and spell book you wouldn't need to buy this one too.she gos over the basic herbs their uses, how to mix drinks and what they represent, how to prepare balms and tinctures, also making /usage of candles and incenses. She gos on to describe the differences in charms, amulets, talismans along with symbols and inscriptions. the last chapter is her iconic ceremonies for the year. the rituals for the 12 months are by far some of the most magical set ups and saying ive came across. tastefully done from the set up to the clothes and jewelry needed for each month. all in all its not a waste to get this book i just didnt like it as much as her other book. even though they are pretty much the same this one is a bit rushed .
