Rating: 5/5

Wonderful story? Just what I needed. Opal has just moved to a new place and is living with her preacher dad in a trailer home after her mum left them both when she was three. When her dad sent her to the shop for some groceries, Opal comes home with a stray dog who she names Winn-Dixie. Feel good story of the year. 4+ stars for me for the good feelings.
