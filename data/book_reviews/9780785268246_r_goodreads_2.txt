Rating: 4/5

I really enjoyed this series and I feel like I learned a lot. There were a lot of historical details that were so interesting although it was a sad time too. It was also amazing to see the faith of these characters in action. They went through a lot. A lot! And yes they struggled, but in the end their faith in God was able to overcome the doubts. Daniel and Gen were wonderful characters that really touched my heart. I felt so bad for all they had to go through and was cheering for them to finally be able to live in peace. Did they get that? You'll have to read it to find out. 
A complaint I have with this book, and really the series, is all of the many points of view. It would often jump to a character that really wasn't all that important. I really didn't care what Aaron's lady friend back east was doing. Along those line I appreciated the epilogue, but would have liked more information on some of the characters. For example  Did Aaron marry Amanda or did he find someone else? Did John finally come to know Christ?  
Over all I enjoyed this series and I would read more by this author.
