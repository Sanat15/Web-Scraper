12/23/2025 review
I failed to take note of the title of this book after my 5th grade teacher read it to us, so my efforts to track it down afterward went nowhere. No one had heard of the Christmas twist on the prince and the pauper that was set just 50 miles from where I grew up, and at some point I started to think maybe I dreamed it up. Until, a few years later, I got a book from a white elephant gift exchange at one of my family Christmas parties—the book I had been yearning to read again. (When I saw that it was written by the guy who wrote Tennis Shoes Among the Nephites, I almost had a stroke.) It was my own bookish Christmas miracle, and for years I read it every year. Eventually I realized that there were other Christmas books to read, but even now, pushing 40, it still doesn't feel like Christmas without a quick revisit to this story. Every time I read it I love it just as much as I did when I was 11.

12/22/2019 review, 5 stars
Always a good book to revisit. I still remember a lot of spots Mr. Applegate stopped reading to spur a class discussion, even some of the voices he did, which only makes this book more special to me.

12/20/2016 review, 5 stars
My love for this book is purely sentimental. My 5th grade teacher had a knack for picking out great books to read to us—most of them are to this day still among my favorites. It's super cheesy at times, but that's kind of to be expected in a Christmas story, right?
