Rating: 5/5

A fine book that compares Engels' non-fiction The Condition of the Working Class in England with the writings of Charles Dickens and other social critics in the field of fiction. Readers of this will likely also enjoy Art and Revolution: Writings on Literature, Politics &amp; Culture, which contains selections from Literature and Revolution, a theoretical work based on Russian authors of the revolutionary period, plus Trotsky's reviews and comments on literature in the larger world.
