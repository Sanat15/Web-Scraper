Rating: 3/5

I found a cornucopia of early 1990s YA pulp fiction in a bookstore that is closing yesterday, and I am in the process of inhaling them like potato chips. How I missed this one from 1992, very much in the wheelhouse of the Lurlene McDaniel kiddie tearjerkers, I'm not quite sure, but possibly it came out the year I finished elementary school and no longer had access to the scholastic book fair where I'd buy all of these cheesy kids with diseases novels. That said, this is a good one, abouy a flamboyant girl named Star living with CF and het friendship with a newly sick girl Courtney with a heart condition caused by bad strep-tutned-scarlet fever. Would I recommend this book? No- I wouldn't even admit I read and liked it. But I did. 3 stars for finding one I'd missed as a kid.
