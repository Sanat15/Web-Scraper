Rating: 3/5

Book Sypnosis:
Her hatred had turned to love

Amanda Raymond had resented Marc Chandler ever since the death of her parents, when he had offered her charity. But now the tables were turned. With his sick mother and young daughter in need of care, it was Marc who desperately needed and sought her help.

Yet how could she cope with the increasing hostility of Marc's wife, who at times appeared half mad?

But more important, how could Amanda hope to combat her ever growing attraction for Marc?
--

Amanda Raymond falls in love with Marc a married man, an the fact that his wife is rather insane means that divorce won't be an open option for him. And the whole Chandler household suffers because of Caroline Chandler's/Marc's wife "excentricities". 

But Amanda's love is a forbidden one and she must face this fact and abandon every hope of having any kind of relationship with Marc.

And emotional story with adorable children and a courageous heroine.
