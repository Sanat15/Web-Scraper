Rating: 5/5

This one was the biggest emotional rollercoaster so far, for me. Lots of highs and lows for our beloved Ross and Demelza, as well as some dear friends. Though there were things here that made me furious at least Ross has finally found that he cares nothing for Elizabeth. I thought all along that he loved more the idea of her, so I would be happy for that if nothing else.

This family has me completely ensnared in their lives! I'll be soon picking up The Black Moon, because I need to know all the things like who the father of Elizabeth's baby is, though I would want to quit reading if it's Ross!!
