Rating: 5/5

This five star review is written from my recollection reading this novel as a horny fifteen year old.

Some background. When I was thirteen my family house burnt down and my small town community put together a collection for clothes, household items and sundry. Knowing I was a massive bookworm, I received a big bag of books kindly donated by Aunties and grandmas in my little pueblo.

This book snuck in with the Harry Potters and Lemony Snickets. I'd never read anything like this before, so imagine the fire lit in my loins when I got to the very steamy sex scene on page 67 (oh yes, I still remember after 14years) 

10/10 would bat to again
