Rating: 4/5

I picked this book up simply because it sounded interesting. I didn't realize it would also be so spiritually encouraging! Not only do you get a glimpse into various authors' journeys, Eble does a great job of showing how things from their journeys can be applied to all of our lives—whatever we do. I particularly enjoyed the opening chapter where I laughed aloud at Bill Meyers' adventures in learning to "never say no to God."
