Rating: 2/5

The story opens with a gruesome murder discovered by Liz Clarke’s pregnant goddaughter. Liz decides the police aren’t doing their job, so she investigates. Pretty standard stuff but it never fully caught my interest. Liz didn’t seem to have a real job, so she could drive all over questioning people who in real life probably wouldn’t have given her the time of day. She held back evidence from the police while griping about them not taking her theories (many of them off-base) seriously. By the end there was just too much going on: babies, dying adult children, human trafficking, smuggling, three murders and the deadly showdown with the real killer.
