Rating: 2/5

This is the type of book one could really love if:
 1) This is all they had to read and;
 2) They were in a remote location, say a hut on Gilligan's Island and;
 3) The weather was bad and;
 4) The people they were with despised them and;
 5) These people were not fluent in any language the reader understood.

Under these circumstance the book could be a 3.
