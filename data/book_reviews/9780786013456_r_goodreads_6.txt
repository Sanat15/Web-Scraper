Rating: 3/5

This is what I suppose would be a typical action novel. The lead character is a government-sanctioned killer and truck driver. The concept is interesting in that aspect.

This particular novel revolves around the main character going into a backwoods county under the control of a corrupt and evil family. In my opinion, the evil family was a bit over the top. But, I suppose what this type of book is pretty much about.

It's not a bad read, although it's a bit cheesey and unbelievable at times. Otherwise, it's not a bad way to waste a few days.
