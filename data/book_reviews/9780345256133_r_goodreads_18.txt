Rating: 5/5

Alethea comes from a big family of boys. With her father being a country scholar, she grew up as the bookish type. She arrives in London to spend a few weeks with her aunt and acquire a 'town polish'. There, she encounters her scheming aunt, her spoiled cousin, various friends and acquaintances, and an incorrigible flirt.

This was one of my favorite reads of the year. Not only is the romance adorable, but the author also manages to write in a way that reveals the depth behind each character. This is a quieter book, rich with characters portrayed very realistically. Some of them are unpleasant, but not in a caricatured way. For example, I found myself warming to the spoiled beauty. She is "wanted" by every man but is conscious of being merely a plaything for them to display. Although her mother seems to dote on her, in reality, she is a cold woman who sees her daughters as puppets. The side romance also subverts the typical good girl/bad guy trope.

The main romance has a very realistic push-pull dynamic. Alethea is attracted to Sir James but has already written him off as a flirt. And indeed he is, so you cannot fault her for deciding to push him away. In fact, I liked how down-to-earth she remains throughout the whole ordeal.

This is a quiet story with fabulous characters and amazing writing!
