Rating: 2/5

This book is packed with information about the development of Christianity. It is very interesting to remember that Jesus was a practicing Jew and that the first "Christians" were attending synagogue and keeping the laws of Moses. It was also interesting to learn more about the political setting for Jesus life and how that influenced the development of the Christian religion. 

However, the writing is convoluted and the book is overwritten and repetitious. Some sentences are 8 or 10 lines long. Along with that, the author changes direction paragraph to paragraph. This mean a lot of rereading to try to understand "where are we now." Schonfield uses the term "we" (as in "we learned in the last chapter") which feels manipulative. Please don't include me in your conclusions. I can come to my own.
