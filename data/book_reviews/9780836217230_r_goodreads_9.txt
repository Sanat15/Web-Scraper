Rating: 5/5

Lots of toddler content. Michael gets his driver's licence. Elizabeth and Dawn get caught up with a friend who is toxic and a bad influence. Elly and Phil help their parents down size. This book also includes the arc where Lawrence comes out as gay. I remember when this was running in the paper and I remember that arc was considered very controversial at the time and caused quite a stir. Reading it now, the part I find controversial is how poorly all the people in his life take the news.
