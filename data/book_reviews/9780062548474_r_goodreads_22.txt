Rating: 4/5

This is a fascinating little book about mythology, symbolism, and the darker aspects of our psychology. Essentially, this book speaks on the integration of our shadow selves into our daily lives. Only when we make peace and reconcile with the parts of ourselves we shame, reject, or despise can our lives really be more peaceful and meaningful. 
There are some really beautiful passages here and lots of great poetry to point to this mystical process. While the book is brief and helpful, it is only a soft introduction to shadow work--which remains a mystery to most. I would recommend this to anyone who is seeking to unify the many different dimensions and aspects of "self."
