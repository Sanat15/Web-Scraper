Rating: 2/5

Another one of those strong and disgustingly behaved heroes who tries to cow down a lovely, penniless, young aristocratic lady who has charge of three very young siblings. What kind of audience do such authors think they are catering to? It is demeaning and not crucial to the story line- could have been handled differently with more creativity and wit but clearly the author didn't have the time and inclination for it. "She shivered a little at the thought of the coming reckoning. " That comment from the book was not in a sexual context but ......
He can say anything he wants to her but she can't do the same (in fact she thinks the above quoted line after some attempted putdowns by two 'ladies' of the 'ton. ' She loudly says a few words to fake what a great time she has having with this jerk, "Drogo" and he acts all weird about it. What an a--hole!) 
The hero wants a submissive female to bully along - he is the tall, angry, cliched rake and in her situation it is difficult for her to push back. She has to swallow her bile and trot along submissively. He doesn't even have the integrity of convictions which make him stubborn, oh yes he had pretty parents but wasn't abused or brow beaten or without money ever. 
So along the way, her intrinsic nature causes him to improve and start showing a better version of himself. Yes it's a HEA ending but I didn't care for it. Too cliched all the way- the only good thing is that it is a clean romance.
