Rating: 5/5

I'm not entirely sure how I got this book, but I unearthed it while cleaning my apartment and decided to officially read it, being as I'm unapologetic Knight Rider trash. It followed the plot of the pilot episode well enough. I feel like some of Michael's characterization went a little off the rails, but it wasn't enough to really detract from it. I didn't go into it expecting high literature, and that's okay. It was a nice reminder of the things that made me fall in love with this series in the first place.
