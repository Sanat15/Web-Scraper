Rating: 5/5

This was so good. Recommended by a friend. I think this was so well put together and such a good resource for pretty much anybody in the church. I feel this is a great tool for identifying issues/mistreatment with numerous real examples, while pointing out behaviors/wordage to be cautious of that could be (hopefully) unintentionally negatively affecting others. Amazing Biblical backing to help with specific situations. I think if you're approaching this as a victim, it does a good job of revealing abusive behavior in a way in which you aren't acquiring more resentment and bitterness from past experiences, but rather validating that it shouldn't have happened, and you may feel or act a certain way because of it. This is helpful in the acknowledgement of abuse, explanation of how healing will be a process (with encouragement to potentially seek help of some sort), and hopefully biblically putting a stop to that mistreatment (which will not look the same in every single circumstance). Regardless of whether or not this is something you've dealt with from some angle, it's honestly just really good info to have if you're in any position of serving/leadership in the church while engaging with other people regularly.
