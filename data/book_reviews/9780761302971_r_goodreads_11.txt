Rating: 4/5

This was much more than I was expecting! It was sort of a juvenile nonfiction compendium (meaning, in less than 20 pages) about how the winter solstice was celebrated or acknowledged in other times &amp; cultures, followed by a page on some basic earth science that really helped to clarify for the person I was reading to how the seasons happen. (Albeit, that person was not a child but an adult with a not-so-great grasp on earth science.) Definitely a good seasonal read!
