Rating: 4/5

Lisa Jackson is a very popular author for one very good reason, She tells a great story that draws you in.

This story is about Marnie Montgomery. Marnie is the daughter of a successful hotelier, and based on how things look- Marnie has everything she ever wanted. She works for her fathers company and is engaged to her fathers right hand man. But the one thing she wants is independence. So things are about to Change. Starting with her resignation.

Adam Drake is out to clear his name. The false charges against him have ruined his career. To do this he needs to find all the secrets about Montgomery's company by any means necessary, even if it means deceiving Montgomery's daughter.
