Rating: 4/5

Another Asimov. This time a short book written for UNICEF as a way to teach about the problem of over population and out of control population growth. This was written in the 70's so it is a bit outdated. It's more simply written as though it's target audience was less educated or children. In the book though it does talk about the importance of educating people about the problem so this was certainly done on purpose. One interesting note is that Asimov projected that by 2005 50% of earth's population would live in cities. That actually happened in 2008. Pretty darn close. There are certain similarities between the population crisis and global warming today. This book got me to look at some population trends and other statistics that I probably wouldn't. I imagine that if Asimov was still alive today he would be doing quite a bit of writing about global warming. Here's hoping we can come together and solve some of these problems.
