Rating: 4/5

Coming out in 1990 with strips from 89 and 90. Johnston does a sort of flash back sequence in which Elly is reminiscing with the kids about when Liz was an infant, but it just reminded me of how much better the strip is now than it was then. But she did another flash back about Elly’s own experiences with romance as a teenager that was pretty good. For the most part it’s the standard strip progression with Michael as a teenager getting lots of coverage.
