Rating: 3/5

Harry goes with his parents to Tikal where they are digging for Maya artifacts. He encounters different animals in these books, some he has to run away from. Make sure you listen to sounds of Howler Monkeys when reading this book.

His parents tell him about the legend of a curse that comes with taking a Mayan king bones and jewels and what happen to each one that did. Then these same parents tell him there is no curse.
Later on he goes to The Lost World (no he didn't meet dinosaurs, just a pyramid and…). There he literally fell into a pit that led to a temple, where there sit a king. Harry takes the skull with him and shows it to his dad. After that things went downhill for Harry and his parents.

Let's just say I told Harry no more than one time in this book. I would have to reread my other books on this series to see where it stands with them, but it felt pretty, I don't know. Normal, compare to other books of this genre.
