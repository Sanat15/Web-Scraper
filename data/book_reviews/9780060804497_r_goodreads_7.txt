Rating: 5/5

Excellent thriller cum whodunit set in Moscow of 1951 with lots of authentic details .While the tension is not as much as in the ashes of Loda ,this one has two great things ...
1. A really standout terrific chapter about life in Russia and the disillusionment when Verney meets Liefschitz .
2. A great twist at the tail . 

The more I read of Garve ,I wonder why he is not reprinted .He is just too good to be forgotten . Anyone who likes Ambler is likely to enjoy Garve .
