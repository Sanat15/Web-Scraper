Rating: 3/5

I just love libraries! I really wanted to read this hard-to-find book, so I did an interlibrary loan request. My library borrowed it from a library 5 hours away and had it sent to me. For free! God bless public libraries!

SPOILER ALERT
Anyway. I read the first book in this series called Switchers many years ago. I recently read it again and enjoyed it just as much. I looked to see what else Kate Thompson had written and found this sequel. To be honest, I found myself enjoying the book and then annoyed when there was a vampire in the story. I kept thinking, Enough already with the vampires! Then I remembered that this book predated all the Twilight books and their followers. That makes this book even cooler. And it means that Kate Thomspon really is imaginative as I thought and not simply a band-wagon follower. Yup. I liked it.
