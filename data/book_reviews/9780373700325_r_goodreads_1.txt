Rating: 3/5

The Great, Big, Terrible Misunderstanding trope should be used sparingly, like salt. I don't mind it when it is sprinkled lightly, as it can season otherwise bland fare. But when you overuse it, the meal becomes inedible. This is what happened here. The GBTM between the protags lasted the entire time: TOO MUCH! Without it, it could potentially have been a good story. 

I loved the setting in a Texas ranch, where a Hollywood crew is filming an epic western. I loved the country-boy/city-girl pairing. The author described the heroine's demanding job as a p.a. on a movie set very plausibly. The details about Texas history may be boring for some readers, but I liked it. 

 It was just the lack of communication and stupid assumptions and jumping to conclusions of both protagonists that drove me batty, eventually making me lose interest before the resolution of their conflict. And that ending was very underwhelming to boot. If you torture the reader for almost 400 pages with the "will they or won't they" theme, at least, give us a conclusion to redeem it all.
