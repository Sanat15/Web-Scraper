Rating: 4/5

The book provides very good timeless advice on trading. Although a bit repetitive towards the end, I found numerous pieces of wisdom throughout. 

Edwin Lefevre teaches you the importance of conducting your own research and constructing a system of strict rules to follow when going long or short. A trader should focus on what he knows best and prefer smaller amounts of profit with higher probability of realising rather than short-term improbable large profits.
