MWB typically knocks it out of the park when bunnies are involved. Just thinking about The Runaway Bunny gets me misty-eyed. But this rabbit romp reaches and falls flat. The best home for a bunny isn't with a bird, or a groundhog, or a frog. It's with a bunny! Holy cow! Next you'll tell me that I should plug my USB device into a USB port.
