Rating: 5/5

This is a beautifully written historical novel, much more than just a historical romance. It’s written around the story of the Princes in the Tower from 15th-century English history and is rich in historical detail and intrigue. For readers who are just looking for a story that takes you from A to Z (boy meets girl, they fall in love, get married), this book may not be for them because it provides so much more of the historical context. But in my opinion that ultimately adds depth and poignancy to the love story. I’ve read many historical romances and this is one I keep coming back to as one of my all-time favorites, and it brings tears to my eyes every time.
