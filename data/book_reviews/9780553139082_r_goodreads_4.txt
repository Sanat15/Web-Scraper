Rating: 2/5

I would rate this 1 but I feel I wouldn't finish a book I was going to rate a 1 so a 1.5 to 2 I'll give it but I also kind of wish I didn't finish this lol. I did not enjoy it at all, first very little romance in this, the description at the back of the book doesn't tell much but it does at least sound interesting and promises lots of romance and intrique, it does not deliver at all, the book is basically just the life story of Fiona, which might have been fine if she was even remotely likeable, she isn't!
The heroine drove me insane, she was insufferable and treated the hero poorly through the whole thing and why you ask? because he offended her sensabilities once lol which he apologised that he misunderstood and took her straight home and she already gave out hell to him for this misunderstanding so you'd think that would be the end and would move on from there, but the heroine is so deeply offended by this lol that she carries it for years and plans revenge against him and blames him for everything and treates him horribly....which was ridiculous to say the least, could the author not have come up with an actual reason for her having such a deep grudge against him? lol. He finally marries another woman for a while towards the end, after her treatment finally pushes him away and I was never so happy to see the hero of a book marry another woman lol I was glad to spite the heroine for a while and one can only imagine why he is still in love with her at the end, he should have left her after the stunt she pulled on the ship.
