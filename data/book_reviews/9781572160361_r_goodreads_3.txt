Rating: 5/5

I am sad to have finished reading this book.......one of the best books of all time for me.......something about the story really captured my imagination.....Nils (Thumbietot) is an unforgettable character.....as is Akka and Morten Goosey Gander.......no wonder Selma received a Nobel prize for this book.....it really stands the test of time.......having recently learned through a DNA test of my Norwegian and Scandinavian roots I particularly connected with this story.......wonderful, wonderful book.
