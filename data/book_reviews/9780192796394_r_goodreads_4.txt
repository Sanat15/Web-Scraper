Rating: 5/5

First published 1968 early in Wildsmith’s career is typical of his bold bright colors schemes and this time it focuses on fish in their various groups and gives us their collective noun for each individual species. For many years I have assumed that the collective noun for fish was “a school” that is not the case.
In this title we see a leap of salmon, a hover of trout, a herd of seahorses, seventeen species in all.
Someday I hope to have read all of Brian Wildsmith’s titles.
