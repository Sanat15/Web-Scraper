Rating: 4/5

I don’t know where I found John Gardner. I’m assuming bookstagram but I haven’t been able to find those posts. Whomever you are, thank you! I’m officially a fan. Having only heard of let alone read, Grendel, the Beowulf story told from the perspective of the “monster” I picked up The Sunlight Dialogues because it just sounded original. And man it was. Big in scope and just short of 700 pages, I absolutely loved. Philosophical in tone it was layered with some of the more original characters. Batavia, NY (near Buffalo) in 1966 was crazy and Chief Clumly and The Sunlight Man were addicting characters. Could read them all day. I’ll give it 4.5 ⭐️ due to the fact that a small handful of chapters/characters were irrelevant. No biggie.

More to come from John Gardner. Hooked.
