Rating: 5/5

As a read-aloud, young children will relate to Ramona, and older, independent readers will feel Beezus's pain. 
This is my favorite Ramona book, because of the episode with the library book (I'm a librarian, see). Ramona, being only four, colors in the steamshovel book Beezus checked out for her from the library, and they have to pay for it. When the librarian stamps the book "Discarded," and prepares to hand it back to the girls, Beezus sees the danger just in time. If they give the book to Ramona, she will learn the lesson that any book you want to keep from the library, all you have to do is color in it! The book is given to Beezus, not Ramona, and a crisis is narrowly averted.
