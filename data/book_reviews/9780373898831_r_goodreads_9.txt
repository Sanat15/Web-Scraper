Rating: 2/5

Thought it would so fun to see Annette and the characters from The Matchmakers (Delaware) grown up.

The “Hero” just ruined this for me with his completely ‘unheroic’ behavior.

His whole multi-page speech as he rejects the heroine is horrid and extremely, deliberately hurtful. Especially this line:
“Haven’t you considered how many other women have lain in this bed with me?”

Yeah, no. Run away and find someone better.

Funny how the “H” takes the h to embarrass her in front of her dad for contemplating revenge sex with an OM, but would’ve slept with her himself if she would’ve been okay with an affair with him. The taking her to tattle and humiliate her with her father was wrong on his part also. 

He didn’t love her and the so-called HEA was both ridiculously short and lacking in any believable feeling on his part.

What a waste of good characters.
