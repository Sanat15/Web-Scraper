Rating: 5/5

The Short, Sweet Life of Eduardo Gutierrez is a story of the injustice that falls on immigrants, from the day they are born to their border crossing to their dangerous work conditions. In this case, those work conditions ended the life of this young man. He wasn’t the only one, either. 

I remember reading Jimmy Breslin’s columns starting in the 1980s, and this book lived up to my memory of the interesting stories that Breslin always wrote. This book sets the scene in Puebla, Mexico. We then get a good idea of the danger crossing the border, and finally the living conditions and meager wages in Brooklyn, NY. The story made me angry at how some people get rich off of others with no regard for the people they hire.
