Rating: 2/5

Mind-blowingly stupid, or stupidly mind-blowing, depending on whether or not you accept the supposed arguments put forward to arrest the "loony" left in their silly ideas of tolerance, complexity and helping the marginalised. Although there were some chapters where I was more aligned with the conservative angle, I am quite firmly of the former position in my summation of this book. It was insultingly dumb, boringly predictable and cringingly unfunny despite its many attempts to be the opposite. 

Honestly, I don't even have the time or energy to get into it all. Suffice to reinstate that this book was devastatingly ineffective in its attempts to justify the right-wing rejection of anything that isn't wholly in praise of America, conservative Christianity, and the right for lunatics to get hold of a weapon and murder innocent schoolchildren because Frank next door thinks the Muslims are out to get him, welfare support is communism, and every insanely rich person has accrued their wealth through sheer determination, hard work and sacrifice, thus should not have to see a penny go to taxes.
