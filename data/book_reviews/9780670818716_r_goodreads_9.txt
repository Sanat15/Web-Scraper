Rating: 4/5

If you love language and words, Robert MacNeil's childhood listening to his mother read poetry aloud, will strike a cord with you. I also love words and how they sound in poetry. And I can remember my own Mother reading and quoting poetry to the family from my earliest years. This memoir is filled with great photos of his parents and tells the story of his family and his own beginnings from childhood to his distinguished career in broadcast journalism.
