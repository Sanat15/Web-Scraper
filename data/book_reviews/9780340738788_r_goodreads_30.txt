Rating: 3/5

The first of Miller’s novels I haven’t thoroughly enjoyed. A story of the infamous
Venetian adventurer, narrated by the man himself in his old age, the reader learns of the only time he actually fell in love with one of his lovers. Although written as well as any other of Miller’s novels, the story didn’t seem to have any impetus or direction, meandering aimlessly through the streets of 18th century London, along with Casanova himself.
