Rating: 3/5

Another quaint story from Beatrix Potter's universe of antropomorphic animals.
This time it is about dilligent, but also forgetful squirrels, who all are stashing nuts for the winter.
The plot is very thin, as it mostly revolves around Timmy Tiptoe's small adventure of being chased by the other squirrels, only to end up in a woodpecker's hollow, where a chipmunk, who doesn't want to go home to his wife resides.
Needless to say Timmy Tiptoe's wife searches for him and he returns home, pretty uneventful. The is with the chipmunk, although his stubborness gave him a cold, as he returns home.
