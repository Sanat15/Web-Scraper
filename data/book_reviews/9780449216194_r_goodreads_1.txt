Rating: 4/5

I'm giving it four stars for the background stories because the actual murder mystery seemed to be less important. It was the glue that held the rest of it together, but who killed Marianna van Wijk seemed to be the lesser plot. 
As always, I liked seeing the interaction between Otani and his wife. How Akira and Akiko's marriage worked or didn't work was also interesting, just as a generational gap in marriage. 
And I liked seeing Ninja, Hara, and Kimura all developing as characters. 
I wouldn't recommend this one if you haven't read the others, it's not a good starting point. And in some ways, I have the feeling it's leading to something else, possibly what the police force will be like if Otani retires?
