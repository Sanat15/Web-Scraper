Rating: 3/5

I generally have liked Camp before, and the main plot/mystery was really interesting here, but the execution was a little rough.

The worst issue was that Sebastian was an aggressive jerk for way too much of the book for me to like him. His lust for Alexandra even while vehemently mistrusting and accusing her of being a con artist left a really bad taste in my mouth because it went on so long and was expressed so nastily. It honestly came across as misogynistic and gross. He was on the sleazy side in his behavior towards Alexandra even before Ursula (who he doesn’t even like!) turns him against her.

An FMC who was supposed to be as spirited, independent, and intelligent as Alexandra should have clocked this tool over the head with something heavy and told him where to shove it. 

I’m torn because I like the idea of the hidden siblings, but I don’t know if I can wade through two more books as frustrating as this one. Thank goodness they’re on KU.
