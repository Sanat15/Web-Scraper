Rating: 5/5

One of the more "hard-core" book that I've read. A look into the mind of a slave, Jamie, as he discovers his own desires and the dynamics of an S/M relationship.

By far the epilogue was my favorite part of the book, so don't skip that part :) It's about the only time that you get to see into the mind of Mr Benson, and the reason behind what and why he does the things he does to Jamie, which in some way are different than how Jamie "saw" or "received" them.
