Rating: 5/5

A friend recommended this book to me, saying she rereads it each year and that it's the best of Margery Allingham's Campion series.

The delightfulness of a good mystery, the profundity of a theological work, and the psychological insights of the best novels--it's all here.

A quote I am still thinking about: 

Mourning is not forgetting,’ he said gently, his helplessness vanishing and his voice becoming wise. ‘It is an undoing. Every minute tie has to be untied and something permanent and valuable recovered and assimilated from the knot.
