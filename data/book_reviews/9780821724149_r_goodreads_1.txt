Rating: 5/5

I couldn't put down this very well-written gothic romance by BCW! I liked the heroine instant partly due to my penchant for doormat heroines but mostly because I felt Elizabeth was infact a very sweet-natured person, and an ideal "victorian" wife in contrast to the very handsome domineering Irish lord Hero. And of course she loves to eat! She is not bothered by her husband's subsequent neglect and she never questions him until ...she finds out why he married her. Anyhow, the mystery starts when the H leaves the newly married heroine without consummation and doesn't appear on the scene until the honeymoon vacation ends. It's the first shock Elizabeth receives and then a major surprise when she reaches her dynamic husband's home at Dunwick ...he had been married before and Hero was father to a mentally disturbed ten year old girl called callie! Callie and the woman who looks after her,Mrs Meehan. hate h on sight but the others idea characters help h to settle down. There are nice details of Irish historical sites and information about irish castles, lots of murder attempts on the heroines life, Rebecca vibes, quest to find out the truth and a HEA. i liked both the Mcs and overall the book gave me a really good time.
