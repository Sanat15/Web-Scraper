I purchased a copy of Mystery Man/ Canton’s Protection: A Protector Hero Romance by Diana Palmer and this review was given freely.
Based on irony, this is a story where exasperation with the help of some meddling children grows into attraction and more.
 Former computer billionaire Tycoon Canton Rourke takes a vacation in Cancun, Mexico, with his daughter to relax, but soon finds himself and his daughter wrapped up in writer Janine and her sick younger brother’s lives. Things become interesting when Janine realizes he looks and sounds like the character she has a crush on in her favorite tv show, and Canton just happens to be a big fan of her books.
