Rating: 3/5

There was a lot to like and I really do enjoy most of Brown's stuff, but also some of it I didn't really find that great. I really didn't like the characters that much, although they aren't all that different from Brown's usual stock. I was getting a bit tired of the sentences with multiple "ands," though he isn't as bad about it as a few writers I've read recently. It does reflect the times. In a way it felt like several short stories broken apart and assembled into a novel. I can see him having a few stories in hand and thinking "I can make a novel out of these."
