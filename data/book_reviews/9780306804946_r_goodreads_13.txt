Rating: 4/5

How do you portray a pop band that is so full of contradictions, pretentiousness and mystique? 
Chris Heath does his best in allowing us to act as the hidden stalker fan as we travel the world with Neil &amp; Chris as they take on their first tour. 
Delightfully dry, very British yet scandalously tabloid this is a wonderfully honest look at life on the road of pop’s odd couple. 
Did I come out at the end knowing The Pet Shop Boys? Not really but then again I wonder if they know themselves, so much still shrouded in secret or cryptic monologues as misdirection or is it clever marketing? 
I still enjoy their music today and this was an entertaining book of bonus material and just like Jason Donovan I’m sorry I missed them live!!
