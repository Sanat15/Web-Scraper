Rating: 4/5

In this anthology of forty-seven fantasy and horror short stories and poems first published in 2001, those I found most impressive were: “To Dream of White Horses” by June Considine; “Prussian Snowdrops” by Marion Arnott; “The Honeyed Knot” by Jeffrey Ford; “Timmy Gobel’s Bug Jar” by Michael Libling; “Onion” by Caitlín R. Kiernan; “Where the Woodbine Twineth” by Norman Partridge; “Struwwelpeter” by Glen Hirshberg; “Cleopatra Brimstone” by Elizabeth Hand; “If Death, a Preprimer” by Sandra J. Lindow; “The Bird Catcher” by S.P. Somtow; “Tom Brightwind, or, How the Fairy Bridge Was Built at Thoresby” by Susanna Clarke; “Oh, Glorious Sight” by Tanya Huff; “Strange Things About Birds” by Scott Thomas; “The Bockles” by Melissa Hardy; and “His Own Back Yard” by James P. Blaylock.
