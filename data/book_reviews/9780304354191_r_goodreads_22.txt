Rating: 3/5

Though I’ll never remember the details of this dynasty of sultans &amp; their many wives and children as they connived with war lords, viziers &amp; caliphs, this was an interesting look at Moroccan history up til 1956 when the regime toppled. 

“Both at their best and worst, they acted in the tradition of their country. Cruelty, torture, and oppression had always been regarded as signs of strength; mercy was evidence of weakness. No man could rule and show pity.”
