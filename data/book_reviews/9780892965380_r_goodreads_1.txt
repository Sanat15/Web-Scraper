Rating: 3/5

I really liked the idea of this book, and it does have some entertaining aspects and sections, but overall I was a bit disappointed. At first I thought it was the style, which seemed to be a bit dismissive of people (and potentially misogynistic) and the sort of negative approach at the beginning. But to be fair that improved as the book progressed, and it wasn’t quite so negative. In fact the two main characters developed quite well, and the various ‘mysteries’ that developed in the hotel were quite fun. I liked the Bob Johnson society. I think the author is writing in a style, which is a little like Carl Hiassen, but not quite as clever.
