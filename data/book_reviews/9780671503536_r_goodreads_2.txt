Rating: 3/5

Ok, I’m trying not to compare this one with the prior Nancy Drew Files I read this weekend. I’m doing my best.

In Wicked Ways Nancy and her best friends Bess and George are trying to enjoy a bit of mall time together while checking out Bess’s sweet job as both a retail clerk and soon to be fashion model when a bit of retail thievery gets in the way of their plans. Although let’s be honest, Nancy is undoubtedly thrilled to find herself in the midst of a mystery!

The plot is a bit convoluted with red herrings everywhere and I lot of competing parties with motives and opportunities. Plus apparently lots of hot guys just running around the mall waiting to get picked up by Nancy and Crew. 

The crime in this one just seemed a bit pedestrian compared to number 29 how can shoplifting possibly compare to high stakes Washington DC intrigue?? I feel like it might be true that these series lose their mojo by the time they hit over 40 books.
