Rating: 5/5

A lovely little architectural exploration of the University of Virginia and what it has to say about Thomas Jefferson’s ideas about education, written by one of the greatest historical and political minds in America. I have returned to the well and drawn up a wonderful text that weaves together architectural analysis, historical synthesis, cultural criticism. In Mr. Jefferson’s university Wills identifies what is most redeeming in Jefferson while also highlighting his contradictions. Read your Garry Wills folks.
