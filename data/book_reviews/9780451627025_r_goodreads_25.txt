Rating: 1/5

I know Hamilton is from a different century but the way she tells the Greek myths irks me to no end. Always talking about love ('Zeus fell in love') when the story is about lust and rape while at the sane time romanticizing the women's fate. For example re Europa: 'Except for a few moments of terror when she found herself crossing the deep sea on the back of a bull, she didn't suffer at all'. Yeah, sure, rape is no reason to suffer. 
Nope.
