Rating: 4/5

Hooray!

J R Ewing is rereleasing her wonderful Regencies. I first read this book when it came out in the late 90s. I loved the entire series. Rereading them now feels like going home. 
This story is a poignant romance with a mature examination of the nature of love. I have all her books in paperback and I can't wait to get them in electronic form.Welcome back Ms Ewing.
