Three stories, three authors, one volume.
For the Sake of Her Child, Kate Welsh
Baby stolen. Widow searches for years when a man comes to tell her that he thinks his son is her son.

Child of Her Heart, Irene Brand
When her husband finds out she is pregnant, there is no joy. He demands an abortion. When she refuses, he leaves and cuts her off financially

Desperately Seeking Dad, Marta Perry
When her friend is dying, she asks the woman to raise her baby and says a name and the town of the father. The man, a sheriff, says, "not me".
