Rating: 1/5

The first volume of Out For Blood was what I'd call uneven, because there seemed to be a good mixture of good and bad stories. This volume, however, was full of bad stories with maybe one or two good ones. The only one I really liked was "Negatives" by Nikki Baker. The one I thought was the very worst was "Access Denied" by Diane Dekelb-Rittenhouse. It was obvious to me she'd never even been inside a strip club, let alone knew how the female strippers and the male customers behave. Ugh. I'll be giving this book away.
