Rating: 4/5

I read this book when I was at school. I have also re-read it twice as an adult, and I'm still impressed with it.

It's a really original story, full of magic and mystery. Not wizardy/witchy magic or fairy/faerie magic or any of the other brands that are currently so popular. This is something all its own.

The characters and settings are vivid and well drawn. The story kept me engaged, even at a second reading. Nimmo creates a world you want to spend time in, and characters that you want to spend time with. 

The writing isn't flawless, and the style in which the story is told may seem dated to some modern readers, but for me, it's still a genuinely enchanting book. I shall probably re-read it at intervals for the rest of my life. It's not just a good children's story, it's a good novel.
