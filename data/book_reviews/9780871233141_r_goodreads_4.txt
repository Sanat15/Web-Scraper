Rating: 4/5

This is a short and delightful read. Phillip Keller's love for his Savior and the Lamb of God is so evident in his writings. May we all develop a love for our Savior and God as he has. He has a gentle style of writing. It is almost poetic in style. We learn a little about sheep in these pages, but more importantly the picture of sheep in correlation with our Savior.
