Rating: 3/5

This was a rather enjoyable, if cheesily written, novel. Claire decides to up and leave her job and discover her past in the small southern town of Valentine. Suddenly she's the talk of the town, getting all the men to fall for her (a father and son? Really?), and taking in the pregnant girlfriend of the man her stole her her car. Eventually she takes over running the small motel from the title and trying to decide which guy she really wants to be with (including her ex-husband). If all this sounds contrived and, as mentioned before, heavy on the cheese factor, it is. But the small town of Valentine and its residents is surprisingly charming. The story has the predictable, happily-ever-after ending, but no complaints about that from me. I give it a C-.
