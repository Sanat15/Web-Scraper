Rating: 5/5

This book was fantastic for a more detailed view into the history of Einstein's theories and the progression of natural sciences into what we know today as cosmology and particle physics. In the chapters themselves he does not talk about dark energy, which is understandable because Dark Energy was not discovered/confirmed until the 21st century. However, I did like his mention of dark matter in the book. If one reads further in the Afterword he confirms the Dark Matter exists and Omega equals 1 confirming a flat universe. So even though some of the stuff is dated, I thoroughly enjoyed reading how easily and simply he explained very complex Notions. However if you don't have a physics and/or cosmology background reading this book will be very difficult. Nonetheless very enjoyable!! Moreover, his section on string theory was a great way to introduce oneself to what it is and why it was such a big deal. I also enjoyed reading the Notes at the end of the book to give me ideas on books that he used to write this book. So it looks like I have a few new reading materials to further pursue!
