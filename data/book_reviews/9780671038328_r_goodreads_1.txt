Rating: 4/5

Salem’s forced to babysit Sabrina’s cousin Ally. Much like Salem himself, Ally was punished by having her magical powers stripped away and she’s not overly excited by being left alone with a cat. Sabrina decides to throw her a magical bone before she leaves, using a bit of magic on a book of fairy tales. But as soon as Sabrina’s gone, Ally throws Salem into the book. Now Salem finds himself trapped in the magical worlds of Alice in Wonderland, Rumpelstiltskin, Cinderella and Aladdin.

Yeah, this one’s really fun. The familiarity with the fairy tales also elevates the story in my opinion.
