I didn't read this edition of the book, but rather the sixth edition. And I must say this book is written with an interesting precision and provides really good information, especially about the history of typography. The initial sections of the book mainly focus on the topic of type and typography, but from the middle of the book, the discussion about posters and contemporary graphic design begins sooner than you might expect. The reason is that graphic design is a relatively new field, and most of its innovations and related movements are recent (after the Industrial Revolution).
