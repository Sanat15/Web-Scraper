Rating: 4/5

As I was reading this book, so many of my own experiences came rushing back. Nothing earth shattering is revealed, but the cross-cultural adaptation process is clearly explained in an easy-to-read format. Sometimes, it felt a bit repetitive, but there were times that I laughed out loud, remembering my own adventures. It is recommended for anyone who is planning to move to a different country, but within the US, it could even be beneficial for those moving to a different state.
