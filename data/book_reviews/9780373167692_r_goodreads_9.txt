Rating: 5/5

Best friends, Dylan and Whitney, have an impulsive one night stand and decide to put it behind them. Well that one night stand leaves Whitney carrying Dylan's baby. The problem is Dylan is set to marry someone else in a prearranged marriage. Does she risk ruining his upcoming marriage and tell him or keep the secret to herself? A well written story that pulls you in and keeps your attention from start to finish.
