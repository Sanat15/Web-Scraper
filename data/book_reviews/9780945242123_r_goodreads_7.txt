Rating: 5/5

This book is written by Chariji ( Sri Parthasarathi Rajagopalachari ) about his guru Babuji(Shri Ram Chandra of Shahjahanpur) . The first half is entirely about Babuji, the reading experience creates a profound effect of love in our heart, a clear picture of Babuji is visible in this portion of the book. The second half gives more emphasis on the concepts in Heartfullness meditation or Sahaj Marg. The author mentions his experiences with Babuji which are answers to the questions in the mind of the seekers. This book is a must read for those practice Heartfullness mediation.
