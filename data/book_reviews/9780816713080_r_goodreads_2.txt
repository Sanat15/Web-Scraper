Rating: 5/5

The setting of this book is modern day, in Shannon's house, and a barn out in the middle of no where. The conflict is Shannon has been kidnapped by this man who claims to be her brother. Katie shows and up and see's this man ,but since she just met Shannon she didn't know who this man really was. The main characters are Shannon Lindstorm, Katie Osborne, and the kidnapper (never really use his real name a lot mostly call him "he" or "him"). Shannon has been kidnapped by someone who is convinced is his sister "Angie" ,but no one knows who or where he's taken her ,except Katie Osborne. Shannon must go through a suspenseful journey to get away, meaning she has to not only outsmart the kidnapper ,but use her studies on disturbed people to become a psychiatrist. Except she is not the only one in danger of this kidnapper, he's after someone else who know's what he looks like and could give him away.
 I thought this was a very good book because it was not only suspenseful but kept you hanging with questions, also it puts you in the shoes of girl who got kidnapped. I thought through this book it was amazing how the author put this into such detailed words that you could almost see what Shannon and Katie was going through and it gave you a kind of agony. Not only does it put you in the shoes of the victim ,but it also makes you think what would I do if this happened to me? How would I get away without getting killed? 
 You might want to read this book not only because you get to see almost what it is like to be in the victims shoes ,but to also wonder what would I do in this situation. Also, this book is very suspenseful leaving you wanting more. No words to put how the author put it. You might also want to read this book if you are a person who likes kidnapping and suspenseful stories. In conclusion, this book is a marvelous book that keeps you on edge and keeps you questioning at the end.
