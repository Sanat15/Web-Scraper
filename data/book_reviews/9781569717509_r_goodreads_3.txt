Rating: 4/5

Buffy's mom is dead. Emotions are running high, and slaying isn't maybe at the top of the agenda any more. But grief can some times lead to anger and the need for revenge - and it may even come from the other side of the Slayer business. Then there are the three little demons that seem intent on causing chaos while feeding off the jealous emotions of the Buffy's closest friends. Secrets, jealousy, and emotion collides, and salvation may just have to come from one very unexpected source... Spike! Fassbender and Pascoe put together two stories here, the one dealing directly with the Slayer and her grief being pitted against another whose grief she caused to powerful effect. The second tale is another emotionally charged story as frustrations, jealousies, and tensions within Buffy's inner circle of friends reaches breaking point - just the crack in the door that demonic forces need to filter through. It's definitely one of the heavier, more drama-filled books in this line of classic Buffy comics, and for the most part there isn't much lightheartedness or quips to be had. It's a satisfying read for fans and followers of the franchise, buoyed by the always reliable and good looking art from Richards and Pimental, who continue their good work at capturing the likeness of all the tv show actors with their clean lines and heavy shadows.
