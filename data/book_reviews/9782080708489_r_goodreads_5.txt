Rating: 5/5

Another work by Plato, this book is phenomenal. outside of the actual apology and Crito there was a lot of information covering everything. Socrates didn't write, he had a lot of dialogues which Plato and Xenophon are most notable from noting down. This can become an issue as it is hard to tell whether or not they are biased, leaning towards oligarchy, democracy, their religious opinions, historical [and the books does a great job of analyzing not only Platos and Xenophons version of the apology and crito (despite the fact the in the book there is only platos version of the apology and criton) but from many other sources as well, with an extraordinary amount of notes and bibliography to back everything up]. As i neared the end of the book, it felt more like a novel, Crito is great. the Apology is more of an attemp to disprove the accusations made against him whilst [Crito] is more so a dialogue. It feels more energetic and a lot more of socrates actual opinions are visible in it. One of my favorite reads of all time. 4.7/5
