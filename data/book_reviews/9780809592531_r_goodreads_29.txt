Rating: 4/5

"When will people understand that it is useless for a man to read his Bible unless he also reads everybody else's Bible?"
Is binge-reading what people did before binge-watching was a thing? Back in the days of Brideshead Revisited—in which the Father Brown stories do actually feature. The dozen stories in this collection are absolutely bingeable—formulaic (comfortably familiar) but full of variety as well as atmosphere—sometimes to the detriment of the mystery. The settings are always excellently drawn and Father Brown (of course) always happens to be conveniently nearby. Chesterton is not always interested in the brilliance of a crime, but rather in what is reveals about the state od society or about human nature. Father Brown is as happy to save a soul as to unravel a mystery, and he's often accompanied by the pretty much useless Frenchman, Flambeau.
