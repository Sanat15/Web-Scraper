Rating: 4/5

Holleran's short stories detail the lives of assorted narrators, who are all similar in style and nature and the lives they live in all of the gay meccas of the late 20th-century. Stories are set in New York, Fire Island, Philadelphia &amp; Florida (where Holleran later moved to take care of one his parents). The stories speak to a particular time and place that any gay men will recognize, and they are wonderful exercises in ideas. In essence a place for Holleran to play with some of the larger ideas and themes that he expands in his novels. The stories are at once sweet and nostalgic capturing a period of time that has since evaporated.
