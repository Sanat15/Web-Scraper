Rating: 3/5

I had to speed read this book because the library needs it back TOMORROW, but I can't imagine spending more than a few hours with it anyway. Not that it isn't worthwhile, but I just don't have that much patience for perfecting my different makeup strategies. The book does have a nice vibe, very much embracing simplicity and natural-ness as far as it goes with style. Who can argue with a man that says no to artificial nails and tons of hairspray? Also, I think he may have persuaded me to try figs.
