Rating: 3/5

with just a few days left in 2025, i realized that i hadn't read any christopher pike yet this year! quick, get me a paperback!!

there are some particularly implausible details to this story - somehow these high schoolers are allowed to use A REAL GUN in their school play, i guess because it's the 80s? and when that gun ends up killing someone during the performance and our protagonist is implicated, the detective befriends her and shares details his investigation with her? despite her being the supposed killer? ummm okay.

i was truly elated with the ANAGRAM IDENTITY REVEAL! i love when a crucial clue is right in front of you, as long as you know the mystery novel conventions. figuring out the killer by typing out the anagrammed names on my phone really gave me that detective dopamine!

this is not one of pike's best, but it's an entertaining murder mystery with a fun measure of melodrama. i was disappointed to find it to be rather tame and normal, almost something that r.l. stine could write. IMO pike is at his best when he's at his weirdest, and this is not his weirdest.
