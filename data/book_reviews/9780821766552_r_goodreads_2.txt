Rating: 3/5

A LOVELY YOUNG WIDOW Diana Milton, widow of the Earl of Windom, dislikes her husband's fusty relations, particularly handsome, arrogant Lord Arnside. When Arnside's fiancee, Penelope, runs away from school, Diana becomes the girl's protector--much to the horror of the gentleman. But when Arnside shows a special affinity for Diana's beloved little boy, she unexpectedly loses her heart to him.

The book is simply okay - not the best book from Kate Huntington that I have read. The story is sporadic at best. There are gaps in the story line and needs closer editing. There is no tension between the hero and heroine.
