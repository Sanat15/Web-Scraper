Rating: 4/5

A Promise of Salt is a very haunting read. Lorie Miseck's memoir of the murder of her sister, Shiela, is something that will just reach in and pull at your heart strings and make you truly appreciate having your siblings alive and well. Written as a letter to Shiela, you go through the entire experience with Lorie. From the disappearance of her sister, to the discovery of her body, to the trial, to seemingly never ending period of grief, to the eventual beginning of healing which anyone who has suffered a loss can relate to. But on another note, it truly brings this horrible tragedy to life. It takes these horrors we hear about in the news and dramatize in shows such as CSI and Criminal Minds and truly reveals to us how real this is and how devastating for the victim's family. This book is not filled with fanfare and action, but is just a simple letter to a lost sister that will leave a huge imprint on its readers.
