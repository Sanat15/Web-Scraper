Rating: 5/5

I found this book riveting. Libertarianism --not conservative, not liberal. Somewhere else--. I came to the read neutral, and I leave fairly neutral, but the read has given me much to think about. Individual liberty versus power of the state. I became interested in Libertarianism when I read Joel Salatin's Book _Folks This Ain't Normal_. If you are interested in Libertarianism, I highly recommend this book as it covers both the historical perspective and also the possible role of Libertarian thought today.
