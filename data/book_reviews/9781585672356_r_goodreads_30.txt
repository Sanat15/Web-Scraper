Rating: 5/5

Lovely autobiography, the second book in a series of six autobiographical books.

Actress and author Carol Drinkwater became famous with her role as Helen Herriot in the 1979 TV series "All Creatures Great and Small", then met her French husband Michel Noll, a producer, while participating in a movie in Australia. 
The couple got married in 1988 and bought an old olive farm 10 minutes from Cannes, Côte d'azur, France. 
Her books tell about the trials and tribulations of running the olive farm Appassionata.

Highly recommended!

I'm waiting now for volume 3.
