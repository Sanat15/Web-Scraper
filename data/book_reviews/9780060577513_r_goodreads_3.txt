Rating: 3/5

I've read 2 of Isenberg's books. When i was reading them i felt as tho i could fit right in w/ her and her 2 cohorts in crime solving. Maybe it's because she teaches in a community college and i've been a teacher all of my life. Maybe it's because her stories are set in north Jersey, an area i am familiar with. Maybe it's because she is a woman of a certain age w/ grown children. Her characters feel real to me. In what other book do you read about post-menopausal incontinence?........lol......I will look for all of her books to read. They are comfortable, humorous and easy to read. I enjoyed these 2 and recommend them.
