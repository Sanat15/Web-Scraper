Rating: 4/5

At first I thought this was going to be a generic book to motivate you to go for your dreams. Half way through I was ready to give it 3 stars, maybe even 2. But the best stuff is at the end. It has some great little sayings that really do inspire you. The way McNally talks about commitment really is different than other books of this nature. If you have been working on a project or struggling to develop an idea you can’t get out of your head - then this book might be good for you.
