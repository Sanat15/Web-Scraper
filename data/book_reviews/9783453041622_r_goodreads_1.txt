Rating: 4/5

Interesting story about a family that moves to Silverdale after the father gets a promotion at Tarren Tech, a company which seems to be connected to everything in town. Silverdale is focused on football, and the home team never loses. The boys on the team are quite large and strong. And they all visit Dr. Ames at a sports center called Rocky Mountain. But not many people know the lengths Dr. Ames is going through to treating the boys. Then things go wrong when the boys start acting and looking strange and violent…

This was a captivating story. The book had some creepy moments and it was fast paced and easy to read. Another chilling story by John Saul.
