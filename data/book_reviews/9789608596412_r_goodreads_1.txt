Rating: 3/5

This book is now 25 years old and is undoubtedly horribly out of date, although I have not yet been to Corfu with it to verify this. Even allowing for this, I imagine it would still be incredibly useful, even today. However, I had to take a star off for the lack of maps. One map of the entire island, lacking in detail, is not good enough for a guide book. It needed more detailed maps for each section, as the directions given will not have stood the test of time.
