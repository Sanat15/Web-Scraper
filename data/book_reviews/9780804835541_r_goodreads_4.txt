Rating: 4/5

Well. this was a good one! Published in 1990's, so not current, but the nature of the story isn't dependent on being current. 
 Turned out to be a story about brutality in Japan during WW II of Japanese troops to Japanese civilians. 
 One of the main characters was a shrine maiden dancer. Sigh... I've always noticed them around shrines in Japan. Another distinctive beauty of Japan. They sometimes even work in the shrine gift shops. One I spoke with briefly and she was so nice and "down to earth."
 Japan is such a mind blowing mix of the ethereal and mundane.
 Oh, and the moniker, "Shinano Columbo," is awesome! Shinano is an old name for the area now known as Nagano Ken. When I lived there with the Takasuna's yeah so many years ago, I kept hearing people use this word and finally realized I didn't know what it referred to. That and the word, "Shinshu," which I think refers to Nagano Ken also. So, I just loved references to the history, local lore and special places there.
