Rating: 5/5

As I start to review my first Berenstain Bears book for Goodreads, I will acknowledge a fondness for this series, back in the day. Raising my son, I liked the appropriate kinds of self-help for children offered in these picture books.

Now my son is 32. The kid lit landscape has changed, and I wind up reviewing many books with self-help advice for children that I consider wildly inappropriate.

Reading this book now, I hope that nothing weird has been added to the familiar cake recipe.

PRACTICAL PROBLEM SOLVING

With a touch of humor added.

FIVE STARS. And my relief, because this book was as good as I remembered this Bear Lit being.
