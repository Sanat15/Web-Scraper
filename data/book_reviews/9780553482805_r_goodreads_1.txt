Rating: 3/5

Todd is such a dunce. This book is basically about how dumb, presumptuous, and rude he is to Elizabeth. 

The Lila parts are great. Feeling a little lonely that she doesn't have a date for the Valentine's Day dance, she invents a dream boy, who conveniently looks just like the gardener's son she treats like crap. Of course! Classic Lila. I loved her dance outfit description: black and velvety and bell bottoms, just perfectly 90s.
