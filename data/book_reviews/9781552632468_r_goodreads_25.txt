Rating: 4/5

It was hard to read as he speaks of what must have been horrific minute by minute experiences during the war. He covers the naivete and total unpreparedness these young men had heading into WWII then moves into the casual description of endless death and waste as they were told over and over to keep going “no matter the cost” for some ridiculous goal or another set out by some callous higher up. This is no romanticized historical fiction but the gritty reality of seeing so many people you know die in brutal, ugly ways when the only medication for battle fatigue was alcohol. It ends a bit abruptly — would have liked to know what it was like to finally get the order to ship out.
