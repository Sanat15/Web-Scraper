Rating: 4/5

I still enjoyed this but definitely not as good as her other Zebra Gothics, mainly due to the fact I didn't even feel like I was reading a gothic, normally these books get straight into the plot from the start and the backstory is a few pages narration but in this it was 115 pages! so the actual story didn't start till a third through the book, and while I did find the beginning somewhat interesting, 20 to 40 pages max would have fulfilled all the backstory we needed, 115 pages was excessive and because of this there was less romance, only 2 short kisses in the whole book. 

Also the problem I have with this author is the couple usually only confess and get together in the second last page (ugh!) when I like at least a few pages or an epilogue of some sort but this was even worse, they confessed and got together in the last few sentences of the last page! we as readers deserve just a tad bit more. Overall though it was still a decent book but left me wanting a lot more.
