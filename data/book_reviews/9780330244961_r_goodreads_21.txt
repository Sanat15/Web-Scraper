Rating: 1/5

I picked this up after having read most of Bury My Heart at Wounded Knee. This was no where near as good.

While it's written in a easy to read way, a good amount of Brown's writing is insensitive at best. Women are treated stereotypically, despite the book being about women and their accomplishments. Similarly, Native Americans are portrayed in a terribly racist and insensitive way, depicted as heartless savages for the majority of the book (and I really don't understand how the same person wrote Bury My Heart at Wounded Knee). Somewhat informative, but also very insensitive and biased.
