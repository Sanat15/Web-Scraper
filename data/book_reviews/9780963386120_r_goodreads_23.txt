Rating: 3/5

This book is written like an infomercial for people who don’t like kids. You know when people say polishing a turd? This is like the opposite of that. I think the basic idea is probably effective and maybe even good, but the presentation was like a shit sandwich. Does this man even like kids? Does he enjoy being married? He makes it all sound horrible. 

This book is condescending to the max. It is written by someone so deep in the patriarchy, he feels the need to tell you that this method is “dad friendly” but also that dads will probably only ever watch the dvd. (My eyes cannot roll hard enough for this book).

He calls all unwanted behavior in children “obnoxious”. And he implies that the only way you can “enjoy” your children is when they are well-behaved and compliant.

Now. I will say that this method of giving three chances and using a neutral tone when talking and not over-explaining especially in the heat of the moment is probably very effective especially when the children have consistent consequences when they misbehave. 

I did like that this books gives you a clear and concrete method for discipline. I’ve read a few parenting books that end up being more philosophy and “do what works for you” - which if I had something that worked for me, I probably wouldn’t be reading so many parenting books. 

I wouldn’t recommend listening to the audiobook unless you like listening to 7 hours of an infomercial, but maybe read the physical book and see what you think (7 hours)
