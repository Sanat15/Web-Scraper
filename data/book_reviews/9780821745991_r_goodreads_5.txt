Rating: 5/5

This is one of the very best regency novels I have read, indeed even romance novels. There is no doubt about the author's writing expertise, the storyline is so well crafted and moves along at a believable pace. The characters are intelligent and attractive. I was very impressed with the historical research and found the subject of antique books very interesting. John and Jessica's relationship was delicious, but I too wish there had been more moments between them. Will be looking for more from this author!
