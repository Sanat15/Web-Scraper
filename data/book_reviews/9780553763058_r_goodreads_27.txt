Rating: 3/5

This is a collection of three Nero Wolfe &amp; Archie Goodwin novellas published posthumously. Probably, most people know that. What I did not know and maybe you didn’t either is that the foreword is a treat in and of itself. It reads like another story about Rex Stout and gives us glimpses in his way of work — and also contains spoilers which was not an issue for me. Everything I read by Rex Stout is a re-read these days.
Do these novellas stand a test of a re-read? For sure.
Am I docking a star because one of these novellas has a variation which I like so much better? Yes.
Am I going to tell you what’s the difference? Absolutely not. Go ahead, do yourself a favor, read this, then read Counterfeit for the Murder. You’re welcome.
