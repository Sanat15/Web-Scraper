Okay seriously… this is one of the best cookbooks I have ever seen! It is like a bible . I practically hand copied the entire book because the recipes are almost always one page recipes and easy to follow instructions. 
This is a cook book there is no fluff story about someone Aunt and how little Susy just loves to eat the tarts right out of the oven. This is honest to goodness easy and delicious cooking. 
You will dog ear every page. This is a book that is straight to the recipe no frills however there are no photos but you can cook anything with this book from apps to pasta to every type of meat
