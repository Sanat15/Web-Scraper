Rating: 3/5

I'm not sure where I picked this up, but probably because I was interested in seeing that part of the world once. As I read it, I kept thinking - when did this occur? It seemed like it could have been written 100 years ago. I was amazed at her bravery/stupidity/naivety in travelling in such a remote area and assuming people in impoverished circumstances would help her and nothing would go wrong. She did get arrested a few times, but it all worked out in the end. 

It has a very simple, easy to read writing style, and there are little bits of historical and cultural info sprinkled throughout.
