So it was a good as I remember his books being, and I thoroughly enjoyed it. I just don’t want to get trapped in to purely nostalgic reads, I’m not that old yet , am I ?

This book looks at 2 mutated races of superhumans (Slans) and how they feel about each other and humans themselves. One set, the least mutated are aggressively anti everyone, the more mutated are far more caring of both “races” and are anti war anti aggression and truly benevolent. 
The book focuses on the life of one of the most superhuman mutations (although still resembling humans they have far better organs, strength and mind powers). And looks at his life to find others of his kind without being killed by humans or the less mutated warlike Slans. Orphaned quite early in life as humans capture and kill his mother, Jommy Cross, is then captured by a grasping evil old lady who should turn him in, but uses him and his powers for her own end. Jommy realises whilst this is not an ideal situation, he is being hidden from the authorities and he can use the next few years safely in hiding to hone his powers and complete his father’s work. So for many years he stays with the evil granny as he searches whenever he can for others of his kind, but also puts in place plans for the future.

Written in the 40s, I’m thinking the book is highly allegorical around the superpowers that existed at that point in human history. Who is who, I leave up to you, although I’m guessing things would be different now !
