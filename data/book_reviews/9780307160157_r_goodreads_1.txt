The story was a bit slow to start. The beginning of the book was largely filler material that helped set up the world that the characters lived in, but didn't really advance the plot. However, the later portion of the book showcased, in a general way, different jobs that can be performed by dogs. It also demonstrated how different breeds of dogs are better suited to certain tasks. I don't know how factually accurate the scenarios in this book were. But I do think the story might make a good starting point for prompting discussion and further reading among children. Still, as an adult who was reading this book primarily for nostalgia reasons, I was not overly impressed.
Did I like it? I felt that this book had some shortcomings. But then, I'm not in the target age group.
Would I reread it? Probably not.
Would I recommend it? Not sure.
