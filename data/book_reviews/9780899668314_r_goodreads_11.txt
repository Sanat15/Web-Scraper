Rating: 5/5

Grandpa is a hard man, and that makes this book, at times, perhaps the hardest to read in the series. But the character development and both endurance and growth through challenging relationships make this book one of the best in the series. 

Ralph is 14-16 in this book, so it's best read with kids when they are in the teen years, IMO. My boys have been growing up with this series, and it's been great food for profitable discussion. My husband has read them aloud to us each time, a great way to experience these books.
