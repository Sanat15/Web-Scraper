Rating: 2/5

This second book in the Norton series was a bit of a disappointment after having loved the first book so much. This book is about the year that Norton and his human companion, Peter, take a year off of work in America and travel to France and live the good life. I liked the beginning of the book before they leave for France, and even after they first arrive in France as the writing still focuses on Norton and some of the non-Norton stories are amusing. But as the book goes on the writing seems to take the focus off of Norton and direct it more on all the places Peter travels to and all the food and wine that is consumed and all the friends that he makes, while trying to fit Norton in here and there. I still liked reading about Norton in this book, but much preferred the first book.
