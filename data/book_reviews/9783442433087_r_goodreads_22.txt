Rating: 4/5

Eleven intriguing tales sure to keep you glued to the page.
Rendell's psychological crime novels are among the best of the genre. This collection of short stories and one longer one, The Strawberry Tree are brilliant, neat in word count, set-up, characters, terribly succinct and a breeze to read in one sitting, mainly because you cannot put down the book.
My fave was the 6 page Lizzie's Lover. Cleverly written with an ending I totally did not see coming. 
The bigger tale, The Strawberry Tree was a heart-break of a story with a wild twist and it all worked. 
Ruth Rendell has always been a writer I've gone back to for a good solid read.
