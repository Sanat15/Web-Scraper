Rating: 4/5

The chapter on Joyce and the discussion of ULYSSES is perhaps one of the best and certainly one of the most concise introductions and overviews of the work and this chapter alone is worth reading this book. But then Wilson provides the same service for Proust, Eliot, Stein and Rimbaud. This is a book about the changing of the guard at the start of the 20th century that looks back at the precedents and forward to possibilities.
