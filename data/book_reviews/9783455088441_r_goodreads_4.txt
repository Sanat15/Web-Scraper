Rating: 3/5

I had a very difficult time to keep reading this book. It was very dry historically at first. At about page 50, I found the writers explanation of an eternal principle intriguing - and determined to finish the book. It WAS interesting to 'visit' the colony from within, to understand the why of the outward presentation of their events, clothing and activities. Because of this, I rated 3 stars rather than 2. 

I do not have to have similar beliefs, in my attempt to understand this community's faith, traditions and hopes. Yet, I did find a few similarities. And differences. I find it difficult to equate sacrifice with punishment and suffering, especially when imposed. Nevertheless, 'freedom' of religion is something rightly sought and fought for.

You, too, may find this an interesting read.
