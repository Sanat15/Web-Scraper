Rating: 4/5

I found this book tucked away on our shelf one night when I wanted a new read, and it was a cozy, funny, and inspiring true story of a couple who couldn't have children of their own and thus opened their home by adopting twelve orphans. Reading how Helen and her husband valued people of every race and poured themselves out to serve their children was a good example of pouring out oneself for the sake of the Gospel! It's full of humorous quotes and stories from the children but also honest admissions of the difficulties and hesitance that came up, but, ultimately, I was encouraged by how they sacrificed their finances, energy, and reputation to love the least of these. A fun yet inspiring read with verbiage and history from 70+ years ago. :)
