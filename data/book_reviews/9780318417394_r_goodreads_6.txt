Rating: 3/5

Unleash your inner child with this whimsical, old-fashioned classic which seems older than its 1956 publication (apart from the presence of a television!). 

Yes, there are some unwelcome intrusions of class and race (even among the dogs themselves!) but sadly, this just comes with the territory of the period. And fortunately, some tongue-in-cheek delights too. 

Revisiting your favourite Disney version after reading is definitely recommended.
