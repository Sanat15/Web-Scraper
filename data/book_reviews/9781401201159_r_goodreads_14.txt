Rating: 4/5

Deep cover agent Carver is so deep he's not sure what's up or down. Originally a 'good guy' spy, now working for the #1 'bad guy' group, high up in the organization, his body count is insane, and the only person who knows he's actually one of the White Hats is in a coma, not likely to wake.
This could have been a cheesy B-Movie or a horrible book in the wrong hands, but Brubaker and Philips are just solid gold.
Carver is also a post-human, which means he has powers, from experiments done on him by the 'good guys'. He's nearly indestructible, which makes it difficult to hurt him, as he cannot feel pain really. He's still trying to figure out how to take out the bad guys, but doesn't really know if the good guys would do anything different than the bad guys if they had the knowledge/control.

Throw in the usual Ed/Sean femme fatale, and you've got the makings of James Bond crossed with Electro. 
I'm looking forward to following this up with Vol. 2, where things should hopefully get even more jacked up and claustrophobic for our "hero".
