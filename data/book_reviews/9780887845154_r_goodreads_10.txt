Rating: 3/5

I read this book, went wtf, and made a friend read it because there is no way to convey just how...much...this book is. You have to read it for yourself to get the whole sick, offensive, darkly irreverent trip it'll take you on. Apparently this was supposed to be comic, but the rampant sexism, sexual violence and haha look at these uncivilized and rude French Canadians didn't really do it for me. To me, it is more satirical than anything. Or maybe I just hope it is next-level satire, a commentary on the ways wars abroad affect or don't affect an insular community until someone comes home in an English coffin. People have probably done studies on this, good for them. As for me, I am content recommending this book to people I know out of a slightly perverse desire to see their reactions. After all, sharing is caring.
