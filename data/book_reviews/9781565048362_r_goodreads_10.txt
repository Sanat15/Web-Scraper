Rating: 3/5

This was a fun anthology overall. My favorite stories were Frost Painting, Gary in the Shadows, Water Snakes, Cloudmaker, Magicked Tricks, Sound of Angels, The Home Town Boy There Are Things Which Are Hidden from the Eyes of the Everyday and the Young Lady Who Loved Catepillars. These short stories had elements that I really loved or connected with. This anthology didn't blow my mind. The short stories listed above were the ones that made an impact on me in some way. Some of the stories had a unique concept/plot or a really genuine protrayal of queer love, affection and life. And that's why I liked them so much. There were three stories I didn't read: The Fall of Kings, In Mysterious Ways and In the House of the Man in the Moon. The last two because I didn't get to in time and the first one because I lost interest in it. The rest of the stories either didn't feel fantasy or queer enough for me. And what is enough for me? Well I like my fantasy like I like my queerness: OBVIOUS! And for the big question...would I read this anthology again? Yes! I really liked the stories I liked, so I would want to read them again.
