Rating: 4/5

After winning a battle against Norway in his name, Macbeth, Thane of Glamis, was appointed Thane of Cawdor for his bravery, and he assassinated the righteous King Duncan. He thus succeeds in acceding to the throne and reigns with an iron fist to keep it, fulfilling the predictions signified to him by the three Fatales. But unable to endure the tyranny of the new sovereign, many plotted to restore the throne to Duncan's rightful heir, Malcolm.
Macbeth is my first Shakespearean tragedy, and it is what I expected: beautiful, lyrical language and a tragic tone throughout a play peppered with conspiracies and murder.
It is a superb work that the author delivers to us here, which urges me to explore it more in depth. Indeed, not my last Shakespeare; therefore, I am more than satisfied emerging from this beautiful reading.
