Rating: 5/5

Martha Hix is a unique writer. Her style is all her own. It took me a while to learn the characters due to the fact they had many nick names. Kind of forced me to pay attention. I too grew up in South Texas hearing the stories of Pancho Villa and his border raids. Ranchers in South Texas always had lookouts for Mexican Raiders, including Pancho Villa. The story line was unpredictable. The characters were different. Same as floating down the Comal or Frio rivers, you never knew where the story was going to take you. I'm a fan of happy endings so spoiler alert, I enjoyed the ending. The story takes place in 1898. A much simpler time. Hard to believe now how people lived without high-speed travel and social media. Now in 2026, with all the meanness in politics, government, and sense of entitlement it was nice to read about forgiveness and willingness to settle disputes without violence.
