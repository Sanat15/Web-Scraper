Rating: 3/5

Love the idea of a boat sailing over a frozen sea.
Love the title :).
Eric is very poetic in places, he often has a lovely turn of phrase.
As for the plot, it's ok. The characterisation is ok.
Otherwise, not bad, but drags on a bit in places.

These books remind me of the Jack Vance 'Dying Earth' series (which I go more like 5-stars for!).
Eric's Sunset Warrier books don't have the wit of Jack's though, or really any humour.
Also, they don't have the clever situations.

I read this book many years ago, and I came back to and read again. Not sure if I needed to really...
