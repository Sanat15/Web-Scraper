Rating: 4/5

I read this collection based on the recommendation from the introduction in Stephen King's "Everything's Eventual." I can see why he recommended it; these stories are filled with very real and relatable characters in some very real and maybe not so realistic situations. Obviously I enjoyed some of the stories more than others but all throughout, I found myself connecting to these characters. Read it and read them all! I'll definitely be checking out more of Carlson's work.
