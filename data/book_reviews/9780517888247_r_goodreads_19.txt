Rating: 5/5

Excellent, which I find true of all of the authors collections of essays. Like a cross country drive, always something new around the next corner. The author is a PhD whose thesis was on Caribbean land snails. A modern Gandalf who knows something about everything. Reminds me of the old British television show Connections, for drawing apparently disparate things into a unity like coal tar and the biro pen. Not his best in my view, but superb nevertheless. 

I loved the frequent references to evolution, and to how our ideas constrain our observations and understanding. Especially enjoyed punctuated equilibrium, snails, dinosaurs, museums, and eugenics.
