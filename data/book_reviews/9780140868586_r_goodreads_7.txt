Rating: 2/5

First the good news. With no Preface, Introduction, or Bibliography, White's biography of Proust makes no pretense to scholarship and it delivers none. Written in a gossipy style with an eye toward titillation, this thin little volume reduces Proust's life and work down to filler between salacious details of the various "handsome" young men with whom Proust had trysts or affairs.
Proust was a homosexual who wrote boldly about male and female homosexuality through much of "In Search of Lost Time." But to claim, as Mr White does, that "in the end most of the characters turn out to be gay" is flat wrong. There are hundreds of characters and most are not gay.
If you are keeping a list of who is gay and who is not throughout history, this cheap little volume serves nicely as your Proust source. If you are looking for well-researched background that adds to understanding Proust's life and work, go to the Tadie or Carter biographies.
I returned White's biography of Proust and got my money back.
