Rating: 5/5

Nice atmosphere (in the sense of a world-ending setting). There's this air of gloom and doom but done very elegantly. It was quite the reread.
There's a lot less Vanye and Morgaine together in this one than the previous, but some new characters were quite interesting.

Things are written quite subtly, and I was surprised by some references to the previous volume, specially regarding the civilizations. 

The setting is quite unique, as the world is slowly flooding and getting submerged and the Gate is the only way out. You do kinda feel bad for Jhirun who is pretty much treated horribly all throughout, and who knows how things will fare after the ending...

When I first read it, many years ago, I wasn't too much on board with the traveling sessions, but now they hit different.
