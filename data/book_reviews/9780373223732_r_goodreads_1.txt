Rating: 5/5

The reopening of Emily Townsend’s new Bed and Breakfast, where an unsolved murder took place 15 years ago, is bringing unwanted publicity to the small town of Paradise. This cozy little town isn’t all it appears to be since it seems many of the townsfolk are harboring some deep dark secrets. And then there’s the handsome new stranger who just rode into town on his motorcycle, who just happens to look a lot like the alleged murderer from all those years ago. Emily is trying to solve the long-ago murder before she gets herself killed, and she’s teaming up with an unlikely partner, the newly arrived handsome stranger in town, Matthew Steele.

This was a thoroughly entertaining romantic suspense story. It had such an interesting cast of characters who really brought the town to life with so many potential suspects among them. I enjoyed the small-town setting. The story took place in the season of autumn so there were wonderful descriptions of the beautifully colored leaves and the cool crisp air, and it made the story so much more atmospheric. A super-fun story and extremely enjoyable read.
