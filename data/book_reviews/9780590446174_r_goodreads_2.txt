Rating: 4/5

I believe I read this book many years ago, but when I brought it at an D.I, I thought to add it to my library for my children to read one day. It was kind of weird reading it and not remembering any of the story line from years ago. I think when I was teen I would have loved it, but as an adult, it was just okay. I loved to read anything that has to do with twins, it is a weakness of mine. I am not sorry that I read it again.
