Rating: 2/5

Honestly, this is a very long book in which absolutely NOTHING happens. And I feel the need to set out a warning: if you really liked Jai Raventhorne in the first book and his enigmatic character is one of the main driving forces that have led you to consider reading this book, don't waste your time. He makes only a cursory appearance in this book and it's not even real or very satisfying. Finding out what happens with his story was what kept me going to the end, but as I have already pointed out, NOTHING happens. It was with loyalty that I fought my way through the first part of the book, thinking my suspicions about Jai were correct, and as such, I suffered through the lives of his children whom I really cared not much about. Amos is the image of his father, but he ultimately has a kind personality. Maya is a spoiled and manipulative brat. Olivia is a whiner who lets almost every circumstance defeat her or send her "bursting in to tears". There is a sufficient amount of intrigue in the book, but some situations, like the sudden entrance of her second child, were wholly unnecessary to the plot and could have saved a few hundred pages of bother if they had been cut out. There is no romance in this book like there was in the last. The only character with any real depth is Kyle, but by the end, nothing happens with him, either. To sum it up, I would not recommend this book to anyone except a die hard fan of the first book, but even then, that may not save this for you.
