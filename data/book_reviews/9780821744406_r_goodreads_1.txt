Rating: 5/5

Sheriff Culpepper (accompanied, as ever, by his fearsome mastiff, Bear) brings in two outlaws. He speaks up for one of them, Wiley, who is sentenced to twenty-five years in the penitentiary. Then Wiley is rescued on the journey there. Or was an attempt made to abscond him? And by whom? Are the Anvil Mining Company (which Wiley had robbed) somehow behind it? And how is their head, Pennrose, involved? There then follows a train robbery, and we follow Culpepper as he ruthlessly and methodically tracks down the gang, and all those implicated, of whatever social position. Throughout the story, Culpepper's passionate relationship with his wife, Merry, is stressed. Does his present job bring her into great danger? A detailed, tremendous Western with a lot of background and character-driven plot. Great read!
