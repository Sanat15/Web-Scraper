Rating: 3/5

I liked the angsty part of the book. The H that wronged the heroine by getting emotionally involved with OW ( I can’t deny I like these stories). 
It bothered me that their relationship was portrayed as initially immature and shallow, based on physical attraction if mainly on his part. It did evolve into something more solid in the end but I don’t like the thought that if the OW didn’t die they probably never would end up together.
