Rating: 4/5

This book sort of gives you a mix of modern perspective on a deity. Though I do t think the author intended it to also possibly be a guide on how to go about writing and developing your own story for those who want to be writers one day the author does give tips throughout the book and what his struggles were in writing, I also found the author gave good advice on how to write and begin writing a character if you wanted to learn how to make your own novel and develop a character ironically this book also gives you that insight. This book pretty much had a little bit of everything with a modern stance on perspective and the world view now a days on religion and different aspects. It was quite a invigorating read.
