Rating: 4/5

A Sports Illustrated April Fool's spoof turned into a full length story. The title doesn't lie, as the book lays out the curious case of an English, Buddhist, french horn playing rookie pitcher who can throw a baseball at speeds of excess of 160 miles per hour. At 90 miles per hour, the ball is hard to see. At 100 miles per hour, the ball begins to "rise". At 160 miles per hour, good luck and prayer won't help you.
