Rating: 3/5

A jolly and readable romp through the busy life of Conan Doyle gleaned from published sources without original research although the author’s comments are generally pithy and apposite. A couple of sentences were absolutely strangled into meaninglessness but they stood out because the rest was so easy to digest. An irritation was the author’s commitment to recording speed and distances in kilometres throughout - as the book was first published in 1997 perhaps Martin Booth bet on metric taking over by now.
