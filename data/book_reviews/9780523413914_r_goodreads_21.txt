Rating: 4/5

8,868 read. Another great Hornblower book. As I am reading the entire series in chronological order it has been interesting to see the writing style of the author and the portrayal of the character evolve in stops and starts. This being the 7th of the series but originally the 2nd written the author was clearly setting the series up as a shorter trilogy with this as the low point for the protagonist with assumably a mighty return in the next book. Still really well written and the nautical wind based combat scenes are incredibly detailed for something the author could only have understood from books 100 years after they ceased with the invention of steam power. 

The whole series is a delightful guilty pleasure that is an escape from some of my other science fiction, non-fiction or crime/thriller fiction I’ve been reading.
