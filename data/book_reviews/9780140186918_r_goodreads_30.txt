Rating: 5/5

Can see why this volume of Green’s work is the only one that is circulation somewhat. All three by far stands heads and shoulders above Green’s other novels(though Living is a little rougher around the edges compared to Loving and Party Going). Unlike the other novels I read from him which felt like an extremely static experience here the more you read the more the novels start to morph into something strange, away from your traditional 1930-40’s novel and instead towards a somewhat fairy tale tone played out through caricatures of fiction of its time.
