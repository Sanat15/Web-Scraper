Rating: 4/5

Interesting take on time travel. Typical dystopian Earth. Abrupt ending- was really looking forward to seeing how all those fantastical concepts at the end would be brought together logically. I guess be author realized that was a problem and decided to just end the book while he was still ahead. All that said- I still really enjoyed this book! And some parts of it genuinely surprised me. So I'll still give it 4 stars, because I love all sci-fi &amp; space travel novels. If you don't have that bias, this book will probably disappoint.
