Rating: 2/5

Maaaaan, I used to love the Shivers books when I was a kid. And I picked this one up because I found an archived copy online and wanted to read a Horrorland-esque book. 

It was okay until the kidnapping (which wasn’t far into things) but honestly is this all it takes to publish a kid’s book? The most trite ending I’ve ever read, and stupid me kept thinking there might be a twist and their parents were robots lol. 

Very cheesy, good fun if you want to read a quick children’s book about a creepy theme park, which apparently I did.
