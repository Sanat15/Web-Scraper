Rating: 3/5

The titular essay was by far my favorite. I quite enjoyed the holistic view it offered, especially when considered with Sontag’s “Against Interpetation.” Somehow I believe both—I’m slowly learning to embrace contradictions. Honestly I wasn’t very rapt by the collection as a whole, though. I think my response was similar to that of Murakami’s writing memoir; my distance from the topic lessened its impact.
