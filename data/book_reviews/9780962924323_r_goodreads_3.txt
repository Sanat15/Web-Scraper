Rating: 4/5

The book definitely showed its age a bit- I had to look up names like Kathy Ormsby, whose "accident" was a few months before I was even born- but certain aspects of this such as the unique challenges of being a high performing distance athlete are timeless. Running culture has made great strides in the past year when it comes for female runners, there is more encouragement to train for strength and a lot less of the "try to be as skinny and weigh as little as possible to be fast" rhetoric, but there is still a ways to go.
