Rating: 3/5

I definitely skipped around with This Realm of England, 1399 to 1688 (A History of England #2) by Lacey Baldwin Smith to check out the sections of times and events I found myself more interested in, but overall this textbook is a slog. I probably would have given it 2 stars if it weren't for a previous owner's notes and commentary in the margins. Sometimes that actually held my attention more than the rest of the book.
