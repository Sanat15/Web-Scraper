Rating: 3/5

(Over the last few years I've read all 5 books in the anthology of the first Father Neil books, and this is the last one of the five-book set.) I enjoyed it on a par with the other volumes in the series. This has some less funny parts, including a holocaust story across a couple of chapters. There are also a couple of chapters about "mixed-up marriages". (Of course in this context that means across religious lines.) Anyway, I found it absorbing and worth reading. Someday perhaps I'll get to the other books that aren't contained in the big anthology.
