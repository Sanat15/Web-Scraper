Rating: 2/5

Not so much a plot as a meander punctuated by shocking events; this reads like a first draft. If you are looking for a love story, the love interest is present in about 5% of the book, if that. It’s not a totally unpleasant meander, but it really doesn’t make any sense. 

Characters and settings come and go, sometimes quite abruptly. I love Joan Aiken, but I can’t quite imagine how this was published. All sense of continuity or story is repeatedly shoved aside to make a plot point or transition fit in the most ham-handed way, usually by means of a surprisingly violent event. I wouldn’t recommend this book; it is deeply unsatisfying.
