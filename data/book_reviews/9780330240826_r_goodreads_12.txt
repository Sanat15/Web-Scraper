I randomly just remembered this strange book from my childhood, where a family of blob-like people build a house. They are able to shape-shift and so use this to expand themselves and create the shape of the house out of something - concrete? mud or clay? I don't know. As a child I remember being fascinated by this, although more so because of how each child got their own individual room. As an adult it's a bit disturbing.
