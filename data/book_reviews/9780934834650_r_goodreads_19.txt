Rating: 3/5

There is something unique in the way that East Asian artists perceive and connect with nature. They always find a clever way to express their mind and souls. It likely has to do with the Taoist beliefs that nature plays a crucial role in a person's life. 

I value Bashō’s sentimentality, you can feel how in touch with his emotions he is, e.g. when he cries in front of an old memorial because the passage of time has emotionally touched him. It reminds me of the Romantic sublime.

Pleasant, quick read.
