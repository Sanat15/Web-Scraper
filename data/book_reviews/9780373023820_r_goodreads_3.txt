Rating: 3/5

Sara is too naive for words.

Her stepfather appears out of nowhere, offers her jet set lifestyle and she doesn't suspect any fowl play until the moment he demands she marries his disgusting crony.
Sara accepts her young friend’s marriage proposal to escape. She doesn’t pretend to love Tim but promises to make him happy. Unfortunately Tim gets killed that night in train crash that leaves Sara to the mercy of his brutal older brother.
