Rating: 4/5

4 Stars! ~ When Sarah abandoned her fashion model life, she was desperate to begin her life anew. Only she didn't know who she was, all her life she'd lived her mother's dream, portraying herself as the glamorous, Sahara. It was a letter she'd received years before from her estranged father, inviting her to his world, that brought her to British Columbia. And on wickedly dark and stormy night, Sarah lost control of her car and crashed in the middle of nowhere. That's where she found Jesse. A man hiding himself away from the world, and holding as many secrets as Sarah. Accidents seem to multiply as in a fall, Jesse twists his ankle and breaks his arm, and Sarah drains the battery on his pickup. Stranded together these two have to find a truce, until help arrives, which could be weeks. Neither are eager to share their identities, but slowly they each come to see what the other hides in their souls. When the world comes to find them, all is revealed and their newly formed trust is shattered. For Jesse is really Harrison Bond the famous author, and Sarah is really Sahara the world famous model and photographer, and Harrison thinks she purposely sought him out to sell his story to a magazine.

From the first pages, we see a very troubled and scared Sarah, struggle from the wreckage of her car and having to enter the world of a complete and mysterious stranger. Both have been painfully used by the people they've trusted, so being able to see the true person before them is nearly impossible. Sarah's like a child exploring her world for the first time and this enchants Jesse. Both learn to open their hearts and allow the other to see what's truly inside. This is a lovely read.
