Rating: 1/5

I think that people have generally misunderstood Freud; he is not anything remotely resembling a scientist, but rather a deeply untalented moral philosopher and cultural critic who inexplicably tried to use anecdotes from psychotherapy sessions as a quasi-‘scientific’ basis for his terrible ideas. 

It’s sort of like Paul Krugman trying to dress up his half-assed political commentary under the mantle of “prize-winning economist”; one of these things has nothing to do with the other. 

To be sure, Freud’s case studies have some limited value (therapist ‘transference’ is a real thing, for example) but Discontents is just a mess. If I wanted a third-rate Nietzsche, I'd just read Foucault.
