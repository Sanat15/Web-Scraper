Rating: 3/5

I waited with rating until I finished the four books, which I am reading in order of the Mabinogi(on)'s branches. This one was a good intro to the writing style, which is nice, but plotwise kind of boring(?) Walton has a very concise writing style that made me go back a few times whenever I caught myself having skimmed over the page just a tad too fast. Maybe that's also indicative of how our current novels are made for the short-form-content-minded. So in any case, this kind of writing is good -- a sort of antidote to that.
