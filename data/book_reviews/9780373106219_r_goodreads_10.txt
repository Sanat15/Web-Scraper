Rating: 2/5

I liked the interaction of Mercy and Croft, and I liked that Croft respected Mercy's boundaries. I wasn't surprised that Croft didn't actually do anything the night of the wedding. This felt too much like Intimate Enemies, which had a similar storyline. It was a cute story until the last chapter, which threw me for a loop. Really? Totally unnecessary and blew the story for me. She ended up a chaste bride, but this is a story I can't recommend.
