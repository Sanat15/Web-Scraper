Rating: 3/5

This is written in the tradition of a sketchbook, or notes from a foreign country, interspersed with fragments written about life in Italy by other writers.

The result is pleasant indeed, and gave me some ideas for things to research (esp. the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, the building of which most likely did not involve, as Nellie Melba reported, transporting an entire Venetian palazzo to Boston), but I was a bit disappointed; the vignettes written by Leavitt and Mitchell are rarely as penetrating as the fragments they quote, and somehow do not carry the book as well as I would wish. Still, the form of the book is something to remember and perhaps copy one day.
