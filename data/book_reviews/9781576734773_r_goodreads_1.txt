Rating: 4/5

I found this book at my local library while searching for information about Mary Magdalene and I'm very glad I did. Although it is a work of fiction, it is based on fact of the New Testament. Since we don't know everything that happened back then, we can only glean information from the Bible. This book gave me a lot to think about and I hope it does the same for you. I think I may look for more of Thom Lemmons' books.
