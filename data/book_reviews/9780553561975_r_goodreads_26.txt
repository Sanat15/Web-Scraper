Rating: 4/5

You know when your TBR list is really long, and you find a book that jumps the line anyway? That’s what happened with this book.

It’s a solid novel with an Indiana Jones-like ending. It sparked my fandom into the novels, so it deserves a lot of credit for that. It’s probably the best of all the books. Rob MacGregor’s are great also, but up and down. Max McCoy's are a little more consistent (Rob’s books might have a higher ceiling overall). In the end, ancient Egypt is the perfect, quintessential setting for an Indiana Jones novel.

I read a review where someone said if you read just one Indiana Jones novel, go with this one—I would probably agree.
