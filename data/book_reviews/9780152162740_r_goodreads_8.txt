Rating: 4/5

This is a cute story but absolutely could NOT happen now, because "kids left to fend for themselves a la Lord of the Flies but in a 1950s/60s small town" wouldn't have the "locksmith who just so HAPPENS to have duplicate keys to EVERY STORE in his house" or easy access to a farm to raid for fresh produce enough to feed 300-500 kids ages 6 to 14ish or even kids 14ish who would know how to self-govern such a group in the event of a mass parental walk-out.

I mean, it happens over the course of two days (or three? I read kind of fast), so it's not like any of the kids would've starved in the meantime, but definitely it wouldn't be as heartwarming(ish) in a modern setting, nevermind that NO parents now would agree to such a walk-out (especially when it was a few kids ruining the reputation of ALL kids older than toddler).
