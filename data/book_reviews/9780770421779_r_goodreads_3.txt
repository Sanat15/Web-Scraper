Rating: 4/5

This is the second novel I've read by Margaret Laurence. While it wasn't quite in the same league as 'The Diviners', it's another fantastic story, which I enjoyed almost as much as its book-twin, 'Olive Kitteredge'.

I can't believe I only heard of Laurence a few months back. Thank goodness, as always, for my Goodreads friends, or else I might never have encountered her at all.
