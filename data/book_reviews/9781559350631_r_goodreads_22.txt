Rating: 3/5

3.5* ... I purposefully saved this one for the dead of winter, and I'm glad I did. The atmosphere is easily the book's strongest suit; the remote snowbound village and a spooky seance creates the perfect "chilly night" reading experience. While the wintry setting was cozy, the mystery itself feels almost secondary to the weather. The characters spend a ridiculous amount of time discussing the difficulty of travel due to the weather instead of focusing on the murder itself!

So yeah, I just found this book weirdly "meh" for reasons I can't quite put my finger on. For some reason, when the killer was revealed, I was just sort of "oh, okay." ... Don't get me wrong- it did all come together and made sense, but I didn't think it was an impactful reveal. The book is not bad, it just didn't hit hard for me. Still recommend ... just be prepared to hear A LOT about how impassable the roads are! ;-)
