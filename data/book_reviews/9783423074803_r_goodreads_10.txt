I was delighted entirely from the get go! Other than Alfred J. Hitchcock not really writing the forewords in books claiming his name; even these introductions open the story with hilarity. This first 'The Three Investigators' novel is captivating in all ways: likeability, humour, adventure, and ample moments of fear. Although I was too little to remember, I inhaled many of these as a child, presumably from the school library because I didn't own any until now. They are superior to ‘Nancy Drew’ by a landslide!
