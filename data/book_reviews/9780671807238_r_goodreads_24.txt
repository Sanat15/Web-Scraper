Rating: 5/5

Just a wonderful journey through the world of Napoleon’s Grande Armee with various characters weaving in and out to show their lives and adventures.
Every one of the seven could in reality, be a soldier of Napoleon’s mighty army.
From the intriguing prologue to the haunting ending, a vivid tale unfolds within.
He (the author) was fascinated with the Napoleonic world, and was such a gifted storyteller bringing us back effortlessly to those stirring times…..
Of all my military books, this, by a mile is my favourite one……
