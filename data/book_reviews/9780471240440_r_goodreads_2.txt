Rating: 4/5

Reviewed by: Erik F

Life by the Numbers is an exciting book about how math plays a massive role in our lives. I was very interested by the way the author, Keith Devlin, describes mathematical concepts and how they work. I was particularly impressed about the research that went into writing the book and his incorporation of so many mathematicians’ ideas.
Keith Devlin brought math alive as he wrote about many ways of using mathematics to improve everything in your life. For example he explained simple things such as ways to improve your serve in tennis, to more complex things such as ways to animate movies and how to understand viruses that cause many of the terrible diseases we face today.
As the author takes you through this amazing mathematical journey, the reader is enlightened about the role math plays in our lives and one realizes that mathematical principles play themselves out everywhere in our lives. This book has truly educated me about the power of math; now, when I watch an animated film I no longer view it as a simple movie, instead I think about how much work has been put into it to make the animations come to life.
Life by the Numbers was written in 1998; since then we have to advance in the world of mathematics. Many of the things in the book have been around for a while but the way Keith Devlin describes them with such detail is fascinating to me.
Without a doubt, if you are interested in learning more about math and its role in everyday life than you will enjoy the book Life by the Numbers. It is a pleasurable read for the young and old if you are interested in mathematics. The book convinces the reader that math is one of the most important tools in our lives.
