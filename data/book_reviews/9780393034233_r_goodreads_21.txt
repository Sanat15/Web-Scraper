Rating: 4/5

I was thinking for a couple of times to buy or not to buy this book. Reading the title, my first impression was “not another love book” :) but after seeing Helen performing on the stage, she convinced me. 
Then I knew it was all about science, evolution and anthropology. It is a perfect combination between real facts, how other species react in various situations. We humans, are not that special :) it is clear that we evolved together with all other species and we still have a lot of things to learn from them. 
Of course there are some theories I don’t agree with, but this book should be read by open minded and not religious people :) 
I definitely recommend this book.
