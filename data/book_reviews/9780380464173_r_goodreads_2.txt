Rating: 5/5

January 2025 Review:

A remarkably succinct but vital piece of Wimsey scholarship built from correspondence with Dorothy Sayers herself and complete with illustrations and genealogies. I had forgotten all the legends allegedly connecting to the family’s heraldry, which were hilariously connected with the Alice books and supplemented by “lost” fragments by Froissart. 
Available on Internet Archive.
