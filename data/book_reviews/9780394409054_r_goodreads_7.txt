Rating: 4/5

A Magical and Decadent Detective Tale Set in Paris in 1861
This a tasty and sensuous noir tale of police detective Picard on the trail of a suave and clever magician and conman through the decadent soirees of 19th century Paris, filled with sybaritic wealthy elites, shady charlatans, Tarot readers, magicians, performers, thieves, prostitutes, impossibly crafted toy dolls, and *possibly* some real and sinister enchantment. Its got many an exotic set piece and roams throughout central Europe on the trail of Ric Lazare and his ravishingly beautiful temptress wife Renee. There is much narrative sleight-of-hand, many passages of dazzling beauty, and many erotic digressions, but you will not be bored. A very delectable and atmospheric novel, even if it stays always out of range of being pinned down.
