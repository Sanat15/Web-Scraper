Rating: 4/5

Such a nice compilation of different stories of how God works through different people and circumstances to spread the gospel and change lives. I loved how he writes that the first duty of the Christian to make the invisible Kingdom visible. And when he says, “it is not what we do that matters, but what a sovereign God chooses to do through us”. Lots of encouragement and examples of how to love God and live out our theology amidst the “mess” of life.
