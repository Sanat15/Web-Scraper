Rating: 5/5

One of the best HRs set during the Revolutionary War that I’ve read in years … it takes place mostly in Georgia during the last few years of the war. Bonus: romance for two very interesting couples!! 

The main couple: Quintin Blackthorne, heir to his father’s estate and riches, reluctantly agrees to marry Madelyne Deveraux … it was arranged by their fathers long ago. Quintin mistrusts all women so he wants a plain, mild mannered wife. And then he discovers that Madelyne isn’t. He secretly supports the patriots cause for independence.

His cousin Devon Blackthorne who’s of mixed white and Native American heritage, is fiercely loyal to the British cause. He unexpectedly meets Lady Barbara who has been banished by her mother from London … she has gambling debts, and is sent to Savannah to live with her brother who’s in the British army.

Henke has woven a totally engaging story full of “hold your breath” moments…. family secrets, dastardly evil characters…, swoon worthy heroes and gorgeous strong heroines. There’s oodles of sizzling passionate scenes scattered throughout the story … especially nice because there’s two couples. 

Finally… it’s great to read a HR where the author has done meticulous research of the era..,, there’s a detailed acknowledgments page, and an author’s note at the end. 

The cover and stepback art for the 1994 paperback edition are by the artist Max Ginsburg.
