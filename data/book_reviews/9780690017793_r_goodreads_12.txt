Rating: 5/5

Despite those chapters being short, I was amazed at the author’s ability to put so many powerful emotions. I’ve enjoyed the book because this story will hook you and keep you turning the pages as there seems to be never a dull moment. The book is called “Fragments of Isabella”. Each chapter is a short fragment of a certain pivotal point in Isabella’s life: the ghetto, the deportation, the train, the camp itself. She had 4 sisters and a brother. Isabella Leithner was born in 1921, Hungary. Isabella‘s father had gone to the United States attempting to get his family there. It got approved after the war started and they were unable to go then. She was deported to Auschwitz on her 23rd birthday. Only 9 months did she spend in Auschwitz. Their mother told them they must live, so they struggled to do that. They were separated from their brother. As a result did her family survive? Did they go to America after all? You can decide.
