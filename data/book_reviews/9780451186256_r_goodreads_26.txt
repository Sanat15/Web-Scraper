Rating: 4/5

well written emotional story.

Jack. a rake drinking and whoring his way through London inherits his father's debts and needs to marry quickly for money to support his family. He meets Mary a spinster who makes him laugh, becomes his friend, and decides to help him find a wife.

There are a few twists to the story, and strong emotional scenes

Closed door romance, spice level low, emotion level high

Trigger Warning
 There are accounts of a fathers physical and emotional abuse. MMC sleeps with other women when they are broken up
