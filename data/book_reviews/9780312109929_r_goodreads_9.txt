Rating: 4/5

This book is a truthful and touching depiction of how cruel old age can be. Amelia is in despair as to how to care for Selma, her widowed grandmother with quickly developing dementia. Selma's son, Robert, has tricked her into moving into an "Elderly Care Facility", sold her house and buggered off to South America. Amelia is thrown over by her man and to top it all off her mother has mental health issues.
 Marika Cobbold describes how looking after an infirm elderly relative requires the same care as for an infant...without the cuteness. 
 An amazing first novel. I will look for more.
