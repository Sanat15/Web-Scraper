Rating: 2/5

For anyone curious, here are some common tropes of Allison Lane novels, based on the half dozen or so I've read:

1. Gossip of the most scurrilous and reputation-ruining kind. Usually accompanied by a mention of (or in one case, an appearance by) Lady Beatrice, London's most (in)famous gossipmonger in Lane's Regency London.
2. The hero and/or the heroine has a secret that they don't want anyone to know! They're either artistically-inclined or just really nice.
3. Mistaken identity, caused by one character impersonating another with malicious intent.
4. One hugely problematic family member (usually a wastrel, but sometimes either crazy or an asshole) (this person sometimes also plays into #1 or #3).

I don't necessarily mean it in a negative way, just that when you read several of her books in close proximity, they get a bit same-y unless you count the above among your favorite tropes (especially the first one).
