Rating: 4/5

This was a very dark, very deranged read about serial killers chasing serial killers and it was amazing. I really did like it. I did have to skip past some of the gruesome torture scenes. They were very graphic and disturbing. I am not going to lie, that was the hardest part, all that torture and killing and I still read on. It is like the scene of an accident, you don't want to look but you still do and want all of the gory details so you can tell your friends what happened. This book is like that. I had to know how it would turn out. I couldn't look away.

The story is about a man whose family was killed by a serial killer so he becomes one to catch them and make them confess. He has an assistant, a prostitute whose friends were killed by serial killers too so they want to bring justice to these men. It is a fascinating story and I was drawn into their lives and I had to know what happened to them. Was he becoming a monster like the ones he was chasing? Part of me thinks yes. The ending just leaves you stunned. 

There is a scene where one of the killers kills his own dog so he can eat him. He is a cannibal. That is a big turn off for me and I almost put down the book. He abused his animals and kept them locked up to use as weapons. I just want to warn everyone. Funny thing, I didn't get as upset at the murders of the people because they were all evil but kill a dog and I go crazy. LOL I hated that part.
