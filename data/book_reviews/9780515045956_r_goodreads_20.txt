Rating: 1/5

Coming up to another family's summer home with only one book, I relied, once it was finished, on what was around the house, knowing that one of the owners was a science fiction fan. Being familiar with Farmer and impressed by some of his work, particularly the first Riverworld volume, I thought this title would be a safe choice. 
 It wasn't. Indeed, this is one of the worst space opera pieces I've ever forced myself to finish. More a comic book than a novel, the plotting involves little more than action sequence followed by action sequence and the characterizations are two dimensional
