Rating: 1/5

Really quite disappointing. Sure, these are children, but their antics were really quite horrible at times. Physically attacking one another, cutting off hair, note passing, holding threats over one another's heads - sure it was juvenile and they are children, but quite frankly, a lot of it was just mean. It's not something I would want children to consider doing, so it's not something I want them reading about. Therefore as a teacher, it's not a book I would recommend students read nor one I would read to a class. Considering I've read other Sachar novels that I thought were quite brilliant and have read to my class I was really quite disappointed with this offering.
