Rating: 4/5

A darker Estleman book than his usual. The background is very reminiscent of Bill Cody's life, even down to the native American performing in a traveling wild west show with a guy who calls himself "Colonel" and a cute little shooter girl.

It is the last adventure of a grizzled group of aging westerners to track down a particularly awful group of criminals. These are hard men who have lived a hard life but are the best for the job. Estlemen handles it very well, especially with characterization and dialog, and the story does not go the way you'd expect a western.
