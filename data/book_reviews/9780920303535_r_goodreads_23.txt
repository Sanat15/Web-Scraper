Rating: 4/5

A cute body book for toddlers and preschoolers, with bright, cheerful illustrations that show many different bodies—different skin tones, hair styles, dis/abilities, body sizes, gender presentations, etc., and which invites direct engagement by asking kids to point to their own body parts. Better for reading at home than in a group/school setting.

Themes: Bodies, We're all the same/we're all different
Age range: Toddler and Preschool
