Rating: 3/5

The Rescuer brings the O’Malley series to a satisfying conclusion, blending emergency response, suspense, and faith into a story that has plenty of heart. Dee Henderson clearly understands how to create believable high-stakes situations, and the rescue scenes were among the strongest parts of the book.

That said, this installment didn’t captivate me quite as much as some of the earlier entries in the series. The pacing felt uneven at times, with portions of the story moving more slowly than I expected. While the character development remained solid, I found myself wanting a little more tension and a few more surprises along the way.

Even so, longtime fans of the O’Malley family will appreciate seeing lingering storylines resolved and spending one last adventure with these familiar characters. The themes of courage, loyalty, and hope continue to shine throughout.

Overall, this was a worthwhile conclusion to a memorable series. While it wasn’t my favorite of the six books, I’m glad I completed the journey and would still recommend the O’Malley series to readers who enjoy inspirational romantic suspense. 
3.5 Stars.
