Rating: 5/5

Interesting addition to the Celestine Prophecy. Extremely different, so if you enjoyed the Celestine Prophecy, be prepared to dive into a totally different style of book on this read. 

This book give some background info on the Celestine Prophecy and is not a story, but an explanation. It goes into real world topics and issues. I found it interesting but also wanting more.

This came out in 1997 so it is interesting to see how our current world looks compared to when this was written. The principles and ideas will remain the same no matter when you read this book, just makes you step back and see how much/how little work we have done as humanity in the past 2 decades. 

This book is a good guide for life. Nothing groundbreaking. But a general philosophy and viewpoint that will better our world.
