Rating: 5/5

Marine Biology coloring book? Intrigued. So I picked it up, opened the pages and discovered that THIS is the book my high school Marine Bio teacher got the copies of our coloring homework. (I didn't go a gifted school alright?) I loved that homework, I love coloring, I love the ocean. All of those things are bound into a lovely little package for me, so I can read about various marine creatures while coloring them in.

Certain parts of my dreams have come true.

With all that being said, I can't believe this even exists.
