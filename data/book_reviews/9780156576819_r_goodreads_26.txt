Funny Story about How I Came to Read The Massacre at Fall Creek and Why I Awarded it 4 Stars
*I am a Hoosier. I read Indiana about history whenever I can.
**The hardback book fell into my wagon at an annual community Book Sale. (Shout-out for the wonder of USED BOOKS and the spectacular random reading experiences that follow a good Book Sale.)
***After years of passing an interstate sign that says "PENDLETON---FALL CREEK HISTORIC SITE I stopped! (Fun Fact: The falls of Fall Creek are about 2 1/2 feet high.)
****I read West's 1975 historical fiction in 2026. It's a poignant yet quirky telling of the first conviction of trial and white guys for killing a family of Indians in a sugar camp near Pendleton. Thought-provoking! As West writes in her Valedictory, "My intention was not to reveal historic fact in a fictional setting, but to open question of more abiding truths." 
*****In 2026, the current massacres in Palestine jump to mind. At what point do colonizers recognize their subjects as human? Read it and think.
