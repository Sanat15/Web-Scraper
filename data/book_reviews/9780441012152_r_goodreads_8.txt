Rating: 3/5

This is an above average paranormal thriller with the Menagerie fighting the forces of evil who are trying to resurrect the most evil of the fallen angels, The Nimble Man, who was hidden in a limbo dimension when Lucifer fell. If they fail, The Nimble Man will destroy mankind. 
 The book was a little loosely woven and convoluted but it still left you wanting to read more on the characters.
