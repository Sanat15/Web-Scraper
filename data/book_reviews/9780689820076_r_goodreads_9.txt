Rating: 1/5

The story was very confusing overall. We don't get much information on what's keeping him in Canara other than his theories. Same at the end, there isn't much on how he can leave other than an "I can/must blah blah blah."(not actually a quote from the book). In my opinion, trying to plot the events is very hard, like a roller coaster that gets stuck going up to the top. Most of the book is uneventful, and there doesn't seem to be a true falling action where the conflict is resolved.

Also, the sudden "tradition", which is skinny dipping in a river with the single people in the village, is misplaced in my opinion. I know it's a fantasy novel, but it's a little far-fetched with the disappearing buildings on the solstices with no form of explanation whatsoever on how this happens.

The magical plant, Sang, also doesn't have much description other than how it looks and that it can be brewed into tea. There is no description of why the Chinese come to Canara to trade for Sang, which also indicates the lack of description in the use of Sang.
