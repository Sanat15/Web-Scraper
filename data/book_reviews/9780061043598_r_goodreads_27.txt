Rating: 3/5

A bit of a mixed bag, but none of the stories were bad. Some were just kinda ok: fun at the moment, but not that memorable. Some were really good, though: the ones that venture just the tiniest bit into horror territory really left an impression and Montague Egg is a really fun protagonist, though not nearly as well drawn as Lord Peter Wimsey, who had some good but not great appearances in here. Some funny moments, some clever twists - I think this was a time well spent but it´s not a must read as a Lord Peter Wimsey fan, since he´s more of an add-on than a main character.
