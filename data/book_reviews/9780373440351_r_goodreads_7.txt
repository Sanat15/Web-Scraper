Rating: 3/5

Dated Harlequin and while it’s not amazing, it’s better than most of the era. It was quite refreshing to have likable characters...no one in the book was mean and that I liked. The first part of the book is highly improbable. It seems the kids are almost too unruly and then take a very fast metamorphosis. Their mother passed 2 years before and had the stability of relatives watching them so I don’t think there’s much reason the kids would be such a handful. I think it was for comedic effect but it just seemed odd. If the heroine was truly uncomfortable with kids she never shows it in this book. Actually she more natural than the nanny or day care workers...which didn’t ring true. I just didn’t feel the romance between the main couple...more of a bonding with Jo and the kids.
