Rating: 4/5

So if you're looking for a book about Heifer International, this is a great title. The artwork is lovely, the text is simple to follow, and readers can see how children in developing countries live. Hillary Rodham Clinton wrote the afterward and explains Heifer International's long role in aiding developing nations.

That being said, the story is really simple. Solving problems in developing nations is not as simple as sending a pregnant goat to a family. If you are doing a Heifer project at your school, this is a good resource for kindergarten - second grade. Older students will need a story that is more advanced and nuanced.
