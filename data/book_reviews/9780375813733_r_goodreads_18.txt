Rating: 4/5

Man, when I tell you this book had me enchanted as a kid! I grew up with Magic Tree House, devouring as many of them as I could get from the library. I read SO many of these books!! And when they came out with Christmas (my favorite holiday) in Camelot, I died and went to heaven.

Quest for Camelot is 1000% responsible for my lifelong obsession with Arthur, Camelot, and all Arthurian legend. But Magic Tree House absolutely build the fan I am today. The glimpses and allusions we got with Morgan fanned to flame the seed that had already been planted. So when this book came out, I needed it. 

Ever since, I've remembered this book fondly, but totally forgot what actually happened in it. XD This year, I decided to listen to the audiobook for a little Christmas magic while I was decorating my home for the season. And man, what a flashback. I love these siblings so much. Jack and Annie are my childhood and that they get to go to Camelot to save Arthur and all of Camelot is just such a delight.

I thoroughly enjoyed rereading this one and think it may need to become an annual Christmas tradition just because it's so much fun. And I definitely need to reread all of the MTH books as an adult! They're such a fun mix of adventure, education, courage, and family love.

Highly recommend this one!!
