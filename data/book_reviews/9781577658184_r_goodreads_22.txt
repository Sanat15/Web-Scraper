VERY FEW books have I ever actually quit because I could no longer endure something I found so agonizing it overrode any enjoyment the book had to offer. This was disheartening to me since I really enjoyed the book when I was younger. I don't know if it was the way it was read to me, my imagination, or a different version perhaps. This time each time all I kept thinking was, "Did that really add to the book in any way?" or "Man does this sound like Charlie Brown's teacher!" I have no interest in finishing the last 20% of the book nor do I recommend at least this version.
