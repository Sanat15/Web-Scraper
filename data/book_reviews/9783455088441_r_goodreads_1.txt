Rating: 4/5

As a Hutterite reading this book, I couldn’t help but laugh out loud, it hit so close to home. Typical Hutterites, we really are all the same in some ways. The part about the men cutting the Sunday duck “with lots of determination but little eloquence” had me chuckling. And when the guy attended a funeral, he was appauled that they were eating lunch right next to the deceased, casually setting down a coffee cup or jam jar beside the deceased persons leg. Talk about accuracy. This book had such honesty about the good and bad. Loved it.
