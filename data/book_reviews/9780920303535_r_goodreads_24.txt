Rating: 4/5

Great reference book for showcasing all kinds of bodies in a celebratory, non judgemental way. Probably a great one to have on a bookshelf for a kid to look through at their leisure as they feel curious about bodies, as I remember being a kid, you'd never look as closely or ask all the questions you had in the moment of reading something like this. My 4.5 year old enjoyed how many bums you could see in the book!
