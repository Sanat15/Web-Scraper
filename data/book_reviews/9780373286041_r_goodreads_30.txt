Rating: 4/5

Rebellion is set mainly in Scotland and begins in 1745. The story revolves around the Jacobite uprising and the hopes of restoring Prince Charles Stuart’s family to the British throne.

Earl Brigham Langston is in a good position to help the Jacobite cause. He has his ear to the ground in London and he has recently been on the continent in the French court. His best friend Coll MacGregor is also passionate about the cause, and together they travel to Coll’s home in Scotland to help gather further support for Charles.

Here Brigham meets Coll’s family and, most notably, his headstrong sister Serena. She has no time for an Englishman who she sees as representing everything that is bad about their enemy. However, Brigham is welcomed by her father, and Serena must show some respect for their guest.

I had not realised that this is book six of the MacGregors series, although they don’t appear to be written in chronological date order. I do remember reading a few of the others in this series which take place in America; this makes sense when the epilogue of this tale is read. A satisfactory historical romance and another solid book by this author.
