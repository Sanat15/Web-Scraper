Rating: 5/5

I am a reader, not a fiction writer, but I could not get enough of this book! I began reading (adult) Christian fiction around 2002–2003, and am familiar with most all of the authors in this book (though I certainly haven’t read books from all of them). I really enjoyed the details of how God orchestrated both personal and professional lives to bring about some of my favorite reads. It also made me really appreciate how much quality Christian fiction has been available from my earliest reading – so many of these authors wrote about being turned down by publishers because there was “just no market for Christian fiction.” 

I enjoyed the short length of the chapters, making this easy to dip in and out of, and that these stories were obviously taken from personal interviews and came across as if told “in their own words.” The only downside to this quick read was the two pages of titles I copied down for my library list!
