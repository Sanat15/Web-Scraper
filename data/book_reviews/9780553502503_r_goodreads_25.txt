Rating: 2/5

So what is the obsession with Danielle Steel?? This is my 2nd book by her recently and it was just not good. The plot was very close to the story of my life right now, so I thought I would check it out. That was a terrible choice. The book was very unrealistic. I was going to give it one star, but then all of a sudden the last 5 pages were really good, so I gave it two. The rest of the book was repetitive and just plain bad. 
Maybe in 15-20 years I’ll try another one.
