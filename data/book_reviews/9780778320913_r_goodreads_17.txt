Rating: 2/5

Sweet Dreams at the Goodnight Motel is a contemporary romance novel by author Curtiss Ann Matlock. This is a time-slip novel: 1963-Modern Day.

Claire Wilder quit her job so she could go back to where she was born and perhaps find her dad who she hasn’t seen since childhood. Andrew her ex is a work-a-holic. Sherrilyn is a young girl that is pregnant with a criminal’s baby.

Did this book meet my expectations? I don’t usually read contemporary fiction, preferring historical fiction. I felt let down by this book because the language was sometimes too rough for my liking and it was much longer than it needed to be. However, the ending brought all the details together and I felt the author did a great job of giving the story a small town feel. I would not recommend this book to readers under 18. I am giving this book 2* because, even though there were some interesting parts, I came away from it feeling disappointed and depressed.
