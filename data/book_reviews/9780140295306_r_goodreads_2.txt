Rating: 4/5

A fascinating look into the lives of a family who, after the mother's/wife's death 'go bush'. Into the outback, learning from their very capable father, the four children learn all there is to know about droving , sheep, cattle &amp; horses &amp; everything else that their lives depend on. What a wonderful &amp; hard way of life &amp; also extremely interesting.
I thoroughly enjoyed the Pieces of Blue. Thankyou for sharing such a big part of your life Kerry McGinnis. What an amazing man your father was.
