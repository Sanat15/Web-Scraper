Rating: 3/5

I visited Dubrovnik and found the place intriguing and was keen to learn more of the history. However this book is long at nearly 500 large pages and more suitable for the serious student rather than the casual reader, I read A History of Venice by John Julius Norwich for the same reason and with the same result. However the author clearly knows his topic and the book gives an interesting insight into how world events especially the Napoleonic Wars drastically effects small Republics.
