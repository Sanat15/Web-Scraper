Rating: 5/5

This is not a hard to read book. But for someone who grew up Christian like me, it is a refresher and a reminder of faith not as what I do, but why I do it. Somebody gave it to me as a gift when I was a freshman in college; I ignored it then and that's probably when I really needed it!

Chuck Colson's style of writing is easy and inviting. It's not overly "dense" but it's very clear and concise. Many of the stories brought me to tears, and the examples that he uses of people who have taken the steps to come closer to loving God brought me to tears, because they're ordinary examples taken out of his life and his experiences.
