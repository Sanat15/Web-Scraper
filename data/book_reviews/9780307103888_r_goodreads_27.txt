Rating: 5/5

This is the greatest emergent reader book. It has small sentences and repeating words. I purchased this book because it looked so cute and to donate to an Elementary Library, after I review it of course. The illustrations remind me of Spring. You can almost smell the flowers.
 Little bunny comes hopping down the road one Spring day. He was looking for a home. He asks a Robin where his home is. Does bunny build him a home there? He asks other animals he meets on the road about their homes. Where does he find a home? You get the fun of read/listening to find out. You don't have to wait till Spring to read this book. It's good for every season.
