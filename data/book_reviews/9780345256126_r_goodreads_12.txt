Rating: 4/5

Diana Chalfont lives at Chesdene Manor with her mother. They live in the newest wing of the manor in genteel poverty. Diana's latest scheme to get some money is to rent out the older part of the manor. 
In walks Christopher Richmond, a Nabob from India. The part of this book where Diana shows off the old &amp; badly maintained part of the house to the potential tenant is a hoot. 
She bravely tries to present even broken chairs in a positive light. For reasons of his own, Christopher decides to rent the manor (after undertaking some repairs to make the house habitable).

Diana's friend Irene meanwhile decides to go to London to visit her aunt and experience something of city life. She wishes for Diana's company.
So Diana, Irene and Irene's brother Mark leave for London. Christopher too goes there to join his friend from India - Jack Chertsey. 
In London, new characters like a new friend Letty, and a cousin Sidney Chalfont join the group as they go for rides in Hyde Park, and take in the London ballrooms. 

The different characters get to know each other, and pairs begin to form.

There is a mystery about Christopher's background, and also about Diana. They add to the story overall.
What was good - The characters are well-drawn and they change slowly as the story progresses. Historical details are spot on with no jarring anachronisms. A clean historical romance.

What could have been better- the romance between the main characters was too abrupt. It could have been better written and expanded on a lot more. 
 
I downloaded this book via Kindle Unlimited. I would rate this around 3.5 stars overall.
