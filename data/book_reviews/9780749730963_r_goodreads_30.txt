Rating: 4/5

A short and sweet story, typical of Morpurgo. I remembered the title from the school library as a young kid, but never got into it to read. Having now given it a go, the book is entirely different to what I expected and I totally see why I didn't fancy it back then. Sometimes I don't have the energy for something too heavy and Morpurgo always delivers something easy to read but sticks with you after.
