Rating: 3/5

This book, translated from the original Swedish, tells the story of an expedition in 1897 that attempted to fly over the North Pole in a gas (hydrogen) balloon. The adventurers were given little chance of succeeding, and in fact they disappeared, only to have their bodies found 30 years later. Much mystery surrounded the events which lead to their deaths. The author has chosen to write this book in the first person, as if he were one of the men on the flight. Of course the actions are approximated, but it appears that detailed log books and personal diaries were found at the site of their final camp. This allows for a timeline to be established. The dialog is mostly fictionalized, but the writer makes it seem realistic. The expedition was basically doomed from the start, but had they not been afflicted by tricanosis from eating polar bear meat they probably would have survivied being stranded on the pack ice. Fascinating story...
