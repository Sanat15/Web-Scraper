Rating: 4/5

This story is where This Rebel Heart left off but it can be a standalone as well. I do suggest reading the first one because it is good. I read this story and the first one back to back because I wanted to know what happened. This book is filled with all kinds of adventures. There are several characters in here that you love to hate like Eliza and her husband despite how nice he seems to be. I hope that there will be another book to continue their story and see what happens to them.
