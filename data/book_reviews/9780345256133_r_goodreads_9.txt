Overall enjoyed it!
I think what I liked most about this was the MC's control over herself and her actions/not allowing herself to be misused or flirted with and the male MC's subtle show of true character in his friendship with Allerton. Better than telling the reader that there's more to this 'beau' than meets the eye, she leaves it up to the reader to determine by judging his actions. And rather than a mopey MC, we get a dignified, realistic reaction throughout. Applause!
