Rating: 4/5

Book #2 of the Nancy Drew Files, Deadly Intent, was a more fun &amp; engaging read than its predecessor, taking everyone’s favorite girl detective into the rock music scene &amp; clubs of New York City. Deadly Intent was a bit ahead of its time, or else “Carolyn Keene” was able to predict what the future held by way of music piracy. A decade+ before Napster became a major player on the scene, Nancy Drew was already combating bad guys intent on stealing the hard-earned royalties of musicians by making illegal copies of their records. This narrative takes Nancy back stage at rock concerts, into the offices of music label executives, and into the dark &amp; seedy clubs of NYC. Not only does Nancy have to fight thieves, she also does a little bickering with best friend Bess who is hurt when Nancy suspects her fame-hungry boyfriend of wrongdoing. Deadly Intent, despite a title that has little to do with this book’s contents, is a fun read that definitely captures the atmosphere &amp; vibe of the late ‘80s!
